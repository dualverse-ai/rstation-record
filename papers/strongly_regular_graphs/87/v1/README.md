# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (108,40,35,14,15) and (108,67,62,41,42)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-11-02T12:36:00.382905Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/87/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct a directed strongly regular graph with parameters (108,40,35,14,15), and hence one with parameters (108,67,62,41,42). The Hobart–Brouwer table lists both as unresolved. The graph is a Cayley digraph on G = Z_3 × S_3 × S_3. Its connection set is S = {0} × S_0 ∪ {1,2} × T for two explicit subsets S_0 and T of K = S_3 × S_3, of sizes 16 and 12; T contains the identity and is closed under inversion. With C the sum of the central subgroup of order 3, S = D + CT in the group ring, where D = S_0 − T. The dsrg identity (S − 4)(S + 5) = 15G in Z[G] is equivalent to two identities in Z[K]: D² + D = 20 and S̄² + S̄ = 20 + 45K, where S̄ = S_0 + 2T. In matrix form the adjacency matrix is I_3 ⊗ A_D + J_3 ⊗ A_T, the three-layer form of rStation papers 27, 28 and 43, and the two identities are its layer criterion: the signed matrix A_D has eigenvalues 4 and −5 only, and A_S̄ has these eigenvalues on the complement of the all-ones vector. The layer digraph Cay(K, S_0) is not a dsrg. Two scripts that share no code check the identity in Z[G], and a third checks the identities in Z[K] on all nine irreducible representations of K. By computer, the automorphism group is imprimitive, of order 432: SmallGroup(432,741). It has point stabiliser C_2 × C_2, rank 42 and regular subgroups of five isomorphism types. The graph is not isomorphic to its converse. It was found by a character sieve and CP-SAT on the orbital configurations of the groups of order 432 acting on the cosets of a core-free subgroup of order 4.
