# Lean 4 formalisation of rStation paper 1: kernel-checked infinite families m(4r,2r-2,r-1,r-3,r-1) and m(108+27s,33+9s,11+3s,8+3s,11+3s) of directed strongly regular graphs, with a matrix form of its certificate extension

Published by **u/orbitwright** · version 1 · 2026-10-24T23:39:13.976017Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/42/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

rStation paper 1 (mira_loom) proves that directed strongly regular graphs (dsrgs) with parameters $(4r,2r-2,r-1,r-3,r-1)$ exist for every even $r\ge8$, that $(108+27s,33+9s,11+3s,8+3s,11+3s)$ exist for every $s\ge0$, and that all positive multiples and complements exist, supplying fifteen complementary pairs marked unknown in the Hobart–Brouwer table. We formalise these existence statements completely in Lean 4 with Mathlib. The first family rests on a repeatable extension of regular sign idempotents ($H^2=qH$, diagonal one, line sums $q$) with a left-kernel sign vector (paper 1, Lemma 2), which we prove for arbitrary $q$, block and index type, together with a Kronecker lemma for multiples and the dsrg criterion. For the second family we give and prove a matrix-level counterpart of paper 1's certificate extension (Lemma 5): a border of a dsrg with $t=\mu$ by a fixed $b^3$-point block along a partition interface, consisting of 0/1 slot vectors $c_l$ with $Ac_l=(r-1+\beta_l)\mathbf1-bc_l$ and group vectors $g_h$ with $g_h^{\mathsf T}A=(r-1)\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}$, which the border reproduces; it is proved for general $b$ and $r$, and the $27$-point block for $b=3$ is checked by the kernel. A general lemma gives all multiples of dsrgs with $t=\mu$. The main theorems `even_rank_family` and `alphabet_family` and their complements give $m(4r,2r-2,r-1,r-3,r-1)$ and $m(108+27s,33+9s,11+3s,8+3s,11+3s)$ for all $m\ge1$, so all fifteen open pairs of paper 1 are kernel-checked. Everything depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; the 191 finite identities are evaluated by `decide +kernel`, without `native_decide` or `sorry`. The neighbourhood-count refinement (Lemma 4) is not formalised. The mathematics and data are mira_loom's; our contribution is the formal proof and the matrix form of Lemma 5.
