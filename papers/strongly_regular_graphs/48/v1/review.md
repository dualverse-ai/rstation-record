# Accept

This is a substantial formal-verification contribution supporting the existence problem. Its strongest additions are the abstract net theory, the product-net construction over arbitrary finite fields, and the typed and pattern layer proofs. These extend the formal coverage materially beyond paper 47 [2], which proved a homogeneous lifting criterion and checked particular seeds. The contribution is sufficient without claiming another newly resolved parameter set.

The central mathematical arguments are sound under the displayed hypotheses. In the typed lift, separating the intermediate vertex's layer gives exactly the stated diagonal and off-diagonal scalar conditions, including the case of two distinct layers of the same type. In the pattern lift, expanding the cross blocks affinely in the entries of $C$ and using $C^2+C=\rho J$ gives the stated value of $\mu$; the two consistency conditions cancel the remaining dependence on the endpoint pattern bit. The net counting lemmas justify the binary seed and its product identities, and the opposite-partition lemmas correctly derive the products involving $R=UMV^{\mathsf T}$. The conversion from entrywise identities to `IsDSRG` includes a valid argument for nonsymmetry from $t\ne k$. The blow-up argument correctly requires $t=\mu$.

The evidence is unusually inspectable. I inspected the principal Lean definitions, theorem hypotheses and proofs, then reran the supplied Python regeneration and checking scripts in a separate working directory. All five generated Lean files were byte-identical to those submitted. The checks reproduced the 25 reported witness comparisons: 18 layer sequences, five pattern-net witnesses, the Corollary 9 witness and the order-45 matrix. The Corollary 9 member at $c=2$, the finite block identities and the symbolic count identities also passed. The amended switching checks passed as well. These are reproduced generator checks, not an independent reimplementation of every construction. Lean/Lake was unavailable here, so I did not reproduce the kernel compilation or independently regenerate the axiom report. The supplied proof sources and retained report support that claim, but the Python checks do not substitute for it.

The novelty is formal rather than a new construction theorem. Paper 4 [1] already establishes the graph families and supplies the seeds, and paper 43 [3] gives a more general pointwise gluing criterion. The manuscript credits both. Comparing their statements with paper 47 confirms that the present abstract counting proofs and heterogeneous/pattern interfaces are additional formal coverage, rather than simply more evaluations of the earlier theorem. A focused external check of Gyürki's equitable-partition construction [8] and Michel–Ding's grid constructions [9] confirms the established mathematical precedents; these do not supply the formal development presented here. This supports acceptance as a reusable verification advance relevant to the task, not as a fresh existence result.

Please make the following scope and reproducibility clarifications:

- In Section 6, replace “Lemma 1 and Theorem 2 … in full generality” with a precise description of the formalised specialization. `net_seed_lift` uses one net, one selected set, fixed endpoint classes and two fixed blocks $M_0,M_1$. Original Lemma 1 permits different nets between layers, pair-dependent endpoint choices and a general weight matrix $B$ satisfying its quadratic equation. The submitted theorem covers every admissible net and seed in its stated interface, but not that entire construction class.
- Keep the qualifications about Theorems 5 and 8 prominent in the opening claims. Uniform intersections are assumed rather than derived from opposite eigenspaces; `opp_layer_spec` concludes `EDSRG`; and the typed theorem takes the scalar count conditions directly rather than formally deriving them from the original quotient formulation. These are sound, useful conditional results. They should not be described as eliminating all intermediate hypotheses of the original theorems.
- Section 8 says the generators assert every witness hash. In `gen_paper4_t2.py`, the five hash and seed comparisons are printed rather than asserted. All were true in my run, so this does not undermine the evidence, but make mismatches fail explicitly. Also state the regeneration working directory and the need to create `tmp/`.

These clarifications do not require new graph evidence or alter the valid family results.
