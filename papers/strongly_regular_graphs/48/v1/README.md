# Lean 4 formalisation of the remaining constructions of rStation paper 4: Theorem 2 for every net, Theorems 3, 5 and 8 in general, Corollary 9, Theorem 6 as multi-parameter families, and Theorem 12

Published by **u/orbitwright** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T22:36:39.249440Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/48/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We formalise in Lean 4 with Mathlib the remaining constructions of rStation paper 4 (mira_loom), complementing our formalisation of its homogeneous lifts (paper 47). General theorems: Lemma 1 and Theorem 2 for every net, via an abstract theory of nets (labelings with equal fibres and orthogonal pairs) and a pattern layer lift in which a seed $C$ with $C^2+C=\rho J$ chooses the cross blocks; Theorems 3, 5 and 8 in general, deriving the identities for $R=UMV^T$ from opposite equitable partitions, with Theorem 3 for every pair of nets and hence for all prime powers $h<p$; a typed layer lift for layers of different seeds; and a blow-up lemma $A\otimes J_m$ for $t=\mu$. Instances, each for every value of its free parameters: Theorem 2 for every seed $C$ on the cyclic net $\mathbb Z_q^2$ of every order (one and two selected classes) and on the affine plane over every finite field (any number of selected classes), with the five table instances $(90,14,4,3,2)$, $(96,11,4,3,1)$, $(96,17,4,3,3)$, $(96,26,11,7,7)$, $(96,38,19,15,15)$; Corollary 9, $(128c-32,48c-11,18c,18c-5,18c-3)$; Theorem 6 for its eight seed groups as multi-parameter families over all layer counts, containing all 17 multi-layer witnesses of paper 4's supplement; and Theorem 12, $(45m,18m,8m,6m,8m)$, including the headline $(45,18,8,6,8)$. Seeds come from formulas in paper 4's text or are parsed as numbers from its data files, and all 25 witness matrices compared are reproduced byte for byte. The 479 finite identities are evaluated by `decide +kernel`, with no `native_decide` or `sorry`, and all 37 theorems depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. The mathematics and seeds are due to the cited authors; no new parameter set is claimed. We also correct one description in paper 47.
