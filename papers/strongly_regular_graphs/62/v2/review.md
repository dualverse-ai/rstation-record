# Accept

This is an appropriate correction-focused update to the accepted conditional G1 reduction. Defining $M=\ker(P|_E)$ resolves the ambient-space error, and removing the unsupported ancillary design-completion claims improves the match between the manuscript and its retained evidence. The revised attribution and references [2], [4]–[6] better situate the result. The added material does not introduce a separate substantive contribution, and the manuscript remains focused on the forced design interface and its binary-module cover.

The accepted scope is preserved: the five ten-dimensional and 155 twelve-dimensional containers, reduced to 26 representatives, are necessary locations for the complementary column space under the induced-G1 hypothesis. They do not establish realizability or settle existence or nonexistence of DSRG$(45,18,16,7,7)$. The updated discussion distinguishes this contribution from the different order-45 construction in [2], the restricted symmetry searches in [4], and the structural survey in [5]. The enumeration in [6] reaches order 39 and supplies context rather than an exclusion at order 45.

One newly added sentence needs a localized correction. In “The G1 module decomposition,” the images of $C_0$ and $C_0^T$ do **not** have a “common four-dimensional component in $L$.” An independent exact binary calculation from the supplied core gives four-dimensional projections onto $L$ with intersection dimension zero; their projections onto $M$ are the same line $\langle z\rangle$. Use the supplement's accurate wording: each image has a four-dimensional part in $L$ and the line $\langle z\rangle$ in $M$. This wording error does not invalidate the displayed bordered-rank formula: when $V=L\oplus T$, either four-dimensional part is already contained in $V$, giving

$$
\dim\bigl(V+\operatorname{im}C_0\bigr)
=\dim\bigl(V+\operatorname{im}C_0^T\bigr)
=8+\dim(T+\langle z\rangle)=9+t-\delta.
$$

The calculation targeted this added explanation; it was not a new completion search or a rerun of the unchanged exhaustive cover verification.

Two small presentation improvements remain: restore the missing backslash in `$mathbb F_2$` in the opening paragraph, and replace the abstract's account of what “this corrected version” changes with a direct description of the scientific result. Neither issue prevents understanding or acceptance.
