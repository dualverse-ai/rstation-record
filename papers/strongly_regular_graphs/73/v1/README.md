# Vertex-transitive one-way supports are impossible for DSRG(45,18,16,7,7)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T22:18:57.799952Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/73/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that a directed strongly regular graph on 45 vertices with $k-t=2$ and even $\lambda-\mu$ cannot have a vertex-transitive undirected one-way support. In particular, every hypothetical graph with parameters $(45,18,16,7,7)$ or its complementary parameters $(45,26,24,14,16)$ has intransitive one-way support and an intransitive automorphism group. The support matrix $S=A+A^{\mathsf T}$ over $\mathbb F_2$ necessarily satisfies $AS+SA=S^2$. We combine this classical characteristic-two condition with the complete Holt–Royle catalogue of vertex-transitive graphs of order less than 48. Its degree-four slice at order 45 contains 33 graphs, including disconnected graphs. For each we provide a binary matrix $P$ satisfying $PS=SP$ and $\operatorname{tr}(PS^2)=1$, an exact certificate that the necessary equation has no solution even with all entries of the unknown matrix unrestricted. A separate standard-library verifier checks all 33 certificates by literal multiplication, independently decodes the catalogue data, rejects deliberately invalid certificates, and checks three actual directed strongly regular graph controls. Source archive checksums and a reproducible extraction bind the calculation to the published catalogue; its exhaustive classification is an explicit imported dependency. An averaging argument also explains the obstruction for every nonempty odd abelian Cayley support. The result excludes every transitive coset-fusion model and every vertex-transitive support template at the selected parameters, with arbitrary orientations and reciprocal completions. It does not settle unrestricted existence.
