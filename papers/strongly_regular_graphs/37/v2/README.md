# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (54,23,22,9,10) and (54,30,29,16,17)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 2 · 2026-10-22T06:24:04.764278Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/37/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Hobart–Brouwer table lists the directed strongly regular graph parameters $(54,23,22,9,10)$ and the complementary $(54,30,29,16,17)$ as unresolved. We construct a graph with each set of parameters, so both exist. When $t=k-1$, the one-way arcs of a dsrg form a permutation $\pi$, and $A=U+P_\pi$ with $U$ symmetric. We show that $A$ is a dsrg if and only if $H=A+A^\top+(\mu-\lambda)I$ and $K=A-A^\top$ satisfy $H^2+K^2=(r-s)^2I+4\mu J$ and $HK+KH=0$. For $\mu-\lambda=1$, when every cycle of $\pi$ is a triangle, the second identity forces every block of $U$ between two triangles to be one of eight matrices. Each such block is zero, all-ones, a reflection $x\mapsto a-x$ of $\mathbb Z_3$, or the complement of one. The first identity then becomes four conditions on the triangle-level data $(M,a)$, where $M_{DC}\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ and $a_{DC}\in\mathbb Z_3$. For $v=54$ these data form an $18\times18$ integer matrix $W$ with entries $\pm1,\pm3$ and $W^2=49I$, together with a symmetric unit weighing matrix $G$ over the sixth roots of unity with $GG^*=13I$. The two are coupled by a common support and common signs. We found such a pair with a SAT solver after splitting the search into cases. We print the triangle-level data in full as two $18\times18$ digit arrays. A dependency-free script rebuilds the 54-vertex graph and counts every ordered two-step walk for it and for its complement. A second independent checker confirms both graphs. The graph has an automorphism group of order 3. At order 30, where no graph exists, all 288 integer matrices $W$ exist, but none admits a compatible $G$. So the obstruction there lies in the coupling between $W$ and $G$.
