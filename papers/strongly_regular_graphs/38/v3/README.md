# Existence of eighteen open DSRG complementary pairs and uniform signed-square families, including (N,(N-8)/2,(N+34)/4,(N-18)/4,(N-14)/4) for N>=674, N=2 mod4

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 3 · 2026-10-24T02:12:39.437290Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/38/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We resolve eighteen open complementary pairs of directed strongly regular graph parameters and construct uniform families on three signed-square lines. Five new primary cases have parameters $(N,(N-4)/2,(N+2)/4,(N-10)/4,(N-6)/4)$ at $N=38,46,62,86,94$; twelve have parameters $(N,(N-6)/2,(N+14)/4,(N-14)/4,(N-10)/4)$ at $N=54,62,66,70,74,82,86,90,94,98,106,110$; the remaining case is $(102,47,34,21,22)$. The first two lines exist at every admissible order at least 26 and 50, respectively, with credited known cases. We now also construct $(N,(N-8)/2,(N+34)/4,(N-18)/4,(N-14)/4)$ for every $N=50+52a+56b$, where $a,b\ge0$ and $a+b>0$, hence every $N\ge674$ congruent to 2 modulo4. Directed complements exist throughout. The method preserves the sign-matrix identity $P^2=q^2I$ and both sums $q$ by adjoining an irregular block whose row defect is cancelled by source eigenvectors. For $q=7$, a new 56-point block uses three left sign eigenvectors of sum $-2$ whose sum is also a sign vector. This relation survives both the 56-point and the earlier 52-point extensions, proving the uniform tail. The lower connecting rectangle has rank three. Compact finite certificates and standard-library constructors verify the graphs exactly; an independent integer-matrix audit checks repeated and mixed extensions and all thirteen uniform bases. The infinite claims follow from proved inheritance. The new uniform family adds no table row through 110: its 106-vertex case was already known, and the square-forty-nine 110-vertex pair remains unresolved. The uniform bound is sufficient; its optimality is not claimed.
