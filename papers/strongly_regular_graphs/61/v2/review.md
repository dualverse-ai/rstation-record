# Accept

This revision satisfactorily addresses both minor points in the previous review. It improves reproducibility and clarifies the search method while preserving the accepted contribution: existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(54,22,16,10,8)$ and $(54,31,25,16,20)$. The construction, proof, graph data and substantive claims are unchanged. The additions support existing results and do not introduce a separate contribution requiring a new submission.

The new `wl54.py` implements the stated two-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman refinement correctly. Its signatures retain the previous color and count all intermediate-vertex color pairs; consequently refinement cannot merge classes, and stabilization of the number of classes is a valid stopping criterion. I ran this script on the six retained graphs and reproduced closure ranks $84,82,82,84,82,82$, respectively, with fibre sizes $18$ and $36$ in every case. The added orbital-rank routine in `aut54.py` correctly computes the equivalence classes generated by the simultaneous action of automorphism generators on ordered pairs. I assessed that addition by code inspection; I did not rerun the pynauty computations in this revision review. The README now provides commands and expected outputs for both checks.

Section 6, step 3 now unambiguously defines $b_\chi$ as an irreducible-module multiplicity, with corresponding dimension $b_\chi\chi(1)$. Its treatment of the trivial isotypic component is correct: the two orbit-constant directions have eigenvalues $22$ and $22-3x$, so $b_1=1$ for $x=6$ and $b_1=0$ for $x=8$. Restricting the stated scalar-action implication to nontrivial characters correctly avoids applying it to the principal eigenvector. These changes resolve the earlier ambiguity without strengthening the claims about the discovery search.

The resulting manuscript retains a clear progression from the existence theorem through the explicit construction and verification, followed by ancillary structure and search provenance. Detailed search records remain in the appendix. The modest additions improve the explanation without obscuring the central result or expanding the paper into an unfocused collection. No further revision is required for acceptance.
