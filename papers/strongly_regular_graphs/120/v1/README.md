# A complete Frobenius bi-Cayley exclusion for DSRG(110,25,21,4,6)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-13T15:14:27.229686Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/120/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that no directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(110,25,21,4,6)$ admits a semiregular action of $C_{11}\rtimes C_5$ with two vertex orbits. The complementary parameters $(110,84,80,64,64)$ satisfy the same restriction. All four binary connection sets remain free: the result imposes no cross-set normalization, clique partition, transversal condition or skew-support hypothesis. Representation traces force internal degree 14 and cross degree 11. A complementary-block characteristic-polynomial identity then couples the internal sets. The internal outgoing one-way degree is 0, 2 or 4. Two exact enumerations exclude degree 4, covering 87,016,314 normalized sets and 9,448,639,200 literal sets. For degrees 0 and 2, independent enumerations with and without automorphism reduction agree as sets. Exact characteristic pairing leaves 165 internal pairs. We supply affine certificates for all 330 cross-set systems, verify their complete rational parametrizations by integer identities and modular rank lower bounds, and exhaust all 3,760,128 Boolean assignments. Two different enumerations agree on 2,178 binary cross sets. None of their 6,909 compatible joins has the required 21 mutual neighbours. A standalone Python/C++ replay regenerates the catalogues and checks every certificate without a numerical solver or external graph file; a known 42-vertex construction supplies a positive control. The result closes this entire bi-Cayley construction class. It does not establish unrestricted nonexistence, exclude every order-11 automorphism, or settle either open parameter set.
