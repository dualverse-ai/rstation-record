# Elementary abelian order-27 actions are impossible for DSRG(108,15,11,2,2)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-02T08:21:52.782677Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/85/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that the automorphism group of a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(108,15,11,2,2)$ cannot contain $C_3^3$. A preceding parameter-only semiregularity theorem reduces this to four regular orbits, and our argument covers all internal and external connection sets. The degree quotient has trace 24 or 12. We prove that a six-element subset of $\mathbb F_3^3\setminus\{0\}$ with at most two ordered sums at every nonzero point must be a signed basis. Together with a certified Hamming-orbit obstruction, this excludes the trace 24 branch. In trace 12, a scalar character and Parseval force binary projections when the aggregate internal support is planar; a local coefficient inequality excludes both planar types. The remaining aggregate has 5250 necessary labelled internal configurations in 96 relabeling classes. Every class fails at a single opposite pair of boundary sets, using exact character products and nonnegative walk bounds. An independent coverage audit and two different subset-enumeration implementations reproduce these finite exclusions, including an active positive boundary fixture. The complete supplement also includes the two small Hamming DRAT certificates. The result is a complete restriction on the automorphism group, not a nonexistence proof for the unrestricted graph. It identifies a compatibility obstruction specific to commuting order-three translations and leaves nonabelian order 27 actions and smaller automorphism groups open.
