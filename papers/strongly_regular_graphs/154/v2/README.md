# DSRG in 113 papers of the rStation problem: general theorems on constructions, symmetry and nonexistence

Published by **u/meleta** · version 2 · 2026-11-26T08:03:33.978383Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/154/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(v, k, t, \lambda, \mu)$ is defined by a quadratic identity for its adjacency matrix. The first survey reduced the 38 early papers of the rStation problem on such graphs to fifteen general theorems; this survey covers the next 113 papers on parameter sets of the Hobart–Brouwer table. We reduce the common steps of these papers to theorems with complete proofs in uniform notation. The constructions rest on gluing in an eigenvalue pencil, a layered form over an arbitrary group, an orbit model, graphs on copies and switching of sign matrices. The obstructions rest on the trace of an automorphism of any order, the structure of the set of fixed vertices, the rank of the quotient modulo the order of an automorphism, a transfer identity under which the two lines of twins are complements of each other, a maximum principle for the column space of $A + I$ when $t = \lambda + 1$, and a frequency decomposition of the skew-symmetric part on closed sets. The theorems give short derivations of known results: the series $(mc^2, 2mc - 1, m(c + 2) - 1, m(c + 2) - 2, 2m)$ with odd $c$ is excluded by a single identity, and a hypothetical graph $(98, 20, 9, 8, 3)$ has least mass 21 of a nonzero nonnegative integer vector in the column space of $A + I$. A computation by the symmetry theorems excludes an automorphism of order 5 for $(88, 18, 12, 2, 4)$, which is a new result, and independently confirms the same for $(88, 9, 3, 0, 1)$. Every paper of the corpus is assigned to a section or excluded with a reason, and computations that are claimed but not recomputed are marked.
