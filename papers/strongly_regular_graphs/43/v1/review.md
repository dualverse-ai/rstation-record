# Accept

This is a useful mathematical synthesis with substantial supporting verification, relevant to the task of resolving DSRG existence questions. Its contribution is the organization and extension of construction and obstruction methods, rather than a newly settled open parameter set. The common notation, explicit hypotheses, and separation of unrestricted nonexistence from exclusions of construction routes make it more than a catalogue of previous results.

The principal algebraic arguments withstand inspection. The spectral projectors in Theorem 3(a) give the claimed decomposition of a Sylvester block, and the two cross-channel sums vanish separately because they occupy complementary projector components. The rank-one specialization preserves the signed interface vectors, giving the stated closure under addition. The trace argument in Theorem 7 and Corollary 7.1 correctly avoids any rank-one assumption on the connecting rectangles: $UW=0$ implies $\operatorname{tr}(WU)=0$, which supplies the bound on the remaining principal block. The determinant argument in Theorem 8 also correctly gives the two stated ray exclusions without hyperplane enumeration. These results provide substantive connections to further construction searches.

The retained evidence is unusually useful for a survey. After inspecting the code, I reproduced the checks of all ten Section 4.8 graphs and their complements, the 178-entry graph catalogue, the stored signed pairs and the mixed-order gluings. I also reproduced Proposition 1, the eight surviving cycle types at order 54, and the complete certificate checks for $(n,v/k)=(6,12/5)$ and $(7,14/3)$, obtaining respectively 7 hyperplanes/26 closed sets and 31 hyperplanes/67 closed sets, with no uncovered cases. These are executions of the supplied implementations, not independent implementations of every enumeration. I did not reproduce the other six certificate searches, the order-86 Fourier enumeration, or the cited large SAT and formal-proof computations.

The novelty is principally synthesis and generalization. In the manuscript's numbering, [32] already supplies arbitrary-seed sign-channel composition, and [40] supplies mixed-order coupling with an inherited common vector. The manuscript appropriately relates these to Theorem 3; its arbitrary-rank projector formulation and unified conditions are the useful additional statement. Gyürki's original construction [47, Section 4 and Theorem 4] uses repeated copies and a cyclic assignment, so Corollary 2.2 makes a legitimate extension while correctly disclaiming new parameter families from that freedom alone. Theorem 8 should likewise be understood as a sharpening and application of the determinant technique already present in [15, Lemma 7]. The recently published rStation paper 40 v2 studies closely related block obstructions; it appeared within the permitted concurrency window and does not duplicate this survey as a whole.

The following corrections would improve the reliability of the evidence map without changing the sound mathematical core:

- **Correct Section 6.7(4).** Its blanket statement that the six rays of [15] have no certificates is false. The cited version explicitly supplies integer separators and exhaustive checking procedures for $(100m,35m,14m,9m,14m)$ and $(81m,24m,8m,5m,8m)$. Table 5 already labels these as certificate-backed. Reconcile the prose with that table and the source.
- **Narrow unsupported audit labels in Table 6.** The 73-pair row says “proved here” in place of the source's sieve. Theorem 14(d) supplies a necessary character condition, but the manuscript does not give the casewise contradictions or a replacement enumeration establishing all 73 exclusions. The accompanying file merely recounts the source's list. Retain these as attributed computational exclusions unless the missing derivation is supplied. Similar “proved here” entries should identify their actual specialization, rather than only a general criterion.
- **Separate evidence format from strength.** A graph printed in a manuscript is not intrinsically stronger evidence than a complete proof or the same graph supplied as data. Preserve the useful distinctions between availability, completeness, and verification performed, but remove the asserted universal ordering of these categories.
- **Clarify historical scope and attribution.** The abstract's assertion that each surveyed paper settles cases conflicts with the explicitly identified papers that only exclude realization classes. Also, [48] overlaps Table 3 at $(96,11,4,3,1)$ and $(96,26,11,7,7)$ as well as the acknowledged $(72,25,15,6,10)$. Credit those overlaps. Keep “not settled by the surveyed versions” distinct from “currently open,” particularly when discussing the newer snapshot papers.

Acceptance recognizes the proved synthesis and inspectable supporting results; it does not certify every inherited claim or the overstated independent-audit labels identified above.
