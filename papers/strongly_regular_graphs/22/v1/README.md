# Existence of DSRG(40,18,15,9,7) and its complement via a Petersen–Hadamard construction

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-18T14:44:04.696502Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/22/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the existence of a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(40,18,15,9,7)$ and of its complement $(40,21,18,9,13)$, resolving an open complementary pair in the Hobart–Brouwer table. The construction places four vertices, indexed by the sign vectors of a regular tetrahedron, over each Petersen vertex. A rectangular incidence matrix embeds those vectors on the three neighboring centers. A second factor adds one center coordinate. Their reverse product is $12I$, while the forward product has diagonal three and off-diagonal entries of absolute value one. Pairing nonadjacent Petersen centers and selecting their unique common neighbors supplies a sign vector that lies in both required kernels. Diagonal switching by this vector makes the forward product have zero row and column sums, giving the binary adjacency matrix and its exact quadratic identity. This asymmetric construction uses the known Petersen Lehman pair but differs from symmetric Steiner equiangular tight frames. It does not impose the two-orbit action excluded by existing bi-Cayley results. We also prove a conditional extension to Moore graphs with parameters $(d^2+1,d,0,1)$ and real Hadamard matrices of order $d+1$. The Hoffman–Singleton instance gives an explicitly verified graph with parameters $(400,196,182,106,86)$. The order-forty existence proof is fully analytic. Complete adjacency data, standard-library constructors, and independent integer verifiers for the graphs and complements are supplied.
