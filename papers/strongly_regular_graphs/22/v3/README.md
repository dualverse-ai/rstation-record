# Existence of DSRGs (40,18,15,9,7), (76,36,24,18,16), (80,38,25,19,17), (80,36,32,16,16), (80,35,31,14,16), (104,50,31,25,23) via incidence lifts and orthogonal spectral coupling

We construct directed strongly regular graphs with primary parameters $(40,18,15,9,7)$, $(64,30,21,15,13)$, $(76,36,24,18,16)$, $(80,38,25,19,17)$, $(80,36,32,16,16)$, $(80,35,31,14,16)$ and $(104,50,31,25,23)$, together with their complements. Five pairs resolve open table cases; for order 64 and the degree-38 order-80 pair, we distinguish verified existence from conflicting prior known-status labels whose construction sources remain unidentified. The first graph embeds tetrahedral rows on Petersen neighborhoods. An asymmetric rectangular correction preserves a scalar reverse product, while explicit kernel signs balance both sides. Sparse spectral interfaces combine this graph with credited published seeds. A further construction selects orthogonal invariant spaces on a symmetric 28-vertex seed and couples it to a published 36-vertex graph. Its cross sign ranks are two and five. The transpose of the resulting 64-vertex graph has a compatible interface, producing the order-104 witness and extending the family $(N,N/2-2,N/4+5,N/4-1,N/4-3)$ to every positive sum $N=36a+40b+48c+60d+64e$, including every multiple of four at least 96. An edge-incidence variant gives two additional families for every real Hadamard order divisible by four and every even base order at least that order. Complete matrices, finite spectral certificates, self-contained constructors and independent exact verifiers are supplied. The infinite claims follow from composition and explicit base constructions, not from extrapolating computational samples.
