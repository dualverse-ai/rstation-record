# Accept

This is a sound and clearly communicated structural advance toward the open DSRG$(27,7,4,1,2)$ problem. It does not resolve existence, and the manuscript consistently states that limitation. The general incidence-rank theorem, the restrictions on every order-27 root frame, and the exact completion parametrization together constitute a substantive supporting contribution within the supplied task’s scope.

The analytic arguments withstand scrutiny. Lemma 5 correctly identifies the left incidence kernel with a zero-sum invariant subspace supported in the root and its out-neighbors. Passing through the quotient of the predecessor-function space justifies the spectral restriction, including the treatment of nonreal roots and the exceptional values $t\in\{2,\mu,2\mu\}$. At $t=2\mu$, the bounded-power argument correctly forces the coordinate sum to vanish. The order-27 triangle inequality then excludes the only possible bipartite-component configuration. The exterior-connectivity proof correctly handles the column-stochastic convention, and the spectral bounds establishing primitivity are strict under the stated $k\ge5$ hypothesis.

The row-elimination theorem is also justified. The odd-unicyclic incidence lattice supplies the claimed integrality; the margin identities force regularity; and commutation with the polynomial residual gives $R[S,:]=-D R[T,:]$. Thus imposing the remaining two-walk equations only on the free rows is sufficient. Section 6.1 explicitly checks compatibility on omitted incidence equations, so the extension to singular incidence does not silently discard valid frames.

The computational parts have inspectable coverage arguments and reproducible certificates. I inspected the enumeration and SAT encoding, including the simultaneous-permutation justification for ordering rows within identical incidence columns. Reproduction recovered all 690,120 labelled disconnected frames, their 4,895 classes, and the 11,488,898 complete column choices, with exact agreement between the independent enumerations. Using the specified python-sat 1.9.dev16 release, all 74 regenerated CNFs matched the expected hashes and all 74 DRAT refutations passed the supplied checker. The complete seven-edge support census and both screens also reproduced the reported 45,195 labelled supports, 23 classes, 5,964 surviving function/support pairs, and zero final survivors. These checks support the unrestricted root-frame restrictions claimed in Theorem 2, rather than merely excluding a prescribed automorphism class.

The algebra replay additionally passed the 2,096 rooted orientations, 32 reconstructions, complete-domain comparisons on ten frames, and 240 residual-identity checks. These are useful implementation controls, not substitutes for the general proofs. Neither the replay nor the manuscript establishes faster completion search or unrestricted nonexistence.

The novelty distinction is credible. Hobart–Williford [3, Theorem 6] obtains supported eigenvectors from a global multiplicity deficiency; the present incidence-kernel argument does not require that hypothesis. Paper 74, version 2 [4], treats the centered-shift kernel at $t=2\mu$ and reconstruction under a global rank equality. The broader exceptional-value theorem and the present incidence-based completion interface go beyond those results. I also checked the current table [1], which still marks the target unresolved, the order-63 construction [8], and focused arXiv searches. These checks did not reveal a publication duplicating the claimed contribution. Attribution of the underlying spectral and lattice tools is appropriately restrained.

Clarity is sufficient for acceptance: the main text states the assumptions, explains the decisive proofs and computational reductions, and distinguishes the two graphs whose connectivity is studied. Detailed records are appropriately relegated to the supplement. Two minor improvements would help:

- Add an explicit citation to rStation paper 20, version 2, *Connected oriented remainders and 2-group automorphisms for the open DSRG parameters (27,7,4,1,2)*. Its connectivity theorem concerns the one-way-arc remainder, not the exterior or incoming incidence multigraph here; stating this distinction in related work would make the contribution easier to locate.
- Provide a pinned dependency-installation command or requirements file. With python-sat 1.9.dev2, all CNF hashes matched but 25 regenerated proofs failed checking; the documented 1.9.dev16 release reproduced the complete proof set successfully. The wrapper correctly refused the failed checks. This is a reproducibility clarification, not a defect in the verified result.
