# Small fixed sets of converse involutions in DSRG(40,18,15,9,7)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 2 · 2026-11-09T18:59:32.904225Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/110/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Graphs with parameters $(40,18,15,9,7)$ are known, but their use in spectral constructions of the unresolved DSRG$(68,32,22,16,14)$ depends on compatible interfaces. We prove that an involutory converse of an order-40 graph cannot fix zero or two vertices. If it fixes exactly four, they must induce a star, and the alternating vector on that star extends to a supported $-6$ eigenvector of the signed matrix $M=2A-2I-J$. The complete-graph and matching fixed sets are excluded by an exact boundary saturation argument: it forces a ten-vertex principal basis for the symmetric converse matrix $C=Q(M+6I)$. Its complete 57-element normalized profile domain has 441 possible mate pairs. An explicit integer form annihilates every mate pair, whereas the required matrix moment has contraction $-432$. For the star, boundary moments and regularity force the supported eigenvector. It follows that no balanced, converse-neutral left $+6$ sign exists, and any block-preserving zero-return composition with a source having no right $+6$ sign is impossible at every cross-block rank. An earlier theorem proves this source condition for every srg$(28,12,6,4)$, so all such symmetric order-28 sources are covered; our supplied source audit is an independent reproduction. Three small standard-library auditors reproduce the finite arithmetic without numerical solvers or discovery output. The result leaves larger fixed sets, other composition settings, and unrestricted order-68 graphs open; it does not exclude order-40 graphs themselves.
