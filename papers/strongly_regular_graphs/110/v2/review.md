# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects attribution and clarifies the scope of the accepted contribution. Sections 1, 2 and 6 now credit [5, Section 9.6] for the absence of adjacency-$4$ sign eigenvectors in every srg$(28,12,6,4)$, and describe the literal Chang-graph audit as a reproduction. The abstract and limitations consistently reflect this correction. The small-fixed-set theorem, its proof and its certificates remain unchanged, and the manuscript retains a clear focus on that structural obstruction.

The broader application is supported by the cited results. Section 9.6 of [5] supplies the stated argument for $T(8)$ and its transfer to the three Chang graphs by Seidel switching. For an adjacency matrix $B$ with these parameters, the eigenspace for $4$ is orthogonal to $\mathbf1$ and is exactly the right $+6$ eigenspace of $2B-2I-J$. Thus the prior result supplies the hypothesis of Theorem 2 for every symmetric order-28 source, without any additional source classification or computational claim by this paper.

The induced-subgraph result in [6] also supports the application in Section 2. It forces the complementary srg$(28,12,6,4)$ and balanced centered cross blocks with zero products. Under a block-preserving involutory converse, their signed versions satisfy the balance, neutrality and Sylvester equations required by Theorem 2. Together with Theorem 1, this excludes zero, two and four fixed vertices on the order-40 side. This is an immediate consequence of the accepted theorem and established results, appropriately included as a scope clarification rather than a separate substantive contribution.

One minor wording improvement: write “an **involutory** converse preserving…” in the new Section 2 corollary. The surrounding theorems and Section 7 make this restriction clear, but it should also appear in that standalone sentence; the argument does not establish the statement for arbitrary non-involutory converses.

The revision improves attribution and practical interpretation without expanding the paper into an unfocused collection. It remains a conditional obstruction relevant to the order-68 problem, not a resolution of unrestricted existence. Verification for this update consisted of checking the revised implications against [5] and [6]; the unchanged computational certificates were not rerun.

Reference numbers refer to the manuscript's bibliography.
