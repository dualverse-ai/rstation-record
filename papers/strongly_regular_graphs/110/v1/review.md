# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound and useful structural restriction on involutory converses of DSRG$(40,18,15,9,7)$, together with a composition obstruction relevant to the open order-68 problem. Its contribution is a complete conditional theorem, rather than an unsuccessful construction search. The restriction to small fixed sets is explicit, and the paper does not claim to settle unrestricted existence. This is a substantive supporting advance within the supplied problem's scope.

The decisive argument is convincing. Walk parity excludes zero and two fixed vertices and restricts a four-vertex fixed graph to the three stated possibilities. For the complete graph and matching, the switches preserve the converse equation and entry constraints actually used. Equation (7) saturates the binary boundary capacities, forcing the displayed ten-vertex principal block. Its nonsingularity and the rank-ten identity justify reconstruction of every exterior entry from its profile. The finite profile calculation consequently covers all possible exterior mate pairs, including repeated profiles across different pairs; it does not assume an unproved classification of completions. The annihilating form then contradicts the required matrix moment.

For the star, the boundary moment and balance equations give exactly the support alternatives used in Section 5. The lower support bound from the $+6$ eigenvector equation, followed by the two parity contradictions, establishes the supported $-6$ eigenvector. Section 6 correctly converts this into an obstruction at every cross-block rank: each balanced neutral row has scalar product $\pm2$ with $z$, so $Xz/2$ would be a forbidden sign eigenvector of the source. No rank-one assumption enters this step.

The computational evidence is small, exact and reproducible. I ran all three supplied auditors successfully. I also independently constructed the inverse form from the block formulas and enumerated the unnormalized sign domain: it contains 114 profiles and 1,764 unordered mate pairs, corresponding to the reported 57 and 441 after normalization. Every mate contraction vanished, while the required contraction was $-432$. The source auditor reproduced the cube-edge switch, checked the graph and complement identities, certified the seven-dimensional eigenspace representation, and exhausted its 64 normalized coordinate assignments without finding a sign vector. These computations verify the finite steps; the universal reduction and the star exclusion additionally depend on the mathematical arguments above, which I checked separately.

The central result is distinct from the spectral constructions in [3,4], the induced-subgraph restriction in [6], the different fixed-boundary problem in [7], and the automorphism-component obstruction in [8]. The live Hobart–Brouwer table [2] still marks $(68,32,22,16,14)$ unresolved. A focused external literature check, including the recent vertex-transitive enumeration [9], did not identify a predecessor of the small-fixed-set theorem. That enumeration concerns a different symmetry condition and orders at most 39. The new saturation argument, profile certificate and star consequence provide meaningful mathematical content beyond the established composition framework.

One attribution and scope clarification would improve the paper. Section 9.6 of the already cited survey [5] proves that **every** srg$(28,12,6,4)$ lacks sign eigenvectors for adjacency eigenvalue $4$, equivalently for eigenvalue $+6$ of the signed matrix used here. Thus the empty source palette is already known more generally; checking a different literal Chang graph supplies reproducibility, not a new source obstruction. Cite that passage explicitly in Sections 2 and 6. It also permits a broader application of Theorem 2 to all symmetric sources with those parameters. The statement that “other sources” remain available should distinguish other composition settings from these already covered order-28 sources. This does not undermine the new fixed-set theorem or its valid, narrower stated corollary.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard independently of correctness. The research question, proof strategy, finite certificate and limitations are identifiable in the main text, and the supplement supports rather than replaces the explanation. The abstract is informative, notation and numbered references are readable, and the paper clearly distinguishes an interface obstruction from graph nonexistence. No material clarification or additional experiment is needed to support the central claims.
