# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted contribution and improves its attribution, explanation and reproducibility. The structural argument excluding $(66,10,5,4,1)$ and $(91,12,6,5,1)$ remains central. The additions support the existing results and do not introduce a separate substantive contribution or strengthen the nonexistence claims beyond their accepted scope.

The corrected attribution is mathematically justified. Equal rows of $B=A+I$ are exactly equal rows of the complementary adjacency matrix $J-B$, so [4, Theorem 10(a)] gives $a\le\lambda+1$. The hypotheses of [7, Lemma 9] hold with $\alpha=\mu$, since $\alpha+q=\lambda+2<K$ is equivalent to $t<k$; Proposition 10 is therefore correctly described as an alternative proof of the known bound $a\le K-q$. Direct comparison also supports the acknowledgment of [8, Lemma 2] and the earlier quotient/rank arguments. These corrections appropriately narrow the novelty claims while retaining the paper's substantive structural contribution.

The revised organization remains coherent. Section 10 now summarizes the evidence, with detailed equality counts, mutation results and review records available in the supplement. The main text retains the decisive proofs and clearly distinguishes the handwritten arguments, Lean formalization and SAT-dependent case. Figure 1 usefully explains the blocks and special subsets of Theorem 14. Independent checks of its three examples reproduce the graph identities, block sizes, coefficient relations and special subsets, including the special block whose vertices all have $c_w=0$.

Independent counting over the updated table snapshot reproduces the totals of 700 parameter sets, with 609 constructed, 67 marked nonexistent and 24 open. It also reproduces the six inequality exclusions, thirteen equality cases, five open sets with $\mu=1$, and the sole narrowing to $\{4,9\}$ among the 19 open sets with $\mu\ge2$. Retaining the compressed LRAT certificate improves the durability of the existing order-28 result. Two minor figure clarifications are recommended. Change the alt text's “no arcs between blocks” to “inter-block arcs are omitted.” Also restrict the caption's convention that an unarrowed line represents mutual arcs to lines **inside blocks**: the upper connecting lines organize the diagram, and some arcs from $x$ to its out-neighbours are not reciprocated. Neither issue affects the proof or acceptance.
