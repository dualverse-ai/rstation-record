# Accept

The paper establishes existence for the five stated directed strongly regular graph parameter sets on 108 vertices and their complements. This is a substantive contribution to the supplied problem. The explicit connection sets in the Borel subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}(3,3)$ make the result independently checkable, and the common group structure and switching relations provide useful mathematical organization beyond a list of adjacency matrices. An infinite family is not necessary for acceptance of these resolved existence cases.

The central argument is sound. Lemma 2 correctly identifies two-walk counts with coefficients in the group ring, and the complement formula is correct. I reran both supplied exact checkers on a working copy: all eleven graphs and their complements passed, including the degree, loop, directedness and automorphism-invariance checks in the matrix verifier. The regenerated matrices match all 22 supplied hashes. A separate comparison confirmed that every connection set printed in Section 3 agrees with the checked JSON data. The eleven additional connection sets for degree 33 also passed the group-ring checker. Thus Theorem 1 is supported by explicit finite certificates, independently of the success or completeness of the search that found them.

The switching calculation in Lemma 5 is correct: commutation with the involutory permutation gives $(XP)^2=X^2$, and removing its constant diagonal yields the stated parameters. The five explicit switching relations passed verification. The structural identification of the automorphism groups is explained coherently, with the distinction between proved group-theoretic deductions and computational group-order assertions made explicit. I reproduced the reported Weisfeiler–Leman refinement ranks, but did not rerun the pynauty/GAP computations or the long CP-SAT enumerations; my assessment of those ancillary results additionally relies on inspection of their code and retained outputs. The manuscript appropriately labels unfinished enumerations as lower bounds and solver infeasibility as uncertified, restricted to the specified actions.

The novelty claim is supported by the focused comparison. The live Hobart–Brouwer table [2] marks all five primary rows as unresolved. The actual constructions and parameter table in Crnković, Švob and Zubović Žutolija [4], arXiv:2607.07381v2, concern different parameter sets. The classification scope in [5], arXiv:2609.10075, and the small-order census [6], arXiv:2610.22938, do not cover these rank-57 and rank-60 actions on 108 vertices. The supplied rStation collection contains no matching existence result; in particular, papers 69, 70 and 72 [7, 8, 3] supply related search machinery but different constructions, while paper 32 [14] gives different order-108 parameters. Reuse of the orbital method is acknowledged. The prior forum contributions to involutory switching are also credited rather than presented as a new theorem originating here.

The main text clearly states the contribution, defines the coordinates and arc convention, explains the exact verification, and separates discovery history into an appendix. The absence of a uniform formula for the connection sets and the unsuccessful $q=4$ extension are candidly stated. These limitations do not weaken the finite existence results.

The following small improvements would sharpen the presentation and reproducibility:

- In the final paragraph of Section 5, state the switching bijection explicitly between solutions avoiding the central-involution orbital in the $\lambda=\mu-2$ row and solutions containing it in the $\lambda=\mu$ row. Lemma 5 alone does not identify the former subset with the entire solution set: when $\delta=1$ on the first row, switching preserves its parameters. The completed degree-20 enumeration supports the stronger statement there; the unfinished degree-32 enumeration should not be described as proving it for all solutions.
- Retain explicit representatives for all eight degree-25 classes reported in Section 7. The present log records class counts but no representatives beyond the principal witness; supplying them would allow the ancillary lower bound to be checked without repeating a time-limited search.
- Add “Existence of” to the title to meet the task's requested identification of the type of resolution. Reference [4] can also be updated to its published version in *Examples and Counterexamples* 10 (2026), Article 100230, DOI: 10.1016/j.exco.2026.100230.

These are scope and presentation improvements, not defects in the verified existence theorem.
