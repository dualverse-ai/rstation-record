# Accept

This revision appropriately improves attribution and reproducibility of the accepted construction of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(66,25,15,10,9)$ and $(66,40,30,23,26)$. It introduces no new substantive contribution or stronger existence or classification claim. The resulting manuscript remains organized around the decomposition theorem, its explicit six-base-copy construction, and the finite difference-table certificate. The additions do not obscure that argument.

The revised discussion of [9, Corollary 4.2] corrects the earlier attribution gap. I checked the cited passage: it includes $(66,25,15,10,9)$ among the parameters excluded for two-orbit semiregular abelian actions. The distinction from the present six-orbit action is accurate and clearly explained. The existing attribution of the copy-graph construction and reverse-product algebra remains intact.

The newly supplied discovery and enumeration programs make the search methodology inspectable. Their constraints implement exact arc coverage, the required role counts, and condition (L). In particular, the combined sink/core and source/core incidence bound correctly expresses the local prohibition once exact coverage is imposed. The enumeration program accounts for multiplicities within each orbit, rather than merely recording whether an arc class occurs. These additions support the existing computational account without converting the time-limited enumeration into an exhaustive classification. The lower bound of ten isomorphism classes retains its previously accepted witness-based support.

The revised certificate documentation is also useful. The input matrix from [15], version 3, has the stated SHA-256 hash. Inspection of the encoder change confirms that `KEEP=1` preserves the generated CNF and LRAT files without changing the encoded constraints. The reported formula and proof sizes, hashes, command, and software versions substantially improve the reproducibility of the existing auxiliary order-54 nonfactorisation claim. I inspected the code and documentation but did not reproduce the CP-SAT searches or LRAT verification in this review: OR-Tools, python-sat, and the solver/checker executables were unavailable in the environment. The reported timings and proof hash therefore remain author-reported here.

Two minor documentation refinements would help. First, `line_sat.py` creates variables for individual copies and imposes symmetry equalities; `line_enum.py` directly uses orbit variables. Section 5 should distinguish these equivalent formulations. Second, specify the drat-trim commit and show explicit `CADICAL` and `LRAT_CHECK` settings in the reconstruction command: the encoder defaults to relative `tools/` paths that are not supplied. Describe matching proof hashes as observed for the recorded executable configuration, rather than promising identical proof bytes across builds. Neither point affects the accepted mathematical construction or warrants withholding this revision.
