# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (66,25,15,10,9) and (66,40,30,23,26)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-11-05T01:28:10.239695Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/99/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (66,25,15,10,9) and, by complementation, (66,40,30,23,26). Both rows are open ("?") in the Hobart–Brouwer table. The construction is a copy graph. Let D_σ be the digraph with a source, a core of σ points carrying all arcs between them, and a sink, with every arc from the source to the core, from the core to the sink, and from the source to the sink. Let n = j(σ²+σ+1)+1. Suppose the arcs of the complete digraph K*_n are partitioned into nj copies of D_σ so that every point lies in the core of jσ copies, and so that whenever the sink of one copy is the source of another the two cores are disjoint. We prove that the digraph on the copies, with e → f when the core-plus-sink of e meets the core-plus-source of f, is a dsrg(nj, (σ+1)²j−σ, jσ²+σ+1, σ²+jσ, (σ+1)²). The proof uses a factorisation A + σI = XY with YX = (jσ−1)I + J. We also show that a node automorphism of prime order larger than σ and j has no fixed points. For σ = 2 and j = 3 we give six base copies on Z_11 × {0,1} whose orbits under Z_11 form such a decomposition of K*_22. A 42-entry difference table, checkable by hand, verifies the cover, the regularity condition and the local condition. The resulting dsrg(66,25,15,10,9) has automorphism group of order 11 and is not isomorphic to its converse. A time-limited enumeration of Z_11-invariant decompositions gives at least ten pairwise non-isomorphic graphs, five converse pairs. Two independent exact checkers verify the stored matrices. The same theorem would give the open row (86,46,37,24,25), and a second construction of (54,29,22,15,16), if suitable decompositions exist; we have not found them.
