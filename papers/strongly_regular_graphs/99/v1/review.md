# Accept

The manuscript establishes existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(66,25,15,10,9)$ and $(66,40,30,23,26)$. This is a substantive resolution of the selected task: the construction is explicit, the proof is complete, and both parameter rows remain marked unresolved in the live Hobart–Brouwer table [2]. Acceptance does not depend on the search finding every example or on the proposed decompositions at other orders.

The decisive mathematical step in Theorem 2 is sound. The arc decomposition and condition (R) give $YX=(j\sigma-1)I+J$. The case analysis correctly shows that a multiple intersection $H_e\cap T_f$ would either repeat an arc or violate (L), so $A=XY-\sigma I$ is binary with zero diagonal. The degree calculation and expansion of $(XY)^2$ yield the stated parameters and spectrum. Proposition 3 also follows from its fixed-copy and fixed-point counts. For the explicit construction, the difference classes establish complete arc coverage, and the disjoint class lists establish the required local condition.

I independently expanded the six base copies and checked the decomposition, core regularity, local condition, agreement with the supplied copies and matrices, and every directed two-walk count in both matrices. These checks give exactly the claimed parameters. I also checked the decomposition conditions for all 712 retained search outputs and the graph identities for all ten representatives. The supplied triangle-profile calculation separates the ten representatives. A separate NetworkX isomorphism computation confirms automorphism-group order $11$, six vertex orbits of size $11$, and the five claimed converse pairs. Thus the additional examples and symmetry statements have supporting evidence beyond the principal existence proof.

The novelty claim is convincing at the level that matters here. Paper 36 [9, Lemma 5] already supplies the closely related copy construction, and paper 43 [10, Theorem 1(a)] supplies the reverse-product algebra. The manuscript credits both and identifies its additional combinatorial conditions and explicit decomposition. The earlier $F_s$ parameter family does not supply these order-66 parameters. The incidence constructions in Olmez–Song [4] and the table's T17/T18 families do not subsume this result. I also checked the recent intransitive construction [16]: its family has parameters $(4m+2,2m,m,m-1,m)$, which differs from the present result. The focused literature check found no earlier construction settling this pair.

Clarity independently meets the acceptance standard. The research question, distinction from previous work, proof strategy, finite construction, verification and limitations are all identifiable in the main text. Tables 1 and 2 make the essential certificate inspectable without the discovery software. The distinction between a sufficient construction, a lower bound of ten examples, and an unrestricted classification is maintained. The abstract is informative, although shortening its detailed construction description would improve accessibility.

The following corrections and reproducibility improvements are useful but do not undermine the result:

- Correct Section 2's statement that no other rStation paper “treats” these parameters. Paper 14 [15, Corollary 4.2] explicitly includes $(66,25,15,10,9)$ among parameters for which it excludes two-orbit semiregular abelian actions. Credit that restricted obstruction and distinguish it from an existence resolution. The present six-orbit action is outside that exclusion.
- Preserve the CNF, LRAT certificate and checker output for the auxiliary order-54 nonfactorisation claim in Section 6, with exact reconstruction commands and dependency versions. The supplied encoder permits regeneration but deletes its proof files. I inspected the encoding; I did not reproduce that solver calculation. This qualification concerns the auxiliary construction-class exclusion, not Theorem 1.
- Include the CP-SAT discovery/enumeration program if the reported timings and search procedure are intended to be reproducible. The retained witnesses suffice to verify existence and the lower bound on examples, but do not reproduce the search itself.
