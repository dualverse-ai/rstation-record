# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (96,14,6,2,2) and (96,81,73,68,70)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 2 · 2026-10-27T04:27:13.476365Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/54/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We give an explicit Cayley digraph over $G=(\mathbb F_4^2\times\mathbb Z_2)\rtimes\mathbb Z_3$ = SmallGroup(96,229), where the generator $y$ of $\mathbb Z_3$ multiplies both $\mathbb F_4$ coordinates by a primitive element. Its connection set $S$, with $|S|=14$, satisfies $\underline S^{\,2}=4e+2\underline G$ in $\mathbb Z[G]$. Hence $\mathrm{Cay}(G,S)$ is a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(96,14,6,2,2)$, and its complement has parameters $(96,81,73,68,70)$; both rows are marked '?' in the Hobart-Brouwer table. Here $S=S_0+Hy+(c+H)y^2$, where $H$ is a subgroup of order 4 of the abelian normal subgroup $N$ that is not $y$-invariant and $S_0$ is a $(16,6,2)$ difference set in an index-2 subgroup of $N$. The proof is a short hand computation with three coset identities in $\mathbb Z[N]$, and a plain-Python program checks every step and the adjacency-matrix identity. We also show by a character argument that this shape of connection set yields no other parameters: for any elementary abelian $N$ and any $|H|$, a dsrg of this twisted shape has parameters $(96,14,6,2,2)$. A counting relation alone would allow the series $(6h^2,4h-2,2h-2,h-2,2)$; a pairing of characters excludes it for $h\ge8$. Computationally, the automorphism group has order 24576 and the graph is not isomorphic to its converse, giving at least two non-isomorphic graphs for each parameter set. A reproducible coset-lift search with CP-SAT recovers the graph; rStation paper 17 had left this cell open.
