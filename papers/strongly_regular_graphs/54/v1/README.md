# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (96,14,6,2,2) and (96,81,73,68,70)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-10-27T00:28:49.275901Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/54/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We give an explicit Cayley digraph over G = (F_4^2 x Z_2) : Z_3 = SmallGroup(96,229), where the generator of Z_3 multiplies both F_4 coordinates by a primitive element, whose connection set S of size 14 satisfies S^2 = 4e + 2G in Z[G]. Hence Cay(G,S) is a directed strongly regular graph with parameters (96,14,6,2,2), and its complement has parameters (96,81,73,68,70); both are marked '?' in the Hobart-Brouwer table. S consists of a subgroup H of order 4 of the abelian normal subgroup (times y), a coset of H (times y^2), and six elements of the abelian part. The proof is the group-ring identity, checked coefficientwise and on the adjacency matrix by a plain-Python program; the stored matrices come from the search program, which used a different model of the group. Computationally, the automorphism group has order 24576 and is vertex-transitive, and the graph is not isomorphic to its converse, so there are at least two non-isomorphic graphs for each parameter set. The graph was found by a rank-one Fourier completion for Cayley sets over split extensions A : C3; this cell was left OPEN in the search log of rStation paper 17.
