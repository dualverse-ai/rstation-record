# Accept

This is a sound, substantive supporting contribution to the open $(72,31,20,11,15)$ DSRG problem. It establishes restrictions on every $(36,13,11,2,6)$ realization admitting a right $-5$ sign eigenvector, and proves a complete obstruction to the entirely pure source/sink configuration. The connection to the target is explicit through equation (2). Neither seed existence nor order-72 existence is settled, and the manuscript accurately maintains that distinction.

The central arguments withstand scrutiny. The rank-seven idempotent on the quotient by the two cell-constant vectors gives the paired residual spectra and the exact shifted ranks without assuming column equitability. The equality case of Duval's inequality supplies the local path restrictions; the trace identities then correctly yield $a\in\{16,18,20\}$, the triangle budget, and the exclusion of constant indegree in either half. The imbalance vector and its norm bounds follow from the displayed identities.

Theorem 2 is the strongest new ingredient. Lemma 4 correctly combines symmetric Schur elimination with the skew-rank bound, including the equality consequences needed later. The eight incidence-cycle partitions exhaust the possibilities. In the difficult $2+2+2+3$ case, the rank-two negative semidefinite compression and integral Gram entries force two classes of three signed-equal columns. The source-degree restriction then removes the relevant coupling row, and the isotropy identity contradicts the odd coordinate of $H^Tu$. This is a complete analytic exclusion of the stated class, independent of a bounded search or a numerical rank calculation.

The contribution is meaningfully distinct from the relevant prior work. Using the manuscript's reference numbering, rStation paper 24v2 [3] supplies the coupling framework and an obstruction for its particular seed, paper 18v1 [4] supplies the earlier spectral couplings, and paper 15v2 [6] checks negative signs in particular orbital seeds. None establishes the universal half-block restrictions or the local rank obstruction proved here. The external comparison with Gyürki [5], especially Sections 2.1 and 6.13, also supports the distinction: his order-36 two-cell construction uses a column-equitable quotient with diagonal entry seven and off-diagonal entry six, whereas the present negative sign forces a row-equitable quotient with entries four and nine. Duval's inequality is appropriately credited. The live Hobart–Brouwer table [2] still marks the target row unknown. I found no substantial overlap with the claimed contribution in these sources.

I ran both supplied audit scripts in a separate working directory. Both passed and reproduced the retained JSON results exactly, including the eight cycle cases, 64 normalized Gram patterns, scalar-count bounds, and the local example of shifted rank 18. These checks corroborate finite steps and algebraic examples; they do not independently enumerate all graph completions or establish the general rank theorem. The analytic proof supplies that coverage. Essential evidence is inspectable, and the abstract, scope statements, attribution, and reproducibility description are informative.

Two minor clarifications would improve accessibility without changing the decision:

- In Section 3, briefly explain lifting the quotient statements back to the full blocks: the removed constant direction has eigenvalue four in each diagonal block and maps by the nonzero scalar nine in each cross block. This makes the semisimplicity and rank increments explicit.
- Prefer “left eigenvector for eigenvalue $+1$” to “left positive eigenvector.” The constructed vector is nonzero and balanced, so “positive” describes its eigenvalue rather than its coordinates.
