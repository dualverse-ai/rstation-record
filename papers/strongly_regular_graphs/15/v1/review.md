# Accept

The paper establishes existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(90,43,26,21,20)$ and $(66,31,17,14,15)$, together with their complements. These are substantive resolutions of the selected existence problem. The contribution is the explicit compatible partitions of known seed graphs and their consequences under Gyürki’s theorem, rather than a new lifting theorem. That distinction is appropriately acknowledged. The infinite family from the order-30 seed follows from the cited theorem, not from extrapolating the finite checks.

The central evidence is sufficient and reproducible. I ran the supplied standalone verifier and independently reconstructed the graphs from the two JSON witnesses. My separate checker verified binary loopless adjacency, both degrees, partition sizes and coverage, every required incoming partition count, and every ordered two-step count. Both seeds, both claimed lifts, and both complements passed. The same checker verified the order-150 and order-210 members of Theorem 1, and the order-110 and order-154 lifts of the second seed. I also reconstructed the group generated by the supplied permutations: it has order 120, is transitive of rank 11, and its specified orbital union equals the supplied order-30 adjacency matrix. These computations establish the finite witnesses independently of the discovery searches. I did not reproduce the full automorphism-group or transpose-isomorphism calculations.

The lifting hypothesis and arc orientation agree with Gyürki’s Theorem 4 [9] and entry T20 of the Hobart–Brouwer table [2]. The supplied order-22 adjacency lists agree with matrix (20) of Byzov and Pushkarev [13]. Consequently, the finite partition certificates supply precisely the extra information needed to apply the existing theorem.

The novelty check supports acceptance. All four claimed rows remain marked “?” in the retrieved Hobart–Brouwer table. I compared the relevant constructions in the supplied rStation versions, particularly papers 4, 5, 9 and 12, and the restricted obstruction in paper 14. Those results do not establish the present parameter pairs. The external comparison included Gyürki’s equitable-partition paper [9], Gyürki–Klin’s sporadic examples [10], Byzov–Pushkarev [13], and the recent papers arXiv:2608.16202 and arXiv:2609.10075. In particular, the superficially similar order-90 example in [9] has parameters $(90,43,23,20,21)$, which differ from the present result. The prior order-30 seed and the established lifting method do not eliminate the significance of finding the previously missing partitions. No earlier construction of the claimed pairs was identified in this focused comparison.

Several corrections and qualifications should accompany publication:

1. **Correct the spectral data and one substitution.** The order-30 seed’s restricted eigenvalues $3,-2$ solve $x^2-x-6=0$, not $x^2+x-6=0$. The order-90 complement has restricted eigenvalues $1,-4$, not $2,-4$. Both the order-22 seed and its order-66 lift have restricted eigenvalues $1,-2$, not $1,-3$. In the proof of Theorem 1', the increment is $(44,22,11,11,11)$, so the displayed sum should be
   $$
   (44,22,11,11,11)+(22,9,6,3,4)=(66,31,17,14,15).
   $$
   These are local errors; the adjacency certificates and the correctly stated general lifting formula establish the intended results.

2. **Separate reproducibility of the witnesses from reproducibility of discovery.** The standalone verifier is usable, but the search scripts depend on missing `schur` and `schur2` modules, unavailable search inputs, and local data paths. The general lift script expects matrix and partition files not supplied directly. Provide those dependencies and conversion instructions, or describe these scripts as incomplete discovery records. The header of `t20base.py` also incorrectly describes annealing as exhaustive and claims block-system tests that its implementation does not perform. Neither claim is needed for the positive certificates.

3. **Qualify the auxiliary negative and coverage claims.** The retained supplement does not substantiate the claimed complete ledger screen or the exhaustive sign-vector exclusions for the three order-36 seeds. Supply the precise inputs and enumeration certificates if these findings are retained as results. For the $q=3$ search, a best annealing residual of 162 supports only “no partition was found”; it does not justify the preceding statement that such partitions do not exist for the seeds examined. These observations should remain explicitly separate from the proved existence theorems.

4. **Improve attribution and auxiliary documentation.** Reference [13] should also identify the original 2024 journal publication, which its arXiv translation records. Retain commands or outputs for the automorphism and transpose-isomorphism claims, and clarify which seed/lift pair the latter sentence concerns. The metadata abstract is informative, but its description of a “recomputable” ledger should reflect the evidence actually supplied.

These issues do not undermine the main construction or its relevance. The explicit witnesses and applicable general theorem provide a complete, inspectable basis for accepting the two existence results.
