# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (45,13,6,3,4), (63,19,8,5,6) and their complements (45,31,24,21,22), (63,43,32,29,30)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-10-19T04:08:44.728054Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/27/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(45,13,6,3,4)$ and $(63,19,8,5,6)$ exist, and hence, by complementation, so do graphs with parameters $(45,31,24,21,22)$ and $(63,43,32,29,30)$. Both complementary pairs are marked open in the Hobart–Brouwer table, which lists no construction for any of the four sets. We give two non-isomorphic graphs of order 45 and one of order 63. Each has vertex set $\{0,1,2\}\times\mathbb{Z}_{3q}$, $q\in\{5,7\}$, with an arc $(i,x)\to(j,y)$ exactly when $y-x$ lies in one of nine explicit base blocks. The full automorphism groups are $C_5\times S_3$ and $C_7\times S_3$, each acting regularly on three vertex orbits. Both parameter sets lie on the progression $(9q,3q-2,q+1,q-2,q-1)$, whose restricted eigenvalues are $1$ and $-2$. For one graph of each order, every block is invariant under inversion of the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ factor of $\mathbb{Z}_{3q}$. The adjacency matrix then has the form $I_3\otimes X+(J_3-I_3)\otimes Y$, and Fourier analysis over $\mathbb{Z}_3$ reduces the dsrg equation to $N^2+N=2I$ for $N=X-Y$, together with a trivial-character equation for $X+2Y$. The graphs were found with an exact constraint model over block-circulant digraphs, optionally with a multiplier symmetry. The existence proof is a direct verification. A standard-library script rebuilds each graph from its blocks and checks every ordered two-path count of the graph and its complement. The other open members of the progression, $(27,7,4,1,2)$, $(81,25,10,7,8)$ and $(99,31,12,9,10)$, are discussed but not settled. No infinite family is claimed.
