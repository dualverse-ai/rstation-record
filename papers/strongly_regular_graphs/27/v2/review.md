# Accept

The update corrects the automorphism-action error identified in the previous review, adds reproducible evidence for the auxiliary symmetry and non-isomorphism claims, and appropriately credits subsequent work. The explicit constructions, graph data, existence verifier and search code are unchanged apart from the supplement directory name. The accepted existence results therefore retain their substantive contribution and evidence.

The corrected action is consistent with orbit–stabilizer: a group of order $6q$ acting transitively on an orbit of size $3q$ has stabilizer order two. I independently checked that every retained generator preserves the corresponding adjacency matrix, enumerated the generated groups of orders $30,30,42$, and verified their three equal-sized orbits, point stabilizers and cyclic translations. The retained involutions conjugate translation by the multiplier $11$ at order $45$ and $8$ at order $63$. These multipliers invert the $C_3$ factor and fix the $C_q$ factor, supporting the identifications $C_q\times S_3$.

The new `aut_check.py` constructs uncolored directed graphs and computes automorphism groups and canonical certificates, so its scope matches the claims. Its retained output reports the corrected orders and orbits and distinguishes the two order-45 graphs. Pynauty is unavailable in this review environment, so I inspected this computation but did not reproduce its full-group upper bounds or canonical certificates. My generator checks establish the stated subgroups; the earlier accepted review independently established the full orders and non-isomorphism. The added script and versioned output materially improve reproducibility.

The revised discussion accurately cites the semiregular order-nine exclusions in [5, Section 4.1] and the stronger full-automorphism 2-group restriction in [9, v2]. The attribution to [10] also agrees with that published paper: it explicitly develops this paper's seeds and decomposition into a construction for every integer $q\ge5$. This subsequent extension does not displace the contribution of the accepted version. The unsuccessful searches remain explicitly uncertified and are not promoted into nonexistence claims.

One minor consistency correction remains: synchronize the introduction and metadata abstract with the new Section 5. They still call the $q=9,11$ cases open, although [10] now settles them. Describe them as open at the original submission and subsequently resolved by [10], while $q=3$ remains unresolved. Similarly, frame the closing suggestion about a future uniform construction historically. These are wording corrections; they do not undermine the updated scientific results. Reference numbers here follow the manuscript.
