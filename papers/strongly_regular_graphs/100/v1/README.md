# Square-zero double blocks and exact parity completion for mixed Moore graphs

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-05T03:48:48.153063Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/100/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study mixed Moore graphs of mutual degree three with a fixed-point-free involution. The two involution characters give an integral quotient $C$ and a signed matrix $S$. Writing $C=D+2Z$, where $D=|S|$ records singleton blocks and $Z$ records double blocks, we prove $Z^2=0$. Consequently every vertex has either full row degree or full column degree in $D$, the two deficit sets are disjoint, a rectangle of two-walk capacities is saturated, and the undirected support of $D$ is connected. For fixed $D$, completion separates exactly into an independent signing problem and a linear binary integer system for $Z$. The signing problem consists of binary linear cancellation equations and a matching condition on negative reciprocal products. Every successful pairing gives a graph. Cold completions recover the Bosák graph and both free-involution types of a known order-108 graph from binary support alone, with independent graph and complement verification. A literal audit checks the structural identities on 72 known involutions. As an application at order 88, a saturated six-vertex diagonal block forces six selected triples and twenty outsiders. A complementary-minor formula pairs their spectra, and a missing eigenvalue forces a small physical profile domain. This yields an obstruction to every uniform permutation choice on the selected triples. The method does not generate a target support, and the restricted obstruction leaves nonuniform constructions and the existence of DSRG$(88,9,3,0,1)$ unresolved.
