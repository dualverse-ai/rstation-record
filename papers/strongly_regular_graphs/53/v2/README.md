# Sign matrix constructions and rigidity in an ordered-pair DSRG family

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 2 · 2026-10-27T06:08:31.795671Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/53/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We give an algebraic construction and two rigidity results for the ordered-pair DSRG family $\mathcal F_p=(p(p-1),(p-1)^2/2,(2p^2-7p+7)/4,p(p-3)/4,(p^2-3p+4)/4)$. At order42, a normalized Paley cross block in $\mathbb Q[C_7:C_3]$ reduces diagonal compatibility to two three-by-three sign equations. Exhausting their 512 possible inputs gives 68 legal pairs, which combine into 534 diagonal pairs. An explicit group-ring inverse recovers the final block and leaves exactly twelve complete graphs, three labelled copies of each of the four published classes of Paper52. Independent full adjacency checks and explicit source permutations verify the construction. For each of those four sources, its labelled one-way arcs also determine the entire degree18 graph: the unrestricted symmetric complementary-support anticommutant has dimension four and exactly two sign completions, recovering the graph and its converse complement. Integer kernels and exact modular-rank certificates cover all completions without imposing automorphisms or degree constraints. For every prime $p\ge7$, $p\equiv3\pmod4$, we prove that order-$p$ automorphisms of a graph in this family are fixed-point-free. We also exclude the entire natural doubly regular tournament fibre model. If $(p-1)/2$ is prime, these results force every affine subgroup $C_p:C_{(p-1)/2}$ to have two regular orbits. The new finite construction explains existing graphs and is complete only for its specified cross. No new isomorphism class or resolution of the open order110 case is claimed.
