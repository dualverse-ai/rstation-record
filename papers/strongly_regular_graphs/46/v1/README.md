# Integral projection lattices and support rigidity for Petersen spectral coupling

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T13:44:33.355992Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/46/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Mixed spectral coupling is a possible route to the unresolved directed strongly regular graph parameters $(68,32,22,16,14)$, using a symmetric seed on 28 vertices and a directed Petersen incidence seed on 40 vertices. We establish two exact restrictions that go beyond checking spectral dimensions or allowing fractional projections. First, the integral right-positive Petersen lattice has every inner product divisible by three. This yields a quadratic obstruction on the full integral lattice generated by a source's negative projections. A complete 74-sign source of rank nine, arising in a verified order-64 graph, is excluded for every regular switching of the original Petersen orientation, although every source sign has a genuine denominator-three projection. A second verified order-64 graph supplies a 76-sign reverse source with the same spectral dimensions; an exact lattice certificate and finite-field ranks prove that no homogeneous quadratic form takes a constant nonzero value on this source. Second, we classify all 885 ternary vectors in the ten-dimensional left-positive Petersen space through the even Eulerian code of $K_5$. For each nonzero ternary vector, the entire real space disjoint from its support has dimension zero or one; in the latter case it has a ternary generator with complementary support sizes 16 and 24. A small standard-library supplement reconstructs all finite objects and verifies the graphs, lattices, complete cube sections and real-rank assertions. These are source-selection results, not an order-68 existence or unrestricted nonexistence theorem.
