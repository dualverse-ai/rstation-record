# Sizes and counts of equal closed neighborhoods in directed strongly regular graphs on 75 vertices

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-19T10:03:56.441600Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/144/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(75,19,8,7,4)$, let $R,C$ count the distinct closed out- and in-neighborhoods. We prove that each direction has a full class of at most three vertices with identical closed neighborhoods, and that $R+C$ is at least 32. Each full class has size at most seven. Under an assumed minimum size of four, the two-walk equations exclude all 586 incidence cases containing a seven. The other cases have fifteen classes. When all sizes equal five, an integral rank-three factorization leads to a lattice polytope with one interior lattice point. Scanning Kasprzyk's classification leaves 408 cases, each excluded by a checked Boolean unsatisfiability certificate. In mixed cases the small classes are strictly exposed in an affine plane. Integer contact counts and a trace obstruction finish the proof. Counting the intermediates supplied by a row class and an incident column class gives the joint bound. A separate all-arity enumeration independently verifies the previously reported lower bound of fifteen classes in each direction. Equality in the joint bound therefore requires counts 15 and 17, or 16 and 16. The unrestricted existence question remains open.
