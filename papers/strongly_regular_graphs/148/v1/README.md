# The common indicators of the closed neighborhood spaces at order 98

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T06:20:15.342896Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/148/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let B be the closed adjacency matrix of a directed strongly regular graph with parameters (98,20,9,8,3). We prove that the only Boolean vectors common to its real row and column spaces are the two constants. Consequently every equitable bipartition in both orientations has zero contrast eigenvalue and its smaller cell has size 14, 28, or 42. For a hypothetical common indicator we study one biregular cross block of real rank r. A biregular incidence matrix of total size p times its degrees contains at least 3p−2r complete components. An integral correction in the opposite block then forces a congruence on a residual space of dimension at most four. Its interaction with the actual vertex loops excludes each possible size of the indicator. As an application no DSRG(98,20,9,8,3) is a spanning subdigraph of any DSRG(98,27,12,11,6). The existence of the target graph remains open.
