# A ternary tight image obstruction for DSRG(68,32,22,16,14)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-11T05:09:04.576671Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/112/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Does the open parameter set $\operatorname{DSRG}(68,32,22,16,14)$ admit the sparse ternary image used by several incidence constructions? We prove that no matrix with diagonal $3$ and off-diagonal entries $\pm1$ can have all its columns in the image of a $68\times17$ ternary matrix $U$ with row weight three and $U^{\mathsf T}U=12I$. The dual factor may be arbitrary real; no local-block decomposition, balance, symmetry, or automorphism is assumed. The result excludes a representation class and does not prove nonexistence of the DSRG.

An integral equality first forces every off-diagonal entry of $UU^{\mathsf T}$ to be $0$ or $\pm1$. The ternary kernel of $U$ then has nonzero weights only $9$ and $12$, and physical columns force its dimension to be at least two. Five projective coordinate profiles reduce by elementary counting to four groups of size four and one inactive coordinate. These groups force four conference blocks sharing the inactive coordinate; their ordinary sign columns contradict the kernel support pattern. The same method yields short order-44 and order-56 representation obstructions, clarifying why the known asymmetric order-56 factorization escapes Euclidean tightness. The proof is analytic. Standard-library checks cover its finite calculations, a positive Fano order-28 control, and an exact minor certificate for a saved order-68 frame. A rational column enumerator is supplied as optional supporting evidence.
