# Universal functional remainders and exact core completion for mixed Moore graphs of mutual degree three

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-04T14:34:35.102052Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/95/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study mixed Moore graphs of diameter two with mutual degree three, equivalently directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(k^2+k-2,k,3,0,1)$. Every ordered mutual edge determines an induced functional remainder on $3(k-1)$ vertices and a core on $(k-1)^2$ vertices. The remainder consists of a prescribed star and a functional digraph exchanging two equal parts; its indegrees remain unrestricted. Two distinguished core columns vanish. The other core vertices split into $k-2$ physical parts, one of size $k-1$ and the others of size $k$, with exactly one outgoing core neighbor in each other part. We give the corresponding boundary incidence counts and an exact finite completion procedure for an arbitrary fixed core: enumerate packing profiles, close their forced successor map, and solve the remaining binary linear equations. A complementary-minor identity relates the core spectrum to the functional map. An independent exact census verifies the representation at all 732 ordered mutual edges of Petersen, Bosak, and both known 108-vertex mixed Moore graphs. Supplied 81-vertex cores uniquely reconstruct both 108 graphs. In separate physical completion experiments, fixing only the two blocks from the core to the remainder and within the remainder suffices to recover the other blocks of both known 108 orientations. The result supplies a universal representation and reusable completion operations for the open $(88,9,3,0,1)$ problem; it neither constructs nor excludes that graph.
