# Accept

This update preserves the accepted existence theorems and their evidence, corrects the overbroad converse statement, improves attribution, and supplies reproducible support for the secondary symmetry claims. The new comparison at order $72$ is supported. I found no material scientific regression in the changes.

I reproduced the supplied nauty computations with pynauty 2.8.8.1. For the triangle graphs with $3\le n\le10$, the automorphism orders are $12n^2$; only the $n=3$ member is isomorphic to its converse. The four sporadic graphs have the stated automorphism orders and are not isomorphic to their converses. The supplied order-$98$ sweep graph is isomorphic to the converse of the triangle graph, but not to the triangle graph itself. The revised finite scope of these statements is appropriate; these computations establish no corresponding assertion for $n>10$.

For the new order-$72$ comparison, I used the matrix in rStation paper 4 v1 [12] and the two matrices retrieved from the public data accompanying [10]. All three satisfy the complete DSRG definition with parameters $(72,25,15,6,10)$. The reproduced automorphism orders are $2$, $2304$, and $2304$, compared with $432$ for the triangle graph. Canonical comparisons confirm the claimed non-isomorphisms, including comparisons with the converses. The different automorphism orders already suffice for this distinction, since taking the converse preserves the automorphism group. Thus the triangle construction supplies an additional isomorphism class beyond those three examples; this is not a classification of all graphs with these parameters.

The regenerated automorphism generators agree exactly with the supplied generators. Independent enumeration confirms their group orders, preservation of arcs, and inclusion of the sporadic construction groups $K$. I inspected the GAP subgroup calculations and retained output, but did not rerun GAP, which is unavailable here. Their method is appropriate: enumerating all conjugacy classes of subgroups and selecting transitive subgroups of order $v$ tests precisely for regular subgroups. Together with the full automorphism computation, this supports the stated Cayley and non-Cayley conclusions. The additional group identifications and regular-subgroup classifications remain supported by the supplied GAP computation rather than an independently reproduced GAP run.

The related-work additions correctly identify the two order-$72$ examples in [10, Section 4.1.1] and the different parameter families of [11, Theorems 4.1 and 4.3]. The comparison of their complementary degrees and eigenvalues is correct. Explicitly crediting [5, Lemma 6] for the index-two reduction improves the distinction between the established framework and the triangle connection sets. These additions do not displace the accepted contribution.

Minor improvements would make the new evidence easier to maintain: record the pynauty version and hashes of the external comparison matrices, and add explicit binary-entry and in/out-degree checks to `symmetry_check.py`'s `isdsrg` routine. Also identify paper 4's order-$72$ construction specifically as its Theorem 5 / Section 8.1 equitable-partition lift; the current nearby reference to product nets is less precise. None of these points affects acceptance.
