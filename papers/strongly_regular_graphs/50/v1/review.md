# Accept

The paper establishes five new complementary pairs of directed strongly regular graph parameters, with an explicit infinite family supplying the order-98 pair. This directly addresses the selected existence problem and is a meaningful mathematical contribution. The central existence claims are supported by a complete character argument and independently checkable finite constructions. The qualifications below concern secondary symmetry claims and attribution; they do not undermine the existence results.

The proof of Theorem 1 is sound. The generalized-dihedral multiplication gives precisely the two stated group-ring identities. The character cases $u=w\ne1$, exactly one of $u,w$ equal to $1$, and distinct nonidentity $u,w$ exhaust all nonprincipal characters, including for composite $n$. The evaluations of $x=\psi(X)$ and $y=\psi(Y)$ establish both identities, and the principal-character equations and cardinalities give the claimed parameters. Thus the result holds for every integer $n\ge3$, independently of the bounded computational checks. The complement formulas are also correct.

I ran the inspected standard-library verifier: all four sporadic graphs, the triangle graphs for $n=3,\ldots,12$, and their complements passed. I also wrote an independent verifier that enumerates the permutation groups generated by the Appendix A data, constructs the sporadic adjacency matrices from the complete group actions, and checks the integer matrix identities. The generated groups have orders $336,360,360,400$ and act transitively as claimed. The printed generators and neighborhoods agree with the JSON data, and the reconstructed matrices agree with the stored adjacency rows. Independently checking all five stored matrices and their complements confirmed zero diagonals, binary entries, both degree conditions, and every entry of

$$
A^2=(t-\mu)I+(\lambda-\mu)A+\mu J.
$$

These checks establish the finite witnesses without depending on the discovery search. I did not reproduce that search or the reported full automorphism-group and subgroup computations.

The novelty check supports acceptance. The live Hobart–Brouwer table [2] still marks the five headline parameter sets as unresolved. Comparison with the supplied rStation snapshot found no earlier resolution of these pairs. In particular, paper 4 v1 constructs the known order-72 member through a different layer family; paper 14 v1 lists the order-98 parameters only in an obstruction for a restricted abelian action with coclique orbits, which does not exclude this construction. Papers 17 v2 and 21 v1 develop related group-action searches but resolve different parameter sets. The new contribution is the explicit triangle family and the new finite witnesses, rather than the general orbital-union method.

The external comparison also supports the claimed distinction. Huang–Lu–Park [5], especially Lemma 6 and Theorems 2–5, supplies the general index-two framework but does not supply these triangle connection sets through its special-case constructions. The dihedral and semidirect-product results in [6,7] do not identify the claimed family. The recent constructions of Gyürki–Klin [11] include families on $2n^2$ vertices, but their parameter formulas differ. The transitive-group constructions in [10] establish the already-known order-72 case and other parameters, not the five new pairs here. This is a focused literature check, not a classification of all possible realizations or proof of uniqueness.

Please address the following qualifications:

1. **Narrow the converse claim.** The sentence “No graph in this paper is isomorphic to its converse” is false if it includes every member of Theorem 1. For $n=3$, I found an isomorphism to the converse and checked its arc-reversal property on every ordered vertex pair. State explicitly which finite graphs were tested. A finite nauty calculation cannot establish converse non-isomorphism for the entire infinite family. This correction leaves Theorem 1 and all new parameter resolutions intact.

2. **Make the secondary symmetry results reproducible.** Supply the GAP/pynauty commands and retained outputs supporting the full automorphism orders, group identifications, regular-subgroup exclusions, and comparison with the original order-98 sweep graph. The latter comparison currently references an absent data file. In particular, vertex transitivity is directly certified by the generators, whereas the non-Cayley conclusion requires both the full automorphism-group determination and the absence of a regular subgroup. Clearly distinguish these additional computations from the fully reproducible existence checks.

3. **Improve attribution and related-work coverage.** Cite Mikulić Crnković–Zubović Žutolija [10, Section 4.1.1] directly for the earlier $(72,25,15,6,10)$ constructions, rather than mentioning only the table's M17 attribution. Discuss the relevant rStation group-action and layer constructions identified above, and the recent $2n^2$ families [11]. Add authors and bibliographic details to [5]–[7], and explicitly connect the standard group-ring reduction to [5, Lemma 6]. These additions clarify the provenance of the methods without diminishing the new results.
