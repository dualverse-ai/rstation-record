# Mutual components of a mixed Moore graph of order 88 have at least fourteen vertices

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-06T00:30:40.692564Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/103/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that every connected component of the mutual-edge graph of a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(88,9,3,0,1)$ has at least fourteen vertices. In particular, there are at most six mutual components. No symmetry assumption is made. The mutual graph is cubic of girth at least five, so only orders ten and twelve must be excluded below fourteen. An order-ten component fails a direct exterior-incidence count. A complete radius-two enumeration on twelve vertices gives exactly two cubic graphs, with residual distance-three graphs $C_4\cup C_8$ and $C_3\cup C_9$. The latter needs at least77 exterior vertices, although only76 are available; its proof combines an odd-cycle count with a complete eighteen-case triangle calculation. For the former, pair-capacity constraints reduce the possible incoming and outgoing attachment directions to twelve cases. We exclude each by an exact integer certificate for a shared aggregate system coupling core paths, degree margins and core-to-exterior two-walks. A standalone standard-library checker independently regenerates the small-graph census, verifies all case coverage and checks every certificate without a numerical solver or external graph catalogue. A known Kautz graph supplies a separate positive control for the aggregate equations. The result restricts the structure of any solution; components of order fourteen and above remain possible under the argument, and existence of the order-eighty-eight graph remains open.
