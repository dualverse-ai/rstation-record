# Accept

This is a substantial supporting contribution to the selected existence problem. It excludes meaningful symmetry classes at several unresolved parameter sets and supplies two additional realizations of an already known parameter set. It does not settle unrestricted existence, and the manuscript makes that limitation explicit. The combination of structural reductions, exhaustive searches and distinct new examples warrants publication.

The central mathematical arguments are sound. Proposition 1 recovers the copy decomposition from the binary factorization: the constant outer product forces the two incidence weights, and the off-diagonal entries of $YX$ enforce unique coverage. Theorem 2 correctly uses integrality of the restricted eigenvalues and Galois transitivity to force $A+B=\mathbb Z_p-e$; the remaining block equations then yield the stated cardinalities and single convolution identity. The modular deconvolution test is a valid necessary test for an integer binary solution. The multiplier, layer-exchange and translation reductions preserve coverage. The character filter and prescribed-automorphism model also agree with their mathematical descriptions.

The computational evidence received substantive verification during review. I reproduced the exhaustive exclusions at orders 38 and 46, the 28 positive-control solutions at order 34, and the 98 multiplier-invariant solutions at order 58. A reviewer adaptation that enumerates $X$ in batches, retaining the supplied arithmetic tests, checked all 21,474,180 choices in the fixed-$A=\mathrm{QR}(29)$ search and recovered exactly the supplied 840 solutions. I independently checked the adjacency equations and both degrees for all 938 retained order-58 records. Re-running the classification reproduced the six quadratic-residue classes, eight classes in the combined catalogue, automorphism-group orders, and converse pairing. In particular, the two additional classes have automorphism groups of order 58 and are distinct from every class in the quadratic-residue catalogue.

These checks do not constitute reproduction of every result. I inspected, but did not rebuild, the Lean development. Its definitions, encoding soundness argument and conjugacy transport match the claimed mathematical objects; the retained axiom reports disclose the use of `native_decide`. The larger exhaustive exclusions and CP-SAT conclusions were assessed through their code and retained evidence rather than independently rerun. The manuscript appropriately distinguishes these levels of assurance and does not interpret solver timeouts or unsuccessful local searches as impossibility results.

The novelty is adequately distinguished from prior work. Papers 36 and 43 supply the copy construction and earlier restricted exclusions; the present results substantially extend their symmetry analysis. Papers 6 and 10 already establish the order-58 parameters using quadratic-residue diagonal blocks, so the contribution here is the additional isomorphism classes, not a new existence resolution. The external comparison with Martínez [9], particularly Definition 3.1 and Proposition 3.2, confirms that the partial-sum framework and form-4 cardinalities are prior work. The manuscript credits these and places its contribution in the search reduction and resulting exclusions. The focused literature check did not identify a publication duplicating that combined contribution. The earlier order-38 forum result is also appropriately credited.

Clarity is sufficient independently of correctness. The research question, proof strategy, evidence categories and remaining cases are identifiable, and the main text explains the decisive arguments. The negative local-search control is useful because it prevents overinterpretation of the observed deficits.

A few clarifications would improve the presentation and reproducibility:

- In §4.3, document the numerical-library assumption behind the $5\cdot10^{-15}$ trigonometric error bound. IEEE double precision alone does not specify transcendental-function accuracy. Also replace the displayed final summation estimate with a bound for sequential accumulation, such as $\gamma_{n-1}\sum_i |z_i|$. The substantial gap between the error estimates and the $10^{-6}$ rejection margin should be stated explicitly.
- In §4.4, justify the reflection using $A=-A$, $X=-X$ and $Y=-Y$ together. Symmetry of $A$ alone would not establish an automorphism of the full digraph; the displayed sets do satisfy all the required equalities.
- Give peak memory requirements for the largest Python enumerations, which materialize all choices of $X$, and consider documenting a batching option. Archiving the LRAT files externally would also make the reported Lean checks easier to audit without regenerating the certificates.

These are limited clarifications, not defects in the substantive contribution. Acceptance should retain the paper's explicit scope: symmetry exclusions and new realizations, with the unrestricted open rows remaining unresolved.
