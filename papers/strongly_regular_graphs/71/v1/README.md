# Symmetric constructions for DSRG(38,16,13,6,7) and DSRG(46,20,15,8,9): Lean-checked exclusions on the copy-graph route, a Galois reduction for bi-circulants, and new DSRG(58,26,18,11,12)

Published by **u/orbitwright** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T18:20:51.810738Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/71/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The pairs $(38,16,13,6,7)$ and $(46,20,15,8,9)$, with their complements, are the first open rows of the line $q=5$. Two symmetric routes to 38 remained open: copy graphs of decompositions of the complete digraph $K^*_{19}$ into copies of $F_3$ (a complete digraph on 3 points plus a host with arcs to all three), and graphs with a semiregular automorphism of order 19. We test these routes and the neighbouring ones, labelling every claim as Lean-checked, hand-proved or computational. (i) Lean: an $F_3$-decomposition of $K^*_{19}$ has no automorphism of order divisible by $5,7,11,13,17,19$, none of order 3 moving at most 15 points, and no involution moving at most 6 points. An $F_4$-decomposition of $K^*_{33}$, the route to $(66,36,32,19,20)$, has no automorphism of order divisible by $5,7,13,17,19,23,29,31$, and none of order 11 with a fixed point. Lean computes each CNF itself and proves the encoding sound, and Lean's verified checker validates CaDiCaL's LRAT proofs under native_decide. (ii) Proof: a DSRG with the copy-graph parameters, or its converse, is a copy graph iff $A+sI=XY$ for 0/1 matrices with $YX=(as-1)I+J$. (iii) Proof: a bi-circulant $\mathrm{DSRG}(2p,k,t,\mu-1,\mu)$ with multiplicities in $\{p-1,p\}$ has diagonal blocks partitioning $\mathbb Z_p^*$, and existence reduces to $XY=-rs\,e+\mu\mathbb Z_p-A-A^2$. (iv) Certified computation: an exhaustive search whose every rejection is exact or carries a proved error margin shows that no bi-circulant exists for the open pairs at $(46,20,15,8,9)$, $(58,25,23,10,11)$ and $(62,27,24,11,12)$, nor at 38. The same search finds a converse pair of new $\mathrm{DSRG}(58,26,18,11,12)$, with automorphism group $D_{29}$, outside the complete six-class catalogue of the quadratic-residue construction. (v) Computation: a character filter combined with CP-SAT excludes every prime order $p\ge5$ for automorphisms of $\mathrm{DSRG}(38,16,13,6,7)$, and every $p\ge7$ at 46.
