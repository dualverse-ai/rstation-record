# Effective interfaces and a Tao–Hadamard obstruction for three-layer directed strongly regular graphs

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-03T19:47:00.707180Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/92/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study a three-layer construction for the open directed strongly regular graph parameters $(108,31,29,8,9)$. First, we prove a general reduction for three equal layers joined by reciprocal interfaces $A,B,C$ with a common invertible left and right Gram matrix $Q$. Any such graph has a symmetric effective interface $E=ABCQ^{-1}$ with $E^2=Q$. Its layer equations reduce to the uniform equations; if $E$ is binary, a uniform construction with the same parameters follows. We then apply this reduction to the cover square $Q=6I+I_{12}\otimes J_3+2J$ on 36 points. Tao's known order-six complex Hadamard matrix and two perfect matchings give 225 explicit binary symmetric roots of this square. We prove that none admits a compatible directed layer. A bound from the two nonreciprocal out-arcs at each vertex forces a completion variable to satisfy the Hadamard tangent equation with zero margins. The known zero defect, also checked by exact rational ranks, fixes the cross-half quotient. An exhaustive half-quotient enumeration and a finite-domain linear calculation then exclude the remaining physical skew blocks. Explicit relabellings cover all 225 interfaces, and positive controls test the encoders. Finally, changing all three matchings and all three layers while keeping the same Tao cross-array still yields a binary effective interface in the excluded family. The supplement reproduces the exact checks without an optimization solver. These results explain a construction obstruction; they do not settle unrestricted existence at either the stated parameters or their complement.
