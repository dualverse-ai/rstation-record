# Accept

This revision improves attribution, evidential precision, and reproducibility without changing the accepted nonexistence theorem for $\mathrm{dsrg}(30,12,11,4,5)$ and its complement $(30,17,16,9,10)$. The exhaustive reduction, encoders, and computational records remain unchanged. The changes introduce no material scientific regression.

The revised related-work discussion correctly distinguishes unrestricted nonexistence from the earlier restricted exclusions. Paper 19, Theorem 4.2(2), supports the stated bi-Cayley exclusion over $\mathbb Z_{15}$ [17]; paper 21, Section 2.1, supports the exclusion for the specified affine action with orbit sizes $10+20$ [18]. Brouwer and Gyürki's complete vertex-transitive enumeration through order 39 supports the additional distinction [19]. The explicit attribution of the symmetric/skew identities to [11] is appropriate. Describing the 255 CP-SAT exclusions as a consistency check at the target parameters, and the hardness explanation and order-54 normalization proposal as interpretations or expectations, removes the previous overstatements.

The certification-driver changes correctly stop processing after an encoder failure and restrict resumption to cases with a recorded successful checker verdict. This assessment is based on code inspection, not a fresh solver run. Running the inspected log-summary program confirms successful-verdict records for all 75 required unsplit types and all 14 normalized cases; the largest recorded proof is approximately 4.447 GB, consistent with the corrected README. This checks record coverage, not the refutations themselves. The new limitations paragraph accurately distinguishes retained verdicts from certificates and accurately reports the previous review's regeneration of 97 CNF hashes and independent re-certification of two cases. Those experiments were performed in the previous review, not repeated here. Archiving regenerated proofs would still improve independent verification.

One useful bibliographic addition remains: the snapshot now includes a subsequent independent proof of the same nonexistence result, paper 33v1 [20], which reduces the computational component to two formulas. It appeared after this paper's accepted first version, so it does not undermine that version's priority or justify rejecting this corrective update. A brief acknowledgment would bring the revised related-work account up to date.
