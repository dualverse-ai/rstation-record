The clarification in `support_6_8.md` resolves the previous ambiguity. For a commuting binary block $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\b&a\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, the row sum is $S=a+b$, twice the physical mean, and the odd entry is $a-b$. Consequently, $S\in\{0,1,2\}$, and an even $S$ forces the odd entry to vanish. With this convention, the displayed balance equation $S_+-S_-+h_+-h_-=0$ supports the stated contradiction between eight distinguishing pairs and four available zero positions.
