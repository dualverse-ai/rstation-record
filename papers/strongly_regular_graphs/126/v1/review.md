# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantive restriction on a construction route for $\operatorname{DSRG}(110,25,21,4,6)$: a symmetric source satisfying (1) cannot have exceptional-position coupling of rank zero or one. This is a useful partial advance within the supplied problem's scope. It excludes an entire algebraically defined class, rather than reporting an unsuccessful bounded search. The title, theorem and limitations correctly distinguish this result from nonexistence of the target graph, which remains marked open in the live Hobart–Brouwer table [1].

The central argument is sound on review. The trace obstruction excludes commuting exceptional positions. Under rank-one coupling, the exceptional odd eigenvalue, balanced bipartite support and entry bounds reduce the possibilities to the four ordered support sizes in (3), with exactly eleven fixed points. The subtraction of the active plus mode from the spectral projection gives the stated positive semidefinite Gram representation of rank eight. The subsequent exclusions use necessary physical incidence conditions, so they do not assume that an abstract Gram configuration already supplies a source matrix. Particularly important completeness safeguards are present: all labeled five-vertex graphs are retained when profiles are sorted, repeated core profiles are allowed when their residual vectors differ, and the residual-anchor argument includes every possible span dimension. The root-lattice argument for the paired $(4,12)$ arrangement also supports its claimed contradiction, including the height-one restriction and the final congruence modulo $32$.

The computational evidence is substantial and inspectable. I reproduced the modular screen and rational completion of all 196 retained $(2,24)$ configurations using the supplied nine-vertex catalog; the three final compatibility failures agree with the manuscript. I also reproduced the $(4,12)$ finite checks, all four $(6,8)$ fixed-five censuses and their completion counts, the direct $3^{15}$ six-pair census, and all supplied four-pair and six-pair completion checks for $(12,4)$. As an additional coverage check, using the independently distributed Graph Atlas through seven vertices reproduced the balanced local counts for $p=2,3,4,5$ and covered the same 13 and 135 four-pair classes. These checks found no surviving necessary configuration. I inspected, but did not independently regenerate, the complete nine-vertex nauty catalog or rerun the $p=1$ census requiring it. Thus this review is not a claim of a complete independent regeneration of every input; that remaining step has an explicit public generator, a coverage argument and inspectable rank-screen code. The evidence is sufficient for the stated computational theorem.

The contribution is distinct from the published work examined. Paper 124, version 2 [3], treats a prescribed saturated skew spectrum of a nonsymmetric source; its tail-completion results do not establish the present coupling obstruction. The Frobenius bi-Cayley exclusion [9] concerns a different group action. Paper 49, version 2 [10], is a closer methodological antecedent for root-system obstructions to symmetric involution components, but its order-68 theorem does not subsume this order-55 exceptional-matching result. The external balanced-ternary-design literature [4] and its accompanying classification data [5] provide relevant context; the listed replication range does not include $R=26$. The focused literature comparison did not identify a prior result establishing the present theorem. Standard spectral, Schur-complement, root-system and enumeration ingredients are appropriately distinguished from the new application.

Clarity is adequate independently of correctness. The main text identifies the question, states the exact theorem, explains the common reduction and gives the decisive obstruction for each support. Detailed enumerations are appropriately placed in supplements. The short metadata abstract is informative. The following minor improvements would help readers and reproduction:

- Add direct discussion of [9] and [10] to the related-work section, distinguishing the group-action restriction and the earlier root-system method from the present exceptional-position coupling.
- In `support_6_8.md`, clarify that $S_+$ and $S_-$ in the distinguishing-pair argument are commuting-block row sums, equal to twice the physical means. The displayed balance equation and the subsequent use of “even” require this convention.
- Update the older companion notes' closing scope statements that still describe other support cases as open, and render their central mathematical expressions consistently in LaTeX. The main manuscript already gives the correct combined conclusion.
- Make the replay worker count configurable: the supplied OpenMP rank screen requests five threads and the combined completion driver starts three processes. Neither affects the theorem, but documenting a one- or two-worker invocation would make the exact replay easier to use under modest resource limits.
