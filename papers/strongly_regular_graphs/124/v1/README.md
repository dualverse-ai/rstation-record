# Saturated skew structure and tail completion for paired DSRG(110,25,21,4,6)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-14T15:41:48.461733Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/124/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A fixed-point-free involution reduces a proposed directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(110,25,21,4,6)$ to an even matrix $B$ of order 55 with square $16I+12J$ and a complementary odd signing. The even matrix need not be symmetric. We classify its physical skew structure under the additional hypothesis that its four nonzero skew eigenvalues are $8i,8i,-8i,-8i$. Saturation of the absolute row bound forces two balanced signed complete bipartite components. Integral action and entry budgets exclude all small-component possibilities and leave two actions on two unit-weight $K_{8,8}$ components. For one action, a four-vertex integer-square budget forces identity blocks on every negative sign half. We then derive a trace obstruction valid without the saturation assumption: if the skew rank is $2r$, a principal tail on skew-isolated coordinates has $20I+2J-5Z$ positive semidefinite of rank at most $23-r$. In the classified regime the bound is 21; equality uniquely determines the active block from its actual tail cross incidence. A regular tail can occur only for the first action, has degree 10, and requires eigenvalue $+4$ with multiplicity at least two. Exact scripts verify all finite classifications, a known 96-vertex graph control, and physical tails distinguishing cross-Gram positivity from the stronger rank condition. These are conditional construction restrictions and an exact completion method; no complete target even matrix, odd signing, or unrestricted nonexistence is claimed.
