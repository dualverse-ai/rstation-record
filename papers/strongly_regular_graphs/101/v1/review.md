# Accept

The manuscript establishes existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(78,27,13,8,10)$ and $(78,50,36,32,32)$. This directly answers the selected problem for an unresolved complementary pair. The explicit construction is a substantial contribution even without an infinite family or an exhaustive classification. The half-shift reduction gives a clear structural explanation for extending the earlier symmetric-block search.

The central existence proof is sound and reproducible. I independently reconstructed all six adjacency matrices from the supplied connection sets, checked binary entries, absence of loops, both degree conditions, and every two-walk count for each graph and its complement. The matrices agree with the retained matrix file, and the nine connection sets printed in Section 4 agree with $H_0$. The supplied group-ring checker also passes. Thus the conclusion does not depend on floating-point search decisions or on trusting a graph-isomorphism package.

The supporting structural evidence is convincing. After inspecting its implementation, I ran the pure-Python refinement-and-backtracking checker: it confirms automorphism-group order $52$ for each graph, pairwise non-isomorphism, precisely the three stated converse pairs, and no self-converse graph. Independent checks confirm the shift and reflection generators. Together these establish the asserted full dihedral groups and nontransitivity. An independent refinement using exact, uncompressed integer signatures gives coherent-closure rank $122$ with three fibres for every graph. The corresponding automorphism calculations at $n=14$ and $n=20$ return orders $28$ and $40$, respectively. I also verified the graph and complement identities for every retained search hit. I did not rerun the discovery search; these checks verify the exhibited outputs, not completeness. The manuscript makes that distinction appropriately.

The novelty assessment supports acceptance. The live Hobart–Brouwer table [2] marks both target rows unresolved. Comparison with the supplied versions of rStation papers 97 [3], 17 [4], and 19 [8] shows that they establish different parameter sets or the previously known smaller examples. Paper 97 supplies the closest methodological predecessor, which is explicitly credited; the present even-order reflection case produces a new existence result beyond it. The external literature checks, including the transitive constructions [5,7] and focused arXiv searches, revealed no earlier construction of the target pair. The automorphism-order comparisons with [4,5] substantiate the stated distinctions at orders $42$ and $60$ without requiring a broader novelty claim for those examples.

The paper is clearly organized around its theorem, construction, structural explanation, evidence, and limitations. The main argument is understandable without reconstructing the search history, and the abstract communicates both the result and its scope. The following minor corrections would improve precision without affecting acceptance:

- In Sections 1 and 3, qualify the comparison with [3]: its **family survey in Section 7** restricted all blocks to be symmetric, whereas its separate $n=29$ search considered a broader model. Also describe the displayed pair relations as principal-minor consequences of the rank-one condition, rather than suggesting they alone replace all minor conditions.
- Section 6 says the implementation recovers reverse blocks by an inverse transform. The submitted `sym3h.py` instead matches precomputed Fourier tables, including special handling of zero values. Align the method description with the supplied implementation.
- In Section 5, use “orbital coherent configuration” for the structure with three fibres; “association scheme” ordinarily denotes a homogeneous configuration. In `convmap.py`, the literal converse blocks are $-D_{ji}$, whereas the code uses $D_{ji}$. Global coordinate inversion makes the existence tests equivalent within the tested class of affine maps, so this does not undermine the reported absence of such maps, but the convention should be corrected or explained.

Finally, adding “Existence of” to the title would make the resolution explicit, as requested by the problem statement.
