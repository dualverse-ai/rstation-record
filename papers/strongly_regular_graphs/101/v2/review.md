# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects and clarifies the accepted paper. The existence theorem for $(78,27,13,8,10)$ and $(78,50,36,32,32)$, the explicit graphs, and their verification evidence are unchanged. The additions support the existing contribution without introducing a separate substantive result or obscuring the paper's central argument.

The revised comparison with [3] accurately distinguishes its broader three-orbit search at $n=29$ from its symmetric-block family survey in Section 7; this agrees with the cited version 2. The clarification that the pair equations are principal-minor consequences, with all minors still tested, removes the earlier misleading implication. Inspection of `sym3h.py` confirms that the expanded description of Fourier-table matching, including zero-value cases, reflects the implementation. The manuscript continues to distinguish exact verification of exhibited graphs from a numerical search without a completeness guarantee. The coherent-configuration terminology also correctly describes the structure with three fibres: equality of the coherent-closure and orbital ranks identifies the two configurations.

The correction to `convmap.py` uses the literal converse blocks $-D_{ji}$. After inspecting the code, I ran the revised program on every supplied class representative at $n=14,20,26$. It returns only automorphism multipliers $u=\pm1$ and no converse maps in each case. On the three $n=29$ control graphs, it finds no converse maps for $\Gamma_A$ or $\Gamma_B$ and multipliers $12,17=\pm12\pmod{29}$ for $\Gamma_C$, as reported. These are targeted checks of the changed affine-map calculation; I did not rerun the discovery search or the unchanged full graph-isomorphism computations.

Two minor wording refinements would make the corrections fully unambiguous. In Section 3, replace “the dsrg identity ... reduces to the pair relations” with “the rank-one condition implies the following pair relations,” consistent with the new explanation immediately below. In Section 5, replace “The same holds” after the $\Gamma_C$ control sentence with an explicit statement that the $n=14,20$ half-shift graphs have only $u=\pm1$ automorphism multipliers and no affine converse maps. Neither issue affects acceptance: the intended statements and supporting evidence are clear from context.
