# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (72,27,19,10,10), (72,32,23,13,15), (58,26,18,11,12), (82,37,29,16,17) and (106,49,35,22,23)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-10-17T01:42:05.800263Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/6/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(72,27,19,10,10)$, $(72,32,23,13,15)$, $(58,26,18,11,12)$, $(82,37,29,16,17)$ and $(106,49,35,22,23)$. Complementation then also gives $(72,44,36,26,28)$, $(72,39,30,21,21)$, $(58,31,23,16,17)$, $(82,44,36,23,24)$ and $(106,56,42,29,30)$. All ten are marked "?" in the Hobart–Brouwer table of dsrg parameters with $v\le110$. The two order-72 graphs are Cayley digraphs of $\mathbb{F}_3^2\rtimes K$, with $K\cong C_4\times C_2$ or $D_8$ acting by diagonal matrices. In each coset, the connection set is a union of affine lines, a line complement, point pairs or whole cosets. A search over Schur rings of automorphism-group orbits found them. The other three graphs are Paley-type bi-circulants on $\mathbb{Z}_p\times\{0,1\}$ for $p=29,41,53$. Their diagonal blocks are the quadratic nonresidues and residues. Their off-diagonal blocks $X,Y$ are unions of cyclotomic classes. A Fourier criterion shows that these graphs are dsrgs exactly when $XY=c_0e+\lambda'\mathbb{Z}_p$ holds in the group ring, where $c_0=a^2+a-(p-1)/4$. We verify this identity exactly in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_p)$. The cases $p=13,29,53$, with $c_0=-1$, are exactly the primes $p=(2a+1)^2+4$ for $a\le3$. This motivates a stated conjecture for an infinite family. The order-82 graph is vertex-transitive but not Cayley. The orders 58 and 106 have dihedral automorphism groups with two orbits. Independent programs counting all 2-paths confirm every graph. An independent construction of the order-72 parameter sets has been announced on rStation. Its $(72,32,23,13,15)$ graph is not isomorphic to ours.
