# Accept

The paper establishes a substantial contribution to the selected existence problem: explicit constructions for the fifteen complementary parameter classes in Table 1, with inspectable exact certificates. The central existence claims are sound. Acceptance rests on these constructions and their synthesis method, with the corrections below to ancillary structural and catalog statements.

Theorem 2.1 has a complete algebraic proof. The compatibility and orthogonality conditions make $A=CR$ binary and loopless, while the transposed outer-product equation gives $RC=-dI+\mu\alpha\mathbf1^T$. Together with $C\alpha=\mathbf1$ and $\mathbf1^TR=\mathbf1^T$, this establishes both degree conditions and $A^2+dA=\mu J$. Invertibility of $RC$ establishes the claimed rank. The divisibility argument, sign constraint, compatibility formulation, and circulant offset calculation are also valid within their stated restrictions.

I ran the inspected standard-library verifier successfully on all 26 supplied primary records and their complements. It checks the exact outer-product identities, compatibility, degrees, and every two-walk count, as well as the specified small doubled examples. Independently written code reconstructed all seven graphs from the printed nine-orbit generators in Table 4 and both graphs from Table 5, and checked their degrees and two-walk counts directly. These checks establish the finite witnesses; the proof, rather than finite testing, establishes all positive multiplicities $c$. I did not reproduce the discovery MILP searches or rerun the earlier paper's exhaustive nonexistence computations.

The novelty claim is supported by a focused comparison. All thirty Table 1 entries are marked unknown in the retrieved Hobart–Brouwer catalog [2]. The relevant published rStation results, particularly [11], [13], and [14], do not already supply these parameter classes; the overlapping constructions from [11] are appropriately excluded in Table 2. The outer-product framework itself builds directly on [14], so the constructive converse alone is a modest extension, but the compatible templates and new exact witnesses make this substantially more than a restatement. I also checked the construction and new-parameter section of arXiv:2609.10075v1 [18]; its transitive-group constructions do not duplicate Table 1. This focused literature check supports the claimed distinction without constituting an exhaustive classification of all constructions.

The following corrections and qualifications should be incorporated:

1. **Correct Corollary 3.3's catalog enumeration.** There are five relevant open primary $t=\mu$, $g=6$ rows in [2]: $(50,30,21,16,21)$, $(56,12,3,1,3)$, $(56,36,27,21,27)$, $(75,30,14,9,14)$, and $(100,60,42,32,42)$. The two order-56 classes are the ones excluded by [14, Theorem 3.5]. The corollary instead lists seven pairs, omits the dense order-56 primary row, and includes $(84,18,4,1,4)$, $(84,54,36,27,36)$, and $(98,21,5,2,5)$, which fail $k(k+\mu-\lambda)=\mu v$. The completion conclusion survives with the correct five-pair list.

2. **Narrow the transition claim in Theorem 4.2.** In Table 4, the $r=10$ generator with head string $0100100111$ has six cyclic transitions, not at most four. It also occurs in the retained certificate. The graph and its nine-orbit construction verify, but the asserted four-transition restriction is not established for this case. Remove that restriction from the general statement, or supply a separately verified witness satisfying it. This qualification does not weaken the parameter-existence result.

3. **Distinguish seeds from standard infinite extensions.** Multiplication by $c$ is Duval's established coclique extension $A\otimes J_c$, explicitly recorded as T10 in [2]; credit it directly. Theorem 3.2 lists four parameter rays, with the order-100 example on the order-50 ray, rather than five distinct infinite parameter families. Likewise, “26 primary families” counts certificate records with overlapping rays. Describe the contribution as new seeds and compatible synthesis templates, and state explicitly that Theorem 4.2 proves only $6\le r\le12$, with unrestricted positive $c$.

4. **Align reproducibility and references with the retained results.** The discovery script `solve_r12_and_multiblock_cyclic.py` writes the incorrect complement $(108,38,13,12,11)$; the final certificate and manuscript correctly give $(108,38,15,14,13)$. Correct the script and document the discovery scripts' missing working-directory inputs and numerical dependencies. The standalone certificate verifier already suffices to reproduce the existence evidence. Reference [18] should give the actual title, *Quasi-strongly regular digraphs constructed from transitive groups of degree $n\le110$*, and authors V. Mikulić Crnković and M. Zubović Žutolija. Add stable catalog URLs and retrieval dates, and include representative resolved parameters and “existence” in the title as requested by the task.

The metadata abstract is informative, and the notation and evidence are readable. Its family counts and structural claims should receive the same corrections. The verified new graphs remain a meaningful, reproducible advance independently of those overstatements.
