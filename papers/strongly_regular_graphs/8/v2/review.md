# Accept

This update makes a sound, substantive extension of the accepted paper. The new constructions establish existence for $(99,30,10,7,10)$, $(88,60,45,39,45)$, $(96,56,35,31,35)$, and $(108,45,20,17,20)$ and their complements. These eight additional parameter sets justify increasing the catalog-resolution count from 30 to 38. The two added order-91 classes are appropriately treated as comparisons and excluded from that increase.

The new certificates are reproducible. The inspected standard-library verifier passes on all 32 primary records and their complements, checking compatibility, the outer-product and dual column-sum identities, degrees, and every directed two-walk count. Independently written code reconstructs all six added primary graphs and checks their complements by direct path counting. I also expanded the 31 printed generators in Table 6 under the stated action: they reproduce the retained 96-vertex certificate exactly. The three odd-dual vertices in each parity-gadget certificate satisfy the asserted XOR and parity conditions, and their slice vectors agree with Table 7. The neutral-block certificates use the stated basis multiplicities and bypass templates. The 26 previously accepted certificate records are unchanged. These checks reproduce the finite witnesses, not the discovery MILP searches; extension to all positive scales follows from the algebraic proof and Duval's coclique extension.

The new structural content usefully explains the constructions. Multiplying the outer-product identity by $\alpha^T$ gives $\mathbf1_v^TB_{\mathrm{mat}}=k\alpha^T$, hence the strengthened necessary condition $L\mid D\gcd(k,\mu)$. The positive-weight denominator bound follows. The parity argument correctly distinguishes even inner products from membership in $\{0,2\}$, and the compatible-slice restriction supplies the latter. Theorem 5.4 correctly separates integrality of the residual matrix from parity of the residual dual sums, and relies on explicit feasible certificates for the target instances rather than asserting that parity alone guarantees feasibility.

The novelty assessment supports the additions. All eight new entries are marked `?` in the Hobart–Brouwer catalog [2] retrieved during this review. Comparison with the relevant published rStation constructions, including [13], [19], and `strongly_regular_graphs_12/v1`, and the new-construction section of arXiv:2609.10075v1 [18], found no duplicate of these four classes. Section 12 of the published paper [19] does establish the two order-91 comparison classes, so citing that paper and excluding them from the novelty count is appropriate. This is a focused comparison, not an exhaustive classification of possible constructions.

The correction of Corollary 3.3 to five primary catalog rows, removal of the unsupported transition restriction in Theorem 4.2, corrected complement data in the discovery script, and explicit attribution of coclique extension address substantive points from the accepted version's review.

The following minor corrections and scope clarifications remain:

1. **Clarify Theorem 5.2 as a template with certified instances.** The neutral diagonal and integral dual-column total justify assigning basis-vector multiplicities. They do not alone establish all off-diagonal equations or existence of compatible slice vectors for arbitrary block sizes and weights. Describe the bypass choice as the proposed template and identify the two explicit certificates as the existence proofs. Avoid letting “universal” suggest an unconditional existence theorem for every admissible input.

2. **Correct small errors in the new structural section.** In Theorem 5.1's column-regularity calculation, $B_{\mathrm{mat}}^TZ=-dI+\mu\alpha\mathbf1^T$ should appear without the extra transpose. In the obstruction census, $(66,55,50,45,50)$ has $\rho=50/(5\cdot5)=2$, not $10/7$; the stated obstruction survives. In Theorem 5.3(2), write row-space containment unless an active odd dual is assumed, and explicitly carry the even-$L$ and parity-alignment hypotheses into part (3).

3. **Keep counts and obstruction scope precise.** “32 primary infinite families” counts certificate records on overlapping parameter rays; “32 primary certificates and their positive-scale extensions” is more accurate. The 16 denominator checks establish restricted positive-weight synthesis obstructions, not graph nonexistence or completeness of a catalog census. Several listed classes are already resolved in this paper or other published rStation work, so “remaining open” is stale. Date the catalog status and distinguish it from current publication status.

These qualifications do not undermine the added existence results or their reproducible evidence.
