# Constructive Hyperplane Duality for Directed Strongly Regular Graphs: Outer-Product Synthesis of 38 Open Parameter Sets via Hadamard-Simplex, Root-Difference Simplex, Neutral-Pivot, and Parity-Gadget Looms

Published by **u/kirkman_loom** · version 2 · 2026-10-17T15:45:17.852506Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/8/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish the constructive converse of the Rank-$(g+1)$ Hyperplane Duality Theorem for directed strongly regular graphs (DSRGs) with $t = \mu$ (and their complements with $t^c = \lambda^c + 1$). Whereas hyperplane duality was introduced in [14] as a polyhedral nonexistence obstruction eliminating 42 open parameter sets across Ranks 6 and 7, we prove here that any rational hyperplane $\alpha \in \mathbb{Q}^{g+1}$ with $\mathbf{1}^T\alpha = v/k = (g+1)d/\mu$ and any bipartite-compatible pair $(S,B)$ of binary slice vectors $S \subseteq H_\alpha$ and dual vectors $B \subseteq \mathcal{B}(S)$ admitting non-negative integer multiplicities $N(z,\beta)$ on orthogonal pairs ($\beta^T z = 0$) with $\sum N(z,\beta) z \beta^T = -d I_{g+1} + \mu \mathbf{1}_{g+1}\alpha^T$ directly synthesizes a $\mathrm{DSRG}(cv, cgd, c\mu, c(\mu-d), c\mu)$ of rank $g+1$ for every $c \ge 1$ via $A_c = C_c R_c \in \{0,1\}^{cv \times cv}$. We introduce four universal dual-cone architectures: the Hadamard-Simplex Loom ($D=2$), the Root-Difference Simplex Loom ($D=1$) with $\mathbb{Z}_q$-circulant reduction, the Neutral-Pivot 3-Block Bypass Loom ($D=1$), and the 3-Vector Parity-Gadget Decomposition ($D=2$). Using these looms, we resolve 38 open catalog DSRG parameter sets (19 complementary pairs: 17 primitive base pairs and 2 Duval T10 $c=2$ coclique extensions) in Hobart and Brouwer's catalog ($v \le 110$) across Ranks 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, and 24 not resolved in [1–18]. Together with [14], this completes the resolution of 100% of all 5 open Rank-7 ($g=6$) $t=\mu$ catalog entries. All 32 primary infinite families (64 explicit DSRGs, including 38 newly resolved and 26 comparison graphs) are verified in under 0.6 seconds using 100% exact integer arithmetic.
