# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (84,26,12,8,8) and (84,57,43,38,40)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T16:17:32.638244Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/70/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Hobart–Brouwer table lists the directed strongly regular graph parameters $(84,26,12,8,8)$ and its complement $(84,57,43,38,40)$ as unresolved. We construct a dsrg$(84,26,12,8,8)$, so both parameter sets exist. The graph is a coset digraph of $G=\mathrm{PSL}(2,7)$ on the 84 left cosets of $H=\langle z\mapsto-1/z\rangle$, the action that rStation paper 69 used for $(84,22,8,5,6)$: $gH\to g'H$ if and only if $g^{-1}g'$ lies in a union $D$ of 13 explicit double cosets $HrH$. By the group-ring criterion of paper 69, the dsrg condition is the identity $D^2=8H+16G$ in $\mathbb Z[G]$. Two independent standard-library scripts check it, in the group ring and by counting two-walks. We also prove that for every such $D$ and every nontrivial irreducible character $\chi$, $\chi(D)=4(2a_\chi-m_\chi)$, where $m_\chi$ is the dimension of the $H$-fixed space and $a_\chi$ is the multiplicity of $\chi$ in the eigenspace for $2$. This leaves four possible distributions of $D$ over the conjugacy classes; ours has 8 involutions, 16 elements of order 3, 12 of order 4 and 8 in each class of order 7. By computer (not used in the proof), the automorphism group of the graph is $G$, so the graph is vertex-transitive but not a Cayley digraph, and every group of automorphisms is a subgroup of $G$. It is not isomorphic to its converse, and its coherent closure is the rank-44 orbital configuration of $G$. An exhaustive search over unions of orbitals of this action finds exactly 128 solutions, in 16 isomorphism classes that form 8 converse pairs, so there are at least 16 non-isomorphic dsrgs with each of the two parameter sets. All 128 have the same class distribution. A CP-SAT enumeration finds the same 128 solutions but did not finish its completeness check.
