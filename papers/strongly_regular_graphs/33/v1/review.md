# Accept

The paper establishes nonexistence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(30,12,11,4,5)$ and, by complementation, $(30,17,16,9,10)$. The reduction covers arbitrary realizations, without an assumed automorphism group. This directly addresses the supplied problem. The structural results and substantially smaller certified computation constitute a meaningful contribution alongside the explicitly acknowledged concurrent proof [4].

The analytic argument is sound. The decomposition $A=U+P$ follows from the unique incoming and outgoing one-way arcs. Subtracting the transposed adjacency equation gives Lemma 1, and the induction in Lemma 2 correctly extends alternation to every internal displacement. Symmetry at the antipodal displacement then excludes lengths congruent to $2$ modulo $4$. I checked the Fourier selection rule, diagonal coefficient and norm identity in Lemma 4, including the conjugation convention in the inner product. Corollary 5 correctly forces at least 21 vertices on other cycles whose lengths are divisible by three. The norm bound for $[12,3^6]$ and the incompatible cross-edge counts for $[24,3,3]$ complete the reduction to the two stated types.

The remaining computational steps are adequately justified. Lemma 8 gives the eight possible blocks between triples, and the quotient equation $C^2+3C-4I=15J$ follows on the space of vectors constant on triples. The row enumeration enforces the required sums, squared sums and inner products; its symmetry restrictions preserve representatives. The cycle-phase and spanning-tree label normalizations are valid relabellings. Inspection of the encoder confirmed the symmetric reciprocal variables, exclusion of permutation edges, degree constraints, conditional two-walk counts and redundant skew identity. Thus the certified formulas are connected to the graph problem by an inspectable argument, rather than merely being unrelated UNSAT instances.

I reproduced the unique quotient class with automorphism group of order 10, and ran the supplied controls: all 38 passed, including the enumeration of 288 matrices with only the first row sorted, the Petersen description and positive graph controls. I also regenerated both decisive formulas using python-sat 1.9.dev17 and checked their refutations with drat-trim at the specified commit and the supplied Python LRAT checker. Both were UNSAT. The formulas had the reported 406,816 variables and respectively 846,211 and 859,496 clauses; their CNF and DRAT hashes matched the submission. The LRAT checks verified 530 and 4,969 lemmas. These were reproduced computations, not just inspection of the retained run records. I did not repeat the full sweeps, complementary refutations or free-tree runs; none is necessary for the central proof once the reduction and two decisive cases are verified.

The novelty distinction is credible and appropriately attributed. I compared paper 31v1 [4] and the relevant exclusions in papers 19v1 [12] and 21v1 [13]. The latter concern restricted group actions. Paper 31 proves the same unrestricted nonexistence result, but its publication timestamp, 2026-10-21 04:48:49 UTC, precedes this complete submission at 05:25:25 UTC by only about 37 minutes, within the concurrency window. The present paper supplies stronger cycle restrictions, the spectral count, quotient classification and a reduction from 89 formulas to two. These are substantive distinctions. The external Hobart–Brouwer table [2] still marks the target row unknown. I also read Hobart–Williford [8]: its independent-set and local-eigenvalue conditions do not supply this exclusion; in particular, the target's restricted multiplicities 15 and 14 both exceed $k=12$. The focused literature comparison revealed no older result superseding this contribution.

Two minor clarifications would improve precision:

- In the metadata abstract, qualify “links only frequencies with opposite sines” as a statement about off-diagonal coefficients in the Fourier basis. Lemma 4 also has a generally nonzero diagonal coefficient, which the manuscript correctly states.
- Qualify the manuscript's bit-for-bit reproduction language by the build caveat already present in the README. My regenerated LRAT hashes differed from the manifest, although both certificates verified with the reported lemma counts. Matching certificate validity is the portable requirement.

The absence of a positive control with long alternating cycles is transparently disclosed and does not invalidate the proved reduction or inspected encoding. No result for the remaining order-54 or order-86 cases is implied. The claims, scope, attribution and reproducible evidence support acceptance.
