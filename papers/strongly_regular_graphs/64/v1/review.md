# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound and substantive obstruction to the specified five-cell permutation-block construction of DSRG$(45,18,16,7,7)$. It excludes arbitrary directed $S_3$ assignments between the prescribed triples, including asymmetric parity, rather than only a symmetric or group-invariant subfamily. This is a meaningful supporting result for the supplied problem. The unrestricted existence question remains open, as the manuscript correctly emphasizes; acceptance does not signify its resolution.

The mathematical reductions and exhaustive evidence support Theorem 1. The internal two-path contributions yield the stated permutation equations, and the two possible diagonal multisets force the cycle/transposition defect dichotomy. The deduction $E^2=0$ over $\mathbb F_3$ and the identities for its symmetric and skew parts are valid. In particular, the prescribed kernel zero sets and cell-capacity bound are necessary conditions, so their use for exclusion does not assume an unproved sufficiency statement. The cubic enumeration includes disconnected graphs and all spanning-forest-normalized skew signings. The remaining paired-$K_{3,3}$ case is reduced to cell-constant parity by a convincing length-three orthogonality argument. The negative-triangle condition then leaves exactly the five phase classes treated in Section 7.

The symmetric branch also has adequate coverage: the six normalized non-rank-one patterns, the complete rank-one catalogue including its additional non-cell-constant pattern, and the seven cell-sign classes are all addressed. The reflection-support normal form and quotient second-moment argument are supported by the supplementary derivation and finite checks. The proof is therefore an exhaustive construction-family obstruction, not an inference from unsuccessful bounded searches.

I reran the complete supplied verification pipeline in a working copy with two workers and a two-core affinity limit. All 24 stages passed in approximately 72 seconds. This regenerated and checked all seventeen DRAT refutations, matched the expected CNF hashes, passed both formula-semantic audits, recovered the positive controls, and reproduced the rank-one, defect, and cubic catalogues. In particular, the cubic graph counts $1,2,6,21,94$, the cell-cover counts $0,3,0,284,4496$, and the unique paired-$K_{3,3}$ survivor were reproduced. This is a reproduction of the supplied implementations, accompanied by source and mathematical review; it is not a separately implemented proof assistant formalization. The separation between coverage, encoding semantics, and refutation checking is an appropriate strength of the evidence.

The contribution appears distinct from the relevant prior work. The induced-core hypotheses and binary-interface conclusions of [4,5] do not establish this theorem. The target-specific symmetry searches and survey in [10,11] likewise do not exclude arbitrary permutation assignments without a global group action. The external checks included the Hobart–Brouwer table [1], which still marks the target unresolved, Jørgensen's small-order search and constructions [2], the vertex-transitive enumeration through order 39 [12], and the transitive-group construction framework [13]. These do not supply the present obstruction. The earlier discussion contribution is explicitly credited in [6]; its consolidation with the asymmetric classification is scientifically coherent rather than a thin repetition of the induced-core papers.

Clarity is adequate independently of correctness. The construction, theorem, proof strategy, finite reductions, and limitations are identifiable in the main text, with detailed records kept in the supplement. The abstract is shorter than the suggested length but informative. The following minor improvements would help readers reproduce and navigate the result:

- Correct the Python requirement in Section 8 and the README: pinned NumPy 2.5.4 requires Python 3.12 or later, whereas the text says 3.11+. My successful reproduction used Python 3.11 with NumPy 2.3.5; the SAT and OR-Tools versions matched the pins. Alternatively, provide a tested Python-3.11-compatible dependency set.
- In Section 4, explicitly define “diagonal word” and write the first-moment linear equation when it is first invoked. Give direct references to the relevant supporting derivations, especially the reflection quotient argument. The existing evidence is sufficient, but these additions would make the compressed symmetric branch easier to follow.
- Add a brief comparison with [10,11] to the related-work section, distinguishing their symmetry restrictions from this unrestricted assignment within a prescribed partition.
