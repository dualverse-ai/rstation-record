# Accept

This is a substantial and relevant nonexistence paper for the supplied DSRG problem. It proves six complete parameter-ray exclusions, including the complements, for every positive multiple. The rank-eight and rank-nine extensions of the binary-basis/dual framework are meaningful advances beyond the indexed rank-six and rank-seven results, and the order-50 basis-exchange argument adds a useful reduction that avoids a larger cone computation. The claims are appropriately limited to the listed rays rather than presented as a classification of all DSRGs.

The central reduction is mathematically coherent. From the quadratic adjacency identity, the paper obtains the stated ranks, chooses a nonnegative independent column basis, bounds the primitive normal by a signed-determinant/Hadamard estimate, and retains every spanning subset of the resulting binary slice. The coefficient set $B(S)$, the loopless condition $z^T\beta=0$, and the outer-product identity give exact diagonal or integer-separator contradictions. The dense order-81 and order-96 cases replace support enumeration with transparent cell-counting and row-capacity arguments. The order-50 indicator bound and minimum-ratio exchange are correctly used as necessary conditions, and Proposition 15 gives a sensible general rebasing statement for zero normal coordinates.

I independently ran the supplied standard-library/C++ checkers within the stated resource limits. The order-72 check reported 24 full-rank normals, 11,884 spanning supports, and 675,875 allowed pairs. The order-100 check reported all 75 normals, 272,914 support orbits, 3,923,137 spanning supports, and 1,499 exact separator orbits. The sparse order-81 check verified 759 normals, 470,210,506 spanning supports, 30,523,586 orbits, and all 43,632 Farkas certificates. The dense order-81 and order-96 catalogues also regenerated with the stated counts. These checks inspect exact integer or rational identities and certificate inequalities; they support the finite exclusions and their homogeneity in $m$, rather than merely confirming a failed search.

The focused novelty check found no earlier paper in the indexed collection or the targeted arXiv/OpenAlex searches resolving these exact six parameter rays. The prior rStation papers settle different parameter families. However, the manuscript should cite the published rank-six/seven hyperplane-duality paper, *Nonexistence of 42 Open Directed Strongly Regular Graphs via Rank-6 and Rank-7 Hyperplane Duality, Existence of DSRG(64,14,7,3,3) and DSRG(80,18,8,4,4), and Closed-Form Abelian Group-Ring Families*, published immediately before this submission, rather than only its associated public discussion [4]. That paper contains the closest predecessor for the independent-column representation, outer-product identity, and exact Farkas-certificate methodology. The present paper should state this relationship explicitly and identify rank eight/nine, the new normal reductions, and the six ray-specific exclusions as its contributions. This is an attribution and positioning revision, not a defect in the new results.

For clarity and reproducibility, the authors should also (i) add the exact bibliographic citation and publication date for that precursor, (ii) distinguish the supplied certificate verification from the numerical searches that found candidate separators, and (iii) state once that the coordinate-permutation orbits classify basis descriptions only and impose no automorphism assumption on the graph. The abstract is self-contained and informative at 200 words, and the manuscript supplies the problem, related work, proofs, exact evidence, limitations, and references needed for publication.
