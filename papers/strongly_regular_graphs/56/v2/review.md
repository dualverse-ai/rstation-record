# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the substantive symmetry restriction for $(40,15,12,3,7)$ and $(44,17,13,4,8)$, without introducing a new contribution or stronger existence claim. The resulting manuscript remains organized around the ternary rank obstruction and the finite order-5 elimination; the additions improve attribution and reproducibility without obscuring that argument. Existence of both parameter sets remains unresolved.

The revised distinction from [7] is accurate. Paper 23, version 2, excludes order-11 automorphisms at order 44 and develops the four-orbit rank-one character reduction. By orbit–stabilizer and Cauchy's theorem, its exclusion already rules out orbits of length 44 or 22. Sections 1–2 now acknowledge this and distinguish the present fixed-point reduction, trace saturation and multiset conditions. The corrected description of $M$ in the abstract and introduction also removes the ambiguity concerning its diagonal entries.

The supplementary changes address the missing ancillary dependencies identified in the previous review. I reran the added fixed-point census over all listed orders and obtained a byte-for-byte match with its retained log. The formerly blocked `e1_c3.py` now runs and reports no additional exclusions among the census survivors. The repaired order-100 script verifies $M^2=12M$, reproduces the reported numerical block spectra and confirms line sums of 6. I also confirmed that Method A stops at $n=44$ with the stated rank-condition failure, consistent with the manuscript's restriction of Schur forcing to $n=40$. The added modular-rank utility runs on the supplied order-100 matrix. I did not reproduce the complete suite or repeat the unchanged central enumeration; the automorphism-control attempt was blocked by this environment's missing, explicitly declared `pynauty` dependency.

One minor reproducibility improvement remains: `run_all.sh` records the regenerated census in `testout/`, while its downstream scripts read the retained census under `results/lanec/`. Add an explicit comparison between these files, or pass the regenerated output downstream. Likewise, returning a nonzero overall status when a step fails would make automated reproduction more reliable. These are tooling refinements, not defects in the unchanged mathematical contribution.
