# Accept

This is a focused correction of the accepted paper. It preserves the explicit construction and existence proof for $(96,46,32,24,20)$ and $(96,49,35,22,28)$, introduces no new substantive contribution, and retains a clear organization around the connection set and its group-ring certificate. The limited additions to the discovery appendix do not obscure that argument.

The revised Section 2 resolves the earlier attribution issue. I checked Sections 4 and 5 of [3]: its orthogonal- and Mathieu-group searches use representations of rank at most 30, its six reported degree-96 parameter sets differ from the present pair, and its classification at degrees 31–110 concerns primitive actions of rank at most 30. The manuscript now distinguishes these searches correctly. The added exclusion argument is sound given Proposition 2: a transitive subgroup of the stated automorphism group preserves its nontrivial block system, and its orbits on ordered pairs refine those of the full group, so its rank is at least 52. The corresponding abstract correction is appropriate.

Appendix A also resolves the multiplicity inconsistency identified in the previous review. Restricting the added scalar-block equations to constituents of multiplicity at least two makes the stated successful pattern consistent with the degree-3 constituent of multiplicity one lying wholly in the $6$-eigenspace. Removing the erroneous account of an earlier Cayley search is appropriate; that account was not evidence for the existence theorem. The revised statement about independent checking accurately reflects the previous review, including its limitation concerning GAP and pynauty.

The new seed observations are acceptable as limited discovery provenance. One success after 214 seconds with the character pattern free supports the clarification that fixing the pattern was not necessary. However, “fixing $a$ made the search much faster on this pair” should preferably read “the reported fixed-pattern run was much faster than the successful free-pattern run.” These few runs, with different seeds and time limits, do not establish a reproducible speedup or isolate its cause. If the numerical comparison is retained, supplying seeds, solver settings, hardware details and logs would improve reproducibility; a broader performance claim would require a controlled comparison. This is a minor qualification because neither the theorem nor the paper's contribution depends on solver performance.

The construction, proof and supplementary files are unchanged from the accepted version. I have relied on the previous review's exact verification of those certificates rather than rerunning unchanged computations; the new search timings were not reproduced. The revision improves precision without weakening the accepted scientific result or materially expanding its scope.
