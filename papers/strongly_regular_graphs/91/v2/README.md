# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (96,46,32,24,20) and (96,49,35,22,28)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 2 · 2026-11-03T18:47:20.727803Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/91/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Hobart–Brouwer table of directed strongly regular graph parameters lists the complementary pair $(96,46,32,24,20)$ and $(96,49,35,22,28)$ as unresolved. We construct a dsrg$(96,46,32,24,20)$, so both parameter sets exist. The graph is a Cayley digraph on the group $K$ of affine maps $x\mapsto ux+b$ of the Galois ring $\mathrm{GR}(4,2)=\mathbb Z_4[w]/(w^2+w+1)$, where $u$ runs over a cyclic unit group of order 6; $K$ is SmallGroup(96,71). We list the connection set $S$ explicitly (46 elements). The proof is the identity $(S-6)(S+2)=20K$ in the group ring $\mathbb Z[K]$. Two dependency-free scripts that share no code check it independently, and one of them also builds the $96\times96$ matrices and checks every two-walk count. Computations with nauty and GAP, not used in the proof, show the following. The automorphism group has order 192, is SmallGroup(192,1008), acts with point stabiliser $C_2$ and rank 52, and is imprimitive. The graph is not isomorphic to its converse, so there are at least two non-isomorphic dsrgs with each parameter set. The six cosets of the translation subgroup form an equitable partition with quotient matrix $-2I+8J$, and each coset induces two copies of $K_{2,2,2,2}$. In every nonlinear irreducible representation $\rho$ of $K$ (degrees 3, 3, 6, 6), $\rho(S)=-2I+8P_\rho$ with $P_\rho$ an idempotent of rank one. The graph was found by a sweep of coset actions of groups of order 192 on the cosets of a subgroup of order 2, combining an exact character sieve with CP-SAT models solved separately for each admissible character pattern. The sweep is incomplete. The only published classification at degree 96 that we know of treats primitive groups of rank at most 30, while the automorphism group of our graph is imprimitive of rank 52.
