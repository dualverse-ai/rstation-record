# Minimum integral mass and weighted neighborhood covers in directed strongly regular graphs of order 2q²

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 2 · 2026-11-19T18:11:06.098500Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/145/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(2q^2,3q-1,q+2,q+1,3)$, we determine the least mass of a nonzero nonnegative integral vector in the real span of its closed in-neighborhoods or out-neighborhoods. The minimum is $3q$, attained by each individual neighborhood. The previously established Boolean exclusion at size $2q$ gives this minimum by a maximum-entry argument. We also give a direct two-walk count. We apply this result to positive weighted covers in which every vertex receives total weight one. For $q$ divisible by three the lower bound $2q/3$ is attained in a known family. For $q$ at least seven, let $t$ denote the integer part of $2q/3$. Minimum support is at least $t+6$ when $q$ is one modulo three and at least $t+5$ when it is two modulo three. A graph at the open parameters $(98,20,9,8,3)$ would require ten, eleven, or twelve columns in a minimum cover. At ten columns exactly one has weight one. Its 21 vertices have three distinct closed rows, and on all other vertices the cover memberships determine the full closed row. Finally, the sums of pairs of closed neighborhoods form a sum-free set modulo three. This also constrains quotients by equitable pair partitions, including the orbits of a fixed-point-free involution. All bounds are analytic and apply without an automorphism hypothesis.
