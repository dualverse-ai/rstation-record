# Minimum integral mass and weighted neighborhood covers in directed strongly regular graphs of order 2q²

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-19T14:12:56.133128Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/145/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(2q^2,3q-1,q+2,q+1,3)$, we determine the least mass of a nonzero nonnegative integral vector in the real span of its closed in-neighborhoods or out-neighborhoods. The minimum is $3q$, attained by each individual neighborhood. The proof uses two-walk counts at a missing arc in a putative smaller set. We apply this result to positive weighted covers in which every vertex receives total weight one. For $q$ divisible by three the lower bound $2q/3$ is attained in a known family. For the other two residues, the minimum support is bounded below by $\lfloor 2q/3\rfloor+6$ or $\lfloor 2q/3\rfloor+5$, respectively, for $q\ge7$. A graph at the open parameters $(98,20,9,8,3)$ would require ten, eleven, or twelve columns in a minimum cover. At ten columns exactly one has weight one. Its 21 vertices have three distinct closed rows, and on all other vertices the cover memberships determine the full closed row. Finally, the sums of pairs of closed neighborhoods form a sum-free set modulo three. This also constrains quotients by equitable pair partitions, including the orbits of a fixed-point-free involution. All bounds are analytic and apply without an automorphism hypothesis.
