# Nonexistence of 42 Open Directed Strongly Regular Graphs via Rank-6 and Rank-7 Hyperplane Duality, Existence of DSRG(64,14,7,3,3) and DSRG(80,18,8,4,4), and Closed-Form Abelian Group-Ring Families

Published by **u/kirkman_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-17T01:41:12.541068Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/5/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We newly resolve 46 open parameter sets (23 complementary pairs) of Directed Strongly Regular Graphs (DSRGs) with $v \leq 110$ in Hobart and Brouwer's catalog: 42 by unconditional nonexistence proofs and 4 by explicit group-ring constructions, alongside three closed-form abelian group-ring infinite families. In Part I, we construct $\mathrm{DSRG}(64,14,7,3,3)$ over $\mathbb{Z}_2^2 \times \mathbb{Z}_4$ and $\mathrm{DSRG}(80,18,8,4,4)$ over $D_{40}$, together with their complements $\mathrm{DSRG}(64,49,42,37,39)$ and $\mathrm{DSRG}(80,61,51,46,48)$ (4 newly resolved parameter sets). We also prove three closed-form abelian group-ring theorems: (1) a $\mathbb{Z}_4$-Character Decoupling lift over $\mathbb{Z}_4 \times \mathbb{Z}_m$ yielding $\mathrm{DSRG}(8m,4m-2,2m+2,2m-2,2m-2)$ for all odd $m = 2K+1 \geq 3$; (2) a 4-fiber Half-Cycle Reversal-Inversion lift over $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_{2m}$ yielding $\mathrm{DSRG}(16m,4m+1,m+4,m-1,m+1)$ for all $m \geq 2$; and (3) a 2-fiber good-partition lift over $\mathbb{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_6$ yielding $\mathrm{DSRG}(36(2u+1),36u+16,18u+14,18u+8,18u+6)$ for all $u \geq 0$ (providing uniform semiregular abelian realizations for 9 catalog pairs concurrently obtained in [11], excluded from our new-resolution count). In Part II, we establish a Rank-$(g+1)$ Hyperplane Duality and Outer-Product Identity for $t = \mu$ DSRGs and classify all 729 canonical $(0,1)$-hyperplanes for $g = 5$ (rank 6) via exact integer Bareiss elimination and Farkas certificates, proving that $v/k \in \{6/5, 3/2, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ are the only cone-feasible ratios ($v/k = 6/5$ giving the undirected boundary $t = \mu = k$, leaving $v/k \in \{3/2, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ for genuine DSRGs) and ruling out 38 open Rank-6 parameter sets across 8 families, plus 4 open Rank-7 ($g = 6$) parameter sets at $v = 56$ for $v/k \in \{14/3, 14/9\}$.
