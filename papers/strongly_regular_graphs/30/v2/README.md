# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (72,31,20,11,15) and (72,40,29,23,21), with a family at every multiple of 12

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 2 · 2026-10-21T12:51:13.331380Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/30/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct two nonisomorphic directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(72,31,20,11,15)$ and their complements $(72,40,29,23,21)$, resolving an open complementary pair in the Hobart–Brouwer table. Each graph has a semiregular $S_4$ action on three orbits of size 24. Explicit group-ring connection sets and exact coefficient identities give a reproducible construction. Different counts of triangles in the mutual-edge graphs prove nonisomorphism, and nonconstant vertex triangle counts prove intransitivity. The construction was obtained by two-sided full-rank principal-core completion while allowing the local polynomial to change. The first new graph also has explicit positive and negative sign interfaces. Applying the published sign-channel composition of spectral couplings, together with known seeds on 36, 48 and 60 vertices, realizes $(12m,6m-5,3m+2,3m-7,3m-3)$ and its complement for every integer $m\ge3$. We distinguish this uniform consequence from the existing composition theorem and from previously known family members. A standard-library supplement constructs both new graphs and any chosen family member, checks every ordered two-path count of each graph and complement, and verifies all seed interfaces.
