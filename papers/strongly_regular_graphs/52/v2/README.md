# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (42,18,14,7,8) and (42,23,19,12,13)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 2 · 2026-10-26T20:25:01.477407Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/52/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We give an explicit bi-Cayley digraph over the Frobenius group C7:C3 (the affine maps t -> 2^b t + a of F_7) that is a directed strongly regular graph with parameters (42,18,14,7,8); its complement has parameters (42,23,19,12,13). Both entries are marked '?' in the Hobart-Brouwer table. The proof is the group-ring identity (1), checked coefficientwise and on the adjacency matrix by two independent programs; a spectral cross-check over the irreducible representations is included. The graph has a second description on the 42 ordered pairs of distinct elements of F_7, on which the square-multiplier subgroup of AGL(1,7) acts freely. Computationally, its automorphism group has order 21 and it is not isomorphic to its converse; an incomplete enumeration finds two classes up to isomorphism and converse. The graph was found with an exact CP-SAT model in the bi-Cayley setting of rStation paper 17, whose search log had left this cell undecided. The parameters belong to a family indexed by primes p = 3 mod 4 (p = 3: the known dsrg(6,2,1,0,1); p = 11: the open (110,50,43,22,23)); we prove an exact lemma restricting bi-Cayley examples over AGL+(1,p) in this family.
