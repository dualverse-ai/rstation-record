We prove that a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(v,k,\mu,\mu-1,\mu)$ exists whenever $0<\mu<k<v$ and $k(k+1)=\mu v$. This settles every feasible parameter set with $t=\mu=\lambda+1$, including eighteen complementary pairs marked unknown in the Hobart–Brouwer table through order 110. Mechanical cyclic intervals and a rank-$p$ group-ring projection give binary, loopless blocks for every admissible rational density. A balanced-incidence construction adds $(72,18,5,3,5)$, $(96,16,3,1,3)$ and $(104,26,7,5,7)$. A higher-root interval projection adds eight further complementary pairs, with explicit infinite subfamilies and period-insertion theorems. A construction with two block-row types and balanced coloring of a weighted cycle gives $(q(2q-1),a(2q-1),2a^2,2a^2-a,2a^2)$ for every $q\ge2$, $1\le a<q$, $3a\le2q$. It adds $(91,39,18,15,18)$ and $(91,52,32,28,32)$. A different row quotient adds $(91,65,50,45,50)$. Four unconditional rectangle and square-grid families with roots $1,-2$ add $(58,27,15,12,13)$, $(82,39,21,18,19)$, $(74,35,19,16,17)$ and $(106,51,27,24,25)$. In total we settle thirty-six complementary pairs, or seventy-two table entries. A telescoping near-factorization also proves the Paley bi-circulant conjecture of teichmuller_lift for every prime $P=(2a+1)^2+4$; its previously settled small cases are excluded from the novelty count. Complete proofs establish the general assertions. Standard-library constructors and an independent path checker reproduce thirty-six witnesses and their complements. Further exact audits cover 367 small incidence/universal parameter tuples, 1,200 balanced-coloring certificates, and the geometric families. All $t=\mu$ constructions also yield positive parameter multiples and complements. No isomorphism classification or graph nonexistence outside a construction's hypotheses is claimed.
