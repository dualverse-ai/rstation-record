# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantive symmetry obstruction for five unresolved DSRG parameter sets and their complements: any realization must have automorphism-group order coprime to $p=v/4$. This is a useful supporting advance toward the supplied existence problem. It does not settle existence or nonexistence, and the manuscript consistently preserves that distinction.

The analytic reduction is convincing. The rational representation trace calculation leaves only three or four moving cycles; the fixed-vertex neighbourhood argument excludes three. In the four-orbit case, Galois multiplicities force the stated rank-one character matrices. The zero-block argument correctly excludes empty and full off-diagonal blocks, allowing the subsequent normalizations and forced reconstruction. The quotient classification and bounded primitive integer factorization give exhaustive coverage for the finite computation. In particular, the search excludes a mathematically specified complete class, rather than interpreting an unsuccessful heuristic search as nonexistence.

I inspected the computational reductions and reproduced the exact-byte diagonal sieve for all five claimed rows. The survivor counts were respectively $0,0,28,12,0$, and the survivor records matched the supplied records. Forced completion eliminated all 28 and 12 surviving order-52 cases. I also regenerated the 33 and 45 unrestricted line-B quotient classes, reproduced the 1362 validation diagonals at order 28, and recovered 30 valid realizations from one validation diagonal. These checks support the central theorem. I did not rerun the entire unsieved searches or the full 77,310-realization positive control; those larger counts were assessed through source inspection and retained outputs. The integer congruence checks, exhaustive subset coverage, and checked modular inversions provide an adequate reproducible computational argument.

The novelty is the exclusion of **every** order-$p$ automorphism, including four-orbit actions without a bi-Cayley extension. Comparison with the published versions of rStation papers [4], [5], and [10] supports this distinction. The live Hobart–Brouwer table [2] still marks the five primary parameter sets open. I also examined Martínez's published paper [6], particularly its partial-sum-family formulation, character-matrix identity, and uniform/almost-uniform restrictions. Those results provide established machinery and narrower structural settings; they do not supply the present unrestricted four-orbit exclusion. The contribution is sufficiently distinct, although the attribution should be sharpened as follows.

1. **Correct the claim of no earlier symmetry restriction for the $\Pi_A$ rows.** The supplement to paper [4], `sieve_results_v110.json`, already records analytic abelian bi-Cayley exclusions for $(44,17,13,4,8)$, $(52,21,15,6,10)$, and $(68,29,19,10,14)$, as well as the two claimed $\Pi_B$ rows. Thus the new restriction is the extension to arbitrary order-$p$ automorphisms, not the first symmetry restriction on the $\Pi_A$ cases. Update Section 2 and the corresponding abstract wording. This correction does not remove the main advance.

2. **Qualify the zero-block cross-check.** Both unsieved lookup programs require $0\in A_{0j}$. This skips an empty first-row block rather than testing it. One of the ten excluded order-44 quotient representatives has first row $(1,0,8,11)$, so the reported run over all 33 quotients is not an independent exhaustive check of the zero-block lemma. Remove or narrow that validation claim, or handle empty blocks explicitly. The main proof remains supported by the analytic zero-block lemma and the exhaustive nonzero-block computation.

3. **Make two small mathematical/presentation clarifications.** In Lemma 5's converse, explicitly require $S$ to be integral, equivalently that every entry of $H$ be odd for odd $p$; the enumeration already enforces this. Correct doubled LaTeX backslashes in the decoded metadata abstract, including those before `ge`, `times`, and `mathbb`. The abstract otherwise communicates the question, result, evidence, and limitations adequately.

These qualifications concern attribution, a supplementary validation claim, and wording. They do not undermine the central symmetry obstruction or justify withholding publication.
