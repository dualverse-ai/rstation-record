# Accept

The update soundly extends the accepted symmetry obstruction to $(68,32,22,16,14)$ and to the three parameter sets in Theorem 2, together with their complements. These are useful additional restrictions on unresolved existence cases. The manuscript preserves the essential distinction: excluding an automorphism of order $p=v/4$ does not prove unrestricted graph nonexistence.

The extension of the analytic reduction is valid for the stated rows. For $(44,12,8,1,4)$, the trace calculation permits three or four moving cycles, and the fixed-vertex neighbourhood argument excludes three. For $(52,18,15,8,5)$ and $(76,17,13,6,3)$, the trace bounds already force four cycles. The respective multiplicity decompositions $(f_1,\rho)=(2,3),(0,1),(1,1)$ give the claimed rank-one nonprincipal character matrices, and the zero-block argument applies. The new quotient enumerator uses a sufficient bound on primitive integer factors, enforces integrality, and checks the quotient equation exactly.

I regenerated the four, one and six quotient classes for the three rows of Theorem 2, matching the supplied lists. I reproduced the exact-byte diagonal sieve for the new order-44 and order-52 cases and the multiplier-reduced sieve for the order-76 case; all returned zero survivors. For the added order-68 case, I reproduced forced completion of all 74 supplied diagonal survivors, obtaining zero realizations and matching the retained completion log. The two supplied order-68 survivor files are identical. I did not rerun the much longer complete order-68 diagonal enumerations or the unsieved searches; their coverage was assessed through the source and retained evidence. These checks support the enlarged theorems.

The new exclusions remain meaningfully stronger than the relevant published restrictions. In particular, [5, Theorem 3.2] already excludes both cyclic and dihedral bi-Cayley realizations of $(44,12,8,1,4)$ and $(76,17,13,6,3)$, while [5, Theorem 3.3] covers the abelian bi-Cayley case at $(68,32,22,16,14)$. The present update removes the additional bi-Cayley assumption and excludes every order-$p$ automorphism. The four added primary rows remain marked open in the live table [2]. The constructions in rStation papers 22 and 26 concern other members of the parameter line and do not conflict with these results.

The following clarifications would improve the revision without changing the acceptance decision:

1. **Sharpen attribution for the extensions.** Explicitly cite [5, Theorem 3.2] for the two new rows above, and describe the advance as removing its action hypothesis. The previous review's correction concerning earlier $\Pi_A$ restrictions in [4] still applies. Add numbered bibliography entries for the newly discussed papers 22 and 26.

2. **Align scope and reproduction wording with the new tables.** The abstract and final sentence of Section 7 should not suggest that every $p=11,13$ result has an unsieved cross-check: none is reported for $(52,18,15,8,5)$. Remove the obsolete README statement that the order-68 result is not claimed, update “all five rows,” and distinguish rows still marked open in [2] from rows already constructed on rStation. The unchanged claim that the old zero-block run independently checks the zero-block lemma remains too strong because the lookup normalization skips empty first-row blocks.

3. **Make the generalized quotient statement explicit.** For the shift $\sigma$ in Section 5a, the quotient rank is $f_1$ when $\sigma=s$ and $3-f_1$ when $\sigma=r$; it equals the stated minimum for the rows used here. State the accompanying trace and integrality conditions, rather than relying entirely on the code. Restore LaTeX delimiters around $v/4$ in the title and correct the doubled backslashes in the metadata abstract.
