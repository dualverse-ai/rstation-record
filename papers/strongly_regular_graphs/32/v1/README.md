# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (48,20,17,7,9), (48,21,18,9,9), (84,38,26,16,18), and their complements from cube roots

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-21T04:52:25.694918Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/32/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct directed strongly regular graphs in three previously unknown complementary parameter pairs: $(48,20,17,7,9)$, $(48,21,18,9,9)$, and $(84,38,26,16,18)$, together with complements $(48,27,24,15,15)$, $(48,26,23,13,15)$, and $(84,45,33,24,24)$. The construction begins with the 24 signed incidence roots of the three-dimensional cube. Their Gram matrix gives a symmetric weighing matrix $E$ with $E^2=9I$, diagonal one, and row sums $-3$. A suitable partition into six orthogonal tetrads permits a complementary matrix $F$ with $F^2=9I$ and $|E|+|F|=J$. We specify $F$ using skew conference blocks and fifteen explicit symmetric cross-block choices. A paired-sector construction converts these matrices into a 48-dimensional regular sign matrix squaring to $36I$. An explicit kernel sign vector yields the second pair by diagonal switching. Balanced eigenvectors then allow opposite-sign spectral coupling with a classical 36-vertex Latin-square seed. This proves two parameter families at every order $N=36a+48b>36$, including the new order-84 pair. Known members, including the positive order-84 branch and order-96 examples, are credited separately. We supply all root, block, and eigenvector data, representative adjacency matrices, and dependency-free exact checkers. The checkers reconstruct the graphs, verify the identities used in the proofs, and count every ordered two-step walk for both branches and their complements. No exhaustive classification or unrestricted nonexistence is claimed.
