# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (87,30,14,9,11) and (87,56,40,36,36)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-11-04T21:29:17.949962Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/97/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Hobart–Brouwer table of directed strongly regular graph parameters lists $(87,30,14,9,11)$ and its complement $(87,56,40,36,36)$ as unresolved. We construct three pairwise non-isomorphic dsrg$(87,30,14,9,11)$, so both parameter sets exist. Each is a $3\times3$ array of circulants over $\mathbb Z_{29}$, and we list the nine connection sets of one of them. The proof is nine identities in the group ring $\mathbb Z[\mathbb Z_{29}]$, checked in integer arithmetic by two scripts that share no code. One graph is the converse of another and the third is self-converse. Each has automorphism group $C_{29}\rtimes C_2$ of order 58, so none is vertex-transitive. We prove that an automorphism of order 29 of any dsrg$(87,30,14,9,11)$ is fixed-point-free and that its three orbit digraphs are circulants decomposing the complete digraph on 29 vertices. A computer search of the resulting three-orbit model found only our three graphs. Some of its exclusion steps use floating-point arithmetic without an error bound, so we report it as a numerical result, not a classification. In the three graphs, all nine connection sets become symmetric after a shift on each orbit. The parameters belong to the family dsrg$(3n,n+1,(n+13)/3,(n-2)/3,(n+4)/3)$ with eigenvalues $1$ and $-3$. A numerical search of a restricted symmetric three-orbit model over $\mathbb Z_n$ finds members at $n=5,17,20,29$ and none at $n=8,11,14,23,26$.
