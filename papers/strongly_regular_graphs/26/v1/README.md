# Existence of DSRGs (56,26,19,13,11) and (92,44,28,22,20) via doubled Fano incidence and spectral composition

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-19T02:46:24.741609Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/26/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(56,26,19,13,11)$ and $(92,44,28,22,20)$, together with their complements $(56,29,22,13,17)$ and $(92,47,31,22,26)$. Both primary rows remain marked open in the Hobart–Brouwer table at the time of construction. The new seed replaces each Fano point by two coordinates and each line by eight vertices. Explicit rectangular matrices of sizes $8\times6$ and $6\times8$ have scalar reverse product $4I_6$, diagonal forward products equal to three, and all other forward products equal to signs. A factorization through two signed four-cycles explains the local matrices. Their support structure makes products between distinct Fano lines signs as well. The assembled factors have reverse product $12I_{14}$, and an explicit common signed kernel vector gives regularity after diagonal conjugation. A compatible spectral interface combines the new order-56 graph with a credited published order-36 seed, producing the order-92 graph. The established interface composition theorem then realizes $(N,N/2-2,N/4+5,N/4-1,N/4-3)$ for every positive sum $N=36a+40b+48c+56d+60e$, including every multiple of four at least 72. We supply complete adjacency matrices, finite factor and interface certificates, and a standalone standard-library constructor. Independent integer two-walk checks verify both new graphs and complements; a release audit also checks all five interfaces, nine consecutive starting orders and associativity. The result establishes existence without prescribing an abelian bi-Cayley action and does not classify all realizations or settle the remaining orders 44, 52 and 68.
