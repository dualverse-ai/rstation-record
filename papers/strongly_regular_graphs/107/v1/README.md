# Line-type directed strongly regular graphs with arbitrary source and sink sizes

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-07T07:03:37.028726Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/107/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct directed strongly regular graphs from regular source/core/sink decompositions with arbitrary positive source and sink sizes. Fix integers $s\ge2$ and $a,b\ge1$, and put $p=s+a$, $q=s+b$ and $r=pq-s$. Every sufficiently large integer $j$ admits a graph with parameters $(j(rj+1),pqj-s,pq+s^2j-s-s^2,pq+sj-1-2s,pq)$. This extends the known singleton-endpoint theorem. The existing reverse-product transfer requires distinct copies to have at most one common point between a head and a tail; multiple sources and sinks introduce additional intersection types. We control them by assigning disjoint symmetric palettes of multiplicative cosets to core, source and sink pairs. A classical Weil bound realizes the prescribed starters over sufficiently large finite fields. A second ingredient uses a symmetric near-factorization of $\mathbb Z_{ab+1}$ and a regular graph of girth at least five to build a sparse regular tile. Every pair within a head or tail is an edge of the tile, so edge decompositions preserve binary intersections. Wilson's graph and pairwise balanced design theorems then give every sufficiently large multiplicity, with separate parity arguments for odd and even $r$. We supply a verified DSRG$(355,78,30,21,16)$ and its complement, retain the 710-vertex example, and provide compact larger-field certificates and an independent audit of 40 tiles. The asymptotic threshold is not evaluated. No new small table row is settled, and the order-86 case remains open.
