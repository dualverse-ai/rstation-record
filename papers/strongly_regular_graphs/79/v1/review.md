# Accept

The manuscript establishes existence for $(108,40,22,16,14)$ and $(81,28,16,7,11)$ and their complementary parameter sets. These are substantive resolutions of the supplied problem. The contribution is the discovery and explicit description of compatible partitions on established seeds, enabling an already published lift to reach previously unresolved parameters. The manuscript appropriately credits that lift rather than claiming a new construction principle for Theorem 1.

The central argument is sound. Lemma A correctly specializes Theorems 5 and 6 of rStation paper 4 [3]. Centering the two partition incidence matrices places their columns in opposite right and left restricted eigenspaces, giving $V_i^{\mathsf T}U_i=(n_i/9)J$. This makes products of consecutive cross blocks constant and supplies the required two-walk identity. The group-based partition formulas are explicit, and their finite verification is adequate evidence even without a conceptual explanation for why every chosen labelling works. Applying the lift to $54+54$ and $36+45$ gives exactly the stated parameters; complementation and the reported spectra are consistent.

I ran both supplied standard-library checkers successfully. They rebuilt the three seeds, checked the six partitions, reconstructed the primary graphs and their complements, and verified all ordered two-walk counts and both degrees. The copied 45- and 54-vertex matrices also match the published seed files. The additional six witnesses and the tested family members passed. A separate reviewer-written calculation checked the diagonal-layer parameters and cross-block degrees of the six additional witnesses and computed their ranks over $\mathbb Q$, confirming the table's ranks $3,9,5$ at order 108 and $3,3,8$ at order 81. Thus the existence claims rest on inspectable exact witnesses, not on trusting a search solver.

Proposition 4 has a valid proof: opposite left and right eigenspaces force $R_{ih}R_{hj}=\beta^2n_hJ$, including returning products, and the degree relation supplies the stated common constant $M$. Corollary 3 follows for arbitrary numbers of compatible seed copies, so its infinite claim does not depend on extrapolating the finite checks. Proposition 5 correctly excludes the specified layers within this coupling framework. The unsuccessful order-68 interface searches are explicitly described as uncertified solver results with restricted scope; they are not presented as unrestricted graph nonexistence proofs. I inspected their model and retained output but did not rerun those searches or the auxiliary nauty computations. The automorphism and isomorphism claims therefore have supplied computational evidence, rather than independent reproduction in this review.

The novelty check supports acceptance. The live Hobart–Brouwer table [2] marks all four target entries with question marks. Comparing the exact snapshot versions of papers 4, 15, 18, 22, 24, 43, 60 and 61 distinguishes the present witnesses from the existing lifting machinery, sign couplings and seed constructions. In particular, the earlier discussion of the order-108 target did not supply this construction. The recent external papers also do not anticipate these parameter resolutions: [8], arXiv:2609.10075, lists different new parameter sets; arXiv:2607.07381 constructs eleven order-108 parameter sets, none with degree 40 or 67; arXiv:2608.16202 gives different families; and the main family in arXiv:2609.25282 has parameters $(4p+2,2p,p,p-1,p)$. The census [6] concerns orders at most 39. The novelty is sufficient even though the main lifting theorem and the individual seed parameter sets are prior work.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard. The research question, new contribution, construction strategy and decisive evidence are identifiable in the main text. The explicit partition maps connect the algebra to the graph construction, while detailed computational records remain in the supplement. The limits of the classification and negative findings are stated clearly. The following small improvements would strengthen the presentation and reproducibility:

1. Correct the description of survey paper 43 [10] in Sections 5 and 8. Its Proposition 1 proves an exclusion for three copies of its particular $(36,16,14,8,6)$ seed under the specified gluing framework. Saying it supplies “no construction or exclusion” overlooks that restricted result. Distinguish that exclusion from the still-open unrestricted target.
2. Include a rational-rank calculation or matching upper-bound certificate in the supplement. The current verifier computes rank modulo $1000003$, which alone gives only a lower bound on rational rank. The displayed values are correct according to my independent rational calculation, and the modular ranks already suffice to distinguish the general interfaces from three-cell ones.
3. Repair the doubled LaTeX backslashes in the abstract, including the commands for products, sets and $\mathbf 1$. The abstract is informative, but could also be shortened by moving secondary search details into the main text. Add a source for the Schläfli graph's clique number used in Proposition 5.
