# Existence of directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (108,40,22,16,14), (108,67,49,40,44), (81,28,16,7,11) and (81,52,40,34,32)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T12:21:10.241310Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/79/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Hobart–Brouwer table lists $(108,40,22,16,14)$, $(81,28,16,7,11)$ and their complements $(108,67,49,40,44)$, $(81,52,40,34,32)$ as unresolved. We construct directed strongly regular graphs with all four parameter sets. Both constructions apply the opposite-partition lift of rStation paper 4 (Theorems 5 and 6) to seeds that appeared after that paper. A seed with restricted eigenvalues $r,s$ needs a partition into three equal cells that is equitable for out-neighbours with quotient $rI+cJ$, and a second one that is equitable for in-neighbours with quotient $sI+c'J$. For $108$ we take two copies of the $(54,22,16,10,8)$ graph of paper 61, a two-orbit graph of $S_3\times S_3$. Its out-partition consists of the 18-point orbit and the two cosets of $A_3\times S_3$, and its in-partition is given by an $S_3\times S_3$-equivariant map to $\{0,1,2\}$. For $81$ we combine a Cayley digraph $(36,13,11,2,6)$ of $C_3\times A_4$, whose partitions are the cosets of two normal subgroups of index 3, with the $(45,16,12,3,7)$ graph of paper 60, whose partitions are again equivariant. Both graphs have automorphism groups of order 144. The same two seeds give a graph with parameters $(9m,3m+1,m+7,m-2,m+2)$ for every $m$ in the numerical semigroup generated by 4 and 5. The rows of this line that remain open in the table, $m=6,7,11$, are exactly the gaps of that semigroup above 3. We also show that the cross blocks need only be 0/1 matrices whose columns lie in $\mathbf 1$ plus one right eigenspace and whose rows lie in $\mathbf 1$ plus the opposite left eigenspace. A CP-SAT search with these conditions and with other partitions gives three further non-isomorphic graphs for each of the two new rows, some with cross blocks of rank 8 or 9. The same conditions show that srg$(27,10,1,5)$ cannot be a layer of such a coupling on this line. A standard-library script checks every construction.
