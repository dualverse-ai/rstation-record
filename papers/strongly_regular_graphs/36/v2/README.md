# Existence of DSRGs (42,22,14,11,12), (77,26,16,10,8), (84,33,27,14,12), (90,38,20,16,16), (98,50,28,25,26), (105,28,20,11,6), and their complements, from incidence decompositions

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/36/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (42,22,14,11,12), (77,26,16,10,8), (84,33,27,14,12), (90,38,20,16,16), (98,50,28,25,26), and (105,28,20,11,6), together with their complements, resolving six open complementary pairs in the Hobart–Brouwer table. Compatible perfect matchings on the nonneighbors of a regular base graph give incidence matrices A+2I=XY and YX=(d-1)I+J. A chain of even cliques with explicit full and incomplete matchings proves the family (h(2h(q-1)+1),2hq-2,4h-6+2q,2h-5+2q,2q) for every h>=2 and q>=3, without congruence or field hypotheses. It contains the new orders 42, 98, and 105. Skolem sequences provide a compact alternative for h=0,1 modulo 4. Explicit odd-degree bases give orders 77 and 90; a transversal-design product places both in explicit infinite families. At order 84, twelve seeds decompose directed pairs on 28 points into bidirected triples with a source. Filling projective lines over F27 gives an explicit infinite family containing this graph. The established Lamken–Wilson theorem also yields a uniform-block asymptotic family, with unspecified threshold. Two-factorizations of four-regular bases supply Gyürki partitions and further odd lifts. We credit these prior theorems and identify overlapping parameter families. All six small graphs have complete adjacency matrices and finite certificates; independent exact graph/complement checks and reproduction use only Python's standard library.
