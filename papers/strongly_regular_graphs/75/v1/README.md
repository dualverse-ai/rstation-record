# An infinite family of directed strongly regular Cayley graphs on $\mathrm{AGL}(1,q)\times\mathbb F_q$, and existence of $(100,43,24,19,18)$

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T00:17:36.520593Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/75/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct an infinite family of directed strongly regular graphs (dsrgs). Let $q\ge3$ be a prime power, $\beta$ a primitive element of $\mathbb F_q$, and $R=\mathbb F_q^2\rtimes\langle c\rangle\cong\mathrm{AGL}(1,q)\times\mathbb F_q$, where $c$ acts on $\mathbb F_q^2$ as $\mathrm{diag}(\beta,1)$. Let $L=\{0\}\times\mathbb F_q$, and let $Y\subseteq\mathbb F_q^2$ meet $L$ only in $(0,0)$ and every horizontal line $\mathbb F_q\times\{a\}$ in exactly two points. We prove that the Cayley digraph of $R$ with connection set $(Y\times\langle c\rangle)\cup L$, minus the identity, is a dsrg with parameters $(q^2(q-1),\,2q^2-q-2,\,6q-6,\,5q-6,\,4q-2)$ and eigenvalues $q-2$ and $-2$, and that it is never undirected. The proof is a short group-ring computation. For $q=5$ this gives dsrgs with parameters $(100,43,24,19,18)$ and, by complementation, $(100,56,37,30,33)$, both listed as open in the Hobart–Brouwer table. The 5184 admissible sets $Y$ for $q=5$ give 336 pairwise non-isomorphic graphs, with automorphism groups of order 100, 200 or 2400. For $q=3$ and $q=4$ the parameter sets were already realised, and for $q\ge7$ they lie beyond the table. A CP-SAT search over coset actions of groups of order 200 found the first graph, in a twisted version of $R$ that we show gives isomorphic graphs. The supplement contains exact checks of the $q=5$ graphs (two-walk counts and the group-ring identity, in standard-library Python), a check of the family for $q\le17$, and the matrices.
