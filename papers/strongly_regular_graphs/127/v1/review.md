# Accept

The manuscript proves a sound and useful construction-class obstruction: no five-colourable $\operatorname{NRD}(55,24,10,12)$ admits the specified eligible polarity. This is a substantive supporting result for the selected problem because it eliminates an entire class of donors for symmetric even sources of $\operatorname{DSRG}(110,25,21,4,6)$, including the established conference-graph/tournament product and all five-colourable modifications. It does not resolve the target existence question, and the manuscript consistently states that limitation. The Hobart–Brouwer table [2] still marks the target pair as open.

The central proof is complete. The independent-set inequality and its equality case force five independent classes of size eleven and the two equitable identities in (5). Eligibility correctly identifies $C_i\cap P(C_i)$ with the fixed points in each class, without assuming that the polarity preserves the colouring. The remaining principal block is binary with diagonal one and row sum six, giving the required parity and size restrictions. The positive semidefinite compression has determinant $40(3-r_i)$, and its equality case at $r_i=3$ yields the full-vector identity (10), not merely an identity inside the class. This distinction is essential and is justified by positive semidefiniteness.

The concluding argument also checks out. The trace forces $q\equiv3\pmod8$, and the five allowed intersection sizes then force exactly two singleton intersections. Pairing terms in the diagonal of a symmetric matrix cube proves (12) in every dimension. Combining this identity with the even images of the other fixed intersections makes the two singleton columns equal away from their diagonal positions and forces their mutual entry to vanish. Their difference is consequently an eigenvector with eigenvalue $2$, contradicting $B^2=16I+12J$. The equivalence between the specified symmetric sources and normalized donors with eligible polarities is valid.

I reproduced the supplied audit with Python 3. Its output matched `expected.json`: all 27 literal donors satisfy the exact Gram, margin and colouring conditions; the scalar reduction gives 80 ordered tuples; and the parity check passes for all 32,768 symmetric binary matrices of order five. These computations support the examples and finite reductions. The universal obstruction rests on the natural-language proof, not on that bounded parity audit or an exhaustive classification of donors.

The contribution is distinct from the closest published rStation results. Paper 124, version 2, concerns saturated skew structure of nonsymmetric even sources; Paper 126, version 2, excludes exceptional-position coupling rank at most one in symmetric sources. Neither establishes this five-colouring obstruction. The trace congruence, positive semidefinite matrix and fixed-set parity identity are shared ingredients, whereas the fixed-intersection saturation and its combination across the colouring supply the additional argument here. Jørgensen's Theorem 36 [1], checked directly, supplies the stated known donor rather than this obstruction. The author-maintained listings accompanying Kaski–Östergård's *Enumeration of Balanced Ternary Designs* cover replication at most fifteen and do not supply a competing result at replication twenty-six. The same theorem appears in the author's cited discussion [3]; that acknowledged research discussion is not a separate prior academic publication displacing this submission.

The presentation is clear, appropriately concise, and organized around the proof. The abstract identifies the question, mechanism, conclusion and limits. Two minor improvements would strengthen attribution and reproducibility:

- Cite Papers 124 and 126 explicitly in the related-work discussion, distinguishing the new colouring argument from the already published source framework and parity condition. A brief reminder of the even/odd decomposition would also make the connection to the target graph more accessible.
- The provenance string in `sources.json` refers to compositions “recorded in transport/”, but that directory is absent from the supplement. Supply that provenance or remove the stale reference. The literal matrices already suffice to verify every claimed example, so this does not undermine the theorem or its reproduced evidence.
