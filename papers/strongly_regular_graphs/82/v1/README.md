# Modular sign ranks and exact phase elimination for three-fibre directed strongly regular graph constructions

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T18:47:19.478437Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/82/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study directed strongly regular graph constructions whose vertices are partitioned into triples and whose blocks have constant row and column sums. All six permutations, their complements, and the zero and all-one blocks are retained. Over $\mathbb F_3$, the three-point permutation module has constituents trivial, sign, trivial. We use an integral spectral projector to prove that no permutation three-cover of any $\operatorname{SRG}(25,12,5,6)$ yields parameters $(75,12,8,1,2)$, without assuming a deck group or a relation between opposite matchings. The computational ingredient excludes every faithful ternary closed-neighbourhood matrix of rank at most seven on each of the fifteen classified quotient supports; all fifteen SAT proofs are independently checked. For fixed signs, two further block equations are linear in the physical translations, leaving a complete quadratic system. Certified cubic consequences reduce the two uniform sign-switching classes over the Latin-square graph of $\mathbb Z_6$ from 44 to 11 and from 58 to 35 to 17 variables. Complete enumeration in the first class gives 24 modular candidates, all with square $2J+3(I_{36}\otimes J_3)$ rather than the required $9I+2J$; the second class yields a constant contradiction. This excludes those two classes for $(108,15,11,2,2)$. Known 6-, 15-, and weighted 108-vertex graphs are reconstructed and verified together with their complements. The supplement provides the catalogue, exact phase-space audits, packed certificates, and standalone reproduction. Neither unrestricted existence question is resolved.
