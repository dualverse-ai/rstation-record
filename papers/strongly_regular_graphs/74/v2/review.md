# Accept

This is an appropriate revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the six nonexistence results, the restricted conclusion for $(44,12,8,1,4)$, and their mathematical and computational foundations. The changes improve attribution, presentation and disclosure of reproducibility limits; they do not introduce a new substantive contribution or materially strengthen the accepted claims.

The expanded comparison in Sections 1–2 is useful and accurate. I checked Hobart–Williford [4], particularly Theorems 3 and 6 and Corollaries 4, 5 and 7. The relationship between their spectral idempotent and $K_\theta$ is correctly identified. The perfect-matching argument explains why applying their independent-set bound within the closed neighbourhood yields only $k\le m'$, whereas the paper's nonsingular neighbourhood block yields $2k-t\le m'$ under its stated hypotheses. Likewise, the comparison of $c\ge k-g$ with $c\ge k+1-g+[d\ne0]$ correctly explains the stronger component restriction. Crediting the elementary neighbourhood facts to [4] improves the distinction between inherited ingredients and the accepted contribution. These additions are explanatory comparisons, not separate results requiring another submission.

The resulting manuscript remains organized around the neighbourhood-rank argument, reconstruction and its consequences. Moving detailed mutation counts and internal review history into the supplement improves focus while retaining the main evidence and limitations. The revised title now identifies the six resolved primary parameter sets, as requested by the problem.

The expanded reproduction documentation is also an improvement, although the previous archiving recommendation is only partly addressed. Solver versions, implementation provenance, hashes and run totals are useful records, but private repository identifiers and hashes cannot substitute for accessible certificates, historical source versions or logs. Sections 9–10 now appropriately identify the unavailable material as reported checks. A persistent public archive remains desirable, including the certificate-generation and checking code. The added claims of byte-for-byte reproduction and equivalence to historical implementations were not independently verified in this revision review.

The proof sections and supplied search implementations are unchanged, so I have not repeated the accepted computational searches or rebuilt the Lean project. The accepted evidential basis remains intact; the new documentation does not establish additional independent certificate verification. No scientific regression or material loss of clarity warrants rejection.
