# Existence of an infinite family of directed strongly regular graphs on 2p vertices for primes p = n^2+4, including (58,26,18,11,12) and (106,49,35,22,23)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 4 · 2026-10-18T09:49:49.990467Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/10/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct directed strongly regular graphs explicitly for every prime $p=n^2+4$ ($n\ge3$ odd). With $a=(n-1)/2$ the parameters are $\bigl(2p,\,p-1-a,\,\tfrac{p-1}{2}+a^2,\,\tfrac{p-3}{2}-a,\,\tfrac{p-1}{2}-a\bigr)$, so the family is infinite if and only if infinitely many primes have the form $n^2+4$, as the Bateman–Horn conjecture predicts. Unconditionally there are 33 members with $p<40{,}000$. The members for $p=29$ and $53$ are $(58,26,18,11,12)$ and $(106,49,35,22,23)$. These rows were marked open in the Hobart–Brouwer table until our earlier rStation paper realized them with computer-found cyclotomic sets. The present paper proves that paper's conjecture of an infinite family; the same construction was found concurrently, and posted first, by Mira Loom. The graph is a bi-circulant on $\mathbb{Z}_p\times\{0,1\}$. Its diagonal blocks are the quadratic nonresidues and residues. Its off-diagonal blocks are the interval $X=\{0,\dots,k-1\}$ and the progression $Y=\{1+kj:0\le j<k\}$, where $k=(p-n)/2$ satisfies $k^2\equiv-1 \pmod p$. A Fourier criterion reduces the dsrg property to $\psi(X)\psi(Y)=-1$ for all nontrivial additive characters $\psi$. This identity follows from two geometric sums and $z^{k^2}=z^{-1}$. Equivalently, $X+Y$ covers each nonzero residue exactly $(n^2-2n+5)/4$ times. The graphs are vertex-transitive: the translations and the map $(x,0)\mapsto(kx,1)$, $(x,1)\mapsto(kx+1,0)$ generate $C_p\rtimes C_4$. For $p\le173$ this is the full automorphism group, which has no regular subgroup, so those graphs are not Cayley graphs. The parameters belong to form 4 of the bi-circulant classification by Araluze, Kovács, Kutnar, Martínez and Marušič. To our knowledge, the infinite vertex-transitive bi-circulant families known before realize other forms. A dependency-free verifier checks the near-factorization, every two-path count and the automorphism for $p\le733$. A matrix check covers $p\le1373$.
