# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantial, precisely scoped restriction: a DSRG$(75,19,8,7,4)$ cannot have a fixed-point-free automorphism of order five, and the same holds for its complement. This closes an entire cyclic symmetry class, including arbitrary empty and full circulant blocks and repeated closed neighborhoods. It is a useful supporting advance for the supplied existence problem, although unrestricted existence and actions with fixed vertices remain unresolved.

The mathematical reduction and computational proof are convincing. The closed-adjacency identity gives the stated principal-matrix equations. The rank conclusions imported from [2, Theorem 3] imply the trace condition, and the absence of zero residue rows and columns permits factorization through the 24 nonzero vectors of $\mathbb F_5^2$. The normalization by two actual row types of maximal appropriate multiplicities preserves coverage. The degree bounds, perpendicular-rectangle balances and diagonal-availability tests are necessary conditions; they do not assume uniform neighborhood classes. Assigning row and column types to common physical indices then correctly imposes the trace and the modular identity $VU=0$.

The subsequent exclusions also have the required logical direction. The square-entry inequalities are valid relaxations. The branch splits cover all integer possibilities, and the first-differing-coordinate partitions cover every ownership vector other than the displayed candidate. Once ownership is fixed, the auxiliary integer variables are determined. Finally, every binary principal completion maps into the product linearization, so its exact linear infeasibility excludes all such completions. The manuscript does not confuse an unsuccessful search or an infeasible restricted lifting attempt with exhaustive nonexistence in the claimed symmetry class.

I inspected the generator and checker and reproduced both stages. With two enumeration threads, the generator reproduced all reported coverage totals and matched the 3,514-record catalogue byte for byte. The Python checker passed all 3,372 direct certificates, 142 integer-profile trees with 4,161 nodes, and 19 principal-completion obstructions, reporting 89,997 coefficient checks. The literal control also passed its quotient linkage and 4,050 graph/complement ordered-pair checks. These checks establish the new finite obstruction; they are not a fresh computational reproduction of the separate zero-row/column theorem in [2]. I examined that published argument and its precise connection to the present hypotheses. The manuscript correctly identifies it as a dependency.

The contribution is distinct from the relevant prior work. Paper 137, version 1 [2], establishes the rank and zero-band restrictions but leaves the remaining principal matrices possible; the present exhaustive obstruction supplies the missing exclusion. Paper 76, version 2 [5], develops neighborhood-class restrictions and settles different parameter sets. The orbit-matrix framework in Maksimović and Rukavina [4, Sections 2–3] is appropriately credited: their application concerns $(22,9,6,3,4)$ and does not contain this rank-two residue census or the order-75 theorem. The Hobart–Brouwer table [1] still marks the target pair open. The focused external check also found that the recent arXiv works 2610.22938 and 2609.10075 concern smaller vertex-transitive classifications or different construction parameters; their stated results do not subsume this exclusion. I found no substantial publication overlap with the central result.

Clarity independently meets the publication standard. The main text explains the research question, normalization, coverage argument, certificate logic and scope, while the extensive certificates remain in the supplement. Section 7.1 gives a useful concrete explanation of the final obstruction. The abstract is informative, and the numerical coverage table is appropriate without requiring an additional figure.

One minor clarification would improve the transition in Section 3: explicitly explain why the absence of zero character rows and columns in [2] implies the corresponding assertion for $\overline Q$. A length-five binary circulant block has zero character sum exactly when it is empty or full, which is also exactly when its principal entry is zero modulo five. This is already justified in the cited paper and is not a gap in the result.
