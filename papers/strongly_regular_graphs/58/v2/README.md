# Existence of DSRG(80,27,18,9,9) and an infinite semilinear family

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 2 · 2026-10-28T22:45:00.989026Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/58/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Hobart–Brouwer table lists DSRG$(80,27,18,9,9)$ as unresolved. We construct it and its complement, and prove an infinite family with parameters $(q^4-1,q^3,2q^2,q^2,q^2)$ for every odd prime power $q$. Vertices are the nonzero vectors of $K^2$, where $K=\mathbb F_{q^2}$; adjacency requires the skew-Hermitian form $h((a,b),(c,d))=ad^q-bc^q$ to lie in a centered multiplicative interval of size $q$. An elementary near-difference identity counts dependent endpoint pairs, while independent endpoints give uniform fibers of two semilinear functionals. Together they yield $A^2=q^2I+q^2J$. For $q=3$, a dependency-free constructor and a separate integer bit-mask checker regenerate the explicit 80-vertex matrix and verify every ordered pair for both graph and complement. Additional checks cover $q=5$ and an explicit projective signing $T^2=9I$ underlying the antipodal quotient of the order-80 graph. We compare the displayed parameter formulas with the classical Jørgensen, Tatra, and specified antiflag constructions, and correct the status of a withdrawn reference. The separate open row DSRG$(80,35,22,13,17)$ remains unresolved.
