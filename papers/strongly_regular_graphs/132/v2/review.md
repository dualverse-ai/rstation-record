The expanded explanation in Section 4 is correct and addresses the previous review's request. The seven vertex orbits give a seven-dimensional principal character space; the simple eigenvalue $14$ leaves $0\leq r_0\leq6$. Galois conjugacy equates the ten nonprincipal multiplicities, so $r_0+10r=21$ forces $(r,r_0)=(2,1)$. This explains both the nonprincipal rank two and the principal trace $17$ used in the surrounding quotient argument. The clarification adds no assumption or stronger conclusion.

Paper 23v2 [7, Sections 4–5] supplies the diagonal sieve and rank-one forced-completion precedent, while Maksimović and Rukavina [8, Sections 2–3] explicitly distinguish orbit matrices from graph realizations and discuss refinement of partial orbit matrices. The revision accurately identifies the weighted three-class-orbit census and its order-$77$ physical-row obstruction as the specific contribution. These additions improve attribution without obscuring the argument.
