# Accept

This is an appropriate revision of the accepted paper. The changes make the finite check in Proposition 3 explicit, clarify the computational support for the automorphism assertions, and update the status of the remaining parameter set with attribution to subsequent work. They do not introduce a new substantive contribution or weaken the accepted existence results. The resulting manuscript remains focused on the 54-vertex construction, its two compatible partitions, and the lift to 99 vertices; the additions improve the explanation without turning it into a collection of unrelated results.

The new table in Proposition 3 correctly supplies the five representative checks needed by the existing symmetry argument. The representatives have $V$-cell labels $0,0,1,2,0$, respectively, and the displayed incoming-count triples put 3 in the vertex's own cell and 8 in each other cell. The action of $L=S_3\times\{e,021\}$ permutes the cells, so these checks extend to the five stated orbits. I inspected and ran the revised `verify.py`; its output agrees exactly with the submitted `verify.out`, including all five added rows. It also passes the full partition and equivariance checks and the existing graph reconstruction, matrix-comparison and parameter checks. Thus the added table is supported by recomputation, rather than merely by the retained output.

The changes to Section 6, the supplement README and `aut.py` correctly distinguish the existence certificates from the computations supporting the automorphism-group and converse non-isomorphism assertions. Recording pynauty 2.8.8.1 improves reproducibility. Inspection of the script differences shows that the automorphism computation itself is unchanged; the executable addition prints the package version. I did not rerun nauty or the discovery search in this revision review, so the reproduced checks above should not be read as independent reproduction of those computations.

The update concerning $(63,22,14,5,9)$ is supported by the supplied published version of paper 83, now reference [13]. Its Theorem 1 constructs this graph and its complement, and its Corollary 2 combines that result with the earlier constructions to establish the stated existence range. Consequently, the revised abstract's assertion that the line exists exactly for integer $m\ge4$ is justified under the paper's definition of a dsrg: the necessity follows from $t<k$, or $m+7<3m+1$, and the new citation supplies the previously missing $m=7$ case. This is a properly attributed update about subsequent work, not an attempt to incorporate that work as a new result of this revision. Retaining the original scope of Corollary 4 is appropriate.

One minor wording refinement would improve precision: replace “not by a lift” in the abstract and the corresponding wording in Section 4 with “not by the opposite-partition lift of Lemma A.” Reference [13] uses a two-layer construction whose reverse cross block is not of the required partition form; the narrower wording identifies the relevant distinction. This clarification is not a condition of acceptance.
