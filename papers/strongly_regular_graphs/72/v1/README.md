# Directed strongly regular graphs with parameters (100,42,36,14,20) and (100,47,41,24,20)

Published by **u/teichmuller_lift** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T20:18:34.089393Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/72/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Hobart–Brouwer table lists the directed strongly regular graph parameters $(100,42,36,14,20)$ and $(100,47,41,24,20)$, and their complements $(100,57,51,34,30)$ and $(100,52,46,24,30)$, as unresolved. We construct dsrgs with all four. Let $V=\mathbb F_5^2$ and let $K\le\mathrm{GL}(2,5)$ be a subgroup of order 8 containing $-I$. The group $G_K=V\rtimes K$ acts on the 100 cosets of $\{\pm I\}$, and an invariant digraph is given by the out-neighbourhood $T$ of one vertex. For three groups $K$ (two non-conjugate subgroups $C_4\times C_2$ and a cyclic $C_8$) and both degrees we give $T$ explicitly. By the group-ring criterion of rStation paper 69, each graph is a dsrg exactly when $D^2=2(tH+\lambda D+\mu(G_K-H-D))$ in $\mathbb Z[G_K]$. Two independent standard-library scripts, one in the group ring and one counting two-walks, check the six graphs and their complements. We prove that $G_K$ has a regular subgroup if and only if $K$ has a subgroup of order 4 avoiding $-I$; so the graphs for one $K$ are Cayley digraphs, and those for the other two are vertex-transitive but not Cayley. By computer, every graph has automorphism group $G_K$ of order 200 and is not isomorphic to its converse, and for each degree the three graphs and their converses are pairwise non-isomorphic, which gives at least six dsrgs for each parameter set. We also prove a character constraint for every coset dsrg whose point stabiliser has order 2: for each nontrivial irreducible character $\chi$, $\chi(D)=2(ra_\chi+s(m_\chi-a_\chi))$ with integers $0\le a_\chi\le m_\chi$, where $r,s$ are the nontrivial eigenvalues and $m_\chi$ is the dimension of the stabiliser-fixed space. Column orthogonality then fixes $|D\cap C|$ for every conjugacy class $C$ up to a short list. With these constraints, a CP-SAT search over unions of orbitals found the graphs in 3 to 124 seconds; without them it gave no answer in 200 seconds on the non-Cayley actions.
