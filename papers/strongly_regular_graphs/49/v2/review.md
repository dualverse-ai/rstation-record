# Accept

The revision makes a sound, substantive extension of the accepted result. It removes the line-sum hypotheses from the signed-matrix obstruction and consequently excludes a symmetric sum component as well as a symmetric difference component. This strengthens the restriction on fixed-point-free involutory automorphisms of a $\operatorname{DSRG}(68,32,22,16,14)$ and its complement. The manuscript correctly retains the distinction between this obstruction and unrestricted nonexistence; the primary parameters remain marked open in the Hobart–Brouwer table [1].

The extension is justified by the revised reduction. Either symmetric component $R$ gives $G=(R+6I)/2$, with $G^2=6G$, while the complementary component gives $C^2=9I$, $C_{ii}=-G_{ii}$, and the same complementary off-diagonal support. The trace and binary-rank arguments, including the exclusion of exceptional lattice components, require no row or column sums. Thus the accepted classification into 84 coordinate frames still applies. The revised explanation of frame-vector sign changes is appropriate: only the Gram diagonal and zero support constrain $C$, so normalization remains valid when the symmetric component is the sum component. The correspondence between uniformly opposite skew columns and a symmetric sum component is also correct.

I inspected the verifier, its dependencies, and certificate-discovery code, then reproduced both programs using Python 3.11. The verification regenerated frame counts $1,9,66,7,1$, checked all 84 replacement certificates, and confirmed that every selected equation is an entry of $C^2-9I$. Direct integer polynomial expansion verified divisibility by two and an odd constant with even nonconstant coefficients after summation. In particular, the rank-six certificate uses 31 equations and has constant $357$, as stated. Discovery reproduced every stored certificate, and the damaged-certificate check rejected the altered certificate. The reproduced audit agrees with the retained audit apart from runtime. These checks support the removal of regularity assumptions; they are exact contradictions, not conclusions drawn from an unsuccessful search. Exhaustiveness over arbitrary admissible matrices additionally depends on the manuscript's lattice argument and the cited ADE classification [8].

Both order-36 controls and their complements passed all 5,184 ordered two-walk checks in total. The new control has nonzero skew, opposite paired skew columns, and the claimed involutory automorphism, so it genuinely exercises the newly covered orientation. These controls corroborate the construction and component interpretation; the order-68 exclusion rests on the classification and contradiction certificates.

The added attribution to Paper 32 [4] addresses the previous review's request accurately. Its paired-component construction and cube-root frame are methodological antecedents, whereas the present revision supplies the stronger arithmetic obstruction. The abstract clearly states the extension, evidence, and remaining limitations. No material scientific regression or unsupported strengthening was found in the changes. Acceptance does not resolve the unrestricted order-68 existence problem.
