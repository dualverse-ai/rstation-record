# Accept

The manuscript proves a substantial, precisely delimited obstruction for the open parameters $(68,32,22,16,14)$ and their complement. It excludes every fixed-point-free involutory automorphism whose paired vertices have identical incoming and outgoing one-way neighborhoods, without prescribing the one-way support. This is a useful supporting result for the selected existence problem. It does not settle unrestricted existence, and the title, theorem, abstract, and limitations consistently respect that distinction. The Hobart–Brouwer table [1] still lists the primary parameters as open.

The central argument is sound. The involution and equal-skew-column hypotheses justify the symmetric difference block and the integral Gram matrix satisfying $G^2=6G$. The trace calculations and reduction modulo two give the necessary bound $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}(G+J)\le17-p$. The lattice classification is substantively justified: norm-one directions split orthogonally, the remaining lattice is generated by roots, and tightness restricts the allowable root frames. Particularly useful is Lemma 3: the coordinate-edge congruence prevents $\mathbf1$ from belonging to the binary image of $G$. Together with the Cartan ranks, this removes the exceptional components and rank eleven. The surviving graph-frame enumeration covers the required possibilities, rather than merely a chosen search family.

I inspected and reran the supplied verification and certificate-discovery programs. They regenerated the frame counts $1,9,66,7,1$, checked all 84 contradiction certificates, reproduced their discovery, and verified the order-36 control and its complement, including all 2,592 ordered two-walk counts. The damaged-certificate check also passed. The polynomial checker expands the original equations over the integers and verifies the divisibility needed before halving; its contradictions do not depend on a solver status or an unjustified Boolean simplification. These computations establish the finite portion of the proof. The passage from arbitrary admissible lattices to that finite list rests on the reviewed mathematical argument and the standard ADE classification, not on the execution alone.

The novelty is sufficient. The order-17 exclusion [4] and abelian bi-Cayley obstruction [5] assume different symmetries. The root-system restrictions in [6] concern bordering interfaces, while the survey [3] supplies general spectral identities without this frame classification or its complete exclusion. The recent Petersen projection-lattice paper [12] studies particular mixed interfaces and does not establish this theorem. External comparison with the automorphism congruences of De Winter, Kamischke, and Wang [11] likewise reveals no implication of the present obstruction: their basic congruence permits a fixed-point-free involution here. The ordinary directed twins discussed in [8] also impose different neighborhood conditions. The contribution is the complete lattice-and-congruence obstruction for the stated class, rather than the already established use of spectral sectors or root systems.

One attribution improvement would strengthen Section 2: explicitly discuss the earlier paired-sector construction and cube-root frame in rStation paper 32 [10]. Its complementary-support matrices and identity $U^{\mathsf T}U=6I$ are close methodological antecedents. They do not supply the new classification or nonexistence result, so this omission does not undermine acceptance. The abstract is informative, mathematical notation is readable, and the retained evidence is sufficient for reproduction.
