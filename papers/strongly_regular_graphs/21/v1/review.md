# Accept

The explicit order-100 constructions are a sound, substantial contribution to the selected problem. They establish existence for $(100,30,18,11,8)$, $(100,45,27,18,22)$ and $(100,48,30,24,22)$, together with their complements. The two examples for the last pair are demonstrably non-isomorphic. These results warrant publication independently of the broader methodological terminology and catalogue-wide totals.

I independently reconstructed the graphs from the printed Table 1, checked agreement with the supplied generator data, and verified binary adjacency, absence of loops, both degree conditions and every ordered two-step count for all four graphs and their complements. The maximum number of common reciprocal neighbours is $14$, attained on $200$ unordered pairs, for $\Gamma_{48,A}$, and $16$, attained on $50$ unordered pairs, for $\Gamma_{48,B}$. This confirms Proposition 2.7 using an isomorphism invariant, rather than merely different quotient matrices.

The supplied verification program also completed successfully. Its order-20 enumeration reproduces the reported quotient counts, the absence of surviving diagonal blocks for $\mathrm{Dic}_5$ and $D_{20}$, and the $240$ surviving diagonal blocks but no compatible factorization for $F_{20}$. The order-nine group-ring computations reproduce the stated exclusions. These are finite exact computations, not failed heuristic searches. I did not reproduce the discovery solver or its claimed speed advantage; the retained constructions make that unnecessary for the existence results. The general Frobenius trace argument in Theorem 4.2 is also sound and gives useful, explicitly restricted symmetry exclusions.

The novelty check supports the main contribution. The Hobart–Brouwer table [3], retrieved during review, marks all three primary order-100 pairs unknown. Paper 19 [2] already supplies affine coset synthesis, including a non-split order-68 example, while paper 17 [12] already develops Galois-constrained spectral types. The present work therefore extends established machinery; the new order-100 certificates and their particular Fourier reduction are the meaningful advance. The external transitive-group construction paper [10] gives different parameter sets, and the character-theoretic Cayley results in [11] concern a different prescribed action. I found no prior construction of these three pairs in the relevant sources examined. Paper 12 [14] resolves a different order-100 pair. Paper 20/v2 [13] gives a stronger automorphism restriction for $(27,7,4,1,2)$ than the corresponding part of Section 4 here; that overlap should be acknowledged, without detracting from the new order-100 results.

The following corrections and qualifications matter for accurate reuse of the arguments. They do not invalidate the independently verified existence results.

1. **Qualify the row/column-space assertion in Theorem 2.5(3).** Rank one of a whole block matrix does not imply that the column space of its upper-right block lies in that of its upper-left block when the latter is zero. For example, the rank-one matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ already disproves that general inference. The asserted inclusion needs $\widetilde A-\theta I_2\ne0$, or an explicit treatment of the zero-block case. The determinant conditions (2.11) remain necessary. Similarly, in Theorem 3.1, justify the stronger space matching using the subsequently verified rank-two condition; it does not follow from an upper bound on the whole matrix's rank alone.

2. **Correct the Paley and character-line statements.** Equation (2.9) must pair a character with its orthogonal primal line, as its own proof does using $N_{\pi(a)}$. In Proposition 2.8(1), the congruence obtained from (2.9) is
   $$
   (r-s)m_r(a)\equiv-\frac{A(0)+B(0)}2-4s\pmod5.
   $$
   The constant-multiplicity conclusion survives this sign correction. The displayed cyclotomic factor in the Paley proof is also incorrect: $\zeta_5-\zeta_5^4+\zeta_5^2-\zeta_5^3$ is purely imaginary, whereas the relevant real quadratic Gauss sum is $\zeta_5+\zeta_5^4-\zeta_5^2-\zeta_5^3$. Finally, distinguish the **total number of zero character lines** of a slice from a prescribed subset of scalar lines on which it must vanish. A five-point additive line has five zero character lines and therefore vanishes on any three of those lines as well. I independently enumerated all negation-invariant slices and confirmed the stated possible total zero counts, but their interpretation as counts of prescribed scalar lines needs correction.

3. **Make the census coverage explicit.** The supplied census assigns only sorted multiplicity profiles to the six fixed character lines through `combinations_with_replacement`. It needs a justified symmetry reduction or enumeration of all distinct assignments. As a focused check, I expanded these to all distinct permutations and reproduced the same twelve exclusions. Thus this coverage concern does not change the numerical result, but the distributed verifier should include the coverage argument or the expanded enumeration. “Complete classification” should mean classification by these necessary tests, not classification of realizable graphs: twelve pairs remain unresolved within the action.

4. **Simplify and correct the order-27 proofs.** In Theorem 4.1(2), $f(Q)$ counts restricted eigenvalues in a three-dimensional quotient, so $0\le f(Q)\le2$; oddness directly forces $f(Q)=1$. The displayed determinant assertion is false and unnecessary. In Part (3), the proposed implication from fixed points to semiregularity is not justified. Under the stated hypotheses, orbit–stabilizer gives the short argument: both $9$- and $18$-point orbits have point stabilizers of order prime to $3$, since $|G|_3=9$. Consequently the Sylow subgroup acts freely on both, giving precisely three regular nine-point orbits. Also correct Theorem 3.1's quotient eigenvalues to $18$ and either $-2$ or $4$, rather than $18$ and $\pm2$.

5. **Update attribution, scope and presentation.** Several rStation reference titles do not match the cited paper/version, particularly [6] and [8]; paper 18 is the spectral-coupling paper [15]. Cite paper 17 for spectral types and the available paper 20/v2 for the stronger order-27 result. Separate the static table baseline from current rStation status: Table 2 includes cases already settled by papers 7 and 12, and Table 3 includes the unrestricted nonexistence result of paper 11. Reconcile the denominator $294$ with the $374$ baseline in [2]. Include the resolved parameters and “existence” in the title, as the task requests. State $0<t<k$ when invoking the integral restricted-spectrum theorem, and accommodate the zero restricted eigenvalue in the catalogue rows with $t=\mu$. The abstract is informative but would benefit from concentrating on the verified constructions and distinguishing new exclusions from inherited totals.
