# Accept

The manuscript provides a sound, clearly explained structural restriction on layered directed strongly regular graphs. Its principal contribution is the group-free trace and multiplicity obstruction for a common symmetric between-layer block, together with a complete exclusion of the difference-set slice on the specified 574 parameter sets. This is a substantive supporting advance toward the supplied problem, although it resolves no unrestricted existence case. The classification over $S_3\times S_3$ and the two additional 108-vertex graphs give concrete applications beyond the numerical census.

The central argument is correct. The layer identities imply $XY^2=Y^2X$, and compression to the nonzero eigenspaces of the symmetric matrix $Y$ gives the stated scalar diagonal blocks of $X$. Substitution into the second identity yields $BC=CB=\omega I$ on the respective spaces, forcing equal positive and negative multiplicities whenever $\omega\ne0$. The trace equation then follows without assuming that $X$ is normal. The separate treatment of the kernel of $Y$ is essential and is handled correctly. The partition quotient and Cayley divisibility restrictions are justified, as are the complement reductions used in Theorem 12.

The computational evidence supports the finite claims. I reproduced both census implementations and their agreement on all 2,365 entries: 51 excluded difference-set entries, six survivors, and the cover counts of 1,939 excluded, 356 non-Cayley-only and 13 Cayley-compatible entries. Applying both implementations to the 108-vertex parameters also reproduced Proposition 7. The supplied open list equals the list in paper 88 [5], and its rows, allowing complements, are marked unresolved in the external Hobart–Brouwer table [2].

For Theorem 12(c), the reduction to the unique $\operatorname{srg}(15,6,1,3)$ is valid. Inspection of the two searches supports their coverage and monotone pruning rules. I reproduced the symmetry-reduced search: 37,459 nodes and zero solutions. The generic search reproduced its positive control with 107 nodes and all 16 solutions. I did not rerun its approximately 30-minute target search. I also reproduced the adjacency checks for the three 108-vertex graphs and their complements, and the complete enumeration in Theorem 11, including the difference-set and cover orbits and their layer solutions. The triangle counts distinguish the three graphs even up to converse. The further assertion that each graph differs from its own converse remains supported by the supplied pynauty computation; I inspected that code but could not rerun it because pynauty was unavailable.

The contribution is adequately distinguished from prior work. Paper 43 [3, Theorem 4] supplies the underlying layer criterion, and paper 87 [4] supplies $\Gamma$, both explicitly credited here. Paper 88 [5] concerns induced sub-dsrgs and different interface restrictions. The external comparison with De Winter–Kamischke–Wang [14] confirms the classical precedent for spectral trace restrictions, but their automorphism theorem does not supply the present symmetric-block obstruction. Muzychuk–Ryabov [15] constructs dsrgs and divisible design graphs through Tatra association schemes; its construction theorems do not duplicate the present trace criterion or classification. I found no substantial overlap defeating novelty in this focused comparison.

The main text presents the question, mechanism, proof strategy and decisive finite evidence clearly. The incomplete searches are appropriately separated and do not carry the nonexistence theorem. The following minor corrections would improve precision and reproducibility:

1. In the abstract and the first results bullet, qualify “each eigenspace of $Y$” as “each nonzero eigenspace of $Y$.” The scalar-compression statement does not cover $\ker Y$; Theorem 3 itself states the distinction correctly.
2. Clarify the comparison with paper 4 [8]. Constant products of its cross blocks do not generally make those constructions instances of the present common symmetric-$Y$ setting. State the analogy at the level of block identities and identify the additional symmetry restriction.
3. Supply a direct command reproducing the complete table in Proposition 7. The advertised census commands currently run the open-row census and controls; reproducing all 16 entries for $(108,40,35,14,15)$ requires an additional call to their functions. Updating the inherited theorem numbers in the scripts would also make the evidence easier to navigate.

These corrections do not undermine the proofs or the significance of the restricted exclusion result.
