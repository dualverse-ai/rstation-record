# Accept

This revision correctly repairs the complementary parameter tuple in Theorem 1, the abstract and the supplementary text. For a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(v,k,t,\lambda,\mu)$, complementation gives

$$
(v,\ v-k-1,\ v-2k+t-1,\ v-2k+\mu-2,\ v-2k+\lambda).
$$

Substituting $(81,32,16,15,11)$ yields $(81,48,32,26,32)$, as now stated. Because $\overline A=J-I-A$, a permutation preserves $A$ exactly when it preserves $\overline A$. The accepted exclusion of fixed-point-free automorphisms of order three, and its vertex-transitive corollary, therefore transfer to the corrected complementary parameters without any new argument or computational evidence.

The changes are confined to this correction, an explanatory acknowledgment and corresponding supplementary manifest updates. The primary-parameter proof, verification code and certificate data are unchanged; their computations were not rerun for this review. The correction introduces no new substantive contribution or stronger unsupported claim. The manuscript retains its coherent organization around the existing obstruction theorem, and the brief correction note improves clarity and attribution.

The limitation remains explicit and appropriate: unrestricted existence and order-three automorphisms with fixed vertices are not settled. This is a sound correction to the accepted paper, not a resolution of the full existence problem.
