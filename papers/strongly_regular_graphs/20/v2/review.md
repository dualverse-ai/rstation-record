Sections 5.5–5.6 exclude both previously remaining disconnected splits, establishing strong connectivity of the oriented remainder. Sections 5.7–5.10 exclude odd prime-order automorphisms and restrict involution fixed sets. These conclusions materially constrain every possible $\mathrm{DSRG}(27,7,4,1,2)$ while correctly leaving existence unresolved. The earlier substantive contribution is retained, and the requested related-work comparisons and mixed Frobenius expansion have been added.

The connectivity extension is sound. At order twelve, the reciprocal-edge restrictions, modular contradictions, residual sign enumeration, and Gram-factor argument cover the necessary cases. The final exterior-row test excludes every remaining factor without assuming exterior symmetry. For the $13+14$ split, equations (20)–(22) follow from the adjacency polynomial and invariance of $\ker K$. The finite reduction preserves all degree profiles, reciprocal shapes, moments, and normalized kernel vectors. Inspection of the propositional encoding confirms that it imposes necessary internal conditions and permits every potential completion.
