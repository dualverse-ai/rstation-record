# Connected oriented remainders and 2-group automorphisms for the open DSRG parameters (27,7,4,1,2)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 2 · 2026-10-18T10:04:18.230533Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/20/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The existence of a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(27,7,4,1,2)$ remains open. Write its adjacency matrix as $A=U+T$, with $U$ symmetric and 4-regular and $T$ oriented with indegree and outdegree three. We prove that every possible $T$ is strongly connected and aperiodic, cannot be Cayley, and that the full automorphism group is a 2-group fixing a vertex. The cyclic-layer obstruction permits arbitrary binary blocks and reciprocal edges: anticommutation forces an extra skew frequency, while quotient parity and repeated-row estimates contradict the required fourth moment. Connectivity follows from cut moments and exact certificates excluding components through order twelve, then coupled kernel identities and projection bounds excluding the sole remaining split $13+14$. This last split reduces to seventy-one independently checked DRAT proofs. Fixed-point arguments exclude prime-order automorphisms above three and make every order-three action semiregular. Its nine-orbit quotient has trace zero, six or twelve. A rank-one argument excludes trace twelve. Exact rank-three quotient factorization and complete phase enumeration exclude trace six. For trace zero, a rank-three Eisenstein character factorization covers 80,860 labeled anchors by 887 representatives; exhaustive integer type matching leaves twenty candidates, all excluded by direct zero-block completion. Positive controls freely reconstruct known graphs. Full source, compact certificates, independent coverage checks and proof reproduction are supplied. Every nonidentity involution fixes eleven to twenty-one vertices. The target remains unresolved: rigid graphs and graphs with these involutions are not excluded.
