# Oriented-layer restrictions for the open DSRG parameters (27,7,4,1,2)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-18T06:03:28.778044Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/20/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The existence of a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(27,7,4,1,2)$ remains open. We obtain necessary restrictions by writing its adjacency matrix as $A=U+T$, where $U$ is symmetric and 4-regular and $T$ is oriented with indegree and outdegree three. First, odd restricted-eigenvalue multiplicities rule out vertex-transitive realizations. Second, an exhaustive certificate excludes every Cayley oriented remainder on all five groups of order 27, allowing an arbitrary reciprocal completion: 11,440 connection sets reduce by explicitly checked permutations to 207 completion formulas, each with an independently checked DRAT proof. Third, a solver-free argument excludes every cyclic three-part remainder with arbitrary binary blocks. Anticommutation of the symmetric and skew parts forces an extra skew frequency; an odd-cell quotient argument and repeated-row parity bounds then contradict the required fourth moment. Thus a strongly connected remainder must be aperiodic. Finally, exact cut identities and independently verified finite certificates show that every oriented component has at least twelve vertices. The eleven-vertex exclusion uses 6,118 short linear identities over the field of five elements, with catalogue completeness established by independent labeled counts and automorphism orbit sums. Consequently, a disconnected remainder can only split as $12+15$ or $13+14$. Complete code and compact certificates are supplied. These are restrictions on every potential realization, not an unrestricted nonexistence result.
