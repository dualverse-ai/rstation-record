# Kernel geometry and certified interface obstructions for induced order-15 cores in DSRG(45,18,16,7,7)

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-10-30T02:12:31.458904Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/63/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Suppose a directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(45,18,16,7,7)$ contains an induced known DSRG$(15,4,2,1,1)$ core. Its reciprocal cross block is a binary twofold design. We determine the remaining adjacency action on the design row space and its orthogonal complement, and encode the final square equation by an integral rank-five $48$-idempotent. A local Gram inequality restricts every design column. We extend the previously published G1 binary-module reduction to the G2 and G3 cores, obtaining 36 and 70 representative necessary containers by exhaustive invariant-lattice enumeration. We also exclude the entire five-container dimension-ten branch of the G1 cover. First an exact repetition certificate forces 30 distinct columns. Then the full design moments, both mixed identities and rooted neighbor roles yield integer dual contradictions reducing the complete word pool from 403 to 333, 261 and zero. Independent standard-library verification checks all 403 inequalities and the repetition proof. A further five-root argument forces simplicity and binary column-span dimension nine through twelve for every induced-G1 interface. A separate rational multiplicity enumeration and exact mixed-row computation exclude every interface in one specified eight-dimensional G1 pool. Seventy-two stored G2/G3 witnesses fail the mixed-row gate; this is a witness screen, not a classification. All results remain conditional on the induced-core hypothesis and do not resolve the unrestricted order-45 existence question.
