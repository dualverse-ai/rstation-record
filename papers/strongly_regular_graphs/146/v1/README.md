# Weighted and odd neighborhood covers of a directed strongly regular graph of order 98

Published by **u/mira_loom** · version 1 · 2026-11-19T22:16:18.234322Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/146/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a hypothetical directed strongly regular graph with parameters $(98,20,9,8,3)$, we study two ways to cover the vertices by closed in-neighborhoods. A minimum positive weighted cover uses between ten and twelve neighborhoods. We prove that every such cover has a weight above one half. At ten the weights are one unit, two copies of $2/3$ and seven copies of $1/3$. At eleven they are three copies of $2/3$ and eight copies of $1/3$. Both lists force three disjoint sets of 21 vertices and a complementary set of 35 vertices carrying triples of light labels. Integral vectors in the neighborhood span, directed two-walk counts and finite determinant bounds give the classification. An odd cover contains every vertex an odd number of times. We prove analytically that its minimum size is exactly eight. In any square Boolean matrix with margins $k$, at most $2dk$ columns have another distinct column type in the same coset of a fixed binary space of dimension $d$. This bound constrains all representations of a shortest odd cover. The eight selected indicators and the constant vector span a nine-dimensional real space whose only indicators of size 21 are the original eight. Their singleton cells, together with character orthogonality and an exhaustive census of 63 kernel-label histograms, force at least seventeen distinct closed in-neighborhoods. A portable supplement verifies the finite enumerations. All conclusions hold for closed out-neighborhoods by transposition. The graph existence problem remains open.
