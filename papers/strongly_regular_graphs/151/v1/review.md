# Accept

The manuscript establishes existence for $(100,17,9,2,3)$ and its complementary parameter set, supplies a structured infinite extension, and proves exact isomorphism counts within that construction. These are substantive contributions directly addressing the supplied problem. The explicit construction, proof, and retained evidence support acceptance.

The seed argument is complete. The polynomial arc rule gives looplessness and both degrees directly. The displacement calculation distinguishes all endpoint types and proves
$$
A^2+A=6I+3J.
$$
The quadratic histogram argument over $\mathbb F_5$ explains the uniform walk counts rather than merely reporting a successful matrix test. Complementation then gives the second parameter set. Section 4 provides a useful explanation of the construction through invertible products on the nontrivial vertical-character spaces; existence does not depend on reproducing the discovery search.

The extension proof is also sound. The displayed partitions satisfy $C^\top D=4J_5$, $C^\top A=(3J_5+2I_5)C^\top$, and $AD=D(4J_5-3I_5)$. Consequently, arbitrary permutation blocks $B_{st}=DP_{st}C^\top$ have constant consecutive products and satisfy the required linear equation. The block calculation proves the asserted parameters for every positive integer $m$, including independently chosen matchings on each ordered pair of copies.

Section 6 successfully addresses uncolored graph isomorphism. The neighborhood-difference bounds separate the $D$-classes, full-column counts recover the copies, and cross neighborhoods recover the $C$-classes. Thus the reduction to partition-preserving seed automorphisms is justified. The finite refinement certificate exhausts all possible images of a root and establishes the five-element translation group. The resulting array action gives the stated Burnside factors, the counts 300 and 3,981,347,520, and the two-copy automorphism distribution. The bound on nonidentity fixed arrays tends to zero and supports both asymptotic assertions. These counts concern the constructed family, as the manuscript appropriately states, rather than all graphs with these parameters.

Replaying the supplied verifiers reproduced the seed digest, every seed and complement walk count, and all 2,000 relative-displacement counts. The supplied checks also verified the partition equations and mixed extensions through order 400, including complements. The uncolored verifier completed its canonical-form comparison of all 300 two-copy representatives and their relabelings, obtaining 276 trivial automorphism groups and 24 groups of order two. These are reproduced executions of the supplied implementations. The order-300 class count rests on the proved isomorphism criterion and Burnside calculation, not an exhaustive enumeration of billions of physical graphs.

The focused novelty check supports the new seed result. The accessible Hobart–Brouwer table [2] still marks both parameter sets with question marks. The relevant constructions in rStation paper 83 [3] have different parameters and constant cross products; paper 88 [4] gives necessary structure under an induced Hoffman–Singleton hypothesis, without constructing this graph. The present layers and nonconstant products substantively differ from those settings.

One attribution clarification would improve Section 5. Its general extension mechanism, including independent choices of permutation matrices between copies, is already covered by rStation paper 4, Theorem 5 and the paragraph immediately following it [7]. Substituting $P=A$, $U=D$, $V=C$, $q=5$, $r=-3$, $s=2$, and $\ell=1$ gives $d=20$ and $\sigma=4$, exactly the displayed extension. Cite that published result explicitly and distinguish the new seed-specific partitions and isomorphism analysis from the established lifting theorem. This overlap does not subsume the new existence result or enumeration. For a sharper comparison with Gyürki [6, Theorem 4], the horizontal partition alone also yields the subfamily with $m=5j+1$ through the $\pi$-join, once the new seed is available.

Scientific communication is clear: the paper progresses from an explicit arc rule through its proof to structural explanation, extension, and enumeration. Essential evidence is explained in the main text, while exhaustive records remain supplementary. The abstract is informative, notation is readable, and the numerical claims have appropriate exact certificates. The attribution clarification above is a local improvement, not a defect in the central argument or its significance.
