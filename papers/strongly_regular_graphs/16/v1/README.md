# Existence of DSRGs (98,63,45,38,45), (98,34,16,15,10), (98,70,55,48,55), (98,27,12,11,6), (99,60,40,34,40) and (99,38,18,17,13) from root and cube flags

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/strongly_regular_graphs/papers/16/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct directed strongly regular graphs with parameters $(98,63,45,38,45)$, $(98,34,16,15,10)$, $(98,70,55,48,55)$, $(98,27,12,11,6)$, $(99,60,40,34,40)$ and $(99,38,18,17,13)$, resolving three complementary pairs marked open in the Hobart–Brouwer table. Balanced D4 roots and cube vectors supply zero-sum subsets; vertices are repeated flags, and adjacency records exclusion of a source label from a target subset. Small integer dual certificates prove every two-step count. A weighted flag lemma allows unequal class sizes and gives the order-99 pair from the same non-antipodal D4 support with one zero vector. Affine changes of class sizes preserve the column pool and make the discovery equations linear. Replication of equal-size flag data gives $(98m^2,35m-1,7m+9,7m+8,10)$ and its complements for all positive integers $m$. A full-cube extension and four finite seeds give $(2u^2,4u-1,u+5,u+4,6)$ and its complements for every integer $u\geq6$. A neutral-class extension of the unequal seed gives $(99+27m,38+9m,18+3m,17+3m,13+3m)$ and its complements for every integer $m\geq0$. It keeps fourteen class sizes fixed and adds triples of nine-vertex classes, using a partition into three zero-sum root subsets of equal weight. Standard independent-set blow-ups of the resulting graphs give an additional scaling parameter. Complete finite incidence tables, explicit adjacency matrices and standard-library exact verifiers are supplied. Independent checks cover eighteen graph pairs up to order 882; algebraic extension identities establish the infinite claims. The displayed degree-63 order-98 graph and the order-99 graph are not vertex-transitive.
