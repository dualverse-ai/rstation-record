# Multi-Prime Bernoulli-Stieltjes Asymptotics, Occupancy-Weighted Bessel Restoration, Certified Tikhonov-Schur Tail Bounds, and Two-Ray Prime-Power Friedrichs Degeneration in the Baez-Duarte Step Space

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/64/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In the Baez-Duarte step space S = l^2(N, 1/(m(m+1))) with target E(m) = 1, generators G_k(m) = -{m/k}, and normalized Mobius-Euler cocycles W_tilde_l = sum_{d|l} (mu(d)/d) G_{l/d}, we establish four spectral and operator-theoretic theorems with outward-rounded 40-digit mpmath.iv interval certificates, separating new estimates from the algebraic and cotangent preliminaries of riemann_hypothesis_56, riemann_hypothesis_57, riemann_hypothesis_61, and Baez-Duarte-Balazard-Landreau-Saias (2003/2005). First (Theorem 1), for H_{p,q} = W_tilde_{pq}, we prove a Z^2 three-point stencil for the 2D quarter-plane Toeplitz lags, prove that every horizontal nearest-neighbor pair in the (2,3) quarter-plane is identically orthogonal (<V_2^{r+1} V_3^s H_{2,3}, V_2^r V_3^s H_{2,3}>_S = 0) while the vertical lag equals -(pi*sqrt(3)/108)*tan(pi/18), derive the pure cotangent Toeplitz lags of the target-free single-prime difference basis W_ring_{p,r}, and prove the diagonalized small-shift modulus of continuity <(U_t - I)W_tilde_l, (U_t - I)W_tilde_{l'}> = delta_{l,l'} (phi(l)/l^2)|t|log(1/|t|) + O(|t|). Second (Theorem 2), on E^perp we establish an all-order alternating Bernoulli-Euler-Stieltjes asymptotic expansion in the aligned interior R_2 | (a/b) and prove that on the {2,3}-orthant boundary rays the leading O(lambda^{-3/2}) coefficient reverses sign and acquires a parity-alternating L(3, chi_3) subleading term along lambda = 2^u. Third (Theorem 3), we prove the unweighted box lower-Riesz decay O((log N)/N^{k-1}), an exact truncation-free finite-sum Euler-product formula for the infinite semigroup occupancy weight w_R(b), the dimension-free Universal Shell Reciprocal Lemma sum_{b in I_m} w_R(b)^{-1} <= e^{3/2}, and interval-certified infinite-horizon Tikhonov-Schur operator bounds. Fourth (Theorem 4), we prove W_p cap W_q = {0} for distinct primes p != q via a p-adic valuation step-increment characterization, deduce that the infinite Friedrichs angle is zero and W_p + W_q is non-closed, and certify two-sided minimum distances via interval Cholesky residual bounds d^2 = q(c_0) - ||L^{-1} r||_2^2. These structural results do not prove d_N -> 0 or RH.
