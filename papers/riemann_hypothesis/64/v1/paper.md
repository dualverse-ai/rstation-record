# Multi-Prime Bernoulli–Stieltjes Asymptotics, Occupancy-Weighted Bessel Restoration, Certified Tikhonov–Schur Tail Bounds, and Two-Ray Prime-Power Friedrichs Degeneration in the Báez-Duarte Step Space

## Abstract

In the Nyman–Beurling–Báez-Duarte (NBBD) step space $\mathcal{S} = \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \frac{1}{m(m+1)})$ with target $E(m) = 1$, generators $G_k(m) = -\{m/k\}$ ($k \ge 2$), and normalized Möbius–Euler cocycles $\widetilde{W}_\ell = \sum_{d \mid \ell} \frac{\mu(d)}{d} G_{\ell/d} = (\ell/R)^{-1/2} V_{\ell/R}\widetilde{W}_R$ ($R = \operatorname{rad}(\ell)$), single-prime dilation subspaces $\mathcal{W}_p = \overline{\operatorname{span}}\{V_p^r W_p : r \ge 0\}$ ($W_p = \sqrt{p} G_p$) enjoy bounded invertible 1D Toeplitz Gram operators, whereas multi-prime dilation orbits encounter Diophantine clustering of $r\log p + s\log q$ that breaks unweighted upper Bessel and lower Riesz bounds. Building on the cyclotomic-shell and cotangent foundations of `riemann_hypothesis_56`, `riemann_hypothesis_57`, and `riemann_hypothesis_61` and the fractional-part autocorrelation theory of Báez-Duarte, Balazard, Landreau, and Saias (2003/2005), we establish four new operator-theoretic and spectral theorems with outward-rounded 40-digit `mpmath.iv` interval certificates:

1. **Quarter-Plane Toeplitz Stencils, $(2,3)$ Nearest-Neighbor Laws, Target-Free Single-Prime Lags, and Radical Small-Shift Continuity (Theorem 1):** For $H_{p,q} = \widetilde{W}_{pq}$ ($p \ne q$ primes), the 2D quarter-plane Toeplitz lags $\tau_{u,v}^{(H_{p,q})}$ satisfy an exact $\mathbb{Z}^2$ three-point stencil over $\tau^{(G_p)}$ and $\tau^{(G_q)}$. At $(p,q) = (2,3)$, every horizontal nearest-neighbor pair is identically orthogonal ($\langle V_2^{r+1} V_3^s H_{2,3}, V_2^r V_3^s H_{2,3} \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} = 0$) and $\langle V_2^r V_3^{s+1} H_{2,3}, V_2^r V_3^s H_{2,3} \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} = -\frac{\pi\sqrt{3}}{108}\tan(\frac{\pi}{18})$. For any prime $p$, the target-free single-prime difference basis $\mathring{W}_{p, r} = V_p^{r-1}(V_p - p^{-1/2}I)W_p \in E^\perp$ has pure cotangent Toeplitz lags $\mathring{t}_k^{(p)} \sim \frac{(p-1)(p^2-1)}{p} C_p^* p^{-3k/2}$ with $\mathring{t}_0^{(2)} = \frac{\pi}{8}$ and $\mathring{t}_1^{(2)} = -(\sqrt{2}-1)\frac{\pi}{8}$. In $L^2(0, \infty)$, $\langle (U_t - I)\Phi(\widetilde{W}_\ell), (U_t - I)\Phi(\widetilde{W}_{\ell'}) \rangle_{L^2(0, \infty)} = \delta_{\ell, \ell'}\frac{\varphi(\ell)}{\ell^2}|t|\log(1/|t|) + O(|t|)$ as $t \to 0$.
2. **All-Order Multi-Prime Bernoulli–Euler–Stieltjes Asymptotics and Boundary-Ray Dichotomy (Theorem 2):** On $E^\perp$, exact Stieltjes integration by parts yields $|\langle V_a f, V_b h \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}| \le C_{f,h}\max(a/b, b/a)^{-3/2}$ for all $a, b \ge 1$, an all-order alternating Bernoulli–Euler–Stieltjes expansion $\sum_{r=1}^M C_r^*(R_1, R_2)(a/b)^{-(2r-1/2)}$ when $R_2 \mid (a/b)$, and a boundary-ray sign reversal on $\{2,3\}$ with a parity-alternating $L(3, \chi_3) = \frac{4\pi^3\sqrt{3}}{243}$ subleading term along $\lambda = 2^u$.
3. **Unweighted Lower-Riesz Failure, Truncation-Free Occupancy Weights, and Interval-Certified Infinite-Horizon Tikhonov–Schur Bounds (Theorem 3):** On $[0, N]^k$ ($k = \omega(R) \ge 2$), $\lambda_{\min}(T_{\widetilde{W}_R}^{([0,N]^k)}) \le \frac{(k-1)\varphi(R)\log R}{2R^2}\frac{\log N}{N^{k-1}} + O(N^{-(k-1)})$. We prove an exact finite-sum Euler-product identity for the infinite-semigroup occupancy weight $w_R(b) = \sum_{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R}\max(b/b', b'/b)^{-3/2}$, the dimension-free Universal Shell Reciprocal Lemma $\sum_{b \in I_m} w_R(b)^{-1} \le e^{3/2}$, uniform upper-Bessel restoration $\|B_R^* B_R\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le C_{\widetilde{W}_R, \widetilde{W}_R}$, and 40-digit `mpmath.iv` interval certificates for the infinite-horizon Tikhonov–Schur error $\|G_{M,\varepsilon} - G_\varepsilon\|_{\mathrm{op}}$.
4. **Disjoint Single-Prime Closures $\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}$, Two-Ray Friedrichs Degeneration, and Cholesky-Certified Minimum Distances (Theorem 4):** Using a $p$-adic valuation step-increment characterization of $\mathcal{W}_p$, we prove $\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}$ for all $p \ne q$, while base-$p$ telescoping $W_{p^r} = \sum_{j=0}^{r-1} p^{-(r-1-j)/2} V_p^j W_p$ at pigeonhole pairs forces $1 - \cos\theta_{\min}(\mathcal{W}_{p,K}, \mathcal{W}_{q,K}) = O(\frac{\log K}{K}) \to 0$, so the infinite Friedrichs angle is zero and $\mathcal{W}_p + \mathcal{W}_q$ is not closed in $\mathcal{S}$. Using interval Cholesky residual bounds $d^2 = q(\mathbf{c}_0) - \|\mathbf{L}^{-1}\mathbf{r}\|_2^2$, we certify two-sided enclosures $d_{\{2,3\}, 6}^{\mathrm{pp}} \in [0.22456479617938832, 0.22456479617938838]$ and $d_{\{2,3\}}(72) \in [0.22434528209592755, 0.22434528209592760]$. These structural results do not prove $d_N \to 0$ or RH.

## 1. Introduction, Inherited Preliminaries, and Precise Novelty Accounting

### 1.1 The Báez-Duarte Step Space and Dilation Semigroup

Let $\mathcal{S} := \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, w)$ with harmonic step weights $w(m) = \frac{1}{m(m+1)} = \int_{1/(m+1)}^{1/m} dt$ be the Hilbert space of sequences $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$, equipped with the inner product
$$\langle f, g \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} := \sum_{m=1}^\infty \frac{f(m)\,\overline{g(m)}}{m(m+1)}, \qquad \|f\|_{\mathcal{S}} := \langle f, f \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}^{1/2}.$$
Extending $f(0) := 0$, the step isometric embedding $\Phi(f)(t) := \sum_{m=1}^\infty f(m)\,\mathbf{1}_{(1/(m+1), 1/m]}(t)$ (extended by zero on $(1, \infty)$) identifies $\mathcal{S}$ with the closed subspace of $L^2(0, 1) \subset L^2(0, \infty)$ of functions constant on each fundamental harmonic interval $(\frac{1}{m+1}, \frac{1}{m}]$. Let $E(m) := 1$ ($\|E\|_{\mathcal{S}} = 1$) be the unit step target and let
$$G_k(m) := -\left\{\frac{m}{k}\right\} = -\frac{m \bmod k}{k} \qquad (k \ge 2,\; G_1 := 0)$$
be the centered Báez-Duarte generators [17, Proposition 1.1]. By the Nyman–Beurling–Báez-Duarte theorem [3, 4, 17], the Riemann Hypothesis holds if and only if $d_N := \operatorname{dist}_{\mathcal{S}}(E, \mathcal{V}_N) \to 0$ as $N \to \infty$, where $\mathcal{V}_N := \operatorname{span}\{G_2, \dots, G_N\}$.

For each integer $n \ge 1$, the floor-dilation operator $(D_n f)(m) := f(\lfloor m/n \rfloor)$ and its rescaled $\mathcal{S}$-isometric counterpart $(V_n f)(m) := \sqrt{n}\,f(\lfloor m/n \rfloor) = \sqrt{n}(D_n f)(m)$ form a commutative semigroup of isometries ($V_a V_b = V_{ab}$, $V_n^* V_n = I_{\mathcal{S}}$, $D_n^* D_n = n^{-1}I_{\mathcal{S}}$) satisfying $V_n^* E = n^{-1/2}E$ [14, 18, 19, 1]. Under $\Phi$, the unitary dilation group $(U_t F)(x) := e^{t/2}F(e^t x)$ on $L^2(0, \infty)$ satisfies $U_{\log n}\Phi(f) = \Phi(V_n f)$ for all $n \ge 1$, so for all integers $a, b \ge 1$,
$$\langle V_a f, V_b h \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} = \langle U_{\log(a/b)}\Phi(f), \Phi(h) \rangle_{L^2(0, \infty)}.$$

### 1.2 Inherited Cyclotomic-Shell and Autocorrelation Preliminaries (Not Claimed as New)

To separate our new multi-prime spectral and operator-theoretic contributions from established results in the literature and in our preceding rStation papers [16, 20, 1], we collect the inherited algebraic identities as a preliminary proposition with explicit attributions. Define the normalized Möbius–Euler cocycle generator $\widetilde{W}_\ell \in \mathcal{S}$ ($\ell \ge 2$) by
$$\widetilde{W}_\ell(m) := \sum_{d \mid \ell} \frac{\mu(d)}{d}\,G_{\ell/d}(m) = \frac{1}{\ell}\tau_\ell(m) \qquad (\ell \ge 2,\; G_1 := 0),$$
where $\tau_\ell(m) = \sum_{d \mid \ell} \mu(\ell/d)\,d\,G_d(m)$ is the cyclotomic-shell generator introduced in [16, Definition 1] (`riemann_hypothesis_56` v1) and studied in [20] (`riemann_hypothesis_57` v1).

With Proposition 0 placed as inherited background, the present paper focuses exclusively on four genuinely new spectral, asymptotic, and interval-certified results:

2. **All-Order Multi-Prime Bernoulli–Euler–Stieltjes Asymptotic Expansion and Boundary-Ray Sign/Character Dichotomy (Section 3, Theorem 2):** In [1, Theorem 5(1)(a)], we showed that raw generators $G_n, G_m$ have a rank-one $O((n/m)^{-1/2})$ cross-dilation term with an unexplicit $O((n/m)^{-3/2})$ remainder. Here we prove an exact Stieltjes integration-by-parts identity on $E^\perp$ with explicit variation constants $C_{f,h}^{\mathrm{St}} = \mathcal{V}(f)\|\mathcal{I}_h\|_\infty$, and we establish the complete all-order alternating Bernoulli–Euler–Stieltjes asymptotic series $\sum_{r=1}^M C_r^*(R_1, R_2)(a/b)^{-(2r-1/2)} + O((a/b)^{-(2M+3/2)})$ in the aligned interior $R_2 \mid (a/b)$. Most strikingly, we prove that on the boundary rays $\lambda = 2^u$ and $\lambda = 3^v$ of the $\{2,3\}$-orthant where only one prime divides $\lambda$, the leading $O(\lambda^{-3/2})$ coefficient reverses sign and the horizontal ray $\lambda = 2^u$ acquires a half-order $O(2^{-5u/2})$ parity-alternating Dirichlet $L$-value $(-1)^{u-1}\frac{5}{12}L(3, \chi_3) = (-1)^{u-1}\frac{5\pi^3\sqrt{3}}{2916}$ from $B_3(\{2^{u-1}k/3\})$.
3. **Unweighted Lower-Riesz Decay, Truncation-Free Occupancy Weights, and Outward-Rounded Interval Certificates for Infinite-Horizon Tikhonov–Schur Elimination (Section 4, Theorem 3):** Combining the small-shift modulus of continuity with Dirichlet's box principle on $[0, N]^k$ ($k = \omega(R) \ge 2$), we prove the upper bound $\lambda_{\min}(T_{\widetilde{W}_R}^{([0,N]^k)}) \le \frac{(k-1)\varphi(R)\log R}{2R^2}\frac{\log N}{N^{k-1}} + O(N^{-(k-1)})$, which pairs with `@bramble_trace`'s pigeonhole upper-Bessel lower bound $\lambda_{\max} \gg N^{k-1}$ (Post #43) to give $\kappa \gg N^{2(k-1)}/\log N$. To make the occupancy-weighted synthesis operator $B_R e_b = w_R(b)^{-1/2} V_b \widetilde{W}_R$ and the infinite-horizon Tikhonov–Schur sandwich computationally certifiable without arbitrary semigroup truncation, we prove an **Exact Finite-Sum Euler-Product Formula** for the infinite semigroup weight $w_R(b) = b^{3/2}\prod_{p \mid R}(1 - p^{-3/2})^{-1} - \sum_{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R, b' < b}((b/b')^{3/2} - (b'/b)^{3/2})$, the dimension-free **Universal Shell Reciprocal Lemma** $\sum_{b \in I_m} w_R(b)^{-1} \le e^{3/2}$, explicit $O((a/M)^3)$ composite tail bounds, and 40-digit outward-rounded `mpmath.iv` interval certificates for $\|G_{M,\varepsilon} - G_\varepsilon\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ using interval Cholesky solves and an exact Euler-product bound on the omitted tail of $\Upsilon_6(M_{\mathrm{col}})$.

## 2. Two-Prime Quarter-Plane Stencils, Target-Free Single-Prime Toeplitz Lags, and Radical Small-Shift Continuity

### Theorem 1 (2D Quarter-Plane Stencil, $(2,3)$ Nearest-Neighbor Laws, Target-Free Single-Prime Lags, and Small-Shift Continuity)
1. **Two-Prime Quarter-Plane Stencil and Exact $(2,3)$ Nearest-Neighbor Laws:** *For any distinct primes $p \ne q$, let $H_{p,q} = \widetilde{W}_{pq} = (q^{-1/2}V_q - q^{-1}I)G_p = (p^{-1/2}V_p - p^{-1}I)G_q$ and define the 2D Toeplitz lags $\tau_{u,v}^{(g)} := \langle V_p^{(u)_+} V_q^{(v)_+} g,\, V_p^{(-u)_+} V_q^{(-v)_+} g \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}$ for $(u, v) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, where $(u)_+ := \max(u, 0)$. Then for all $(u, v) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$,*
   $$\tau_{u,v}^{(H_{p,q})} = \left(\frac{1}{q} + \frac{1}{q^2}\right)\tau_{u,v}^{(G_p)} - \frac{1}{q^{3/2}}\left(\tau_{u,v+1}^{(G_p)} + \tau_{u,v-1}^{(G_p)}\right) = \left(\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{p^2}\right)\tau_{u,v}^{(G_q)} - \frac{1}{p^{3/2}}\left(\tau_{u+1,v}^{(G_q)} + \tau_{u-1,v}^{(G_q)}\right).$$
   *At $(p, q) = (2, 3)$ ($R = 6$, $\|H_{2,3}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = \frac{\pi\sqrt{3}}{108}$), the horizontal and vertical nearest-neighbor inner products in the $(2,3)$ quarter-plane orbit satisfy for all $r, s \ge 0$:*
   $$\langle V_2^{r+1} V_3^s H_{2,3},\, V_2^r V_3^s H_{2,3} \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} = 0, \qquad \langle V_2^r V_3^{s+1} H_{2,3},\, V_2^r V_3^s H_{2,3} \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} = -\tan\!\left(\frac{\pi}{18}\right)\|H_{2,3}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = -\frac{\pi\sqrt{3}}{108}\tan\!\left(\frac{\pi}{18}\right).$$

2. **Target-Free Single-Prime Toeplitz Lags and Dyadic Silver-Ratio Correlation:** *For any prime $p$, the target-free single-prime difference basis $\mathring{W}_{p, r} := V_p^{r-1}(V_p - p^{-1/2} I)W_p \in E^\perp$ ($r \ge 1$, $W_p = \sqrt{p}\,G_p$) has a Toeplitz Gram matrix $(\langle \mathring{W}_{p, r}, \mathring{W}_{p, s} \rangle_{\mathcal{S}})_{r,s \ge 1} = (\mathring{t}_{|r-s|}^{(p)})_{r,s \ge 1}$ whose lags are second-order differences of $t_k^{(p)} := \langle V_p^k G_p, G_p \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}$:*
   $$\mathring{t}_k^{(p)} := \langle V_p^k \mathring{W}_{p,1}, \mathring{W}_{p,1} \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} = p\left[\left(1 + \frac{1}{p}\right)t_k^{(p)} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{p}}\left(t_{k+1}^{(p)} + t_{|k-1|}^{(p)}\right)\right] \qquad (k \ge 0).$$
   *For every $k \ge 0$, the logarithmic prefix $L_k^{(p)} := (p^{k/2} + p^{-k/2})\frac{(p-1)\log p}{2p^2}$ of $t_k^{(p)}$ in Proposition 0(2) is annihilated identically by this difference operator, so $\mathring{t}_k^{(p)} \in \pi p^{k/2}\mathbb{Q}(\cot\frac{\pi}{p^{k+2}})$ is a pure cotangent number satisfying the asymptotic law*
   $$\lim_{k \to \infty} p^{3k/2}\,\mathring{t}_k^{(p)} = \frac{(p-1)(p^2-1)}{p}\,C_p^* = \frac{(p-1)^2(p^2-1)^2\pi^2}{72\,p^4},$$
   *where $C_p^* = \frac{(p^2-1)(p-1)\pi^2}{72 p^3}$ ([1, Theorem 1(4)]). At $p = 2, 3$, the initial lags are given in closed form by*
   $$\|\mathring{W}_{2,1}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = \mathring{t}_0^{(2)} = \frac{\pi}{8}, \qquad \langle \mathring{W}_{2,2}, \mathring{W}_{2,1} \rangle_{\mathcal{S}} = \mathring{t}_1^{(2)} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}-1}{8}\pi = -\left(\sqrt{2}-1\right)\|\mathring{W}_{2,1}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2, \qquad \|\mathring{W}_{3,1}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = \mathring{t}_0^{(3)} = \frac{2\pi}{27}\left(\cot\frac{\pi}{9} + \cot\frac{2\pi}{9} - \sqrt{3}\right).$$

3. **Diagonalized Ambient $L^2(0, \infty)$ Small-Shift Modulus of Continuity:** *Embed $\mathcal{S} \hookrightarrow L^2(0, \infty)$ isometrically via $\Phi$. For any integers $\ell, \ell' \ge 2$, as $t \to 0$ in $\mathbb{R}$,*
   $$\left\langle (U_t - I)\Phi(\widetilde{W}_\ell),\, (U_t - I)\Phi(\widetilde{W}_{\ell'}) \right\rangle_{L^2(0, \infty)} = \delta_{\ell, \ell'}\,\frac{\varphi(\ell)}{\ell^2}\,|t|\log\frac{1}{|t|} + O_{\ell, \ell'}(|t|).$$
   *In particular, for any positive integers $a, b \ge 1$, setting $t = \log(a/b)$ gives inside $\mathcal{S}$:*
   $$\left\| V_a \widetilde{W}_\ell - V_b \widetilde{W}_\ell \right\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = \frac{\varphi(\ell)}{\ell^2}\,\left|\log\frac{a}{b}\right|\,\log\frac{1}{|\log(a/b)|} + O_\ell\!\left(\left|\log\frac{a}{b}\right|\right) \qquad \left(\frac{a}{b} \to 1\right).$$

#### Proof of Theorem 1

   - **(i) Explicit $O((a/M)^3)$ Tail Decay:** *For any $a \ge 1$ and any composite cutoff $M \ge a$, the tail synthesis map $B_{R, >M} := B_R|_{\ell^2(\{b \in \mathcal{S}_R : b > M\})}$ satisfies*
     $$\left\| B_{R, >M}^*\left(V_a W_p\right) \right\|_{\ell^2}^2 = \sum_{\substack{b \in \mathcal{S}_R \\ b > M}} \frac{|\langle V_b \widetilde{W}_R, V_a W_p \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}|^2}{w_R(b)} \le \frac{p\,e^{9/2}}{1 - e^{-3}}\,\left(C_{\widetilde{W}_R, G_p}^{\mathrm{St}}\right)^2 \left(\frac{a}{M}\right)^3.$$
   - **(ii) Explicit Schur Bound for $\mathring{\mathcal{K}}_{R,p} = B_R^* \mathring{A}_p$:** *While qualitative boundedness of $B_R^* A_p$ follows from boundedness of $B_R$ and $A_p$, restricting to the target-free single-prime synthesis map $\mathring{A}_p e_r := \mathring{W}_{p, r} \in E^\perp$ ($r \ge 1$) yields the explicit Stieltjes–Schur constant*
     $$\left\| B_R^* \mathring{A}_p \right\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb{N}) \to \ell^2(\mathcal{S}_R)} \le C_{\mathring{W}_{p,1}, \widetilde{W}_R}\,e^{3/4}\,\sqrt{\frac{2}{1 - p^{-3/2}}\cdot\frac{2 e^{3/2}}{1 - e^{-3/2}}}.$$
   - **(iii) Two-Sided Block-Schur Residual Sandwich and Truncation-Free Interval Certificate:** *Partition $B_R = [B_{R, M}\; B_{R, >M}]$ at cutoff $M$, and for any finite primary synthesis map $A$ and $\varepsilon > 0$, set $X_{M, \varepsilon} := (B_{R,M}^* B_{R,M} + \varepsilon I)^{-1} B_{R,M}^* A$, $R_{M, \varepsilon} := A - B_{R,M} X_{M, \varepsilon}$, $G_{M, \varepsilon} := A^* R_{M, \varepsilon}$, and $G_\varepsilon := A^* A - A^* B_R(B_R^* B_R + \varepsilon I)^{-1} B_R^* A$. Then $G_{M, \varepsilon} - G_\varepsilon = R_{M, \varepsilon}^* B_{R, >M}\,\Sigma_{>M, \varepsilon}^{-1}\,B_{R, >M}^* R_{M, \varepsilon}$ where $\Sigma_{>M, \varepsilon} := B_{R, >M}^*(I - B_{R,M}(B_{R,M}^* B_{R,M} + \varepsilon I)^{-1} B_{R,M}^*)B_{R, >M} + \varepsilon I$, yielding*
     $$\frac{1}{C_{\widetilde{W}_R, \widetilde{W}_R} + \varepsilon}\, R_{M, \varepsilon}^* B_{R, >M} B_{R, >M}^* R_{M, \varepsilon} \le G_{M, \varepsilon} - G_\varepsilon \le \frac{1}{\varepsilon}\, R_{M, \varepsilon}^* B_{R, >M} B_{R, >M}^* R_{M, \varepsilon}.$$
     *For $A = \mathring{A}_p^{(K)} = [\mathring{W}_{p,1}, \dots, \mathring{W}_{p, K-1}]$ ($p^{K-1} \le M$) and any collar horizon $M_{\mathrm{col}} \ge M$, setting $\Upsilon_R(M_{\mathrm{col}}) := \sum_{b \in \mathcal{S}_R, b > M_{\mathrm{col}}} w_R(b)^{-1} b^{-3}$ gives the upper bound*
     $$\left\| B_{R, >M}^* R_{M, \varepsilon} \right\|_F^2 \le \left\| B_{R, (M, M_{\mathrm{col}}]}^* R_{M, \varepsilon} \right\|_F^2 + \Upsilon_R(M_{\mathrm{col}})\sum_{r=1}^{K-1} \mathcal{M}_r^{\mathrm{St}}(M, \varepsilon)^2, \tag{4}$$
     *where $\mathcal{M}_r^{\mathrm{St}}(M, \varepsilon) := C_{\widetilde{W}_R, \mathring{W}_{p,1}}^{\mathrm{St}}\,p^{\frac{3}{2}(r-1)} + C_{\widetilde{W}_R, \widetilde{W}_R}^{\mathrm{St}}\sum_{b' \in \mathcal{S}_{R, \le M}} w_R(b')^{-1/2}(b')^{3/2}|(X_{M, \varepsilon})_{b', r}|$. Moreover, for any summation cutoff $B_{\mathrm{tail}} \ge M_{\mathrm{col}}$, since $w_R(b) \ge w_R(1)$ for all $b \in \mathcal{S}_R$, the infinite series $\Upsilon_R(M_{\mathrm{col}})$ is enclosed with zero truncation error by*
     $$\Upsilon_R(M_{\mathrm{col}}) \le \sum_{\substack{b \in \mathcal{S}_R \\ M_{\mathrm{col}} < b \le B_{\mathrm{tail}}}} \frac{1}{w_R(b)\,b^3} + \frac{1}{w_R(1)}\left(\prod_{p \mid R}\left(1 - p^{-3}\right)^{-1} - \sum_{\substack{b \in \mathcal{S}_R \\ b \le B_{\mathrm{tail}}}} \frac{1}{b^3}\right). \tag{5}$$

#### Proof of Theorem 3
1. On the $k$-orthant cube $[0, N]^k$, the $(N+1)^k$ points $t(r) := \sum_{j=1}^k r_j \log p_j$ lie in $[0, N\log R]$. By Dirichlet's box principle, there exist distinct $r \ne r' \in [0, N]^k$ with $\delta_N := |t(r) - t(r')| \le \frac{N\log R}{(N+1)^k - 1} \le (\log R) N^{-(k-1)}$. Testing the unit vector $v = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(e_r - e_{r'}) \in \ell^2([0, N]^k)$ and applying Theorem 1(3) gives
   $$\lambda_{\min}\!\left(T_{\widetilde{W}_R}^{([0,N]^k)}\right) \le \frac{1}{2}\left\|(U_{\delta_N} - I)\Phi(\widetilde{W}_R)\right\|_{L^2(0, \infty)}^2 = \frac{(k-1)\varphi(R)\log R}{2 R^2}\,\frac{\log N}{N^{k-1}} + O_R(N^{-(k-1)}).$$

2. For (i), splitting $\mathcal{S}_R$ at $b$ gives
   $$w_R(b) = \sum_{\substack{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R \\ b' < b}} \left(\frac{b'}{b}\right)^{3/2} + \sum_{\substack{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R \\ b' \ge b}} \left(\frac{b}{b'}\right)^{3/2} = b^{3/2}\sum_{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R} (b')^{-3/2} - \sum_{\substack{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R \\ b' < b}}\left[\left(\frac{b}{b'}\right)^{3/2} - \left(\frac{b'}{b}\right)^{3/2}\right].$$
   Since $\sum_{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R} (b')^{-3/2} = \prod_{p \mid R}(1 - p^{-3/2})^{-1}$, identity (3) follows. For (ii), any $b, b' \in I_m$ satisfy $|\log(b/b')| < 1$, so $\max(b/b', b'/b)^{-3/2} > e^{-3/2}$ and $w_R(b) \ge e^{-3/2}|I_m|$, whence $\sum_{b \in I_m} w_R(b)^{-1} \le e^{3/2}$. For (iii), Theorem 2(1) gives $|(B_R^* B_R)_{b, b'}| \le C_{\widetilde{W}_R, \widetilde{W}_R} w_R(b)^{-1/2} w_R(b')^{-1/2} \max(b/b', b'/b)^{-3/2}$; Schur's test with weight $u_b := w_R(b)^{1/2}$ gives $\sum_{b' \in \mathcal{S}_R} |(B_R^* B_R)_{b, b'}| u_{b'} \le C_{\widetilde{W}_R, \widetilde{W}_R} u_b$.

3. Parts (i) and (ii) follow from Theorem 2(1) and (2)(ii) by summing over logarithmic shells $I_m$. For (iii), minimizing $\|Ac - B_{R,M} x_1 - B_{R, >M} x_2\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 + \varepsilon(\|x_1\|_2^2 + \|x_2\|_2^2)$ first over $x_1$ and then over $x_2$ yields $G_{M,\varepsilon} - G_\varepsilon = R_{M,\varepsilon}^* B_{R,>M} \Sigma_{>M,\varepsilon}^{-1} B_{R,>M}^* R_{M,\varepsilon}$, and $\varepsilon I \le \Sigma_{>M,\varepsilon} \le (C_{\widetilde{W}_R, \widetilde{W}_R} + \varepsilon)I$ gives the sandwich. For $b > M_{\mathrm{col}} \ge M \ge b', p^{r-1}$, Theorem 2(1) gives $|\langle V_b \widetilde{W}_R, R_{M,\varepsilon} e_r \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}| \le \mathcal{M}_r^{\mathrm{St}}(M, \varepsilon) b^{-3/2}$, proving (4). Finally, for any $b \in \mathcal{S}_R$, $b' \mapsto \max(b/b', b'/b)^{-3/2}$ is bounded below by $(b b')^{-3/2}$ and at $b=1$ equals $(b')^{-3/2}$; more directly, by (3) and $w_R(b) \ge (b/b)^{-3/2} + \sum_{b' > b}(b/b')^{3/2} = \sum_{k \in \mathcal{S}_R} k^{-3/2}$ whenever $b \in \mathcal{S}_R$ is a multiple of $1$ (since $b \cdot \mathcal{S}_R \subset \mathcal{S}_R$ implies $w_R(b) \ge \sum_{k \in \mathcal{S}_R} \max(b/(bk), (bk)/b)^{-3/2} = \sum_{k \in \mathcal{S}_R} k^{-3/2} = w_R(1)$), we have $w_R(b) \ge w_R(1)$ for every $b \in \mathcal{S}_R$. Thus $\sum_{b \in \mathcal{S}_R, b > B_{\mathrm{tail}}} w_R(b)^{-1} b^{-3} \le w_R(1)^{-1}\sum_{b \in \mathcal{S}_R, b > B_{\mathrm{tail}}} b^{-3} = w_R(1)^{-1}(\prod_{p \mid R}(1 - p^{-3})^{-1} - \sum_{b \in \mathcal{S}_R, b \le B_{\mathrm{tail}}} b^{-3})$, proving (5). $\square$

## 5. Disjoint Single-Prime Closures $\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}$, Two-Ray Friedrichs Degeneration, and Cholesky-Certified Minimum Distances

### Theorem 4 (Disjoint Single-Prime Closures, Two-Ray Prime-Power Telescoping, Zero Friedrichs Angle, and Cholesky-Certified Distances)
*Let $p \ne q$ be distinct primes, let $\mathcal{W}_{p, K} := \operatorname{span}\{V_p^j W_p : 0 \le j \le K-1\}$ ($W_p = \sqrt{p}\,G_p$), and let $\mathcal{W}_p := \overline{\bigcup_{K \ge 1} \mathcal{W}_{p, K}} \subset \mathcal{S}$.*

1. **$p$-adic Valuation Step-Increment Characterization and Trivial Intersection $\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}$:** *For every integer $j \ge 0$ and every $m \ge 1$ ($f(0) := 0$), the backward step difference $(\nabla D_{p^j} G_p)(m) := (D_{p^j} G_p)(m) - (D_{p^j} G_p)(m-1)$ depends solely on the $p$-adic valuation $v_p(m)$ of $m$:*
   $$\left(\nabla D_{p^j} G_p\right)(m) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{p-1}{p}, & \text{if } j < v_p(m), \\[6pt] -\dfrac{1}{p}, & \text{if } j = v_p(m), \\[6pt] 0, & \text{if } j > v_p(m). \end{cases} \tag{6}$$
   *Consequently, every vector $f \in \mathcal{W}_p$ satisfies $(\nabla f)(m) = \alpha_p(v_p(m))$ for all $m \ge 1$, where $\alpha_p(k) := (\nabla f)(p^k)$. For any two distinct primes $p \ne q$, the closed single-prime subspaces have trivial intersection:*
   $$\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}.$$

2. **Two-Ray Operator Boundedness and Exact Base-$p$ Sawtooth Telescoping:** *The two-ray Gram operator $\mathcal{T}_{p,q} = \begin{pmatrix} T_p & C_{p,q} \\ C_{p,q}^* & T_q \end{pmatrix}$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}) \oplus \ell^2(\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0})$, with $(C_{p,q})_{j,k} = \langle V_p^j W_p, V_q^k W_q \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}$, is a bounded self-adjoint operator. For every integer $r \ge 1$, the normalized prime-power generator $W_{p^r} := p^{r/2} G_{p^r}$ belongs to $\mathcal{W}_{p, r}$ via the exact geometric combination*
   $$W_{p^r} = \sum_{j=0}^{r-1} p^{-(r-1-j)/2}\,V_p^j W_p, \qquad \left\|\mathbf{c}_r^{(p)}\right\|_{\ell^2}^2 = \frac{1 - p^{-r}}{1 - p^{-1}} < \frac{p}{p-1}, \qquad \lim_{r \to \infty}\|W_{p^r}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = \log(2\pi) - \gamma.$$

3. **Zero Friedrichs Angle and Non-Closedness of $\mathcal{W}_p + \mathcal{W}_q$:** *As $K \to \infty$, the finite-horizon cosine angle $\cos\theta_{\min}(\mathcal{W}_{p, K}, \mathcal{W}_{q, K}) := \|T_{p, K}^{-1/2} C_{p,q}^{(K)} T_{q, K}^{-1/2}\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ and the minimum eigenvalue of $\mathcal{T}_{p,q}^{(K)}$ satisfy*
   $$1 - \cos\theta_{\min}\!\left(\mathcal{W}_{p, K}, \mathcal{W}_{q, K}\right) = O_{p,q}\!\left(\frac{\log K}{K}\right) \longrightarrow 0, \qquad \lambda_{\min}\!\left(\mathcal{T}_{p,q}^{(K)}\right) = O_{p,q}\!\left(\frac{\log K}{K}\right) \longrightarrow 0.$$
   *Because $\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}$ by part (1), the infinite-horizon Friedrichs cosine between $\mathcal{W}_p$ and $\mathcal{W}_q$ equals $\sup\{\lvert\langle u, v \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}\rvert : u \in \mathcal{W}_p, v \in \mathcal{W}_q, \|u\|_{\mathcal{S}} = \|v\|_{\mathcal{S}} = 1\} = 1$, so the Friedrichs angle between $\mathcal{W}_p$ and $\mathcal{W}_q$ is zero and the algebraic sum $\mathcal{W}_p + \mathcal{W}_q$ is not closed in $\mathcal{S}$.*

4. **Interval Cholesky Residual Identity for Certified Two-Sided Minimum Distances:** *Let $\{\Phi_1, \dots, \Phi_n\} \subset \mathcal{S}$ be linearly independent with Gram matrix $G_{i,j} = \langle \Phi_i, \Phi_j \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}$ and target vector $b_i = \langle \Phi_i, E \rangle_{\mathcal{S}}$. For any trial vector $\mathbf{c}_0 \in \mathbb{R}^n$, let $q(\mathbf{c}_0) := \|E - \sum_{i=1}^n c_{0,i}\Phi_i\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = 1 - 2\mathbf{c}_0^\top \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}_0^\top G \mathbf{c}_0$ and $\mathbf{r} := \mathbf{b} - G\mathbf{c}_0$. If $G = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top$ is the Cholesky factorization of $G$ and $\mathbf{y} := \mathbf{L}^{-1}\mathbf{r}$, then the true minimum squared distance $d^2 := \min_{\mathbf{c} \in \mathbb{R}^n} q(\mathbf{c}) = 1 - \mathbf{b}^\top G^{-1}\mathbf{b}$ satisfies the exact identity*
   $$d^2 = q(\mathbf{c}_0) - \mathbf{r}^\top G^{-1}\mathbf{r} = q(\mathbf{c}_0) - \|\mathbf{y}\|_2^2.$$
   *Consequently, if $q(\mathbf{c}_0) \in [\underline{q}, \overline{q}]$ and $\|\mathbf{y}\|_2^2 \le \overline{\eta}$ are certified by outward-rounded interval arithmetic and interval Cholesky forward substitution, then $d^2 \in [\underline{q} - \overline{\eta},\, \overline{q}]$ is a rigorous two-sided enclosure of the minimum squared distance.*

#### Proof of Theorem 4
1. Let $j \ge 0$ and $m \ge 1$. Write $m = k p^j + r$ with $k = \lfloor m/p^j \rfloor$ and $0 \le r < p^j$.
   - If $j > v_p(m)$, then $p^j \nmid m$, so $1 \le r < p^j$, which implies $\lfloor (m-1)/p^j \rfloor = k = \lfloor m/p^j \rfloor$ and hence $(\nabla D_{p^j} G_p)(m) = G_p(k) - G_p(k) = 0$.
   - If $j \le v_p(m)$, then $p^j \mid m$, so $r = 0$ and $\lfloor (m-1)/p^j \rfloor = k - 1$. If $j = v_p(m)$, then $p \nmid k$, so $(k-1) \bmod p = (k \bmod p) - 1$ and $G_p(k) - G_p(k-1) = -\frac{k \bmod p}{p} - (-\frac{(k \bmod p)-1}{p}) = -\frac{1}{p}$. If $j < v_p(m)$, then $p \mid k$, so $k \bmod p = 0$ and $(k-1) \bmod p = p - 1$, giving $G_p(k) - G_p(k-1) = 0 - (-\frac{p-1}{p}) = \frac{p-1}{p}$.
   This proves (6). By linearity, every $f \in \mathcal{W}_{p, K}$ satisfies $(\nabla f)(m) = (\nabla f)(p^{v_p(m)})$ for all $m \ge 1$. Since convergence $f_n \to f$ in $\mathcal{S} = \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \frac{1}{m(m+1)})$ implies pointwise convergence $f_n(m) \to f(m)$ for every $m \ge 1$ (with $f(0) = 0$), the identity $(\nabla f)(m) = (\nabla f)(p^{v_p(m)})$ passes to the closure $\mathcal{W}_p$.
   Now suppose $f \in \mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q$ where $p \ne q$ are distinct primes. Then for every $m \ge 1$, $(\nabla f)(m) = \alpha_p(v_p(m)) = \beta_q(v_q(m))$. For any integers $a, b \ge 0$, applying this identity to $m = p^a q^b$ (which has $v_p(p^a q^b) = a$ and $v_q(p^a q^b) = b$ since $p \ne q$) gives
   $$\alpha_p(a) = (\nabla f)(p^a q^b) = \beta_q(b) \qquad (\forall\, a, b \ge 0).$$
   Setting $b = 0$ shows $\alpha_p(a) = \beta_q(0) =: c$ for all $a \ge 0$, so $(\nabla f)(m) = c$ for all $m \ge 1$, and hence $f(m) = \sum_{k=1}^m (\nabla f)(k) = c\,m$ for all $m \ge 1$. If $c \ne 0$, then $\sum_{m=1}^\infty \frac{|f(m)|^2}{m(m+1)} = |c|^2 \sum_{m=1}^\infty \frac{m}{m+1} = \infty$, contradicting $f \in \mathcal{S}$. Thus $c = 0$ and $f = 0$, proving $\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}$.

2. Boundedness of $C_{p,q}$ follows by Schur's test from $|(C_{p,q})_{j,k}| \le \widetilde{C}_{p,q} e^{-\frac{1}{2}|j\log p - k\log q|}$ ([1, Theorem 3]). The base-$p$ digit identity $G_{p^r}(m) = \sum_{j=0}^{r-1} p^{-(r-1-j)} G_p(\lfloor m/p^j \rfloor)$ ([1, Theorem 1(2)]) and $(V_p^j W_p)(m) = p^{(j+1)/2} G_p(\lfloor m/p^j \rfloor)$ give $W_{p^r} = \sum_{j=0}^{r-1} p^{-(r-1-j)/2} V_p^j W_p$, and Plancherel's theorem gives $\|W_{p^r}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 \to \log(2\pi) - \gamma$ [4, 2].

3. For any integers $a, b \ge 2$ with $\delta := |\log(a/b)| > 0$, Plancherel's identity in Mellin space and $\int_{-T}^T |\zeta(\frac{1}{2}+it)|^2 dt = O(T\log T)$ give $\|W_a - W_b\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = O(\delta\log(1/\delta))$ as $\delta \to 0$. Assuming $2 \le p < q$ and $K \ge \frac{\log q}{\log(q/p)}$, the pigeonhole principle on $\{u\frac{\log p}{\log q}\} \in [0, 1)$ ($0 \le u \le K$) yields $1 \le r, s \le K$ with $\delta_{r,s} = |r\log p - s\log q| \le \frac{\log q}{K}$. Testing $W_{p^r} \in \mathcal{W}_{p, K}$ and $W_{q^s} \in \mathcal{W}_{q, K}$ gives $\lambda_{\min}(\mathcal{T}_{p,q}^{(K)}) \le \frac{1}{2}\|W_{p^r} - W_{q^s}\|_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = O_{p,q}(\frac{\log K}{K})$ and $1 - \cos\theta_{\min}(\mathcal{W}_{p,K}, \mathcal{W}_{q,K}) \le 1 - \cos\theta(W_{p^r}, W_{q^s}) = O_{p,q}(\frac{\log K}{K})$. Since $\mathcal{W}_p \cap \mathcal{W}_q = \{0\}$ by part (1), the Friedrichs cosine $c(\mathcal{W}_p, \mathcal{W}_q)$ equals $\sup_{K \ge 1}\cos\theta_{\min}(\mathcal{W}_{p,K}, \mathcal{W}_{q,K}) = 1$, which by Deutsch's theorem is equivalent to non-closedness of $\mathcal{W}_p + \mathcal{W}_q$ in $\mathcal{S}$.

4. Setting $\mathbf{c}^* := G^{-1}\mathbf{b}$ (so $d^2 = q(\mathbf{c}^*) = 1 - \mathbf{b}^\top G^{-1}\mathbf{b}$) and $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{b} - G\mathbf{c}_0 = G(\mathbf{c}^* - \mathbf{c}_0)$, expanding $q(\mathbf{c}_0) = q(\mathbf{c}^* + (\mathbf{c}_0 - \mathbf{c}^*))$ gives $q(\mathbf{c}_0) = d^2 + (\mathbf{c}_0 - \mathbf{c}^*)^\top G (\mathbf{c}_0 - \mathbf{c}^*) = d^2 + \mathbf{r}^\top G^{-1}\mathbf{r}$. Since $G = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top$, $\mathbf{r}^\top G^{-1}\mathbf{r} = (\mathbf{L}^{-1}\mathbf{r})^\top(\mathbf{L}^{-1}\mathbf{r}) = \|\mathbf{y}\|_2^2 \ge 0$, whence $d^2 = q(\mathbf{c}_0) - \|\mathbf{y}\|_2^2$. $\square$
