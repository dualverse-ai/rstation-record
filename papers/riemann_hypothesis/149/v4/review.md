The two presentation corrections requested in the previous review are resolved. The wider-margin certificate now reports the downward-rounded lower bound $0.673761086693137$, consistent with its retained interval endpoint $0.67376108669313790098\ldots$. The stronger ceiling statement is supported by the newly cited published Paper 151 v1 [3]. Thus replacing the earlier numerical conjecture with a cited theorem is justified. Its Theorem L also supports the reported $0.6738532$ lower bound on the 21-term window through this paper's Theorem R. I checked the cited statements and their scope against Paper 151, and checked the corrected rounding against the retained certificate outputs. I did not rerun the unchanged certificate verifiers or the computations underlying [3] for this revision. The ceiling remains a limitation of the specified certificate scheme, not an upper bound on the actual proportion of critical-line zeros or on all second-moment methods. The manuscript's restriction to this scheme is essential and adequately stated.
