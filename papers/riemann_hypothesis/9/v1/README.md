# Step-Space Annihilator Duality in the Shifted Nyman-Beurling Criterion: Power-Saving Head Norm Asymptotics, Flat-Top Hermite Spline Zero Annihilators, Summation-by-Parts Periodic-Mean Tail Enclosures, and Interval-Certified Conjugate-Kernel Projections

In Beurling's shifted Nyman criterion on $L^2((0, 1], d\mu_\sigma)$ ($d\mu_\sigma(x) = 2\sigma x^{2\sigma-1}\,dx$, $\sigma \in (1/2, 1)$), $\zeta(s)$ is zero-free in $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \sigma$ iff $E = \mathbf{1}_{(0,1]}$ lies in the closed span of $\tilde{\rho}_{1/k}(x) = -\{\lfloor 1/x \rfloor / k\}$ ($k \ge 2$) on $I_m = (1/(m+1), 1/m]$ with weights $w_m^{(\sigma)} = m^{-2\sigma} - (m+1)^{-2\sigma}$. Building on the Vasyunin-Balazard basis $\{\mathcal{C}_n\}_{n\ge 1}$ and the head null-vector identification of riemann_hypothesis_7, we prove four results. First, for $\mathcal{L} = \alpha_1 \mathcal{F}_N + \sum_{n=N+1}^M \alpha_n \mathcal{C}_n$, the pure-tail squarefree functional $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sqf}}$ yields $d_N(\sigma)^2 \ge c(\sigma)N^{-2\sigma}$, while the head functional $\mathcal{F}_N = \sum_{k=1}^N k^{-1}\mathcal{C}_k$ satisfies $\|\mathcal{F}_N\|_{S_\sigma^*}^2 = \frac{\zeta(2\sigma+1)}{2\sigma\,\zeta(4\sigma+2)}N^{2\sigma-1} + O_\sigma(N^{\sigma-1/2})$. Second, to constructively realize the existential rate $d_N(\sigma)^2 \gg_\varepsilon N^{-2(\sigma-\beta_0)-\varepsilon}$ of riemann_hypothesis_6 at any zero $\rho_0 = \beta_0 + i\gamma_0$ ($\beta_0 \in [1/2, \sigma)$), we show that polynomial tapers $(1 - u)^r$ suffer an $O((M+1)^{-1})$ lower-end obstruction from $\zeta(\rho_0 - 1)$, and resolve it via the $C^{r-1}$ flat-top Hermite spline $W_r(u)$ ($W_r \equiv 1$ on $[0, 1/2]$, $r \ge 2$), combining $\gamma_k^{(M; W_r)}(\rho_0) = O(k^r / (M+1)^{\beta_0 + r})$ with a universal divisor-sum norm bound $\|\sum_{k=2}^N \gamma_k \mathcal{C}_k\|_{S_\sigma^*}^2 \le C_\sigma N \sum_{k=2}^N k^{2\sigma}|\gamma_k|^2$ at $M = \lfloor \Lambda N^{1 + 1/(\beta_0 + r)} \rfloor$ to prove $d_N(\sigma)^2 \gg_{\sigma,\rho_0,r} N^{-2(\sigma-\beta_0)(1 + 1/(\beta_0 + r))}$. Third, we prove a periodic Hurwitz-zeta formula for $(A_N(\sigma))_{j,k}$ (validated via 60-digit interval Euler-Maclaurin summation) and a summation-by-parts periodic-mean tail enclosure with exact integer fluctuation bounds ($92.2\times$--$137.8\times$ tail reduction), certifying $\kappa(\sigma)$, $d_{40}(\sigma)^2$, and $d_{160}(\sigma)^2$ in 60-digit mpmath.iv arithmetic. Fourth, extending riemann_hypothesis_2 via the hybrid kernel $k_{\rho, M}^{(\sigma)}$, we prove strict Loewner monotonicity of $B_{\mathrm{2pt}, M}^{(S_\sigma)}(\sigma; \beta, \gamma) = b_\rho^* ((K_{\rho, M}^{(\sigma)})^T)^{-1} b_\rho$ from $B_{\mathrm{2pt}, 0}^{(H^2)} = \frac{8\sigma(\beta-\sigma)[\beta^2+\gamma^2 - 2\sigma(\beta-\sigma)]}{(\beta^2+\gamma^2)^2}$, certifying $1.35\times$--$30.11\times$ gains and $N=40, 160$ zero-exclusion comparisons in 60-digit interval arithmetic.
