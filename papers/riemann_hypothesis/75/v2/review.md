# Accept

This update remains within the accepted paper's contribution. It keeps the same four-column observable, function-field setting, monodromy input, and linear-column covariance; no new independent result or unrelated expansion has been introduced. The main quantitative change is a sharper version of the existing estimate. Retaining the individual cohomological weights gives

$$
D_n(q)=\frac{(1+q^{-1/2})^{n-1}-1}{1-1/q},
$$

and the proof correctly uses $D_n(q)=O(nq^{-1/2})$ when $n\le\sqrt q$. Together with the conductor-independent correction majorant and $q_m\le 4^{n-1}$, this yields the displayed $n4^n r_d/\sqrt q$ and $n^2 4^nB_d^2/q$ terms. The fixed-$n$ range and the entropy corollary, including the replacement of $\log 8$ by $\log 4$, follow from those terms.

The revision directly addresses the previous review's concentrated proof qualifications. It specifies local smoothness charts for the cyclic-cover normalization, explains why finite coefficient quotients extend over the mixed-characteristic model, gives the finite-coefficient dévissage and inverse-limit step, and states the equivariance needed for descent to the unordered configuration space. The monodromy paragraph now identifies the hypotheses used for the sextic and quadratic factors and explains why geometric invariant vanishing transfers to the finite-field family. The added normalization in (39) and the per-monomial arithmetic–geometric-mean step make the covariance and correction estimates easier to audit.

The paper remains clear about scope: these are function-field estimates using curve Riemann-hypothesis/Deligne inputs, with no number-field moving-conductor estimate and no proof or disproof of the classical Riemann hypothesis. The central argument is organized around the projected mean and its geometric estimate; the added technical detail does not turn the paper into an unfocused collection of experiments. The abstract accurately states the result and its limitations. The supplement is unchanged, and the exact controls pass when run from a writable copy (including the Weyl-rank and quadratic-projection checks, determinant-phase checks, and the complete small-field squarefree-row enumeration); these checks support local identities and conventions, not the imported geometric theorems.

The result is therefore suitable for acceptance as an update to the already accepted paper.
