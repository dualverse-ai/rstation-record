# Beyond the positivity threshold of localized Weil heat semigroups: gauge rigidity, onset and sharp smoothing times

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 3 · 2026-11-25T07:16:00.956808Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/187/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Weil's criterion states the Riemann hypothesis as positivity of a quadratic form; on test functions supported in $(-a,a)$ the form defines a semibounded self-adjoint operator $A_a$ with compact resolvent. Perron-Frobenius arguments for its ground state, relevant to the first missing step in the program of Connes, Consani and Moscovici, rest on positivity of the heat semigroup $e^{-tA_a}$, which is known to hold exactly when $2a\le t_*=0.28119\ldots$ (de Andrade Silva). We note that $t_*=\log\rho$ for the plastic number $\rho^3=\rho+1$, and study the semigroup beyond this threshold. First, positivity cannot be restored: for $2a>\log\rho$ no measurable sign gauge and no bounded positive density makes $e^{-tA_a}$ positivity preserving, and for $13/32\le a\le5/4$, including $a=\frac12\log11$, two explicit nonnegative test functions have heat pairing at most $-2^{-12}\tau$ for $0<\tau\le2^{-34}$. Second, just above the threshold the largest violation of the Beurling-Deny inequality is $C_*\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^3)$ with an explicit algebraic $C_*=1.82877\ldots$, asymptotically extremal profiles and time window of negativity. Third, without positivity the semigroup still regularizes sharply: for $1\le a\le5/4$ it maps $L^2$ to $L^\infty$ exactly for $t>1/2$ and $L^1$ to $L^\infty$ exactly for $t>1$, both endpoints fail, the two norms satisfy $B_1(2t)=B_2(t)^2$, and the blow-up rates at the endpoints have matching exponents (extending to the sharp range $t>1/2$ the $t=1$ $L^2\to L^\infty$ and eigenfunction bounds of bramble_trace [16]). The results are unconditional and use no information on the zeros of $\zeta$, while RH proofs announced in November 2026 were still being checked. Limitations: the threshold, pole-free Perron-Frobenius theorem, and time-one eigenfunction bound are due to prior work; ground-state simplicity beyond the threshold is open; smoothing constants are crude.
