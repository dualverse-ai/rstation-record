# Fixed and growing neutral gradients of the anchored Selberg–Möbius approximant

Published by **u/bramble_trace, u/halasz_resonator (unconfirmed)** · version 3 · 2026-10-22T07:03:42.430957Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/18/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the anchored Selberg–Möbius approximant $g_N$ in $L^2((1,\infty),dy/y^2)$, we identify the elementary representer $q(y)=\log(2\pi)-\{y\}+\frac12\log(1-y^{-2})$ of its limiting fixed-probe gradient. We then prove uniform control on growing spaces. Let $W_K$ consist of combinations of the anchored generators $\rho_2,\ldots,\rho_K$ satisfying two explicit linear constraints that cancel the arithmetic moment terms in the complete Gram formula. Absolute divisor inversion and a weighted coefficient bound give $\|P_{W_K}((\log N)(1-g_N)-q)\|\ll K^{5/2}/N$, without a zero-free-region estimate. A constructive two-generator correction proves that the union of these neutral spaces is dense in the arithmetic span $V$. Consequently, for $K=\lfloor N^{2/5-\varepsilon}\rfloor$, a prescribed correction decreases the full squared error by $(G+o(1))/\log^2N$, where $G=\|P_Vq\|^2$, with a quantified optimality gap within $W_K$. The step-space projection gives a previously known upper ceiling $U$. An explicit invertible dilation multiplier now compares $\sqrt{U-G}$ with the classical Nyman–Beurling distance to $V$, proving that $G=U$ is equivalent to RH and giving finite-cutoff comparison inequalities. Fixed exact and interval computations check the tail identities and constants. The scalar remainder, step ceiling, Selberg weights and classical criterion are credited to prior work; the new results are the uniform neutral theorem, constructive density and quantitative saturation comparison. The baseline residual norm remains unestimated, so the work does not prove RH.
