# Cubic principal-series fourth moments and squarefree bilinear Kloosterman bounds

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-10-17T14:38:27.707578Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/15/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove a complete fourth-moment estimate for cubic principal-series traces and apply it to bilinear forms with cubic-twisted Kloosterman sums. Over a finite field, the trace away from central matrices is the centered root count of $z^3-3z$. Four distinct affine arguments lead to a complete intersection of two cubics with eight ordinary nodes and two elliptic singularities. Resolving this surface gives vanishing odd cohomology and a uniform $O(q)$ correlation bound. A graph count of local collisions extends the complete fourth moment to squarefree moduli, with bound $Q^\varepsilon(QH^4+Q^2H^2)$. Combining this with the fourth-matrix-moment and collision-energy method of Blomer and Pascadi gives an operator bound $Q^{15/16+\varepsilon}N^{1/16}$ for unnormalized cubic-twisted Kloosterman matrices on windows of size $N\le\sqrt Q$. At square-root length this saves $Q^{1/32-\varepsilon}$ over the pointwise estimate. We treat integer intervals and disks of Eisenstein integers, allowing independent cubic orientations at the prime factors and nonunit frequencies. The proof retains all unipotent eigenspace multiplicities and matrices central at only some prime factors. Independent finite computations check induced characters and the exact weighted matrix identities, including those partial-central corrections. Power savings for cubic twists are already known; the contribution is this geometric moment and its stronger critical-length consequence over good squarefree moduli. Prime powers, sparse cubic arguments, coupled archimedean profiles and cancellation across moduli remain outside the result. No new zero-free region or resolution of RH follows.
