Bandlimited zero-bundle operators on consecutive integer grids $\{1,\dots,X\}$ decouple from shifted primes only below $L_X = \log((X+1)/X)$ and cannot detect critical-strip off-line zeros ($\frac{1}{2} < \operatorname{Re}\rho < 1$) through $S_X$ alone, while fixed-degree Paley–Wiener kernels lose collective positivity above $X \asymp T/(\log T)^2$. We resolve both regimes by passing to general multiplicative index sets $\mathcal{K} \subset \mathbb{N}$ at fixed bandwidth $L_0 > 0$ and adaptive cardinal B-spline kernels.

First (Theorem 1), $(\mathcal{Z}_{\mathcal{K},L_0}^{[\mathcal{L}]}(T))_{a,b}$ depends solely on the reduced ratio $k_b/k_a$; for $0 < L_0 < \log 2$, diagonal prime sums vanish identically and $\sum_{M=1}^\infty Z_M^{[\zeta]}(T_0)M^{-w} = \sum_\rho w_\rho(T_0)\zeta(w - \rho + \frac{1}{2})$ renders the Riemann Hypothesis equivalent, at any single fixed $T_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, to the $2\times 2$ Carathéodory bound $|Z_M^{[\zeta]}(T_0)| \le Z_1^{[\zeta]}(T_0)$ ($M \ge 2$). Second (Theorem 2), on geometric progressions $\mathcal{K}_{q,R} = \{1,q,\dots,q^{R-1}\}$, $\mathcal{Z}_{\mathcal{K}_{q,R},L_0}^{[\mathcal{L}]}(T)$ collapses to an $R\times R$ Hermitian Carathéodory–Toeplitz matrix whose exact rank-2 Kreĭn gain and background rank deflation unmask high-ordinate critical-strip zeros masked in $2\times 2$ pairs, certified in 80-dps `mpmath.iv` on Epstein $E_1(s;Q_5)$ and Davenport–Heilbronn $\mathcal{D}(s)$ ($\gamma_0 \approx 5009.46, \beta_0 \approx 0.7261$). Third (Theorem 3), prime-power disjointness yields collision-free 4th vertical moments for $L_0 < \log 2$ and exact Steinhaus $2k$-th moments ($k \ge 1$) with sub-Gaussian vertical tails for $L_0 \le \frac{1}{2}\log 2$. Fourth (Theorem 4), adaptive cardinal B-splines $\Phi_{[K]}$ prove PNT-free collective positivity $\mathcal{Z}_{X,L}^{[\zeta]}(T;\Phi_{[K]}) \succ 0$ across $2 \le X \le \lfloor c_* T/\log\log T\rfloor$ for every $c_* \in (0, 1/e)$, certified up to $T = 10^{40}$ ($123\times$ beyond $T/(\log T)^2$).
