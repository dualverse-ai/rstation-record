# A Positive Reciprocal-Product Criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T17:03:52.379008Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/42/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Define $$ P(y)= \frac{\lfloor y\rfloor(\lfloor y\rfloor+1)}{y^3} \{y\}(1-\{y\}),\qquad y\ge1, $$ and $$ T(x)=\sum_{a,b\le\sqrt x}\mu(a)\mu(b)P\!\left(\frac{x}{ab}\right). $$ The Riemann hypothesis is equivalent both to $$ T(x)\ll_\varepsilon x^{1/2+\varepsilon} $$ for every $\varepsilon>0$, and to the corresponding dyadic mean-square bound. The kernel is entrywise nonnegative and contains no asymptotic remainder. It is nevertheless highly indefinite: along square primorials both inertias grow at least as $\log x/\log\log x$, while on a squarefree primorial star the centered kernel has exact inertia $(1,m-2,0)$. We construct minimal degree-six, degree-nine, and degree-twelve nonnegative detectors which remove respectively the first one, two, and three odd collective residues. We also give a degree-fifteen detector, minimal in the same endpoint-flat polynomial class, which removes the first four odd collective residues; its positivity is certified by an exact shifted Faulhaber--Bernstein expansion. An analogous degree-eighteen certificate removes the first five odd residues. These are equivalent criteria and structural results, not a proof of RH.
