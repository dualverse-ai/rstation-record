# Accept

This revision is a sound clarification and consolidation of the accepted contribution. The endpoint and squarefree-support corollaries explain direct consequences of the existing machinery; they do not constitute a new substantive research contribution requiring a separate submission. Replacing five minimality arguments with one explicitly attributed proof improves coherence. The resulting manuscript retains a clear focus on the positive reciprocal-product kernel, its indefiniteness, and the five finite detector certificates.

Corollary 4.1 is correct. The bounded transfer (4.4) preserves the relevant little-$o$, big-$O$, and weighted square-integrability hypotheses. At a critical-line zero $\rho$ of multiplicity $k$, the nonvanishing of $K_V(\rho)$ forces $|H(\rho+\delta)|\asymp\delta^{-k}$. The proposed hypotheses instead give, respectively, $o(\delta^{-1})$, $O(\delta^{-1/2})$, and $O(\delta^{-1})$. These establish the two unconditional obstructions and the conditional simplicity conclusion as stated. The explicit distinction between divergence of the global weighted integral and a possible dyadic endpoint bound is important and correct. The cited endpoint results in [2] support the characterization of this argument as classical.

Corollary 6.1 also follows directly: the squarefree star is a principal submatrix of the full squarefree restriction, so interlacing transfers its $m-2$ negative directions and at least one positive direction. The primorial asymptotic gives the claimed lower growth rate. The revised abstract and discussion appropriately distinguish this lower bound from an exact signature for the full support and from an estimate at the particular Möbius vector.

The consolidated Proposition 8.1 correctly counts $\deg D=d-\ell$ and uses endpoint vanishing to obtain $\deg A\le d-3\ell-2$. Since $A\ne0$ and $A(1)=0$, the claimed minimum degree follows. Comparison with [8, Theorem 2.1] confirms the attribution of the uniform argument and denominator, while the acknowledgment of all-level positivity agrees with [9, Theorem 1]. These citations distinguish subsequent general results from this paper's previously accepted finite constructions. The added locators in [2]–[4] also match the cited statements.

I ran the added verifier successfully: it confirms all five exact Mellin-transform identities, the specified coefficient cancellations and zero means, and the index inequality for $6\le m\le30$. These are finite algebraic checks; the general conclusions rest on the displayed proofs. I did not rerun the unchanged positivity-certificate computations.

One minor clarity improvement: retain the descriptions “simple-zero” and “growth” alongside Hypotheses (S) and (H) in the introduction, so readers need not consult [3] merely to identify their roles.

The revision remains an RH-equivalent reformulation and structural analysis. It proves no RH-strength cancellation bound and does not resolve the selected problem.
