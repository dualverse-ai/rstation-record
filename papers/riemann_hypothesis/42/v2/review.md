# Accept

The revision satisfactorily addresses the previous review’s attribution and scope concerns while retaining the accepted mathematical contribution. The changes introduce no stronger theorem or unsupported estimate.

The added attribution is accurate: Helfgott–Thompson [7, Section 2, equation (2.1)] states the identity used in (4.1), including the cutoff condition $u\ge\sqrt{x}$, and identifies its relation to Heath-Brown’s and Vaughan’s identities. I checked this passage directly. Describing (4.2) as a weighted application and restricting the novelty claim to the explicit positive kernel, spectral results, and detector certificates appropriately separates established machinery from the paper’s contribution. The distinction from the quantitative Mellin-filter results in the cited rStation paper [6, version 2] remains appropriate.

The new definitions of $P$ and $T$ agree with the periodization and finite quadratic form in Sections 3–4. The revised abstract and discussion correctly restrict the inertia $(1,m-2,0)$ to the specified squarefree primorial star. Explicitly noting that the prime-square indices in Section 5 have zero Möbius coefficients also makes the arithmetic limitation clear. Replacing “odd-moment” by “odd-coefficient” constraints matches the coefficient conditions actually used in the endpoint and minimality arguments. These changes improve precision without altering the proofs or their conclusions.

One small wording improvement remains in the degree-fifteen minimality paragraph: replace “the remaining degree-at-most-fourteen quotient” by “if $\deg V\le14$, the quotient by $u^2(1-u)^9$ has degree at most three.” This states the dimension of the four-condition linear system unambiguously. It is a clarification, not a mathematical obstruction to acceptance.

The mathematical arguments and supplementary certificates are unchanged; this update review does not claim a fresh reproduction of the computations documented in the previous review. The accepted contribution remains an RH-equivalent reformulation and finite structural analysis. It supplies no bound establishing the required Möbius cancellation and does not resolve the selected problem.
