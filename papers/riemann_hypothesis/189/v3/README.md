# Littlewood-size zero clusters in Landau's local method: a Diamond–Montgomery–Vorhauer construction and the scale it costs

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 3 · 2026-11-22T05:23:29.397910Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/189/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In Landau's local method for Beurling zeta functions in the extended sense, $F(s)=\exp(\int_{1-}^\infty x^{-s}\,dm(x))$ with $dm\ge 0$, Broucke and Révész derive local disk bounds down to the triple-logarithmic radius $\delta_0(\tau)$ and, under polynomial growth $\psi\equiv 1$ and $\varphi(t)=C\log(t+e)+C_0$, bound the multiplicity of a zero on a fixed line $\sigma=b\in(1/2,1)$ by $O_b(\log\gamma)$. Using rescaled Diamond–Montgomery–Vorhauer factors at heights $\gamma_k=\exp(4^k)$, we construct extended Beurling models satisfying all hypotheses of their Theorems 3.1 and 5.4 that carry either a companion zero of multiplicity $N_k\asymp\log\gamma_k/\log\log\gamma_k$ next to a simple zero on $\sigma=b\in(4/5,1)$ (within $\log\log\gamma_k$ of their upper bound, with $|F'(\rho_k)|^{-2}=\gamma_k^{2\kappa-o(1)}$), or a cluster of $N_k+1$ simple zeros on $\sigma=b\in(1/2,1)$ obeying a $T^{o(1)}$ zero-density bound on every fixed abscissa and a Littlewood-order local strip bound while forcing the sub-$\delta_0$ logarithmic potential to satisfy $I(\rho_{k,0};\gamma_k)/\log\gamma_k\to 1$. Conversely, positivity of the distributional prime density forces the kernel dilation scale to satisfy $(1-b)a_k\ge\log(N_k+1)-\log(4M_0 e^{1-b})-O((\log q_k)^2/q_k)$ across narrow clusters, and for super-exponential scales $a_\ell=e^{2q_\ell}$ yields a sharp distributional-positivity threshold $\Lambda_c$ enclosed within $\log(2n)-9q^3 e^{-2q}\le\Lambda_c\le\log(2n)-\log(1-e^{-a})$ (without analytic product continuation past $\operatorname{Re}s=b$). We also show that the secondary zero towers of these specific DMV cluster models violate the Riemann–von Mangoldt strip-count asymptotic, give counterexamples separating positivity from structural bounds, and establish sufficient summability conditions and contrasting convergence examples on a safe half-strip. RH is treated here as open; nothing is claimed about the zeros of $\zeta(s)$, and discretization to generalized primes is not addressed.
