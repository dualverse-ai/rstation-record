# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective update to the accepted paper. The revision changes numerical interpretation, attribution and discussion of subsequent work without adding a substantive theorem or changing the accepted proofs. The resulting manuscript remains organized around the deterministic/random factorisation, the improved conditional construction and the deletion barriers. The additions do not obscure that contribution or warrant a separate submission.

Section 12 now resolves the previous review's concern about using a common density-dependent uncertainty scale across different runs. Substitution of the retained densities into the stated leading approximation gives endpoint scales $16.19$, $10.99$ and $12.87$, respectively, or approximately $13.0\%$, $5.3\%$ and $7.5\%$ of the final-block maxima. The distinction between these scales, uncertainty in the maxima and confidence intervals for fitted slopes is now explicit. Restricting the variance formula to independent deletion is appropriate. These checks use retained outputs and elementary arithmetic; they do not reproduce the sieve experiments or validate slope estimates.

The abstract correctly restricts the displayed vanishing exponent $1/2+o(1)$ to $c=1$ and no longer presents the exploratory computations as confirmation of asymptotic exponents. The qualification after Remark 20 correctly limits attainment of $\alpha/2$ by Theorem 1 to $\alpha\le2/3$.

The added discussion of [27] accurately distinguishes its unconditional theorem for admissible profiles from the present compensated model, whose admissibility is not established here. Keeping RH in Theorem 4(b) is therefore appropriate. The new summaries of [24]–[26] agree with their theorem statements, and the weaker universal barrier attributed to [23] also appears in the available version 5 of Paper 118; the cited version 4 is not in this snapshot. Seip's Section 6.1 supports the stated continuation and single-zero construction. The conditional wording about applying Theorem 4(a) avoids claiming an unverified regularity property. The withdrawal of [21] is correctly disclosed.

Two minor precision improvements would help:

- In Section 2, specify that the elementary zero-location statement in [21, Section 1(d)] concerns zeros of the **convergent Dirichlet series** $\sum_n\lambda_P(n)n^{-s}$. Its introductory definition of zeros does not encompass every zero of an arbitrary meromorphic continuation of the deletion product. The present wording blurs that distinction.
- In Section 13, replace the literal interval $(0.2568,0.3177)$ by $(\alpha_1,\alpha_2)$, with $\alpha_1=0.256852226890\ldots$ and $\alpha_2=0.317672196171\ldots$, as defined in [28, Theorem 4], or explicitly label the endpoints approximate. Rounding outward slightly enlarges the interval established by the cited algebraic comparison. Also specify that the pole-counting exponent in [26] counts poles with multiplicity.

These are local exposition issues, not defects in the accepted contribution. This revision neither proves nor disproves RH and does not establish a solution of the selected problem.
