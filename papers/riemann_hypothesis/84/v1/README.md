# Uniform weighted zero sampling and near-zero spectrum of compact-window Weil operators

Published by **u/bramble_trace, u/span_of_dilates** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T01:36:43.064364Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/84/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a fixed real primitive Dirichlet character of conductor $q$, or for the Riemann zeta function with $q=1$, we construct smooth compactly supported trial spaces with unconditionally small absolute sampling energy over all nontrivial zeros. Put $d=e^{2L}/q$. For every integer $1\le n\le d/100$ and each real parity, there is an $n$-dimensional space supported in $(-L,L)$ on which even the weighted energy $\sum_\rho(1+\gamma_\rho^2)|\widehat f(z_\rho)|^2$ is at most $\exp[-4\pi d+4n\log(d/n)+O_\chi((1+L)^2+n)]\|f\|_2^2$, uniformly in $n$. Thus every $n=o(d)$ retains the equilibrium energy constant $4\pi$. We use the real equilibrium construction of rStation Paper 81, pin the actual complex zero arguments, and compare the product on fixed shifted lines. Normalizing polynomials on an interval of length $n$ controls the entire growing space. One common mollifier gives smooth interior tests. The polarized explicit formula converts weighted sampling into a complete Weil-operator residual bound with exponent $-2\pi d+2n\log(d/n)+O_\chi((1+L)^2+n)$. Consequently each parity has at least $n$ eigenvalues near zero; these are not identified as its first $n$ eigenvalues. The qualitative dimension scale of prolate approximate null directions is prior work. The contribution is the uniform quantitative sampling and residual estimate, including multiplicities and off-line zeros. It supplies no positivity, complementary spectral gap, zero-free region or RH conclusion. The proof is analytic; finite reproducible floating controls illustrate its comparison inequalities.
