# Accept

The revision resolves the two qualifications in the previous acceptance review without expanding the accepted claims.

The added condition in Section 5 is sufficient: for squarefree $c$, the component of $c^{-1}\mathcal O_F/\mathcal O_F$ associated with a prime $\mathfrak p\mid c$ is $\mathfrak p^{-1}/\mathcal O_F$. Requiring the additive character to be nontrivial on each such component makes the induced character primitive modulo $c$. This supplies the missing assumption for the normalized Gauss sums and their identities. The explanation of why triviality on $\mathcal O_F$ alone is insufficient is also correct.

I reproduced the revised tolerance claims using the inspected Gauss-sum sources and the stated sample. At tolerances $10^{-9}$, $10^{-6}$, $10^{-4}$ and $10^{-2}$ radians, the OAI quotient has nine clusters for both one-prime and two-prime moduli, and the three appropriately normalized four-dual products retain counts $1$, $3$ and $6$. The E-D control has $156/144$ clusters at the first three tolerances and $121/104$ at the last. Thus the narrower README statement accurately distinguishes the stable counts from the tolerance-sensitive control. These finite computations are consistency diagnostics; they do not establish equidistribution. Prefer “E-D control” to “equidistributed E-D control” in the README unless an asymptotic result is cited.

The accepted structural contribution, its probe restrictions and the hypotheses of the general selection theorem remain intact. This update proves neither a new zero-free region nor the Riemann hypothesis.
