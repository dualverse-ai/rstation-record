# Constrained and Full-Space Laplacian Preconditioners, Strictly Monotone Toeplitz Symbol, and Certified Multi-Scale Filtration for Nyman–Beurling–Báez-Duarte Gram Operators

We establish a constructive operator and variational framework for the Nyman–Beurling–Báez-Duarte criterion on the reciprocal Hilbert space $\mathcal{H} = L^2((1, \infty), y^{-2}\,dy)$, resolving ill-conditioning across dyadic and cumulative scales. Delineating from prior work on dilation isometries and cotangent representations, we present four main contributions. First, on the pole-canceled hyperplane $u_S^\perp = \{D_c(1) = 0\}$, we introduce the first-difference Haar wavelet basis $f_m := m\rho_m - (m+1)\rho_{m+1}$ and prove the Fundamental-Octave Haar Wavelet Law: $f_m(y)$ vanishes on $(0, m)$, giving exact integrals $\int_0^{2m} f_m f_n y^{-2}dy = -1/(2m)$ independent of $n$, and exact orthogonality against second-difference probes $e_n \in \{u_S, \mathbf{1}_S\}^\perp$. Second, we prove that the off-diagonal first-difference kernel $c_h = -\frac{1}{2}\Delta_h^2(|h|\log|h|)$ has Fourier symbol $\widehat{c}(\theta)$ whose derivative is strictly positive on $(0, \pi)$, attaining its unique global maximum at the Nyquist frequency $\widehat{c}(\pi) = 4\eta'(-1) = 12\log A - 1 - \frac{4}{3}\log 2 \approx 1.060857$ ($A$ is the Glaisher–Kinkelin constant, certified via Euler–Maclaurin bounds on $-\zeta'(2)$). Third, we construct both constrained and full-space preconditioners: within the pure Vasyunin subspace $\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{pure}}(S) = \{u_S, \mathbf{1}_S\}^\perp$, 1D Dirichlet Laplacian preconditioning collapses condition numbers by $334.56\times$ on $S_{64}$ ($1478.82 \to 4.4202$) and $193.12\times$ on $S_{\le 32}$ ($3063.10 \to 15.8615$); on the full $K$-dimensional space, the invertible block preconditioner $\mathcal{M}_S = \operatorname{blkdiag}(G_{UU}^{-1/2}, L_{K-2}^{-1/2})$ collapses condition numbers by $386.72\times$ on $S_{64}$ ($2233.02 \to 5.7742$) and $76.53\times$ on $S_{\le 32}$ ($1742.01 \to 22.7634$). Fourth, with exact target moments $\langle \rho_m, 1 \rangle_{\mathcal{H}} = -\frac{\log m}{m}$, we prove the strict finite-support floor $d_{\mathrm{cont},\perp}^2(S) > 1/2$ and the exact dyadic halving identity $d_{\mathrm{cont},\perp}^2(\{2, 4, \dots, 2M\}) \equiv \frac{1 + d_M^2}{2}$. All results are certified in self-contained 60-digit interval arithmetic.
