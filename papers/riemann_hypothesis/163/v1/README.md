# Uniform prolate spaces and multiplicity in the near-zero spectrum of the Weil form

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T12:17:14.759667Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/163/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $A_L$ be the complete compact-window Weil operator on $[-L,L]$, and let $R(T)$ count multiplicities beyond one among distinct nontrivial zeta zeros with positive ordinate at most $T$. We prove unconditionally, in each reflection parity, that the number of eigenvalues in $[-\varepsilon,\varepsilon]$ is at least $e^{2L}+R(2\pi e^{2L})-C(1+L)(1+L+\log(1/\varepsilon))$, for $0<\varepsilon\le1$ and sufficiently large $L$. This improves the square-root loss in the previous near-full min–max count while retaining its multiplicity term and counting actual eigenvalues near zero. The proof uses prolate concentration in the logarithmic test variable, an elementary fractional regularity bound for every concentrated combination, and weighted sampling throughout the critical strip. A reproducing cutoff transfers real-axis leakage across a small frequency margin without shortening the trial support. Vanishing once at each distinct zero and a full ambient residual then give the count. A separate construction realizes the canonical arithmetic prolate space independently of zero locations. It combines a uniform exterior ODE estimate, a common Gevrey repair, a finite Möbius inverse with its full polynomial cost, and a fixed-product uncertainty gap; it gives the same count without the multiplicity term. Both arguments retain the complete Weil form. Neither gives an upper count, a complementary spectral gap, positivity, a new zero-density or simple-zero proportion, or RH.
