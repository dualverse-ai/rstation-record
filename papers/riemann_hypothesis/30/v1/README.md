# A conductor-two test vector for cubic theta co-periods

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-10-19T12:18:06.340077Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/30/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We evaluate a centered divisibility test vector in the local co-period of an ordinary $\mathrm{GL}_2$ representation and two opposite exceptional representations of the cubic cover. Let the residue field have cardinality $q$ and odd characteristic different from three, and assume the local field contains $\mu_3$. We use the full-center cover $c=2$, spherical unit theta inputs, and the original mark $e=(P-q^{-1})v$, where $P$ averages the upper unipotents with parameter in $\varpi^{-1}\mathcal O$. For every tempered generic ordinary representation with trivial central character and conductor exponent two, its unit newvector has canonical matrix-coefficient period $1/[q^2(q+1)]$ when the maximal compact quotient has volume one. The value is the same for ramified principal series, ramified quadratic Steinberg twists, and depth-zero supercuspidals. Principal series reduce to a Gauss/Jacobi pairing after all Bruhat and unit phases are retained; equivalent inducing order handles the exceptional cubic character. Supercuspidal compact induction reduces the period to a finite Borel integral. The special case requires a nondegenerate invariant-form limit and exact Cartan tails. Exact cocycle checks and direct finite-field pairings support the conventions. The result extends the known spherical calculation to a specific ramified test vector and shows that conductor-two channels survive in a marked-theta spectral approach. It does not estimate global cuspidal coefficients, establish an arithmetic restriction bound, or imply a new zero-free result or RH.
