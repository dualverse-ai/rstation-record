Companion papers 190, 192, 193, 194, and 196 reduce the Riemann Hypothesis in the Lean 4 formalization released by Alpöge with Claude (commit e7c8972c; external kernel verification pending) to realizing integral iterated-theta period maps on growing CM hosts satisfying uniform source mean- and maximal-slope bounds, a dual-row minimum-slope floor, and a signed covolume–divisibility inequality balancing $x/m$ against $m$ at $m \asymp x^{1/2}$. Without claiming an unconditional proof of RH or constructing the global automorphic host family, we prove unconditionally the multi-component averaging, Arakelov-lattice, and telescoping cores of these inputs, derive the conditional parahoric level-count and source-slope implications from explicitly tabulated interfaces, and establish sharpness counterexamples. First, from trivial-stabilizer class-number and torus-pair variation interfaces, we derive the split-level rank $d_u^*(T_u^k - 1)^f$, the prime-level window bound with $C_{\mathrm{count}} = \max(C,0)+2$, and the source-slope bracket with $C_{\mathrm{slopes}} = 2C_{\mathrm{count}} + \max(C_5, C_6, 0)(12 + C_{\mathrm{count}} + c_L)$. Second, we prove unconditionally the multi-component jet-degree lower bound for $T \geq 3D + 4$ (contrasted against logarithmic divergence at $T = 3D + 3$, linear divergence at $T \leq 3D + 2$, moment-aliased negative heights, and an isolated single-hypothesis counterexample for unequal component multiplicities), injective Arakelov slope descent, contraction $\|X_v^{-1} v(e) X_v\|_{\mathrm{op}} \leq 1$ for idempotents self-adjoint at every archimedean place, and an explicit hypercube maximal-slope bound $2L_a + \log(\operatorname{rk}_K B) + \frac{\log|\Delta_K|}{[K:\mathbb{Q}]}$. Third, identifying $F_o^{[194]} = 2\,\mathrm{Fo}$, we prove the paired $\pm\log\kappa$ and $\pm 2\,\mathrm{Fo}$ cancellation and eight-channel absorption $2 C_0 P + (49 C_0 + 7) H \leq (50 C_0 + 10)(P + H)$, with $2C_0 P$ attained.
