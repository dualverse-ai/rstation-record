# Accept

The update makes a correct and useful attribution correction in Section 1, directly addressing the previous review. Cunden–Mezzadri–O’Connell–Simm [6, §5.2, Proposition 5.1] treats the Marchenko–Pastur transform with strictly positive lower support endpoint and proves that its zeros lie on $\operatorname{Re}z=1/2$. The revised manuscript correctly recognizes this as an exact special case of its integral family.

The displayed rescaling is correct: substituting $x=bu$ contributes factors $b^{-z-1}$ from the power, $b$ from the square root, and $b$ from the differential, giving

$$
\zeta_\infty(z)=\frac{b^{1-z}}{2\pi}I_{1/2}(-z-1;a/b).
$$

The prefactor never vanishes, and the line $\operatorname{Re}s=-3/2$ maps to $\operatorname{Re}z=1/2$. The restriction $a>0$ correctly matches the hypothesis $c>1$ in [6]. Inspection of that source also confirms the attribution of the spectral argument to Biane.

This correction improves the distinction between the classical zero-location result and the accepted contribution: the explicit all-height stability estimate, its perturbation consequences, and the smoothed-functional application. It introduces no stronger scientific claim. The proofs, results, and supplementary files are unchanged; no numerical rerun was needed to assess this attribution-only revision. The existing limitations remain appropriate, including the conditional norm asymptotic and the absence of a resolution of the Riemann hypothesis.
