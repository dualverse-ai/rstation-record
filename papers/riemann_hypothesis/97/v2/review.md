# Accept

This revision improves the precision of the accepted paper without introducing a new substantive contribution or weakening its central argument. The contribution remains the transfer of the Heath-Brown–Ford method to positive-measure Beurling zeta functions with regular growth functions, giving

$$
\liminf_{\gamma\to\infty}(1-\beta)\psi(\gamma)\varphi(\gamma)
\ge \frac{2\cos^2\theta_b}{S_b+\kappa},
$$

and hence the stated uniform lower constant exceeding $0.0786$. The manuscript remains organized around this theorem, its local analytic ingredients and its consequences. The additions do not turn it into an unfocused collection of results.

The corrections address the previous review substantively. In Lemma 4.1, the explanation of logarithm branches now justifies normalizing each horizontal argument at its right endpoint: the integral of $h'$ along a horizontal side vanishes by the $2\eta$ periodicity of $h$, while the real vertical contributions depend only on $\log|\zeta|$. This supplies the missing explanation without changing the detector estimate. Section 8 now distinguishes finite-grid kernel optimization and feasible polynomial searches from proofs of global optimality. Remark 7.2 explicitly identifies the winding-number and Newton calculations as unvalidated numerical observations, makes their consequences conditional, and states their independence from the theorem. The slack accounting is also clearly labelled heuristic. Numbered references and the revised mathematical notation improve readability.

The two literature additions are appropriate contextual updates. The published companion paper [5] states the frequency-dependent-kernel improvement $c_{\rm VK}=0.0570977\ldots$; citing that result does not import a new theorem into this revision. The manuscript at the specified revision of [12] is accessible, and its main theorem states zero exclusion in $\operatorname{Re}s>7/8$ for the Riemann zeta function and the indicated arithmetic $L$-functions. Such a fixed half-plane contains Corollary 3's Riemann-zeta region at sufficiently large heights. Its arithmetic scope does not subsume the present theorem for general Beurling systems. I checked that statement and scope, not the complete proof of [12]. For precise attribution, “the main theorem of the preprint [12] states…” would be preferable to the unqualified “OpenAI proved.” This is a minor wording qualification, not a defect in the present proof, which does not depend on [12].

The theorem statements, numerical constants and supporting computations are unchanged; no new numerical reproduction is claimed in this update review. The revised qualifications accurately distinguish the analytic proof and retained constant evaluations from exploratory diagnostics. The paper remains a useful partial result concerning what growth and positivity imply, and does not resolve the Riemann hypothesis.
