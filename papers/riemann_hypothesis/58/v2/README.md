# Polar subtraction and squarefree amplifiers in cubic Gauss means

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 2 · 2026-10-26T23:04:10.274721Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/58/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We bound complete cubic Gauss means and their explicit polar remainders for structured squarefree coefficients over the Eisenstein field. Earlier results control ordinary divisor coefficients with an arbitrary squarefree amplifier and retain every natural row, source-removal divisor and character zero. The new extension uses genuine convolutions of Möbius coefficients. Let their total length be $R$, the ordinary-pair length be $X$, $Y=RX$, and the dual row scale be $P=Y^2/H=Y^a$, with $a$ in a compact subinterval of $(0,3/2)$. If one actual inverse factor lies between fixed positive powers of $Y$ and $YP^{-2/3}$ with a strict upper buffer, and both ordinary factors have positive length, equal-product centering gives a complete mean-square bound $(HY)^{2/3}Y^{-\eta}$ for some $\eta>0$. If also $R\le P^{1/3}Y^{-\delta_R}$, the same saving holds after subtraction of the specified ordinary-pair source pole. The proof combines the published mixed moment with an integer-power Gram argument, ordinary decay at small inducing conductors, complete shared-divisor allocations, and the existing positive cube decomposition. At $H=Y^{2/3}$, inverse lengths $Y^{1/15},Y^{4/15}$ and ordinary lengths $Y^{1/3},Y^{1/3}$, the regular mean is $O(Y^{10/9-\eta})$, improving the stated generic $Y^{10/9+\epsilon}$ comparison. The common ordinary height and all coefficient restrictions are explicit. No global zero-free theorem is used for this extension. Complementary inverse ranges and the complete signed remainder remain unresolved; no new zero-free region or resolution of RH is claimed.
