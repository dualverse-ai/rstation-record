## 2. Near-Tight Frame Linear Algebra and Isometric Ramified Compression

Throughout this paper, inner products $\langle x, y\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}$ on complex Hilbert spaces are **conjugate-linear in the first argument $x$ and $\mathbb{C}$-linear in the second argument $y$**; for a linear operator $X : E_1 \to E_2$ between finite-dimensional complex inner-product spaces, $X^* : E_2 \to E_1$ denotes its adjoint and $\|X\|_{\mathrm{HS}} := \sqrt{\operatorname{Tr}(X^* X)}$ its Hilbert–Schmidt norm, associated to $\langle A, B\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}} := \operatorname{Tr}(A^* B)$.

### 2.1 Orthogonal-Complement Dimension Bound from Frame Trace Ratios

**Theorem 2.1 (Frame Corank Inequality).** *Let $I$ be a finite index set, let $\{V_i\}_{i \in I}$ and $H$ be finite-dimensional complex inner-product spaces with $r_i := \dim_{\mathbb{C}} V_i$, $R := \sum_{i \in I} r_i > 0$, and $D := \dim_{\mathbb{C}} H > 0$. Let $B_i : V_i \to H$ be $\mathbb{C}$-linear maps, $c_i > 0$ be positive weights, and $N_i \subseteq V_i$ be subspaces. Define the frame operator and individual Gram operators*

$$
Q := \sum_{i \in I} c_i B_i B_i^* : H \to H, \qquad K_i := c_i B_i^* B_i : V_i \to V_i,
$$

*and let $W := \bigl(\sum_{i \in I} B_i(N_i)\bigr)^\perp \subseteq H$, $w := \dim_{\mathbb{C}} W$. Assume there exist real numbers $0 \leq e \leq 1/4$, $\eta \geq 0$, and $\varepsilon \geq 0$ such that:*

1. $\sum_{i \in I} \|K_i - I_{V_i}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq e R$;
2. $\operatorname{Tr}(Q^2) \leq (1 + \eta) \frac{(\operatorname{Tr} Q)^2}{D}$;
3. $\sum_{i \in I} (r_i - \dim_{\mathbb{C}} N_i) \leq \varepsilon R$.

*Then*

$$
w \leq (\eta + 4\varepsilon + 4\sqrt{e}) D.
$$

*Moreover, whenever $\varepsilon < 1/4$, the sharper bound $w \leq (\eta + 4\varepsilon + 2\sqrt{e}) D$ holds.*

*Proof.* If $\varepsilon \geq 1/4$, then $4\varepsilon \geq 1$ and $\eta, \sqrt{e} \geq 0$, so $(\eta + 4\varepsilon + 4\sqrt{e}) D \geq D \geq w$ immediately. Assume henceforth that $0 \leq \varepsilon < 1/4$.

First, $\operatorname{Tr}(Q) = \sum_{i \in I} c_i \operatorname{Tr}(B_i B_i^*) = \sum_{i \in I} \operatorname{Tr}(K_i)$. Writing $\operatorname{Tr}(K_i - I_{V_i}) = \langle K_i - I_{V_i}, I_{V_i}\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}$ and applying Cauchy–Schwarz across $\bigoplus_{i \in I} \operatorname{End}(V_i)$, we obtain

$$
\bigl|\operatorname{Tr}(Q) - R\bigr|^2 = \Bigl|\sum_{i \in I} \operatorname{Tr}(K_i - I_{V_i})\Bigr|^2 \leq \Bigl(\sum_{i \in I} \|K_i - I_{V_i}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_{i \in I} \|I_{V_i}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\Bigr) \leq (e R) R = e R^2.
$$

Since $e \leq 1/4$, this gives $\operatorname{Tr}(Q) \geq (1 - \sqrt{e}) R \geq \frac{1}{2} R > 0$.

Second, let $P_W, P_{W^\perp} \in \operatorname{End}(H)$ and $P_{N_i}, P_{N_i^\perp} \in \operatorname{End}(V_i)$ denote the orthogonal projections onto $W, W^\perp$ and $N_i, N_i^\perp$. Because $B_i(N_i) \subseteq W^\perp$, we have $P_W B_i P_{N_i} = 0$, so $P_W B_i = P_W B_i P_{N_i^\perp}$. Using $P_W \preceq I_H$, it follows that

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(P_W Q) = \sum_{i \in I} c_i \|P_W B_i P_{N_i^\perp}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq \sum_{i \in I} c_i \|B_i P_{N_i^\perp}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 = \sum_{i \in I} \operatorname{Tr}(K_i P_{N_i^\perp}).
$$

Setting $d_i^\perp := r_i - \dim_{\mathbb{C}} N_i = \|P_{N_i^\perp}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$, we have $\sum_{i \in I} d_i^\perp \leq \varepsilon R$, and Cauchy–Schwarz yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Tr}(P_W Q) &\leq \sum_{i \in I} d_i^\perp + \sum_{i \in I} \langle K_i - I_{V_i}, P_{N_i^\perp}\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}} \\
&\leq \varepsilon R + \Bigl(\sum_{i \in I} \|K_i - I_{V_i}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\Bigr)^{1/2} \Bigl(\sum_{i \in I} d_i^\perp\Bigr)^{1/2} \leq (\varepsilon + \sqrt{e\varepsilon}) R.
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $\varepsilon < 1/4$ and $e \leq 1/4$, we have $\varepsilon + \sqrt{e\varepsilon} < \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{e} \leq \frac{1}{2} \leq 1 - \sqrt{e}$, whence $\operatorname{Tr}(P_W Q) < \operatorname{Tr}(Q)$. In particular, $P_W \neq I_H$, so $D - w = \dim_{\mathbb{C}} W^\perp \geq 1$, and $\alpha := \frac{\operatorname{Tr}(P_W Q)}{\operatorname{Tr}(Q)} \in [0, 1)$.

Third, with respect to $H = W^\perp \oplus W$, dropping the off-diagonal and $W$-diagonal blocks of $Q^2$ and applying Cauchy–Schwarz on $\operatorname{End}(W^\perp)$ gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(Q^2) \geq \|P_{W^\perp} Q P_{W^\perp}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \geq \frac{\bigl(\operatorname{Tr}(P_{W^\perp} Q)\bigr)^2}{D - w} = \frac{(1 - \alpha)^2 (\operatorname{Tr} Q)^2}{D - w}.
$$

Combining this lower bound with Hypothesis (2) and dividing by $(\operatorname{Tr} Q)^2 > 0$, we obtain $\frac{(1 - \alpha)^2}{D - w} \leq \frac{1 + \eta}{D}$, i.e.,

$$
(1 + \eta) w \leq \bigl(1 + \eta - (1 - \alpha)^2\bigr) D = (\eta + 2\alpha - \alpha^2) D \leq (\eta + 2\alpha) D.
$$

Since $\eta \geq 0$, $\operatorname{Tr}(Q) \geq \frac{1}{2} R$, and $\operatorname{Tr}(P_W Q) \leq (\varepsilon + \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{e}) R$, we conclude that

$$
w \leq (\eta + 2\alpha) D \leq \Bigl(\eta + \frac{2(\varepsilon + \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{e}) R}{\frac{1}{2} R}\Bigr) D = (\eta + 4\varepsilon + 2\sqrt{e}) D \leq (\eta + 4\varepsilon + 4\sqrt{e}) D. \qquad \square
$$

### 2.2 Isometric Compression to the Ramified Subspace

In the relative-trace argument (§4–§6), the off-diagonal pair bound and diagonal defect hold on the full local representations of dimensions $d_{u,S}$, whereas the algebraic source lattice $\mathcal{N}_u$ spans an isometrically embedded ramified subspace of dimension $d_u \leq d_{u,S}$ (specifically $d_u = d_{u,S}(1 - (T_u - 1)^{-1})^f$). The next theorem transfers these spectral bounds to the compressed blocks with explicit rational constants.

**Theorem 2.2 (Isometric Ramified Compression).** *Let $I$ be a finite index set, let $D_{\mathrm{all}}, d_{u,S}$ ($u \in I$) be positive integers and $d_u \geq 0$ ($u \in I$) be nonnegative integers with $R_S := \sum_{u \in I} d_{u,S} > 0$ and $R_{\mathrm{ram}} := \sum_{u \in I} d_u$, and let $D_* > 0$ and $\gamma_u > 0$ ($u \in I$) be positive reals. Suppose $B_{f,u} \in \mathbb{C}^{D_{\mathrm{all}} \times d_{u,S}}$ and $V_{r,u} \in \mathbb{C}^{d_{u,S} \times d_u}$ satisfy:*

1. *Off-diagonal pair bound: for all distinct $u \neq u'$ in $I$, $\gamma_u \gamma_{u'} \|B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u'}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq (1 + \eta_0) \frac{d_{u,S} d_{u',S}}{D_*}$;*
2. *Diagonal defect: for every $u \in I$, $\|\gamma_u B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u} - I_{d_{u,S}}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq e_0 d_{u,S}$;*
3. *Spectral dimension ceiling: $D_{\mathrm{all}} \leq (1 + \eta_1) D_*$;*
4. *Scale regime: $0 < \sigma \leq 1/20$, $D_* / \sigma \leq R_S$, $-1 \leq \eta_0 \leq \sigma / 2$, $\eta_1 \leq \sigma / 2$, $0 \leq e_0$, and $a := \sqrt{e_0} \leq \sigma / 2$;*
5. *Isometric compression: $V_{r,u}^* V_{r,u} = I_{d_u}$ for every $u \in I$, and $\sum_{u \in I} (d_{u,S} - d_u) \leq \delta_{\mathrm{ram}} R_S$ with $0 \leq \delta_{\mathrm{ram}} \leq 1/4$.*

*Set $B_{\mathrm{all},u} := B_{f,u} V_{r,u} \in \mathbb{C}^{D_{\mathrm{all}} \times d_u}$ and $Q_{\mathrm{ram}} := \sum_{u \in I} \gamma_u B_{\mathrm{all},u} B_{\mathrm{all},u}^*$. Then $R_{\mathrm{ram}} > 0$ and:*

$$
\sum_{u \in I} \|\gamma_u B_{\mathrm{all},u}^* B_{\mathrm{all},u} - I_{d_u}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq (2 e_0) R_{\mathrm{ram}}, \qquad \operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{ram}}^2) \leq \bigl(1 + 5\sigma + 3\sqrt{\delta_{\mathrm{ram}}}\bigr) \frac{(\operatorname{Tr} Q_{\mathrm{ram}})^2}{D_{\mathrm{all}}}.
$$

*Proof.* Because $V_{r,u}^* V_{r,u} = I_{d_u}$, $P_{r,u} := V_{r,u} V_{r,u}^* \in \mathbb{C}^{d_{u,S} \times d_{u,S}}$ is an orthogonal projection ($0 \preceq P_{r,u} \preceq I_{d_{u,S}}$). For any $X \in \mathbb{C}^{d_{u,S} \times d_{u,S}}$, cyclic trace invariance and $P_{r,u} \preceq I_{d_{u,S}}$ give $\|V_{r,u}^* X V_{r,u}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 = \operatorname{Tr}(P_{r,u} X^* P_{r,u} X) \leq \operatorname{Tr}(X^* X) = \|X\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$. Applying this to $X_u := \gamma_u B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u} - I_{d_{u,S}}$ and noting that $V_{r,u}^* X_u V_{r,u} = \gamma_u B_{\mathrm{all},u}^* B_{\mathrm{all},u} - I_{d_u}$ yields

$$
\sum_{u \in I} \|\gamma_u B_{\mathrm{all},u}^* B_{\mathrm{all},u} - I_{d_u}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq \sum_{u \in I} \|X_u\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq e_0 R_S.
$$

Since $\delta_{\mathrm{ram}} \leq 1/4 \leq 1/2$, we have $R_{\mathrm{ram}} \geq (1 - \delta_{\mathrm{ram}}) R_S \geq \frac{1}{2} R_S > 0$, whence $e_0 R_S \leq 2 e_0 R_{\mathrm{ram}}$.

For the trace-square bound, let $Q_{\mathrm{full}} := \sum_{u \in I} \gamma_u B_{f,u} B_{f,u}^*$. Since $B_{\mathrm{all},u} B_{\mathrm{all},u}^* = B_{f,u} P_{r,u} B_{f,u}^* \preceq B_{f,u} B_{f,u}^*$, we have $0 \preceq Q_{\mathrm{ram}} \preceq Q_{\mathrm{full}}$, and therefore $\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{ram}}^2) \leq \operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{ram}}^{1/2} Q_{\mathrm{full}} Q_{\mathrm{ram}}^{1/2}) = \operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{ram}} Q_{\mathrm{full}}) \leq \operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{full}}^2)$. Expanding $\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{full}}^2)$ gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{full}}^2) = \sum_{u \in I} \|\gamma_u B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 + \sum_{u \neq u'} \gamma_u \gamma_{u'} \|B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u'}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2.
$$

By the triangle inequality, $\|\gamma_u B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u}\|_{\mathrm{HS}} \leq \|I_{d_{u,S}}\|_{\mathrm{HS}} + \|X_u\|_{\mathrm{HS}} \leq (1 + a)\sqrt{d_{u,S}}$ when Hypothesis (2) holds termwise, and more generally by Cauchy–Schwarz across $u \in I$: $\sum_{u \in I} \|\gamma_u B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 = R_S + 2\sum_{u \in I}\operatorname{Tr}(X_u) + \sum_{u \in I} \|X_u\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq (1 + 2a + a^2) R_S = (1 + a)^2 R_S \leq (1 + a)^2 \sigma \frac{R_S^2}{D_*}$ using $D_* / \sigma \leq R_S$. Since $1 + \eta_0 \geq 0$, the off-diagonal sum is at most $\frac{1 + \eta_0}{D_*} \sum_{u \neq u'} d_{u,S} d_{u',S} \leq \frac{1 + \eta_0}{D_*} R_S^2$. Using $\frac{1}{D_*} \leq \frac{1 + \eta_1}{D_{\mathrm{all}}}$, we obtain

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{ram}}^2) \leq (1 + \eta_1)\bigl((1 + \eta_0) + (1 + a)^2 \sigma\bigr) \frac{R_S^2}{D_{\mathrm{all}}}.
$$

Meanwhile, let $P_{r,u}^\perp := I_{d_{u,S}} - P_{r,u}$ ($\operatorname{Tr} P_{r,u}^\perp = d_{u,S} - d_u$) and $t := \sqrt{\delta_{\mathrm{ram}}} \in [0, 1/2]$. By Cauchy–Schwarz as in Theorem 2.1, $\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{full}}) \geq (1 - a) R_S$, while

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{full}} - Q_{\mathrm{ram}}) = \sum_{u \in I} \operatorname{Tr}(P_{r,u}^\perp) + \sum_{u \in I} \langle X_u, P_{r,u}^\perp \rangle_{\mathrm{HS}} \leq t^2 R_S + \sqrt{e_0 R_S}\sqrt{t^2 R_S} = (t^2 + t a) R_S.
$$

Since $a \leq 1/40$ and $t \leq 1/2$, we have $1 - a - t^2 - t a \geq 1 - \frac{1}{40} - \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{80} = \frac{57}{80} > 0$, so $\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{ram}}) \geq (1 - a - t^2 - t a) R_S > 0$. It therefore suffices to verify two scalar polynomial inequalities:

1. For $0 \leq \sigma \leq 1/20$ and $\eta_0, \eta_1, a \leq \sigma / 2$ ($1 + \eta_0 \geq 0$, $1 + \eta_1 > 0$, $a \geq 0$), monotonicity in $\eta_0, \eta_1, a$ reduces $(1 + \eta_1)\bigl((1 + \eta_0) + (1 + a)^2 \sigma\bigr) \leq (1 + 5\sigma)(1 - a)^2$ to $\eta_0 = \eta_1 = a = \sigma / 2$, where exact expansion gives
   $$
   (1 + 5\sigma)\Bigl(1 - \frac{\sigma}{2}\Bigr)^2 - \Bigl(1 + \frac{\sigma}{2}\Bigr)\Bigl(1 + \frac{\sigma}{2} + \Bigl(1 + \frac{\sigma}{2}\Bigr)^2 \sigma\Bigr) = \sigma\Bigl(2 - \frac{13}{2}\sigma + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 - \frac{1}{8}\sigma^3\Bigr) \geq 0
   $$
   (strictly positive for $0 < \sigma \leq 1/20$, and $0$ at $\sigma = 0$).
2. For $(\sigma, a, t) \in [0, 1/20] \times [0, 1/40] \times [0, 1/2]$, exact polynomial expansion gives
   $$
   (1 + 5\sigma + 3t)(1 - a - t^2 - t a)^2 - (1 + 5\sigma)(1 - a)^2 = t(1 - a)\Bigl(3(1 - a) - 2(1 + 5\sigma + 3t)(t + a)\Bigr) + (1 + 5\sigma + 3t) t^2 (t + a)^2 \geq 0,
   $$
   since $3(1 - a) \geq 3 \cdot \frac{39}{40} = \frac{234}{80}$ whereas $2(1 + 5\sigma + 3t)(t + a) \leq 2\bigl(1 + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{2}\bigr)\bigl(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{40}\bigr) = \frac{231}{80} < \frac{234}{80}$.

Chaining (1) and (2) gives $(1 + \eta_1)\bigl((1 + \eta_0) + (1 + a)^2 \sigma\bigr) R_S^2 \leq (1 + 5\sigma + 3t)(\operatorname{Tr} Q_{\mathrm{ram}})^2$, completing the proof. *(Remark: Retaining the diagonal subtraction $\sum_{u \in \mathcal{S}} d_{u,S}^2$ on arbitrary subsets $\mathcal{S} \subseteq I$ yields the subset trace-square bound $U_{\mathcal{S}}$ and synthesis operator-norm bound $U_{\mathcal{S}}^{1/4}$ of [18, Theorem 3], whereas Theorem 2.2 specializes to the linear-constant full-family form $\eta = 5\sigma + 3\sqrt{\delta_{\mathrm{ram}}}$ consumed by `TopDensityNGeSixteen.lean` and [17, §3.2, (7)–(8)].)* $\square$

## 3. Single-Place $\mathrm{GL}$-Invariant Subspace Bridge, Antilinear Period Factorization, and Triple Cancellation

### 3.1 Hermitian $\mathcal{O}_K$-Lattices, Stuhler–Bost Slopes, and Map Heights

Let $K$ be a totally complex number field of degree $n_K = [K : \mathbb{Q}] = 2 r_2$ with ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_K$, discriminant $\Delta_K$, and archimedean places $\Sigma_\infty(K)$ ($|\Sigma_\infty(K)| = r_2$). Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $K$-vector space with $K$-basis $\mathbf{b} = (b_1, \ldots, b_r)$ ($r = \dim_K V$), and equip $V$ at each $v \in \Sigma_\infty(K)$ with an invertible matrix $X_v \in \mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb{C})$ defining the local Euclidean norm $\|x\|_v := \|X_v^{-1} v([x]_{\mathbf{b}})\|_2$ on $V_v := V \otimes_{K, v} \mathbb{C}$.

Under the Minkowski embedding $x \mapsto \bigl(\operatorname{Re}(X_v^{-1} v([x]_{\mathbf{b}})), \operatorname{Im}(X_v^{-1} v([x]_{\mathbf{b}}))\bigr)_{v \in \Sigma_\infty(K)} \in \mathbb{R}^{n_K r}$, every finitely generated $\mathcal{O}_K$-submodule $\mathcal{L} \subset V$ realizes a discrete Euclidean $\mathbb{Z}$-lattice of rank $\operatorname{rk}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathcal{L}) = n_K \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L})$, where $\operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L}) := \dim_K(K \cdot \mathcal{L})$. Following Stuhler [5], Grayson [7], and Bost [6], a nonzero $\mathcal{L}$ has normalized Arakelov slope

$$
\mu(\mathcal{L}) := -\frac{2}{n_K \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L})} \log \operatorname{covol}_{\mathbb{R}}(\mathcal{L}) + c_K = \frac{1}{n_K \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L})} \sum_{j = 1}^{n_K \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L})} \bigl(2 \nu_j(\mathcal{L}) + c_K\bigr),
$$

where $c_K := -\log 2 + \frac{1}{n_K} \log |\Delta_K|$ is the field-discriminant normalizing constant (ensuring $\mu(\mathcal{O}_K) = 0$ since $\operatorname{covol}_{\mathbb{R}}(\mathcal{O}_K) = 2^{-r_2} |\Delta_K|^{1/2}$) and $\nu_1(\mathcal{L}) \geq \cdots \geq \nu_{n_K \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L})}(\mathcal{L})$ are the repeated slopes of the canonical Harder–Narasimhan concavity polygon of $\mathcal{L}$. Set $\lambda_j(\mathcal{L}) := 2 \nu_j(\mathcal{L}) + c_K$, $\mu_{\max}(\mathcal{L}) := \lambda_1(\mathcal{L})$, and $\mu_{\min}(\mathcal{L}) := \lambda_{n_K \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L})}(\mathcal{L})$. For any real threshold $\Lambda \in \mathbb{R}$, the canonical Harder–Narasimhan step $\mathcal{L}_{\geq \Lambda} \subseteq \mathcal{L}$ (corresponding to threshold $\theta = (\Lambda - c_K)/2$ on $\nu_j$) is an $\mathcal{O}_K$-submodule satisfying three standard properties [5], [6], [7]:

1. $\#\{1 \leq j \leq n_K \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L}) : \lambda_j(\mathcal{L}) < \Lambda\} = n_K\bigl(\operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L}) - \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{L}_{\geq \Lambda})\bigr)$;
2. If $\mathcal{L}_{\geq \Lambda} \neq 0$, then $\mu_{\min}(\mathcal{L}_{\geq \Lambda}) \geq \Lambda$;
3. Universal maximality: every nonzero finitely generated $\mathcal{O}_K$-submodule $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathcal{L}$ with $\mu_{\min}(\mathcal{F}) \geq \Lambda$ satisfies $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathcal{L}_{\geq \Lambda}$.

For a nonzero $K$-linear map $\varphi : V \to W$ between normed spaces $(V, \mathbf{b}, X)$ and $(W, \mathbf{b}', X')$, define its mean archimedean operator height in normalized $2\nu + c_K$ units by

$$
h_{\mathrm{mean}}(\varphi) := \frac{2}{r_2} \sum_{v \in \Sigma_\infty(K)} \log \|\varphi\|_v, \qquad \|\varphi\|_v := \bigl\|X'^{-1}_v \cdot v(\operatorname{Mat}_{\mathbf{b}, \mathbf{b}'}(\varphi)) \cdot X_v\bigr\|_{\mathrm{op}},
$$

so Bost's mean slope inequality [6] states that every finitely generated $\mathcal{F} \subset V$ with $\varphi(\mathcal{F}) \neq 0$ satisfies $\mu_{\min}(\varphi(\mathcal{F})) \geq \mu_{\min}(\mathcal{F}) - h_{\mathrm{mean}}(\varphi)$.

### 3.2 Single-Place $\mathrm{GL}$-Invariant Rank Bridge and Continuous Layer-Cake Integration

**Lemma 3.1 (Single-Place $\mathrm{GL}$-Invariant Subspace Bridge).** *Let $W$ ($D_0 := \dim_K W$) and $\{V_i\}_{i \in I}$ ($r_i := \dim_K V_i$, $R := \sum_{i \in I} r_i > 0$) be $K$-vector spaces with bases $\mathbf{b}', \mathbf{b}_i$, let $B_i : V_i \to W$ be $K$-linear maps, and let $\mathcal{F}_i \subseteq V_i$ be $\mathcal{O}_K$-submodules. Fix **one** archimedean place $v \in \Sigma_\infty(K)$ and an auxiliary dimension $D_1 \geq 0$ ($D_{\mathrm{all}} := D_0 + D_1$). Suppose there exist matrices $B_{\mathrm{all}, i} \in \mathbb{C}^{D_{\mathrm{all}} \times r_i}$, invertible right factors $Y_i \in \mathrm{GL}_{r_i}(\mathbb{C})$ ($i \in I$), and an **$i$-independent** invertible left factor $Y' \in \mathrm{GL}_{D_0}(\mathbb{C})$ such that the top $D_0$ rows of $B_{\mathrm{all}, i}$ satisfy*

$$
P_{\mathrm{top}} B_{\mathrm{all}, i} = Y'^{-1} \cdot v\bigl(\operatorname{Mat}_{\mathbf{b}_i, \mathbf{b}'}(B_i)\bigr) \cdot Y_i \qquad (\forall i \in I).
$$

*If $\{B_{\mathrm{all}, i}\}_{i \in I}$ with weights $\gamma_i > 0$ satisfies Hypotheses (1)–(2) of Theorem 2.1 on $H = \mathbb{C}^{D_{\mathrm{all}}}$ and $\sum_{i \in I} (r_i - \operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{F}_i) \leq \varepsilon R$, then the algebraic sum $\mathcal{M} := \sum_{i \in I} B_i(\mathcal{F}_i) \subseteq W$ satisfies*

$$
D_0 - \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{M}) \leq (\eta + 4\varepsilon + 4\sqrt{e}) D_{\mathrm{all}}.
$$

*Proof.* For each $i \in I$, let $F_{i, v} \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{r_i}$ be the $\mathbb{C}$-span of $\{v([x]_{\mathbf{b}_i}) : x \in \mathcal{F}_i\}$. Because $v : K \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is a field embedding, linear independence over $K$ and over $\mathbb{C}$ coincide on $K$-rational vectors, so $\dim_{\mathbb{C}} F_{i, v} = \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{F}_i)$ and $\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \bigl(\sum_{i \in I} v(\operatorname{Mat}(B_i))(F_{i, v})\bigr) = \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{M})$. Set $N_i := Y_i^{-1}(F_{i, v}) \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{r_i}$; since $Y_i \in \mathrm{GL}_{r_i}(\mathbb{C})$, $\dim_{\mathbb{C}} N_i = \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{F}_i)$, so $\sum_{i \in I} (r_i - \dim_{\mathbb{C}} N_i) \leq \varepsilon R$.

Let $S := \sum_{i \in I} B_{\mathrm{all}, i}(N_i) \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{D_{\mathrm{all}}}$. Applying $P_{\mathrm{top}} : \mathbb{C}^{D_0} \oplus \mathbb{C}^{D_1} \to \mathbb{C}^{D_0}$ gives

$$
P_{\mathrm{top}}(S) = \sum_{i \in I} (P_{\mathrm{top}} B_{\mathrm{all}, i}) Y_i^{-1}(F_{i, v}) = Y'^{-1}\Bigl(\sum_{i \in I} v\bigl(\operatorname{Mat}(B_i)\bigr)(F_{i, v})\Bigr),
$$

where $Y'^{-1}$ factors outside the sum **because $Y'$ does not depend on $i$**. Since $Y' \in \mathrm{GL}_{D_0}(\mathbb{C})$, $\dim_{\mathbb{C}} P_{\mathrm{top}}(S) = \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{M})$. Because $\ker(P_{\mathrm{top}}) = \{0\} \oplus \mathbb{C}^{D_1}$ has dimension $D_1$, rank-nullity gives $\dim_{\mathbb{C}} S \leq \dim_{\mathbb{C}} P_{\mathrm{top}}(S) + D_1 = \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{M}) + D_1$. Subtracting from $\dim_{\mathbb{C}} S + \dim_{\mathbb{C}} S^\perp = D_0 + D_1$ and applying Theorem 2.1 to $S^\perp$ yields $D_0 - \operatorname{rk}_K(\mathcal{M}) \leq \dim_{\mathbb{C}} S^\perp \leq (\eta + 4\varepsilon + 4\sqrt{e}) D_{\mathrm{all}}$. $\square$

**Theorem 3.2 (Harder–Narasimhan Frame Slope Transfer; cf. [17, §3.2, eq. (9)]).** *Under the setup of Lemma 3.1, let $\mathcal{N}_i \subset V_i$ ($\operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{N}_i = r_i$) and $\mathcal{C} \subset W$ ($\operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{C} = D_0$) be full-rank finitely generated $\mathcal{O}_K$-submodules such that $B_i(\mathcal{N}_i) \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ and $h_{\mathrm{mean}}(B_i) \leq h_i$ whenever $B_i \neq 0$. Suppose $D_0 \geq (1 - \delta) D_{\mathrm{all}}$ with $0 \leq \delta < 1$, and suppose $s \in \mathbb{R}$, $u_{\mathrm{cap}} \geq 0$, and $H \geq 0$ satisfy:*

1. *Weighted mean source slope: $\sum_{i \in I} r_i\bigl(\mu(\mathcal{N}_i) - h_i\bigr) \geq s R$;*
2. *Source maximal-slope cap: $\mu_{\max}(\mathcal{N}_i) - h_i \leq s + u_{\mathrm{cap}}$ for every $i \in I$;*
3. *Target minimal-slope floor: $\mu_{\min}(\mathcal{C}) \geq s - H$.*

*Then (interpreting $u_{\mathrm{cap}} \log(1 + H / u_{\mathrm{cap}}) := 0$ when $u_{\mathrm{cap}} = 0$)*

$$
\mu(\mathcal{C}) \geq s - \frac{1}{1 - \delta}\Bigl(4 u_{\mathrm{cap}} \log\Bigl(1 + \frac{H}{u_{\mathrm{cap}}}\Bigr) + (\eta + 4\sqrt{e}) H\Bigr).
$$

*(Relation to companion paper 194 [17]: This single-embedding common-target-change argument and the resulting logarithmic Harder–Narasimhan penalty overlap directly with [17, §3.2, eq. (9)], while [17, §3.3] gives a further integrated refinement avoiding $u_{\mathrm{cap}}$ when integrated source-tail bounds are supplied directly. We include the short proof here so that Theorem 3.3's multi-embedding antilinear factorization, fractional-ideal clearance, and Proposition 3.4's sharpness counterexamples are self-contained.)*

*Proof.* For each $\tau \in [0, H]$, set $\mathcal{F}_i(\tau) := \mathcal{N}_{i, \geq s - \tau + h_i}$ and $\mathcal{M}(\tau) := \sum_{i \in I} B_i(\mathcal{F}_i(\tau)) \subseteq \mathcal{C}$. Across all $i \in I$ and $1 \leq j \leq n_K r_i$, the shifted slopes $\tilde{\lambda}_{i, j} := \lambda_j(\mathcal{N}_i) - h_i$ satisfy $s + u_{\mathrm{cap}} - \tilde{\lambda}_{i, j} \geq 0$ by (2) and $\sum_{i, j} (s + u_{\mathrm{cap}} - \tilde{\lambda}_{i, j}) \leq u_{\mathrm{cap}} n_K R$ by (1). By Markov's inequality, for every $\tau > 0$ (and $\tau = 0$ when $u_{\mathrm{cap}} > 0$),

$$
(u_{\mathrm{cap}} + \tau)\, n_K \sum_{i \in I}\bigl(r_i - \operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{F}_i(\tau)\bigr) = (u_{\mathrm{cap}} + \tau)\, \#\bigl\{(i, j) : \tilde{\lambda}_{i, j} < s - \tau\bigr\} \leq u_{\mathrm{cap}} n_K R,
$$

giving $\sum_{i \in I} (r_i - \operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{F}_i(\tau)) \leq \varepsilon(\tau) R$ with $\varepsilon(\tau) := \frac{u_{\mathrm{cap}}}{u_{\mathrm{cap}} + \tau}$ ($\varepsilon(\tau) = 0$ for $\tau > 0$ if $u_{\mathrm{cap}} = 0$).

Whenever $B_i(\mathcal{F}_i(\tau)) \neq 0$, Bost's slope inequality gives $\mu_{\min}(B_i(\mathcal{F}_i(\tau))) \geq \mu_{\min}(\mathcal{F}_i(\tau)) - h_i \geq s - \tau$. By universal maximality of the Harder–Narasimhan step $\mathcal{C}_{\geq s - \tau} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$, we have $B_i(\mathcal{F}_i(\tau)) \subseteq \mathcal{C}_{\geq s - \tau}$ for every $i$, so $\mathcal{M}(\tau) \subseteq \mathcal{C}_{\geq s - \tau}$ and $\operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{M}(\tau) \leq \operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{C}_{\geq s - \tau}$. Applying Lemma 3.1 at $\tau$ and using $D_{\mathrm{all}} \leq (1 - \delta)^{-1} D_0$ yields

$$
\#\bigl\{1 \leq q \leq n_K D_0 : \lambda_q(\mathcal{C}) < s - \tau\bigr\} = n_K\bigl(D_0 - \operatorname{rk}_K \mathcal{C}_{\geq s - \tau}\bigr) \leq n_K D_0\, w(\tau), \qquad w(\tau) := \frac{\eta + 4\varepsilon(\tau) + 4\sqrt{e}}{1 - \delta}.
$$

Since $\lambda_q(\mathcal{C}) \geq \mu_{\min}(\mathcal{C}) \geq s - H$ for all $1 \leq q \leq n_K D_0$, we have $s - \lambda_q(\mathcal{C}) \leq \max(s - \lambda_q(\mathcal{C}), 0) = \int_0^H \mathbf{1}_{\{\lambda_q(\mathcal{C}) < s - \tau\}}\, d\tau$. Summing over $q = 1, \ldots, n_K D_0$, integrating $w(\tau)$ over $[0, H]$, and dividing by $n_K D_0 > 0$ completes the proof. $\square$

### 3.3 Antilinear Period-to-Lattice Identification, Triple Cancellation, and Sharpness Controls

We now construct the multi-embedding period-to-matrix bridge assumed as external input in [17, §3.2] and [18, §1, eq. (6)]. Let $\mathcal{R} \subset V_{\mathcal{R}}$ ($\dim_K V_{\mathcal{R}} = D_0$) be the target $\mathcal{O}_K$-lattice and $\mathcal{C} := \mathcal{R}^\vee = \{f \in \operatorname{Hom}_K(V_{\mathcal{R}}, K) : f(\mathcal{R}) \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K\}$ its dual lattice with dualized archimedean norms, for which Stuhler–Bost duality [5], [6] gives $\mu(\mathcal{R}^\vee) = -\mu(\mathcal{R})$. For each word $u \in \mathcal{W}$, let $\mathrm{Pt}_u : V_u \times V_{\mathcal{R}} \to K$ be the $K$-bilinear iterated-theta period pairing, inducing $\mathrm{Bk}_u : V_u \to W = \operatorname{Hom}_K(V_{\mathcal{R}}, K)$ via $\mathrm{Bk}_u(\varphi)(F) = \mathrm{Pt}_u(\varphi, F)$. At each finite place $w$ of $K$ lying above the rational prime $p_w$, let $e_w$ and $f_w$ be the ramification index and residue degree ($N\mathfrak{p}_w = p_w^{f_w}$, $\log N\mathfrak{p}_w = f_w \log p_w$), let $v_w$ be the valuation normalized by $v_w(p_w) = 1$ ($v_{\mathfrak{p}_w} = e_w v_w$), and set $\ell_w := \frac{f_w \log p_w}{n_K}$ and $l_w := e_w \ell_w = \frac{e_w f_w \log p_w}{n_K}$.

**Hypothesis 3.1 (Local Period Divisibility, Target Bracket, and Antilinear Spectral Intertwine).** *Assume:*

1. *For each finite place $w$ of $K$, every $\varphi \in \mathcal{N}_{u,\mathrm{lat}}$ and $F \in \mathcal{R}$ satisfies $v_w(\mathrm{Pt}_u(\varphi, F)) \geq \mathrm{sg}_u(w)$, where $\mathrm{sg}_u(w) = \mathrm{ga}_u(w) + \mathrm{fo}(w) - \mathrm{err}_u(w)$ decomposes into a local gain $\mathrm{ga}_u(w)$, a word-independent twisted-place divisor $\mathrm{fo}(w)$, and a local type/error charge $\mathrm{err}_u(w)$ (supported on finitely many places). Define in normalized logarithmic slope units*
   $$
   \mathrm{Ga}_u := 2\sum_w l_w \mathrm{ga}_u(w), \qquad \mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}} := \sum_w l_w \mathrm{fo}(w), \qquad \mathrm{Fo} := 2\sum_w l_w \mathrm{fo}(w) = 2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}},
   $$
   *and $E_{\mathrm{loc}, u} := 2\sum_w l_w \mathrm{err}_u(w) + 2\sum_w \ell_w \{e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\}$, where $\{t\} := t - \lfloor t\rfloor \in [0, 1)$.*
2. *Writing $\overline{Z} := R_{\mathrm{ram}}^{-1} \sum_{u \in \mathcal{W}} d_u Z_u$ ($R_{\mathrm{ram}} := \sum_{u \in \mathcal{W}} d_u > 0$) for the rank-weighted word average, the target divisibility bracket gives $\mu(\mathcal{R}^\vee) + \overline{h} - \mathrm{Fo} \leq \overline{\mathrm{MAIN}} + \mathrm{Err}_{\mathrm{DIV}}$ with the same $\mathrm{Fo} = 2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}}$ (cf. [20, §4]).*
3. *At a fixed archimedean embedding $v_0 \in \Sigma_\infty(K)$, there exist a $D_0$-dimensional complex inner-product space $EE$, a complete complex inner-product space $\mathcal{H}$ ($\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathcal{H} = D_{\mathrm{all}} = D_0 + D_1$), a **$u$-independent antilinear isometric embedding** $s_{EE} : EE \to \mathcal{H}$ satisfying*
   $$
   s_{EE}(\alpha x + \beta y) = \overline{\alpha}\, s_{EE}(x) + \overline{\beta}\, s_{EE}(y), \qquad \bigl\langle s_{EE}(x),\, s_{EE}(y)\bigr\rangle_{\mathcal{H}} = \langle y, x\rangle_{EE} \qquad (\forall x, y \in EE,\, \alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}),
   $$
   *so that $\mathcal{H} = \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{top}} \oplus \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{aux}}$ with $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{top}} := s_{EE}(EE)$ ($\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{top}} = D_0$) and $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{aux}} := \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{top}}^\perp$ ($\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{aux}} = D_1$); together with a **$u$-independent** $\mathbb{C}$-linear isomorphism $j_E : V_{\mathcal{R}} \otimes_{K, v_0} \mathbb{C} \xrightarrow{\sim} EE$, word-dependent $\mathbb{C}$-linear isomorphisms $j_{N,u} : V_u \otimes_{K, v_0} \mathbb{C} \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathcal{M}_u$ ($\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathcal{M}_u = d_u$), nonzero scalars $\alpha_u \in \mathbb{C}^\times$, isometric embeddings $\iota_{\mathcal{C},u} : \mathcal{M}_u \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}_u$ ($\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathcal{C}_u = d_{u,S}$), and spectral Bessel operators $B_{f,u}^0 : \mathcal{C}_u \to \mathcal{H}$ such that the **$\mathbb{C}$-bilinear** Bessel pairing*
   $$
   \mathrm{besS}_u : \mathcal{M}_u \times EE \longrightarrow \mathbb{C}, \qquad \mathrm{besS}_u(\psi, w) := \bigl\langle s_{EE}(w),\, B_{f,u}^0 \iota_{\mathcal{C},u}(\psi)\bigr\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}
   $$
   *satisfies, for all $\varphi \in V_u$ and $F \in V_{\mathcal{R}}$,*
   $$
   v_0\bigl(\mathrm{Pt}_u(\varphi, F)\bigr) = \alpha_u^{-1}\, \mathrm{besS}_u\bigl(j_{N,u}(\varphi \otimes 1),\, j_E(F \otimes 1)\bigr).
   $$

**Theorem 3.3 (Antilinear Period-to-Lattice Factorization and Triple Cancellation).** *Assume Hypothesis 3.1, define the fractional $\mathcal{O}_K$-ideal $I_u := \prod_w \mathfrak{p}_w^{-\lfloor e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\rfloor}$, and set $\mathcal{N}_u := I_u \mathcal{N}_{u,\mathrm{lat}} \subset V_u$. Fix either conjugate embedding $v = \varepsilon \circ v_0$ with $\varepsilon \in \{\mathrm{id}, \mathrm{conj}\}$, and let $B_{f,u} := \varepsilon(B_{f,u}^0) \in \mathbb{C}^{D_{\mathrm{all}} \times d_{u,S}}$ and $V_{r,u} := \varepsilon(\iota_{\mathcal{C},u}) \in \mathbb{C}^{d_{u,S} \times d_u}$ in orthonormal coordinates extending $(s_{EE}(w_r))_{r=1}^{D_0}$. Suppose $\{B_{f,u}, V_{r,u}\}_{u \in \mathcal{W}}$ satisfies Hypotheses (1)–(5) of Theorem 2.2, $D_0 \geq (1 - \delta_{\mathrm{irr}}) D_{\mathrm{all}}$ ($0 \leq \delta_{\mathrm{irr}} < 1$), and $\{\mathcal{N}_u\}_{u \in \mathcal{W}}, \mathcal{R}^\vee$ satisfy Hypotheses (1)–(3) of Theorem 3.2 with $s = \overline{\mu}_{\mathrm{lat}} - \overline{h} + \overline{\mathrm{Ga}} + 2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}} - \overline{E}_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Then:*

1. **Ideal-shift clearance and logarithmic norm shift:** *$\mathrm{Bk}_u(\mathcal{N}_u) \subseteq \mathcal{R}^\vee$ for every $u \in \mathcal{W}$ with zero finite-index loss, and*
   $$
   \mu(\mathcal{N}_u) - \mu(\mathcal{N}_{u,\mathrm{lat}}) = \frac{2}{n_K} \sum_w \lfloor e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\rfloor \log N\mathfrak{p}_w = \frac{2}{n_K} \sum_w f_w \log p_w\, \lfloor e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\rfloor = \mathrm{Ga}_u + 2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}} - E_{\mathrm{loc}, u}.
   $$
2. **Antilinear matrix factorization and elimination of $\alpha_u, j_{N,u}, j_E$:** *The pairing $\mathrm{besS}_u$ is $\mathbb{C}$-bilinear in $(\psi, w)$. With $B_{\mathrm{all},u} := B_{f,u} V_{r,u}$, setting $Y' := \varepsilon(M_E^\top)^{-1} \in \mathrm{GL}_{D_0}(\mathbb{C})$ (where $M_E \in \mathrm{GL}_{D_0}(\mathbb{C})$ expresses the orthonormal basis $(w_r)$ of $EE$ in the $K$-basis $j_E(\mathbf{b}_E \otimes 1)$, independent of $u$) and $Y_u := \varepsilon(\alpha_u J_{N,u}^{-1}) \in \mathrm{GL}_{d_u}(\mathbb{C})$ (where $J_{N,u}$ expresses $j_{N,u}(\mathbf{b}_u \otimes 1)$ in the orthonormal basis of $\mathcal{M}_u$) yields*
   $$
   P_{\mathrm{top}} B_{\mathrm{all},u} = Y'^{-1}\, v\bigl(\operatorname{Mat}_{\mathbf{b}_u, \mathbf{b}_E^\vee}(\mathrm{Bk}_u)\bigr)\, Y_u \qquad (\forall u \in \mathcal{W}),
   $$
   *so Lemma 3.1, Theorem 2.2, and Theorem 3.2 give, completely independently of $|\alpha_u|$ and the condition numbers of $J_{N,u}$ and $M_E$,*
   $$
   \mu(\mathcal{R}^\vee) \geq s - \frac{1}{1 - \delta_{\mathrm{irr}}}\Bigl(4 u_{\mathrm{cap}} \log\Bigl(1 + \frac{H}{u_{\mathrm{cap}}}\Bigr) + \bigl(5\sigma + 3\sqrt{\delta_{\mathrm{ram}}} + 4\sqrt{2 e_0}\bigr) H\Bigr).
   $$
3. **Twisted-place cancellation:** *Adding this lower bound for $\mu(\mathcal{R}^\vee)$ to Hypothesis 3.1(2) cancels $\mu(\mathcal{R}^\vee)$, $\overline{h}$, and $+2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}}$ against $-\mathrm{Fo} = -2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}}$ identically, leaving*
   $$
   \overline{\mu}_{\mathrm{lat}} + \overline{\mathrm{Ga}} - \overline{E}_{\mathrm{loc}} \leq \overline{\mathrm{MAIN}} + \mathrm{Err}_{\mathrm{DIV}} + \frac{1}{1 - \delta_{\mathrm{irr}}}\Bigl(4 u_{\mathrm{cap}} \log\Bigl(1 + \frac{H}{u_{\mathrm{cap}}}\Bigr) + \bigl(5\sigma + 3\sqrt{\delta_{\mathrm{ram}}} + 4\sqrt{2 e_0}\bigr) H\Bigr).
   $$

*Proof.* (1) Every element of $\mathcal{N}_u = I_u \mathcal{N}_{u,\mathrm{lat}}$ is an $\mathcal{O}_K$-linear combination of elements $x \varphi$ with $\varphi \in \mathcal{N}_{u,\mathrm{lat}}$ and $v_{\mathfrak{p}_w}(x) \geq -\lfloor e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\rfloor$ for all $w$. For any $F \in \mathcal{R}$, Hypothesis 3.1(1) gives $v_{\mathfrak{p}_w}(\mathrm{Pt}_u(\varphi, F)) = e_w v_w(\mathrm{Pt}_u(\varphi, F)) \geq e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w) \geq \lfloor e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\rfloor$ since $v_{\mathfrak{p}_w}$ is integer-valued. Hence $v_{\mathfrak{p}_w}(\mathrm{Pt}_u(x \varphi, F)) = v_{\mathfrak{p}_w}(x) + v_{\mathfrak{p}_w}(\mathrm{Pt}_u(\varphi, F)) \geq 0$ at every finite place $w$, proving $\mathrm{Bk}_u(\mathcal{N}_u)(F) \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K$, i.e., $\mathrm{Bk}_u(\mathcal{N}_u) \subseteq \mathcal{R}^\vee$. Scaling an $\mathcal{O}_K$-lattice of rank $d_u$ by $I_u$ multiplies its $\mathbb{Z}$-covolume by $N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(I_u)^{d_u} = \prod_w (N\mathfrak{p}_w)^{-d_u \lfloor e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\rfloor}$ ($\log N\mathfrak{p}_w = f_w \log p_w$), shifting $\mu(\mathcal{N}_u)$ by
$$
-\frac{2}{n_K d_u} \log N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(I_u)^{d_u} = \frac{2}{n_K} \sum_w f_w \log p_w\, \lfloor e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\rfloor = 2 \sum_w \ell_w \bigl(e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w) - \{e_w \mathrm{sg}_u(w)\}\bigr) = \mathrm{Ga}_u + 2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}} - E_{\mathrm{loc}, u}.
$$

(2) Because $\langle \cdot, \cdot\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}$ is conjugate-linear in the first slot and $\mathbb{C}$-linear in the second slot while $s_{EE}$ is antilinear, for any $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$ we have $\mathrm{besS}_u(\alpha \psi, \beta w) = \langle \overline{\beta}\, s_{EE}(w),\, \alpha B_{f,u}^0 \iota_{\mathcal{C},u}(\psi)\rangle_{\mathcal{H}} = \overline{(\overline{\beta})}\, \alpha\, \mathrm{besS}_u(\psi, w) = \alpha \beta\, \mathrm{besS}_u(\psi, w)$, proving $\mathbb{C}$-bilinearity. *(Replacing the antilinear isometry $s_{EE}$ by a $\mathbb{C}$-linear map $L : EE \to \mathcal{H}$ would multiply the right-hand side by $\overline{v_0(\lambda)}$ and the left-hand side by $v_0(\lambda)$ under $F \mapsto \lambda F$ for $\lambda \in K \setminus \mathbb{R}$, forcing $\mathrm{Pt}_u \equiv 0$.)* Choose $K$-bases $\mathbf{b}_u = (b_{u,q})_{q=1}^{d_u}$ of $V_u$ and $\mathbf{b}_E = (e_p)_{p=1}^{D_0}$ of $V_{\mathcal{R}}$ (dual basis $\mathbf{b}_E^\vee$ of $W$), and orthonormal bases $\mathbf{o}_u = (\psi_{u,s})_{s=1}^{d_u}$ of $\mathcal{M}_u$ and $\mathbf{o}_E = (w_r)_{r=1}^{D_0}$ of $EE$. ## 4. Bruhat–Mirabolic Unfolding, Open-Cell Bessel Normalization, and $(r_S, k+1, r_S)$ Parahoric Mass Cancellation

We now establish the unfolding Loewner bound, conditional archimedean open-cell tie, and exact finite-place parahoric subgroup cancellation assumed in [18, §1, eqs. (2)–(3)] for the relative-trace Gram operator $K(u, u') := \sqrt{\gamma_u \gamma_{u'}}\, B_{f,u}^* B_{f,u'}$ ($\gamma_u := c_0(u)^{-1}$, $u \neq u'$ in $\mathcal{W}$). Let $E / F$ be an unramified CM quadratic extension of totally real $F$ of degree $[F : \mathbb{Q}] = f$, let $G$ be the unitary group of real signature $(r_S + k, r_S + 1)$ and ambient rank $n = r := 2r_S + k + 1 = k + m - 1$ ($m := 2(r_S + 1)$), and let $H_u, H_{u'} \subset G$ be the stabilized sub-unitary subgroups of rank $2r_S + k$ attached to distinct prime levels $T_u \neq T_{u'}$ satisfying $T_u \equiv T_{u'} \pmod{g_S^2}$, where $g_S = g_0 g_1$ is the product of two distinct rational primes $g_0, g_1 \geq (k b)^8$ split completely in $E$.

### 4.1 Four-Stratum Double-Coset Split and Mirabolic Gauss Price $P_g$

Decomposing $K(u, u') = K_0(u, u') + K_{\mathrm{pp}}(u, u')$ separates the principal-level projection $K_{\mathrm{pp}}(u, u')$, which vanishes for $u \neq u'$ by orthogonality across distinct prime levels $T_u \neq T_{u'}$. Unfolding $K_0(u, u')$ over rational double cosets $\tau \in \mathcal{T} := H_u(F) \backslash G(F) / H_{u'}(F)$ partitions $\mathcal{T}$ into four pairwise disjoint strata:

$$
\mathcal{T} = \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{id}} \sqcup \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{deg}} \sqcup \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{ng}} \sqcup \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{gen}},
$$

where $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{id}}$ is the maximal-intersection identity stratum (contributing $0$ when $u \neq u'$), $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{deg}}$ consists of degenerate orbits on which the local Bessel character integrates to $0$, $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{ng}} = \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{ng}, 1} \sqcup \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{ng}, \geq 2}$ consists of non-generic orbits split into corank $1$ and corank $\geq 2$, and $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{gen}} = \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{sc}} \sqcup \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{nsc}} \sqcup \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{gen}, \geq 2}$ consists of generic orbits split into the scalar main row $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{sc}}$ (open Bruhat cell), non-scalar corank-$1$ orbits $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{nsc}}$, and generic corank $\geq 2$ orbits $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{gen}, \geq 2}$.

At each of the $2f$ finite places $v \mid g_S$ of $F$ (residue cardinality $q_v \in \{g_0, g_1\}$), $G(F_v) \cong \mathrm{GL}_{2r_S + k + 1}(F_v)$ is split, and the local test operator incorporates a mirabolic Gauss projector on the $\mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ Levi factor modulo the upper unitriangular subgroup $U_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ ($|U_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})| = q_v^{k(k-1)/2}$).

**Proposition 4.1 (Mirabolic Gelfand–Graev Kernel, Loewner Domination, and Residue Price).** *For each split place $v \mid g_S$ with residue cardinality $q_v \geq 3$ and $k \geq 1$, set $G_v := \mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$, let $U_v := U_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ be the upper unitriangular subgroup, let $\psi_v : U_v \to \mathbb{C}^\times$ be $\psi_v(u) := \chi_v\bigl(\sum_{a=1}^{k-1} u_{a, a+1}\bigr)$ for a nontrivial additive character $\chi_v : \mathbb{F}_{q_v} \to \mathbb{C}^\times$, and define the mirabolic Gelfand–Graev convolution kernel*

$$
\gamma_v(x) := \frac{1}{|U_v|} \sum_{y \in G_v} \mathbf{1}_{U_v}(y x y^{-1})\, \psi_v(y x y^{-1}) \qquad (x \in G_v),
$$

*with identity value $\gamma_{1, v} := \gamma_v(I_k) = \frac{|G_v|}{|U_v|} = \prod_{i = 1}^{k} (q_v^i - 1)$ and $\Gamma_1 := \prod_{v \mid g_S} \gamma_{1, v}$. Let $G_{\mathrm{aux}} := \prod_{v \mid g_S} G_v$ act freely on the right on a finite coset-shift set $I$, and define $\Gamma = (\Gamma_{i, j}) \in M_I(\mathbb{C})$ by $\Gamma_{i, j} := \prod_{v \mid g_S} \gamma_v(x_{i, j, v})$ whenever $j = i \cdot x_{i, j}$ ($x_{i, j} \in G_{\mathrm{aux}}$) and $\Gamma_{i, j} := 0$ across distinct $G_{\mathrm{aux}}$-orbits. Then:*

1. **Loewner domination:** *$0 \preceq \Gamma \preceq P_g \Gamma_1 I_I$ in the Loewner order, where $P_g := \prod_{v \mid g_S} (1 + 3 / q_v)$; hence for any vectors $\{a_i\}_{i \in I}$ in a complex inner-product space $\mathcal{H}_1$, $\operatorname{Re} \sum_{i, j \in I} \Gamma_{i, j} \langle a_i, a_j\rangle_{\mathcal{H}_1} \leq P_g \Gamma_1 \sum_{i \in I} \|a_i\|_{\mathcal{H}_1}^2$.*
2. **Residue Euler-product price:** *For all $q_v \geq 3$ and $k \geq 1$,*
   $$
   \prod_{i = 1}^{k - 1} \bigl(1 - q_v^{-i}\bigr)^{-1} \leq \prod_{i = 1}^{k} \bigl(1 - q_v^{-i}\bigr)^{-1} \leq 1 + \frac{3}{q_v}, \qquad P_g = \Bigl(1 + \frac{3}{g_0}\Bigr)^f \Bigl(1 + \frac{3}{g_1}\Bigr)^f \leq \exp(6 b^{-7})
   $$
   *whenever $g_0, g_1 \geq (k b)^8$, $f \leq k$, $k \geq 1$, and $b \geq 1$.*

*Proof.* (1) Partitioning $I = \bigsqcup_{\mathcal{O}} \mathcal{O}$ into disjoint free right $G_{\mathrm{aux}}$-orbits expresses $\Gamma$ as a block-diagonal direct sum of Kronecker products $\bigotimes_{v \mid g_S} R_{\gamma_v}^{\mathrm{reg}}$ of right-regular convolution matrices $(R_{\gamma_v}^{\mathrm{reg}})_{g, h} := \gamma_v(g^{-1} h)$ on $\mathbb{C}[G_v]$, so it suffices to prove $0 \preceq R_{\gamma_v}^{\mathrm{reg}} \preceq (1 + 3/q_v)\gamma_{1, v} I_{\mathbb{C}[G_v]}$ for each $v \mid g_S$.

Every $u \in U_v$ satisfies $(u^{-1})_{a, a+1} = -u_{a, a+1}$, so $\psi_v(u^{-1}) = \overline{\psi_v(u)}$, $P_{\psi_v} := |U_v|^{-1} \psi_v \mathbf{1}_{U_v}$ satisfies $P_{\psi_v}^2 = P_{\psi_v} = P_{\psi_v}^*$, and $\gamma_v = \sum_{y \in G_v} \delta_y * P_{\psi_v} * \delta_{y^{-1}}$ is a real-valued central class function (conjugation by $s_{\pm} := \operatorname{diag}(1, -1, 1, \ldots) \in G_v$ negates the superdiagonal). In every irreducible unitary representation $(\pi, V)$ of $G_v$, $\pi(P_{\psi_v})$ is the orthogonal projection onto $V^{(U_v, \psi_v^{-1})}$, and $R_{\gamma_v}(\pi) = \sum_{y \in G_v} \pi(y)\pi(P_{\psi_v})\pi(y)^{-1} \succeq 0$ commutes with $\pi(G_v)$, acting by $\lambda_\pi I_V$ with $(\dim V)\lambda_\pi = |G_v|\dim V^{(U_v, \psi_v^{-1})}$. By Gelfand–Graev multiplicity one for $\mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ [13], [14], $\dim V^{(U_v, \psi_v^{-1})} = 1$ if $\pi$ is generic and $0$ otherwise, giving $\lambda_\pi = \gamma_{1, v}(|U_v|/\dim V)$ for generic $\pi$ and $0$ otherwise.

By induction on $m \geq 1$, every representation $(\tau, W)$ of the mirabolic subgroup $P_m(\mathbb{F}_{q_v}) = \mathrm{GL}_{m-1}(\mathbb{F}_{q_v}) \ltimes \mathbb{F}_{q_v}^{m-1}$ with $W^{(U_m(\mathbb{F}_{q_v}), \psi_m^{-1})} \neq 0$ satisfies $\dim W \geq \prod_{i=1}^{m-1}(q_v^i - 1)$ [14]: for $m \geq 2$, any nonzero $(U_m, \psi_m^{-1})$-eigenvector lies in the eigenspace $W_{\theta_0}$ of $\mathbb{F}_{q_v}^{m-1}$ for $\theta_0(b) := \chi_v(-b_{m-1}) \neq 1$, whose stabilizer in $\mathrm{GL}_{m-1}(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ is $P_{m-1}(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ ($\dim W_{\theta_0} \geq \prod_{i=1}^{m-2}(q_v^i - 1)$) while $\mathrm{GL}_{m-1}(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ permutes the $q_v^{m-1} - 1$ nontrivial characters transitively. Restricting generic $(\pi, V)$ to $P_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ yields $|U_v|/\dim V \leq \prod_{i=1}^{k-1}(1 - q_v^{-i})^{-1}$. Diagonalizing $P_g \Gamma_1 I_I - \Gamma \succeq 0$ proves the vector inequality.

(2) For $x = q_v^{-1} \in (0, 1/3]$, induction on $k \geq 1$ gives $\prod_{i = 1}^{k} (1 - x^i) \geq 1 - \frac{x(1 - x^k)}{1 - x} \geq 1 - \frac{3}{2}x > 0$, and $(1 - \frac{3}{2}x)(1 + 3x) = 1 + \frac{3}{2}x(1 - 3x) \geq 1$ implies $\prod_{i=1}^k (1 - x^i)^{-1} \leq 1 + 3x$ (sharp in $q_v \geq 3$: at $q_v = 2, k = 3$, $\prod_{i=1}^2(1 - 2^{-i})^{-1} = 8/3 > 1 + 3/2 = 5/2$). Finally, $g_0, g_1 \geq (kb)^8 \geq f b^7$ gives $P_g \leq \exp(3f/g_0 + 3f/g_1) \leq \exp(6 b^{-7})$. $\square$

### 4.2 Conditional Real-Place Open-Cell Scalar Cancellation

We record as a conditional normalization deduction how the archimedean open-cell orbital integral on $G(F_\tau) \cong U(r_S + k, r_S + 1)$ ($\tau \mid \infty$, $f$ real places of $F$) normalizes against Harish-Chandra's formal degree $\deg(\pi_\infty)$ [8] under Obligation O3 (supplying $q_{uv} = d_\sigma I_{\mathrm{oo}} = 1$ in [18, §1, eq. (3)]). While archimedean Bessel multiplicity one (Sun–Zhu [9]) characterizes continuous Bessel functionals up to scalar, fixing the orbital constant requires the explicit local amplitude and formal-degree inputs isolated in O3.

**Theorem 4.2 (Conditional Real-Place Open-Cell Scalar Cancellation).** *Write $r := r_S \geq 0$ throughout §4.2, $k \geq 1$, $n := 2r + k + 1$, and $\rho := 2v = \mathrm{rd}_E > 0$. Assume Obligation O3:*

   $$
   \frac{\prod_{\tau \mid \infty} \mathrm{MM}_v(\tau)}{\mathrm{covN}(u)\,\mathrm{covN}(u')} = \mathrm{IntOO}(X, u, u', 1), \qquad \mathrm{IntOO}(X, u, u', 1)\cdot \mathrm{degX}(X) = 1.
   $$

2. **Embedded middle Levi $\mathrm{IsH}_A$ and middle unipotent $\mathrm{IsUk}_A$:** $\mathrm{IsH}_A := \{\mathrm{hEmb}_{r_S}(h) : h \in \mathrm{GL}_k(A)\}$ and $\mathrm{IsUk}_A := \{\mathrm{hEmb}_{r_S}(u) : u \in U_k(A)\}$;
3. **Parahoric residue stabilizer $\mathrm{IsS}_A$ and unipotent-middle subgroup $\mathrm{IsNU}_A$:** $\mathrm{IsS}_A := \{n y : n \in \mathrm{IsN}_A,\, y \in \mathrm{IsH}_A\}$ and $\mathrm{IsNU}_A := \{n u : n \in \mathrm{IsN}_A,\, u \in \mathrm{IsUk}_A\}$;
4. **Pointwise frame stabilizer $\mathrm{IsInertia}_A$ (`IsInertiaInfiniteCyclotomic`):** $x \in \mathrm{IsS}_A$ satisfying $b_{11}(x) = I_{r_S}$, $b_{33}(x) = I_{r_S}$, $b_{12}(x)_{a, \star} = 0$ for all $a \in \mathrm{Fin}(r_S)$, and $b_{23}(x)_{\star, b} = 0$ for all $b \in \mathrm{Fin}(r_S)$.

*(Crucial distinction from the naive vector/covector stabilizer: In ambient dimension $n = 2r_S + k + 1$, the subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$ fixing only the first basis vector $e_1$ and the last dual covector $e_n^\vee$ has middle block $\mathrm{GL}_{2r_S + k - 1}(\mathbb{F}_q)$ of size $2r_S + k - 1 \neq k$. Already at $(r_S, k, q) = (0, 2, 3)$ inside $\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb{F}_3)$ of order $11{,}232$, the actual subgroup $\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_3} = \operatorname{diag}(\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_3), 1)$ has order $48$ and $\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_3} = \operatorname{diag}(U_2(\mathbb{F}_3), 1)$ has order $3$, giving index ratio $48/3 = 16$, whereas the first-vector/last-covector stabilizer has middle block $\mathrm{GL}_1(\mathbb{F}_3)$, order $54$, and unipotent-middle subgroup of order $27$, giving the incompatible ratio $54/27 = 2$.)*

**Lemma 4.3 (Exact Block Subgroup Orders, Principal Congruence Kernel vs. Residue Preimages, and `Levels` Indices).**

1. **Subgroup structure and explicit block characterizations:** *Over any commutative ring $A$, $\mathrm{IsN}_A, \mathrm{IsH}_A, \mathrm{IsUk}_A, \mathrm{IsS}_A, \mathrm{IsNU}_A$, and $\mathrm{IsInertia}_A$ are subgroups of $\mathrm{GL}_n(A)$, with internal semidirect-product decompositions $\mathrm{IsS}_A = \mathrm{IsN}_A \rtimes \mathrm{IsH}_A$ and $\mathrm{IsNU}_A = \mathrm{IsN}_A \rtimes \mathrm{IsUk}_A$. Explicitly, $x \in \mathrm{IsS}_A$ (resp. $\mathrm{IsNU}_A$) if and only if $b_{11}(x), b_{33}(x) \in U_{r_S}(A)$, $b_{21}(x) = b_{31}(x) = b_{32}(x) = 0$, and $b_{22}(x) = \operatorname{diag}(h, 1)$ for some $h \in \mathrm{GL}_k(A)$ (resp. $h \in U_k(A)$); and for $x \in \mathrm{GL}_n(A)$, $x \in \mathrm{IsInertia}_A$ if and only if $x$ fixes every basis vector $e_b$ for $b \in \mathrm{Fin}(r_S) \sqcup \{\star\}$ (left block and marked coordinate) and every dual covector $e_a^\vee$ for $a \in \{\star\} \sqcup \mathrm{Fin}(r_S)$ (marked coordinate and right block).*
2. **Exact $\mathbb{F}_q$-orders and index ratios:** *Over a finite field $\mathbb{F}_q$, setting $\gamma_q := |\mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_q)| / |U_k(\mathbb{F}_q)| = \prod_{i = 1}^{k} (q^i - 1)$ and $|U_k(\mathbb{F}_q)| = q^{k(k-1)/2}$,*
   $$
   \begin{gathered}
   |\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_q}| = q^{r_S(r_S - 1) + 2 r_S(k + 1) + r_S^2} = q^{r_S(2 r_S + 2 k + 1)}, \\
   |\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_q}| = |\mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_q)| \cdot |\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_q}|, \qquad |\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_q}| = |U_k(\mathbb{F}_q)| \cdot |\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_q}|, \qquad \frac{|\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_q}|}{|\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_q}|} = \gamma_q, \\
   |\mathrm{IsInertia}_{\mathbb{F}_q}| = |\mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_q)| \cdot q^{r_S(2k + r_S)}, \qquad \bigl[\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_q} : \mathrm{IsInertia}_{\mathbb{F}_q}\bigr] = q^{r_S(r_S + 1)}.
   \end{gathered}
   $$
3. **Principal congruence kernel vs. local-ring residue preimages:** *Let $\mathcal{O}_v$ be a local commutative ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}_v$ and finite residue field $\mathbb{F}_{q_v} = \mathcal{O}_v / \mathfrak{m}_v$, and let $\mathrm{red}_v : K_v := \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_v) \to \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ and $\mathrm{red}_{H, v} : K_{H, v} := \mathrm{GL}_k(\mathcal{O}_v) \to \mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ be entrywise reduction modulo $\mathfrak{m}_v$. Then $\mathrm{red}_v$ and $\mathrm{red}_{H, v}$ are surjective homomorphisms with **principal congruence kernel** $K_v(1) := \ker(\mathrm{red}_v) = \{x \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_v) : x \equiv I_n \pmod{\mathfrak{m}_v}\}$. Defining the residue-preimage level subgroups*
   $$
   K_{\mathrm{pp}, v} := \mathrm{red}_v^{-1}\bigl(\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}\bigr), \quad K_{\mathrm{nat}, v} := \mathrm{red}_v^{-1}\bigl(\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}\bigr), \quad K_{1, v} := \mathrm{red}_v^{-1}\bigl(\mathrm{IsInertia}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}\bigr), \quad J_{H, v} := \mathrm{red}_{H, v}^{-1}\bigl(U_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})\bigr),
   $$
   *(`Levels.Kpp`, `Levels.Knat`, `FrameLevel.K1`, and `Levels.JH`), we have $K_v(1) \trianglelefteq K_{\mathrm{pp}, v} \leq K_{\mathrm{nat}, v} \leq K_v$ and $K_v(1) \trianglelefteq K_{1, v} \leq K_{\mathrm{nat}, v}$ with $[K_{\mathrm{nat}, v} : K_{1, v}] = q_v^{r_S(r_S + 1)}$, product decompositions $K_{\mathrm{nat}, v} = \mathrm{IsS}_{\mathcal{O}_v} \cdot K_v(1)$ and $K_{\mathrm{pp}, v} = \mathrm{IsN}_{\mathcal{O}_v} \cdot \mathrm{hEmb}_{r_S}(J_{H, v}) \cdot K_v(1)$, and exact finite indices*
   $$
   \begin{gathered}
   [K_v : K_{\mathrm{pp}, v}] = \frac{|\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})|}{|\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}|\cdot |U_k(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})|}, \qquad [K_v : K_{\mathrm{nat}, v}] = \frac{|\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})|}{|\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}|}, \\
   [K_{\mathrm{nat}, v} : K_{\mathrm{pp}, v}] = [K_{H, v} : J_{H, v}] = \gamma_{q_v}, \qquad [K_{\mathrm{pp}, v} : K_v(1)] = |\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}|.
   \end{gathered}
   $$
   *(In particular, $K_{\mathrm{pp}, v}$ is the inverse image of the unipotent-middle subgroup $\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}$ rather than the principal congruence kernel $K_v(1)$: e.g. for $\mathcal{O}_v = \mathbb{Z}/9\mathbb{Z}$ at $(r_S, k) = (0, 2)$, $|K_v(1)| = 3^9 = 19{,}683$, $|K_{\mathrm{pp}, v}| = 3 \cdot 19{,}683 = 59{,}049$, and $|K_{\mathrm{nat}, v}| = 48 \cdot 19{,}683 = 944{,}784$.)*

*Proof.* (1) Every $n \in \mathrm{IsN}_A$ is upper unitriangular ($\det n = 1$), and block multiplication preserves $(U_{r_S}, I_{k+1}, U_{r_S})$ on the block diagonal and zeros below, so $\mathrm{IsN}_A \leq \mathrm{GL}_n(A)$. Conjugating $n \in \mathrm{IsN}_A$ by $y = \mathrm{hEmb}_{r_S}(h)$ ($h \in \mathrm{GL}_k(A)$) keeps $b_{11}(n), b_{13}(n), b_{33}(n)$ unchanged and replaces $(b_{12}(n), b_{23}(n))$ by $(b_{12}(n)\operatorname{diag}(h^{-1}, 1),\, \operatorname{diag}(h, 1)b_{23}(n))$, so $\mathrm{IsH}_A$ normalizes $\mathrm{IsN}_A$ with $\mathrm{IsN}_A \cap \mathrm{IsH}_A = \{I_n\}$. Multiplying $n \cdot \mathrm{hEmb}_{r_S}(h)$ leaves $b_{11}(n), b_{33}(n)$ unchanged and sets $b_{22} = \operatorname{diag}(h, 1)$ while $b_{12}(n)\operatorname{diag}(h, 1)$ ranges bijectively over $M_{r_S \times (k+1)}(A)$, yielding the explicit block characterization of $\mathrm{IsS}_A$ and $\mathrm{IsNU}_A$. For $x \in \mathrm{GL}_n(A)$, fixing the first $r_S$ columns and column $\star$ together with row $\star$ and the last $r_S$ rows forces $b_{11}=I_{r_S}, b_{21}=0, b_{31}=0$, column $\star$ of $(b_{12}, b_{22}, b_{32})^\top$ equal to $(0, e_\star, 0)^\top$, row $\star$ of $(b_{21}, b_{22}, b_{23})$ equal to $(0, e_\star^\top, 0)$, and $(b_{31}, b_{32}, b_{33}) = (0, 0, I_{r_S})$, so $\det x = \det h \in A^\times$ and $x \in \mathrm{IsInertia}_A$.

(2) Over $\mathbb{F}_q$, an element of $\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_q}$ has $\frac{r_S(r_S-1)}{2}$ free entries in $b_{11}$, $\frac{r_S(r_S-1)}{2}$ in $b_{33}$, $r_S(k+1)$ in $b_{12}$, $r_S^2$ in $b_{13}$, and $(k+1)r_S$ in $b_{23}$, totaling $r_S(r_S - 1) + 2r_S(k + 1) + r_S^2 = r_S(2r_S + 2k + 1)$ free $\mathbb{F}_q$-coordinates. The semidirect products give $|\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_q}| = |\mathrm{GL}_k(\mathbb{F}_q)|\cdot|\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_q}|$ and $|\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_q}| = |U_k(\mathbb{F}_q)|\cdot|\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_q}|$. Imposing the $\mathrm{IsInertia}_{\mathbb{F}_q}$ conditions sets $r_S(r_S - 1) + 2r_S = r_S(r_S + 1)$ free unipotent entries to $0$ (leaving $r_S k + r_S^2 + k r_S = r_S(2k + r_S)$ entries), giving $[\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_q} : \mathrm{IsInertia}_{\mathbb{F}_q}] = q^{r_S(r_S + 1)}$.

(3) Over a local ring $\mathcal{O}_v$, $d \in \mathcal{O}_v$ is a unit if and only if $\bar{d} \neq 0$ in $\mathbb{F}_{q_v}$. Lifting entries of $\bar{g} \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$ arbitrarily to $g \in M_n(\mathcal{O}_v)$ gives $\overline{\det g} = \det \bar{g} \in \mathbb{F}_{q_v}^\times$, so $g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_v)$ and $\mathrm{red}_v$ is surjective (similarly for $\mathrm{red}_{H, v}$). Lifting the free entries of $\bar{n} \in \mathrm{IsN}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}$ while keeping $1$s and $0$s fixed yields $n \in \mathrm{IsN}_{\mathcal{O}_v}$ with $\mathrm{red}_v(n) = \bar{n}$; combined with surjectivity of $\mathrm{red}_{H, v}$, every element of $\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}$ lifts to $\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathcal{O}_v}$ and every element of $\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_v}}$ lifts to $\mathrm{IsN}_{\mathcal{O}_v}\cdot \mathrm{hEmb}_{r_S}(J_{H, v})$, giving the stated product decompositions with $K_v(1) = \ker(\mathrm{red}_v)$. The index identities follow immediately from the isomorphism theorem $K_v / K_v(1) \cong \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_{q_v})$. $\square$

**Theorem 4.4 (`TypedArithmetic` and Open-Cell Mass Cancellation).** *At each split auxiliary place $p \in \mathcal{P} = \{v \mid g_S\}$, equip $G(F_p) \cong \mathrm{GL}_n(F_p)$ ($n = 2r_S + k + 1$), $H_{\mathrm{mid}}(F_p) \cong \mathrm{GL}_k(F_p)$, and the unipotent radical $N_{o, p} := \mathrm{IsN}_{\mathcal{O}_p}$ with Haar measures assigning positive volumes $\mu(K_p), \mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}, p}), \mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}), \mu(K_{H, p}), \mu(J_{H, p}), \mu(N_{o, p}) > 0$ to the compact-open subgroups of Lemma 4.3(3), so that $\mu(K_p) = [K_p : K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}]\,\mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}) = [K_p : K_{\mathrm{nat}, p}]\,\mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}, p})$ and $\mu(K_{H, p}) = \gamma_{q_p} \mu(J_{H, p})$. Then:*

1. **Local typed-level identities (`TypedArithmetic`):** *Writing $c_{G, p} := |\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_{q_p})|$, $c_{S, p} := |\mathrm{IsS}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_p}}|$, and $\gamma_p := \gamma_{q_p}$, every $p \in \mathcal{P}$ satisfies*
   $$
   \begin{gathered}
   \frac{\gamma_p c_{S, p}}{c_{G, p}} = \frac{\gamma_p^2}{[K_p : K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}]}, \qquad \frac{\mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}, p})}{\mu(K_p)} = \frac{c_{S, p}}{c_{G, p}}, \qquad \frac{\mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}) / \mu(J_{H, p})^2}{\mu(K_p) / \mu(K_{H, p})^2} = \frac{\gamma_p^2}{[K_p : K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}]}, \\
   \gamma_p \cdot \frac{\mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}, p})}{\bigl(\mu(N_{o, p}) \mu(K_{H, p})\bigr)^2} = \frac{\mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}) / \mu(J_{H, p})^2}{\mu(N_{o, p})^2}.

   $$
2. **Global open-cell mass cancellation (`S3.MainTheorem`, `MassDataInst.lean:142–157`):** *Let $\mu(K_o), \mu(S_o), \mu(S'_o), \mathrm{unitVol}_o > 0$ denote the **ordinary finite-place** level volumes away from the auxiliary comparison primes $\mathcal{P}$ (`μKo`, `μSo`, `μSo'`, `volPP`/ordinary product in `MassDataInst.lean:142–157`), and define the global finite-adele level products $\mu(K) := \mu(K_o)\prod_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \mu(K_p)$, $\mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}}) := \mu(K_o)\prod_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}, p})$, $\mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}}) := \mu(K_o)\prod_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}, p})$, $\mu(S_{\mathrm{pp}}) := \mu(S_o)\prod_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \mu(S_{\mathrm{pp}, p})$, and $\mu(S'_{\mathrm{pp}}) := \mu(S'_o)\prod_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \mu(S'_{\mathrm{pp}, p})$.    $$
   \Gamma_1 \Sigma_{\mathrm{open}} \leq (1 + \delta) e_1 \frac{d_{u,S}\, d_{u',S}}{D_*}, \qquad P_g \Gamma_1 \Sigma_{\mathrm{open}} \leq P_g (1 + \delta) e_1 \frac{d_{u,S}\, d_{u',S}}{D_*}.

*Proof.* (1) By Lemma 4.3(2)–(3), $c_{S, p} = \gamma_p |\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_p}}|$ and $[K_p : K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}] = c_{G, p} / |\mathrm{IsNU}_{\mathbb{F}_{q_p}}|$, so $\frac{\gamma_p c_{S, p}}{c_{G, p}} = \frac{\gamma_p^2}{[K_p : K_{\mathrm{pp}, p}]}$ and $\frac{\mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}, p})}{\mu(K_p)} = [K_p : K_{\mathrm{nat}, p}]^{-1} = \frac{c_{S, p}}{c_{G, p}}$. (2) Substituting $\mu(K_{\mathrm{nat}, p}) = \gamma_p \mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}, p})$, $\mu(S_p) = \gamma_p \mu(S_{\mathrm{pp}, p})$, and $\mu(S'_p) = \gamma_p \mu(S'_{\mathrm{pp}, p})$ into $\mathrm{MM}$ cancels one factor of $\gamma_p$ at each $p \in \mathcal{P}$, leaving $\frac{\mathrm{MM}}{\mathrm{unitVol}\cdot\mu(K)} = \frac{\mu(K_{\mathrm{pp}})}{\Gamma_1 \mu(S_{\mathrm{pp}})\mu(S'_{\mathrm{pp}})}$. ## 5. Singular-Gram Local Densities, Complete `StatementTB` Tower Recursion, and `StatementPB.PairBound`

### 5.1 Closed-Form Primitive Densities and Comparison with Paper 196 [18] and Unrestricted Lattice Counts

When counting integral paired flags $(F_0, F')$ inside the self-dual Hermitian $\mathcal{O}_E$-lattice $\Lambda_0 \subset V$ of rank $r = k + m - 1$ ($m := 2(r_S + 1) = \dim_E U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')$), the local paired-flag Gram matrix $\mathrm{Ga}_+(Z)$ can degenerate modulo finite primes $\mathfrak{p}$ of $F$ even when $\mathrm{Ga}_+(Z)$ is globally nondegenerate. Companion paper 196 [18, Theorem 1, eqs. (1), (8)] independently proves the exact primitive depth-$h$ count (under the identical conjugate-linear-first Gram convention $(X^* J X)_{a,b} = h(x_a, x_b)$) and the two-sided product inequality $-15f\,2^{-(k-m)} \leq \log\prod_{\mathfrak{p}}(\mathrm{dens}_{\mathfrak{p}}/\alpha_{\mathrm{ref}}) \leq 12f\,2^{-(k-m)}$; we include the concise Stiefel-frame proof here to keep §5.2–§5.4 self-contained and to contrast primitive Hensel stabilization with non-primitive overlattice strata.

**Theorem 5.1 (Primitive Local Representation Density at Arbitrary Mod-$\mathfrak{p}$ Corank; cf. [1, `StandardLattice.lean`], [18, Theorem 1]).** *Let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a finite place of $F$ unramified in $E / F$ with residue cardinality $q_{\mathfrak{p}} = N\mathfrak{p} \geq 2$, uniformizer $\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}} \in \mathcal{O}_{F, \mathfrak{p}}$, and splitting sign $\eta_{\mathfrak{p}} = +1$ (split) or $\eta_{\mathfrak{p}} = -1$ (inert). Let $(\Lambda_0, h)$ be a self-dual Hermitian $\mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}}$-lattice of rank $r = k + m - 1$ with $m = 2(r_S + 1)$ and $k \geq m + 3$ (with $h$ conjugate-linear in its first argument). For any integral Hermitian matrix $G_+ \in M_m(\mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}})$ diagonalizable under $\mathrm{GL}_m(\mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}})$ as $A^* \operatorname{diag}(a_1, \ldots, a_m) A$ ($a_i \in \mathcal{O}_{F, \mathfrak{p}}$), let $c_{\mathfrak{p}} := \#\{1 \leq i \leq m : \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}} \mid a_i\} = m - \operatorname{rk}(G_+ \bmod \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}) \in \{0, \ldots, m\}$ be its mod-$\mathfrak{p}$ corank, and set $\alpha_{\mathrm{std}}(\mathfrak{p}, j) := \prod_{i = 1}^{j} (1 - \eta_{\mathfrak{p}}^i q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-i})$. Then for every $\ell \geq 1$, the normalized count of primitive $m$-frames $v = (v_1, \ldots, v_m) \in (\Lambda_0 / \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^\ell \Lambda_0)^m$ ($\operatorname{rk}(v \bmod \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}) = m$) with Gram matrix $G_+ \bmod \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^\ell$ equals*

$$
\mathrm{dens}_{\mathfrak{p}}(G_+) := q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-\ell m(2r - m)} \#\bigl\{v \in (\Lambda_0 / \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^\ell \Lambda_0)^m : \bar{v}\text{ primitive},\ h(v_a, v_b) \equiv (G_+)_{a,b} \pmod{\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^\ell}\bigr\} = \prod_{i = k - c_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r} \bigl(1 - \eta_{\mathfrak{p}}^i q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-i}\bigr) = \frac{\alpha_{\mathrm{std}}(\mathfrak{p}, r)}{\alpha_{\mathrm{std}}(\mathfrak{p}, k - 1 - c_{\mathfrak{p}})}.
$$

*Proof.* Because $\Lambda_0$ is unimodular and $\bar{v}$ has full rank $m$, the differential $\delta v \mapsto \bigl(h(\delta v_a, v_b) + h(v_a, \delta v_b)\bigr)_{a,b}$ of the Gram map $\Psi : \Lambda_0^m \to \operatorname{Herm}_m(\mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}})$ (between free $\mathcal{O}_{F,\mathfrak{p}}$-modules of ranks $2rm$ and $m^2$) is surjective modulo $\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}$, so Hensel's lemma lifts every depth-$1$ primitive solution to $\Lambda_0 / \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^\ell \Lambda_0$ with fiber cardinality $q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{(\ell - 1)(2rm - m^2)}$. Reordering indices so $\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}} \nmid a_1, \ldots, a_{m - c_{\mathfrak{p}}}$ and $\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}} \mid a_{m - c_{\mathfrak{p}} + 1}, \ldots, a_m$, right-multiplication of the frame by $A^{-1} \in \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}})$ reduces $G_+$ to $\operatorname{diag}(a_1, \ldots, a_m)$ (since $h(\sum_c v_c M_{ca}, \sum_d v_d M_{db}) = (M^* G_+ M)_{a,b}$); since $E_{\mathfrak{p}} / F_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is unramified, the unit norm $N_{E_{\mathfrak{p}} / F_{\mathfrak{p}}} : \mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}}^\times \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{F, \mathfrak{p}}^\times$ is surjective (both at inert places by Hensel lifting of the surjective finite-field norm $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}^\times \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{F}_q^\times$, and at split places on $\mathcal{O}_{F, \mathfrak{p}}^\times \times \mathcal{O}_{F, \mathfrak{p}}^\times$), so choosing $u_i \in \mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}}^\times$ with $\bar{u}_i u_i = a_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq m - c_{\mathfrak{p}}$) and right-multiplying further by $\operatorname{diag}(u_1^{-1}, \ldots, u_{m - c_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{-1}, 1, \ldots, 1) \in \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}})$ reduces the Gram matrix modulo $\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}$ to $\operatorname{diag}(I_{m - c_{\mathfrak{p}}}, 0_{c_{\mathfrak{p}}})$. At a split place ($\eta_{\mathfrak{p}} = +1$, $q = q_{\mathfrak{p}}$), pairs $(X, Y) \in M_{m \times r}(\mathbb{F}_q)^2$ of rank $m$ solving $X \Phi Y^\top = \operatorname{diag}(I_{m - c_{\mathfrak{p}}}, 0_{c_{\mathfrak{p}}})$ number $\bigl(\prod_{i=0}^{m-1}(q^r - q^i)\bigr) q^{(m - c_{\mathfrak{p}})(r - m)} \prod_{i=0}^{c_{\mathfrak{p}}-1}(q^{r-m} - q^i)$; at an inert place ($\eta_{\mathfrak{p}} = -1$), Witt's theorem over $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$ counts $(m - c_{\mathfrak{p}})$-orthonormal frames extended by totally isotropic $c_{\mathfrak{p}}$-frames inside the $(r - m + c_{\mathfrak{p}})$-dimensional orthogonal complement ($2c_{\mathfrak{p}} \leq r - m + c_{\mathfrak{p}}$ since $k \geq m + 3$). Dividing by $q^{2rm - m^2}$ in both cases yields $\prod_{j = k - c_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r} (1 - \eta_{\mathfrak{p}}^j q^{-j})$. $\square$

*Primitive Hensel stabilization vs. unrestricted representation counts.* In both `StandardLattice.lean` [1] and [18, Theorem 1], the exponent $m(2r - m) = 2rm - m^2 = \dim_{F_{\mathfrak{p}}} M_{r, m}(E_{\mathfrak{p}}) - \dim_{F_{\mathfrak{p}}} \operatorname{Herm}_m(E_{\mathfrak{p}})$ is the $F_{\mathfrak{p}}$-dimension of the smooth Gram fibers (having codimension $m^2$ inside the $2rm$-dimensional frame space; cf. the classical local Hermitian representation theory of Braun [10], Hironaka [15], Gan–Yu [21], and Gan–Hanke–Yu [12], without asserting a convention-by-convention scalar match with external conventions not reproduced here). If one omits the primitivity condition $\operatorname{rk}(X \bmod \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}) = m$ and counts all $X \in (\Lambda_0 / \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^\ell \Lambda_0)^m$ solving $X^* J X \equiv G_+ \pmod{\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^\ell}$ as $\ell \to \infty$, factoring any full-rank $\mathcal{O}_{E,\mathfrak{p}}$-frame $X = X_{\mathrm{prim}} C$ into a primitive frame $X_{\mathrm{prim}}$ and an integral overlattice transition $C \in M_m(\mathcal{O}_{E,\mathfrak{p}}) \cap \mathrm{GL}_m(E_{\mathfrak{p}})$ shows that non-primitive frames ($\operatorname{rk}(X \bmod \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}) < m$) **may** contribute additional positive strata depending on the full elementary-divisor valuations $\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}}(a_i)$ rather than $c_{\mathfrak{p}}$ alone. Notably, existence of non-primitive frames requires more than $c_{\mathfrak{p}} \geq 1$: at an inert unramified place, $G_+ = \operatorname{diag}(1, \ldots, 1, \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}})$ has $c_{\mathfrak{p}} = 1$ and $\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}}(\det G_+) = 1$, whereas any non-primitive $X$ has every $m \times m$ minor divisible by $\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}$ and hence $\varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^2 \mid \det(X^* J X)$ by Cauchy–Binet, so zero non-primitive representations exist; by contrast, $G_+ = \operatorname{diag}(1, \ldots, 1, \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^2)$ admits non-primitive frames via $C = \operatorname{diag}(1, \ldots, 1, \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}})$. Restricting to **primitive** $m$-frames isolates the smooth open locus of $\Psi$, so $\mathrm{dens}_{\mathfrak{p}}(G_+)$ stabilizes at $\ell = 1$ even when $\det G_+ = 0$ and depends on $(a_1, \ldots, a_m)$ solely through $c_{\mathfrak{p}}$.

   - *(`LatticeSums`)* *Every finite $L \subset \Lambda^\vee_i \setminus \{0\}$ ($1 \leq i \leq r_S + 1$) satisfies $\sum_{l \in L} \operatorname{maj}(\mathrm{sz}_D(i), l) \leq \varepsilon_P$, and for $(i, s) \neq (r_S + 1, 1)$, every finite $L \subset \Lambda'_{i - s + 1, s} \setminus \{0\}$ satisfies $\sum_{l \in L} \operatorname{maj}(\mathrm{sz}_L(i - s + 1, s), l) \leq \varepsilon_P$.*
5. **Base limit, top cutoff, and leading row domination (`EstE`, `Cutoff`, `LeadingDomination`):** *For every $w \in \mathcal{W}_b(N_{\mathrm{top}})$, $\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} \frac{\operatorname{Re}\,\mathrm{gram}(\varepsilon, 0, w, [], [])}{\mathrm{wt}(w)} = j_n$; for every finite $Y \subset \{p : \mathrm{IsPt}(p, r_S + 1)\}$, there exists $\varepsilon_Y > 0$ such that $0 < \varepsilon < \varepsilon_Y$ and $\mathcal{K}_{r_S + 1}(p, q) \neq 0$ ($p, q \in Y$) imply $\mathrm{gram}(\varepsilon, r_S + 1, \mathrm{emp}, p, q) = \langle V_t(q), V_t(p)\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}$; and $c_\rho(p) \neq 0 \implies \mathrm{IsPt}(p, r_S + 1)$, $\bigl(\mathcal{K}_{r_S + 1} - \sum_{\rho \in R} c_\rho c_\rho^*\bigr)|_Y \succeq 0$ for all finite $Y \subset \{p : \mathrm{IsPt}(p, r_S + 1)\}$ and finite $R \subset \mathbb{N}$, and $\sum_{p \in \mathrm{Pt}(E)} |c_\rho(p)|\,\|V_t(p)\|_{\mathcal{H}} < \infty$ for every $\rho \in \mathbb{N}$.*

**Theorem 5.3 (Multi-Level Schur–Poisson–Theta Tower Theorem `TB.TheoremA` and Zero-Inflation `RowMain` Specialization; cf. [1, `StatementTB.lean:284–294`, `TheoremPIntegralOfPlaces.lean:31–33`], [18, eq. (4)]).** *Given $C_1, C_4 \geq 1$, set $C_0 := \max(3 C_1, 2 C_4)$, $C := \max(C_0, e^9)$, and $C' := 2 C_0$. Under Hypothesis 5.1, if $K \geq 4$, $f \geq 1$, $\Theta \geq C'$, and $r_S (C / \Theta)^f \leq 1$, then:*

1. **General tower bound (`TB.TheoremA`):** *For every finite subset $R \subset \mathbb{N}$,*
   $$
   \sum_{\rho \in R} \Bigl\|\sum_{p \in \mathrm{Pt}(E)} c_\rho(p)\,V_t(p)\Bigr\|_{\mathcal{H}}^2 \leq \bigl(1 + \delta_{\mathrm{TB}}\bigr) \Bigl(\prod_{i = 1}^{r_S + 1} (1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}})\Bigr) j_n, \qquad \delta_{\mathrm{TB}} := C (r_S + 1)\Bigl(\frac{C}{\Theta}\Bigr)^f.
   $$
2. **Zero-inflation specialization for `RowMain` (`TheoremPIntegralOfPlaces.lean:31–33`):** *When $e_i^{\mathrm{TB}} = 0$ for all $1 \leq i \leq r_S + 1$ (as instantiated by `concrete_axioms` via `fun _ => 0`), $\prod_{i = 1}^{r_S + 1}(1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}}) = 1$. If furthermore $\delta_{\mathrm{TB}} = C(r_S + 1)(C/\Theta)^f \leq \delta \leq b^{-7}$ (achieved whenever $\Theta \geq C\bigl(C(r_S + 1)b^7\bigr)^{1/f}$) and the normalized base limit equals $j_n = e_1 D_{\mathrm{ref}}$ (with $e_1$ denoting the Euler-product ratio of Theorem 5.2(2)), then for every finite $R \subset \mathbb{N}$,*
   $$
   \sum_{\rho \in R} \Bigl\|\sum_{p \in \mathrm{Pt}(E)} c_\rho(p)\,V_t(p)\Bigr\|_{\mathcal{H}}^2 \leq \bigl(1 + \delta_{\mathrm{TB}}\bigr) e_1 D_{\mathrm{ref}} \leq (1 + \delta) e_1 D_{\mathrm{ref}}.
   $$
   *(Interface comparison with [18, eq. (4)]: Theorem 5.3 bounds the leading row-vector sums directly via enclosing finite towers $X_{r_S + 1} \supseteq Y_0$ in `LimitStep` and thereby supplies `RowMain` in Theorem 5.5 without requiring [18, eq. (4)]'s stronger Hilbert-norm inequality on arbitrary non-tower finite point subsets.)*

*Proof.* (1) Fix any finite tower $T = (\mathrm{Sg}, X)$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. By Schur's product theorem (`GramPSD`, `KernelPSD`, `WordsPos`), $A \mapsto \operatorname{Re}\sum_{p,q \in X_i} \mathcal{K}_i(p,q)\mathrm{gram}(\varepsilon, i, w, p, q)\mathbf{1}_A(p)\mathbf{1}_A(q)$ is the squared seminorm of $\sum_{p \in A} e_p$, so Minkowski's triangle inequality across $X_i = \bigsqcup_{s = 1}^{i} X_{i, s}$ gives (`SplitBySize`)
$$
\sqrt{Q(T, \varepsilon, i, w)} \leq \sum_{s = 1}^{i} \sqrt{\frac{S_f(\varepsilon, i, w, X_{i, s})}{\mathrm{wt}(w)}}.
$$
For `SchurStep`, fix $1 \leq s \leq i \leq r_S + 1$, $w \in W_{\mathrm{all}}$ with $\mathrm{cnt}(w) + K \leq N_{\mathrm{top}}$, and suppose $\mathrm{Dg}(T, \varepsilon, i, s, w') \leq M$ for all $w' \in \mathcal{W}_b(\mathrm{cnt}(w) + K)$. Expand $\frac{S_f(\varepsilon, i, w, X_{i, s})}{\mathrm{wt}(w)} = \mathrm{Dg}(T, \varepsilon, i, s, w) + \sum_{t \neq t' \in \mathrm{Sg}(i - s + 1, s)} \operatorname{Re}\,\mathrm{Nst}(\varepsilon, i, s, w, X_{i - s}, \operatorname{blk}_{s, t, t'}(\mathcal{K}_i), t, t')$. Terms with $\operatorname{blk}_{s, t, t'}(\mathcal{K}_i)|_{X_{i - s}} \equiv 0$ contribute $\mathrm{Nst} = 0$, so restrict the off-diagonal sum to active pairs $A_{i, s} := \{(t, t') \in \mathrm{Sg}(i - s + 1, s)^2 : t \neq t',\ \operatorname{blk}_{s, t, t'}(\mathcal{K}_i)|_{X_{i - s}} \not\equiv 0\}$. If $(i, s) = (r_S + 1, 1)$, Hypothesis 5.1(2) (`KernelSupport`) forces $A_{r_S + 1, 1} = \varnothing$, so the off-diagonal sum vanishes identically. Otherwise $(i, s) \neq (r_S + 1, 1)$: by `KernelSupport`, every $(t, t') \in A_{i, s}$ satisfies $l_{t, t'} := t^{-1} - t'^{-1} \in \Lambda'_{i - s + 1, s} \setminus \{0\}$. Applying `EstLift` to $\kappa = \mathcal{K}_i$ on each $(t, t') \in A_{i, s}$, bounding $\max_\pi$ by $\sum_{\pi : \mathrm{Fin}(f) \to \mathrm{Fin}(3)}$ ($3^f$ labels), and observing that for each fixed $t$ the map $t' \mapsto t^{-1} - t'^{-1}$ injects $\{t' : (t, t') \in A_{i, s}\}$ into $\Lambda'_{i - s + 1, s} \setminus \{0\}$ so `LatticeSums` bounds $\sum_{t' : (t, t') \in A_{i, s}} \operatorname{maj}(\mathrm{sz}_L(i - s + 1, s), t^{-1} - t'^{-1}) \leq \varepsilon_P$ (and symmetrically for fixed $t'$), summing over $(t, t') \in A_{i, s}$ and $(w_1, w_2)$ yields $\frac{S_f(\varepsilon, i, w, X_{i, s})}{\mathrm{wt}(w)} \leq \mathrm{Dg}(T, \varepsilon, i, s, w) + 3^f \varepsilon_P M$.

For `DiagStep`, choose a finite set $R_{i, s} \subset E$ of distinct coset representatives of $\Lambda_{i - s + 1, s}$ covering the finite set $\mathrm{Sg}(i - s + 1, s)$, partitioning $\mathrm{Sg}(i - s + 1, s) = \bigsqcup_{t_0 \in R_{i, s}} S(t_0)$ where $S(t_0) := \{t \in \mathrm{Sg}(i - s + 1, s) : t - t_0 \in \Lambda_{i - s + 1, s}\}$. By Hypothesis 5.1(3)(ii)–(iii) and Schur's product theorem against $\mathrm{gram}(\varepsilon, i, w) \succeq 0$, $\operatorname{blk}_{s, t, t}(\mathcal{K}_i)|_{X_{i-s}} \preceq \Theta_{i, s}(t)|_{X_{i-s}} = \Theta_{i, s}(t_0)|_{X_{i-s}}$ for every $t \in S(t_0)$, giving
$$
\mathrm{Dg}(T, \varepsilon, i, s, w) \leq \sum_{t_0 \in R_{i, s}} \sum_{t \in S(t_0)} \operatorname{Re}\,\mathrm{Nst}\bigl(\varepsilon, i, s, w, X_{i - s}, \Theta_{i, s}(t_0), t, t\bigr).
$$
Now suppose $N_0(\varepsilon, i - s, w'', X_{i - s}, \mathcal{K}_{i - s}) \leq M_0$ on the shifted word ball:
- **Case $s = 1$ (`OneStep`):** Applying `EstWave` and `PoissonStep` to $\kappa = \Theta_{i, 1}(t_0) \succeq 0$ for each $t_0 \in R_{i, 1}$ (using that $\{\theta_A\}$ in `EstWave` does **not** depend on $\kappa$ or $t_0$, while $\sum_{l \in L} \sum_A \operatorname{maj}(\mathrm{sz}_D(i), l) \leq 2^f \varepsilon_P$ by `LatticeSums`), then summing over $t_0 \in R_{i, 1}$ and applying Hypothesis 5.1(3)(iv) ($\sum_{t_0 \in R_{i, 1}} \Theta_{i, 1}(t_0)|_{X_{i-1}} \preceq (1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}})\mathcal{K}_{i - 1}|_{X_{i-1}}$) inside the linear PSD functional $N_0(\varepsilon, i - 1, w', X_{i - 1}, \cdot)$ yields $\mathrm{Dg}(T, \varepsilon, i, 1, w) \leq (1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}})\bigl(Q(T, \varepsilon, i - 1, w) + 2^f \varepsilon_P M_0\bigr)$.
- **Case $s \geq 2$ (`MultiStep`):** Since $\operatorname{vol}(i - s + 1, s) \leq \Theta^{-2f} \leq 1$, applying `EstBlock` to $\kappa = \Theta_{i, s}(t_0) \succeq 0$ (where $\theta$ is independent of $t_0$), summing over $t_0 \in R_{i, s}$, and applying Hypothesis 5.1(3)(iv) inside $N_0(\varepsilon, i - s, w', X_{i - s}, \cdot)$ gives $\mathrm{Dg}(T, \varepsilon, i, s, w) \leq C_4^{fs}\bigl(\prod_{s'=1}^{s-1}\operatorname{vol}(i - s + 1, s')\bigr)(1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}}) M_0$.

Notice the derivative-budget closure (`budget(i) = 4 K (r_S + 1 - i)`): for $w \in \mathcal{W}_b(\mathrm{budget}(i))$, `SchurStep` shifts $\mathrm{cnt}(w)$ by $+K$, after which `OneStep` ($s = 1$) shifts by $+K$ to $\mathrm{cnt}(w) + 2K \leq \mathrm{budget}(i) + 4K = \mathrm{budget}(i - 1)$ (while at $(i, s) = (r_S + 1, 1)$ off-diagonal blocks vanish and $\mathrm{cnt}(\mathrm{emp}) + K = K \leq 4K = \mathrm{budget}(r_S)$), and `MultiStep` ($s \geq 2$) shifts by $+(s + 1)K$ to $\mathrm{cnt}(w) + (s + 2)K \leq \mathrm{budget}(i) + 4sK = \mathrm{budget}(i - s)$ since $s + 2 \leq 4s$. Hence inductively bounding $Q(T, \varepsilon, k, u) \leq \sigma_k^2$ on $\mathcal{W}_b(\mathrm{budget}(k))$ for $k < i$ establishes the one-level recurrence (`OneLevel`, `StatementTB.lean:262–274`):
$$
\sigma_i := \sqrt{(1 + 3^f \varepsilon_P)(1 + 2^f \varepsilon_P)(1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}})}\,\sigma_{i - 1} + \sum_{s = 2}^{i} \sqrt{(1 + 3^f \varepsilon_P)(1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}}) C_4^{fs} \prod_{s' = 1}^{s - 1} \operatorname{vol}(i - s + 1, s')}\,\sigma_{i - s}.
$$
We now verify the scalar constants (`OneLevelCoefficients.lean` and `RecText.lean`). Since $K \geq 4$ and $\Theta \geq C' = 2 C_0 \geq 6 C_1 \geq 6$, we have $3^f \varepsilon_P = (3 C_1 \Theta^{-(K - 5/2)})^f \leq (C_0 \Theta^{-3/2})^f \leq \frac{1}{2}(C_0 / \Theta)^f$ and $2^f \varepsilon_P \leq \frac{1}{3}(C_0 / \Theta)^f$, so $(1 + 3^f \varepsilon_P)(1 + 2^f \varepsilon_P) \leq (1 + a)^2$ where $a := (C_0 / \Theta)^f \leq 2^{-f} \leq 1/2$ (`eps_small`). For $s \geq 2$, since $e_i^{\mathrm{TB}} \leq 1$, $3^f \varepsilon_P \leq 1$, and $s \leq 2(s - 1)$, we have $(1 + 3^f \varepsilon_P)(1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}}) \leq 4 \leq 2^{2f(s - 1)}$, $C_4^{fs} \leq C_4^{2f(s - 1)}$, and $\prod_{s' = 1}^{s - 1} \operatorname{vol}(i - s + 1, s') \leq \Theta^{-2f(s - 1)}$ by `SizesBelow`, so the $s$-th coefficient is bounded by $\sqrt{(2 C_4 / \Theta)^{2f(s - 1)}}\sqrt{1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}}} \leq a^{s - 1}\sqrt{1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}}}$. Dividing by $\Pi_i := \prod_{m = 1}^{i}\sqrt{1 + e_m^{\mathrm{TB}}} \geq 1$, the normalized sequence $\tilde{\sigma}_i := \sigma_i / \Pi_i$ satisfies $\tilde{\sigma}_i \leq (1 + a)\tilde{\sigma}_{i - 1} + \sum_{s = 2}^{i} a^{s - 1}\tilde{\sigma}_{i - s}$. Inductively $\tilde{\sigma}_i \leq (1 + 2a)^i \sigma_0$, because
$$
\frac{1 + a}{1 + 2a} + \sum_{m = 1}^{i - 1} \frac{a^m}{(1 + 2a)^{m + 1}} < \frac{1 + a}{1 + 2a} + \frac{a}{(1 + 2a)(1 + a)} = \frac{1 + 3a + a^2}{1 + 3a + 2a^2} \leq 1.
$$
Squaring at $i = r_S + 1$ with $x := (r_S + 1)a = r_S a + a \leq r_S (C / \Theta)^f + \frac{1}{2} \leq 1 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$ (using $a \leq (C_0/\Theta)^f \leq \min((C/\Theta)^f, 1/2)$ and $r_S(C/\Theta)^f \leq 1$) gives $(1 + 2a)^{2(r_S + 1)} \leq e^{4x} \leq e^{6x} \leq 1 + \frac{e^9 - 1}{9}\cdot 6x \leq 1 + e^9 x \leq 1 + C(r_S + 1)(C / \Theta)^f$ by convexity of $\exp$ on $[0, 9]$. Thus $S_f(\varepsilon, r_S + 1, \mathrm{emp}, X_{r_S + 1}) \leq B\,\sigma_0(\varepsilon)^2$ with $B := \bigl(1 + C(r_S + 1)(C/\Theta)^f\bigr)\prod_{i=1}^{r_S+1}(1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}})$ and $\sigma_0(\varepsilon)^2 := \max_{w \in \mathcal{W}_b(N_{\mathrm{top}})} \frac{\operatorname{Re}\,\mathrm{gram}(\varepsilon, 0, w, [], [])}{\mathrm{wt}(w)}$.

Finally, we prove `LimitStep` without assuming monotonicity of $S_f$ in the point set. Fix a finite $R \subset \mathbb{N}$ and $\delta_0 > 0$. By summability in `LeadingDomination`, choose a finite subset $Y_0 \subset \{p : \mathrm{IsPt}(p, r_S + 1)\}$ such that $\sum_{p \notin Y_0} |c_\rho(p)|\,\|V_t(p)\|_{\mathcal{H}} < \delta_0$ for every $\rho \in R$ (noting $c_\rho(p) = 0$ when $\neg\mathrm{IsPt}(p, r_S + 1)$). Construct an enclosing finite tower $T = (\mathrm{Sg}, X)$ with $Y_0 \subseteq X_{r_S + 1} \subset \{p : \mathrm{IsPt}(p, r_S + 1)\}$ explicitly by setting $\mathrm{Sg}(b, s) := \{p[k].2 : p \in Y_0,\ k < |p|,\ \operatorname{blockStart}(p, k) = b,\ p[k].1 = s\} \subset E \setminus \{0\}$ and letting $X_i$ be all level-$i$ lists built from $\mathrm{Sg}$ (finite since $s \geq 1$ bounds list length by $i$). Apply `LeadingDomination` and `Cutoff` **directly on $Y = X_{r_S + 1}$**: for $0 < \varepsilon < \varepsilon_{X_{r_S + 1}}$, `Cutoff` equates $\mathrm{gram}(\varepsilon, r_S + 1, \mathrm{emp}, p, q)$ with $G^{(0)}_{p, q} := \langle V_t(q), V_t(p)\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}$ whenever $\mathcal{K}_{r_S + 1}(p, q) \neq 0$, so $S_f(\varepsilon, r_S + 1, \mathrm{emp}, X_{r_S + 1}) = \operatorname{Re}\sum_{p, q \in X_{r_S + 1}} \mathcal{K}_{r_S + 1}(p, q)\,G^{(0)}_{p, q}$. Since $G^{(0)}|_{X_{r_S + 1}} \succeq 0$ and $\bigl(\mathcal{K}_{r_S + 1} - \sum_{\rho \in R} c_\rho c_\rho^*\bigr)\big|_{X_{r_S + 1}} \succeq 0$, Schur's product theorem gives
$$
\sum_{\rho \in R} \Bigl\|\sum_{p \in X_{r_S + 1}} c_\rho(p)\,V_t(p)\Bigr\|_{\mathcal{H}}^2 = \operatorname{Re} \sum_{p, q \in X_{r_S + 1}} \Bigl(\sum_{\rho \in R} c_\rho(p)\overline{c_\rho(q)}\Bigr) G^{(0)}_{p, q} \leq S_f(\varepsilon, r_S + 1, \mathrm{emp}, X_{r_S + 1}) \leq B\,\sigma_0(\varepsilon)^2.
$$
Letting $\varepsilon \to 0^+$ across the finite set $\mathcal{W}_b(N_{\mathrm{top}})$ sends $\sigma_0(\varepsilon)^2 \to j_n$ by `EstE`, while $Y_0 \subseteq X_{r_S + 1}$ bounds the $\ell^1$ tail $\bigl\|\sum_{p \notin X_{r_S + 1}} c_\rho(p)V_t(p)\bigr\|_{\mathcal{H}} \leq \sum_{p \notin Y_0} |c_\rho(p)|\,\|V_t(p)\|_{\mathcal{H}} < \delta_0$. Letting $\delta_0 \to 0^+$ yields (1). Part (2) follows immediately upon substituting $e_i^{\mathrm{TB}} = 0$ ($\prod_{i=1}^{r_S+1}(1 + e_i^{\mathrm{TB}}) = 1$), $\delta_{\mathrm{TB}} \leq \delta$, and $j_n = e_1 D_{\mathrm{ref}}$. $\square$

### 6.1 Unramified CM Different Cancellation and Even-Weight Local Ratio Bounds

In the global Bruhat-cell lattice pricing feeding `StatementTB` (`FamilySeparationBoundOfNorms.lean`), $E/F$ is an unramified CM extension of a totally real field $F$ ($[F:\mathbb{Q}] = f$, $[E:\mathbb{Q}] = 2f$) with absolute root discriminant $\mathrm{rd}_E := |\Delta_E|^{\frac{1}{2f}}$. Because $E/F$ is unramified ($\mathfrak{D}_{E/F} = \mathcal{O}_E$ and relative discriminant ideal $\mathfrak{d}_{E/F} := N_{E/F}(\mathfrak{D}_{E/F}) = \mathcal{O}_F$), the discriminant tower formula gives
$$
|\Delta_E| = |\Delta_F|^2 N_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathfrak{d}_{E/F}) = |\Delta_F|^2 N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathfrak{D}_{E/F}) = \Delta_F^2,
$$
so $|\Delta_F| = |\Delta_E|^{1/2} = \mathrm{rd}_E^f$, and for any fractional ideal $\mathfrak{D}_E^{-e} (g_S \mathcal{O}_E)^{-1}$ ($\mathfrak{D}_E = \mathfrak{D}_F \mathcal{O}_E$), $|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathfrak{D}_E^{-e} g_S^{-1}\mathcal{O}_E)|^{1/2} = |\Delta_F|^{-e} g_S^{-f} = \mathrm{rd}_E^{-ef} g_S^{-f}$. Across a Bruhat block $(i, s)$ with starting index $i \geq 1$ and step size $s \geq 1$ satisfying $i - 1 + s \leq r_S + 1$ (note that in `FamilySeparationBoundOfNorms.lean`, $i$ denotes the block **starting** index $\operatorname{blockStart}$, whereas §5.3 indexed tower levels by terminal level $i_{\mathrm{term}} = i - 1 + s$), the archimedean step-volume scaling carries base $\beta := \kappa\,\mathrm{rd}_E / g_S$, and a naive worst-case estimate would accumulate powers of $\mathrm{rd}_E^f$ into the tower scale $\Theta$.

**Theorem 6.1 (Exact Unramified CM Different Cancellation and Even-Weight Local Ratio Bound; [1, `FamilySeparationBoundOfNorms.lean:105–250`]).** *Let $E/F$ be an unramified CM extension ($[F:\mathbb{Q}] = f$) with $\mathrm{rd}_E = |\Delta_F|^{1/f} > 0$, $g_S > 0$, $\kappa > 0$, and $\beta := \kappa\,\mathrm{rd}_E / g_S$.*

1. **Algebraic step identities (`szf_alg`, `szf_algP`) and three Bruhat-cell product formulas:** *For any real scalars $C, A, N, S \in \mathbb{R}$, any $I > 0$ (or $I \neq 0$), integers $a, e \geq 0$ with $n = a + e$, and integers $j, e \geq 0$ with $n' = 2j + 1 + 2e$,*
   $$
   \begin{gathered}
   C \beta^{-n} A \Bigl(\frac{C \beta N}{I}\Bigr)^a \mathrm{rd}_E^e g_S = \frac{C g_S^{n + 1} A}{\kappa^n}\Bigl(\frac{C \kappa N}{g_S I}\Bigr)^a, \\
   (\mathrm{rd}_E^e g_S)^2 C \beta^{-n'} A \beta^{2j + 1} S \Bigl(\frac{C N}{I}\Bigr)^{2j} = \frac{C g_S^{n' + 1} A}{\kappa^{n'} g_S^{2j}}\,\kappa\Bigl(\frac{C \kappa N}{I}\Bigr)^{2j} S.
   \end{gathered}
   $$
   *Here $\mathrm{aaV}(i, s) = \mathfrak{D}_E^{-(2r_S + 4 - 2i - s)} g_S^{-1}\mathcal{O}_E$ and $\mathrm{aaP}(i, s) = \mathfrak{D}_E^{-(2r_S + 3 - 2i - s)} g_S^{-1}\mathcal{O}_E$. Specializing $(a, e)$ across all three Bruhat-cell regimes—(i) $s = 1$, $1 \leq i \leq r_S + 1$ (`product_formula_first`, $\mathrm{aaV}(i, 1)$): $a = 2i - 2$, $e = 2r_S + 3 - 2i$, $a + e = 2r_S + 1$; (ii) $1 \leq i$ and $2 \leq s \leq r_S + 2 - i$, i.e. $i - 1 + s \leq r_S + 1$ (`product_formula_second`, where $\mathrm{aaV}(i, s)$ and $\mathrm{aaP}(i, s - 1)$ carry the **same** ideal exponent $e = 2r_S + 4 - 2i - s = 2r_S + 3 - 2i - (s - 1)$ with $a = 2(i - 1) + s - 1 = 2i + s - 3$, $a + e = 2r_S + 1$); and (iii) top pivot step $1 \leq i \leq r_S$ with $s = r_S + 2 - i$ (`product_formula_third`, $\mathrm{aaP}(i, r_S + 2 - i)$ with ideal-square multiplicity $2$ before taking a square root): $j = i - 1$, $e = 2r_S + 3 - 2i - (r_S + 2 - i) = r_S + 1 - i$, $2j + 1 + 2e = 2r_S + 1$—cancels $\mathrm{rd}_E$ **identically** from the $f$-place product in every case (whereas shifting the ideal exponent by $+1$ leaves an uncancelled factor $\mathrm{rd}_E^f$).*
2. **Even-weight local ratio bound (`szf_prodAbs`):** *Suppose $k \in 2\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ is an **even** positive integer, $C_0 \geq 1$, $0 \leq E_{\mathrm{err}} \leq \frac{35}{4} k$, and real local ratios $\{r_\tau\}, \{r'_\tau\}$ satisfy $e^{-E_{\mathrm{err}}}\prod_\tau r_\tau^k \leq C_0 e^{k/4}$ and $e^{-E_{\mathrm{err}}}\prod_\tau (r'_\tau)^k \leq C_0 e^{k/4}$. Then with $t_V(u, u', \tau) := \sqrt{\max(0, r_\tau r'_\tau)}$,*
   $$
   \prod_\tau |r_\tau| \leq C_0 e^9, \qquad \prod_\tau |r'_\tau| \leq C_0 e^9, \qquad \prod_\tau t_V(u, u', \tau) \leq C_0 e^9.
   $$
   *(Sharpness of even weight $k$: At odd weight $k = 5$ with $E_{\mathrm{err}} = 0$ and $C_0 = 1$, a single negative ratio $r_1 = -10$ satisfies $r_1^5 = -100{,}000 \leq e^{5/4}$ while $|r_1|^5 = 100{,}000 > e^{5/4}$ already breaks the step $r_1^k = |r_1|^k$ passing to absolute values, and $r_1 = -10{,}000$ satisfies $r_1^5 = -10^{20} \leq e^{5/4}$ while violating the conclusion $|r_1| \leq e^9 \approx 8103.08$ directly.)*

*Proof.* (1) Expand $\beta^{-n} \beta^a \mathrm{rd}_E^e g_S = (\kappa\,\mathrm{rd}_E/g_S)^{-e} \mathrm{rd}_E^e g_S = \kappa^{-e} g_S^{e + 1} = \kappa^{-n} g_S^{n + 1} (\kappa/g_S)^a$, and similarly $(\mathrm{rd}_E^e g_S)^2 \beta^{-(2j + 1 + 2e)} \beta^{2j + 1} = \mathrm{rd}_E^{2e} g_S^2 (\kappa\,\mathrm{rd}_E/g_S)^{-2e} = \kappa^{-2e} g_S^{2e + 2} = \frac{g_S^{n' + 1}}{\kappa^{n'} g_S^{2j}}\,\kappa\,\kappa^{2j}$. Checking $(2i - 2) + (2r_S + 3 - 2i) = 2r_S + 1$, $(2i + s - 3) + (2r_S + 4 - 2i - s) = 2r_S + 1$, and $2(i - 1) + 1 + 2(r_S + 1 - i) = 2r_S + 1$ proves complete cancellation of $\mathrm{rd}_E$ across all $f$ real places.

(2) Because $k \geq 2$ is **even**, $r_\tau^k = |r_\tau|^k \geq 0$ for every $\tau$, so $\bigl(\prod_\tau |r_\tau|\bigr)^k = \prod_\tau r_\tau^k \leq C_0 e^{E_{\mathrm{err}} + k/4} \leq C_0 e^{9k} \leq (C_0 e^9)^k$ since $E_{\mathrm{err}} + \frac{k}{4} \leq \frac{36}{4}k = 9k$ and $C_0 \geq 1$. Extracting the $k$-th root gives $\prod_\tau |r_\tau| \leq C_0 e^9$ and $\prod_\tau |r'_\tau| \leq C_0 e^9$, whence $\prod_\tau \sqrt{\max(0, r_\tau r'_\tau)} \leq \sqrt{(\prod_\tau |r_\tau|)(\prod_\tau |r'_\tau|)} \leq C_0 e^9$. $\square$

### 6.2 Two-Norm Cartan Bergman Cusp Decay: `dHS` vs. `dOp` and Quadratic-Branch Separation

To suppress deep-cusp matrix coefficients of holomorphic discrete series on $U(p, q)$ ($n = p + q$, $q := \min(p, q) \geq 1$), the Lean proof combines a Cauchy–Binet/log-sum-exp minor lower bound (`NuclearBound.lean`, `HalfDifferenceL1Bound.lean`) expressed in the **Hilbert–Schmidt Cartan norm** $d_{\mathrm{HS}}$ with a cusp-threshold envelope (`CuspidalityThreshold.lean`, `LayerProd.lean`) expressed in the **operator Cartan norm** $d_{\mathrm{op}}$. Conflating these two norms breaks the inequality on the near-identity quadratic branch; the next theorem proves both the general minor lower bound and the exact two-norm comparison unconditionally.

For $g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$ in the Cartan model of $U(p, q)$ with singular values $e^{t_1}, \ldots, e^{t_q}, e^{-t_1}, \ldots, e^{-t_q}, 1, \ldots, 1$ ($t_1 \geq \cdots \geq t_q \geq 0$), write $X = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & B \\ B^* & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr) \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ ($B \in M_{p \times q}(\mathbb{C})$ having singular values $t_1, \ldots, t_q$) so that $g = k_1 (\exp X) k_2$ ($k_1, k_2 \in U(p) \times U(q)$). Following `LayerProd.lean:18–24` and `HalfDifferenceL1Bound.lean:18`, define:
$$
\begin{gathered}
d_{\mathrm{HS}}(g) := \|X\|_{\mathrm{HS}} = \sqrt{2 \sum_{j = 1}^{q} t_j^2}, \qquad d_{\mathrm{op}}(g) := \|X\|_{\mathrm{op}} = \max_{1 \leq j \leq q} t_j = t_1, \\
\operatorname{decExp}(h, q, t) := \frac{h}{8}\min\Bigl(t^2,\, \frac{t}{\sqrt{2q}}\Bigr), \qquad \operatorname{decE}(q, h, d_{\mathrm{op}}) := \exp\Bigl(-\frac{h}{8}\min\Bigl(2 d_{\mathrm{op}}^2,\, \frac{d_{\mathrm{op}}}{\sqrt{q}}\Bigr)\Bigr) = \exp\bigl(-\operatorname{decExp}(h, q, \sqrt{2}\,d_{\mathrm{op}})\bigr).
\end{gathered}
$$

**Theorem 6.2 (Cauchy–Binet/Log-Sum-Exp Minor Bound, Two-Norm Bergman Decay, and Cusp Scaling; [1, `NuclearBound.lean`, `HalfDifferenceL1Bound.lean`, `CuspidalityThreshold.lean`, `LayerProd.lean`]).** *Let $n = p + q$ with $p \geq q \geq 1$ and $h > 0$.*

1. **Weighted leading-minor bound (`NuclearBound`):** *Let $Y = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & B \\ B^* & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr) \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ be block off-diagonal Hermitian with $\|Y\|_{\mathrm{HS}} = d_{\mathrm{HS}}$, and let $s_1, \ldots, s_{n - 1} \geq 0$ satisfy $\sum_{i = a}^{b - 1} s_i \geq h$ for all $1 \leq a \leq p < b \leq n$. Letting $\Delta_i(A) := \det(A_{1..i, 1..i}) > 0$ denote the $i$-th leading principal minor of $A \succ 0$,*
   $$
   \sum_{i = 1}^{n - 1} s_i \log \Delta_i(\exp Y) \geq \operatorname{decExp}(h, q, d_{\mathrm{HS}}) = \frac{h}{8}\min\Bigl(d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2,\, \frac{d_{\mathrm{HS}}}{\sqrt{2q}}\Bigr).
   $$
2. **Scalar Bergman decay, `dHS` vs. `dOp` comparison, and quadratic-branch norm-conflation failure:** *Every Cartan parameter $t = (t_1, \ldots, t_q) \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^q$ satisfies $\sqrt{2}\,d_{\mathrm{op}} \leq d_{\mathrm{HS}} \leq \sqrt{2q}\,d_{\mathrm{op}}$ (with $d_{\mathrm{HS}} = \sqrt{2}\,d_{\mathrm{op}}$ iff at most one $t_j > 0$, in particular whenever $q = 1$, and $d_{\mathrm{HS}} = \sqrt{2q}\,d_{\mathrm{op}}$ iff $t_1 = \cdots = t_q$), and*
   $$
   \prod_{j = 1}^{q} (\cosh t_j)^{-h} \leq \exp\bigl(-\operatorname{decExp}(h, q, d_{\mathrm{HS}})\bigr) \leq \operatorname{decE}(q, h, d_{\mathrm{op}}),
   $$
   *with strict first inequality whenever $d_{\mathrm{HS}} > 0$. Conversely, substituting $d_{\mathrm{HS}}$ directly into $\operatorname{decE}(q, h, \cdot)$ replaces $d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$ by $2 d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$ on $4q d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq 1$ and **fails strictly** for every $t \neq 0$ in $4q d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq 1$ because $\sum_{j=1}^q \log\cosh t_j < \frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^q t_j^2 = \frac{1}{4} d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$.*
3. **Near-identity cusp exponent (`LayerProd.lean`) and conditional host divergence (`StatementDS.lean`, `BridgeCountE.lean`):** *Fix $C_g \geq 0$, independently of $x$ (`LayerProd.CountHyp:108–115`). At the `LayerProd` gap threshold $h_{\mathrm{gap}} := 8 \times 10^{14}(C_g + 4) n^{19} f^5 \log(b\,\mathrm{rd}_E)$ and near-identity Cartan radius $\varepsilon_N := \frac{1}{10^6 n^8 f^2}$ ($2q\varepsilon_N^2 < 1$), the quadratic branch applies and yields exact exponent*
   $$
   \operatorname{decExp}(h_{\mathrm{gap}}, q, \varepsilon_N) = \frac{h_{\mathrm{gap}}}{8}\,\varepsilon_N^2 = 100(C_g + 4) n^3 f \log(b\,\mathrm{rd}_E).
   $$
   *Under the host-parameter bounds $f \leq n = O(x^{14/3})$, $b \geq 16$, and $\log\mathrm{rd}_E \leq x^{1/60}$ with $n^3 f \geq x^{1/60}$, this exponent dominates every polynomial denominator $O(x^{112 + 1/60})$ with net super-polynomial margin $\exp(\Omega(x^{1/60}))$.*

*Proof.* (1) Diagonalize $Y = U \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_n) U^*$ ($U \in U(n)$). Since $\operatorname{rk}(Y) = 2\operatorname{rk}(B) \leq 2q$, Cauchy–Schwarz on the at most $2q$ nonzero eigenvalues gives $\sum_{m=1}^n |\lambda_m| \leq \sqrt{2q \sum_{m=1}^n \lambda_m^2} = \sqrt{2q}\,d_{\mathrm{HS}}$. Let $P_i$ be the orthogonal projection onto $\operatorname{span}(e_1, \ldots, e_i)$. By Cauchy–Binet applied to $\exp(Y/2)e_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \exp(Y/2)e_i$, $\Delta_i(\exp Y) = \sum_{|J| = i} w_J \exp(x_J)$ where $w_J := |\det U_{1..i, J}|^2 \geq 0$ and $x_J := \sum_{j \in J} \lambda_j$ satisfy $\sum_{|J|=i} w_J = 1$, $\sum_{|J|=i} w_J x_J = \operatorname{Tr}(P_i Y P_i) = 0$ (as $Y$ has zero diagonal blocks), and $\sum_{|J|=i} w_J x_J^2 = \operatorname{Tr}(P_i Y^2 P_i) - \operatorname{Tr}((P_i Y P_i)^2) = \operatorname{Tr}(P_i Y (I_n - P_i) Y P_i) = \sum_{a = 1}^{i} \sum_{b = i + 1}^{n} |Y_{a, b}|^2 =: V_i$. Moreover, since $\sum_{m=1}^n \lambda_m = \operatorname{Tr}(Y) = 0$ implies $\sum_{j \in J} \lambda_j = -\sum_{j \notin J} \lambda_j$, we have $|x_J| \leq \frac{1}{2}\sum_{m=1}^n |\lambda_m| \leq \frac{\sqrt{2q}}{2} d_{\mathrm{HS}} \leq M := \max\bigl(1, \sqrt{2q}\,d_{\mathrm{HS}}\bigr)$ and $V_i \leq \frac{1}{4}\bigl(\sum_{m=1}^n |\lambda_m|\bigr)^2 \leq \frac{1}{2}q d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$. Setting $u_J := x_J / M \in [-1, 1]$ (so $\sum w_J u_J = 0$ and $\sum w_J u_J^2 = V_i / M^2 \leq \frac{1}{4}$, since either $M = 1$ with $2q d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq 1$ so $V_i \leq \frac{1}{4}$, or $M = \sqrt{2q}\,d_{\mathrm{HS}}$ so $V_i / M^2 \leq \frac{1}{4}$), we have $e^{u_J} \geq 1 + u_J + \frac{1}{2}u_J^2 - \frac{1}{6}|u_J|^3 \geq 1 + u_J + \frac{1}{3}u_J^2$, and Jensen's inequality on $t \mapsto t^M$ ($M \geq 1$) gives $\Delta_i(\exp Y) = \sum w_J (e^{u_J})^M \geq \bigl(\sum w_J e^{u_J}\bigr)^M \geq \bigl(1 + \frac{V_i}{3 M^2}\bigr)^M$, whence $\log \Delta_i(\exp Y) \geq M \log\bigl(1 + \frac{V_i}{3 M^2}\bigr) \geq \frac{V_i}{4M}$ (using $\log(1 + \frac{t}{3}) \geq \frac{t}{4}$ on $[0, 1/4]$). Summing over $i = 1, \ldots, n - 1$ and using $Y_{a, b} = 0$ outside $1 \leq a \leq p < b \leq n$ yields
$$
\sum_{i = 1}^{n - 1} s_i V_i = \sum_{a = 1}^{p} \sum_{b = p + 1}^{n} |Y_{a, b}|^2 \sum_{i = a}^{b - 1} s_i \geq h \sum_{a = 1}^{p} \sum_{b = p + 1}^{n} |Y_{a, b}|^2 = \frac{h}{2}\,d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2.
$$
Dividing by $4M = 4\max(1, \sqrt{2q}\,d_{\mathrm{HS}})$ yields $\frac{h}{8}\min(d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2, d_{\mathrm{HS}}/\sqrt{2q}) = \operatorname{decExp}(h, q, d_{\mathrm{HS}})$.

(2) Clearly $2 d_{\mathrm{op}}^2 = 2 t_1^2 \leq 2\sum_{j=1}^q t_j^2 = d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq 2q t_1^2 = 2q d_{\mathrm{op}}^2$. Permuting odd coordinates before even coordinates in $\bigoplus_{j=1}^q \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & t_j \\ t_j & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ yields the $(q, q)$ block off-diagonal Hermitian matrix $Y = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & D_t \\ D_t & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr) \in M_{2q}(\mathbb{C})$ with $D_t := \operatorname{diag}(t_1, \ldots, t_q)$ and $\|Y\|_{\mathrm{HS}} = d_{\mathrm{HS}}$, whose exponential $\exp Y = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} \cosh D_t & \sinh D_t \\ \sinh D_t & \cosh D_t \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ has $q$-th leading principal minor $\Delta_q(\exp Y) = \prod_{j=1}^q \cosh t_j$. Applying (1) with $p = q$, $s_q = h$, and $s_i = 0$ for $i \neq q$ (so $\sum_{i=a}^{b-1} s_i = s_q = h$ whenever $1 \leq a \leq q < b \leq 2q$) gives $h\sum_{j=1}^q \log\cosh t_j = s_q \log\Delta_q(\exp Y) \geq \operatorname{decExp}(h, q, d_{\mathrm{HS}})$; when $d_{\mathrm{HS}} > 0$, $V_q = \sum_{j=1}^q t_j^2 = \frac{1}{2} d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 > 0$ so $v := V_q / M^2 \in (0, 1/4]$ satisfies $\log(1 + v/3) > v/4$, making the inequality strict. Since $t \mapsto \operatorname{decExp}(h, q, t)$ is nondecreasing on $[0, \infty)$ and $\sqrt{2}\,d_{\mathrm{op}} \leq d_{\mathrm{HS}}$, $\exp(-\operatorname{decExp}(h, q, d_{\mathrm{HS}})) \leq \exp(-\operatorname{decExp}(h, q, \sqrt{2}\,d_{\mathrm{op}})) = \operatorname{decE}(q, h, d_{\mathrm{op}})$. Meanwhile, for $0 < \sum_{j=1}^q t_j^2 \leq \frac{1}{8q}$ ($4q d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \leq 1$), $\log\cosh t_j = \int_0^{t_j}\tanh u\,du < \int_0^{t_j} u\,du = \frac{1}{2}t_j^2$, so $\prod_{j=1}^q (\cosh t_j)^{-h} > \exp(-\frac{h}{4}d_{\mathrm{HS}}^2) = \operatorname{decE}(q, h, d_{\mathrm{HS}})$.

(3) Direct cancellation gives $\frac{1}{8}\cdot 8 \times 10^{14}(C_g + 4)n^{19}f^5\log(b\,\mathrm{rd}_E)\cdot (10^{-6}n^{-8}f^{-2})^2 = 100(C_g + 4)n^3 f \log(b\,\mathrm{rd}_E)$, and the 6722nd Taylor term $\frac{y^{6722}}{6722!}$ of $e^y$ at $y = x^{1/60}$ dominates $x^{24 \cdot 14/3 + 1/60} = x^{112 + 1/60}$ by $\frac{6722}{60} - \bigl(112 + \frac{1}{60}\bigr) = \frac{1}{60}$. $\square$

This appendix gives the exact mathematical definitions from `FlagEEqHyperbolic.lean`, `IsPrimitiveSub.lean`, `CntLocal.lean`, `SameGenusFix.lean`, `JordanExists.lean`, `StepsR.lean`, `IsometryFixFlagAnisotropicVectorOfFixFrame.lean`, and `Assemble.lean`, states Hypotheses A.1–A.4 (Obligation O4), proves Proposition A.5 (`rowsGroupoid_of` and `formulaExplicit_of`), and records both finite groupoid realizations ($r_S = 0$ on $\mathbb{F}_4^4$ and $r_S = 1$ on $\mathbb{F}_4^5$ with $|N_0| = 32$).

### A.1 Ambient Hermitian Space, Row-Quotient Weights `autLz`, Divided Lattice `MMof`, and `GaPlus`

Let $E / F$ be an unramified CM quadratic extension ($[F : \mathbb{Q}] = f$, `HostDict`) with involution $x \mapsto \bar{x}$, integers $\mathcal{O}_E / \mathcal{O}_F$, $E$-different $\mathfrak{D}_E = \mathfrak{D}_{E/\mathbb{Q}}$, $r_S \in \mathbb{N}$, $m := 2(r_S + 1)$, $k \geq m + 3$, and $r := k + m - 1 = k + 2r_S + 1$. Let $g_0, g_1 \in \mathbb{N}$ be distinct odd primes splitting completely in $E / \mathbb{Q}$ ($g_S := g_0 g_1$), and fix positive integers $T_u \equiv T_{u'} \pmod{g_S^2}$. Let $(V, h_T)$ (`(VS D).V` with conjugate-linear-first Hermitian form `(VS D).hT` and standard unimodular lattice `L0` in `CntLocal.lean:23–26`) be the nondegenerate $r$-dimensional Hermitian $E$-space of archimedean signature $(k + r_S, r_S + 1)$.

1. **Word flags, row lattices, and finite orthogonal-quotient weights (`FlagG`, `LFlag`, `IsRowLat`, `autLz`, `FlagEEqHyperbolic.lean:87–120`, `IsPrimitiveSub.lean:72–107`):**
   - A flag $F = (\mathbf{e}, z) \in \mathrm{FlagG}(V, r_S, T)$ consists of a linearly independent totally isotropic $r_S$-tuple $\mathbf{e} = (e_0, \ldots, e_{r_S - 1})$ spanning $X(F) := \bigoplus_{a = 0}^{r_S - 1} E e_a$ (with partial flags $X_j(F) := \bigoplus_{a < j} E e_a$) and an anisotropic vector $z \in X(F)^\perp$ with $h_T(z, z) = -T$, ordered as the $(r_S + 1)$-frame $\mathrm{fvec}(F) := (e_0, \ldots, e_{r_S - 1}, z)$ spanning $U_{\mathrm{base}}(F) := X(F) \oplus E z$. A **marked flag** $F' = (\mathrm{fl}, z_t) \in \mathrm{LFlag}(V, r_S, T')$ pairs $\mathrm{fl} = (\mathbf{e}', z') \in \mathrm{FlagG}(V, r_S, T')$ with a lift vector $z_t \in V$; `lf(F_0) := (F_0, z_0)`, and `IsLift`($\Lambda, F'$) requires $z' \in \Lambda$ and $z_t = z'$.
   - Define the different-weighted isotropic $\mathcal{O}_E$-lattice $\mathrm{XS}(F) := \bigoplus_{a = 0}^{r_S - 1} \mathfrak{D}_E^{-(r_S - a)} e_a \subset X(F)$. An $\mathcal{O}_E$-lattice $\Lambda \subset V$ is a **row lattice** for $F_0 = (\mathbf{e}, z_0)$ (`IsRowLat(F_0, \Lambda)`) iff $\Lambda$ lies in the unimodular genus of `L0` ($\Lambda^\# = \Lambda$), satisfies `FixedHyp`($\Lambda, \mathrm{lf}(F_0)$) (unimodular, $z_0 \in \Lambda$, primitive $z_0$ in $L_{\mathrm{sub}}(\Lambda, F_0)$, and localizations of $\Lambda \cap X(F_0)$ at $P \mid g_S$ equal $\bigoplus_a \mathcal{O}_{E,P} e_a$), and satisfies $\Lambda \cap X(F_0) = \mathrm{XS}(F_0)$.
   - Let $\operatorname{stab}_F(F_0) := \{\gamma \in U(V, h_T) : \gamma(z_0) = z_0 \land \forall j \leq r_S,\, \gamma(X_j(F_0)) = X_j(F_0)\}$. Because $\gamma \in \operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$ preserves $X(F_0)$ and $X(F_0)^\perp$, any $\gamma \in \operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$ with $\gamma(\Lambda) = \Lambda$ induces an $\mathcal{O}_E$-automorphism of the **orthogonal quotient** $W_{\mathrm{bQ}}(F_0) := X(F_0)^\perp / X(F_0)$. For $r_S \geq 1$ over an infinite ring $\mathcal{O}_E$, the full stabilizer $\{\gamma \in \operatorname{stab}_F(F_0) : \gamma(\Lambda) = \Lambda\}$ is **infinite** (because of its unipotent nilradical $N_0(F_0, \Lambda)$), whereas the induced group $\operatorname{autLz}(F_0, \Lambda) \subseteq \operatorname{Aut}(W_{\mathrm{bQ}}(F_0))$ fixes $[z_0]$ and preserves the projected lattice inside the **positive-definite** rank-$k$ orthogonal complement of $[z_0]$ in $W_{\mathrm{bQ}}(F_0)$, hence is **finite** (`autLz_finite`). The row weight is $w_{\mathrm{row}}(F_0, \Lambda) := |\operatorname{autLz}(F_0, \Lambda)|^{-1} > 0$, and $\mathrm{what}_2$ (`IsRowReps`) is a finite irredundant set of $\operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$-orbit representatives of row lattices.

2. **Relative-position matrix, `frFac`-scaled local Gram `GaLoc`, and split-place divided Gram `GaPlus` (`BlockEnds.lean:14, 53–54`, `CntLocal.lean:45–86`, `IsPrimitiveSub.lean:105–114`, `LocalGroupInFamilyNontrivial.lean:25–29, 148–150`):**
   - For $F_0$ and $F' = (\mathrm{fl}, z_t)$, the **relative-position matrix** $Z = \widehat{Z}(F_0, F') := \mathrm{Zhat}(F_0, F'.\mathrm{fl}) \in M_{r_S + 1}(E)$ has entries $Z_{a, b} := \mathrm{br}_S\bigl(\mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl})_b,\, \mathrm{fvec}(F_0)_a\bigr) = h_T\bigl(\mathrm{fvec}(F_0)_a,\, \mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl})_b\bigr)$ (`BlockEnds.lean:14, 53–54`, matching [18]'s $(X^* J X)_{a,b} = h_T(x_a, x_b)$) on the **underlying frames** $\mathrm{fvec}(F_0) = (e_0, \ldots, e_{r_S-1}, z_0)$ and $\mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl}) = (e'_0, \ldots, e'_{r_S-1}, z')$ (which coincides with $(e'_0, \ldots, e'_{r_S-1}, z_t)$ on $E_Z$ because `IsTerm` enforces `IsLift`, i.e. $z_t = z'$). Because $h_T$ is conjugate-linear first, scaling $\mathrm{fvec}(F_0)_a \mapsto d_a \mathrm{fvec}(F_0)_a$ and $\mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl})_b \mapsto d'_b \mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl})_b$ multiplies $Z_{a,b}$ by $\overline{d_a}\,d'_b$. Writing $Z_{r_S}$ (`leadMinorE Z r_S`) for the upper-left $r_S \times r_S$ minor and $D_T := \operatorname{diag}(0_{r_S}, -T)$, the global $m \times m$ Gram matrix and scalar invariant are
     $$
     \mathrm{Ga}(T_u, T_{u'}, Z) := \begin{pmatrix} D_{T_u} & Z \\ Z^* & D_{T_{u'}} \end{pmatrix} \in \operatorname{Herm}_m(E), \qquad G_Z(T_u, T_{u'}, Z) := |\det Z|^2 - T_u T_{u'} |\det Z_{r_S}|^2 \in F.
     $$
   - At each finite place $\mathfrak{p}$ of $F$ with local different generator $\delta_{\mathfrak{p}} \in F_{\mathfrak{p}}^\times$ ($\delta_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}} = \mathfrak{D}_E \mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}}$), define $g_{\mathrm{part}}(\mathfrak{p}) := g_0$ if $\mathrm{char}(\mathcal{O}_F / \mathfrak{p}) = g_0$, $g_1$ if $\mathrm{char}(\mathcal{O}_F / \mathfrak{p}) = g_1$, and $1$ otherwise, and set the canonical rational unit $u_P(\mathfrak{p}) := g_{\mathrm{part}}(\mathfrak{p}) / g_S \in \mathbb{Q}^\times \cap \mathcal{O}_{F, \mathfrak{p}}^\times$ (a $\mathfrak{p}$-adic unit at every finite place since $g_0 \neq g_1$ are distinct rational primes, giving $1/g_1$ above $g_0$, $1/g_0$ above $g_1$, and $1/g_S$ away from $g_S$; `LocalGroupInFamilyNontrivial.lean:25–29, 148–150`). Define the local different-rescaling factor $\mathrm{frFac}_{\mathfrak{p}}(a) := \bigl(\delta_{\mathfrak{p}} u_P(\mathfrak{p})\bigr)^{-(r_S - a)}$ for $0 \leq a \leq r_S - 1$ and $\mathrm{frFac}_{\mathfrak{p}}(r_S) := 1$. Setting $C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z)_{a, b} := \mathrm{frFac}_{\mathfrak{p}}(b)\overline{\mathrm{frFac}_{\mathfrak{p}}(a)}\,\iota_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z_{a, b})$ (`CntLocal.lean:66–68`), the local Gram matrix of the $\mathrm{XS}$-rescaled basis is $\mathrm{Ga}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathfrak{p}, Z) = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} D_{T_u} & C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z) \\ C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z)^* & D_{T_{u'}} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$. At places $\mathfrak{p} \nmid g_S$, $\mathrm{Ga}_+(\mathfrak{p}, Z) := \mathrm{Ga}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathfrak{p}, Z)$. At places $\mathfrak{p} \mid g_S$ with local split modulus $q_{\mathfrak{p}} = g_{\mathrm{part}}(\mathfrak{p}) \in \{g_0, g_1\}$, conjugating by $A_{\mathfrak{p}} := \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} I_{r_S + 1} & -q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-1} I_{r_S + 1} \\ 0 & q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-1} I_{r_S + 1} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ gives (`CntLocal.lean:75–86`)
     $$
     \mathrm{Ga}_+(\mathfrak{p}, Z) := A_{\mathfrak{p}}^* \mathrm{Ga}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathfrak{p}, Z) A_{\mathfrak{p}} = \begin{pmatrix} D_{T_u} & q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-1}\bigl(C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z) - D_{T_u}\bigr) \\ q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-1}\bigl(C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z)^* - D_{T_u}\bigr) & q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-2}\bigl(D_{T_u} + D_{T_{u'}} - C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z) - C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z)^*\bigr) \end{pmatrix}.
     $$
     Thus $\mathfrak{p}$-integrality of $\mathrm{Ga}_+(\mathfrak{p}, Z)$ at $\mathfrak{p} \mid g_S$ requires both the off-diagonal congruence $C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z) \equiv D_{T_u} \pmod{q_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}}}$ and the second-order diagonal/cross congruence $D_{T_u} + D_{T_{u'}} - C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z) - C_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z)^* \equiv 0 \pmod{q_{\mathfrak{p}}^2 \mathcal{O}_{E, \mathfrak{p}}}$ (matching `AdmWit ... 1` and $T_u \equiv T_{u'} \pmod{g_S^2}$), and $|\det A_{\mathfrak{p}}|^2 = q_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-m}$ accounts for dividing $G_Z$ by $g_S^m$ in $D_Z$.

3. **Admissible generic partner set `EZ`, nilradical orbits `cntE`, divided lattice `MMof`, and Jordan norm `nM` (`CntLocal.lean:99–116`, `IsPrimitiveSub.lean:139–278`, `SameGenusFix.lean:49–118`, `StepsR.lean:23–42`):**
   - For a row lattice $\Lambda$, `EZ`$(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z)$ consists of all $F' \in \mathrm{LFlag}(V, r_S, T_{u'})$ satisfying five conditions: (i) `IsTerm`: `IsLift`($\Lambda, F'$), `IsAdmissible`($\Lambda, g_S, \mathrm{lf}(F_0), F'$), and $G_Z(T_u, T_{u'}, Z) \neq 0$; (ii) `AdmWit`($\Lambda, g_S, \mathrm{lf}(F_0), F', I_{r_S + 1}$): $\mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl})_b - \mathrm{fvec}(F_0)_b \in g_S \Lambda_P$ for every prime $P \mid g_S$ of $\mathcal{O}_E$; (iii) `IsGeneric`($\Lambda, g_S, \mathrm{lf}(F_0), F'$): there exists an upper-unitriangular witness $\xi \in M_{r_S + 1}(\mathcal{O}_E)$ such that $\mathrm{BLat}(\Lambda, F_0, F') := \Lambda \cap U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')$ localizes at every $P \mid g_S$ to $\mathrm{Mplus}(\Lambda, g_S, F_0, F', \xi) := (\Lambda \cap X(F_0)) + \mathcal{O}_E z_0 + \sum_b \mathcal{O}_E g_S^{-1}(\mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl})_b - \mathrm{frameAct}(\mathrm{fvec}(F_0), \xi)_b)$ and at every $P \nmid g_S$ to $\mathrm{UU}(\Lambda, F_0, F') := (\Lambda \cap X(F_0)) + \mathcal{O}_E z_0 + (\Lambda \cap X(F')) + \mathcal{O}_E z'$; (iv) `FrameOK`($\Lambda, F'$): `IsFixedConv`($\Lambda, g_S, F'$) and $\Lambda \cap X(F') = \mathrm{XS}(F'.\mathrm{fl})$; and (v) $\widehat{Z}(F_0, F') = Z$.
   - Let $G_0(F_0) := \{g \in U(V, h_T) : \forall a,\, g(e_a) = e_a \land g(z_0) = z_0\} \leq \operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$, $H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda) := \{h \in G_0(F_0) : h(\Lambda) = \Lambda\}$, and define the unipotent nilradical (`CntLocal.lean:99–101`)
     $$
     N_0(F_0, \Lambda) := \bigl\{n \in H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda) : \forall x \in X(F_0)^\perp,\ n(x) - x \in X(F_0)\bigr\} \trianglelefteq H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda),
     $$
     acting on $F' = (\mathrm{fl}, z') \in E_Z$ by moving its frame $(n(e'_a), n(z'))$; $\mathrm{cnt}_E(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z) := |E_Z(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z) / N_0(F_0, \Lambda)|$.
   - On the glued side (`StepsR.lean:23–42`), for $(F_0, F')$ with $F'.z_t = F'.\mathrm{fl}.z$ set $U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F') := U_{\mathrm{base}}(F_0) + U_{\mathrm{base}}(F'.\mathrm{fl})$, $\mathrm{UUS}(F_0, F') := \mathrm{XS}(F_0) + \mathcal{O}_E z_0 + \mathrm{XS}(F'.\mathrm{fl}) + \mathcal{O}_E z'$, and $\mathrm{MMg}(F_0, F') := \sum_b \mathcal{O}_E\,\mathrm{fvec}(F_0)_b + \sum_b \mathcal{O}_E\, g_S^{-1}\bigl(\mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl})_b - \mathrm{fvec}(F_0)_b\bigr)$, and define the Dedekind-patched $\mathcal{O}_E$-submodule
     $$
     \mathrm{MMof}(F_0, F') := \bigcap_{P \in \operatorname{Spec}_{\neq 0}(\mathcal{O}_E)} \begin{cases} \mathrm{MMg}(F_0, F')_P & \text{if } g_S \in P, \\ \mathrm{UUS}(F_0, F')_P & \text{if } g_S \notin P, \end{cases}
     $$
     together with $\mathrm{GlueSet}(h_T, U_{\mathrm{Pair}}, \mathrm{MMof}) := \{\Lambda \subset V \text{ unimodular } \mathcal{O}_E\text{-lattice} : \Lambda \cap U_{\mathrm{Pair}} = \mathrm{MMof}(F_0, F')\}$, $\mathrm{isomFix}(h_T, U_{\mathrm{Pair}}) := \{g \in U(V, h_T) : g|_{U_{\mathrm{Pair}}} = \mathrm{id}_{U_{\mathrm{Pair}}}\}$, and $\operatorname{stabFix}(U_{\mathrm{Pair}}, \Lambda) := \{g \in \mathrm{isomFix}(h_T, U_{\mathrm{Pair}}) : g(\Lambda) = \Lambda\}$. (Because $g_S$ is coprime to $\mathfrak{D}_E$, $\mathrm{XS}(F_0)_P = \bigoplus_a \mathcal{O}_{E,P} e_a$ at $P \mid g_S$, so $\mathrm{MMof}(F_0, F')$ glues the exact local models of `IsGeneric` at $\xi = I_{r_S + 1}$.)
   - Finally (`SameGenusFix.lean:49–118`), for any **$h_T$-integral** full $\mathcal{O}_E$-lattice $M$ in a nondegenerate $m$-dimensional subspace $U \subset V$ ($h_T|_U$ nondegenerate) and maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p} \subset \mathcal{O}_F$, a local Jordan orthogonalization $(b_1, \ldots, b_m)$ of $M_{\mathfrak{p}}$ (unramified $E_{\mathfrak{p}}/F_{\mathfrak{p}}$) satisfies $h_T(b_i, b_j) = \delta_{ij} a_i$ with $a_i \in \mathcal{O}_{F, \mathfrak{p}} \setminus \{0\}$ ($\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak{p}}(a_i) \geq 0$); set $c_J(h_T, U, M, \mathfrak{p}) := \min_{(b,a)} \#\{i : \mathfrak{p} \mid a_i\} \leq m$ and $v_J(h_T, U, M, \mathfrak{p}) := \min_{(b,a)} \sum_{i=1}^m \operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak{p}}(a_i)$, and define the intrinsic Jordan norm $\mathrm{nM}(F, h_T, U, M) := \prod_{\mathfrak{p}} (N_{F/\mathbb{Q}}\mathfrak{p})^{v_J(h_T, U, M, \mathfrak{p})}$. (In `SameGenusFix.lean:49–68`, $c_J$ and $v_J$ are formally defined for arbitrary submodules via $\inf \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ over `IsJordanIn`, which returns $0$ when no integral Jordan basis exists, e.g. $M_{\mathfrak{p}} = \varpi_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{E,\mathfrak{p}}$; Hypotheses A.3(2) and A.4 evaluate $c_J, v_J, \mathrm{nM}$ solely when $M = \mathrm{MMof}(F_0, F'_{\mathrm{ref}})$ is $h_T$-integral.)

**Proposition A.5 (Unconditional Quotient-Stabilizer Deduction of `RowsGroupoid` and Conditional Deduction of `FormulaExplicit`; [1, `IsometryFixFlagAnisotropicVectorOfFixFrame.lean`, `Assemble.lean:158–248`]).** *Under Hypotheses A.1–A.2, `RowsGroupoid` (the first display of Theorem 5.2(1)) holds unconditionally; together with Hypotheses A.3–A.4, `FormulaExplicit` (the second display of Theorem 5.2(1)) holds.*

*Proof.* Fix $Z$ with $G_Z(T_u, T_{u'}, Z)$ totally positive and $F'_{\mathrm{ref}}$ from Hypothesis A.1, and write $U := U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F'_{\mathrm{ref}})$, $M := \mathrm{MMof}(F_0, F'_{\mathrm{ref}})$. We prove `RowsGroupoid` in four steps following `IsometryFixFlagAnisotropicVectorOfFixFrame.lean`:

1. **Free action of $N_0(F_0, \Lambda)$ on $E_Z$ (`eq_one_of_fix`):** Suppose $\Lambda$ is a row lattice, $F' \in E_Z(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z)$, and $n \in N_0(F_0, \Lambda)$ fixes $F'$ ($n(e'_a) = e'_a$, $n(z') = z'$). Since $n \in G_0(F_0)$ also fixes $e_a$ and $z_0$, $n$ acts as the identity on $U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')$. By Hypothesis A.1 (`PerpDef`), $V = U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F') \oplus U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')^\perp$. For any $x \in U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')^\perp \subseteq X(F_0)^\perp$, the definition of $N_0(F_0, \Lambda)$ gives $n(x) - x \in X(F_0)$, while $n \in U(V, h_T)$ fixing $U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')$ preserves $U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')^\perp$ so $n(x) - x \in U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')^\perp$. Because $X(F_0) \subseteq U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')$, $X(F_0) \cap U_{\mathrm{Pair}}(F_0, F')^\perp = \{0\}$, forcing $n(x) = x$ and hence $n = \mathrm{id}_V$.
2. **Stabilizer bijection under a free normal subgroup (`card_stab_q`):** Let a group $H$ act on a set $S$ and let $N \trianglelefteq H$ act **freely** on $S$. For any $t \in S$, the projection $\pi : H \to H / N$ restricts to a **bijection** $\operatorname{Stab}_H(t) \xrightarrow{\sim} \operatorname{Stab}_{H / N}([t]_N)$: if $h \in \operatorname{Stab}_H(t) \cap N$, freeness gives $h = 1$ (injectivity); if $(h N)\cdot [t]_N = [t]_N$, then $h\cdot t = n\cdot t$ for some $n \in N$, so $n^{-1} h \in \operatorname{Stab}_H(t)$ and $\pi(n^{-1} h) = (n^{-1} h)N = h(h^{-1} n^{-1} h)N = h N$ since $N \trianglelefteq H$ (surjectivity).
3. **Integral hyperbolic lifting (`card_quot`) and two-lattice row reduction (`class_red`):** Notice first that lifting only diagonal units of an upper-triangular restriction $A \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathrm{XS}(F_0))$ fails already on $A = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, which has unit diagonal entries without fixing the ordered frame; the Lean proof establishes single-lattice quotient surjectivity (`card_quot`) and two-lattice row reduction (`class_red`) via full integral hyperbolic splitting:
   - *(3a) Single-lattice quotient isomorphism $H_{\mathrm{sub}} / N_0 \cong \operatorname{autLz}$ (`SaturatedPf.lean:973, 1183–1256`, `IsometryFixFlagAnisotropicVectorOfFixFrame.lean:572–606`):* For any row lattice $\Lambda$, since $\Lambda^\# = \Lambda$ is unimodular and $\Lambda \cap X(F_0) = \mathrm{XS}(F_0) = \bigoplus_{a=0}^{r_S-1}\mathfrak{D}_E^{-(r_S-a)} e_a$ is saturated, `split_row_iso` decomposes $\Lambda = \mathrm{XS}(F_0) \oplus L \oplus \mathrm{XS}^\vee(\mathbf{f})$ integrally for a dual isotropic $r_S$-tuple $\mathbf{f} = (f_0, \ldots, f_{r_S-1})$ ($h_T(e_a, f_b) = \delta_{ab}$, spanning $Y(\mathbf{f}) := \bigoplus_a E f_a$ with $\mathrm{XS}^\vee(\mathbf{f}) := \bigoplus_{a=0}^{r_S-1}\mathfrak{D}_E^{r_S-a} f_a$) and unimodular orthogonal complement $L = \Lambda \cap (X(F_0) \oplus Y(\mathbf{f}))^\perp \ni z_0$, with $\Lambda$-integral orthogonal projection $\mathrm{pj}_{e, f} : V \to (X(F_0) \oplus Y(\mathbf{f}))^\perp$. (In coordinates on $X(F_0) \oplus L \oplus Y(\mathbf{f})$, any upper-triangular $A \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathrm{XS}(F_0))$ lifts to the isometry $A \oplus I_L \oplus (A^*)^{-1}$, preserving $\mathrm{XS}(F_0)$, $\mathrm{XS}^\vee(\mathbf{f})$, and $z_0 \in L$.) Intrinsically, for any $\gamma \in \operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$ with $\gamma(\Lambda) = \Lambda$, `bmap` defines $\beta(v) := (v - \mathrm{pj}_{e, f}(v)) + \mathrm{pj}_{e, f}(\gamma(\mathrm{pj}_{e, f}(v)))$. Because $\gamma$ preserves $X(F_0)$, $X(F_0)^\perp = X(F_0) \oplus (X(F_0) \oplus Y(\mathbf{f}))^\perp$, $\Lambda$, and $z_0 \in L$, $\mathrm{pj}_{e, f} \circ \gamma|_L$ is an isometry of $(L, h_T|_L)$ fixing $z_0$ and preserving $L$, while $\beta$ acts as the identity on $X(F_0) \oplus Y(\mathbf{f})$; hence $\beta \in H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda)$ fixes $(e_0, \ldots, e_{r_S-1}, z_0)$ pointwise and induces the identical map on $W_{\mathrm{bQ}}(F_0) = X(F_0)^\perp / X(F_0)$ as $\gamma$ ($\beta(x) - \gamma(x) \in X(F_0)$ for all $x \in X(F_0)^\perp$). Thus $H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda)$ surjects onto $\operatorname{autLz}(F_0, \Lambda)$ with kernel $N_0(F_0, \Lambda)$, giving $Q_\Lambda := H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda) / N_0(F_0, \Lambda) \xrightarrow{\sim} \operatorname{autLz}(F_0, \Lambda)$ (`card_quot`). Applying orbit-stabilizer to $Q_\Lambda$ acting on $\mathcal{O}_\Lambda := E_Z(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z) / N_0(F_0, \Lambda)$ and Step 2 (`card_stab_q`) yields
     $$
     w_{\mathrm{row}}(F_0, \Lambda)\,\mathrm{cnt}_E(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z) = \frac{|\mathcal{O}_\Lambda|}{|Q_\Lambda|} = \sum_{[F']_{H_{\mathrm{sub}}} \in \mathcal{O}_\Lambda / Q_\Lambda} \frac{1}{|\operatorname{Stab}_{Q_\Lambda}([F']_{N_0})|} = \sum_{[F']_{H_{\mathrm{sub}}}} \frac{1}{|\operatorname{Stab}_{H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda)}(F')|}.
     $$
   - *(3b) Two-lattice row reduction (`class_red`, `IsometryFixFlagAnisotropicVectorOfFixFrame.lean:839–970`, `BridgeRowsLift.lean:22–77, 158–265`):* Suppose $\Lambda, \Lambda'$ are two row lattices for $F_0$ and $\gamma \in \operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$ satisfies $\gamma(\Lambda) = \Lambda'$. Applying `bmap` from (3a) to a splitting of $\Lambda$ produces $\beta \in G_0(F_0)$ such that $\Lambda_1 := \beta(\Lambda)$ is unimodular with $\Lambda_1 \cap X(F_0) = \mathrm{XS}(F_0) = \Lambda' \cap X(F_0)$, $z_0 \in \Lambda_1 \cap \Lambda'$, and identical projected lattice in $X(F_0)^\perp / X(F_0)$ ($\gamma(\beta^{-1}(x)) - x \in X(F_0)$ for all $x \in \Lambda_1 \cap X(F_0)^\perp$). Because $E/F$ is unramified CM, $\operatorname{Tr}_{E/F}(\mathcal{O}_E) = \mathcal{O}_F$ furnishes $\theta \in \mathcal{O}_E$ with $\theta + \bar{\theta} = 1$ (`rlift_spl_of_retr`, `BridgeRowsLift.lean:22–77`), enabling the unipotent shear lift `rlift_main` (`BridgeRowsLift.lean:158–265`, `IsometryFixFlagAnisotropicVectorOfFixFrame.lean:880–965`) to construct a unipotent isometry $n \in G_0(F_0)$ fixing $(e_0, \ldots, e_{r_S-1}, z_0)$ pointwise and acting trivially on $X(F_0)^\perp / X(F_0)$ with $n(\Lambda_1) = \Lambda'$. Setting $c := n \circ \beta \in G_0(F_0)$ gives $c(\Lambda) = \Lambda'$, reducing $\operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$-equivalence of row lattices to $G_0(F_0)$-equivalence (`class_red`).
4. **Witt transport to $\mathrm{GlueSet}$ (`MMChar`, `rowsGroupoid_of`, lines 1012–1024):** For any $\Lambda \in \mathrm{what}_2$ and $F' \in E_Z(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z)$ ($z'_t = z'$), both $(F_0, F')$ and $(F_0, F'_{\mathrm{ref}})$ span nondegenerate $m$-dimensional subspaces with identical nonsingular Gram matrix $\mathrm{Ga}(T_u, T_{u'}, Z)$, so Witt's theorem furnishes $\sigma \in G_0(F_0)$ sending $\mathrm{fvec}(F'.\mathrm{fl}) \mapsto \mathrm{fvec}(F'_{\mathrm{ref}}.\mathrm{fl})$ (hence $\sigma(F') = F'_{\mathrm{ref}}$). Transporting $(\Lambda, F')$ by $\sigma \in G_0(F_0)$ gives $\mathrm{IsRowLat}(F_0, \sigma(\Lambda))$ and $F'_{\mathrm{ref}} \in E_Z(T_{u'}, F_0, \sigma(\Lambda), Z)$, which by Hypothesis A.2 (`MMChar`) holds iff $\sigma(\Lambda) \in \mathrm{GlueSet}(h_T, U, M)$. Conversely, every $\Lambda_{\mathrm{glue}} \in \mathrm{GlueSet}(h_T, U, M)$ satisfies $\mathrm{IsRowLat}(F_0, \Lambda_{\mathrm{glue}})$ and $F'_{\mathrm{ref}} \in E_Z(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda_{\mathrm{glue}}, Z)$ by `MMChar`; since $\mathrm{what}_2$ is an irredundant complete set of $\operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$-representatives of row lattices, there is a unique $\Lambda \in \mathrm{what}_2$ with $\gamma(\Lambda) = \Lambda_{\mathrm{glue}}$ for some $\gamma \in \operatorname{stab}_F(F_0)$, and by Step 3b (`class_red`) there exists $c \in G_0(F_0)$ fixing $F_0$ pointwise with $c(\Lambda) = \Lambda_{\mathrm{glue}}$. Then $F' := c^{-1}(F'_{\mathrm{ref}}) \in E_Z(T_{u'}, F_0, \Lambda, Z)$ satisfies $c(F') = F'_{\mathrm{ref}}$, uniquely determining the $H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda)$-orbit of $F'$ for the $\mathrm{isomFix}(h_T, U)$-orbit of $\Lambda_{\mathrm{glue}}$ (if $g \circ c_1(\Lambda) = c_2(\Lambda)$ with $g \in \mathrm{isomFix}(h_T, U) \leq G_0(F_0)$, then $h := c_2^{-1} g c_1 \in H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda)$ satisfies $h(c_1^{-1}(F'_{\mathrm{ref}})) = c_2^{-1}(F'_{\mathrm{ref}})$). Moreover, $h \in H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda)$ fixes $F'$ iff $\sigma h \sigma^{-1} \in \mathrm{isomFix}(h_T, U)$ preserves $\sigma(\Lambda)$, establishing $|\operatorname{Stab}_{H_{\mathrm{sub}}(F_0, \Lambda)}(F')| = |\operatorname{stabFix}(U, \sigma(\Lambda))|$ and proving `RowsGroupoid`.

Finally (`Assemble.lean:173–248`), if $M$ is not $h_T$-integral, $\mathrm{GlueSet}(h_T, U, M) = \varnothing$ so `RowsGroupoid` gives $\nu = 0$, while Hypothesis A.3(1) gives $\prod_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathrm{dens}_{\mathfrak{p}}(Z) = 0$.
