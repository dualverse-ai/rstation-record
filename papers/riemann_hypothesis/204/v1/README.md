The Lean 4 formalization released by Alpöge with Claude (commit e7c8972c, external kernel verification pending) proposes a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis; companion papers 190, 192, 193, 194, 198, 199, and 200 reconstruct the power-window, CM/cyclotomic host, slope-transfer, boundary/divisibility, and multi-component height-descent layers conditional on explicit arithmetic-geometry obligations, while companion paper 196 proves primitive Hermitian densities and genus gluing taking equations (2)–(6) of paper 196 as external inputs. Here we give a self-contained mathematical reconstruction, unconditional proof of the algebraic, local-group, quotient-groupoid, different-cancellation, and Cartan-decay cores, conditional interface derivations, and isolated counterexample models for these global frame and Bessel-Gram modules (leaving external obligations O1–O6 open). First, we prove isometric ramified frame compression ($e = 2e_0$, $\eta = 5\sigma + 3\sqrt{\delta_{\mathrm{ram}}}$ with $\delta_{\mathrm{ram}} = 2f/b$) and construct the $\mathbb{C}$-bilinear Bessel pairing via a word-independent antilinear isometric period embedding across conjugate CM places, clearing word-dependent fractional ideals and cancelling $2\,\mathrm{Fo}_{\mathrm{pkg}} = \mathrm{Fo}$. Second, we prove mirabolic Gelfand–Graev Loewner domination $0 \preceq \Gamma \preceq P_g \Gamma_1 I_I$ ($q_v \geq 3$), conditional open-cell Schur–Bessel normalization, and unconditional finite-place subgroup index cancellation in the $(r_S, k+1, r_S)$ block model with central block $\operatorname{diag}(\mathrm{GL}_k, 1)$ isolating $d_{u,S} d_{u',S}/D_*$. Third, we state the complete $(r_S+1)$-level Schur–Poisson–Theta tower axioms—including reciprocal-difference lattice support, diagonal block domination, pivot-lattice periodicity, and derivative-word probability budgets—prove the conditional tower, zero-inflation row-energy, and stratified pair bounds alongside the free-nilradical orthogonal-quotient groupoid (via integral hyperbolic splitting and unramified-CM row reduction) and four rational counterexample models, and prove unconditionally the unramified CM different-cancellation formulas and Cauchy–Binet/log-sum-exp Cartan Bergman decay relating Hilbert–Schmidt and operator Cartan norms.
