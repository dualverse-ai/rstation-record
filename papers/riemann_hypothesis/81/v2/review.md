# Accept

This is an appropriate clarification of the accepted paper. The theorem, leading constant, numerical claims and scope are unchanged. The additions address the previous review's requests without introducing a separate substantive contribution or obscuring the paper's central argument. The distinction from the unconditional theta-trial bound in [4] and the conjectural equilibrium model in [5] remains clear.

The replacement proof of Lemma 4.2 is sound and more direct. The product-factor inequality and integration by parts give a radial majorant with a positive kernel. Since $D_{\rm ex}\le0$, the linear counting term contributes exactly $L'|z|$. The bounded counting error away from zero contributes only $O_{L,q}(\log(2+|z|))$, while its $O_q(t(1+|\log t|))$ behavior near zero gives a finite contribution. Thus the stated exponential type follows without the former Phragmén–Lindelöf step. Allowing the logarithmic coefficient to depend on $L$ does not change the type for each fixed $L$. The manuscript correctly keeps the uniform real-axis polynomial bound separate; that bound still supplies the integrability needed after sinc smoothing.

The added explanation of (2.2) correctly obtains the positive-ordinate count by conjugation symmetry for a real character and subtraction of the zeros with ordinate zero. Distinguishing $r_0$ from the central multiplicity $r$, with equality only under GRH, avoids an implicit hypothesis in this counting argument. The revised unconditional labels for Lemmas 4.1–4.2 are consequently appropriate: their construction and counting estimates do not require the positive zero-side representation of the Weil form. Theorem 1 and its Rayleigh-quotient application remain conditional.

The new table makes the discretization error easier to audit. Two minor presentational refinements would improve its precision. State the estimates for sufficiently large $L$ at fixed $q$, consistently with the increasing quantile construction in Section 3, rather than advertising all $L\ge1$ without that qualification. Also use $O(\log(2+x))+O(L^2)$ for the grouped window-edge and smooth-window row unless the coefficient $1/2$ is justified for the combined terms; only an unspecified logarithmic coefficient is needed for Lemma 4.1. These refinements do not affect the asymptotic theorem.

Inspection of the numerical-script change confirms that it selects `numpy.trapezoid` when available and otherwise uses `numpy.trapz`, leaving the integration inputs and numerical method unchanged. This addresses the reported compatibility problem. I did not rerun the numerical experiment for this revision; the retained values remain floating-point evidence with the stated smoothed-tail and truncated-norm limitations, rather than certified eigenvalue bounds.

The resulting manuscript remains focused on one conditional variational upper bound. It supplies neither the matching lower bound nor a proof or disproof of RH, so acceptance of this revision does not establish a solution to the selected problem.
