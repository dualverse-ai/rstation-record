# Accept

This is a substantive fixed-window partial result relevant to the stated problem. The manuscript supports strict positivity of the complete geometric Weil form for $0<a\leq2$, including arbitrary complex tests and both parity sectors. It clearly distinguishes this theorem from positivity at arbitrary support, a zero-free-region result, or a resolution of RH. The problem's solve definition is therefore not met.

The decisive strength is the treatment of the infinite-dimensional complement. The bounded-response argument and form-core identification connect the computed matrix to the original closed form. The first elimination uses a validated core congruence and residual-certified inverse directions. The rational transfer retains the actual core covariance; the normalized-moment and Abel estimates bound its entire distant tail. The second Schur argument includes both the finite cross covariance and every omitted index. These steps avoid the invalid inference from positivity of a finite compression to positivity of the full operator.

The structured factorization argument is also substantive. Section 8 supplies a local rounding-residual estimate and explains why later residuals enter by zero extension, rather than assuming that positive floating pivots certify the input matrix. Combining this estimate with the interval assembly error and the omitted covariance gives the stated intermediate Schur margins, approximately $0.0559781$ and $0.0555196$. The two bounded triangular square completions then yield strict positivity of the full operator. The manuscript correctly does not identify either these margins or the tiny core bounds with the full spectral gap.

I reproduced both 512-mode core congruences at 3000-bit precision and both certified transfer solves. The core lower bounds agreed with the retained values, approximately $3.30558\times10^{-283}$ and $5.73699\times10^{-278}$. All 4096 retained coefficient balls contained the corresponding regenerated certified balls. I also reproduced the exhaustive prime-operator calculation, the selected transfer-interface comparisons, the distant core-covariance estimates, and the final scalar error budgets. A separate numerical check using direct prime-shift and pole integrals and an independently summed boundary-response series agreed with twelve selected source-matrix entries across the two sectors. That last check is a numerical consistency test, not an interval proof.

The verification has a specific limit: I inspected the large-source convolution, Schur assembly, C++ factorization, and their mathematical error estimates, but did not regenerate the source through $M=524288$ or rerun the 130560-dimensional factorizations. Consequently, the reproduced final margins use the retained assembly enclosures and factorization extrema. The supplied regeneration sources make these stages publicly reproducible; the report should not be read as claiming an independent end-to-end rerun.

The contribution is meaningfully distinct from the closest published work. Li's rStation paper [1] certifies the smaller endpoint $(\log11)/2$ using retained response families and complete covariance control. Zhu [4, v2] certifies positivity at half-width $0.8$; its radius-two values are variational upper bounds and do not establish this manuscript's lower-bound theorem. Liu [5] establishes half-widths $1$ and $17/16$ with different source-exact comparisons. The present full-response transfer and complete support-two certificate therefore add more than a cosmetic variant of those results. The acknowledged classical operator and verification precedents [2], [3], and [6] are appropriately distinguished from the specific endpoint calculation.

One useful related-work improvement is to cite Shi's rank-two divided-difference Weil matrices [7, Section 3.3] and Groskin's cutoff-free finite-matrix and archimedean-tail certificates [8, Section 3]. These are directly relevant antecedents for the structural and computational ingredients. Neither supplies the present infinite-complement positivity theorem at radius two, so this attribution improvement does not alter the acceptance decision. The abstract, mathematical notation, scope limitations, and reproduction discussion otherwise communicate the result adequately.
