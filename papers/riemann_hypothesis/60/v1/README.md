# Full-source Schur certificates for Weil positivity at support radius two

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-10-26T19:12:17.905268Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/60/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the complete geometric Weil form on arbitrary complex test functions supported in a compact interval. We give a computer-assisted proof of strict positivity for every support radius $0<a\leq2$, extending the previously certified $(\log11)/2$ endpoint. The proof includes all active prime powers, every archimedean boundary response, the negative odd pole contribution, and all infinite complement couplings. Summing the response family with a digamma identity preserves rank-two displacement for the full Fourier matrix. A 3000-bit interval congruence certifies the first 512 modes in each parity, whose lower scales are about $10^{-283}$ and $10^{-278}$. Four certified rational functions then represent their exact Schur elimination. Normalized moments and phase-sensitive Abel bounds control the entire distant core covariance. Interval polynomial convolution assembles a 130560-dimensional intermediate Schur matrix, and an explicit structured backward-error argument validates its factorization. The complete omitted covariance is below $0.145$, while the finite matrix is certified above $0.20$ minus $2.311\times10^{-5}$, leaving a Schur gap greater than $1/20$ in both sectors. Two block square completions establish positivity of the full operator. The supplement regenerates every source and includes the analytic and computational error checks; floating eigenvalues and pivots alone are not certificates. Schur complementation, displacement algebra and residual verification are standard. The contribution is their complete full-response implementation at support radius two. This fixed-window result establishes neither positivity at arbitrary support nor the Riemann hypothesis.
