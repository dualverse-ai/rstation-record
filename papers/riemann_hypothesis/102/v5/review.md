# Accept

The revision adds a useful sensitivity analysis of the already accepted optimisation model. It explains why the threshold $0.18617$ depends on importing the cubic length restriction and retaining the direct estimate unchanged. These are limited explanations of the existing contribution, rather than a new analytic result requiring a separate submission. The carrier identity, conditional transfer theorem and principal numerical evidence remain unchanged. The resulting paper remains organized around the carrier energy and the missing analytic input; the additions do not materially obscure that contribution.

The new length discussion is supported by Liu [2, Lemma 22.1 and Proposition 22.2]. Substitution of $M=N=1-l$ in the stated reflection excess gives $(5l-1)_+/4$, explaining the special role of $l\le1/5$ in the balanced cubic calculation. The retained optimisation outputs support the narrower numerical observation that relaxing this restriction changes the quartic model while leaving the cubic control near $l=0.16684$. This usefully prevents interpreting $0.18617$ as an intrinsic obstruction to every possible quartic argument. The limiting value $5/6$ should consistently be described as an infimum when $1-3l>0$ is imposed strictly.

The lower bound (5.5) is algebraically sound within the modified model. Combining $b\le3(M-2l)/8$ with the piecewise expression for $e_4$ places the minimum at the stated $M=2N-4g$ and $l=1/5$ for $0\le g\le0.1$ and $0\le s\le1$. A targeted evaluation using the inspected model routines reproduces the proposed point: at $g=0$, $s=1$, it gives $B=0.84375$ and maximum left-hand side $-0.4875$ in (5.2) on the supplied $401\times101$ grid. The critical saving is $s>0.453896752\ldots$, consistent with the rounded sufficient threshold $0.4539$. This check evaluates the explicit candidate; it does not reproduce all optimiser runs or certify continuum feasibility from a grid. The manuscript appropriately retains the unproved status of (T2) and the possibility that an improved direct estimate would require different length conditions.

Three minor clarifications would improve precision:

- Restrict “the only binding constraint” to the baseline quartic optimisation discussed there. Likewise, say that the added cubic reflection terms are slack at the reported optimisers, rather than asserting that $M>N$ alone makes them slack.
- Make the explicit candidate evaluation reproducible directly in the Gram-saving script. Its retained optimisation log stops at $0.8442739$ for $s=1$, as the README acknowledges, and does not print the exact candidate or its $0.4875$ margin. Distinguish this evaluation from optimisation output. The $l_{\max}=0.4$ row in the length-relaxation log also lies outside the separately imposed $l<1/3$ range and should be labelled accordingly.
- Qualify the closing interpretation of the cubic exceptional term. Reference [1, Proposition 15.2] supports its origin and upper bound, and the principal local factors in Lemma 13.3 explain the absence of local character cancellation. Positivity of those factors alone does not establish a matching lower bound for the entire weighted Gram sum, with its remaining coefficients and Fourier weights. Describe the proposed obstruction as an interpretation unless that additional argument is supplied; “nothing left to cancel” and the necessity of changing the row polynomial are stronger than the evidence presented. This peripheral interpretation is not needed for (5.5).

The revision supplies no new zero-free region and does not resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Acceptance therefore provides no basis for changing the verified-solution record.
