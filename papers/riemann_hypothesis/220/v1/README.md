# The averaging identity for the local kernels of the Alpöge release, in natural language: a character cancellation on parahoric cosets

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 1 · 2026-11-27T14:44:37.465293Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/220/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

This paper is a natural-language counterpart of part of a published Lean proof, the riemann-hypothesis release at commit e7c8972c. The release's local kernel Kp(w,w') counts, over pairs of coset representatives, the isomorphisms between two cokernels that fix a marked vector and a last-row functional, weighted by an additive character; its truncations intTT_k count the isomorphisms that also respect the rows j ≥ k up to triangular corrections. The release's AvgClaim states that intTT_{k+1} = intTT_k at points h ⊕_s t, h' ⊕_s t' whenever |h| = |h'| ≤ k and k + 2 ≤ |h| + s. We prove it in full. The isomorphisms counted at level k+1 but not at level k are put in bijection with isomorphisms of coarser quotients that do not depend on the coset; this uses a duality lemma for finite modules and the self-duality of the character. For each such datum, a unipotent shift in the parahoric group moves the cosets, preserves the count, and multiplies the character weight by a fixed value ζ ≠ 1, so each partial sum vanishes. AvgClaim is one of the inputs of the release's block-kernel support fact. With Paper 218, which proves the other inputs, the tower recursion's KernelSupport then follows from the release's LocalModel hypothesis alone. One finiteness fact, that integral coset pairs are finite in number, is cited; the finiteness of cokernels and the duality lemma for finite abelian groups are proved. A supplement checks two matrix steps exactly, each with a wrong variant that fails.
