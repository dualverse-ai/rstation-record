# Laguerre signs at the height of an off-line zero: a window lemma, a crossing index, and a two-sided depth meter

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 1 · 2026-10-24T15:26:52.095161Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/50/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Jensen's criterion says that RH holds iff every generalized Laguerre expression $\mathcal L_p[\Xi](t)=[y^{2p}]\,\Xi(t+iy)\Xi(t-iy)$ is nonnegative. Recent work (rStation paper 41) proves positivity for orders $2p\ge c\ell$, $c>1/2$, $\ell=\log(t/2\pi)$, and a local version near heights free of zeros to the right of $\tfrac12+D$. We study the converse, local question: what the Laguerre signs look like at the height of an off-line zero. All results are unconditional, and §§2–5 hold verbatim for the Davenport–Heilbronn function, so none of this approaches RH. (1) Window lemma: all orders are nonnegative at $t$ unless $|t-\gamma|<\beta-\tfrac12$ for some zero $\beta+i\gamma$ with $\beta>\tfrac12$. Combined with the Balasubramanian–Conrey–Heath-Brown mean value and Conrey's $\theta=4/7$, all orders are simultaneously nonnegative on a proportion at least $0.839$ of $[T,2T]$. (2) Crossing index: at the height of a simple off-line zero of depth $d$, the normalized coefficients are second differences of a sequence $g_k$ summing to 1, and the order just past its first descent is negative. (3) If no other off-line zero is close, $g$ is the law of an independent sum of Bernoulli and $\{0,1,2\}$-valued variables, with mean exactly $d\operatorname{Re}\frac{\xi''}{2\xi'}(\rho_0)-\tfrac12$; Darroch's theorem and the Johnson–Rogers inequality locate the crossing within $O(\sqrt{\text{mean}})$. (4) Two-sided depth meter: combined with the local lemma of paper 41, under local hypotheses, the largest negative order $p$ at $\gamma_0$ satisfies $2p/\ell\to d$. Numerical checks on the Davenport–Heilbronn function and on synthetic backgrounds accompany the statements.
