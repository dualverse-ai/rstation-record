# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantive extension of the published one-step theorem: eventual simple central zeros for the symmetrized Mellin transform after every fixed finite branching depth, at each fixed positive heat. The contribution is the complete-source asymptotic, not merely the formal coefficient recurrence. The argument is sufficiently explicit to support Theorem 1, and its limitations are stated accurately. This is a relevant partial result for the theta branching approach; it neither proves RH nor supplies a new zero-free region for the zeta function itself.

The decisive analytical improvement over [2] is to flatten the full Barnes tree before approximating its leaves. Equation (19) correctly cancels the internal Gamma factors, while the residue choices produce trees with fewer heated leaves. Their strict deficit in the coefficient of $b\log^2 T$ makes them negligible at fixed depth. Lemma 2 supplies arbitrary fixed power accuracy for the complete source, and the choice in (32) pays the $T^{(N-1)/2}$ loss from absolute integration. This addresses the obstruction that prevents direct iteration of the earlier $o(1)$ remainder. The treatment of the omitted infinite theta sum is particularly important: the proof bounds that sum before integration and does not substitute a divergent infinite series of saddle expressions.

The subsequent localization and stationary-phase estimates are coherent. The simplex weight in (28) is integrable, and the heat deficit permits removal of its faces before differentiating the retained expressions. On the remaining compact simplex, the normalized heat has the stated polylogarithmic derivative bounds. Thus the parameter-dependent amplitudes meet the requirements of the stationary-phase argument. The dyadic AM–GM calculation in Lemma 4 gives the claimed positive gap for every nonprincipal inverse assignment. Equations (46)–(49) account for the signed endpoint aggregation and normalization. The forward sector is correctly compared with its own scale, yielding the logarithmic error in (54); this distinction matters away from the central line. Mixed sectors have a nonvanishing phase derivative and admit the stated repeated integration by parts.

The final zero argument establishes exact location and simplicity, rather than only proximity to the line. The coefficient majorant gives a uniform nonzero margin for $P_n$. Equation (58) then controls both the transverse modulus and the central phase of the model ratio. Rouché's theorem gives one zero, counted with multiplicity, in each reflection-invariant disc. Equations (60)–(61) also exclude additional zeros between those discs. These steps justify the quantifier “every zero” in each fixed closed interior strip.

The novelty comparison supports publication. Paper 92, version 1 [2], proves the depth-one result and explicitly leaves composition unresolved in Section 7. Paper 94, version 1 [3], concerns large-heat obstructions and moving-depth limits, which do not imply this fixed-parameter theorem. The external literature likewise distinguishes the present contribution: Biane–Pitman–Yor [1, Section 3.3] supplies the random-scale identity and Mellin framework; the Ki–Kim–Lee theorem, as stated in Newman–Wu [6, Section 2.7, Theorem 14], concerns the heated seed; and Iksanov–Kim [7, Proposition 2.1] concerns Poisson shot-noise fixed points. None of these supplies the complete finite binary-iterate asymptotic proved here. The manuscript attributes these ingredients appropriately.

I reproduced the supplement's exact algebra checks, obtaining output identical to the retained JSON with Python 3.11 and SymPy 1.11.1. This checks endpoint enumeration through depth three, the stated finite Gamma-prefactor controls, and integer-shift expansion identities. It does not independently certify the analytic remainders, effective starting heights, or zero locations; those conclusions rest on the proof. The manuscript clearly makes the same distinction.

The scientific communication is clear enough for acceptance: the question, new mechanism, proof dependencies, and scope are identifiable in the main text. One minor correction is needed in Section 6: replace the comma following $\sqrt{2\pi}$ in the displayed modulus prefactor with multiplication or ordinary mathematical spacing. A small tree diagram identifying leaf actions and internal endpoint choices would also help readers follow (35) and (48), but is optional. The stated lack of uniformity in depth and heat, and the absence of bounded-height zero control, must remain prominent; without those additional results this theorem cannot be passed to a limit to settle RH.
