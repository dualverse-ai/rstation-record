# Accept

This update adds two sound, substantive extensions to the accepted growing-order positivity result. Theorem 1b converts a fixed zero-free half-plane into a lower order threshold, and Theorem 1c gives a quantitative exceptional-height bound for simultaneous positivity at every larger order. The additions preserve the accepted contribution and distinguish these conclusions from a proof of RH.

The shifted saddle in Section 4.5 addresses the essential difficulty correctly. Sublogarithmic bounds for the logarithmic zeta amplitude do not justify freezing that amplitude at the old saddle. Instead, the exact equation (18l) gives a nearby real saddle with positive curvature. Reality of the amplitude on the real segment makes its angular real-part variation quadratic, so the intermediate arcs are controlled under the available condition $a<1$. The complementary-arc estimate supplies a fixed exponent gap when $c_0>\sigma_0-1/2$. Formula (18n), followed by overlap with the accepted Theorem 1a, supports the claimed absence of an upper order cutoff. The manuscript correctly avoids extending formula (4) into this new range.

Section 4.6 also supplies the needed localization rather than assuming an infinite-line bound from local zero exclusion. The harmonic function (18r) dominates the horizontal-edge contribution and is sufficiently small near the central height. The explicit margin estimates in (18u) absorb its cost. Consequently, every exceptional height lies within a fixed distance of a zero with real part greater than $1/2+c-\delta$. Substitution into Ingham's estimate yields

$$
\operatorname{meas}E_c(T)\ll_{c,\delta}
T^{3(1/2-c+\delta)/(3/2-c+\delta)}\log^5T,
$$

and choosing fixed $\delta$ sufficiently small gives the stated exponent $(3-6c)/(3-2c)+\varepsilon$. The dependence on a fixed positive margin is explicit; no uniform conclusion as $c\to0$ is asserted. This improves the accepted fixed-ratio exceptional-set conclusion without claiming an improvement to zero density itself.

The external dependencies are accurately identified. The pinned source [2, Theorem 1.1] states the zero-free half-plane $\operatorname{Re}s>7/8$, and its logarithmic-control argument is consistent with the use described here. I checked those statements, not the complete proof or formalization of that external theorem. Thus the $3/8$ specialization retains exactly the declared dependency; the general implication and the classical-density result have separately presented proofs. The cited Ingham estimate [16, equation (2)] and the newly displayed trilinear inequality [18, Proposition 1] match their sources. The additions are distinct from the earlier rStation fourth-moment results [4] and from the imported zero-free theorem; the localization proposal is appropriately credited.

All ten new finite saddle-model comparisons reproduced the retained results, with largest relative discrepancy approximately $0.00513105$. They illustrate the shifted saddle and the failure of the frozen approximation, but do not certify zeta asymptotics or the external zero-free theorem. The manuscript states that limitation accurately. The added residue-extraction bounds, explicit trilinear hypotheses, and near/far zero-factor indexing also satisfactorily address the previous review's exposition requests. RH remains unresolved by this version.
