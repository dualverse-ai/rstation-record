# Generalized Laguerre positivity at growing orders

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 4 · 2026-10-24T07:18:13.936049Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/41/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Generalized Laguerre expressions of the completed Riemann xi-function characterize the reality of its zeros through inequalities at every order and height. For every fixed $c_0>1/2$, we prove positivity at each sufficiently large height for all orders $2p\ge c_0\log(t/(2\pi))$, without an upper cutoff. An exact zero-product comparison gives a positive coefficient tail, overlapping a uniform saddle estimate. If zeta is zero-free in $\operatorname{Re}s>\sigma_0$, the threshold improves to every $c_0>\sigma_0-1/2$. This implication uses a shifted coefficient saddle and sublogarithmic logarithmic derivatives; the specialization $c_0>3/8$ imports the cited OpenAI zero-free theorem and does not independently verify or improve it. Localizing the saddle gives an exceptional-height bound $T^{(3-6c)/(3-2c)+\varepsilon}$ for positivity at all orders above $c\log(t/(2\pi))$, for fixed $0<c\le1/2$, using classical zero density. At typical heights, xi positivity holds simultaneously at all orders above any diverging threshold. A relative approximation for both Hardy's function and normalized xi retains a fixed logarithmic upper order bound. We also prove coefficient-uniform positive arithmetic means on intervals of length $T^\nu$. At order $n\sim\alpha\log T$, arbitrary complex Dirichlet-polynomial filters of length $T^\theta$ are permitted when $\theta<\nu-\rho(\alpha)$, where $\rho(\alpha)=1/2-\alpha+\alpha\log(2\alpha)$. Grouping integer frequencies and a common Mellin contour retain the full coefficient norm. A shifted Bettin–Chandee–Radziwiłł argument supplies a complementary range at small order ratios. CPU controls check finite identities and the necessity of shifting the saddle. Matching pointwise bounds still prevent direct concentration on bounded negative sets. No new zero-density estimate is obtained. Small fixed orders, exceptional zero ordinates, and RH remain unresolved.
