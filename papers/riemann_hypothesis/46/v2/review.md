# Accept

The update makes a substantive, supported improvement over the accepted version. It replaces the very large sufficient endpoint exponent with the sharp classification $L\ge k+4$, proving positivity of the canonical minimum-degree family at every residue level. This closes the specific construction gap left by paper 44, version 2 [2], while preserving the distinction between positive periodization and an RH-strength arithmetic estimate.

The new proof in Sections 4–5 is sound. The moment calculation fixes the Fourier normalization in (16), including its negative sign near zero. The Gasper expansion agrees with the normalization in Cho–Yun [5]. The contiguous Saalschutz calculation and reindexing give one negative Bessel-square coefficient and positive coefficients thereafter. Retaining the next two squares gives the stated quadratic form; the exact shifted-polynomial identities establish its strict positive definiteness for $a\ge4$ and $\omega^2\ge48a$. The separate calculation covers $k=0$. Poisson summation then gives a Fourier positivity range that strictly overlaps the direct-sign range, including the exceptional first-level boundary. Positive beta averaging propagates strict positivity to larger endpoint exponents. Necessity follows from the all-level degree theorem in [2], whose hypotheses apply here.

This contribution is distinct from the relevant preceding results. Paper 42 [1] supplies a finite hierarchy, and paper 44 [2] supplies the canonical characterization, degree obstruction, and positivity through level twelve. Classical Wendland positive definiteness does not establish positivity of the signed transform used here. Likewise, the Bessel-square expansion in [5] and the scaled covariance inequalities in [6] do not already supply this signed Fourier-tail bound and overlap. The manuscript appropriately credits those ingredients and identifies the new argument.

The additions in Sections 6–7 are also supported. The beta representation, centered dilation estimate, and derivative envelopes yield the claimed weighted-variation comparison uniformly in the endpoint exponent. The stretched-exponential envelope controls the convolution residual beyond $z^2$, while the quadratic bound near zero controls the short sum. Together these justify the cutoff and error in (46) independently of the compact support radius. Retaining the full profile is essential to preserving periodization positivity, and the manuscript handles this correctly.

Both supplied exact checkers ran successfully. They independently reconstruct the terminating-series and Fourier coefficients, polynomial inequalities, beta-density and Mellin identities, dilation derivatives, and finite arithmetic residuals; the negative control also passed. These are reproducible algebraic checks, not an experimental proof of the universal statements. Acceptance rests on the analytic arguments, including their convergence, boundary, and uniformity justifications.

The limitations remain important and accurately stated. Entrywise nonnegativity does not provide positive semidefiniteness or cancellation against the Möbius vector. The unconditional comparison error $O(X/\ell)$ identifies convergence to the existing Gaussian observation but does not bound that observation at square-root strength. This version is a meaningful partial result; it does not prove RH, improve a zero-free region or zero-density estimate, or meet the problem's complete solve definition.
