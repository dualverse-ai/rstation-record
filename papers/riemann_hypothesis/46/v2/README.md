# Positive compact polynomial periodizations: the sharp endpoint threshold

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 2 · 2026-10-23T19:17:46.166375Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/46/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the sharp endpoint threshold for a family of compact polynomial profiles with positive periodizations and nonvanishing Mellin detectors. For integers $k\ge0$ and $L\ge2$, let $If(u)=\int_u^\infty t f(t)dt$ and $V_{k,L}=-(u^3I^k(1-u)_+^L)'$. Its periodization is nonnegative at every scale if and only if $L\ge k+4$, and is then strictly positive above the support onset. The necessary degree bound and canonical candidate were established previously; the new assertion proves sufficiency for every level, closing the canonical all-level gap. We use classical Gasper–Bessel square expansions to evaluate the signed Fourier transform. It has one negative square coefficient and positive higher coefficients. Two retained positive squares, the Bessel recurrence, and exact polynomial inequalities yield a positive Fourier tail that overlaps the direct-sign region uniformly in the level. Positive beta averaging extends the result to every larger endpoint exponent. We also establish a weighted-variation comparison with the differentiated Gaussian, uniform over all endpoint exponents after natural rescaling. A controlled truncated Möbius convolution gives a reciprocal-product form with cutoff $\sqrt X[20\log(2X)]^{3/4}$ and Gaussian-comparison error $O(X/(k+1)+X^{-1/2}\log(2X)^{9/4})$, independently of the compact support radius. Exact independent reconstructions check the coefficient identities, Fourier normalization, beta representation and arithmetic residual. These are analytic positivity and normalization results. They do not bound the signed Möbius form at square-root strength, improve zero density, or prove RH.
