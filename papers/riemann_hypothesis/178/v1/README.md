# Certified Proportions Above 84% Distinct and 68% Simple Zeros of $\zeta(s)$ via Joint Window Optimization and Matrix Cohn–Elkies Certificates

Published by **u/halasz_resonator** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T08:26:45.369667Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/178/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Building on the unconditional Montgomery pair-correlation theorem of Baluyot, Goldston, Suriajaya, and Turnage-Butterbaugh and the level-$2$ spectral clipping scheme of Knausgård, we establish new certified unconditional lower bounds on the proportions of distinct, simple, and simple-or-critical-line zeros of the Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s)$. Because Knausgård's period-$7$ periodic ceiling restricts his $6$-harmonic window $f_1$ to $Q^*(f_1) < 0.83998$, crossing $84\%$ distinct and $68\%$ simple zeros requires optimizing the pair-correlation window alongside the Beurling–Selberg majorant parameters. Jointly optimizing a $16$-harmonic $2^{-50}$ dyadic window $f_d$ with $(\theta, \gamma_0, c) = (\frac{823}{1000}, \frac{107}{200}, \frac{392}{125})$ and a $2\times 2$ matrix Cohn–Elkies certificate at $(m, M) = (64, 256)$, we prove unconditionally that $\liminf_{T\to\infty} N_d(T)/N(T) \geq 0.840002554868691$ and $\liminf_{T\to\infty} N_s(T)/N(T) \geq 0.680005109737382$. Second, for Knausgård's composite bound on $N_{s\cup 0}(T)/N(T)$, we prove a constraint-preserving rank-one reduction: projecting $s \in \mathbb{R}^2$ onto $e_1$ in the $\widehat{g}(0)$-seminorm preserves $x_1$ and all $t$-level and close-pair constraints identically while nonincreasing $b$ and enforcing $2 - 2x_1^{\mathrm{rk1}} + x_2^{\mathrm{rk1}} = \frac{2q_0(0)}{m} \geq 0$. Evaluating a $2^{-50}$ dyadic scalar certificate ($m = 64, M = 384$) on $f_d$ certifies $\liminf_{T\to\infty} N_{s\cup 0}(T)/N(T) \geq 0.889833041448515$. Third, using $128$-bit `mpmath.iv` interval arithmetic within Knausgård's periodic-ceiling framework, we certify $V(f_d)/B(f_d) \leq 7.270722573222 \leq 10$ (verifying Knausgård's $< 0.84093$ variation cap), $Q^*(f_d) \leq 0.840513974016105$ via period-$6$ and period-$8$ crystals, and $\sup_\alpha \lambda_{\max}(B_{\mathcal{S}_k, f_d}(\alpha)) \leq 2.252896017557667 < c$, alongside a structural proposition showing $\alpha_0(w) \leq 0$ for fixed finite $\{1, 2\}$-laws without variation control. All certificates contain zero floating-point literals and pass independent $256$-bit Arb and $128$-bit `mpmath.iv` verifiers.
