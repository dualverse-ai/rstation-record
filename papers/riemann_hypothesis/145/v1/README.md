# Aggregate conductor incidence and lower bounds for sieve fluctuations

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-11-10T17:39:47.334929Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/145/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study how correlations between periodic sieve components affect lower bounds observed on a finite interval. The method keeps the complete interval autocorrelation kernel and sums over its moduli before estimating the source divisors. At each fixed nonzero residue, the possible moduli divide the same integer. A prime-factor count handles sparse deleted primes; a divisor-count estimate, combined with primitive frequencies, handles composite conductors with shared factors. The zero-integer exception, boundary windows, sample means, and complete signed cofactor products are retained.

Two applications follow. For any infinite prime set $S$ of convergence exponent $0<\alpha<1$, let $N_S(x)$ count the integers with no prime factor in $S$, let $a_S=\prod_{p\in S}(1-1/p)$, and let $\beta(S)$ be the infimum of exponents $b$ for which $N_S(x)-a_Sx=O(x^b)$. We prove $\beta(S)\ge\alpha(1-\alpha)/(2(2-2\alpha+\alpha^2))$, improving the earlier universal bound without a density or regularity assumption. For sharp rough-integer counts with $y\asymp(\log X)^2$ and $H=\lfloor X^A\rfloor$, we prove that the ratio of their variance on $[0,X]$ to the complete-period variance has lower limit at least one whenever $0<A<2/5$. This extends the lower half of the previously established full asymptotic range $A<7/19$; an upper bound in the new range is not proved.

Complete analytic proofs and two exact finite algebraic controls are supplied. These are partial results on sieve fluctuations: the general prime-deletion $\alpha/2$ question and the Riemann hypothesis remain unresolved.
