# The Báez-Duarte subspace has codimension equal to the number of zeta zeros off the critical line, with distance and rate bounds not assuming RH

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 2 · 2026-10-16T01:00:37.494246Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/1/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $S$ be the closed subspace of $L^2(0,1)$ made of step functions that are constant on each interval $(1/(m+1),1/m]$. Let $V\subset S$ be the closed span of the Báez-Duarte functions $\rho_{1/k}(t)=\{1/(kt)\}-\{1/t\}/k$, $k\ge2$. By Báez-Duarte's theorem, the Riemann hypothesis holds iff $V=S$. We prove, with no hypothesis, that $\dim S/V$ equals the number $|Z^+|$ of zeros of $\zeta$ in $\operatorname{Re}s>1/2$, counted with multiplicity. In the Hardy-space model of Noor and Calderaro–Manzur–Noor–Santos this reads $\dim\mathcal N^\perp=|Z^+|$, which yields the converse of their Theorem 21. For the lower bound we show, via pole orders of $\zeta^{(i)}$, that jets at points of $Z^+$ are independent on $S$. For the upper bound when $Z^+$ is finite we adapt Bagchi's proof. We approximate $P/s$, with $P$ a Dirichlet polynomial vanishing on $Z^+$, by smoothed partial sums of $P/\zeta$, using a $\xi$-ratio estimate with finitely many exceptional factors. Then $V$ is exactly the subspace of $S$ where all jets at $Z^+$ vanish, and $\lim_N d_N^2$ is an explicit Gram-matrix quantity, strictly larger than Burnol's continuous Nyman–Beurling distance if $Z^+\ne\emptyset$. Unconditionally, a zero $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with $\beta>1/2$ forces $\lim_N d_N^2\ge(2\beta-1)|\rho|^{2\beta-3}/36$, exceeding the continuous bound by a factor of order $|\rho|^{2\beta-1}$. When $Z^+$ is finite we also prove, without RH, Burnol-type second-order bounds: $\liminf_N(d_N^2-\lim d_N^2)\log N\ge\sum m(\rho)^2|u(\rho)|^2$ over zeros on the line, where $u$ is the projection of the target onto $V$. The limiting-distance bounds hold, up to a factor $0.857$, for the unconstrained Dirichlet-polynomial distance of Bettin–Conrey–Farmer, and the rate bound holds there with $u$ replaced by the projection of a shifted target. Spectral synthesis for infinite $Z^+$ remains open. We prove it for $\overline{QS}$, for Dirichlet polynomials $Q$ with $c_1\ne0$ whose Bohr symbol has finitely many torus zeros, and reduce the general case to one generator $\rho_{1/k}$.
