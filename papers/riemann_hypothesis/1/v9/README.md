# The Báez-Duarte subspace has codimension equal to the number of zeta zeros off the critical line, with distance and rate bounds not assuming RH

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 9 · 2026-10-26T20:34:43.972912Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/1/v9) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $S$ be the closed subspace of $L^2(0,1)$ of functions constant on each interval $(1/(m+1),1/m]$. Let $V\subset S$ be the closed span of the Báez-Duarte functions $\rho_{1/k}(t)=\{1/(kt)\}-\{1/t\}/k$, $k\ge2$. By Báez-Duarte's theorem, the Riemann hypothesis holds iff $V=S$. We prove unconditionally that $\dim S/V$ equals the number $|Z^+|$ of zeros of $\zeta$ in $\operatorname{Re}s>1/2$, counted with multiplicity. In the Hardy-space model of Noor and Calderaro–Manzur–Noor–Santos this reads $\dim\mathcal N^\perp=|Z^+|$, the converse of their Theorem 21. The lower bound shows, via pole orders of $\zeta^{(i)}$, that jets at $Z^+$ are independent on $S$. For the upper bound when $Z^+$ is finite we adapt Bagchi's proof, approximating $P/s$, with $P$ a Dirichlet polynomial vanishing on $Z^+$, by smoothed partial sums of $P/\zeta$. Then $V$ is exactly the subspace of $S$ where all jets at $Z^+$ vanish, and $\lim_N d_N^2$ is an explicit Gram-matrix quantity. This extends to infinite $Z^+$ when the points $2^{1/2-\rho}$ satisfy the Blaschke condition, and is open otherwise. For every nonempty $Z^+$, finite or not, $\lim_N d_N^2$ strictly exceeds Burnol's continuous Nyman–Beurling distance. A zero $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with $\beta>1/2$ forces $\lim_N d_N^2\ge(2\beta-1)|\rho|^{2\beta-3}/36$. For finite $Z^+$ we also prove Burnol-type second-order bounds: $\liminf_N(d_N^2-\lim d_N^2)\log N\ge\sum m(\rho)^2|u(\rho)|^2$ over zeros on the line, where $u$ is the projection of the target onto $V$. The limiting-distance bounds hold, up to a factor $0.857$, for the unconstrained Dirichlet-polynomial distance of Bettin–Conrey–Farmer, as does the rate bound with a shifted target. For Dirichlet polynomials $Q$ we prove spectral synthesis for $\overline{QS}$ when $|A|\le C|Q|$ on the line for some Dirichlet polynomial $A$ with $c_1(A)\ne0$ and no zeros in $\operatorname{Re}s>1/2$; hence $Q$ is cyclic iff $c_1(Q)\ne0$ and $Q$ has no such zeros. A logarithmic cutoff of $f/Q$ gives synthesis for all $Q$ in $2^{-s},3^{-s}$ with $c_1(Q)\ne0$ and squarefree Bohr symbol. The general case reduces to one generator $\rho_{1/k}$.
