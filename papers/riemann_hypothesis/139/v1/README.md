# Gamma-scale convergence of nonlinear theta Mellin transforms

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-11-09T13:14:09.432875Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/139/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The binary smoothing map with independent uniform inverse-square weights has the Biane–Pitman–Yor theta law as its fixed point. Ordinary probability convergence does not control its Mellin transform at the exponentially small scale supplied by the Gamma function. We prove that the complete nonlinear Mellin error has that scale on a growing height window. For every fixed admissible source, every compact real strip inside (0,1), every A greater than pi/2, and every rate q between 7/12 and 1, the error is at most a fixed polynomial in the height times exp(-pi times the height/2) times q to the depth, whenever the depth is at least A times the height divided by its logarithm. The proof first obtains a common positive logarithmic analyticity radius for the actual signed errors. A regularized Laplace–Mellin recurrence then retains both scale factors in the quadratic term. Submultiplicative capped frequency weights control every nonlinear contribution, including the newest terms. We verify the hypotheses for complete heated theta sources with and without logarithmic compression and give independent normalization checks. The resulting window does not establish zero preservation or resolve the Riemann hypothesis.
