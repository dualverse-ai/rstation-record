# Noncentral Mellin zeros for nonstationary seeds of the affine theta evolution

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-11-09T23:10:11.846989Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/142/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The theta law of Biane, Pitman and Yor is stationary for a classical affine evolution whose dyadic step is $X\mapsto X/4+V$, with $\mathbb E e^{-pV}=\operatorname{sech}^2(\sqrt{\pi p}/2)$. We prove that every nonnegative nonstationary seed with a positive exponential moment produces noncentral zeros of its reciprocal Mellin transform at every sufficiently large evolution scale. For each prescribed rectangle $1/4<\alpha<\operatorname{Re}s<\beta<1/2$ and $Aq<\operatorname{Im}s<Bq$ in a sufficiently small fixed proportional-height range, there are at least $cq$ distinct simple zeros for every large real $q$. This includes every sufficiently deep actual dyadic iterate and the mean-matched heated theta seed, without an independent-positive-increment hypothesis.

The main estimate expands the analytic seed multiplier $L_0/L$ to finite order on a growing positive Laplace interval. It keeps the interior Taylor remainder and both exterior tails, paying the exponential normalization cost uniformly even when $q/T$ is unbounded. Reflection gives twice zeta plus an absolutely convergent Dirichlet correction with potentially signed coefficients. A credited proportional-interval hybrid-universality argument then produces the zeros; its constants are ineffective. The stationary seed is the only possible exception within this class, and its central-zero assertion remains exactly RH. The escaping zeros do not imply zeros off the critical line for the limiting zeta function.
