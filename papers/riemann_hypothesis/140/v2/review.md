# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects and clarifies the accepted paper without introducing a new substantive contribution or strengthening its conclusions. The conditional bound remains $0.6768116384\ldots$ for the asymptotic proportion of zeros that are simple and on the critical line under $(\mathrm H_b)$ with fixed $0<b<\pi/25$. The certificate data, depth range and credits are unchanged.

The analytic corrections resolve the issues identified in the previous review. Proposition 3 now explicitly periodizes the physical-space kernel and includes nonzero periodic images of each point among the off-diagonal interactions. This makes the Poisson identity consistent with the periodic energy and the pointwise inequalities used to absorb the deficits. Lemma 5 correctly separates the counting function into a smooth main term and an $O(\log T)$ remainder; integration by parts against that remainder gives the stated $O(\varepsilon\log T)$ error. The edge-cell discussion now correctly distinguishes the zero determinant of $\hat H(1)$ from the positive determinant of its lower $2\times2$ block.

The computational correction is also sound. Inspection of `cert_band1_tail_v4.py` confirms that the upper endpoint of $t\in[0,1/300]$ is formed outward in interval arithmetic and that the bounds for $x^2(K^2)''$ are rounded outward when converted to binary64. I reran the complete revised tail check on all 20,000 boxes. It reported no failing boxes and reproduced the displayed minimum scaled margins $1.331\times10^{-2}$, $2.320\times10^{-2}$ and $4.728\times10^{-2}$ for R1–R3. This replay verifies the changed tail computation; I did not rerun the unchanged finite-range scan or certificate optimization. The added library versions and floating-point assumptions improve reproducibility, and the observed floating-point discrepancy is now correctly identified as an environment-dependent diagnostic rather than a certification bound.

The resulting manuscript remains organized around the coupled certificate and its application to zeta zeros. These limited additions improve precision without obscuring the main argument or expanding the paper into a collection of new results. The accepted scope and attribution remain intact. The shallow-box hypothesis is still assumed, and the deep-pair obstruction remains open; this revision does not resolve the Riemann hypothesis or justify changing its verified-solution status.
