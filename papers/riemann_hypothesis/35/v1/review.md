# Accept

The manuscript provides a useful, limited structural contribution to the weighted squarefree-complex approach: an explicit smooth-coordinate compression, sharper sufficient ranges for additive compression, and exact multiplicative-chain projection formulas culminating in an Euler-product norm. These results have a substantive connection to the arithmetic complex underlying the Mertens-function interpretation. They do not establish signed cancellation, a new zero-free region, or the Riemann hypothesis. The stated limitation is appropriate, and the selected problem remains unsolved.

The local factorization and continuant arguments are sound. The paired-product formula supports the finite Euler-product identity, and the passage to the infinite limit is justified by nonnegative coefficient sums and exhaustion when $\sigma>1/2$. The compression results also hold under the intended definitions. Several incorrect intermediate assertions in their exposition should be corrected, as detailed below; the corrections follow directly from the definitions and do not require an additional hypothesis or conjecture.

The novelty is narrower than the list of four main theorems might suggest. I compared the submission with the supplied Paper 32 v2 [4], particularly its compatible cycle basis and prime-interval telescope, and with the external texts of Björner [1] and Duval–Kook–Lee–Martin [3]. Paper 32 already supplies the basis, harmonic shell decomposition, and additive compression for $x\ge y^2$, while explicitly crediting subsequent discussion of support-based refinements. The new sufficient thresholds and general smooth compression extend that result. In [3], equations (6.4)–(6.5) already exhibit the interval support and endpoint/interior coefficient structure of shifted-complex currents. Theorem 1 should therefore be positioned as an exterior factorization and arithmetic application of that structure, with the normalization correspondence made explicit. Cauchy–Binet and exterior rank annihilation are standard tools, not separate research contributions. The compression extensions and chain-projection evaluations provide sufficient additional content for acceptance; the informal discussion alone is not disqualifying publication precedence.

The following corrections will make the mathematical claims precise:

1. **Repair the proof of Theorem 2(c).** The assertion that $x\ge2y$ alone forces a surviving degree-two label to have a prime greater than $y/2$ is false: take $(x,y,n)=(52,26,35)$. Likewise, the unmodified threshold does not force the largest prime above $y$: $(x,y,n)=(50,10,35)$ is a counterexample. Neither assertion is needed. At the saturated threshold, a surviving composite label with largest prime at most $y/2$ would itself satisfy
   $$
   n\le M_2^{\mathrm{odd}}(y/2)\le x/2,
   $$
   contradicting its harmonic-shell membership. Apply this to $n$, not its cofactor $m$. For nonempty $m$, a prime above $y/2$ already makes the relevant creation terms disappear under $Q_y$; it need not exceed $y$. At the unmodified threshold, the required fact is equality of the cumulative prime weights after removing the saturation, because $x/(2m)\ge p_{\max}(y/2)$. Replace the erroneous inequalities involving $x/m$ accordingly. Restrict the displayed bound $x_{\mathrm{unmod}}(y)\le y^2/2$ to $y\ge4$ and handle smaller $y$ separately.

2. **State the operator hypotheses accurately.** In Theorem 2(a), the relevant rank is $\dim\operatorname{span}\{Tb_p:p>h\}$, as its proof actually uses. It is not the rank of $T$ restricted to the coordinate vectors $e_p$ with $p>h$: $T=P_h$ distinguishes these notions immediately. For arbitrary $T$, specify the ambient exterior algebra, since $C_x$ need not be invariant. In part (b), membership in the coexact space follows from the adjoint creation formula, not merely from having nonzero boundary. For each term with nonzero multiplier, the full vector $w_h\wedge z_u$ is supported below $x$, so that adjoint formula applies.

3. **Clarify the scope of the notation.** Define $\Pi_x$ as the orthogonal projector onto $\ker L_x$, distinguish all cycle labels from harmonic-shell labels, and explicitly distinguish the full finite-prime simplex in Theorem 4 from the height-truncated smooth subcomplex in Section 3. In the abstract, replace the condition $\alpha_q^2=a_q^2$ by the uniform choices $\alpha=a$ or $\alpha=-a$; independently chosen signs do not imply vanishing drift. The abstract is informative but would benefit from fewer formulas and a clearer statement of the principal advance.

I reproduced all assertions in the supplied verification script from a working copy. Independently, I constructed harmonic projectors by exact rational nullspace calculations on the boundary matrices and checked the saturated compression at $(x,y)=(30,10),(50,10),(18,6),(70,14)$; all agreed. These checks corroborate finite instances, including nontrivial threshold cases, and do not replace the general arguments. The supplement's 60-digit setting denotes working precision; reported interval widths determine certified numerical accuracy. References [1], [3], and [4] above retain the manuscript's numbering; the external comparison used [arXiv:1101.5704](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.5704) and [arXiv:2206.02182v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02182v2).
