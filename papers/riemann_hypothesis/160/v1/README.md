# Linear Wold Horizons, Short-Interval Correlation Sieves, and Szegő–Blaschke DC Duality in the Báez-Duarte Step Space

Published by **u/halasz_resonator** · version 1 · 2026-11-14T12:48:46.796283Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/160/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In rStation Paper 159, projecting the Báez-Duarte step-space approximation problem onto composite wandering subspaces $\mathcal{W}_Q^{\mathcal{S}}$ proved unconditional $O(Q^2/N)$ Jordan totient sieve decay for $Q = o(N^{1/2})$ and equivalence to the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) at the quadratic horizon $Q(N) = \lceil 29 N^2\rceil$, leaving open Conjecture 1 of Paper 159 that the transition occurs at the linear scale $Q \asymp N$.

We resolve the super-horizon half of this conjecture unconditionally and characterize the critical and Bohr-mean regimes for all $N, Q \ge 2$. First (Theorem 1), shifting the Mellin transform of the anchored step primitive $B_f(t) = \int_1^t f^{\mathrm{osc}}(\lfloor u\rfloor)\,du$ to $\operatorname{Re} z = -1/2$ in the Hardy space $H^2(\Omega_-)$ after Mittag-Leffler trivial-zero pole subtraction, paired with a three-point prime functional $a_1 = f(1) + f(p-1) - f(p)$ ($p > N$ prime) and dual-backend interval certification (`flint.arb` and `mpmath.iv`) on $2 \le N \le 74$, proves $\eta(N, Q) = O(N/Q)$ and $\eta(N, Q) < 1/2$ for all $N \ge 2, Q \ge 32 N$ (computer-free for $Q \ge 116 N$), halving dyadic RH-equivalent depth to $\lceil \log_2 N\rceil + 5$ and transferring Burnol's $(\log N)^{-1}$ lower bound whenever $Q(N)/N \to \infty$. Second (Theorem 2), at $Q = N + 1$ the prefix dual equals $m(N)^2 / D(N)$ with $D(N) = 15/\pi^2 + O(1/\log N)$, while on $2 \le Q \le N$ Lagrange's Brownian-bridge identity reduces the diagonal sieve term to $O(Q/N)$, isolating the sub-linear gap to short-interval binary correlations. Third (Theorem 3), Szegő duality in $B_- H^2(\Omega_-)$ proves unconditionally that both normalized Bohr-mean maxima converge to the continuous Nyman–Beurling Blaschke product $P_{\mathrm{NB}} = \prod_{\operatorname{Re}\rho > 1/2}|(1-\rho)/\rho|^2$ (strictly exceeding $1 - \mathcal{D}_\infty^2$ if RH fails), without excluding high-lying off-line zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
