In our prior work (`riemann_hypothesis_12`), we showed that smoothing Balazard's harmonic head functional $\mathcal{F}_N = \sum_{n=1}^N n^{-1}\mathcal{C}_n$ by a flat-top spline $\Omega_\delta = W_{r+1, \theta}$ ($\theta = \frac{1}{1+\delta}$) across a linear tail $N < n \le N_\delta := \lfloor(1+\delta)N\rfloor$ yields unconditional strong dual-norm vanishing $\lim_{N\to\infty}\|\mathcal{F}_{N, \delta}^{\mathrm{smooth}}\|_{S_\sigma^*}^2 = 0$ on $[1/2, 1)$ and the conditional power-saving law $\|\mathcal{F}_{N, \delta}^{\mathrm{smooth}}\|_{S_\sigma^*}^2 \sim \mathcal{C}_{\sigma, \delta}N^{-2(1-\sigma)}$ for $\sigma \in (\Theta, 1)$ ($\Theta := \sup\{\operatorname{Re}(\rho) : \zeta(\rho) = 0\} \in [1/2, 1]$). However, a single taper $W_{r+1, \theta}$ leaves a countable exceptional scale set $\mathcal{E}_r \subset (0, 1]$ where ${}_2F_1(1-\rho, r+1; 2r+2; \frac{\delta}{1+\delta}) = 0$, and suffers from log-periodic nodal zeros at finite $N$. Here we resolve both limitations at a single fixed support $N_\delta$ without varying the knot $\theta$. First, adjoining the knot-derivative defect functional $\mathcal{G}_{N, \delta}^{(r)}$ ($\Phi_{r+1, \theta} := -(1-\theta)\partial_\theta W_{r+1, \theta}$) yields $\widehat{\Phi}_{r+1, \theta}(s) = \frac{z}{s}w'_s(z)$; by Cauchy-Picard uniqueness for the Gauss hypergeometric ODE, $|\widehat{W}_{r+1, \theta}(\rho-1)|^2 + |\widehat{\Phi}_{r+1, \theta}(\rho-1)|^2 > 0$ for every $\delta > 0$ and every nontrivial zero $\rho$, eliminating $\mathcal{E}_r$ unconditionally at every rational scale $\delta \in (0, 1] \cap \mathbb{Q}$. Second, we embed $\{W_{r+2, \theta}, \Phi_{r+2, \theta}\}$ into the co-located Jacobi-Rodrigues wavelet hierarchy $\Psi_{r, \theta, k}(u) = \frac{(-1)^{k-1}}{k!}\partial_\tau^{k-1}[\tau^{r+k}(1-\tau)^{r+k}]$, whose alternating midpoint parity places consecutive modes in phase quadrature and whose span exhausts $\operatorname{Ann}_{S_\sigma^*}^{(N_\delta)}(\tilde{\rho}_{1/2}, \dots, \tilde{\rho}_{1/N})$. Third, 60-digit interval arithmetic certifies 18 rational witness superpositions at $N \in \{40, 80, 160\}$.
