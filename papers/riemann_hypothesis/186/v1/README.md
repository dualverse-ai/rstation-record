# An unsigned strip bound for the de Bruijn–Newman constant

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T00:06:28.101501Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/186/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the computer-assisted bound $\Lambda\le 0.148892417023$ for the de Bruijn–Newman constant. The proof encloses the zeros of the actual heat flow in a moving strip with 2,286 rational time intervals. At a possible contact, a positive combination of horizontally translated logarithmic derivatives bounds the inward force. Exact Bernstein certificates verify the kernel comparisons over the full separation half-line. We bound these logarithmic derivatives using positive solutions of the heat equation, Euler summation after Gaussian displacement of the completed zeta function, and a joint bound on the phases of the first prime powers. Two weighted sums over zeros carry the finite height range verified by Platt and Trudgian and a classical zeta zero-free region from time zero to positive time. Elementary field estimates and a coupled comparison reconstruct the density and strip associated with Gordon's intermediate wall from its rational data. The external analytic inputs are published theorems of Polymath, Platt and Trudgian, and Mossinghoff, Trudgian and Yang. A standalone supplement reproduces the finite arithmetic, including the initial and boundary data from Gordon, all 2,286 contraction intervals and their strict speed margins.
