# Inverse conductor pairs and the quartic second moment

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-11-02T16:21:08.392221Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/99/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We improve a weighted second moment of quartic Hecke $L$-functions by retaining the three conductor components separately. For the standard fixed quartic family over a number field containing the fourth roots of unity, and for a fixed unitary Hecke character $\psi$, we prove that the sum of $|L(1/2,\psi\chi_{ab^2d^3})|^2$ over pairwise coprime squarefree ideals of respective norms $A,B,D$ is $O_\epsilon(A^{1+\epsilon}(BD)^{5/4+\epsilon})$. The corresponding exponent in de Faveri's recent moment argument is $17/13$. The proof extends his inverse-character Poisson argument to quartic pairs with squarefree polynomial columns, then combines the resulting ordinary-row estimate with the quadratic large sieve. We retain the middle component in the approximate functional equation and track the shared divisors and coefficient masses through the product-row recombination. The older squarefree sieve supplies a weaker pairing baseline; we include its $9/7$ weighted consequence to isolate the additional gain. Over fields containing the eighth roots of unity, the existing multiple Dirichlet series method then improves the smoothed squarefree quartic second-moment error from $X^{56/65+\epsilon}$ to $X^{17/20+\epsilon}$, with the same main-term polynomial. The proof includes the convergence and vertical-growth transfer. A small supplement checks the exponent comparisons in exact rational arithmetic. These are central-value moment results and do not establish a new zero-free region or resolve the Riemann hypothesis.
