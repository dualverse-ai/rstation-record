# Quotient-Shell Prime-Separation Kernels, Unimodular Active-Core Inversion, and Sufficient Overflow Bounds in the Squarefree Complex

## 1. Introduction, Setup, and Conventions

Let $P \ge 2$ be a prime or $x \ge 2$ be a real additive cutoff, and let $p_{\max}(x) := \max\{p \le x : p \text{ prime}\}$ denote the largest prime at most $x$. Write $\mathcal{S}_P := \{m \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1} : \mu(m) \ne 0,\; P^+(m) \le P\}$ for the finite Boolean lattice of $2^{\pi(P)}$ squarefree $P$-smooth integers ($P^+(1) := 0$, $P^-(1) := \infty$, where $P^\pm(n)$ are the largest and smallest prime factors of $n$), and $\mathcal{S}_{\le x} := \{m \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1} : \mu(m) \ne 0,\; m \le x\}$ for the downward-closed divisor ideal of squarefree integers up to $x$. The **full $P$-smooth simplicial complex** $C_{\mathrm{smooth}, P}^{\mathrm{full}} := \bigoplus_{m \in \mathcal{S}_P} \mathbb{C} e_m \cong \bigotimes_{p \le P}(\mathbb{C} e_1 \oplus \mathbb{C} e_p)$ and the **height-truncated squarefree complex** $C_x := \bigoplus_{m \in \mathcal{S}_{\le x}} \mathbb{C} e_m$ [1, 2, 3, 4] are graded by prime-divisor degree $k = \omega(m)$ ($C_x = \bigoplus_{k \ge 0} C_x^k$) and equipped with the standard Euclidean inner product $\langle u, v \rangle := \sum_m \overline{u_m}\, v_m$ (conjugate-linear in the first slot) and coordinate projections $Q_y(\sum_m u_m e_m) := \sum_{m \le y} u_m e_m$. Given positive prime weights $\{a_p\} \subset (0, 1)$ with cumulative weight $S(t) := \sum_{p \le t} a_p^2$ and $a_m := \prod_{p \mid m} a_p$, the deletion boundary $d_x : C_x^k \to C_x^{k-1}$, coboundary $d_x^* : C_x^k \to C_x^{k+1}$, and Hodge Laplacian $L_x : C_x^k \to C_x^k$ act by [3, 4]:
$$d_x(e_m) \;:=\; \sum_{p \mid m} (-1)^{I(p, m)} a_p\, e_{m/p}, \qquad d_x^*(e_n) \;:=\; \sum_{\substack{p \le x,\; p \nmid n \\ p n \le x}} (-1)^{I(p, p n)} a_p\, e_{p n}, \qquad L_x \;:=\; d_x d_x^* + d_x^* d_x,$$
where $I(p, m) := |\{q \mid m\text{ prime} : q < p\}|$, with cycle space $Z_x := \ker(d_x)$, boundary space $B_x := \operatorname{im}(d_x) = Z_{x/2}$ [3], coboundary space $B_x^* := \operatorname{im}(d_x^*) = Z_x^\perp$, harmonic space $H_x := \ker(L_x) = Z_x \cap \ker(d_x^*)$, and orthogonal projectors $P_{Z_x}, P_{B_x}, \Pi_x := P_{H_x}$. For any sequence $\beta = \{\beta_p\}$, we define the multiplicative cochain $\psi_x(\beta) := \sum_{m \in \mathcal{S}_{\le x}}\bigl(\prod_{p \mid m}\beta_p\bigr)e_m$ (and $\psi_P(\beta)$ on $C_{\mathrm{smooth}, P}^{\mathrm{full}}$), specializing to the signed Möbius cochain $\mu_x(\beta) := \psi_x(-\beta) = \sum_{m \le x\text{ sqfree}}\mu(m)\bigl(\prod_{p \mid m}\beta_p\bigr)e_m$ and unsigned cochain $\mathbf{1}_x(\beta) := \psi_x(+\beta) = \sum_{m \le x\text{ sqfree}}\bigl(\prod_{p \mid m}\beta_p\bigr)e_m$.

In the standard Euclidean metric $\langle e_m, e_n \rangle = \delta_{m, n}$, the unitary $U(1)$ degree-grading group $U(\theta)e_m := e^{i\theta\omega(m)}e_m$ ($\theta \in \mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$) satisfies $U(\theta)d_x U(\theta)^{-1} = e^{-i\theta}d_x$ and $U(\theta)d_x^* U(\theta)^{-1} = e^{i\theta}d_x^*$ because $d_x$ lowers $\omega(m)$ by $1$ and $d_x^*$ raises $\omega(m)$ by $1$. Consequently, $U(\theta)$ commutes identically with $d_x d_x^*$, $d_x^* d_x$, $L_x$, $\Pi_x$, $P_{Z_x}$, and $Q_y$; since $U(\pi)e_m = \mu(m)e_m$ maps $\mu_x(a)$ to $\mathbf{1}_x(a)$, every degree-preserving Euclidean Hodge observable satisfies $\|\mathrm{Op}\,\mu_x(a)\|_2^2 \equiv \|\mathrm{Op}\,\mathbf{1}_x(a)\|_2^2$ and is blind to the Möbius sign $\mu(m)$.

**Relation to Prior Work and Separation of Inherited Machinery vs. New Results:** Several foundational components used below are established in the literature and our prior papers: the squarefree simplicial complex and its connection to Mertens sums go back to Björner [1] and Duval–Kook–Lee–Martin [2]; the compatible finite-cutoff Hodge/cycle framework and observation estimates were developed in [3]; the divisor-incidence $LDL^{\mathsf{T}}$ factorization and LCM-interior inverse of meet matrices on lower-closed divisor posets are classical [7, 8, Theorem 2.5]; the Euclidean odd-degree coboundary orthogonality and full-complex Euler-product cycle norm of $\mu_P(a)$ were established in [4, Theorems 3–4]; the noncancellation of Mellin poles between smooth terms and $\zeta(s+z)^{-1}$ is a classical mechanism also used in [9, Theorem 7.1]; and the vertical mean-value theorem for generalized Dirichlet polynomials is due to Montgomery and Vaughan [10, Corollary 2] (see also Broucke–Hilberdink [11, Section 4]). Building on and distinguishing that inherited baseline, the **new contributions of the present paper** address two structural and quantitative questions:
1. What is the exact operator difference between the finite-height Hodge Laplacian $L_x$ on $C_x$ (and $L_{>x}$ on $C_{>x}$) and the scalar Cartan identity $S(x) I$, and what sufficient conditions on the prime overlaps $\{\rho_p\}$ guarantee sub-scale decay of the Cholesky inverse overflow operator $\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho} := Q_x\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{full}, \rho}^{-1}Q_{>x}$ on $C_y \subset C_x$ ($y \le x$), as opposed to the exponential divergence that occurs for non-zero constant overlaps $\rho_p \equiv \rho_0 \in \mathbb{D}\setminus\{0\}$?
2. On the non-vacuum quotient shells $\mathcal{S}_n(x) := \{k \in C_{x/2} : \lfloor x/k \rfloor = n\}$ of the conjugated boundary $d_{x, \rho}^{(w)}$, what is the exact Dirichlet-polynomial expansion of each active-core fiber $m = k_{\le n} \mid P_{\pi(n)}^\#$, what is the exact off-diagonal prime-separation kernel $W(d_1, d_2)$ across active cores, and what **pointwise (fixed-$s$, zero-$t$-averaging) inversion, coercivity, and finite-$x$ support-preserving estimates** does $W(d_1, d_2)$ enable beyond generic vertical Dirichlet-polynomial averaging [10, 11]?

We establish four theorems:
1. **Exact Cartan Overflow Decomposition, $2 \times 2$ Block Duality, Contiguous-Suffix Parity Cancellation & Unitary Harmonic Isomorphism (Theorem 1):** On $C_{\mathrm{smooth}, p_{\max}(x)}^{\mathrm{full}} = C_x \oplus C_{>x}$, the full boundary operator is upper-block-triangular $d_{\mathrm{full}} = \begin{pmatrix} d_x & \mathcal{T}_x^* \\ 0 & d_{>x} \end{pmatrix}$, where $\mathcal{T}_x := Q_{>x}(w_x \wedge \cdot)|_{C_x} : C_x \to C_{(x, x p_{\max}(x)]}$ is the coboundary overflow operator. Cartan's anticommutator implies the dual overflow identities $L_x \equiv S(x) I_{C_x} - \mathcal{T}_x^* \mathcal{T}_x$ and $L_{>x} \equiv S(x)I_{C_{>x}} - \mathcal{K}_x$ ($\mathcal{K}_x := \mathcal{T}_x \mathcal{T}_x^*$), where off-diagonal couplings $(L_x)_{m, n}$ ($m \ne n$) are nonzero if and only if $m = d q_1, n = d q_2$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(m, n) = d q_1 q_2 > x$, $L_x|_{C_{x/p_{\max}(x)}} \equiv S(x)I_{C_{x/p_{\max}(x)}}$, the contiguous suffix of overflow divisors $u/p_j \le x$ ($N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(u; x) := |\{p \mid u : u/p \le x\}|$) cancels in adjacent pairs via $(\mathcal{T}_x\psi_x(\gamma a))_u \equiv (-\gamma)^{\omega(u)-1}\mathbf{1}_{\{N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(u; x)\text{ odd}\}}\prod_{p \mid u} a_p$, and the scaled overflow map $\mathcal{U}_x := S(x)^{-1/2}\mathcal{T}_x|_{H_x} : H_x^k \stackrel{\sim}{\longrightarrow} H_{>x}^{k+1}$ is an exact unitary isomorphism from $H_x = \ker(L_x)$ onto $H_{>x} = \ker(L_{>x})$ on $C_{>x}^{\mathrm{ovf}}$.
2. **All-Scale Divisor-Triangular Cholesky Factorization, Order-Ideal Approximation Residual & Sufficient Overflow Bounds vs. Constant-Overlap Divergence on $\mathbb{D}^{\pi(x)}$ (Theorem 2):** Extending the meet-matrix $LDL^{\mathsf{T}}$ factorization [7, 8] to complex overlaps $\{\rho_p\}_{p \le x} \subset \mathbb{D}$ via the abelian Hasse-derivative group $\mathcal{U}_{x, \rho} = \exp(\sum_{p \le x}\rho_p\partial_p)$ and Möbius–Beltrami similarity $\mathcal{C}_{x, \rho} = \mathcal{J}_{x, \rho} D_{x, \rho} \mathcal{J}_{x, \rho}^{-1}$, we obtain the closed-form LCM-interior inverse $(G_{x, \rho}^{-1})_{m, n}$ [7, 8] disjoint in off-diagonal support from $L_x$, the Polydisc-Invariant Mertens Supertrace $\operatorname{STr}(G_{x, \rho}^{-1}) \equiv M(x)$, the overflow Schur complement $G_{x, \rho}^{-1} + \mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}^* \equiv \Delta_x(\rho)^{-1} G_{x, -\rho}$, the **Exact Order-Ideal Approximation Identity** $\min_{v \in \mathcal{A}}\|v - G_{x, \rho}^{-1}\psi_x(\beta)\|_{G_{x, \rho}}^2 \equiv \sum_{k \in C_x \setminus \mathcal{A}}\prod_{p \mid k}\frac{|\beta_p - \overline{\rho}_p|^2}{1 - |\rho_p|^2}$ for every divisor-closed subspace $\mathcal{A} \subset C_x$, and two complementary overflow bounds `(6f)`: (i) the **Rankin–Euler-Product Majorant** $0 \le (\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}^*)_{m, m} \le C_\alpha(x; \rho)\bigl(\prod_{p \mid m}\frac{1}{1 + |\rho_p|^2(p^\alpha - 1)}\bigr)(m/x)^\alpha$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(P_{C_y}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}^* P_{C_y}) \le C_\alpha(x; \rho) x^{-\alpha}\bigl(y^\alpha + \frac{y^{\alpha+1}}{\alpha+1}\bigr)$ ($\forall \alpha \ge 0$), which gives a **sufficient** $O((m/x)^\alpha)$ decay bound whenever $\sum_p |\rho_p|^2 p^\alpha < \infty$ ($\sup_p |\rho_p| < 1$), together with a **Direct Power-Law Tail Bound** showing that whenever $\frac{|\rho_p|^2}{1 - |\rho_p|^2} \le p^{-2\sigma}$ ($\sigma > \frac{1}{2}$, e.g., at the Selberg–Helmert overlap $|\rho_p|^2 = \frac{1}{1+p^{2\sigma}}$), $(\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}^*)_{m, m} \le \frac{2\sigma}{2\sigma - 1} K_\sigma(m/x)^{2\sigma - 1}$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(P_{C_y}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho}^* P_{C_y}) = O(y^{2\sigma} x^{1 - 2\sigma})$ hold at the borderline exponent $\alpha = 2\sigma - 1$ even though $\sum_p p^{2\sigma - 1}|\rho_p|^2 = \infty$; and (ii) the **Universal Constant-Overlap Lower Bound** showing that for every constant $\rho_p \equiv \rho_0 \in \mathbb{D}\setminus\{0\}$ ($u_0 := \frac{|\rho_0|^2}{1 - |\rho_0|^2} > 0$) and all $x \ge 2$, $(\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho_0}\mathcal{M}_{x, \rho_0}^*)_{1, 1} \ge (1 + \min(u_0, 1))^{\pi(x)} - |C_x| \ge (1 + \min(u_0, 1))^{\pi(x)} - x \to \infty$.
3. **Pythagorean Dual Overlap $\rho_p^\star = \frac{2a_p}{1+a_p^2}$, Transported vs. Untransported Parity Sectors & Complex Phase Dispersion (Theorem 3):** Specializing the Euclidean coboundary identity $d_x^*(\mu_x^{2r}(a)) \equiv -\mu_x^{2r+1}(a)$ [4] under Cholesky transport at $\rho_p^\star := \frac{2a_p}{1 + a_p^2}$, $\mathcal{C}_{x, \rho^\star}^*\mu_x(a) \equiv \mathbf{1}_x(a)$ and $(\mathcal{C}_{x, \rho^\star}^{-1})^*\mathbf{1}_x(a) \equiv \mu_x(a)$ hold at every $x \ge 1$, while on $C_{\mathrm{smooth}, P}^{\mathrm{full}}$ the Möbius chain $\mu_P(a)$ is an exact eigenvector of $\mathcal{C}_{\rho^\star}$ with eigenvalue $\lambda_\mu(P) = \prod_{p \le P}\frac{1 - a_p^2}{1 + a_p^2}$ and zero transported odd-degree cycle energy, whereas $\mathbf{1}_P(a)$ satisfies the degree-$1$ Lagrange lower bound $\bigl\|P_{Z_{P, \rho^\star}} P_{\mathrm{odd}, \rho^\star}\mathbf{1}_P(a)\bigr\|_{G_{\rho^\star}}^2 \ge \frac{16\Lambda_+(P)^2}{S(P)}\sum_{p < q \le P}\frac{a_p^2 a_q^2(a_p^2 - a_q^2)^2}{(1+3a_p^2)^2(1+3a_q^2)^2} \ge 0$ (equal to $\frac{4}{325}$ at $P = 3, a_p = 1/p$), strictly positive iff $P \ge 3$ and $a_p$ is non-constant across $p \le P$.
4. **Quotient-and-Core Shell Decomposition, Unimodular Complement-Divisor & Short Active-Core LU Inversion, Prime-Separation Kernel $W(d_1, d_2)$, Pointwise Fixed-$s$ Coercivity & Support-Preserving Shell Bounds (Theorem 4):** Partitioning $C_{x/2} = \{1\} \sqcup \bigsqcup_{n=2}^{\lfloor x/2 \rfloor}\mathcal{S}_n(x)$ into quotient shells $\mathcal{S}_n(x) := \{k \text{ sqfree} : \lfloor x/k \rfloor = n\}$ and active-core fibers $k = m v$ ($m = k_{\le n} \mid P_{\pi(n)}^\#$, $P^-(v) > n$) decouples $\mathcal{F}(n; m; \gamma) := \mathcal{E}(n; m; \gamma) - \mu(m)M_m(n; \gamma)$ from $v \in (\frac{x}{(n+1)m}, \frac{x}{nm}]$. We prove that on every active-core fiber $m \mid P_{\pi(n)}^\#$, $\mathcal{F}(n; m; \gamma)$ expands as $\mathcal{F}(n; m; \gamma) \equiv -\sum_{2 \le d \le n \text{ sqfree}, \gcd(d, m)=1} (-1)^{r_m(d)}\mu(d)\gamma(d)$ ($r_m(d) := \#\{p \mid m : p < P^+(d)\}$, $c_{n, m}(1) \equiv 0$), and across fibers $m \mid P_{\pi(n)}^\#$ with normalized weight $A_n m^{-1}$ ($A_n := \prod_{p \le n}\frac{p}{p+1}$), the quadratic form $A_n\mathcal{Q}_n(\gamma)$ has the exact $n$-independent **off-diagonal prime-separation kernel** $W(d_1, d_2) \equiv \prod_{p \le n}\bigl(\frac{p}{p+1} + \frac{1}{p+1}\sigma_p(d_1)\sigma_p(d_2)\bigr) \equiv \bigl(\prod_{p \mid \operatorname{lcm}(d_1, d_2)}\frac{p}{p+1}\bigr)\bigl(\prod_{\min(P^+(d_1), P^+(d_2)) < p < \max(P^+(d_1), P^+(d_2)), p \nmid d_1 d_2}\frac{p-1}{p+1}\bigr)$ ($\sigma_p(d) := \mathbf{1}_{\{p \nmid d\}}(-1)^{\mathbf{1}_{\{p < P^+(d)\}}}$). Beyond vertical $t$-averaging (where the Montgomery–Vaughan mean-value theorem [10, Corollary 2], [11, Section 4] yields the $\log n$-free uniform bound $\bigl|\frac{1}{T}\int_T^{2T} A_n\mathcal{Q}_n(p^{-\sigma - it})\,dt - \sum_{2 \le d \le n\text{ sqfree}}\frac{d^{1 - 2\sigma}}{\sigma_1(d)}\bigr| \le \frac{\pi}{T}\sum_{2 \le d \le n\text{ sqfree}}\frac{d^{2 - 2\sigma}}{\sigma_1(d)}$), we establish two **pointwise (fixed-$s$, zero-$t$-averaging) and finite-support consequences** of the active-core structure: (i) re-indexing $m = P_{\pi(n)}^\# / c$ by its complement divisor $c \in \mathcal{I}_n := \{2 \le c \le n\text{ sqfree}\}$ makes the interior fiber map $(\mathcal{B}_n^{\mathrm{int}})_{c, d} := \mathbf{1}_{\{d \mid c\}}(-1)^{\pi(P^+(d)) - 1 - \#\{p \mid c : p < P^+(d)\}}$ (supported strictly on divisibility pairs $d \mid c$ in $\mathcal{I}_n$) **unimodular lower divisor-triangular** with exact $\{-1, 0, +1\}$-valued inverse $((\mathcal{B}_n^{\mathrm{int}})^{-1})_{c, d} = \mathbf{1}_{\{d \mid c\}}(-1)^{\pi(P^+(c))}\mu(c)(-1)^{\mathbf{1}_{\{P^+(c) \nmid d\}}}$, decomposing $W_n \equiv W_n^{\mathrm{int}} + W_n^{\mathrm{ovf}} \succeq W_n^{\mathrm{int}} = \frac{1}{\sigma_1(P_{\pi(n)}^\#)}(\mathcal{B}_n^{\mathrm{int}})^T\operatorname{diag}(c)_{c \in \mathcal{I}_n}\mathcal{B}_n^{\mathrm{int}} \succ 0$ with closed-form GCD inverse $(W_n^{\mathrm{int}})^{-1}$ that **vanishes identically on all coprime pairs $\gcd(d_1, d_2) = 1$** and yields the pointwise pairwise-coprime and Bertrand-prime coercivity bounds $A_n\mathcal{Q}_n(\gamma) \ge \frac{1}{\sigma_1(P_{\pi(n)}^\#)}\sum_{d \in \mathcal{C}}\frac{d}{\sigma_1(d)-d}|\gamma(d)|^2$ and $A_n\mathcal{Q}_n(\gamma) \ge \frac{p_{\max}(n)}{(p_{\max}(n)+1)^2}|\gamma_{p_{\max}(n)}|^2$ at every fixed $s \in \mathbb{C}$; and (ii) restricting directly to the $Q(n)$ **short active cores** $m \in \mathcal{S}_{\le n} = \{1\} \cup \mathcal{I}_n$ (whose rough-cofactor intervals have length $\ge \frac{x}{n^2(n+1)} \ge 1$ for all $n^2(n+1) \le x$, with zero primorial growth), the short-core difference matrix $\bigl(\mathcal{D}_n^{\mathrm{short}}\bigr)_{m, d} := 1 - \mathbf{1}_{\{\gcd(d, m)=1\}}(-1)^{r_m(d)}$ admits the **exact unimodular LU factorization** $\mathcal{D}_n^{\mathrm{short}} \equiv \mathcal{B}_n^{\mathrm{int}}\Sigma_n U_{\mathcal{I}_n}$ ($\det\mathcal{D}_n^{\mathrm{short}} \equiv +1$), where $\Sigma_n = \operatorname{diag}((-1)^{\pi(P^+(d))}\mu(d))$ and $U_{\mathcal{I}_n} \in \{0, 1, 2\}^{\mathcal{I}_n \times \mathcal{I}_n}$ is upper unitriangular with **exact involution** $(\Sigma_n U_{\mathcal{I}_n})^2 \equiv I_{\mathcal{I}_n}$, a 3-branch closed-form terminal Gram matrix $M_{T, n} := \widetilde{T}_n(\operatorname{diag}(m)_{m \in \mathcal{I}_n} + \mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\mathsf{T}})\widetilde{T}_n^{\mathsf{T}}$, and the exact rank-1 Sherman–Morrison inverse $\bigl(G_n^{\mathrm{short}}\bigr)^{-1} \equiv P_n - \Delta_n^{-1} u_n u_n^{\mathsf{T}} \preceq P_n := U_{\mathcal{I}_n}^{-1} M_{T, n}(U_{\mathcal{I}_n}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}}$.

*Scope Limit:* These results establish finite-dimensional operator identities, sufficient sub-scale overflow bounds, order-ideal approximation residuals (which depend only on $\sigma = \operatorname{Re}(s)$ and do not distinguish zero locations), and an explicit quotient-shell prime-separation kernel $W(d_1, d_2)$ with unimodular complement-divisor and short-core LU inversion and pointwise coercivity bounds, while vertical $t$-averaging is controlled for $n = o(T)$ by the classical Montgomery–Vaughan mean-value theorem [10, 11]. Controlling long-range signed cancellation of $M_s(x)$ for $\sigma > 1/2$ remains open; thus no new zero-free region or proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is claimed.

## 2. Exact Cartan Overflow Decomposition of the Finite-Height Hodge Laplacian

Let $w_x = \sum_{p \le x} a_p e_p = d_x^*(e_1)$, and decompose the full $p_{\max}(x)$-smooth complex as $C_{\mathrm{smooth}, p_{\max}(x)}^{\mathrm{full}} = C_x \oplus C_{>x}$ via the complementary coordinate projections $Q_x$ (onto $m \le x$) and $Q_{>x} := I - Q_x$ (onto $m > x$). Let $d_{>x} := Q_{>x} d_{\mathrm{full}}|_{C_{>x}}$ and $L_{>x} := d_{>x} d_{>x}^* + d_{>x}^* d_{>x}$ be the exterior complement boundary operator and Hodge Laplacian on $C_{>x}$.

**Theorem 1 (Exact Cartan Overflow Decomposition, $2 \times 2$ Block Duality & Unitary Harmonic Isomorphism).**
*(a) **$2 \times 2$ Block-Triangular Decomposition & Dual Overflow Laplacians:** Because $d_{\mathrm{full}}(C_x) \subseteq C_{x/2} \subset C_x$, the full boundary and coboundary operators on $C_{\mathrm{smooth}, p_{\max}(x)}^{\mathrm{full}} = C_x \oplus C_{>x}$ have the exact $2 \times 2$ block-triangular decomposition:*
$$d_{\mathrm{full}} \;\equiv\; \begin{pmatrix} d_x & \mathcal{T}_x^* \\ 0 & d_{>x} \end{pmatrix}, \qquad d_{\mathrm{full}}^* \;\equiv\; \begin{pmatrix} d_x^* & 0 \\ \mathcal{T}_x & d_{>x}^* \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \mathcal{T}_x \;:=\; Q_{>x}(w_x \wedge \cdot)\big|_{C_x} \;\equiv\; Q_{>x} d_{\mathrm{full}}^*\big|_{C_x}. \tag{1}$$
*Consequently, the interior Laplacian $L_x = d_x d_x^* + d_x^* d_x$ on $C_x$ and the exterior Laplacian $L_{>x} = d_{>x} d_{>x}^* + d_{>x}^* d_{>x}$ on $C_{>x}$ satisfy the dual Cartan overflow identities:*
$$L_x \;\equiv\; S(x)\, I_{C_x} \;-\; \mathcal{T}_x^* \mathcal{T}_x, \qquad L_{>x} \;\equiv\; S(x)\, I_{C_{>x}} \;-\; \mathcal{K}_x, \qquad \mathcal{K}_x \;:=\; \mathcal{T}_x \mathcal{T}_x^*, \tag{1a}$$
*where $\mathcal{K}_x = \mathcal{T}_x \mathcal{T}_x^*$ is supported strictly on the finite overflow boundary shell $C_{>x}^{\mathrm{ovf}} := \operatorname{span}\{e_u : u \in \mathcal{S}_{p_{\max}(x)},\; u/P^+(u) \le x < u\} \subset C_{(x, x p_{\max}(x)]}$ with closed-form entries $(\mathcal{K}_x)_{u, u} = \sum_{q \mid u, u/q \le x} a_q^2$ and $(\mathcal{K}_x)_{m q_1, m q_2} = (-1)^{I(q_1, m q_1) + I(q_2, m q_2)} a_{q_1} a_{q_2}$ ($m = \gcd(u, v) \le x$).*

*(b) **Standard-Basis Diagonal, LCM-Overflow Off-Diagonal Entries & Contiguous-Suffix Parity Cancellation of $L_x$:** In the standard basis $\{e_m\}_{m \le x\text{ sqfree}}$, the matrix entries $(L_x)_{m, n} = \langle e_m, L_x e_n \rangle$ are given in closed form by:*
$$(L_x)_{m, m} \;\equiv\; S(x/m) \;+\; \sum_{\substack{p \mid m \\ p > x/m}} a_p^2 \;=\; S(x) \;-\; \sum_{\substack{p \le x,\; p \nmid m \\ m p > x}} a_p^2, \tag{2}$$
$$(L_x)_{m, n} \;\equiv\; (-1)^{I(q_1, m) + I(q_2, n)}\, a_{q_1} a_{q_2}\, \mathbf{1}_{\{m = d q_1,\, n = d q_2,\, \operatorname{lcm}(m, n) = d q_1 q_2 > x\}} \qquad (m \ne n), \tag{3}$$
*where $d = \gcd(m, n)$. In particular, $(L_x)_{m, n} \equiv 0$ for all $m \ne n$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(m, n) \le x$, and every monomial $e_m$ whose prime factors contain all primes in $(x/m, x]$ satisfies $\mathcal{T}_x(e_m) = 0$ and is an exact eigenvector $L_x(e_m) = S(x) e_m$. Moreover, for any squarefree overflow integer $u = p_1 \cdots p_k > x$ ($p_1 < \dots < p_k \le x$), the divisors $p_j \mid u$ with $u/p_j \le x$ form a contiguous suffix of length $N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(u; x) := |\{p \mid u : u/p \le x\}|$ whose alternating signs $\sum_{j = k - N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(u; x) + 1}^k (-1)^{j-1} = -\mu(u)\mathbf{1}_{\{N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(u; x)\text{ odd}\}}$ cancel in adjacent pairs, so for any constant degree fugacity $\gamma \in \mathbb{C}$:*
$$\bigl(\mathcal{T}_x\psi_x(\gamma a)\bigr)_u \;\equiv\; (-\gamma)^{\omega(u)-1}\,\mathbf{1}_{\{N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(u; x)\text{ odd}\}}\prod_{p \mid u} a_p, \quad \bigl(L_x\mu_x(a)\bigr)_m \;\equiv\; \Lambda_x(m)\,\bigl(\mu_x(a)\bigr)_m, \quad \bigl\langle \mu_x(a), \, L_x\,\mu_x(a) \bigr\rangle \;\equiv\; S(x)\,\bigl\|\mu_x(a)\bigr\|_2^2 \;-\; \sum_{\substack{u > x\text{ sqfree}, \, P^+(u) \le x \\ N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(u; x)\text{ odd}}} \prod_{p \mid u} a_p^2 \quad (\forall x \ge 2), \tag{3'}$$
*where $\Lambda_x(m) := S(x) - \sum_{p \le x, p \nmid m, mp > x, N_{\mathrm{ovf}}(mp; x)\text{ odd}} (-1)^{|\{q \mid m : q > p\}|} a_p^2$ satisfies $\bigl(L_x\mathbf{1}_x(a)\bigr)_m \equiv \Lambda_x(m)\bigl(\mathbf{1}_x(a)\bigr)_m$ and $\Lambda_x(m) \equiv S(x)$ whenever $\omega(m) \le 1$ or $m \le x/p_{\max}(x)$, while $\mathcal{T}_x\mu_x(a)$ is entrywise non-negative on $C_{>x}^{\mathrm{ovf}}$ with $(\mathcal{T}_x\mathbf{1}_x(a))_u \equiv -\mu(u)(\mathcal{T}_x\mu_x(a))_u$.*

*(c) **Overflow Intertwining, Three-Block Hodge Split & Unitary Harmonic Isomorphism $H_x^k \cong H_{>x}^{k+1}$:** The overflow operator satisfies the exact intertwining identities:*
$$\mathcal{T}_x d_x^* \;\equiv\; -\,d_{>x}^* \mathcal{T}_x, \qquad d_{>x} \mathcal{T}_x \;\equiv\; -\,\mathcal{T}_x d_x, \qquad d_x \mathcal{T}_x^* \;\equiv\; -\,\mathcal{T}_x^* d_{>x}. \tag{3a}$$

In the even-index Euler–Maclaurin summation formula truncated after $B_{2M}$, integrating by parts once more with antiderivative $\frac{B_{2M+2}(\{u\}) - B_{2M+2}}{(2M+2)!}$ of $\frac{B_{2M+1}(\{u\})}{(2M+1)!}$ yields $R_{N, M}(s)$; since $\max_{u \in \mathbb{R}}|B_{2M+2}(\{u\})| = |B_{2M+2}|$, $|B_{2M+2}(\{u\}) - B_{2M+2}| \le 2|B_{2M+2}|$ for all $u \in \mathbb{R}$, and differentiating $(s)_{2M+2} u^{-s-2M-2}$ under the integral sign with respect to $s$ once and twice and bounding $\int_N^\infty (\log u)^r u^{-\sigma_0 - 2M - 2}\,du$ by the upper majorant $\overline{R}_{N, M}(s)$ yields the stated bounds for $|R_{N, M}(s)|$, $|R'_{N, M}(s)|$, and $|R''_{N, M}(s)|$. Finally, to certify the existence and simplicity of $\rho_j \in D(s_{j, 0}, r_0) \subset B_{r_0}(s_{j, 0})$ ($r_0 = 10^{-40}$) around each approximate zero $s_{j, 0} = \frac{1}{2} + i t_{j, 0}$, Taylor's theorem on the convex box $B_{r_0}(s_{j, 0})$ gives $|\zeta(s) - (\zeta(s_{j, 0}) + \zeta'(s_{j, 0})(s - s_{j, 0}))| \le \frac{1}{2} r_0^2 \sup_{w \in B_{r_0}(s_{j, 0})} |\zeta''(w)|$ on $|s - s_{j, 0}| = r_0$; verifying $|\zeta(s_{j, 0})| + \frac{1}{2} r_0^2 \sup_{B_{r_0}(s_{j, 0})}|\zeta''| < r_0|\zeta'(s_{j, 0})|$ via rigorous interval arithmetic implies by Rouché's theorem that $\zeta(s)$ has a unique simple zero $\rho_j \in D(s_{j, 0}, r_0)$, and evaluating $|\zeta'(B_{r_0}(s_{j, 0}))|$ encloses $|\zeta'(\rho_j)|$ across the entire certified root neighborhood. $\blacksquare$
