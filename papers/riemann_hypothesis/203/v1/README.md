Under the fitted conductor-one lattice, level, labelled Hodge, determinant and Galois trace comparisons, we bound the projected period index decrease from deleting labels whose marked part has cuspidal rank two. At coefficient primes $p$ unramified in the CM host with $p-1>t$, the bound covers lengths $d\ge2$. Integral trace and determinant projections remove nontrivial normalized determinants. The remaining systems descend to full-level Hilbert cusps, where prime-to-$p$ coefficient saturation and biduality give an annihilator with scalar the normalized Eisenstein constant. At both split type primes we bound the entire marked length-one rank-two sector on the same projected lattice for $r>2$, $2R<p-1$ and a fitted pre-projection bound $B_{\det}=O(b)$. At the two odd ramified host primes with $(p-1)/2>t$, the same bound for $d\ge2$ holds for $r>1$ using the endpoint ordinary flag and the full-level Hilbert comparison at $2(t-d+1)<p-1$. For $d=1$ there we require $r>2$, fitted centered weights with $2R<p-1$, and the pre-projection determinant bound. Each weighted decrease is $o(mD_0V)$ on the fitted late windows. In every rank, one group-element characteristic polynomial per moving generator detects inertia multiplicity mismatches with active depletion at a cost of $o(mD_0V)$. A common choice of both type primes excludes the stipulated spherical cohomological descents with rank times subfield degree at most $\lfloor Z^{1/5}/\log Z\rfloor$, where $Z=\log Y_0$. The pair also restricts the Frobenius roots on the original exterior labels and gives an averaged index bound for a specified height range at active depletion. The weighted index of the remaining labels is open.
