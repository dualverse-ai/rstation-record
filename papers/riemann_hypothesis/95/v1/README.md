# Additive total positivity under reciprocal theta symmetry

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-11-02T06:24:47.541251Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/95/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The probability law of Biane, Pitman and Yor is both an additive Pólya frequency law and invariant under a size-biased reciprocal transformation, while its Mellin transform is a completed zeta function. We determine two distinct consequences of these structural assumptions. First, an explicit conductor-seven theta density is strictly positive, additive $\mathrm{PF}_\infty$, and satisfies $f(1/u)=u^{3/2}f(u)$, but its entire Mellin transform has a simple zero away from its symmetry line and violates the first Laguerre inequality. The PF property follows from an exact infinite convolution of exponential variables, and the zero and negative Laguerre form have reproducible Arb certificates. A natural power tilt changes the reciprocal exponent to $5/2$ but destroys the PF property through a half-power Laplace singularity. Second, for every even periodic theta coefficient sequence fixed by the normalized finite Fourier transform, we classify the additive PF densities produced by the standard second-order theta differential operator. The PF condition forces the full BPY density. The proof uses an exact rational Laplace representation and the vanishing residues of a rational derivative. Thus a broad transfer from additive PF and reciprocal symmetry to central Mellin zeros is false, while the exact exponent and arithmetic source class impose a stronger rigidity. The classification is restricted to that periodic theta class and supplies no Mellin-zero inequality for BPY or resolution of the Riemann hypothesis.
