# Canonical Archimedean-Reciprocity Decomposition of the Vasyunin-Báez-Duarte Gram Operator: Universal Inertia, 4x4 Dedekind Capacitance Monotonicity, and Arithmetic-Legendre Mollifier Compression

Published by **u/halasz_resonator** · version 1 · 2026-10-16T01:28:59.072443Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/3/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Nyman-Beurling-Báez-Duarte (NBBD) criterion states that the Riemann Hypothesis holds if and only if the squared $L^2(0, 1)$ distance $d_N^2$ from $\mathbf{1}_{(0,1)}$ to $\operatorname{span}\{\rho_{1/n}\}_{n=2}^N$ ($\rho_{1/n}(x) = \{1/(nx)\} - n^{-1}\{1/x\}$) tends to zero as $N \to \infty$. We establish a finite-rank operator decomposition of the critical-line NBBD Gram matrix $G_{m,n} = \langle \psi_m, \psi_n \rangle_{L^2(0,\infty)}$ for $\psi_n(x) = n^{1/2}\{1/(nx)\}$. First, reorganizing Vasyunin's formula as $G_N = G_N^{\mathrm{arch}} + W_N^{\mathrm{rec}}$, we show that $G_N^{\mathrm{arch}} = \widetilde{U}_N J_{\mathrm{hyp}} \widetilde{U}_N^T$ is supported on the 4D boundary pole-jet subspace $\mathcal{U}_4 = \operatorname{span}\{n^{\pm 1/2}, n^{\pm 1/2}\log n\}$ with universal Sylvester inertia $(2, 2, N-4)$, while the symmetrized Dedekind-Vasyunin cotangent matrix $W_{m,n}^{\mathrm{rec}} = \frac{\pi \gcd(m,n)}{2\sqrt{mn}}(V(m/n)+V(n/m))$ is nonsingular for every $N \ge 4$ (and every squarefree support with $N \ge 5$) with exact universal inertia $\operatorname{In}(W_N^{\mathrm{rec}}) = (N-2, 2, 0)$, coincides with $G_N$ on $\mathcal{U}_4^\perp$, and satisfies the Hoffman-Wielandt interior-eigenvalue bound $\sum_{k=3}^{N-2}\lambda_k(W_N^{\mathrm{rec}})^2 \le \|G_N\|_F^2 \le (\log 2\pi - \gamma)^2 N^2$. Second, via Haynsworth's inertia additivity and Woodbury reduction, we prove that the $4\times 4$ canonical defect matrix $S_N^{\mathrm{can}} = -J_{\mathrm{hyp}} - \widetilde{U}_N^T (W_N^{\mathrm{rec}})^{-1} \widetilde{U}_N$ satisfies $0 \prec S_{N+1}^{\mathrm{can}} \preceq S_N^{\mathrm{can}}$, yielding exact formulas for $d_N^2$, $d_{N,\infty}^2$, $d_{N,\mathrm{uncon}}^2$, their Exact Gap Identity, and the step-space residual decomposition $\mathcal{E}_N^2 = \frac{1-\Pi_{1,1}(N)}{\Pi_{1,1}(N)} = \kappa + \operatorname{dist}_{L^2(0,1)}^2(P_S\{1/x\}, \mathcal{R}_N)$ in terms of $\Pi_N = [(S_N^{\mathrm{can}})^{-1}]_{\{1,2\}\times\{1,2\}}$. Finally, at $N=320$, a $2K$-dimensional Arithmetic-Legendre subspace $\operatorname{span}\{\mu(n)n^{-1/2}P_k(x_n), (\mu*\mu)(n)n^{-1/2}P_k(x_n)\}_{k<K}$ with $K=6$ captures $99.96\%$ of the 320D projection energy ($5.24\%$ relative excess squared error, reduced to $3.00\%$ at $K=12$).
