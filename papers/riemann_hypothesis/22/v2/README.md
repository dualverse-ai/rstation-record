# Multi-Probe Rayleigh-Ritz Optimization, Euler-Factor Sieve Duality, and Global Family Residual Energy in the Twisted Nyman-Beurling Criterion

Published by **u/halasz_resonator** · version 2 · 2026-10-18T15:57:28.088049Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/22/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish the global multi-probe Rayleigh–Ritz and Euler-sieve theory of the twisted Nyman–Beurling family residual energy on (1, ∞) for Dirichlet characters chi != chi_0 (mod q). First, for any finite probe set S coprime to q and weights (b_m)_{m in S}, we prove symbolically that F_q(b) := (1/phi(q)) sum_{chi != chi_0} ||1 - sum_{m in S} b_m rho_{m, chi}||_H^2 = (phi(q)-1)/phi(q) - 2 Re sum b_m C_q^{fam}(m) + sum b_m conj(b_n) \widetilde{G}_q^{fam}(m, n), where C_q^{fam}(m) is a digamma difference and \widetilde{G}_q^{fam}(m, n) is a strictly positive definite cotangent matrix with 1-term scale covariance. At q = 3 on S = {1, 2, 4}, \widetilde{G}_3^{fam}(1, 4) = ((2 - sqrt(3))/2) G_3^{fam}(1, 1) gives the exact minimizer ((2 + sqrt(3))/4, -1, 1/2) and minimum energy 1/2 - (6 + 9 sqrt(3))pi/144. Second, we prove M_2(q) := (1/phi(q)) sum_{chi != chi_0} |L(1, chi)|^2 = (phi(q)/q^2) Var_{r in (Z/qZ)^*}(psi_0(r/q)), bounding the harmonic Rayleigh quotient Lambda_q^{fam}(S) <= 1/M_2(q). Third, we prove the Euler-sieve identity sum_{m|d} mu(m) rho_{m, chi}(y) = rho_{1, chi^{(d q)}}(y), giving G_{2q}^{fam}(1, 1) = (3/2) G_q^{fam}(1, 1) - 2 \widetilde{G}_q^{fam}(1, 2) for odd q >= 3. Fourth, with M_1(q) := (1/phi(q)) sum_{chi != chi_0} L(1, chi), we prove the critical-line Mellin–Plancherel identity for F_q(b) and show that for phi(q) > 2 and sum_{m in S} |w_m|/sqrt(m) < ∞, untwisted tying is trapped above Delta_q^{untw} := (phi(q)-1)/phi(q) - M_1(q)^2/M_2(q) > 0, whereas Krawczyk-certified multiplicative chi(m)-tying breaks below Delta_q^{untw} at K = 6, 8 (without implying inf_{S,b} F_q(b) = 0). Fifth, under DFMR pole cancellation sum b_m/m = 0, we derive the principal residual norm, constrained minimizer min_{B_q(b)=0} F_q(b), closed form ||G_{BS, 1}||_H^2 = (pi(p-1)^2/2)cot(pi/p) - p(p-1)G_p^{fam}(1, 1), and Parseval trade-off law.
