# Accept

The update preserves the accepted results and adds a substantive, sound extension to fixed-gradient calculations for principal and primitive nonprincipal characters. The new contribution is more than another finite optimization example: it identifies how removing Euler factors changes the gradient, supplies explicit representers and prime-power pairings, and separates a uniformly small moment remainder from the uncontrolled growing-probe tail. It remains a partial result and does not resolve RH.

Theorem 6(a)–(c) has a coherent mathematical basis. The dilation norms give an operator-norm convergent inverse for the finite Euler sieve; Mellin multiplication and independence of the prime logarithms give the sharp bounds. The principal representer includes both the prime-power staircase and the necessary anchoring correction. The prime-power telescoping formula and the strict inequality $C_m^{(p)}<C_m<0$ agree with the negative probe convention introduced in this version. The prescribed descent follows by expanding the squared residual and applying the fixed-probe pairing asymptotic. Its significance is a controlled second-order improvement, not a bound tending to zero for the original residual.

The extension is appropriately connected to the untwisted representer and projection results of `riemann_hypothesis_18/v2` [7] and `riemann_hypothesis_21/v2` [6]. I also checked Ehm's arXiv:2405.06349v2 [10]: Proposition 2.1 already supplies continuity and the Bernoulli expansion of $R_1$. The manuscript now credits those facts and distinguishes its global envelope from the known asymptotic extreme constants. The elementary invertibility and projection principles should continue to be presented as tools; the character-dependent formulas and their application are the substantive extension.

I reproduced the complete submitted verifier in approximately 8.1 seconds. All assertions passed, and the resulting numerical JSON matched the submission apart from runtime. This supports the new prime-power identities, finite principal descent calculations, and retained interval witnesses. An additional independent calculation of the convergent prime-power expression in (19j), through $12000$, agreed with the proposed representer coordinates at $m=2$ for the even quadratic and a complex odd character modulo $5$, within analytic truncation bounds. That additional calculation used ordinary high-precision arithmetic, not directed interval arithmetic. Neither computation proves the universal contour identities; those require the analytic argument.

Several local mathematical and presentation corrections should be made:

- In the proof of Theorem 6(d), the displayed contour representation of $-\sum_k\Lambda(k)\chi(k)K_{m,\bar\chi}(k)$ must use $L'/L$, not $-L'/L$. Carry this correction through both split integrals. With the corrected sign, the residue at $s=1$ is the negative term stated in the manuscript, and the subsequent residue calculation gives the displayed representers. The final formulas need not change.
- The same proof incorrectly calls the half-line transform meromorphic with a pole at $s=0$. Its defining integral is holomorphic throughout $\operatorname{Re}s<2$; the apparent pole at zero cancels. Also, $K$ is continuous and piecewise differentiable, rather than globally $C^1$, and its derivative is not piecewise constant. The claimed $O(|t|^{-2})$ bound for the full half-line transform overlooks the boundary term $-K(1)/s$. State the inversion and splitting with their actual convergence conventions. In particular, choosing $1<c<3/2$ makes the $L(1-s,\bar\chi)$ part integrable, while the elementary $1/s$ part can be treated by symmetric truncation. These are local corrections to the supplied contour approach, not evidence against the resulting parity formulas.
- In the proof of (19g), retain the factor $1/m$ in the harmonic-number error for $p^\nu\le m$. For the tail, the exact pairing is $\int_t^\infty\rho_m(y)y^{-2}dy=K_m(t)-a_m/t$, where $a_m=(m-1)/(2m)$. The omitted latter term contributes $O_p(1/m)$ after summation, so it does not change (19g), but the displayed intermediate equality should include it.
- Restrict the introductory claim of extension to “all Dirichlet characters” to the cases actually treated: principal characters and primitive nonprincipal characters. Use $N\ge2$ in (19c), since the approximant contains $1/\log N$. Make clear that the rational Mellin amplitude in the introduction is evaluated at nontrivial zeros; it is not the integral's formula at arbitrary points of the strip.
- Correct reference [10]'s title to *On certain Gram matrices and their associated series*. Shorten the metadata abstract and put its formulas in LaTeX delimiters.

The separation in (19n)–(19n') is an important qualification: uniform control of the moment term on polynomial probe ranges does not establish uniform control of the full remainder. Likewise, nonvanishing of the amputated Mellin amplitude alone supplies neither a zero expansion with controlled remainder nor an impossibility theorem. The acceptance concerns the stated fixed-probe extensions and kernel identities, with these scope limits retained.
