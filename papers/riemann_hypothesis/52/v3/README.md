# Long Möbius means and explicit zero density for cubic Hecke characters

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 3 · 2026-10-25T18:41:46.491220Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/52/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove an explicit zero-density bound and a long Möbius mean for cube-free primary cubic characters of the Eisenstein field. For every fixed $5/6\leq\sigma<1$ and $\epsilon>0$, the number of zeros with real part at least $\sigma$ and height at most $P^h$, over rows of index norm $P$, is $O(P^{E_4(\sigma)+\epsilon})$ for a sufficiently small fixed $h>0$, including multiplicities. The explicit exponent satisfies $E_4(7/8)=9/22$, improving the earlier Gram value $4/9$. For the displayed finite envelope using Burgess orders two through six, the first improvement begins at $37/44$, and $E_4(17/20)=15/28<6/11$. We combine the buffered scalar detector and product-moment method of the September 2026 OpenAI preprints with a smooth Eisenstein Burgess bound and the published natural cubic fourth moment. We prove the Burgess input for squarefree composite conductors, retaining the gcd average, common Fourier seminorms, natural zeros, and polynomial height costs. A fixed eighth-order inverse identity factors an actual saturated Möbius witness. Every inverse factor is short enough for a bounded integer power; an ordinary fourth moment controls a larger full factor. All powers are at most twenty-three, and the argument uses one common frequency net. The intermediate individual-pair envelope and its separate proof are retained. Independently, a cube-free Gram estimate gives a raw Möbius squared mean $O(P^{2/3-\eta}Y^{5/3})$ on every fixed interval $P^{1-\kappa}\leq Y\leq P^{2-\tau}$. No global zero-free, Weyl, or zero-isolation hypothesis is used. The family is ordered by index norm, and the height exponent is not uniform as $\sigma\to1$. These results give no new individual zero-free region and do not resolve RH.
