# Dirichlet multiplier synthesis and common eigenfunctionals in the Báez-Duarte space

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-10-26T10:50:29.567846Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/59/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove structural results for Dirichlet multipliers and the zeta annihilator in the Báez-Duarte step space $S$. If $Q(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}c_nn^{-s}$ has $c_1\ne0$ and $\sum |c_n|n^{-1/2+\eta}<\infty$ for some $\eta>0$, the closure of $QS$ consists exactly of the elements of $S$ vanishing at every zero of $Q$ in $\Re s>1/2$, with multiplicities. This includes every finite Dirichlet polynomial and removes prime-count and repeated-factor restrictions from the preceding squarefree two-prime theorem. A logarithmic cutoff retains derivatives of the entire multiplier. Compact analytic translate families, local product rules and Blaschke cancellation bound derivative quotients without root-gap denominators; explicit operator tails handle infinitely many coefficients. We also classify every finite-dimensional common dilation-invariant subspace of the actual zeta annihilator: it is a finite direct sum of initial derivative-evaluation chains at zeta zeros, with lengths bounded by their multiplicities. For ordinary eigenfunctionals, the published multiplicative-eigenvector mechanism and Klurman's Kátai rigidity theorem give a geometric-or-summable dichotomy; an Euler product excludes the summable case. For finite blocks, continuity controls all matrix coefficients, and additive rigidity reduces the commuting unipotent matrices to an exponential in $\log n$. A two-generator example clarifies the limits of principal-range synthesis. Complete analytic proofs and reproducible finite structural checks are provided. Constants depend on the fixed multiplier and its extension. Zeta fails the multiplier hypotheses, and the finite invariant subspaces are not proved to span the annihilator. Infinite-zeta synthesis and RH remain unresolved.
