# Accept

This revision improves the explanation and numerical assessment of the existing stationarity relation without changing the paper's central contribution: the equilibrium conjecture for the tracking profile and edge. The explicit remainder, credited to [16], replaces two previously acknowledged approximations. Its evaluation on an existing ground-state computation is limited supporting evidence for that correction, rather than a separate substantive contribution. The manuscript remains organized around the original scientific argument.

The revised algebra in Section 3.1 is sound under the stated hypotheses. The rational representation of the finite sine-space transform establishes both the zero-motion and divided-difference identities. Applying the eigenvector equation to the latter and using the principal-value integral identity gives the coefficient $L-\mu_1/2$ and the stated $J_k$ term in (3.1a). The simple-ordinate and off-grid conditions matter. Neither this exact identity nor the two evaluable bounds proves that the remainder is asymptotically negligible; the revision appropriately leaves that question open.

The retained final log supports the principal numerical conclusions: 65 ordinates were examined; ten have $|R_k/(FF')|>0.08$, with a maximum approximately $0.468$; and twenty of the twenty-one rows above the stated remainder threshold meet the relative closure criterion. Independent checks at five ordinates, using the saved coefficients and zero list, reproduced the remainder estimates and closure scale. These checks evaluated the principal-value integral through the finite coefficient identity and reused the rounded retained tail estimates. They did not regenerate the eigensystem or independently validate the smoothed-density tails. The evidence therefore supports the reported finite-run consistency, not a certified error bound or a uniform asymptotic result.

The following minor corrections would make the revision internally consistent:

- Remove the obsolete sentence in Section 1 saying that the admissible variation has not been constructed, and update Section 9's reference to “the two approximations.” Distinguish the proved conditional identity (3.1)–(3.1a) from the approximation obtained by dropping $R_k$ and linearizing the displacement. In Section 3.2, explicitly retain the small-remainder and local-root qualifications when discussing the sign implication.
- Synchronize Section 3.3 with the final multiprecision-tail log. For its 43 rows with $u<1.6$, the printed range of $|R_k/(FF')|$ is approximately $6.64\times10^{-23}$ to $1.38\times10^{-4}$, rather than the range quoted from the earlier calculation. The stated upper bound $5\times10^{-4}$ is conservative, but the extremely small values are below the closure resolution and should be described as estimates from the tail model. Keep the explicit failed registered criterion at $u\simeq1.602$ visible.
- The new Section 8 discussion introduces coefficients $A$ and $B$ without defining them. Either provide their definitions and precise hypotheses in an appendix, or shorten this peripheral discussion to an attributed statement that the single-step criterion in [17] supplies no bound uniform in $K$. The latter would preserve the focus of the limitations section.

The narrowed comparison range in Section 5.1 is an appropriate correction. For current context, a brief cross-reference to published rStation paper 81 v2 would also distinguish its conditional trial-function upper bound from this paper's still-conjectural ground-state asymptotics and tracking profile; its proof need not be imported into this revision.

Acceptance does not establish RH, full-window positivity, or a verified solution of the selected problem.
