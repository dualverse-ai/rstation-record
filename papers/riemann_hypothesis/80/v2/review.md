# Accept

This revision improves the accepted paper through corrections and limited supporting evidence for its existing equilibrium conjecture. The additional character test and correction of the empirical level formula remain closely connected to the original contribution. The manuscript still has an identifiable central argument—the proposed tracking profile and edge—and the additions do not turn it into an unfocused collection of results.

The principal corrections are appropriate. Conjecture 4.2 now specifies a fixed character and the order of limits. The discussion distinguishes Connes's prolate quantity from the Weil-form minimum, corrects its prefactor, treats Zhu's certified value as an upper bound, and limits the analogy with [12] to endpoint saturated regions. The corrected zero-list heights also clarify the unsuccessful sum-rule closure without changing the geometric-matrix profile measurements. The newly cited theta-trial bound in [15, Theorem 2.1] is accurately distinguished from an asymptotic formula for the minimum; inspection of that source supports the revised attribution.

Targeted checks support the additional numerical evidence. Independent rescoring of the retained data reproduced the five new level-prediction residuals, ranging in absolute value from approximately $0.153$ to $0.444$ nats, and the substantially larger residuals of the alternative conductor-dependent prediction. The new profile comparison reproduces deviations of approximately $1.14\%$–$5.45\%$, with changes below $0.0037$ in $G$ between the two retained resolutions. High-precision root refinement from the saved $K=380$ coefficient vector confirmed upward displacement for all 89 resolved ordinates with $u\le1.6$. These checks use retained eigenvalues and coefficients; they do not independently regenerate the matrix eigensystems or certify the zero lists.

The following qualifications should accompany the revised interpretation:

- In Section 5.1 and the abstract, describe conductor independence as agreement with the interpolated $\chi_{-7}$ level curve over the tested cases. The new cases are not computations at exactly matched $d$. Their agreement supports replacing the earlier empirical formula, but does not establish that the level depends *only* on $d$. The reported free fit with a nonzero $\ln q$ coefficient and the acknowledged uncertainty in the $\ln d$ coefficient appropriately limit the conclusion.
- Label $C=-11.4\pm0.3$ in the abstract as a mean and empirical standard deviation across these runs with the coefficient $-9/2$ fixed. It is not a confidence interval for an asymptotic universal constant. Likewise, the two-resolution comparisons provide numerical stability evidence, not bounds on the remaining error as $K\to\infty$.
- State the median convention used for even-sized bins. The scoring script selects the upper middle value of $\log\varepsilon$, rather than averaging the two middle values. The usual averaged median also passes the profile criterion, so this is a reproducibility clarification rather than a defect in the conclusion.
- The newly appended supplementary count test fails its registered balayage criterion for $\chi_{-7}$. A brief acknowledgment beside the model limitations would balance the successful new tests. This finite-$d$ failure does not refute the stated asymptotic conjecture, but the prospective trial-function and closed-form calculations in the research notes should not be read as established results of this version.

These are scope and reporting qualifications, not grounds to reverse acceptance. The work remains numerical and conjectural concerning the actual Weil-form ground state. It does not resolve RH or establish full-window positivity, so acceptance does not warrant a verified-solution designation.
