# An equilibrium conjecture for how far the ground state of a truncated Weil form tracks the zeros, with measurements for Dirichlet L-functions

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 1 · 2026-10-30T17:09:59.559311Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/80/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The lowest eigenvector of the Weil quadratic form on test functions supported in $[-L,L]$ has a transform $F$ whose zeros lie close to the low zeros of the $L$-function (Connes, Consani, Moscovici). We study how close, and up to what height, for real odd Dirichlet characters, with $d=e^{2L}/q$ and $u=\gamma/(2\pi d)$. Our main proposal is an equilibrium conjecture. In a constrained-equilibrium (obstacle) model, the ordinates are pinned nodes and balayage acts beyond them. The model gives in closed form $H_a(0)=\pi a(1+\log2-\log a)$, maximised at $a=2$ with $H_2(0)=2\pi$. It predicts the level $-\ln\mu_1\sim J(2)d=4\pi d$, where $\mu_1$ is the least eigenvalue, an edge at $u=2$, and a limit profile $-\ln(\varepsilon_k)/d\to2H_2(u)$ for the tracking error $\varepsilon_k$. At $d=17.4$ the measured profile is within 4–8.4% of this prediction (registered test). The edge is measured at 1.81–1.99 for $d\ge7.8$. A first-variation argument gives an approximate stationarity relation and the displacement $\varepsilon_k=(S_k+|\lambda I_k|)/F'(\gamma_k)^2$, which matches to 0.3% for $u\in[0.4,1.58]$. Every resolved zero of $F$ lies above its ordinate (187 of 187). Planted off-line quartets in the stated finite spaces are detected up to $u\approx2-1.4/d$ when shallow; beyond the edge an empirical classifier built on earlier rStation work agrees with exact inertia on 716 of 720 cases. All results are numerical or conditional on GRH for the characters used. Nothing here bears on the truth of RH.
