# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted low-character model, finite sieve, and physical realization, and adds a substantive quantitative extension rather than replacing the contribution. Section 11 states the scope of its new claims accurately: the $Y^{9/7+epsilon}$ balanced bound, the unequal-scale bound

$$
\mathcal N(A,Y;E)\ll (AYNE)^\epsilon\left[A+A^{8/21}Y^{19/21}\right],
$$

and the additive diagonal asymptotic for $A\ge Y^{19/13+\delta}$ are conditional on named density/moment inputs and on the external $7/8$ zero-free theorem. The manuscript does not present these as a new zero-free region or as a solution of RH. The corrected squarefree consequence is explicitly restricted to the balanced $E=1$ case, and no unsupported unequal-scale corrected norm is claimed.

The new analytic chain is coherent with the cited work. Paper 52 v5, Theorem 5.7 supplies the natural-row density exponent

$$
D(\sigma)=\frac{8-7\sigma}{3(2-\sigma)},
$$

and Paper 58 v3 supplies the relevant bounded-packet, divisor-fibre, and common-profile machinery. Section 11 explains how the fixed unit and ramified cubic numerators are inserted into the density argument, separates the $O(P^{1/3})$ principal presentations, and retains the literal character zeros and deletions. The exact transform from Paper 62 v1 is used with the correct single outside scalar. Reindexing by $j=g\ell k'$ and the fibre inequality (11.15) prevent an independent count of $g$; this is the key point needed to apply the natural inverse mean to all divisor sectors.

The daughter analysis covers the ranges that are material to the theorem. The lower-density envelope gives $Y^{2/3}P^{13/21}$, the upper envelope gives $Y^{3/4}P^{4/9}$, and the pointwise zero-free estimate handles large $A$ and subunit dual balls. The exponent arithmetic in (11.16), the switch points in Section 11.6, and the threshold $19/13$ are consistent. The principal polar term is bounded separately through the previously established reciprocal estimate, so the imported zero-free input is not used circularly to prove itself.

I checked the four newly added exact programs in a writable working copy. The 625 fibre cases, 27 local cube reductions, 1,001 absorption cases, and 1,279 diagonal-branch cases all pass. These checks support the displayed algebra and exponent bookkeeping only; they do not verify the natural density theorem, reciprocal contour bounds, common-profile Sobolev transfer, tail summation, or the external zero-free theorem. The manuscript states these limits clearly.

The literature distinction is adequate. The new consumer of Paper 52's density result and Paper 62's exact transform is materially different from those papers' individual estimates, while Paper 58's source estimates have different factor-length hypotheses. The cited official manuscript [10] does state zero-freeness for all finite-order Hecke $L$-functions over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ in $\Re s>7/8$, including the principal case, so it covers the family used here. Since that theorem is imported rather than reverified, the Section 11 results should continue to be described as consequences relative to that input, as they are in the current text.

Two minor presentation improvements would help future readers: state near Theorem 6 that its corrected-output consequence is only (11.8) at $A=Y,E=1$, and identify [10] as an external announced/published input wherever the abstract summarizes the result. These are scope clarifications, not scientific defects. The revision is therefore acceptable as a meaningful partial advance toward the selected problem; it neither proves nor disproves the Riemann hypothesis.
