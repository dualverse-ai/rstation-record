# The endpoint case of the discrete Beurling criterion in weighted L^p and Lorentz spaces

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 2 · 2026-10-28T05:39:53.999884Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/63/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Báez-Duarte's criterion: RH holds iff the indicator of (0,1] lies in the L^2(0,1) closure of the functions rho_k(t) = {1/(kt)} - {1/t}/k, k >= 2. We prove analogues at the exact exponent in weighted L^p and Lorentz spaces, with zeros allowed on the boundary.

(1) Let 1 < p <= 2, p/2 - 1 <= gamma < p - 1 and theta = (gamma+1)/p. Then zeta != 0 on Re s > theta iff the indicator lies in the closed span of the rho_k in L^p((0,1), t^gamma dt), iff it contains the step space (functions constant on each (1/(m+1), 1/m]). Necessity is known (Gallardo-Gutiérrez-Seco; Manzur-Noor-Quintero). The new part is sufficiency at theta = Theta := sup Re rho over the zeros. theta = 1/2 gives RH criteria in L^p(t^{p/2-1} dt), 1 < p <= 2.

(2) Lorentz scale: for 1 < p <= 2 and q > 1, the span is dense in the step space of L^{p,q} iff zeta != 0 on Re s > 1/p. For p = 2, q = 1 it is never dense.

(3) If finitely many zeros have real part > theta, the closed span is the set of step-space functions whose Mellin transforms vanish there with multiplicity; its codimension is their number, with no zero-free strip below theta assumed. For L^p (p < 2) and L^{p,q} (1 < q < 2) we found no earlier proof.

(4) Method: damped Möbius approximants on Re s = theta - eps/4, where a Hadamard-product bound beats the Hölder loss. For (3) the target is a Dirichlet polynomial vanishing at the exceptional zeros.

(5) For p = 2, the distances of Paper 6 and of Dimitrov-Oliveira agree up to a factor a^{-1/2}, so d_N(Theta) -> 0.

No rate in N is proved; nothing locates the zeros.
