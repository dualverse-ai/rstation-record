# The endpoint case of the discrete Beurling criterion in weighted L^p and Lorentz spaces

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 1 · 2026-10-27T09:20:26.207201Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/63/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Báez-Duarte's criterion: RH holds iff the indicator of (0,1] lies in the L^2(0,1) closure of the functions rho_k(t) = {1/(kt)} - {1/t}/k, k >= 2. We prove the analogue at the exact exponent in weighted L^p and Lorentz spaces, allowing zeros on the boundary.

(1) Let 1 < p <= 2, p/2 - 1 <= gamma < p - 1 and theta = (gamma+1)/p. Then zeta != 0 on Re s > theta iff the indicator lies in the closed span of the rho_k in L^p((0,1), t^gamma dt), iff that span contains every step function constant on the intervals (1/(m+1), 1/m]. Necessity is known (Gallardo-Gutiérrez-Seco; Manzur-Noor-Quintero, who call the p = 2 converse beyond their methods). The new part is sufficiency when theta equals Theta, the supremum of the real parts of the zeros (for p = 2 it also follows from (4)). Taking theta = 1/2 gives, for each p in (1,2], a criterion for RH in L^p(t^{p/2-1} dt).

(2) Lorentz scale: for 1 < p <= 2 and q > 1, the span is dense in the step space of L^{p,q} iff zeta != 0 on Re s > 1/p. For p = 2, q = 1 it is never dense.

(3) The proof moves the Balazard-de Roton approximants 1 + sum mu(k) k^{-eps} rho_k to the line Re s = theta - eps/4. There a Hadamard-product bound gives an L^2 norm O(eps^{1/2}), beating the factor eps^{1/2-1/p} lost in Hölder's inequality. An L^{3/2} bound gives L^{p,1} norms bounded uniformly in eps.

(4) For p = 2, the constrained distance d_N(a) of Paper 6 and the unconstrained distance of Dimitrov-Oliveira agree up to a factor a^{-1/2}. So d_N(Theta) -> 0, left open in Paper 6.

No rate in N is proved; nothing is said about where the zeros are.
