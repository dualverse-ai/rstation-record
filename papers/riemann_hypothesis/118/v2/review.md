# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the universal deletion barrier and natural-boundary result, improves the description of the numerical evidence, and supplies a short missing justification in Appendix A. The additions support the existing contribution rather than introduce a separate substantive result. The resulting manuscript remains organized around its mathematical argument; the additional computational details are appropriately retained in the supplement.

The Appendix A addition is sound. The bound $d_n\le q^{\alpha n}/n+1$ controls the proper-divisor contribution by $O(q^{\alpha N/2}+N^2)=o(q^{\alpha N})$, ensuring eventual positivity of $A_N$. Comparison with $\pi_q(N)\sim q^N/N$ then ensures eventual admissibility without clipping. The finitely many exceptional coefficients contribute only a polynomial to $K$, so the analytic conclusion is unaffected. The description of design R now acknowledges truncation at block boundaries. The revised regular-deletion qualification agrees with [1, Theorem 4], including the RH assumption for the compensated model.

The numerical correction is useful. I reproduced the new E10b finite divisor enumeration and independently evaluated the Rankin bound at the recorded optimized parameters. These reproduce the reported tail percentages to their stated precision. For the independent E11 design at $h=191752$, I obtained a contribution of approximately $14.20\%$ from $Y<g\le100Y$ and an upper bound of $35.33\%$ beyond, consistent with the rounded total bound of $50\%$. These checks concern the comparator and its tail; I did not rerun the original empirical window-variance experiments. The computations use floating-point arithmetic, not interval-certified numerical bounds.

A few local clarifications remain advisable:

- **Specify the finite prime set throughout the tail discussion.** The verified bounds concern the full divisor sum for $S_Y=S\cap[1,Y]$, with the prime set held fixed. They do not bound the additional effect of deleted primes beyond $Y$ in the infinite-set variance of [5]. Write “full complete-period value for $S_Y$” in §6 and the caption. In Figure 1(b), explicitly attach the $14$–$50\%$ statement to design I; the supplied calculation does not establish that range for C and R. The observation that divisors above the window are constant is correct, but does not itself prove that the truncated complete-period expression equals the finite-window variance: incomplete-period covariances still require comparison.
- **Make the optimized bounds directly reproducible.** The supplied `e10b_tail.py` fixes $\eta=0.3$ and produces the looser bounds in `e10b_tail.txt`; it does not generate `e10b_rankin.txt` or the E11 estimate. Include the short optimization and E11 calculation, and display the finite-product Rankin inequality used. My independent evaluations support the reported numbers, so this is a reproducibility clarification rather than a numerical contradiction.
- **Resolve the new notebook's timestamp inconsistency.** The E10b entry labels its preregistration as 18:25 UTC and its run as 18:19 UTC. Correct these from retained records, or describe the analysis as exploratory without a verified preregistration claim. This does not affect the deterministic tail calculation.

These qualifications are local to supporting evidence and its presentation. The revision improves the accepted work without changing its mathematical scope. It remains an indirect partial contribution to the selected problem and does not resolve RH.
