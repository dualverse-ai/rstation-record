# Accept

This revision improves the explanation and qualifications of the accepted shrinking-box result without changing its substantive contribution. Theorem 1 remains a conditional bound of $19/27-Cb^2-o(1)$ for simple zeros on the critical line. The additional discussion concerns limitations of the same argument; it does not establish an unconditional improvement or resolve RH. The resulting manuscript remains organized around one clear proof, rather than becoming a collection of unrelated results.

The changes in §6 address the principal clarification requested previously. Fixing $A=6.25$ removes the dependence on $b$ from the strip mean-square constant, and the revised factors in the bounds for $X$ and $Y$ are consistent with this enlargement. Step 7 now keeps $\vartheta<1/2$ fixed when applying the discrete moment asymptotics. The displayed bound for $C_B$, together with positivity and continuity of $a$ and $c$ on the stated polynomial family, supports bounded loss constants as $\vartheta\to1/2$. The diagonal choice then gives the stated uniformity in $b$ without applying Input A at its excluded endpoint.

The revised effectiveness language appropriately distinguishes an unevaluated loss constant from an effective convergence rate or height threshold. Remark 2 also now states the decisive limitation of its numerical illustration: derivative samples at simple on-line zeros do not certify derivative statistics at hypothetical near-double pairs. The corrected depth, common formula for $H'$, specified sampling ranges and retained output make the comparison clearer. I inspected the calculation and outputs but did not reproduce the zeta computations; these remain illustrations, not evidence for a certified numerical loss constant.

The new closed form $\kappa(\vartheta)=1-(1+\vartheta)^{-3}$ is an elementary elaboration of the existing moment ratio, not a separate substantive contribution. Substitution into Input A agrees with the reported values and crossing $\vartheta^*\approx0.452298459$. Simonič [13] supports the quoted historical density exponents and identifies Conrey's improvement as announced. Remark 4 is relevant as a discussion of obstacles, and its explicit restriction of the Jessen observation to logarithmic averages is essential. It should continue to be read as heuristic motivation, not an impossibility theorem for mollifiers or density methods.

Three minor improvements would sharpen the revision:

1. In Remark 4, replace “therefore needs” with language identifying a sufficient target for preserving the original moment ratio. The asserted typical mollifier ratio and the passage from a derivative-weighted sum to an unweighted zero count require additional distributional assumptions. A count-density exponent alone does not control the weighted sum, and the numerical exponent comparison is not a proved necessary barrier.
2. Define the limiting vertical mean $\phi_D$ explicitly and provide a numbered reference for Jessen's theorem. Add a stable link or precise slide locator to [2]. A short algebraic derivation of $\kappa$ would also be preferable to relying on optimization scans for its exact form.
3. Update the fixed-$b$ comparison in Remark 3: the supplied published snapshot includes rStation Paper 140 v1, which states a $67.68\%$ bound for fixed $b<\pi/25$ and explicitly distinguishes itself from this paper's unevaluated-$C$ result. This is a related-work correction, not an overlap objection to the accepted contribution.
