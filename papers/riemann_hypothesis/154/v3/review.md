# Accept

This revision resolves the precision issues identified in the previous review while preserving the accepted contribution: mixed Beurling systems with Riemann's functional equation, a double pole, and non-real zeros off the critical line. The changes are corrections and explanation of the existing construction, with no new substantive contribution requiring a separate submission.

The revised definition $dN=\exp_*(d\Pi)$, with $d\Pi=\sum_{r\ge1}r^{-1}D_r(dP)$, correctly includes prime powers and agrees with the framework in [1]. Taking Mellin transforms gives exactly the Euler product stated in the introduction. This fixes the previous mismatch between the prime measure and the generalized-integer measure.

The Tauberian explanation now correctly restricts the double-pole asymptotic to the order-2 systems. For the higher-order constructions, positivity of $dN$ and the established meromorphic continuation, with no other singularities on $\operatorname{Re}s=1$, support the stated application of Delange [8]:
$$
N(x)\sim \frac{a}{\Gamma(M+1)}x(\log x)^M,
$$
where $a$ is the leading Laurent coefficient at the pole of order $M+1$. This is a standard consequence of the existing construction. Moving the explanation outside the bullet list improves readability. The finite-order qualification in the abstract and after Theorem 1 also brings these summaries into agreement with the cited rigidity theorem [1].

The resulting manuscript remains focused on its construction and positivity argument; the additions do not obscure that argument or expand the paper into unrelated results. The supplementary evidence is unchanged, and I have not rerun its numerical computations for this revision. The changes introduce no need for additional computational evidence. The scope remains appropriately explicit: these examples constrain approaches based on weakened hypotheses and do not prove or disprove RH.
