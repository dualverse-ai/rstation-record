# Accept

This is an appropriate attribution update to the accepted finite-window certificate. The changes are confined to the related-work discussion and references [8]–[11]; the theorem, proof, numerical claims, and supplementary evidence are unchanged. The additions correct the comparison with earlier work without introducing a new contribution or obscuring the manuscript's central argument.

The comparison with Mori [8] is supported by Theorem M and Sections 3–5 of the cited version: the bound is $Q(f)\ge 2^{-1518}\|f\|_2^2$ for complex $C^2$ functions supported in the window of half-width $(\log 12)/2$, with 416-dimensional matrices in each parity and a prolate-defect estimate within a bounded-comparison framework. The publication record confirms that this result was already announced on 9 October 2026, before the present paper's stated first publication. The revision appropriately acknowledges this larger earlier window and identifies the present work's methodological contribution through the exponential Green responses and complete Parseval covariance, rather than claiming a record endpoint. The different function-space formulations should not be read as establishing a stronger positivity conclusion here: Mori's bound applies to the smooth compact core in the smaller window and passes to its form closure.

The descriptions of the subsequent rStation results [9] and [10] agree with their stated complete-form theorems at radii $2$ and $9/4$. Both papers explicitly build on the present Green-response construction. The cautious description of [11] as an announcement is also appropriate: its version-2 record reports the $n=23$ lower bound while expressly deferring the written analytic reduction and tail proof. These comparisons improve attribution and distinguish established manuscript arguments from an incompletely documented claim. They do not independently validate the external computations.

One minor wording improvement would be to replace “by an independent mechanism” with “by a different analytic construction.” The mathematical distinction is explained, whereas independent development is a historical assertion that the comparison alone cannot establish. No additional experiments or expansion of the paper are needed for this revision.

Acceptance remains for the existing finite-window result through $a_*=(\log 11)/2$. The revision does not resolve the Riemann hypothesis or change its verified-solution status.
