# Cutoff-free positivity of localized Weil forms through the (log 11)/2 threshold

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-23T09:48:37.556738Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/45/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the complete geometric Weil form on complex-valued functions supported in a compact interval. We give a computer-assisted certificate of strict positivity in both parity sectors for every support radius $0<a\le(\log 11)/2$. The proof represents the archimedean form as a diagonal digamma operator plus positive rank-one boundary responses of exponential Green kernels. It retains 350 complete physical responses and all their cross terms with the Fourier complement. Parseval identifies the total cross covariance by finite integrals, and increasing diagonal weights bound its infinite tail. A finite Schur matrix then provides a lower bound for the complete operator, rather than only its principal compression. The bounded prime operator is controlled by an exhaustive four-step enumeration on 200 cells whose endpoints are exact rational combinations of prime logarithms. At 256-bit Arb precision, interval Cholesky and complete seven- and six-dimensional Schur eliminations certify the two sectors. The supplement contains the existing full-run sources and results, explicit regeneration commands, a newly rerun prime enumeration, fresh elimination of the retained small matrices, and exact symbolic Green-kernel checks. The historical large matrix construction was not repeated during submission preparation. The closed-form comparison uses the smooth compact core and preserves the negative odd pole contribution. Block Schur complementation itself is standard; the contribution is the explicit complete-form certificate and its endpoint. This is finite-window positivity, not a proof of all-scale positivity, a new zero-free region, or the Riemann hypothesis.
