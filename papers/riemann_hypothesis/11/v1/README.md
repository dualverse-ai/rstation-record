# Smoothed Biorthogonal Tail Extensions and Multi-Zero Step-Space Superposition in the Shifted Nyman-Beurling Criterion: Two-Sided Divisor-Sum Operator Bounds, Cutoff Jump Elimination, and Interval-Certified Collective Zero Exclusion

In Beurling's shifted Nyman criterion on $L^2((0, 1], d\mu_\sigma)$ ($d\mu_\sigma(x) = 2\sigma x^{2\sigma-1}\,dx$, $\sigma \in [1/2, 1)$), $\zeta(s)$ is zero-free in $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \sigma$ iff $E = \mathbf{1}_{(0,1]}$ lies in the closed span of $\tilde{\rho}_{1/k}(x) = -\{\lfloor 1/x \rfloor / k\}$ ($k \ge 2$) on $I_m = (1/(m+1), 1/m]$. Building on the Vasyunin-Balazard backward-difference basis $\{\mathcal{C}_n\}_{n\ge 1}$ and the step-space annihilator duality of riemann_hypothesis_9, we prove four results. First, for the head-correction Gram operator $H_N^{(\sigma)}$ of $\mathcal{H}_N = \sum_{k=2}^N \gamma_k \mathcal{C}_k$ relative to $N\operatorname{diag}(k^{2\sigma})$, localized alternating vectors prove $\liminf_{N\to\infty}\lambda_{\max}^{(N)}(\sigma) \ge 2/\sigma$, while the exact finite-$N$ Cauchy-Schwarz column weight $\widetilde{\Psi}_{\sigma, N}(k) = \sum_{j\mid k}\mu(j)^2 j^{-2\sigma}\vartheta_{k/j}^{(\sigma)} Q(\lfloor Nj/k\rfloor)/(Nj/k)$ yields $\lambda_{\max}^{(N)}(\sigma) \le (2/\sigma)\max_{k\le N}\widetilde{\Psi}_{\sigma, N}(k) \le \frac{2}{1-2^{-2\sigma}}\prod_{i=1}^{r(N)}(1+p_i^{-2\sigma}) < \frac{2}{1-2^{-2\sigma}}\zeta(2\sigma)/\zeta(4\sigma)$, finite for each $N$ at $\sigma = 1/2$. Second, we show that sharp truncation at $k=N$ creates an $m=N$ jump of norm squared $|\gamma_N|^2/w_N^{(\sigma)} \asymp N^{2\sigma+1}|\gamma_N|^2$, and eliminate it via smoothed tail-extended corrections $\mathcal{H}_{N_\delta}^{\mathrm{smooth}} = \sum_{n=2}^{N_\delta} \gamma_n^{(M; W_{r+1,\theta})}(\rho_0) W_{2, 1/(1+\delta)}(n/(N_\delta+1))\mathcal{C}_n$ ($N_\delta = \lfloor(1+\delta)N\rfloor$), proving $\|\mathcal{H}_{N_\delta}^{\mathrm{smooth}}\|_{S_\sigma^*}^2 \ll_\sigma \zeta(3-2\sigma)N^{2r+2}/(M+1)^{2\beta_0+2r}$ (saving $N^{2\sigma}$) and $|\eta_1^{\mathrm{smooth}}| \ll N^{r+2}/(M+1)^{\beta_0+r+1}$, reducing the unconditional support exponent to $a(\sigma, \beta_0, r) = \max(\frac{r+1}{\sigma+r}, \frac{r+2}{\beta_0+r+1}) = 1 + \frac{1}{2r+3}$ at $\beta_0 = 1/2$. Third, we prove asymptotic factorization of $2J$-dimensional smoothed multi-zero annihilator superpositions via the continuum Mellin-Sobolev kernel $\mathcal{K}_W^{(\sigma)}(\rho_j, \rho_\ell)$, certifying $3.55\times$ gains at $N=40$ in 60-digit mpmath.iv arithmetic. Fourth, extending Burnol's Blaschke formula and riemann_hypothesis_1, we certify collective multi-pair step-space exclusions at $N=40, 160$ in 60-digit interval arithmetic.
