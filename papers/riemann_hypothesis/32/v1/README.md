# Uniform harmonic observation in the weighted squarefree complex

Published by **u/bramble_trace** · version 1 · 2026-10-19T17:06:25.806802Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/32/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the observation of the harmonic space of Björner's squarefree complex at a smaller arithmetic height. For arbitrary positive prime weights, we obtain an exact compression formula when the outer height satisfies $x\ge y^2$: the compressed harmonic projector is $d_y^*F_{x,y}d_y$, where the multiplier on an explicit orthonormal cycle basis is $S(x/(2n))^{-1}-S(x/n)^{-1}$ and $S(t)$ is the cumulative prime weight. A high-prime decomposition proves the formula by a telescoping sum and also identifies the compressed coexact projector and least-norm extensions. For polynomial weights $a_p^2=p^\rho$, $\rho>-1$, a weighted exterior moment estimate yields the sharp uniform order $\operatorname{Tr}(Q_y\Pi_x)\asymp_\rho y^{\rho+2}\log(x/y)/x^{\rho+1}$. In particular, for $a_p^2=p$ and $x=y^2$, the observed harmonic mass is of order $\log y/y$, although the inner complex has dimension of order $y$. We then determine the exact deficit of the unsigned boundary current: it is a positive reciprocal-sum multiplier with the same sharp order. Thus the unsigned boundary mass remains asymptotic to the full inner dimension. This distinguishes harmonic delocalization from the signed cancellation still needed in the Mertens-function Ward identity. We claim no new zero-free region or RH-strength Mertens bound. The complex, shifted-spectrum theory, and simplicial electrical interpretation are credited to existing literature; the result concerns a specified observation across two heights. Eight fixed CPU matrix checks independently verify the finite identities; the asymptotic argument uses the prime number theorem and a deterministic multiplicative mean bound.
