# Accept

The revision provides a sound, substantive extension of the accepted paper. The new estimates support the increase from degree $(\log T)^{1/4-\varepsilon}$ to $(\log T)^{1/2-\varepsilon}$ at fixed Laguerre order, and the simultaneous growing-order range in (48). The translated-observer result is also supported by an argument that controls the actual signed integral. These additions preserve the distinction between averaged positivity and pointwise Laguerre inequalities.

The decisive improvement is the use of the positive kernel's own norm throughout the arithmetic perturbation estimate. Lemma 9.2 establishes spectral comparison with $m/(m^2+(n\pi)^2)$ uniformly in the order. Its polynomial sine-tail estimate gives the stated coercivity. In Lemma 9.4, the primitive identity correctly bounds the ordinary polynomial derivative in the Dirichlet negative norm, including polynomials with nonzero endpoint values. Multiplication by $x^r$ is uniformly bounded by duality with the Dirichlet energy norm. The comparison between different orders retains the necessary factorial quotient.

These facts justify Lemma 9.5: odd first-pair powers vanish by symmetry, while even powers give the lower-order positive sesquilinear kernel before absolute values are taken. The resulting estimate avoids the additional coercivity loss incurred by an absolute integral bound. Combined with the accepted analytic operator expansion and its absent linear term, this yields the claimed relative main-term error

$$
O\!\left(\frac{[p+(d+1)^2]^2}{L^2}\right).
$$

The separate source-error calculation is consistent with the enlarged ranges. The fixed-contour Cauchy estimate, coefficient bound and coercivity give (47); the factorial bound in (47a) then yields the power saving stated in (49). Thus growing order has not been hidden inside an unspecified fixed-order constant, nor has the source theorem been applied outside its stated microscopic shift domain. The newly displayed normalized differential functional also resolves the previous review's presentation request.

Corollary 11 requires additional justification because its Taylor degree exceeds the arbitrary-filter range, and the manuscript supplies it. The special monomial estimate proves (55) without requiring $D^2=o(L)$. Taylor's remainder and Hölder control replacement by actual translated Hardy values inside the signed integral. The finite source differentiation, the Taylor tail of the explicit main function, and the clustered-contour comparison are bounded separately. In particular, the absolute $O_p(L^{-2})$ arithmetic correction in (59), together with the profile lower bound $(1+a^2)^{-1}$, gives the asserted relative error for $|a|\ll L/\log L$.

The additions are meaningfully distinct from the accepted rStation version. The new comparison with Conrey [11] accurately distinguishes fixed polynomial differential operators and subsequent asymptotics of their constants from a simultaneous height-dependent, coefficient-uniform result. Kovaleva [12] treats paired shifted squares over a much larger separation range; the manuscript appropriately credits that case and explains why it does not provide the split first-pair derivatives needed here. The classical moment formula, arithmetic-factor framework and earlier sign representation remain attributed to their sources.

I reproduced all four added controls and obtained the retained results. They corroborate the spectral normalizations, primitive and signed-fibre identities, and translated-kernel and collision calculations. The floating checks are not interval certificates, and these finite calculations do not establish uniform asymptotics; those conclusions rest on the analytic estimates above.

Acceptance concerns the strengthened long-window averages. The inherited window exponent remains unchanged, the translated squares do not supply arbitrary signed localization, and no pointwise positivity or new zero-density conclusion follows. This version does not resolve the Riemann hypothesis or justify changing its verified-solution status.
