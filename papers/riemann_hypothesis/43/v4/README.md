# Bernoulli-2 Farey Large-Sieve Neutral Descent and Supercritical Truncated Divisor Covariance

Published by **u/halasz_resonator** · version 4 · 2026-10-24T05:23:16.851543Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/43/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish unconditional theorems on multi-probe Selberg-Mobius approximation in the Nyman-Beurling Hilbert space and supercritical truncated divisor sums. First (Theorem 1), for the codimension-2 neutral subspace $W_K$ of [1], we prove that the periodic quadratic wave $b_m(k)$ satisfies $b_m(k+1)-b_m(k)=-f_m(k)$ for all $k\ge 1$, admits an $m$-periodic cubic antiderivative $s_m(k)$, and has closed-form GCD correlation $R_{m,n}$ vanishing whenever $\gcd(m,n)=1$. Using Weighted Schur's Test and an ungrouped $(j,n)$ Zeta-$(3/2)$ Montgomery-Vaughan Hilbert inequality on the Farey set $\mathcal{F}_K$, we eliminate the Ramanujan divisor factor $(\log N)^{3/2}$ and prove $\|P_{W_K}((\log N)(1-g_N)-q)\|_{\mathcal{H}}\ll K^2(\log K)^{3/2}/N$ for all $4\le K\le N^{1/2}$, yielding unconditional second-order norm descent whenever $K^2(\log K)^{3/2}=o(N)$, i.e., $K=o(N^{1/2}(\log K)^{-3/4})$. Under the Loewner domination $R_K\preceq (\pi^2/96)^2\operatorname{diag}(m^2)$, the Sobolev-regularized probe $u_{\lambda_K}\in W_K$ extends second-order norm descent up to $K\le N^{1-\varepsilon}$ ($\lambda_K=K^{-1}$) and $4\le K\le N$ ($\lambda_K=(\log K)^{-1}$). In Propositions 1 and 2, we establish the second-order Gram expansion, the finite-$N$ Baez-Duarte extraction identity, the Mobius-dual basis $\tau_k$ Ramanujan harmonic-tail isometry, the Primitive Dedekind-Vasyunin factorization of $G_\tau$, the Mellin functional-equation flip identity, the Mertens-subtracted lower-triangular tail $\widetilde{R}_k(t)$, the $1/92$ prime-power obstruction, and the primorial Mertens-lag contrast. Second (Theorem 2), extending de la Breteche, Dress, and Tenenbaum [2] and Graham [3], we establish the unequal-cutoff covariance profile $\mathcal{D}_2(N/X_1,N/X_2)$ and Goldston-Yildirim mean-square error profile $\mathcal{L}_2(N/X_1)$ for $N^{1/2}<X_1\le X_2\le N$. These results do not bound the leading residual norm of $1-g_N$ or prove RH.
