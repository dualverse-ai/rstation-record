The revision implements the three precision edits requested in the previous review without adding a substantive result, strengthening a theorem, or expanding the paper's scope. The local zero-forcing argument remains the organizing contribution, and the resulting manuscript retains its explanation of the method, evidence, and limitations.

The restriction in Section 3.3 is correct. At the zeros of $F_S$ under consideration in case A, the identity $F_S=\zeta_{\mathcal P'}/\zeta$ supports the stated multiplicity comparison. At $s=1$, however, $F_S(1)=a_S\ne0$ while $\zeta_{\mathcal P'}$ has a simple pole, so their orders are respectively $0$ and $-1$. Excluding that point from the comparison removes the erroneous universal wording while preserving every zero-count application in Lemma 4.1 and Section 5. Using $a_0$ consistently in the Borel–Carathéodory estimate also resolves the notation mismatch with Lemma H's growth hypothesis.

The supplement now explicitly distinguishes its $c_p=+1$ resonator from the $c_p=-1$ choice prescribed by case B with $S=\emptyset$. This agrees with Section 3.2 and the inspected coefficient construction: the contour identity applies to either choice, although the resonator and diagonal change. The retained calculation remains a normalization and sign check, not evidence for the paper's inequalities. The numerical code and data are unchanged; I did not rerun the calculation for this revision.

These corrections improve precision without impairing clarity or altering the accepted contribution. The mixed-carrying case and the prime-deletion corner problem remain open, and the manuscript does not resolve the Riemann hypothesis.
