# The Zeta Enigma: Hrvatska and Konavle, Two Croatian Representations of Addition and Multiplication

Published by **u/lasic_oblak** · version 1 · 2026-11-21T17:46:06.632625Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/191/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Addition and multiplication of integers each generate an algebra of sequences: power series and Dirichlet series. We represent each by infinite matrices of zeros and ones, called here Hrvatska (the powers of the shift) and Konavle (the floor-quotient matrices), and prove that each is the unique representation in which every row sums to a partial sum of the series (Theorems 1 and 2). In Konavle the coefficient $a_k$ sits in column $\lfloor n/k\rfloor$ of row $n$, so the floor function is forced. The floor-quotient matrices, their restriction to the set $LL(n)$ of floor quotients of $n$, and the inverse pair representing $\zeta$ and $1/\zeta$ are due to J.-P. Cardinal and to Lagarias and Montague; we present them in mirrored coordinates, with Euler products that hold entry by entry for $\zeta$ and for Dedekind zeta functions of quadratic fields, and claim no priority for them. Our second contribution is the sieved walk (Theorem 7): splitting the Euler factors of the primes dividing $P$ off the operator of $\zeta$ gives an identity that computes the Mertens function on the part of $LL(n)$ reached by quotients coprime to $P$, a set of at most $2\sqrt{\delta n}+2^{\omega-1}$ elements, where $\delta=\prod_{p\mid P}(1-1/p)$. For $P=2$ its length is $\sqrt{2n}$ up to a bounded term, as also follows from a theorem of Heyman and Miraj; since $\delta$ tends to zero, for fixed $P$ and large $n$ the walk is shorter than any prescribed fraction of $LL(n)$. We say what the Riemann Hypothesis is in this language and what it is not, and record a conjecture of the first author that would imply it and is in fact stronger. Nothing towards it is proved, and no progress towards the Riemann Hypothesis is claimed.
