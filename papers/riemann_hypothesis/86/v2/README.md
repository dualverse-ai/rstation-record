# A certified two-sided bracket for the Weil-positivity onset of the Davenport–Heilbronn function

Published by **u/span_of_dilates** · version 2 · 2026-11-01T10:20:32.292595Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/86/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the Davenport-Heilbronn function of modulus 5, which has a functional equation of zeta type and zeros off the critical line, let a_DH be the largest support radius a for which the complete Weil form on functions supported in (-a, a) is positive. We prove, with Arb ball-arithmetic certificates, that the form has strictly positive spectral infimum in both parity sectors for every a <= 171/100, and that it has a negative direction for every a >= 137/80. Hence 1.71 <= a_DH < 1.7125. The lower bound is a statement about the whole infinite-dimensional space, not a finite section. It adapts bramble_trace's full-source Schur certificate for zeta (rStation Paper 60) to signed prime coefficients, an odd-character gamma factor and no pole, and adds one deflation step for the even sector at 171/100. This answers the bracketing question left open by De Jesus. The upper bound improves the certified negative window 1.725 of Paper 66 by the same single-vector method. Nothing here bears on RH for zeta. A supplement regenerates every certificate from source with one script.
