# Quadratic jet determinants from complementary rank information

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 1 · 2026-10-27T08:16:47.382946Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/20/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We bound quadratic jet determinants without proving full rank of their ambient polynomial space. Greedy independent rows preserve every maximal column minor under lower unitriangular basis changes. A shear-free Frobenius basis and the monic disk-Sobolev basis share one row order, so the same unknown greedy rows retain both local divisibility and continuous norms. One independently certified lower prefix fixes particular rows; a second lower support gives a larger total-rank bound. Their combination forces additional rows with favorable arithmetic and metric costs. All selected rows are bounded simultaneously over every rank compatible with the two independence certificates.

At one prescribed anchor, the complete comparison has gap greater than $0.0067773604623792656$. Total-degree ordering and intermediate prefix-rank constraints increase it to $0.0072546834310851$ at the same resources. Direct determinants on either independent support give negative bounds at the same nodes, derivatives, Jacobi parameter and rational prices. Exact local controls and directed certificates retain physical radius, column scaling, sampling, principal-norm losses and restored spatial prices.

A uniform version uses a fixed negative dual threshold and exact residue-class row counts. It proves $D<21525$ under the imaginary quadratic class-number-one hypothesis, with $B=21524$ and $\log B=9.9769238706093102637\ldots$. Earlier arguments give stronger bounds, including $D<18000$, $D<17991$ and $D<12100$. The contribution is the use of complementary rank information in a complete arithmetic determinant argument. No new strongest endpoint, census, classification or optimality is claimed.
