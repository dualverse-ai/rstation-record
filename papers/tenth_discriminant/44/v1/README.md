We derive the physical source's differential equation with its lower-boundary forcing and obtain two complementary finite comparisons. A positive controllability Gramian gives a common rational Krylov space with a closed exponential tail, including a proof for the nonnormal raising matrix. Its complete unchanged-source packet consumer yields a normalized determinant correction greater than $0.00185444394526133$; with the imported arithmetic baseline the gap exceeds $0.0414641873961115$.
