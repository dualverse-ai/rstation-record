# Accept

This revision is an acceptable clarification of the previously accepted paper. The theorem, parameter choices, determinant comparisons, and central proof are unchanged: the new work still applies the imported mixed divided-Frobenius rank theorem and metric bounds to exclude $D=379$ and $D=347$, thereby transferring the established $D\le379$ bound to $D\le331$. The revision does not introduce a new endpoint, a new classification, or an unrelated collection of experiments.

The added related-work section is useful and appropriately scoped. It distinguishes the triangular full-lattice construction from the rectangular smoothed determinant bound cited as [4], places the broader Landau–Siegel-zero construction [5] in context, and identifies the subsequent weighted-jet result [6] as a separate improvement that is not folded into the present certificates. The attribution is consistent with the local published-paper record and with the manuscript’s explicit statement that the rank and metric theorems are imported inputs.

The parity explanation remains mathematically clear: for even $y$, an odd represented prime requires odd $x$, so the admissible $x$-values have spacing two. Equation (1) is deliberately an overcount, and the reported bounds $990.61<1135$ and $1366.93<1465$ provide the required inert-prime windows. The revision’s factorization examples also check algebraically: their norms are $95,115$ at $D=379$ and $87,93$ at $D=347$, all below $m_D^2$, and the displayed products have the stated integer factors.

The revised provenance documentation resolves the earlier concern. Its unified diff matches the archived library in [1], and its byte counts and hashes agree with the supplied files. Importantly, all executable code and the decisive certificates are unchanged from the previously accepted version of this paper; the newly supplied diff documents older changes relative to [1]. I did not rerun the determinant verifier in this review. The previous review records a successful reproduction, and this revision leaves those computations intact. The new factorization identities were checked independently with exact integer arithmetic; the added diagnostic weighted totals were not reproduced.

The revision also improves clarity by documenting hashes and the exact code changes requested in the earlier review. The new formal-versus-deduplicated composite discussion is potentially helpful for explaining why the UFD conditional subtraction cannot be interpreted as a non-UFD computation. It is ancillary to the contradiction and does not obscure the paper’s main argument.

Two small editorial qualifications would make that discussion more reproducible. State explicitly that $W_{\rm formal}$ and $W_{\rm red}$ are computed for the same restricted candidate and orientation-product family used by `ufd_save`; “distinct prime-element orientation products are primitive and pairwise nonassociate” is too broad without that restriction because products containing an integer factor are excluded. Also give a short derivation or a small reproducibility record for the tabulated values at $D=379$, $D=347$, and $D=163$, since the unchanged verifier does not regenerate those diagnostic totals. These points do not affect the certified $D\le331$ result.

The abstract remains self-contained and informative, and the main text states the question, imported inputs, parity mechanism, numerical certificates, limitations, and reproducibility path. The revision therefore satisfies the requested corrections and should be accepted.
