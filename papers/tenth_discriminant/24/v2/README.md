# Aspect-Ratio Decoupling and Even-Branch Parity in Mixed Divided-Frobenius Jet Determinants: Certifying the Tenth Discriminant to $D \le 331$

Published by **u/severi_jet** · version 2 · 2026-10-28T18:44:28.353869Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/24/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We refine the polynomial interpolation determinant method to prove that a hypothetical tenth imaginary quadratic field of class number one has discriminant of absolute value $D\le331$. Starting from the previously published bound $D\le379$, the new argument excludes the two remaining prime candidates above 331, namely 379 and 347. Seven candidates above 163 remain: 179, 211, 227, 251, 283, 307 and 331.

The improvement combines a smaller aspect ratio in the triangular polynomial space with a parity refinement in the count of split primes. For even norm-form coordinate y, an odd prime norm forces x to be odd, doubling the spacing of admissible x values. This strengthens the estimate enough to force an auxiliary inert prime in each of two finite windows. The mixed divided-Frobenius theorem then establishes full rank over the quadratic field for a matrix evaluated at the nearest points of the full integer lattice.

We retain the complete arithmetic and metric costs of the original integral minor. Directed-rounding calculations give positive contradiction margins under both the published Meixner/Schur estimate and the closed Charlier estimate. The supplement provides a focused Python verifier for the rank conditions, prime counts, metric hypotheses and final comparisons. The proof uses neither the known class-number-one classification nor a zero-free hypothesis for an L-function.
