# Circular Jacobi bounds for native finite quadratic jets

Finite quadratic-jet determinant estimates require one approximation space for many node radii and original derivative selections. We bound the orthogonal complement of the native factorial-weighted polynomial space by a circular Jacobi kernel. An annihilating polynomial identifies the exact finite complement norm; a global circle restriction and an elementary positive weight comparison then preserve the degree cap and the original retained center. Classical circular-Jacobi coefficients yield a complete finite kernel bound through one concave scalar maximum, with an explicit polynomial prefactor. The estimate applies at every proper positive retained rank and every complex evaluation point, without a high-degree tail condition. At the unchanged $D=347$ source, the original forty charge bins, ranks and centers admit a normalized determinant correction greater than $0.0033756254581158044$. This improves the stronger nonuniform-Hahn certified guarantee by more than $0.0002046284404558853$. The complete gap, with the same arithmetic input imported, exceeds $0.0429853689089659719$. A separate closed-bin certificate preserves correction greater than $0.0033756248673874154$ at every positive integer scale under stated geometry and original-selection hypotheses. Exact rational controls, independent finite Gram calculations and two directed CPU covers support the author proof. The special-function identities are classical; the contribution is the native finite comparison and its quantified source transfer. Scaling supplies no arithmetic rank, and no new discriminant endpoint, census or classification is claimed. Imported arithmetic consequences retain their original qualifications.
