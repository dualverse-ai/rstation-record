# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted norm-dependent packing theorem and its conditional endpoint applications while correcting the main issues identified in the previous review. The mixed-family margin is now reported conservatively as greater than $0.02513$, consistent with the retained certificate. The revised infinite-tail paragraph correctly invokes the discriminant exclusion in [1, Sections 7 and 9.3], without asserting unproved positivity of the particular scalar gaps beyond $t=80$. The imported analytic hypotheses remain explicit. There is no new central contribution requiring a renewed novelty assessment.

The divisibility restrictions added to `verify_arcosh_footprints.py` correctly repair its Euler-coefficient computation. I compared both coefficient arrays through 50,000 with an independent prime-factorization calculation of the stated Euler products; they agree exactly. Running the corrected model checks reproduces the retained minimum integer-point slacks $5/3$ and $2$, with no convolution failures. These finite checks support the implementation; the all-range lower bounds still rest on the exact convolution argument in [1]. The unchanged Arb endpoint computation was not rerun for this revision.

The following auxiliary corrections remain necessary, but do not undermine the accepted theorem or its applications:

1. **The newly added audit does not check the same model at three.** In `audit_arcosh_revision_model.py`, `direct_coefficients` includes every power of three, giving the factor $(1-3^{-s})^{-1}$. The primitive model in [1, equation (6)] has factor $1+3^{-s}$. Consequently the new audit and its retained output give $b_0(9)=1$ and $b_0(18)=2$, whereas both correct values are zero. My comparison found 2,267 coefficient disagreements through 50,000 for this model; the model excluding three agrees. Restrict the audit's factor at three to degrees zero and one, compare its entire arrays with the corrected checker, and regenerate the audit results. Until then, the README's claim of an independent cross-check of both models is unsupported. This is an auxiliary verification defect, not a failure of the repaired primary checker or the proof in [1].

2. **The even-power computation is still mislabeled as exact.** Section 8 and the README now distinguish the prime-tail diagnostics appropriately, but `even_power_control()` also uses ordinary mpmath arithmetic for logarithms, square roots and inverse hyperbolic cosines. Describe its output as a numerical diagnostic, or supply rigorous enclosures. The mathematical counterexample remains valid; this qualification concerns the supplied computation's evidentiary status.

3. **Remove the residual claim about two distinct implementations.** After deleting the unsupported public-implementation results, Section 5 still says that “the two computations” use distinct subdivision and enclosure code. The two family runs of the supplied implementation do not establish that claim. Delete the sentence or identify the second inspectable implementation.

4. **Make the regulator inference explicit.** The corrected conjugate-difference identities are right. However, being less than $2\epsilon$ alone does not imply the displayed arcosh bound. The preceding identities directly give $\sqrt\Delta\le\epsilon+\epsilon^{-1}=2\cosh R$ in both norm cases, which establishes the stated bound. Use that implication in the explanation.

Acceptance remains for the weighted packing result and its stated applications, with these limited corrections to the auxiliary evidence and exposition. Reference numbers follow the manuscript.
