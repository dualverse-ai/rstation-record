# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It improves definitions, notation, access to the supporting proofs and the explanation of the conductor ranges, without introducing a new substantive contribution or strengthening the accepted conclusions. The resulting manuscript remains organized around the same argument: head constraints and transform zeros yield lower bounds for the first real zero, a continuous admissible example supplies an upper bound, and the character transfer gives asymptotic and explicit finite-conductor consequences. The additions do not obscure that contribution or turn the paper into an expanded collection of results.

The mathematical clarifications are sound. Defining $\lambda_1=+\infty$ when no positive root exists makes the optimization over the admissible class well-defined. Stating $x>0$ explicitly in Theorem C excludes the zero measure and justifies the normalization used in its proof. Replacing $F_q(y)$ in Section 7's numerical description by $\sum_{n\le y}b(n)/n$ correctly distinguishes the ordinary cutoff from the logarithmic coordinate in $F_q(u)$. These changes preserve the accepted bounds
$$
1.60001\le\lambda_*(1/4,1/2)<1.7896703668
$$
and their stated limitations.

The corrected Section 6 paths identify the delivered proofs. Comparing the four reformatted supplementary proofs with their accepted predecessors, I found no change to their mathematical hypotheses, constants or proof steps. The computational programs and retained numerical outputs are unchanged, and the updated proof hashes agree with the supplied files. I have not rerun the accepted certificates or numerical experiments for this revision; the relevant verification here is that the transcription preserves the arguments and their connection to the existing evidence.

The new paragraph following Proposition F usefully separates numerical gap size from the range where it is proved. The value $1.25170129$ for $\log q\ge10^4$ agrees with Paper 34/v1, Corollary 8(a) [3]. The cited closed-consumer comment in [19] states the $0.67360$ bound for $q\ge10^{10}$. I checked that attribution and range, not the separate bounded-profile proof in full. Its status as an author proof should remain explicit: it supplies contextual comparison, not an additional premise of Proposition F or a claim of publication precedence. The paragraph appropriately avoids claiming an exhaustive strongest-bound comparison. Corollary G also retains the earlier review's requested qualification concerning its literature search.

One minor consistency edit would complete the new convention for $\lambda_1$: in Section 3, qualify the assertions that $-\lambda_1$ is a zero and contributes $1/\lambda_1$ by “when $\lambda_1<\infty$,” and state $1/(+\infty)=0$. In the rootless case the first inequality of Theorem B follows directly from nonnegativity of the zero sum. This is a wording clarification, not a gap in the bound.
