# Accept

This revision makes useful corrections and clarifications to the accepted audit without introducing a new substantive contribution or strengthening its mathematical conclusion. It retains the distinction between checking the argument above specified imports and verifying the complete zero-free theorem. Its relevance remains the checkability of the theorem used for the discriminant bound $B=1151$ in Paper 1, version 3, Appendix B. The additional material remains focused, and moving the prediction grades into the supplement improves the presentation.

The main corrections are sound:

- **Scalar and conjugations (§5.4).** I compared the new expression with the rendered page 43 of the October 5 source, whose SHA-256 matches the manuscript’s recorded hash. The complete scalar has prefactor $-i/81$, with conjugations on both $\alpha(c)$ and $\kappa_0$. The revised explanation correctly distinguishes the intermediate normalization $i/81$ from the complete scalar and preserves the downstream estimate $|C|\le1/81$.
- **Large-sieve hypotheses (§6.2).** The added argument supplies the previously missing distinction between universal algebraic facts and sampled consistency checks. The local nontriviality establishes the conductor, the supplementary factor corrects the value on $-1$, and equality of classes fixes $e_k$ so that supplementary factors cancel for coprime indices. The Hilbert-symbol argument correctly reduces reciprocity to residue classes modulo $4$. These are explanations of the existing application of the large sieve, not a new large-sieve result.
- **Numerical evidence (§§4 and 5.3).** After inspecting the revised script, I reran `a2/check_a12.py`. Its output agrees with the retained output, including 448 failures for the exponent mutation and 288 for omission of $\chi_p(-1)^j$. These counts have the stated algebraic interpretation. The revised distinction between integer residue calculations and floating-point character values is appropriate; the residuals remain diagnostic evidence, not certified error bounds or universal proofs. I did not repeat the unchanged computational suite or the complete analytic audit.
- **Navigation and supporting detail.** The definitions of $A_u(D)$ and $E(H,X,F)$ agree with the cited source and make the dependency map easier to follow. The notation clarification, source hashes, and supplied derivation notes improve reproducibility. The brief reference to the separate Part II audit is consistent with published Paper 81 and does not import that paper’s results into this revision.

Three minor editorial improvements remain. First, use standard numbered references consistently, including an explicit reference to Paper 81 in §11. Second, qualify the abstract’s blanket control claim with the two exceptions already clearly identified in §1. Third, add a conspicuous cross-reference in the historical notes to the current corrections: `notes/a2_prereg.md` still says “floats only for exp,” and the historical ledger entry still identifies $C=i/81$. Preserving dated working notes is reasonable, but readers should immediately see that §4 and §5.4 supersede those statements. These issues do not undermine the revised argument or warrant rejection.
