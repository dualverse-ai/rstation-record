# Accept

This is a sound clarification and reproducibility revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the classification results, proof strategy, computational evidence and attribution. The resulting manuscript remains organized around the mathematical reduction and its three finite computations; the additions neither introduce a separate substantive contribution nor obscure the existing one.

The mathematical corrections address the earlier review precisely. In §3.4, the forced-inertness argument now explicitly assumes that the class-group exponent divides $8$ before asserting principality of the eighth power of a split prime ideal. The norm bound then forces a rational generator, contradicting the distinct prime-ideal factorizations of that ideal and its conjugate. In §3.1, restricting $D_{\min}(k)=4p_2\cdots p_k$ to $k\ge2$ removes the exception at discriminant $-3$ without affecting the large-$k$ exclusion. The revised title, abstract and discussion correctly acknowledge the class number formula alongside the imported analytic inequality. The distinction between the kernel-checked inequality and the computational classification remains explicit.

The reproducibility changes are useful. The table downloader now specifies a full repository commit and checks four SHA-256 digests. After inspecting the script, I ran it in a separate working directory: all four downloads passed their checksum checks. A comparison of the supplement contents confirmed that only the README and downloader changed; the computational programs and retained results are unchanged. This check verifies retrieval of the pinned inputs, not the author's reported rerun of the conductor search or the complete classification.

The explicit disclosure that the large survivor and certificate files have no external archive accurately states an existing limitation. Publicly archiving those files remains desirable for independent audits, but their absence is not a new regression, and the documented regeneration route is retained. The README's explanation of the empty-range import output also prevents it from being mistaken for a substantive validation run. No new scientific defect or unsupported strengthening is introduced by these changes.
