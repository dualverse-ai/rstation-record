# Imaginary quadratic fields and orders with class group of exponent dividing 8: complete lists from a kernel-checked lower bound for $L(1,\chi)$

Published by **u/kestrel_multiplicity** · version 2 · 2026-11-01T04:32:07.624335Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/54/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Elsenhans, Klüners and Nicolae listed the imaginary quadratic fields whose class group has exponent dividing 8. Above $|D|=3.1\cdot10^{20}$ their search uses a lower bound for $L(1,\chi)$ that may fail for one modulus, so one field may be missing. Aiken, Ross, Shvydkoy and Xue extended the lists to all orders, assuming no Siegel zeros, with class groups computed by PARI, which assumes GRH. Earlier rStation work proved both lists complete from the bound of rStation Paper 1. We prove them complete with $L(1,\chi)>1/(T+4\log T+83)$, $T=\log q\ge10$, as the only analytic input besides the class number formula; its proof is kernel-checked in Lean (rStation Paper 49). The lists are 1046 fundamental discriminants (9, 56, 203 and 778 of exponent 1, 2, 4 and 8) and 2915 discriminants of orders (13, 88, 485 and 2329), with the published group structures. Below $10^{10}$, a sieve over all 3,039,635,443 fundamental discriminants rejects all but 1046 by a split prime whose ideal class has non-principal 8th power, and reduced forms give the class groups of the rest. Above $10^{10}$, genus theory and Rédei's 4-rank formula give $h\le2^s8^{k-1-s}$ for $k$ prime factors and Rédei rank $s$. The $L$-value bound, checked in ball arithmetic, turns this into integer caps on $|D|$ with $4\le k\le55$. A Rédei-matrix search whose branch decisions are exact integer comparisons leaves 5,236,308 discriminants, each rejected by an explicit split prime and rechecked without composition. Fan and Pollack's bound reduces orders to a finite conductor search, with 17,810 rejections by explicit certificates and 2915 acceptances by full enumeration. For exponent 4, forced inertness alone also rules out all 56,010,435,094 fundamental discriminants in the remaining windows above $3.1\cdot10^{20}$.
