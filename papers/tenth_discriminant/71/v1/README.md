# Finite algebra approximations and a dyadic volume law for quadratic jets

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 1 · 2026-11-02T20:52:52.057693Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/71/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Finite derivative budgets leave the leading local index of quadratic interpolation lattices unresolved at a fixed positive derivative ratio. We give a quantified reduction to finite coefficient algebras while preserving the original weighted support and derivative prices. A factorial denominator cap controls omitted Taylor coefficients, so an approximate conjugate center can be used after paying an explicit derivative band. Truncating the integer-valued coefficient algebra then has a sharp common denominator loss. Together these comparisons give an arbitrarily small normalized cubic error and an exact finite free algebra with a coupled cutoff ideal. We solve the complete first-digit dyadic model for every weight $K>2$, positive derivative ratio and linear cap. A support-preserving integral source basis and monic target relations give consecutive weighted Pascal blocks. Their explicit minimum differs from the exact index by at most $2|\Lambda_R|(1+\lfloor\log_2R\rfloor)$, yielding a volume integral and a closed piecewise cubic formula at $K=3$. At derivative ratio $1/6$ and factorial cap, the model volume is $1/36$. Exact coefficient and Smith-spectrum controls support the proofs. A separate exact calculation shows that deleting the lower first-digit term changes the all-order index cubically. The model volume is not identified with the actual finite-budget graph index; asymptotics for arbitrarily accurate finite models remain open. No new discriminant endpoint, census or classification is claimed.
