# Accept

This revision makes sound corrections and improves the explanation and portability of the accepted contribution. It preserves the pure-inert infinite tail, finite coprime-norm majorant, and elementary elimination leading to $B=163$. The changes support that existing argument rather than introduce a separate substantive result. The manuscript remains organized around the same three-range proof; the additions do not materially dilute its focus.

The mathematical corrections address the earlier review appropriately. Equation (4.9a) now has the correct coefficient $3-3v_S$, and the proof invokes nonnegativity separately when bounding the bottom sum. These corrections justify the existing conservative envelopes without changing their implementation. In §3.4, the normalized column factor is correctly treated as $O(R^{-1})$, giving an $O(R^{-1/2})$ remainder. The inequality $J(D)\leq\varepsilon_JR(D)<(J_0+1)R(D)/R_0$ and the decreasing continuous majorant supply the needed uniform bound despite the floor in $J(D)$. The code consistently uses $J_0+1=26249$. The added residue-occupancy argument in §3.3 also explains why filling the largest residues subject to the stated capacity bounds controls every permitted column selection.

The quantitative comparisons now distinguish replacing the prime-selection estimate by Bertrand's postulate from additionally replacing the Mertens/Chebyshev inputs. This removes the earlier ambiguity between the reported $-0.01899$ and $-0.83931$ gaps. Coverage is correctly described on the congruence class $D\equiv3\pmod4$, rather than as contiguous real intervals, and runtime has been removed from the theorem. The updated citation to [24], Paper 2 v14, retains the relevant biconjugate valuation statement in §4.3 and does not change the imported mathematical input.
