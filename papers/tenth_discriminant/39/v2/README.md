# A Closed-Charlier Divided-Frobenius Jet-Determinant Proof of the Class-Number-One Theorem via Pure-Inert Superfactorial Tails and Coprime Norm Majorants

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/39/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Paper 22 v5 (Sections 8.3 and 8.10) established an elementary-input Hasse-Schmidt jet-determinant classification for imaginary quadratic fields $K=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-D})$ of class number $h(-D)=1$ from the Mixed Divided-Frobenius rank theorem, principal-norm obstruction, lattice covering, Bertrand's postulate, and the closed-form Charlier disk bound, while Papers 24 and 36 combined hybrid Frobenius-ghost small-prime valuations with Rosser-Schoenfeld prime bounds in the infinite tail and composite norm sieving in the finite windows. Building on these imported rank, local valuation, and closed-Charlier theorems, this joint paper contributes four specific advances that certify $B=163$ ($\ln B=\ln 163\approx 5.0937502008$) without spectral grids, complex-Hermite matrices, Rosser-Schoenfeld bounds, or composite norm sieving: (1) a single closed-Charlier determinant family certifying $[1{,}955,1{,}000{,}003]\cup\{1{,}747\}$ across $18$ windows covering every $D\equiv 3\pmod 4$ in $[1{,}955,1{,}000{,}003]$ at upper-endpoint radial covering parameter $\eta(D_{\mathrm{hi}})$ with a discrete coprime norm majorant (minimum window margin $+0.024436>0$), plus the singleton $D=1{,}747$ ($+0.071675>0$) and a $6$-prime Rabinowitsch filter below $1{,}955$; (2) a pure-inert $K=19$ tail at $D_0=10^6$ ($S(D)=\lfloor(D-3)/(4K)\rfloor$, $R(D)<m_D=(D+1)/4$, so the principal-norm debit vanishes identically) closed using Erdős's elementary proof of Bertrand's postulate ($\Delta_{\det}(10^6)\ge +0.075432$, asymptotic slope $+0.168396$); (3) an exact-remainder two-trapezoid superfactorial bound on $\Lambda=\{(r,s):r+Ks<R\}$ refining the Paper 17 Section 5.1 / Paper 22 v5 Section 8.3 factorial baseline by $+0.001249$ nats at $S_0=13{,}157$ (and gaining $+0.0944$ nats / $+0.9147$ nats over the Mertens/Chebyshev split using Bertrand with [17, Theorems 6 and 9] retained / Bertrand with elementary Chebyshev-Mertens bounds), paired with an exact non-negative division-remainder identity for weighted floor sums $A_m(S)$ ($m=p^{2\ell}\ge 4$) and a uniform lower bound $A_p(L)\ge L^3/(6p)-L^2/4$ for all primes $p\ge 2$; and (4) a standalone pure-Python $26$-digit directed-rounding verifier.
