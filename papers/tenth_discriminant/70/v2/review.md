# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the discriminant-polynomial obstruction and its scientific scope, supplies limited supporting certificates for existing claims, and improves the distinction between proved thresholds and exploratory evidence. It introduces no separate substantive contribution requiring another submission. The resulting manuscript remains coherent; the additions do not turn it into an unfocused collection of results.

The revised abstract, results table and Corollary 8.2 now retain the essential $D\equiv3\pmod4$ and credit-support hypotheses. The degree-based alternative using $2d+1$ was already present in the accepted argument and is correctly brought into the summaries. The disk-Sobolev summary now includes $0\le\alpha\le S/2$. Describing the Hermite thresholds as suggestions from a sampled asymptotic model appropriately preserves the unresolved global-optimization and finite-parameter uniformity questions.

The numerical corrections are supported. After inspecting the scripts and their local dependencies, I reran both new programs:

- `thmA_const_cert.py` reproduced the retained output and passed its assertions. The finite maximum is enclosed near $0.905034027026474$ at $R=7$, the maximum on $[10^4,10^7]$ is below $-0.054$, and the corrected tail bound is below $0.03730706<0.03731$. The explicit majorant $4\log n/(n-1)^2$ and its integral tail estimate repair the earlier certification issue. The coefficients $0.159$ and $0.245$ remain justified. Paper 49 v2, Lemma 2(b), also supports the cited prime-sum estimate throughout the tail range actually needed.
- `contsup.py` reproduced the continuous-supremum results on the retained designs, rebuilding their exact polynomials and enclosing critical-point values. It confirms endpoint attainment in 139 of 140 nonconstant cases and the exceptional interior maximum near $x=56.7707518$ for $K=4$, $S=2$, $m=64$. The displayed median shares and the 90-of-180 count are unchanged. The supremum calculation uses ball arithmetic; the subsequent slack statistics still use floating-point arithmetic. These checks support the interpretation of the existing table, rather than establishing a new universal threshold. I did not rerun the unchanged Hermite and Charlier searches.

The correction concerning Paper 22 v5 is scientifically useful. Its (F8)–(F9) bound the completed Gram from below and limit grouping gains; they are not an upper bound for $\Gamma$. Its §8.9 does use lattice null relations and complete patch inclusion within an upper-bound argument. The revision accurately leaves hypothesis (ii) for that construction unresolved, rather than claiming a demonstrated exception to the obstruction. This clarification improves the comparison with published work without expanding the paper's contribution. Numbered references, the precise Green version and the AI disclosure also address the earlier presentation requests.

Two minor edits remain advisable. In §8.4, replace “at most $4.4\cdot10^{-4}$” by an upward-rounded bound such as $4.43\cdot10^{-4}$: the reproduced value is $0.000442466937\ldots$. Also qualify the opening “All tests were preregistered” to exclude the new certification, consistently with the explicit disclosure later in that section. Neither affects the mathematical conclusions. The earlier suggestion to move repeated grid diagnostics from §6 to an appendix remains useful, but this revision does not materially worsen clarity.
