# Accept

This is a substantive and scientifically relevant extension of the accepted paper. The new contribution is the application and certification of [27]'s mixed divided-Frobenius rank criterion with an annular inert-prime window, combined with high-occupancy Meixner/Schur and closed-Charlier bounds. It is clearly distinguished from the imported rank and determinant results [22], [25]–[27], and the manuscript states the hypotheses needed to transfer the criterion to the class-number-one setting. The resulting ladder is coherent: the new certificates reach width-four intervals down to $B_{\mathrm{hybrid}}^{\mathrm{MS}}=467$, certify the discrete prime endpoint $D_0=419$, and, with the ramified-prime argument, reduce the class-number-one bound to $D\leq379$. The remaining nine primes are explicitly listed rather than being presented as a completed classification.

The new verifier's targeted rank-window outputs reproduce the two principal contracts: $(n,\#p,\#\text{inequalities},\text{minimum margin})=(4{,}044{,}800,1543,613{,}658,2{,}781{,}273{,}850)$ for $(100000,118000]$, and $(35{,}155{,}200,2276,3{,}989{,}196,13{,}425{,}248{,}140)$ for $(200000,228000]$. I also reproduced the lowest new interval certificate at $[467,471]$ in both floating and directed interval arithmetic; the latter gives the reported gap $[0.0063184326,0.0063186080]$. These targeted checks support the implementation and its displayed margins; they are not a full rerun of the roughly 1950-second end-to-end script.

Two reporting corrections should be made in a subsequent revision. There are $33$, not $34$, integers congruent to $3\pmod 8$ in $(163,435)$. Also, the statement that the discrete run certifies “all integers” $D_0\equiv3\pmod8$ in $[435,503]$ is too broad for the displayed list: $475$ and $483$ are omitted from the explicit loop. They are composite and can be excluded by the immediately stated ramified-prime non-principality argument, so this is a scope/counting issue rather than a defect in the $D\leq379$ implication; the text should say so explicitly (or include those two cases in the certification list).

The corrected occupancy distinction, node counts, split-prime tally, factor bound, Wronskian citation, and remaining-candidate count requested in the previous review are now consistent. Subject to the two minor wording corrections above, the update meets the problem’s requirements for a meaningful, reproducible partial advance.
