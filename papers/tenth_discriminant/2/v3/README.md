# Explicit Bounds for the Tenth Discriminant via Maximal-Order Euclidean Disks, Hasse-Schmidt Divided Jets, and Convex Variational Duality

Let $-D < 0$ be a fundamental discriminant with ideal class number $h(-D)$ in $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-D})$ and maximal order $\mathcal{O}_K = \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1+\sqrt{-D}}{2}]$, and let $S = \{3, 4, 7, 8, 11, 19, 43, 67, 163\}$. Building on the polynomial interpolation method of OpenAI, Banks, Kerr, and Green, we derive explicit class-number-one bounds without $L$-functions or zero-repulsion estimates. Evaluating polynomials on a Euclidean disk $E_{\rho_n} \subset \mathcal{O}_K$ lowers the Archimedean radius exponent to $\alpha + \beta = 3/4 < 1$ and makes $p = 2$ unramified and inert for $D \equiv 3 \pmod{8}$. Replacing the rectangular bidegree box $[0, R) \times [0, S)$ by the weighted triangle $\Lambda_{\text{tri}} = \{r + Ks < KS = R\}$ halves the Sylvester resultant bound to $RS$, yields an exact quadratic floor-sum weight $w_r(p)\log p = \frac{S_r(R,K,p)\log p}{K W_{\text{main}}}$ that is strictly decreasing on $[3, R]$, and supports a biconjugate Hasse-Schmidt Frobenius basis and a staircase concavity-ramp variational functional $\mathcal{F}(w)$ with exact Pascal hockey-stick $\Delta_0$, certified by supporting-hyperplane duality and self-certifying interval arithmetic. Purely within the Euler-Rabinowitsch inert window $p \leq \frac{D-3}{4}$, we certify $D \leq B_{\text{RS}} = 5,795,003$ ($\log B_{\text{RS}} \approx 15.572507$) under Rosser-Schoenfeld bounds and $D \leq B_{\text{prime}} = 4,700,539$ ($\log B_{\text{prime}} \approx 15.363188$) via finite prime sums; combining $\Lambda_{\text{tri}}$ with the split-prime $z'$-tower valuation $v_p(s!)$, joint 1D column remainder sorting, and forced UFD composite-norm subtraction certifies $D \leq B_{\text{hybrid}} = 875$ ($\log B_{\text{hybrid}} \approx 6.774224$), and finite elimination of the remaining $89$ candidates via $p \in \{3, 5, 7, 11\}$ completes the proof.
