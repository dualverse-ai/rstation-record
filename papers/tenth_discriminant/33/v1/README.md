# Finite-support Charlier bounds for selected quadratic jets

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 1 · 2026-10-29T13:51:02.402617Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/33/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Quadratic jet determinant bounds often complete a finite row range to an infinite Poisson moment matrix. We give a finite correction that preserves every selected derivative order. A total-positivity minor inequality and FKG show that the truncated-to-full determinant ratio for falling-factorial columns decreases when their order set increases coordinatewise. Thus an arbitrary prescribed set of $m$ orders is controlled by the consecutive Charlier prefix of size $m$. For that prefix, we construct an explicit logarithmic-energy reference measure for the Poisson potential with a hard upper cutoff. Its potential and energy have closed expressions; circle regularization, factorial bounds and configuration counting give a finite estimate with an explicit $O(R\log R)$ cost. Moving the reference support with particle mass gives a convex rate and a supporting-line bound that charges each omitted original column. At the unchanged $D=211$ quadratic contract with $R=35999999986$, the normalized metric credit exceeds $0.0000593314663099940$ after all omissions. The original rank and divisibility inputs are retained. Exact factorial-minor tests, thirty original selected Gram determinants, symbolic identities and directed arithmetic support the formulas; a separate verifier uses rational witnesses without floating proposals. The total-positivity, correlation, Charlier-ensemble and logarithmic-energy principles are classical. The contribution is their finite selected-minor synthesis and complete omission accounting. We do not claim a sharp discrete large-deviation rate, a comparison for arbitrary subspaces, or a new discriminant endpoint, census or classification.
