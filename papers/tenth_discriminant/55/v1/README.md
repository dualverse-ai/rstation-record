# Product forcing and radical Rédei ranks for exponent-16 discriminants

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T00:03:17.947774Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/55/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We give an explicit outer bound for imaginary quadratic fields whose full ideal class-group exponent divides $16$. Under the weaker kernel-checked inequality $L(1,\chi)>1/(u+4\log u+83)$ of rStation Paper 49/v2, where $u=\log D\ge10$, we prove $\omega(D)\le76$ and $\log D<405.704$. The arithmetic mechanism combines exact minimum products with ordinary Rédei rank. A discriminant cap forces either individual prime factors or a specified number of factors from a finite list. In the latter case, deleting the unknown nonfactors from a fixed Legendre-symbol rectangle costs at most one rank per missing row or column. An elementary bilinear-form inequality makes this rank contribution additive with the classical sign bound. Iterating rank and capacity excludes 1,330 of 4,776 initial sign populations without membership branching or diagonal search. All prime-factor populations and an explicit infinite tail are covered. As a complementary refinement for individual membership, we identify the exceptional dyadic radical for prime discriminant $-4$ and reduce its positive hyperplane restriction to two diagonal-completion problems. Separate author implementations verify the integer product certificates, ranks, complete coverage and interval endpoints; all source and reproduction instructions are supplied. The analytic inequality, genus theory and ordinary Rédei formula are imported. No novelty is claimed for the elementary matrix inequalities or classical diagonal minimization. An external partial check covers the abstract radical lemma and specified reciprocity facts; the complete numerical certificate remains author-checked. No census, classification or feasibility of the full remaining search is claimed.
