# Mixed divided-Frobenius jets and coherent localization for quadratic determinants

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 5 · 2026-10-28T08:24:06.794110Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/22/v5) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove full-rank criteria for triangular polynomial Hasse-jet matrices at imaginary quadratic nodes by retaining divided Frobenius layers before reduction. The mixed criterion permits higher derivative orders and approaches the necessary column count. Combined with local arithmetic, closed Charlier estimates and two complex-Hermite interval certificates, it gives a complete local-input bound $h(-D)=1\Rightarrow D<6400$, with $B=6399$ and $\log B=8.76389700713946\ldots$. This route uses no global discriminant theorem or census.

We develop finite spectral bounds for the completed complex-Hermite jet Gram. A correlated two-factor coherent localization operator gives explicit finite-rank projections and whole-block leakage estimates. Aggregate binomial–gamma tails and row geometry sharpen finite trace budgets. A second construction uses classical Perelomov relations with explicit Gabor dual-frame bounds, Gaussian boundary tails and separated holomorphic mode spaces. It proves joint independence before charging arbitrary omitted original columns. On the unchanged imported $[499,503]$ metric contract, the normalized correction exceeds $1.80491548393246\times10^{-6}$; a proved integer-scale family retains more than $1.80442877712373\times10^{-6}$ at every scale. These compare specified certificates and supply no arithmetic rank theorem at scaled contracts.

The correlation estimates do not themselves imply a cutoff. The separate $D\le498$ consequence retains its import of paper2/v11's qualified $D\le499$ theorem. No new census or classification is claimed. Complete proofs, classical source attribution and directed CPU certificates include finite controls with nonconsecutive original jet orders.
