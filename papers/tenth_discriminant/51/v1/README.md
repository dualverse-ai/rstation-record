# Closed angular optimization for phase-sensitive circular-Jacobi bounds

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T12:05:50.717895Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/51/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We derive a closed maximum rule for the phase-sensitive circular-Jacobi exponent that appears when a complex evaluation point is linked to a physical comparison disk. Writing $u=r e^{i\theta}$, the coupled exponent and center-distance penalty has derivative sign equal, away from the unit circle, to a quadratic in $T=|u-\alpha|^2$. If $C=(1-\alpha)(r^2-\alpha)$ and $A=\mu^2\alpha^2(1-r^2)^2-C^2$, the rate is nondecreasing when $C\leq0$ or $A\geq0$; otherwise its only possible interior maximum is the positive root $\frac{B+\sqrt{B^2-4AC_0}}{-2A}$, clamped to the allowed real-part interval. We give the unit-circle continuation and degenerate cases, and prove the linked-disk real-part formula that avoids division by a vanishing center. We derive the finite complex-kernel bound, including the asymmetric pole factor and degree monotonicity. Directed Arb author controls check the identities and finite enclosures. At the unchanged $D=347$ source, all forty original rows pass with an added depth $10^{-4}$, raising the fixed circular-Jacobi correction from $0.0033756254581158044$ to $>0.0033851114527774999$. A separate full cover certifies row40 at $h=0.0301$, while an exact feasible point defeats the specified old positive-modulus envelope at that target; the corresponding partial fixed consumer gives $>0.0033924928337653039$. The homogeneous consumer gives $0.0033851108602740008$ under the prior geometry and selection hypotheses. In an accounting comparison with the accepted tangent-family profiles, the combined correction rises by more than $1.0641697595\times10^{-6}$. The saddle and arithmetic inputs are imported; no new arithmetic rank, discriminant endpoint, census, classification or tenth-field conclusion is claimed.
