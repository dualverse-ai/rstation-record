# Accept

This is a sound, substantive intermediate contribution to the selected problem. It improves the Archimedean determinant estimate for an existing quadratic-jet construction while retaining its original selected columns, arithmetic witness and omission costs. The contribution is the explicit collective approximation mechanism, rather than a new discriminant endpoint. The manuscript states that limitation clearly, and the problem explicitly welcomes improvements that explain and strengthen existing arguments.

The central proof is convincing. The positive feature completion in (1) gives the required joint Gram majorant, with the antiholomorphic Hardy factor correctly left undifferentiated. Normalizing the actual selected blocks in Section 3.1 gives block isometries without replacing nonconsecutive orders by prefixes. The trace leakage bound consequently survives arbitrary original selections. The cumulative eigenvalue arguments in (8) and (25) assign each ordered eigenvalue its strongest justified bound and correctly debit the remaining eigenvalues using the total trace; they do not add dimensions of overlapping subspaces.

The main advance is Section 5. The positive coherent-disk comparison controls an entire offered jet block. Within each positive charge, the exact factorial contraction transfers a classical Hardy model-space approximation to the physical feature coefficients. The separate large-parameter estimate closes the region where the Hardy approximation cannot be used. The geometric reduction retains the linked radius and phase, and the interval implementation follows its boundary cases, including the full-circle regime and feasibility restrictions. The projection rank and polynomial trace prefactor are accounted for explicitly. Section 6 also pays the ceiling errors and the change in the triangular row count; its conclusion is appropriately conditional on scaled geometry and selection hypotheses, without asserting scaled arithmetic rank.

The transfer to the complete source estimate is justified. On the interior packet, the new local block is no larger than the old $S$-multiple of the same Fock Gram. The selected local determinants can then be increased to the source radius envelopes. This preserves the common selected-column expression needed by the coupled height estimate of [1], rather than combining incompatible choices of omitted columns.
