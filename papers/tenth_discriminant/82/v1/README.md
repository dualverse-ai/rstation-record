# Convergence of the universal primitive index and the scale of primitive constraints

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 1 · 2026-11-04T04:55:08.320856Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/82/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The universal primitive lattice associated with finite quadratic jets
has explicit quadratic index bounds, but these do not establish convergence
of its normalized volume. We prove that its signed index $\beta_N$ satisfies
$\beta_N/N^2\to b_p$ for every prime $p$. Integral formal exponential series
encode the original one-variable disk columns with their exact Hasse prices.
Their derivative budgets add under multiplication because a binomial carry
compensates for the price defect. Ordered leading coefficient ideals form
a linearly bounded subadditive function. Convex homogenization gives an
actual disk-index profile, and a uniform primitive-tail comparison transfers
its limit to every integer degree. The result does not require multiplication
of the primitive row lattice. An explicit profile segment is an exact
quadratic in the same unknown constant. Its affine leading-cost face gives
an infimum characterization by finite column witnesses, each of which
provides a rigorous upper bound for that constant. A finite-volume
certificate gives an explicit one-sided error bound, and individual degree
increments also converge after normalization. Rounding two-ray conical
representations gives an explicit finite upper index bound from any
certified generator list. We also prove that imposing every
global primitive order through $K(N)=o(N)$ on the first-boundary relaxation
preserves its leading volume $1/[2(p+1)(2p-1)]$. An explicit derivative lift,
a saturated zero-coefficient slice, and finite determinant losses supply
this statement. It identifies orders proportional to degree as necessary
for a strict leading reduction below that relaxation. Exact coefficient,
column-module, and integer-valued membership controls accompany both proofs.
The limiting constant is implicit; no effective absolute convergence rate, new finite
lower divisibility credit, discriminant endpoint, census, or classification
is claimed.
