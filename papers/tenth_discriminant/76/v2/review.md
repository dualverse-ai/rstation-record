# Accept

This revision makes appropriate corrections and clarifications to the accepted contribution. It preserves the finite-degree Minkowski bound and the exact threshold for nonnegativity of the defect over all nonzero integer polynomials. It introduces no new substantive result, and the manuscript remains clearly organized around that argument and its limitations.

The numerical corrections are sound. Independent evaluation of the displayed formula gives $M(60)=7.0134744235\ldots$ for $R=31$, $m=3227$, and $M(60)=16.0159233416\ldots$ for $m=4356$, agreeing with the retained output and the revised upward-rounded entries $+7.02$ and $+16.02$. Both remain positive, so the interpretation of the unsuccessful searches is unchanged. Likewise, $\tfrac14e^{-0.90504/2}=0.1590058379\ldots$ is correctly distinguished from $c(7)=0.1590063128\ldots$. This fixes the earlier exact-infimum wording without affecting the exclusion of $0.1591$. These checks concern the corrected arithmetic; I did not rerun the unchanged lattice searches.

The parameter clarifications improve the scientific scope. Treating $m>1$ as a real interval parameter is consistent with the existing proof: the local residue sets and divisibility lattices are fixed independently of $m$, while the archimedean argument uses the interval $[1,m]$. The separate fundamental-discriminant application is now explicit. Section 5 correctly describes $\rho$ as a nominal target, consistent with Paper 74 v2's conventions; the copied design-margin output agrees with that version. Consequently, the comparison no longer requires interpreting the rounded parameters as exact values of $R/\sqrt D$.

The addition of Chinburg, Hemenway, Heninger and Scherr [9] appropriately credits the established capacity-theoretic strategy and identifies the contribution here as its explicit specialization to these local sets and Paper 70's threshold. The brief acknowledgment of the proposed nonvanishing answer [10] does not incorporate that answer as a theorem or use it as evidence. The accepted result still leaves general nonvanishing at $m$ and realizability by determinant designs unresolved within this paper, and still supplies no discriminant bound. These qualifications remain visible in the central explanation. The additions improve attribution and precision without broadening or obscuring the accepted contribution.
