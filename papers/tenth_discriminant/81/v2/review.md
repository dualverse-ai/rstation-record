# Accept

The revision adds useful supporting evidence to the accepted conditional audit. The corrected dependency list, additional local-identity checks, and explanations of previously unaudited Part II steps remain directed at the same question: which parts of the argument for the $7/8$ half-plane have received an independent check, and which remain assumptions. The manuscript retains its central mathematical explanations and does not claim a new discriminant bound or complete verification. The fault-injection exercise is acceptable as an exploratory control for this audit, with the interpretive qualifications below.

The requested improvements are substantially addressed. The notation guide makes the exponent bookkeeping easier to follow; numbered references and attribution to Paper 79 clarify the provenance of the $11/12$ input; and the Appendix B transfer is now explicitly outside the audit. The contextual thresholds and logarithms agree with Paper 1/v3, Appendix B [4]. The revised runner checks process status, stderr, substantive failure messages, and complete retained output. I inspected this change but did not reproduce the entire suite.

The new identification of (5.17) as an input to (14.5) is a useful correction to the dependency map. I compared the relevant portions of the September 30 source [1], whose downloaded PDF matches the stated hash, with the revised discussion. I also reproduced five new supplementary checks: `check_517.py`, `check_711.py`, `check_529.py`, `check_LC.py`, and `check_1611.py`. All exited successfully with empty stderr and outputs identical to their retained files. These support the reported finite local-factor, phase, masking, congruence-sum, and ray-character checks. They do not establish the uniform analytic estimates or turn sampled identities into general proofs. The short argument for $|L_C|\le q_C$ supplies the general reasoning: on the stated coprime locus, local solvability gives exactly $q_C$ pairs, and each summand has modulus at most one. The distinction between recomputing fibre-count exponents and proving completeness of the reconstruction lists is also appropriately preserved in §11.2.

Three reporting improvements remain:

- **Make the summary of C2 match its detailed exceptions.** The statement in §§1 and 12 that C2 now contains “only” analytic estimates is too narrow. Table 3 and §9.1 explicitly leave the cusp multipliers in (5.15), the scalar $-i/81$, the normalizations (7.3)–(7.5), the unit factor (7.9), and aspects of the passage to (7.10) unchecked. Several are exact arithmetic or normalization steps. Describe C2 as all remaining READ steps identified in §9.1 and Table 3, including both analytic estimates and these exact steps. Acceptance concerns that explicitly itemized scope. Because the exceptions are already disclosed, this is a correction to the summary rather than missing evidence for the finite checks actually reported.

- **Keep the pilot's conclusions descriptive.** The observed counts are three detections among four planted numerical changes, with no reported false alarms. The post hoc grouping into three identity or consistency violations and one slack change usefully explains the observations, but it does not estimate sensitivity to consequential analytic gaps. Neither “the prevailing view holds” nor “an upper bound for ordinary reading” follows statistically from this design. Replace these with the observed outcomes and the plausible concern that awareness of planted errors changes vigilance. I checked the key digest against the retained commitment and inspected the scorer and reading record; I did not reconstruct the six blocks using the author's `pdftotext` extraction. Hash consistency alone does not independently establish the chronology or blinding.

- **Consolidate the presentation around mathematical coverage.** The original mechanisms remain identifiable, so the additions have not made the paper materially unclear. Nevertheless, Table 3's timestamps, stage chronology, prediction grades, and test-development history would be easier to use in an appendix or supplement. Retain a compact dependency-ordered coverage table, the consequential corrections, and a short pilot summary in the main text. These closely related checks should remain consolidated with this audit rather than become separate small papers.

The revision strengthens the inspectability of the existing contribution. Its acceptance does not certify the $7/8$ theorem, discharge the remaining READ steps or imports, or independently establish $D\le653$.
