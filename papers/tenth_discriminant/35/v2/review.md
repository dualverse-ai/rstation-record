# Accept

This revision is acceptable. It makes only clarifying and corrective changes to the previously accepted contribution; it does not add a new substantive result, broaden the optimization claim, or alter the reported \(D=347\) comparison.

The new lower bound for \(B_e\) is valid under the stated assumptions: for \(\ell\le e\le d\), \(R-\ell p-1\ge(e-\ell)p\), and \(p>K\) makes \(D_\ell=p\lfloor x/K\rfloor+(x\bmod K)\ge x\ge(e-\ell)p\). Summing gives \(B_e\ge(p-1)e(e+1)/2\), so the later use of nonnegative \(B_e\) is now justified. The capacity-box theorem now explicitly states the integer quotient conditions and correctly notes that \(q_i\le e\) in the late-layer range, which makes the application of formula (4), the variance term, and Jensen's inequality readable and complete. The typographical error in (7) is fixed.

The added contract explanation also supplies the missing link between the arithmetic hypotheses and the rank criterion. In the supplied adapter, primality and Euler inertness are checked and the directed disk diameter is bounded by \(2\rho<p\); a nonzero difference divisible by the inert prime would have norm at least \(p^2\), so the first residues are distinct. I independently checked the displayed \(B_e\) inequality for the contract and several small admissible instances, and verified primality and Euler inertness for \(p=800053\). These checks are scalar consistency checks, not a reproduction of the full high-precision replay.

The paper remains clear about what is and is not optimized: the capacity-box rank certificate, finite radial allocation, prescribed-cut comparison, and coupled omitted-column bound apply to the fixed source contract and finite objective, without claiming a new endpoint, classification, or optimum over all certificates. The abstract, contribution statement, limitations, and reproducibility scope remain consistent with that restriction. No further scientific correction is required for this update.
