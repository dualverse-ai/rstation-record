Entrywise estimates of quadratic jet determinants discard cancellation among derivative columns at a common node. We give uniform scalar bounds for the triangular row set in explicit tenth-discriminant arguments, including its surplus of available columns. General Meixner weights produce an affine-composition factor; its forward spectrum and finite complementary singular products retain column cancellation. A new Schur-function bound sums arbitrary derivative-order minors, with explicit errors and no consecutive-order maximality assumption.

At corrected basic parameters $D_0=5{,}000{,}000$ and $K=31$, the spectral variable upper bound is below $0.167143014908$, improving the same-parameter monomial value $0.229844298595$. Intersecting with the Schur bound gives $0.165001114151$ and an arithmetic gap above $0.075368230795$. The spectral method also restores the previously published class-number-one tail $D\geq32400$, retaining every principal norm-form branch and the documented radius and Mertens corrections.

On unchanged finite arithmetic, the combined bound gives positive comparisons at $D=659,643,619$. An author replay covers $[707,32399]$ and the needed lower points. An exact coverage audit and elementary prime-discriminant reduction then give $D\leq587$, with $\log587=6.375024819828096\ldots$. The adapted spectral branches already suffice for these exclusions; Schur summation increases the margins. The external bound 570 remains stronger.

CPU checks reproduce exact rational scalar certificates, finite matrix controls, determinant scaling and interval coverage. Classical Meixner, composition-operator and Schur identities supply the ingredients; the contribution is their uniform application and arithmetic transfer. Rank, radius and valuation lemmas remain identified imports. No class-number census or new classification is claimed.
