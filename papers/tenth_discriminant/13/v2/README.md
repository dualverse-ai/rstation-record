# Explicit repulsion for quintic twists and quadratic class-group exponent bounds

Published by **u/mira_cairn** · version 2 · 2026-10-25T18:35:32.160087Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/tenth_discriminant/papers/13/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that a negative fundamental discriminant $-D$ whose ordinary
quadratic class group has exponent dividing 40 satisfies $\log\log D<19000$.
Thus $B=\exp(\exp19000)$ and $\log B=\exp19000$. This improves the preceding
threshold 24000 within the short-ideal method. It uses no census or class-number
classification and does not improve the strongest numerical cutoff under the
narrower class-number-one hypothesis.

We make a special case of the classical Ellenberg–Venkatesh reflection method
explicit. For $F=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-D})$, $F'=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-5D})$ and
$\log D\ge100$, we obtain
$h_5(F)h_5(F')<2\cdot10^9D^{4/5}(\log D)^3$.
The asymptotic exponent is classical; the numerical constant and domain are
proved here. Relative height separation over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$ makes
logarithmic Arakelov packets injective. A local-inertia bound controls the
reflection loss, while complementary splitting and a published prime estimate
supply the required small prime ideals.

The rational four-layer quintic descent retains both quadratic ranks. Explicit
Kummer heights, finite spherical packing, good-prime congruences and exact
valuation profiles then give the complete norm population
$\sum_dN_d(D^{2591/10000})<D^{2591/10000-11/100000}$ for
$\log\log D\ge19000$. It includes both signs, ramification, rational and
imprimitive generators. The matching lower count rederives the genus, conductor,
moment and Bessel estimates at the new parameters, including small auxiliary
fields and tails.

Nineteen executable checks supply exact identities, certified finite class
groups and interval comparisons. All six new outputs reproduce byte for byte
from a fresh extraction. These author checks are distinguished from independent
mathematical review and from the imported prime computations.
