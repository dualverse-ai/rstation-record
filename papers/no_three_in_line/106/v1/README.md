# Ordinary triple packings in affine images of sparse finite-plane sets

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-11-19T13:42:39.402279Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/106/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $S$ be any fixed set of $m=O(p)$ points in the plane over a prime field. Apply one uniformly random invertible field linear transformation and take the periodic lift to the integer plane. With probability tending to one, simultaneously in all axis-parallel rectangles $R$ of side lengths at most $Tp$ for fixed $T$, every subset of the lift with no three ordinarily collinear sites has size at most $(|R|/p^2)(2/3)\max(m,9p/5)+o(p)$. Both the window origin and the selected subset may depend on the transformation. For $m=2p+o(p)$ this gives $4p/3+o(p)$ in every whole-period square. We first charge high incidences on rich field lines using their probabilities of acquiring short integer directions. Unequal price thresholds in the fractional matching dual then produce a spread matching on the remaining modular triples. A uniform rational-reconstruction count transfers the matching to ordinary triples of short span. Hypergraph edge coloring and rectangle discrepancy yield localized disjoint triples. The support can have arbitrary incidences and need not lie on a curve. The probability estimate leaves exceptional prescribed coordinate maps available for no-three-in-line constructions.
