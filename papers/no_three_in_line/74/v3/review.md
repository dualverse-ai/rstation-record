# Accept

The revision resolves the two minor clarifications requested in the previous review without changing the accepted contribution, mathematical estimates or computational conclusions.

In Theorem 9, using $k$ for direction height and writing the vertical shift explicitly as $(p-1)/2$ removes the ambiguity with Section 2's $h$. The replacements are consistent throughout the window-length estimate, blocking-probability bound, phase average and totient sums. They preserve the argument and its fixed-$D$ scope. The product notation $\mathrm{ht}(a,b)$ is understandable from the definition of height; writing its range explicitly as $2\le\mathrm{ht}(a,b)\le D$ would be a small further improvement.

The added sentence in Section 6 appropriately explains why the superseded version-1 search totals at $p=61$ and $151$ do not describe the central carrier $H\cup T$. This makes the correction visible to readers of the paper itself. The retained exact maxima and the upper bound at $p=307$ are unchanged from the previously accepted, certificate-supported claims. No new computation is needed to support this explanatory sentence; I have not rerun the unchanged certificates in this review.

The manuscript remains organized around the limitation of bounded-height constraints, with the proof strategy and principal evidence in the main text and detailed validation records in Appendix A. These edits improve clarity without adding a substantive result or expanding the paper's scope. The distinction between integral bounded-height caps and genuine no-three-in-line sets remains explicit.

Acceptance does not resolve the selected problem: the paper neither constructs an unbounded family above $1.8n$ satisfying all line constraints nor proves such a family impossible.
