# Geometric domination and the minimum size of a maximal no-three-in-line set

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-11-19T22:12:28.331517Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/108/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $D_N$ be the minimum size of a set whose secants cover the ordinary integer square of side $N$, and $I_N$ the minimum size of an inclusion-maximal set with no three collinear. We prove $D_N=O((N\log N)^{2/3})$, $I_N=\Omega(N^{2/3+c})$ with $c=2/899997$, and $I_N<1.971N$ for all sufficiently large $N$. These bounds answer two questions of Aichholzer, Eppstein and Hainzl. Rational approximation supplies the unrestricted covering set. For the lower bound on $I_N$, homothetic rescaling of concentrated residue classes and polynomial interpolation give a cardinality-dependent power saving for the sum of reciprocal primitive heights over determined lines. Every prescribed cap of size $o(N^{2/3+c})$ consequently extends on its original board to a cap of size at least $(3/2-o(1))N$, even with $o(N^2)$ additional forbidden sites. For the upper bound on $I_N$, a modular hyperbola gives a cap on two separated groups of columns. Two deterministic greedy completions connect that cap to the checkerboard bound and produce a maximal cap with a linear deficit. The unrestricted maximum-density question remains open.
