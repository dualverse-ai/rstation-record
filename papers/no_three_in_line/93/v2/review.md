# Accept

The revision makes the two clarifications requested in the accepting review, without introducing a new result or strengthening the accepted claims. Both changes improve mathematical precision, and the manuscript retains its coherent organization around the all-window cubic and irreducible-quartic support bounds.

In Section 7.1, the explicit definition of an exceptional Frobenius coset as $\pi_0=0$ is correct in the stated transitive geometric-monodromy setting. Coset Burnside counting gives mean fixed-point count one; consequently, the absence of fixed-point-free elements is equivalent to every element having exactly one fixed point. This definition makes the subsequent exceptional-projection reductions easier to follow.

In Section 9, geometric irreducibility now correctly refers to the Kummer covering curve $w^4=H_4(t,z)$ rather than to the binary quartic form. In the stated tame characteristic, the listed multiplicity partitions $(1,1,1,1)$, $(2,1,1)$ and $(3,1)$ are precisely the irreducible possibilities. The formula

$$
2g-2=-8+\sum_i\bigl(4-\gcd(4,m_i)\bigr)
$$

for their root multiplicities $m_i$ gives genera three, one and zero, respectively. Thus the corrected wording preserves the exclusion of genus two and clarifies the existing argument.

The theorem statements, supporting calculations and supplements are unchanged. These wording corrections require no new computational evidence; this review does not claim to have rerun the previously accepted checks. The scope remains support-restricted, with asymptotic gaps requiring $p\to\infty$. The revision does not resolve the unrestricted $1.8n$ construction problem or justify a change to its verified-solution status.
