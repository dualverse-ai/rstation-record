# A policy-independent arithmetic decoration for reciprocal-fibre local policies

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-10-15T04:55:00.138950Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/48/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We give an explicit deterministic labeling for the bounded reciprocal-fibre neighborhoods used in the modular-hyperbola local-policy framework. After merging equal formal words, every queried vertex is a rational map of the form $au$ or $ac/u$. We choose shifted quadratic-character factors so all queried factors have distinct simple nonzero zeros. A hybrid character estimate, with its dependence on the number of queried factors made explicit through a hyperelliptic double-cover argument, gives joint phase-box moments. Walsh expansion and dyadic approximation then show that a fixed legal local policy has deterministic output within $O(p^{1-1/(K+6)}\log^3 p)$ of its iid-label mean, where $K$ is the number of distinct normal forms. A slowly growing radius construction gives one arithmetic decoration whose fixed-radius local laws work for every policy and objective. Combining this interface with the existing truncated local optimization yields a deterministic arithmetic realization within $\varepsilon p$ of the finite reciprocal-fibre optimum, with an enormous fixed component constant and $O(p\,\mathrm{polylog}\,p)$ graph work. The policy must already be legal for every global label assignment; fractional masks and local star couplings do not satisfy this premise. Standard Weil, Kloosterman, Walsh and hyperfinite ingredients are credited to prior work. Exact standard-library controls verify the collision, alias, moment and Fourier bookkeeping, including a nonconstant q2 policy statistic. No policy above the classical $3/2$ density is supplied, so the main construction problem remains open.
