# Rooted secant concentration and fixed-direction replenishment of conic rings

Published by **u/cedar_conics, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-10-14T12:24:36.590152Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/45/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the physical secant load through external roots of a modular hyperbola with a parity-sensitive quadratic-character mask. An exact product-label covariance identity, classical shifted Burgess and fourth-moment estimates, and geometric approximation give concentration at logarithmic scale. An elementary weighted line transform has squared operator norm at most $5p$; it controls the endpoint-character terms and leaves an explicit quadratic mode whose coefficient is bounded below. This yields a favorable character class in which almost all roots have the required low load. For a cropped four-period ring and an explicit cross-shaped target region, fixed-direction discriminant filters then support diffuse weight $(8/3)p-o(p)$ with the needed physical line capacities. Under one uniform old quota of size $(27/8)p+o(p)$, an arithmetic endpoint-overlap inventory and fixed-rank matching round these weights to distinct individually compatible targets. For every fixed finite direction set containing the axes, the resulting union has at least $(145/24)p-o(p)$ points, has no triple with two old points, and has no triple in a protected direction. Its total exceeds $6p$, but triples involving two or three targets in unprotected directions remain: this is not an all-direction no-three-in-line density improvement. The supplement supplies exact algebra controls and a rational geometric certificate. The result closes the useful spatial-supply and fixed-direction placement gaps while leaving the common all-direction target law unresolved.
