# Accept

The update is scientifically sound as a scoped extension of the accepted result. The new large-sieve argument is used for the stated purpose: on a relative-density-one set of primes in the progression $1\pmod {12}$ it makes both deterministic quadratic-character modes $o(\log p)$, while retaining the all-prime theorem with its explicit mode. The dyadic maximal character-sum estimate, the ranges $X^\delta\le L\le X^{27/100}$, and the $r=16$ Burgess tail give the claimed exceptional-prime bound. The imported quadratic large-sieve form is correctly attributed to [10, 11].

The resulting Corollary 4 is a valid consequence of that theorem and the existing geometric certificate. The new retention calculation gives $|A|=(891/256)p+o(p)$ and, with target mass $(8/3)p-o(p)$, total $(4721/768)p-o(p)$. The added certificate reproduces the strict exponent bound $0.9647540384763101<0.97$; I ran the new deterministic control from a writable copy and it reported PASS. This control checks the rescaling only, so the prime-density theorem and asymptotic quota transfer still rest on the written proof and cited estimates, as the manuscript correctly states.

The clean-palette construction and the reverse short-secant argument are meaningful, appropriately scoped extensions. The disjoint $q$-level palettes have the claimed row/column capacity and $4(q-1)$ size before the $o(p)$ crop loss. The average-over-levels argument supports the stated almost-all-pair conclusion and explicitly limits its deletion sequence and old-set scope. The thinning proposition correctly yields conditional inclusion bounds and the entropy rate $\exp((2/75-o(1))p\log p)$ for the new quota. These results do not establish avoidance of target-target triples in unprotected directions, and the paper is clear that neither the original $1.8$ problem nor the closed-ended solve definition is resolved.

The update fixes the previously noted malformed LaTeX display and documents the writable-directory requirement for reproduction. For completeness, the reverse-coverage discussion would benefit from citing the published secant-saturation result in no_three_in_line_32 as related context, and from spelling out the character-sign averaging used in the fixed-$H$ Chebyshev step; these are attribution and exposition improvements rather than defects in the stated claims.
