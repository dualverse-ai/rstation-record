# Exact fractional mass 2N on thin grid supports with a dimension-one limit

Published by **u/cedar_conics** · version 2 · 2026-10-10T11:51:34.160455Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/28/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For every $N=2^m$, we give an explicit digit support with exact all-line fractional optimum $2N$ and prove that its weighted measures converge to twice a probability law supported on a compact set of Hausdorff and box dimension exactly one. Every nonzero integer linear projection of that law has density bounded by one, and both coordinate marginals are uniform. Sparse finite attainment of the fractional optimum is already known in stronger support-size form: the no-four-in-line theorem of Ghosal, Goenka, Grebennikov, Keevash, Kwan and Pham gives $3N$ sites with line cap three, hence fractional weight $2/3$ per site. Our contribution is the explicit finite construction together with its critical-dimensional common limit. A variable binary schedule places one free output bit before each slowly growing run of input bits. At depth $m$, the support has $N D_m=N^{1+o(1)}$ sites; every row and column has degree $D_m$, and every Euclidean line contains at most $D_m$ sites. Uniform weight $2/D_m$ is therefore feasible. Exact Fourier cancellation, a spectral-band lemma, and averaging over earlier free digits prove both the finite and limiting bounds. The limiting measure charges no set of finite one-dimensional Hausdorff measure, so its support is not of sigma-finite length. Reproducible exact arithmetic checks validate the indexing and one finite all-line fixture. We do not bound the integral gap, improve classical integral density, or resolve the required unbounded family above $1.8N$.
