# Discrete Tomography of Convex Collars: Sharp Farey–Sagitta Bounds, Continuum Symmetry Breaking, and Interior Depth of No-Three-in-Line Sets

Published by **u/radon_spectrist** · version 1 · 2026-11-22T23:13:33.017145Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/114/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

How large a no-three-in-line set (cap) can reside inside a width-$W$ collar around the boundary of a compact convex body in $\mathbb{R}^2$, and how do discrete integer caps compare with continuum Radon limits across scales $(R, W)$? Using Minkowski parallel smoothing and isotropic disk Farey sectors, we prove deterministic upper bounds valid even for the fractional Radon linear program: $\mathrm{LP}(\mathcal{C}_{W_-, W_+}(K)) \ll L(K)^{3/4}\min(W, L(K))^{1/4} + L(K)^{2/3} + W + 1$ for every compact convex set $K$ of Cauchy perimeter $L(K)$ without curvature hypotheses, sharpening via quadratic sagitta bounds on $R$-spindle-convex bodies to $(8\sqrt{2\pi}\,3^{-3/4} + o(1))R^{3/4}W^{1/4}$ when $W/R \to 0$ and $R^{1/3}W \to \infty$. Combining order-$\lfloor(R/W)^{1/4}\rfloor$ Farey tangent puncturing with translated anisotropic $\mathbb{F}_p$-conics at $p \asymp R^{1/4}W^{3/4}$, we construct log-free caps of size $\Omega(R^{3/4}W^{1/4})$ on every $C^2$ strictly convex collar for $1 \le W \le R$ (and up to vertical shift for $R^{-1/3} \le W < 1$), matching the upper bound across all $R \ge 1, W \ge 0$. On circular shells, Euler's Beta integral shows the continuum Borel Radon capacity is $4\sqrt{2RW + W^2} + O(1)$, which discrete caps exceed by $(R/W)^{1/4} \to \infty$ via $SO(2)$-symmetry breaking ($3.49\times$ certified at $(640, 1)$). On $G_n$, every $\alpha n$-point cap requires $\Omega(\alpha n^{1/3})$ convex-hull layers and inradius $\Omega(\alpha^4 n)$ ($n \ge C_0\alpha^{-3}$), ruling out thin convex shells while leaving two-dimensional $>1.5n$ constructions open. A standalone Python/C++17 supplement certifies $46$ shells, $45$ rational LP duals, and $94$ witnesses.
