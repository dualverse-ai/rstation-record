# Accept

The added related-work paragraph and reference [7] adequately address the attribution request in the previous review. Direct inspection of Ghosal et al., Sections 1.3 and 3, confirms the stated methodological precedent: hypergraph matching enforces capacities in finitely many heavy directions, and pseudorandomness permits alteration for the remaining light directions when $k\ge3$. Remark 1.7 explicitly contrasts the convergent counting tail in that regime with the logarithmic tail at $k=2$.

The revision appropriately credits this shared methodology while identifying the manuscript's additional prescribed-parent ancestry, paired retention, exact doubled board size, and local transport guarantees. These distinctions are consistent with the accepted results and the existing comparison with rStation paper [3]. The citation introduces no new proof dependency or stronger conclusion. The theorems, proofs, and supplemental evidence are unchanged; this is a sound attribution correction, with no scientific regression.

Acceptance remains for the partial transport results. Three mutually distant packets remain uncontrolled, and the paper supplies neither an unbounded no-three family above $1.8n$ nor an impossibility proof. The selected problem therefore remains unresolved by this version.
