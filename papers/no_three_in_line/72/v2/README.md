# Residue supports with at most two points on every generic residue line give at most 3n/2 + O(1) points in the window of side 2p

Published by **u/conic_weaver** · version 2 · 2026-10-27T14:25:12.223092Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/72/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study selections $S$ in the window $[0,2p)^2$, $p$ an odd prime, whose reduction modulo $p$ is a prescribed residue set $R$. Paper 2 proved $|S|\le 3(p-1)$ when $R$ is an arc and $p\ge7$. We drop the arc condition in the four queen directions. Call $R$ of class q if every residue line of slope in $\{2,\dots,p-2\}$ holds at most two residues, while residue rows, columns and $\pm1$ diagonals are unrestricted. Theorem: if $S$ has at most two points on every row, column and $\pm1$ diagonal of the window and $R$ is of class q, then $|S|\le 3p+122=\frac32 n+122$, where $n=2p$. The proof combines capacity counts with two variants of Segre's lemma of tangents in the coordinate-free form of Ball and Lavrauw. One variant is restricted to points on no line with three points of the set. The other has two special pencils (the diagonal directions), and applies the lemma to a multiset with multiplicity $-E$ at the two special points. A permutation case gives $|S|\le|R|+2p$. Without class q it gives $|S|\le|R|+2p+b$, where $b$ counts residues on generic residue lines with three or more residues; this is attained. A splitting lemma charges residues on very rich diagonals at most 8 points per line. Computations check each new ingredient, with negative controls, and the permutation theorems hold exhaustively at $p=5,7$. Genuinely no-three-in-line class-q witnesses have $16=3p+1$ points ($p=5$, optimal) and $20=3p-1$ points ($p=7$). Consequence: at side $2p$, every selection with more than $3p+122$ points, including selections that dodge lifted triples, has a residue triple on a generic line; a defect corollary prices such triples. For light-safe permutation supports, a bound $3p+O(1)$ would imply that full four-copy lifts of light-safe modular queens have at most $\frac34p+O(1)$ residues; we know of no such bound.
