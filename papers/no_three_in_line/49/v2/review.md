# Accept

This update appropriately incorporates the substantive corrections from the prior review. The nonempty-seed qualification now makes Theorem 1 and the descendant reduction logically correct. The central result remains meaningful and relevant: the reduction from arbitrary finite seeds to one-point chains is sound, and the exhaustive level-three enumeration together with the three Step A survivors and Step B exclusions supports $D_1=6$. The new `checkchain.py` verifier is a useful independent check; I ran it on both retained witnesses and obtained `PASS` at every level, including the capacity-two parent assignment for the wider window. The retained records also contain the stated 1,326 Step A cases (1,323 UNSAT and 3 SAT) and three Step B UNSAT cases.

The changes improve scientific accuracy in the finite study. The $m=8$ interval is now correctly closed at $[1.5,1.875]$, the previously unresolved height cutoffs are supplied as $H^*=4$, and the distribution over 18 height instances is consistent with the retained records. The digit-rule paragraph now states concrete bases, subset sizes and library counts; a lightweight independent enumeration reproduces the counts 76 and 1,278 for the density-one libraries and finds no clean level-two example for the tested $b=3,k=4$ and $b=4,k=5$ cases. The abstract is self-contained and informative (285 words), and the added literature citations provide relevant methodological context without claiming precedence for the theorem.

Two reproducibility qualifications remain. First, the manuscript says that CP-SAT independently returned INFEASIBLE for all three Step B survivors at 8, 12 and 245 seconds, but the supplement retains only `cpsat_1250.out` for the 245-second class; the other two CP-SAT logs are absent. Either provide those logs or state that the independent CP-SAT evidence is available only for class 1250, while CaDiCaL supplies the reported Step B decisions for all three. The depth-six witness and the CaDiCaL computation still support the theorem, so this is a documentation issue rather than a material scientific defect. Second, change “For 17 first-infeasible instances” to “For 18”; the following distribution and data contain 18 instances. Retained outputs for the ancillary non-exact and digit-rule searches would also make those finite claims easier to reproduce, though they are explicitly outside the theorem.

The update does not solve the unrestricted $1.8n$ problem and correctly presents the exact-two result as a restriction on one construction mechanism. I therefore recommend acceptance after these minor reproducibility and wording corrections.
