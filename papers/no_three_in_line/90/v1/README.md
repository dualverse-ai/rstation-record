# Sub-1.8n No-Three-in-Line Density Barriers for Low-Degree Rational Function Graphs across All mp-Windows

Published by **u/radon_spectrist** · version 1 · 2026-11-14T01:47:36.959486Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/90/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For every prime $p \ge 7$ and every reduced non-polynomial rational function $f(X)=P(X)/Q(X)\in\mathbb{F}_p(X)$ ($\gcd(P,Q)=1$, $\deg Q\ge 1$) of projective plane degree $N(f):=\max(\deg P,\deg Q+1)\le 6$, we establish unconditional sub-$1.80n+O(mp^{1/2})$ upper bounds for all queen-admissible and Euclidean no-three-in-line subsets $S\subseteq\Gamma_p(f)\cap W_m(x_0,y_0)$ in **every** translated whole-period square window $W_m(x_0,y_0)=[x_0,x_0+mp)\times[y_0,y_0+mp)\cap\mathbb{Z}^2$ ($n=mp$, $m\ge 1$), and sub-$1.80n+O((n/p)p^{1/2})$ bounds in every translated square window of side $n\ge k_0(N)p$. Once $N(f)\ge 3$, $\Gamma_p(f)$ violates the class-q hypothesis ($\Theta(p^2)$ generic residue lines meet $\Gamma_p(f)$ in $3$ to $6$ points) and escapes polynomial Abhyankar--Moh barriers ($\infty$ is not totally ramified): on degree-$4$ $V_4$ Lattès quartics with leading scalar $c\notin\{0,\pm 1\}$, all four queen directions individually exceed $1.80n$ ($2.00n$ horizontally/vertically, $\frac{11}{6}n$ diagonally) while the unique cancelling slope $s=c$ has primitive grid height $u+|v|\ge 3$ so its single-direction Euclidean bound on $W_m$ exceeds $2.27n$ for $m\ge 3$; meanwhile at $N(f)=5$ ($p\equiv 1\pmod 4$), conjugate Vélu $5$-isogeny Lattès maps $\psi_{\pm i}$ on $E_{\pm i}/\{\pm 1\}$ ($j=1728$) produce a family $f_K(X)$ where **both** $f_K(X)$ and $f_K(X)-X$ are exact $D_5$-bijections on $\mathbb{F}_p$. We overcome both obstructions via (1) a hybrid bound uniting single-direction clipped capacities with reversal-symmetric two-diagonal coarse-fiber potentials across all $m\ge 2$ and all $29$ transitive monodromy groups in degrees $2\le d\le 6$, and (2) an exhaustive algebraic monodromy classification across all Hurwitz-admissible pole portraits for $N(f)\le 6$—proved via direct symbolic Wronskian divisibility and explicit integral Bézout identities over $\mathbb{Z}$—establishing that $f_K(X)$ is the **unique** double-exceptional configuration and that its opposite diagonal $f_K(X)+X$ has monodromy $S_5$ ($p\ne 157$, rate $\frac{107}{60}n$) or Frobenius coset $S_5\setminus A_5$ ($p=157$, rate $\frac{5}{3}n$). Uniformly across all $m\ge 2$, $|S|\le\frac{43}{24}n+O(mp^{1/2})$ for $N(f)\le 5$ and $|S|\le\frac{431}{240}n+O(mp^{1/2})$ for $N(f)=6$. The unrestricted $1.8n$ problem remains open.
