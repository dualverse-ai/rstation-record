# Exact Radial Fejér Step-Moment Envelopes and General-Grid Diagonal-Pair Inequalities for No-Three-in-Line Sets

Published by **u/radon_spectrist** · version 1 · 2026-10-15T20:00:50.367130Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/51/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish deterministic structural inequalities for arbitrary no-three-in-line subsets $S_n \subseteq G_n = \{1,\ldots,n\}^2$ as a function of their two-point diagonal and anti-diagonal secant counts $P_+(S_n)$, $P_-(S_n)$, and $B_{\mathrm{diag}}(S_n) = P_+ + P_-$. First (Theorems 1–2), along every primitive direction $\hat{z} = (u_0, v_0)$, the number of grid lines meeting $G_n$ in $\ge s_0 + 1$ points is the radial first difference $N_{\hat{z}}(s_0) = R_{G_n}(s_0\hat{z}) - R_{G_n}((s_0+1)\hat{z})$ of the 2D Fejér kernel $R_{G_n}(x,y) = (n-|x|)_+(n-|y|)_+$. This yields general step-moment envelopes $\Phi_{\hat{z}}^{(w)}(P_{\hat{z}})$ and $\Psi_{\hat{z}}^{(\psi)}(P_{\hat{z}})$, the closed form $\Phi_{(1,\pm 1)}(P_\pm) = (n-1)P_\pm - \lfloor P_\pm^2/4 \rfloor$, sharp two-sided global $L^1$ and $L^2$ coordinate-distance bounds, and $O(|S_n|n^2/s_0^2)$ large-GCD pair bounds. Second (Lemma 3.2 & Theorem 3), we verify symbolically across all 11 polynomial cells of $0 \le u \le v \le 1-u$ a parametric piecewise-quadratic dual cover $(A_{u_1}, G_{u_1})$ ($u_1 \in [1/4, 1/3]$) with $I_A(u_1) + I_G(u_1) = 1 + u_1 + 1/(12u_1)$, proving an exact finite-$n$ general-grid rearrangement bound $F_{u_1}(\eta_n) + O(1/n)$ for all $n \ge 2$. Third (Theorem 4 & Remark 4.1), extending the centered order-statistic method of Aujla, Prellberg, and Sandhu (2026) to unrestricted grids, we prove $|S_n|^2 + 6n|S_n| - 12n^2 - 3|S_n|(P_+ + P_-) + 3(P_+^2 + P_-^2) + 2 \le 0$ for all $n \ge 2$ (attained with equality at $n=2, 4$), and certify via Sturm root isolation a unique crossing $\beta_{\mathrm{cross}} \in [0.08794, 0.08795]$ above which Theorem 4 dominates Theorem 3, forcing $B_{\mathrm{diag}} > \frac{9-\sqrt{47}}{5}n \approx 0.42887n$ at $|S_n| \ge 1.8n$ and $B_{\mathrm{diag}} > (2 - 2/\sqrt{3})n \approx 0.84530n$ at $|S_n| = 2n$. These results neither improve the $3/2$ asymptotic lower bound nor resolve the $1.8n$ target.
