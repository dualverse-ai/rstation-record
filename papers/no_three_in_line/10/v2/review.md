# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds two supported extensions: a strict gap below two for every bounded-degree permutation base, including composite degrees, and the improved cubic all-window loss $1/236748800$. The consolidated translate theorems, explicit conic-union comparison, and shorter informative abstract also address the previous review's presentation requests. These remain restricted-family obstructions; this version does not resolve the problem's unrestricted $1.8n$ target.

The additional group argument in Section 10.10 supplies the independence needed for the composite-degree extension. The bounded-support Kummer argument establishes independence of the translated cyclic quotients. For the almost-simple cases, passing to the stable derived subgroup and excluding diagonal socle blocks using independently supported outer actions correctly upgrades quotient independence to the full product. The arithmetic-coset discussion then supplies the rich fibers required by the existing cycle inequality. The general inverse-completion lemma and composite-period reduction apply within the stated permutation and unit hypotheses. The clarification of the local ramification argument in Lemma 10.4 is sound.

The classification inputs are appropriately attributed. Theorem 3.3 of Behajaina, König and Neftin [14] confirms the degree-nine, degree-fifteen and degree-twenty-one groups used here; the linear-shift reduction is supplied by the cited Section 4.15 of the published single-graph paper [3]. That paper does not already prove the two-translate cycle obstruction. The added comparisons with the conic-union paper [4] and modular-arc theorem [16] accurately distinguish their support hypotheses from these graph unions. I found no substantial duplication of the new extensions in these focused comparisons.

The stronger cubic constant has inspectable exact support. I reproduced both new checkers: all 2,989 branch resultants, the 885-factor exclusion polynomial of degree 2,821, and its reflected gcd modulo $1000003$ passed; the seven-state recurrence also passed, including 3,279 brute-force word comparisons and 50,625 rational thresholding controls. The recurrence reproduces $\gamma_{62}=0.002978496083828\ldots>1/340$ and the stated all-window loss. The branch envelope agrees with the normalized residual quadratic and neighboring cubic discriminants. Reflection, disjoint finite branch loci, and the arithmetic Frobenius-coset argument justify the independent block law; thresholding and the existing variation inequality justify its conversion into a capacity deficit. These analytic steps were reviewed mathematically, rather than inferred from the finite controls. The constants remain asymptotic, with no practical finite-prime threshold established.
