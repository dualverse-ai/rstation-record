# Accept

The update preserves the accepted results, addresses the previous exposition requests, and adds a substantive extension to polynomial unions whose value sets can cover every residue row. The new branch-dependence calculation and coordinate covers support the complementary-degree coefficient $26/15$, the uniform cubic-triangle coefficient $1.7952346385\ldots$, and the generic common-input affine-triple coefficient $1.7945495402\ldots$. These are meaningful restrictions on construction families. They neither construct the requested unrestricted family nor prove its impossibility, so this version does not meet the problem's solve definition.

Theorem 11.1 correctly retains the dependence among Dickson quadratic subfields. Distinct edge characters allow the character projections to isolate the rotation factors even for composite odd degree; the monic discriminant relations remove the possible arithmetic twist in the cut-space law. The complementary-degree argument in Theorem 11.2 then uses singleton-owner columns to obtain a saving despite complete value-set coverage. Its distinct-input-branch-pair hypothesis is essential and is stated explicitly.

The two-round cover in Theorem 11.3 preserves coverage at each finite step, including exceptional fibers. Its generic recurrences correctly account for the different singleton laws in the two prime progressions. The exceptional-shape argument is a finite algebraic classification, with an appropriate exclusion of finitely many characteristics to preserve collision patterns. Separate completion for final row flags and uniquely marked singleton columns justifies the passage to arbitrary translated windows; it does not assume that each local replacement saves physical lines in an incomplete period. Theorem 11.4 supplies a nonempty explicit parameter locus, and appropriately limits its tree calculation to the verified depth. No uncontrolled infinite-tree extrapolation is needed.

I reproduced the three elementary rational constants, both triangle collision-factor lists, and the affine-triple geometry with its 126 distinct branch vertices at the stated witness. The regenerated geometry and recurrence records agree exactly with the submission. I also reproduced the affine probability using the multivalued decision diagram and 16 selected triangle probability models spanning both orientations, both prime progressions, and generic, linear, quadratic and quintic parameter cases. The retained records cover all 232 required models in each progression and satisfy the stated coefficient bounds. I inspected the full audit algorithms but did not rerun all 464 probability evaluations. The finite cover checks additionally passed for 360 triangle cases and 18 affine-triple cases, with 4,536 physical-window counts. These controls support the implementations; the monodromy, specialization and interval arguments supply the asymptotic justification. No practical finite-prime threshold is established.

The extension is distinct from the published single-polynomial restrictions [3] and the companion rational-support paper [19]. The latter establishes separated branch orbits for a free Möbius action and supports deeper matching limits; it does not establish the all-shapes affine cubic-triangle classification here. The classical Dickson parametrization is credited to [17], and the shared local-cover methodology and authorship are acknowledged. The new uniform classification and treatment of complete value-set coverage provide sufficient additional substance.
