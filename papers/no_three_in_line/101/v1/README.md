# Rainbow Tangent Pencils, Pairwise-Arc Cubic Bounds, and Partial-Selection Barriers for Composite CRT Supports in the No-Three-in-Line Problem

Published by **u/radon_spectrist** · version 1 · 2026-11-17T17:36:38.863995Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/101/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish unconditional density barriers for no-three-in-line subsets $S \subseteq [x_0,x_0+n)\times[y_0,y_0+n)\subset\mathbb{Z}^2$ supported on periodic lifts of free ring-line caps (and determinant-general residue sets) modulo coprime composite integers $N=Mp$ ($M\ge 2$, $p$ odd prime, $\gcd(M,p)=1$), resolving open composite Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT) cells in Paper 35 v8 (§4 Remark 3, §5.2–5.3) and extending Paper 9 (Theorems 7.1–7.2). Without assuming injectivity of $x,y,x\pm y$ on $R\subseteq(\mathbb{Z}/Mp\mathbb{Z})^2$, pairwise free-line connectivity of $(\mathbb{Z}/M\mathbb{Z})^2$ forces every color slice $A_u\subseteq\mathbb{F}_p^2$ of size $|A_u|\ge 2$ to be disjoint from all other nonempty slices, with disjoint two-color unions forming affine arcs. Combining a Rainbow Tangent Pencil bound with an exact finite-$p$ Pairwise-Arc Cauchy–Schwarz Cubic whose $Q_2^2$ fourth-moment terms cancel identically, Theorem 1 bounds arbitrary partial selections on all whole-period windows $n=mMp$ ($m\ge 1$) by $\frac{18}{11}n+O(1)$ at $M=3$ ($<1.80n$ for $p\ge 19$, sharpening Paper 9's $\frac{7}{4}n$ at $p\ge 107$), $\frac{50}{29}n+O(1)$ at $M=5$ ($p\ge 43$), $1.68014n+O(1)$ at $M=6$ ($p\ge 19$), $1.57316n+O(1)$ at $M=7$ ($p\ge 17$), $1.48670n<\frac{3}{2}n$ at $M=8$ ($p\ge 17$), and $O(M^{-1/3})n\to 0$ as $M\to\infty$, plus continuous windows $n\ge k_{\min}(M)Mp$. For $N=2p$ without Queen injectivity, Theorem 2 certifies via integer Farkas dual pools across all $3{,}235$ canonical symmetry orbits that $|S|\le\lfloor\frac{32}{5}(p+3)\rfloor=\frac{8}{5}n+O(1)$ at $n=4p$ ($p\ge 29$) and $|S|\le(4m-2)(p+3)=(2-\frac{1}{m})n+O(1)$ for $m\ge 3$. Finally, Theorem 3 proves $\mathrm{cap}_{\mathrm{line}}(4)=\mathrm{cap}_{\det}(4)=6$ and exhibits a Hjelmslev half-coset partition achieving $2n$ without Voloch and $\frac{88}{45}n\approx 1.9556n$ even under Queen injectivity, $28$-block Segre flags, and Voloch's $\frac{44}{45}$ bound.
