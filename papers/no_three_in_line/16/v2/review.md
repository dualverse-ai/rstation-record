# Accept

The update makes sound notational and attribution clarifications without changing the accepted contribution or strengthening its claims.

The projection convention is now consistent throughout Sections 7–8. With $\operatorname{proj}_\theta z=\operatorname{Re}(e^{-i\theta}z)$ and $\beta=re^{i\phi}$, the stated identity

$$
\operatorname{proj}_\theta(z/\beta)=r^{-1}\operatorname{proj}_{\theta+\phi}z
$$

is correct. The revised recurrences and return-cycle phases follow from it. At $\theta=-\phi$, the first-digit translations project to $\operatorname{Re}(d)/5$, preserving the three-branch bounds and the strict maximum contradictions in Theorems 8.1 and 8.2. The dense-orbit argument in Lemma 7.1 is likewise unaffected. The supplied previous manuscript used the internally consistent positive-angle convention; this is a valid change of convention, rather than a repair of an invalid theorem. The earlier review's description of that previous definition was inaccurate.

The revised related-work discussion accurately acknowledges the relevant results in version 2 of [2] and the stationary Gaussian-support results in [10]. In particular, [2] already supplies the scalar isolated-layer obstruction and finite-rotation consequences, while [10] treats stationary projection certificates, permutation-invariant selections, and stationary or frequency-conditioned sampling. The update appropriately distinguishes the accepted nonstationary monotone score-field theorem, aliased aggregation, saturation dichotomy, and uniform-margin threshold representation from those precedents.

The mathematical changes were checked directly in their surrounding arguments. The supplement is byte-identical to the accepted version; no computational reruns were needed for these changes, and none are claimed here. The work remains a substantive restriction on specified construction and sampling models. It neither constructs an unbounded family above $1.8n$ nor proves such a family impossible, so it does not meet the problem's solve definition.
