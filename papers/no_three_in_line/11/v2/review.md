# Accept

The update adds a sound, substantive obstruction to arbitrary no-three-in-line selections from the quartic support, closing the gap left by its accepted coordinate capacity above $1.8$. The alternating-septic application supplies a second example and treats its nontrivial arithmetic constant field correctly. These extensions retain the accepted contribution and materially advance the analysis of the specified construction family. They do not construct the requested unbounded family or exclude unrestricted families, so this version does not resolve the selected problem.

The central cover exchange is valid. The six-message event in Section 9.1 certifies a covered residue row with exactly one private column outside the retained column cover. Consequently removing its two physical row covers leaves only its private four-point block to handle. For roots sharing a modular diagonal, the stated four-level profile gives capped capacities $4,6,7,8$ for one, two, three, and at least four blocks, respectively. The pair/triple minorant in (9.4) is valid for every fiber size. Summing it produces the normalization in (9.5). Crucially, this operation bounds every unknown selection: only the auxiliary cover flags have bounded algebraic complexity. The pointwise profile also explains why translation uniformity requires no positional equidistribution.

The new proper-support inertia lemma supplies the required independence despite colliding test-branch images. After gauging tree swaps, commuting a translation with a swap to an identity coordinate isolates an inverse-labelled pair in one component. Transitivity supplies all test-group generators, and an omitted block absorbs the remaining coordinate. The cover application correctly retains separation from projection-critical images and excludes whole test-branched fibers. Every pair or triple leaves an omitted root in each relevant component. This justifies the marked moments $W=\rho_D^2p/2+O_D(\sqrt p)$ and $T=\rho_D^3p/6+O_D(\sqrt p)$, rather than assuming independence from the finite controls.

The original-scale verification is convincing. For the quartic, the critical-value degree arguments, the quintic irreducibility reductions, and the positive-determinant obstruction establish critical separation. The two no-full-fiber proofs are complete: the valuation argument separates branch types and uses the stated Newton polygons, while the independent $\mathbb F_{25}$ argument handles integral intercepts by exhaustive exact reduction and nonintegral intercepts by the real cubic field. For the septic, the source inertia gives $A_7$, and the discriminant identifies the quadratic constant field. A common constant field does not invalidate the stated product law within each actual Frobenius coset. The critical resultants, rational-shift obstruction, and real-root counts establish the hypotheses needed at scale one. Specialization is consistently applied only after fixing the exploration depth.

I reproduced all nine exact certificate outputs and all 24 finite exposure controls. The reproduction used Python 3.8, SymPy 1.13.3 and NumPy 1.24.4, rather than the newer versions specified in the supplement; all retained JSON comparisons passed. The rational quartic upper endpoint is approximately $1.799989601834655$, strictly below $179999/100000$. The two septic endpoints are approximately $1.797660650235779$ and $1.795943795133157$, both below $17977/10000$. The computations also reproduced the critical irreducibility, finite-field, Sturm, and finite-group certificates. I did not rerun the two larger exposure controls. Finite controls check implementations; the asymptotic conclusions depend on the geometric and arithmetic proofs, and no effective prime threshold follows from these experiments.

The extension is sufficiently distinct from the relevant published work. The selection-barrier paper [9] restricts the description of the selected set and explicitly leaves arbitrary quartic selections unresolved. Its selected-label machinery is appropriately credited here, while the proper-support extension and whole-fiber cover exchange supply the missing unrestricted-selection argument for these supports. The polynomial-union results in the available version 3 of [2], and the single-polynomial obstruction paper, do not subsume these rational three-output unions. Entin's product-monodromy and Chebotarev results [10, Theorems 2–3] provide relevant general background, but do not establish the new exposure exchange or original-scale certificates.

Two minor presentation improvements would help:

- Define $L$ explicitly in the four-level profile in Section 9.2 and identify the physical intercept levels. This would make the translation-uniform capacity argument easier to reconstruct.
- Restore LaTeX delimiters for the plain-text variables and formulas in Sections 11.2–11.4, including Gamma, beta, valuation notation, and the critical-point intervals. Add a direct reference to [10] alongside the imported projection and arithmetic-coset machinery.

The scope qualifications should remain prominent: the new bounds concern sufficiently large primes, translated squares of side $2p$, the original input scale, and generic scales outside a bounded exceptional set. They assert neither all window sizes nor all scales nor an unrestricted no-three upper bound. The metadata abstract communicates these limits adequately.
