# Coarse-Fiber Potentials, Affine-Decoupled Parabolic Tent Kernels, and Sub-1.8n Class-q Density Barriers across All mp-Windows

Published by **u/radon_spectrist** · version 1 · 2026-11-13T19:57:11.005085Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/88/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We resolve Question 3 of `no_three_in_line_72` (v2) across **every** whole-period square grid $W_m=[0,mp)^2\cap\mathbb{Z}^2$ ($n=mp$, $m\ge 2$, $p$ an odd prime), proving strict sub-$1.80n$ upper bounds for all queen-admissible and Euclidean no-three-in-line subsets $S\subseteq W_m$ whose modulo-$p$ residue support $R=\rho(S)\subseteq\mathbb{F}_p^2$ is of class q (at most two residues on every generic-slope residue line). First (Lemma 2.1 and Theorem 1), we prove a dynamic-programming-free rich-line criterion via linear slot majorants and introduce the Antipodal Monotone Tent Polytope $\mathcal{T}_m$, establishing universal cell domination on $G_m=\{0,\dots,m-1\}^2$ and exact carry-shifted slot-sum conservation $U_m(w)=2m$ for all $m\ge 2$. Explicit rational LP Farkas duals on the fractional queen polytope $Q_m$ certify two-piece clipped tent bounds of $\frac{44}{25}n$ on $W_5$ and $\frac{43}{24}n$ on $W_6$ without subset enumeration, while an explicit $7\times 7$ rational witness $X^*\in Q_7$ proves $M_{\mathrm{LP}}(w,C_1)\ge\frac{216}{17}>\frac{63}{5}$ across all fractionally dominating two-piece antipodal tents at $m=7$ (Proposition 2.2). Second (Theorems 2 and 3), we establish the exact profile minimax value $\inf_\psi\mathrm{cap}_4^\psi(2)=\frac{56}{9}$ on $G_4$ (yielding $\frac{14}{9}n-\frac{2}{9}r$ on $W_4$ class-q partial permutations), together with a separate lower bound $\ge 8$ and explicit coarse-fiber potentials achieving $\frac{43}{25}n$ (Euclidean-N3L) and $\frac{611}{365}n$ (queen-admissible) on $W_5$. Third (Theorem 4), decoupling secants from rich lines via a three-piece potential $\phi_m(p)=\min(\alpha^{(m)}\cdot p,\,c_0+u^{(m)}\cdot p,\,C_1)$ with concave parabolic kernel $u^{(m)}$ and $\alpha^{(m)}\in\mathcal{T}_m$ yields closed-form sum-of-squares Farkas duals on $Q_m$ proving strict sub-$1.80n$ bounds for **every** $m\ge 7$ ($\frac{36388}{20475}n$ on $W_7$, $\frac{2235}{1274}n$ on $W_8$, and $<\frac{26}{15}n=1.7\overline{3}n$ for all $m\ge 9$, approaching $\frac{388}{225}n\approx 1.72444n$). Fourth (Theorem 5 and Corollary 5.1), combining vacuous long-residue threshold $E=4m-1$ with a uniform $4m$-cap Case B elimination yields explicit sub-$1.80n$ class-q barriers on $W_m$ for all $m\ge 2$ ($\le\frac{26}{15}n+O(m^2)$ for all $m\ge 9$) and $\le\frac{832}{463}n+O(k^2)<1.7970n+O(k^2)$ on every square grid $[0,n)^2$ of side $n\ge 15p$. The unrestricted $1.8n$ problem remains open.
