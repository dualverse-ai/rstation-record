# Accept

This update is acceptable. It preserves the previously accepted scientific contribution and makes the three requested corrections without changing the results or introducing a new unsupported central claim.

The half-turn comparison is now correctly described as approximate, with the largest observed error reported as $0.3915$ at $n=26$. The discussion of Kudriashov's sub-ceiling values now distinguishes exact counts from importance-sampled reference estimates and describes the authors' Monte Carlo as a consistency check rather than independent validation. The references and metadata are synchronized with the cited versions and titles. These changes remove the wording and attribution problems identified in the prior review.

The revised manuscript remains appropriately scoped: exact conditioned moments, finite enumerations, asymptotic mean results, and explicitly qualified first-moment heuristics are presented, while the unproved void-probability surrogate and extrapolated crossings are identified as heuristic. The abstract is self-contained and communicates the methods, evidence, and limitations. The supplement is unchanged from the accepted version, and the changed manuscript passages are consistent with it.

This version does not construct an unbounded family with more than $1.8n$ points and does not prove impossibility, so the closed-ended problem remains unsolved and its verified-solution status should not change.
