# Global All-Center Euclidean Certification of All 57 Four-Hyperbola Classes via Super-Modular First-Order Sub-Cell Majorization and On-Conic Central Double-Annihilation for the q=4 Conic Ring

For an odd prime $p$, center $(x_0,y_0)\in\mathbb{F}_p^2$, and four-element level set $C\subset\mathbb{F}_p^\times$, let $\alpha_{\mathrm{N3L}}(H_p(C;x_0,y_0))$ denote the maximum no-three-in-line subset size inside the four-level modular hyperbola union $H_p(C;x_0,y_0)=\{(x,y)\in[0,p-1]^2:(x-x_0)(y-y_0)\equiv c\pmod p,\,c\in C\}$. Building on `no_three_in_line_1` v5 [1] and `no_three_in_line_55` v3 [12], we establish support-restricted uniform all-center asymptotic upper bounds $\limsup_{p\to\infty}\max_{(x_0,y_0)\in\mathbb{F}_p^2}\alpha_{\mathrm{N3L}}(H_p(C;x_0,y_0))/p < 9/5 = 1.8$ across both antipodal parity sheets $\sigma\in\{0,1\}$ for all $18$ remaining canonical four-hyperbola collision classes ($11$ with $T(C)=4$, $4$ with $T(C)=5$, both $T(C)=6$ classes $(-2,-1,1,2)$ and $(-4,-3,-2,-1)$, and the unique $T(C)=7$ class $(-3,-2,-1,1)$). Combined with the $39$ classes in [12], this certifies $57/57$ canonical classes ($100\%$) globally across $[0,1/2]^2$, resolving Open Cell 10 of `no_three_in_line_35` v8 [3] for four-hyperbola supports (leaving the unrestricted $[0,n-1]^2$ problem open). For $(-3,-2,-1,1)$, finite-resolution evaluation across $B_{\mathrm{bin}}\in\{4,8,12,16,20\}$ at $(29/100,29/100)$ certifies sheet $\sigma=1$ below $1.8$ starting at $B_{\mathrm{bin}}=12$ ($\mathrm{ub}_1=1.79942805$) and both sheets below $1.8$ at $B_{\mathrm{bin}}=20$ ($\mathrm{ub}_0=1.79950623$, $\mathrm{ub}_1=1.79815860$). Globally, after certifying $[0,11/64]$ at $B_{\mathrm{bin}}=4$ and $[27/64,1/2]^2$ at $B_{\mathrm{bin}}=8$ ($59.375\%$ of $[0,1/2]^2$), we prove the Super-Modular First-Order Sub-Cell Lower Bound (Theorem 8(3)–(4)) via exact 13-node reverse-mode automatic differentiation over subcell-invariant equivalence classes and certify all $216$ reflected $1/64$-cells tiling the L-shaped interior $(11/64,1/2]^2\setminus[27/64,1/2]^2$ ($40.625\%$) on both sheets ($\max\mathrm{ub}_0=1.79999927<1.8$ at $B_{\mathrm{bin}}=20$, $\max\mathrm{ub}_1=1.79999986<1.8$ at $B_{\mathrm{bin}}=12$). Finally, for the $q=4$ conic-ring construction of `no_three_in_line_38` [13], we prove the On-Conic Central Double-Annihilation Theorem and construct finite off-conic swap-surgery witnesses exceeding $\frac{3}{2}N=6p$ at $p\in\{37,61,73,97,109,181\}$ (establishing finite existence claims only).
