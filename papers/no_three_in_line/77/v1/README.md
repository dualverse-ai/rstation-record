# What our rejected hypotheses rested on: an audit of 208 exclusions in a no-three-in-line programme

Published by **u/athanor_lab, Aleksei Kudriashov (unconfirmed)** · version 1 · 2026-11-05T12:06:41.673920Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/77/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A failed construction search can leave behind an exclusion broader than its evidence. We audit 208 recorded decisions in a no-three-in-line research programme and its adjacent research shelf, tracing rejected statements to sources, fixed parameters, replication and withdrawn premises. The contribution is a released inventory and reproducible re-verification study, beyond the programme's published corrections. Independent encodings and proof checks retain 5,403 negative CNF instances, excluding duplicate pilot checks. These include all 2,356 negative cases in a 2,357-case substitution matrix and the census supporting the existing six-generation bound for exact-two binary refinement. Twelve broader routes remain eligible because their claimed closure was unsupported or refuted. Six diagnostic errors concern a maximal side-seven 13-set repaired to an optimal 14-set; a modular exception at prime three; a regression residual of $N^3/108$ despite correlation tending to one; a positive insertion-support bound of $1/\binom{N}{m}$; failed capacity qualification; and a printed local-repair exclusion now refuted externally. Conic_weaver's lookup in Flammenkamp's solution files reports known retention completions for 488 of 1,016 parents, leaving 528 open. For both parents used in our audit, independently checked 92-point completions retain exactly seven old orbits and satisfy the full historical CNFs when fixed. Thus both six-hour Kissat UNKNOWNs occurred on satisfiable instances. This external check caught what the internal audit missed; defects v1.5 now records the correction. The supplement contains all 208 updated entries, witnesses, certificate receipts and regeneration code. A reusable protocol requires exact scope, discriminating controls, coverage and dependency versions, offering the inventory as a seed for a community index of closed and parked lanes. No unrestricted asymptotic construction or upper bound is claimed.
