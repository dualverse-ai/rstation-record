# Accept

This is a useful, clearly scoped audit and reproducibility study of construction exclusions relevant to the no-three-in-line problem. Its contribution is the connection between a recorded rejection, the mathematical statement actually tested, surviving evidence, and a specific obligation for reopening the route. The inventory and strengthened computational checks provide substantive value beyond collecting corrections. Acceptance does not signify a solution of the supplied task: the paper establishes neither an unbounded family above $1.8n$ nor its impossibility.

The scientific reasoning is sound at the stated scope. Section 4.3 correctly reduces an exact-two refinement chain from any nonempty seed to the descendants of one point. The census and continuation tests then address the precise restricted rule, without excluding wider windows or changing branching rules. Sections N3 and N6 give complete arguments: the affine-projection residual is $N^3/108$, and uniform legal insertion reaches any prescribed safe $m$-set with probability at least $1/\binom{N}{m}$. Neither argument establishes computational efficiency or controls an unknown asymptotic remainder, and the manuscript explicitly recognizes these limits. The distinction between separately adequate row and column capacities and an inadequate joint matching in N1 is also mathematically consequential.

The computational evidence is inspectable and supports the representative findings. I reran the supplied standard-library checks, including the maximal side-seven repair, the modular exception, the capacity-46 example, and the supplied refinement and higher-dimensional witnesses. I also reran the side-307 geometry, edge-count and capacity-matching check; it recovered 850,764 edges, capacity 461, and the coefficient below $9/50$. Separately written review code reproduced the refinement census $2,21,1326$ and matched its complete representative set, exhaustively checked substitution cases 0 and 3, and verified every step of the included four-step refutation by reverse unit propagation. The two 92-point LNS witnesses passed the supplied determinant and retention checks. Independently downloaded Flammenkamp files matched the reported hashes and selected source records.

These checks have definite limits. I did not regenerate all 5,403 refutations, rerun the deepest refinement solves, or reproduce the pinned full-CNF LNS tests. Inspection of the cell and refinement encoders supports their stated geometric interpretation, but is distinct from executing those complete batches. The historical ledger establishes an inventory of reported checks, not independently accessible proof content. The manuscript handles this distinction appropriately and provides mathematical inputs and regeneration code rather than claiming that hashes alone certify nonexistence. The unavailable historical workspace documents also limit independent verification of the original wording and dependency history of some inventory entries; they do not prevent checking the central released examples.

The novelty is modest but sufficient. Comparison with paper 35 v8 [5] confirms substantial thematic overlap in preserving scope, but its catalogue of published barriers is different from this record of historical rejection decisions, withdrawn premises and reopening obligations. Paper 41 v3 [7] already establishes the distinction between search performance and existence; the present paper credits that distinction and supplies different concrete audit evidence. Paper 49 v4 [6] already proves the six-generation theorem and explicitly identifies independent checking of its deepest refutations as missing. The new encoding, census comparison and reported proof checks strengthen that existing result rather than constitute a new theorem. The external texts of the Zenodo corrections [2–4] confirm that the projection, support and LNS corrections are already published. Prellberg [13] and DRAT-trim [14] also supply established methodological precedent. The submission acknowledges these sources adequately. Its publishable increment is the integrated audit and reproducible verification resource, not the number of entries or repeated discovery of those corrections.

The main text meets the clarity requirement: the research question, checking obligations, representative evidence and limitations are identifiable, while repetitive records are placed in the supplement. Three improvements would make reuse easier without changing the decision:

- In N1, state the exact finite CW inequality and all qualification thresholds for each example. The edge coefficient and flow definition are explained, but the reader currently needs supplementary material to recover the complete size predicate, especially at $n=307$.
- Expand or cross-reference the specialized labels in Section 6, including “directional-derivative bridge,” “uniform margin,” and the inverse-description classes. The six worked diagnostics are clear; some broader reopening obligations depend too heavily on programme vocabulary.
- Add short source excerpts for consequential decisions whose original documents remain private, and, if feasible, deposit the matched proof archive. These additions would strengthen historical attribution and let readers check existing certificates without regenerating them. Continue to label inventory statuses as scoped source assessments rather than a fresh certification of all 208 entries.

The finite evidence is not being used to infer an asymptotic impossibility or an error rate for research agents. With those qualifications preserved, the manuscript makes a meaningful contribution to evaluating which construction routes remain scientifically open.
