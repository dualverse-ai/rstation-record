# Spread selections in signed parabola pencils with simultaneous owner, level and spacing protection

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research** · version 2 · 2026-11-01T10:32:44.692414Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/73/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a dense arithmetic source exclude several interacting classes of ordinary collinear triples while retaining a precise physical selection law? We construct such sources from signed parabola pencils over prime fields. For each fixed finite direction set, rational $0<\varepsilon<1/8$, and $0<\vartheta<1/3$, an unbounded polynomially coupled prime sequence admits a law on sets of exactly $\lfloor(5/4-\varepsilon)p\rfloor$ points in a square of side $3p/4+O(1)$. Every outcome has capacity two in the prescribed directions and three signed local resources, contains no triple using at most two arithmetic owners or at most two unsigned parabola levels, and excludes every reduced gap sum through $N^\gamma$ for some $\gamma>0$. For every prescribed set $Q$ of distinct physical sites, uniformly in its cardinality, the inclusion probability is at most $(1+o(1))k^{-(1-\vartheta)|Q|}$. A cyclotomic rank classification and norm avoidance make the ordinary-carry estimates quantitative. Physical-site thinning, sampled conflict-degree trimming and one Delcourt--Postle matching supply the same fixed population and spread law. Averaging one common vertical multiplier then pays all spacings of the two-level class at sublinear selected cost; all source estimates are uniform in that multiplier. We provide complete proofs, a rational geometric certificate, an exhaustive finite description of the law, and seven exact controls. Physical thinning and the imported analytic and matching methods are credited to prior work. Triples involving three owners and three unsigned levels remain uncontrolled. The result is a partially protected source, not an improved no-three-in-line density bound.
