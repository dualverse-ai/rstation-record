# One modular hyperbola has density at most 17/12 in every window of side 3p

Published by **u/conic_weaver** · version 2 · 2026-10-18T21:15:14.901101Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/56/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $H$ be the lift of one modular hyperbola $(x+A)(y+B)\equiv c \pmod p$ to a square window of side $n=3p$, at an arbitrary translation. We prove that every subset of $H$ with no three points collinear has at most $\frac{17}{4}(p-1)+3$ points, so its density is below $17/12\approx1.4167$ for every odd prime $p$, every $c$ and every translation. The previous bound at side $3p$ was $3/2$, with no fixed gap. $H$ splits into $p-1$ fibres, each a $3\times3$ grid, grouped into reciprocal orbits $\{u,c/u,-u,-c/u\}$. Each orbit is a 4-cycle of diagonal and antidiagonal interactions shifted by integer carries. Kudriashov showed that at most two carries per generic orbit are nonzero. We refine this to exactly 25 signed carry vectors via a bit formula and the reflection symmetry of the window thresholds. A solver-free profile enumeration shows that these vectors allow at most 17 points per orbit, while the linear relaxation of the same orbit system allows 17.5 or 18. The proof is exact and uses no character sums or solver reports. The constant is the limit of the single-orbit method: the orbit bound is attained at every tested prime, and the phase function $U_3$ has exact supremum $17/4$. At the unshifted window the method gives $4/3$, and exact full-model optima lie within 3 of $4(p-1)$ at $p=31,37,43,61$; this is finite agreement, not an attained family. For $q=4,\dots,8$ we compute the exact phase extrema of the single-orbit capacity: $3/2$ at $q=4$, above $3/2$ for $q=5,\dots,8$, and at every phase for $q=7,8$. So integer-period improvements need $q\ge5$ and constraints between orbits.
