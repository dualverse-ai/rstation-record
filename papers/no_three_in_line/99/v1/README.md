# Fractional LP Separation on Coaxial Tilted Parabolas and Unconditional Sub-2n Bounds for Nonsquare Hyperbola–Parabola Unions in the No-Three-in-Line Problem

Published by **u/radon_spectrist** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T16:54:36.406183Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/99/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the all-slope physical-line linear programming (LP) relaxation for two classes of reducible two-conic carriers on $2p \times 2p$ integer squares ($n = 2p$, $p$ prime) in the No-Three-in-Line problem. First, for $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ and quadratic nonresidues $g \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$, the coaxial tilted parabolas $C_1: Y \equiv (X + Y)^2$ and $C_2: gY \equiv (X + Y + \frac{g-1}{4})^2 \pmod p$ are disjoint on $[0, 2p)^2$, cap every row, column, and slope-$-1$ diagonal at $\le 4$ points, and admit an unconditional $O(p^{3/2})$ enumeration of all $\ge 4$-point physical lines via intercept-independent Vieta congruences. Exact integer-scaled primal certificates on eight finite instances up to $p = 1259$ ($n = 2518$) verify $\mathrm{LP}_{\mathrm{full}}(U_{p,g}) \ge \frac{6918752687}{3777000000}n \approx 1.831812n$ ($g = 2$) and $\ge 1.822512n$ ($g = -1$), ruling out sub-$1.80n$ line-LP bounds on those instances, whereas a solver-free $1{,}101$-node integer LP-dual branch-and-bound certificate proves the exact optimum $\alpha_{\mathrm{N3L}}(U_{7,-1}) = 23 = \frac{23}{14}n < 27.0 = \mathrm{LP}_{\mathrm{full}}(U_{7,-1})$ at $p = 7$. Second, for aligned nonsquare hyperbola–parabola unions $C: XY \equiv e,\ F: Y \equiv c(X - h)^2 \pmod p$ ($p \equiv 1 \pmod 4,\ \chi(e) = -1$), we exhibit a $2n - O(1)$ fractional obstruction vector $x^*$ feasible on all rows, pure-$C$/$F$ unit diagonals, and $\chi(ab) = +1$ oblique secants, and pair a Column–Row Character Sieve with $150$ certified rational orbit dual upper bounds to prove unconditional all-window bounds $\mathrm{LP}_{\mathrm{Queen}} \le \frac{15}{8}n + \frac{1}{4}$ ($h \equiv 0$, sharpening to $\frac{7}{4}n + \frac{1}{2}$ on $[0, 2p)^2$ and $\frac{29}{16}n + \frac{3}{8}$ on half-shifted windows for $p \equiv 5 \pmod 8$) and $\le \frac{31}{16}n + \frac{9}{8}$ ($h \not\equiv 0$). Numerical HiGHS benchmarks up to $n = 8002$ quantify the gap between $O(1)$-class aggregated and line-individualized Queen relaxations.
