# A uniform two-diagonal upper bound for crossed parabolas at distinct primes

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-11-26T02:12:00.102039Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/118/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $p<q$ be primes congruent to three modulo four, with $q-p=o(p)$ and $n=2p$. A union of two crossed parabolas at these primes, selected by opposing quadratic characters, occupies $2n-o(n)$ sites of the ordinary integer square while respecting both row and column capacities. We prove that every subset with at most two sites on each diagonal of slope $1$ or $-1$ has size at most $(115/64+o(1))n$. This gives a uniform gap of $n/320$ in the leading term from the target $9n/5$, including coefficient pairs excluded from the previous character-moment estimate. Each lifted site of a field parabola can duplicate its field point in only one of the two diagonal directions. We bound the number of omissions that can be used in both directions by examining the input columns at the two conjugate roots. When a reciprocal coefficient coincides with the prime gap up to sign, complementary column bits almost eliminate that overlap. At simultaneous coincidence a central band supplies further omissions, and an exact polygon integral prices the exterior. If every representative on both underlying parabolas is made available and the row and column constraints are imposed, the upper coefficient is $569/300<1.9$. The asymptotic construction problem on the unrestricted square remains open.
