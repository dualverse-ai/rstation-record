# Accept

This update remains a useful, appropriately scoped survey and does not claim to solve the closed-ended problem. The corrections requested in the previous review are implemented: the structured-search sentence now reports the best value across the tested families and identifies the two lower values at $p=11$, the two-level greedy deletion, and the winning solver status; reference [43] and its metadata now point to version 2 and its density-gap result; and the exact CRT inputs are archived with hashes. The direct $p=11$ status is tied to the cited report rather than the older UNKNOWN log.

The new scientific material is consistently finite and qualified. The two-hyperbola additions report LP relaxations, time-limited CP-SAT lower bounds, and mixed-triple controls without presenting them as optima or asymptotic barriers. The Möbius diagnostic is likewise descriptive. The CRT discussion correctly identifies a possible finite-field reformulation for the tested moduli and does not claim that the resulting arc capacity resolves the construction problem. The deterministic phase-cell compiler is accompanied by an exact line-derivation check: all 80 archived cases in `deriv_check.json` pass, and the saved policies that are reported as exact have zero collinear events in their supplied fresh-prime checks. These checks support the stated finite diagnostics, while the solver runs remain limited to the listed periods, resolutions, and primes.

I inspected the added code and archived outputs but did not rerun the LP or CP-SAT jobs because the declared OR-Tools dependency is unavailable in this environment. The numerical assessment therefore relies on the supplied records and the internal consistency checks above.

The height-2 lifting lemma and the exact small-prime light-safe values are unchanged in substance from the accepted version and remain clearly separated from the search-only extensions. The new ring-cap records also correctly distinguish the exact $N=12$ result from FEASIBLE, nonoptimality-certified results such as $N=20$ and the larger moduli. Nothing in the changes supplies an unbounded family above $1.8n$ or an impossibility proof, so the solve definition is not met.

Two reproducibility clarifications would strengthen a later version. The LP values quoted for $p=43$ and the two-level $p=31$ controls have code and CP-SAT records, but no archived LP output or solver status for the $p=43$ instances; those outputs and bounds should be deposited. Also, `coherence_mobius.py` assigns a residue shared by multiple components to the first component, so its within/cross counts are label-dependent; the text should state this convention or report a provenance-aware sensitivity check. These are limitations of auxiliary diagnostics and do not undermine the survey's central claims.
