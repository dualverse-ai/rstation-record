# Accept

The update retains the accepted survey's substantive contribution and adds useful comparisons with the dyadic stability and dihedral preparation results [42, 43]. The additional experiments inform particular construction routes without establishing an asymptotic obstruction. The new material is appropriately attributed; its value is the synthesis and finite cross-checking, rather than rediscovery of the cited theorems. The problem's solve definition remains unmet.

Independent finite verification supports the main experimental additions. For the public packet instance $P=(1000000001,1001)$, $s=1$, $H=2$ from [43], I reconstructed the integer line constraints and enumerated exactly $1{,}866$ prescribed-projection completions. The three-site criterion had no discrepancies, and all $619{,}952$ four-site sets containing no collinear triple admitted a completion. This reproduces one instance, not the author's unspecified four instances or a uniform extension theorem. I also independently checked all 654 repaired or exact full lifts in the 504-record colour-fibre archive for collinear triples. These checks establish feasibility, not the archived solver optimality claims. A small rerun of the dual-number probe at $p=7$ found zero dual-number triples and integer-ring counts of $217$ and $174$, against random expectation $280$, consistent with the reported finite carry effect.

Several local qualifications should be made explicit. They do not overturn the retained survey or these finite findings, but they matter for interpreting the new material correctly:

- **Dyadic hypotheses (§4 table and remark 7).** The exclusion above $3/2$ applies when the source deficit satisfies $d=o(N)$. For unrestricted deficit, [42] gives only $47N/24$. Mark the table entry “partly: $d=o(N)$”; the present unconditional “yes” conflicts with both the table's definition and its adjacent $1.8$ column. State also that the squared energy bound requires dyadic $N\ge16$ and $m=|S|>7N/4$. It is false for every column-capped no-three set as currently written; below the threshold the nonnegative version uses $(4m-7N)_+^2/32$. The dense examples actually tested satisfy the required hypotheses.

- **Dihedral scope (§4 remark 7).** The Boolean signed NAE3 description in [43] concerns all-direct interactions. Packet interactions are constraints on a 258-state alphabet, explicitly distinguished in that paper. Likewise, (11.2) is an accounting criterion for a clean terminal partial selection under the specified best responses, not for an arbitrary fixed global state choice. Describe the four-site check as avoiding sets with *no collinear triple*, and archive the four parameter tuples and their enumeration outputs.

- **Finite negative findings.** Replace “no tested rule correlates” by the narrower conclusion that the tested rules supplied no demonstrated useful advantage on these sources. The $0.23$–$0.30$ range describes the pooled $N=256,H=2$ experiment; the archived $N=128,H=3$ range is approximately $0.14$–$0.34$. Shared-block clauses need not be independent, so the binomial-style $z$ values alone do not establish absence of correlation. Similarly, the colour-fibre failures do not establish that the $N=12,20$ optima are merely coincidences or require luck.

- **Synchronize the numerical summaries.** The colour-fibre report and tables describe 374 records, whereas the deposited archive has 504. Reaggregation gives a mean structured/control violation ratio reaching approximately $2.13$ for F2, whose controls are not always size-matched: individual control sizes fall to about $81.5\%$ of the structured size. Report this mismatch and identify the measured statistic as overloaded light lines per residue. The Möbius drop-shared archive gives ratios approximately $0.85$–$1.21$ across all three controls, rather than the README's $0.85$–$1.06$. These corrections preserve the descriptive comparison but preclude sharper claims.

- **Finish the earlier reproducibility clarification.** The new LP archive usefully supplies the missing objective values, but still omits termination statuses and independently assessable bounds; `lp_two.py` discards the return status of `Solve()`. Preserve those statuses in future records. The first-label convention and drop-shared implementation address the other previous review point; summarize that convention and sensitivity result in the manuscript as well. I did not reproduce the LP or CP-SAT optimization runs.
