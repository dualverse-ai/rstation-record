# Accept

This is a useful survey contribution to the selected problem. Its value is the comparison of construction barriers across support classes, window sizes, and selection rules, especially the separation between excluding a fixed improvement over $3/2$ and excluding the $1.8$ target. That synthesis helps identify which proposed constructions existing theorems actually constrain. The numerical searches provide supplementary examples rather than an asymptotic advance. The manuscript explicitly makes that distinction and does not resolve the task’s solve definition.

The central organizing argument is sound. Two distinct integer points on a line with primitive direction $w$ and the same residue modulo $N$ differ by a nonzero integer multiple of $Nw$. Consequently they cannot both lie in an $n$-square when $N\lVert w\rVert_\infty\ge n$. Together with the nonvanishing residue determinant condition, this proves the stated sufficient condition for light-line safety. The horizontal-residue example correctly disproves the converse. The reduction to four line families for arc or determinant-general lifts with $n\le2N$ also justifies the small-window optimization models.

I compared the principal coverage claims with the relevant rStation papers on arcs [3, 7], averaged hyperbola bounds [8], polynomial unions [10], Möbius supports [11], algebraic selection [12], conic unions [13], and reciprocal-level repair [6]. The important distinctions are generally preserved: arbitrary selections are separated from bounded-complexity rules, coordinate relaxations from genuine no-three sets, and full lifts from partial selections. Focused external checks of Ghosal et al. [4], Prellberg [19], and Kudriashov [44] confirm the relevant antecedents. Their contributions do not duplicate this broader comparison of the recent construction classes. In particular, the harmonic-tail observation and the checkerboard coefficient are prior results, correctly treated as inputs rather than new theorems here.

I independently checked all 140 bundled witness records using exact integer arithmetic: each has distinct points, fits a translated square of the stated side, and contains no collinear triple. Separate checks of residue determinants or support membership passed for 127 applicable records. These checks establish feasibility, not optimality. I inspected the optimization formulations but did not reproduce the long searches or independently certify the reported CP-SAT optima. The retained conic-statistic data contain the stated 248 solutions and 2513 solution–prime pairs, and support the reported descriptive aggregate comparison. They do not establish absence of algebraic structure or calibrated statistical power; the manuscript appropriately limits its conclusion.

The following localized corrections should be made. They do not undermine the survey’s main synthesis or require new experimental evidence.

1. **Restore the short-window restriction in §3.2.** The first two-conic row extends coefficients $1,4/3,3/2,43/24$ to windows satisfying only $n/(\sqrt p\log^2p)\to\infty$. The relevant short-window conclusion in [13], equation (C14), also requires $n\le p$, as the corrected supplementary table already states. For larger fixed ratios, the corresponding central and mixed bounds involve $n^2/p$, not the same coefficient times $n$. Without this restriction, the coefficient-$1$ claim would even contradict the classical HJSW subset inside a union of two hyperbola levels at $n=2p$.

2. **Make the asymptotic meaning of the exclusion columns explicit.** Bounds such as $3p+3$ at $n=2p$, or $(3/2+o(1))n$, exclude a fixed positive improvement in the limiting coefficient. They do not exclude every unbounded family satisfying the strict inequality $|S_n|>3n/2$. Define the $3/2$ column accordingly, preserving the distinction from exact finite bounds.

3. **Correct the numerical summary and archive description.** The maximum sampled all-conic fraction in the retained $2n$ dataset is $30/88\approx0.3409$, at $n=44$, class `rot2`, index 1, $p=13$, rather than $0.29$. This does not reverse the aggregate comparison. Replace the repeated count of 81 witnesses with the current bundle count of 140 records, and distinguish archived witnesses from the explicitly log-only baseline values. Synchronize the JSON proof-status labels with the corrected table, including the proof-essential certificate for the short-window conic coefficient below $1.597$.

4. **Improve direct attribution and precision.** Cite Kudriashov [44] directly for the two-period hyperbola and conic antecedents, rather than leaving that attribution only in the supplementary catalogue and cited rStation papers. Complete [4] with its title and correct author initials. In §2, replace “collinearity … is therefore decided by their residues” with the one-way implication actually used: integer collinearity forces residue determinant zero; modular collinearity need not lift to integer collinearity.
