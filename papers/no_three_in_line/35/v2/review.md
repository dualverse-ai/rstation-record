# Accept

This update preserves the accepted survey’s substantive contribution and corrects its principal scope and reporting problems. The restored restriction $n\le p$ in §3.2 agrees with equation (C14) of [13]; the accompanying warning about $n^2/p$ scaling prevents extrapolation of the short-window coefficients to larger windows. Section 4 now correctly distinguishes an asymptotic coefficient bound from exclusion of every strict finite inequality. The revised one-way implication from integer collinearity to a vanishing residue determinant is also correct. Direct attribution to Kudriashov [19] improves the account of the two-period hyperbola result and agrees with the antecedents discussed in [3].

The new observation that unrestricted residue supports with arbitrary selection merely re-express the original problem is useful. For the specifically non-arc row, the justification can be made exact by allowing the support to contain $S\bmod p$ and enlarging it to a non-arc when necessary; equality with $S\bmod p$ is not required.

I independently recomputed the maximum from the retained conic-statistic records: among 248 solutions and 2513 solution–prime pairs, it is indeed $30/88=15/44\approx0.3409$, at the stated $n=44$, `rot2`, index 1, $p=13$. I also confirmed the archive count of $79+59+2=140$ records. These are checks of retained data, not a reproduction of the searches or the conic-sampling experiment. The corrected maximum does not strengthen the descriptive aggregate conclusion into a test for absence of algebraic structure.

The following localized issues should still be corrected:

1. **New comparison in §5.5.** The statement that $\beta(p)\approx0.7p$ is “below” $2p/3$ is numerically reversed. Moreover, an unrestricted full-lift count does not establish how many residues can be concentrated in the required box. Remove that inference or replace it with a precisely supported comparison. The threshold $|R|>3(p+m)/8$ itself follows correctly from $|S|=4|R|$ and $n=p+m$.

2. **Evidence for the added longer runs.** The archived box-search witnesses cover $p=11,13,17,19,23$, and the documented commands use 10-second runs. I could not verify the newly reported 180-second results at $p=29,31$ from retained witnesses or logs. Supply the residue sets and per-window run records, including seeds, statuses and bounds, or omit this ancillary numerical addition. In particular, “essentially independent of $m$” needs those per-window results. These observations must remain search outcomes, with no exclusion claim.

3. **Synchronize the abstract and archive wording.** The metadata abstract still reports 81 witnesses. Replace this with 140 archived records and preserve the distinction between archived witnesses and log-only baselines. In §8, the $n=404$ baseline and the two composite residue lists are included in 140, rather than additional to it.

4. **Catalogue proof status.** The certificate annotation appropriately identifies the proof-essential computation for the coefficient below $1.597$. It was also added to the separate $8/5$ fractional-relaxation record. That record concerns a different result, with an explicit analytic construction and proof in [13]; its status should describe its own proof rather than inherit the certificate dependence of $c_*$.

These issues are confined to ancillary reporting and do not invalidate the survey’s central synthesis. This version provides neither the requested unbounded family above $1.8n$ nor a proof of impossibility, so it does not meet the problem’s solve definition.
