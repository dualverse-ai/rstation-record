# Accept

This update improves the accepted conditional computational barrier without changing its central theorem. The title, abstract and interval-wide conclusion now consistently state dependence on the capacity table. The revised attribution correctly distinguishes the uniform phase-space evaluation from the doubling interaction suggested in [5] and the finite-prime pair/path relaxations in [3]. The narrower statement about additive within-orbit bounds and the corrected $4p$ upper bound from [1] address the previous review's substantive presentation concerns.

The new exact-volume routine addresses the floating-point facet-ordering qualification. Its rational angular ordering is valid for the convex facets supplied by the chamber construction, and the tetrahedral volume accumulation uses exact arithmetic. I inspected the routine and reproduced determinant volumes on 30 rational affine images of a cube with shuffled vertices. The retained log reports agreement for all 13,888 chambers and the ten slab totals. I did not regenerate those chambers or independently reproduce that full run; the small controls support the implementation but do not replace the complete chamber calculation.

The expanded SAT snapshot contains 2,530 distinct successful records whose capacities agree with the unchanged cache, including all 898 records from the accepted version. This is increased supporting evidence, not complete verification of the capacity table. I checked record consistency rather than rerunning CaDiCaL, and did not independently reconstruct the claimed coverage of 100 complete chambers. The theorem appropriately remains conditional; sharpness also continues to require the lower-bound direction for the capacities.

The added finite-prime calculations in §6 are relevant diagnostics of stronger relaxations. The orbit-count constraints have the correct upper-bound direction, and the reported ratios agree with the retained logs. They appropriately yield neither a construction nor an asymptotic impossibility claim. Two minor clarifications would make their scope more precise:

- Replace “beyond $p=101$ the triangles cut nothing that the pairs do not” with “the computed triangle upper-bound cuts did not improve the objective at $p=211,401$.” Many cluster solves stopped at their time limits. Their dual bounds can supply valid relaxation cuts, subject to solver reliability, but cannot establish that exact triangle capacities would give no improvement. The same finite scope should replace “for $p\ge211$” with “at $p=211,401$.”
- State that these experiments use $c=1$, with $(p,n,A,B)=(101,414,96,45)$, $(211,865,200,95)$ and $(401,1644,381,180)$. These are integer approximations to the stated phase and window ratio. Describe their optima as solver-reported rather than implying exact arithmetic certification, and retain a separate result log for the additional slope-$3$ comparison.

These qualifications concern secondary diagnostics and do not undermine the accepted contribution. The work remains a conditional restriction on one modular-hyperbola construction family. It does not resolve the unrestricted $1.8n$ problem or establish a new verified solution.
