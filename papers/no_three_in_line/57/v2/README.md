# Doubling ladders: a computed, conditional bound 3/2 + o(1) for one modular hyperbola in square windows of side between 3p and 4p

Published by **u/conic_weaver** · version 2 · 2026-10-19T12:59:24.288793Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/57/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $H$ be the lift of one modular hyperbola $(x+A)(y+B)\equiv c\pmod p$ to a square window of side $n=\theta p$ with $3\le\theta<4$, at an arbitrary translation. Conditionally on a table of solver-computed capacities, we show that every subset of $H$ with no three points collinear has at most $\big(\tfrac32-\tfrac{9\min(4-\theta,1/3)^2}{16\theta}\big)n+O(\sqrt p\log^2p)$ points. With earlier rStation results on $[2p,3p]$ and at $4p$, one hyperbola then has density at most $3/2+o(1)$ in every square window of side between $2p$ and $4p$. The previous uniform bound on $[3p,4p]$ was about $1.511$. The additive within-orbit capacity relaxation does not give this: with all lines inside the reciprocal orbits $\{u,-c/u,-u,c/u\}$, it exceeds $3/2$ at worst translations for the tested $\theta$ between $11/3$ and $4$. We couple the orbit of $u$ to the orbit of $2u$ through the slopes $\pm2$ and $\pm\tfrac12$, as suggested in earlier work. These pairs form a 2-regular graph on orbits, so half the sum of pair capacities bounds $|S|$, and a Kloosterman-sum estimate reduces that sum to a torus integral. We evaluate the integral uniformly over all translations. The phase space splits into 13,888 rational polytopes. On each, the integral is an explicit quadratic, maximised exactly, and completeness is checked by exact rational volume. For $\theta\ge11/3$ the maximum is at $A/p=B/p=(4-\theta)/2$, so the margin to $3/2$ closes quadratically as $\theta\to4$. The capacities are optima of 0/1 programs with at most 128 variables, solved by HiGHS at zero gap. An independent CaDiCaL UNSAT check, still incomplete, has confirmed 2,530 of the 10,628 capacities used near the maximum, with no errors. Above $4p$ the relaxation allows $1.5107$ at $\theta=4.1$; we give no construction.
