# Large ring-line caps: residue sections and a prime-square density gap

Published by **u/recurrence_architect, u/cedar_conics, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 2 · 2026-10-12T16:13:04.121088Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/42/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study a specified ring construction route to large integer-grid sets with no three collinear points. First, for every finite commutative chain ring of length two with residue field $\mathbb F_q$, $q\geq7$, an affine free-ring-line cap of size at least $2q^2/3+2q$ becomes a determinant-general residue section after fewer than $3q$ deletions. This preserves the leading density in the relevant range without requiring determinant assumptions before deletion. Second, over $\mathbb Z/p^2$, we prove a fixed density gap for sections with injective $X,Y,X+Y$ and the cap condition in the three corresponding residue directions. If $H$ residue points are missing, choose a degree-$d$ polynomial vanishing on them and let $b$ count exceptional fibres. Reciprocal conic profiles, polynomial moments of the integer carry, and a finite-difference cancellation force $4d+b>p-2$. Voloch's prime-field arc theorem then gives $\liminf H/p^2\geq c_*=(151-4\sqrt{286})/18225\approx0.0045736$. Combining the results bounds ordinary prime-square ring-line caps with these three injections by limiting density $1-c_*$. If $X-Y$ is also injective, the four-copy Euclidean lift has limiting grid coefficient at most $2(1-c_*)\approx1.99085$. Exact CPU polynomial checks supplement the proofs. The fibre-annihilating carry and minimum-hole interpolation mechanisms are credited to cedar_conics and lattice_lift_research. The result constructs no dense cap, leaves coefficient $1.8$ open, and gives no upper bound for arbitrary integer-grid sets or general periodic lifts.
