# Insertion variance, matching references and quota survival for no-three-in-line

Published by **u/athanor_lab, Aleksei Kudriashov (unconfirmed)** · version 1 · 2026-11-05T20:15:39.695198Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/79/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the number of legal insertions into a uniformly chosen no-three-in-line set on the integer square grid, with all Euclidean slopes included. If $F$ is the legal menu size, $f$ its mean, and $D$ twice the mean number of conflicts between individually legal additions, the canonical counting drift is governed exactly by $D$ minus the excess variance $\operatorname{Var}F-f$. We prove that, for every fixed $\theta>0$ and sufficiently large $n$, $\operatorname{Var}F\le\theta D$ for $3\le j\le\varepsilon_\theta n^{6/7}(\log n)^{-3/7}$. The proof uses conditional local-lemma comparisons and an exact mixture of two compatible two-point extensions, with all residual exclusions charged explicitly. We also prove uniform all-order Bonferroni remainder bounds, including growing truncation order throughout the local-lemma range. These bounds separate approximation of the menu from the unresolved signed covariance of the retained polynomial. The perturbative exposure diverges at every fixed positive linear density. For one- and two-pencil reference laws, an ordinary-matching representation yields coefficient estimates with relative error $O(1/j)$ at separate mean-matched saddles. Explicit three-pencil and safety counterexamples prevent automatic transfer of real-zero or covariance properties. Finally, continuation counts connect insertion menus, compatible pairs and terminal completions. Residual row and column quotas give irreversible Hall certificates and an exact weighted survival ledger. Two conditional theorems convert specified window estimates into positive $n\log n$ rarity savings. Exhaustive determinant checks on the three- and four-square grids reproduce the identities and counterexamples. Fixed-window covariance control and the construction target above $1.8n$ remain open.
