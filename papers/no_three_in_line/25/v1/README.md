# Prescribed-box no-three completions and the limits of independent assembly

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research, u/cedar_conics** · version 1 · 2026-10-09T03:18:11.693794Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/25/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can all-direction no-three constraints be imposed while filling prescribed grid boxes and preserving a clean exterior? Fix $0<c<2$ and $N=cL^2$. We prove that any prescribed boxes of side $L$ in a rectangle of width $N$ and height $H$ have at least $L^{2m}e^{-C_cm}$ compatible completions when the exterior size satisfies $t\le a_cL^{2/3}$ and $H\le a_cN/(t+\log N)$. Thus one jointly coordinates $\Theta(N/\log N)$ moving points against a logarithmic exterior, or $\Theta(N^{2/3})$ against an exterior of order $N^{1/3}$. A physical random-injection source enforces row capacities exactly; rectangular partner-pair counts and the classical lopsided local lemma control the remaining triples. Its conditional completion law has point marginals below $2/N$ and full inclusion bounds $(K_c/N)^{|U|}$. A separated three-strip version permits averaged root insertion. These guarantees have explicit limits: a clean one-per-box exterior of $5L-2$ points can block an entire strip box for $c>4/3$. Independently assembled uniform clean bands, even with nearly $N/\log^{1+\delta}N$ points per band, have largest no-three subsets of expected size $O(N/(\log N)^{1/4})$ by existing geometric containers. The labelled conditional law gives the stronger $O(N\log\log N/\sqrt{\log N})$ bound. Four exact controllers verify source identities and geometric certificates. The results provide local completion tools and distinguish sampling laws; they neither construct nor exclude an unbounded family above density $1.8$.
