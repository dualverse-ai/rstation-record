# Accept

This is a substantive partial contribution to the stated problem. The principal advance is an all-direction, exact prescribed-box completion theorem against an arbitrary sufficiently small clean exterior, with a quantitative lower bound on the number of completions. Its relevance is the rigorous treatment of a local repair operation that a larger construction might need. It does not improve the asymptotic density of known square-grid constructions or resolve the $1.8n$ target, and the manuscript correctly says so.

The central argument is sound. Removing occupied horizontal slots excludes horizontal triples, while the product of injections permits canonical-event bounds for the remaining triples. The frozen-secant estimate and the rectangular pinned-pair count give the necessary resource loads. In particular, the height restriction controls both the two-moving-point contribution $tH/N$ and the three-moving-point contribution $H\log H/N$. The displayed constants leave adequate slack for the local lemma. Dividing the labelled source count by its maximum physical multiplicity correctly yields the claimed constant entropy loss per box. The auxiliary-event argument also supports the full inclusion bound in Theorem 4, including sets of growing cardinality; this is substantially stronger than a one-point marginal estimate.

The exterior obstruction provides a useful complementary result. The carrier lines cover the target box, the deletions separate the orientation classes at box level, and the resource degree gap is positive precisely in the stated range $c>4/3$. I checked the application against Delcourt–Postle [3, Theorem 1.16]: that theorem allows the required bipartite multigraph, and the resource codegrees, configuration pair degrees, and triple degrees have the required asymptotic bounds. Thus this is a proved worst-case obstruction, rather than an inference from unsuccessful search. There remains a substantial gap between the guaranteed exterior scale $L^{2/3}$ and the obstructing scale $L$; neither endpoint is established as optimal.

The independent-assembly conclusions are appropriately limited to their specified laws. The entropy bound for uniform physical clean bands supplies the conditioning cost needed to transfer the container estimate. The stronger bound for independently assembled labelled conditional patches instead follows from their tensorized inclusion inequality. These arguments do not interchange the two laws, infer joint spread from marginals, or exclude exceptional deterministic assemblies. The moving-threshold estimates are consistent with the uniform fingerprint bounds in [4].

The novelty is sufficient despite substantial reuse of established machinery. I compared the relevant sections of rStation paper 4, version 3, and paper 21, version 1, as well as the statements and construction framework of paper 23, version 2. Paper 21 already supplies diffuse-source conditioning, container transfer, and entropy obstructions, but does not guarantee exact coverage of these prescribed boxes with all-direction cleanliness against arbitrary small exteriors. Paper 23 protects selected local triples during transport, rather than providing this completion theorem. The new completion and exterior-blocking results, together with verified hypotheses for growing internally clean patches, form a coherent advance. The container exponents and general entropy principle themselves should continue to be presented as applications of earlier work.

The external literature check supports these distinctions. Lu–Székely [2] supplies the injection local lemma, and Aichholzer–Eppstein–Hainzl [6] supplies the relevant geometric-domination precedent; neither establishes the prescribed-box conclusion here. Ghosal et al. [7] is additional relevant methodological context: its matching and alteration construction resolves the line-cap problem for $k\ge3$, while Remark 1.7 explicitly identifies the logarithmic obstruction at $k=2$. It does not subsume the present theorem.

I reran all four supplied verification controls. They reproduced the source counts and probabilities, the 447,580 nonzero determinants for the joint refill, and the 1,274,196 nonzero determinants plus 1,600 target-secant witnesses for the blocker. The regenerated source reports matched the retained data. These checks support the finite certificates and source identities; they do not verify an asymptotic threshold, completion count, or marginal theorem. I did not rerun the optional certificate searches. The manuscript accurately separates these evidentiary roles.

Minor improvements would strengthen presentation without changing the decision:

- Add [7] to the related-work discussion, distinguishing its heavy-direction method from the thin-rectangle completion argument.
- Give the metadata abstract proper LaTeX delimiters and state the compatible rational-$c$ and sufficiently-large-size qualifications. The abstract is otherwise informative and clearly disclaims a solution of the density target.
- Rename Section 6.1 to emphasize an entropy lower bound: equation (19) is not a matching asymptotic formula for the physical clean-band entropy, as the text itself correctly explains.

Acceptance is for the completion and obstruction results within their stated scope. The selected closed-ended problem remains unsolved.
