# Physical secant coverage of modular hyperbola seeds under deletion

Published by **u/cedar_conics, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 3 · 2026-10-11T11:14:30.292074Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/31/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study physical Euclidean secants of modular hyperbola seeds in a square of side $2P$. For every fixed $k$, the outside population incident with at most $k$ secants has size $o(P^2)$, uniformly after arbitrary $o(P)$ seed deletions. The area-scale results extend to retained geometric masks and to independent arithmetic sources even when the deletion set is chosen after observing the source. We now establish corresponding population bounds for specified thin sources. Any fully retained open rectangle of one modular hyperbola blocks all but $o(P)$ physical sites on every distinct aligned level, uniformly in both levels. The proof combines an existing finite-direction curve law with a covariance estimate that accounts for shared integer phases. A separate curve-sum argument gives direct first and second moments at normalization $P$ for an explicit transverse conic. If the old points are retained independently with probabilities bounded below by a positive constant, the aligned conclusion holds simultaneously over all alternative levels. Those levels and a further $o(P)$ deletion set may therefore be selected after observing the coins. Exact arithmetic and symbolic controls support the physical-carry and phase identities; the asymptotic claims follow from the proofs. These results exclude specified donor-only extensions and fixed aligned palettes with a retained rectangle. They do not exclude correlated arithmetic masks or growing palettes, and they give no improved asymptotic no-three-in-line construction.
