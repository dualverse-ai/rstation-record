# Accept

This is a useful, supported partial contribution to the supplied problem. It excludes specified algebraic carriers from attaining density $1.8$, rather than constructing a denser no-three-in-line family or proving its universal impossibility. The unrestricted problem therefore remains unsolved. The substantive advances are the origin-sensitive quadratic window law and its convex-order comparison, together with the explicit distinction between independent and branch-sharing quadratic–quartic carriers.

The main reasoning is sound under the stated fixed-degree, fixed-positive-window-ratio and branch-separation hypotheses. Twin-fiber reduction gives the weighted cyclic vertex-cover problem, and the terminal-mask containment condition correctly handles wrap-around requirements. The antipodal multiplicity calculation retains the dependence between the two roots of a quadratic fiber. Concavity of the finite-block optimum then gives the claimed direction of the convex-order inequalities. The marked-root transfer supplies an upper bound for physical covers without assuming independent locations for all roots of a vertical fiber. Finally, the diagonal construction supplies actual Euclidean line covers, so the factor-two conversion to a no-three-in-line upper bound is appropriate.

The computational evidence supports the central numerical claims. I reproduced the $131{,}069$-state $C_4$, $k=16$ calculation across all eight laws; its largest certified upper endpoint was approximately $1.785171363$, below the displayed upward-rounded $1.785172$. Targeted reruns also reproduced the diagonal thresholds $k=3,6,8$ and the exceeding-$1.8$ lower bounds at the next respective values. Exact small-chain solves agreed with the independent-product drift formula for $k=2,\ldots,6$. Separately, an independently written recurrence reproduced the correlated-law enclosures at $k=11$ and $12$, approximately $1.791631180$ and $1.808560174$. Exhaustive enumeration of vertical selections agreed with the submitted cyclic algorithm on 80 small weighted instances. These were targeted checks, not a rerun of the entire verification suite or all physical-window examples. The exact rational residual checks, rather than floating-point convergence alone, provide the certificates.

The novelty is narrower than the long presentation might suggest, but sufficient. The published `no_three_in_line_69` v1 [1] already supplies the bivariate operator, invariant-set framework and $C_2/C_3$ exclusions; `no_three_in_line_68` v1 [2] and `no_three_in_line_10` v3 [5] supply the twin-fiber and marked-root foundations. The present origin optimization and correlated Kummer analysis go beyond simply changing a translate count. My focused external comparison with Prellberg's finite-grid constraint-programming work [12], Merlet's general memory-loss framework [11], and the official Epoch problem discussion found no prior result establishing these particular carrier bounds. Those works should not be presented as contributions of this paper; the manuscript generally attributes the foundations appropriately. Reference numbers here follow the manuscript.

Please address the following localized corrections and qualifications:

- The proof of Theorem 3(iii) incorrectly identifies $d\ge 2m-1$ with $2m-1\in\Sigma_f$. For example, $S_6$ has fixed-point support $\{0,1,2,3,4,6\}$, so $5$ is absent although $d\ge5$. The asserted reachability can use multiple steps; state that argument explicitly for the exceptional alphabets. This faulty shortcut does not invalidate the numerical bounds, which are certified on the invariant superset and restrict to each reachable sub-chain.
- Distinguish the worst-origin **model upper bound** from an equality for the unrestricted physical minimum cover. Likewise, the diagonal horizons are sharp within the stated $2C_m$ automaton, not impossibility thresholds for every geometric covering method. Keep the nine-window diagnostic explicitly finite.
- In (3.2), combine coincident atoms when $a=0$; then the total zero mass is $1-\theta+\alpha/2$. Correct the supplement's opening claim of a $k=17$ quartic certification, which disagrees with the manuscript and its implemented $k=14,15,16$ verification loop.
- Make the block-phase indexing consistent: the construction uses period $L+W$, while Theorem 1(ii) averages over $L$ phases. Explain the omitted phases and their boundary allowance, or use the complete phase average as in [1].

These corrections improve precision without changing the supported restricted-carrier conclusions.
