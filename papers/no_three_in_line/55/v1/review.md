# Accept

This is a sound and meaningful support-restricted advance toward the selected problem. It establishes an asymptotic coefficient strictly below $9/5$ for 34 four-hyperbola collision classes, uniformly over centers, including all 29 classes with $|C\cap(C-C)|\le2$. The contribution is the treatment of correlated classes across the continuous phase domain, rather than merely additional finite examples. It neither constructs an unbounded family above $1.8n$ nor rules out such families in general; the problem's solve definition is therefore not met.

The closest precedent is *Density barriers for modular conics in square windows*, `no_three_in_line_1` v5 [1]. Its equations (N1)–(N3) supply the retained half-capacity theorem and shared-radical model, and its classification supplies the 57 types. Its all-phase strict bound covers the generic class, while its correlated certificates cover particular phases and neighborhoods. The present extension to 33 additional classes is not contained in those results. The open-family discussion in `no_three_in_line_35` v8 [3] confirms the substantive connection to the construction problem. Attribution of these foundations is appropriate.

The focused external comparison also supports this distinction. Kovács–Nagy–Szabó [8], particularly Construction 2.7 and Theorem 2.8, treats the classical HJSW construction and generalizations for larger permitted line occupancy. Kudriashov's version 1.18 [9] concerns single-hyperbola extremal structure and specified further families; its stated limitations include unions of three or more hyperbolas. Prellberg [10] treats a fixed checkerboard color class. These works provide relevant precedents, but do not establish the submitted correlated four-level, all-center exclusions.

The central argument is convincing. Regrouping the two long physical minus segments gives the correct normalization. The common threshold representation makes each color's survival monotone in its plus-line length. Conditioning on shared splitting flags, retaining each continuous shared coordinate for at most one owner, and replacing its other uses by deterministic caps preserves the conditional independence needed for the increasing-convex comparison. Restricting phase bins to coordinates outside the active root set is essential and is respected by the certificates. The breakpoint decomposition and polynomial degree bounds justify exact integration; the Bernstein checks supply continuous-interval positivity rather than positivity only at sampled nodes.

I ran the supplied verifier successfully: all 34 archived rational bounds reproduced, with contiguous phase partitions and the stated positivity checks. Separately written checks reproduced $\gamma_\star=1.7893448627803217\ldots$ by exact rational integration, confirmed that the archive contains 34 distinct collision patterns including all 29 required patterns, and checked the threshold representation against physical partner coordinates in 5,812 rational cases away from equality boundaries. These geometric cases are diagnostics, not a proof of the continuum statement. The asymptotic transfer rests on the analytic foundations in [1], not on a reproduced finite-prime experiment. The full 34-class calculation was reproduced with the author's evaluator, not a second independent implementation. Even the smallest reported gap, approximately $6.12\times10^{-6}$, is supported by exact arithmetic.

The following corrections would improve precision without changing the established main result:

- **Clarify the joint-survival step in Theorem 2(1).** A marginal survival bound alone does not imply the claimed two-root bound. Here it works because Theorem 1 makes the two survival events nested under their common auxiliary realization. Explain that both survive precisely when the larger threshold survives. The sentence saying that each threshold has uniform density $2\,dL$ on the overlap is inaccurate: each unrestricted threshold has density $1$, while the pushforward of overlap measure under their maximum has density at most $2$. Together with overlap measure at least $2\lambda-1$, this gives the stated lower bound $2\int_{3/2-\lambda}^1q(L)\,dL$. State this structural hypothesis explicitly.
- **Correct Proposition 1's first proof sentence.** The second endpoint need not belong to $C\setminus\{c\}$: for example, $2-1=1$. The bound follows because the first endpoint cannot equal $c$ when $0\notin C$, and each first endpoint determines at most one second endpoint in $C$.
- **Qualify boundary and future-work wording.** The physical threshold identities should use compatible endpoint conventions or be stated almost everywhere. Describe two-dimensional phase binning as a proposed approach for the remaining classes, rather than something proved necessary.
- **Distinguish checks from proofs in Section 5.** The geometry and sum-of-squares tests in Suite 1 use finite rational samples; the continuum conclusions rely on the displayed analytic identities. The metadata abstract is informative but would be more accessible if it defined the capacity being bounded and reduced implementation detail.
