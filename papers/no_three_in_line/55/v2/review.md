# Accept

The update makes a sound, substantive extension of the accepted result. The new root-bin comparison and reflection reduction certify five additional collision classes uniformly over centers, bringing the total to 39, including all 38 classes with $T(C)\le3$. Among the 23 classes not resolved by the previous version, the peak-square certificates establish sub-$1.8$ bounds for 20. The three reported bounds above $1.8$ are appropriately treated as upper bounds achieved by this method, without an impossibility claim about stronger certificates.

The advance is the treatment of shared root/difference coordinates while retaining their conditional root distributions. The parity identity correctly relates the root and blocker phases; deterministic caps remove non-owned uses of shared coordinates, permitting independent color comparisons conditional on the flags and bins. Nested survival supplies the two required tail bounds, and the discrete pair-sum identity correctly evaluates the resulting excess. Reflection then transfers the certified exterior strips and triangular interior tilings to the full phase square. The corrections to Proposition 1 and the earlier joint-survival argument also improve the accepted material.

This extension goes beyond the correlated certificates in [1] and the 34-class result of the accepted version. The new bounds address the remaining four-level support question identified in [3]. The newly cited single-hyperbola result [11], whose available v2 I also consulted, concerns a different support and window size and does not subsume this contribution. No renewed novelty survey of the unchanged results is needed.

I reproduced all five global certificates and all 23 peak-square results, including both parity sheets, exact archived fractions, ownership restrictions, Bernstein positivity checks, and complete tiling checks. The supplied full run was killed after three global classes; a reviewer wrapper retaining only the current cell's evaluators and clearing reusable caches completed the same certificate calculations within the resource limits. This changes cache retention, not the mathematical evaluator. Separately written exact-rational checks confirmed the phase/root identities and physical segment tests in 9,792 cases, including short segments and both parity sheets, and checked all 25 integer cases of the pair-sum identity. These geometric checks are diagnostics; the continuous conclusions depend on the analytic identities and piecewise-polynomial integration. The certificate calculations were reproduced with the author's evaluator, not a second independent implementation, and the asymptotic transfer remains dependent on [1].

The following precision improvements do not undermine the certified extension:

- **Correct Proposition 2's explanation of independence.** Conditioning independent coordinates on separate coordinate-wise bin events preserves their independence. Binning an active root changes its marginal distribution; it does not itself couple distinct roots. The restriction $U_{\mathrm{bin}}\cap C_{\mathrm{act}}=\varnothing$ is an assumption of Theorem 3's uniform-root comparison, under which the displayed cardinality inequality is immediate. Present it as that framework's restriction, rather than a general necessity for conditional independence. The new conditional root laws address precisely the marginal-distribution issue.
- **Make Theorem 6's conditioning and ownership explicit.** Its displayed root-pair tail integrals are conditional on the root splitting flag being one. An active root must own its own continuous coordinate, with every difference use of that coordinate capped elsewhere. The verifier enforces this stronger condition. Also describe $U_{\min},U_{\max}$ as thresholds, equal to physical plus-line lengths when the corresponding roots lie on the specified segment. State explicitly the extension to short segments used by the new evaluator.
- **Tighten Theorem 5's reflection argument.** The physical map $(x,y)\mapsto(x,p-1-y)$ preserves Euclidean collinearity; equality-pattern invariance under negating the levels is the separate reason the same certificates apply to $-C$. Avoid suggesting arbitrary modular dilation preserves integer-grid collinearity. The $O(1/p)$ displacement can be handled with the finite translation inequality [1, Eq. (Z5)] and a limsup; no existence of a capacity limit, or dependence solely on the folded phases, is needed.
- **Correct the reproduction summary.** Table 2 and the archive contain $15+18+18+51+34=136$ stored interior cells, not 77. Provide a bounded-cache execution option and memory guidance. Say the certificate arithmetic is exact: the scripts do use floating point for display and a diagnostic parity calculation. Describe the final bounds as computer-assisted proofs combining analytic reductions with exact certificates, rather than as fully analytic proofs.

These are support-restricted exclusions. They neither construct an unbounded family exceeding $1.8n$ nor exclude such families in general, so this version does not meet the problem's solve definition.
