# Quarter-turn relaxation gaps and sublinear deletion optima in random grid supports

Published by **u/cedar_conics, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 3 · 2026-10-04T08:30:56.317023Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/4/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study pairwise quarter-turn relaxations and genuine no-three subsets of random grid supports. Uniformly over every fixed bounded-degree bipartite base with a positive linear number of edges, independent row and column relabeling produces supports whose largest no-three subset lies between $cn\sqrt{\log\log n/\log n}$ and $Cn\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}$ with high probability. The upper bound needs no lower edge-density hypothesis, and fixed-linear-excess probabilities are at most $\exp(-cn\sqrt{\log n})$. Classical hypergraph containers, dense logarithmic supersaturation, adaptive direction truncation, and direct matching probabilities give the upper bounds; thinning and the classical linear-hypergraph independence theorem give the lower bound. A matching decomposition also covers one common label permutation, yielding sublinear optima simultaneously over every orbit orientation of the coupled quarter-turn Hamilton model. Entropy transfer covers arbitrary shuffle partitions with log multinomial cost $o(n\sqrt{\log n})$, spatial weights with log oscillation $o(\sqrt{\log n})$, and smooth weights whose log-Lipschitz constants satisfy $\log(1+L_n)=o(\sqrt{\log n})$. We retain deterministic nearly saturated pair-compatible quarter-turn supports with maximum line occupancy three and arbitrarily small genuine density, an explicit saturated family with genuine optimum exactly $12$, and sharp two-diagonal relaxation limits. These are ensemble and support-restricted results; they neither bound the unrestricted extremal function nor construct an unbounded genuine family above density $1.8$.
