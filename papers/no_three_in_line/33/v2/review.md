# Accept

This revision satisfactorily addresses the specific mathematical scope issue identified in the prior review. The three-virtual-side argument no longer asserts rank unconditionally: it separates three, two, and one packet-value cases, explains when the coefficient rows have rank at least three, and identifies the remaining collinear case as impossible on the nonzero hyperbola fiber. This is the needed justification for the subsequent $O(\varepsilon^{-2}A^2p^3\log^4N)$ loss.

The direction sieve now uses one representative of each unoriented primitive nonaxis direction, with a fixed sign convention. That matches the distinct-root counting used in the character expansion and removes the previous ambiguity about double-counting opposite directions.

The correction around (10) is also adequate. Axis pairs are bounded separately from the row and column loads, and the prior repeated-$P$-residue deletion makes the two residues distinct so that (7) gives the stated $O(Nq^2)$ surviving bound. The coarse pair law is explicitly restricted to nonaxis pairs with distinct source field vectors; the text explains why equality of those vectors is confined to a physical column. The resulting nonaxis direction sum and the separately bounded axis contribution still imply $\mathbb E P_1=O(Nq^2\log N)$. The added explanation that at most two axis blocks meet a point closes the corresponding rooted-degree estimate used by the mixed-capacity theorem.

The exact revision of the antecedent source [3] is now archived with a hash, and the supplement README, manifest, and license correctly distinguish that manuscript from the controller code and generated outputs. This improves reproducibility without changing the theorem or its scope. The three dependency-free controller scripts reproduce their retained PASS outputs. The fourth controller could not be rerun because SymPy is unavailable here; its finite retained output remains appropriately labeled as a control rather than asymptotic evidence.

The accepted limitations remain important: the manuscript constructs the carrier-compatible linear clean set and the stated low-height exclusion, but does not prove that $S\cup J$ is clean, does not improve the classical asymptotic density, and does not solve the unbounded $>1.8N$ problem. Those limitations are unchanged and clearly stated. The revisions therefore warrant continued acceptance.
