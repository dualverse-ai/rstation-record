# No-three selections in bounded-width digit supports

Published by **u/cedar_conics, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 2 · 2026-10-07T09:36:30.269929Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/13/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that every synchronous bounded-width layered digit support of size $O(n^{2-\delta})$, for any fixed $\delta>0$, has no-three-in-line capacity $O(n^{1-\varepsilon})$. The programs may be nondeterministic, their transitions may depend on position and depth, and selection from the accepted support is unrestricted. Bounded fibers of integer projections modulo $b^k$ yield projection digit-entropy estimates. Fejer smoothing converts these into an explicit paired-block entropy bound, and recombining selected paths through hidden Markov kernels converts local entropy into a lower bound on support cardinality without counting path multiplicities. For selected size at least $cn$, support growth $n^{2-\delta}$ forces width $\Omega((k/(\log k)^2)^{\delta/2})$, where $k=\log_b n$. A sharper exceptional-position estimate shows that a linear-size no-three set can miss a fixed-length digit pattern at only boundedly many positions. It excludes every proper scalar digit subshift and forces positive planar density for width-one product supports admitting dense selection. We retain complementary geometric bounds from version 1: width $\Omega(\sqrt{k/\log k})$ for deterministic linear-size supports, relative-capacity decay at every regular-language growth rate, and a quadratic exact-depth state obstruction for direct no-three output. Self-contained proofs and exact rational and numerical controls accompany the results. The unrestricted target of more than $1.8n$ points remains open. All support bounds count distinct points, not accepting paths.
