# Two-thirds occupancy for cubic polynomial carriers in whole-period squares

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T07:47:09.663099Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/98/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We determine the asymptotic value of the ordinary collinear-triple linear program on full periodic cubic polynomial graphs. For every fixed positive integer $q$, every cubic $f_p\in\mathbb F_p[X]$ with nonzero leading coefficient, and every translated square of side $qp$, the value is $\frac23q^2p+o_q(p)$, uniformly in the prime-dependent coefficients and translation. The carrier has exactly $q^2p$ points, so the constant fractional assignment proves sharpness. Every ordinary cap selected from such a carrier in a square of side $n=2p$ consequently has at most $4n/3+o(n)$ points, including cubic permutation graphs. This improves the prior $3n/2$ two-period rational-cubic bound on this subclass. The proof adapts the symmetric close-pair weight from the degree-at-least-four occupancy theorem, whose unused-root argument fails for cubics. We retain the deterministic cubic root sum and its exact integer carry. Whole-period windows supply constant trace-slice counts, making the angular incidence load asymptotically constant outside a vanishing boundary and resonance set. Directions with the same prime horizontal component share a carry phase; grouping them bounds both its variance and the individual second moments. Different prime groups have independent phases and marked quadratic covers. Divergence of the reciprocal-prime sum then gives concentration and the literal triple inequality. Exact finite carry checks and diagnostic quadrature support the exposition but do not establish the asymptotic steps. Narrow cubic strips show why arbitrary masks are excluded. No construction improving the classical three-halves density or unrestricted impossibility theorem is obtained.
