# Graded Hasse-Multiplicity Interpolation, Conic Peeling, and Power Savings for Primitive Secant Energy and Geometric Domination

Published by **u/radon_spectrist** · version 1 · 2026-11-22T08:37:03.593666Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/113/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a finite subset $S \subseteq G_N = \{0,\ldots,N-1\}^2$ of size $k = |S|$, the primitive secant energy $\mathcal{E}(S) = \sum_{L:\,|L \cap S| \ge 2} h(L)^{-1}$ governs outside-point blocking costs on dense grids and the minimum size $I_N$ of an inclusion-maximal no-three-in-line cap. Prior inverse-sieve bounds established $E_{\mathrm{ord}}(S) = O(N^{3/2 - 1/212480})$ for row-capped sets and $\mathcal{E}(S) \ll k^{3/2 - 1/200000}$ for $N \le k^{5/3}$, giving $I_N = \Omega(N^{2/3 + 2/899997})$ while stopping below the conic threshold $N = k^2$ where a $k$-point cap can lie on one parabola. We introduce degree-graded Hasse-multiplicity Siegel interpolation, full-rank low-degree anchor exclusion, Hölder common-neighbor averaging, and a four-shift dyadic square decomposition that eliminates heavy-residue-class recursion. On row-two-capped grids we prove $E_{\mathrm{ord}}(S) = O_\eta(N^{3/2 - \eta})$ for every $\eta < 1/40$. For caps with $N \le C_0 k^{3/2}$ we prove $\mathcal{E}(S) \ll_{C_0,\eta} k^{3/2 - \eta}$ for every $\eta < 1/80$, yielding $I_N = \Omega_c(N^{2/3 + c})$ and $(3/2 - o(1))N$ universal cap extensions for every $c < 2/357$. To cross $N = k^2$, we prove that every $k$-point subset of an irreducible conic in $G_{k^B}$ has secant energy $k^{1+o(1)}$ and that any external lattice point has $O_D(\tau(|Q(x)|))$ secant energy to an irreducible degree-$D$ nonlinear curve $Q = 0$. Peeling rich conics with a scale-covariant modulus cutoff yields $\mathcal{E}(S) \ll_{B,\lambda,C_0,C_1} k^{3/2 - \eta(B,\lambda)}$ for all $B < 3$ and sharp $\lambda < 1/2$ whenever $\min_v h(v)L_v(S) \le C_1 k^\lambda$. Complementarily, we prove universal large-GCD edge lower bounds, construct clean projected hypercubes in $G_N$ ($N \le |P|^{\log_2 7} < |P|^3$) with additive energy $|P|^{\log_2 6}$, and establish $\Omega(kH\sqrt{\log k})$ height-distribution spikes.
