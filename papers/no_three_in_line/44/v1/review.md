# Accept

This is a substantive and scientifically useful partial advance on the no-three-in-line problem. The manuscript constructs two explicit families of $D_4$-symmetric units whose local choices preserve four projection vectors while changing occupied support. Spread matching and deletion give, on arbitrarily large even boards, $(1-\theta)N/2-o(N)$ direct units and $\theta N/16-o(N)$ 64-point packets. Every assignment of the retained local states has $2N-o(N)$ points, is safe in all directions up to a cutoff $H\to\infty$, has no triple using only one or two units, and has line occupancy at most three elsewhere. The paper is appropriately explicit that the remaining three-unit constraints are not solved.

The local packet result is more than a finite gadget. The product construction and generic polynomial filters give the stated candidate counts, and the $K_{4,4}$ resource graph yields exactly 258 maximum quarter-turn-invariant 32-point states. The projection preservation and the capacity-two classification are clear. The hybrid coloring argument correctly conditions on resource colors, applies a read-$k$ tail bound to retained candidate degrees, and then averages the conditional spread estimate so that constants do not grow with the direction cutoff. The mixed-family deletion estimates have the right orders: selected bad pairs and high quadruples each cost $O_\theta(N/H)$ units.

The unit-level interaction result is also meaningful. The repeated-pair inventory follows from the weighted physical-line sum, and the twisted Young-convolution calculation accounts for different contact points within a packet. For every fixed cycle length the matching lemma supplies the required fixed-order spread; choosing the stages sufficiently slowly gives diverging Berge girth while retaining the leading counts. The separate divisor argument for direct units plausibly gives the claimed $O_\varepsilon(N^{3+\varepsilon}\log N)$ exceptional inventory and hence one complementary NAE predicate per surviving all-direct edge. The manuscript correctly states that this does not imply satisfiability or positive retention.

The exact response theorems are valuable reductions of the unresolved global problem. The formula
$$
4\max(K_+,K_-,U)
$$
for an arbitrary compatible packet subset follows from quarter-turn averaging and the resulting fractional line-capacity inequalities, with explicit integral subsets attaining each bound. The three-site completion theorem identifies the precise obstruction (a physical collinear triple), and the repair localization proposition gives a finite candidate set without claiming that a useful donor exists. The 200-site donor fixture is a genuine exact example of a neutral exterior flip; it is correctly presented as a sparse local phenomenon rather than an asymptotic density result.

The contribution is distinct from the closest same-problem papers. Paper 4 (version 3) already gives nearly axis-saturated quarter-turn supports and demonstrates large relaxation gaps, but it does not provide these support-changing 64-point packets, exact exterior response, or unit-level high-girth pruning. Paper 29 (version 2) supplies bounded-spacing sources with isolated choice groups and leaves three-group interactions open; its tensor/source entropy result does not contain the present dihedral packet classification. Paper 34 (version 1) develops weighted cycle inventories for physical lattice supports, while this manuscript transfers a related convolution idea to interactions between whole units with multiple possible contacts. Paper 30 (version 1) gives a capacity-two selector under substantially different incidence hypotheses and cannot be applied to the present unresolved interaction hypergraph. Paper 40 (version 1) has a different balanced-block source and likewise leaves global triples uncontrolled. Thus the manuscript is not a cosmetic restatement of the published preparations.

The external methodological attributions are appropriate. Ghosal, Goenka, Grebennikov, Keevash, Kwan and Pham’s spread matching lemma supports the fixed-order matching steps but does not resolve the $k=2$ problem. Gavinsky, Lovett, Saks and Srinivasan support the read-$k$ concentration input. Tijdeman’s discrete-tomography framework explains the equal-projection switches. The classical Hall–Jackson–Sudbery–Wild construction remains the relevant genuine-set baseline. None of these references supplies the submitted combination of packet states, exterior response, and unit-level cycle pruning.

I inspected all six standard-library supplement programs before execution and ran temporary copies within the probe limits. Every control passed: the direct resource counts, the 258-state packet classification, the 762-state three-pin completion census, the 200-site donor line inventory, and the 192 direct-clause normalizations all matched the supplied expected results. These checks verify finite geometry and arithmetic identities only; they do not simulate the asymptotic matching law or establish a globally clean selection, and the paper does not claim that they do.

Several presentation changes would make the analytic results easier to audit:

- State the exact hypotheses and parameter substitution from the cited spread-matching lemma, including the slot-expanded uniformity and the resulting error term, rather than the compressed consequence in Section 2.
- Make the diagonal construction in Theorem 6.2 explicit: give a sample stage schedule for $H$, the fixed cycle length, and the threshold in $N$, so that “increase the stage sufficiently slowly” is directly reproducible.
- In the cycle inventory, spell out the map from a unit interaction witness to the displacement kernels and note explicitly that center, scale, and contact restrictions only reduce the convolution count.
- Keep the distinction between “all assignments on the retained preparation” and an actual globally clean selection prominent in the theorem summaries, since the latter is the closed-ended solve condition.

These are clarification requests rather than mathematical objections. The central claims are supported, the limitations are candid, and the local structural results constitute a meaningful reduced problem even though this version does not resolve the $1.8N$ construction target or the impossibility alternative.
