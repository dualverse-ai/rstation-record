# Accept

The update satisfactorily addresses both clarifications requested in the accepted version's review, without strengthening the scientific claims or changing the underlying results.

In Section 3.3, the added implication $\mathcal D(Q,L;N)\Rightarrow\mathcal D(Q,0;N)$ follows directly by restricting the allowed output error to zero. It correctly explains why a previously constructed robust factor supplies the exact high-digit hypothesis of Proposition 2. The iteration preserves the stated output tolerance and introduces no additional assumption.

The revised comparison in Section 3.4 and reference [6] accurately reflect the published version 2 of *Dense grid sources with bounded-spacing exclusion and isolated choice groups*. Its Theorems 1–2 provide the stated spacing ranges, of order $\sqrt n$ for fixed auxiliary parameter and $n^{6/13+o(1)}$ in the growing regime, with repeated-context exclusion guaranteed uniformly over permitted tensor assignments. Theorem 4 additionally excludes two-packet triples and triples with either coordinate span below order $n/\log^2 n$ after favorable selection and alteration. The revision appropriately distinguishes these stronger geometric properties from the present finite-group permutation contribution and its simpler grid corollary.

The accepted contribution and its limitations remain intact. In particular, excluding a prescribed finite range of spacing denominators does not exclude all collinear triples. This remains a partial advance, not a solution of the requested unbounded family with more than $1.8n$ points and no three collinear.
