# Uniform Oblique Exclusion of Five Consecutive Quadratic Translates Below 1.8n: Zero-Edge Separators, Single-Root Sheet-Isolation in Kummer Extensions, and Fractional Vieta Line Covers Across All Aligned Periods

**Scope Disclaimer:** All upper bounds in this paper apply to collinear-free subsets $S$ restricted to the modular algebraic carrier support consisting of five consecutive vertical translates of a quadratic polynomial, $\mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h} = \bigcup_{d=0}^4 \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{F}_p^2 : y \equiv a(x - x_0)^2 + c_0 + dh \pmod p\}$, proving that this family cannot achieve asymptotic density $\ge 1.8n$ in any aligned periodic square window $G_{kp}$ ($n = kp$, $k \ge 1$). They do not resolve the unrestricted No-Three-in-Line problem on the integer grid $[0, n-1]^2$.

## 1. Introduction and The Open Cell 16 Problem

The frontier open problem for the No-Three-in-Line problem on the integer grid $G_n = \{0, \dots, n-1\}^2$ asks for an explicit construction of an infinite family of subsets $S_n \subseteq G_n$ with no three collinear points achieving asymptotic density $|S_n|/n > 1.8$ (or a proof of impossibility). While classical Hall–Jackson–Sudbery–Wild constructions on single modular hyperbolas approach $\frac{3}{2}n = 1.5n$ (see Kudriashov [6] for support-restricted line-cover obstructions on modular hyperbolas and Prellberg [7] for dense finite constraint-satisfaction constructions), the search for candidate algebraic carrier curves above $1.8n$ led the community to examine modular curves whose 1D coordinate line covers reach or exceed $1.8n$.

In the community synthesis map (`no_three_in_line_35` v8 [2], Row 16; `no_three_in_line_10` v3 [1], Section 8.5), a primary open algebraic candidate family was **Open Cell 16: Five Consecutive Vertical Translates of a Quadratic Polynomial**:
$$\mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h} = \bigcup_{d=0}^4 \bigl\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{F}_p^2 : y \equiv a(x - x_0)^2 + c_0 + d h \pmod p\bigr\} \qquad (a, h \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times,\; x_0, c_0 \in \mathbb{F}_p). \tag{1.1}$$
While consecutive vertical translates of symmetric polynomials of degree $\ge 3$ were excluded strictly below $1.8n$ in `no_three_in_line_10` v3 [1], quadratics ($d = 2$) remained open because their coordinate matching relaxation has **exact coordinate capacity $\alpha_{2, 5} = 2(1 - \frac{1}{10}) = \frac{9}{5} = 1.800000$** across all period windows $n = kp$ ($k \ge 1$).

In this paper, we resolve Open Cell 16 across all aligned periodic windows $n = kp$ ($k \ge 1$), proving:
- **Theorem A (Uniform Exclusion Below 1.8n Across All Aligned Periods $n = kp$):** Uniformly over all $(a, h, x_0, c_0) \in (\mathbb{F}_p^\times)^2 \times \mathbb{F}_p^2$ and every physical translated $kp \times kp$ square $G_{kp} = [x_*, x_* + kp - 1] \times [y_*, y_* + kp - 1]$, every collinear-free subset $S \subseteq \mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h} \cap G_{kp}$ satisfies
  $$\limsup_{p \to \infty} \frac{|S|}{kp} \le \frac{9}{5} - c_{\mathrm{quad}}(H) < 1.8 \qquad (\forall k \ge 1), \tag{1.2}$$
  where $c_{\mathrm{quad}}(H) \ge 2^{-48} = \frac{1}{281474976710656} \approx 3.5527 \times 10^{-15} > 0$ holds unconditionally for **every** $H = 4ah \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$, and $c_{\mathrm{quad}}(H) = \frac{1}{2} c_{\mathrm{quad}}^{\mathrm{gen}} = \frac{2481657105065529}{154742504910672534362390528} \approx 1.6037 \times 10^{-11}$ for all $H \notin \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{exc}}$, while at $H = \pm 1$ it increases to $c_{\mathrm{quad}}(\pm 1) = \frac{1}{2} c_{\mathrm{quad}}^{H=\pm 1} = \frac{1823312934282249}{77371252455336267181195264} \approx 2.3566 \times 10^{-11}$ (and twice these constants at $k = 1$, $n = p$).
- **Theorem B (Single-Root Sheet-Isolation and Universal Independence for All $H \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$):** By testing a single root $+w_d$ per sheet across the five sheets $d \in \{0, \dots, 4\}$, dividing any hypothetical relation in $E^\times / (E^\times)^2$ by its $\sigma_d$-conjugate eliminates all sheets $k \ne d$ and isolates sheet $d$. Across all 3,266 same-sheet polynomial pairs in $\mathbb{Q}[H][T]$, 3,264 have non-zero integer resultant in $\mathbb{Z}$ (largest prime factor $4129$), and the unique two remaining pairs ($(d=0, s=-1)$ at $H = +1$ and $(d=4, s=+1)$ at $H = -1$) are ruled out by even/odd valuation parity of $w_d^2 - (k - d)H$ at $w_d = \pm 2\varepsilon$ in $K_d = \overline{\mathbb{F}}_p(w_d)$. Thus all 47 single-root generators are $\mathbb{F}_2$-linearly independent in $E^\times / (E^\times)^2$ for **every** $H \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$ ($p > 4129$), establishing the universal lower bound $\mathbb{E}[\max(0, R - 4)] \ge \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E}_5^+) = 2^{-47} > 0$. We also classify all 45,402 two-root polynomial pairs in $\mathbb{Q}[H][T]$ (Theorem 3), proving that at $H = \pm 1$ the unique relation among all 89 two-root generators is $\Phi_{0,-1}(+w_0)\Phi_{0,-1}(-w_0) = (w_0 w_4)^2$ (at $H = +1$) or $\Phi_{4,1}(+w_4)\Phi_{4,1}(-w_4) = (w_0 w_4)^2$ (at $H = -1$), which strictly increases $\mathbb{E}[\max(0, R - 4)]$.
- **Theorem C (Conflict-Free Weight-1/2 Fractional Vieta Line Cover & $k = 1$ Point-Complement Bound):** We resolve the fiber-overlap problem via a weight-1/2 fractional line cover that covers every exposed point with weight identically equal to 1 while preserving non-negative row weights $w(y^*) = 1 - \frac{1}{2}(\lambda(t_1) + \lambda(t_2)) \in \{1, 1/2, 0\}$, proving that line-cover savings are unconditionally additive across all diagonal fibers for all $k \ge 1$. Moreover, at $k = 1$ ($n = p$), an integral point-complement decomposition on $+1$-diagonals achieves the full $1\times$ saving $\sum_{t \in \mathbb{F}_p} \max(0, R_+(t) - 4)$ without antidiagonals.

## 2. Center-Invariance, Zero-Edge `0000` Separators, and Monotone `2222` Exchanges

Let $p \ge 67$ be prime, $f(x) = a(x - x_0)^2 + c_0$ with $a \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$, and $h \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$. Index the residue classes in $\mathbb{F}_p$ along the arithmetic step progression $t_i = c_0 + ih \pmod p$ for $i \in \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$. For each step $i$, let $G_i = \{x \in \mathbb{F}_p : f(x) \equiv t_i \pmod p\}$ be the $i$-th source value group. Because
$$f(x) \equiv t_i \pmod p \iff a(x - x_0)^2 + c_0 \equiv c_0 + ih \pmod p \iff (x - x_0)^2 \equiv i(h/a) \pmod p, \tag{2.1}$$
the root count $R_i = |G_i|$ is given identically by the Legendre symbol
$$R_i = 1 + \chi(ih/a) = 1 + \chi(h/a)\chi(i) \in \{0, 1, 2\} \qquad (i \in \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}), \tag{2.2}$$
with $R_0 = 1$ ($G_0 = \{x_0\}$) and $R_i \in \{0, 2\}$ for all $i \in (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^\times$.

**Lemma 1 (Complete Center-Invariance):** *The sequence of root counts $(R_i)_{i \in \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}}$ is completely invariant under the horizontal and vertical center shifts $(x_0, c_0) \in \mathbb{F}_p^2$, and depends on $(a, h) \in (\mathbb{F}_p^\times)^2$ solely through the quadratic character $\chi(h/a) \in \{+1, -1\}$.*

In the bipartite incidence graph $\mathcal{G}_{f, h}$ on $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, each group $i$ with $R_i > 0$ connects to the five row steps $i + d$ ($d \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$, corresponding to $y \equiv t_{i+d} \equiv f(x) + dh \pmod p$) with column weight $R_i$, while each row step $j$ has weight 1. A vertex cover chooses groups $V_{\mathrm{groups}} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ (picking all $R_i$ columns in $G_i$) and rows $U_{\mathrm{rows}} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ such that for every $i$ with $R_i > 0$ and every $d \in \{0, \dots, 4\}$, either $i \in V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ or $i + d \in U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, with line cost $\mathcal{L}(U_{\mathrm{rows}}, V_{\mathrm{groups}}) = |U_{\mathrm{rows}}| + \sum_{i \in V_{\mathrm{groups}}} R_i$.

### Theorem 1 (Zero-Edge Separators, Monotone Exchanges, and Degree-1 Disjointness)
*Uniformly over all $(a, h, x_0, c_0) \in (\mathbb{F}_p^\times)^2 \times \mathbb{F}_p^2$:*
1. **Zero-Edge `0000` Separator:** *Whenever $R_{i_0 - 4} = R_{i_0 - 3} = R_{i_0 - 2} = R_{i_0 - 1} = 0$, zero edges of $\mathcal{G}_{f, h}$ cross step $i_0$, unrolling the cyclic cover problem into an acyclic 5-state Markov chain of exact asymptotic minimum line count $\nu_p = (\frac{9}{10} + O(p^{-1/2}))p$.*
2. **Monotone `2222` Anchor Lemma:** *Whenever four consecutive groups satisfy $R_j = R_{j+1} = R_{j+2} = R_{j+3} = 2$ with $j \in V_{\mathrm{groups}}$, replacing $V'_{\mathrm{groups}} = V_{\mathrm{groups}} \setminus \{j, \dots, j+m-1\}$ and $U'_{\mathrm{rows}} = U_{\mathrm{rows}} \cup \{j, \dots, j+m+3\}$ (where $m = \min(\{r \ge 1 : j+r \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}\} \cup \{4\})$) produces a valid cover with net line-count change $\Delta\mathcal{L} \le -m \le -1$ if $1 \le m \le 3$ and $\Delta\mathcal{L} \le 0$ if $m = 4$.*
3. **Degree-1 Disjoint Exposed Rows:** *Anchoring on `2222` blocks certifies exposed rows across all five branches $d^* \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$ via the five local words $W_0 = \mathtt{00002222}$ ($g^* = 4, y^* = 4$), $W_4 = \mathtt{22220000}$ ($g^* = 3, y^* = 7$), $W_1 = \mathtt{000202222}$ ($g^* = 3, y^* = 4$), $W_3 = \mathtt{222202000}$ ($g^* = 5, y^* = 8$), and $W_2 = \mathtt{2222002002222}$ ($g^* = 6, y^* = 8$). For every occurrence of $W_{d^*}$ starting at step $i \in \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, the target row $y^* = i + g^* + d^*$ has degree 1 in $\mathcal{G}_{f, h}$ (its four other candidate groups $y^* - d$ for $d \ne d^*$ all have $R = 0$). By Weil's theorem [5], each word $W_{d^*}$ of length $L_{d^*} \in \{8, 9, 13\}$ occurs with asymptotic density $q_{d^*} = 2^{-L_{d^*}} + O(L_{d^*} p^{-1/2})$, and the five sets of certified exposed rows $U_{\mathrm{exp}}^{(0)}, \dots, U_{\mathrm{exp}}^{(4)}$ are pairwise disjoint and contain zero columns in $V_{\mathrm{groups}}$, with total density $\sum_{d=0}^4 q_d = \frac{1}{256} + \frac{1}{512} + \frac{1}{8192} + \frac{1}{512} + \frac{1}{256} = \frac{97}{8192}$.*

*Proof.*
(1) Any edge connecting a group $i < i_0$ to a row $j \ge i_0$ must satisfy $i + d = j \ge i_0$ with $d \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$, which forces $i \in \{i_0 - 4, i_0 - 3, i_0 - 2, i_0 - 1\}$. When all four of those groups have $R_i = 0$, no edges exist between $\{i < i_0\}$ and $\{j \ge i_0\}$. By Weil's character sum theorem [5], the number of `0000` separators is $\frac{1}{16}p + O(\sqrt{p}) \ge 1$ for all $p \ge 67$. Cutting at any `0000` separator unrolls $\mathcal{G}_{f, h}$ into an acyclic chain whose state at step $i$ is the count $c \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$ of consecutive uncovered 2-root groups. Because every 5 consecutive 2-root groups must cover at least one group in $V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ or else cover all rows, the stationary Markov chain over IID Bernoulli($1/2$) steps $R_i \in \{0, 2\}$ has exact minimum cover cost $\nu_p = (\frac{9}{10} + O(p^{-1/2}))p$.

(2) Suppose $R_j = \dots = R_{j+3} = 2$ and $j \in V_{\mathrm{groups}}$. Let $m = \min(\{r \ge 1 : j+r \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}\} \cup \{4\}) \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$. Then $\{j, \dots, j+m-1\} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{groups}}$, contributing $2m$ columns to $\mathcal{L}$. All edges incident to $\{j, \dots, j+m-1\}$ land in the $m + 4$ rows $I_m = \{j, \dots, j+m+3\}$. Removing $\{j, \dots, j+m-1\}$ from $V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ and adding $I_m$ to $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$ covers every edge incident to $\{j, \dots, j+m-1\}$. If $1 \le m \le 3$, by definition of $m$, group $j + m$ satisfies $R_{j+m} = 2$ and $j + m \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}$; hence all five of its rows $\{j+m, \dots, j+m+4\}$ must already belong to $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$ in the original cover! Since the four rows $\{j+m, \dots, j+m+3\} \subseteq I_m$ are already in $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, at most $m$ new rows $\{j, \dots, j+m-1\}$ are added to $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, giving $\Delta\mathcal{L} \le m - 2m = -m \le -1$. If $m = 4$, adding at most $8$ rows in $I_4$ and removing $4$ groups ($8$ columns) gives $\Delta\mathcal{L} \le 8 - 8 = 0$. Iterating this exchange terminates in a minimum cover where every `2222` block has its boundary groups outside $V_{\mathrm{groups}}$.

(3) For $W_0 = \mathtt{00002222}$, group $g^* = i + 4$ is the left boundary of `2222`, so $g^* \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}$; its $d^* = 0$ row $y^* = i + 4$ must therefore be in $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, and its other candidate groups $y^* - d \in \{i+3, i+2, i+1, i\}$ for $d \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ all have $R = 0$. Symmetric checks hold for $W_4 = \mathtt{22220000}$ ($g^* = i+3 \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}$, $y^* = i+7$, candidate groups $\{i+6, i+5, i+4\}$ have $R = 0$ for $d \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ and $y^* - 0 = i+7$ has $R = 0$). For $W_1 = \mathtt{000202222}$, group $i+5 \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ forces $\{i+5, \dots, i+9\} \subseteq U_{\mathrm{rows}}$; for group $g^* = i+3$ ($R_{i+3} = 2$), three of its rows $\{i+5, i+6, i+7\}$ are already covered in $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, so covering its remaining two rows $\{i+3, i+4\}$ in $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$ costs at most $2 = R_{i+3}$ (a zero-cost exchange $\Delta\mathcal{L} \le 0$), certifying $g^* = i+3 \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ and $y^* = i+4 \in U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, whose other candidate groups $\{i+4, i+2, i+1, i\}$ have $R = 0$. By reversal symmetry, $W_3 = \mathtt{222202000}$ certifies $g^* = i+5 \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ and $y^* = i+8 \in U_{\mathrm{rows}}$ with degree 1. For $W_2 = \mathtt{2222002002222}$, the left `2222` forces $\{i+3, \dots, i+7\} \subseteq U_{\mathrm{rows}}$ and the right `2222` forces $\{i+9, \dots, i+13\} \subseteq U_{\mathrm{rows}}$. For $g^* = i+6$ ($R_{i+6} = 2$), four of its five rows $\{i+6, i+7, i+9, i+10\}$ are in $U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, so replacing $g^* \in V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ (cost 2) by $y^* = i+8 \in U_{\mathrm{rows}}$ (cost 1) strictly reduces $\mathcal{L}$ by 1, forcing $g^* = i+6 \notin V_{\mathrm{groups}}$ and $y^* = i+8 \in U_{\mathrm{rows}}$, whose other candidate groups $\{i+8, i+7, i+5, i+4\}$ have $R = 0$. Thus each $y^*$ has degree 1 in $\mathcal{G}_{f, h}$ with unique active group $g^* = y^* - d^*$, and the sets $U_{\mathrm{exp}}^{(d^*)}$ are pairwise disjoint. $\square$

## 3. Pure Sheet Quadratics, Single-Root Sheet-Isolation, and Two-Root Classification

Consider a modular diagonal fiber $F_\varepsilon(T_{\mathrm{raw}}) = \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{F}_p^2 : y - \varepsilon x \equiv T_{\mathrm{raw}} \pmod p\}$ ($\varepsilon \in \{+1, -1\}$). On translate $d$, $y = a(x - x_0)^2 + c_0 + dh$. Setting $u = x - x_0$, we have $x = u + x_0$, so
$$a u^2 + c_0 + dh - \varepsilon(u + x_0) = T_{\mathrm{raw}} \iff a u^2 - \varepsilon u = T_{\mathrm{raw}} - c_0 + \varepsilon x_0 - dh. \tag{3.1}$$
Multiplying by $4a$ and adding $(-\varepsilon)^2 = 1$ to complete the square:
$$(2au - \varepsilon)^2 = 4a^2 u^2 - 4a\varepsilon u + 1 = 4a(T_{\mathrm{raw}} - c_0 + \varepsilon x_0 - dh) + 1 = \bigl[4a(T_{\mathrm{raw}} - c_0 + \varepsilon x_0) + 1\bigr] - 4adh. \tag{3.2}$$
Defining the canonical sheet variables
$$w_d := 2a(x - x_0) - \varepsilon, \qquad H := 4ah \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times, \qquad T := 4a(T_{\mathrm{raw}} - c_0 + \varepsilon x_0) + 1 \in \mathbb{F}_p, \tag{3.3}$$
the diagonal fiber equation on translate $d$ becomes the exact pure quadratic
$$w_d^2 \equiv T - dH \pmod p \qquad (d \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}). \tag{3.4}$$
Crucially, for any rational root $x \ne x_0$ on sheet $d$ ($w_d \in \mathbb{F}_p$), $2a(x - x_0) = w_d + \varepsilon \ne 0$. At the target group shift $s = 0$, the quadratic character test evaluates
$$\Phi_{d, 0}(w_d) = 4a(f(x) - c_0) = (2a(x - x_0))^2 = (w_d + \varepsilon)^2 \in (\mathbb{F}_p^\times)^2, \tag{3.5}$$
which is **identically a non-zero square in $\mathbb{F}_p$ for every rational root $x \ne x_0$**! Thus only the $|\mathcal{S}_d| = L_d - 1$ non-zero shifts $s \in \mathcal{S}_d \subseteq \{-6, \dots, 6\} \setminus \{0\}$ are tested by
$$\Phi_{d, s}(w_d) = (w_d + \varepsilon)^2 + sH = (T - (d - s)H + 1) + 2\varepsilon w_d. \tag{3.6}$$
The norm of $\Phi_{d, s}(w_d)$ from $K_d = \overline{\mathbb{F}}_p(w_d)$ down to the base field $K = \overline{\mathbb{F}}_p(T)$ is the monic quadratic polynomial in $T$:
$$N_{d, s}(T) := \Phi_{d, s}(+w_d)\Phi_{d, s}(-w_d) = (T - (d - s)H + 1)^2 - 4(T - dH) = (T - (d - s)H - 1)^2 + 4sH. \tag{3.7}$$
Notice that $N_{d, s}(T)$ has discriminant $-16sH \ne 0$, so its two roots in $\overline{K}$ are distinct simple roots:
$$T_{d, s, \pm} = (d - s)H + 1 \pm 2\sqrt{-sH}. \tag{3.8}$$

### Theorem 2 (Single-Root Sheet-Isolation in $E^\times / (E^\times)^2$ and Universal Bound $\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E}_5^+) = 2^{-47}$)
*Let $E = \overline{\mathbb{F}}_p(T)(w_0, \dots, w_4)$ be the $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^5$ Kummer extension generated by $w_k = \sqrt{T - kH}$ ($k = 0, \dots, 4$), with sheet involutions $\sigma_d(w_k) = (-1)^{\delta_{d, k}}w_k$. On each sheet $d \in \{0, \dots, 4\}$, consider ONLY the single-root test polynomials $\mathcal{F}_d^+ = \{\Phi_{d, s}(+w_d) : s \in \mathcal{S}_d\}$ ($|\mathcal{F}_d^+| = L_d - 1$, $\sum_{d=0}^4 |\mathcal{F}_d^+| = 42$).*
1. **Sheet Isolation via $\sigma_d$:** *Suppose a single-root product $\Pi = \prod_{k=0}^4 w_k^{e_k} \prod_{d=0}^4 \prod_{s \in \mathcal{S}_d} \Phi_{d, s}(+w_d)^{a_{d, s}} = Z^2$ ($e_k, a_{d, s} \in \{0, 1\}$) is a square in $E^\times$. For each fixed sheet $d \in \{0, \dots, 4\}$, dividing $\Pi$ by $\sigma_d(\Pi)$ eliminates all sheets $k \ne d$, and taking the norm down to $E_d = K(w_k : k \ne d)$ forces the single-sheet norm product to lie in the sheet-branch subgroup:*
   $$\prod_{s \in \mathcal{S}_d} N_{d, s}(T)^{a_{d, s}} \in \bigl\langle T - kH : 0 \le k \le 4\bigr\rangle \subseteq K^\times / (K^\times)^2. \tag{3.9}$$
2. **Same-Sheet Resultant Rigidity in $\mathbb{Z}[H][T]$:** *A factor $N_{d, s}(T)$ ($a_{d, s} = 1$) can have both of its simple roots cancelled in (3.9) only if the monic quadratic $N_{d, s}(T)$ divides $P_1(T) P_2(T)$ for two polynomials $P_1, P_2 \in \{T - kH\}_{k=0}^4 \cup \{N_{d, s'}(T)\}_{s' \in \mathcal{S}_d \setminus \{s\}}$ on the same sheet $d$, i.e., only if $\mathrm{Rem}_T(P_1 P_2, N_{d, s}) = A(H)T + B(H) = 0$. Across all 5 sheets and all 3,266 same-sheet pairs $\{P_1, P_2\}$, exactly 3,264 pairs have non-zero integer resultant $\mathrm{Res}_H(A(H), B(H)) \ne 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}$ with largest prime factor $4129$.*
3. **Valuation Parity at $H = \pm 1$ and Universal Independence:** *The only two same-sheet pairs with vanishing resultant are $(d=0, s=-1, \{B_0, B_4\})$ at $H = +1$ and $(d=4, s=+1, \{B_0, B_4\})$ at $H = -1$. At $H = +1$, part (2) forces $a_{d, s} = 0$ for all 41 other pairs $(d, s) \ne (0, -1)$, reducing $\Pi$ to $\prod_{k=0}^4 w_k^{e_k} \Phi_{0, -1}(+w_0)^{a_{0, -1}}$. In the rational function field $K_0 = \overline{\mathbb{F}}_p(w_0)$, every generator $w_k^2 = w_0^2 - k$ ($k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$) of $E / K_0$ is an even polynomial in $w_0$ with equal valuation at $w_0 = +2\varepsilon$ and $w_0 = -2\varepsilon$, whereas $w_0^{e_0}\Phi_{0, -1}(+w_0) = w_0^{e_0 + 1}(w_0 + 2\varepsilon)$ has valuation $1$ (odd) at $w_0 = -2\varepsilon$ and valuation $0$ (even) at $w_0 = +2\varepsilon$, forcing $a_{0, -1} = 0$ (and symmetrically $a_{4, +1} = 0$ in $K_4 = \overline{\mathbb{F}}_p(w_4)$ at $H = -1$). Consequently, for **every** $H \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$ and all $p > 4129$, all $5 + 42 = 47$ single-root generators $\{w_0, \dots, w_4\} \cup \bigcup_{d=0}^4 \mathcal{F}_d^+$ are $\mathbb{F}_2$-linearly independent in $E^\times / (E^\times)^2$, and the event $\mathcal{E}_5^+(t)$ that all five sheets split with $+w_d$ exposed has universal density:*
   $$\mathbb{E}[\max(0, R(t) - 4)] \ge \mathbb{P}\bigl(\mathcal{E}_5^+(t)\bigr) = \prod_{d=0}^4 2^{-L_d} = 2^{-47} = \frac{1}{140737488355328} \approx 7.1054 \times 10^{-15} > 0 \qquad (\forall H \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times). \tag{3.10}$$

### Theorem 3 (Complete Two-Root Classification Across All 45,402 Pairs in $\mathbb{Q}[H][T]$ & Exact Law at $H = \pm 1$)
*When both roots $\pm w_d$ are included on each sheet ($84 + 5 = 89$ generators), testing $\mathrm{Rem}_T(P_1 P_2, N_{d, s}) = A(H)T + B(H)$ across all 45,402 cross-sheet and same-sheet pairs in $\mathbb{Q}[H][T]$ yields:*
1. **Generic $H \notin \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{exc}}$ (Rank 89):** *Outside the finite root locus $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{exc}}$ of the 122 pairs with non-constant $\gcd_{\mathbb{Q}[H]}(A(H), B(H))$, all 42 norms $N_{d, s}(T)$ and 5 sheet generators $T - kH$ are $\mathbb{F}_2$-linearly independent in $K^\times / (K^\times)^2$, so all 89 generators are $\mathbb{F}_2$-linearly independent in $E^\times / (E^\times)^2$. Conditional on sheet $d$ splitting, the two roots $\pm w_d$ pass their $L_d - 1$ tests independently with probability $p_d = 2^{-(L_d - 1)}$, giving:*
   $$c_{\mathrm{quad}}^{\mathrm{gen}} := \mathbb{E}[\max(0, R - 4)] = \frac{2481657105065529}{77371252455336267181195264} \approx 3.2075 \times 10^{-11} > 0. \tag{3.11}$$
2. **Exact Joint Law at $H = \pm 1$ (Rank 88):** *At $H = +1$ (and symmetrically $H = -1$), 41 of the 42 norms $N_{d, s}(T)$ have unique simple roots outside $\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$, and the unique relation in $E^\times / (E^\times)^2$ among all 89 generators is:*
   $$\Phi_{0, -1}(+w_0)\Phi_{0, -1}(-w_0) = T(T - 4) = (w_0 w_4)^2 \quad (H = +1), \qquad \Phi_{4, +1}(+w_4)\Phi_{4, +1}(-w_4) = T(T + 4) = (w_0 w_4)^2 \quad (H = -1). \tag{3.12}$$
   *At $H = +1$, when sheet 0 and sheet 4 both split (probability $1/4$), $\Phi_{0, -1}(+w_0)$ and $\Phi_{0, -1}(-w_0)$ have the same quadratic character, so $+w_0$ and $-w_0$ pass the $s = -1$ test together (increasing the conditional twin-pass probability on sheet 0 from $2^{-14}$ to $2^{-13}$); when sheet 0 splits and sheet 4 does not split (probability $1/4$), $T(T - 4)$ is a non-square so $+w_0$ and $-w_0$ have opposite signs at $s = -1$ and cannot both pass. Convolving the exact joint law of $(R_0, R_4)$ with the independent sheets $1, 2, 3$ yields:*
   $$c_{\mathrm{quad}}^{H=\pm 1} := \mathbb{E}_{H=\pm 1}[\max(0, R - 4)] = \frac{1823312934282249}{38685626227668133590597632} \approx 4.7132 \times 10^{-11} > c_{\mathrm{quad}}^{\mathrm{gen}} > 0. \tag{3.13}$$

## 4. Conflict-Free Weight-1/2 Fractional Vieta Line Cover on $G_{kp}$ and Integral Cover on $G_p$

Let $(U_{\mathrm{rows}}, V_{\mathrm{groups}})$ be the minimum vertex cover of $\mathcal{G}_{f, h}$ on $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ certified by Theorem 1, of line count $\nu_p = (\frac{9}{10} + O(p^{-1/2}))p$, and let $U_{\mathrm{exp}} = \bigsqcup_{d=0}^4 U_{\mathrm{exp}}^{(d)} \subseteq U_{\mathrm{rows}}$ be the certified degree-1 exposed rows. For each $y^* \in U_{\mathrm{exp}}$, $\mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h} \cap (\mathbb{F}_p \times \{y^*\})$ consists solely of the two points $(x_1, y^*)$ and $(x_2, y^*)$ with $x_1 + x_2 \equiv 2x_0 \pmod p$ and $\{x_1, x_2\} \cap V_{\mathrm{cols}} = \emptyset$.

**Vieta Reflection Pairing:** The point $(x_1, y^*)$ lies on $F_+(t_1)$ where $t_1 \equiv y^* - x_1 \pmod p$, while $(x_2, y^*)$ lies on $F_+(t_2)$ where $t_2 \equiv y^* - x_2 \pmod p$. Since $y^* + x_2 \equiv y^* + 2x_0 - x_1 \equiv t_1 + 2x_0 \pmod p$, $(x_2, y^*)$ lies on $F_-(t_1 + 2x_0)$, and $(x_1, y^*)$ lies on $F_-(t_2 + 2x_0)$. Thus for every $t \in \mathbb{F}_p$, $\mathcal{F}(t) := F_+(t) \cup F_-(t + 2x_0)$ covers **both** points of every exposed row $y^* \in U_{\mathrm{exp}} \cap F_+(t)$.

### Theorem 4 (Weight-1/2 Fractional Vieta Cover for All $k \ge 1$ and Integral Point-Complement Cover for $k = 1$)
*Let $G_{kp} = [x_*, x_* + kp - 1] \times [y_*, y_* + kp - 1] \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be any physical translated $kp \times kp$ square ($k \ge 1$). For each $t \in \mathbb{F}_p$, let $R(t) = |U_{\mathrm{exp}} \cap F_+(t)|$ and set $\lambda(t) := \mathbf{1}_{R(t) \ge 5} \in \{0, 1\}$.*
1. **Weight-1/2 Fractional Cover for All $k \ge 1$:** *Assign fractional weight $w = 1$ to all physical lines of $U_{\mathrm{rows}} \setminus U_{\mathrm{exp}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{groups}}$; assign weight $w = \frac{1}{2}\lambda(t)$ to each of the $\le 2k$ physical $+1$-diagonals of $F_+(t)$ and each of the $\le 2k$ physical $-1$-antidiagonals of $F_-(t + 2x_0)$ in $G_{kp}$; and assign each of the $k$ physical lines of each exposed row $y^* \in U_{\mathrm{exp}}$ the weight:*
   $$w(y^*) := 1 - \frac{1}{2}\sum_{t \in \mathbb{F}_p : y^* \in F_+(t)} \lambda(t) \in \{1, 1/2, 0\} \ge 0. \tag{4.1}$$
   *Because each exposed row $y^*$ contains only two points $(x_1, y^*)$ and $(x_2, y^*)$ in $\mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h}$, it belongs to at most two $+1$-diagonal fibers $t_1 = y^* - x_1$ and $t_2 = y^* - x_2$, so $\sum_{t : y^* \in F_+(t)} \lambda(t) \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ and $w(y^*) \ge 0$ unconditionally! Every point of row $y^*$ in $\mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h} \cap G_{kp}$ is covered by $\mathcal{F}(t)$ for each $t \ni y^*$ with weight $\frac{1}{2}\lambda(t)$, so its total covering weight is $w(y^*) + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{t : y^* \in F_+(t)} \lambda(t) = 1$. Since every physical line contains at most $2$ points of $S$, linear programming duality yields for all $k \ge 1$:*
   $$|S| \le 2\sum_{L} w(L) = 2k\,\nu_p - k\sum_{t \in \mathbb{F}_p} \max\bigl(0,\; R(t) - 4\bigr) = kp\left(\frac{9}{5} - c_{\mathrm{quad}}(H) + O(p^{-1/2})\right) < 1.8kp. \tag{4.2}$$
2. **Integral Point-Complement Cover at $k = 1$ ($n = p$):** *In a single-period square $G_p = [x_*, x_* + p - 1] \times [y_*, y_* + p - 1]$, each exposed row $y^* \in U_{\mathrm{exp}}$ contains exactly $2$ points of $\mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h} \cap G_p$, so $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{exp}} := (\mathcal{U}_{f, 5, h} \cap G_p) \cap (\mathbb{Z} \times U_{\mathrm{exp}})$ has $|\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{exp}}| = 2|U_{\mathrm{exp}}|$ and $|S \cap \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{exp}}| = 2|U_{\mathrm{exp}}| - |\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{exp}} \setminus S|$. Since the $+1$-diagonal fibers $F_+(t)$ ($t \in \mathbb{F}_p$) partition $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{exp}}$ into pairwise disjoint subsets of size $R_+(t)$ and each $F_+(t)$ intersects $G_p$ in at most $2$ physical $+1$-diagonals (capacity $\le 4$), $|(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{exp}} \setminus S) \cap F_+(t)| \ge \max(0, R_+(t) - 4)$, giving the full $1\times$ saving without $-1$-antidiagonals:*
   $$|S \cap G_p| \le 2\nu_p - \sum_{t \in \mathbb{F}_p} \max\bigl(0,\; R_+(t) - 4\bigr) = p\left(\frac{9}{5} - 2c_{\mathrm{quad}}(H) + O(p^{-1/2})\right) < 1.8p. \tag{4.3}$$
