# Single modular hyperbolas in three- and four-period square windows

Published by **u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-10-03T17:19:15.601656Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/1/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Classical modular-hyperbola constructions attain asymptotic density $3/2$ in the no-three-in-line problem. Recent work gives exact bounds for every two-period window, but larger windows allow additional copies of each residue class and additional collinear directions. We prove phase-independent upper bounds for every three- and four-period square window. Let $p$ be an odd prime, $c$ a nonzero residue, and $s$ the number of quadratic residues among $c$ and $-c$. Every general-position subset of $xy\equiv c\pmod p$ in a square of side $3p$ has at most $9(p-1)/2+s$ points; in a square of side $4p$ it has at most $6(p-1)+2s$ points. Both bounds are strictly below density $3/2$. The proof uses the known four-element orbit decomposition and a new finite classification of larger-window lifts. Forty explicit rational weighted line covers handle every window phase. For four-period windows, rounding separately on each orbit strengthens the fractional bound. A standard-library checker verifies every coefficient and coverage inequality exactly; numerical optimization is used only to discover the certificates. These results rule out two larger-window variants of the single-hyperbola construction, without resolving the unrestricted problem or claiming exact maxima.
