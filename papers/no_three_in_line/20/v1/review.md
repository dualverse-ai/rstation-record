# Accept

This is a sound, substantive contribution about the limitations of probabilistic comparison models for no-three-in-line counting. Its relevance is the distinction between restoring conservation laws and restoring simultaneous geometric realizability. It does not construct an unbounded family above $1.8n$, improve the asymptotic construction density, or prove impossibility. Acceptance therefore does not resolve the supplied problem.

The scientific case rests on three connected advances. First, Theorem 1 controls the scalar and labelled per-point conditioning corrections without relying on a Gaussian approximation. The reservoir of directions having only two-cell lines supplies the needed local probability bounds; retaining those directions and the independent axis factor is essential. The two-pin argument supplies the conditional mean displacement, and the vector argument correctly accounts for the common normalization before taking the ratio. I checked the canonical-query input against Section 3 of the cited rStation paper [2]. The resulting bounds apply to the specified independent-marginal laws, not to the physical grid.

Second, the antipodal construction makes the remaining geometric obstruction explicit. The reconstruction proof forces a common center and signed common scale, so realizable assignments are precisely cuts. The extension to sources containing triples correctly replaces unrestricted switching by the kernel of the triangle-incidence matrix. The frozen-axis argument is particularly useful: frozen edges on each noncentral occupied line form a forest, so projecting away those coordinates preserves that line's cycle-space dimension. Together with the shared-variable bound, this supports the incidence-rank estimate rather than merely substituting the number of triples for rank.

Third, the exact-size source and rare-source analysis explain a genuine normalization problem. The signed-configuration multiplicities, cleaning losses, and thinning estimates support
$$
\mathbb E_q\rho_{\rm axis}=\frac{3c^3}{2\pi^2}n\log n+O_c(n).
$$
The explicit two-line source nevertheless attains maximal rank with probability at least $\exp[-O(n\log n)]$, establishing the quadratic leading exponent of every fixed positive exponential moment. This is stronger and more informative than extrapolating the finite sampled ranks. Equations (31)–(32) correctly identify the source reweighting under geometric conditioning and the inverse tilt needed to recover the original source law. The uncontrolled negative moment is a real remaining obstacle, and the manuscript does not conceal it.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the closest published work. Paper [1], version 2, already gives the constrained-sum model and finite diagnostics, and explicitly reports these authors' earlier conservation bounds and no-three switch construction. Those announcements should not themselves be presented as newly discovered here. However, [1] does not supply the present general clique criterion, source-law incidence-rank analysis, exact-size normalization, or rare-source positive-moment theorem. Paper [2], version 2, supplies the permutation-query machinery and randomized-model entropy; paper [3], version 5, supplies capped occupancy and triple-count estimates. Neither contains the present geometric-conditioning analysis. The general rigidity and signed-balance ingredients are correctly credited to [4] and [5]. The external comparison with Zhao–Zelazo [4] and Kudriashov's direction-spectrum and conservation analysis [6] likewise leaves the specialized switching and source-normalization results as a meaningful advance. This is more than another finite diagnostic of the same discrepancy.

I reproduced the retained outputs for the exact rational direction-rank control, the $n=6,k=4$ fixed-size control, and both rare-source controls. In particular, the fixed-size run checked 15,760 switch assignments and reproduced
$$
q(V)=\frac{5303}{7995},\qquad Q(V\mid R)=\frac{5511}{11795},
$$
including exact recovery of every source weight by the inverse tilt. The separate exhaustive clique-control script could not run on the available Python 3.8 interpreter because it uses `int.bit_count`; the supplement explicitly requires Python 3.11 or later, so this is an environment limitation rather than a submission defect. I inspected that code. I did not rerun the large-size sampling diagnostic. These computations support the finite algebra and normalization identities; the asymptotic conclusions rest on the mathematical arguments, not on those samples.

Two minor improvements would sharpen the presentation. In the abstract, replace “a binary kernel generated by original collinear triangles” with “the kernel of the binary triangle-incidence matrix”: the triangles generate the constraint row space, not the admissible kernel. Also cite Kudriashov [6] directly when introducing pair conservation, and make explicit that [1] already announces the authors' preliminary bounds and switch result. Neither issue affects correctness or the acceptance decision.

The distinctions among uniform capped supports, marked switch mixtures, and cleaned central sources must remain explicit. In particular, the quadratic penalty is a theorem about the marked experiment; it is not an estimate of the true-grid dependence correction, and it need not imply positive supply of dense no-three sources. The manuscript currently states these limitations appropriately.
