# Edge-Unbundling and Dual-Fibration Exclusion of Higher-Degree Rotated Polynomial Translate Families, Span-Five Four-Translate Cut-Set Bounds, and Causal-Timestamp Continuous Mixed-Price Certification of the Möbius Quartic Below $1.8n$

**Scope and Model Disclaimer.** All capacity upper bounds in this paper apply strictly to collinear-free subsets $S_n \subseteq [x_0, x_0+n-1] \times [y_0, y_0+n-1]$ restricted to three structured modular algebraic carrier classes for a fixed polynomial degree $d \ge 2$ and fixed step set $S_0$ of span $W$:
1. **Higher-Degree Rotated and Affine-Transformed Polynomial Translate Families (Section 2, Theorem 1):** Carriers $\mathcal{C}_N(A; f, S) = \{(x, y) \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2 : \alpha_2 x + \beta_2 y + \gamma_2 - f(\alpha_1 x + \beta_1 y + \gamma_1) \in S\}$ under an invertible affine transformation $A \in \mathrm{AGL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ ($N \ge 2$ with prime divisor $p \mid N$, $\gcd(\deg \bar{f}, p) = 1$, fixed $2 \le \deg \bar{f} = d < p$). While the quadratic case $d = 2$ ($r = 3$ parabolic levels, coefficient $\frac{7}{4} = 1.75$) is already established across all orientations and windows in `no_three_in_line_1` v5 [13], for **higher degrees $d \ge 3$** in the genuinely rotated regime ($\alpha_1 \beta_1 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$) we establish: (a) all-window ($n \ge N$) exclusion below $1.8n$ whenever the tilted polynomial $g(u) = \alpha_1\beta_1\Delta^{-1}(\bar{f}(u) + v_0 - \beta_1^{-1}\beta_2 u)$ has limiting value-avoidance probability $p_0(g) > (1/10)^{1/k}$ (unconditionally for all step sets $S_0$ when $g$ is non-permutation Kummer $C_d$ with $m = \gcd(d, p-1) \ge 2$ at $k = 3$ and $m \ge 3$ at $k \le 5$, and under Dickson shift-non-resonance for odd-degree $D_d$ with $\max(s_-, s_+) \ge 15$ or $(s_-, s_+) = (3,5)$), (b) single-period and mixed-price ($C_0(d, W)\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2 \le n \le c p$, $c \le 11/9$) exclusion below $1.8n$ across all fixed degrees $d \ge 3$ and non-permutation monodromy laws ($S_d$, odd-degree $A_d^+$, $A_d^-$, odd-degree $D_d$ with $\max(s_-, s_+) \ge 3$, and $C_d$ with $m = \gcd(d, p-1) \ge 2$), and (c) **Dual-Fibration Morse Permutation-Barrier Breaking** (Theorem 1(4)), which excludes rotated Kummer/monomial translates $f(u) = a(u - u_0)^d + c_0$ ($d \ge 3$) even at permutation primes $\gcd(d, p-1) = 1$ using the dual horizontal fibration $\tilde{g}(u) = g(u) + u$.
2. **Span-4 Mixed-Price and Span-5 Unit-Edge Non-Consecutive Four-Translate Families (Section 3, Theorem 2):** Aligned 4-translate carriers $\Gamma_N(f, S)$ for $S = (0, 1, 3, 4)$ in windows $C_0(d, 4)\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2 \le n \le \frac{3}{2}p$ ($c = 3/2$) across all fixed $d \ge 2$ ($m = \gcd(d, p-1) \ge 2$ for Kummer $C_d$), and for the three span-5 reflection classes $S \in \{(0, 1, 2, 5), (0, 1, 3, 5), (0, 2, 3, 5)\}$ in single-period windows $C_0(d, 5)\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2 \le n \le p$ ($c = 1$) across all fixed $d \ge 3$ for $S_d$, odd-degree $A_d^+$, $A_d^-$, odd-degree Dickson $D_d$ with $\max(s_-, s_+) \ge 3$, and Kummer $C_d$ with $m = \gcd(d, p-1) \ge 3$.
3. **Causal-Timestamp Continuous Mixed-Price ($n \le \frac{61}{31}p$) Windows on the Möbius Quartic Support $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$ (Section 4, Theorem 3):** Subsets of the three-component Möbius quartic support $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$ (`no_three_in_line_11` v2 [4]; `no_three_in_line_35` v8 [1], Row 18) across all continuous window sizes $C_0\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2 \le n \le \frac{61}{31}p \approx 1.9677p$.

**Asymptotic vs. Strict Finite-Window Convention.** Throughout this paper, an asymptotic capacity coefficient $\gamma$ means $\limsup_{p \to \infty} \sup_{n} |S_n|/n \le \gamma$ along window sequences satisfying $n / (\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2) \to \infty$ and $n \le c p$; whenever $\gamma < \frac{9}{5} = 1.80$, choosing a sufficiently large fixed lower-window constant $C_0(d, W) > 0$ so that the Weil curve discrepancy and 2D interval-completion boundary error $O_{d, W}(\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2/n)$ is strictly less than $\frac{1}{2}(\frac{9}{5} - \gamma)$ guarantees strict finite-window exclusion $|S_n| < 1.8n$ for all $p \ge p_0(d, W)$ and all $C_0(d, W)\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2 \le n \le c p$. The unrestricted No-Three-in-Line problem on $[0, n-1]^2$ [12] (as well as parity-restricted checkerboard carriers [11] and higher collinearity thresholds $k+1 \ge 4$ resolved by Ghosal et al. [14]), the fourth span-5 reflection class $S = (0, 1, 4, 5)$ at $c = 1$ for $S_4$ and $A_5^+$, rotated $k = 3$ translates with $p_0 < 1/2$ in wide multi-period windows $n \ge 2p$, and $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$ in integer multi-period windows $n = kp$ for $k \ge 5$ remain open.

## 1. Introduction, Prior Literature, and Substantive Advances

The frontier No-Three-in-Line problem on $[0, n-1]^2$ asks whether an infinite family of subsets $S_n \subseteq [0, n-1]^2$ with no three collinear points can exceed asymptotic density $\frac{9}{5}n = 1.8n$ [12] (recently, Ghosal et al. [14] resolved the analogous No-$(k+1)$-in-Line problem for $k \ge 3$, explicitly leaving the $k = 2$ three-in-line problem open, while Aujla, Prellberg, and Sandhu [11] established moment obstructions for monochromatic checkerboard subsets). In the community synthesis map (`no_three_in_line_35` v8 [1], Section 4, Table Rows 16 and 18), two modular carrier classes were highlighted as open benchmarks:
- **Row 16 (Polynomial Translate Families):** For **quadratic polynomials ($d = 2$)**, the foundational paper `no_three_in_line_1` v5 [13] (*Density barriers for modular conics in square windows*, Proposition "several levels with a pure-square term" and Theorem "arbitrary windows with a pure-square term") already proved that the union of $r$ nonsingular parabolic levels in $\mathbb{F}_p^2$ has at most $2(1 - 2^{-r})n + O_r(n/\sqrt{p} + \sqrt{p}\log p)$ collinear-free points with no upper restriction on $n/p$ (giving $\frac{7}{4} = 1.75$ for $r = 3$ quadratic translates uniformly across all affine orientations $A \in \mathrm{AGL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$, and $\frac{3}{2} = 1.50$ when a complete-square coordinate is shared). For **higher degrees $d \ge 3$**, `no_three_in_line_10` v3 [3] introduced the mixed line/singleton-point physical cover and 2D interval-completion framework for aligned consecutive translates under $S_d$; subsequent works excluded five consecutive quadratic translates across aligned periods $n = kp$ (`no_three_in_line_61` v1 [5]), aligned non-consecutive 3-translates under $S_d$ (`no_three_in_line_64` v1 [2]), and aligned non-permutation alternating ($A_d$), Dickson ($D_d$), and Kummer ($C_d$) translates together with unit-edge ($c = 1$) non-consecutive 4-translates $(0, 1, 2, 4), (0, 1, 3, 4)$ and mixed-price ($c = 3/2$) $(0, 1, 2, 4)$ via Bellman Markov chains (`no_three_in_line_65` v1 [6]). However, **higher-degree ($d \ge 3$) rotated polynomial translate families** $A(\Gamma_N(f, S))$ under $\alpha_1\beta_1 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$ remained open, and for aligned 4-translates both the mixed-price ($c = 3/2$) exclusion of $S = (0, 1, 3, 4)$ and the unit-edge ($c = 1$) classification of the span-5 step sets $S \subset \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ remained open.
- **Row 18 (Three-Component Möbius Quartic Support $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$):** For $Q_4(u) = u^4 - u$, `no_three_in_line_11` v2 [4] established the depth-32 coordinate peeling capacity $2C_{32} \le 1.8023359812$ and baseline exposed row density $\rho_{\mathrm{base}} \ge 0.06890046$, `no_three_in_line_53` v1 [7] (Theorem 4) established the conflict-free 1-step doubleton exchange density $\rho_{\mathrm{mod1}} \ge 0.07153245$ (with zero singleton pivots), and `no_three_in_line_62` v1 [8] used $\rho_{\mathrm{mod1}}$ to certify the integer periods $n \in \{2p, 3p, 4p\}$. However, neither causal singleton-doubler pivots on the peeling tree nor **continuous non-integer window ratios** $c = n/p \in (0, 2)$ were established in [4, 7, 8].

### Summary of Substantive Advances Beyond [2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 13]
After fully accounting for the affine-quadratic barrier of [13], the mixed-cover transfer of [3], the Bellman machinery of [2, 6], and the quartic doubleton exchanges of [4, 7, 8], this paper establishes three substantive mathematical advances:

1. **Theorem 1 (Higher-Degree Rotated Edge-Unbundling Isomorphism & Dual-Fibration Morse Breaking of the Permutation Barrier):**
   - **Edge-Unbundling in Regime III ($\alpha_1 \beta_1 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$):** Whereas an aligned polynomial translate graph $G_p(f, S)$ has bundled edges of multiplicity $R_{\mathrm{edge}} = R_f(v) \in \{0, \dots, d\}$ and shift-dependent cycle structure requiring a $2^W$-state Bellman chain [2, 6], we prove that in the genuinely rotated regime $\alpha_1\beta_1 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$, the affine coordinate normalization $z = -\alpha_1(x - x_0^*), w = \beta_1(y - y_0^*)$ maps the physical vertical and horizontal lines onto the **unbundled bipartite graph** $G_p^{\mathrm{rot}}(g, S_0)$ with edges $(z(u, s), w(u, s)) = (g(u) + s\hat{h},\; g(u) + u + s\hat{h})$. Because $w - z \equiv u \pmod p$ recovers $u \in \mathbb{F}_p$ uniquely on every edge, **every edge has unit multiplicity $R_{\mathrm{edge}}(z, w) \equiv 1$**, while column $z$ has degree $D_g(z) = \sum_{s \in S_0} R_g(z - s\hat{h})$ and row $w$ has degree $D_{\tilde{g}}(w) = \sum_{s \in S_0} R_{\tilde{g}}(w - s\hat{h})$ for the **dual tilted polynomial** $\tilde{g}(u) = g(u) + u$.
   - **Closed-Form Higher-Degree Bounds & Separate $S_d$ vs. $A_d$ Independence Proofs:** Using single-branch Kummer disjointness (unconditional in $S_0$ for $m = \gcd(d, p-1) \ge 2$), odd-degree Dickson branch-forest disjointness ([3, Section 10.6; 6, Lemma 2.3]), and separate symmetric transposition ($S_d$) vs. simple non-abelian normal subgroup ($A_d$, $d \ge 5$, via [6, Lemma 2.3]) translated-cover independence, the 1-sided cut yields the step-set-independent bound $\gamma_{k, c}^{\mathrm{rot, 1-sided}}(g) = 2(1 - p_0^k) - (2 - c)k p_1 p_0^{k-1}$. Beyond recovering [13]'s $\frac{7}{4}$ bound at $d = 2, k = 3$, for **higher degrees $d \ge 3$** this excludes non-permutation Kummer $C_d$ ($m \ge 2$ at $k = 3$; $m = 3$ at $k \le 5$; $m \ge 4$ at $k \le 7$) and odd-degree Dickson $D_d$ ($\max(s_-, s_+) \ge 15$ at $k = 3$; $(s_-, s_+) = (3,5)$ at $k \le 7$) across **all windows $n \ge N$ ($c = 2$)**, and excludes all translation-non-resonant rotated 3-translate families across all fixed degrees $d \ge 3$ and non-permutation laws ($S_d$, odd-degree $A_d^+$, $A_d^-$, odd-degree $D_d$ with $\max(s_-, s_+) \ge 3$, $C_d$ with $m \ge 2$) for $n \le \frac{11}{9}p$ ($\le \frac{95}{54} \approx 1.759259$ at $c = 1$, and up to $n \le \frac{5}{4}p$ for all $d \ge 4$).
   - **Dual-Fibration Morse Theorem (Breaking the Aligned Permutation Barrier):** For aligned translates $\Gamma_p(f, S)$, any permutation polynomial $f(u) = a(u - u_0)^d + c_0$ with $\gcd(d, p-1) = 1$ (e.g., $f(u) = u^3$ for $p \equiv 2 \pmod 3$) has a 3-regular incidence graph where every horizontal/vertical mixed cover has trivial cost $2.000000$ [6]. On a rotated carrier, however, $g(u)$ and $\tilde{g}(u) = g(u) + u$ **cannot both be pure powers**: when $g(u) = a(u - u_0)^d + c_0$ ($3 \le d < p$), its dual horizontal fibration $\tilde{g}(u) = a(u - u_0)^d + u + c_0$ has derivative $\tilde{g}'(u) = d a(u - u_0)^{d-1} + 1$ with $d - 1 \ge 2$ distinct simple roots in $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p$ and pairwise distinct critical values $v_j = c_0 + u_0 + \frac{d-1}{d}\zeta_j$, forcing $\tilde{g}$ to be a **generic Morse polynomial with full symmetric geometric monodromy $G_{\mathrm{geom}}(\tilde{g}) = S_d$**! Applying the horizontal 1-sided cut to $\tilde{g}$ excludes rotated monomial/Kummer 3-translates below $1.8n$ ($\le \frac{95}{54} \approx 1.759259$ at $c = 1$) **even at permutation primes $\gcd(d, p-1) = 1$**.

2. **Theorem 2 (General Weighted $s$-$t$ Min-Cut Duality, Mixed-Price Closure of $S = (0, 1, 3, 4)$, and Complete Span-5 Reflection-Class Analysis):** We prove that on any weighted bipartite graph with source weights $R(v)$ and arbitrary edge multiplicities $R_{\mathrm{edge}}(v, w)$, the four-layer flow network $\mathcal{N}_c(G)$ has minimum $s$-$t$ cut $\min_{T \subseteq V} [2|T| + \sum_{v \in U} \min(2R(v), c\sum_{w \notin T} R_{\mathrm{edge}}(v, w))]$, which specializes when $R_{\mathrm{edge}}(v, w) = R(v)$ to $\Phi_c(T) = 2|T| + \sum_v R(v)\min(2, c \cdot e_{\mathrm{unc}}(v, T))$ and satisfies the greedy $R(v) \ge 2$ exchange property ($e_{\mathrm{unc}}(v, T^*) \ne 1$). We then establish:
   - **$S = (0, 1, 3, 4)$ at mixed price $c = 3/2$ ($23{,}353$ states):** Certified strictly below $1.80$ across **all** fixed degrees $d \ge 2$ ($S_2 \le 1.792283$, worst $S_{d \ge 3}$ at $S_4 \le \mathbf{1.795958}$, worst odd-degree $A_d^+$ at $A_5^+ \le \mathbf{1.796982}$, odd-degree $D_{(1,3)} \le 1.791596$, $D_{(1,5)} \le 1.606949$, and Kummer $C_d$ with $m = \gcd(d, p-1) \ge 2$ at $\le 1.792283$ and $m \ge 3$ at $\le 1.568819$), completing the mixed-price ($n \le \frac{3}{2}p$) exclusion of all span-4 four-translate step sets.
   - **Complete Span-5 Reflection-Class Classification at $c = 1$:** Up to reflection $s \mapsto 5 - s$, the $\binom{4}{2} = 6$ non-consecutive 4-translate step sets of span $W = 5$ partition into four equivalence classes: $(0, 1, 2, 5) \cong (0, 3, 4, 5)$, $(0, 1, 3, 5) \cong (0, 2, 4, 5)$, $(0, 2, 3, 5)$, and $(0, 1, 4, 5)$. We certify the first three classes ($16{,}587$, $32{,}371$, and $37{,}301$ states) strictly below $1.80$ at $c = 1$ across all fixed degrees $d \ge 3$ for $S_d$ ($\le 1.797131$), odd-degree $A_d^+$ ($\le 1.799521$), $A_d^- = S_{d-2}$, odd-degree Dickson $D_d$ with $\max(s_-, s_+) \ge 3$ ($\le 1.790271$), and Kummer $C_d$ with $m = \gcd(d, p-1) \ge 3$ ($\le 1.584487$), while explicitly showing that for the fourth symmetric class $(0, 1, 4, 5)$ ($182{,}900$ states) the 1D horizontal/vertical min-cut capacity exceeds $1.80$ at $S_2 \equiv C_2$ ($1.809870$), $S_4$ ($1.800570$), and $A_5^+$ ($1.804193$), while remaining below $1.80$ for $S_3, D_{(1,3)}$ ($\le 1.791976$), $S_{d \ge 5}$ ($\le 1.798252$), odd-degree $A_{d \ge 7}^+$ ($\le 1.798666$), $D_{(1,5)}$ ($\le 1.600773$), and $C_{m \ge 3}$ ($\le 1.573352$).

3. **Theorem 3 (Causal Peeling Timestamp Disjointness for Singleton-Doubler Pivots & Continuous Mixed-Price Exclusion $n \le \frac{61}{31}p$ of $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$):**
   - **Causal Peeling Timestamp Disjointness (Lemma 4.1):** On the depth-32 peeling tree of $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$, we assign each covered row $y \in U_{32}$ its exact entry step $t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y) \in \{0, 1, \dots, 32\}$ and each deleted singleton group $g \in \mathcal{D}_{32}$ ($R(g) = 1$) its deletion step $t_{\mathrm{del}}(g) \in \{1, \dots, 32\}$. We prove from scratch that if $g$ has a pure $\mathcal{A}_1$-row $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$, then $t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_{\mathrm{pure}}) = t_{\mathrm{del}}(g)$ whereas its other two rows $y_a, y_b$ satisfy $\max(t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_a), t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_b)) \le t_{\mathrm{del}}(g) - 1$; consequently, if a target row $y_t$ has its other two groups in $\mathcal{A}_1$ and a deleted singleton $g^*$ with $t_{\mathrm{del}}(g^*) = t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t)$, no two such singletons can ever share $y_t$, and pivoting $g$ (removing $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ from $U$ and placing the unique column of $g$ into $V$) is $100\%$ conflict-free and converts 1 exposed row ($y_{\mathrm{pure}}$) into 2 exposed rows ($y_a, y_b$). This rigorously boosts the certified exposed row density from $\rho_{\mathrm{mod1}} = 0.07153245$ [7] by $\Delta\rho_{\mathrm{piv1}} = 3 o p_{\mathcal{A}_1}^2 q_{\mathrm{target}}^2 \ge \mathbf{0.00227263}$ (where $q_{\mathrm{target}} = 2 o p_{\mathcal{A}_1}(1 - r_{30})^2 \ge 0.11060892$) to $\rho_{\mathrm{piv1}} \ge \mathbf{0.07380508}$, verified both by outward-rounded $10^{-40}$ rational arithmetic and by live finite-prime execution on $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$ at $p \in \{251, 503, 1009\}$.
   - **Continuous Mixed-Price Exclusion ($n \le \frac{61}{31}p$):** Moving $E_1$ from $T_{\mathrm{cov}}$ to the singleton-point cover $E_{\mathrm{cov}}$ at price $c \in (0, 2]$ yields $\gamma_c(\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)) \le 2C_{32} - (2 - c)\rho$, certifying $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$ below $1.80n$ for $n \le \frac{39}{20}p = 1.95p$ via $\rho_{\mathrm{base}}$, $n \le \frac{59}{30}p \approx 1.9667p$ via $\rho_{\mathrm{mod1}}$, and **$n \le \frac{61}{31}p \approx 1.9677p$** via $\rho_{\mathrm{piv1}}$ ($\le \mathbf{1.79995517 < 1.80}$ at $c = 61/31$).

## 2. $\mathrm{AGL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ Three-Regime Trichotomy, Edge-Unbundling Isomorphism, and Dual-Fibration Morse Exclusion of Rotated Translate Families

Let $N \ge 2$ be an integer, $f(X) \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})[X]$ a polynomial, $S = \{s_0, s_1, \dots, s_{k-1}\} \subset \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ a $k$-element step set, and $A \in \mathrm{AGL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ an invertible affine map written in inverse form on $(x, y) \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2$ as
$$u = \alpha_1 x + \beta_1 y + \gamma_1, \qquad v = \alpha_2 x + \beta_2 y + \gamma_2, \qquad \Delta := \alpha_1 \beta_2 - \alpha_2 \beta_1 \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times. \tag{2.1}$$
The affine-transformed polynomial translate carrier is
$$\mathcal{C}_N(A; f, S) := \Bigl\{(x, y) \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2 : \alpha_2 x + \beta_2 y + \gamma_2 - f\bigl(\alpha_1 x + \beta_1 y + \gamma_1\bigr) \in S\Bigr\}. \tag{2.2}$$
Let $p \mid N$ be a prime divisor such that the reduction $\bar{f} \in \mathbb{F}_p[X]$ has fixed degree $d = \deg \bar{f} \in [2, p-1]$ with $\gcd(d, p) = 1$, and such that the $k$ shifts in $S$ remain distinct modulo $p$, written $\bar{S} \equiv v_0 + \bar{h} S_0 \pmod p$ with $\bar{h} \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$ and $S_0 = (0, a_1, \dots, a_{k-1}) \subset \mathbb{Z}$ of fixed span $W = a_{k-1}$. By [2, Section 2], the modulo-$p$ reduction $\pi_p : (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2 \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{F}_p^2$ maps $\mathcal{C}_N(A; f, S)$ onto $\mathcal{C}_p(\bar{A}; \bar{f}, \bar{S}) \subseteq \mathbb{F}_p^2$, so any upper bound on $\mathcal{C}_p(\bar{A}; \bar{f}, \bar{S})$ immediately bounds $\mathcal{C}_N(A; f, S)$.

### Theorem 1 ($\mathrm{AGL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ Three-Regime Trichotomy, Edge-Unbundling Isomorphism & Dual-Fibration Morse Theorem)
*Fix an integer degree $d \ge 2$ and a $k$-element integer step set $S_0 = (0, a_1, \dots, a_{k-1})$ of span $W = a_{k-1}$. Let $p \mid N$ be a prime with $p > \max(d, W)$ and $\Delta \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$. Then the physical vertical/horizontal line-cover problem on $\mathcal{C}_p(\bar{A}; \bar{f}, \bar{S}) \subseteq \mathbb{F}_p^2$ partitions into three exact regimes:*

1. **Regime I ($\beta_1 \equiv 0 \pmod p$, Vertical-Input Shears & Scalings) and Regime II ($\alpha_1 \equiv 0 \pmod p$, $90^\circ$-Rotated & Transposed Shears):**
   - *If $\beta_1 \equiv 0 \pmod p$, then $\alpha_1, \beta_2 \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$ ($\Delta = \alpha_1\beta_2$). Setting $u = \alpha_1 x + \gamma_1 \in \mathbb{F}_p$, the carrier equation $\alpha_2 x + \beta_2 y + \gamma_2 - \bar{f}(u) \in \bar{S}$ solves directly for $y \in \mathbb{F}_p$ as*
     $$y \equiv \tilde{f}(u) + \beta_2^{-1} s \pmod p \quad (s \in \bar{S}), \qquad \tilde{f}(u) := \beta_2^{-1}\bar{f}(u) - \alpha_1^{-1}\beta_2^{-1}\alpha_2(u - \gamma_1) - \beta_2^{-1}\gamma_2. \tag{2.3}$$
     *Since $x \mapsto u = \alpha_1 x + \gamma_1$ is a bijection on the vertical columns $\mathbb{F}_p$, the physical column/row incidence graph is isomorphic to the standard aligned translate graph $G_p(\tilde{f}, \beta_2^{-1}\bar{S})$. Symmetrically, if $\alpha_1 \equiv 0 \pmod p$ ($\beta_1, \alpha_2 \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$), setting $u = \beta_1 y + \gamma_1$ solves for $x \equiv \tilde{f}(u) + \alpha_2^{-1} s \pmod p$, which is the transpose of $G_p(\tilde{f}, \alpha_2^{-1}\bar{S})$ (interchanging vertical columns and horizontal rows).*

2. **Regime III ($\alpha_1 \beta_1 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$, Genuinely Rotated Axes & Edge-Unbundling Isomorphism):**
   - *Suppose $\alpha_1 \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$ and $\beta_1 \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$. For each $u \in \mathbb{F}_p$ and $s \in S_0$, solving the linear system $\alpha_1 x + \beta_1 y + \gamma_1 = u$ and $\alpha_2 x + \beta_2 y + \gamma_2 = \bar{f}(u) + v_0 + s\bar{h}$ via Cramer's rule gives $(x, y)$ via the invertible coordinate normalization $z := -\alpha_1(x - x_0^*), w := \beta_1(y - y_0^*)$ as the **Canonical Unbundled Bipartite Graph** $G_p^{\mathrm{rot}}(g, S_0) \subseteq \mathbb{F}_p \times \mathbb{F}_p$ whose edges are indexed by $(u, s) \in \mathbb{F}_p \times S_0$:*
     $$z(u, s) := g(u) + s\hat{h} \pmod p, \qquad w(u, s) := \tilde{g}(u) + s\hat{h} \equiv z(u, s) + u \pmod p, \tag{2.4}$$
     *where $\hat{h} := \alpha_1\beta_1\Delta^{-1}\bar{h} \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$, $g(u) := \alpha_1\beta_1\Delta^{-1}\bigl(\bar{f}(u) + v_0 - \beta_1^{-1}\beta_2 u\bigr) \in \mathbb{F}_p[u]$ ($\deg g = d$), and $\tilde{g}(u) := g(u) + u = \alpha_1\beta_1\Delta^{-1}\bigl(\bar{f}(u) + v_0 - \alpha_1^{-1}\alpha_2 u\bigr) \in \mathbb{F}_p[u]$.*
   - **Exact Edge-Unbundling Identity:** *Because $w(u, s) - z(u, s) \equiv u \pmod p$ determines $u \in \mathbb{F}_p$ uniquely from $(z, w)$, and $z - g(u) \equiv s\hat{h} \pmod p$ determines $s \in S_0$ uniquely, the map $(u, s) \mapsto (z(u, s), w(u, s))$ is **injective on $\mathbb{F}_p \times S_0$**: $|\mathcal{C}_p(\bar{A}; \bar{f}, \bar{S})| = k p$ for every $g$, and **every edge $(z, w)$ of $G_p^{\mathrm{rot}}(g, S_0)$ has multiplicity $R_{\mathrm{edge}}(z, w) = 1$**. Meanwhile, the degree of column $z \in \mathbb{F}_p$ and the degree of row $w \in \mathbb{F}_p$ are the $k$-fold shifted root-count sums*
     $$D_g(z) = \sum_{s \in S_0} R_g(z - s\hat{h}), \qquad D_{\tilde{g}}(w) = \sum_{s \in S_0} R_{\tilde{g}}(w - s\hat{h}), \qquad R_g(t) := \bigl|\{u \in \mathbb{F}_p : g(u) \equiv t \pmod p\}\bigr|. \tag{2.5}$$

## 3. Concave Edge-Capacity $s$-$t$ Min-Cut Duality, Mixed-Price Closure of $S = (0, 1, 3, 4)$, and Span-5 Four-Translate Classification

We now return to aligned 4-translate families $\Gamma_N(f, S)$ ($k = 4$) in the mixed line/singleton-point physical cover regime of `no_three_in_line_10` v3 ([3], Section 8.7) and `no_three_in_line_65` v1 ([6], Section 4). For a general weighted bipartite graph $G = (U, V, E)$ with source weights $R(v) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$, edge multiplicities $R_{\mathrm{edge}}(v, w) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$, and price $c \in [1, 2]$, choosing a target subset $T \subseteq V$ incurs total cut cost
$$\Phi_c^{\mathrm{gen}}(T) := 2|T| + \sum_{v \in U} \min\Bigl(2R(v),\; c \sum_{w \in N(v) \setminus T} R_{\mathrm{edge}}(v, w)\Bigr). \tag{3.1a}$$
In the aligned polynomial translate graph $\Gamma_N(f, S)$, every shift edge $(v, v+s)$ ($s \in S$) out of fiber $v \in U_p$ has multiplicity $R_{\mathrm{edge}}(v, v+s) = R(v)$, so $\sum_{w \in N(v) \setminus T} R_{\mathrm{edge}}(v, w) = R(v) e_{\mathrm{unc}}(v, T)$ and (3.1a) specializes to the normalized mixed-cover objective
$$\frac{1}{p}\Phi_c(T) := \frac{2|T|}{p} + \frac{1}{p}\sum_{v \in U_p} R(v)\min\bigl(2,\; c \cdot e_{\mathrm{unc}}(v, T)\bigr), \qquad e_{\mathrm{unc}}(v, T) := \bigl|\{s \in S : v + s \notin T\}\bigr|. \tag{3.1b}$$

## 4. Causal Peeling Timestamp Disjointness for Singleton-Doubler Pivots and Continuous Mixed-Price Exclusion ($n \le \frac{61}{31}p$) of $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$

### Lemma 4.1 (Causal Peeling Timestamp Disjointness for Singleton-Doubler Pivots on $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$)
*During the synchronous depth-32 peeling process on $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$, assign each covered row $y \in U_{32}$ the exact round $t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y) \in \{0, 1, \dots, 32\}$ at which $y$ first enters $U$, and assign each deleted singleton value group $v \in \mathcal{D}_{32}$ ($R(v) = 1$, unique column $u_v$) the exact round $t_{\mathrm{del}}(v) \in \{1, \dots, 32\}$ at which $v$ peels. Suppose a deleted singleton $v \in \mathcal{D}_{32}$ has three distinct incident rows $\mathrm{rows}(v) = \{y_{\mathrm{pure}}, y_a, y_b\}$ (each incident to 3 distinct groups) such that:*
1. *(Pure $\mathcal{A}_1$-row): Both other groups incident to $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ belong to $\mathcal{A}_1$ (hence have zero uncovered columns outside $V_{\mathrm{mod1}}$, and neither ever peels in rounds $1, \dots, 32$, or if a doubleton in $\mathcal{A}_1 \setminus \mathcal{A}_0$ peeled, $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ is its target row where $t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_{\mathrm{pure}}) = t_{\mathrm{del}}(v)$).*
2. *(Causal target rows $y_t \in \{y_a, y_b\}$): For each $y_t \in \{y_a, y_b\}$, $t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t) < t_{\mathrm{del}}(v)$, and the two other groups incident to $y_t$ consist of one group $g_{t, 0} \in \mathcal{A}_1$ (zero uncovered columns outside $V_{\mathrm{mod1}}$) and one deleted singleton $g_t^* \in \mathcal{D}_{32} \setminus \mathcal{A}_1$ ($R(g_t^*) = 1$) whose deletion timestamp equals the coverage timestamp of $y_t$: $t_{\mathrm{del}}(g_t^*) = t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t) \le 31$.*

*Then:*
- *(i) **Exact Timestamp Disjointness:** No two distinct candidate singletons $v \ne v'$ can ever share their pure row $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ or either of their target rows $y_a, y_b$, nor can the pure row of $v'$ coincide with a target row of $v$.*
- *(ii) **1-to-2 Exposed-Row Doubling:** Performing the 1-for-1 pivot $(U_{\mathrm{piv}}, V_{\mathrm{piv}}) = \bigl(U_{\mathrm{mod1}} \setminus \{y_{\mathrm{pure}}\},\; V_{\mathrm{mod1}} \cup \{u_v\}\bigr)$ simultaneously across all such candidate singletons $v$ preserves $|U_{\mathrm{piv}}| + |V_{\mathrm{piv}}| = |U_{\mathrm{base}}| + |V_{\mathrm{base}}|$, covers every point of $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$, removes $y_{\mathrm{pure}} \in E_1^{\mathrm{mod1}}$, and converts both 2-column rows $y_a, y_b$ into 1-column exposed rows in $E_1^{\mathrm{piv}}$ (with unique uncovered columns $u_{g_a^*}$ and $u_{g_b^*}$), increasing $|E_1|$ by exactly $+1$ per pivoted singleton and yielding the certified exposed density*
  $$\rho_{\mathrm{piv1}} = \rho_{\mathrm{mod1}} + \Delta\rho_{\mathrm{piv1}} \ge \mathbf{0.07380508}, \qquad \Delta\rho_{\mathrm{piv1}} := 3 o p_{\mathcal{A}_1}^2 q_{\mathrm{target}}^2 \ge \mathbf{0.00227263}, \qquad q_{\mathrm{target}} := 2 o p_{\mathcal{A}_1}(1 - r_{30})^2 \ge 0.11060892. \tag{4.1}$$

*Proof.*
(i) On $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$, both other incident groups belong to $\mathcal{A}_1$, so $v$ is the unique group in $\mathcal{D}_{32} \setminus \mathcal{A}_1$ incident to $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$. Moreover, $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ cannot be a target row of any candidate $v'$, because a target row has only one incident group in $\mathcal{A}_1$ whereas $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ has two. On each target row $y_t \in \{y_a, y_b\}$, its three incident groups are $g_{t, 0} \in \mathcal{A}_1$, $g_t^*$ (which satisfies $t_{\mathrm{del}}(g_t^*) = t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t)$), and $v$ (which satisfies $t_{\mathrm{del}}(v) > t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t)$). Thus $v$ is the **unique** group incident to $y_t$ with deletion timestamp strictly greater than $t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t)$! Furthermore, $g_t^*$ itself cannot be a candidate singleton, because on row $y_t$ (where $t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t) = t_{\mathrm{del}}(g_t^*)$), $y_t$ is the unique row of $g_t^*$ that was not yet covered before round $t_{\mathrm{del}}(g_t^*)$, so any pure $\mathcal{A}_1$-row of $g_t^*$ would have to be $y_t$ itself; but $y_t$ is incident to $v \notin \mathcal{A}_1$, so $g_t^*$ has no pure $\mathcal{A}_1$-row. Hence all candidate singletons $v$ act on pairwise disjoint 3-row neighborhoods $\{y_{\mathrm{pure}}, y_a, y_b\}$ and never pivot $g_a^*$ or $g_b^*$.

(ii) Before pivoting $v$, the uncovered columns outside $V_{\mathrm{mod1}}$ on $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ are $\{u_v\}$ (1-column exposed row), and on $y_t \in \{y_a, y_b\}$ are $\{u_v, u_{g_t^*}\}$ (2-column row, since $g_t^* \notin \mathcal{A}_1$ is never pivoted). Removing $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ from $U$ and adding $u_v$ to $V$ covers $(u_v, y_{\mathrm{pure}})$ via column $u_v \in V_{\mathrm{piv}}$ and reduces the uncovered columns on $y_a$ and $y_b$ to $\{u_{g_a^*}\}$ and $\{u_{g_b^*}\}$, so $y_a, y_b \in E_1^{\mathrm{piv}}$ ($+2 - 1 = +1$ net exposed row per pivot, verified on $p \in \{251, 503, 1009\}$ in Section 3 of `verify_rotated_translates_and_mobius.py`). In the local tree law on $\Gamma = \langle M_1, M_2 \rangle$ ([4, Lemma 4]), a singleton $v$ (density $o = 1/3$, 3 choices of $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$) has its two other groups on $y_{\mathrm{pure}}$ in $\mathcal{A}_1$ with probability $p_{\mathcal{A}_1}^2$, and on each target branch $y_t \in \{y_a, y_b\}$ has 2 choices for which child is in $\mathcal{A}_1$ (probability $p_{\mathcal{A}_1}$) and which child is a singleton $g_t^*$ (density $o$) whose two outward child rows are both covered by round $\le 30$ (so $t_{\mathrm{del}}(g_t^*) = t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_t) \le 31$ and $t_{\mathrm{del}}(v) = 1 + \max(t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_a), t_{\mathrm{cov}}(y_b)) \le 32$, probability $(1 - r_{30})^2$), giving $q_{\mathrm{target}} = 2 o p_{\mathcal{A}_1}(1 - r_{30})^2$ and (4.1). $\square$

Setting $A_{\mathrm{cov}} := V$, $T_{\mathrm{cov}} := U \setminus E_1$, and $E_{\mathrm{cov}} := \{(u(y_0), y_0) : y_0 \in E_1\}$ yields a valid mixed line/point cover $(A_{\mathrm{cov}}, T_{\mathrm{cov}}, E_{\mathrm{cov}})$ of $\mathcal{P}_p(Q_4)$ satisfying $|A_{\mathrm{cov}}| + |T_{\mathrm{cov}}| = |V| + |U| - |E_1|$ and $|E_{\mathrm{cov}}| = |E_1|$.*
2. **Oblique-Free Continuous Mixed-Price Bound for All $c \in (0, 2]$:**

   $$\frac{|S|}{n} \le 2C_{32} - (2 - c)\rho + O\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{p}(1+\log p)^2}{n}\right). \tag{4.2}$$
