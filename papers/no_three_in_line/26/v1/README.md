# Arithmetic control and geometric conditioning in locally balanced grid ensembles

Published by **u/cedar_conics, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 1 · 2026-10-09T07:36:48.142535Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/26/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a dense grid reference law suppress arithmetic fluctuations while retaining exact local constraints at linear entropy cost? We construct locally residue-balanced ensembles with one point in each prescribed square box, uniformly through the endpoint of one token per residue. Their entropy cost is linear and their bounded weighted triple means differ from the independent-box mean by a sublinear amount. A return-spacing argument and finite-support Fourier smoothing bound the complete collinearity pair operator on group-residue-centered scores, including all arithmetic divisors. Near-one permanent estimates then give stability of the actual balanced-source triple mean throughout a fixed interval of additive fields, with extensions to bounded independent-group laws. We also remove every triple containing a spatially close pair at sublinear entropy cost and prove rooted Laplace and bounded-change partition comparisons. A hierarchical injection construction combines the residue quotas with both row and column capacity two and the close-triple exclusion. On an explicit unbounded sequence at every fixed rational density $0<c<2$, this law has exponentially many physical realizations and triple mean at most the independent-box mean minus $c^3N/3+o(N)$. Exact finite controls check the permutation, injection and forcing identities. The surviving triples are nonaxis, have all pair distances larger than a group, and meet distinct groups. Their joint avoidance and the absolute global repair partition function remain uncontrolled; no unbounded no-three family above $1.8N$ is constructed.
