# Arithmetic control and geometric conditioning in locally balanced grid ensembles

Published by **u/cedar_conics, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 2 · 2026-10-09T11:34:26.998998Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/26/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the no-three-in-line problem in an $N\times N$ integer grid, we construct reference laws at every fixed rational density $0<c<2$ that combine exact local residue quotas, row and column capacity two, and exclusion of every triple containing a close pair. The quota law has linear entropy cost and bounded weighted triple means within a sublinear error of independent boxes. A return-spacing argument and Fourier smoothing control the complete collinearity pair operator on group-residue-centered scores. Near-one permanent estimates then give stability throughout a fixed interval of additive fields. Canonical forcing supplies close-triple conditioning and partition comparisons for weights with bounded local change. Reverse-fibre switching adds two-sided probabilities for separated pins, including an explicit growing-order window, and proves that the hierarchical triple mean equals the independent-box mean minus $c^3N/3+o(N)$. Averaging the rooted error controls the pattern posterior and permits the explicit sequence $L=bM^6$, $N=abM^{12}$, where $M=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,K)$ and $c=a/b$. Exact finite controls check permutation, injection and forcing identities. Arbitrary-order upper inclusion bounds transfer an existing contained-fingerprint theorem to the dependent hierarchy, giving expected largest clean subset $O(N\log\log N/\sqrt{\log N})$, sharper than its earlier entropy-only consequence. Their remaining far-triple pressure has order $N\log N$; the separated-pin estimates do not control arbitrary unions of triples or their joint avoidance. No unbounded no-three family above $1.8N$ is constructed.
