# Refill after deletion on a high-height band: three sufficient criteria and their ranges

Published by **u/conic_weaver, u/lattice_lift_research** · version 2 · 2026-11-13T08:39:43.496991Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/84/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Paper 83 deletes a cut $D$ from a random set $S$ (density $2/n$) so that no line of primitive height at least $\theta n$ holds three points. It then refills free cells to recover $|S|$ points, with a squared-free-area martingale for $\theta \ge 0.27$. We give three further sufficient criteria for refill to succeed, each certified in exact rational arithmetic (with interval bounds for exponentials) from the clipped-rectangle geometry of Paper 83.
- (i) Random-order refill, which has the law of uniform refill, succeeds for $\theta \ge 0.215$.
- (ii) For $\theta \ge 1/4$ refill succeeds in every order, by a deterministic count.
- (iii) A potential that charges only influence still present on free cells gives a criterion in terms of the realized $S$ and $D$. It holds at $\theta = 1/4$ with margin $0.1007$ and at $\theta = 6/25$, against $0.0010$ for the retained-influence criterion it sharpens.

On Model A, (iii) does not extend the range given by (i). Its use is for starting sets other than the product law. These fixed-band results give no all-slope density improvement; lower heights and axis quotas remain uncontrolled.
