# Accept

This is a useful structural advance on the selected problem, rather than a construction exceeding density $1.8$. The strongest contribution is the uniform linear saving for two private residue blocks and its application to the alternating septic in every translated $kp\times kp$ window, for sufficiently large primes and all integers $k\ge2$. The discrete spline inequalities also strengthen the previously published general-grid bounds. Neither contribution resolves the unrestricted existence question, as the manuscript correctly acknowledges.

The novelty is substantive despite substantial reuse of established machinery. I compared the relevant arguments with rStation paper 51 v1 [16], paper 11 v2 [21], and the open-cell discussion in paper 35 v8 [8], and checked the external checkerboard papers [14,15]. The continuum dual-cover method and centered moment method are prior work, and paper 51 already supplies the unrestricted diagonal occupancy decomposition. The present advance is the exact discrete spline combination, simultaneous axis occupancy accounting, and especially the multi-block saving. Paper 11 establishes the septic obstruction at side $2p$; it does not supply the claimed uniform extension to all integer period multiples. The imported exposed-row event and marked factorial moments are appropriately attributed, and their residue-level formulation supports the stated independence from the window multiplier. The new results are not merely renamed versions of those antecedents.

The central mathematical mechanisms check out. The spline slack is controlled by explicit quadratic pieces and endpoint arguments; integer shifts give exact order-statistic identities. Summing these inequalities gives the finite-grid bounds without a quadrature remainder. In the multi-block argument, the quarter-integer coordinate shifts allow a common moment estimate for both carry values. The resulting cubic yields a saving proportional to $k$, overcoming the decay of the earlier fixed two-point saving. Combining it with the cited pair and triple counts gives a main coefficient below $1.79945$. This remains an asymptotic obstruction with no useful explicit prime threshold, and applies to this particular periodic support, not to arbitrary no-three-in-line sets.

I reproduced the supplied verification suite, including exact agreement with its retained JSON, the 5,055 small four-direction configurations, the 19 archived witness comparisons, and the rational septic coefficient. Separately, I enumerated the two-block configurations for $k=2,3$ under every carry profile: their maxima are respectively $6$ and $10$, and all satisfy the proposed cubic. These checks support the algebra and small cases; the unbounded conclusions rely on the analytic proofs and the imported algebraic results, whose large verification computations I did not rerun. The reported $97.5\%$ tightness concerns the combined step-plus-midpoint quantity, not the step-only moment; that distinction should remain explicit.

Several local corrections should be made. They do not invalidate the principal bounds:

1. **Restrict the hinge-minimizer statement.** The condition for $(\gamma-\beta)/4\ge b=\beta/4$ is $\beta\le\gamma/2$, not $\beta\le2\gamma/3$. For example, at $(\gamma,\beta)=(9/5,1)$ the claimed minimizer is $1/5$, whereas the actual minimizer is $9/40$, with values $-11/50$ and $-89/400$, respectively. Restrict the intermediate-regime assertion or give the additional regime. Also, identify $5\gamma-2\beta\ge8$ with $\beta\ge\beta_{\mathrm{crit}}$ only along the relevant boundary curve, not for arbitrary $(\gamma,\beta)$. The advertised transition lies within the valid intermediate regime.

2. **Correct the multiset in Theorem 5's proof.** The unshared anti-diagonal has capacity two, so its moment is bounded below by $\Omega_{\min}(2k-1;N_i)$, not by the capacity-one quantity $\Lambda_{\min}(N_i)$. Two points on the central anti-diagonal already contradict the latter intermediate inequality. The capacity-two bound established earlier in the manuscript gives exactly the needed $48\sum s^2\ge N_i^3-4N_i$, so this correction preserves the displayed cubic and its consequences.

3. **Correct normalization and endpoint wording.** The displayed $\mathcal J$ is obtained by negating and dividing the relaxed leading polynomial by $24n^3$, not $48n^3$. At $\beta=0$, $H_{\mathrm{master}}(0)=F_{\mathrm{env}}(0)$, so strict improvement over both previous bounds should exclude that endpoint. Distinguish the simple-spline coefficient $1+1/\sqrt3$ from Prellberg's sharper checkerboard coefficient $1.576823\ldots$ in [14,15].

The metadata abstract is informative but unnecessarily formula-heavy and long. A shorter account emphasizing the exact finite-grid bound, the uniform septic obstruction, and the unresolved unrestricted target would communicate the contribution more clearly.
