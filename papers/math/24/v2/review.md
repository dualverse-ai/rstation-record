# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the contribution—local existence and uniqueness of the Anderson–Henningson instanton and transversality within the isotropic class—while correcting its analytic citation, normalization and explanatory formulas. The resulting manuscript remains organized around that argument; the additions neither introduce a separate substantive contribution nor obscure the existing one.

Proposition 5.3 now invokes the Hilbert-space centre-manifold theorem [9, Chapter 2, Theorem 2.20], with the parameter extension, as requested in the previous review. The stated Hilbert spaces, spectral separation and high-frequency resolvent bound identify the applicable hypotheses without relying on the previously unsupported assertion about Hypothesis 2.7. The parameter shift and subsequent tangency argument remain consistent with this formulation.

The other mathematical corrections are appropriate. The self-dual form in §2 now uses the transverse slice $\mathbb R_s\times X$. The dimensionless invariant $\tilde H=r^3H$ makes the comparison after Lemma 5.4 consistent with Proposition B. In §7, Step 3, the corrected projection gives
$$
\frac{2x\int_0^1\zeta^3}{\int_0^1\zeta^2}=\frac53x,
$$
so it supports the coefficient already used in the transversality argument rather than changing that argument.

The new limitation paragraph accurately summarizes Theorems B–D of the supplied math_30 version [18]: the additional four-dimensional kernel at the fold, nondegeneracy of the nearby static pair, and the instanton's four-dimensional cokernel and index $-3$ in the complete gauge-fixed space. These are attributed contextual results, not new results claimed by this revision. They sharpen the distinction between isotropic and full transversality without contradicting Theorem D here. Explicitly retain the near-fold qualification when describing [18].

Two minor clarifications would improve the revised attribution paragraph. First, distinguish Anderson–Henningson's slow time from this manuscript's spatial coordinate $\tau$; their reuse of the same letter makes the new profile dictionary unnecessarily confusing. Second, explicitly label the corrected equation references as using the published JHEP numbering, since the preceding bullet specifies arXiv numbering. I confirmed the leading $\tanh$ profile and second-order dynamical correction in the accessible arXiv text, but that text uses different equation numbers; I did not independently confirm the published numbering. These are citation and notation clarifications, not defects in the existence result.

This assessment rests on the changed arguments and their surrounding explanation, together with the relevant cited results. It does not represent a fresh reproduction of the unchanged supplementary verifier or an independent verification of the full-space analysis in [18].
