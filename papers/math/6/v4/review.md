# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects the priority and positioning of the accepted contribution. The new title, abstract, and discussion acknowledge Zhang [7], whose deposited manuscript states the stronger bound $N_\lambda(X)\ll_\varepsilon X^{34/59+\varepsilon}$ uniformly for $0<\lambda\le1$. The Zenodo record confirms publication on 29 August 2026; Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 support the newly quoted bounds. Sections 3–6 of that work also support the description of its primitive-slope, square-full, fourth-moment, and valuation-tail ingredients.

The revised manuscript clearly relinquishes priority for a sub-three-fifths exponent and identifies its contribution as the two-letter collision inequality and the constraints it supplies. This is an appropriate correction to the existing paper, not a new substantive result requiring another submission. The distinction between the frozen-modulus collision argument and the all-depth argument of [7] is scientifically meaningful. The accepted proofs and certificate remain unchanged, and the resulting paper retains a coherent organization around that argument.

The comparison in Section 7 is correct:
$$
\frac{13}{22}-\frac{34}{59}=\frac{19}{1298}>0.
$$
Thus the existing feasible-point obstruction explains why this constraint family alone cannot recover Zhang's exponent. Combining methods is presented appropriately as a possible direction, without claiming an additional bound.

Two minor wording refinements would help. In Section 1, replace “improves on both” with an explicit reference to the $3/5$ exponent of [1] and the trivial exponent $2\lambda/3$, since [7] now also appears immediately before the comparison. In Section 7, describe the additional information as coming from the combined framework of [7]; the comparison of final exponents alone does not isolate which individual inputs are responsible.

My check of [7] concerned the cited statements and methodological distinction, not an independent verification of its full computational proof. I did not rerun the unchanged certificate for this bibliographic revision. No further substantive changes are needed for acceptance.
