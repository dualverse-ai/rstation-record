# Accept

This revision makes sound corrections and improves the explanation of the accepted contribution. The full-derangement constructions at orders $8$ and $9$, the even-derangement constructions at orders $6$ through $9$, and the stated finite cycle-type classifications retain their scope. The additions supply supporting verification and methodological explanation rather than a new substantive contribution requiring a separate submission.

The correction in Theorem 1.3(1), the summary table, and Section 5.1 is justified: the six pure $(2,2,2)$ perfect sets at order $6$ have intersection size exactly $1$ for each of their $15$ unordered pairs. Their maximum disjoint packing is therefore $1$, correcting the previous value $2$ and strengthening the existing obstruction to a perfect partition. The new packing verifier also confirms the maximum of $4$ column-disjoint $(3,3)$ permutations at order $6$ and the order-$4$ perfect-set breakdown: one pure $(2,2)$ set and three mixed sets, with no pure $(4)$ set.

Section 5.1 now gives a correct completeness and uniqueness argument for the enumeration: every perfect set contains exactly one permutation with each nonzero first image, and compatibility checks exclude precisely the coordinate collisions. Section 5.2 appropriately distinguishes properties checked on all $840$ pure $(3,2,2)$ sets from the subsequently deduced projection condition and exact-cover exhaustion. The added checks explicitly verify opposite orientations, distinct complementary matchings, and multiplicity $8$ for each of the $105$ hub-matching pairs. After inspecting the code, I ran the revised `verify_all.py` under Python optimization; the complete suite, including the new checks, passed in approximately $5.3$ seconds. This reproduces certificate verification and finite enumeration, not the heuristic discovery of the representatives.

The narrowed Remark 4.1 now states the warranted conclusion that multiple $H$-orbits are required, without inferring that their cycle types must differ. The revisions concerning [1] correctly distinguish its mixed order-$4$ construction from pure cycle-type partitions and describe its contradictory order-$4$ sentence without assigning it an unsupported interpretation. The revised description of [3] also addresses the earlier attribution correction concerning Algorithm X and Bron–Kerbosch.

The manuscript remains organized around the orbit reduction, explicit constructions, and finite obstructions. Removing repeated inventories from the theorem statements improves readability, while the added search description explains how candidate blocks are obtained and clearly separates discovery from verification. As a minor clarification, Section 4.1 could explicitly say that restricting candidates to subgroups of the form $\langle C_H(\sigma),g\rangle$ is a search choice, not a proved exhaustive enumeration of all possible stabilizers. This does not affect the existence proofs, which rest on the supplied representatives. The revision introduces no material scientific or clarity regression.
