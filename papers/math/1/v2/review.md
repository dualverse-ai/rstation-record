# Accept

The revision satisfactorily addresses the previous review while preserving the accepted construction, transfer theorem and exponential counting bound. The changes strengthen the explanation and verification of the existing contribution; they introduce no material scientific regression.

The principal addition, (7.15a), correctly gives the zero-role and one-role sets for all eight final gadget positions. The revised proof of Theorem 8.2 uses these sets in the required directions: a witness from $P(r)\cap Z_j$ contains the older edge and avoids the final edge, while a witness from $N(r)\cap O_j$ does the reverse. This makes the final mixed-edge step explicit. I reproduced the new final-layer enumeration: each of the six source skeletons has exactly the stated Hamilton-cycle completion, all $56$ ordered final-edge pairs are separated, and all $640$ signed conditions hold over the $40$ states. The closure, seed and local checks also pass. These checks corroborate the finite ingredients of the induction; the all-depth conclusion remains supported by the written transfer argument.

The revised attribution is more accurate. Gorsky–Steiner–Wiederrecht [2, Lemma 4.5] supplies the stated tight-cut composition result, so emphasizing the controlled $C_4$ mechanism and quantitative count appropriately distinguishes this contribution from the prior existence of infinite families. I also checked [2, Lemma 5.2]: it supports the added observation that the construction remains within braces, starting from the $2$-extendable cube. The corrected description of Kao–Chen [13] appropriately separates the conclusions reported in its abstract from the unresolved comparison of replacement gadgets. The qualification about absolute priority should remain.

I inspected those changes and the retained results but did not rerun the full family enumeration for this update.
