# Accept

The paper gives a sound, substantive construction within the mathematics community’s scope. Its contribution is an explicit family with global ordered-edge Hamiltonian separation, together with a rigorous lower bound on the number of unlabelled graphs:
$$
B_{H^{+-}}(8+4k)\geq\left\lceil\frac{2^{\max(k-2,0)}}{(8+4k)3^7}\right\rceil.
$$
This is a useful restricted-family result, independently of Barnette’s conjecture. The limitations concerning arbitrary expansion sites, construction-history collisions and small-order weakness of the bound are appropriately stated.

The essential proof steps withstand scrutiny. The six local traversals distinguish connectivity from degree constraints, including the forbidden linkage pairing. The signed-support argument retains concrete witnesses: its joint-separation condition supplies the information that marginal positive and negative supports alone would not provide. Nonempty source transitions preserve an existing separating witness, and the local incidence rows establish separation for newly introduced edges. Separately, the intrinsic adjacency test and reversible peeling establish the rooted classification, with the explicit cap isomorphism accounting for the lost final choice. The passage to unlabelled graphs correctly bounds embeddings of the connected residual cube by $n3^7$; it does not assume that the old core is uniquely recognizable.

I reproduced the local-pattern, cover, seed and 40-state closure checks. Additional checks verified the final-gadget mixed-edge conditions for all 40 signed states and the invariance of the transfer relations under the alternative orders of saturated terminals in the path skeletons. Exhaustive Hamilton-cycle checks reproduced the standard-family results through depth $6$, including the reported rooted and unlabelled counts, and the reflected results through depth $5$. These finite computations corroborate the arguments; the all-depth conclusion rests on the transfer induction and grammar proof. The original batch run reached the 2 GiB memory limit during reflected depth $5$. A wrapper collecting garbage between graphs completed all 256 depth-$5$ reflected cases using the supplied routines.

The focused literature comparison supports novelty of the specific construction and quantitative result, with a qualification about priority. Gorsky–Steiner–Wiederrecht [2], particularly Lemma 4.5 and Section 5, already establish preservation of global $H^{+-}$ under tight-cut composition and hence supply infinite families. Their results do not supply the present controlled $C_4$ transfer or its rooted enumeration. Florek [1] uses the facial property, while Bej [5] establishes Hamiltonicity conditions for annular families and counts Hamilton cycles within certain graphs; these are different conclusions. I found no substantial duplication of the manuscript’s combined result in the material checked. The full Kao–Chen paper [13] remained inaccessible, so this assessment does not establish absolute priority over that scheme.

The following improvements are warranted but do not undermine acceptance:

1. **Make the final mixed-edge step explicit.** Lemma 8.1 states its signed bit conditions for the six positions retained in a nonfinal layer. Theorem 8.2 also needs the analogous conditions for all eight final positions, using the Hamilton-cycle rows of (7.15). These conditions hold for every state in $\Sigma^*$, as checked directly, but should be stated. The supplement’s check labelled “final ordered separation” currently examines source-to-target interface patterns rather than the final Hamilton-cycle rows. Add direct checks of (7.15), including older-edge/new-edge pairs.

2. **Sharpen the distinction from earlier constructions.** Emphasize the controlled expansion mechanism and explicit growth rate rather than the existence of infinite global-$H^{+-}$ families. The preservation of 2-extendability in [2, Lemma 5.2] also offers a useful connection to the brace setting. Correct the statement that the abstract of [13] does not specify its quantifiers: its indexed abstract explicitly describes single-edge fault tolerance, deletion of an opposite-colour vertex pair, and Hamiltonian laceability. What remains unresolved is the replacement gadget and its relationship to this construction.

3. **Improve the verification packaging.** Record the tested dependency versions and reduce accumulation of objects between exhaustive searches. Turn printed checks, particularly seed joint separation and the cap isomorphism, into assertions so failures produce an unsuccessful exit status.

4. **Correct mathematical rendering.** Remove nested math delimiters inside display environments and the doubled LaTeX backslashes in portions of the metadata abstract. The abstract is otherwise informative and appropriately limits the claims.
