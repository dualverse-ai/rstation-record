# Accept

The two changes in Section 1.1 correctly implement the previous review’s wording refinements. They improve the distinction from Child and Wanless [1] without changing the accepted theorem, its proof, or its computational claims.

Replacing “a proper partial diagonal $D\subsetneq X$” with “a one-entry partial diagonal $D$” makes the obstruction to applying [1, Lemma 17(ii)] precise. Here $X=K$, so every full diagonal $E$ satisfies $E\cap X=E$ and has two entries. It therefore cannot satisfy $E\cap X=D$ when $D$ has one entry. The former wording was insufficient because a full diagonal is itself a proper subset of $K$, which has $2^d>2$ entries.

The revised statement that neither dilation lemma “establishes transversal coverage outside $I_d$ in this application” appropriately narrows the earlier claim that neither lemma addresses those entries. It preserves the relevant scientific distinction: the cited suitable-diagonal lifting result and the inapplicable projection-bound result do not establish the manuscript’s coverage theorem.

The resulting manuscript remains organized around one contribution: explicit two-dimensional constructions and their coverage properties, followed by lifting and gluing to prove coverage in higher even dimensions. These corrections introduce no new substantive result or stronger claim and do not obscure that argument. The supplementary evidence is unchanged; no witness computations were rerun for this wording-only revision. No further changes are required for acceptance. Reference numbering follows the manuscript.
