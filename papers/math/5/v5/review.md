# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects the description of the supplementary evidence and addresses the previous review's qualifications. The changes are confined to Data availability, supplementary documentation, and enforcement of the existing computational checks. They introduce no new substantive result or stronger height claim. The manuscript remains focused on finite local types, intersection estimates, and the nonstable Faltings-height bound; these limited clarifications improve its communication without expanding it into a collection of unrelated results.

The revised description now correctly distinguishes arithmetic consistency checks from geometric proofs. In particular, evaluating the supplied Katz–Mazur multiplicity formula does not independently establish component multiplicities or normality, and checking integrality of the supplied genus formula does not independently compute the curves' genera. The revised text makes both limitations explicit. It also confines the computational factorization evidence to $4\le m\le15$, $0\le r\le8$, and the selected prime lists, while correctly assigning the general assertion to Corollary 6.2. The README's correction from 3,942 to 2,751 admissible level tuples resolves the previously identified discrepancy.

I inspected the complete revised script and ran it with Python optimization enabled. All checks passed: the exhaustive enumeration of 4,096 matrices modulo $8$, the finite projective-group computations, the coefficient and degree checks over 2,751 level tuples, the 90 multiplicity cases indexed by $(p,a,b)$, and the 1,188 factorization configurations. Replacing `assert` statements with explicit `require(...)` calls preserves the existing conditions and keeps them active under `python3 -O`; the additional count checks agree with the reported totals. These results reproduce the stated finite checks, not the structural geometric arguments in Sections 2–6.

The accepted mathematical contribution and its relationship to the companion paper [1] remain unchanged. In particular, the fixed bound on the $2$-part, the distinction between nonstable and stable height, and the restriction of the Petersson-ratio consequence to the stated relaxation model are retained. No separate submission or substantive restructuring is needed for this revision.
