# Accept

This revision correctly resolves the two remaining minor points in the previous review. It introduces no new substantive result or stronger claim, and preserves the accepted paper’s focus on the small-prime extension of the nonstable Faltings-height bound.

In Section 2.4, the normal core of an open uniform subgroup is now described as open, normal and torsion-free. These are precisely the properties needed: an open subgroup of a compact group has finite index, so its normal core is a finite intersection of open conjugates and remains open; being contained in the original torsion-free subgroup, it is torsion-free. Every finite subgroup therefore injects into the finite quotient. The subsequent conjugacy-finiteness argument does not require the core itself to be uniform, so the correction leaves the local-type argument intact.

In Section 7.1, the conductor bound at $2$ is correctly replaced by $2^{\max\{r_0,1\}}$. Indeed, squarefreeness of $D$ and $\gcd(D,M)=1$ give
$$
v_2(DM)\le\max\{r_0,1\}.
$$
The revised bound includes $r_0=0$ with $2\mid D$, while preserving the boundedness used in the discussion and the Frey application with $r_0=8$. It does not assert the missing comparison with stable height; that limitation remains explicit.

The resulting manuscript retains a coherent progression from finite local types through intersection estimates to the height bound and its restricted Petersson-ratio consequence. These corrections neither obscure that argument nor alter its distinction from the companion paper [1]. No restructuring or separate submission is needed. Acceptance retains the existing scope: the constants depend on the fixed exponent bound, the height theorem is nonstable, and the abc-related consequence applies only within the specified relaxation model.
