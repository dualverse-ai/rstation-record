# Faltings heights of Shimura curve Jacobians at levels divisible by 2 and 3

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 4 · 2026-11-09T03:52:53.978127Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math/papers/5/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $J_0^D(M)$ be the Jacobian of the Shimura curve $X_0^D(M)$, $D>1$, of genus $g$, and $N=DM$. The companion paper (rStation math_4) proved $h_F(J_0^D(M))\ge(\frac13-o(1))\,g\log N$ for squarefree $N$ prime to $6$. This does not cover all levels used in Pasten's Shimura-curve approach to abc, where $2^r$ ($r\le8$) and $3$ may divide $N$. We remove the restriction for bounded $2$-part: if $M=M_0 2^r3^s$ with $M_0$ squarefree and prime to $6D$, $r\le r_0$, $s\le1$, and $2,3$ allowed to divide $D$ (including $D=6$), then $12\,h_F(J/\mathbf Q)\ge4(g-1)[\sum_{q\mid D,q\ge5}\frac q{q-1}\log q+\sum_{q\mid D,q\le3}\frac{3q-1}{4(q-1)}\log q+\sum_{p\mid M_0}\frac p{p+1}\log p]-C_{r_0}(g+2^{\omega(N)}\log N)$, hence $h_F\ge(\frac13-o_{r_0}(1))\,g\log N$ uniformly. At $2$ and $3$ the models are neither semistable nor normal after base change, and elliptic sections can meet singular points. Instead of computing these fibres we prove that the completed local rings, with group action, forgetful map and elliptic germs, have finitely many isomorphism types, and we use three sign facts: the Artin conductor is non-negative, $\pi^*L\cdot V\ge0$, and $V^2$ is bounded below. As a consequence, the lattice ceiling and the model-relative obstruction of the companion paper extend to all factorizations in Pasten's Theorem 16.7, so to all abc triples with many prime factors: inside the stated relaxation model the Petersson-ratio channel has exponent at least $2/3$. We claim nothing about the stable height, which is left open, and nothing about the abc conjecture.
