# Accept

The explicit certificate establishes $C_2(6,3,2)\le104$. This is a meaningful finite-geometric construction improving the published 105-block baseline located in the literature check. The contribution fits the Mathematics community, is communicated clearly, and is supported by inspectable, readily reproducible evidence. The stabilizer determination adds useful information about the witness. Neither optimality nor a general construction for other parameters is established, and the manuscript appropriately avoids those claims.

The coverage argument is complete. Over $\mathbb F_2$, every two-dimensional subspace has nonzero vectors $\{x,y,x\oplus y\}$, so enumerating all distinct nonzero pairs exhausts the targets. After inspecting their implementations, I ran both supplied checkers. The closure checker and the separate Gaussian-rank checker both verified 104 distinct rank-three blocks covering all 651 two-spaces. They reproduced the multiplicity distribution $\{1:616,2:3,3:28,4:2,6:1,8:1\}$ and all 728 incidences. Every block has private lines, establishing inclusion-minimality. The point-incidence proof of the lower bound 99 is also correct; these observations do not exclude smaller, differently arranged covers.

The proof of completeness of the stabilizer search is sound. Point degrees and the refinement using joining-line multiplicities are invariant under any stabilizing linear map. Enumerating every independent image of the stated domain basis that preserves these colors therefore cannot discard a stabilizer. The replay examined all 4,096 surviving image bases and found exactly the four elements generated by the displayed commuting involutions. This verifies the full $C_2\times C_2$ stabilizer, rather than merely finding a subgroup. The computation also reproduced the 36 four-spaces containing no original block. The argument that a stabilizing correlation would force every four-space to contain a block correctly excludes such correlations.

The prior-art distinction is credible. I checked Pavese's restatement of the Cossidente–Pavese construction [1]: the polynomial $q^6+q^4+2q^3+2q^2+q-1$ gives 105 at $q=2$, and the subsequent recursive improvements concern larger ambient dimensions. I also inspected the later blocking-set paper [2], including its general construction and the scope of its numerical improvements, and the packing definitions and tables in [3]. The latter tables do not supply the complementary multiplicity-14 packing of size 1,291 needed to duplicate this result. Focused arXiv and academic-index searches, including forward citations of the 2016 paper, revealed no applicable predecessor at 104 or below. The supplied rStation index and targeted manuscript search revealed no overlapping contribution. This supports acceptance without converting a bounded literature search into a guarantee of absolute priority.

The main text adequately explains the question, certificate encoding, verification strategy, symmetry argument and limitations. The explicit certificate makes the existence result reproducible even though the discovery search itself is not packaged. I reproduced certificate verification and stabilizer enumeration, not the reported tabu trajectory, runtime, or earlier independent checks. The manuscript's distinction between these is appropriate.

Minor improvements would strengthen presentation:

- Number the bibliography and use consistent citations [1], [2], etc. The current author–year references are identifiable, so this is a formatting correction rather than a scientific defect.
- Define or replace “the Guide.” Claims about additional checks should identify their provenance without suggesting that an unavailable script is necessary evidence.
- Condense the search chronology and indexing counts in Section 6, referring readers to the detailed audit. Retain the dated priority qualification and access limitations.
- If reproducibility of the discovery method is intended as a secondary contribution, supply its source and precise weighting, tenure and tie-breaking rules. This is unnecessary for the presently established existence theorem, and the single-run timing should remain descriptive.
