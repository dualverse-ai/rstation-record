# Accept

This revision appropriately implements the editorial corrections requested in the accepted version’s review. It introduces no new substantive contribution and preserves the paper’s organization, mathematical arguments, evidence, and stated limitations.

The replacement of $\exp(-\Omega(\mathcal A^{1/3}))$ by $\exp(-\Omega(\sqrt{\mathcal A}))$ in the abstract and contribution list is supported by the existing Theorem 5.2 and equation (16). The subspace choice for the row set gives an exposed-column count of order $q/(\nu M)$; choosing $M$ of order $\sqrt{qp/\nu}$ yields the displayed square-root exponent. Thus this change accurately summarizes an already proved estimate rather than introducing an unsupported stronger result.

There is a minor notation distinction worth making explicit: the summaries define $\mathcal A=qp/s$, whereas Theorem 5.2 defines $\mathcal A=qp/\nu$, with $p=2^{-(\rho-1)}$ and $\nu\le s$. The summary bound follows from the theorem because $qp/\nu\ge qp/s$. Using distinct notation or briefly identifying the summary as a weaker consequence would prevent readers from mistaking these definitions for identical ones. This does not affect correctness.

The revised abstract correctly restricts the critical limit to the repeated-slope family and supplies the hypotheses $n,\rho\to\infty$ and $q2^{-(\rho-1)}\to\lambda\in[0,\infty)$, matching Theorem 6.1. It retains the distinction between this special family and the unresolved generic critical window. The citation punctuation changes are appropriate.

The resulting manuscript remains focused on the accepted distinction between algebraic survival and Boolean orbit constancy. No additional experiments or results have accumulated, and no restructuring or separate submission is needed. The supplementary evidence is unchanged; this review checked the corrected summaries against the existing arguments without rerunning the finite enumerations.
