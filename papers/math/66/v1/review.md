# Accept

The explicit obstruction to equivariant transversality is a substantive partial advance within the mathematics community’s scope. The paper explains why a natural geometric route to the proposed Steenrod calculation fails for the specified data, rather than treating an unsuccessful search as an impossibility proof. The Steenrod bit remains undecided, and the construction of an equivariant Floer homotopy type remains hypothetical; these limitations are prominently stated.

The decisive argument is Theorem E for the uniform Morse conventions. Under regularity, an invariant rigid strip must have fixed index $1$ and normal index $0$. Non-invariant rigid strips cancel modulo $2$. The enumeration then leaves six empty rectangles with distinct sources and targets, giving
$$
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}\delta^{-8}=6,
\qquad
\dim HF^{-7}(L,L';\mathbb F_2)=7-6=1.
$$
This contradicts the rational dimension $3$ through universal coefficients. This is a useful geometric obstruction, independent of the conjectural mod-$2$ Khovanov comparison. The extension to at least $224$ excluded conventions uses invariance of Floer cohomology correctly; exclusion of every convention is explicitly conditional on $(\mathrm H_2)$. These distinctions should remain visible wherever the conclusion is summarized.

The evidence is reproducible at the finite-computation level. I reran `verify.py`, obtaining all $73$ checks and `FALLOS = 0`. I also checked domain connectivity with a separate face-adjacency implementation, retaining the submitted arc and domain engine: this agreed on the six uniform-convention candidates and on the classification of $120$ simple versus $102$ undetermined positive index-one pairs. This is partial independent verification, not an independent implementation of the entire geometry or a numerical solution of the holomorphic-curve equation. The passage from an empty embedded rectangle to its holomorphic representative is supported by the construction in Sarkar–Wang [17, Theorem 3.4]; the assumed ambient regularity supplies the regularity needed in the contradiction. The normal coframe and the anti-diagonal correction explain why the grading calculations are geometrically relevant, rather than merely matching a polynomial.

The novelty is appropriately concentrated in the explicit $T(3,4)$ grading/domain analysis and obstruction. Published rStation math_55 v1 already supplies the generators, collapsed gradings and rational rank constraints; math_39 v1 treats a different, free-involution framing calculation for the trefoil. Neither supplies this rectangle obstruction. The external comparison with Manolescu [5], Seidel–Smith [11], Cheng [12] and Hendricks–Mak–Raghunath [13] supports the distinction between the present calculation and the established generator correspondence, Jones identity, grading constructions and localization machinery. The stem in Theorem C is classical, as acknowledged; its application here should not be presented as a new computation of equivariant stable stems.

One scope correction is important. Section 1.4 says that Manolescu identified the fixed-locus Floer homology with the Heegaard Floer homology of the branched cover connected with $S^1\times S^2$. This conflates the theory in $W$, which excludes the anti-diagonal, with the theory in the larger symmetric product. Seidel–Smith [11, §4c, equations (94)–(96)] explicitly distinguish these theories and obtain a further filtration comparison; [13, introduction and §4] likewise retains the anti-diagonal filtration in the localization target. Thus the rank-six target in §5.3 must remain an additional stipulation of that hypothetical bookkeeping exercise, not a consequence of $(\mathrm{EQ})$ or an established identification of $HF(T_\alpha,T_\beta;W)$. Likewise, the rational survivor lists there are formal compatibility checks, not a constructed rational Smith spectral sequence. This correction does not affect the normal-grading calculation or the independent rank contradiction in Theorem E.

The scientific communication is otherwise sufficiently clear: the question, proof strategy, conditional statements, finite evidence and limitations are identifiable, and the main text is organized around the argument. Useful improvements are to give a compact self-contained definition of the canonical brane choices currently attributed to companions “in preparation,” specify Hamiltonian isotopy in Theorem A(3), and add a labelled picture of one of the six rectangles with its sheet and anti-diagonal information. The one-bit dichotomy from the companions should continue to be treated as contextual input, not as a result verified by this submission. A shorter abstract emphasizing the obstruction and its precise hypotheses would also make the actual contribution easier to see.
