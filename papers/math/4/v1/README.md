# A uniform lower bound for the Faltings height of Jacobians of Shimura curves X_0^D(M)

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-30T05:02:02.441561Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math/papers/4/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let J_0^D(M) be the Jacobian of the Shimura curve X_0^D(M) attached to an indefinite quaternion algebra of discriminant D>1 over Q and an Eichler order of level M, and let g be its genus. For D=1, Jorgenson and Kramer proved h_F(J_0(N)) ~ (g/3) log N for squarefree N prime to 6; for D>1 the size of the Faltings height in terms of the level has been open. We prove that if N=DM is squarefree and prime to 6, then 12 h_F(J_0^D(M)) >= 4(g-1)[sum_{q|D} q/(q-1) log q + sum_{p|M} p/(p+1) log p] - C(g + 2^{omega(N)} log N) with an absolute constant C, hence h_F(J_0^D(M)) >= (1/3 - o(1)) g log N uniformly in (D,M). The proof applies the arithmetic Riemann-Roch theorem of Freixas i Montplet and von Pippich for orbifold curves. The self-intersection of the orbifold canonical bundle is computed from Yuan's modular height formula and an error-free level-raising recursion on the normal coarse model, in which thick nodes enter through Mumford intersection numbers and the Eichler mass formula. We also show that elliptic sections are disjoint at primes >= 5 and have bounded intersections at 2 and 3, compute their heights, and give a one-sided bound for the Selberg zeta value. As a consequence, inside an explicitly stated exponent-profile relaxation model, the Petersson-ratio channel of Pasten's Shimura-curve approach to abc cannot lower the Stewart-Yu exponent 1/3 on an infinite family of triples whose Frey curves have conductor prime to 6. We claim no upper bound or asymptotic for the height, and nothing about the abc conjecture itself. The manuscript was produced with AI research agents and has not been reviewed by a human expert.
