# Accept

This is a sound, limited update to the accepted paper. The changes incorporate Pasten’s new bound [27] and clarify its relationship to the restricted obstruction in Section 7. They introduce no new substantive contribution and preserve the manuscript’s organization around the uniform Faltings-height lower bound. No restructuring or separate submission is needed.

The replacement of the example $C\asymp R\log R$ by $C\asymp R$ in Corollary 7.4 is supported by [27, Corollary 1.3]. That result gives $h(E)\ll_S N$ for elliptic curves semistable outside a fixed set $S$; for the semistable curves in $\mathcal F$, the constant is absolute. Together with the discriminant–height comparison and $N=R/2$, this supplies a uniform per-prime bound of the asserted size. The fixed correction at $2$ between the Frey discriminant and the minimal discriminant is harmless. The proof of Corollary 7.4 remains valid: its admissible profile only requires $C\ge R^{2/3}$, so the improved example does not change the lower-bound exponent $2/3-o(1)$.

The added distinction in Section 7.7 is also justified by the proof of [27, Theorem 1.1]. It bounds the elliptic curve’s height through congruence indices, Hecke eigenvalue multiplicities and eigenvalue bounds, rather than the independently estimated Petersson norms used in Corollary 7.4. Its resulting direct bound on $\log c$ is outside Definition 7.1. The statement that $\log c\ll R$ does not improve the Stewart–Yu exponent $1/3$ is correct. Explicitly excluding this argument in the introduction usefully prevents an overbroad reading of the accepted obstruction. The new citation concerns upper bounds for elliptic curves and does not duplicate the paper’s central lower bound for Shimura-curve Jacobians.

Two minor presentation refinements would make the update more precise. In Section 7.7, define $\ell$ as a prime not dividing $N$ when quoting [27, Theorem 1.1]. In Corollary 7.4, write the source’s discriminant comparison as $\log|\Delta|\ll\max\{1,h(E)\}$, or explicitly restrict the displayed shorthand to sufficiently large conductor. Neither refinement affects the conclusion or the acceptance of this revision.
