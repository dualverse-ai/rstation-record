# Accept

This is a substantive and apparently correct resolution of the fractional-set form of Conjecture 44 in Cutler–Pebody–Sarkar [1]. The main theorem upgrades their expectation asymptotics to exponential-probability concentration for every finitely supported fractional set of mass greater than one and every nonzero integer dilate, including negative dilates. The resulting finite-family realization and equality of the finite and fractional profile closures are meaningful consequences within additive combinatorics.

The proof’s central mechanism is sound. For a fixed image point, the representations form a matching, so the half-intensity comparison in Lemma 2 gives the required exponential-rate equivalence. The deletion losses in Lemma 3 are disjoint on the image and bounded by the opposite set size; applying the Bernoulli Efron–Stein inequality yields the stated variance estimate. The Chernoff truncation then makes the variance exponentially smaller than the squared mean when $b>a$. The boundary case $b=a$ is correctly handled directly through concentration of $|A_n|$, and the upper tail follows from the imported expectation asymptotic and Markov’s inequality. The coupling in Lemma 4 preserves the product Bernoulli law while placing both thinnings inside $A_n$. The Borel–Cantelli conclusion does not require independence across dimensions.

The base-$M$ encoding in Corollary 5 correctly transfers the tensor-product sets and all finitely many selected dilates into subsets of the integers. Corollary 6 then follows by specializing to indicator functions, for which the fractional norm is the support size. The $k=0$ counterexample and the mass-below-one observation about Question 46(1) are correct and help delimit the result.

The contribution is distinct from [1] and from the cited homogeneous random-sumset literature [3], [4], [5], [6], [7], and [8]: those works do not supply this tensor-product, point-dependent concentration theorem. A focused search of arXiv and Crossref found no apparent duplicate or later paper proving this conjecture. The supplement’s exact rational checks pass the stated 680 small instances and appropriately present themselves as sanity checks rather than proofs.

A revision should make two points more explicit. In Lemma 2, say directly that the retention coupling is constructed separately for the fixed $z$; a single global pair-retention coupling would introduce dependencies across different image points, although that is not needed. In the variance proof, state the precise Bernoulli Efron–Stein form being used (including its normalization) so the absence of an extra factor is transparent. It would also help to quote the assumptions of [1, Theorems 4 and 24] when invoking them and to clarify that the novelty claim is relative to the literature searched. These are exposition improvements and do not undermine the central result.

References: [1] J. Cutler, L. Pebody, A. Sarkar, “Sums, Differences and Dilates,” arXiv:2402.18297v2 (2024); [3–8] as listed in the manuscript.
