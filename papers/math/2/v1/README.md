# Concentration of Random Tensor Sumsets

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T19:34:52.552986Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math/papers/2/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Cutler, Pebody and Sarkar introduced a fractional size $\lVert\gamma\rVert$ for finitely supported weights on $\mathbb{Z}$ and showed that if $A_n\subset\mathbb{Z}^n$ includes each point independently with probability $\prod_i\alpha(x_i)$, for a fractional set $\alpha\colon\mathbb{Z}\to[0,1]$ of total mass greater than one, then $\mathbb{E}|A_n+kA_n|$ grows at the exponential rate $\lVert\alpha+k\alpha\rVert$. They conjectured that the random cardinality itself has this rate, and proved it only in a strictly spartan regime. We prove that for every such $\alpha$ and every nonzero integer $k$, $\frac1n\log|A_n+kA_n|\to\log\lVert\alpha+k\alpha\rVert$ almost surely, with exponentially small failure probabilities, with no regularity assumption. The proof couples two independent half-intensity thinnings inside $A_n$, compares their expected two-set image with the known expectation asymptotics, and bounds its variance by $\mathbb{E}[(|R|+|T|)|R+kT|]$ via the Efron–Stein inequality and a deletion-counting argument; the boundary case where the rate equals the mass follows from $|A_n+kA_n|\geq|A_n|$. Consequences: any finite family of nonzero dilate profiles of a fractional set is realized by finite subsets of the integers, and the closures of finite-set and fractional-set nonzero-dilate profiles coincide. We also note that the simultaneous-realization conjecture as printed fails at $k=0$, and that the fractional version of the question $|A||A+2A|\leq|A+A|^2$ fails below mass one and is equivalent to the finite question above mass one. Limitations: only fractional sets (weights at most one) and nonzero dilates are covered; constants are not explicit; the expectation asymptotics are imported from Cutler–Pebody–Sarkar; the profile regions themselves are not determined. Small exact enumerations of the finite lemmas are supplied as sanity checks.
