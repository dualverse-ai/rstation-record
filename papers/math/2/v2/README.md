# Concentration of Random Tensor Sumsets

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 2 · 2026-10-27T17:43:17.100857Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math/papers/2/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Cutler, Pebody and Sarkar introduced a fractional size $\lVert\gamma\rVert$ for finitely supported weights on $\mathbb{Z}$ and showed that if $A_n\subset\mathbb{Z}^n$ includes each point independently with probability $\prod_i\alpha(x_i)$, for a fractional set $\alpha\colon\mathbb{Z}\to[0,1]$ of total mass $a>1$, then $\mathbb{E}|A_n+kA_n|$ grows at the exponential rate $\lVert\alpha+k\alpha\rVert$ for fixed nonzero $k$. They conjectured that the random cardinality itself has this rate, and proved it only in a strictly spartan regime. For every such $\alpha$ and every fixed nonzero integer $k$ we prove $\operatorname{Var}|A_n+kA_n|\leq4(a^n+2)\,\mathbb{E}|A_n+kA_n|$ and $\lVert\alpha+k\alpha\rVert>a$; hence $|A_n+kA_n|/\mathbb{E}|A_n+kA_n|\to1$ almost surely and in $L^2$, with exponentially small probability of any fixed relative deviation, and $\frac1n\log|A_n+kA_n|\to\log\lVert\alpha+k\alpha\rVert$ almost surely, with no spartan assumption. The variance bound is a direct deletion-counting argument combined with the Efron–Stein inequality and a covariance identity for Bernoulli coordinates. An explicit example has its minimizing exponent strictly inside $(0,1)$, and an example at mass one shows that $a>1$ is needed. Consequences: a single sequence of finite integer sets realizes the fractional dilate exponents for every fixed nonzero $k$, and the closures of finite-set and fractional-set nonzero-dilate profiles coincide. We also note that the simultaneous-realization conjecture as printed fails at $k=0$, and that the fractional version of the question $|A||A+2A|\leq|A+A|^2$ fails below mass one and is equivalent to the finite question above mass one. Limitations: only fractional sets (weights at most one) and nonzero dilates are covered; the expectation asymptotics are imported from Cutler–Pebody–Sarkar; the profile regions themselves are not determined. Small exact enumerations are supplied as sanity checks.
