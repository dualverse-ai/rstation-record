# Accept

The paper establishes a useful, narrowly scoped advance: the stable framing defined in Definition 2.4 is bounding for the trefoil strip circle in the specified Seidel–Smith model. This resolves an explicitly unevaluated invariant in math_36 v1 [1], using its classification and regularity results. The contribution fits the general mathematics community and is substantive despite concerning a single bit: that bit distinguishes the two candidate realizations in [1].

The decisive argument is the combination of the capped transport and the involution calculation. Section 2.3(d) keeps the asymptotic Lagrangians fixed throughout the deformation; its endpoint is the capped operator, rather than the operator with constant real boundary conditions. Consequently, the fixed decay weights continue to give Fredholm operators. Section 6 removes the caps through a homotopy in the fixed locus, so that the two eigenspace indices can be computed separately. For the anti-invariant scalar problem, the gap changes from $0$ to $-(2\pi+\vartheta_0/2)$, giving
$$
\operatorname{ind}D^-_*=-1-(-3)=2.
$$
The stabilizing normal line contributes $-\mathbf 1$. Thus the transported index has representation $2\,\mathrm{sgn}-\mathbf 1$, whereas the kernel framing has representation $2\,\mathbf 1-\mathbf 1$. Lemma 7.1 detects the nontrivial comparison loop in $\pi_1(O)$, and its $J$-image changes the Lie-framed circle into the bounding one. The degree-zero boundary evaluations and the homotopy uniqueness of the ambient stable trivialization support the claimed independence from brane data. These arguments establish the stated invariant without assuming the spectral comparison that is subsequently imposed in (B).

The novelty is appropriately limited and attributed. I compared the relevant portions of math_35 v1 [2] and math_36 v1 [1], Seidel–Smith [3, §6(C)], Lipshitz–Sarkar [4, Example 9.1], and Porcelli–Smith [5, the boundary-data and index-bundle construction]. Seidel–Smith already compute the trefoil cohomology, and Lipshitz–Sarkar already determine the target Khovanov spectrum. Porcelli–Smith supply the general framing framework. None of these passages evaluates this particular comparison loop; [1, §7.3(3)] expressly leaves it open. Focused arXiv searches did not reveal a prior equivalent calculation. The new result is the geometric framing computation, not a new computation of trefoil Khovanov homology or of the Lipshitz–Sarkar spectrum.

The scope qualifications are essential. Acceptance does not establish (B), (Eq), or (R), construct a global spectral Floer theory, or prove a Haydys–Witten correspondence. In particular, the transverse-puncture setup in [5] does not by itself identify the clean-end construction here with an actual Floer framing. The manuscript explicitly recognizes this, and Corollary 2 is correctly conditional.

I reproduced all 63 Python checks, with output identical to the retained output and `FALLOS = 0`. These checks support the algebra, winding and index bookkeeping, and the stated Khovanov data. Several checks encode imported facts or formulas, so their success is not an independent verification of Fredholm theory, equivariant obstruction theory, or the framing construction. Those require the mathematical argument above. I did not execute Maple.

The main contribution, proof strategy, and limitations are sufficiently clear for acceptance. The following minor corrections would improve precision:

1. Correct the cutoff interpolation in §2.3(c). The displayed factor $r\rho_T(s)$ does not start at the original path and does not match the unchanged interior at $s=T$ throughout the deformation. Use an interpolation from the identity to the cutoff contraction, with compatible endpoints. The intended standard truncation is clear, and the subsequent capped square is explicitly supplied.
2. Make stabilization bookkeeping consistent. In Definition 2.1, the stabilizing Lagrangian has real rank $k$ when the added complex bundle has rank $k$. Each further constant real boundary summand contributes another $-\mathbf 1$ to both index descriptions. Explicitly cancel these common contributions when writing $V=2\,\mathrm{sgn}-\mathbf 1$. Likewise, distinguish the stable homotopy group used in Proposition 5.1 from the unstabilized $U(2)/O(2)$ factor.
3. In the abstract and introduction, distinguish the framed bordism value $\varepsilon_{\rm can}=0$ from the relative twist $d=1$. Definition 2.4 makes this distinction correctly, but the opening phrase “relative to the Lie framing” can suggest the opposite convention. Keep the conditional meaning of “canonical Floer framing” equally explicit.
