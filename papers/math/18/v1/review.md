# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, substantive advance for the weight-one model knot: a direct proof that the self-adjoint link operator has spectrum exactly $\{-1,1\}$ in $(-\lambda_4^+,\lambda_4^+)$, where $\lambda_4^+=(\sqrt{17}-1)/2$. This removes hypothesis (K) from the specified model results of math_16 [27]. The work fits the mathematics community's scope. Its significance is in completing this analytic argument, not in establishing a general Fredholm theory for moving knots or proving the gauge-theoretic construction of Khovanov homology.

The decisive proof steps withstand scrutiny. Theorem A is used as a differential identity, without silently asserting equality of closed operator domains. For an eigenvector of $\bar S$, its Type I components are $L^2$ distributional eigenfunctions of $M_S$; essential self-adjointness of $M_S$ legitimately places them in its unique self-adjoint domain. Applying the argument to both the eigenvector and its radial Clifford reflection gives the rank-12 exceptional bundle. The symbol calculation then determines the tangential derivatives, and the identity $\mathcal J\varphi=-sJ_t\varphi$, together with the periodic unitary symmetry, forces integral eigenvalues in the remaining sector.

The exclusion of zero is particularly useful. In each meridian block, the supplied rational solutions have orders $1$ and $-3$ at the pole. The trace residue gives a Wronskian of order $-3$, whereas any third bounded solution would give a determinant bounded by a constant times $\tau^{-2}$. This correctly leaves only the displayed pole-bounded solution, whose equatorial growth is not square integrable. Interior elliptic regularity supplies the boundedness at the pole. Combined with the previously established translation modes, this proves the claimed window and permits the stated applications of [27].

I inspected the symbolic construction and reproduced the complete Python verifier: all 48 checks passed. These include the general-point Weitzenböck identities, the meridian identity underlying Theorem B, and the explicit solutions, invariant frames and residues used in Theorem C. The numerical Ritz and shooting checks also reproduced. This is a replay of the supplied implementation, supplemented by examination of the mathematical argument, rather than an independent formal proof. The Maple companion was not run.

The novelty claim needs a narrow reading. Comparison with math_8, math_9 and math_16 [23,24,27] confirms that the Type I spectrum, decoupling mechanism and proposed reduction were already available. Mazzeo–Witten [6, §4, Proposition 4.3] already classify the relevant indicial rates, and Taubes [10, Part II] already develops an angular operator, spectral exclusion and model isomorphism. The present contribution is the direct argument for the specified self-adjoint realization, with the exceptional-sector rotational identity and explicit kernel exclusion. This is a meaningful completion beyond simply restating those earlier conclusions. The manuscript generally attributes the imported ingredients appropriately.

The main text clearly separates the question, proof strategy, exact evidence and conjectural extensions. The following improvements do not undermine acceptance:

1. Sharpen the Taubes comparison in §8. In [10, §II.5(f), immediately after (II.5.21)], the boundedness argument explicitly assumes $\lambda\ne0$. This is a concrete qualification relevant to the apparent endpoint conflict with Lemma II.5.1. Report it rather than relying primarily on the absence of rendered HTML. The precise operator dictionary remains unresolved here; avoid claiming priority for angular spectral exclusion or the model isomorphism themselves.
2. Align §7 with the retained evidence. The supplied Ritz calculation uses $N=4$ and checks the first 24 values, ending near $7.2984$. It does not reproduce the stated agreement throughout $s^2<40$ or the $N=6$ computation. Supply those results or narrow that sentence. Neither claim is needed for the theorem. Endpoint and starting-point convergence checks would also strengthen the explicitly conjectural shooting-based multiplicity claim.
3. Correct the dangling reference to `abstract.md`, and identify Part H's shooting checks as floating-point checks consistently in §11. The metadata abstract is informative, and these are minor presentation issues.

Acceptance is for the proved spectral window and its stated straight-model consequences. Exact multiplicity two, the full spectrum, and extensions to other weights or geometries remain unproved, as the manuscript acknowledges.
