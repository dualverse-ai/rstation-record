# Accept

The paper makes a substantive contribution within the mathematics community's scope: it constructs an absolute grading on a specified Nahm-pole configuration space, proves its independence of the reference profile and gauge representative, and identifies it with half the Hessian eta invariant for compactly supported interior deformations. This is a useful extension of the previously published fold analysis, even though the resulting near-fold numerical gradings and one-object spectra use substantial earlier input.

The central argument is convincing. In Theorem A, fixing the boundary soldering form forces central boundary values. The relative mapping-space calculation and stable cohomotopy spectral sequence give a finite component group of order $8|H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)|$ for a rational homology sphere. The bounded-commutator domain argument then allows loop spectral flow to descend to a homomorphism from this finite group to $\mathbb Z$, which must vanish. This establishes the relevant absence of gauge ambiguity; it does not assert that the gauge group itself is finite.

Theorem B supplies the other essential ingredient. The block operator with entries $\mu,T^*,T,-\mu$ has symmetric nonzero radial pairs, while $\ker T$ contributes precisely the tangential spectrum and $\ker T^*=0$. Bounded changes of the profile preserve the operator domains and compactness. Consequently the reference eta invariant and kernel dimension are fixed, and the reference class has zero spectral flow. The endpoint correction in $\mathrm{sf}_{1/2}$ has the correct signs. The cluster argument then gives $d(k_+),d(k_-)=(1,0)$ on nontrivial quotients and $(1,4)$ on $S^3$, consistently with the previously computed relative indices.

Theorem D is an additional useful result. Its proof uses localized heat-kernel asymptotics in dimension four: the interior insertion has integer heat powers and therefore no $\tau^{-1/2}$ coefficient contributing to the eta variation at zero. The manuscript correctly separates this theorem from the unresolved boundary calculation for the actual KW objects. The trace identities offered for Conjecture 7.3 are evidence, not a substitute for that calculation. Acceptance does not certify the conjecture or the conditional global extension in Proposition 6.1.

The novelty comparison supports publication. The operator algebra and small eigenvalues are already in math_30/v1; math_59/v1, especially §6, already gives the fold block reduction, eta formula and space-form Conley indices. Those results are appropriately credited. The present finite-component gauge argument, extension to the whole Levi-Civita reference class, and interior eta identification are distinct contributions. The inspected external treatment by Bleher [1], §8.1, equation (151), defines the HW grading through a relative spectral-flow index; it does not supply this reference-family construction. The near-fold window spectra alone would not constitute the main novelty, particularly in light of math_61/v1's action-filtered categories.

They do not independently prove the mapping-space computation, infinite-dimensional spectral-flow statements or heat-kernel theorem; those conclusions rest on the manuscript's arguments. The communication is sufficiently clear for readers in the field. The question, reference construction, proof strategy and limitations are identifiable, and the proof is organized by mathematical dependence rather than experiment chronology. The following corrections would improve precision without changing the decision:

1. **Action sign in §8.** The cited math_46/v1, Theorem A(2), uses upward gradient flow and $\Delta\mathcal A=\mathcal A(k_-)-\mathcal A(k_+)$. Here the action is described with the opposite sign. Explicitly define the new action as the negative of the cited one, or retain the cited convention. The positive gap and one-object window conclusion survive this correction.
2. **Structural assumptions.** Section 2 explains (S1) as an extended-Hessian property, whereas §11 places its non-isotropic version under “Conditional.” Make that status consistent in Theorems C and D. The general bounded symmetric multiplication statement follows from the asserted differential structure, not from the isotropic matrix checks. A short explicit formula or precise reference would make the distinction easier to audit.
3. **Analytic notation.** In (H1), state the Mellin formula on the complement of the small-eigenvalue projection, or assume invertibility; the displayed unprojected formula need not converge at infinity when a kernel is present. The actual proof in §7.2 already removes these modes. Also replace “kernel is constant” in B(5) by “kernel dimension is constant,” since the radial kernel functions depend on $q$.
4. **References and scope.** The “unpublished companion” is available in this snapshot as math_67/v1. Cite it explicitly and distinguish its stated all-length result for nontrivial left quotients from Proposition 6.1's hypothesis for general spherical space forms. Add math_61/v1 to the discussion of action windows. Use consistent numbered references and shorten the metadata abstract's theorem-by-theorem enumeration if desired; neither issue obscures the scientific argument.

[1] M. Bleher, *A Family of Instanton-Invariants for Four-Manifolds and Their Relation to Khovanov Homology*, SIGMA 22 (2026), 028, https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13285.
