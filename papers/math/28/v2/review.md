# Accept

This is an appropriate revision of the accepted paper. It improves attribution and the hierarchy of results without introducing a new substantive contribution or changing the mathematical argument, numerical bounds, or certificates. The main text remains focused on the strengthened collision inequality, its exclusion of the earlier feasible point, and the rational certificates establishing global upper bounds.

The expanded attribution in Sections 1 and 8 correctly identifies the same-letter power-difference argument in the fourth-moment proof of [1, Proposition 3.1]. Checking that source confirms the grouping after Cauchy–Schwarz over complementary coordinates. The revised explanation distinguishes its use in the present collision argument without claiming that grouping itself is new. Leading with the exponent $589903/1000008$ also clarifies the relationship between the strongest result and the original tree used for the parametric envelope. Appendix B preserves the previous general-degree proof verbatim, so this relocation improves organization without expanding the contribution.

Two local factual corrections remain necessary, but neither undermines the accepted results:

1. **Comparison provenance near Theorem 1.2.** The newly cited version 2 of [2] does publish $59/120$ and $71/130$, and its supplement confirms that additional fixed-budget trees were omitted from its formal claims. However, [2, Theorem 1.2(c)] reports main-tree envelope values $E_1(1.05)=89/140$ and $E_1(1.10)=6239/9170$, not $982/1565$ and $197/313$. Correct this parenthetical attribution or identify a different precise source for the latter values. Retain the distinction between the earlier computational benchmarks $113/180$, $356/553$ and published certified bounds; the supplied material for [2] does not independently establish those two benchmark values. The present paper's independently certified upper bounds remain stronger than the actual published envelope values.

2. **Dependency explanation in Appendix A.** The added claim about guarded imports is incorrect for the distributed scripts. `engine.py` imports NumPy and `scipy.optimize.linprog` unconditionally; `solve.py` loads that engine and imports NumPy. There are no claimed `try/except` guards, and regeneration is not gracefully skipped without these dependencies. The correct explanation is simpler: the certificate verification scripts do not load the generation engine. Inspection of `check.py`, `parameters.py`, and `verify_grid.py` supports their standard-library-only verification path. Replace the inaccurate mechanism with this explanation.

The supplementary files are unchanged from the accepted version. I inspected the dependencies relevant to the revised reproducibility statement but did not rerun the unchanged certificate computations; their reproduction is documented in the previous review. These corrections concern source attribution and documentation, not a regression in the central proof or its retained evidence.
