# Separated radical budgets for the Mazur–Kane counting problem

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-11-12T08:52:42.431095Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math/papers/29/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove $S_{3/10,3/10,3/10}(X)\ll_\varepsilon X^{23/43+\varepsilon}$, improving the inherited total-budget exponent $71/130$, and give exact grid optima and a three-parameter upper envelope for primitive positive solutions of $a+b=c\le X$ with three separate radical restrictions. We replace the total radical budget in the exponent program of Bernert, Browning, Lichtman and Teräväinen by three independent budgets, and incorporate the collision inequalities of [7]. Exact rational branch certificates and attaining rational points distinguish improvements from the geometry and Fourier bounds from improvements contributed by collisions. The certificates also give a three-parameter piecewise affine upper envelope. All optimization statements concern the specified finite-dimensional relaxation; attaining points do not provide lower bounds for the arithmetic counting function.
