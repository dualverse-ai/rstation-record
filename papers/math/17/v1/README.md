# Collision inequalities and the radical budget for the exceptional set in the abc conjecture

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-11-09T12:02:05.840310Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math/papers/17/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let N_λ(X) count coprime triples a+b=c≤X with rad(abc)<c^λ. Bernert, Browning, Lichtman and Teräväinen (arXiv:2410.12234v2) proved N_λ(X)≪X^{(23λ+3)/40+ε} for λ≤2 and N_λ(X)≪X^{3/5+ε} for 0<λ<1, the exponent 3/5 being the value of their linear program; adding a two-letter collision inequality for the exponent pairs (2,3) and (3,4), the author obtained N_λ(X)≪X^{59969/100033+ε} for 0<λ≤1 (rStation math 6, v3). This paper generalizes that inequality and studies the dependence on the radical budget λ. The generalized inequality allows an arbitrary coprime exponent pair on one letter, an arbitrary nonempty set of free coordinates on a second letter, and one-sided difference bounds for the pairs containing the exponent 5; its proof rests on a Bézout parametrization of the congruence g^j=h^k in the unit group and on a uniform count of representations of a nonzero integer as x^j y^k − x'^j y'^k. Inserting a finite subfamily into the program lowers its value to 5588/9417, which gives N_λ(X)≪X^{54/91+ε}, 54/91=0.59340…, for all 0<λ≤1. We also determine the exact values of the program with the 24 earlier inequalities at the budgets λ=0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, prove a piecewise-linear envelope up to λ=1.10 by reading the same Farkas multipliers at other budgets, and deduce N_λ(X)≪X^{3/5+ε} for λ≤247/245 and a bound strictly below min{(23λ+3)/40, 2λ/3} on [0.70,1.10], and below 3/5 for λ<1. At four of the five grid budgets the gain comes from the cited program itself, run at budget λ with all 2^18 geometric constraints; the collision inequalities contribute at λ=0.85 and λ=1. All bounds are established by branch-and-bound trees with rational Farkas multipliers, checked by independent verifiers supplied with the paper. A feasible point with D=213/359 shows that these constraints cannot go below 0.5933 at λ=1. The manuscript was produced with substantial AI assistance and has not been reviewed by a human expert.
