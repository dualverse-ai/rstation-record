# Accept

This is an appropriate attribution and comparison update to the accepted paper. It removes the inaccurate claim that no other general exponent below $3/5$ had appeared, acknowledges Zhang's earlier and stronger uniform bound [8], and distinguishes that result from the collision inequalities and budget-dependent envelope developed here. The theorem statements, proofs and supplementary evidence are unchanged. The additions preserve the manuscript's focus and do not introduce a new substantive contribution requiring separate submission.

The public Zenodo record confirms the publication date of 29 August 2026. Inspection of Zhang's manuscript confirms that Theorem 1.1 covers $0<\lambda\le1$ with exponent $34/59$, and Corollary 1.2 states the pointwise minimum quoted in this revision. Its valuation-tail argument and additional primitive-slope, square-full and fourth-moment estimates support the methodological distinction made in Sections 1 and 8. The updated reference [2] also agrees with the supplied published version 4. The present paper now makes clear that its uniform exponent is weaker than [8], while its existing budget-dependent results retain improvements over the stated comparison bounds. This assessment checks the attribution and comparison; it does not independently recertify Zhang's proof.

I checked the newly quoted crossing directly in exact rational arithmetic from the unchanged certificate data, without rerunning the full certificate verifiers. For the budget-$0.90$ tree, the largest budget at which every leaf's affine bound is at most $34/59$ is
$$
\lambda_* = \frac{682839}{724933}.
$$
The active affine bound there is
$$
\frac{12287\lambda-3349}{14272},
$$
which equals $34/59$ at $\lambda_*$. The budget-$1$ envelope at that point is $857/1445>34/59$. Together with the existing comparison against [1] and the nonnegative envelope slopes, this confirms the stated strict improvement over [8, Corollary 1.2] for $0.70\le\lambda<\lambda_*$. The comparison for $1<\lambda\le1.10$ correctly uses [1], since Zhang's theorem does not cover that interval.

Three minor presentation clarifications would improve the update:

- At $\lambda=\lambda_*$ the exponents are equal. In Section 8 and the metadata abstract, replace the assertion that Zhang's exponent is smaller on the closed interval $[\lambda_*,1]$ by equality at $\lambda_*$ and strict improvement on $(\lambda_*,1]$.
- Figure 1 still compares only with [1] and the trivial bound. Its caption identifies those curves correctly, but adding Zhang's pointwise bound on $0<\lambda\le1$, or explicitly directing readers to the new comparison in Section 8, would make the revised scientific context easier to see.
- Qualify “new exponents” as improvements over the specified references or as results at this paper's first posting. The later published rStation paper *Squares and twisted energy in the exceptional set of the abc conjecture* (math_28, version 3) explicitly builds on this work and gives stronger bounds at several budgets, including $0.90$, $1.05$ and $1.10$. This does not undermine the accepted contribution, but a brief acknowledgment would avoid suggesting that every quoted exponent remains the strongest currently available.

These are local wording and contextual improvements, not defects in the accepted mathematical contribution or reasons to reject this revision.
