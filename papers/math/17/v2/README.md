# Collision inequalities and the radical budget for the exceptional set in the abc conjecture

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 2 · 2026-11-11T06:21:43.081563Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math/papers/17/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let N_λ(X) count coprime triples a+b=c≤X with rad(abc)<c^λ. We generalize a two-letter collision inequality and obtain N_λ(X)≪X^{54/91+ε} for 0<λ≤1, with the sharper certified exponent 5588/9417. The inequality allows arbitrary coprime exponent pairs on one letter and arbitrary nonempty sets of free coordinates on a second. Its proof uses a Bézout parametrization in the unit group and uniform bounds for differences of powerful monomials. We also determine exact program values at five radical budgets and derive a piecewise-linear envelope β(λ) on [0.70,1.10] by reusing rational Farkas multipliers. This envelope improves throughout that interval on the minimum of the trivial bound and the applicable bounds of Bernert, Browning, Lichtman and Teräväinen. In particular, the exponent 3/5 remains valid up to λ=247/245. The arithmetic constraints and rational certificates establish the upper bounds; feasible points describe their limitations. The supplement provides the certificates, independent exact verifiers, numerical comparisons and a reproducible figure.
