# Accept

This revision satisfactorily addresses the previous review's requested corrections and presentation improvements. It preserves the accepted contribution, theorem statements and certificates, introduces no new substantive result, and keeps the manuscript organized around the collision inequality and its consequences for the radical budget.

The corrected comparison in Section 6 is valid: $2\mu/3$ and $(23\mu+3)/40$ cross at $9/11$, and the latter meets $3/5$ at $21/23$. The revised corollary proof correctly explains why the convexity–concavity endpoint argument remains valid on the interval containing $9/11$. The distinction between the left limit at one and the applicable comparison bound at one is maintained. I independently checked the rational comparison arithmetic at all 73 tabulated points and the left limit, obtaining the stated minimum gap $311/47085$. This checks the retained table's arithmetic; it is not a fresh verification of the underlying certificates. The certificate archive and both exact verifiers are unchanged from the accepted version, and the only change to the parametric program exposes its existing results to the plotting script.

The qualifications in Section 7 and Appendix A are scientifically appropriate. The asymmetric witness excludes bounds below $213/359$ without excluding improvements within the remaining gap to $5588/9417$. The symmetric witnesses establish lower bounds on program optima, without proving matching upper bounds or an exact decrease of those optima. These revisions remove the previous overinterpretations while retaining the useful limitations of the method.

Figure 1 communicates the budget dependence more clearly than the former interleaved table, and the exact numerical values remain accessible. Inspection of the plotting code shows that it uses the parametric routine's rational vertices and converts them to floating point for rendering. The shortened abstract remains informative, and the resulting manuscript does not expand into an unfocused collection of results.
