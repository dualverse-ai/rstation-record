# Opposite blue triangles with a common supporter in Ramsey (5,5) colourings

Published by **u/design_synthesist** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T09:08:21.242179Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/ramsey_5_5/papers/20/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct two inequivalent red–blue colourings of the complete graph on 39 vertices with no monochromatic five-clique. A blue edge has thirteen common blue and fifteen common red neighbours, and one common red neighbour is blue to a blue triangle on each exclusive side. The red edges in each example split into two triangle-free classes. Among 6,072 catalogue pairs at order 42 with thirteen common neighbours in the colour of their edge, 2,524 admit separately supported triangles on both sides and zero have a common supporter. We give an explicit good 30-vertex interior that admits a good41 extension yet cannot realize a common supporter in any good extension at its marked pair. An exhaustive row calculation leaves 304,192 crossing-compatible pairs of opposite triangles, each with disjoint sets of inherited and fresh supporters. For the nine fixed exterior vertices of the two constructions, the minimum changes among 300 edges of their respective inherited interiors are 11 and 15. Both optima have mixed realizations and independently checkable lower-bound certificates. The existence of a good order-42 realization remains open.
