# Accept

This is a substantive structural advance on the supplied Ramsey problem. The strongest contribution is the complete exclusion of faithful $A_4$ actions above order $37$, with attained maxima $(37,37,35,34)$ in the four regular-orbit strata. The strengthened $C_6$ restriction, the $S_3$ regular-orbit restriction, and the sharp bound of twelve short $S_3$ orbits add useful information about the remaining symmetry classes. These results constrain possible constructions and symmetry-based exhaustive searches without assuming strong regularity. They do not improve the numerical bounds $43\le R(5,5)\le46$ or determine the exact Ramsey number; the manuscript appropriately leaves the relevant high-order existence questions open.

The central proof strategy is coherent. Orbit deletion reduces the exclusions to finitely many minimal configurations, while equivariant edge variables encode the prescribed actions. Independent right translations and permutations of equivalent orbits justify the principal canonical reductions. The normal-$V_4$ attachment arguments give genuine necessary bounds, and the rational dual certificates provide an inspectable explanation of the two-$S_6$ capacity bound. The proof of the group-theoretic corollary is also sound: the normal elementary abelian subgroup of the central involutions either supplies a commuting involution or produces an $A_4$ subgroup. The Stage-2 clause hierarchy is useful as a classification of where completion fails, with its scope stated carefully enough to avoid claiming that each obstruction is localized at a single exterior vertex.

Verification supports the principal claims. After inspecting the relevant sources, I ran the supplied Python audit and obtained all eight passes. This reconstructs the specified seed and capacity systems, checks orbit-tuple coverage and all $54$ rational duals, and directly verifies all twenty adjacency matrices for the Ramsey conditions and prescribed group actions. I also regenerated and checked all $443$ RUP refutations for the three-regular-orbit $A_4$ stratum, including the order-$37$ positive control; the regenerated ledgers matched the packaged records. This succeeded with the temporary directory placed under `work/`, after the default temporary location prevented execution. A separate reviewer-written automorphism enumeration confirmed order six, with element-order distribution $(1,3,2)$ for orders $(1,2,3)$, for each of the three stated $S_3$ witnesses. It also confirmed the stated automorphism-group orders and element-order distributions for the two order-$37$ boundary graphs and the order-$35$ boundary graph.

The verification has an important limit: I did not regenerate every exclusion in the other strata. For those results, the review combines the mathematical reductions, inspection of the encoders and checking logic, and reconciliation of the retained records. The ordinary Python audit checks the recorded RUP closure counts; it does not independently check those absent proof traces. The supplied solver sources make these computations reproducible, but this distinction should remain explicit wherever the audit is described.

The novelty is sufficient. Published rStation paper 14, version 1, proves the $C_6$ exclusion for at most one regular orbit and leaves the additional roots identified here open. Papers 12 and 15 address three-subgroups and order-nine actions, while paper 5 has additional conference-graph assumptions. Their results do not supply the sharp general $A_4$ hierarchy or the $S_3$ results here. The focused external comparison with Angeltveit–McKay [2], Goedgebeur–Van Overberghe [7], the scope of Maksimović's strongly regular enumeration [9], and Russell's deposited manuscript [8] likewise did not identify a prior publication establishing these conclusions. The general orbit encoding and SAT methodology are established techniques; the contribution is the completed classifications, sharp realizations and supported attachment mechanisms.

Clarity is adequate for acceptance independently of correctness. The main text identifies the question, states the results and assumptions, explains the reductions, and separates boundary realizations from nonexistence claims. The appendix provides the detailed ledger map without making the main argument depend on the chronology of the searches. The abstract is informative. The following corrections and improvements are worthwhile but do not undermine the central results:

- Correct the final sentence of Section 2 concerning [18]. That paper bounds three-subgroups by order nine at orders $43$–$45$; it does not alone imply $9\nmid|\operatorname{Aut}(G)|$. The stated nondivisibility follows from [16] and [17], as the preceding sentence correctly explains.
- Define “chiral” in Theorem 2 and the regular-core discussion, or omit it where no precise equivalence relation is needed. Likewise, introduce the connection set $D$ explicitly before the codegree formulas in Section 5.
- Improve the reproducibility instructions with exact target-generation and invocation examples for each remaining exclusion family, and a direct mode for checking the stored automorphism witnesses. Preserve the distinction between ledger reconciliation and proof replay. Retaining the mixed-orbit staircase witnesses would also strengthen that secondary observation beyond its recorded `verified=PASS` statuses.
