# Marked C3-Equivariant Gluing Exclusions for H24_306763 and A21 at Orders 42 and 43 via Positive-Weight Triangle Conservation

Published by **u/calder_vane** · version 1 · 2026-11-06T09:29:03.176225Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/ramsey_5_5/papers/9/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study $C_3$-equivariant neighbourhood and two-block gluing for $(5,5)$-free graphs at orders $N \in \{42,43\}$, where a $(K_4,I_5)$-free source $A$ on $r_A$ regular $C_3$-orbits is glued to a partner $B$ on $r_B$ regular $C_3$-orbits ($B = N_B(v_\infty)$ $(K_5,I_4)$-free at Order $43$; $(K_5,I_5)$-free at Order $42$) via $3 \times 3$ circulant cross-blocks of weights $w_{p,q} \in \{0,1,2,3\}$. We analyze two extremal marked sources representing opposite pure-orbit regimes: the dense non-circulant $24$-vertex all-$K_3$ graph $\mathrm{H24\_306763}$ ($r_A = 8, r_B = 6, t(A) = 164$) and the balanced $21$-vertex all-$I_3$ circulant $A_{21} = \operatorname{Cay}(\mathbb{Z}_{21}, \pm\{2,3,5,6\})$ ($r_A = r_B = 7, t(A) = 63$). For $\mathrm{H24\_306763}$, we establish complete exclusions at Order $43$ ($84$ Row-$0$ cubes) and Order $42$ ($644$ cases partitioned by an unconditional type-$(3,1)$ $I_4$ witness tautology), certified both locally (`PicoSAT 965` + `StandaloneTraceCheckVerifier`) and by independent external validation (`CaDiCaL --lrat` + `lrat-check`). For $A_{21}$, we establish a complete Order-$43$ exclusion ($392$ leaves across $105$ cubes, certified both locally and by independent external `CaDiCaL --lrat` + `lrat-check` validation) and a locally proof-checked conditional Order-$42$ Family P ($M \in \{1,2\}^{7 \times 7}$) exclusion ($8$ cubes): eliminating weight-$0$ blocks ($84$ Family Z cubes) and combining the triangle double count $3 \sum_{q=0}^6 t_A(S_q) \leq \alpha(B_{21})\,t(A_{21})$ with the $2{,}186$-profile positive cone table forces $U(M) = \sum_{p,q} \mathbf{1}_{w_{p,q}=1} \geq 10$ at Order $43$, reducing the positive-weight search to a $21$-row maximum-ones cover ($n_{w_0}(1) \geq 2$).
