# Accept

This is a sound corrective revision of the accepted paper. The substantive change in contribution 6 and Section 7.1 retracts the inference that the combinatorial conditions keep candidate local graphs far from the spectral window. The five supplied witnesses provide limited, directly relevant evidence for that correction. They do not constitute an unrelated expansion or a new central contribution requiring a separate submission. The edge identity and cap-two exclusion remain the paper's principal results, and the resulting manuscript retains a coherent explanation of their relevance to the conference case.

I independently checked all five witness matrices using integer graph calculations and rational elimination. Each is a simple 10-regular graph on 22 vertices, has no $K_4$ or independent 5-set, satisfies the edge-codegree and triangle bounds of Proposition 6.1(i), and satisfies Lemma 6.4 at every vertex. All elimination pivots of $F=11I-X-X^2+10J$ are positive, establishing $F\succ0$ and hence the spectral-window condition. Their exact determinants agree with the retained results and are nonsquares; for example, the first is $251322198322745=5\cdot11^3\cdot79\cdot478030601$. Thus none can have the required square integral factor $F=MM^{\top}$. Each also has a vertex incident with at least three edges of codegree four. These checks substantiate the revised interpretation without reproducing the heuristic searches. No claim that these witnesses satisfy the full hypermetric family is made, which appropriately limits the correction.

The reproducibility changes address the previous review constructively. The missing final lazy-search matrix is now supplied. The Gram-factor program and manuscript distinguish numerical candidate filtering from a certified negative decision, and the program explicitly checks any returned factor. Inspection of the new regeneration driver confirms that it classifies roots, supplies the case-B flag, compares CNF hashes, checks fixed-root clauses, and invokes the solver and proof checker for every selected case. The retained summary reports success for all 16 cases. I have not independently regenerated those refutations or repeated their DRAT checks in this revision review; the accepted encoding and theorem are unchanged.

Minor improvements would make the revision more precise:

- In Section 8, replace “do not exclude $K_4$-free local graphs” with “do not exclude candidate local graphs satisfying the listed conditions.” The witnesses are explicitly proved not to be conference-local graphs. The same distinction would improve the terminology for solver-generated samples elsewhere.
- Shorten the run-by-run description in Section 7.1 by moving the starting-file names and individual run outcomes to the supplement. Retain the search principle, the exact witness checks, and their scientific implication in the main text. The current addition is understandable and does not materially obscure the contribution.
- Make `regen_cap2.py` return a nonzero exit status when a root check, solver result, or proof verification fails. It currently records these failures in its summary but can still exit successfully; the documented summary must therefore be inspected. Keep hash-version differences explicitly distinguished from verification failures.

The revision neither determines $R(5,5)$ nor improves its bounds or excludes the full conference case. Its appropriately limited claims do not change the problem's verified-solution status.
