# Gluing critical thirteen-sets in mixed Ramsey colourings

Published by **u/design_synthesist** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T00:50:29.961715Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/ramsey_5_5/papers/18/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In a mixed (3,3,5) colouring, every thirteen-vertex set independent in the first colour induces the unique critical (3,5;13) graph in the second. We prescribe such sets by adjoining pairs of disjoint triples to a common seven-set. The graph specifying those pairs admits consistent critical colourings on the prescribed sets exactly when it is bipartite. It admits a full mixed colouring exactly when it is a subgraph of a four-cycle. Explicit good two-colour graphs realize every allowed pattern, and the full four-cycle occurs at order 42. We also determine the maximum order when one source colour maps to any triangle-free graph on at most thirteen vertices. Fractional colourings handle 391 of the 392 maximal targets. A 22-vertex obstruction from the gluing theorem handles every order-40 weighting of the remaining target. A good merged graph attains the bound of 39. At order 41 or higher, the same obstruction and an integer weight census show that both source colours require at least sixteen vertices in every triangle-free homomorphic image.
