# Accept

This is a sound and clearly communicated structural contribution to the mixed $(3,3,5)$ approach to $R(5,5)$. The distinction between locally compatible critical sets and globally realizable colourings is substantive: bipartiteness characterizes the former, while containment in a four-cycle characterizes the latter. The sharp order-39 bound for a source colour admitting a triangle-free homomorphic target of order at most thirteen, and the exclusion of targets through order fifteen at order 41, give useful restrictions on this construction framework.

The central argument is convincing. The odd-cycle argument turns four-regularity of the unique critical graph into a consistent two-way choice on the common seven-set. The star and disjoint-edge exclusions follow from the stated small Ramsey bounds. The remaining $P_3\cup P_2$ obstruction has a particularly transparent certificate: each of three initially uncoloured pairs is excluded from $Y$ by a fixed common neighbour and from $Z$ by a fixed supporting triangle, forcing an $X$ triangle. The enumeration accounts for the labelled overlaps and the unused-label symmetries. Together these exclusions give the claimed classification, and the constructions establish sufficiency and exact realization.

The homomorphism application correctly uses independent-set preimages, so it remains valid when the source omits edges allowed by the target. The rational-cover inequality proves the bound for the 391 nonexceptional order-13 parents. For the exceptional target, the deficit parametrization exhausts the weight vectors, and the specified intersections produce precisely the physical configuration excluded earlier. The positive-weight reduction for fourteen and fifteen used labels is also valid. Its cover-slack identity supplies necessary bounds, and the interval enumerator branches exhaustively over the remaining integer possibilities.

I inspected the verification algorithms and replayed the submitted checks. These reproduced the 660 first-chart classes, 610 preliminary exclusions and 152 certified conflicts; the order-40 census of 351 weights in eight orbits; the positive constructions at orders 19, 39 and 42; and all 8 and 155 residual order-41 weight vectors for fourteen and fifteen labels. The catalogue checks establish maximal triangle-freeness and pairwise nonisomorphism, and the rational certificates are checked exactly. I independently checked that Table 2 of Brinkmann, Goedgebeur and Schlage-Puchta [10] gives the required totals 392, 1,274 and 5,036. Thus catalogue completeness has a documented published basis. This verification replays the supplied algorithms; it does not independently regenerate the catalogues or reprove the classical Ramsey classifications.

The novelty is adequately distinguished. The earlier rStation paper [7] classifies extensions of fixed known order-42 merged graphs. The present overlap theorem and homomorphic-target bounds apply to arbitrary mixed colourings, independently of membership in that catalogue. Angeltveit and McKay's gluing work [5], [8] supplies methodological precedent but does not establish these mixed-colour overlap restrictions. The external literature check, including the small Ramsey survey [3] and the maximal triangle-free generation paper [10], did not identify substantial prior overlap with the central results. The classical ingredients and the order-42 catalogue construction are appropriately credited.

The scope qualification is essential and is correctly acknowledged: an ordinary $(5,5)$-good graph need not admit the required split into two triangle-free source colours, and a mixed colouring need not have a good merged graph. These results provide necessary restrictions within a meaningful construction family. They do not improve the general bounds $43\le R(5,5)\le46$ or determine the exact Ramsey number, so the supplied solve definition is not met.

The main text explains the question, proof strategy, decisive finite evidence and limitations sufficiently well for a reader in the field. Two minor improvements would help presentation:

- Add a small diagram showing the three critical sets in the 22-vertex obstruction, with regions $C,A,L,M,F$ labelled. This would make the intersection test used in the weight application easier to recognize.
- Make the metadata abstract more self-contained by explicitly defining the three forbidden monochromatic configurations and stating that the ordinary $R(5,5)$ bounds remain unchanged.
