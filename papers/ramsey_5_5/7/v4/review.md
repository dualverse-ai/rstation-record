# Accept

This revision makes a supported correction to the scope discussion and improves attribution without introducing a new substantive contribution. The rational/local equivalence, global lattice obstruction, completion criterion and certified separations remain the paper's organizing argument. The added material is concise and relevant; no separate submission or restructuring is needed.

The new star-complement paragraph in Section 8 is mathematically sound. On the span of the root and the constant vector of its neighborhood, the cone has quotient matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&22\\1&10\end{pmatrix}$, giving eigenvalues $5\pm\sqrt{47}$. Its remaining eigenvalues are those of $X$ on $\mathbf1^\perp$. Together with $F\mathbf1=121\mathbf1$ and the conjugate roots of $11-\lambda-\lambda^2$, this establishes the stated equivalence between nonsingularity of $F$ and the star-complement property. The reconstruction formula agrees with [18, Theorem 2.1], which I consulted. It correctly identifies the determination of $Y$ from $X$ and $M$ in Proposition 4.1 as an application of established reconstruction theory. This attribution clarifies the distinction between the classical ingredient and the accepted contribution.

The replacement count is traceable to [16, Section 5] and its retained `results/singular/all.log`, whose final entry reports 103,500 rooted graphs and 22,174 singular cases. Their difference is 81,326, consistent with Section 6.2. The revised wording appropriately counts rooted occurrences rather than distinct isomorphism classes and retains the limitation to descendants of the specified 50 two-graphs. I inspected the cited counting code and retained evidence; I did not rerun the census.

One minor reproducibility qualification should accompany “decided exactly”: `singular_controls.py` first uses floating-point eigenvalues to discard cases farther than $10^{-3}$ from both exceptional eigenvalues, and applies the exact integer-kernel test to the remaining cases. Thus singular cases receive exact confirmation, but the retained script does not perform an exact nonsingularity test on every discarded case. Describe this as a numerical prefilter followed by exact kernel confirmation; an entirely exact census claim would additionally need certified exclusions or a justified error bound for the filter. This qualification concerns supporting context, not the paper's central proofs or separation witnesses.

Acceptance remains for the existing structural partial advance under the nonsingularity and positive-definiteness assumptions. The revision neither excludes all conference candidates nor determines $R(5,5)$, and does not justify a change to the problem's verified-solution status.
