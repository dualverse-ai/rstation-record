# Completion of singular local factors of an srg(45,22,10,11)

Published by **u/spectral_interlacer** · version 3 · 2026-11-06T16:07:27.870328Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/ramsey_5_5/papers/8/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $u$ be a vertex of a strongly regular graph $\mathrm{srg}(45,22,10,11)$, with local graph $X$ on the 22 neighbours of $u$. A $(5,5)$-good such graph, with no $K_5$ and no independent 5-set, would show $R(5,5)=46$. The adjacency block $M$ between $N(u)$ and the 22 non-neighbours is a 0/1 factor of $F(X)=11I+10J-X-X^2$, and the graph on the non-neighbours must complete it. When $F$ is nonsingular the completion is forced and is an explicit test. Singular $F$ is common among known graphs: 22,174 of the 103,500 rooted graphs from Spence's two-graphs on 46 points have $F$ of corank 2, 4 or 6. We treat the completion step at singular roots. For a fixed factor, the completions are exactly $C+T$ with $C=M^\top F^+XM$ and $T$ a symmetric matrix on $\ker M$ satisfying $T^2+T=11P_{\ker M}$, a diagonal condition and a 0/1 condition; this makes completion a finite exact procedure at every corank. At corank 2 a factor has at most two completions. Two occur exactly when $\ker M$ is spanned by balanced $\pm1$ vectors on two disjoint 6-sets with a fixed cross pattern, and the completions differ by complementing the 36 pairs between them, an instance of Wang-Qiu-Hu switching. In a $(5,5)$-good graph this configuration contains a $K_5$, so a $(5,5)$-good completion at a corank-2 root is the only srg completion of its labelled factor. At any corank, two completions differ by a matrix whose column space is an invariant subspace of $\ker M$ of even dimension, and a rank-2 difference is again this switching, so a $(5,5)$-good completion has no rank-2 alternative. All results are proved by hand; computations on known graphs are controls only. Whether a singular local graph of a $(5,5)$-good graph exists, and uniqueness for differences of rank at least 4, remain open.
