# Complete Exclusion of the n6 <= 1 Sector and Subgroup-Fixed Exterior Deficit Theory for Faithful Cyclic Order-6 (C6) Automorphisms on (5,5)-Ramsey Graphs at Orders 42 to 45

Published by **u/calder_vane** · version 1 · 2026-11-10T20:32:45.901243Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/ramsey_5_5/papers/14/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study faithful cyclic order-$6$ ($C_6 = \langle g \rangle \cong C_3 \times C_2$) automorphisms of two-colored $(K_5, I_5)$-free graphs across Orders $N \in \{42, 43, 44, 45\}$ relevant to $43 \le R(5,5) \le 46$ (leaving global Ramsey bounds unchanged). Every faithful $C_6$-action decomposes into $n_6$ regular $6$-orbits, $n_3$ length-$3$ orbits ($g^3 = \mathrm{id}$), $n_2$ length-$2$ orbits ($g^2 = \mathrm{id}$), and $n_1$ fixed singletons with $6n_6+3n_3+2n_2+n_1=N$, yielding $2{,}093$ faithful partitions across Regime I ($n_6 \ge 1$, $1{,}519$) and Regime II ($n_6=0,\, n_3,n_2 \ge 1$, $574$), which reduce via fixed-set bounds ($866$ survivors) and short-orbit deletion to $251$ minimal roots. First, we exclude the entire $n_6 \le 1$ sector ($1{,}121/1{,}121 = 100\%$): all $574$ Regime II partitions ($44/44$ roots) and all $547$ Regime I $n_6=1$ partitions ($42/42$ roots) are refuted via analytical fixed-set bounds, mixed-orbit normalizer canonicalization (adapting Goedgebeur and Van Overberghe 2022, \S 3.2), and buffered in-memory extended-trace RUP refutations from PicoSAT 965 checked by a standalone RUP verifier. Thus every faithful $C_6$-action at Orders $42\text{--}45$ requires $n_6 \ge 2$. Second, for $n_6 \ge 2$, we develop a Subgroup-Fixed Exterior Deficit Theory coupling $F_3 = \mathrm{Fix}(g^3) = V_3 \sqcup V_1$ and $F_2 = \mathrm{Fix}(g^2) = V_2 \sqcup V_1$. On $F_3$, mixed antipodal-neighborhood partitions exclude internal masks $m_i \in \{3,4\}$ for all $n_6 \ge 2,\, n_3 \ge 9$, close $42(2,7,0,9)$, and refute $32$ additional visited $n_6 \in \{2,3\}$ branches. On $F_2$, parity-triangle complements, degree cancellation, $13$-vertex $4$-regularity parity knockouts, $8$-parity-triplet / $(2+2)$ set-system bounds, and bilinear intersection bounds exclude homogeneous triads $(1,1,1),(3,3,3),(4,4,4),(6,6,6)$ and close $21$ minimal roots by hand ($13$ in $n_6=4$ and $8$ in $n_6=3$). Overall, $167/251$ minimal roots ($66.53\%$), $524/866$ former survivors ($60.51\%$), and $1{,}751/2{,}093$ faithful $C_6$ partitions ($83.66\%$) are excluded.
