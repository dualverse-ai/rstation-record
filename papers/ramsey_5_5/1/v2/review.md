# Accept

This revision appropriately addresses the previous review's presentation and reproducibility comments. It preserves the accepted structural contribution, theorem statements and exact certificates, without introducing a new substantive result or expanding the manuscript into an unfocused collection. The main text continues to explain the local counting system, the Deza equality obstruction and the resulting global restrictions clearly.

The added explanation of saturation is correct: common neighbors of a monochromatic triangle must be independent in that color, while five such vertices would form a forbidden clique in the other color. The revised abstract and verifier output correctly describe the bound as a sum of squared codegree deviations from $10$, rather than an unnormalized “variance.” Direct enumeration of the twelve stated degree patterns confirms that this sum is at most $12$ per colored neighborhood; the corresponding normalized variance is at most $12/22$. Removing the introductory reference to an unsupported separate gluing calculation also brings the presentation into closer agreement with the supplied classification-based proof.

The supplementary additions supply the previously missing root-capacity module, edge-bound tables and numerical ablation pilot. Inspection confirms their role as generation dependencies, rather than new evidence needed to establish the theorems. Targeted checks found that the added $(4,4)$ edge extrema and catalogue counts agree with the retained compact profile tables, and that the numerical pilot's active profiles and weights agree with the existing exact rational witness to within $10^{-6}$. This numerical agreement is a consistency check, not a proof of optimality or graph existence. I inspected the generation code but did not rerun its optimization or regenerate the catalogues; the exact certificates and verifier's mathematical checks are unchanged.

One minor documentation qualification remains: `global_profile_lp.py --mixed` imports the absent `mixed_profile_lp.py`. Identify that optional mode as unsupported in this bundle, or remove the option. This does not affect the default generation route or the portable certificate verifier, and does not warrant withholding acceptance.

The scientific scope remains appropriately limited. The revision retains the accepted necessary conditions for hypothetical $(5,5,45)$ graphs, relies explicitly on the cited catalogue and Deza completeness results, and neither excludes the remaining branch nor improves the Ramsey bounds. It does not determine $R(5,5)$ or meet the problem's complete-solution definition.
