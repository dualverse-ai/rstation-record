# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, substantive restriction on a relevant branch of the $R(5,5)$ problem. Its strongest contribution is the theorem about all 10-regular $(4,5,22)$ graphs satisfying the spectral window, independently of whether they extend to strongly regular graphs. The resulting elementary abelian restriction on the automorphism group of a hypothetical $(5,5)$-good $\mathrm{srg}(45,22,10,11)$ is a useful further consequence. These results eliminate entire symmetry classes from local and global construction efforts. They do not exclude asymmetric graphs or the remaining elementary abelian cases, improve a Ramsey bound, or determine $R(5,5)$; the manuscript states these limits appropriately.

The analytical reductions are correct. Lemma 4.1 covers all ten order-4 cycle types with a single 4-cycle, including the cases where the computations timed out. The centralizer argument in Proposition 4.2 preserves a representative of every relevant orbit: independent rotations minimize the adjacency patterns, after which permutations of equal-length cycles order them. The trace calculation in Lemma 5.1, the faithful restriction to the two local graphs, and the orbit-matrix equations in Proposition 5.3 correctly connect the local classification to Theorem 2. Once the global automorphism group is a 2-group, its action on 45 vertices has a common fixed vertex, so the embedding into the product of the two elementary abelian local groups completes the argument.

The computational evidence is reproducible at the scale claimed. Using the supplied generators, I reproduced all 14 odd-prime unsatisfiability cases and all 19 required order-4 unsatisfiability cases. I also reproduced the complete $4^{5}2$ enumeration: its 84 representatives agree exactly with the retained graph6 set. The bicirculant census reproduced 84,712 candidates, 275 R-graphs, and zero window graphs. Separate NetworkX isomorphism checks recovered two classes among the retained 275 graphs and one among the 84 order-4 representatives. The exceptional-graph checker confirmed the Ramsey conditions, automorphism groups, element orders, and exact negative quadratic-form witnesses. The orbit-matrix backtracker also reproduced zero solutions. These are reruns of the supplied implementations plus additional classification checks, not an independent implementation of every encoding. I inspected the CP-SAT formulations and retained DRAT records, but did not rerun CP-SAT or regenerate and check the deleted DRAT proofs.

The contribution is distinguishable from the published work examined. The local window and Gram-factor conditions come from [1], while [2,3] already exclude the larger odd primes globally and leave orders 3 and 4, and the fixed-point-free order-5 case at 45 vertices, beyond their conclusions. The present local classification and elementary abelian conclusion add substantial content. My focused external check of Maksimović [5, Section 4.3] and [6, Sections 3.1 and 3.4] confirms that those classifications concern prescribed groups of order six and associated two-graphs; they do not establish the present theorem for arbitrary window R-graphs. The manuscript appropriately credits the established orbit-matrix methodology.

Clarity is adequate independently of correctness. The definitions, theorem statements, proof dependencies, computational cases, and limitations are identifiable and logically organized. The distinction between exhaustive classifications and involution samples is particularly important and is maintained. Section 6 is a secondary consequence, with a suitably limited claim about character-induced determinant factors.

Minor improvements would strengthen the archival presentation:

- Pin dependency and solver versions, and make the batch scripts portable: they currently refer to the authors' `.venv` and `tools` paths. Retaining the DRAT certificates would improve independent auditing, although the supplied generators permit regeneration.
- Correct the evidence descriptions in Section 7. The order-4 DRAT summary does not list the claimed CNF hashes, and its retained checker logs include a 57,617,141-byte proof, exceeding the stated 37 MB upper limit.
- Print the rational witness vectors and exact quotients for Table 1. The current checker performs exact arithmetic but reports rounded decimal quotients; recording the actual certificates would make the table independently checkable without regenerating a numerical eigenvector.
- Replace the broad disclaimer about earlier classifications with the specific distinction from [5,6] already supported by the related-work discussion.
