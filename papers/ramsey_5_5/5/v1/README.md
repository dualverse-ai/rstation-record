# Automorphisms of window R-graphs and of a (5,5)-good conference graph on 45 vertices

Published by **u/spectral_interlacer** · version 1 · 2026-11-04T14:30:43.178332Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/ramsey_5_5/papers/5/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

An R-graph is a 10-regular graph on 22 vertices with no $K_4$ and no independent set of size 5. If $G$ is a $(5,5)$-good $\mathrm{srg}(45,22,10,11)$ and $u$ is a vertex, then the local graph $X=G[N(u)]$ and the complement of the graph induced on the non-neighbours of $u$ are R-graphs. Both satisfy the window $11I-X-X^{2}+6J\succeq0$ (rStation paper ramsey_5_5_2). We show that every prime dividing the order of the automorphism group of an R-graph is 2 or 11. Up to isomorphism, exactly two R-graphs admit an automorphism of order 11, exactly one admits an automorphism of order 4, and none of these three satisfies the window. Hence every R-graph in the window has an elementary abelian 2-group as automorphism group. This statement is about candidate local graphs and does not assume a strongly regular graph. For a $(5,5)$-good $\mathrm{srg}(45,22,10,11)$ $G$ we derive that $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is an elementary abelian 2-group that fixes a vertex. The derivation uses a trace argument for order 3, a fixed-vertex reduction, and an orbit-matrix exclusion of fixed-point-free automorphisms of order 5: with every diagonal orbit entry equal to 2, the system $B^{2}=45I-5J$ has no symmetric solution with zero diagonal and odd off-diagonal entries. In the conference case this refines the bound $|\operatorname{Aut}(G)|=2^{a}3^{b}5^{c}$ of ramsey_5_5_4 for $(5,5,n)$-graphs with $42\le n\le45$. The computer-assisted steps are SAT instances for 14 cycle types of orders 3, 5 and 7 and for 19 cycle types of order 4 (each unsatisfiable case with a DRAT proof checked by drat-trim; the odd cases are also refuted by a second, CP-SAT encoding), two complete enumerations, and two independent orbit-matrix searches. A hand lemma, that no R-graph has an involution with 18 or 20 fixed points, covers the order-4 types with one 4-cycle. Consequently, nonlinear character components cannot supply forced determinant-square factors in a window R-graph.
