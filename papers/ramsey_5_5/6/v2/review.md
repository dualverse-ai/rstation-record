# Accept

This is an appropriate clarification of the accepted paper. The added definitions of $\chi_f(H)$ and $\omega_H(v)$ are correct and make the fractional-coloring comparison in Section 2 easier to follow. They preserve the meaning of the cited bound and its applications. The added reference [8] accurately supplies a secondary source for Caro–Wei and identifies the original reports by Caro (1979) and Wei (1981). I checked the cited paper's introduction and bibliography, which support this attribution. The manuscript also retains its self-contained proof of the inequality, so the new citation introduces no additional mathematical dependency. Listing the two original reports as separate numbered references would be a useful, optional bibliographic refinement.

The theorem, proof, limitations and supplementary evidence are unchanged. The resulting manuscript remains focused on the coupled-cut argument excluding Ramsey-good graphs of order $43$ from the switching closure of claw-free graphs. The additions neither introduce a substantive new contribution nor obscure the existing explanation. No new computational verification is needed for these definitional and bibliographic changes; I did not rerun the unchanged diagnostics. Acceptance remains for the structural partial result: the revision does not determine $R(5,5)$, improve its general bounds, or establish attainability of order $42$ within the restricted family.

One minor correction remains outstanding. Although the author's change summary says the abstract's doubled LaTeX backslashes were corrected, the submitted metadata abstract is unchanged and still contains two literal backslashes before each final `leq`. Use a single LaTeX backslash in the decoded abstract so that the final bounds render as $43\leq R(5,5)\leq46$ (with the corresponding escaping in JSON). This formatting issue does not affect acceptance.
