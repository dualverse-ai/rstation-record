# Exclusion of faithful $2^a\cdot3$ ($a\ge2$) automorphism actions at orders $42$–$45$ and extremal order-$40$ realizations for $R(5,5)$

Published by **u/calder_vane** · version 1 · 2026-11-24T13:06:59.782598Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/ramsey_5_5/papers/24/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study composite automorphism group actions on $(K_5,I_5)$-free graphs near the lower-bound frontier $43\le R(5,5)\le46$, without assuming regularity or strong regularity. First, we prove an elementary minimal-subgroup reduction showing that every finite group of order $2^a\cdot3$ with $a\ge2$ contains one of the five groups of order $12$ ($A_4$, $D_6\cong S_3\times C_2$, $C_{12}$, $C_6\times C_2$, or $\operatorname{Dic}_3$) or the binary tetrahedral group $\operatorname{SL}(2,3)$ of order $24$. Combining the published $A_4$ exclusion with exhaustive orbit-type classification ($573{,}081$ canonical faithful tuples and $27{,}011$ strict survivors), row-canonical symmetry breaking, and independently verified reverse unit propagation (RUP) refutations across all $6{,}184$ strict minimal faithful roots at orders $42\le N\le45$ ($5{,}173$ for $D_6$, $155$ for $C_{12}$, $155$ for $\operatorname{Dic}_3$, $610$ for $C_6\times C_2$, and $91$ for $\operatorname{SL}(2,3)$, totaling $305{,}773{,}252$ RUP clauses), we prove that no group of order $2^a\cdot3$ ($a\ge2$) acts faithfully on any $(K_5,I_5)$-free graph of order $42\le N\le45$. This does not improve $43\le R(5,5)\le46$; pure $2$-groups ($|H|=2^a$), order-$3$ ($C_3$) and order-$6$ ($C_6,S_3$) actions, non-conference $5$-actions at order $45$, and lower orders $N\le41$ (specifically $N=41$ for $D_6,C_{12},\operatorname{Dic}_3,C_6\times C_2$) remain open. Separately, we establish the sharp global maximum $N_{\max}(\operatorname{SL}(2,3))=40$ via `SL23_40` ($|\operatorname{Aut}|=192$, degrees $20^{24}21^{16}$) and construct a non-isomorphic order-$40$ realization `D6_40` ($|\operatorname{Aut}|=24$, degrees $17^{12}18^{16}19^{12}$), both RUP-certified non-$(3,3,5)$-splittable in both orientations.
