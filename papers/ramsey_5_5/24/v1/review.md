# Accept

This is a meaningful structural advance toward the stated Ramsey problem, although it neither determines $R(5,5)$ nor improves its numerical bounds. The main contribution is the exclusion of an entire family of automorphism subgroups, rather than another isolated orbit configuration. Combined with the cited prime-order and order-nine exclusions, it restricts the full automorphism group at orders $42$–$44$ to a $2$-group, $C_3$, $C_6$, or $S_3$. The sharp $\operatorname{SL}(2,3)$ cutoff and the two distinct order-$40$ witnesses provide useful boundary information. The manuscript correctly distinguishes these results from a global nonexistence proof.

The mathematical reduction is convincing. Lemma 1 accounts for the central-extension case of order $48$, which is essential to reducing arbitrarily large groups of order $2^a\cdot3$ to the six stated targets. Lemma 2 supplies a valid fixed-point bound: the three relevant attachment classes have capacities $4$, $3$, and $13$. The treatment of a nonnormal order-three subgroup, including the six-vertex transitive case, avoids assuming an unjustified permutation of its orbits. The orbit taxonomy, kernel-intersection criterion for faithfulness, and hereditary reductions give a coherent finite coverage argument. The order-$41$ descendant check is explicitly included in the sharp-cutoff argument. The catalogue-overlap bounds also follow from the displayed edge-count inequalities.

The contribution is distinct from the closest published work. Paper [3] establishes the $A_4$ exclusion and the $C_6/S_3$ orbit bounds used here, but does not exclude the other five target groups. Papers [4]–[6] supply complementary prime-power restrictions. The mixed-extension classification in [20] addresses a different completion problem. A focused external check of Angeltveit–McKay [2] and Russell's public write-up [15] likewise found different results: the global upper bound and prescribed prime-order exclusions/local rigidity, respectively. The orbit-variable SAT method itself is established; the new value here lies in the complete composite-group exclusion and its structural consequence, supported by the extremal witnesses.

The computational evidence is reproducible, with minor build corrections noted below. I ran the standalone audit successfully, checking the seven witnesses, their stated automorphism counts and actions, the catalogue invariants, and the tuple/ledger accounting. I also regenerated and RUP-checked selected exclusions: the $\operatorname{SL}(2,3)$ order-$41$ descendant, the documented $C_{12}$ and $D_6$ order-$42$ examples, and the harder $D_6$ tuple $(0,2,5,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$. The last run exhausted all $3{,}867$ branches with no unknown cases. Separately, I reproduced the splitting checks for both order-$40$ witnesses and all $328$ catalogue representatives, and all $25+101$ subproblems for the zero-, one-, and two-flip extension exclusions. These checks agree with the reported conclusions.

This verification was selective: I did not regenerate all $6{,}184$ root refutations or the complete auxiliary sub-action computations. The fast audit checks ledger coverage and consistency, not the truth of every recorded UNSAT status. The manuscript appropriately distinguishes that audit from regenerating and checking proofs. Inspection of the relevant encoding, branch-coverage logic, and RUP checker, together with the successful targeted reproductions, supports acceptance; the total of $305{,}773{,}252$ clauses remains the authors' reported total rather than a total independently replayed in this review.

The central question, proof strategy, dependencies, and limitations are sufficiently clear for readers in the field. The following improvements are minor and do not change the scientific decision:

- Fix the documented build on GCC 12.2. The unused `order6_rup::groups` constant in `order6_block_rup.hpp` becomes an error under `-Werror`, and `solve_coupled_sinz_surgery.cpp` uses `std::optional` without including `<optional>`. For the reported probes I used `-Wno-error=unused-variable` and, for the surgery driver, `-include optional`; no algorithmic source changes were made.
- Describe the full workflow consistently as proof regeneration and checking, since the original traces are not retained. Add a machine-readable record of successful settings and approximate runtimes for difficult roots; default bounded runs can legitimately return `UNKNOWN_BUDGET`.
- Simplify the abstract's ledger detail and replace the informal term “crystals” with “witness graphs.” In Section 4.2, a short explanation that independent equivariant relabellings canonicalize attachment blocks, permutations order equivalent blocks, and complementation bounds the anchor's red degree would make the coverage strategy easier to follow without consulting the implementation.
