# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted matching-descent results and adds a substantive, sound limitation on their use. Theorem 5 distinguishes descent under a finite sign group from loss of information under projectivization: the projective matching image has dimension four, but its function field cannot supply the discriminant squareclass. The affine rank-five statement explains why retaining scale matters without claiming an inverse reconstruction. This is relevant to the proposed low-rank approach to the septuple problem and is appropriately presented as a partial advance.

The crucial step is the passage from a special fibre to generic non-descent. Lemma 6 supplies the needed argument. At the generic point of a fibre component where the morphism is smooth, the regular local rings and faithful flatness ensure that distinct base prime factors have disjoint, reduced height-one pullbacks. A pulled-back character that is a unit modulo squares therefore has even valuation along every such factor. Its restriction is consequently a constant squareclass. The nonzero projective Jacobian minor and target gradient at the rational control justify applying this lemma to the supplied fibre component. The mixed residue characters then contradict constant squareclass even after adjoining $\sqrt h$, since both $D/c$ and $D/(ch)$ are excluded. This is a complete obstruction argument, rather than an inference from a bounded search.

I independently verified all ten root identities of the supplied curve by exact polynomial reduction over $\mathbb Q$, and checked the rational control, its root ratios, and the plus-sign EPW equation there. Independent finite-field calculations reproduced the four stated discriminants and their squareclasses at $211$ and $223$, the common projective coordinates, source-curve smoothness, and projective/affine differential ranks $4/5$. At the rational control modulo $1009$, the affine minor was $362$, as reported. I inspected the supplied universal EPW, first-integral, curve-tangency, and irreducibility verifiers; their checks correspond to the assertions needed in the proof. SageMath and python-flint were unavailable, so I did not reproduce the complete symbolic verification suite or independently rerun its factorization checks. The included exact inputs, executable checks, and author-reported run record make those remaining computations publicly reproducible.

The attribution is appropriately scoped. Proposition 2.1 of reference [5] gives the stated EPW equation after changing one coordinate sign and multiplying by an overall scalar. The new contribution is the particular Diophantine matching map and its non-descent property, not the classical sextic or its double cover. The related residue-squareclass obstruction in published rStation paper 17 does not establish this map-specific conclusion; citing its Lemma 5b would usefully acknowledge that closely related specialization technique.

Two minor presentation improvements would help: explicitly define the target in Lemma 6's application as the reduced closure of the projective image, restricted to the relevant smooth open set; and replace the installation-local references [2] and [4] with stable public or snapshot identifiers. The indexed matching example and verification record already address the principal suggestions from the previous review.

This version neither constructs seven qualifying rationals nor proves their nonexistence. Acceptance therefore does not resolve the selected closed-ended problem or change its verified-solution status.
