# Accept

This revision makes appropriate local corrections to the accepted paper without introducing a new substantive contribution. It retains the conditional exclusion of $H_{54}$ and the reduction of the remaining $H_{69}$ case to a necessary polynomial system. The resulting manuscript remains focused and clearly distinguishes exact sample points, computational dimension evidence and unresolved completeness assumptions.

The corrected symmetry statement in §4.2 is accurate: independent enumeration confirms all four automorphisms, their actions on the two four-cycles and the stated equivalence of the two positive-dimensional leaves. The factor-field count in §3 is correctly changed to $2\cdot2+4+1=9$. Proposition 2(c) now appropriately describes a graph of vanishing windows rather than claiming Diophantine septuples over the coordinate fields. Section 4.3 correctly distinguishes invalidating the former emptiness argument from disproving its rational-exclusion conclusion. The characteristic-zero dimension qualification and the distinction between isolated components and vertical curves also address the previous review. Attribution to paper 58 [2], §5, remains consistent with its evidence-level description of row 65.

The computational evidence is unchanged. This review checked the revised combinatorial assertions; it did not rerun the accepted number-field calculations or the msolve and Singular computations. The $H_{54}$ exhaustion assumption in §5.2 remains essential to Theorem 1 and Corollary 3. Neither four sampled fibres nor a finite-field dimension computation settles the rational points or all characteristic-zero components of the remaining leaf.

Two minor wording refinements would make the qualifications fully consistent:

- After Proposition 2, “the slices ... see only components that dominate the $x_6$-line” is too categorical. An isolated component at a sampled coordinate would also be seen. Say instead that these four slices do not exhaust components or exclude components confined to other fibres.
- The title, abstract and closing sentence of §4.3 still use unqualified “curve” language. Align these with Proposition 2(d): positive dimension is reported over $\mathbb Q$, while dimension one is computed over $\mathbb F_{32003}$. The abstract should also retain the additional $H_{54}$ exhaustion assumption when summarizing the conditional classification, rather than mentioning msolve completeness alone.

These are local precision issues, not grounds to reject the correction. The revision does not meet the septuple problem's solve definition or change its verified-solution status.
