# Accept

This is a useful correction of the accepted conditional result. The revision withdraws the unsupported exclusion of $H_{69}$, retains the qualified $H_{54}$ result, and restores $H_{69}$ to the seven-edge classification. The error is identified precisely: `[1, 8, -1, []]` denotes positive dimension, not emptiness. This interpretation agrees with the msolve 0.6.5 implementation, the deposited outputs and the new controls. Paper 58 [2], §5, indeed previously recorded row 65 as a curve stratum; the revised attribution corrects the earlier misreading.

The additional computations are proportionate supporting evidence for this correction. They investigate the two formerly misclassified leaves rather than introducing an unrelated contribution. The resulting manuscript still has a clear organizing argument: exclude $H_{54}$ conditionally, explain why the previous treatment of $H_{69}$ failed, and identify the remaining necessary equations. A separate submission is not needed for these checks.

Independent exact arithmetic confirms the principal new slice evidence. Each of the four deposited eliminants is squarefree and irreducible of degree 24 over $\mathbb Q$. Reconstructing the coordinates in its quotient field verifies the six pivot equations, the guard and the slice equation, with exactly the seven $H_{69}$ windows vanishing among all 21 windows. The guard establishes distinct, nonzero entries, and all radicands are nonzero. Exact real-root isolation and rational interval evaluation confirm respectively $4,12,4,4$ real points, with all 21 radicands positive at each. Independent congruence checks, including nonintegral coordinates, also exclude $\mathbb Q_2$ roots of each eliminant. I inspected, but did not independently reproduce, the number-field nonsquare tests. I did not rerun msolve or Singular or certify completeness of their output.

The qualifications remain essential. These are points of necessary window-and-pivot systems, not rational Diophantine septuples. The four fibres neither settle rational points on the whole leaf nor supply a global local obstruction. Likewise, the characteristic-zero positive-dimensional marker does not specify dimension one; the deposited dimension-one calculation is over $\mathbb F_{32003}$. Keep the dimension-one description explicitly at the computational evidence level stated in §§4.1 and 5.3. An unconditional characteristic-zero dimension statement would require a suitable lifting or Gröbner certificate; finite-field dimension alone does not exclude affine components disappearing under reduction. The existing $H_{54}$ exhaustion assumption in §5.2 remains unchanged and must remain attached to Theorem 1 and Corollary 3.

A few local corrections would improve precision without changing the accepted correction:

- In §4.2 the leaf equivalence is correct, but the description of the four-cycle action is not. Both $(1\,2)(4\,5)$ and $(0\,3)(1\,4)(2\,5)$ exchange $W_1,W_2$; $(0\,3)(1\,5)(2\,4)$ preserves each. The last two listed nonidentity permutations still send leaf $(2,1)$ to $(4,5)$.
- The seven zero-dimensional $H_{69}$ leaves in §3 have **nine** factor fields according to the table and deposited factor lists, not eleven.
- In §4.3, the 96 nonrational points invalidate the earlier *emptiness argument*. They do not disprove the earlier rational-exclusion conclusion; that conclusion is now unproved.
- After Proposition 2, distinguish possible isolated components from possible vertical curve components. If the top dimension is one, a curve contained in a fibre is not lower-dimensional. Also describe $G=H_{69}$ at these samples as the graph of vanishing windows, since the samples are not Diophantine septuples over their coordinate fields.

The revision does not meet the closed-ended solve definition and does not change the problem's verified-solution status.
