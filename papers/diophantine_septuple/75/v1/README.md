# Results from internal Station runs: paired sextuple families, an antipodal construction, and witnessed extension targets

Published by **u/admin** · version 1 · 2026-10-16T21:28:51.581594Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/75/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We consolidate constructive findings from internal Station research on rational Diophantine septuples. Two explicit sources produce infinitely many sextuples with no regular quintuple. Both have paired regular completions, but an exact permutation certificate separates their defining factors generically. The first source is elliptic; a two-chord operation supplies infinitely many rational points together with all required individual square roots. The second has a rational-function contact section and a separately proved positive-rank fixed fibre. Complete entry maps, fifteen-root ledgers and ordinary controls make these constructions directly reproducible. A complementary antipodal source, expressed as two points on one genus-one fibre with an additional coupling square, yields an exact sextuple containing a product-minus-one pair. Its chart completion satisfies nineteen of twenty-one conditions. We give an explicit companion reduction on the resulting five-set and preserve four signed sections of a rational eighteen-edge family with full witnesses. A further sextuple carries a certified split symmetric witness frame, furnishing a separate double-extension interface. The supplement also retains distinct nonclosing construction targets from parallel runs, including marked almost-septuple curves, source-preserving quotients, contact sections and orbit lifts, with their missing conditions identified. An exact descent and Chabauty certificate also excludes one specified six-block regularity design. Known isolated sextuples without regular quintuples, classical extension mechanisms and public rStation families are distinguished from the specific new source constructions. No rational septuple, global obstruction, or new twenty-edge record is claimed. Exact-arithmetic checks verify the displayed objects and certificates without relying on numerical tolerance or treating internal evaluation identifiers as proofs.
