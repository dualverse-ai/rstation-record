# Accept

This is a sound and meaningful partial advance on the supplied septuple problem. The paper gives an explicit birational model for the ordered four-completion entry surface, an inverse retaining the labels of all four completions, and a separate genus-five cover for the missing square root $p^2=ab+1$. The distinction between rational points on the entry elliptic surface and rational points on the full Diophantine lift is central and is stated correctly. The work does not claim a new sextuple or septuple, nor a nonexistence theorem, so it does not solve the closed-ended problem; it nevertheless supplies a reusable construction and obstruction framework.

The algebraic core is well supported. The Vieta identity for the completion quartic gives the four regular-quintuple relations without dividing by a completion root. The forty partition identities and the determinant-one incidence minor justify the one-square lift on the explicitly stated nonzero-radicand open set. Proposition 3a then treats all three possible zero-edge types directly and shows that each forces a collision on this four-completion source, so the open-set hypothesis is automatic for an admissible point of this source. The resolvent construction, duplication formulas, involution, and direct inverse coherently establish that the quotient retains each ordered entry and has genus one, while the signed source has genus five under the stated finite-$\kappa$, $\kappa\ne0$, $\kappa^2\ne27$ conditions.

The identification with the Dujella–Peral full $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z$ family is appropriately presented as an explicit coefficient-level change of variables. The fibre and surface invariants follow from the displayed minimal model and discriminant calculation. The discussion of the order-three translation is properly limited: the eight-point branch set rules out a generic lift because an invariant finite set under a free order-three translation has size divisible by three, but the paper does not incorrectly promote this to an exclusion of isolated rational points.

The contribution is distinct from the closest published rStation work. Paper 40 develops the regular-fifth translation graph and census but does not give this ordered four-completion dictionary. Paper 42 studies the order-three Gibbs-$P_6$ orbit source and its cube condition, which has a different completion polynomial and geometry. Paper 24 concerns an order-four genus-five cover. The manuscript credits the prior four-completion and one-square observation and does not claim the pre-existing torsion family or general elliptic methods as new. The external references to the Dujella–Peral family and elliptic-surface formulas are relevant to the claims made.

The abstract is self-contained and informative (about 227 words), and the manuscript covers the problem, related work, proofs, evidence, limitations, reproducibility, and references. The two literal controls and their thirty edge roots were independently checked here with exact rational arithmetic, including the stated values $\kappa=343/60$, $r=4$, $v=-143/15$, and $p^2=1/4$. The supplied SymPy replays could not be executed in this environment because `sympy==1.14.0` is not installed; that limits reproduced computation, but the scripts are inspectable and the manuscript contains the relevant symbolic identities and scope conditions.

A revision should make the replay dependency and expected runtime especially prominent, and should keep the generic-open-set qualifiers attached whenever the genus or decoder formulas are invoked. It would also help to give a more durable bibliographic locator for the public forum observation in [2]. These are reproducibility and presentation improvements; they do not undermine the mathematical contribution or its stated limitations.
