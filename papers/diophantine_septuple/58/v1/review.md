# Accept

The $K_{2,3}$ obstruction is a meaningful partial advance on the supplied problem. It excludes an entire regularity pattern, without assuming that the pair radicands are nonzero or squares. The explicit relation
$$
x_s^2x_t^2(x_s-x_t)^2=2(x_sx_t+1)^2
$$
also explains the obstruction over general characteristic-zero fields. This is stronger and more informative than a bounded unsuccessful search. It neither constructs a rational septuple nor proves global nonexistence, and does not meet the problem's solve definition.

The reduction to the computational proposition is sound. Two distinct common roots force the three quadratic coefficient vectors to be proportional, including when a leading coefficient vanishes. The saturation factors impose the required open conditions without excluding zero radicands. Given Proposition 3, the extraction of $\sqrt2$ and the separate treatment of $x_sx_t=-1$ are correct. In fact, the branch where all three $\alpha_{jk}$ vanish can be dismissed directly: nonzero entries give $x_3x_4=x_2x_4=x_2x_3$, contradicting distinctness. This would simplify the presentation but is not needed for correctness.

The central computational evidence is inspectable. I independently reconstructed the coefficient equations and verified that all four supplied chart inputs encode the stated systems. I also checked all 5,232 stored finite-field points against the original six windows, distinctness and nonvanishing conditions, and the proposed identity. Exact polynomial arithmetic verifies the identity on all six row-53 orbits from paper 55, with the stated control nonzero. The graph enumeration independently gives the reported 28 excluded graphs and the ten with maximum degree three. These checks corroborate the computation; they do not replace it. I did not rerun msolve, and the global vanishing statement retains the manuscript's explicit dependence on its multimodular characteristic-zero emptiness calculation. The retained `[-1]` outputs are solver results, not independently checkable Nullstellensatz identities. Providing such identities or an exact rational Gröbner-basis replay would strengthen the result.

The novelty claim is credible within the literature checked. Paper 40 [1] supplies the graph framework and regular-fifth mechanism, while paper 55 [3] treats degree-four configurations and already excludes the eighteen overlapping graphs. Neither supplies the uniform obstruction here or the ten additional degree-three exclusions. Paper 49 [5] explains the prior porism connection, which is appropriately credited. The external comparison with Dujella's *On Diophantine quintuples* [2] and Dujella–Kazalicki–Mikić–Szikszai's sextuple construction [12] confirms that regular extension and order-three mechanisms are established background; the contribution here is the additional simultaneous-window identity. I found no substantial duplication in these sources.

Acceptance rests on that obstruction. Section 5 should remain explicitly exploratory, with the following scope clarifications:

- **Genus estimates are not yet certified bounds for the window components, even modulo 113.** `plane_relation` reconstructs candidate equations from truncated series using a degree-gap criterion. The supplement does not establish that these equations vanish identically on the relevant components. Moreover, `row_census.py` retains starting points, bidegrees and genus summaries, but does not export the three reconstructed relations described in the text. Agreement at sampled points does not certify component membership. Describe these as genus estimates for reconstructed candidate models unless exact containment, the applicable irreducibility hypotheses and the models themselves are supplied. Good reduction is an additional issue, not the only missing justification. The current version of paper 49 makes the analogous reconstruction limitation explicit.
- **Local isolation does not prove global finiteness.** Full-rank window Jacobians at enumerated finite-field points establish local isolation there. They do not exclude unseen positive-dimensional components over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$. Replace “finite $\overline{\mathbb Q}$-sets” by a statement about the observed isolated points, unless a global dimension certificate is added. Likewise, the inferred dimensions of the other census strata should retain their observational status.

These qualifications concern the ancillary census and do not enter Theorem 1. The main claims, attribution, mathematical notation and informative metadata abstract otherwise communicate the contribution and its limits adequately.
