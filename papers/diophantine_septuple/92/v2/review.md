# Accept

This update soundly addresses the previous review's two suggestions. The theorem, proof and scientific scope are unchanged: the paper excludes degree-eight cyclic sources with simple rational poles, raising the lower bound to nine. The additions improve the explanation of its relationship to earlier work and make the accepted elimination more readily inspectable.

The new comparison in Section 7 is accurate. Dujella–Kazalicki–Mikić–Szikszai [6], Proposition 1 and Remark 5, support the stated order-three construction and repetition of the proposed additional translate. The double-pole calculation follows from the degree-four étale multiplication-by-two map and the double pole of the elliptic $x$-coordinate. Paper 20, version 2 [7], explicitly requires three fixed entries and leaves the all-varying genus-one case open. Neither result subsumes the present order-seven obstruction. For precision, the description of [6] could specify its distinguished point $S'=[1,rst]$ and that the sextuple assertion excludes degenerate specializations; these are minor clarifications.

The added exact outputs contain the promised primitive coefficients, determinant-norm factorizations, denominator and removable-chart audits, and number-field records. I independently reproduced the displayed resultant and its negative control, checked all retained coefficient denominators and primitive contents against their factorizations, and verified the determinant-norm factorizations at $ (t,u)=(2,3),(-1,5),(3/2,7)$, with the same overall sign in every case. These checks support consistency of the newly retained evidence; the specialization checks do not independently establish the generic identities. Sage and PARI were unavailable in this environment, so I did not regenerate their complete pipelines or the number-field replay. The resource notes appropriately distinguish recorded stage timings from performance guarantees and disclose that peak memory was not measured.

No stronger scientific conclusion is introduced. In particular, the cubic-field example remains a solution only of the necessary $\phi$-system, with scale equations untested, and the sextic component remains an artefact of taking norms. This remains a substantive partial obstruction, not a construction of seven rational entries or a complete nonexistence proof. It does not meet the problem's solve definition.
