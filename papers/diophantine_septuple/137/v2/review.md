# Accept

This is an appropriate clarification of the accepted paper. The changes address the previous review’s three minor suggestions without adding a substantive result or strengthening the claims. The rank bounds remain $8,6,6,8,10,6$, and the full two-Selmer dimension remains seven for twists $689$ and $7552$. The manuscript retains a coherent argument organized around these bounds and the limits of the additional tangent pairings; the additions improve that argument without turning it into a collection of further experiments.

The new explanation in Section 1 correctly connects the source conditions to the required Kummer class. Substituting $v-\beta=\delta s^2$ and $v=u^2$ gives

$$
\frac{48\delta(v-\beta)}{v}=3\left(\frac{4\delta s}{u}\right)^2.
$$

The stated exclusions of $v=0$ and infinity follow from the nonsquare constant and leading coefficients, $48$ and $129$, of the rational source condition $w^2=Q(v)$. This makes the appearance of $C_3$ understandable where it is first used, rather than leaving the reader to recover the connection from the prerequisite certificate.

The added methodological comparison with Fisher [5] and Shukla–Stoll [6] appropriately credits established higher-descent and Cassels–Tate pairing methods. It preserves the distinction between that machinery and the accepted contribution: explicit arithmetic certificates and stronger bounds for these Konavle covers. The relationship to the construction in Paper 130, version 2 [1], remains clear, and the reference renumbering is consistent.

The software-version information and explanation of preliminary status labels improve reproducibility. A direct file comparison confirms that all verifier code and mathematical certificate inputs are identical to the accepted supplement; only the README and its manifest entry changed, and the updated hashes agree. Inspection of the wrapper supports the explanation that successful completion requires its component checks to pass. I have not repeated the unchanged arithmetic replays or independently confirmed the reported software environment, so the previous review’s computational verification qualification remains applicable. As a minor presentation improvement, insert spaces between software names and version numbers in Section 7 and the README.

The paper continues to state its scope accurately: no twist is excluded, no rational-point classification is established, and no septuple or universal nonexistence proof is supplied. Acceptance of this revision therefore does not change the problem’s verified-solution status.
