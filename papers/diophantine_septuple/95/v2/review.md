# Accept

This is an appropriate, focused revision of the accepted paper. Table 1 makes the classification and its unresolved cases easier to navigate. The additional characteristic-zero computations strengthen evidence for an existing result, while Appendix A.5 supplies limited evidence about an already identified open case. Theorems A–D retain their scope. These changes neither introduce a separate substantive contribution nor turn the manuscript into an unfocused collection; the detailed computational records remain in the supplement.

The change to Section 6, Step 4 is supported by the supplied code and logs. Four of the six previously unchecked main-chart emptiness cases now have completed Singular computations using `std`; the remaining two return the unit ideal using `slimgb` over $\mathbb Q$. Together with the eight previously checked chart0 cases, this supports the stated fourteen emptiness checks. The nonempty control remains nonempty under both algorithms. The manuscript appropriately retains the separate qualification that completeness of the two surviving branches relies on msolve's multimodular computation. These checks strengthen the existing identification with the components of Paper 93 [1]; they do not claim new families. I inspected the readers, algorithm choices, and retained outputs, but did not independently rerun the Gröbner-basis computations.

The new P1 screen is relevant to Section 8's mixed-edge open case. Its six retained logs report no full configuration, and their counts sum to $2{,}770{,}632$. The construction includes rational half-pairs and conjugate pairs, both finite and infinite zero/pole charts, and same-fibre as well as opposite-fibre conditions. The brute-force comparisons and the positive control from Paper 93 provide useful checks. Crucially, the paper leaves this case open and does not turn absence at six primes into a characteristic-zero nonexistence theorem. I assessed this evidence from the scripts and logs, rather than reproducing the full searches.

Some precise wording improvements are warranted in Appendix A.5 and its summary in Table 1:

- Replace “which every constructed edge passes” with the actual positive-control scope. The retained extension-field log tests **53 randomly selected constructed cases**, each on 60 sampled valid points, whereas all 349 additional brute-force cases are tested and rejected. Passing these sampled character tests is supporting evidence, not an exhaustive algebraic certificate for every constructed edge. This correction does not undermine the six-prime absence result, which has no full survivors even at the initial filter.
- Describe $2{,}770{,}632$ as distinct source-and-shift-class records. The stored key includes $s$ and the choice $S=s$ or $2s$, so this is not a count of distinct underlying sources alone.
- Qualify the statement that the pair conditions “fix $\beta$ and $A$.” For opposite fibres they determine $\beta$ when the relevant denominator is nonzero; when $w(R)^2=w(R+T)^2$, the code enumerates the remaining $\beta$ values. State this branch explicitly and record the implemented restriction $A\notin\{0,-1\}$ alongside the finite-field conclusion.

The reduction statement should also mean good reduction of the full admissible configuration, preserving the required distinctness and nonvanishing conditions, rather than merely good reduction of the elliptic curve. With that interpretation, the stated arithmetic limitation is appropriate: the screen concerns configurations defined over the tested fields and does not exclude arbitrary number-field configurations or rational configurations with exceptional reduction at those primes.

These are local reporting clarifications, not defects in the accepted central contribution. The revision remains a useful partial classification of degree-two translate constructions. It neither supplies a rational Diophantine septuple nor proves universal nonexistence, so it does not meet the problem's solve definition.
