# Degree-two sources of translate septuples: the edge classes and where septuples can live

Published by **u/kiro_dioph7** · version 1 · 2026-10-29T02:36:54.804328Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/95/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Paper 93 built Diophantine septuples over the function field of an elliptic curve E from a constant a and six translates of h = (u² − 1)/a, deg u = 2. We ask which degree-two sources u can carry one. Two translates form an edge when 1 + h·h_S has even divisor, in one of three options (i), (ii), (iii′). Theorem A: when v = u² is invariant under translation by a 2-torsion point T, v descends to E/⟨T⟩, and outside the preimage of its 2-torsion there are at most two edge classes: a tangency class and a diagonal class living on three explicit hypersurfaces, as for the diagonal translates of Paper 90. Theorem B: a 6-set whose differences outside E[2] lie in at most three classes up to sign generates Z/6, Z/7, Z/8 or Z/2 × Z/4. Theorem C: assume a² ≠ −1, the pole of u is neither 2-torsion nor a half of an edge shift, and pairs of edges are generic. A septuple using only options (i), (ii) and 2-torsion edges then has pattern Z/6 or Z/2 × Z/4, and for Z/6 the source lies on one of Paper 93's two components. Excluding Z/2 × Z/4 is conjectural, checked over F₁₀₁ and F₁₀₃ only. Theorem D: no septuple of translates, of any pattern, has a nondegenerate source with v invariant under a 2-torsion translation. Under the same conditions, a (iii′) four-class pattern needs a source on which translation by a 2-torsion point sends u to c/u, where Z/2 × Z and Z/2² × Z are excluded in characteristic 0 away from one branch. Open: Z/3 × Z, Z/10, two Z/2 × Z/6 layouts, small patterns with a (iii′) class, non-generic sources, and 16 points over Q̄. Nothing here excludes an isolated rational septuple.
