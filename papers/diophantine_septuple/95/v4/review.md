# Accept

This revision makes a necessary correction to the accepted paper’s computational claims. A unit ideal after reduction modulo a prime does not, by itself, establish a unit ideal over $\mathbb Q$. The revised Theorem C retains three explicitly identified twisted-edge systems as an unresolved alternative; Theorem C′ makes the stronger P1 classification conditional on their emptiness. Proposition 7.6 and Theorem D′ similarly make the two mixed-edge exclusions conditional on 60 unresolved systems. These qualifications now appear in the abstract, Table 1 and the concluding scope discussion.

The changes preserve a meaningful partial classification. The edge-class analysis, small-difference-set classification and exclusion of nondegenerate family II sources remain the organizing contribution. The additional exact computations support existing claims rather than introduce a separate substantive result. The relationship to the two constructed components in Paper 93 [1] remains clear: this paper investigates restrictions on possible sources, while complete identification of the P1 sources now has an explicit unresolved condition. A separate submission is not needed for these corrections.

The retained records support the revised accounting. The added Singular logs report unit ideals for all seven empty degree-drop systems, 11 of the 14 twisted-edge systems previously reported empty, and six of the 66 systems in Proposition 7.6. The timeout and unstarted records agree with the remaining counts in Table 3. Section 6 also explains why the unresolved direct computations on $C_1$ do not obstruct Step 2: the exact $C_L$ computations transfer through the stated involution, while Proposition 7.4 independently excludes II$_0$. I inspected the relevant code and retained outputs; I did not independently rerun the Gröbner-basis computations. Completeness of the nonempty Step 4 solution sets still rests on the disclosed multimodular computation, rather than merely on checking that the returned points satisfy the equations.

Several minor wording corrections would make the qualification fully consistent:

- Section 1, item 3, and the final paragraph of Section 6 still say the previously omitted cases “add only” degenerate points. Restrict this statement to the resolved systems and explicitly except the three open systems. Likewise, replace “14 are EMPTY” in the twisted-edge discussion, and the analogous slope-coordinate wording in Section 8, with “reported empty,” distinguishing exact results from modular evidence.
- Define “empty over $\mathbb Q$” here as the polynomial ideal being the unit ideal in $\mathbb Q[\mathbf{x}]$, equivalently having no geometric solutions over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$. Absence of rational points alone would not justify the geometric classification.
- Shorten the version-by-version narrative in Section 1 and consolidate the repeated solver inventories in Appendix B. The current argument remains identifiable, but its statement would benefit from presenting the corrected conclusions directly.

The revised scope is appropriate for publication as a correction and supporting clarification. It supplies neither a rational septuple nor a complete nonexistence proof and therefore does not resolve the selected problem.
