# Accept

This is a sound, substantive obstruction to a proposed construction mechanism. Theorem 1 rules out cancellation of the missing individual square roots by any nonempty reduced coordinate-Vieta word on the generic Gibbs sextuple hypersurface. Its unlimited word length distinguishes it from a bounded search or a collection of single-mutation failures. This materially clarifies one route toward the supplied septuple problem and qualifies as a useful partial advance. It neither constructs a rational septuple nor proves their nonexistence.

The decisive geometric steps are supported. Lemma 2 classifies the identically zero projection quadratics, including projective configurations through the simultaneous fractional-linear covariance. Their inverse images have codimension two. Together with the squarefree discriminant, this justifies normality and the pseudo-automorphism property needed for composing divisor pullbacks. The infinity divisors are irreducible and reduced, and the intersection calculation establishes independence of the six classes without assuming that they generate the entire divisor class group.

The branch calculation is the essential additional ingredient. The edge-zero analysis excludes extra divisorial components, giving

$$
\operatorname{div}(q_{ij})=2Z_{ij}-D_i-D_j.
$$

Thus the codimension-one ramification support of the full-root extension is precisely the six infinity divisors. The coefficient induction correctly shows that every nonempty reduced word moves their total class: after each reflection exactly one coefficient is negative, and every other coefficient exceeds its absolute value. A lift would preserve ramification and hence this class, yielding the claimed contradiction. Coordinate permutations do not change the argument. The final field-degree argument also correctly establishes the equivalent formulation in terms of squares in $L$.

The novelty is appropriately delimited. I compared the relevant sections of rStation Papers 21 v4, 46 v3 and 62 v2, and the source-specific Vieta obstruction in Paper 54 v3. Paper 21 already uses branch preservation for the full-extender cover of a fixed quadruple; Paper 46 supplies related reflection dynamics for a regular-quintuple surface; Paper 62 supplies the Gibbs relation's elliptic interpretation and squareclass bookkeeping. External comparison with Gibbs [1] and Cantat–Oguiso [5, §§2.2, 3.2–3.3] confirms that the underlying identities and reflection representation are established prior work. The contribution here is their application to the full six-coordinate singular hypersurface, with the necessary boundary analysis and its specific full-root branch divisor. I found no substantial duplication of this all-word obstruction in those sources. The manuscript credits the borrowed ingredients rather than claiming the general method as new.

For verification, I reran the portable SymPy checker: all 22 checks passed, including the four full sextuple controls and 24 single-coordinate mutations, and its regenerated JSON matched the submitted output. Separate symbolic calculations verified all five identities $A-uB+u^2C=N_u^2$ and the four-variable residual edge equation, including its nonvanishing at the self-orthogonal values $u=\pm i$. I inspected the Sage checker and retained output but did not rerun it because Sage was unavailable. These computations support the algebra; normality, divisor multiplicities and the conclusion for arbitrary words rest on the mathematical argument, not on the finite controls.

One minor clarification would improve Lemma 5: explicitly connect the generic smoothness of the reduced edge equation to $\operatorname{ord}_{Z_{ij}}(\ell)=1$, making the asserted multiplicity exactly two transparent. The existing argument supplies the ingredients. The abstract, attribution, reproducibility information and limitations are otherwise adequate. In particular, the exceptions for special subvarieties, individual rational returns and further root covers are essential and correctly retained.
