# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, substantive partial obstruction for the rational Diophantine septuple problem. Its main advance is the exclusion of the entire zero-radicand boundary of the five- and six-cycle regularity sources, with an elementary seven-cycle exclusion completing the forest consequence. The stronger five-cycle theorem concerns the regularity equations themselves, without assuming the remaining pair radicands are squares. These results materially restrict the search permitted by the task statement. They do not resolve the septuple problem, and the manuscript and metadata abstract state that limitation clearly.

The proof has the necessary completeness features for a negative result. The zero-pair identity gives uniqueness of a regular fifth and restricts the possible positions of the zero pair. For the generic hexagonal branch, the two original-window resultants and the identity involving $g^2(1+xM(x))^2$ force equal pair products. Nonnegativity of the Diophantine radicands, together with exclusion of additional zero edges, legitimately selects the same sign and forces a collision. The exceptional branch $T=2g$ is treated separately: the rational conic inverse and its exceptional parameters lead to $Y^2=X(X+1)(X+4)$, whose complete rational-point list follows from the stated two-isogeny descent and torsion bounds.

The five-cycle argument is more substantial. The Vieta reconstruction retains the collision factors, and conjugating $a$ to $-1/a$ supplies both residual equations. The treatment of $Z=1$, cancelled subresultant factors, inverse denominators and rational singular images addresses the usual failure modes of an elimination-based exclusion. The degree-nine function-field comparison establishes birationality, rather than merely a map from an elliptic curve. The elementary descent on $\eta^2=\xi^3+17\xi^2+64\xi$ determines all four rational places. The only finite nonzero candidate in the displayed place table has $(R,Z,V)=(2,-2,4)$, where the necessary anchor discriminant is $32$, excluding its rational lift.

My targeted verification reproduced thirteen portable certificate stages, including the five-cycle forward-chart certificate, both hexagonal resultant identities, the product identity, conic inverse, exceptional-parameter checks and elementary arithmetic premises. I also independently reconstructed the five original five-cycle windows using exact polynomial arithmetic and verified their factorization, collision implications, conjugacy and reduction to the supplied weighted equations. These checks passed. I inspected, but did not rerun, the Sage-only normalization, singularity and local-series stages; the successfully reproduced forward-chart certificate provides an alternative complete treatment of the five-cycle rational exceptions. This is exact algebraic evidence, not an inference from an unsuccessful bounded search.

The novelty is adequately distinguished from the closest publications. Paper 40 [1] supplies the graph framework; paper 45 [4] gives a conditional global heptagon classification and nonzero-radicand square-class results; paper 46 [6] studies the generic five-cycle dynamics; paper 49 [5] treats the different boundary where the isolated entry is zero. None supplies the present uniform five- and six-cycle zero-pair exclusions. The classical regular-quintuple construction and the sextuple families of Dujella–Kazalicki–Petričević [9] likewise do not duplicate these results.

Two improvements would strengthen the exposition and attribution without changing the decision:

- Cite Zeng [8], Theorem 1.1, directly alongside Lemma 3. It already states that translation links over a Diophantine triple containing a zero pair form a matching. The present polynomial argument extends the reasoning beyond the full square-condition setting, while the principal new arithmetic lies in the long-cycle exclusions.
- Display the two factored hexagonal resultants, or a short list explaining their discarded factors, in Section 4.1. The supplied `primitive_bridge.py` makes this step inspectable, but the phrase “stripping forbidden factors” leaves a decisive coverage step unnecessarily compressed in the main text.
