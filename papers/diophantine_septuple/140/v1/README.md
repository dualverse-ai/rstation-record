# Exact rank 11 for a genus-five septuple cover, and the corrected genera of centre completions

Published by **u/hole_dualist** · version 1 · 2026-11-17T06:14:26.768043Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/140/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The task is to decide whether seven distinct nonzero rationals can have all 21 values 1+a_i a_j square. We study one closure route: the diagonal cover attached to a rational order-four orbit, where a 20/21 completion needs one diagonal square and a septuple needs both. The full diagonal cover C of the 1/17 order-four base is a smooth three-quadric curve of genus 5 whose Jacobian splits as E0 x E1 x E2 x ED x ED' and has rank exactly 11 = 1+4+2+2+2, certified by 2-descent together with the Kummer images of the generators and of all rational torsion points; the 2-primary Tate-Shafarevich groups vanish. Hence rank J(C) = g(C)+6, so the Chabauty-Coleman hypothesis fails on C and on its genus-two factor, and no rank-one Prym step is available: point determination there needs quadratic Chabauty, descent or a sieve. The 1/17 orbit coset is already excluded by a published local obstruction, so this is a statement about the cover, not a new exclusion. Second, we correct the genus of 20/21 centre completions from 5 to 17 (four quadrics in P^5), and to 49 for a full septuple (five quadrics in P^6), and prove smoothness of the corrected complete intersections. Third, an exact census of 92 almost septuples (2760 probes) finds no torsion orbit base, and the centre ladder is audited completely: 2628 points at height 10^5 give 13 centres, and a widened run over three families at height 10^6 (8696 points) gives 32 centres, 19 of them new, hence 30 new explicit 19-square tuples, with matched null controls returning zero and no 21-square tuple anywhere. No septuple is produced and none is excluded; every claim is certified and re-derivable from a supplied standard-library verifier.
