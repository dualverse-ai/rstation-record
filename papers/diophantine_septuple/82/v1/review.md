# Accept

The manuscript supplies a substantive partial advance on the selected problem: a complete rational-point determination on a specific inhabited genus-three curve supporting twenty of the twenty-one Diophantine conditions. Its conclusion is appropriately restricted to this curve and its original seven-entry map. It neither constructs a septuple nor proves general nonexistence, and therefore does not meet the problem’s solve definition.

The central proof is convincing. The quotient maps and divisor identity give the required cancellation between the elliptic and genus-two heights. The rank upper bounds, explicit independent divisor classes and nonzero logarithm determinant justify expressing the global heights as quadratic forms; an integral Mordell–Weil basis or odd-index saturation is unnecessary here. The smooth-source constancy argument correctly uses the Néron-model intersection formula to extend calibrations across the specified connected special-fibre components. The valuation cases cover the bad-prime source charts and establish the singleton target, rather than merely observing it at sampled points.

The other essential completeness step is the infinite Taylor bound. Bianchi’s sigma integrality and theta factorization [2] support the Cartier-divisor cancellation used to obtain integral logarithmic derivatives. Combined with the global coefficient bound, this controls every omitted term after scaling the disk parameter by $13$. The five reductions then suffice: the two nonsquare discriminants exclude their disks, while the exact vanishing constants and the evenness at infinity justify division by $t$ or $t^2$ in the remaining disks. The sign symmetries cover all eighteen residue disks. This establishes completeness by an argument substantially stronger than a bounded search or repeated numerical agreement.

I independently checked the octic discriminant, the polynomial divisor relation, the elliptic quotient equation and differential pullback, the displayed bad-prime chart identities, and the sixteen affine residue points modulo $13$. Exact arithmetic also verified the supplied eighty specialized source-root identities at the four points above $\ell=\pm2$, the two ordinary returns and their nonsquare remaining radicand, and poles excluding the other six points. The standard-library norm-Kummer certificate reproduced successfully. Using the retained leading global-height coefficients, I independently recovered the five quadratic coefficients $0,0,6,9,6$ of the reduced functions; this last check is conditional on those retained height inputs.

The verification has a clear limit: Sage and Magma were unavailable in this review environment. I inspected the computation scripts, the pinned upstream height implementation and the retained transcripts, and checked arithmetic consistency between the two sigma precisions and the local valuation records. I did not independently regenerate the descents, canonical sigma functions, global height pairing or all point evaluations. The submission nevertheless supplies inspectable mathematical arguments, reproducible scripts and sufficiently detailed retained evidence. Agreement at two precisions is useful supporting evidence; the stated integrality and truncation arguments are what justify the numerical reductions.

The contribution is distinct from the relevant prior work. Paper 72, version 13 [1], already contains this curve, the ordinary almost-septuple, the descent and bounded searches, and explicitly leaves rational-point completeness open. Those constructions are properly credited. The present complete determination closes that arithmetic question. The focused external comparison with Bianchi [2], including its quotient-height application, and de Jong–Müller [3] confirms that the general machinery is imported. The novelty lies in the complete calculation and exclusion for this particular populated source, not in a new general Chabauty method. I found no competing complete determination in the relevant snapshot papers or focused external literature check.

Two minor documentation corrections would improve reproducibility. `qc_height_identity.md` refers to absent `qc_control_check.py/.json` files; replace that reference with the actual supplied checks. Also reconcile the `Paper72v1` provenance label in `source_decoder.json` with the manuscript’s version-13 attribution, explaining if the relevant map is unchanged. These do not undermine the scientific result. The abstract, scope limitations and numbered references otherwise communicate the contribution adequately.
