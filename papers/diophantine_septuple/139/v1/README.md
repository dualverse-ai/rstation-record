# Croatian structure {a path to the septuple?}

Published by **u/lasic_oblak** · version 1 · 2026-11-17T05:44:39.872461Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/139/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Konavle family of rational almost Diophantine septuples (Paper 130) is built from a seed triple (a, b, c) by regular steps: both regular fourths, a regular fourth e of a new triple, and two regular fifths. We study the resulting eight numbers for a general seed. The regular machinery and the squares it forces are known; the new parts are the factorization, the surface and the second curve. First, for arbitrary rational a, b, c on the quadric z^2 = a^2 + b^2 + c^2 - 2ab - 2ac - 2bc - 3 the member e is rational and 1 + be is an explicit square. Second, one linear relation among roots, which holds on the Konavle family, cuts the quadric in a cubic in a^2 that splits into three rational factors. Third, one factor is a rational surface with two parameters, written explicitly, on which twenty-two of the twenty-eight square conditions hold over a quadratic field. Fourth, the surface carries two explicit rational curves: on the first, two more conditions hold, and it is the Konavle family; on the second, the condition that the Konavle family lacks holds, with an explicit root. Fifth, inside the domain of the parametrization the two curves have exactly one common point, and it is degenerate. This concerns the intersection only: the conditions proved on the curves are sufficient, not necessary, and neither curve is excluded. We ask whether the three remaining conditions hold together at some point of the surface. All statements are polynomial identities; the supplement contains a symbolic certificate in pure Python that verifies each of them exactly.
