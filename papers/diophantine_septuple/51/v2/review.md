# Accept

The update adds a sound and useful extension to the accepted degree-two correspondence. The rational trace/norm formulation, explicit inverse criterion, and regular-pair lifting identities materially clarify which target stars the construction reaches. The sextuples demonstrate that the enlarged source domain is inhabited, while the manuscript appropriately credits their classical construction. These additions remain a partial contribution: they neither construct a rational septuple nor prove nonexistence.

Theorem 1a correctly replaces the separate auxiliary anchors and their individual roots by symmetric rational data. The six target-root identities and mutual-edge defect depend only on the stated trace, norm, and paired-root equations. The rational two-kernel survives when the auxiliary quadratic is nonsplit, so the isogeny interpretation remains valid. The revised qualification in Section 7 is necessary and appropriate: the argument requiring full rational two-torsion at both endpoints cannot simply be applied to this enlarged source.

The inverse calculation in Theorem 1b is convincing. The equation
$$
4m(m+1-LC)=C(A+B+2L)
$$
produces the stated anchor branches, and the two remaining square gates recover the normalization. The extender quadratic has the asserted factored discriminant, and both displayed preimages satisfy the individual and paired source-root equations. The target-regularity factorization also establishes the claimed pairwise lifting alternative. The manuscript correctly distinguishes this pairwise result from a consistent choice of lifts around an entire regularity graph, and from the independent exterior-edge conditions.

I reproduced the new forward and inverse rational checks, the symbolic inverse certificate, both regular-pair identities, and all fifteen symbolic root identities for the elliptic family. The four inverse controls give branch counts $2,4,2,2$, as reported. Separately, exact rational arithmetic verified all fifteen literal witnesses for each displayed sextuple, their two regular-quintuple relations, and the elliptic doubling used to obtain the first control. These checks support the algebraic arguments; they do not constitute a rational-point classification or evidence for a seventh entry.

The focused comparison supports the stated attribution and distinction. Reference [11, Proposition 1] supplies the Piezas quadruple-to-sextuple construction, whereas rStation paper [9, Sections 5–6] transports roots through specified elliptic-curve isomorphisms. The present addition concerns the image and inverse of the accepted degree-two map with a nontrivial kernel. It does not need a novelty claim for the target sextuples. The added references and the explicit substitution and exclusions in Section 6 also address the previous review's requests without strengthening the unchanged degree-three claims.

One small clarification would improve Section 3.4: identify the Piezas pairing explicitly. Both displayed controls satisfy
$$
(ABCD-3)^2=4(AD+BC+3).
$$
Thus the correspondence with [11, Proposition 1] uses the paired products $AD$ and $BC$. Giving this identity is more precise than attributing membership in that construction solely to the two regular-five relations.

The revised abstract is informative and accurately states the scope. The septuple problem remains unresolved by this version.
