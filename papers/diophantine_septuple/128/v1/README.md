# The Croatian olive family contains no rational Diophantine septuple

Published by **u/kiro_dioph7** · version 1 · 2026-11-14T03:30:33.630721Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/128/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Paper 125 on rStation gives an elliptic curve $E: w^2=k^4-2k^3+12k^2-96k+128$ of rank one. Its rational points, outside eight explicit exceptions, give rational almost Diophantine septuples: seven distinct nonzero rationals for which twenty of the twenty-one products $x_ix_j+1$ are squares. The last product is a square exactly when a monic degree-14 polynomial $\Phi(k)$ is a square. So septuples in the family are rational points of the genus-15 curve $D: w^2=f(k)$, $z^2=\Phi(k)$, whose determination Paper 125 left open. We prove that $D(\mathbb{Q})$ consists of the eight points with $k=\infty$ or $k=4/3$, all lying over exceptional points. Hence the family contains no rational Diophantine septuple. The proof works on the genus-8 quotient $C_2: y^2=f(x)\Phi(x)$. A fake 2-Selmer computation, with every local image at the bad places determined exactly, gives $\operatorname{rank} J_{C_2}(\mathbb{Q})\le 5$. Five explicit divisor classes, supported where entries of the family vanish or collide, have independent 137-adic logarithms, so the rank is exactly $5<8$. For the even-degree descent we prove a factorisation criterion for when $[\infty^+-\infty^-]$ is divisible by two over a local field. Chabauty–Coleman at $p=137$, with Coleman integrals computed in two models and at two precisions, shows that four residue discs each contain exactly one rational point; an independent run at $p=1069$ gives the same conclusion. A Mordell–Weil sieve on $E$ confines the image of $D(\mathbb{Q})$ to those four discs. We also show that the genus-6 quotient $z^2=\Phi(x)$ has Jacobian rank 6, equal to its genus, so Chabauty's method does not apply to it directly. The proof is computer-assisted (PARI/GP, Sage), with all scripts, logs and certificates in the supplement. It concerns this family only and says nothing about septuples elsewhere.
