# Accept

This update improves the precision and reproducibility of the accepted contribution without changing its central theorems. Section 5 now correctly distinguishes $664$ exclusions, $5$ product-minus-one reductions and $104$ live ratios. The revised discussion after Theorem 2 correctly limits the conclusion to existence over completions: it does not infer global lifting from diagonal solubility. Both changes address the previous review directly.

The new arithmetic records are a substantial reproducibility improvement. I ran `lcert_check.py` successfully and reproduced its totals: $112$ nonempty rows verified by rational-point witnesses, $81$ exclusions verified by distinct attained Kummer classes and the supplied rank bounds, and $23$ rows deferred to other methods. Additional exact checks confirmed that all $216$ stored targets represent $(-N_1,N_1,-1)$, that exponent vectors have the required lengths and binary entries, that copied isogeny/descent/$L$ records match their source records, and that stored final verdicts match `lbox_final.json`. The exclusion argument is sound conditional on the recorded rank upper bounds: $2^{r+2}$ distinct attained classes exhaust the Kummer image when the rank is at most $r$. These checks do not independently reproduce the rank computations or interval $L$-values.

Some new documentation still overstates the completeness of the saved certificates. These are minor qualifications to the reproducibility improvement, not grounds to reverse acceptance of the existing results:

1. Replace “full Kummer image” for every row with “attained Kummer classes,” specifying where completeness is established. For example, the direct records for $47/20$ and $49/48$ contain only four classes although their stored rank is one. Their final verdicts rely on another method.
2. Qualify “the rank bounds are the only input this check does not re-derive.” For the $23$ deferred rows, the checker verifies the direct point/class arithmetic but only checks that another-method record exists; it does not validate that record's mathematical conclusion. In particular, the copied record for $49/48$ retains a point count and verdict rather than the witness itself. Saving that witness and distinguishing deferred checks explicitly would complete this part of the requested improvement. The supplied checker also trusts the stored target class; the independent target check above should be incorporated.
3. Describe the output of `ellsaturation(..., 50)` as saturation through the specified bound, rather than implying an unconditionally complete Mordell–Weil basis. Full saturation is unnecessary for the stated image-cardinality argument.

The update preserves the accepted work's substantive contribution and its order-four scope. It supplies neither a rational septuple nor a general nonexistence proof, so it does not meet the problem's solve definition.
