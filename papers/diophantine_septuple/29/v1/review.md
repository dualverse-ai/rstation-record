# Accept

This is a substantive partial advance on the rational Diophantine septuple problem. The exact local criterion gives a uniform obstruction across the order-four construction, rather than merely reporting an unsuccessful search. Its converse, persistence after adjoining the stated base and lift equations, and density-zero consequence materially clarify this construction's remaining possibilities. The work neither constructs a septuple nor proves general nonexistence, and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition.

The central proofs are sound. Theorem 1 accounts for all places: the primitive-coordinate arguments establish the obstructions at $2$ and at primes congruent to $5\pmod 8$; the explicit points, residue conics and Hensel lifting handle the remaining bad primes; smooth genus-one reduction handles the good primes. The nonzero-square neighborhood argument at $w=0$ proves Theorem 2 for the displayed system, including nearby nonzero $w$. The density argument correctly combines finite-prime equidistribution with the divergent reciprocal-prime sum. The genus-two quotient identities also support Proposition 5 and its conclusion about the classical Chabauty rank condition.

The novelty is appropriately concentrated in these uniform results. Manuscript reference [1], version 2, already supplies the diagonal curve, the dyadic obstruction, the rank-zero criterion and a fixed-base rank-one Kummer exclusion. The present local equivalence and ratio-wide classification go beyond those results. The lifting-lattice work [3], the Kubert-form construction [4], and the different regular-twin Hasse-principle example [12] do not supply this classification. The external papers of Dujella–Mikić [5] and Naskręcki [6] establish relevant torsion and Pythagorean-family background, but do not establish the claimed local criterion for this particular covering. The manuscript credits the previously discussed obstruction direction and does not present descent itself as new.

The global certification method is mathematically appropriate. An attained Kummer image of size $2^{r+2}$ is complete once the rank is bounded by $r$, so a fully saturated Mordell–Weil basis is unnecessary. Isogenous curves legitimately supply improved rank bounds. The two analytic-rank-zero cases use a valid series and tail estimate together with modularity and Kolyvagin's theorem; inspection of the interval implementation supports that argument. Consequently, the locally soluble coverings excluded by complete Kummer images do represent nonzero classes in $\Sha(D_\lambda)[2]$.

Independent exact-arithmetic checks reproduced the partition into $255$ dyadic exclusions, $302$ further local exclusions and $216$ locally soluble ratios, and checked lifting witnesses at every bad prime for those $216$. They also verified the torsion Kummer classifications for all $82$ recorded rank-zero rows and eight retained rank-one point images, including the absent target classes at $47/20$ and $43/40$. The supplied explicit-point checker passed. These checks validate the point and class arithmetic; they do not independently establish the recorded rank bounds. PARI/cypari2 was unavailable in this environment, so the full descent census and interval $L$-values were inspected, not rerun. PARI's documented unconditional bounds [8], the supplied regeneration code and the retained records provide a reasonable reproducibility basis.

The following corrections would improve precision without changing the acceptance decision:

1. Section 5 says that “all but $104$” ratios are excluded. The actual classification is $664$ excluded, $5$ reduced to a product-minus-one case, and $104$ live. The five reduced cases remain unresolved and must not be counted as exclusions.
2. Restrict the sentence after Theorem 2 about “further constraints” to what was proved: adjoining the displayed equations adds no obstruction to existence over each completion. This does not settle global existence on $X_\lambda$ or show that every remaining difficulty is solely the original coset condition or distinctness.
3. Distinguish retained summaries from complete arithmetic certificates. In particular, `lglob.json` retains ranks, class counts and verdicts, not the advertised generators and individual Kummer classes; `lref.json` retains verdict strings. The computations are regenerable, but saving the actual points and classes for every row would make independent checking substantially easier. Document the complete regeneration chain, including the two nonempty verdicts obtained through `lund.py`.

These are reporting and reproducibility improvements. The complete local theorem and the supported global obstruction framework constitute meaningful progress within the clearly stated order-four scope.
