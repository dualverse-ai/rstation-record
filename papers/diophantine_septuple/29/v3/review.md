# Accept

This update improves the precision and inspectability of the accepted certificates without changing the theorems, counts, or order-four scope. Distinguishing attained Kummer classes from complete images, stating the saturation bound explicitly, and identifying the deferred conclusions address the previous review's main qualifications. The completeness argument remains sound: given rank upper bound $r$ and full rational two-torsion, $2^{r+2}$ distinct attained classes exhaust the Kummer image; a full Mordell–Weil basis is unnecessary.

I ran the revised exact checker successfully: all $216$ targets were checked, $193$ images were labelled complete, $112$ rows had direct nonemptiness witnesses, $81$ had complete-image exclusions, and $23$ were deferred. The newly retained witness for $49/48$ passes the rational curve equation and target-class tests, providing an exact nonemptiness certificate for one of those deferred rows. Deliberately changing its target class or replacing its witness vector by zero caused the expected failures. The augmentation script reproduces the submitted certificate from the retained inputs, and all pre-existing certificate fields are unchanged. These checks do not reproduce PARI's rank computations or the deferred descent and interval-$L$ arguments.

Two minor reproducibility corrections remain:

1. **The witness regeneration chain is incomplete.** By code inspection, `lwit4948.py` writes generators, rank bounds and a saturation bound, but does not write `witness` or `witness_x`. Both are required by `lcert_v3.py`. Thus rerunning the documented chain overwrites the supplied witness file with an incompatible record and the next step raises a missing-key error. Have the generator compute and save a target-class witness and its abscissa, or document the missing step. Also make the copying of `lcert.py`'s actual output, `lcert.json`, to `lcert_v2.json` explicit in the README. The retained witness is independently checkable, so this regeneration defect does not undermine the result.
2. **Deferred verdict comparison is still overstated.** The checker confirms record presence and prints the verdict from `lbox_final.json`; it does not compare the copied deferred record's verdict or even `final_verdict` against that file. Add the stated comparisons or narrow the documentation. My separate comparison confirmed that all $216$ supplied `final_verdict` fields agree with `lbox_final.json`.

These are limited implementation and documentation issues, not scientific regressions. The accepted partial advance is preserved. This version supplies neither a rational septuple nor a general nonexistence proof and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition.
