# Accept

This is a substantive partial advance on the supplied problem. The explicit double plane establishes the previously conjectured two-dimensional component dominating the isolated-entry line. The elliptic section and the two component-specific rationality obstructions give additional arithmetic information about that construction. The manuscript appropriately leaves the existence of a rational Diophantine septuple open.

The dimension argument is convincing: the recovered squared ratios give a two-dimensional image, the nonzero five-row Jacobian minor bounds the local dimension from above, and the two displayed anchor values establish nonconstancy on the integral source. Passing to $g^2=u$ preserves the relevant dimension and dominance. This supplies an explicit mechanism beyond the three fixed-anchor porisms of [3], rather than merely extending their numerical evidence.

The squareclass calculation also has a clear mathematical purpose. It separates the nine cross edges from the two groups of internal and anchor edges, and the elliptic section makes the cross character a square. The remaining classes explain the extension $\mathbb Q(\sqrt u,\sqrt J)$. Positivity at the marked point, together with density of an infinite-order elliptic orbit and nonconstancy of the anchor, supports infinitely many distinct configurations over varying totally real fields of degree at most four. This is not a claim of infinitely many configurations over one fixed field.

The negative results are suitably restricted and supported by complete arguments rather than bounded searches. In Section 4, the resultant and homogeneous residue reductions lead to the stated elliptic and genus-two problems. The descent describes the global candidates, the real restrictions, and a full local image at $2$; the resulting rank bound and torsion argument account for the rational points. Section 5 independently certifies a particular seven-window component. Its normalization and exceptional-fibre analysis address the possibility of singular rational images, an essential qualification for the rational-anchor exclusion. Neither argument purports to exclude the other components or all rational points of the double plane.

Verification was substantial but targeted. I reproduced the degree-26 plane substitution, the degree of the primitive norm, the rational discriminant zeros, all seven window identities, and both stated Jacobian minors. Independently implementing the displayed reconstruction recovered both marked anchor values and exactly the six prescribed windows. Exact rational-function arithmetic verified all nine deposited cross-root identities over $\mathbb Q(t,V)$ and the generic anchor squareclass. At three supplied elliptic parameters, exact arithmetic also verified all 21 edge classes; the marked positive example has the stated biquadratic field. The SageMath-only bivariate calculations and number-field local descent were inspected, not independently rerun. These checks should not be described as reproduction of the entire computational supplement.

The novelty distinction is adequate. The closest antecedent, [3], explicitly leaves dominance conjectural. Paper [4] supplies the connected-graph context and local controls, not this normalization or rational-point exclusion. The fixed-field translation families in [5] have a different regularity pattern and do not supply the present surface. The external construction in [8], checked against its arXiv text, concerns sextuples with two regular quadruples and one regular quintuple; its regular-extension machinery does not duplicate the six-window result. The contribution is the explicit geometry and its arithmetic consequences, not the general use of elliptic curves or regular fifths.

The main argument is readable for specialists and is organized around results rather than experiment history. The detailed coefficient data are appropriately relegated to the supplement. The following minor improvements would help:

- State the dominance result as a numbered theorem, explicitly distinguishing the source double plane, its image in $(u,h_0,\ldots,h_5)$, and the finite cover adjoining $g$.
- In Section 3, say “rational point $(t,V)$ on the elliptic quartic” when discussing rational parameters. This avoids suggesting that arbitrary $t\in\mathbb Q$ makes $V$ rational. Briefly recall the already-known quadratic-field examples from almost septuples, noted in [5], so the degree bound is not mistaken for a general minimal-degree claim.
- Record tested SageMath and python-flint versions, and give an explicit source or short rank certificate for the elliptic quotient labelled 62a1. Update [8] to its published reference, *Acta Mathematica Spalatensia* **1** (2020), 19–27, DOI: 10.32817/ams.1.1.2.

These are presentation and reproducibility improvements, not defects in the central contribution. Acceptance does not constitute a solution of the closed-ended septuple problem.
