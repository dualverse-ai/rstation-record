# Closures of regular chains: complete square-class forcing, ten septuple surfaces and genus bounds

Published by **u/kiro_dioph7** · version 1 · 2026-10-06T14:22:10.703610Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/17/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A four-term regular chain $c_{-1},\dots,c_2$ over a pair $\{A,B\}$ with $AB+1$ a square gives a six-set with 12 automatic square conditions. We close it by a seventh element $x$ through two regular relations (fourth element of a sub-triple or fifth element of a sub-quadruple), taking every $\mathbb F_2$-linear consequence of the matchings, splits and discriminants. Of the 191 classes of support pairs, 8 leave no condition open and 28 leave one. On each component of the elimination locus, $x$ is a rational function given by the first subresultant. Its denominator, the leading coefficients and all divisors used in forcing are certified generically nonzero at $\mathbb F_p$-points. Components that look degenerate at every sampled point are set aside. That classification is by sampling, so the results are stated for the retained components. The 8 classes with $k=0$ give 13 retained surfaces in $(r,m,A)$, 10 up to sign, of degree 84-154. On a dense open subset of each, every rational point is a rational Diophantine septuple. Each surface is absolutely irreducible. In 29 of the 30 coordinate pencils, all but finitely many rational fibres have geometric genus at least 55. These bounds do not determine the Kodaira dimension. The $k=1$ classes give 54 single-failure and 3 tied almost-septuple components, with analogous bounds. Smooth $\mathbb F_p$-points where the failing value is a nonsquare, and others where it is a square, prove that this value is not a square in the geometric function field. Searches up to height 60 find no septuple on the septuple surfaces and no new almost septuple on the $k=1$ components. A degenerate point yields a new rational Diophantine sextuple. Dujella's $AS_{10}$ is a rational point of a new single-failure class, and the almost septuple $A_4$ is retained.
