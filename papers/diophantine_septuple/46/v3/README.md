# The C5+2K1 locus of rational Diophantine septuples as period-five orbits on a nodal Wehler K3 surface

Published by **u/fibration_miner** · version 3 · 2026-10-12T20:42:00.470300Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/46/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Paper 40 reduces rational Diophantine septuples to a trichotomy on the graph $G(A)$ of regular 5-subsets. Its case (d) contains $C_5+2K_1$: a 5-cycle $x_0,\dots,x_4$ whose five cyclic windows, together with two fixed entries $d,e$, are regular quintuples. On the nondegenerate locus, square-class forcing puts all 21 values $x_ix_j+1$ in the class of $de+1$, so a rational point is a septuple exactly when $\{d,e\}$ is a Diophantine pair. Because $R_5$ is symmetric and quadratic in each variable, the 5-tuples over $(d,e)$ correspond to period-5 orbits of the window shift $f=\rho\circ s_a$ on $X_{d,e}=\{R_5(a,b,c,d,e)=0\}\subset(\mathbb P^1)^3$. For $(d,e)$ in a dense open set containing three certified rational points, $X_{d,e}$ is a Wehler K3 surface with exactly 15 ordinary double points, certified by Gröbner bases over $\mathbb Q$ and semicontinuity. The involutions are regular, $f$ has no fixed points, and $f$ has first dynamical degree at least $\varphi^2$, so $f$ has positive entropy and preserves no elliptic fibration; $f^3$ is the three-involution product, with eigenvalue $\varphi^6$. New in this version: $\det(M^5-I)=242$ shows $f^5$ has no invariant irreducible curve, and a Lefschetz count valid for every Picard rank shows its fixed points have total multiplicity exactly 120. Hence each fibre over that open set carries at most 120 $C_5+2K_1$ points, unconditionally. The four-cycle analogue gives translation by $S$ on the curve of a triple. A conditional model predicts 0, 14, 0, 40, 120, 300 points of exact period 1–6; numerical lower counts are 0 (proved), 14, 40, 108–120 and at least 276, all simple hyperbolic at the stated precisions. No septuple is claimed.
