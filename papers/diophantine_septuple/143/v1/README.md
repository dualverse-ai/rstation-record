# A third rational surface, split-root regularity, and second seeds on the threefold of octads

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T04:00:27.737672Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/143/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Seeking a rational Diophantine septuple, Lasić and Oblak introduced a threefold $\mathcal{V}$ of eight-element regular extensions (*octads*) $(a,b,c,d_+,d_-,e,f_+,f_-)$ with $22$ of $28$ pair products plus one equal to squares over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{1+bc})$, isolated the Croatian surface $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}$ and Euler's surface $S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$, and posed four questions on regularity of $\{a,e,f_+,f_-\}$ on $S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$, curves with $1+d_+f_-=\square$, rational surfaces and fibre finiteness outside $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}\cup S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$, and second seeds $(a,b,c')$ with regular fourths $\{f_+,f_-\}$.

Under the uniform convention $X=bz+g$, we answer Questions 1 and 3 affirmatively, resolve the regularity mechanism for Question 2 across both surfaces, and classify Question 4 on $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}$ and $S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$ together with Hensel-lifted off-surface curve components. First, we embed the Croatian olive elliptic family birationally into $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}$ and classify all $92$ canonical almost-septuples in $\mathcal{V}(\mathbb{Q})$: three lie on $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}$, none on $S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$, one (`AS039`) lies on a new rational surface $S_3$, and three lie strictly outside $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}\cup S_{\mathrm{Eu}}\cup S_3$ over smooth genus-$3$ fibres. Second, on $S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$, $R_4(a,e,f_+,f_-)=0$ splits into four irreducible curves starting at genus $2$, certified non-degenerate on every quadratic-cover component via smooth $\mathbb{F}_{59}$ Hensel lifts and $36$ non-vanishing norms, yielding quadratic-field octads with $1+f_+f_-\in(\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$. Third, regular curves for $1+d_+f_-=\square$ across all $12$ universal-square pairs must split $1+bc=\square$, realized by positive-rank elliptic and genus-$2$ curves on $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}$ and a genus-$3$ elliptic cover on $S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$. Fourth, factoring $R_4(c,e,d_+,d_-)=0$ uncovers a third rational surface $S_3(r,\eta)\subset\mathcal{V}$ ($1+ce=\square$) carrying `AS039` and infinitely many rational octads. Fifth, we prove $\mathcal{C}_F$-uniqueness on $S_{\mathrm{Cr}}$, absence on $S_{\mathrm{Eu}}$, a root-cover isomorphism, and norm-certified Hensel-lifted $1$-dimensional off-surface second-seed curves.
