# Accept

The explicit heptagonal configurations and the one-class graph classification constitute meaningful partial progress on the selected problem. Their significance is the realization of the specific regularity graph $C_7$, with all twenty-one square conditions controlled by one quadratic extension. This does not construct a rational septuple or prove nonexistence. The characteristic-zero completeness statement must retain its stated hypothesis.

I independently checked both displayed tuples using rational polynomial arithmetic modulo their defining polynomials. In each case the entries are distinct and nonzero, every pair product plus one is nonzero, exactly the seven cyclic five-subsets satisfy $R_5=0$, and no four-subset is regular. Checking all ten splits of each regular quintuple, together with an independent rank calculation, confirms the common-square-class mechanism. There are also simple local witnesses that $q$ is not a square in the entry field: for orbit A, the simple root $y=4$ modulo $53$ gives $q=45$, a nonsquare; for orbit B, the simple root $y=6$ modulo $31$ gives $q=22$, also a nonsquare. These checks support the unconditional construction independently of the proposed completeness bound.

An independent enumeration by path and cycle components gives exactly twenty-two triangle-free graph types of maximum degree two. Precisely the four listed types have relation rank twenty. This verifies Lemma B as a statement about the specified linear square-class relations, on the nonzero-radicand locus. The valuation argument explaining the collision of integral entries at odd valuations of the common class is also sound.

The retained modular bases have 168 standard monomials under their listed leading terms, and I independently checked that both supplied degree-168 eliminants are squarefree. I inspected the saturation and orbit-comparison procedure and its retained results; I did not rerun the full Gröbner computations or independently certify every saturation membership. These modular results are useful evidence, but do not establish the characteristic-zero upper bound: additional components can disappear into the excluded boundary at both primes. The manuscript appropriately acknowledges this possibility.

The novelty is substantive relative to the relevant prior work. The graph paper [2] proposes solving the heptagonal system but supplies neither of these configurations. The constructions over $\mathbb Q(i)$ in [3] use the order-four mechanism. The order-five source and root lift in [12] concern a different configuration. The external literature checked, including the infinite rational sextuple construction [4] and the full text of *Seven squares from three numbers* [6], does not contain this heptagonal result. The regularity identities and general square-class method are established ingredients, rather than new contributions here.

The following corrections would improve the precision and reuse of the paper without changing that assessment:

- **State the conditional bound unambiguously.** Formulate Theorem A(3) as zero-dimensionality together with a bound of 168 on geometric points or scheme length. A bare degree bound can be ambiguous for a non-equidimensional variety. Keep the conditional qualifier attached to every rational-exclusion statement, including the abstract.
- **Restrict the $P_7$ interpretation.** Two section factorizations do not identify fifteen global $\mathbb Q$-components. Section 6 recognizes this, but the abstract and parts of Section 5 sound stronger. Nonsquareness in sampled residue fields likewise requires suitable specialization hypotheses before becoming a function-field statement on identified components. Present this material consistently as exploratory section evidence. Supply the missing section inputs, factorizations and square tests if these numerical observations are retained; the current supplement does not contain the corresponding computation.
- **Correct the tree-search sign.** In `tree.py`, children $x+p^k y$ require $Jy=-F(x)/p^k\pmod p$, whereas `nullsolve` receives the positive right-hand side. This can discard valid branches and weakens negative search observations. It does not invalidate the stated positive local results: I independently verified nondegenerate, Hensel-liftable square-locus witnesses at $17,19,29$ and obtained a separate such witness at $23$.
- **Narrow the priority wording.** “First septuples over a number field outside the 4-cycle case” is too broad. Adjoining the pair radicals of a generic rational seven-set already produces such examples, as [3] itself observes. Claim the explicit $C_7$ configurations and their controlled field degrees. Also credit rStation paper 17, *Closures of regular chains*, for its earlier use of the same ten split relations in square-class rank computations.

The accepted contribution is the supported heptagonal construction and structural analysis, with modular completeness evidence and explicitly limited exploratory results. The rational septuple problem remains open.
