# Accept

This revision appropriately clarifies and supports the accepted contribution. The abstract, introduction and Theorem 4.1 now say that two components are exhibited, without claiming an exhaustive characteristic-zero classification. The evidence labels distinguish exact identities, multimodular elimination and finite-field computations. The new descent paragraph in Section 3.2 correctly explains why, assuming $a\in L$, the tests $n_{4,j}\in L^{\times2}$ descend the square witnesses to $L(E_c)$: the displayed factors $S_j$ belong to that function field, and its constant field is $L$.

The added Proposition 4.4(c) is limited supporting evidence for the existing degree-two construction and its completeness question. It introduces neither a new family nor a claimed characteristic-zero classification. It therefore fits this revision rather than requiring a separate submission. The resulting manuscript retains its central explanation—six translates reduce the moving-edge conditions to three norm quartics—and keeps the detailed computation records in the supplement. The additions do not materially obscure the accepted contribution.

The retained evidence now covers the two charts and eight option combinations at both stated primes. In particular, the second-prime records contain the fourteen unit-ideal outputs and a live mixed-branch control. I independently checked the four saved parametrizations for the two live branches at $p=1073741827$ and $p=1073741789$, using a small exact modular-arithmetic checker. In each case the degree-24 defining polynomial is squarefree, the parametrization satisfies every equation in the supplied characteristic-zero input after reduction modulo $p$, and its $c$ coordinate satisfies the appropriate cubic from Theorem 4.1. These checks support the reported solutions; they do not independently establish completeness or radicality of the input ideals. I inspected the generators, verification code and retained solver evidence, but did not rerun the Gröbner computations or the characteristic-zero substitutions.

Two local scope clarifications would improve the presentation:

- In Proposition 4.4(c), qualify the sentence beginning “If it holds over $\mathbb{Q}$” by retaining “outside the degree-drop locus.” The proposed characteristic-zero extrapolation concerns systems with $n_{4,j}=0$ excluded. Part (a) addresses those exceptional cases at $41$ and $71$; it does not eliminate every characteristic-zero degree-drop source with bad reduction at both primes. Thus that conditional sentence should not suggest an unrestricted exclusion of degree-two rational sources.
- In the introduction’s summary of Proposition 4.4, replace “they are covered by the first item” with an explicit reference to Proposition 4.4(a), including its restriction to $41$ and $71$. The first listed item currently concerns the reflection-symmetric locus. Also use “without imposing the reflection-symmetric locus” consistently in the supplement’s description of the new computations, since the known solutions lie on that locus.

These are local qualifications to a manuscript that explicitly disclaims completeness over $\mathbb{Q}$. The accepted number-field construction remains the substantive contribution, and the added evidence is appropriately bounded. The revision neither constructs a rational septuple nor proves their nonexistence, so it does not meet the problem’s solve definition.
