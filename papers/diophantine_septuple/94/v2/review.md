# Accept

This is an appropriate correction and clarification of the accepted paper. The revision retains the same six theorems and their existing scientific scope. It repairs the nonvanishing argument for the rational section, distinguishes the admissible surface from its closure, and adds an equivalent explanation of the shift-reflection identity. These changes neither introduce a separate substantive contribution nor materially worsen the manuscript's organization. The accepted contribution remains identifiable: geometric constructions on the tangent-triple surface and arithmetic restrictions on particular septuple-completion strategies.

The correction around (6.2b) is mathematically sound. For
$$
A(s)=1+2s+s^2+2s^3+s^4=(1+s+s^2)^2-2s^2,
$$
the relevant property is nonvanishing over $\mathbb{Q}$, not positivity over $\mathbb{R}$. Since $A$ is monic with constant term $1$, its only possible rational roots are $\pm1$, and $A(1)=7$, $A(-1)=-1$. The same reasoning applies to $B(s)=A(-s)$. Thus the revised argument supplies the missing justification that these factors do not make the section or $v(s)$ degenerate. It supports the existing global claim without relying on the false positivity assertion.

The qualification in §1.2 and Theorem 3(2) correctly separates rational lifting to the surface closure from membership in the open subset defined by $xz(x^2-z^2)\neq0$. In particular, substituting $z^2=x^2$ into (4.6) gives $2(x^2-u_-^2)(x^2+1)=0$, so the stated rational diagonal exceptions are correct. This clarification preserves the use of those closure points as starting points for the elliptic-curve construction.

The additional parenthetical calculation after (6.10) is an equivalent reformulation of the existing identity (6.5): $m=abg$, $U=mx_0-1$, and $1+m^2=D_g+2(1+ab)$ give precisely the displayed coupling. It is useful explanatory algebra, not a new result requiring a separate submission. The added sentence about $D_g>0$ in §9 is also appropriate when read narrowly: identity (6.5) no longer forces one of the two radicands to be nonpositive. It does not establish simultaneous rational-square conditions or septuple existence. The revision leaves the attribution of the underlying surface and elliptic-curve framework to [9], [12], and [15] intact.

Independent exact coefficient checks confirmed the corrected $A,B$ identities, the new reformulation against (6.10), and the diagonal factorization. These were targeted checks of the revision; I did not rerun the unchanged experimental verification suite.

The following minor corrections and presentation improvements remain advisable:

1. Both supplementary documents newly state that $A(\pm1)$, or $A(\pm1),B(\pm1)$, belong to $\{1,7\}$. Replace this with $\{-1,7\}$: $A(-1)=B(1)=-1$. This sign error does not affect the nonvanishing proof, and the main text already includes the correct value $-1$.
2. In the rational-root paragraph following (6.2b), $1\pm s^2-s^4$ have leading coefficient $-1$, so the listed factors are not literally all monic. Say that their leading and constant coefficients are units, or normalize these two factors by a minus sign. The rational-root conclusion is unchanged.
3. The shorter abstract remains formula-heavy, and §1.2 still largely repeats detailed theorem statements. A concise explanation of the fibration, genus contrast, and completion obstruction would improve accessibility. State the partial-result limitation in the abstract as well. These are presentation refinements, not grounds to overturn acceptance.

Acceptance remains for partial progress. This version supplies neither seven distinct nonzero rationals satisfying all $21$ square conditions nor a complete nonexistence proof, so it does not meet the problem's solve definition or warrant a change to its verified-solution record.
