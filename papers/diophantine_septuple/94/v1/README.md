# Bilinear Null-Cone Elliptic Fibrations, S_3-Triad Markov Calculus, and Universal Shift-Reflection Syzygies on the Symmetric Tangent-Triple Surface

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-28T17:41:47.964166Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/94/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We investigate the arithmetic geometry of the weighted projective tangent-triple surface $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{sym}}:W^2=2(x^2+y^2)(x^2+z^2)(y^2+z^2)\subset\mathbb{P}(1,1,1,3)$ introduced in [15, \S9(c.1)]. First, in bilinear null-cone coordinates $(u_-,u_+)$ on $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{sym}}$, fixing $u_-$ cuts out a smooth biquadratic elliptic fiber $\mathcal{E}_{u_-}$ equipped with a rational osculating section and a two-term Bäcklund translation $\mathcal{T}_{u_-}$ of infinite order, certified on the generic fiber over $\mathbb{Q}(u_-)$ and at four rational specializations via Mazur's Torsion Theorem; by contrast, fixing the hyperbolic product $\Omega=u_-u_+$ yields three quadrics linked by a sphere syzygy, cutting out a $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^3$-cover $\mathcal{F}_\Omega\to\mathbb{P}^1_\eta$ of genus five with finite $\mathcal{F}_\Omega(\mathbb{Q})$ by Faltings' theorem. Second, on the $S_3$-triad matrix $M_{\mathrm{triad}}(x:y:z)$, we establish a single square-class theorem, Markov and Joukowsky–Markov surface identities, fixed-$(x,z)$ multi-pair factorizations and rational-square multipliers on $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{sym}}$, and root-free projective recovery. Third, across the two-dimensional base surface of arbitrary tangent triples, we prove three general-$D_g$ shift-reflection syzygies on $P_0+2E_Y(\mathbb{Q})$, construct a globally certified rational non-collapse section covering every Pythagorean mirror $g=2s/(1-s^2)$, establish the sign-chamber inequality $\min(1+x_0\varphi_g(x_0),1+x_-x_+)\le 0$ for $D_g\le 0$, and show on the four-torsion curve $D_g=0$ (where the all-coset $[-2]$ exclusion was proved in [10, \S5.2] and [14, Theorem 6]) that the zeros $x_0=g\pm h$ of $1+x_0\varphi_g(x_0)=0$ satisfy $1+gx_0\in 2(\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$ and lie outside $P_0+2E_Y(\mathbb{Q})$. Finally, we prove that the canonical secants $m=-2t/(t\pm 1)$ of $1-\rho^2=\square$ reduce to $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{self}}:w^2=t^4-14t^2+1$ with $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{self}}(\mathbb{Q})=\{(0,\pm 1)\}$ via $2$-Selmer descent on $E_{48}$, and classify all five factors of the step-three cross-reflection defect.
