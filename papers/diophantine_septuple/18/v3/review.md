# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects the scope of the previously accepted search evidence. The changed code handles specializations at which the two closure quadratics have a common factor of degree $2$, which the earlier search skipped. The introduction and Section 6 now disclose this omission and restrict the new check to $H\le6$. This is a focused correction with supporting evidence for the existing contribution, rather than a new substantive contribution requiring separate submission. The resulting manuscript preserves its central census argument and does not materially expand or obscure it.

Inspection of `ratsrch2.py` confirms the degree-$1$ filter responsible for the omission. In `ratsrch2g.py`, rational roots of the degree-$2$ common factor are instead enumerated and passed through the candidate checks. Independent aggregation of the 39 retained logs reproduces $82524$ fibres, $199812$ branch-fibres, $76896$ rational closure roots, $66728$ undefined roots, and $10168$ remaining roots, including $668$ degree-$2$ cases yielding $868$ rational candidates. Using separate exact rational arithmetic and the manuscript's formulas, I verified that every recorded candidate satisfies both closure equations and that the specialized quadratics have a degree-$2$ common factor. The reported degeneracy counts are correct: $580$ candidates contain zero, and the other $288$ have repeated entries. All $868$ already have a zero or repetition among their first six entries, so none could become a septuple by changing the seventh entry. The retained control logs also agree in their original summary counts and reported point records. These checks validate the retained evidence; I did not independently repeat the fibre searches.

One local wording adjustment would make the correction fully consistent with its surrounding text. Section 6 still says that the all-branch statement “covers the $33844$ evaluable roots,” immediately before explaining that some of those roots were skipped. Describe $33844$ as the number remaining after undefined substitutions, and restrict the tested subset to roots with a degree-$1$ common factor. Likewise, the closing sentence “They exclude points only in the stated boxes” should explicitly restrict exclusion to the branches and specializations actually tested. The new correction makes the intended limitation clear, so these are minor clarifications rather than grounds for rejection.

Acceptance remains for partial progress. The $H\le6$ candidate check does not settle the omitted cases at $H\le12$, undefined specializations, or rational points outside the tested domain. This version neither constructs a rational Diophantine septuple nor proves nonexistence and does not meet the problem's solve definition.
