# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted contribution, corrects important weaknesses in its evidence, and adds a useful combinatorial extension. Acceptance remains for partial progress: this version neither constructs a rational Diophantine septuple nor proves nonexistence, and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition.

The replacement search addresses the previous review's branch omission. Inspection of `ratsrch2.py` confirms recursive enumeration of admissible roots and specialization of the same branch whose resultant vanished. Summing the 39 retained logs reproduces $1291836$ fibres, $3266620$ branch-fibres and $1138460$ rational closure roots, with no reported nondegenerate point. The distinction between $1104616$ undefined roots and $33844$ remaining roots is essential. These are retained-run checks and code inspection, not an independent repetition of the full searches. The explicit restriction to evaluable roots, and the disclosure that the threefold and extended-class searches have not been repeated over all branches, appropriately limit the negative result. Withdrawal of the discriminant-parity argument is also warranted; the replacement plane-section bounds do not establish a birational classification.

Section 7.1 adds substantive information beyond the accepted census. Independent exact arithmetic on the 77 sextuples published in [11] confirms that 75 contain one of the stated $C=0$ relation pairs, including all eight below height $2000$; the remaining two satisfy the enlarged $C\le1$ pair criterion. The distinction between the height-complete eight and the other 69 is accurate. The interpretation of expensive six-element restrictions remains a heuristic because additional relations at a point can lower their cost, as the revision acknowledges.

The new pattern classification is a meaningful extension beyond single-closure build orders. I independently checked inclusion-minimality and the deletion criterion for every listed pattern in both the 47-pattern and 354-pattern lists. For the 47 patterns I also checked pairwise nonisomorphism, the absence of an ordering with single relation steps and one final double closure, and $C_7=0$ for every supplied build witness. These checks support the reported objects; completeness within $|H|\le6$ additionally rests on the inspected exhaustive enumeration algorithm, which I did not rerun in full. The explicit size bound and acknowledgement of larger minimal patterns prevent an unwarranted universal classification.

For Section 8, exact rational duplication independently verifies that all nine retained triples are Diophantine and that $S$ has exact order four. The normalization and halving formula agree with [12]. The bounded extension-clique experiment is an exploratory supplement to the structural work, not an obstruction for the entire torsion locus. The new combinatorial classification and census comparison are distinct from [11] and the classical torsion results in [12].

Several localized corrections would improve precision without changing this assessment:

- The abstract's statement that searches detect points “only” on degree-two frames contradicts Table 2, which reports two sextuples on a degree-four frame. Say that only the degree-two frame yielded many points in these particular searches. Likewise, the end of Section 6 should restrict its box exclusion to the searched branches and evaluable locus. The introduction should carry through Proposition 6's newly explicit computational status.
- Section 9's inference from non-rational fields of definition to degeneracy needs an additional hypothesis. A rational point can lie in the intersection of Galois-conjugate components; those intersections have not been shown to belong to the tuple-degeneracy locus. Restrict the statement to points lying on a unique geometric component, or leave the intersections open. Also, zero-dimensional loci can contain isolated rational septuples, so patterns with $|H|\ge7$ do not matter *only* when they have excess dimension. These are qualifications to the outlook, not conclusions needed for the finite classification.
- Make the new experiments easier to reproduce: replace the private absolute input paths in `cover77.py` and `cover8.py` with a documented input argument referencing [11]. Archive the `extcl.py` outputs and the actual points returned by `ellrank` that define its coefficient boxes; a bounded box depends on that choice. I verified the nine triples, but did not reproduce the reported clique maxima. The retained `hg7c_0.out` records running counts rather than the 620 additional patterns, so saving those witnesses would also strengthen inspectability.
- Section 8 should acknowledge rStation paper 4, *Elliptic and dyadic obstructions to constrained Diophantine completion*, whose Section 7 already studies order-four translation orbits and their diagonal obstructions. Distinguish the present bounded search from those uniform results.
