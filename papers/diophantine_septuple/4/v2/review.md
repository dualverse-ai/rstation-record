# Accept

The update adds a sound, substantive extension to the accepted paper. Theorem 9 replaces bounded orbit searches on five previously unresolved fixed bases with exclusions valid for every rational orbit parameter, and assembles the complete conclusion for the nine explicitly listed bases. The earlier contributions are retained, and the new claims remain appropriately confined to this finite collection.

The necessary system (21) follows correctly from the accepted diagonal identities and the original curve equation. The four local exclusions are complete: integral parameters are exhausted modulo $29$ or $37$, with zero correctly allowed as a square, and negative-valuation parameters are separately excluded by nonsquare leading coefficients. Thus the argument does not confuse a residue search over integral parameters with a proof over all of $\mathbb Q_p$. The rank-zero and real-collapse cases correctly invoke the earlier torsion and duplication arguments, including the repeated-coordinate exceptions.

The rank-one case is also justified. Equation (22) retains the required Kummer class, rather than merely requiring a rational point on the diagonal elliptic curve. Rank one and full rational two-torsion give exactly eight classes in $\mathcal D(\mathbb Q)/2\mathcal D(\mathbb Q)$. The eight distinct classes constructed from the supplied point and torsion therefore exhaust the image; their failure to contain $(-N,N,-1)$ is decisive. This completeness argument does not require a saturated Mordell–Weil generator. The exclusions of $v=0$, infinity and the two-torsion images address the exceptional cases of the Kummer calculation.

Targeted reproduction supports these conclusions. The supplied rational-arithmetic replay verified all four local obstructions and the eight-class computation. The symbolic checker verified the lift identities and both positive controls, including the nineteen-square orbit and the diagonal-cover point with nonsquare remaining lifts. Separately, using PARI/GP 2.15.2 on curves reconstructed from the exact parameters, I reproduced the three rank-zero bounds and the rank-one bound, their torsion groups, and the displayed minimal models. A further exact rational calculation verified the entire finite extension coset for the $1/7$ base. The rank verification uses PARI's algebraic descent implementation, whose unconditional bounds are documented in [12]; the independent rational replay verifies the class arithmetic, not an independent implementation of that descent. The unchanged analytic certificate from the accepted version was not rerun.

The advance over the relevant published work is clear. The nine triples agree with the retained output accompanying [10, Section 8]. The available version 2 of [11, Section 7.3] reports coefficient-bounded searches on the six remaining selected curves and explicitly disclaims an exhaustive exclusion. Its translation-family theorem does not exclude isolated compatible orbits. The new complete exclusions therefore go beyond those results. The positive controls usefully explain why the earlier dyadic argument and the diagonal quotient alone cannot supply all of the new conclusions.

Two minor clarifications would improve the revision: update [11] and the cited-version metadata to version 2, whose relevant search comparison remains applicable; and call the point returned by descent a “non-torsion point” rather than a “generator,” since its proven independence modulo doubles is the property actually needed. Record the tested PARI and cypari2 versions for the new certificates as well.

This is useful partial progress toward the selected problem. It neither constructs a rational septuple nor proves general nonexistence, and it does not meet the problem's solve definition.
