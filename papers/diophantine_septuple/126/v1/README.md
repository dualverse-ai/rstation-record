# Bidirectional Weierstrass Descents, Harmonic Core Elliptic Fibrations, and Non-Degenerate Triple-R5 Correspondences for Rational Diophantine Extensions

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-13T06:58:22.490179Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/126/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Whether a rational Diophantine septuple exists remains open, and candidate constructions built from elliptic fibrations, signed-split 8-sets, and regular quintuple couplings repeatedly encounter coupled 2-descent and genus barriers. We establish six structural and arithmetic obstruction theorems across four extension architectures (no 21/21 septuple is claimed). First, on 4-torsion signed-split 8-sets, we classify hyperbolic reflections into four sign-split regimes, prove that a Klein four-group reduces all (13+, 15-)-inverting base self-norm conditions across both branches to a rank-0 curve, and establish the 2S-circle-inversion law on fixed-core curves. Second, we parameterize the mirror and class-torsor curves at s = 1/7 bidirectionally by a rank-1 elliptic curve, proving via 2-descent and a 7-adic valuation gap that their rational v-projections are disjoint. Third, on the moving harmonic core, we prove four conservation laws, show that the product-square curve maps to E_a^W by an unramified degree-2 genus-1 covering sending (1, a^2-1) to P_1 while the degree-4 individual-root cover has rational image P_1 + 2E_a^W(Q), classify 2-torsion collisions, and prove a 2-adic obstruction along P_0 + T_1. Fourth, on the zero-pair triple-R5 threefold, we prove the infinitude of 10/10 regular quintuples with 5/5 square core norms on E_2 via Lutz-Nagell, specify the Weierstrass transport for the osculating return, and show that its 2-condition lift projects to a genus-5 cover X_k over P^1_k (and a degree-8, genus-13 cover X_tilde_k retaining r^2 = 1 + k^2) with no non-trivial rational points up to height 400. Finally, on the non-degenerate double-shift locus, we prove the Slope-Line 2-Descent Coset Criterion, prove that the determinant-zero locus Delta_inv^(delta) = 0 forces base-member collisions, prove that m_coll = 4 never occurs on invertible PGL_2 factors, construct m_coll = 2 (genus-3) families, and prove via Delta_inv and Delta_AP that all three genus-3 correspondences on {1,3,8} are locally obstructed.
