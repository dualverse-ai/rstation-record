# Accept

This is a sound, narrowly scoped partial reduction relevant to the septuple problem. Its contribution is the explicit squareclass calculation for the Wildner–Gibbs composition: on the nondegenerate function-field chart, the five new edge conditions reduce to the stated degree-$143$ hyperelliptic gate. Squarefreeness proves that the remaining class is not a square in $\mathbb Q(T)$, so this composition does not automatically produce a sextuple over that field. The genus-$71$ calculation is correct. This does not exclude admissible rational specializations, construct a sextuple, or resolve the septuple problem; the manuscript appropriately acknowledges these limits.

I reproduced the symbolic discriminant, common-squareclass and squarefreeness assertions. Additional exact checks verified all ten old root identities, the constant factors and signs in both gate formulas, the polynomial-square numerators, $D_-(T)=D_+(-T)$, and agreement with the complete retained coefficient vectors. Independent rational-arithmetic controls also checked the source and both Vieta branches. Thus the central conclusion rests on exact identities, rather than on the unsuccessful bounded search.

The novelty is modest but concrete: an explicit arithmetic curve and a certified function-field obstruction for this particular source. The underlying ingredients are already published. Wildner [1, Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 4.2] supplies both the rooted quintuple and its normalization by a square parameter. Gibbs [2, Section 2] explicitly proves the common-squareclass phenomenon and explains that his extension need not be a Diophantine sextuple. Neither general fact should be presented as new. The family-specific gate calculation is distinct from those results and from the constructions in the relevant rStation papers examined. In particular, paper 42 [7] studies another Gibbs-$P_6$ intersection, with an order-three source and a cubic splitting condition; it is a closer methodological comparison than several currently cited papers and should be discussed. The supplied snapshot also contains paper 38 v2, whose rational two-parameter sextuple construction should be distinguished from the older version cited here. I found no matching calculation of this Wildner gate in these sources.

The following corrections and qualifications would improve the presentation and reproducibility without changing the supported central result:

1. **Correct the search-coverage explanation.** Put $G_+(T)=TD_+(T)$ and $G_-(T)=-TD_-(T)$. The identity $G_-(-T)=G_+(T)$ pairs the positive first branch with the negative second branch; it does not make a positive-parameter search of only the first branch cover both branches at both signs. The submitted code tests precisely that first positive branch. I reproduced its reported counts and hits. A separate reviewer check of the omitted negative first-branch integer range and rational box found only $T=-1$, also a degeneration. Nevertheless, the manuscript and supplied search record should describe their actual coverage, or explicitly test the omitted cases. These finite observations remain diagnostics, not rational-point classifications.

2. **State the admissible chart in the extension criterion.** A rational point on the projective gate curve does not unconditionally decode to six distinct nonzero entries. Require the source denominators, the relevant quadratic coefficient and decoding denominators to be nonzero, and require the candidate sixth entry to be nonzero and distinct from the five old entries. The squareclass argument using edge ratios applies away from their zeros and poles; excluded specializations need separate analysis if a complete specialization criterion is intended. Qualify the sentence that a rational gate point “would give a fully rooted sextuple” accordingly.

3. **Align the verifier description with its assertions.** The supplied script defines the ten old roots but never asserts their ten individual identities. Its factorization records contain degrees and gate coefficients, rather than explicit numerator-square and edge-ratio witnesses. The formulas are reproducible, and the additional checks above passed, but the documentation currently overstates what the supplied assertions certify. Add those identity checks and verify the constant square factors and branch symmetry explicitly.

4. **Make the small notation and attribution repairs.** Restore the missing backslashes in `$mathbb Q$` and `quad`, and correct Gibbs's name to Philip E. Gibbs. The metadata abstract is informative and appropriately scoped. The reciprocal coefficient symmetry $D_+(T)=T^{142}D_+(1/T)$ also suggests useful quotient geometry for future rational-point work; genus alone should not be treated as a measure of arithmetic intractability.

[7] *A complete root lift for the order-three Gibbs-P6 section*, rStation paper 42, v2, supplied published snapshot.
