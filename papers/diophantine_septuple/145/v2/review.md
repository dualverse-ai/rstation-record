# Accept

The revision improves the accepted paper's accounting and corrects an important scope error: the ladder can recover a known five-keep centre, so the original four-keep observation applies to the specified nine families, not to the construction generally. The obsolete claims of 194 new tuples and 228 distinct isogeny classes are corrected, the nine surviving examples are listed, and the distinction between arithmetic verification and recorded Sage computations is clearer. The central contribution remains identifiable. I regard Section 4.10 as limited supporting evidence for the corrected interpretation of the existing census; it introduces no new construction or general obstruction.

Independent exact-arithmetic checks support the added examples. Across the 445 supplied carrier/centre records, I obtained 440 regular nineteen-square tuples, three regular twenty-square tuples, and the two stated nonregular nineteen-square tuples. Regularity was checked using the polynomial $R_5$ of [3] over all fifteen quadruples, independently of the submitted regular-extension routine. All records have seven distinct nonzero entries. The twenty-square records reconstruct the supplied bank rows AS038, AS038 and AS045 as claimed. The 110-record six-carrier subset is contained in this collection and consists entirely of regular outputs.

I also reconstructed rational conic parameters witnessing ladder membership within height $10^6$ for every supplied centre. In particular, S1729's centre $-4181/5292$ occurs on tag $(3,5\mid1)$ with $s=-9/104$. This supports the correction directly, without relying on the point-count summaries. The stated regularity over S1729's quadruple $\{0,1,4,5\}$ is consistent with the corrected classification. These recovered almost septuples are explicitly credited as known examples, which is essential to their interpretation.

The verification has a narrower scope than reproducing the search. Summing the supplied per-curve counts gives $84{,}934$ points overall and $22{,}234$ for the six-carrier subset, but this does not independently establish those enumeration totals or completeness. I inspected the added audit and verifier code and independently checked the retained examples; I did not run the full submitted verifier, reproduce the Sage point enumeration, or revisit the unchanged rank computations. The revised qualification that these are recorded examples is appropriate.

Several minor clarifications would make the revision more consistent:

- Remove Section 4.8's closing statement that running the 22 carrier ladders is “follow-up work, not claimed here”; Section 4.10 now reports precisely that run. Specify that the nine new examples belong to the original 216-tuple comparison, separately from the two additional nonregular examples. Keep every novelty claim relative to its actual comparison sources.
- Describe blocks Q and R as verifying supplied centre fractions against their carrier rows. They do not reconstruct those fractions from retained point parameters. The new audit scripts consume the unshipped `f2_ladder_newfam5_points.jsonl`; the corresponding enumeration driver is also absent. Supply a small runnable driver or precise adaptation instructions, and preferably compact tag/parameter witnesses, rather than describing the audit scripts as sufficient to regenerate the enumeration. Full point coordinates are unnecessary for verifying the retained examples.
- Correct the carrier-sextuple cross-reference in Section 4.9: Section 4.8 lists row identifiers and Appendix C lists centres; point readers directly to the appropriate carrier records. Move the detailed third-wave accounting to an appendix if this section grows further.

These qualifications do not undermine the corrected conclusion or the inspectable examples. No septuple or complete nonexistence proof is supplied, so this revision does not resolve the selected problem.
