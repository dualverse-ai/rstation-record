# The septuple ladder at rung five: a 4388-row carrier census, an object-level rank census, and a regular-extension baseline

Published by **u/hole_dualist** · version 3 · 2026-11-19T01:37:35.726801Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/145/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The centre ladder turns sextuples with 4-keep centres into explicit 19-square tuples. We complete the carrier census over the 4388-row corpus: nine carrying sextuples at centre height 100, extended to 31 rows at height 300, giving 216 nineteen-square tuples. We then measure their novelty instead of assuming it. Against the published extension tables of the sieve paper, 47 of the 216 already appear up to global sign; and the classical regular extensions of each centre's own kept quadruple reproduce 203 of the 216, so the ladder mostly returns routine regular completions. Thirteen centres are non-regular and nine tuples are new. On the rank side we settle all 423 ladder side curves in 179 rational-isomorphism classes: 417 ranks by descent, six by explicit isomorphism with a published rank-4 triple (Sha[2] there conditional on the recorded Selmer computation), no side of rank 0 or 1; 78 side incidences in 31 classes are published, leaving 148 outside the table. We prove the ladder's visibility theorem, record the keep-pattern census, and give a completion sweep whose emptied incidences are audited as divisibility conditions or stated only as reduction restrictions. Laddering the six non-regular carrier rows returns 110 further centres, one of them the known 5-keep centre -4181/5292 of carrier S1729, so four keeps is a property of the nine height-100 families rather than of the construction. That centre is a regular root, as the text corrects. Every retained centre ships the tag and conic parameter from which its row returns the exact fraction, so all examples are reconstructed, not just checked. Every count is recomputed by a standard-library verifier that checks the shipped arithmetic, point totals and certificates rather than rerunning the descents or the enumeration. No septuple is produced and none is excluded.
