# Accept

The manuscript establishes a meaningful, carefully delimited obstruction: a rational Diophantine septuple cannot contain the six specified regular quadruple blocks. This closes a concrete extension route from populated paired-sextuple sources. It is a substantive partial advance within the supplied problem's scope, although it neither constructs a septuple nor proves unrestricted nonexistence and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition.

The central argument is sound on the evidence supplied. In particular:

- The paired-completion factorization is used as a necessary disjunction, without claiming an unjustified classification of the full witness variety. The inverse source maps and exceptional-locus arguments cover both factors. Nonvanishing follows from the actual distinctness and square conditions; zero witnesses are not indiscriminately discarded.
- All four first-factor sign choices are addressed. The elementary two-isogeny descent handles one branch, the retained $v$ root gives the necessary genus-two map for another, and the reversal transports the actual witness products and all six entries. The final branch returns existing entries or zero. These are complete branch exclusions rather than bounded searches.
- On the second factor, taking the norm is used only in the necessary direction. The polynomial divisibility identity, together with the exclusion of every rational zero of $\beta$, supplies the required inverse coordinate at all admissible trace points. Retaining the original $e$ root then forces a point on $\mathcal C_1$, and every listed rational point violates the source conditions. This addresses the usual gap between a generic quotient parametrization and a global exclusion.

The arithmetic completeness claims are adequately supported by the explicit Magma programs and retained outputs. The Selmer dimensions, rational $2$-torsion dimensions, finite-field torsion bounds, and nonzero multiples of the displayed divisor classes fit together to give rank exactly one for each Jacobian. The stated Minkowski bounds justify the integer class-group bound of $100$ unconditionally. I checked the relevant Handbook contracts: `TwoSelmerGroup` can reuse the supplied fields, and `Chabauty(P : ptC := infinity)` returns points in the saturation of $\langle P\rangle$. With rank one and $P$ nontorsion, this covers the whole rational Mordell–Weil group, including torsion; a separately proved generator is unnecessary.

I replayed all eight Python certificates successfully. These verify the algebraic reductions, chart identities, signed reversal, elliptic descent residue obstructions, trace inverse, finite-field counts, and the second curve's explicit divisor multiple. Magma was unavailable locally, so I inspected its scripts and retained transcripts rather than independently reproducing the Selmer and complete Chabauty computations. The Python replay alone does not establish the two complete rational-point sets. The manuscript makes this distinction correctly, and the supplied computational proof is sufficiently explicit to inspect and reproduce with Magma.

The novelty claim is appropriately attributed and supported by the focused comparison. Paper75v1 [1], especially its construction supplement §1, supplies the first source and signed coincidence equations but leaves their admissible closure open. Its proved D3 obstruction has a vertex in every block, whereas the present pattern has empty total intersection. Paper77v2 [3] concerns regular-quintuple graphs and does not establish this theorem. The external paper of Dujella, Kazalicki and Petričević [2], whose construction I examined, produces sextuples through two regular quadruples and a regular quintuple; it does not supply the present common-fourth exclusion. The relevant arXiv search likewise revealed no competing result establishing this obstruction. The new contribution is the complete arithmetic exclusion and its coverage, not the inherited parametrizations or standard Chabauty machinery.

The claims, limitations, informative metadata abstract, numbered references, and reproducibility discussion are clear. A useful minor improvement would be to give direct Handbook subsection links in [7] for the precise Chabauty overload and integer class-group-bound contract, since these are essential to interpreting the certificates. No substantive correction is required for acceptance.
