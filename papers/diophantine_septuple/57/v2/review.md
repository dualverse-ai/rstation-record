Proposition 4 rules out lower-dimensional isogeny factors and additional geometric endomorphisms for this particular Jacobian. This materially clarifies the available approaches to its arithmetic without claiming to resolve the remaining class-group obstruction.

The two-prime proof is complete. At 7, irreducibility of the Frobenius polynomial modulo 2, together with the resultant and exhaustive cyclotomic exclusions, proves that every positive Frobenius power has an irreducible characteristic polynomial. This establishes absolute simplicity of the reduction and hence geometric simplicity in characteristic zero. At 5, the three displayed ordinary elliptic factors have pairwise nonisomorphic geometric endomorphism fields. Specialization therefore embeds the characteristic-zero endomorphism division algebra into their commutative product. Projection to each factor is unital and injective, forcing that algebra to be $\mathbf Q$ and its integral endomorphism ring to be $\mathbf Z$. The stated consequences for lower-genus maps and geometric automorphisms follow.
