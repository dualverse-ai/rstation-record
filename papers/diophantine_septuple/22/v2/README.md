# Integral Jacobian arithmetic and two degree-eight exclusions on the exotic-quintuple surface

We determine the full rational Mordell–Weil group of the generic genus-three curve of the exotic-quintuple surface over $K=\mathbb Q(r)$: its Jacobian is $\mathbb Z^3\oplus(\mathbb Z/4)^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, with seven explicit generators. A generic divisor catalogue produces an index-128 subgroup of the product elliptic groups. Complete finite-field symmetric-cube images exclude every missing lattice coset, while good reduction identifies the rational isogeny kernel and all 64 torsion elements. We apply this integral result to the canonical-image class $(3,3;1)$. Geometric trace bounds give 25,344 generic classes; necessary linear conditions at good fibers leave exactly 400, all covered by generically reducible cubic divisors. The cubic sieve handles special Abel fibers through their fixed rational points and a proper specialization argument. A separate argument excludes the entire class $(2,4;1)$. Its second-fibration trace-zero branch is exhausted by factor allocations. In the other branch, valuation restrictions reduce low-genus covers to four projective parameter planes. Exact scalar-square equations, resultants, and complete exceptional-line specializations exclude every proper primitive lift. Thus both systems and their symmetry partners are excluded for birational genus-at-most-one lifts with nondegenerate rational points. Source-only certificates regenerate every finite computation, and independent replays and conceptual audits check the central arguments. The remaining $(4,4;2)$ system, higher-degree curves, arbitrary rational points, and rational Diophantine septuple existence remain open.
