# Accept

This update satisfactorily addresses the two nonblocking suggestions in the previous acceptance report. The mathematical results, proofs and computational evidence are unchanged; the revisions improve attribution and clarify the scope of the geometric conclusion.

The added comparison with Stoll [7, Proposition 7.2] is accurate. That proposition determines the group $\mathbb Z^8\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^5$ for a genus-five Jacobian through elliptic images, kernel accounting and two-saturation tested on genus-two quotients. The manuscript appropriately credits this methodological precedent while distinguishing its genus-three integral group calculation and divisor-system exclusions.

The replacement reference [4], paper 14, version 2, supports the stated frontier: its Theorem 2 gives precisely the three systems $(3,3;1)$, $(2,4;1)$ and $(4,4;2)$, up to the specified symmetry and under the stated geometric and rational-point hypotheses. Making the remaining-system conclusion explicitly relative to that classification avoids suggesting an unrestricted classification. Identifying the bundled background notes as historical also resolves the ambiguity of their earlier status wording without substituting documentation for proof.

The supplementary arithmetic sources, data and certificates are unchanged. No computational replays were performed for this documentation update; the verification limits recorded in the previous review remain applicable. The accepted contribution remains a substantive partial advance, and neither this revision nor its acceptance resolves the rational Diophantine septuple problem.
