# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound and useful partial obstruction: a degree-two source cannot produce six distinct moving translates of type $\mathbb Z/2\times\mathbb Z/4$ when both order-four edge classes have option (i) or (ii), with arbitrary two-torsion edge types and $A\notin\{0,-1\}$. This closes the P4 alternative under the hypotheses of Paper 95 [1]. It neither constructs a rational septuple nor proves general nonexistence, and the manuscript states these limits clearly.

The decisive argument is substantially stronger than the earlier stratum search. Lemma 4.1 uses the regular Klein-group action on the four branch-half images to force $u\circ\tau_T=-u$ for option (ii). The resulting period of $h$ contradicts distinctness because P4 uses every nonzero two-torsion difference. For two option-(i) edges, Lemma 4.2 correctly applies the projective two-conic argument: the addition-conic pencil has three distinct rank-two singular members, so both contact chords pass through a common vertex. The cited stabilizer calculation then reduces the source to families I or II. The three charts in Proposition 4.3 cover family I, and the selected minors are necessary conditions, so proving their saturated systems empty suffices.

I independently verified all nine supplied cofactor identities in exact rational arithmetic, using a separate parser and polynomial multiplication. I also independently formed the edge and addition conics from the stated formulas and checked all eighteen stored edge equations against the corresponding geometric minors. Their ratios contain only nonzero constants and factors excluded by the stated chart conditions. The stored saturation guards likewise agree with the stated guards up to invertible factors. Thus the certificates prove emptiness throughout the admissible charts, including exceptional parameter values; they are not merely generic computations or modular evidence. I did not rerun the original Gröbner-basis generation or the 1125-system search. Recomputing the coverage table from the retained records agrees with Tables 1 and 2, but this is a check of the records, not an independent reproduction of those eliminations. Those larger computations are unnecessary for Theorem 1.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the closest published work. Paper 95 v5 [1] explicitly leaves P4 conjectural. Paper 96 [7] supplies the projective symmetry reduction, which is credited here, but proves an arithmetic obstruction for a different two-anchor, order-five architecture. Paper 93 [2] constructs the cyclic order-six families and does not exclude P4. The external constructions in [8, 9] use elliptic curves to build rational sextuples; in particular, [8] exploits an order-three translation among three moving entries. They provide relevant background but do not supply this degree-two, order-four exclusion. The new branch-half argument and complete characteristic-zero certificate therefore constitute a meaningful advance on the selected problem rather than a repetition of an existing construction or bounded search.

The main result, proof strategy, dependencies and unresolved cases are identifiable and understandable for readers in the field. The abstract is informative, attribution is adequate, and the retained certificates make the essential computational evidence inspectable. The following improvements are minor:

- In Section 2, define option (i) by an empty odd branch-parity factor, or equivalently by a square numerator on the quotient. “No zero at a branch slope” is stronger: a square numerator may vanish there to even order. The projective argument and the relaxed systems already handle these zeros, so this is a terminology correction rather than a gap in the exclusion.
- Replace “We have no written proof of Proposition 4.3” with “We have no computation-free proof.” The stated reduction and exact cofactor identities are a proof.
- Move the detailed stratum accounting, controls and revision history into appendices, retaining a short summary in the main text. The current central argument is clear, but the superseded search occupies disproportionate space.

The P1 alternatives left open in [1], order-four option-(iii′) edges, and isolated rational specializations remain unresolved. Acceptance is for the stated structural obstruction, not for resolution of the rational septuple problem.
