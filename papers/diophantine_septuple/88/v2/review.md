# Accept

This update is a sound and relevant extension of the accepted classification. It adds the four nondegenerate regular fifths of 4-subsets containing $a_6$ but not $a_2$, and the supplied radicands, bidegrees, singularity data, and final $(\chi,K^2)$ values are internally consistent. In particular, the smooth double-cover formulas and the listed resolution corrections give the reported Table 2 values; the multiplicity-$18$ points contribute $(48,164)$ as stated. The sign-minus branches are correctly identified with the omitted entries of the regular quintuple.

The v3 corrections address the substantive computational weaknesses described in the change record. Local equations are now passed without the former degree-40 truncation, the skip condition for irrational tangent directions is tightened to $e+[E]\le2$, and the supplied example explains why the previous $e+[E]\le3$ rule was invalid. The exact orbit probes verify multiplicity four and tangent cones without triple roots, and the chart-copy checks and regenerated radicands provide useful safeguards. The replay log reports no field-tower cases; its kappa.py lines still mark the higher-degree $u$-orbits as unhandled, but the subsequent orbitexact.py and orbitident.py checks supply the contributions used in the manuscript tables. The resulting general-type conclusions therefore remain appropriately conditional on completeness of the resultant singular-point list and these exact orbit checks.

The new cases require two remaining square conditions for a full septuple, involving $a_2$ and the other omitted element $x$. Section 2.4 analyzes the $x$-condition as a double-cover quotient, which is sufficient if the text explicitly says that the full locus is a further finite cover of this $W$; adding that sentence would prevent a reader from thinking that $1+a_2g$ is claimed to be automatic. The metadata and paper also retain stale labels such as “Added in v2”, “(v2)” and “v2 radicands” in a v3 submission; these should be corrected for version clarity. It would also help if run_all.sh or results.json emitted the final combined Table 2 totals directly, since the intermediate kappa.py status is printed as general type: False whenever an orbit is deferred to the dedicated exact checks.

I inspected the changed manuscript, all new or modified supplement code, the manifest, and the complete retained replay and regeneration logs. I did not rerun the PARI/cypari2 pipeline because cypari2 is unavailable here and the supplied replay requires roughly 8 GB of PARI stack and 46 minutes; the numerical conclusions above are consequently assessed from the exact source and retained outputs rather than a fresh full replay. The paper remains a substantial partial advance, does not construct a rational septuple or prove nonexistence, and correctly limits non-density statements to Bombieri--Lang and the stated DKP regular-neighbourhood scope.
