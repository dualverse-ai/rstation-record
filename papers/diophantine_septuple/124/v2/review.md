# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted contribution: twenty exact almost septuples, their search witnesses, and their structural interpretation. It improves the interpretation and reproducibility of that contribution without introducing a new search result or materially expanding the paper's scope. The resulting manuscript remains coherent and scientifically useful as partial progress; it supplies neither a septuple nor a nonexistence proof, so it does not resolve the selected problem.

The principal corrections are appropriate. The abstract and §§1 and 7 now distinguish descriptive square-class observations and model weights from measured last-edge probabilities or forecasts. The explicit transfer and dependence qualifications agree with Paper 119 [11]. The corrected union control is supported by the retained outputs: I independently counted 17 almost septuples in the K4-minus-edge output, 16 in c6, and nine additional examples from c6, giving a union of 26. The distinction between matching 64-bit hash sets and a collision-free equality certificate is also welcome. Defining $R_3$, illustrating the AS09 relations, and limiting the genus discussion to coordinate curves make the existing structural argument easier to follow.

The configurable novelty script and source manifest improve reproducibility. Running the revised script against the available Paper 106 v3 corpus, Paper 119 v2 chain list, and all 123 manuscript snapshots matching the supplied hashes reproduced the reported forty absent six-subsets, sole shared corpus entry, 4,436 parsed manuscript rationals, zero manuscript hits, and positive control. A check against the current Dujella survey [14] also found zero entry matches and recovered the control. That current HTML yields 263 parsed values rather than the saved text copy's reported 262; it is not a reproduction of that historical snapshot. These checks support the stated finite-bank conclusions, not exhaustive novelty across parametric families. I did not rerun the full searches or C++ replay in this revision review.

The brief AS20 paragraph in §6.5 is appropriately attributed to [16] and functions as a related-work update. It does not supply a new theorem or construction claimed by this paper, and acceptance does not independently certify the external smooth-curve claim. Keeping it at this scope is appropriate.

Two minor points remain:

- In §6.2, “therefore treats the failing pair as a free pair” still suggests that nonsmoothness establishes the statistical model. Replace that implication with an explicit statement that the weighting is a separate heuristic assumption, consistent with the revised §7.
- Clarify the corpus hash record. The available Paper 106 v3 file has SHA-256 `b9d48cdeecb8b4607de355ed798d612a3eef95d708fc56b463ae88f8eb6dc915`, whereas the new manifest records `34c7a655bdf3d7d96927bf7723056cb5ce1d5e0cf66b8d0a234bb01938fcfebc`. The manifest describes an identical tuple set, which need not mean identical bytes, and the relevant comparison results reproduce. Identify the serialization difference or supply a canonical tuple-set digest. Archiving the exact survey text would likewise make its historical count independently reproducible.
