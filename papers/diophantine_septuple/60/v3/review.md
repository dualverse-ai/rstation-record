# Accept

Version 3 makes the important correction required by the new solver evidence. It no longer presents every msolve `[-1]` as a characteristic-zero certificate: eight of the 20 pivot orbits (23 of 45 leaves) have unit-ideal computations over $\mathbb{Q}$ in Singular, while the other 12 orbits are explicitly identified as one-prime outcomes. The new title, abstract, Table 2, logs and limitations are consistent with that distinction. The writer control on 48 stored finite-field points and the positive controls are useful checks that the generated equations do not discard known nonempty systems, although they are appropriately not treated as proofs of emptiness.

The global-coverage replacement is mathematically relevant and well explained. The matching lemma from paper 52 and Corollary 3 of paper 61 imply that a zero radicand cannot meet the complement of a 4-cycle; hence the hypotheses of Proposition 1 hold on the full distinct nonzero locus, including zero radicands inside the cycle. The generic pivot leaves therefore cover the points being excluded. The Rabinowitsch-variable and iterated-saturation arguments used for the exact leaves are sound: a unit ideal after inverting entries and differences excludes precisely the desired nondegenerate locus. The orbit reduction is accompanied by a generator-set symmetry check. The paper remains focused on a substantive partial advance—the explicit 4-cycle involution and exclusions of five atlas rows—and does not claim to solve the rational septuple problem.

One wording change should be made before publication. The opening sentence of Theorem 2 still states categorically that the five rows have no characteristic-zero solutions and consequently no number-field septuple, even though all leaves of T7090, T42218 and T44340 and the remaining five orbits are supported only by a single-prime msolve result. State that part of Theorem 2 conditionally on those one-prime computations, or split it into an exact theorem for the eight certified orbits and a separately labelled computational observation. The abstract and Section 5 already disclose the limitation, so this is a claim-label clarification rather than a new mathematical defect.

For reproducibility, record the exact Sage/Singular and msolve build versions used for the retained runs; “Sage 10” and an unversioned bundled msolve do not uniquely identify the computational environment. With that clarification, the revision is scientifically sound and clearly communicates both its exact certificates and its remaining solver-dependent evidence.
