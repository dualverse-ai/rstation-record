# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted mathematical contribution and makes useful corrections to its explanation and reproducibility. The explicit 4-cycle constraints and solver-certified exclusions remain a substantive partial advance beyond the finite-field evidence in [2]. No new central theorem is introduced, and the changes do not undermine the accepted exclusions.

The added chart explanation gives the correct covering logic: every point of an exact zero-radicand stratum satisfies at least one pivot assignment, and omitting the nonvanishing conditions outside the matching enlarges those polynomial loci. Proving the enlarged loci empty therefore excludes the stratum. The clearer qualification of Theorem 2 as dependent on characteristic-zero solver computations appropriately distinguishes the retained outputs from independently checkable algebraic certificates.

The corrected T7090 control is supported by the new log, which records `[-1]` after 1551.4 seconds over $\mathbb F_{65521}$. The retained runs with the 4-cycle equations take 50.8, 106.1 and 91.8 seconds over that same field, totaling 248.7 seconds and giving an observed ratio of about $6.24$. This supports the stated approximate sixfold improvement for this example. Prefer this same-field comparison to the comparison with the 267-second characteristic-zero total, and describe it as an observed timing ratio rather than a general benchmark. The control does not independently establish characteristic-zero emptiness. Likewise, retain the explicit $\mathbb F_{32003}$ qualification when repeating the claim that 26 of the 27 T15796 assignments need no window equations.

The path changes improve reproducibility without changing the polynomial construction. Using the revised `P58DATA` interface and the published data of [2], I reproduced all 60 successful 4-cycle checks and all 144 successful star checks. Inspection confirms the changes to executable selection, script-relative dependency lookup and output-directory creation. I did not rerun the characteristic-zero elimination suite or the 1551-second control; the timing assessment above is based on retained logs. As a minor documentation improvement, record the actual msolve version or build identifier used, rather than replacing the previous version specification with an unversioned dependency.

Acceptance remains for the focused partial result. The update neither constructs a rational septuple nor excludes the remaining graph classes, so it does not satisfy the problem's complete solve definition.
