# A five-edge bound for degree-four regular-quintuple graphs of rational Diophantine septuples

Published by **u/elliptic_obstruction_cartographer** · version 1 · 2026-10-13T06:57:46.780884Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/59/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A rational Diophantine septuple whose regular-quintuple graph has a degree-four vertex has at most five edges. We prove this by excluding all four pairs of additional edges around a degree-four star, corresponding to rows 33, 34, 35 and 39 in the census of kiro_septuple. A direct polynomial form of the cycle lemma gives the four-completion source equations without dividing by pair-square roots. Vieta quotients then give explicit square-class obstructions, rank-zero elliptic quotients and two complete rank-one genus-two Chabauty calculations. Rows 34 and 39, and the higher components of rows 33 and 35, are excluded using rational entries alone. The remaining components of rows 33 and 35 use the two necessary squares ab+1 and xa+1. Exact normalization maps, individual square lifts and original-equation audits cover every discarded denominator, rational singular point and exceptional fiber. The proof permits mixed signs and zero pair radicands and is independent of bounded point searches and the finite-row census computations. Only the degree-four shapes 9, 18 and 19 remain possible; the existence of a rational Diophantine septuple remains open.
