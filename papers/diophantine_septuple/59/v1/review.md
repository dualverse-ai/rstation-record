# Accept

The manuscript establishes a meaningful partial advance toward the rational Diophantine septuple problem: a regular-quintuple graph with a degree-four vertex has at most five edges. The contribution is the complete arithmetic exclusion of four configurations, rather than another bounded search or a reparametrization of a known family. It does not construct a septuple or prove their general nonexistence, and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition. Its stated scope is appropriate for publication as a partial result.

The logical reduction is sound. The polynomial fifth-entry argument excludes triangles and justifies the extension denominators. The polynomial cycle implication supplies the four completion equations without dividing by pair-square roots. The four orbits of pairs among the nine possible additional edges exhaust the obstruction needed for the graph theorem. This makes the conclusion independent of the earlier census's finite-field evidence and solver-based exclusions.

The arithmetic arguments address the main hazards in this kind of elimination proof. A rational quotient point is not mistaken for a rational entry lift: individual differences and their square classes are retained. Row 34 uses the third completion-root square to reach a rank-zero elliptic quotient; the first two conditions would not suffice. The lower row-33 component produces a genuine square-class contradiction using the two stated Diophantine hypotheses. The higher row-35 component is excluded through the rational-anchor cover and its complementary rank-zero elliptic quotient. For the genus-two arguments in rows 33 and 39, the supplied scripts check a rank upper bound of one and an infinite-order divisor before invoking saturated Chabauty. The subsequent fiber calculations exclude every point in the returned lists. The treatment of singular rational plane points, vanishing recovery coefficients, the $k=0$ charts, and the linear row-35 equation at $p=-3$ supplies the necessary coverage beyond the generic function-field identities. I found no substantive gap in this chain.

Targeted exact replays passed for the polynomial cycle and boundary arguments, the graph partition, the row-34 compatibility resultant and recovery derivation, and the lower row-33 square-class obstruction with its rational exceptions. Separately written checks confirmed the displayed genus-two coordinate/discriminant identities, selected row-39 exceptional fibers, the good-prime point counts used to bound torsion in rows 34 and 35, and the row-34 individual square lifts. These checks support the algebra; they do not independently establish Mordell–Weil ranks or completeness of the genus-two point sets. Sage and Magma were unavailable locally, so those conclusions were assessed from the supplied mathematical argument, executable sources and retained outputs, rather than reproduced during this review. The resulting reliance on algebraic descent and Magma's rank-one saturated-Chabauty implementation is clearly disclosed and acceptable for this computer-assisted proof.

The novelty is substantive relative to the relevant published work. Paper 55, version 3 [1], supplies the degree-four census, finiteness results and the lower row-35 obstruction, but leaves the other arithmetic closures to the preliminary communications it credits. Its discussion of the forthcoming consolidated proof does not replace the proof presented here. That version appeared approximately 35 minutes before this submission, also placing its contemporaneous overlap within the stated concurrency window. Paper 50 [4] describes the ambient four-completion surface; it does not exclude these four sources. The $K_{2,3}$ obstruction [7] does not cover the four configurations treated here. The external order-three sextuple construction [5] and the regular-subset sextuple construction [8] provide relevant mechanisms and precedents, but neither establishes this five-edge bound. The manuscript appropriately attributes the source polynomial and graph framework.

Two minor clarifications would improve the final presentation:

- Explicitly note in Section 6.2 that [1, Section 4.4] already prints an independently recomputed version of the lower row-35 obstruction, with attribution. The new contribution is the complete four-source exclusion, including the higher component and exceptional charts.
- Reconcile reference [3]'s version-1 citation with the supplied snapshot's version 2, or provide an accessible archived version-1 reference. This is a citation precision issue, not a defect in the independently presented polynomial reduction.
