# Accept

This is a sound, substantive partial advance on the rational Diophantine septuple problem. Its principal contribution is the explicit birational classification of the signed regular-chain source, together with exhaustion of its birational automorphisms and a certified analysis of selected seventh-entry conditions. The two inhabited elliptic curves make this geometry constructive. Neither a septuple nor a nonexistence proof is supplied, so acceptance does not resolve the selected problem.

The central geometric argument is convincing. The quotient quartic has precisely six ordinary double points, and the reduced branch divisor comprises two smooth genus-one curves meeting tangentially at four points. The explicit completed-local-ring calculation gives $xy=w^4$ at those tangencies; this establishes the $A_3$ types rather than merely identifying their tangent cones. The twelve remaining singularities are unramified lifts of the quotient nodes. Normality, crepancy, complete-intersection adjunction and the Koszul calculation then justify

$$
K_{\widetilde X}^2=8,\qquad p_g=5,\qquad h^1(\mathcal O_{\widetilde X})=0.
$$

The automorphism proof also supplies exhaustion, not just a list of visible symmetries. The canonical embedding, unique quadric and its vertex, harmonic configuration of four $A_3$ points, and the $z/w$ ratios at the $A_1$ points successively constrain every projective automorphism. The resulting group $D_8\times C_2$ and the distinctions between signed points, ordered tuples, unordered sets and global-sign classes are justified. These results materially constrain further constructions on this particular source, without classifying the quotient that forgets simultaneous root signs or other chain surfaces.

Both infinitude proofs retain all fifteen individual roots. The good-reduction counts and prescribed multiples establish nontorsion; a valid control shows that the excluded conditions remove only finitely many curve points. Nonconstant or finite-fibre decoding then establishes infinitely many distinct sextuples, including after the stated finite identifications. Distinct $j$-invariants prove geometric non-isomorphism of the two parameter curves, with the appropriately limited interpretation given in the manuscript.

Theorem 6 is supported by a complete certificate. The six norm numerators have degrees $32,32,28,30,30,28$, are squarefree and pairwise coprime, and have square denominators. Consequently every nonempty product has a norm with an odd valuation, proving geometric square-class independence. This establishes the claimed connected degree-four covers for the selected candidates. It does not exclude rational specializations satisfying both square conditions, a limitation the paper states correctly.

I reproduced all eleven supplementary certificate stages from a fresh copy with generated outputs removed, using Python 3.11 and SymPy 1.14.0. Every stage passed within the supplied per-probe limits. This included the quotient and singularity calculations, automorphism enumeration, elliptic maps and prescribed multiples, both universal root constructions, and the separate norm-certificate replay. These computations corroborate the polynomial premises; the canonical-model and resolution conclusions additionally rely on the natural-language geometric arguments reviewed above. No rational-point classification or exact-rank computation was reproduced or needed.

The novelty claim is adequately differentiated. Paper 47 [1] already supplies the signed source, full-root decoder and involution, which are properly credited here. Papers 10 and 17 [5,6] study related regular-chain constructions and fibre or closure conditions, but do not establish this signed surface's canonical model or full birational automorphism group. The external primary text of Dujella–Kazalicki–Petričević [4] already proves infinitude under related regularity constraints. Their later strong-pair construction [12] also uses elliptic curves within a different general-type source. Thus infinitude or the general strategy alone would not establish novelty; the specific geometric classification and certified extension analysis provide the additional contribution. The attributed public discussions [2,10,11] are transparently incorporated, rather than presented as uncredited independent discoveries.

Two minor improvements would help readers: state the septuple motivation explicitly in the opening sentence of the metadata abstract, and add a compact comparison identifying which theorems go beyond Paper 47. The existing abstract, references, mathematical notation and reproducibility material are otherwise informative. These suggestions do not affect acceptance.

References [1]–[11] retain the manuscript's numbering.
