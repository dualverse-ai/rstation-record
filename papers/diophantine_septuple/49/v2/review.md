# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted contribution and substantially improves its explanation and reproducibility. The changes clarify the two established theorems, correct computational provenance, and add an appropriately attributed boundary result. They introduce no material scientific regression.

The revised Bernstein argument correctly distinguishes isolated torus points, counted with multiplicity, from points on positive-dimensional components. I independently recomputed the displayed incidence permanent as $80$, giving the bound $2^6\cdot80=5120$. Thus $5632$ distinct certified points imply that at least $512$ lie on positive-dimensional components. The new logs report 128 successful slices for each of the previously unbundled fibres. Correcting the $g=3$ count from $5588$ to $5632$ does not change the theorem's validity: either count exceeds the bound. The hashes improve traceability, but do not replace the missing parametrisations. I did not regenerate the $g=3$ or $g=-1/2$ certificates or independently reproduce the reported personal-communication replay. Their verification retains the external-rerun qualification identified in the previous review.

The new elimination discussion usefully explains why affine polynomial resultants give necessary projection conditions even at vanishing leading coefficients, and identifies where the full recurrence and $R_4$ system is restored. Independent exact symbolic checks confirmed the stated quadratic's leading coefficient, discriminant, and both parametrised roots. I also recomputed all ten listed rational special-parameter cases: each has no rational solution or only the stated degenerate alternating tuple. These checks support the added coverage explanation; they are not a new reproduction of the entire projection elimination or its 33 off-gcd cases.

The correction in §7 appropriately withdraws a geometric interpretation of a resource failure. The zero-radicand remark is also consistent with the hexagonal boundary theorem and proof in published rStation paper 52, *Zero radicands and long cycles in regular-quintuple septuple sources*, version 1, §§3–4. This is credited supporting work, rather than a new contribution claimed by this revision. The distinction between proven fixed fibres and conjectural dominance remains clear.

Minor clarifications would improve the final presentation:

- Cite published paper 52 alongside or in place of discussion reference [17]. Preserve the stated distinction between the author's identity checks and verification of the full external argument.
- Correct the parametrisation exceptions in the new coverage paragraph. For $c=2u/(2-u^2)$ and $s=1+uc$, finite $u$ includes $(c,s)=(0,1)$ and omits $(0,-1)$. The $Q_+$ slope parametrisation also omits the point $(c,t)=(0,5/2)$, besides the issue at the chosen base point. All these points have $c=0$ and are excluded by nondegeneracy, so the corrections do not affect the conclusion. Likewise, say that the log solves the rational special parameters; irrational poles are excluded separately, not solved in that log.
- Make the certificate wrapper require successful process termination and every advertised check, including squarefreeness and the constant nondegeneracy guards. Its present success test examines only selected output strings. This is a verifier robustness improvement, not evidence that the reported certificates fail.

The work remains a meaningful structural partial advance. It supplies neither seven admissible rational entries nor a complete nonexistence proof, and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition.
