# Accept

This is a sound and useful partial advance on the selected problem. Its contribution is the explicit intersection of the order-three construction with Gibbs regularity, together with a sufficient rational lifting criterion and a concrete equation for the remaining arithmetic problem. No nonboundary rational point, new sextuple, or septuple is established. Acceptance does not mean that the problem's solve definition is met.

The central argument survives inspection. The factorization of $P_6$ isolates the stated nonzero component. On the admissible smooth seed chart, the discriminant identity makes $-s/w$ a rational square when the orbit cubic splits. The quotient identity then supplies rational ordinates. Crucially, the proof addresses individual square roots: evaluation of the chord identity at a seed root gives adjacent edge-product squares, and the nonzero row-product square supplies each individual cross edge by division. Evaluation at zero supplies the three mutual orbit edges. Thus the theorem does not confuse rational abscissas or square products with a fully lifted Diophantine tuple. The exclusions are consistent with the denominators used in this argument.

I independently checked the $P_6$ factorization, orbit polynomial, discriminant, quotient identity, and row-product formula by exact symbolic arithmetic. Replaying the inspected symbolic portions of the supplement also verified the torus identities, the source-coordinate identities, and the Möbius classification: 12 stabilizer maps, three square-product maps, and 18 pairs, all on the stated boundaries. Exact factorization confirms the claimed irreducibility over $\mathbb Q(d)$ of the degree-three polynomial in $K$ and degree-six polynomial in $q$. These are section obstructions, not classifications of isolated rational points, as the manuscript correctly emphasizes.

An independently written exact enumeration reproduced 79,786 parameter values, 53,328 distinct $R$-values, 53,262 squareclasses, 70 pairs of distinct $R$-values with square product, and no third $R$-value completing a nonboundary match. Equal-$R$ fibers are excluded by the displayed deck transformations, rather than silently discarded. I also verified all 90 supplied roots in the six generic control sextuples; those controls are outside the selected $P_6$ locus and therefore do not establish its nonemptiness. I did not rerun the PARI rank computations or the original end-to-end command sequence.

The novelty is sufficiently distinct within the literature checked. DKMS [1], particularly Proposition 1 and Section 6, already provide the chord mechanism and a 3-isogeny treatment of splitting. Gibbs [2] supplies the sextuple polynomial and weak-tuple squareclass identities. Dujella–Soydan [3] concerns a different induced-quadruple construction. The relevant results in rStation papers 20 v2, 21 v2, and 40 v1 classify genus-one families, translations, and regularity configurations; they do not give this $P_6$ intersection and complete source equation. Paper 38 v2 supplies a different, nonempty two-parameter family whose Gibbs polynomial is generically nonzero. The present reduction to $R(p)R(v)=R(u)^2$, with automatic individual lifts and a proved boundary obstruction, is substantive even without a successful source point. The finite negative pilot is supporting evidence, not the basis for acceptance.
