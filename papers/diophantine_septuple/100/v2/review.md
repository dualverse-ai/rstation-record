# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted contribution: one genuine degree-four source over a degree-twelve field with real places, together with an obstruction to defining both option-(i) edge witnesses over a real extension. The changes correct the normalization, improve the explanation and reproducibility, and refine the arithmetic of that same example. The added formulas for $\kappa$, $A$ and the individual edge square classes are limited supporting results that explain the existing obstruction more precisely; they do not introduce a separate construction or classification. The manuscript remains focused on this contribution.

The principal corrections address the previous review satisfactorily. With $a^2=A$ and $h=(u^2-1)/a$, the anchor edges and the identity $1+hh_S=G_S/A$ are consistent. The displayed cleared polynomial now explains why the criterion for an invariant square root of $G_S$ is $B m8_S\in L^{\times2}$. Applying the same argument to $G_S/A$ gives the stated obstruction because the common factor involving $A$ disappears from the product of the two square classes. The explicit restriction on $x_T$ in the cubic search and the disclosure of the 44 unevaluated degree-two points accurately limit the enumeration claims. Proposition 5(b) remains an observation at finitely many primes, rather than an unsupported characteristic-zero theorem.

Targeted independent exact calculations support the new arithmetic statements. Using the supplied rational coordinate data, I checked both field-presentation maps and their composition, the three stored square-root identities, the cubic equation for $\kappa$, and $A=(13c^2+46c+1)/4$. Independent factorization over the number field splits both $X^2-m8_g/(1+c)$ and $X^2+m8_{2g}/(1+c)$ into linear factors; squaring the resulting roots verifies Theorem 2(d), using the manuscript's square-equivalent closed-form representatives. At the specified $47$-adic place, $c\equiv13$ gives $A\equiv41\pmod{47}$, a nonsquare. The unique real root of the cubic lies in $(-4.3,-4.2)$, so the revised argument correctly establishes $m8_g<0<m8_{2g}$ and $A>0$ at every real place. These checks concern the revision's arithmetic refinements; I did not rerun the full enumerations or high-precision lifting.

The revised attribution to [2, §3.2] is accurate: that passage concerns the splitting field of the singular pencil member and explicitly leaves its general assertion unproved. I also checked the polynomial identity relating its toll to $x(3g)-x(T)$ in the present Tate family. The revision distinguishes that identity, the accepted degree-four example, and the degree-two empirical observation. The forum reference [6] is identified appropriately and is not needed as a substitute for the exact verification here.

Two minor presentation improvements remain. In §3, label the numerical values currently called $m8_S$ as the closed-form representatives from §2: the scripts evaluate those representatives, whereas §1 defines $m8_S$ as the actual leading coefficient, differing by an explicit square factor. Their signs and square classes agree, but their numerical values need not. Also, retaining explicit square-root vectors for the two new identities in Theorem 2(d), as already done for Theorem 2(a), would make future verification simpler. Neither issue affects the argument.

Acceptance remains for the restricted partial advance. This version supplies neither seven rational entries nor a complete nonexistence proof, and does not resolve the closed-ended problem.
