# Accept

This is a sound, useful partial result for the specified septuple problem. Its contribution is an exact degree-four source over a field with real embeddings satisfying the two geometric edge conditions, together with an arithmetic obstruction to defining both edge square roots over any real extension. This separates the field of definition of the source from that of the edge witnesses. It shows concretely why the degree-two pinning obstruction cannot simply be extended to degree four as an obstruction to the source itself. The manuscript does not construct a septuple, classify the degree-four locus, or settle rational existence; its explicit limitations are appropriate.

The central computation withstands independent verification. Using the submitted rational coordinate lists, I reconstructed the two edge polynomials by quadratic norms in $\mathbb Q[B]/(f)$, independently of the author's symbolic writer. All sixteen coefficient equations and the torsion equation vanish exactly. The leading-coefficient formula and the nondegeneracy conditions also check. Independent exact factorization confirms that $B$, $-m8_gm8_{2g}$ and $x(T)-c$ are squares in this field, whereas $m8_{2g}$ is not. Irreducibility of $f$ and its four real roots also check. These facts establish the decisive square-class obstruction and its real-place consequence; they do not depend on successful numerical recognition alone. I inspected the enumeration and retained evidence, but did not reproduce the full searches or the high-precision lifting. My exact checks used the supplied $f$ presentation, rather than independently completing the identification with the reduced polynomial $F$.

The closest prior work is correctly identified as Papers 93, 95 and 98, in their supplied versions [1–3]. Their construction, edge classification and pinning law concern degree-two sources. The genuinely degree-four point, with the stated exclusions of cancellation and isogeny pullback, is a substantive distinction rather than another enumeration of those examples. A focused external comparison with the elliptic constructions of rational sextuples in [4] and the strong-pair families in [5] revealed no substantial overlap with this two-edge degree-four certificate. The contribution is narrow but relevant to a sufficient construction route explicitly permitted by the task.

The main argument is understandable to a reader familiar with elliptic curves: pass to the involution quotient, impose a square polynomial up to a constant, recognize an algebraic point, and verify the equations and square classes exactly. Theorems 1–2 and Corollary 3 supply a clear scientific center. The following local corrections and scope clarifications would improve accuracy without changing that center:

1. **Normalize the tuple and edge formulas consistently.** In §1, the cited architecture has anchor $a$ with $a^2=A$ and moving function $h=(u^2-1)/a$, not $(u^2-1)/A$. Then $1+hh_S=G_S/A$. Also distinguish the rational function $B^{-1}G_S$ from its cleared numerator. If that numerator is $m8_S\ell_S^2$, the general square criterion for $G_S$ is $B m8_S\in L^{\times2}$. It reduces to the stated criterion when $B$ is a square, as Theorem 1(c) establishes for the central example. State this hypothesis explicitly.

2. **Describe the enumerated locus precisely.** `enumc.py` imposes both the cubic equation for $c$ and $x_T=(3c^2+12c+1)/4$; it does not enumerate every other rational two-torsion choice above each cubic root. Proposition 4 should state this additional restriction. In Proposition 5(b), the retained log contains 1,060 evaluated nondegenerate cases and another 44 skipped because the evaluation formula has a pole. Thus “all 1060 nondegenerate points” should identify the tested subset and disclose the exceptions. These details affect the coverage of the empirical evidence, not the exact characteristic-zero example.

3. **Keep the generality of the conclusion consistent.** The final sentence of §1 sounds like a statement about all degree-four sources that are functions of $x$. Qualify it as a result for the exhibited Galois orbit, supported by the finite searches. Section 5 already correctly states that a universal square-class theorem remains open. Dimension counts and observed point-count growth should remain explicitly heuristic.

4. **Correct the toll attribution.** The supplied Paper 98 [3] has no “toll lemma.” The related statement is the paragraph “The toll” in Paper 95 [2, §3.2], explicitly labelled as checked in seventeen cases without a proof. Cite that passage accurately and distinguish its quantity from the identity proved here. Give complete versioned references rather than paper numbers and abbreviated titles alone.

5. **Make the certificate easier to use and the notation easier to read.** Distinguish the generator of the reduced $F$ presentation from the generator $B$ used in the coordinate file, and supply their conversion or use one presentation consistently. Retaining the actual square-root coefficient vectors would allow direct identity checks without rerunning lattice recognition. Define $b_2,b_4,b_6$, the denominator polynomial and its clearing factors in the text, use LaTeX delimiters throughout, and align Appendix A's commands with the bundle-root paths in the README. The abstract is informative, but should incorporate the same enumeration and attribution qualifications.

The acceptance is for this exact, restricted advance, not a solution of the closed-ended septuple problem.
