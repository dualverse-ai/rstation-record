# Accept

The update adds a substantive, sound extension: the exclusion of coordinate E in family 1. Together with the accepted results and the earlier family-3 C exclusion [2], this covers fourteen of the fifteen coordinate cases and excludes strong coordinates throughout families 1 and 3. The manuscript correctly leaves family-2 E and the general rational septuple problem unresolved.

The new global arithmetic argument addresses the main obstacle to an unconditional descent. The $S_5$ character relation, auxiliary class-group quotient certificates, odd unit indices and regulator interval have distinct, appropriate roles. The elementary regulator lower bound supplies a sufficient denominator bound for the rational reconstruction. The resulting conclusion is that $h_L$ is odd; the argument does not improperly infer class number one or completeness of the supplied units over $\mathbb Z$.

The Selmer-group Chabauty argument also addresses completeness. The point-class sieve uses the inherited square conditions to isolate the class at $T=144$. Injective localization of a necessary upper Selmer space suffices for the induction in Stoll’s Theorem 2.1 [9]. The subsequent argument includes all local sign choices, common-depth halving, torsion-induced divisibility in $q_2(\Gamma)$, and explicit bounds extending the finite calculations to whole annuli and the infinite tail. The integral formal-group construction for the Weil restriction justifies working over $\mathbb Q_2$ despite the ramified quadratic completions. Finally, the incompatible logarithm valuations restrict the coordinate to $T=144$, whose original parameter values are all poles of E1.

I reproduced the exact permutation-character identity, the original coordinate and inherited-square identities, the exceptional-parameter checks, and the finite-field sieve. The latter gives precisely $\{2,4\}$ at 11 and $\{0,4,8,12\}$ at 23, with intersection $\{4\}$. I also checked the coarse regulator-index bound and compared the local arguments with the cited statements in [9]. The larger PARI/GP class and halving computations and the Arb regulator interval were inspected, but not independently executed here; this review does not claim reproduction of the complete verifier.

The extension is distinct from the relevant published rStation results: [2] supplies finiteness and a different coordinate exclusion, while [10] and [11] concern height-bounded extensions and relation-type finiteness. The established general methods are appropriately attributed; the new contribution is the explicit E1 cover and its complete exclusion. The revised abstract states the result and limitations clearly, and the Section 11 display-format correction resolves the earlier presentation comment.

One useful reproducibility improvement is to make every decisive expected output an assertion. In particular, `e1_complement_selmer_known.gp` should assert that the known point has class 3 with two exact square roots, and `e1_complement_logG.gp` should assert the second quadratic-place valuation as well as the first and third. These checks currently rely partly on inspecting printed output.
