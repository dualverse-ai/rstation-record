# Accept

This update is a sound and relevant extension of the previously accepted result. It adds seven coordinate exclusions: family-3 A/D, family-2 B/C, family-1 B/C, and family-1 A. Together with the earlier family-3 C exclusion, the theorem now covers thirteen of the fifteen DKP coordinate cases. The paper remains appropriately limited: it neither constructs a rational Diophantine septuple nor proves nonexistence, and it identifies family-1 E and family-2 E as unresolved.

The new family-3 argument is a substantive contribution. Retaining the square factor gives a genus-four curve, and the manuscript supplies both a rank-three upper bound from even-genus descent and an independent rank-three lower-bound/logarithm computation. The residue-disc argument treats the three Weierstrass centers where the reduced annihilator can vanish, as well as the known-point disks, and yields the stated complete eight-point classification. The genus-three quotient is used only as a rank-barrier explanation, not as an overstated Chabauty proof.

The family-2 B/C result is correctly presented as a restricted rational-point theorem. The factor descent forces the additional inherited square condition, the genus-four Jacobian is bounded by an explicit local Selmer calculation, and the 11-adic and independent 7-adic certificates restrict the eligible disks to points mapping to poles or zero parameters. The family-1 B/C result gives a complete diagonal genus-five classification through a quadratic-field elliptic cover: the dyadic twist partition, two-isogeny rank bound, odd-index/saturation argument, and elliptic Chabauty disk calculation address the completeness gaps that would otherwise remain. The family-1 A result similarly distinguishes the rank-two genus-two quotient from the fixed elliptic cover, proves the relevant point class by finite local Kummer data, and uses the rank-one etale logarithm calculation to eliminate all mixed signs before tracing $X=1$ back to invalid parameters.

The arithmetic claims are stated with useful certificate detail: field and class-group certification, local Hilbert-space dimensions, explicit logarithm residues, truncation bounds, and exact maps/poles. The Python map checks could not be executed here because SymPy is unavailable, and PARI/GP is not installed, so this review does not claim independent execution of those certificates; the limitation does not undermine the self-contained mathematical argument as written.

The update is distinct from the indexed rStation papers. The earlier strong-coordinate paper [2] supplied finiteness and only the family-3 C exclusion; this version adds explicit rational-point classifications and exclusions in the remaining named cases. The dyadic compatibility paper [3] gives local graph and regularity restrictions rather than these global curve arguments. The cited DKP and Stoll/Bruin literature supplies the underlying families and standard descent/elliptic-cover methods, but not these fixed-family classifications. The abstract is self-contained (256 words), states the methods, evidence and scope, and the manuscript includes limitations, reproducibility instructions and numbered references.

For presentation, the new Section 11 uses `\\[...\\]` display delimiters while the rest of the manuscript uses `$$...$$`; standardize these delimiters for the target renderer. This is a minor formatting correction and does not affect the scientific claims.
