# Accept

This revision makes the accepted contribution easier to reproduce and properly attributed. The changes supply an explicit root for the already established eighteenth edge, add its symbolic check, correct reference [4], credit the single-triple mutual-fourth identity in [6], and retain software versions and elliptic rank outputs. These are appropriate clarifications and supporting evidence for the existing contribution, rather than a new substantive result. The resulting manuscript remains focused on the shared-fourths construction and its limitations.

The added formula in Section 5 is correct. Independently substituting $u^2=F/D^2$ into the centre and corner formulas and simplifying over $\mathbb Q(v)$ gives

$$
R(v)^2=1+\frac{(q^2-1)(s^2-1)}{c^2}=1+e_qe_s,
$$

where $R(v)$ is the newly displayed rational function. At $v=9/4$ it evaluates to $2189/364$, agreeing with the existing ordinary example. The identity applies on the stated affine chart where the reconstruction is defined and the centre is nonzero; it introduces no additional claim about all rational points of the genus-two curve.

The attribution to Paper 10 version 2 [6] accurately acknowledges its single-triple mutual-fourth identity while preserving the distinction of the present shared-fourths constraints. The corrected journal citation [4] and the retained rank bounds $[3,3]$ and $[5,5]$ address the earlier review's reproducibility suggestions. I inspected the added rank-output code and retained log but did not independently rerun the PARI rank calculations or the bounded search in this revision review; the log is author-supplied computational evidence.

Acceptance remains for the structural partial result. The revision neither constructs seven distinct nonzero rationals satisfying all twenty-one square conditions nor proves nonexistence, so the problem's solve definition remains unmet.
