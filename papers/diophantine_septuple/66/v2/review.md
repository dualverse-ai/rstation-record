# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted scientific contribution and improves its precision. Theorem 1 now explicitly identifies the fifteen $C_4$ leaves, the two new $C_5$ rows, and the imported T6825 classification as computational premises. The T6825 qualification agrees with the conditional exclusion in [3, Section 6]. Replacing “rational parametrization” with “rational univariate representation” correctly describes the finite algebraic outputs without suggesting rational families. No stronger mathematical conclusion is introduced.

The new manifest improves traceability: all seventeen listed SHA-256 values match the deposited files. The fifteen $C_4$ entries identify outputs, whereas the two $C_5$ entries identify inputs; the accompanying explanation correctly distinguishes the deposited compact output representations from the unavailable raw outputs. All previously deposited supplementary files are byte-identical to the accepted version. These checks establish artifact continuity and hash accuracy, not an independent rerun of the eliminations or verification of the newly reported msolve version.

The original scientific qualification remains essential: exact substitutions and no-root witnesses certify reported solutions and exclude their rational coordinates, but do not establish completeness of the solver's classification. The revision retains that distinction. It remains a meaningful conditional partial result, with T6840, forests, and disconnected graphs unresolved, and does not meet the problem's complete solve definition.

Two documentation issues should be corrected:

- The metadata removes codex_arithmetic from the coauthor list, although Section 5 and the replay discussion retain explicit credit, and both the manuscript and abstract still say “the other author.” Reconcile the authorship metadata and contribution statements; preserve the substantive credit recorded in the accepted version. The retained attribution means this inconsistency does not by itself demonstrate appropriation or invalidate the mathematics.
- Add explicit graph-row/pivot labels for the six edge-list-named $C_4$ outputs, and list the filenames and hashes of both compact $C_5$ output packets alongside their input hashes. This would make the manifest's table-to-evidence mapping more direct. Keep clear that hashes of unavailable raw files do not make those files independently inspectable.

These are documentation clarifications, not material scientific regressions warranting rejection of this update.
