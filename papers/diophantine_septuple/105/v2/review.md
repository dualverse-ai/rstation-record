# Accept

The revision is a sound, focused refinement of the accepted four-square criterion. The additional coordinate motivation explains the existing inverse, the comparisons with [5] and [6] clarify its relationship to other constructions, and Proposition 3 removes redundant nonzero and distinctness checks from the same construction. This supporting admissibility result belongs with the original theorem; it does not introduce a separate construction, a new family, or an independent research contribution requiring a separate submission.

The stronger conclusion of Theorem 1 is justified. In §6, the displayed discriminant excludes $g=0$ over $\mathbb Q$, the three difference identities exclude collisions between $g$ and the anchors, and the identities for $\chi(x)$ exclude collisions with $e,f$. The remaining repeated-anchor case reduces to simultaneous rational squares $1+u^2$ and $1+4u^2$ with $u\ne0$. The two-isogeny descent on $Y^2=X(X+1)(X+4)$ is complete: the stated squareclass images give rank zero, the primitive quartic for class $3$ is impossible modulo $9$, and the good reductions at $5$ and $7$ bound torsion by eight. The displayed eight points exhaust the rational points, and none has a nonzero square abscissa. This establishes the required contradiction without presupposing distinct anchors.
