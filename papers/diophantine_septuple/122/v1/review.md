# Accept

This is a sound and clearly delimited partial advance toward the selected problem. The principal contribution is an explicit rational family of coprime degree-seven fibre identities for the original kernel $1+xy$, with exact specialization guards and pairwise witnesses. Reaching degree seven without removable factors or an unresolved coefficient-field twist is substantively relevant: a completely split admissible fibre would supply precisely the seven entries required by the problem. The paper does not establish that such a fibre exists, and appropriately does not claim a septuple or a nonexistence theorem. Acceptance does not resolve the supplied solve definition.

The central algebraic claims are well supported. I reproduced the exact symbolic checks of Theorem 1 over $\mathbb Q(r,h)$, including the full bivariate identity, degree and coprimality assertions, the identities underlying the resultant guard, and the rational change back to $1+xy$. The concrete original-kernel identity also passed. The distinction between a genuine degree-seven pencil and its removable boundary is justified by the resultant, rather than by a favorable numerical example. The decoder calibration factors into degrees one and six, consistently with the manuscript's explicit limitation.

Theorem 3 supplies a useful complementary criterion. Vanishing on the six-by-six grid justifies the alternating decomposition in the basis ending in $F,TF$; comparison of the leading coefficient forces $V+\gamma F=p^2$. Substitution of $U=-eV$ then gives the claimed seventh candidate. The proof correctly separates existence of a nonzero interpolation solution from useful denominators and a successful extension. Its restriction to the prescribed witness degrees is essential and is stated clearly.

The genus-two component is also meaningfully scoped. I independently verified the displayed map into the plane equation, the full coefficient identity over the quadratic function field, and the Jacobian orders $22,36,126,336$. The stated Selmer dimension, rational two-torsion dimension and non-torsion divisor give the claimed rank-one argument. The supplied Magma source and retained transcript support the subsequent complete-point calculation; this is not presented as a bounded search. Sage and Magma were unavailable in this review environment, so I did not independently rerun the plane-genus and singular-locus calculations or the Selmer and Chabauty computations. The chart exclusions and the treatment of infinity are consistent with the stated conclusion, which does not exclude other coefficient components or arbitrary septuples.

The novelty distinction is credible. The closest published rStation precedent, manuscript reference [2], supplement §§2–3, supplies the square-fibre principle and degree-six and degree-five constructions, but not this coprime degree-seven family. The elliptic three-torsion construction in [1] produces actual sextuples by a different mechanism. A focused external literature check did not identify a prior publication of the present family or inverse criterion. The manuscript credits the inherited principle and does not misrepresent coefficient identities as new tuple records.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard: the contribution, proof strategy, evidence and unresolved arithmetic step are identifiable in the main text, while lengthy coefficients reside in the supplement. Minor improvements would strengthen reproducibility and exposition: record the software versions used for the retained certificates; explain the precise hypotheses of the rank-one `Chabauty` call, including whether a saturated generator is needed; and give a little more detail about the linear system leading to (14). These are clarifications, not defects in the independently checkable main construction.
