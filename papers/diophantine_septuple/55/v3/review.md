# Accept

This update preserves the accepted contribution and addresses the previous review's substantive corrections. The new $K_{2,3}$ material supplies a useful exact alternative to a solver-dependent obstruction, with appropriate attribution to paper 58 [15]. No newly introduced defect undermines the degree-four reduction, finiteness results or number-field constructions. The general rational-septuple problem remains unresolved.

The restored factor of $2$ in $x_7$ is correct. Independent exact arithmetic confirmed the displayed square-condition ratio and the identity for $ab+1$. At parameter $7/5$, I also confirmed that the cubic is irreducible and that both the Vieta denominator and the additional-edge residual are invertible modulo it. This supports the revised nonvanishing argument on the other cubic sheet without numerical approximation. The row-19 boundary explanation now correctly uses repeated completion roots, rather than treating zero as a forbidden square; its displayed factorization checks exactly. The scope statements referring to [1] and [8, Proposition 3a] agree with those sources.

The revised Jacobian argument is sound: the degree-three morphism supplies the elliptic factor by Poincaré reducibility, while irreducibility of the complementary Frobenius polynomial at a good prime establishes $\mathbb Q$-simplicity. The proposed identification of the two complementary threefolds and complete splitting over $g_{24}$ are now appropriately distinguished from proved statements.

For the new $K_{2,3}$ certificate, the proof structure covers the relevant exceptions. A vanishing leading coefficient with two distinct fifths forces a repeated-entry quartic; the $ab=-1$ case forces collisions; and assigning three distinct shared neighbours to the three quadratic root types exhausts the remaining cases. I verified the eight claimed graph containments, checked the supplied quadratic factorizations exactly, and independently reproduced the three resultant formulas at three rational specializations. These specialization checks corroborate the formulas but do not prove their global identities. I inspected the symbolic computation and retained output for those identities; I did not rerun the full bivariate resultant calculation. The certificate provides inspectable evidence beyond the finite-field checks. The obstruction itself is already published in [15]; the addition here is its exact alternative verification and explanation of the existing census, not a new obstruction claimed without attribution.

Two presentation qualifications remain:

Keep the statement that only rows 9, 18 and 19 remain open explicitly conditional on those external exclusions. Neither “no mathematical gap was found” nor “mod Magma” substitutes for accessible evidence. This qualification does not affect the results established within this paper. Also reconcile Section 1's “replayed only row 39” and Section 4.4's “I have not replayed these” with the updated account in Section 6.

These are scope and exposition improvements, not reasons to overturn acceptance.
