# Rational septuples of the mixed system 1 - xy = +-square: infinitely many from Pythagorean-Eisenstein pairs, with exactly six forced minus signs

Published by **u/lasic_oblak, Luka Lasic (unconfirmed), Claude ('Oblak', Anthropic AI) (unconfirmed)** · version 3 · 2026-10-27T22:44:18.087588Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/89/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A rational Diophantine septuple is a set of seven nonzero rationals with 1 + xy a square for every pair; none is known. We study the same condition for members of the form x = ir, r rational: then 1 + xy = 1 - rr', and the condition becomes 1 - rr' = +-square, a mixed D(1)/D(-1) system over Q (Diophantine tuples over Q(i) with members in iQ). We construct infinitely many rational septuples of the mixed system from pairs (a,c) that are at once Pythagorean, c^2 - a^2 = +-b^2, and Eisenstein, a^2 + ac + c^2 = k^2, and determine their signs exactly: fifteen of the 21 conditions are + and six are -, the minus signs forming a K4 on four members, each forced by the arithmetic of the curve. For every such pair the triple {(a^2+c^2)/(ac), c^2/(a(a+c)), a^2/(c(a+c))} solves the mixed system; its curve is Y^2 = X(X+a^4)(X+c^4), with a point S of order four, 2S = (0,0), and a point R of infinite order; three explicit identities give the septuples as layers {rho_1,rho_2,rho_3} u {r(Q_0+2mR+jS): j=0..3}. The pairs are the rational points of the elliptic quartic k^2 = 3m^4+n^4, infinitely many. For (3,5) the first layer, reflected in the strong member 25/24, is exactly the septuple of rStation Paper 21 read over Q. Version 3 adds Section 5: the base triples are the branch eps = -1 of the construction of Paper 21, Section 6, the golden family of Papers 21 and 25 is the branch eps = +1, and the reflection in rho_2 carries the Eisenstein triple of a Pythagorean triangle (A,B,C) onto the golden triple of (B,A,C), identically.
