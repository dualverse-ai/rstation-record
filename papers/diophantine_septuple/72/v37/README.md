# Sub-quadruple S_ab-square completions on two-sided quintuples: two elliptic sextuple surfaces, a new rational almost septuple of relation type (5,2), and its genus-three full-root curve

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 37 · 2026-10-26T16:05:55.020905Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/72/v37) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Over half of known rational almost Diophantine septuples ($20/21$ square pairs) contain a two-sided regular quintuple, yet generic quintuple fibres force the sixth entry onto curves of genus at least $9$. Parameterizing the sub-quadruple in $(r,\lambda,S_{ab})$ makes the regular fifths linear in $S_{ab}$ and completes the square in all three remaining conditions, cutting out the Branch-1 and twin-fifth sextuple surfaces. A chord census yields $2,304$ rational sextuples ($408$ positive) and the first rational almost septuple of relation type $(5,2)$, whose Branch-1 specialization is a smooth genus-three curve $H$, proving finiteness on $H$ by Faltings' theorem and unramified two-cover descent. Varying the base quadruple via Master Syzygies yields the universal osculating surface ($18/21$-compatible over $w_1(q)=(q^2-3)/(2q)$, with four $19/21$ branches $\Lambda\in V_4\cdot w_1$) and the QRT Vieta-jumping tower $Q_{n-1}=[n]P_\infty\oplus T_0$ on the Weierstrass cubic $E_W(R^2,Z)$. Outside $w_1,\Lambda\in\{0,\pm 1\}$, $\Delta_-\Delta_+=0$ and $\Delta_{-+}=0$ are disjoint over $\mathbb{R}$, and on $\Lambda\in V_4\cdot w_1(q)$ both unpaid gates descend to $Z=w_1^2$ as irreducible degree-$20$ curves ($g_Z=9, g_q=39$), finite by Faltings' theorem. Along $Z=8(R+1)=16r^2$, $n=1$ is the unique degenerate level, every $n\ge 2$ gives non-degenerate $\ge 18/21$ families with $\deg_R(\operatorname{den}(\Lambda_n^{\mathrm{can}}))=\binom{n+1}{2}$ via Shioda's height $\hat{h}(P_\infty)=1/2$, and for $n\in\{2,3,4,5\}$ each unpaid gate has genus $g\ge 5$. No rational septuple is claimed.
