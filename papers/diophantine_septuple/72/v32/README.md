# Sub-quadruple S_ab-square completions on two-sided quintuples: two elliptic sextuple surfaces, a new rational almost septuple of relation type (5,2), and its genus-three full-root curve

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 32 · 2026-10-25T11:11:33.926758Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/72/v32) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Over half of known rational almost Diophantine septuples ($20/21$ square pairs) contain a two-sided regular quintuple, yet generic quintuple fibres force the sixth entry onto curves of genus at least $9$. Parameterizing the sub-quadruple in $(r,\lambda,S_{ab})$ makes the regular fifths linear in $S_{ab}$, and all three remaining conditions complete the square in $S_{ab}$, cutting out the Branch-1 and twin-fifth sextuple surfaces. A chord census yields $2,304$ rational sextuples ($408$ positive) and the first rational almost septuple of relation type $(5,2)$, whose Branch-1 specialization is a smooth genus-three curve $H$, proving finiteness on $H$ by Faltings' theorem and unramified two-cover descent. Beyond fixed-quintuple fibres, we establish Master Syzygies unifying $3$-torsion chains, Chebyshev and reflection towers, the universal $(C_0,w_1)$ osculating surface ($18/21$-compatible over $w_1(q)=(q^2-3)/(2q)$, whose sign group $V_4$ spans four $19/21$ branches), and the Vieta-jumping tower. Across all $16$ branch pairs in $(w_1,\Lambda)$, $\Delta_-\Delta_+=0$ and $\Delta_{-+}=0$ are disjoint over $\mathbb{R}$, and on all four $19/21$ branches $\Lambda\in V_4\cdot w_1(q)$ both unpaid gates descend to $Z=w_1^2$ with coprime square-free degree-$20$ numerators ($g_Z=9, g_q=39$), finite by Faltings' theorem. Vieta jumping on the master biquadratic curve is an explicit QRT translation $Q_{n-1}=[n]P_\infty\oplus T_0$ on the Weierstrass cubic $E_W(R^2,Z)$, yielding halving-step division laws, $2$-descent telescoping, and all-level disjointness of $\Delta_-=0$ and $\Delta_+=0$ away from $R\in\{0,\pm 1\}, B_W=0$. Along $Z=8(R+1)=16r^2$, Period-$4$ Laurent expansions at $R=0$ prove that $n=1$ is the unique degenerate level, while every $n\ge 2$ yields non-degenerate canonical families in $\mathbb{Q}(r)$ (and mirror duals over $R=1-2w^2$) satisfying $\ge 18/21$ septuple and $\ge 34/91$ pool conditions ($\ge 35/91$ on $5r^2-1=\rho^2$; exact at $n=2,3$). Shioda's height $\hat{h}(P_\infty)=1/2$ on the minimal K3 surface proves $\deg_R(\operatorname{den}(\Lambda_n^{\mathrm{can}}))=\binom{n+1}{2}$ for all $n\ge 1$ ($1\le n\le 12$ certified), and for $n\in\{2,3,4,5\}$ each unpaid gate has genus $g\ge 5$, finite by Faltings' theorem. No rational Diophantine septuple is claimed.
