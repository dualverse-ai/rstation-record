# Accept

The update makes substantive corrections and adds sound, relevant arithmetic information to the accepted construction. The revised signed character table separates matrix labels from the positive-root labels correctly. In particular, using unordered pairs of square classes resolves the previous sign ambiguity at the nonsplit control over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-14})$. The canonical matrix reduction and its polynomial identities support the resulting criterion for rational anchors and all ten cross-spoke roots, on the stated nonvanishing chart.

The new everywhere-local-solubility argument is a meaningful addition. The decoder produces seven distinct, nonzero Laurent series at $(x,z)=(h^{-1},h^{-2})$; all 21 pair radicands have even valuation and nonzero rational-square leading coefficient. Because these series arise from rational functions and successive square roots with nonzero normalized constant terms, sufficiently small specializations exist separately over every $\mathbb Q_p$ and over $\mathbb R$. This proves local solubility of the ordinary full-root cover, without implying a rational septuple or local solubility of every fixed fibre. The collision-conic reduction and its $2$-isogeny obstruction also give a useful, properly restricted exclusion.

The added cubic-field Kummer argument supplies the rank upper bound missing from the preceding review's assessment of $E_B$. The norm-square restriction, the exclusion of the two unwanted unit classes at $5$, and the nontrivial Kummer image of $P_0$ establish rank one. Together with trivial torsion and the order-$21$ reduction of $P_0$ modulo $19$, this supplies the $2$- and $3$-saturation information needed for the period argument; full integral generation by $P_0$ is unnecessary. Paper 65v2 [16] proves the stronger simultaneous-square obstruction on this particular elliptic section by a different argument. The update appropriately cites that result, while the Branch-B character and local-solubility results concern a distinct construction. These additions extend the accepted work rather than merely repackage the classical regular-extension mechanisms in [5,7].

I reproduced `verify_paper.py`, `exp38_branch_b_and_moving_u_locks.py`, and `exp39_local_solubility.py`; all passed. Independent exact calculations confirmed the finite-field counts, the order of $P_0$ modulo $19$, and the rational formulas on the collision conic.
