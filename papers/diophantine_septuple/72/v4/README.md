# Sub-quadruple S_ab-square completions on two-sided quintuples: two elliptic sextuple surfaces, a new rational almost septuple of relation type (5,2), and its genus-three full-root curve

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 4 · 2026-10-16T16:20:51.742185Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/72/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study sub-quadruple extensions $c^*_\mp = e_\mp(a,b,d_-,d_+)$ of two-sided rational Diophantine quintuples $\{a,b,c,d_-,d_+\}$ with $a b + 1 = r^2$ and $d_- d_+ + 1 = w_1^2$. Writing $c - a - b = \frac{\lambda^2(2r-1)+(2r+1)}{2\lambda}$, we prove that $c^*_\mp$ are linear in $S_{ab} := a + b$, and that the residual conditions for $\mathcal{S}_7^{(c^*)} = \{a,b,c,d_-,d_+,c^*_-,c^*_+\}$ are quadratics in $S_{ab}$ with square leading coefficients and discriminants in $(r,\lambda)$, cutting out Branch 1 ($\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{Br1}}$) and $\mathcal{E}_{F_1}, \mathcal{E}_{F_3}$. Moreover, $R_4(p,q,e_-,e_+)$ factors universally with symmetric factor $P_4^2 - 6 P_4 - 4(p q + u v) - 3$, defining the type-$(4,2)$ surface $\mathcal{S}_{4,2} : W_\tau^2 = (P_4-1)(P_4-9)$ where $a_\pm c_\pm + 1 = \square$. On Branch 1, $\mathcal{S}_{4,2}$ specializes to the genus-3 type-$(5,2)$ curve $\mathcal{H}: W^2 = (5\lambda^4+10\lambda^2+1)(5\lambda^4-22\lambda^2+33)$, proving finiteness and yielding a new $20/21$ almost septuple $\mathcal{S}_7^{(20/21)}$ at $(\lambda,W) = (-2,55)$. In the global $(r,\mu,z)$ chart, we put $\Sigma_\mu$ into cubic Weierstrass form $\Sigma_\mu^+ : W_+^2 = -K L$ with 2-torsion involution $\tau_{T_0}(\mu) = -\frac{\mu+4r}{K}$, construct three families on $\mathcal{S}_{4,2}$ (including all-positive $19/21$ septuples in $\mathbb{Q}(k)$ for $k \in (1, k_*)$ and the genus-1 family $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{III},\pm}$), and establish the factor cancellation on $w = 2u^2 y - L$ and moving-$u$ family $\mathcal{E}_B$. Finally (Theorem 6), we prove over $\mathbb{Q}$ that $\operatorname{num}(R_5(d_-,d_+,c,c^*_-,c^*_+)) = H^2 L_1 L_2 Q_4$ in $\mathbb{Q}[r,\mu,z]$, that $L_1(r,\mu,z)=0$ is a Universal Triple-Square Section with a $15+6$ Square-Class Collapse in $(R,v)=(r^2,r\mu)$, and that $v=-1/5$ yields a closed-form $\mathbb{Q}(\lambda)$ family of $14/15$ sextuples and all-positive $10/10$ quintuples with a rank-0 2-isogeny proof on $\mathcal{E}_{16}$. No rational septuple is claimed.
