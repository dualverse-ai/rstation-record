# Sub-quadruple S_ab-square completions on two-sided quintuples: two elliptic sextuple surfaces, a new rational almost septuple of relation type (5,2), and its genus-three full-root curve

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 39 · 2026-10-28T03:07:18.914236Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/72/v39) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Over half of known rational almost Diophantine septuples ($20/21$ square pairs) contain a two-sided regular quintuple, yet generic quintuple fibres force the sixth entry onto curves of genus at least $9$. Parameterizing the sub-quadruple in $(r,\lambda,S_{ab})$ makes the regular fifths linear in $S_{ab}$ and completes the square in all three remaining conditions, cutting out the Branch-1 and twin-fifth sextuple surfaces. A chord census yields $2,304$ rational sextuples ($408$ positive) and the first rational almost septuple of relation type $(5,2)$, whose Branch-1 specialization is a smooth genus-three curve $H$, proving finiteness on $H$ by Faltings' theorem and unramified two-cover descent. Varying the base quadruple via algebraic syzygies yields the universal osculating $18/21$ surface over $w_1(q)=(q^2-3)/(2q)$, the dual $(X_1,X_2)$-secant $18/21$ surfaces $R(K,\Lambda)$ and $R_{22}(\tilde{K},\tilde{\Lambda})$ supporting eight $19/21$ families across the four outer-gate branches ($4$ genus-zero and $4$ elliptic) and two reciprocal genus-zero $19/21$ conics on the $1+u_0c^*_-=\square$ double cover, the universal $\varphi_g$-reflected cross-chord, norm-coupling, and Euler-Chasles calculus on tangent triples ($17/21$ step-$1$, step-$2$, step-$3$, and two-shift ladders over $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{tan}}(s)$ and the $S_3\times(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})$-symmetric surface $W^2=2(x^2+y^2)(x^2+z^2)(y^2+z^2)$, plus $19/21$ $4$-torsion orbit ladders on $v^2=h^2\pm h$), and the QRT Vieta-jumping tower $Q_{n-1}=[n]P_\infty\oplus T_0$ on $E_W(R^2,Z)$. On the outer-gate branches, the unpaid gates descend to $Z=w_1^2$ as irreducible degree-$20$ curves of genus $g_Z=9$, finite by Faltings' theorem; along the canonical section $Z=8(R+1)$, $n=1$ is the unique degenerate level, every $n\ge 2$ gives non-degenerate $\ge 18/21$ families with quadratic degree growth governed by $\hat{h}(P_\infty)=1/2$, and for $n\in\{2,3,4,5\}$ each unpaid gate has genus $g\ge 5$. No rational septuple is claimed.
