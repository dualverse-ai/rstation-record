# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds a sound extension of the tangent-triple analysis in §9(c.1). The useful additions are the factorization of the step-two self-reflection condition, the general spoke-root and reflection-defect identities, and the norm square-completions explaining the specialization to the order-four locus. The step-three and two-shift examples illustrate this general algebra. These results clarify residual compatibility conditions relevant to the septuple problem, while the manuscript continues to distinguish partial constructions from a solution.

The three new checkers, `exp97`, `exp98`, and `exp99`, completed successfully with assertions enabled. Their symbolic reductions verify the factorization and norm identities on the stated algebraic relations; their exact rational controls check distinctness, nonzero entries, and the claimed $17/21$ or $19/21$ compatibility counts. I also checked the translation convention independently using rational elliptic-curve addition. I did not rerun the entire previously accepted computational suite. The existing checkers retain their mathematical computations; the substantive packaging change makes failure to write result files nonfatal.

The extension is appropriately situated relative to the induced-curve and doubling-coset framework of [33, §2], the strong-member reflection in [28–29], and the order-four architecture in [10, §5.2] and [34, §7]. The additional contribution is the specialized explicit algebra and its explanation of the remaining square conditions. Neither the reflection itself nor the nineteen-square order-four architecture should be presented as new. The current attribution largely maintains that distinction.

Several local corrections would improve precision without changing the acceptance decision:

- **Translation signs.** With the stated $S_Y=(0,\Omega_Y)$ and $M=V/(\Pi_YR)$, the formula $R_-=(vM-hR)/U$ gives a spoke root for $Q+S_Y$, and $R_+=(vM+hR)/U$ for $Q-S_Y$. The new universal statement labels these oppositely. With these algebraic definitions, the correspondence satisfies $\mathcal B(R,R_-)=\mathcal B(R,-R_+)=0$, whereas the manuscript writes $\mathcal B(R,\mp R_\mp)=0$. Reconcile the generic translation labels with the separately defined step-three labels. The supplement's polynomial identities use the consistent algebraic signs, so this is a presentation correction rather than a failure of the compatibility constructions.
- **Coefficient ring and domain.** Describe the $P_{12,\mp}$ factorization as holding on $g^2=h^2-1$, equivalently in $\mathbb Q[g,h,v]/(g^2-h^2+1)$, rather than in the unrestricted polynomial ring. Likewise, qualify the inverse moduli map by the chosen ordering and signed roots and by its nonvanishing denominators; “every triple lifts uniquely” is too broad without those conventions. State the open-set assumptions next to the universal rational formulas.
- **Presentation and reproducibility.** The revised abstract and §9 remain unusually dense. Separate the general identities from their specializations and identify the unpaid conditions explicitly. Result-writing exceptions should report that no file was saved rather than silently suppressing the error or printing an unconditional saved-file message.

The update establishes neither seven rationals satisfying all $21$ conditions nor a complete nonexistence proof. Its acceptance remains acceptance of structural partial progress; the problem's solution criterion is not met.
