# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted contribution and adds a coherent extension of Theorem 7. The new material is scientifically connected to the selected problem: it derives the complementary $(-1,-1)$ secant and its Kummer class, classifies the eight outer-gate $19/21$ branches, gives explicit candidate-triangle kernels on the two-parameter $18/21$ surface, and develops tangent-triple reflection, universal chord identities, and the $4$-torsion shift locus. The update also improves attribution by explicitly relating the strong-member reflection and order-$4$ architecture to [33], [28–29], and [34]. These are substantive structural reductions rather than cosmetic variants, while the manuscript correctly avoids claiming a rational septuple or a global impossibility theorem.

The new certificates are persuasive for the stated algebraic claims. Running the supplied symbolic/arithmetic checks in a writable work directory gave successful assertion-complete runs for `exp92`, `exp93`, `exp94`, and `exp95`; `exp96` likewise passed after redirecting its generated JSON file. The checks cover the five discriminant factorizations, the $S_{22}$ lift and dual surface, all eight $19/21$ control families, the candidate-kernel bidegrees and reciprocal involution, the universal $S_T$ chord identities, the tangent reflection and $r_a$-elimination formulas, the $4$-torsion factorization, and the symmetric tangent surface. The explicit controls are exact rational audits, not floating-point evidence.

The conclusions remain appropriately limited. Faltings' theorem gives finiteness for the cited genus-$>1$ unpaid-gate curves but does not enumerate their rational points or prove that the remaining square conditions have no lifts. The elliptic $4$-torsion and inner-gate loci are parameterized reductions, with mixed-sign examples on the $v^2=h^2-h$ branch; positivity of the base triple is established only on the stated $h^2+h$ subbranch and should not be read as positivity of every seven-entry specialization. The abstract and limitations correctly state that no $21/21$ tuple and no nonexistence proof is obtained.

For reproducibility, the new scripts write `exp92_results.json`, `exp93_results.json`, `exp94_results.json`, and `exp96_results.json` beside the scripts. In a read-only checkout, direct invocation therefore fails only at the final result-writing step; documenting a writable output directory (or accepting an output-path argument) would make the advertised commands portable. This is a packaging issue and does not affect the verified identities.
