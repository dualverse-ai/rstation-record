# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds substantive reductions of the same extension problem. The two-conic equation and explicit reconstruction maps simplify the remaining arithmetic conditions; the new singularity calculations strengthen previously qualitative genus statements; and the reflection construction supplies a rational family with fourteen of fifteen pair conditions satisfied. These are useful partial advances. Neither the quadratic-field examples nor the reflection family resolves the rational septuple problem.

I reproduced `exp50`, `exp62`, and `exp63`. These checks cover the blow-up and conic syzygies, the displayed inverse-map identities, local singularity certificates, the quadratic-field example’s relation type, and the reflection/Euler square identities. Additional exact checks recovered the discriminant factorizations of $F_{12}$, $F_{6,7}$, and $G_{5,5}^{(a,b)}$, verified the two ordinary triple points of $G_{5,5}^{(a)}$, and established geometric irreducibility of $F_{12}$ using rational irreducibility and its smooth rational control point. The local calculations and ramification counts support the reported genera $5$, $8$, $4$, and $5$, respectively. Independent rational arithmetic also confirms that the displayed $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{133})$ configuration has eleven rational-square radicands and nineteen square radicands over that quadratic field. I did not rerun the complete verification suite or the height-bounded censuses.

The additions remain distinguishable from the regular-completion construction of Dujella–Kazalicki–Petričević [5], the chain constructions in [4], and the paired-source and signed-frame results in [20]. In particular, the sextuple recovered in Section II is already present in [4, §7], as the manuscript acknowledges. The incremental contribution is its explicit geometric reconstruction together with the new reductions and certificates, rather than rediscovery of that sextuple or of regular completion itself.

The following local corrections and qualifications would improve the update without changing this judgment:

1. **Correct the ramification label in supplemental item 9.** Its heading calls the genus-five double cover of the genus-two $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{hyp}}^{(8)}$ “unramified.” An unramified double cover would have genus three. For the displayed model $q_U(s,U)=0$, direct substitution gives $q_U(s,1)=4(s+8)$ and $q_U(s,-1)=2s(s+2)(2s+3)$. These give four simple branch points for $\kappa^2=(U+1)/(U-1)$, consistent with genus five. The equation and genus can stand; the heading’s adjective cannot.

2. **Synchronize the Euler formula in the retained output.** The `euler_sixth_entry` string in the `exp63` JSON, and the corresponding output string in its script, use the factor $2(2r^2-1)t^2-1$. The mathematical statement and the executed symbolic calculation instead use $(2r^2-1)^2t^2-r^2$. The latter is the formula whose twelve square identities passed. Generate this output from the verified expression to prevent another transcription mismatch.

3. **Keep the reconstruction domain visible.** The $4:1$ statement is generic, and the birational dictionary applies on the specified open chart. State that qualification beside the compressed manuscript claim, together with the square-lifting and distinctness requirements. Rational points on an invariant quotient, and configurations over quadratic fields, must remain distinct from ordinary rational tuples. Likewise, exact relation types of parameterized families should be understood generically, with exceptional specializations excluded.

4. **Make the new results easier to audit.** Split Theorem 4(1)(c) into separate statements for reconstruction, sections, and genera. Replace the abstract’s catalogue of symbols with the main mathematical outcomes and remaining lifting conditions. Also distinguish executable assertions from descriptive output: for example, `exp62` advertises the bounded $F_{12}$ census, but its third part checks the relation type; the census loops are in `exp55`.

The general rational septuple problem remains open, and this update does not warrant changing its verified-solution record.
