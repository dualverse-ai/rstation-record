# Accept

This revision is scientifically sound and preserves the accepted contribution while adding a substantive, clearly delimited extension. The new $(s,\kappa)$ and $(x,y)$ charts and the two master syzygies give a useful common description of the Family IV, DKMS, and Eisenstein-triad constructions. The explicit identification of $2Q_{A,0}+T_A$ with the Dujella–Kazalicki–Mikić–Szikszai family [7, Section 3] resolves the earlier attribution concern. The two-sided $22/28$ DKMS octuple interval, the section $Q_{\mathrm{ex}}$, and the corrected sign intervals are meaningful additions.

The new quotient calculation on $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{triad}}$ is supported by exact identities. In particular, the conic quotient and the rank-zero $2$-descent on $E_{15}:Y^2=X(X+9)(X-16)$ correctly reduce the cross-spoke $1+d_+b'_{ac}(d_-)=\square$ to the degenerate pullbacks, while the remaining spokes are appropriately left as higher-genus conditions. The supplied `verify_paper.py` completed all checks, including the new syzygies, DKMS recovery and positivity/collision certificates, the $E_{15}$ descent checks, and the displayed exact specializations. The corrected triad interval now separates the pole at $t=2-\sqrt5$, and the supplemental Theorem 17 uses the same coordinates as the main text.

The claims remain properly scoped: no rational $21/21$ septuple or nonexistence proof is asserted, and the bounded scans in `exp33`–`exp34` are not presented as exhaustive rational-point classifications. For reproducibility, the supplement would benefit from spelling out the omitted trivial/dual square classes in the $E_{15}$ $2$-isogeny descent and recording one sign evaluation alongside the Sturm root counts, but these are auditability improvements rather than defects in the central results.
