# Sub-quadruple S_ab-square completions on two-sided quintuples: two elliptic sextuple surfaces, a new rational almost septuple of relation type (5,2), and its genus-three full-root curve

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-15T19:22:15.270585Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/72/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study sub-quadruple extensions $c^*_\mp = e_\mp(a,b,d_-,d_+)$ of two-sided rational Diophantine quintuples $\{a,b,c,d_-,d_+\}$ with $a b + 1 = r^2$ and $d_- d_+ + 1 = w_1^2$. Expressing $c - a - b = \frac{\lambda^2(2r-1)+(2r+1)}{2\lambda}$, we prove that $c^*_-$ and $c^*_+$ are linear in $S_{ab} := a + b$, and that the three residual conditions ($c c^*_- + 1 = \square$, $c c^*_+ + 1 = \square$, $c^*_- c^*_+ + 1 = \square$) for $\mathcal{S}_7^{(c^*)} = \{a,b,c,d_-,d_+,c^*_-,c^*_+\}$ are quadratics in $S_{ab}$ with square leading coefficients and discriminants depending only on $(r,\lambda)$. Killing the discriminant of $c c^*_- + 1$ cuts out Branch 1 ($r = \frac{3\lambda^2+1}{2(\lambda^2-1)}$), where $R_4(c,d_-,d_+,c^*_-) = 0$ and $c c^*_- + 1 = \square$ hold identically, making $\{a,b,c,d_-,d_+,c^*_-\}$ a sextuple at every point of the elliptic surface $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{Br1}}(\lambda,\mu)$ (yielding 17 parametric families and 408 positive sextuples). Dually, killing the discriminant factors of $c^*_- c^*_+ + 1$ gives the twin-fifth surfaces $\mathcal{E}_{F_1}, \mathcal{E}_{F_3}$. Furthermore, we prove that $R_4(p,q,e_-,e_+)$ factors universally with the pair-symmetric factor $(p q u v)^2 - 6 p q u v - 4(p q + u v) - 3$. On Branch 1, vanishing of this factor for $\{a,d_+\}$ and $\{b,d_-\}$ simultaneously enforces $R_4(a,d_+,c^*_-,c^*_+) = R_4(b,d_-,c^*_-,c^*_+) = 0$, cutting out a curve of relation type $(|\mathcal{R}_4|,|\mathcal{R}_5|) = (5,2)$ whose full-root cover collapses to the smooth genus-3 hyperelliptic curve $\mathcal{H}: W^2 = (5\lambda^4+10\lambda^2+1)(5\lambda^4-22\lambda^2+33)$, proving finiteness by Faltings’ theorem and yielding a new rational almost septuple $\mathcal{S}_7^{(20/21)}$ at $(\lambda,W) = (-2,55)$. No rational septuple is claimed.
