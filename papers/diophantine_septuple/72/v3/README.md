# Sub-quadruple S_ab-square completions on two-sided quintuples: two elliptic sextuple surfaces, a new rational almost septuple of relation type (5,2), and its genus-three full-root curve

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 3 · 2026-10-16T12:21:49.900244Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/72/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study sub-quadruple extensions $c^*_\mp = e_\mp(a,b,d_-,d_+)$ of two-sided rational Diophantine quintuples $\{a,b,c,d_-,d_+\}$ with $a b + 1 = r^2$ and $d_- d_+ + 1 = w_1^2$. Writing $c - a - b = \frac{\lambda^2(2r-1)+(2r+1)}{2\lambda}$, we prove that $c^*_-$ and $c^*_+$ are linear in $S_{ab} := a + b$, and that the residual conditions for $\mathcal{S}_7^{(c^*)} = \{a,b,c,d_-,d_+,c^*_-,c^*_+\}$ are quadratics in $S_{ab}$ with square leading coefficients and discriminants depending only on $(r,\lambda)$, cutting out Branch 1 ($\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{Br1}}$) and the twin-fifth surfaces $\mathcal{E}_{F_1}, \mathcal{E}_{F_3}$. Furthermore, $R_4(p,q,e_-,e_+)$ factors universally with symmetric factor $P_4^2 - 6 P_4 - 4(p q + u v) - 3$, whose vanishing defines the 2-dimensional type-$(4,2)$ surface $\mathcal{S}_{4,2} : W_\tau^2 = (P_4-1)(P_4-9)$ where $a_\pm c_\pm + 1 = \square$ holds identically. On Branch 1, $\mathcal{S}_{4,2}$ specializes to the genus-3 type-$(5,2)$ curve $\mathcal{H}: W^2 = (5\lambda^4+10\lambda^2+1)(5\lambda^4-22\lambda^2+33)$, proving finiteness, lifting affine points to an unramified $d=1$ cover, and yielding a new rational almost septuple $\mathcal{S}_7^{(20/21)}$ at $(\lambda,W) = (-2,55)$. In Version 3, we establish a global $(r,\mu,z)$ trace decomposition on $\mathcal{S}_{4,2}$, put $\Sigma_\mu$ into cubic Weierstrass form $\Sigma_\mu^+ : W_+^2 = -K(r,\mu)L(r,\mu)$ with 2-torsion involution $\tau_{T_0}(\mu) = -\frac{\mu+4r}{K}$, and construct three explicit 1-parameter families on $\mathcal{S}_{4,2}$, including a closed-form genus-0 family in $\mathbb{Q}(k)$ of all-positive $19/21$ septuples for $k \in (1, k_*)$ (where $k_* \approx 2.026846$ is the unique positive root of $3k^4-4k^3-2k^2-4k-1=0$) and a non-boundary genus-1 family $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{III},\pm}$. Finally, we prove the explicit factor cancellation on the trace-lock section $w = 2u^2 y - L$ of Paper 65 and construct a closed-form $\mathbb{Q}(k)$ moving-$u$ elliptic family $\mathcal{E}_B$ of irregular-quadruple regular-fifth quintuples. No rational septuple is claimed.
