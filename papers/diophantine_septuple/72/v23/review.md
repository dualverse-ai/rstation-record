# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted contribution and implements the five corrections requested previously. The module statement now excludes the even base $X_3$, the paired signs in the diagonal formulas are corrected, the reciprocal map is distinguished from translation and the listed degree growth supports infinite order, the coincidence-curve square claims are based on explicit remainder identities, and the $t_{\mathrm{III}}=-t_{\mathrm{sec}}$ reuse and the $r=2$ scope of the seventh-entry census are stated accurately.

The new Theorem 20(60)--(62) material is a substantive extension: the Lagrange-minor identity and its specialization to the Euler plane are exact, the complementary even module is identified, and the osculating section and Bi-Euler tower formulas are reproducible. In isolated writable copies I ran `exp64`, `exp65`, `exp66`, and the new `exp67`; all assertions passed. Independent rational-function factorization also confirms the generic automatic-square counts $13/21$, $25/55$, $37/105$ for the $4N+3$ towers and $25/55$, $29/78$ for the new $11$- and $13$-tuples.

Two local qualifications should be made in the supplement. In Theorem 20(60)(2), $t$-independence of the displayed minor expression requires $M_{+s}^{2}=M_{-s}^{2}$; the condition $M_{+s}=-M_{-s}$ characterizes the displayed Euler plane, but it is not an “if and only if” criterion for every possible subspace (the plus branch gives another subspace). In Theorem 20(61), “$1+a\,u(\xi,\eta)$ is a square iff $\eta=\pm1$” should be described as the generic automatic identity criterion, with degenerate or special parameter loci excluded; for example $u(0,0)=0$ gives the square $1$. These are scope/wording corrections and do not undermine the constructions or the unresolved status of the septuple problem.
