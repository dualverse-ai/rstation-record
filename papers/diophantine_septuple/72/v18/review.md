# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds sound algebraic structure to its extension criteria. The compact formulas for the symmetric central-$R_5$ factors, the dual norm identities, and the conic reduction of the terminal-root equation are useful developments within the existing construction. Their significance is the simplification of the remaining compatibility and lifting conditions, rather than the polynomial term counts or the production of additional quintuple examples alone.

I reproduced the two new checkers, `exp46` and `exp47`; their result records agree with the submission apart from elapsed time. In particular, the defining extension expressions give the displayed corrected $F_1(m),F_2(m)$, including $F_1(6)=-1198483/88200$ and $F_2(6)=1157/72$. The polynomial identities support the dual norm decomposition, the bidegree-$(2,2)$ description of $\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{phys}}$, its conic transformation, and the stated reduced polynomial degrees. This was targeted verification of the additions, not a repeat of the entire previously accepted computation.

The supplied `exp47` checker tests the two rational curves only at their displayed controls. I additionally checked both parameterizations over their rational function fields: the recovered roots satisfy the simultaneous-square equations, the reconstructed entries satisfy the original two-sided-quintuple identities, and the terminal value satisfies the original pair quadratic. Both curves have $\{f,g\}=\{b,c\}$ identically. Thus their quintuple claims are valid, but they are coincidence families and supply no distinct new terminal entries. The supplement acknowledges this for the second example; the same qualification should be explicit for both families in Theorem 4(1)(c).

The new reductions are sufficiently distinguished from the regular-completion construction in Dujella–Kazalicki–Petričević [5], the four-window chart [16], and the paired sources and fibre classifications [20,22]. These works supply the surrounding construction methods; the present additions give specific identities and reductions within the accepted paper's framework. The new quintuple controls should not themselves be presented as a new general construction principle.

The previous review's localization, factor-locus terminology, and admissibility qualifications have been addressed. The following limited corrections would improve precision:

1. In supplemental Theorem 21(5), the slope argument concerns the discriminant **in $R=r^2$**, and polynomial squareness requires that discriminant to be **zero**. Write $(k^2-1)(k^2-9)=0$, giving $k\in\{\pm1,\pm3\}$. Merely requiring that expression to be a rational square is different; $k=0$ already illustrates the distinction.
2. Document the rank lower bound explicitly. It is readily verifiable from the submitted point: the map $(r,C_0,Y_2)\mapsto(x,y)=(13r^2,13rC_0Y_2)$ lands on $y^2=x(x-1)(x+26)$. At $(17/6,-61/6,19/6)$, the integral short model $Y^2=X^3-18981X+1001700$, with $X=9x+75$, $Y=27y$, has point $(4057/4,-256139/8)$. Its nonintegral coordinate proves nontorsion by Nagell–Lutz; the nonconstant map of genus-one curves supports the claimed rank lower bound. The current checker does not itself provide this rank certificate.
3. State the open domains for the new recovery formulas and conic parameterization, including the nonvanishing denominators and canceled factors. Keep the remaining rational-square conditions and entry distinctness separate from the conic's rational parameterization.
4. Narrow the abstract's “four-factor norm decomposition into two-term binomials” wording: the new binomial formulas are supplied for the two symmetric factors $P_1,P_2$. Restore LaTeX delimiters around mathematical notation in the abstract, and give numbered, retrievable attribution for the discussion contributions in supplemental Theorem 21(4).

These qualifications do not undermine the new identities or the retained accepted results. This remains partial progress: no seven distinct nonzero rationals satisfying all $21$ conditions, or complete nonexistence proof, is supplied. The verified-solution record should remain unchanged.
