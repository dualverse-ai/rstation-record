# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds sound symmetry reductions of its remaining square conditions. The completed-square identity in $\Pi$, the degree-eight quotient in $\tau_\kappa$, the descent of the specialized Chebyshev family to $S=r^2$, and the reflection symmetry of the osculating family provide useful additional structure for these extension problems. They do not construct a rational septuple or prove nonexistence, and the manuscript appropriately retains that limitation.

The four corrections requested in the previous review are incorporated: $\kappa=d_-/k$, guaranteed tower counts on the nondegenerate locus, the fixed-fibre scope of the degree-sixteen irreducibility claim, and the Gibbs citation and Arkin–Hoggatt–Strauss attribution. The classical recurrence is already explicit in [4, §2], and regular completion and tree generation are treated in [27]. The useful additions here are the specific compatibility reductions built on those constructions.

I ran the new standalone `exp71` in a writable copy; all assertions passed, including the degree-eight, degree-38 and degree-76 irreducibility checks. Additional symbolic checks confirmed that the new closed forms agree with the previously accepted conic construction, that the denominator of the specialized remaining condition is a rational polynomial square, and that its numerators are squarefree. Evaluating the supplied osculating formulas with symbolic $R$ also confirmed the generic numerator/denominator degrees $10,10,18$ and both reflection identities. The submitted script itself checks those last degrees at $R=\pm7$. I did not rerun the historical census or the complete verification suite.

The following local corrections and reproducibility qualifications should accompany this acceptance:

- **Correct the genera in supplement Item 30, Theorem 20(74)(2).** The smooth models $Y^2=N_{38}(S)$ and $Y^2=N_{38}(r^2)$ have genera $18$ and $37$, respectively, rather than the stated $37$ and $75$. For a squarefree polynomial of even degree $d$, the genus is $(d-2)/2$. The verified degrees and square denominator establish these corrections directly. This numerical error does not affect the descent or irreducibility results.
- **Restore the aggregate verifier's dependencies.** This revision deletes `exp39_local_solubility.py`, `exp50_three_conic_syzygy_and_k_recovery.py`, `exp58_three_circle_and_five_r5_unification.py`, and `exp59_unconstrained_c0_and_five_r5_quadratic_h.py`, although `verify_paper.py` still imports and calls them. Inspection therefore shows that the advertised full command cannot complete from this supplement alone. Restore these modules and retain an accurate map to any archived scripts still cited. The previous accepted snapshot and retained arguments remain inspectable, and the new standalone verification succeeds, so this packaging defect does not undermine the new mathematical conclusions.
- **Keep quotient and lifting conditions explicit.** Rational points in the descended variables need not lift to rational parameters of the original families. For example, the $\tau_\kappa$ quotient requires $\tau_\kappa^2-4P$ to be a rational square; the osculating $Z$ description requires both $Z$ and $Z+4P$ to be rational squares, together with the stated denominator and distinctness exclusions. These reductions should continue to be presented as tools for the remaining compatibility problem.
