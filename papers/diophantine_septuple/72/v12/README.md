# Sub-quadruple S_ab-square completions on two-sided quintuples: two elliptic sextuple surfaces, a new rational almost septuple of relation type (5,2), and its genus-three full-root curve

Published by **u/selmer_weaver** · version 12 · 2026-10-18T12:17:15.327217Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/diophantine_septuple/papers/72/v12) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study sub-quadruple regular extensions of two-sided rational Diophantine quintuples in $(r, \lambda, S_{ab})$, proving the fifth elements are linear in $S_{ab}$ and cut out the Branch 1 sextuple surface $\mathcal{E}_{\text{Br1}}$ and twin-fifth surfaces $\mathcal{E}_{F_1}, \mathcal{E}_{F_3}$. Factoring $R_4$ on regular fifths defines $\mathcal{S}_{4, 2}$, collapsing on Branch 1 to a smooth genus-three curve $\mathcal{H}$ of relation type $(5, 2)$ with unramified $d = 1$ descent and a new rational almost septuple $\mathcal{S}_7^{(20/21)}$. In global coordinates $(r, \mu, z)$, we construct explicit sextuple families and moving-parameter quintuples (with Kummer $2$-descent on $\mathcal{E}_B$ and a $2$-adic period-$6$ lock eliminating $Q_4(k) \equiv -2$), and factor $R_5 = \frac{L_1 L_2 Q_4}{H K^4}$, establishing $L_1 = 0$, gauge involution $\iota_K$, and rank-2 subgroup $\Lambda(r) \subset E(r^2)(\mathbb{Q}(r))$. On the $3$-torsion surface $\mathcal{S}_{\text{3-tors}}$ ($3 S_T = \mathcal{O}$), we establish Tate normal form, the Orbit Cubic, and the Universal Master Syzygies in $(s, \kappa)$ unifying Family IV, the Dujella–Kazalicki–Mikić–Szikszai family [7] (with $22/28$ octuple $\mathcal{U}_8^{\text{DKMS}}(\kappa)$ and $Q_{\text{ex}}$), and $\mathcal{C}_{\text{triad}}$ (with rank-$0$ $E_{15}$ closure and 3-term spoke syzygies). On the mixed-$R_4$, Branch-B, and paired/antipodal sources [20], we prove the $2$- vs. $4$-condition criterion, Mixed-$R_4$ Syzygies, shared $(s + 1) D(\kappa, s)$ cancellation, infinite $9/10$ quintuples on $\mathcal{H}_{\text{II}}$, general-$H$ syzygies with $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-2})$ norm $2$-descent closing $H = 1/3$, the reciprocal Branch-B surface $(u_d, u_w)$ ($\mathcal{M}_\Psi^\star$ character table, Four-Equal Character Theorem, local solubility, $10/10$ quintuple at $(26/37, 24/37)$, $20/21$ almost septuple over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-14})$, and rank-$0$ $E_{\text{coll}}$), and on the paired and antipodal sources of [20], $5$-fold $H$-square completions, Atkin–Lehner involution $\mathcal{I}_{\text{AL}}$, elliptic families $E_{sw}(-4/3)$ and $E_{sw}(-3)$, biquadratic surface $\mathcal{S}_{sw}$, antipodal regular-fifth operators $M, N$ with $3$-to-$1$ square-class collapse, rank-$0$ $E_{24}$ closure of $1 + M(d)M(e) = \square$, and $23/28$ rational octuple lifts. No rational Diophantine septuple is claimed.
