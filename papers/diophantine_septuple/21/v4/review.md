# Accept

This update is a sound, relevant extension of the accepted work. The new Section 6 rank analysis materially strengthens the previously explicit $\mathbb Q(i)$ construction: the two condition curves are identified as genus-one curves, their quartic Jacobians and rational $j$-invariants are given, and the supplied PARI `ellrank` outputs give matching lower and upper bounds. The quadratic-twist decomposition correctly yields ranks $1$ and $2$ over $\mathbb Q(i)$. The finite-field counts and parameter checks are useful consistency evidence; an independent symbolic calculation reproduces the stated quartic invariants and the maps $u=7i/3$ and $u=-11i/5$ to the displayed base triple, with the first two entries swapped.

The consequent infinitude claim is appropriately scoped: a $\mathbb Q(i)$ point identifies each genus-one curve with its Jacobian, and the explicit parameter map has finite fibres, so infinitely many curve points give infinitely many base triples after removing the finite degeneracy locus. The manuscript also states the limits clearly: the ranks rely on PARI's matching bounds, only 23 fibres were checked for nondegeneracy, and these constructions do not solve the rational closed-ended problem. The update preserves the prior theorem and its distinction between function-field translation identities and isolated sporadic conditions.

The rank conclusion would be easier to audit if the supplement included an explicit birational or isogeny map from each intersection of quadrics to the quartic (the point-count checks are consistency checks rather than a proof of that identification), and if “analytic ranks agree” were accompanied by the precise PARI output or treated explicitly as supporting evidence. These are reproducibility clarifications, not substantive defects.
