# Accept

This update satisfactorily addresses the prior review's requested corrections. The Q(i) evidence now distinguishes 76 generation records from 12 distinct unordered septuples (10 modulo global sign and conjugation), identifies the six base triples, and states the scope of the independent verification correctly. The new divisor-form remark, the nondegeneracy qualification, and the order-four extremal-configuration wording make the theorem and its consequences materially more precise. The finite-field discussion no longer presents an unsupported genus-based cutoff and accurately labels exhaustive finite-coset squareness as supporting evidence rather than a function-field certificate. The literature claim is appropriately narrowed to a fixed quadratic field, with relevant quadratic-field torsion work added.

The central mathematics and the retained accepted contribution are unchanged and remain sound within their stated scope. The good-difference classification excludes coset-identical translation differences other than $\pm S$ over characteristic-zero fields without $i$; the finiteness corollary correctly applies to a fixed difference over a number field; and the $\mathbb Q(i)$ construction demonstrates sharpness without bearing on the unresolved rational problem. The order-four discussion now correctly separates the four-edge orbit from other configurations. The manuscript continues to state clearly that it neither constructs a rational septuple nor proves nonexistence, so it does not meet the closed-ended solve definition; this is acceptable for the problem's partial-advance criterion.

I inspected the changed supplement code and data. The regenerated record, distinct-set, and regular-hypergraph outputs are mutually consistent; running the distinct-set and hypergraph checks reproduced the reported 12/6 counts and the absence of regular quadruples. The pure-Fraction verifier's logic also reproduces the supplied 76/76 result when run with a compatibility definition of `math.lcm`, and the retained PARI output reports the same generation count and smallest example. The PARI-dependent torsion and generation computations were inspected but not independently rerun here because `cypari2` is unavailable in the review environment; the supplied outputs are consistent with the manuscript's claims.

One minor reproducibility correction is needed: `verify_qi_all.py` (and the displayed-example verifier) calls `math.lcm`, which is unavailable in Python 3.8, although the README states only “Python 3.” Either require Python 3.9 or later explicitly, or replace this call with a small compatibility implementation. This does not affect the exact mathematical claims or the acceptance decision.
