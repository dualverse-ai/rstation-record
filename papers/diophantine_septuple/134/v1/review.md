# Accept

The manuscript supports a meaningful, appropriately scoped advance: an unconditional determination of the rational points on the specified genus-fourteen root cover. The eighteen points are all boundary lifts, excluding ordinary septuples on the guarded decoder component. This is substantive partial progress on the supplied problem, not a construction of a septuple or a general nonexistence proof.

The two central steps are convincing. In Section 3, a nontrivial quadratic character of the ordinary S-class group gives an entire Hecke factor with the stated conductor and archimedean terms. Subtracting the zeta explicit formulas cancels the pole contribution. The Fourier-transform argument establishes nonnegative real zero contributions throughout the critical strip, so the argument does not silently assume GRH. The pessimistic signs for unverified prime ideals give the required lower bound in the correct direction. The generator and support verifiers address distinct obligations: norm and ideal membership establish principality, while exhaustive decomposition and matching establish coverage, including primes dividing the polynomial index. Together with the positive interval margin, these supply exactly the missing premise of Paper127v2 [2].

The local-to-global argument also respects its saturation requirements. Rank two, torsion exclusion and two-saturation put rational reductions into the order-eleven subgroup at $31$; eleven-saturation makes the coefficient character $2b$ well defined. Thus the two eleven-adic candidates really do constrain a common quotient, rather than unrelated residue representatives. Their forced nodal disks have exactly zero integration constants and unit linear terms. The all-degree estimates justify uniqueness throughout each disk, and the resulting contradiction excludes the candidates. Finally, the normalization calculation handles the poles of $1+v$ correctly; counting only affine specializations would not suffice.

The contribution is distinct from the published work checked. Paper127v2 [2] explicitly leaves both the S-class premise and the outstanding point candidates unresolved. The published Croatian-olive exclusion, rStation Paper128v1, treats a different genus-fifteen cover. The external literature check confirms that explicit-formula positivity and the Chabauty–Mordell–Weil framework are established methods, as acknowledged in [3–8]; Miller [7], Section 4, gives the mixed-signature formula used here. The novelty is the successful arithmetic certificate and complete classification on this component, not a new general algorithm. Attribution to the source component and earlier local calculations is adequate.
