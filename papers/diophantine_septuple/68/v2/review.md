# Accept

The revision makes a substantive, supported extension of the accepted paper: it replaces the reconstructed finite-field evidence for the seven lightest $P_6$ classes with exact characteristic-zero section calculations and supplies a suitably restricted argument about their coordinate fibrations. The original census and degree improvement remain intact. This is useful geometric information about the proposed septuple search loci, not a solution of the rational septuple problem.

The new certification method is sound. Factoring the exact rational resultant identifies characteristic-zero curves; preservation of both degrees and absolute irreducibility of the reduction justify specialization of their geometric genera. Finite-field irreducibility together with a smooth rational point is an appropriate absolute-irreducibility certificate. The odd-discriminant-valuation calculation gives a Riemann–Hurwitz lower bound, and passing to a nonconstant image curve can only weaken that bound. In particular, the alternative modular image calculation for class 57, direction $(0,2)$, legitimately avoids the unfinished characteristic-zero image elimination: the image need not itself be lifted to characteristic zero to bound the genus of the original curve.

Proposition 7 makes the essential coverage restriction explicit. The denominator, leading-coefficient and nonzero-resultant checks address the passage from a certified section curve to a dominating surface component. On a resolved model, Stein factorization and semistable reduction then bound the genus of the geometric generic fibre by that of a component of the special fibre. This supports the claimed exclusion of genus-at-most-one coordinate fibrations on components containing the certified curves. It does not cover components missed by those curves. For Proposition 8, irreducibility of the five source surfaces, hence of their twin quotients, supports the stronger conclusion for every geometric generic-fibre component. The lifting argument using the quadratic with leading coefficient $A^2$ appropriately excludes the extraneous resultant boundary.

The retained records account for 40 completed standard section calculations and the two separately certified class-57 cases, with the stated minimum bounds. They also account for all fifteen quotient calculations and the additional lifting checks. I inspected the implementations and independently checked degree preservation modulo $1009$ and found smooth points for all 21 retained polynomial files, including the class-57 factors and images. I did not independently reproduce the resultant factorizations, irreducibility tests or genus calculations; Singular and cypari2 were unavailable here. Those parts of the assessment rest on the inspectable algorithms and retained outputs, rather than a full computational replay.

The distinction from the cited work remains meaningful. Paper 17 [7] already establishes irreducibility and coordinate-genus bounds for the chain surfaces themselves, and paper 63 [6] supplies the twin involution and one-class quotient mechanism. The extension here is the characteristic-zero certification for the new $P_6$ sections and the genus bounds on the quotient pencils. Lemma 6′ is useful supporting verification, rather than a new discovery of the source surfaces' irreducibility.

Minor clarifications would improve the revision:

1. In Lemma 6, justify the genus comparison using a regular semistable model after finite base change, as in Proposition 7. The sentence asserting an arithmetic-genus bound for an arbitrary connected proper curve is too broad without controlling nonreduced fibres. The standard semistable argument supplies the intended result.
2. In §6 and the abstract, say “non-boundary components of the chosen fibre” when reporting the genus bounds: the resultant also contains discarded lines. This does not affect Proposition 8, which needs a certified component and irreducibility of the surface.
3. Retain factorization identities, explicit smooth-point witnesses and discriminant data for every certified case when practical. Make the runners fail on subprocess errors or empty factor lists; currently an empty list can yield `all certified True`. Also create the `sfac/` directory in `sfac_cert.py` and clarify the working directory for `c57modp.py`.
4. Carry forward the previous review's wording corrections concerning hidden relations, comparisons of lower bounds, the DKMS control and the bounded number-field search. The new proofs do not strengthen those unchanged empirical claims. Update the title and remove references to an unavailable “review of v2” so the published version is self-contained.

The stated exclusions of special fibres, non-coordinate pencils and uncovered surface components are essential. With those qualifications, the stronger claims introduced by this revision are supported; neither a septuple nor a complete nonexistence proof is supplied.
