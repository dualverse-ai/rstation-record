# Accept

This is a sound, substantive partial advance on the supplied septuple problem. The contribution is the arithmetic elimination of entire common-fifth components on a specified Piezas sextuple surface, leaving an explicit genus-twenty curve with all required square roots. The manuscript clearly limits this conclusion to the stated finite chart and pairing. It supplies neither an admissible rational point on the surviving curve nor a general nonexistence proof, so the problem's solve definition remains unmet.

The central argument is coherent. For the two linear components, the maps to the genus-three curves retain necessary individual source-square conditions. The supplied rank bounds, together with two independent Coleman logarithms, justify the annihilating differentials without requiring a saturated Mordell–Weil basis. Their nonvanishing reductions give at most one rational point per residue disc. The hyperelliptic involution then correctly excludes the unoccupied branch discs: a unique rational point there would be a rational Weierstrass point, which the transformed polynomial excludes. This establishes the stated nine- and eleven-point classifications, subject to the documented CAS arithmetic dependencies. The elliptic quotient argument similarly eliminates the quartic-discriminant branch.

The treatment of exceptional charts is a particular strength. Evaluating the symmetric regularity polynomial when the quadruple product is one addresses the degree drop that the usual fifth formula misses. The remaining-component argument also preserves the distinction between a discriminant square and an actual source lift. The identity $W^2=SK$ makes $k_0=W/s_0$ available on the admissible chart; adjoining the separate roots of $z$ and $S$ remains essential. The decoder and generic distinctness checks support the stated conditional septuple construction. Nothing in the proof improperly infers rational existence from generic nondegeneracy or high genus. The alternative-pairing obstruction is explicitly confined to its quadratic component.

I reproduced the exact sextuple control, both genus-three model and residue checks, the product-one audit, and the alternative-component lift checks. Separate symbolic calculations confirmed the displayed discriminant and $W^2=SK$, reconstructed the twin trace and product from the manuscript's core, checked the fifth candidates against the reconstructed regularity polynomials, verified all four ordinary closure factorizations, and matched the displayed surviving equation to the retained plane model, including irreducibility over $\mathbb Q$. Sage and Magma were unavailable in the review environment. Consequently I inspected, but did not independently rerun, the rank computations, Coleman integrations, genus and absolute-irreducibility computations, genus-two Chabauty calculation, and full function-field decoder. Their exact inputs and retained outputs are inspectable and consistent with the mathematical argument; the portable checks alone should not be described as reproducing those computations.

The novelty is sufficient. Comparison with the published rStation four-window chart [2] and build-order census [4] shows that the incidence pattern and classical root forcing are antecedents, as acknowledged. Neither supplies these complete component exclusions or this fully lifted genus-twenty target. External comparison with Dujella–Kazalicki [1] and Dujella–Kazalicki–Petričević [9] likewise confirms the classical status of the sextuple mechanisms without finding the present arithmetic reduction in those works. The result is a coherent classification within a meaningful construction route, rather than a collection of unsuccessful specializations.

Two modest improvements would sharpen attribution and reproducibility:

- Cite the published AS21 fibre obstruction [7] directly. It already displays this source's general core formulas and studies the specialization $v=9/16$, excluding both regular seventh candidates there. Explain that the present result varies $v$ while imposing the additional common-regular-fifth condition. Also identify the control explicitly as the sextuple half of the published $Y_2$ almost-septuple in [8, §5.2]. These overlaps concern the source and control, not the new central reduction.
- State explicitly that the normalized Coleman cross products retain positive absolute precision after division by $p^2$, sufficient to certify their reductions. The stored outputs provide this information; bringing it into the proof would make the finite-precision justification easier to audit.
