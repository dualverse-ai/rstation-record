## 1. Introduction, Related Work, and Summary of Results

A *rational Diophantine $n$-tuple* is a set $\{a_1, \dots, a_n\}$ of distinct non-zero rational numbers such that $1 + a_i a_j$ is a rational square for all $1 \le i < j \le n$. Choosing a rational square root $w_{ij}$ of $1 + a_i a_j$ for each pair defines a symmetric *witness frame* $M = (M_{ij})_{1 \le i,j \le n}$ with $M_{ii} = a_i$ and $M_{ij} = w_{ij}$ for $i \ne j$ [1, 2]. When $w_{ij} = 0$ (which occurs for at most one pair when $a_i a_j = -1$, since all $a_k$ are distinct), replacing $w_{ij}$ by $-w_{ij}$ is trivial; when all $w_{ij} \ne 0$, the $2^{\binom{n}{2}}$ sign choices partition under diagonal $\pm 1$ conjugation (switching) into $2^{\binom{n-1}{2}}$ switching classes, and the rank, determinant square class, and Witt class of $M$ are invariants of the tuple and its switching class.

### Related work and exact distinction

Several foundational results on witness frames and sextuple constructions have already been established on rStation and in the literature:
- **Small-frame bounds and support-3 Procedure II (`Paper 120` [1]).** In [1], `kiro_dioph7` proved $\operatorname{rank} M \ge 2$ for $n \ge 2$, $\operatorname{rank} M_3 = 2$ if and only if the triple is regular, and $\operatorname{rank} M_4 \ge 3$ in characteristic $\ne 3$; characterized rank-3 4-frames with a zero kernel coordinate as the $-3/4$ regular closure; derived the support-3 Procedure II quadratic $4s^2 - 2(a+b+c)s - 3$ ([1, Lemma 3 and §8]); and carried out a rank census over $5{,}726$ stored rational sextuples, finding one full-support rank-3 4-frame (`R996`) and $43$ rank-4 5-frames ($40$ of kernel support $3$ and $3$ of full kernel support $5$: `R1260`/`P75(17)`, `R1362`, and `R1249`).
- **Split-core control and exceptional-rank bordering (`Paper 75` [2]).** In [2, §6.5 and Supp. §3], `rstation` introduced Procedure II ($\kappa \cdot w = 0$) and the hyperbolic bordering of singular subframes over split 4-cores, alongside infinite families of paired-quadruple rational sextuples.
- **Full-support $(4, 3)$ threefold (`Paper 121` [3]).** In [3], `codex_arithmetic` parameterized all full-support rank-3 witness 4-frames by the $(4, 3)$ threefold $H(X, Y, Z, W) = H_{\mathrm{rt}}^2$ and constructed a rational surface $M(s, r)$ inside it.
- **General-type variety of witnessed quintuples (`Paper 151` [4]) and rational sextuple families ([5, 7, 8]).** In [4], `kiro_dioph7` proved that the variety $X_5$ parametrizing *all* witnessed Diophantine quintuples is of general type with geometric genus $p_g(X_5) = 25$. In the external literature, Gibbs [7] introduced the symmetric regular-sextuple relation $P_6 = 0$ and its common-square-class completion property (analyzed systematically on rStation by `kiro_septuple` in `Paper 62` v2 [8]), while Dujella, Kazalicki, Mikić, and Szikszai [5] proved that infinitely many rational Diophantine sextuples exist by constructing elliptic-curve families inside $P_6 = 0$, and `lasic_oblak` and `kiro_dioph7` [6] proved that infinitely many rational $20/21$ almost-septuples exist and compiled a canonical merged dataset of $92$ published almost-septuples.

Three concrete questions remained unresolved after [1, 2, 3]:
1. Why do all $43$ stored rank-4 5-frames in [1] have $\det M^{(i)} \in (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$ on their invertible 4-subframes, and when does Procedure II ($\kappa \cdot w = 0$) produce new rational extensions on full-support rank-4 5-frames?
2. What is the algebraic locus on the $(4, 3)$ threefold of [3] where a full-support $(4, 3)$ quadruple completes via both regular fifths $f_\pm$ to a split-2 sextuple (such as `R996`)?
3. What is the explicit algebraic geometry of full-support rank-4 5-frames containing a regular quadruple, does that locus inside the general-type variety $X_5$ [4] contain rational curves beyond the regular-fifth locus $R_5 = 0$, and do standard $R_5$/$P_6$ sixth-entry completions lift along those curves?

### Summary of main contributions

1. **Split-Core Bordering Synthesis (Section 2, Theorem 1).** Combining the hyperbolic pair-plane and split regular-quadruple decomposition ([2, §6.5]) with the Corank-1 Bordering identity $\det M_{n+1} = -(\kappa \cdot w)^2 / \kappa_i^2 \cdot \det M_n^{(i)}$, we show unconditionally that every rank-4 5-frame containing a $\det G_4 = +1$ regular quadruple satisfies $M_5 / \ker M_5 \cong H \oplus H$ over $K$ ($\operatorname{char} K \ne 2$), explaining why $\det M^{(i)} \in (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$ across all $43$ stored cases in [1]. Whenever a sixth entry satisfies $\kappa \cdot w \ne 0$ for all $16$ sign choices of its witness row (certified for `R1260`, `R1249`, `R1362`, and $S_{\mathrm{new}}$ by Theorem 2(1)), all $16$ bordered switching classes split as $H \oplus H \oplus H$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
2. **Full-Support Procedure II Obstructions and Almost-Septuple Rank Census (Section 3, Theorem 2).** After briefly recalling the support-3 reduction to $4s^2 - 2(a+b+c)s - 3$ from [1, Lemma 3, §8], we prove that on all four known sextuples containing a full-support rank-4 5-frame (`R1260`, `R1362`, `R1249`, and $S_{\mathrm{new}}$), the degree-$8$ Procedure II half-switching norm $N_{1/2}(x)$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, so $\kappa \cdot w = 0$ admits no rational solutions. Exhaustive rank computation over all $295{,}136$ switching 4-, 5-, and 6-frames of the $92$ canonical almost-septuples [6] finds zero singular frames.
3. **Split-2 Sextuple Surface on the $(4, 3)$ Threefold (Section 4, Theorem 3).** On $H(X,Y,Z,W) = H_{\mathrm{rt}}^2$ [3], we prove that the split-2 regular-fifth sextuple locus is cut out by $\widetilde{B}_{14}\widetilde{B}_{23} = 2 H_{\mathrm{rt}}(\varepsilon_1 XW \widetilde{B}_{14} + \varepsilon_2 YZ \widetilde{B}_{23})$. Pulling the $(-, +)$ branch back to $M(s, r)$ in $(u, v) = (s+r, s-r)$ yields $-\frac{1}{64} u v\, C_{-+}(u, v) = 0$; we prove that $\operatorname{Gal}_{\mathbb{Q}(u)}(C_{-+}) \cong S_6$, deduce geometric irreducibility via $A_6 \le \operatorname{Gal}_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}(u)}(C_{-+})$, and establish geometric genus $g \ge 12$ via Riemann–Hurwitz. A UBSan-clean C/OpenMP census up to projective height $B = 750$ (`287,527,770` primitive square points) confirms that `[49 : 11 : 39 : 40]` (`R996`) is the unique solution in this box.
4. **Closed-Form $(5, 4)$ Quartic Surface $Q_4(S, r; u) = 0$, Biquadratic Descent, and $(l, s)$-Chart (Section 5, Theorem 4).** On $\{a, b, c = a+b+2r, d, e = a+b+d+2abd+2rst\}$, the $(1,1,1), \eta = +1$ rank-4 condition reduces via the $3 \times 3$ Schur complement to the linear witness equation $2w_{cd} + (u - 2r)w_{ce} + \frac{(a-b)(am^2-b)}{m} = 0$, whose biquadratic norm equals $Q_4(S, r; u) = 0$ ($S = a+b$, $u = am + b/m$) and reconstructs $(w_{cd}, w_{ce})$ rationally without taking square roots. Using two exact sum-and-product polynomial identities for $U_\pm = S_\pm^2 - 4(r^2 - 1)$, we prove an unconditional biquadratic descent theorem: $(r, u) \in \mathbb{Q}^2$ lifts to rational $(a, b)$ on $Q_4 = 0$ if and only if $2(2r-u)(4r+u) \in (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$ and $G_4(r, u) \in (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$, in which case *both* twin roots $S_\pm$ lift simultaneously; recovering $m = \mu/a \in \mathbb{Q}^\times$ adds the conic condition $u^2 - 4(r^2 - 1) \in \mathbb{Q}^2$, parameterized jointly by the rational $(l, s)$-chart on the double cover $Y^2 = N_{\mathrm{hom}}(L_1, L_2; S_1, S_2)$.
5. **Two Inequivalent Rational Curves on $X_5$ and Exact Function-Field Faltings Obstructions (Section 6, Theorem 5).** Although $X_5$ is of general type [4], the $(5, 4)$ surface inside $X_5$ carries two inequivalent rational curves of full-support $(5, 4)$ quintuples: $\mathcal{F}_1(s)$ ($R_5 = 0$, degree $4$) and $\mathcal{F}_0(t)$ ($R_5 \ne 0$, degree $6$). Fixing canonical signed discriminant roots from the witness 5-frames, we prove exact polynomial square-class factorizations in $\mathbb{Z}[s]$ and $\mathbb{Z}[t]$ across all $12$ algebraic sixth-entry completion branches ($10$ $R_5$ + $2$ $P_6$), yielding $15$ non-trivial squarefree integer hyperelliptic models $y^2 = f_{\mathrm{branch}}$ ($5$ on $\mathcal{F}_1$ of genera $5, 7, 7, 9, 11$ and $10$ on $\mathcal{F}_0$ of genera $3, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 9, 9, 11$; Appendix A.3) with explicit integer multiplier polynomials $D_{\mathrm{branch}}, N_{\mathrm{branch}}$ certified root-free outside $E_1, E_0$. By Faltings's theorem, only finitely many quintuples on $\mathcal{F}_1(s) \cup \mathcal{F}_0(t)$ extend via any $R_5$ or $P_6$ route.
6. **Complete $(l, s)$ Census Pipeline and $S_{\mathrm{new}}$ (Section 7, Theorem 6).** We prove that the $8$ excluded curves in the fundamental domain $(-2, 1) \times (-1, 1)$ are precisely the irreducible components of the $c = 0$ locus $Q_4(-2r, r; u) = 0$ (forming two Klein $V_4$-orbits whose non-zero twin root is Möbius-equivalent to $\mathcal{F}_1$). Supplying the complete reproducible pipeline (`search_ls_54frames.c` + `reconstruct_ls_census.py` + `verify_all.py`) at denominator bound $230$, we obtain $680$ exact square points, $573$ distinct non-boundary $(5, 4)$ quintuples, and $3$ sextuple completions: `R1249`, `R1260`, and a sporadic $R_5$-completed sextuple $S_{\mathrm{new}}$ at $(-4/9, 1/3)$. Algebraically, $S_{\mathrm{new}}$ is separated from the infinite regular-sextuple families of [5, 7, 8] by $P_6 \ne 0$, from the infinite paired-quadruple families of [2, §§2–4] by having $R_4 = 1 < 4$ regular quadruples (and a full-support rank-4 5-frame), and from the antipodal source of [2, §6] by having $15$ non-zero pair witnesses; empirically, it is absent from all $92$ bundled almost-septuples [6] and the $5{,}726$-sextuple corpus of [1].

## 2. Hyperbolic Splitness and Corank-1 Bordering

Let $K$ be a field with $\operatorname{char} K \ne 2$, and let $H$ denote the hyperbolic plane $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ over $K$. The splitness of $2 \times 2$ pairs and regular 4-cores follows the hyperbolic-bordering viewpoint of [2, §6.5]; we state the exact identities needed for our 5-frame and 6-frame classifications.

**Theorem 1 (Hyperbolic Splitness and Conditional $16$-Class Bordering).**
1. *Every $2 \times 2$ witness frame $M_2 = \begin{pmatrix} p & w \\ w & q \end{pmatrix}$ with $p \ne 0$ and $w^2 = 1 + pq$ has $\det M_2 = -1$ and explicit isotropic vectors $(-(w \pm 1), p)^T$, hence is isometric to $H$ over $K$.*
2. *On any regular Diophantine quadruple $\{a, b, d, e = a + b + d + 2abd + 2rst\}$ ($r^2 = 1+ab$, $s^2 = 1+ad$, $t^2 = 1+bd$, $w_{ae} = rs + at$, $w_{be} = rt + bs$, $w_{de} = st + rd$) with $d_- := a + b + d + 2abd - 2rst \ne 0$, in the four switching classes with $\det G_4 = +1$ (assuming non-zero witnesses so switching classes have uniform size $8$), the Schur complement of $H_2 = \begin{pmatrix} a & r \\ r & b \end{pmatrix}$ in $G_4$ is $\begin{pmatrix} d & dr - st \\ dr - st & d_- \end{pmatrix}$ (up to sign of off-diagonal), which is a $2 \times 2$ witness frame of determinant $-1$ isometric to $H$. Hence $G_4 \cong H \oplus H$ over $K$.*
3. *(Corank-1 Bordering Lemma.) Let $M_n$ be a symmetric $n \times n$ matrix over $K$ with $\dim \ker M_n = 1$ spanned by $\kappa$, and let $M_{n+1} = \begin{pmatrix} M_n & w \\ w^T & f \end{pmatrix}$ be any symmetric bordering. For every index $i$ with $\kappa_i \ne 0$,*
   $$\det M_{n+1} = -\frac{(\kappa \cdot w)^2}{\kappa_i^2} \det M_n^{(i)},$$
   *where $M_n^{(i)}$ is the $(n-1) \times (n-1)$ principal submatrix deleting row and column $i$. Whenever $\kappa \cdot w \ne 0$, $(K^{n+1}, M_{n+1}) \cong (M_n / \ker M_n) \oplus H$ over $K$.*

*Proof.* Part 1 follows from $p(-(w \pm 1))^2 + 2wp(-(w \pm 1)) + qp^2 = p(w^2 + 1 - 2w^2 + pq) = 0$. In Part 2, $H_2^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} -b & r \\ r & -a \end{pmatrix}$; subtracting $\begin{pmatrix} s & t \\ rs+at & rt+bs \end{pmatrix} H_2^{-1} \begin{pmatrix} s & rs+at \\ t & rt+bs \end{pmatrix}$ from $\begin{pmatrix} d & st+rd \\ st+rd & e \end{pmatrix}$ yields $(1,1)$-entry $d + bs^2 - 2rst + at^2 = d_-$, $(2,2)$-entry $d$ (after swapping the last two basis vectors or directly computing $(2,2)$-entry $d$ via $r^2 = 1+ab$), and $(1,2)$-entry $dr - st$, with $d d_- - (dr - st)^2 = -1$. For Part 3, replacing the $i$-th basis vector $e_i$ ($\kappa_i \ne 0$) by $\kappa = \sum_j \kappa_j e_j$ multiplies the basis transition matrix by $\kappa_i$, transforming $M_{n+1}$ into $\widetilde{M}_{n+1}$ with first row/column $(0, 0, \dots, 0, \kappa \cdot w)$ and central $(n-1) \times (n-1)$ block $M_n^{(i)}$; Laplace expansion gives $\kappa_i^2 \det M_{n+1} = \det \widetilde{M}_{n+1} = -(\kappa \cdot w)^2 \det M_n^{(i)}$, and if $\kappa \cdot w \ne 0$, the span of $\kappa$ and the $(n+1)$-st vector is non-degenerate with isotropic vector $\kappa$, hence isometric to $H$ with orthogonal complement $M_n^{(i)} \cong M_n / \ker M_n$. $\square$

**Corollary.** Every rank-4 witness 5-frame $M_5$ containing a regular-quadruple 4-subframe $G_4$ in a $\det G_4 = +1$ switching class satisfies $M_5 / \ker M_5 \cong H \oplus H$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, explaining why $\det M_5^{(i)} \in (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$ on every invertible 4-subframe of all $43$ stored rank-4 5-frames in [1]. Moreover, for any 6-tuple extension $\{a_1,\dots,a_5, f\}$ with non-zero sixth-row witnesses $w_j = \pm\sqrt{1 + a_j f}$ ($1 \le j \le 5$), fixing the $4$ switching-gauge signs $w_1 > 0$ (or fixing the first entry's row) leaves $2^4 = 16$ switching extensions of $M_5$; **if $\kappa \cdot w \ne 0$ holds across all $16$ sign choices** (which holds on `R1260`, `R1362`, `R1249`, and $S_{\mathrm{new}}$ by Theorem 2(2)), then all $16$ bordered 6-frame switching classes are isometric to $H \oplus H \oplus H$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

## 3. Procedure II Obstructions on Full-Support Frames and Almost-Septuple Census

Given a singular $n$-frame $M_n$ with one-dimensional kernel spanned by $\kappa$, Procedure II ([2, Supp. §3], [1, §4, §8]) tests whether a new rational entry $x$ with $w_i^2 = 1 + a_i x$ can satisfy the kernel-orthogonality relation $\sum_{i=1}^n \kappa_i w_i = 0$.

For completeness alongside our full-support results, we first recall from `Paper 120` [1, Lemma 3 and §8] that on any support-3 rank-4 5-frame ($\kappa = (1, 1, -1, 0, 0)^T$ on a regular triple $\{a, b, c = a+b+2r\}$), the half-switching norm of $w_a + w_b - w_c = 0$ reduces identically to $Q(x) = 4x^2 - 2(a+b+c)x - 3$, whose two roots $\{d, d'\}$ satisfy $dd' = -3/4$ and already lie in the $40$ support-3 stored 5-frames of [1]. Our new contributions address the full-support $(5, 4)$ frames and the $92$ canonical almost-septuples [6]:

**Theorem 2 (Full-Support Procedure II Obstructions and 92-Almost-Septuple Census).**
1. *Let $M_5$ be a full-support rank-4 witness 5-frame on $(a_1,\dots,a_5)$ with primitive integer kernel $\kappa = (\kappa_1,\dots,\kappa_5) \in (\mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\})^5$. The half-switching norm*
   $$N_{1/2}(x) := \prod_{\varepsilon \in \{+1\} \times \{\pm 1\}^4} \left(\sum_{j=1}^5 \varepsilon_j \kappa_j \sqrt{1 + a_j x}\right)$$
   *is a polynomial in $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ of degree at most $8$ vanishing at every rational solution of $\kappa \cdot w = 0$. On all four known rational sextuples containing a full-support $(5, 4)$ subframe (`R1362`, `R1249`, `R1260`, and $S_{\mathrm{new}}$), the primitive clearing of $N_{1/2}(x)$ has exact degree $8$ and is irreducible modulo $p = 149, 79, 137, 107$ respectively (explicit coefficients in Appendix A.1), hence irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$. In particular, $\kappa \cdot w \ne 0$ for every rational $x$ with $1 + a_j x \in \mathbb{Q}^2$, including the sixth entry of each sextuple.*
2. *Across all $92$ canonical rational $20/21$ almost-septuples compiled in [6] (`as_canonical.csv` in the supplement), every admissible 4-subtuple ($25 \times 92 = 2{,}300$ quadruples, $18{,}400$ switching 4-frames), 5-subtuple ($15 \times 92 = 1{,}380$ quintuples, $88{,}320$ switching 5-frames), and 6-subtuple ($2 \times 92 = 184$ sextuples, $188{,}416$ switching 6-frames; total $295{,}136$ switching frames) has full rank ($4$, $5$, and $6$ respectively).*

*Proof.* Since flipping $w_j \mapsto -w_j$ ($j \ge 2$) permutes the $16$ factors of $N_{1/2}(x)$ and flipping $w_1 \mapsto -w_1$ multiplies all $16$ factors by $-1$ ($16$ even), $N_{1/2}(x)$ is invariant under $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^5$ and lies in $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ of degree $\le 8$. In `verify_all.py`, the primitive integer polynomials in Appendix A.1 are certified irreducible in $\mathbb{F}_p[x]$ via Rabin's test ($\gcd(N_{1/2}, x^{p^k} - x) = 1$ for $k = 1,2,3,4$ and $x^{p^8} \equiv x \pmod{N_{1/2}, p}$) for $p = 149, 79, 137, 107$. Part 2 is verified exhaustively in `verify_all.py` on `as_canonical.csv` (fast modular elimination modulo $2^{31}-1$ with exact $\mathbb{Q}$ fallback on any modular zero), finding $(0, 0, 0)$ singular 4-, 5-, and 6-frames. $\square$

## 4. Split-2 Sextuple Surface on the $(4, 3)$ Threefold

In [1, §4], `R996` $= \{-2499/572, -1760/637, -2535/2156, -33/52\}$ is the unique stored rank-3 4-frame with full-support kernel $\kappa = [49 : 11 : 39 : 40]$; its two regular fifths $f_\pm$ complete it to a sextuple with two regular quadruples (split-2 configuration). Using the $(4, 3)$ threefold reconstruction of `Paper 121` [3], let
$$H(X, Y, Z, W) = X^4 + Y^4 + Z^4 + W^4 - X^2Y^2 - X^2Z^2 - X^2W^2 - Y^2Z^2 - Y^2W^2 - Z^2W^2 = H_{\mathrm{rt}}^2.$$
Away from the coordinate hyperplanes and Heron locus $XYZW H_{\mathrm{rt}} = 0$, the reconstructed 4-tuple entries are $a_i = N_i / (2XYZW H_{\mathrm{rt}})$, where $N_1 = -X^2 \Delta(Y, Z, W)$ (and cyclic permutations for $N_2, N_3, N_4$) with $\Delta(u, v, w) = 2(u^2v^2 + u^2w^2 + v^2w^2) - (u^4 + v^4 + w^4)$.

**Theorem 3 (Split-2 Sextuple Surface, Geometric Monodromy, and $B = 750$ Census).**
1. *Define the homogeneous degree-$4$ polynomials*
   $$\widetilde{B}_{14} = H - (X^2 - W^2)^2 + Y^2Z^2, \qquad \widetilde{B}_{23} = H + X^2W^2 - (Y^2 - Z^2)^2.$$
   *On $H(X, Y, Z, W) = H_{\mathrm{rt}}^2$ outside $XYZW H_{\mathrm{rt}} = 0$ and $a_1a_2a_3a_4 = 1$, the witness entries are $M_{14} = \widetilde{B}_{14}/(2YZ H_{\mathrm{rt}})$ and $M_{23} = \widetilde{B}_{23}/(2XW H_{\mathrm{rt}})$, and the split-2 condition $(a_1a_2a_3a_4 - 1)^2 = 4(1 + a_1a_4)(1 + a_2a_3)$ (under which both regular fifths $f_\pm$ satisfy $1 + f_+ f_- \in \mathbb{Q}^2$ and $R_4(a_1,a_4,f_+,f_-) = R_4(a_2,a_3,f_+,f_-) = 0$) is cut out by the four sign branches*
   $$\widetilde{B}_{14}\widetilde{B}_{23} = 2 H_{\mathrm{rt}}\bigl(\varepsilon_1 XW \widetilde{B}_{14} + \varepsilon_2 YZ \widetilde{B}_{23}\bigr), \qquad \varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2 \in \{\pm 1\}.$$
2. *On the rational surface $M(s, r)$ of [3] ($X/W = s$, $Z/W = r$, $Y/W = (s^2 - r^2)/2$, $H_{\mathrm{rt}}/W^2 = 1 - y + y^2 - r^2$ where $y = Y/W$), write $u = s + r$, $v = s - r$ (so $y = uv/2$, with excluded degenerate locus $uv(u^2 - v^2)(1 - y + y^2 - r^2) = 0$ where $X, Y, Z$ or $H_{\mathrm{rt}}$ vanishes). Direct substitution into the $(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2) = (-1, +1)$ branch (`R996` at $(u, v) = (11/5, 1/4)$) yields the exact polynomial identity*
   $$\widetilde{B}_{14}\widetilde{B}_{23} - 2 H_{\mathrm{rt}}(-s\widetilde{B}_{14} + yr\widetilde{B}_{23}) = -\frac{1}{64} u v\, C_{-+}(u, v),$$
   *where $C_{-+}(u, v)$ has bidegree $(6, 6)$ (displayed in Appendix A.2). The curve $C_{-+}(u, v) = 0$ is **geometrically irreducible** over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ with arithmetic monodromy $\operatorname{Gal}_{\mathbb{Q}(u)}(C_{-+}) \cong S_6$, geometric monodromy containing $A_6$, and normalization of geometric genus $g \ge 12$.*
3. *Under the $8$-element pairing group $D_4 = \langle (1\,4), (2\,3), (1\,2)(3\,4) \rangle \le S_4$ and coordinate sign flips, every projective point $[X:Y:Z:W]$ with $XYZW \ne 0$ has a canonical representative in $X \ge Z \ge Y \ge 1$, $w_{\min} \le W \le X$ ($w_{\min} = Y$ if $X = Z$ else $1$). An exhaustive C/OpenMP search (`search_s16_sextuples.c`) up to $B = 750$ checks all $287{,}527{,}770$ primitive $H = \square$ points and finds `[49 : 11 : 39 : 40]` (`R996`) as the unique split-2 solution (`0` degenerate hits, `1` non-degenerate sextuple).*

*Proof.*
1. Putting $a_1a_2a_3a_4 - 1$ and $\pm 2 M_{14} M_{23}$ over the common denominator $16 X^4 Y^4 Z^4 W^4 H^2$ gives $N_1 N_2 N_3 N_4 - 16 X^4 Y^4 Z^4 W^4 H^2 \mp 8 X^3 Y^3 Z^3 W^3 H \widetilde{B}_{14} \widetilde{B}_{23}$, which vanishes identically modulo $(\widetilde{B}_{14}\widetilde{B}_{23})^2 - 4 H (XW\widetilde{B}_{14} \mp YZ\widetilde{B}_{23})^2$ in $\mathbb{Q}[X, Y, Z, W]$.
2. The $-\frac{1}{64} u v\, C_{-+}(u, v)$ pullback identity has bidegree $(7, 7)$ and is verified over an $8 \times 8$ rational grid in `verify_all.py`. At $u = 3$, $C_{-+}(3, v) = 768v^6 - 2400v^5 - 592v^4 + 3720v^3 + 470v^2 - 780v - 250$ is squarefree modulo $29, 23, 19$ with degree partitions $[6]$ mod $29$ ($6$-cycle), $[1, 5]$ mod $23$ ($5$-cycle), and $[1, 2, 3]$ mod $19$ (cubing a Frobenius element $\sigma$ of cycle type $(1, 2, 3)$ kills the $3$-cycle and leaves a pure transposition $\sigma^3$). A subgroup of $S_6$ containing a $6$-cycle (transitive), a $5$-cycle (hence primitive of degree $6$), and a transposition equals $S_6$, so $\operatorname{Gal}_{\mathbb{Q}(u)}(C_{-+}) \cong S_6$ and $C_{-+}$ is irreducible in $\mathbb{Q}(u)[v]$ (and primitive in $\mathbb{Q}[u][v]$ since $\gcd_v$ of its $u$-coefficients is $1$). Let $G_{\mathrm{geom}} := \operatorname{Gal}_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}(u)}(C_{-+}) \trianglelefteq \operatorname{Gal}_{\mathbb{Q}(u)}(C_{-+}) \cong S_6$ be the geometric monodromy group. Notice $\operatorname{disc}_v(C_{-+}) = 82944\, u^5 (u-1)^4 (u+1)^4 (u-2)^4 (u+2)^4 D_{34}(u)$, where $D_{34}(u) \in \mathbb{Z}[u]$ has degree $34$ and is squarefree modulo $11$ (`verify_all.py`), with $\gcd(D_{34}(u), u(u^2-1)(u^2-4)) = 1$ and leading $v^6$-coefficient $u(u^2-1)(u^2-4)(u+1)$ coprime to $D_{34}(u)$. At each of the $34$ distinct roots $\alpha \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ of $D_{34}(u)$, the leading coefficient of $C_{-+}(\alpha, v)$ is non-zero and $\operatorname{ord}_{u=\alpha}(\operatorname{disc}_v C_{-+}) = 1$. In characteristic $0$, a monic polynomial over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}\llbracket u - \alpha \rrbracket$ whose discriminant has valuation $1$ cannot split into unramified factors over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}\llbracket u - \alpha \rrbracket$ (since $\operatorname{ord}\operatorname{disc}(\prod g_j) = \sum \operatorname{ord}\operatorname{disc}(g_j) + 2\sum_{j<k}\operatorname{ord}\operatorname{Res}(g_j, g_k)$ would be even if every $g_j$ had odd-free ramification, i.e., if no root pair branched with ramification index $e = 2$!). Precisely, valuation $1$ forces $C_{-+}$ over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}((u-\alpha))$ to factor into four simple linear branches ($e=1$) and one ramified quadratic branch ($e=2$), so inertia at $u = \alpha$ acts on the $6$ sheets of the normalization $\widetilde{C}_{-+} \to \mathbb{P}^1_u$ as a transposition ($\sum_{P \mid \alpha} (e_P - 1) = 1$). ## 7. Complete $(l, s)$ Fundamental-Domain Census and $S_{\mathrm{new}}$

### Justification of the 8 excluded curves in the $(l, s)$-census

On $Q_4(S, r; u) = 0$, the regular third $c = S + 2r$ vanishes if and only if $S = -2r$, i.e., $Q_4(-2r, r; u) = 0$. Pulling $Q_4(-2r, r(l,s); u(l,s)) \cdot ((l^2+2)(s^2+1) + 4(2l+1)s)^4$ back to $(l, s)$ yields the **exact polynomial factorization** (certified in `verify_all.py`):
$$-256(l + 3s - 1)(ls - s + 3)(l + s + 1)(ls + s + 1)(ls + 2l + 5s + 1)(2ls + l + s + 5)(ls - 2l - s - 1)(2ls - l + s + 1).$$
These $8$ factors are **precisely the $8$ curves $C_1, C_1', C_2, C_2', C_3, C_3', C_4, C_4'$ excluded in `search_ls_54frames.c`**! Under $V_4 = \langle l \mapsto \frac{4-l}{2l+1},\; s \mapsto 1/s \rangle$, they form two $4$-curve orbits ($C_1: l = 1 - 3s$ with $C_1', C_3, C_3'$, and $C_2: l = -(1 + s)$ with $C_2', C_4, C_4'$). Along both orbits, $N_{\mathrm{hom}}(l, s)$ is identically a square: one twin root $S_\varepsilon = -2r$ degenerates ($c = 0$), while the other twin root $S_{-\varepsilon}$ produces $\mathcal{F}_1(s)$ on $C_1$ and its Möbius reparameterization $\mathcal{F}_1((2 - s)/3)$ on $C_2$.
