# Accept

This is a sound, reproducible partial advance on the specified Earth–Moon construction problem. The contribution is a uniform list-extension theorem for cyclic selected-center systems, together with a composition rule permitting both matching and selected edges to connect different blocks. The simultaneous three-element selected-port lists are substantive: they allow the selected graph itself to become connected while preserving solvability across an arbitrary finite network. The result excludes a precisely defined construction family rather than merely reporting unsuccessful searches.

The central proof is convincing. The transfer transitions correctly account for the matching sign and the three quadratic factors. For the block orders used in the theorem, closed walks record exactly the cyclic coefficient contributions; discarding excessive pending powers cannot discard a term within the target bounds. The prefix degrees and endpoint exponents match the claimed list sizes. Equality of successive word spaces establishes stability under the same linear-image operation, so the extension to every larger gap is justified. Finally, the compatible rank-one witnesses give

$$
\operatorname{tr}(E_{d_1}\cdots E_{d_r})=1,
$$

forcing an odd integer coefficient for every admissible sequence. Applying the real Combinatorial Nullstellensatz through the imported divided-cubic encoding is valid: parity certifies a nonzero integer coefficient, while the coloring argument takes place over the reals.

I inspected and ran all three supplied checkers on working copies using Python 3.11. The main replay reconstructed the transfers, verified both subspace containments, reproduced dimensions $159,65,48,96$, reconstructed all four witnesses, and checked all sixteen pairings. The separate original-factor calculation reproduced $15,-49,897,23,739,13445$ and agreed with signed transfer multiplication. The literal control verified the stated 352 vertices, 528 selected edges, 176 matching edges, connected selected graph, endpoint lists, and preservation of earlier colors. These are reproduced certificate checks; the six coefficients and one network control are corroboration, not an empirical substitute for the unbounded proof.

The external-rewiring argument also holds. An already colored matching neighbor excludes one color; an already colored selected neighbor either imposes no restriction or leaves exactly its color together with the two palette colors. Disjoint port endpoints prevent accumulated restrictions at one vertex. Retaining deleted local relations as additional sufficient constraints is legitimate, and the stated detours justify connectivity after removing selected ports.

The novelty claim is appropriately scoped against Paper 50 v6 [1]. Its matching-containing, one-exception, endpoint-capacity, and doubled-matching results do not supply this joint boundary theorem. In particular, the present simple cubic blocks have every actual matching pair exceptional; the direct-edge formula in its Theorem 31 gives $a_i=1+2\cdot3=7$, outside the required bound. Papers 45 v1 and 49 v2 impose different host or light-component conditions and do not establish these universal mixed-port lists. The external literature check, including Dvořák’s paper [4] (arXiv:2301.06571), supports the manuscript’s distinction between established polynomial coefficient methods and the new explicit compatible witnesses. I found no substantial duplication in the relevant material examined.

The connection to the task is conditional but substantive through [1, Proposition 26]. The cover must admit the specified center selection, and its six remaining host fibers must induce a graph with no $K_6$ component. Neither biplanar occurrence nor arbitrary simple-cubic extension is proved. Acceptance therefore does not establish the problem’s solve definition.

Two minor clarifications would improve the paper. Identify the provenance and precise statement of the otherwise unspecified “matching-only port theorem” mentioned near the end. Also briefly define $G_{25}$ and “ordinary cover,” and describe the six remaining host fibers when stating the cover consequence, so readers can understand the application without first consulting [1]. The abstract is informative, and these clarifications do not affect the validity of the stated results.
