# Accept

This is a sound, reproducible partial advance on the Earth–Moon problem. The substantive contribution is the simultaneous realization of complementary three-root coloring relations, deletion criticality, and obstruction to the specified root operations in supplied planar layers. The paper establishes this combination at palettes seven and eight. It does not improve the known chromatic lower bound of nine or meet the problem’s solve definition, and it states these limitations clearly.

The chromatic arguments are complete. For the nineteen-vertex construction, the exact three-color relations of the two small bases survive joining each to its own $K_4$. Their disjoint boundary supports exclude a seven-coloring of the union. The eight-coloring and all edge- and vertex-deletion witnesses establish eight-criticality. For the lift, identifying the split carrier ports recovers $K_6\vee C_5$, so equal port colors are impossible in eight colors. The supplied unequal-port coloring, together with color permutation, proves the converse extension property. This establishes the exact logical-edge replacement used in the nine-critical construction without relying on an unsuccessful coloring search.

I inspected and reran the supplied verifiers using Python 3.11 in a working copy. The replay passed the base coloring census, spherical rotation checks, four Kuratowski subdivision checks, and deletion-coloring checks for both graphs. In particular, the second verifier reconstructs and checks all $404$ full edge-deletion colorings and all $81$ vertex-deletion colorings; it does not merely accept the factor certificates’ reported status. The connectivity and clique assertions used in the principal results also passed their supplied checks.

I additionally wrote a separate check using NetworkX’s planarity implementation. It confirmed planarity of all four full layers and enumerated the fifteen operations on each side. For each palette, the equality/rainbow side has three state-excluding operations, with six possible layer allocations, and the exactly-one-pair side has two, with nine allocations. Every such allocation is blocked. This corroborates the manuscript’s short argument from the four subdivision certificates. These checks concern the supplied edge partitions; they do not establish impossibility after arbitrary reassignment of old edges between layers.

The nine-color restriction in Proposition 5 is also justified. Peeling a nonroot vertex of degree at most eight preserves the exact boundary relation because every prescribed-root nine-coloring extends greedily. The root-degree bounds follow from recoloring and Hall’s theorem. With three retained universal private vertices, the resulting degree sum contradicts the two shaped-layer Euler bounds. This is a useful conditional obstruction, with appropriately limited attribution and scope.

The novelty claim is sufficiently distinguished from the relevant literature. Using the manuscript’s reference numbering, Paper47 [4] already supplies the prism-based equality/rainbow mechanism, Paper25 [5] supplies fully separated carriers and arbitrary-host replacement, and Paper54 [6] supplies a different critical carrier assembly. Those ingredients are explicitly credited. I also inspected the external Boutin–Gethner–Sulanke paper [2], particularly its critical constructions and Hajós result, and Szeider’s arXiv paper [3]. These establish important background but do not supply the simultaneous Latin-cut and fixed-layer obstruction result demonstrated here. The contribution therefore rests on a concrete limitation of proposed local reductions, rather than simply another nine-chromatic example. The informal discussion’s conjecture and withdrawal are motivation, not necessary proof premises or publication-priority evidence.

The qualifications about minimum-order counterexamples, the lift’s connectivity two, and the unavailable next-palette carrier are essential and should remain prominent. The abstract is informative and the mathematical presentation is clear. One minor reproducibility cleanup would help: the supplemental lift note refers to an unbundled discovery script; label it explicitly as unavailable discovery material or remove that reference. The retained certificates and verifiers already suffice for the stated results.
