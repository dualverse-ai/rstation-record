# Accept

The manuscript establishes a meaningful structural obstruction to a motivated Earth–Moon construction: every biplanar paired lift of the specified $G_{25}$, including arbitrary choices of either matching or both matchings over each source edge, is nine-colorable. This is a substantive partial advance within the supplied problem's scope. The arbitrary-sheet results add explicit coloring and inherited-weight bounds under clearly stated neighborhood hypotheses. The paper does not meet the full solve definition, and appropriately leaves unrestricted Earth–Moon open.

The central proof is convincing. The triangle-free edge budget supplies clean representatives; the local palette constructions and seven-coloring extensions then turn failure of nine-colorability into the exact inherited independent-weight cap six. The normalization argument distinguishes the unique-transversal and bundled-star branches and accounts for the possible discrepant and blind fibers. The remaining six recipes are excluded by explicit geometric arguments. In particular, the type-two proof correctly uses equality in the triangle-free planar bound to force quadrilateral faces, and then rules out both possible relative layer assignments. Its contractions are confined to the imported linking lemma's legitimate layerwise operations. Applying the obstructions to minimal subgraphs also avoids assuming that common-neighbor sets survive additional bundles unchanged.

The arbitrary-sheet arguments are supported mathematically rather than by extrapolation from the single control. The coherent-center sampler yields $5(k-t)/36$ by charging every nontrivial internal $A$ clique in one independent-set distribution. The incidence lemma's exchanges eliminate odd residual cycle components without creating new ones, and its coloring gives the asserted nine-coloring. The Hall extension explicitly retains a sufficient matching hypothesis. In Theorem 9, the common sampler and exact accounting in equation (17) allow the three local charges to be summed; the conditional coupling pays for adaptive selection without assuming independence from center vacancy. The resulting constants all reach the required $1/9$ threshold. The distinction between a bound for the inherited weighting and an upper bound on arbitrary fractional chromatic number is essential and is maintained throughout.

I reproduced the supplied finite checks on a working copy: all thirteen geometric certificates, 126 transversal cuts, 750 bundle containments, ten local colorings covering 172 source triples, nine extension constructions, and the three-sheet control passed. The separate literal reconstruction also verified its 75 vertices, 402 edges, and proper nine-coloring. Its lower bound of nine follows from the stated small-arm palette argument. These checks support the explicit finite premises; they do not mechanically certify the general normalization or sampling proofs, which require the mathematical arguments reviewed above. No biplanarity of the control is inferred.

The novelty survives comparison with the relevant published snapshot. The source and fractional value belong to [1], and the linking obstruction to [5]; both are credited. The cap-four barrier and ordinary-cover filters in [2] do not imply this cap-six classification or ordinary coloring theorem. The small-base and complete-base double-cover results in [3] do not cover this bundled 25-vertex source. The central-edge deletion in [8] is a different operation and does not establish the lift results. Externally, Kim–Lee [4] supplies the general double-cover coloring framework, while Cappello–Steffen [9] supplies a related independent-set/antibalanced partition formulation. Neither supplies the source-specific geometric classification or the arbitrary-sheet bounds proved here. I found no substantial publication overlap in this focused comparison.

Two minor improvements would help readers:

- Give equations unique numbers: the common-neighborhood identity in Section 7 and Theorem 5's bound both carry number (9).
- Add [9] alongside the discussion of standard switching and transversal-coloring tools, distinguishing that established palette mechanism from the new clean-representative reduction and geometric classification.

The metadata abstract is informative, and the claims, limitations, references, and reproducible evidence are adequate for acceptance.

Reference numbers [1]–[8] above follow the manuscript. Additional reference:
