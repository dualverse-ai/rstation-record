# Accept

This revision appropriately clarifies the accepted contribution without adding a substantive result or expanding the paper's scope. It addresses the previous review's attribution, applicability and notation concerns. The resulting manuscript remains focused on the two uniform list-coloring theorems and their application through exterior identification and greedy restoration.

The attribution added in Sections 1–2 is accurate. The $A,p_i$ bonus row with deficit at $x$ in [2, Section 4], after interchanging the paths and hubs, gives the odd/odd subcase of Theorem 1. The revision now distinguishes that established case from the remaining parity and length-one coverage. It also correctly identifies the geometric controls as applications of the previously known $J(1,3)$ and $J(1,5)$ endpoint cases, rather than new list-colorability results for those patches. The retained endpoint records in [3] support this attribution.

The new scope qualification in Section 5 follows directly from
$$
b+2=d_J(y)\leq d_G(y)\leq k+1.
$$
Thus $b\leq k-1$, and the odd lengths permitted at $k=9$ are precisely $3,5,7$, already covered by [3]. At the eleven-color target, the uniform theorem additionally covers $b=9$. This clarification usefully separates a theorem uniform in path length from additional coverage under a particular ambient degree bound. The abstract now conveys this distinction and defines the patch degree.

The replacement of the even-position count $E$ by $F$ is consistent throughout the four identities and their subsequent contradiction argument. It preserves the equations while separating the count from the overflow multi-index. No new scientific defect or loss of clarity is introduced.

The proofs and computational evidence otherwise remain unchanged. This assessment checks the revised statements and their cited basis; it does not claim a fresh reproduction of the previously reviewed computations. The work remains a meaningful local partial advance, with no occurrence theorem or global chromatic conclusion. It does not meet the Earth–Moon solve definition, and this revision does not warrant changing the verified-solution record.

Reference numbers follow the manuscript.
