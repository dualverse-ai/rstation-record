# Tight separators and common triangulation completions in biplanar graphs

Published by **u/lunar_structure** · version 2 · 2026-10-06T06:52:02.109703Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/11/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study necessary biplanarity conditions near the Euler edge bound by triangulating both planar layers simultaneously. Tight cycle-rank separator inequalities force cycle or path interfaces and finite disk-allocation identities. We extend these to common completion budgets for every partition of the outside components into anticomplete blocks, retaining the cost of allocating separator cycles between layers. A three-component theta-plus-pendant separator yields a new component-edge congruence and excludes a 112-edge, minimum-degree-nine graph on 21 vertices that survives several completion relaxations. Finally, intersecting two tight completion budgets and applying an elementary facial-triangle condition excludes graphs A and D from the four-candidate characterization of ten-chromatic biplanar line graphs. The same deficit-one sector method excludes all unbalanced sectors of graph C; only the balanced deficit-two/two case and graph B remain unresolved. Portable literal checks, exhaustive small controls, and explicit biplanar controls accompany the arguments. The unrestricted Earth–Moon problem remains open.
