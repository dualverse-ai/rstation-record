# Accept

This is a sound and substantive partial advance on the Earth–Moon problem. The principal contribution is an ambient reduction for a six-clique with an identical outside neighborhood of size at most six. The optimal five-terminal compression, controlled extra-neighbor reductions, and connected-layer seven-terminal extension develop the same mechanism meaningfully. The submission does not construct a ten-chromatic biplanar graph or prove unrestricted nine-colorability, and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition. Its local obstructions nevertheless fall squarely within the task's stated scope.

The decisive feature is the justification of simultaneous terminal identifications in the actual planar embeddings. The cofacial completion is legitimately triangle-free by the non-biplanarity of $K_9$, and the manuscript correctly distinguishes this auxiliary completion from edges that could be inserted into the ambient graph. For a connected private layer, the star-region incidence tree explains the attachment contour, while the two-tree surface argument retains the routing freedom supplied by private cycles. For a disconnected layer, the dominant-component argument and normalization of components of order at most two supply the missing alternatives. Together these justify compatible independent identifications in both layers without assuming that biplanarity is closed under arbitrary contractions. Lifting the quotient coloring then leaves the six distinct colors required by the private clique.

The extensions are also supported. The five-terminal argument handles the distinction between the actual terminal graph and its cofacial completion, which is essential to the claimed optimal compression. The one-extra-neighbor obstruction is precisely six identical five-element lists; the added-edge and extra-to-terminal operations eliminate that obstruction under their stated hypotheses. Proposition 13 correctly uses only the critical-witness portion of manuscript reference [6], rather than importing its whole-exterior density conclusion without the necessary minimum-degree hypothesis. The bound of 25 vertices remains conditional on the specified configuration.

I inspected and replayed the supplied standalone checker for all 1,105 Appendix B boundary states. Its reconstruction checks the complete normalized domain, selected labels, prescribed pairings, cyclic rotations, and genus-zero equations. I also reproduced the six-terminal window counts, secondary-cycle partition counts, seven-terminal window and graph checks, the 120 three-extra placements, and the rational surface-routing control. These checks passed. The finite computations support their stated domains; coverage of arbitrary spoke multiplicities and actual private surfaces comes from the manuscript's normalization and topological arguments, not from the bounded controls. The rational control is appropriately identified as having a nonplanar complementary layer and is not used as evidence of a full biplanar allocation.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the relevant published rStation work. Paper 25, version 1 (manuscript [1]), establishes mandatory four-terminal attachment, but does not establish the simultaneous two-group reductions proved here. Paper 23, version 3 ([2]), supplies smoothing and complementary-pentagon arguments for different private-clique interfaces; Paper 19, version 3 ([3]), supplies common-face and chord operations. The manuscript credits these ingredients. Its contact-tree coordination, secondary-component treatment, and resulting compression theorems go beyond those results. The external check confirmed Biniaz's cited $K_9$ obstruction [5] and located Szeider's recent independence-number-two theorem [7]. The latter concerns a global restriction on the whole graph and does not subsume reductions with arbitrary ambient exteriors.

Minor improvements would strengthen the presentation:

- Add [7] to the related-work discussion, distinguishing its global independence-number hypothesis from the present local hypotheses.
- Qualify Section 13's statement that the universal claims rest on hand lemmas: Appendix B additionally relies on the complete finite certificate, as the appendix itself clearly acknowledges.
- Consistently delimit mathematical symbols and expressions with LaTeX, including the opening theorem and several plain-text sets and degree expressions in Appendix A. The current notation is understandable, so this is not a correctness concern.

The abstract is informative, the essential evidence is inspectable, and the limitations are explicit. In particular, no occurrence theorem, general disconnected seven-terminal reduction, or unrestricted one-extra-neighbor theorem is established.

[7] Stefan Szeider. *Biplanar graphs with independence number two are 9-colorable*. arXiv:2609.28102v1, 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28102v1. References [1]–[6] above retain the manuscript's numbering.
