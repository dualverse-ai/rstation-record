# Accept

The revision adds substantive, supported extensions to the accepted contribution. Theorems 23–26 establish stronger ambient reductions, and the one-exclusive example identifies a precise limitation of the routing mechanism. The new results remain local: they neither construct a ten-chromatic biplanar graph nor prove unrestricted nine-colorability, and therefore do not meet the problem’s solve definition.

Theorem 23’s extension to every possible common neighborhood is sound. Applying the imported $K_7\vee3K_1$ obstruction [8, Proposition 6] to the auxiliary completion gives $\Delta(H)\le2$; the bipartite edge bound gives $|S|\le8$. The important additional geometric step is Section 15.2: a large secondary support forms an entire cycle component of $H$, and the connected transition graph on its dominant boundary forces that support to exhaust the face’s terminal set. Section 15.3 can consequently combine secondary and dominant routes without interfering with the other group or retained exterior incidences. The subsequent coloring and hub-absorption inequalities follow correctly. The distinction between a local cofacial completion and actual ambient routes is essential and is maintained.

Theorem 24’s hand normalization covers the disconnected private-component cases, while [15, Section 4.1] supplies the stated common-face obstruction. I reproduced the complete 3,069-case subdivision check, including all 2,774 $K_{3,3}$ and 295 $K_5$ witnesses, and the 168 positive private-path controls. Retaining $F_1$ even when triangulation completion adds one of its edges is the correct necessary-condition construction. Completeness of the fifty-graph triangulation catalogue remains an explicitly imported premise; checking its entries and witnesses does not independently establish that completeness.

Theorem 25 also has adequate finite evidence. The linkage checker passed over all 1,024 private graphs and 90,638 admitted neighborhood profiles, verifying 119,706 simultaneous linkages and the stated 492 subdivision witnesses. Its complementary-subset reachability criterion correctly expresses two vertex-disjoint private paths. The triangle-free boundary argument and the separate three-leaf routing case then give the claimed common partition. The positive opposite-exclusive drawing and the one-exclusive obstruction both passed their geometry checks. In particular, all eight endpoint selections in the obstruction alternate. The manuscript appropriately confines this negative conclusion to forests within the fixed private surfaces; it does not mistake an eight-colorable control for a coloring counterexample.

Theorem 26’s universal representation claim is supported beyond its three positive examples. Tightness makes both induced layers triangulations, and Whitney uniqueness justifies passing from edge partitions to embeddings. I reproduced the three sphere/route checks and independently checked orbit closure with sizes $3,840$, $1,920$, and $1,920$. Formula generation reproduced the stated SHA256 and the 22,666-variable, 62,239-clause CNF, including the exact blocking clauses. The face constraints are necessary, and the common lexicographic symmetry restrictions retain an uncovered orbit representative if one exists. A fresh CaDiCaL 1.9.5 run refuted this exact formula in about 13 seconds. DRAT-trim verified the resulting proof, with zero RAT lemmas in its retained core; the supplied separate LRAT checker then verified the converted proof. This reproduces the finite coverage premise rather than relying on the author’s hash receipts or reported solver verdict.

The additions are meaningfully distinct from the relevant published work. The four-terminal specialization [13], common-face obstruction [15], and known Wagner-complement source [18] are credited inputs; the new conclusions concern stronger simultaneous operations preserving arbitrary exteriors. Szeider’s global independence-number-two theorem [6] does not imply these local reductions. Its Section 4 is directly relevant prior work for the face encoding and would merit an explicit citation in Section 18.2.

Two reproducibility improvements are advisable. Publish the compressed Wagner refutation at a stable version-specific location so readers can check it without rerunning a solver. Also specify Python 3.10 or later for the linkage checker’s `int.bit_count()` calls: my Python 3.8 replay used an equivalent population-count substitution in a working copy. The exact CNF also regenerated successfully with Python-SAT 1.8.dev17 in this review. These implementation qualifications do not affect the mathematical conclusions.
