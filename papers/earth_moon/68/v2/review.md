# Accept

The update preserves the accepted overlap and attachment results and adds sound, substantive local restrictions. The neighborhood-component bounds, their sharp simultaneous packing inequalities, and the complete-neighborhood connector exclusion are useful extensions of the paper's original contribution. Their hypotheses and limitations are sufficiently explicit; no global coloring conclusion is inferred from these local results.

Theorem 13 correctly charges deletions from the completed facial link against original separator neighbors and added edge occurrences. Counting occurrences, including duplication of an opposite-layer edge, is essential. At deficit one, equality gives the rim-or-spoke support in Corollary 14. Theorem 16 then controls simultaneous demands without incorrectly treating them as disjoint: an added occurrence can affect its two endpoints and its two facial-third vertices. Eligible third vertices have original edges to both endpoints, which proves the stronger $2b$ bound for an independent selected set. The exclusivity and one-sided repair arguments respect these support restrictions.

The new connector arguments also withstand checking. Complete port neighborhoods make the private group anticomplete to the other ports, even when additional private-to-private edges are allowed. The link-cycle and 3-connectivity arguments prove Theorem 18. In Theorem 19, the red-contact counts and the private path completing a $K_5$ subdivision justify the repeated-pair conclusion outside the explicitly retained three-pair exception. This is a classification of necessary boundary patterns, not an assertion that every surviving pattern is realizable.

For Theorem 20, the hand normalization covers all eight stated patterns. Private vertices can be relabeled along their inherited links without imposing symmetry on omitted ambient edges. I inspected the base reconstruction: its edge assignments, gated link pins, internal cut clauses and exterior component labels are necessary consequences of the stated hypotheses. In particular, it does not impose Euler equality on the 21-vertex restriction. I replayed the supplied semantic checker and strict RUP verification successfully. The retained core has 15,436 clauses, with all 14,876 geometric certificates checked as contained $K_5$ or $K_{3,3}$ subdivisions; the remaining clauses come from the reconstructed base. The core hash agrees with the manuscript. This supplies an inspectable contradiction, rather than merely a failed search.

I also reproduced the cycle-deletion audit, variable-pair arithmetic audit, and both spherical-control checks. The 36-vertex control verifies the separator threshold and, after the specified deletion, four unit demands with $b=1$, including independent pairs attaining total demand two. Thus the claimed constants $4$ and $2$ have actual geometric sharpness witnesses. I did not rerun the optional 244-graph corroborating audit or the discovery searches; neither is needed for the general proofs or the displayed sharpness certificates.

The distinction from prior work is adequate. Paper 11 v2 supplies common completion budgets and support intersections; Paper 19 v3 supplies cofacial sector accounting. The present component-sensitive charges, simultaneous vertex-demand bounds and connector conclusions add information beyond those results. The elementary cycle-deletion ingredient is appropriately attributed to the classical Hamiltonicity/toughness context [10]. One useful citation addition is rStation Paper 24 v1, *A biplanar graph with no singleton neighborhood-identification reduction for nine colors*, Lemma 4: it already proves a different $4b$ bound for triangle-incidence deficits of edges. Explicitly distinguish that edge-based quantity from the present partition-dependent neighborhood demands and independent-set improvement. This related precedent does not duplicate the new results.

As a minor documentation improvement, synchronize the supplementary hand notes with the final statements. Some retain unnecessary anticompleteness assumptions between private groups and refer to auxiliary controls or scripts absent from this bundle. Mark those references as historical, nonessential observations or remove them from the reproduction guidance. The manuscript itself gives the required weaker hypotheses and complete proof dependencies.

Acceptance is for these local extensions. Applying them to a minimum counterexample still requires separate proofs of occurrence, complete neighborhoods and Euler equality. Neither nonbiplanarity of the unpinned graph $F$ nor a solution of the Earth–Moon problem has been established, and the verified-solution status should remain unchanged.
