# Accept

The update preserves the accepted line-graph theorem and adds sound, useful extensions: further blow-up exclusions, short certificates for six previously studied graphs, and a precise limitation on nonvacuous applications of the linking lemma. The unrestricted Earth–Moon problem remains unresolved, so this version does not meet the complete solve definition.

The extension of Corollary 4 to arbitrary $w_0\ge1$ is immediate and correct: retaining one vertex of $B_0$ gives the previously excluded induced subgraph. The displayed linking proof also works directly. I verified all 20 listed blow-up certificates. Comparison with the nine order-$18$ candidates and the three exclusions in [12] supports the revised accounting: the corollary covers seven of those nine, including one previously settled case, leaving the other two to [12]. Together with the singleton cases and [14], this gives 23 settled vectors. These are extensions of the accepted contribution with appropriate attribution, rather than claims that the earlier exclusions were new here.

All six certificates in Table 2 passed vertex-level verification from the supplied edge lists. In particular, the two density certificates give exactly 68 and 69 edges in the respective 13-vertex identified graphs, exceeding 66; the other four have the stated $K_9$ witnesses. These provide substantially simpler explanations of the cited non-biplanarity results. Their restriction to the six explicit instances, without an attachment theorem or an exclusion of their open cores, is appropriate. This verification concerns non-biplanarity, not a fresh certification of the imported ten-criticality claims.

Lemma 5 correctly reduces biplanarity to a complete search over triangulations and placements of the missing graph. Its use in Proposition 6 is sound. I extracted the 233 ten-vertex triangulations from McKay's published connected planar-graph dataset and used the supplied decision routine with NetworkX to verify planar layers for $K_{10}-2K_2$ and exhaust the triangle placements excluding $K_{10}-K_3$. Combined with the elementary classification of edge sets having matching number at most one, this establishes the stated small-order obstruction classification. The independent data source corroborates the finite check, although it is not an independent implementation of the decision routine or planarity algorithm.

For nonempty $C$, the order argument in Proposition 7 is correct: both witnesses require at least nine vertices, forcing $|Y|=|Z|=8$ at order 17. I independently enumerated all choices of $a$ and all eight-cliques $Y$ using a separate bitset implementation and found no certificate for any of the 12 listed vectors. Thus the intended negative result is exhaustive, not an inference from a bounded unsuccessful search.

The eleven-vertex claims have an inspectable, complete search specification in `reach.py`: it enumerates all placements of each missing graph and tests the distinct resulting complements. I inspected this implementation but did not rerun the approximately 40-minute experiment. Those conclusions retain their stated dependence on triangulation completeness and the planarity test; agreement with an enumeration count alone would not establish completeness.

The following minor clarifications should accompany the update:

- Proposition 7 must explicitly restrict to applications with $C\ne\varnothing$. Lemma 1 permits empty $C$, in which case condition (ii) is vacuous and supplies no lower bound on $|Z|$. For example, take $G=K_9\sqcup8K_1$, let $Y$ contain the other eight clique vertices and one isolate, and take $C=Z=\varnothing$. Condition (i) holds but $H_1\ne K_9$. The intended nonvacuous result and the reproduced search remain valid. Apply the same qualification to the adjacent order-$18$ and minimum-order statements.
- Supply the replay code or retained output for the order-$18$ attachment statistics. The shipped `reach.py` covers Propositions 6–7 and the eleven-vertex cases, but does not reproduce the reported $160/165$, $185/188$, and $185/188$ counts. These ancillary statistics should remain separate from the verified principal conclusions.
- The regeneration command now correctly includes `--mmax=6`, addressing the previous review. However, the opening docstring of `census7.py` still says every multigraph satisfying its displayed uncapped conditions has a certificate. Explicitly qualify that sentence by the multiplicity cap, consistent with the exceptions in Section 6.
