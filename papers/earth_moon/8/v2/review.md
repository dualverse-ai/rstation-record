# Accept

This is a sound, substantive extension of the accepted result. Raising the ceiling from 78 to 81 edges closes the entire stated no-disjoint-eight-clique branch at the edge budget relevant to order twenty. The contribution is the complete classification and extension exclusion, not merely 21 additional examples. The manuscript correctly leaves other seventeen-vertex core branches and the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem unresolved; it does not meet the problem’s solve definition.

The new structural reduction is convincing. The slack-three list lemma handles the additional degree and edge-deletion freedom, including the exceptional complete low-vertex graphs through explicit Hall arguments. The common eight-color palette is used essentially in the three-independent-highs case. I checked the exceptional arguments mathematically and reproduced the structural control over all 2,808 deletion/excess assignments. The thirteen-vertex independence-two proof also closes its new 44-edge boundary: the additional overlap-five clique union forces a degree-choosable remainder, while the overlap-one case has total blocked fraction at most $4/5$. Gallai’s decomposition and equality statements agree with [5, Theorems 2.1–2.2]. The two claw encodings correctly express failure to extend each core partition; I regenerated both refutations and checked them with DRAT-trim.

The census dependency is appropriate and clearly delimited. False-twin cloning and reverse deletion justify lowering the complete order-fifteen maximal-triangle-free census to order thirteen. The matching conditions characterize the required critical complements. I reproduced the 2,915 reverse deletions, 392 isomorphism classes, and eight retained complements with edge distribution $(3,1,1,3)$ at 41–44 edges, and checked their correspondence with the supplied endpoint graphs. Completeness still imports the generator underlying [2,10]; checking its output properties alone would not establish exhaustiveness. The manuscript explicitly acknowledges this distinction.

The finite classification has inspectable coverage and criticality evidence. I reproduced all 1,772 edge-deletion colorings, the direct eight-color nonexistence checks for all 22 cores, their nonisomorphism, and their stated clique, independence and universal-vertex properties. A separate reconstruction of the endpoint domain reproduced 111, 2,005 and 23,571 literal cases, covered by 10, 39 and 128 types respectively. Inspection of the attachment encodings confirms the degree constraints, partition-blocking condition, legitimate color-name anchors, and treatment of excluded representatives. Fresh DRAT checks also passed for three 79-edge endpoint cases and six 81-edge cases, including cases with retained critical representatives. These are targeted reproductions, not a replay of all 182 refutations: the broader 79-edge run reached its 60-second limit after three successful checks, and I did not execute the full cumulative verifier. The remaining evidence was assessed through the supplied reconstruction code and finite data.

The extension theorem correctly permits arbitrary added core edges. The budget

$$
r+j_1+j_2+j_3\le81-e(Q)
$$

and the cumulative blocker bounds give a valid union bound. Neighborhood and added-edge restrictions computed from the base core conservatively cover those possible in the final $K_9$-free host. I reproduced all 30 added extension inequalities: eight at 79 edges, six at 80, and sixteen at 81. Their margins agree with the certificates, including the smallest margin of 48. These checks use exact integers and require no optimization assumptions. Together with the retained seven-profile result, they support the claimed extension exclusion independently of census completeness.

The extension is sufficiently distinct from the closest published results. Paper [2] treats sixteen-vertex cores, and [4] constrains independent four-sets in seventeen-vertex cores without proving the present triple classification or its host-coloring extensions. The clique-switch and weighted-coloring methods are appropriately credited. Although individual ingredients adapt that earlier framework, completing the branch through its full edge ceiling is a meaningful advance.

Two minor clarifications would improve presentation. State the corollary explicitly for a hypothetical non-nine-colorable host, instead of relying on “surviving” to convey that qualification. Also record the Python requirement and a tested `pynauty` version alongside the other dependencies. My probes used Python 3.10, NetworkX 3.2.1, python-sat 1.8.dev30 and pynauty 2.8.8.1, rather than the pinned NetworkX and SAT-package versions. Neither clarification affects acceptance.
