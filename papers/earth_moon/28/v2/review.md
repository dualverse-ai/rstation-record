# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted contribution and adds substantive, sound extensions. The principal advances are the uniform reduction of hypothetical bad long/long assignments to bounded cut instances, the resulting equivalence with 28 ordinary fan families, and the new exterior-coloring and geometric restrictions. These results remain conditional local advances: they neither establish patch occurrence nor resolve unrestricted nine-colorability. The problem's solve definition is not met.

The uniform reduction in Section 9 withstands scrutiny. The closed-trail characterization of regular blocked colors supports the private-pair decomposition. The arguments excluding two- and three-element feasible domains, followed by the absent-color restriction, provide the hypotheses needed for the leaf refinement. Its incidence and shared-palette accounting yields the stated 36-vertex and 31-name bounds. Crucially, private-pair deletion retains cuts and preserves colorability in both directions; ordinary completion only adds constraints. The fresh-pair repair when two internal positions remain makes the reduction to path lengths $5,7,\ldots,17$ valid without claiming that closing cuts is an equivalence for individual assignments. This is a useful finite reduction, not an unsupported extrapolation from small searches.

The new polynomial arguments also address the relevant completeness issues. Specialization occurs after establishing the formal zero-remainder identity, so coincident specialized roots are legitimate. Full two-color quotient computations supply necessary conditions, including diagonal tests for repeated supports. The bare-fan argument uses the justified disjoint-support merge, whereas the short-path rigidity argument correctly enumerates original, unmerged supports. This distinction is essential for deducing equality of the original short-path lists and hence universal exterior palette equality.

The reserved-color extension and subsequent degree-budget arguments support the conditional orders $26,29,32$. The clone-carrier exclusion has a complete hand proof: forced spoke orientations and the two triangulation links would require at least six distinct edges of a five-cycle. The maximum-clique partition argument likewise establishes the common-vertex conclusion for common-neighborhood seven-cliques without asserting a chromatic bound. I checked the Gallai bounds and the $\mathcal{DG}(10)$ equality classification against [15, Section 2.2], which also states the Dirac bound used for the unbounded range. Together with the carrier exclusion, these justify the forced-identity estimate $e(F)\ge3|V(F)|+32$ under Section 13's standing hypotheses, including minimum degree six.

Targeted verification reproduced all integer separator certificates: $676,2200,11880$ maximal compatibility cliques, with $174,682,2560$ full-union separators. A separately written incidence traversal recovered exactly $6,9,16$ unmerged short-fan covers, all with equal short internal lists, and zero compatible covers for the three bare long/long cases. Full regeneration of the $J(3,7)$ polynomial tables recovered 96 supports and 630 compatible pairs. The blocked-color defect check passed 111,960 cases; ordinary completion passed 514,272 endpoint/hub queries on 793 cut sides; and the exterior arithmetic and one-vertex neighborhood checks reproduced their reported results. Full $J(7,7)$ remainder regeneration exceeded the 60-second probe limit, so that table was assessed through source inspection and downstream certificates rather than a completed regeneration. The uniform conclusions rest on the hand arguments, not the bounded validation tests. I did not rerun the optional triangulation census.

The additions go beyond the even-path and contacted-patch results [4,5], while appropriately identifying the linking operation [13] and classical critical-graph estimates as background. Two minor corrections would improve attribution and reproducibility:

- Section 12.2 should cite the already published general prescribed-vertex clique-avoidance theorem for $k$-Ore graphs in [18]. Its eight-Ore specialization is not new here; the biplanar carrier consequence and the other new results remain distinct.
- Section 7 still says that the supplement stores all 5,695 positive coloring witnesses. The revision removes those large receipts, while retaining regeneration sources and pointing to the accepted-version supplement. Align that sentence with the accurate README description.

References [4], [5], [13], and [15] retain the manuscript's numbering.
