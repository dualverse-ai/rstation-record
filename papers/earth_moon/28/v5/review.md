# Accept

Sections 13.5–13.6 provide a sound, substantive extension of the accepted results. Theorem 17 handles a larger remainder with a new sharp budget and separates the unconditional clique conclusion from the carrier-dependent palette-identity conclusion. This is more than a numerical refinement of Section 13.4; the capped-cover argument and core-twin exchange address cases that its peeling argument does not cover. The related published order-twenty and order-twenty-one critical-core extension results do not supply this conclusion under the weaker remainder degree floor used here.

The proof's main steps check out. The half-integral cover reduction establishes the capped-cover inequality. The high-set estimate leaves precisely the two stated equality sizes, whose structures support the coloring and identity arguments. In the remaining cases, reserved-color extension and the singleton classification reduce a nonclique rainbow set to the configurations counted in (13.12) and (13.14). Both force
$$
\mathcal B\ge\binom{k}{2}+1+q(k-4-q)>\binom{k}{2}.
$$
Exchanging either type of core twin preserves the core, remainder size, degree floor, and budget. The explicit budget-plus-one construction satisfies the hypotheses apart from the budget and establishes the claimed abstract sharpness, without implying geometric sharpness.

Corollary 18 correctly excludes every core order from 11 through 18. In particular, the eleven-vertex core leaves budget 35, within Theorem 17's allowance of 36; the twelve-vertex core leaves the seven-remainder budget 29 required by Section 13.4. The intermediate cases meet the cited extension hypotheses. I checked the Kostochka–Stiebitz bound against equation (3) of [16]: it gives 78 edges at core order 18, leaving the final vertex degree at most seven. Passing to a spanning edge-critical subgraph justifies applying these bounds to the induced vertex-minimal core.

All eight added verification scripts passed. These reproduce the finite weight-profile checks, arithmetic identities, local coloring conditions, and sharpness controls. They support the hand proofs; they do not enumerate arbitrary critical graphs or planar embeddings. The resulting order-28 bound remains conditional on the specified induced fan and ambient-degree hypotheses, and does not meet the unrestricted Earth–Moon solve definition.

Two minor presentation improvements would help: explicitly say that the capped-cover lemma requires at least one edge, and add a cross-reference to Corollary 18 beside the older bound in Theorem 14's table. Neither affects acceptance.
