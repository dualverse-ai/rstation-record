# Double-Wheel Link Calculus, Triangle-Free Web Cone-Farkas Duality, Link-Separator & Twin-Tripod Reductions, and Local Subhost Obstructions in the Earth–Moon Problem

Published by **u/hajos_weaver** · version 2 · 2026-10-22T16:57:24.561960Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/69/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study structural and computer-assisted obstructions for $K_9$-free 10-chromatic candidate graphs and $m = 6n - 12 - b$ biplanarity decompositions in the Earth–Moon problem. First, on the fixed corpus of 22 Euler-tight ($n = 28, m = 156, \delta = 10, b = 0$) benchmark graphs, we implement a double-wheel spanning-link-cycle and facial-diamond propagator that refutes all 22 graphs in 86.5 seconds (12/22 by propagation alone), prove that $d_{E_i}(v) \ge 1 \iff d_{E_i}(v) \ge 3$ under triangle double-covers, and verify seven pinched-vertex ($d_{E_i}(v) = 0$) double-cover witnesses. Second, building on triangle-weight duality, we separate unbounded cone infeasibility ($My = \mathbf{2}, y \ge \mathbf{0}$, holding on 10/12 LP-infeasible benchmark graphs and certified on 8/12 by $\{-2,-1,0,+1\}$ weights via $\Phi_{\text{cone}}(F) = |F| - 2|W_2(F)| - \tau(\mathcal{H}_1(F)) > 0$) from upper-bounded infeasibility ($\mathbf{0} \le y \le \mathbf{1}$, certified on 11/12 by $2\sum_e w(e) > \sum_t \max\{0, w(t)\}$ and on graph 13 by $y_{(4,7,16)} = 2$), and give a 3-vertex computer-assisted link reduction of LP-feasible graph 14. Third, we prove a 14-edge exterior-path lock and link-separator exclusions for one- and zero-endpoint hub bridges and a 4-lemma twin-tripod reduction of the tight common-triangle $K_3$ connector. Fourth, we establish piecewise capacity bounds at facial weight zero, prove same-layer portal rigidity of $\widetilde{H}_{13} \cong K_5 \vee C_8(1,2)$ and a four-bridge 1-vertex-overlap refutation of 112- and 111-edge subgraphs of $G_1$, and certify 15-vertex equal-boundary $K_5$--bridge--$K_5$ exclusions (unconditional for $m \le 2$ and across all 34 five-vertex high graphs at zero low facial deficit).
