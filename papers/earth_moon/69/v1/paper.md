## 1. Double-Wheel Link Calculus & the $2n$ Non-Isolated-Vertex Clauses Theorem on $n = 28, \delta = 10, b = 0$ Graphs

### Theorem 1.1 (Double-Wheel Link & Facial-Diamond Conditions at $b = 0$)
Let $G = (V, E)$ be an $n$-vertex graph with $m = 6n - 12$ ($b = 0$) and $\omega(G) \le 8$. If $G = T_1 \cup T_2$ is biplanar, then:
1. **Maximal Triangulations:** Both $T_1 = (V, E_1)$ and $T_2 = (V, E_2)$ are simple maximal planar triangulations with $|E_1| = |E_2| = 3n - 6$ and $2n - 4$ facial triangles each.
2. **Vertex-Disjoint Link Cycles:** For every $v \in V$, the neighborhood $N_G(v)$ partitions into $N_1(v) \sqcup N_2(v)$ with $|N_1(v)| = p \ge 3$ and $|N_2(v)| = q \ge 3$ ($p + q = \deg_G(v)$) such that the facial link of $v$ in $T_1$ is a simple $p$-cycle $C_p \subseteq T_1[N_1(v)]$ and the facial link of $v$ in $T_2$ is a simple $q$-cycle $C_q \subseteq T_2[N_2(v)]$. When $G[N_G(v)]$ admits no wheel split with a chord inside $N_i(v)$ creating a separating triangle, $T_1[N_1(v)] \cong C_p$ and $T_2[N_2(v)] \cong C_q$ are induced cycles.
3. **Facial-Diamond Parity & Triangle Double-Cover:** Every edge $e = (u, w) \in E(G)$ lies in **exactly two** facial triangles $t_1(e) = (u, w, x_1)$ and $t_2(e) = (u, w, x_2)$ of the same layer $T_i$, forcing all 5 edges of the facial diamond $t_1(e) \cup t_2(e)$ into the same 2-XOR equivalence class ($\text{layer}(e') = \text{layer}(e)$ for all $e' \in t_1(e) \cup t_2(e)$).

### Theorem 1.2 (The $2n$ Non-Isolated-Vertex Clauses Theorem)
Let $G = (V, E)$ be any graph in which a boolean edge assignment $x: E \to \{0, 1\}$ and a subset of monochromatic triangles $\mathcal{F} \subseteq T(G)$ satisfy the **triangle double-cover condition** (every edge $e \in E$ belongs to at least two monochromatic triangles in $\mathcal{F}$). Then for every vertex $v \in V$ and every layer $i \in \{0, 1\}$:
$$d_{E_i}(v) \ge 1 \iff d_{E_i}(v) \ge 3.$$
*Proof.* Suppose $d_{E_i}(v) \ge 1$, and pick any edge $e = (v, u_1) \in E_i$ incident to $v$. By the triangle double-cover condition, $e$ lies in at least two monochromatic triangles $t_1 = (v, u_1, u_2) \in \mathcal{F}$ and $t_2 = (v, u_1, u_3) \in \mathcal{F}$ with $u_2 \neq u_3$. Since $t_1$ and $t_2$ are monochromatic and contain $e \in E_i$, their other incident edges $(v, u_2)$ and $(v, u_3)$ also belong to $E_i$. Because $u_1, u_2, u_3$ are three distinct neighbors of $v$, we have $d_{E_i}(v) \ge 3$. $\blacksquare$

**Algorithmic Consequence:** Without vertex-degree constraints, the pure triangle double-cover CNF on $G$ admits a spurious "pinched-vertex" loophole in which a vertex $v$ places **all** its incident edges into a single layer ($d_{E_1}(v) = \deg_G(v), d_{E_2}(v) = 0$) and covers $E_1(v)$ inside a dense clique $K_8$ while leaving $v$ isolated in $E_2$. By Theorem 1.2, appending just the **$2n = 56$ non-isolated-vertex clauses**
$$\bigvee_{u \in N_G(v)} x_{vu} \quad \text{and} \quad \bigvee_{u \in N_G(v)} \neg x_{vu} \qquad (\forall v \in V(G))$$
closes this loophole completely and forces $d_{E_1}(v) \ge 3$ and $d_{E_2}(v) \ge 3$ at every vertex $v \in V(G)$.

## 2. Exact Bichromatic Link-Cut Identity, Triangle-Free Web Cone-Farkas Duality, and the 3-Step Hand Proof of Survivor `#14`

### Theorem 2.1 (Exact Bichromatic Link-Cut Identity)
For **any** graph $G = (V, E)$ and **any** 2-coloring $E = E_1 \sqcup E_2$ inducing the neighborhood partition $N_G(v) = N_1(v) \sqcup N_2(v)$ at each vertex $v \in V$:
$$|T_{\text{bi}}(G)| = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{v \in V(G)} |\delta_{G[N_G(v)]}(N_1(v), N_2(v))|, \qquad |T_{\text{mono}}(G)| = |T(G)| - \frac{1}{2} \sum_{v \in V(G)} |\delta_{G[N_G(v)]}(N_1(v), N_2(v))|.$$
*Proof.* Every monochromatic triangle $t = (u, v, w)$ has all three vertices’ incident edge pairs in the same layer, contributing $0$ to the link cut $\delta_{G[N_G(x)]}(N_1(x), N_2(x))$ at $x \in \{u, v, w\}$. Every bichromatic triangle $t = (u, v, w)$ has two edges in one layer and one edge in the other, so at exactly **two** of its three vertices the two incident edges of $t$ have opposite colors (contributing $+1$ each to $\delta_{G[N_G(x)]}(N_1(x), N_2(x))$), while at the third vertex both incident edges of $t$ have the same color (contributing $0$). Summing over all $v \in V(G)$ counts every bichromatic triangle with multiplicity $2$. $\blacksquare$

### Theorem 2.2 (Triangle-Free Web Cone-Farkas Functional & Bipartite-Shell Specialization)
Let $M \in \{0, 1\}^{E(G) \times T(G)}$ be the edge-triangle incidence matrix of $G$. Any $b = 0$ biplanar decomposition requires a vector $y \in [0, 1]^{T(G)}$ satisfying $M y = \mathbf{2}$, and in particular requires the unbounded non-negative cone system $M y = \mathbf{2}, y \ge \mathbf{0}$ to be feasible.
Let $F \subseteq E(G)$ be any **triangle-free** subset of edges ($|t \cap F| \le 2$ for all $t \in T(G)$). Define:
- $W_2(F) := \{e \in E(G) \setminus F : \exists t \in T(G) \text{ with } t \setminus \{e\} \subseteq F\}$ (the **wedge-closing edges** of 2-$F$-edge triangles), and
- $\mathcal{H}_1(F) := \{t \setminus F : t \in T(G), |t \cap F| = 1, t \cap W_2(F) = \emptyset\}$ (the 2-uniform hypergraph of residual 1-$F$-edge triangles), with minimum vertex cover (edge cover in $G$) $\tau(\mathcal{H}_1(F))$ attained by $C_{\text{cover}} \subseteq E(G) \setminus (F \cup W_2(F))$.

Assigning edge weights $w: E(G) \to \{-2, -1, 0, +1\}$ by
$$w(e) := \begin{cases} +1 & \text{if } e \in F, \\ -2 & \text{if } e \in W_2(F), \\ -1 & \text{if } e \in C_{\text{cover}}, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
yields $w(t) = \sum_{e \in t} w(e) \le 0$ for **every** triangle $t \in T(G)$ ($|T^+| = 0$). Consequently, whenever the **Triangle-Free Web Cone-Farkas Functional** satisfies
$$\Phi_{\text{cone}}(F) := |F| - 2|W_2(F)| - \tau(\mathcal{H}_1(F)) > 0,$$
the unbounded cone $M y = \mathbf{2}, y \ge \mathbf{0}$ is infeasible:
$$0 \ge \sum_{t \in T(G)} w(t) y_t = \sum_{e \in E(G)} w(e) (M y)_e = 2 \sum_{e \in E(G)} w(e) = 2\,\Phi_{\text{cone}}(F) \ge 2, \quad \text{contradiction.}$$
In particular, when $F = E_G(A, B)$ is the bipartite cut between disjoint vertex subsets $A, B \subseteq V(G)$, $W_2(F) \subseteq E(G[A]) \cup E(G[B])$ and covering $\mathcal{H}_1(F)$ at each exterior common neighbor $c \notin A \cup B$ by the smaller side $\min(|N_G(c) \cap A|, |N_G(c) \cap B|)$ yields the **Bipartite-Shell Cone-Farkas Functional**:
$$\Phi_{\text{cone}}(A, B) := |E_G(A, B)| - 2e(G[A]) - 2e(G[B]) - \sum_{c \notin A \cup B} \min(|N_G(c) \cap A|, |N_G(c) \cap B|) > 0.$$
