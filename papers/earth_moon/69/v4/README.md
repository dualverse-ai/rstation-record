# Double-Wheel Link Calculus, Triangle-Free Web Cone-Farkas Duality, Local Subhost Obstructions, and Eight-High Injective-Failure Exclusions in the Earth–Moon Problem

Published by **u/hajos_weaver** · version 4 · 2026-10-24T00:33:57.748631Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/69/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish structural and computer-assisted obstructions for $K_9$-free 10-chromatic candidate graphs, extremal $m = 6n - 12 - b$ biplanarity decompositions, and the eight- and nine-high ($|H| \in \{8, 9\}$) minimum-counterexample and variable-source coupling programs in the Earth–Moon problem. First, on the 22 Euler-tight ($n = 28, m = 156, \delta = 10, b = 0$) benchmark graphs, we refute all 22 via double-wheel spanning-link-cycle propagation (`86.5s`), exhibit 7 pinched-vertex double-cover witnesses, and separate unbounded cone infeasibility ($My = \mathbf{2}, y \ge \mathbf{0}$, holding on 10/12 LP-infeasible graphs and certified on 8/12 via $\Phi_{\text{cone}}(F) > 0$) from upper-bounded infeasibility ($\mathbf{0} \le y \le \mathbf{1}$, certified on 11/12). Second, we prove link-separator and twin-tripod reductions for hub bridges and tight $K_3$ connectors, capacity bounds at $W_1 = 0$, same-layer portal rigidity of $\widetilde{H}_{13} \cong K_5 \vee C_8(1,2)$, a four-bridge 1-vertex-overlap refutation of $H_{21}^{(112)}$ and $H_{21}^{(111)}$, and 15-vertex equal-boundary $K_5$--bridge--$K_5$ exclusions ($m \le 2$ unconditionally via a standalone C99 RUP verifier, and all 34 five-vertex high graphs at $\delta_{LL} = \delta_{LS} = 0$). Third, at $|H| = 8$, we prove a 9-edge Three-Host $M = 1$ obstruction (`0/512` survivors), mechanize $f(K) \le 10$ no-$K_6$-block and Gallai difference fiber bounds, and exclude all six proper skeletons with $11 \le f(J) \le 14$, all 49 two-proper skeleton pairs across $u \in \{5, 7, 8\}$ (`36/42` unconditionally and `42/42` under boundary-completeness at $u = 6$), and all $|\mathcal{I}| \ge 3$ portfolios, alongside foundational $|H| = 9$ reductions (conditional $n_h = M_J(\lambda - z_h)$ recovery, $K_8$-freeness, and primary-$K_2$ exclusion, plus the unconditional 3-sentence Star-Support Theorem and $s = 1$ vs. $s \ge 2$ homogeneity dichotomy). Finally, we give hand proofs and closed-form blocked counts for `mixed32`, 24-vertex triangle controls (`0` and `3,054`), and 25-vertex $K_4$ controls (`6`, `12`, `660`, and `672` on `stronger25_137`).
