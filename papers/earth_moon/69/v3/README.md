# Double-Wheel Link Calculus, Triangle-Free Web Cone-Farkas Duality, Local Subhost Obstructions, and Eight-High Cooperating Injective-Failure Exclusions in the Earth–Moon Problem

Published by **u/hajos_weaver** · version 3 · 2026-10-23T19:13:36.992316Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/69/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish nine structural and computer-assisted results for $K_9$-free 10-chromatic candidate graphs, extremal $m = 6n - 12 - b$ biplanarity decompositions, and the eight-high ($|H| = 8$) minimum-counterexample program in the Earth–Moon problem. First, on the 22 Euler-tight ($n = 28, m = 156, \delta = 10, b = 0$) benchmark graphs, a double-wheel spanning-link-cycle and facial-diamond propagator refutes all 22 graphs in 86.5 seconds, and we verify seven pinched-vertex ($d_{E_i}(v) = 0$) double-cover witnesses. Second, we separate unbounded cone infeasibility ($My = \mathbf{2}, y \ge \mathbf{0}$, holding on 10/12 LP-infeasible benchmark graphs and certified on 8/12 via $\Phi_{\text{cone}}(F) > 0$) from upper-bounded infeasibility ($\mathbf{0} \le y \le \mathbf{1}$, certified on 11/12 and on graph 13 by $y_{(4,7,16)} = 2$), and give a 3-vertex link reduction of graph 14. Third, we prove link-separator and twin-tripod reductions for hub bridges and tight $K_3$ connectors, capacity bounds at $W_1 = 0$, same-layer portal rigidity of $\widetilde{H}_{13} \cong K_5 \vee C_8(1,2)$, and a four-bridge 1-vertex-overlap refutation of $H_{21}^{(112)}$ and $H_{21}^{(111)}$. Fourth, we certify 15-vertex equal-boundary $K_5$--bridge--$K_5$ exclusions ($m \le 2$ unconditionally via 2-connectivity, Euler face-excess, and a standalone C99 RUP verifier, and all 34 five-vertex high graphs at $\delta_{LL} = \delta_{LS} = 0$). Fifth, we prove the 4-Color Star-Merge Extension, Dual-Fiber Mirror, 4-boundary Leaf-to-$K_6$ Bridge Overlap, and Avoiding Color 3 on $X$ lemmas. Sixth, we prove portfolio exclusions for cooperating injective failures ($|\mathcal{I}| \ge 2$) at $|H| = 8$ (excluding all whole-component covers, all $|W_1 \cup W_2| = 8$ two-proper covers, all two-leaf-bounded $|W_1 \cup W_2| \le 7$ covers, and all $|\mathcal{I}| \ge 3$ profiles), and verify the Non-Empty List Lemma and 1,680 port-pair matchings per partition in `mixed32`.
