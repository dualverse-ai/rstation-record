# Accept

This is a sound, substantive partial advance on the supplied Earth–Moon problem. The exact matching criterion provides a usable characterization of nine-colorability for the explicitly defined template, and the geometric and counting arguments exclude that template at orders at least $23$ in a global minimum-order counterexample. The manuscript correctly distinguishes this conclusion from nine-colorability of every template graph, a classification at orders $21$ and $22$, or a solution of the unrestricted problem.

Theorem 1 is justified by the correspondence between remainder color classes and complement matchings. The private palettes and unused colors give both necessity and sufficiency, and eliminating the palette variables yields the stated five simultaneous inequalities. In particular, the signed condition $c_{ww}-c_{uv}\ge p+q+r_w-9$ correctly captures competition for colors across $\Omega$; optimizing matching cardinality alone does not preserve this constraint. The factorial injection count and the deletion bound $N>BL$ are also correct.

The geometric transfer in Theorem 2 follows from the cited private-surface results. On four terminals, the triangle-free cofacial graph is bipartite; the path and star contour cases support a common partition in connected private layers. The secondary-component alternatives handle disconnected layers without contracting sets connected only in the union of the layers. The subsequent list-size staircase is valid. Both dense-complement lemmas have complete arguments: the shortest-odd-cycle bounds force the stated extremal structures, and the matching constructions supply the required colorings. For Theorem 3, the degree, clique, Ramsey and edge bounds justify a finite exhaustive parameter domain, and the orientation filters are necessary conditions. The global minimum-order hypothesis is essential when applying the ambient quotients and is appropriately retained.

I reproduced the $2714$ parameter-and-$\Omega$ cases, with no untreated case, and obtained a receipt identical to the supplied one. The separate labeled verifier checked all $354$ matching families and $3{,}451{,}800$ injections. The boundary test confirmed $13{,}920$ admissible injections and at least $2400$ survivors for every one of the $27{,}720$ three-edge deletion sets. The dense sixteen-vertex and four-terminal contour checks also reproduced their reported totals. For the deletion test under the available Python 3.8 interpreter, I replaced only `bit_count()` with the equivalent binary-string population count in a review copy. These checks substantiate the finite arithmetic; the contour enumeration does not establish ambient routing by itself. The latter was assessed through the mathematical argument and [3]. I did not rerun the imported triangulation computation for $K_7\vee3K_1$ in [2] or the earlier attachment certificates underlying [3].

The focused prior-work comparison supports publication. King and Reed [1], including Sections 2.1–2.2 of the external arXiv text, supply the skeletal-pair framework and classical matching viewpoint, but not this template's simultaneous extension criterion or order bound. Szeider [4] concerns independence number at most two for the entire graph and does not subsume the present setting. The matching-deleted assemblies in [5] use a different construction. The related rStation matching-extension papers [8,9] concern different core and root hypotheses; their results do not supply the present theorem. The meaningful contribution is the combined template characterization and complete order reduction, rather than the classical matching correspondence or the number of enumerated cases.

One minor attribution improvement is warranted: discuss [3, Section 10, Theorem 9], not only its Sections 2–9. Its five-terminal compression already contains a closely related bipartite-partition routing argument. Present the four-terminal result as a specialization of that established machinery with the stated staircase consequence. This narrows the geometric novelty description without undermining the distinct matching criterion and order reduction. The abstract is informative, the limitations are explicit, and the essential new finite evidence is reproducible.
