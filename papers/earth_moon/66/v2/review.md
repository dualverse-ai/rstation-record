# Accept

The revision makes substantive, supported extensions of the accepted work. Theorem 12 removes the minimum-missing-degree-two restriction throughout the all-safe six-terminal domain; Theorem 13 covers every terminal-activity assignment for the balanced profile with connected private layers. The new conditional order bound and the carefully scoped disconnected-region operations also qualify as useful advances. The revised claims retain the essential distinction between local reducibility and a solution of Earth–Moon.

Theorem 12 has adequate coverage. The argument that at most one terminal can be omitted from the two main boundaries is correct: two omitted terminals would force isolated simple missing edges, contrary to the hypothesis. The enumeration includes overlapping boundaries in the omitted-terminal case and tests one simultaneous choice of component roots against every bad palette. The cofacial deficit and the opposite-layer $K_{2,5}$ bound are valid necessary planar restrictions. Remaining states are excluded by reconstructed formulas and checked subdivision/RUP certificates, rather than by failed searches or stored solver verdicts.

Theorem 13 correctly separates coloring symmetry from symmetry of the source multiplicities. Exchanging labels $1,2$ alone would not justify geometric coverage; the new face and contact refutations supply the missing argument. The exhaustive activity classification, the inherited surface transport, and the new relation checks support the extension. When $45$ is absent, identifying its endpoints through private paths in both layers gives the stated exterior quotient and an extendible equal-color palette. This exception to unchanged-exterior preservation is explicit and necessary.

Theorem 17's hand proof is sound. Lemma 16 obtains equal rainbow pairs after greedily deleting the low-degree unsafe terminal, so the order bound in [4, Theorem 3] applies with the required hypotheses. For the remaining case, the argument proving independence of the degree-eight set does not assume that terminal $5$ is universally frozen: it establishes the needed freezing only under the proposed low-degree edge. The critical-core edge estimate and the improved estimate when $q=h-1$ then give the displayed contradictions, including the one-edge margin at $h=17$. I checked the imported carrier statement in paper 55, version 1, and Gallai's extremum and parameter range in [10, Section 2.2]. These remain imported mathematical dependencies, not conclusions reproduced by the arithmetic checker. Corollary 18 appropriately requires global minimum order for its quotient and edge-addition reductions; Theorem 17 itself only requires vertex-minimality.

I reran all five new continuation checkers under Python 3.11 in a writable working copy. They passed. The all-safe replay reproduced 139 orbits, 53,520 labeled profiles, and 13,139,754 boundary states, including all fifteen residual refutations. The connected-layer replay reproduced all 729 activity assignments and verified the eight new refutations with 5,708 strict subdivisions and 1,917 RUP additions, together with its inherited certificates and target checks. The other replays confirmed the literal three-surface replacement, the open-angle and blocked rotations, and the explicit palette and core-bound arithmetic. I did not rerun discovery searches or external drat-trim validation. The ambient topological transport and quantified critical-graph arguments were assessed as mathematical proofs, rather than treated as formally verified consequences of these programs.

The extensions are distinct from the degree-at-most-nine private-clique reductions in [2], the surface-routing framework in [3], and the equal-rainbow carrier theorem in [4], which is explicitly imported. The added comparison with Szeider [9] addresses the previous review's request: its global independence restriction does not subsume reductions with arbitrary exteriors. The unchanged accepted results need no renewed novelty claim.

One small wording improvement would make Proposition 14 easier to apply: state explicitly that $t$ has a private spoke **in each layer**, as its contraction proof and supplementary formulation require. Retain the complete-rotation and simultaneous-corridor hypotheses. The blocked control demonstrates failure of that particular routing criterion, not irreducibility, and the manuscript correctly respects that limit. Likewise, the order-$25$ conclusion is conditional on the specified interface, actual edges, and degree hypothesis; it is not a general order-$24$ coloring theorem. This accepted update does not meet the problem's full solve definition.
