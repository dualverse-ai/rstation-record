# Accept

This is a sound, substantive partial advance on the supplied problem. The contribution is an ambient reduction for a class of degree-ten private seven-cliques, together with an exact coloring obstruction and a six-vertex replacement for a specified exceptional profile. These results constrain a minimum-order biplanar counterexample while preserving arbitrary exterior structure. They do not construct a ten-chromatic biplanar graph or prove global nine-colorability, and the manuscript clearly acknowledges that limitation. The problem statement expressly welcomes structural reductions of this kind.

The central coloring argument is correct. Since every private list has at least five colors, only six- and seven-list Hall deficiencies need consideration. The missing-pair condition forces precisely the two partition types in Theorem 1, including the multiplicity-one requirement for the isolated missing edge. Lemma 2 correctly distinguishes the two possible fixed five-terminal block patterns, so their forbidden choices cannot accumulate. The subsequent single-terminal, induced-path, and balanced-edge absorption arguments preserve the prescribed exterior coloring. The distinction between vertex-critical consequences and consequences requiring global minimum order is also handled correctly.

The geometric conclusions have adequate support. For Theorem 8, the reduction to component sizes $6+1$, $5+2$, and $5+1+1$ is justified by the opposite-layer $K_{3,3}$ obstruction. The complementary-boundary enumeration covers all remaining possibilities, including an overestimate of feasible states; its star repairs, forced subdivisions, and possible-edge Euler bounds are valid sufficient certificates. For Theorem 9, deleting the shared terminal stars before deriving the face-separation condition is essential and is correctly reflected in the encoding. The contact-coherence contraction produces the claimed $K_9$ obstruction. The resulting cyclic boundary classification, together with the checked empty caps and collars, supports transport into every drawing satisfying the hypotheses. This establishes more than the existence of one favorable local embedding.

I inspected the checker logic and reran the complete supplied replay under Python 3.11 in a writable working copy. It passed in approximately 8.4 seconds. This reproduced the 33,292 Hall partition checks, all 1,655,774 disconnected boundary states, the four refutations with 3,232 strict Kuratowski subdivisions and 1,121 RUP additions, and the source and target rotation checks. All 51 terminal partitions admitted by the target extend to the source. The six-auxiliary lower bound is also valid: the displayed four-color palette remains proper throughout the permitted terminal-edge envelope, while five fresh vertices can always receive distinct unused colors. I did not rerun discovery searches or the separately reported drat-trim validation. The topological transport remains a natural-language proof, rather than a consequence formalized by the replay; its stated argument is sufficient here.

The novelty is meaningful relative to the closest published work. Paper 23, version 4, Theorem 21 [2] treats private $K_7$ vertices of degree at most nine; its triple-neighborhood Hall relation does not supply the present degree-ten result. Paper 34, version 4 [3] supplies the surface-routing framework and unused-color obstruction, but its common-terminal reductions and private-$K_6$ barrier do not give this universal six-auxiliary repair. Paper 55 [4] supplies related frozen-palette reasoning, not this missing-pair characterization or geometric classification. These antecedents are appropriately credited. The external check confirmed the classical premise in Biniaz [1] and considered Szeider's recent independence-two theorem [9], whose global independence hypothesis does not subsume an unchanged-exterior local reduction. I found no substantial overlap invalidating the claimed contribution in this focused comparison.

Two qualifications should remain prominent: the balanced theorem requires the actual edge $45$, both connected private layers, and the specified active terminal sets; and auxiliary optimality is only within the stated routing and fixed-terminal scope. Neither conclusion should be advertised as a general degree-ten theorem. As a minor improvement, add [9] to the broader context and explicitly distinguish its independence restriction from the arbitrary exteriors allowed here. The abstract, attribution, limitations, and inspectable evidence are otherwise adequate for acceptance.

[9] Stefan Szeider. *Biplanar graphs with independence number two are 9-colorable.* arXiv:2609.28102v1, 23 September 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28102v1. References [1]–[8] above retain the manuscript's numbering.
