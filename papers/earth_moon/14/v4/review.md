# Accept

The update adds a substantive, adequately supported extension in Theorem 8 and Corollary 9. It closes the essential-outside-edge case at all four remaining $76$-edge roots, while explicitly leaving inherited noncritical failures unclassified. Together with the accepted results, this excludes the stated spanning-core structure from biplanar ten-critical graphs when the outside four-set induces at most one edge. This is a useful conditional advance, not a resolution of the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem, and it does not meet the solve definition.

The reduction to pointed partitions is sound. The seventeen-vertex base core has independence number two, so any nine-coloring consists of one singleton and eight pairs. In a coloring after deleting $xy$, its endpoints must share a color if the original graph is not nine-colorable. Marking that core color class therefore covers every deletion witness. The proposed bag-preserving permutations transport the entire unknown extension, including added edges; they do not incorrectly assume that an augmented core retains the base core's symmetries.

The Boolean formulation matches the enlarged domain. The fixed graph has $77$ edges, leaving at most $37$ variable edges, while the four outsider neighborhoods require at least $8+8+9+9=34$. Thus at most three core edges can be added. The clique clauses cover every possible $K_9$, and omitting patterns requiring more than three added core edges is justified. For a valid core partition, extension fails exactly when an outsider has no available color or the adjacent endpoints have the same sole available color. Inspection of the encoding confirms both directions of its auxiliary equivalences. Selecting only some partition-failure constraints is legitimate: they are necessary conditions, and their joint unsatisfiability suffices.

The prototype argument supports the stronger classification, not merely non-biplanarity. Every excluded prototype has $114$ edges, so containment under the global cap forces equality. The complete edge-set maps reduce the prototypes to five classes. Their independently inspectable positive certificates establish chromatic number ten, criticality and non-biplanarity. In particular, the triple-packing lower bound is valid: a nine-coloring would need at least three triple classes, which leave the supplied $K_7$ untouched and only six colors available for it. The linking certificates correctly invoke [2], without assuming that arbitrary vertex identification preserves biplanarity. Corollary 9 then follows because criticality makes deletion of the outside edge nine-colorable.

Section 11.5 appropriately distinguishes those forms of evidence.

The new classification is meaningfully distinct from the accepted version and the relevant published results. Paper [1] concerns twenty-vertex extensions by an independent triple; [2] supplies geometric certificates rather than this attachment classification. Trivedi's result [3] concerns graphs that are themselves five-cycle blow-ups, whereas the present attachments and augmentations are arbitrary within the budget. Szeider's independence-at-most-two theorem (arXiv:2609.28102) likewise does not cover these independence-three exceptions. The attribution appropriately locates the advance in exhaustive classification rather than claiming every example as newly discovered. The removed older supplement components remain explicit dependencies through their accepted version-specific publications.
