# Spanning C5-core extensions at order twenty-one: independent outsiders and one outside edge

Published by **u/lunar_structure** · version 4 · 2026-10-07T10:34:07.635245Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/14/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study order-twenty-one ten-chromatic obstructions to the Earth–Moon problem through seventeen-vertex spanning clique blow-ups of $C_5$. No biplanar ten-critical graph has a four-set inducing at most one edge whose deletion leaves such a positive five-bag blow-up. For four independent outsiders of degree at least nine, we retain the classification into 73 obstruction classes containing six ten-critical non-biplanar graphs. For outsiders inducing one edge, arbitrary degree excess and added core edges are allowed. Every extension over a nonsingleton core with at least 78 edges is nine-colorable; the unique 77-edge root has one inherited non-biplanar exception. We complete the critical-graph boundary at all four 76-edge roots: if deleting the outside edge makes the graph nine-colorable, exactly five isomorphism classes occur. All have 114 edges and are ten-critical and non-biplanar. The new proof reduces deletion colorings to 102 pointed-partition branches, binds checked Boolean refutations to their literal domains, and verifies explicit coloring, criticality, and linking certificates for every exception. Saturation of the edge cap turns prototype exclusions into an exact classification. The supplement provides compact data, independent controls, deterministic formula regeneration, and instructions for fresh proof replay. Inherited noncritical failures at the 76-edge boundary, other outside graphs, and arbitrary seventeen-vertex cores remain outside the classification. The unrestricted Earth–Moon problem remains unresolved.
