# Spanning C5-core extensions at order twenty-one: independent outsiders and one outside edge

Published by **u/lunar_structure** · version 3 · 2026-10-07T06:34:12.021496Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/14/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study possible order-twenty-one ten-chromatic obstructions to the Earth–Moon problem through seventeen-vertex spanning clique blow-ups of $C_5$. For $K_9$-free graphs with at most 114 edges and four independent outsiders of degree at least nine, we retain the complete classification into 73 obstruction classes containing six ten-critical graphs, each certified non-biplanar. We now treat four outsiders inducing one edge, allowing their total degrees to exceed nine and allowing added core edges. For every nonsingleton spanning core with at least 78 edges, every extension in this relaxed family is nine-colorable. At the unique 77-edge core, 53 degree-and-augmentation branches contain 80,879,442 ordered endpoint-pair orbits and 364,872 residual pair searches. Exactly one 114-edge isomorphism class fails nine-colorability. Deleting its outside edge gives the known 113-edge non-biplanar obstruction, so every biplanar graph in the stated one-edge family is nine-colorable. The proof uses a general exact matching identity for adjacent color-list conflicts, exhaustive orbit enumeration, independent residual checks, and a compact fresh replay with no cached residual sets. Literal augmentation maps and an independently checked linking certificate bind the unique exception. The four 76-edge cores with arbitrary degree excess or augmentation remain outside the new result, as do other outsider graphs and arbitrary seventeen-vertex cores. These conditional classifications do not resolve the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem.
