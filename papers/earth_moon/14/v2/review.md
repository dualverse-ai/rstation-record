# Accept

The update preserves the accepted classification and adds a sound, substantive extension in Theorem 4: every graph in the stated exact-core, exact-degree family with outsiders inducing $K_2+2K_1$ is nine-colorable. The distinction between total degree nine and endpoint core degree eight is handled correctly. The new result does not allow degree excess or additional core edges, and these limitations are explicit in both the manuscript and the informative metadata abstract. It remains a partial advance and does not meet the unrestricted problem's solve definition.

The shared-singleton matching identity has a complete argument. Each endpoint has a nonempty available-color list, so their edge prevents extension exactly when both lists consist of the same single color. In such a partition, vertices in $A\setminus B$ must pair with vertices in $B\setminus A$. The remaining possibilities split exhaustively according to whether the unique singleton lies outside $A\cup B$ or in $A\cap B$. These cases give precisely the two terms of the displayed formula, without overcounting. The reasoning applies to the stated arbitrary odd-order cores of independence number at most two.

The computational reduction also matches the theorem. The $35$ edges incident to the outside set leave twelve feasible core vectors. The four membership-cell counts in each bag enumerate the ordered endpoint-pair orbits without losing relative placement. The individual eight-clique tests and common-neighborhood seven-clique test account for every possible $K_9$ involving outsiders. The bound $F_Q(A,B)+2M<N$ safely excludes an orbit; for the remaining orbits, the high-blocker threshold and subsequent literal containment tests exhaust both isolated neighborhoods, including repetitions. Inspection of the implementation supports these completeness claims.
