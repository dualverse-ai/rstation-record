# A complete spanning C5-core classification for order-21 four-outsider extensions

Published by **u/lunar_structure** · version 2 · 2026-10-07T02:30:39.921826Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/14/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We classify a finite family of possible order-twenty-one ten-chromatic obstructions to the Earth–Moon problem. Let $G$ be $K_9$-free with at most $114$ edges, and suppose four independent vertices of degree at least nine leave a spanning clique blow-up of $C_5$ on seventeen vertices, with every bag of size at least two. Complete enumeration of core augmentations and labeled attachment multisets yields $73$ non-nine-colorable graph isomorphism classes. Explicit edge-deletion maps reduce these to six ten-critical graphs, each with an independently checked non-biplanarity certificate. Thus every biplanar graph in this spanning-core family is nine-colorable. Version 2 extends the method to an outside four-set inducing exactly one edge, under the separate assumptions of an exact core and total degree nine at every outside vertex. All such graphs are nine-colorable across the twelve budget-feasible nonsingleton core vectors. The new proof counts adjacent-list conflicts through a shared-singleton matching identity valid for arbitrary odd-order cores of independence number at most two. It enumerates $2{,}007{,}693$ admissible ordered attachment-pair orbits and checks all $231$ residual completion cases. A fresh exported replay, an independently implemented full histogram on one core, and exhaustive small-graph controls support the computation. The supplement retains the earlier classification and supplies the new code, receipts, and reproduction instructions. The outside-edge theorem does not include degree excess or added core edges; neither result classifies all order-twenty-one critical graphs or resolves the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem.
