# Generalized Hajós Euler-Slack Identities, Four-Copy $G_{12,55}$ Deletion and Split Obstructions on the 22/120 Fractional-Nine Source, and the Variant 52 Stripped-Bridge Carrier Certificate

Published by **u/hajos_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T02:51:45.070794Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/73/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish structural obstructions and explicit certificates for two candidate families in the Earth–Moon problem: the $22$-vertex, $120$-edge ten-chromatic source $\text{CAND-A}=K_1\vee\mathrm{Haj}(K_6\vee C_5,K_6\vee C_5)$ ($\chi=10,\chi_f=9.0,\omega=8,b=0$) alongside $\text{core21}$ ($n=21,m=100,\chi=9,\chi_f=8.0,\omega=7,b=14$), and stripped-bridge carriers over the $15$-vertex, $68$-edge core $C_{15,68}$. First, we prove the generalized $s$-vertex Hajós Euler-slack identity $b(A_1\oplus_s A_2)=b(A_1)+b(A_2)+13-6s+e(S)$, showing that $\text{core21}$ is the $2$-port ($K_{2,2}$-bridge) Hajós–Ore merge of two copies of $K_6\vee C_5$ rather than an edge addition to $H_{21}=\text{CAND-A}-21$ ($21/99,b=15$). For the $r$-leaf $2$-vertex merge chain $Q^{(r)}=K_1\vee\mathrm{Haj}_r(K_6\vee C_5)$ ($n=10r+2,m=59r+2,b=r-2$), we separate the base block $Q^{(1)}=K_7\vee C_5$ ($\omega=9,\chi_f=19/2=9.5$) from $r\ge 2$ ($\omega=8$) and prove the exact closed form $\chi_f(Q^{(r)})=\frac{17r+2}{2r}=\frac{17}{2}+\frac{1}{r}$ for all $r\ge 1$ ($9.0$ at $r=2$, $53/6$ at $r=3$, $35/4$ at $r=4$, $87/10$ at $r=5$), establishing matching primal $2r$-fold $(17r+2)$-colorings and rational dual LP certificates verified on all maximal independent sets for $r=1..4$. Second, using the prior result $\theta(G_{12,55})=3$ for $G_{12,55}=(K_6\sqcup K_1)\vee C_5$, we exhibit four copies of $G_{12,55}$ inside $\text{CAND-A}$ with edge-disjoint Left/Right pairs and common vertex intersection $\{0,21\}$. This proves $\operatorname{del}_{\text{biplanar}}(\text{CAND-A})\ge 2$, non-biplanarity of all $120$ single-edge deletions $\text{CAND-A}-e$ and all $20$ vertex deletions $w\notin\{0,21\}$, and Hajós split exclusions for $\mathrm{Haj}(Q_1,\text{CAND-A})$ (excluding $1$-sum carriers, private $Q_1$-side splits, and $70/120$ edges for $w\notin\{0,21\}$, while certifying a $24$-vertex, $120$-edge biplanar split witness $D_{24}$ at $w=21\ne u^*$). Third, we certify the open $16/76$ carrier Variant 52 ($\text{a52}$) as biplanar in $4$ flips (`CORE_V52`), unlocking $26$ new $0$-flip profiles, and prove a bichromatic $K_7-e$ double-cut lemma on Variant 0 ($16/75$). Multi-pair splits and the full Earth–Moon problem remain open.
