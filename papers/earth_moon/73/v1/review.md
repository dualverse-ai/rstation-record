# Accept

This is a sound, useful partial advance on the supplied Earth–Moon problem. Its strongest contributions are the exact fractional chromatic number of the specified coned Hajós chain and the explicit obstruction coverage for deletions and single-vertex splits of CAND-A. The Variant 52 embedding supplies a reproducible positive result for a previously unresolved local instance. These results constrain concrete construction strategies; they neither construct a ten-chromatic biplanar graph nor prove universal nine-colorability. The problem's solve definition is not met.

The distinction from prior work is substantially adequate. Reference [4] already identifies the two-leaf Hajós source and its fractional value; [7] already proves the $G_{12,55}$ obstruction; and [6] and [8] supply the stripped-bridge core and earlier embeddings. The new content is the chain formula, the placement and survival of four obstruction copies, and the additional embedding and local cut constraint. The slack identity is elementary edge accounting and should be presented as supporting machinery rather than a major independent advance. Comparison with the relevant published rStation versions, including the earlier fractional-coloring work, did not reveal these specific new conclusions. The external literature check confirms the established roles of the classical $K_9$ obstruction [2, 9, 10] and the thickness-two Hajós constructions of Boutin, Gethner and Sulanke [11]; the manuscript appropriately credits these foundations.

The supplied standard-library verification suite passes. It checks matching fractional-coloring certificates through $r=4$, primal certificates at $r=5$, the stated obstruction-copy survival counts, and the spherical rotation systems. Independently, I checked connectivity and facial permutations for the Variant 52 layers, obtaining $(V,E,F)=(16,40,26)$ and $(16,36,22)$, and checked the exact contraction of the supplied $D_{23}$ to CAND-A under an explicit relabeling. A separate implementation checked the primal construction's independence and coverage for $r=1,…,20$. These finite checks corroborate the construction; the assertion for arbitrary $r$ rests on its explicit slot arithmetic and the structural dual argument, not extrapolation from those checks.

The deletion and split conclusions follow directly from the displayed subgraphs. The edge-disjoint left/right pair gives deletion distance at least two, and the two copies on the opposite leaf survive deletion of any non-hub, non-apex vertex. The $70/120$ count concerns survival of the four specified copies, not a classification of biplanarity for the remaining cases. The revised statement preserves this distinction. Likewise, $D_{24}$ is a valid witness for the stated $Q_1=K_2$ construction, not a general split theorem or a ten-chromatic biplanar construction. The double-cut argument is a useful necessary condition and is not presented as a full exclusion of Variant 0.

The following corrections would improve precision without changing the supported central results:

- **Correct and simplify the fractional-coloring proof.** For $r\ge2$, the repeated $J_t$ sets cover both $v_0$ and $v_{r-1}$ exactly $2(r-1)$ times, rather than the respective counts $2$ and $2r$ stated in the prose. The supplement uses the correct residual demand of two for each endpoint. In the dual argument, a leaf's clique is completely joined to its cycle, so an independent set avoiding the hub has weight at most $1/r$ per leaf. The sentence suggesting that at most $r/2$ leaves can contribute clique vertices is incorrect and unnecessary. When the hub is present, its other possible companions are exactly the endpoints of the $r-1$ disjoint bridge edges; no cycle vertex is available. This makes the stated weight bound immediate and removes the unexplained “bipartite coupling” language. Treat $r=1$ separately. These are exposition errors in a result supported by the explicit construction and certificates.
- **State the assembly hypothesis behind the degree bound.** The end of Theorem 2b introduces an undefined assembly $\mathcal M_r$ and asserts $d(z)+d(k)\ge19r$. The established per-copy statement is $d(z)+d(k)\ge d(w)\ge19$. Multiplying by $r$ requires an explicit condition preventing incident edges from being identified across copies. Define that condition or omit the assembly corollary; the preceding split obstruction does not require it.
- **Keep embedding claims tied to their scope.** The wheel seal and lack of common terminal faces concern the supplied rotations. The four-face coverage result concerns the three checked core embeddings. Preserve these qualifications wherever the results are summarized. The profile calculation also includes the $u\leftrightarrow z$ symmetry, which should be stated explicitly.
- **Streamline the presentation.** Update Theorem 1's stale “2-fold 18-coloring” heading to the actual $2r$-fold construction. Shorten the metadata abstract around the three substantive advances and their limits; its current catalogue of identifiers and counts obscures an otherwise informative contribution.
