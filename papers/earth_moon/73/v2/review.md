Three corrections should accompany the update:

- **Distinguish the two bridge graphs in Theorem 1(4).** As explicitly defined on all $5r$ private clique vertices, $H_{\mathrm{br}}(T)$ does not have independence number $r-1$. Already for $T=K_2$, choosing an unused vertex from each clique gives an independent set of size two. More generally, one can choose one vertex per clique recursively along any tree, avoiding its single forbidden parent choice. The intended equality is
  $$
  \alpha\bigl(H_{\mathrm{br}}(T)[P^{\mathrm{del}}(T)]\bigr)=r-1.
  $$
  The actual dual proof correctly restricts the hub's companions to these deleted-hub ports, so this correction does not invalidate the fractional formula. Likewise, unused ports remain adjacent to the hub; they are not what permits the cyclic hub-containing independent set. That role belongs to the directed-cycle construction later in the proof. Correct the introductory summary accordingly.

- **Explain the general upper-bound constructions explicitly.** Simply ordering a tree's edges does not turn it into the path construction. The supplement uses a different, sound construction: take the parent-directed port selection for every possible root, twice, and allocate the residual clique demands recursively. State that construction and its slot-capacity argument. In the cyclic case, justify the eight residual clique sets: after selecting one deleted-hub port per block, the remaining graph consists of $K_4$ blocks with matching bridge edges. Under $\Delta(G)\le4$, every block retains a bridge-free vertex. Removing those vertices leaves maximum degree at most three, so the residual graph is four-colorable; repeat its color classes twice. These arguments establish the universal claims without extrapolating from the seven tested topologies.

- **Correct the four-neighbor insertion tests.** In `verify_post173_carriers_and_4face.py`, the four-neighbor branches of `check_n1_t8`, `check_n1_t9`, and `state_can_accommodate_l1` add every pair in the neighbor set. This unnecessarily demands a $K_4$. For a chosen temporary diagonal $xy$ with remaining vertices $a,b$, the two required facial triangles need the boundary edges and $xy$, but do not require adding $ab$. A four-cycle with a hub on one side is a concrete control: inserting another vertex adjacent to the four rim vertices gives the planar octahedron, whereas completing the rim to $K_4$ creates a $K_5$ with the original hub. I reproduced this false rejection and checked the positive insertion's spherical rotation system, obtaining $(V,E,F)=(6,12,8)$. In the targeted replay, I omitted the unnecessary opposite diagonal from newly added edges in all three routines, retaining any such edge already present. The reported counts, including the $158$ and $922$ state lists and zero surviving pairs, remain unchanged. Correct the reusable predicates and retain this positive control.

The Variant 0 conclusion remains a finite, computer-assisted exclusion of the specified graph. Its scope does not extend to the remaining $17/83$ assembly, arbitrary carrier embeddings, or universal nine-colorability.
