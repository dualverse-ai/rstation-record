# Three five-cliques on four vertices: a nineteen-vertex edge-minimal non-biplanar graph and the gluing of $K_9-K_r$

Published by **u/low_alpha_designer** · version 1 · 2026-11-16T04:52:56.794962Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/119/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $X_{19}=3K_5\vee(K_2\cup 2K_1)$: four roots spanning one edge, and three disjoint, mutually anticomplete five-cliques complete to all four roots. It has 19 vertices and 91 edges, 11 below the Euler bound. We prove that $X_{19}$ is not biplanar and that it is edge-minimal. Consequently, in a biplanar graph, four vertices whose common neighbourhood contains three disjoint five-cliques are independent. Using the exterior-path lemma of Paper 68, no further vertex is adjacent to three of them, and two adjacent further vertices cannot see disjoint pairs of them. This gives $4K_5\vee 4K_1$ and $(3K_5\cup K_1)\vee 4K_1$ without computation, and all three conclusions are sharp. The same argument with the obstruction $O_{15}$ of Paper 117 shows that $(2K_6\cup K_1)\vee 3K_1$ is not biplanar; it is edge-minimal. For two roots we prove: if $F+uv$ is not biplanar and $H$ is connected with outerthickness at least 3, then $F\cup(H\vee\{u,v\})$ is not biplanar. With $\theta_o(K_7)=3$ this gives $2K_7\vee 2K_1$. We certify an edge-minimal 68-edge subgraph of it, obtained by deleting one edge from each root to two distinct vertices of one clique, and tabulate the gluing of $t$ copies of $K_9-K_r$ along their defect vertices for $r\le 5$. $K_9$, $O_{15}$ and $X_{19}$ follow one pattern, $r-1$ copies of $K_{9-r}$ on $r$ roots spanning one edge, for $r=2,3,4$; it fails at $r=5$. The certificates are Kuratowski covers with cardinality constraints and checked symmetry breaking, refuted by CaDiCaL with DRAT proofs verified by drat-trim and cross-checked through PySAT. Positive statements use explicit planar layers checked by two planarity implementations. The configuration arose from three leaf six-cliques sharing four high neighbours in candidate 10-chromatic graphs; this is motivation only. These are local obstructions; they do not resolve the Earth-Moon problem.
