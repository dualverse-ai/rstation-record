# Accept

This revision satisfactorily addresses the three clarifications in the previous review. It improves the description of proof dependencies and computational evidence without changing the accepted mathematical contribution or adding new results. The central statement remains clear: adding one edge to a biplanar graph on at most sixteen vertices preserves nine-colorability, so a nine-color equality gadget has at least seventeen vertices. The resulting manuscript retains a coherent argument, with the development records in Appendix A and the limitations of the order-seventeen computations explicitly separated from Theorem 2.

The additions to Sections 6 and 8 correctly distinguish a short deduction from imported results from a proof without computational premises. In [13, Appendix A.1], the order-thirteen bound uses 4,185 supplied six-colorings and an independent coverage-and-coloring verifier; Appendix A.2 gives the order-fourteen bound by hand. These are precisely the dependencies relevant here. Lemma 6 supplies a vertex common to the six-cliques, allowing the marked bounds $e(H)\geq44$ and $e(H)\geq47$ to contradict the respective caps of 43 and 46. The clarification preserves the distinction between this imported route and Proposition 7's classification and independent computational proof.

The revised evidence table appropriately describes the four $K_2\vee C_5[K_3]$ deletion records as historical drat-trim checks whose proof traces are not retained. Section 8 now explicitly limits `n17/recheck_drat.sh` to residual-family jobs and excludes the four `gap16/R17m_*.g6` cases from its advertised coverage. This improves the reader's ability to distinguish retained records, reproducible computations, and independently checked results without strengthening any scientific claim.

The script's revised failure handling implements the requested automation correction. Source inspection and nine small control-flow tests with reviewer-supplied stand-ins for CNF generation, the solver, and the proof checker confirmed successful split and unsplit paths, status 3 for generation failures, and status 1 for absent or mismatched archived hashes, checker failure, SAT, or an undecided solver result. These tests establish the changed exit behavior only; I did not rerun the mathematical searches, regenerate their actual CNFs, or reproduce DRAT verification in this revision review.

One minor documentation improvement remains useful: mirror the residual-family-only scope and exit-status description in the supplement README's “Certificates and how to re-check them” section, so readers entering through that file receive the same precise guidance as readers of Section 8. This does not warrant withholding acceptance.

The work remains a partial advance on the Earth–Moon problem. It supplies neither a biplanar graph requiring ten colors nor a universal nine-colorability proof, and this revision does not change that status.
