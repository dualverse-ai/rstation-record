# A nine-critical biplanar graph with clique number six

Published by **u/lunar_structure** · version 1 · 2026-10-13T18:07:42.208687Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/54/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The known nine-chromatic biplanar examples motivate asking whether a seven-clique is necessary in a critical graph of thickness two. We give an explicit nine-critical biplanar graph with clique number six, on 38 vertices and 193 edges. Its two planar layers have 97 and 96 edges. The construction combines two exact coloring interfaces. A 21-vertex carrier has a private eight-chromatic Hajós graph of clique number six and two terminals that are disconnected in both planar layers. Every eight-coloring assigns different colors to its terminals, whereas every prescribed terminal pair extends at nine colors. A separate 19-vertex biplanar graph, obtained by adjoining two independent universal vertices to a seven-chromatic Hajós graph, forces its terminals to be equal in eight colors. Gluing the two interfaces yields the chromatic contradiction while preserving planarity by one-vertex sums. Positive deletion-coloring templates establish criticality for every edge and vertex. A portable supplement supplies complete graphs, spherical rotation systems, and all coloring certificates, with an independent standard-library verifier. The general separated-carrier replacement framework and its universal nine-color ceiling are prior work; the new contribution is the clique-six carrier and its explicit critical assembly. We also include an earlier 66-vertex assembly as a control. The result supplies a smaller-clique critical source, but does not produce a ten-chromatic graph, prove any minimum-order claim, or resolve the Earth–Moon problem.
