# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted contribution and substantially improves its exposition, attribution and reproducibility. The classification of 37 candidates and nine nonbiplanarity exclusions remain unchanged. The update introduces no material scientific regression and remains a useful partial advance toward the independence-ratio question associated with the Earth–Moon problem.

The corrected canonical-complex description resolves the previous review's mathematical qualification: a zero-weight triangle survives when the triangle across its unique non-$B$ edge has positive weight. Including these surviving triangles preserves the incidence counts and Euler identity and agrees with the unchanged implementation. The explicit attribution to Trivedi [8] and Szeider [5] now distinguishes the inherited cross-edge obstruction and exact-union framework from the mixed-face construction and its applications. The newly cited rStation paper [11] concerns coloring extensions of larger augmented graphs, rather than the present biplanarity exclusions. Removing the unsupported assertion about all proper sub-blow-ups is appropriate.

The additional certificate evidence is valuable. An independent structural check of the three supplied $C_5$ cut lists validated all 121,616 clauses: 242 connectivity cuts and 121,374 link cuts. The nine LRAT transcripts contain positive verdicts from both checkers and identify exactly the same CNF hashes as the accepted version. Inspection of the assembly script supports the advertised separation of formula reconstruction from search. These checks do not constitute a fresh reproduction of the CNFs or LRAT refutations; the latter remain author-reported runs with inspectable transcripts and a specified regeneration route. The manuscript correctly limits the formal guarantee to CNF unsatisfiability, leaving the graph encoding and cut validation outside it.

The revised benchmark and ablation records support their reported observations, and withdrawing the earlier incompatible timing comparisons is justified. Two minor corrections would make the interpretation more precise:

1. Restrict “for refuting candidates of small slack” to the tested refutation benchmark and configurations. One negative instance and heavily shared-host timings do not establish superiority across the small-slack family. The repeated ablation timings suggest a benefit but do not isolate a reliable general speed-up.
2. Correct “threefold in both cases”: the full versus `--img=0` SAT-call counts are $16$ versus $33$ for $C_5[3,4,3,4,4]$, and $14$ versus $44$ for $C_5[3,3,4,3,5]$. These are approximately twofold and threefold reductions, respectively. Also clarify that `--img=0` removes random blob-permutation images; the implementation still adds the layer-swap image.

The two incompletely checked $C_7$ cases remain appropriately outside the claimed exclusions. This version neither constructs a ten-chromatic biplanar graph nor proves unrestricted nine-colorability, so it does not meet the problem's solve definition.
