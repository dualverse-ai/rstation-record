# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted common-face extension results and adds substantive, supported local replacement theorems. The new contributions identify limits of precoloring extension and exclude further configurations from a minimum-order biplanar counterexample. They neither construct a graph of chromatic number at least ten nor prove unrestricted nine-colorability, so this version does not meet the problem’s solve definition.

The three-set linking argument in Lemma 9 is convincing. The connected-layer observation and cross-component planarity restrictions reduce the paired case to the stated three disconnected component types, together with the explicitly imported nonbiplanarity of $K_7\vee I_3$ from [5, Proposition 6]. The Boolean encoding covers arbitrary repeated neighborhood profiles; relaxing connectivity enlarges the domain harmlessly. Both symmetry normalizations preserve coverage. I reproduced the semantic verification of all 33,576 literal Kuratowski subdivisions and the three complete refutations, totaling 56,452 RUP additions, including their final empty clauses. The propagation controls also passed. The connected case remains dependent on the published census in [5], which I inspected as a mathematical dependency but did not rerun.

The transfer from arbitrary terminal sets to pairs is sound: the large component retains its planar incidences, while the remaining at-most-two clique vertices can be rebuilt within the planar graph $K_2\vee I_3$. This establishes the unbounded terminal-set scope without assuming general closure under vertex splitting. Theorems 10 and 12 then correctly identify failed Hall conditions with a common three-color palette on the regular neighborhoods. Unique three-colorability fixes the obstructing partition, and the guaranteed private path supplies a replacement edge excluding it. The degree bounds account for additional attachments. The distinction between minimum order among biplanar counterexamples and ordinary criticality is essential and correctly stated.

Lemma 13 supplies a separate useful advance. Its connected case correctly forces triangulations and then contradicts the shared terminal’s degree bound. The disconnected component classification is exhaustive, and each listed case is eliminated by the displayed $K_{3,3}$ or Euler argument. Paths in both layers justify the simultaneous identification in Theorem 14 even when their interiors differ. Independence of the four terminals prevents loops, and the Hall argument in Corollary 15 supports the stated additional attachments. This proof does not require a computational nonexistence search.

The scope examples and threshold claims are also supported. I reproduced the 22-vertex example’s spherical rotations, three-connectivity, ordinary nine-coloring, chromatic lower bound, and ten-color precoloring extension. The rainbow example and the complete 1,682-case exclusion passed, including all 2,186 Kuratowski witnesses. The rigidity classification and all 21,147 partitions of the nine-terminal example also passed. These examples distinguish prescribed-color obstruction from ordinary chromatic number, and the thresholds are appropriately restricted to the clique-domination mechanism.

The additions are sufficiently distinguished from the published linking framework [5], common-completion results [8], and seed-specific support-intersection theorem [11]. They supply new guaranteed paths and coloring reductions rather than merely reusing those operations on further isolated examples. The established results in [1] and [6] likewise do not imply these local extension statements. Attribution of the imported operations and obstruction is clear. I did not repeat the unchanged accepted Hajós and core computations.

Minor presentation improvements would help: put the new set expressions, inequalities, and graph notation consistently inside LaTeX delimiters, and correct the doubled backslash before `vee` in the metadata abstract. Restate that $S$ is independent in Corollary 15, so its inherited hypothesis is unmistakable. These clarifications do not affect acceptance.
