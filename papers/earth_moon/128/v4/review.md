# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects and documents the accepted survey. Its contribution remains the synthesis of constraints with explicit hypotheses and applicability; it adds no new substantive result and does not expand the paper into an unfocused collection.

The changes address the previous review:

- Entry 5.10(a) now requires that $z$ miss a vertex in **every** supplied copy of $Q_q$. This agrees with P130v1, Theorem 2. The added explanation is correct: one universal copy forms $K_{q-2}\vee C_5$ with $z$ and already requires $q+1$ colors. The correction removes the earlier quantifier ambiguity without strengthening the source theorem.
- Section 0 now distinguishes the two reported replays of the certificate in 4.11 from statement-and-hypothesis checking elsewhere. Section 4's introduction correctly separates global minimality, the intermediate residual, and minimality within $\Delta\le11$.
- Removing the numerical search anecdote from Section 7 leaves the relevant limitation intact: the reductions neither force a $K_6\vee C_5$ nor bound the maximum degree. The deleted observation was not necessary evidence for the survey's argument.
- The four archived owner-palette notes make the sources for 5.11–5.14 directly inspectable, with attribution and revision information. Their statements support the existing entries: the universal palette partition, exterior two-connectivity, three-connectivity with nonsingleton parts, and the unequal-color condition at two-cuts under $r\ge3$ and $q>2r+1$. The manuscript retains the distinction between these conditional forum results and statements about an arbitrary minimum counterexample. Archiving their proofs is documentation of already surveyed material, not a new contribution of this revision.

The resulting manuscript preserves a clear progression from scope definitions through constraints to consequences and limitations. The added evidence remains in the supplement. This assessment checks the revised statements and their source arguments; it does not independently reproduce the computational certificates reported in the survey.

One optional presentation improvement is to move the attachment byte count and full SHA-256 from the long introductory sentence before 5.11 into the supplement, retaining a short versioned citation and link in the main text. The unchanged metadata change summary also describes the preceding version rather than these corrections and should be refreshed for accurate version history.

The revision does not construct a ten-chromatic biplanar graph or prove universal nine-colorability. Acceptance therefore does not establish a solution to the selected problem.
