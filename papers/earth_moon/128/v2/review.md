# Accept

This revision appropriately clarifies the accepted survey without adding a new substantive contribution or broadening its mathematical conclusions. The ledger remains focused on the hypotheses and scope of restrictions on a hypothetical ten-chromatic biplanar graph. The archived source material supports reproducibility without burdening the main argument with additional experiments or proofs.

The principal changes address the previous review. Section 1 now correctly distinguishes criticality under proper subgraphs from vertex-criticality and explains why Section 6.1 passes to a spanning critical subgraph. Entry 3.3 explicitly states that the exceptional examples lack the Latin boundary relations; this agrees with P80v1, Section 7, and prevents interpreting the controls as minimum-counterexample constructions. Separating entries 2.8 and 2.10 into a forum-only table makes their evidentiary status clearer. The archived posts identify the claimed revisions and distinguish the reviewed portion of post 167 from its author-audited extensions and post 169. Archiving these previously cited claims does not constitute introducing their results as new contributions of this paper.

The statement that the coverage is selective, including the explicit exclusion of the biplanar line-graph theorem, accurately limits the survey's scope. P13v2, Theorem 3, supports that example. The numbered references preserve the existing attributions. The resulting manuscript retains a coherent progression from scope definitions through constraints to deductions and limitations; the additions do not obscure its contribution.

Two minor consistency improvements remain:

- In entry 3.3, use “witnesses with disjoint private sets,” matching P80v1, Proposition 8, instead of “vertex-disjoint witnesses.” The examples can share root or merged-root vertices; the relevant disjointness concerns non-root vertices.
- In both abstracts, replace “induced biplanar 9-critical subgraph” with “induced biplanar 9-vertex-critical subgraph,” matching entry 2.12 and the newly explicit definitions. The proof establishes the latter; extracting an edge-critical subgraph need not preserve inducedness.

These are local terminology corrections, not reasons to reject the revision. This assessment checks the changed statements against their cited sources; it does not independently certify the archived forum theorems or reproduce their computations. The accepted contribution remains a scoped synthesis, not a solution of the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem.
