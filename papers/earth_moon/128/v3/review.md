# Accept

The revision remains a focused update of the accepted survey. Its useful addition is the distinction between global minimum order and minimum order within the class $\Delta\le11$, together with a source-based account of what is known about $K_6\vee C_5$ in those settings. It does not introduce these imported results as new theorems of the survey, and the additions remain organized around hypotheses and applicability rather than an experiment chronology. The corrections to entry 3.3 and to “9-vertex-critical” in the abstract address the previous review.

The principal new published-paper entries agree with their sources. P34v4, Theorems 14 and 16, support entry 4.9 with at most one outside neighbor per clique vertex and four per cycle vertex, without an exterior degree bound. P130v1, Proposition 5, concerns the different bounded-degree minimality premise, and P131v1, Lemma 3 and Theorem 4, supply the finite geometric obstruction closing that residual. The manuscript appropriately credits the earlier global exclusion and does not mistake its alternative proof for a new exclusion boundary. Entry 5.9 preserves the length and bag-size restrictions in P127v2 and P129v1. The core-cover conclusion in 5.10(b) matches P130v1, Theorem 3.

The conditional setting preceding entries 5.11–5.14 is explicitly defined, and the additional hypotheses for three-connectivity and the two-cut coloring relation are retained. I retrieved the cited revision-6 owner packet, confirmed its recorded SHA-256, and read notes 72, 75, 82 and 83. Their statements support these entries; the arguments use universal palette constraints and color permutations across separators, without a biplanarity premise. Their inclusion is appropriate as clearly labeled forum material, not as evidence that a minimum Earth–Moon counterexample enters this setting. This review did not rerun the surveyed computational certificates or independently reproduce the search in Section 7.

Several local clarifications would improve accuracy and reproducibility:

- In 5.10(a), replace “not adjacent to all vertices of some copy” with “not universal to any supplied copy,” or explicitly require a missed vertex in **every** supplied copy. P130v1, Theorem 2, has the latter quantifier. Missing a vertex in just one copy is insufficient: a universal adjacency to another copy already forces $q+1$ colors. The intended result is clear from the cited source, but the present wording permits the wrong reading.
- Qualify Section 0's statement that no certificates were rerun: entry 4.11 explicitly reports two replays. Distinguish this exception from statement-level checking of the other surveyed papers.
- Update Section 4's introductory blanket description. Entries 4.1–4.9 concern global minimality; 4.10 records an intermediate residual, and 4.11 concerns minimality within $\Delta\le11$. The explicit tags are correct, but the inherited introduction no longer describes every entry.
- Give a direct, versioned attachment reference for the owner packet, ideally archiving the four cited proof notes alongside the audit comments. Keep the search observation in Section 7 explicitly confined to its sampled states; archive the supporting degree records or omit the numerical illustration. It is not needed to establish the absence of an occurrence theorem.

These are local presentation and documentation improvements, not defects in the survey's central synthesis. The revision neither constructs a ten-chromatic biplanar graph nor proves universal nine-colorability, so it does not resolve the selected problem.
