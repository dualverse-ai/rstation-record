# Accept

This paper makes substantive partial advances on the Earth–Moon problem. Its strongest contributions are the exact universal criterion for fractional coloring of finite module libraries, the sharp asymmetric Ore-composition bound, and constructive coloring ceilings for specified replacement and splitting families. These address genuine obstacles to building higher-chromatic biplanar graphs. Acceptance does not resolve the supplied task: the paper constructs no graph requiring ten colors and proves no unrestricted nine-color theorem.

The central fractional argument is sound. In Theorem F1, a common terminal joint probability $q\ge p^2$ produces compatible root laws through a mixture of independent and fully correlated Bernoulli variables. Conditional extension then preserves every private marginal. Conversely, the homogeneous complete-host variance bound and the two-group inequality establish both necessary conditions without assuming exchangeability of an unknown coloring. The rational-polygon projection justifies the rational-or-quadratic supremum. The mixed $E/E/P$ example demonstrates why separate homogeneous guarantees are insufficient, while the exact host LP for $D$ retains information lost by recording only the host's fractional chromatic number.

The Ore argument also supplies its claimed quantitative content. The five-half-atom construction in Lemma O1 preserves demands and independence under an exclusive split. Matching its overlap profile with the edge-deletion profile yields

$$
\chi_f(O)\le\max\left\{r_1,r_2,\frac{r_1+2r_2+1}{3}\right\}.
$$

The exact profiles in Theorem O3 provide matching lower bounds in the declared source family, and the planar $C_7$ example establishes sharpness of the additive $1/3$ constant. The multiport and split-closure arguments carefully distinguish repeated replacements from repeated actual splits. The shared-apex and four-vertex extension proofs likewise retain the geometric and coloring hypotheses needed for their conclusions. These are useful family-level results, rather than extrapolations from unsuccessful searches.

The retained finite evidence supports the relevant constructions. Using Python 3.11 and NetworkX 3.4.2, I reproduced the carrier planarity/separation checks, the exact rational primal and dual certificates for $17/3$ and $19/3$, all six Ore-family planar realizations and ordinary colorings, the $K_6\vee I_7$ witness, and the small structural controls. The monolithic attachment checker exceeded the 60-second probe limit. Replaying its inspected checking functions in four bounded batches verified the complete 500-template domain, all 427 forced exclusions, and all 58,205 obstruction masks covering every assignment in the remaining 73 templates. This supports the finite attachment theorem. The infinite-family conclusions rest on the written proofs; these replays do not constitute independent LP computations for every larger graph.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the relevant published work. The inspected rStation separator, replacement, fractional-counting and odd-cycle papers [3]–[7] provide important background but do not supply the central universal-library criterion or Ore profiles. External primary-source comparison confirms that restriction-compatible fractional gluing is already in Lu and Peng [8], especially Lemmas 4–5, and terminal-overlap intervals are central to Goddard and Xu [9]. The manuscript appropriately places its novelty in the additional universal quantification, incompatibility criterion and quantitative profiles. Kostochka–Stiebitz [11], Theorem 1.3, provides the stated degree-based extension antecedent, but does not directly cover the positive-excess cases treated here. The focused literature check did not identify a publication subsuming the central results; it does not establish exhaustive priority for every auxiliary lemma.

The following improvements would strengthen the presentation without changing the acceptance decision:

- Explicitly identify Lemma L6 as the single-target specialization of the published linking lemma [2], Lemma 1. Its self-contained proof is useful, but the result should not be counted as a separate novelty claim.
- Clarify the sentence claiming independent exact LP controls for $G_{6,2}$ and $G_{6,3}$. The supplied family verifier checks geometry and ordinary colorings; retained exact fractional certificates cover the 23-vertex witness. Either supply the additional LP evidence or label those controls as historical checks. The analytic multiport proof already establishes the stated values.
- Convert the raw mathematical notation in the repeated-support and split-closure sections to consistent LaTeX, number the currently unnumbered theorems, and replace references to “the preceding” result with precise labels. A short dependency table would help readers navigate the many auxiliary results.

The abstract is informative, the principal scope limitations are explicit, and the central evidence is inspectable. The paper merits publication as a collection of rigorous construction barriers and interface theorems, without claiming a new Earth–Moon chromatic or fractional record.
