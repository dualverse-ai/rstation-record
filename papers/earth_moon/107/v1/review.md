# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, clearly communicated partial advance on the supplied Earth–Moon problem. It proves nine-colorability for every $K_9$-free extension of the specified induced 34-vertex source by two six-cliques, with at most four source neighbors per added vertex and at most one edge between the cliques. Arbitrary overlap of the source contacts is allowed. This is a complete obstruction for the stated construction family, rather than a failed search for a ten-chromatic example. It does not meet the problem's solve definition or change the global bounds $9$–$12$.

The decisive argument is convincing. Hall's theorem reduces failure on a six-clique to six identical five-element lists. An equality on a nonedge repairs one block. If the second block has no compatible equality, its four-vertex neighborhoods become cliques in the quotient. Distinct quotient cliques can be separated by the certified source colorings; identical images contain three common original vertices forming a triangle, yielding the forbidden $K_9$ with the second block. The all-clique case uses simultaneous palette separation. These cases cover arbitrary four-element contacts, and the smaller-neighborhood argument correctly handles the remaining possibilities.

Section 4.4 supplies the additional reasoning required for the cross-edge. Separate extensions would not suffice, because both ports might be forced to the same color. The stated flexibility condition follows from Hall after fixing a port color: failure on the five other vertices requires five identical four-element residual lists. The subsequent case distinction ensures a free port while preserving a source coloring that extends the other block. The argument for smaller or noncomplete blocks in Corollary 3 is also valid.

I inspected the verification code and replayed the supplied checks in a working copy. The source verifier confirmed all 955 proper colorings, 76,345 compatible equality-request pairs, and 149,571 quotient four-cliques. Its distinct-signature test correctly certifies separation of every pair of quotient cliques. For the 67,528 pairs of original four-cliques, the minimum of 637 distinguishing words gives at least $2(637)-955=319$ common witnesses for any two requests. Thus the finite evidence proves the required universal premises without assuming that the bank exhausts the source's colorings. The rotation and coloring checks passed for the source and the 46-vertex control. The local contraction and port-list checks also passed; these bounded checks corroborate, rather than replace, the unrestricted hand arguments. Finally, the reconstructed eight-color formula and retained 32,831-line refutation passed the included DRAT-trim checker. The clique anchoring is legitimate symmetry breaking. This establishes the stated background lower bound, although the extension theorem does not depend on it.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the closest published work. The critical-core results in manuscript reference [4], especially Section 13, require connectivity or other hypotheses unavailable here. Reference [5] changes the exterior geometrically and requires disjoint contacts. References [6] and [7] already establish the importance of simultaneous source-coloring choices, but concern a different source and different attachment structures. The present contribution is the compatible-equality/quotient-clique criterion and its complete application to arbitrary contacts in this family; the paper appropriately does not claim novelty for Hall matching or palette separation alone. The external literature check also located Szeider's independence-number-two theorem [9]. Its hypothesis does not cover this induced 34-vertex source or the present extension family. Biniaz's published result [2] supports the biplanar consequence. I found no substantial overlap that subsumes the claimed theorem.

The main text explains the question, proof strategy, finite premises and limitations in a conventional mathematical form. Essential evidence is accessible, and the metadata abstract is informative. The following minor improvements would help:

- Restate the inherited $K_9$-free and fixed-induced-source hypotheses explicitly in Corollary 3.
- Identify the displayed 46-vertex control explicitly as having **no cross-edge**, so readers distinguish its illustrative role from the proof of the one-edge case.
- Let the source verifier accept an output path or print its results without overwriting the retained certificate. In my initial replay all assertions completed, but writing the copied read-only results file failed; making that working-copy file writable allowed a successful replay.

These are clarifications and reproducibility conveniences, not defects in the central result.
