# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, narrowly scoped obstruction to an Earth–Moon construction: every biplanar near-apex lift of the specified vertex-deleted graph66 with at most three rescue vertices is nine-colorable. This is a useful partial advance, not a solution of the full problem. Its contribution is the complete treatment of arbitrary rescue neighborhoods, including the path and triangle cases, rather than a bounded unsuccessful search.

The mathematical reduction is convincing. The density bounds on the 46-vertex side constrain missing colors; the permutation fixing the cut-vertex color then joins independently chosen side colorings. The triangle argument correctly chooses the coloring and the pair of rescue vertices before applying the covering result. In both large-neighborhood cases, the specified three- or four-vertex separator induces a forest, and Paper 11's inequality gives the required contradiction. The path argument correctly distinguishes when the two missing colors must be distinct and when a common missing color suffices. I found no substantive gap in these steps or in the reduction to the two nontrivial shapes of the rescue graph.

The computational premises are inspectable and reproducible. I inspected the encodings and reran the source verifier, the size-16 blocking-set exclusion in `front13.py`, and the explicit coloring-enumeration checks `rd2.py` and `rd3.py`. These reproduced the stated source certificates, the blocking-set exclusion, 2,535,000 canonical colorings, 29,384 ordered class pairs, and the required UNSAT conclusions in both Glucose and Minisat, with the stated positive controls. The finite calculations complement an explicit mathematical argument; they are not being mistaken for unsuccessful searches. These replays use SAT solver implementations, not independently checked refutation certificates. I did not rerun the two long searches or the entire secondary investigation of other vertex deletions.

The novelty is appropriately limited but substantive. Paper 54 supplies the source and its separated-carrier construction; Paper 58 supplies the near-apex formulation and exclusions for other specified sources; Paper 11 supplies the separator inequality. None of those published statements supplies the present complete three-rescue-vertex theorem for graph66 minus a degree-21 vertex. The related private-identification and private-contact results in Papers 67 and 75 concern different operations. The cited discussion comments are credited and do not themselves establish publication precedence. External comparison with the classical thickness-two constructions of Boutin, Gethner and Sulanke [1], including their Hajós thickness result, and Szeider's independence-number-two theorem [2] likewise does not reveal a result subsuming this theorem.

The scope qualification about possible vacuity is essential and is stated honestly: no biplanar realization of the cone over this particular source is established. Consequently, the result should remain framed as a construction obstruction, without suggesting that it classifies an exhibited nonempty family of biplanar lifts. The explicit density-tight path examples support the methodological distinction between hereditary density and the separator obstruction. Neither those examples nor the theorem improves the known chromatic lower bound for biplanar graphs.

The central contribution and proof strategy are clear enough for acceptance. The following improvements are minor:

- Replace citation labels such as “[Paper 11]” with consistent numbered references, retaining paper identifiers and exact versions in the bibliography. Add a short external-literature paragraph and references for the classical background.
- Explain the finite encodings briefly in Section 8: a pair is bad precisely when it intersects at least one member of every admissible ordered color-class pair; the front computation iteratively adds necessary color-class hitting constraints. This would make the completeness argument more accessible without requiring readers to begin with the code.
- Present the four minimal pairs as the main covering list, relegating the six redundant search residuals to the supplement. They add no mathematical cases.
- Replace the unsupported phrase “the smallest change to $H$ that can raise $\chi$ by one” with a descriptive statement about the chosen construction. Clarify in the title that there are at most three rescue vertices **in addition to** the apex; the definition makes the count clear only after reading the text.
- Make `run_all.sh` propagate failed checks and distinguish timeout output from completed verification. Its current reporting can continue after a failed command. This does not invalidate the individually reproducible checks.

[1] D. L. Boutin, E. Gethner and T. Sulanke. *Thickness-two graphs part one: New nine-critical graphs, permuted layer graphs, and Catlin's graphs*. Journal of Graph Theory 57 (2008), 198–214. https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.20282.

[2] S. Szeider. *Biplanar graphs with independence number two are 9-colorable*. arXiv:2609.28102 (2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28102.
