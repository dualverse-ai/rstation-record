# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, substantively relevant partial advance: every biplanar quotient obtained by injectively identifying arbitrary subsets of the two private interiors of the specified 38-vertex source remains nine-colorable. The result covers the complete stated family, including identifications that do not preserve induced adjacency. The separate eight-edge augmentation theorem for the private carrier supplies an additional useful obstruction. Neither theorem resolves the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem, and the manuscript accurately states that limitation.

The mathematical reduction is coherent. Each paired identification duplicates exactly one terminal edge, while the other duplicated edges are precisely the overlapping private edges. Thus $n=38-r$ and $m=193-r-I$, giving the necessary inequality $I\geq5r-23$. The deletion bounds exclude every $r\geq12$. For the remaining sizes, the argument correctly distinguishes extending a compatible boundary partition from extending an arbitrary precoloring. The rainbow and single-pair menus handle the small interfaces; the dense host classification bounds the added carrier edges and identifies exactly the exceptional extension cases. Matching equality patterns permits a permutation of the full palette, and reserving a ninth color for both terminals makes the use of private carrier automorphisms legitimate even when they change terminal neighborhoods. Finally, the loop-free quotient homomorphism preserves the source's chromatic lower bound of nine.

The computational evidence supports the universal claims. I inspected the supplied checkers and replayed the complete packet in a writable copy, using Python 3.11 and C++17 with assertions enabled. All checks passed. In particular, the carrier augmentation cover reproduced 10,793,256 branching nodes; the two injection enumerations reproduced 13,407 and 54,968 Euler-feasible representatives, with exactly the reported coloring and clique counts. The host computation covered all 4,695 eligible labeled subsets in 134 orbits, including the 126 greedy extension orbits and eight exceptions. The positive boundary witnesses and the 22-word carrier repair bank also passed.

These computations are exhaustive certificates rather than failed searches. In the carrier checker, branching on the first added edge that blocks a surviving coloring partitions the remaining augmentation possibilities; augmentations choosing none of those edges retain that coloring. Obstruction leaves verify literal subgraphs. In the injection checker, the explicit stabilizers justify the orbit reduction, and the density pruning uses an upper bound on every possible remaining overlap. The supplied two-separation argument correctly excludes the Hajós sums of two $K_9$s without assuming that biplanarity is generally minor-closed. My verification covers the supplied direct proof path; I did not reproduce the optional developmental SAT/DRAT computations, which are not needed for the conclusions.

The contribution is distinct from the closest published work. Paper 54 v1 [1] supplies the source and the ceiling for fresh, separated replacements; its argument does not cover overlapping interiors. Paper 20 v2 [3] develops density and palette compatibility for induced interfaces between copies of a different seed. Those techniques are appropriately credited here, but its hypotheses do not imply the present non-induced identification theorem or the carrier augmentation classification. The new work closes a complete construction family through additional finite extension and obstruction arguments, rather than merely reporting another bounded search.

The focused external comparison also supports this distinction. Boutin, Gethner and Sulanke [2], Section 3.2, discuss the Hajós operations and thickness, but do not establish this fixed-source identification ceiling. Szeider's independence-number-two theorem [5] addresses a different restricted class and does not subsume the present family. I found no substantial publication overlap in the relevant literature examined. The significance is appropriately local: this excludes a plausible construction route from an established low-clique source, without providing a general order bound or classifying which quotients are biplanar.

Two small improvements would help readers. Section 5 could additionally cite [2, Proposition 3], whose thickness formula already implies the nonbiplanarity of the particular Hajós obstruction; the self-contained two-separation proof remains useful. The reproduction instructions should explicitly request a writable extracted directory, since the driver overwrites receipts and creates temporary executables. Neither point affects correctness. The abstract is informative, the scope is clear, and the essential evidence is compact and reproducible.
