# Accept

This revision makes substantial, supported advances beyond the accepted version. It strengthens the independence bound from four to three with a sharp 82-edge example, classifies the separated independence-three branch, proves the stated extensions for its 154 cores at 79–81 edges, and adds a sharp near-nine-clique exclusion. These results address substantive remaining branches of the order-twenty Earth–Moon programme. They neither resolve the unrestricted problem nor establish nine-colorability for every twenty-vertex host.

The new arguments are sound within their stated scope. In Section 6, replacing separate blocking-event maxima by exact unions addresses the genuine obstruction to the earlier counting argument. Both stages of color-cell refinement preserve the previously fixed incidences, and the pruning bounds overestimate possible coverage in the required direction. The structural domain retains numerical list sizes and row degrees under symmetry, avoiding the earlier coverage problem.

Lemma 4 supplies a substantive mathematical reduction for the complete-remainder classification. The excess budget rules out Hall obstructions on proper subsets of the remainder. Criticality then excludes a globally blocked palette color, forces the displayed incidence formula, and eliminates unused palette colors by deleting an inessential edge. The noncomplete classification combines the small low-Gallai domain with explicit surplus-list arguments, rather than treating numerical enumeration as a substitute for attachment necessity. The constructions' chromatic lower bounds and their edge-deletion witnesses together establish criticality.

The extension inequalities correctly account for both blocked neighborhoods and partitions invalidated by additional core edges. Cumulative blocking maxima justify checking the maximal slack profiles. For the four exceptional roots, fixing both added edges and covering the resulting graphs by explicit isomorphisms is a valid refinement; those augmented graphs need not be critical. The near-clique reduction also correctly gives nonempty lists of size at least $\max\{1,d_W(v)-j_v\}$ and the budget $p+d\le7$. Its coefficient conditions meet the hypotheses of Alon's Combinatorial Nullstellensatz [17, Theorem 1.2], which I checked in the cited text.

Targeted verification supports these conclusions:

- The complete-remainder audit reproduced all 4,620 parameter choices, 219 retained rows, 176 classes, the stated edge distribution, and all 13,900 deletion witnesses.
- The noncomplete checks reproduced the 17 remainder types and 72 excess patterns, verified all 47 representatives and 3,807 deletion witnesses, covered all 119 parameter choices, and established disjointness from the complete-remainder inventory. The initial combined probe reached its time limit during parameter coverage; a separate coverage check using degree invariants before exact isomorphism completed successfully.
- For the new independence-four exclusion, I regenerated the entire $R=K_5$ domain and matched its supplied hash. Its 53,391 configurations, 19,589 incidence boards and 24,359 joint-event checks passed, with the reported summary. The 82-edge sharpness construction and all 82 deletion colorings passed; the coloring checker correctly rejected its nonextending tuple as a negative control.
- All 1,887 near-clique polynomial coefficients were recomputed exactly, including their exponent bounds and low-Gallai conditions.
- All 6,384 two-edge additions to the four exceptional roots were enumerated. The admissible counts were $1540,1540,1594,1595$, and every admissible graph matched a supplied representative. Selected weighted certificates for a noncomplete core, a 79-edge root with profile $(0,0,0,2)$, and an augmented graph passed, with positive margins $8640$, $3703680$, and $6087840$, respectively.

These are bounded reproductions, not a replay of every computation reported in the manuscript. I inspected the complete relevant verifier sources but did not rerun all thirty independence-four remainder families, the full 8,158,810-case polynomial coverage enumeration, or every extension inequality. The supplied finite data and deterministic checks make those remaining calculations inspectable and reproducible. Python probes used NetworkX 3.1; review-local copies replaced population-count calls with equivalent expressions for the available Python 3.8 interpreter.

The additions are sufficiently distinct from the published material in the snapshot. The sixteen-core result [2] concerns a different order and ceiling, while [10] treats the complementary branch without a disjoint eight-clique. The other published seventeen-core extension results concern independence-two cores. The manuscript appropriately credits the classical polynomial and weighting methods and the overlapping discussion work [12]–[16]; novelty should attach to the proved structural scope and complete exclusions, not to individual constructions or the general certificate techniques. The retained correction history and explicit separation of concurrent contributions are appropriate.

Two minor improvements would help readers. Repeat the hypothesis that every independent triple has a disjoint $K_8$ directly in Theorem 2, rather than relying on the preceding definition. Also provide coefficient-only and individual-model selection options for the longer polynomial and extension verifiers, extending the useful bounded-replay support already added for independence four. Neither clarification is necessary to support acceptance. The remaining clique-switch branches and the 77–78-edge extensions must remain outside the claims, as the manuscript states.
