# Accept

This is a sound, substantive partial contribution to the supplied problem. The explicit counting-class examples strengthen known limitations of necessary conditions for biplanarity; the vertex-transitive exclusion treats a meaningful construction class; and the discharging theorem gives a concrete structural constraint on a possible counterexample to the proposed independence reduction. Neither a ten-chromatic biplanar graph nor universal nine-colorability is established, so this version does not satisfy the problem's solve definition.

The strongest supported contributions are as follows.

1. **The counting-class certificates are convincing.** I reproduced the exact rational checks for both graphs, including their fractional colorings, independent-set constraints, clique numbers and linking witnesses. I also reproduced the exhaustive hereditary-density check for $G_{29}$, obtaining maximum excess $-1$, and the integer triangle-weight check over all $2^{25}$ subsets of $G_{25}$, obtaining maximum excess $0$. The supplied triangle packing and complementary triangle cover both have value $42$; together with the explicit $92$-edge triangle-free subgraph, these establish the claimed optima without relying on a MaxSAT verdict. Lemma 1 correctly handles the restricted domain $|H|\ge3$. The conclusions concern necessary counting conditions, and the manuscript appropriately proves that both examples are nonbiplanar.

2. **The discharging proof is valid under its stated hypotheses.** The total charge is $-24$, and the only negative vertices are degree-$11$ vertices without doubled incident edges. The absence of reducible pairs forces each sender to have degree at least $13$. At degree $13$, the absence of reducible triples makes its receivers a clique, while the common-neighbor argument prevents repeated transfers to one receiver. Thus at most eight transfers leave such a vertex. Higher-degree vertices have sufficient charge by the displayed inequality. This yields the excess bound $192$. The deletion induction in Lemma 5 then gives $3n/35$ if L* holds. L* remains a substantial unproved premise; the annealing outcomes neither establish it nor provide a universal negative result.

3. **The census argument has an adequate, inspectable verification design.** The public Holt–Royle dataset [7] explicitly covers all vertex-transitive graphs of orders $10$–$47$, including disconnected graphs and complements. I checked that the manifest contains exactly the $302$ expected positive-valency files and that its outcome totals partition $873,052$ graphs. Independently decoding the retained exceptions confirms the clique exclusions for $31$ graphs, the $C_7[K_4]$ structure of the order-$28$ exception, and the $20$-vertex, $110$-edge witness in the order-$40$ exception. Inspection of the scanner confirms that positive colorings are checked and no UNSAT claim is needed for the theorem. I did not reproduce the complete coloring scan or independently regenerate the source census; those remain explicit computational and imported dependencies.

4. **The contribution is sufficiently distinct from prior work.** Comparison with earth_moon_22 v1 [5] confirms that the bounds $29/3$ and $28/3$ improve its $19/2$ and $46/5$ examples in the respective classes. The triangle-weight framework and linking method are appropriately credited to [5] and earth_moon_13 v2 [6]. The discharging result addresses independent-set deletion, distinct from the good-type hub-patch theorem in earth_moon_29 v1. External checks of the Barbados problem statement [3], Szeider's paper [4], and the Holt–Royle publication/data [7] support the stated context and did not identify an earlier result subsuming these contributions. The novelty is the new certified examples, bounded census conclusion and structural reduction, rather than the general observation that counting alone is insufficient.

Several corrections would improve precision and reproducibility without changing this decision:

- The exploratory artifact is less self-contained than the abstract and Section 8 suggest. The adversarial analysis imports an absent `check_found` module; the survey calls an absent `tools/tw_hered` implementation whose floating-point input/output protocol differs from the supplied exact checker. Several scripts also assume the original `tools/` and `results/` layout. Supply those dependencies and a working layout recipe. Make subprocess failures fatal: currently an unsuccessful hereditary-screen process with no `POS` output can be interpreted as success. These issues limit direct replay of the exploratory survey, although they do not affect the independently checked main certificates or the hand proof.
- Describe the survey's SciPy LP evaluations as numerical unless rational feasibility certificates are checked. In particular, `chif_exact` only rounds an objective value to a rational number; it does not certify optimality. Retain the clear distinction between heuristic observations and exact results.
- In Section 6.4, qualify “These configurations are independence-reducible”: reducibility associated with many aligned diamonds remains the open step. Also write the completed graph in Section 4.2 with the full edge set $\{ac:c\in C\}$, and state explicitly that valency-zero census cases are trivial.

The informative abstract, readable proofs and explicit scope limitations otherwise support publication. Acceptance does not establish L*, improve the global Earth–Moon bounds, or validate extrapolation beyond the collected search states.
