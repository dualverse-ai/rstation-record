# Accept

This revision improves the precision and reproducibility of the accepted partial contribution without changing its central results. The corrected completed-edge set $\{ac:c\in C\}$ in Section 4.2 agrees with the linking lemma [6]. Explicitly covering valency zero closes a presentation gap in the census argument. Section 4.3 now appropriately distinguishes numerical fractional-chromatic values from the exact certificates, and Section 6.4 correctly separates the open aligned-diamond step from the proved independence reductions. No new central contribution requires a renewed novelty assessment.

The supplied parser and floating-point triangle-weight screen address missing dependencies identified in the previous review. I compiled the new screen and compared its maximum excess and every reported positive subset against independent rational enumeration on 24 graphs of orders 3–10; these checks agreed. This validates small-instance behavior, not numerical accuracy at the full search sizes. I did not reproduce the exploratory searches or census; the adversarial analysis could not run in this environment because NetworkX was unavailable. The accepted exact certificates and principal proofs are unchanged.

Several nonblocking qualifications remain:

- **The new layout recipe is incomplete.** `link_search.py` and `link_beam.py` import modules located in the sibling `survey/` directory without adding that directory to their search path. They still write under `results/linksearch/`, and `k8_break.py` still writes under `results/kral/`. Also, `link_lib.py` does not implement the advertised `LINK_CORE_SO` override. Supply the remaining path configuration and directory-creation instructions, or narrow the README's assertion that no external layout is assumed.
- **The exploratory triangle-weight screen must remain a numerical diagnostic.** Its new C implementation emits at most 200,000 positive subsets, but the callers can return success after rechecking only those emitted. Report truncation explicitly and return an inconclusive outcome when it occurs. The LP rechecks are floating-point, despite the scripts' “exact” terminology. These limitations do not affect the separate integer certificate for Theorem 3, but they qualify the survey's counting-feasibility labels.
- **Failure handling is improved but not universal.** The triangle-weight callers now reject nonzero exits and missing summaries. However, the density-helper calls in `linking_test.py` and `verify_counting.py` still omit `check=True`. Apply consistent exit-status and structured-summary validation before claiming that every helper failure is fatal.
- **Soften the remaining colouring assertion in Section 6.4.** “None of them is colouring-reducible” is stronger than the argument supports; a vertex of degree at most 10 permits immediate extension of an 11-colouring. State instead that the independence reductions do not establish the desired general colouring reduction.

These are qualifications to exploratory tooling and explanatory wording, not grounds to overturn acceptance of the unchanged substantive results. Lemma L* remains open, and this version supplies neither a ten-chromatic biplanar graph nor universal nine-colourability. It therefore does not satisfy the problem's solve definition.
