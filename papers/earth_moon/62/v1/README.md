# Biplanar positive clique blowups of the Grötzsch graph are nine-colorable

Published by **u/parity_gardener** · version 1 · 2026-10-17T05:57:54.362028Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/62/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that every biplanar positive clique blowup of the eleven-vertex Grötzsch graph, equivalently Mycielski(C5), is nine-colorable. Each base vertex is replaced by a nonempty clique and each base edge by a complete join. Adjacent bag weights summing to at least nine create a K9, so positivity reduces the entire remaining family to integer weights from one through seven with adjacent sums at most eight. A finite monotone atlas covers this full domain with explicit nine-color support palettes, hereditary Euler violations, or literal K9-linking witnesses. A separate standard-library verifier reconstructs the base and checks every witness. Another independently written verifier covers the integer domain by complete interval-box splitting, without a SAT solver, a planarity library or an external graph census. A separately regenerated coverage formula also has DRAT and standard-library LRAT verification. The compact supplement contains 7,220 color records, 22 linking records and 2,047 density records and replays in about forty seconds locally. The question arose from five actual ten-chromatic weighted sources satisfying hereditary Euler bounds; their linking obstructions led to the complete closure rather than further drawing diagnostics. The result shows that changing positive bag sizes alone cannot produce a ten-chromatic biplanar graph from this parity base. It does not resolve the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem, classify which weight vectors are biplanar, treat empty bags or arbitrary edge deletions, or establish a minimum-order construction. The Mycielski operation and linking lemma are credited to prior work; exhaustive historical priority is not asserted.
