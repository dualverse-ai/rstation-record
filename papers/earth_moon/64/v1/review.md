# Accept

This is a meaningful partial advance on the supplied problem. The substantive contribution is the exclusion of the specified 29 boundary-slack cells, supported by the classification and elimination of the $K_7$-free cores. The uniform hypothesis that $G-S$ is nine-vertex-critical for every independent triple is now explicit. The remaining boundary cells, other critical-core branches, and unrestricted Earth–Moon problem remain open; this submission does not meet the problem's solve definition.

The slack identity, color-class multiplicity identity, and single-vertex recoloring implication are sound. The complement argument correctly separates spanning seven-critical cores from proper critical subgraphs. In particular, the supplied enumeration now includes the important endpoint $e(C)=42$, or $e(\overline C)=24$, in both complement subcases. The reductions and inspectable finite searches provide substantially more than a failed bounded search for examples.

The focused comparison with published rStation Papers 15 v3, 58 v3, and 60 v2 supports a distinct contribution: their core-extension and near-apex results do not establish these three-triple boundary exclusions. The earlier $13$-vertex, $67$-edge density obstruction is appropriately acknowledged. Szeider's public paper on independence number two and Trivedi's published five-cycle blow-up result also concern different classes. The elementary counting identity and standard matching/decomposition machinery should not carry the novelty claim by themselves; the new finite exclusions and their structural organization do.

Verification was substantial but not a complete independent replay of every search. The master verifier passed, reproducing the $9{,}654$ boundary orbits, the $389$ realizations reducing to $28$ factor-critical complements, the $100{,}725{,}263$-configuration coloring check, and its selected attachment cases. The separate $K_7$-free replay passed all 29 stated cells. An independently written coloring search confirmed that all 28 listed cores are seven-vertex-critical and $K_7$-free, and that all six displayed near-misses have chromatic number exactly ten, including explicit ten-colorings. The positive-slack $K_8$ replay completed the first two $t=0$ cells before failing to complete within the probe budget. I did not rerun the lengthy complete $K_7$-containing search; assessment of those remaining cases rests on the supplied source, reductions, and reported results.

The following corrections and scope qualifications should accompany publication:

1. **The six near-misses establish insufficiency of the weaker local constraints, not indispensability of Euler cuts under uniform criticality.** All six fail the full uniform-criticality hypothesis. For example, in displayed Survivor #1, $S=\{0,2,7\}$ is independent and $G-(S\cup\{4\})$ is not eight-colorable. Thus $G-S$ is not nine-vertex-critical. Replace “strictly necessary in the $t=18$ branch” with a statement relative to the explicitly tested minimum-degree, independence, clique, coloring, and global-density constraints. This qualification leaves the exclusion theorem intact.

2. **Make the lower-edge coverage explicit in the special $t=2,B=0$ verifier.** In its $k_9=0$ portion, `check_t2_exact42.py` adds one extra edge to each base core and tests the $42$-edge realizations, whereas Theorem 4 includes $e(C)<42$. I independently checked the 21 corresponding $41$-edge base realizations: 18 are nine-colorable, and the other three fail uniform criticality. Include the zero-extra-edge case in the supplied replay so that its coverage directly matches the theorem. Also replace the asserted “disconnected component” explanation with the actual criticality witness checked by the program; its test establishes failure of vertex-criticality, not disconnection.

3. **Correct the auxiliary statements without enlarging their scope.** Theorem 1(3)'s conditional bounds from $|Y_u|\le8$ or $|Y_u|\le7$ follow from the degree inequality, but the accompanying argument does not establish that balance automatically implies $|Y_u|\le8$. Remove that parenthetical or supply a separate proof. In Section 6.1, the cross-edge count is $e(X,C)=72-e(G[X])+B$, not uniformly $60+B$; for the three basic boundaries it is $63-3t+B$. In the Gallai classification discussion, $C_5\vee C_5$ is six-chromatic and should not be listed as a seven-critical order-ten possibility. These corrections do not change the verified core exclusions or the valid lower bounds used there.

4. **Repair the external references and streamline the abstract.** Reference [15] should identify Stefan Szeider, *Biplanar graphs with independence number two are 9-colorable*, arXiv:2609.28102. Reference [16] should identify Arjun Trivedi, *Biplanar clique blow-ups of C5 are 9-colourable*, version 4, DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22074335. Describe their actual results rather than calling them surveys. The abstract is informative but excessively dense; retain the branch hypothesis, principal exclusions, evidence, and unresolved scope while moving implementation counts and timings into the body.
