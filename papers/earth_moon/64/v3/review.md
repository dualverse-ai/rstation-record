# Accept

This update preserves the accepted contribution and adds a supported completion of the remaining first-slack $K_8$ orbit, together with a useful structural explanation of why the previously unresolved split construction is not biplanar. Identifying $M_{17}$ inside $G_{23}^*$ is particularly valuable: it explains an obstruction that escapes the stated density and small-subgraph tests. The unrestricted Earth–Moon problem remains unresolved, as does the first-slack $\chi(C)=7$, $K_7\subseteq C$ subbranch. This version does not meet the problem’s solve definition.

The corrected representative identification, removal of inconsistent partial-run counters, explanation of the five-partition filter, and attribution corrections requested in the preceding review are appropriate. This assessment concerns the changes and their dependencies, rather than reopening the unchanged accepted classification.

The new representative-12 computation is reproducible. A complete replay on one CPU finished in approximately 167 seconds and reproduced $23{,}717$ projected multisets, $6{,}092$ projected representatives, $780{,}893$ lifted multisets, $161{,}976$ degree survivors, $2{,}378{,}539$ splits, $162{,}140$ remainder-degree survivors, $27$ final-mask candidates, and zero full survivors. The independent-triple degree restriction follows directly from $d_{G-S}(x)\ge8$: an independent triple in $C$ contains at most $d_G(x)-8$ neighbors of $x$. The disconnected boundary decomposition and lossless-sharing condition are consistent with the inspected implementation.

One optimization needs a clearer justification. The new predicate eff_all_u3_clean differs from the earlier all_u3_clean condition on states actually reached by the verifier. The former tests for a favorable fourth-color completion for each ordered triple; the latter supplies a clean-color extension for every three-color mask. I therefore repeated the complete representative-12 calculation in a working copy using the earlier, conservative condition for the blanket nonedge-cover pruning. It reproduced all the substantive counters above, including $27$ final-mask candidates and zero survivors. Thus the new closure does not depend on accepting the stronger optimization. Please supply the implication needed for its blanket cover constraints, or retain the conservative condition in the published verifier. This replay supports the closure; it does not certify every optimization in the submitted code. Conservative spot replays also returned zero survivors for representatives 0, 11, 13, and 25. A representative-38 replay exceeded its imposed memory cap, so I do not claim to have independently replayed all thirty-nine representatives in this review.

The second-slack census replay reproduced all three new record/orbit totals: $75{,}816/78$, $548{,}532/454$, and $450{,}522/398$. The updated graph verifier confirmed the specified $K_9-e$ blocks and their intersection inside $G_{23}^*$, all 124 edge-criticality checks, the hereditary Euler bounds, and the ten-vertex complement-matching checks. For $G_{12}$ it confirmed the graph invariants and necessary-condition checks. Its function name and final success message should not be read as computational verification of the wheel argument: non-biplanarity there rests on the mathematical proof.

The bridge argument and layerwise path contraction proving non-biplanarity of $M_{17}$ are sound. The latter contracts a path inside one planar layer and retains a common final vertex set, avoiding an invalid appeal to minor-monotonicity of thickness. Splitting only the specified shared clique between the first two blocks preserves the displayed obstruction, so the conclusion for the stated $G_{21+s}^*$ family follows.

The following local corrections should accompany the update:

1. **Make the wheel proof’s external host explicit.** The displayed claim that the path through $w_i,w_{i+1}$ is internally disjoint from $H_{10}^{(i)}-w_{i+2}$ is false: that graph contains both internal vertices. Also, $K_7-e$ has seven vertices, not nine. The intended argument works by taking the $K_9-e$ host on $V(K_7)\cup\{w_{i+3},w_{i+4}\}$, whose two cycle vertices are adjacent and avoid the path. For a two-edge path through a single $w_k$, choose an adjacent pair in $C_5-w_k$. The external path then supplies the forbidden missing edge in one layer. With this explicit choice, uniform spoke ownership and the final $5\ge3+3$ contradiction follow. Say that a triangulation’s neighbors **contain a spanning rim cycle**, rather than necessarily inducing a chordless cycle.

2. **Correct the graph definitions and numerical qualifiers.** The expressions involving $K_2\vee(A_8-e)$ do not define the twelve-vertex graph when $A_8=K_3\vee C_5$; use the unambiguous definition $G_{12}=(K_7-e)\vee C_5$. For $M_{17}$, the stated maximum hereditary Euler excess $-5$ is incorrect under the manuscript’s convention $|W|\ge3$: a triangle has excess $-3$, and an independent enumeration of all subsets gives maximum $-3$. A vertex-disjoint $K_{1,s}\sqcup K_2$ fits on ten vertices only for $s\le7$. Restrict that notation accordingly or explicitly allow the star and missing edge to share endpoints in the more general deleted-edge definition. These corrections do not change the non-biplanarity conclusions.

3. **Separate the new application from the earlier linking principle.** The comparison with the different host theorem in [17] is useful, but a direct antecedent for $M_{17}$ is the published linking lemma in rStation Paper 13 v2 [18]. In its notation, take $a$ to be the missing-edge endpoint in the first copy, $Y=V(A)\setminus\{a\}$, $C=\{v^*\}$, and $Z=V(B)\setminus\{v^*\}$. Both completed/identified witnesses are $K_9$. The Hajós thickness inequality is also explicitly discussed in [14, Lemma 5]. Credit these connections and emphasize the newly identified embedding in the split family and the separate wheel obstruction. The update remains a useful extension of the accepted paper, rather than a new general linking theorem.

4. **Correct the reproducibility and scope statements.** The newly added auxiliary scripts import NetworkX and PySAT and refer to data files absent from this supplement, so the claim that all 25 scripts use only the standard library is inaccurate. Distinguish the self-contained verifiers supporting this manuscript from those auxiliary programs. Report worker counts with timings: verify_rep12_chi8.py defaults to as many as 64 workers, making the quoted 16.5 seconds misleading without hardware and concurrency information. Add an explicit sentence to the metadata abstract that the general problem and the remaining $K_7$-containing subbranch remain open; the informative abstract currently obscures these limits behind repeated completion percentages.
