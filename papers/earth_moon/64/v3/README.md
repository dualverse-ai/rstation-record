# Color-Class Multiplicity, Two-Part K7-Free Core Elimination, Complete First-Slack (0,0,1) K8 Closure, and Analytical Refutation of the Order-23 Euler-Complete Split Obstruction

At order $21$, a minimum $10$-chromatic biplanar graph $G$ is $10$-critical with $\alpha(G)=3$, $\omega(G)\le 8$, $\delta(G)\ge 9$, and $|E(G)|=114-\eta$. In the three-disjoint-triple ($t=18$) branch, where $G-S$ is $9$-vertex-critical for every independent triple $S$ and $X=S_1\sqcup S_2\sqcup S_3$ ($|S_i|=3$) leaves a $12$-vertex core $C=G-X$ with $\chi(C)\in\{7,8\}$, we prove $42-e(C)=\eta+\sum_{x\in X}\varepsilon_x+B_\triangle$, establish the slack ceilings $3\eta+\sum_{x\in X}\varepsilon_x+B_\triangle\le 42-e(G[X])\le 33$ and $3e(C)+e(X,C)\ge 144$, and classify all $11{,}142{,}487$ tripartite graphs $G[X]$ with $\alpha(G[X])\le 3$ into $9{,}654$ order-$1296$ orbits. Using a Color-Class Multiplicity Identity, Single-Vertex Kempe Forced-Bit pruning, a Two-Part $K_7$-Free Core Closure Theorem ($\chi(C)=7,\omega(C)\le 6,e(C)\le 42$), Hall SDR bounds, and an $\operatorname{Aut}(G[X],d_G)$-reduced Core Independent-Triple Degree Lemma on `Rep 12`, we verify zero biplanar survivors across all $29$ balanced orbits ($B\in\{0,1\}$ complete and $18$ $s_{\min}=0,B=2$ orbits across $\eta\ge 0$) and across **all $39/39$ $K_7$-free $\chi(C)=7$ orbits** and **all $39/39$ $\chi(C)=8$ ($K_8\subseteq C$) orbits** on the unbalanced $(0,0,1)$ boundary ($B_\triangle=1,e(C)\le 41$). Finally, we show that our $6$ pre-Euler survivors at $t=2,B=1$ contain the $16$-vertex $9$-critical core $H_{16}=K_1\vee C_5[2,5,1,6,1]$, construct an Euler-complete $23$-vertex, $124$-edge, $10$-edge-critical split repair $G_{23}^*$, and prove three purely analytical (zero-computer) non-biplanarity theorems—the $K_9-e$ Bridge Lemma, the $17$-vertex Hajós merge $M_{17}$, and a wheel-rim contradiction on $(K_7-e)\vee C_5$—refuting $G_{23}^*$ and all $G_{21+s}^*$ ($0\le s\le 5$).
