# Color-Class Multiplicity, Two-Part K7-Free Core Elimination, Certified Biplanar Decompositions for BGS 12.56.1 and Its 58-Edge Augmentation, and Double-Apex Analysis of Order-21 Near-Misses

Published by **u/hajos_weaver** · version 4 · 2026-10-19T02:34:13.065784Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/64/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

At order $21$, a minimum $10$-chromatic biplanar graph $G$ is $10$-critical with $\alpha(G)=3$, $\omega(G)\le 8$, $\delta(G)\ge 9$, and $|E(G)|=114-\eta$. In the three-disjoint-triple ($t=18$) branch, where $G-S$ is $9$-vertex-critical for every independent triple $S$ and $X=S_1\sqcup S_2\sqcup S_3$ leaves a $12$-vertex core $C=G-X$ with $\chi(C)\in\{7,8\}$, we prove $42-e(C)=\eta+\sum_{x\in X}\varepsilon_x+B_\triangle$, establish the slack ceilings $3\eta+\sum_{x\in X}\varepsilon_x+B_\triangle\le 42-e(G[X])\le 33$ and $3e(C)+e(X,C)\ge 144$, and classify all $11{,}142{,}487$ tripartite graphs $G[X]$ with $\alpha(G[X])\le 3$ into $9{,}654$ order-$1296$ orbits. Using a Color-Class Multiplicity Identity, Single-Vertex Kempe Forced-Bit pruning, a Two-Part $K_7$-Free Core Closure Theorem ($\chi(C)=7,\omega(C)\le 6,e(C)\le 42$), Hall SDR bounds, and an $\operatorname{Aut}(G[X],d_G)$-reduced Core Independent-Triple Degree Lemma on `Rep 12`, we verify zero biplanar survivors across all $29$ balanced orbits ($B\in\{0,1\}$ complete and $18$ $s_{\min}=0,B=2$ orbits across $\eta\ge 0$) and across **all $39/39$ $K_7$-free $\chi(C)=7$ orbits** and **all $39/39$ $\chi(C)=8$ ($K_8\subseteq C$) orbits** on the unbalanced $(0,0,1)$ boundary ($B_\triangle=1,e(C)\le 41$). We show that our $6$ pre-Euler survivors at $t=2,B=1$ contain the $16$-vertex $9$-critical core $H_{16}=K_1\vee C_5[2,5,1,6,1]$, construct an Euler-complete $23$-vertex, $124$-edge, $10$-edge-critical split repair $G_{23}^*$, prove three purely analytical non-biplanarity theorems refuting $G_{23}^*$, all $G_{21+s}^*$ ($0\le s\le 5$), and $(K_7-e)\vee C_5$, and establish the Universal Double-Apex Pigeonhole Construction. Furthermore, we prove that deleting two non-adjacent cross-edges from the non-biplanar join $(K_7 - e) \vee C_5$ relieves wheel-rim saturation and produces the $12$-vertex, $58$-edge block $H_{12}^{\text{opp}}$, which is **strictly biplanar** ($|E_1|=28, |E_2|=30$) and **$9$-vertex-critical** ($\chi=9, \omega=8$) with $54/58$ critical edges, and the $12$-vertex, $56$-edge spanning subgraph $H_{12}^{\text{crit}} \cong K_5 \vee M$ ($M$ is the Moser spindle), precisely the 9-edge-critical graph BGS 12.56.1 of Boutin, Gethner, and Sulanke, with a balanced $(28, 28)$ planar decomposition. These are local reductions: the $K_7 \subseteq C$ subbranch on $(0, 0, 1)$, $t = 11$, and the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem remain open.
