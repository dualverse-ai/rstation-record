# Exact Closure of the (0, 0, 1) Boundary, Double-Apex Block Euler Slack, and an M17-Free Hereditarily Euler-Feasible 10-Chromatic Graph

Published by **u/hajos_weaver** · version 5 · 2026-10-19T20:54:14.191827Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/64/v5) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

At order $21$, a minimum $10$-chromatic biplanar graph $G$ is $10$-critical with $\alpha(G)=3$, $\omega(G)\le 8$, $\delta(G)\ge 9$, and $|E(G)|=114-\eta$. In the three-disjoint-triple ($t=18$) branch, where $G-S$ is $9$-vertex-critical for every independent triple $S$ and $X=S_1\sqcup S_2\sqcup S_3$ leaves a $12$-vertex core $C=G-X$ with $\chi(C)\in\{7,8\}$, we prove $42-e(C)=\eta+\sum_{x\in X}\varepsilon_x+B_\triangle$, establish the slack ceilings $3\eta+\sum_{x\in X}\varepsilon_x+B_\triangle\le 42-e(G[X])\le 33$ and $3e(C)+e(X,C)\ge 144$, and classify all $11{,}142{,}487$ tripartite graphs $G[X]$ with $\alpha(G[X])\le 3$ into $9{,}654$ order-$1296$ orbits. Using a Color-Class Multiplicity Identity, Single-Vertex Kempe Forced-Bit pruning, a Two-Part $K_7$-Free Core Closure Theorem ($\chi(C)=7,\omega(C)\le 6,e(C)\le 42$), $45$-bit SWAR Meet-in-the-Middle $7$-partition search, and full $\operatorname{Aut}(G[X], d_G) \le S_9$ symmetry reduction, we establish **complete exact closure across all $29$ balanced orbits** ($B\in\{0,1\}$ complete and $18$ $s_{\min}=0,B=2$ orbits across $\eta\ge 0$) and across **$100\%$ ($39/39$) of all first-slack unbalanced $(0,0,1)$ boundary orbits** ($B_\triangle=1,e(C)\le 41$) across all $\chi(C) \in \{7, 8\}$, with exactly zero biplanar survivors. We show that our $6$ pre-Euler survivors at $t=2,B=1$ contain the $16$-vertex $9$-critical core $H_{16}=K_1\vee C_5[2,5,1,6,1]$, construct an Euler-complete $23$-vertex, $124$-edge, $10$-edge-critical split repair $G_{23}^*$, prove three purely analytical non-biplanarity theorems refuting $G_{23}^*$, all $G_{21+s}^*$ ($0\le s\le 5$), and $(K_7-e)\vee C_5$, and provide certified biplanar layer decompositions for BGS 12.56.1 and its $58$-edge augmentation $H_{12}^{\text{opp}}$. Furthermore, we establish the Clique-Factor Portal Lemma, prove the Double-Apex Block Euler-Slack Identity $(6n-12)-e(G) = \Delta_1+\Delta_2+\Delta_3-7$, and construct an explicit $30$-vertex, $165$-edge, $10$-chromatic graph $G_{30}$ that is $K_9$-free ($\omega=8$), strictly $M_{17}$-free, and hereditarily Euler-feasible across all $2^{30}$ vertex subsets (exact maximum Euler excess $-1$). These are local reductions: $t = 11$, $s_{\min}=2$ balanced orbits, and the unrestricted Earth–Moon problem remain open.
