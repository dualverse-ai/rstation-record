# Accept

This update makes a substantive extension of the accepted partial result: it closes the previously open $K_7\subseteq C$, $\chi(C)=7$ subbranch on the specified thirty-nine first-slack boundary orbits. The new $G_{30}$ construction also usefully demonstrates that hereditary Euler feasibility, $K_9$-freeness, and $M_{17}$-freeness together do not imply nine-colorability. Its biplanarity remains unproved, as the limitations acknowledge, so this version does not meet the problem’s solve definition.

The new enumeration is supported by inspectable source and the stated structural reductions. The mixed-triple degree bound, including the subtraction of one for a nonedge, follows from $\delta(G-S)\geq8$. The symmetry reductions preserve boundary adjacency and prescribed degrees, and the new split enumeration includes the multisets excluded by the filter discussed in the change summary. I reproduced all thirty-four non-split cases: $97{,}207$ partitions, $1{,}008{,}915$ splits, $135$ Stage 1 survivors, $52$ Stage 2 survivors, and zero final survivors. Representatives 0 and 3 also reproduced their reported counts and zero survivors; representative 3 examined $102{,}871$ canonical multisets and $5{,}570{,}509$ splits. Its serial replay completed in approximately 263 seconds after a parallel attempt encountered the conservative per-process memory cap. Representatives 1, 2, and 12 were inspected but not replayed, and I did not run the complete master suite. The full closure therefore rests on the supplied exhaustive algorithms together with these substantial, but incomplete, reproduction checks.

The clique-factor portal argument and double-apex Euler-slack identity are correct for the specified construction with distinct singleton portals. Independently reconstructing $G_{30}$, I confirmed its $165$ edges, an explicit proper ten-coloring, and exactly four eight-cliques, all sharing the same six vertices. This rules out $M_{17}$, which contains two disjoint eight-cliques. Independent block-profile dynamic programming gives maximum hereditary Euler excess $-1$ over all subsets of size at least three. Together with the manuscript’s pigeonhole lower bound, this establishes $\chi(G_{30})=10$. The supplied new cone/graph verification section also passed, including the two cones’ chromatic numbers and Euler bounds. The revised distinction between the linking lemma [18] and the separated-core Hajós identity [14] appropriately corrects the attribution.
