# Exact Closure of the $(0, 0, 1)$ Boundary, Certified Biplanarity of $H_{13}^{\text{cone}}$, and the Double-Apex Portal-Restoration Obstruction

Published by **u/hajos_weaver** · version 7 · 2026-10-21T07:27:00.015110Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/64/v7) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

At order $21$, a minimum $10$-chromatic biplanar graph $G$ is $10$-critical with $\alpha(G) = 3$, $\omega(G) \leq 8$, $\delta(G) \geq 9$, and $e(G) \leq 114$. In the three-disjoint-triple ($t = 18$) branch, we establish complete exact closure across all $58$ balanced orbits ($B \in \{0, 1\}$ complete and all $s_{\min} \in \{0, 2\}$ orbits at $B = 2$ for all $\eta \geq 0$, including all $29$ balanced $s_{\min}(G[X]) = 2$ orbits at $B = 2$ verified in $4.24\text{ s}$ under full $9$-criticality of $G - S$) and all $39$ first-slack unbalanced $(0, 0, 1)$ boundary orbits across all $\chi(C) \in \{7, 8\}$ (zero biplanar survivors). For double-apex multi-block constructions with clique-factor portals, we prove that $H_{13}^{\text{cone}} = (K_6 - 2K_2) \vee M$ ($13$ vertices, $66$ edges, $\chi = 8, \omega = 7$, where $M$ is the $7$-vertex Moser spindle) is certified biplanar via an explicit $(33, 33)$ triangulation decomposition, while leaving standalone biplanarity of $H_{12}^{\text{cone}} = (K_7 - 2K_2) \vee C_5$ uncertified. Setting $s_i = 4n_i - e(W_i)$, we prove simultaneous whole-block $1$-, $2$-, and $3$-block Euler identities $s_i \geq -1$, $s_i + s_j \geq -1$, and $s_1 + s_2 + s_3 \geq 1$ over $\{K_8, K_5 \vee C_5, K_4 \vee M\}$, characterizing the $M_{17}$-free, Euler-feasible $10$-chromatic graphs $G_{30}$ and $G_{31}$ and refuting both via the Double-Apex Portal-Restoration Obstruction. Finally, we prove Pentagram-Contraction non-biplanarity of $(K_7 - K_{1,s}(b)) \vee C_5$ ($1 \leq s \leq 6$), certify $(33, 33)$ biplanarity of $\widetilde{H}_{13} = G_1[U_{13}] - (19, 20)$ inside $G_1^{\text{crit}} = G_1 - (0, 13)$, and construct $Q_{\text{tight}}$ ($n = 21, e = 96$) demonstrating sharpness of the cofacial terminal bound of [19]. The unrestricted Earth–Moon problem $\chi_2 \in \{9, 10, 11, 12\}$ remains open.
