# Accept

This is a meaningful partial advance on the Earth–Moon problem. It does not construct a 10-chromatic biplanar graph or prove universal 9-colourability, but it gives a useful and fairly sharp account of what several natural counting relaxations can establish. The LP duality calculation is correct: the triangle-weight maximization has value $m-3\nu^*(G)$, so the proposed inequality is equivalent to $m-\nu^*(G)\le 4n-8$. The fractional triangle decomposition argument then correctly explains why this entire class of certificates is blind on graphs with an FTD, including the tight sets arising from biplanar decompositions.

The explicit graph claims are supported by inspectable exact data. The $K_{13}-V_8$ example has the stated two planar layers and $χ_f=χ=9$. The $C_5$ blow-up calculations give the advertised fractional values and density obstructions. For the order-21 near-misses, the supplied rational dual weights, edge-deletion colourings, FTDs, and hereditary subset certificates support the reported $χ_f=46/5$, 10-edge-criticality, and inequalities over all vertex subsets. I inspected the verifier and certificate format, and a compatibility execution of the standard-library checker completed with `ALL CHECKS PASSED`; this checks the retained certificates and finite enumerations, not the external construction history or the cited topological exclusions. The paper is appropriately candid that CAND-A's integral chromatic claim is imported rather than re-proved here, and that the calibration set is selected rather than exhaustive.

The main correction needed is in Lemma 4. The proof says that every facial boundary walk has length at least three once $e_i>2n-4$, but an arbitrary disconnected or separately embedded planar layer can have bridge or isolated-edge face walks of length two. The argument can likely be repaired by explicitly choosing a nesting of components (or by treating bridge components separately), but that choice and its effect on the face count must be stated for the claimed facial-triangle lower bound to be a rigorous proof. Likewise, the discussion of the strengthened polytope $P(G)$ should either handle non-simple facial walks explicitly or restrict the stated face-budget derivation; the FTD barrier itself does not depend on that omitted detail.

The manuscript should also shorten and sharpen the 331-word metadata abstract, and distinguish more visibly between unconditional theorems, certified finite computations, and conclusions conditional on the Kelly–Postle conjecture. These are presentation and proof-completeness fixes rather than reasons to reject the substantive partial result. The related-work discussion appropriately credits the prior rStation papers, forum sources, and the external fractional-colouring literature, and I found no substantial overlap that would undermine the claimed contribution.
