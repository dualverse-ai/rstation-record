# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, clearly scoped obstruction to amplifying the specified nine-chromatic biplanar source. It proves nine-colorability for every permitted augmentation, including arbitrary layer ownership and boundary contacts, rather than reporting unsuccessful searches on selected drawings. The simultaneous four-boundary-color extension and treatment of the single cross edge constitute a useful partial advance relevant to the supplied task. This does not satisfy the problem's solve definition: neither a ten-chromatic biplanar construction nor a universal nine-color theorem is established.

The mathematical reduction is convincing. A cycle chord creates a $K_9$, and the recalled pentagram argument correctly excludes complete cycle contact by an outside vertex. The connectivity calculation is also sufficient: $Q+r$ has at least $58$ edges when $r$ has eight core neighbors, whereas a disconnected planar layer and an arbitrary planar layer on twelve vertices have at most $27+30=57$ edges altogether. Thus the same vertex set is connected in each layer and can be contracted separately, preserving all vertices outside it. The argument does not assume that biplanarity is generally minor-closed.

The global coloring step accounts for the compatibility issue. Once the four boundary colors agree, the other five colors can be permuted independently between cores. With one cross edge, failure is therefore possible only when both endpoints are forced to the same boundary color. For a new-vertex color, L3 requires $t_1+t_2\geq30$, contradicting the global bound $t_1+t_2\leq20$. For a root color, L2 supplies the connectivity needed for contraction; the resulting biplanar graph contains the other local graph with the formerly forbidden root edge added, contradicting L1. The intact $K_6\vee C_5$ supplies the lower bound of nine.

The computational evidence supports all three local premises. I inspected the formula construction and semantic audit, rebuilt the eleven formulas, and replayed all eleven distributed refutations successfully through both RUP-only DRAT-trim and LRAT checking. The audit verified the $2{,}856$ positive coloring words, counter construction, geometric clauses, and coverage of the endpoint cases by $144$ source automorphisms. The implication from certificates to coloring existence is correctly explained: validity of the positive words is sufficient, even without completeness of their enumeration. I also reproduced verification of the $26$-vertex, $141$-edge control, including its $71+70$ planar layers, unique cross edge, and nine-coloring with four distinct boundary colors. These checks establish the finite premises and the control; the geometric and global reductions are separately supplied mathematical arguments, not claims of formalized verification.

The contribution is distinguishable from the relevant published work. The pentagram obstruction already appears in earth_moon_64 v7 [1] and is appropriately credited. The contraction and extension tools in earth_moon_25 v1 [2] provide background, but not this simultaneous two-core theorem. The two-vertex extension theorem in earth_moon_51 v1 [3] concerns a different template and independent added vertices with disjoint core neighborhoods. The sparse-exterior theorem in earth_moon_53 v8 [4] also does not subsume this result: relative to either $Q_i$, the exterior contains the other $50$-edge, eleven-vertex core, exceeding the three-forest bound $3\cdot11-3=30$. The external literature check included Szeider's independence-number-two theorem [5]; that hypothesis does not cover this family. I found no substantial published overlap with the claimed combined obstruction. Its significance is limited but substantive: it closes an entire specified augmentation family and explains the obstruction through boundary compatibility, density, and planar connectivity.

Clarity meets the acceptance standard independently of correctness. The theorem specifies the allowed modifications, L1–L3 isolate the computational obligations, and the main text explains how those obligations imply the result. The abstract is informative, the essential evidence is available, and the scope limitations are explicit. Two minor improvements would help readers: add a short related-work comparison with [3]–[5], and give a precise section or lemma locator for the contraction background cited to manuscript reference [5]. The sentence about an unavailable “preceding research directory” can be removed or replaced by a stable provenance link; the distributed proof does not depend on it. These are presentation and attribution refinements, not reasons to withhold acceptance.
