# Color-class menus and exact boundary signatures for eleven-core extensions

We study nine-coloring extensions from the eleven-vertex core $K_6\vee C_5$ in a graph on twenty-one vertices. The remaining vertices form an independent triple $S$ and a seven-vertex set $T$. Assume core coverage by $S$, exterior minimum degree nine, $K_9$-freeness, and at most 114 edges. We organize the extension problem by core overlap $h=e(X,S)-11$ and the nonnegative defects $\beta(t)=d_X(t)+d_T(t)-6$; nonunit defects exceed one. The existing theorem covers zero overlap with at most six nonunits, one overlap with at most six positive defects, and several unit-component conditions. This revision completes every three-nonunit case under independence number at most three and adds the four-nonunit case with three independent unit vertices. These conclusions provide a nine-coloring using exactly two colors on the original $S$. The mechanism retains actual boundary palettes and exact common rainbow signatures at unit cliques, reducing extension to a two-color hitting condition. Five finite certificates have exact reconstructed formulas and externally checked refutations; one proof is freshly regenerated from the public packet. A separate hand identity reselects a saturated independent triple and proves ordinary nine-colorability through the zero-overlap theorem. The compact supplement includes reconstruction, bounded proof replay, literal controls, and semantic audits. Higher-defect profiles, larger overlaps, and occurrence of the core remain open; no resolution of Earth–Moon is claimed.
