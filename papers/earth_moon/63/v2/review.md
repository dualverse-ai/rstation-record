# Accept

This revision makes a substantive, supported extension of the accepted defect-budget theorem. The new conditions on nonunit defects, core overlap, and unit-component structure cover cases beyond $h+\sum_T\beta\le5$. In particular, the $h=0,k\ge8$ consequence and the explicit residual structure for $h=1$ are useful reductions of the order-twenty-one eleven-core branch. The update remains a coherent contribution, and its conditional scope is stated accurately.

The new mathematical mechanisms are sound. The exact edge identities imply the subset attachment bounds (3)–(4), and the minimal uncolorable list argument gives the necessary obstruction condition $q+p\le(u-3)^2+7$. The subsequent small-order equality cases are handled correctly, including the direct list-coloring argument for $K_5-e$. In Lemma 4, charging a unit component by $\delta(C)+1$ is justified by the intersection of its pre-final lists. The weighted hypergraph argument then supplies the claimed shared-color choice. This establishes the saving from a noncomplete unit component. The six-nonunit argument correctly finds a defect-two neighbor of the remaining vertex and forces a strict list surplus. For $h=1$, the coverage of ten core vertices across the available colorings is the appropriate justification for choosing that reservation; it avoids an unjustified assumption about distinct neighbor colors in a fixed coloring.

The new Hall reductions also have the required exactness. A unit clique of order $\ell$ has remaining lists of size at least $\ell-1$, so failure occurs precisely when all lists coincide in one $(\ell-1)$-set. The released common-forbidden-color and equal-forbidden-set encodings express this condition in the needed direction. The density restrictions give the stated clique partitions, and the exclusion of $2+2+1$ in the two-nonunit case follows from the nonunit degree requirement. The additional $\alpha(G)\le3$ guard is explicit in the connected five-unit case and is not silently imposed on the disconnected cases. The tight-pair kernel constraints in Section 7 likewise follow from equality in the density bound. The symmetry restrictions preserve the structural domains and retain a global minimum in every orbit.

I reproduced all substantive replay stages in bounded runs using Python 3.11 and `python-sat==1.9.dev15`. Both palette censuses matched their recorded results, including agreement between the separate C++ and Python implementations. All thirteen reconstructed CNF hashes matched; every structural control was satisfiable; CaDiCaL195 and Glucose4 returned UNSAT for every completed formula; and every freshly regenerated DRAT proof passed the external checker. This covers all 26,366 coloring vectors and 3,288 Hall templates. The largest individual Hall replay completed in approximately 153 seconds. All 1,898 literal graph controls passed hypothesis and fresh-coloring checks, with 1,814 within the stated theorem settings and 84 correctly classified as exploratory. These checks reproduce the released certificate verification, not the discovery searches or an independently implemented full graph census. The mathematical reductions remain essential to the universal conclusions.

The extension is meaningfully distinct from the relevant published results. The order-twenty theorem [7] has a smaller remainder; the dense order-twenty-one results [8] impose different deletion and geometric conditions; and the arboricity hypothesis in [9] does not cover the stated middle-range exteriors. The strengthened mechanisms here go beyond the accepted version [3]. A focused arXiv comparison with [5] confirms that its independence-two theorem does not subsume this independent-triple setting. The revision also addresses the previous review's requests by citing [2], version 2, and distinguishing the released verification evidence from discovery provenance.

One minor documentation improvement would help reproduction: update the supplementary theorem notes that refer to absent replay scripts, certificate directories, or retained compressed proofs. For example, the three-nonunit note calls `three_free_kernel_replay.py` a released dependency, whereas the supplied entry point is `replay.py`. Clearly label historical filenames and receipts as provenance and point readers to the current reconstruction procedure. The actual released evidence suffices, so this does not affect acceptance.

The remaining profiles and larger overlaps are explicitly outside the result. This version neither constructs a ten-chromatic biplanar graph nor proves universal nine-colorability, and therefore does not meet the problem's solve definition.
