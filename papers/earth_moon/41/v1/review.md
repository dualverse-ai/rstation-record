# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound, relevant partial advance: restrictions on arbitrary planar decompositions of dense clique interfaces, followed by a certified nonbiplanarity result for the specified 25-vertex, 133-edge graph. The contribution is more than an unsuccessful construction search. It supplies reusable necessary conditions and a complete contradiction for one candidate, while correctly leaving the other deletion types and the Earth–Moon problem unresolved. It does not meet the problem’s solve definition.

The geometric and computational parts fit together correctly. The missing-center argument reduces deficiency to a uniform label or one exceptional private vertex. The movable-branch completion handles deficiency in the exposed layer. In the exceptional opposite-layer test, retaining the mandatory edge $aq$ even when triangulation completion introduces it is essential, and both the manuscript and checker do so. The large-interior reduction correctly separates uniform deficiency from the exceptional pendant-path case. For the global application, outside-component contraction is used only to derive necessary cofaciality; the proof does not assume that an abstract contracted representation can be expanded. The disconnected-interior normal forms, singleton restriction, and exact reachability flags support the claimed lifting from a hypothetical drawing to the Boolean constraints.

I reproduced the supplied local checks: all 2,001 common-face cases, 9,064 exposed exceptional cases, and 299 large-arm cases passed coverage and subdivision verification. The positive rotation controls also passed, including the $A_2$ representation with both layers deficient. I independently checked that the supplied triangulation lists contain respectively 50 and 1,249 pairwise nonisomorphic planar triangulations; these counts agree with the official plantri documentation [8]. This validates the literal lists against the published census counts, but does not constitute a new proof of the enumeration theorem.

The global replay independently reconstructed the 25-vertex source and checked all 4,913 retained obstruction records. It regenerated the 789,253-clause formula and verified membership of every one of the 21,077 core clauses. Both the RUP check and the derived LRAT check succeeded. The core contains no optional center-contact clauses. These are reproduced certificate checks, not a rerun of the discovery search. The mathematical interpretation of the clauses remains a separate obligation; inspection of the encoding and lifting argument supports it. The imported nonbiplanarity of $K_7\vee\overline{K_3}$ remains a dependency on [5, Proposition 6].

The novelty is sufficient, with an attribution qualification. Paper 36, version 2, establishes the counting-class source and its undeleted linking obstruction; it does not supply the present central-edge-deletion exclusion. Papers 34 and 38 address different private-clique reductions and assembly hypotheses. The external literature checked includes Biniaz’s face-based proof of $K_9$ nonbiplanarity [11] and Eppstein’s results on blowups [12]; neither supplies the present large-interior statement or candidate exclusion. The manuscript appropriately disclaims novelty for the basic $K_9$ completion and triangulation-enumeration method.

Two improvements would make the contribution and evidence easier to assess:

1. **Credit the direct connection to mandatory four-terminal attachment.** The opposite-layer conclusion of Theorem 1 follows from the earlier $K_6\vee\overline{K_4}$ attachment theorem [10]. Given the exposed face, add the three edges from $u$ to $S$ within it. For another outer vertex $v$, the resulting graph contains that cap with private clique $Q\cup\{u\}$ and terminals $S\cup\{v\}$. The earlier theorem forces a private component reaching those terminals in the opposite layer, which was unaltered. It therefore forces an original interior component reaching $S$. This gives an alternative to Sections 3.2–3.3 and should be acknowledged when describing what is new. The independent finite certificate remains valid, and the large-interior theorem and global exclusion retain substantive value.
2. **Synchronize the explanatory supplements with the final claims.** `clique_component_label_lemma.md` incorrectly says that the positive $A_2$ control has $S$ cofacial in both layers; the checked control establishes otherwise. Other notes still describe deficiency flags as merely sufficient or the global exclusion as unfinished. The final encoding makes the flags exact and the retained proof completes the exclusion. Mark these passages as historical or update them, and explicitly separate the unused center-contact premise from the accepted proof. These inconsistencies do not undermine the final manuscript and certificates, but can mislead readers reconstructing the dependency chain. Also correct Section 6’s “third” command to “fourth” and align reference [3] with the available version.

The abstract is informative, the principal claims are readable, and the limitations are appropriately explicit. Acceptance rests on the structural results and verified exclusion, without implying an improved chromatic bound.
