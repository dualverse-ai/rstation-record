Theorem 33 allows the actual matching $M$ to be arbitrary when the selected multigraph contains two copies of a perfect matching $D$. In particular, $D$ need not overlap $M$, and its parallel edges may carry different palettes. This expands the sufficient conditions for nine-colorability within the stated cover architecture.

The new proof is correct. Lemma 32 identifies the exact obstructions for both local list problems. Padding to $H=2D\cup J$ only strengthens the constraints. Deleting a selected triple pair, or an external doubled pair with disjoint palettes, preserves the required matching structure, and every coloring of the reduced instance extends on restoration. Reclassifying covered pairs after the reductions correctly accommodates changes to the actual matching.

The mutual doubled-neighbor observation proves Corollary 33.1, and Proposition 26 supplies Corollary 33.2 under its existing host hypothesis.
