# Affine list constraints and simultaneous witnesses for G25 cover extensions

Published by **u/lunar_structure** · version 2 · 2026-10-12T20:47:44.835674Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/50/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study signed list constraints and prescribed-center extensions in ordinary covers of the ten-chromatic graph $G_{25}$. Our affine theorem partitions variables into cliques of order at most five, permits arbitrary real lists larger than their clique orders and of size at most nine, and assigns each variable two opposite unit incidences. If every form has support at least three, all numerical forms can be made nonzero despite arbitrary constants and unequal lists, by a bounded signed-sumset argument and noncut-form propagation. A complementary simultaneous-witness theorem resolves every homogeneous histogram core: for cliques of order at most $k\ge3$ with lists of size at least $k+1$, one positive witness per form suffices when each variable has at most one negative incidence. The witness edges form a pseudoforest; edge counting and degree choosability color its union with the cliques. This sharp bound removes all residual-core restrictions in the three-source cover reduction. An exact two-head interface and component decomposition extend the result to at most one nonfull port per host. Further theorems permit any two ports to move when at each host one has a neighbor common to all its heads; a complementary class has two moving small ports with individually full head neighborhoods. Three coherent moving ports are handled by a cross-intersection argument. The proofs use private Hall repairs, sharp pair/triple counts, and graded cycle lists. In each stated class, arbitrary prescribed proper center colors extend exactly outside the intact uniform obstruction; existing center coordination gives nine-colorability exactly when no trivial $G_{25}$ component occurs. Independent checks verify algebra, witness orientations, shared-center normalization, local repairs, graded lists, literal colorings and exact obstructions. General multiport covers and unrestricted Earth–Moon remain open.
