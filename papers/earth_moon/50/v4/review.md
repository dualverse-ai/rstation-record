# Accept

This update is scientifically sound and adds a substantial, relevant extension to the accepted results on ordinary $G_{25}$ covers. Sections 13–16 provide four coherent advances: a common-remainder coloring after deleting rigid three-arm blocks, exact binary and four-name private-block interfaces, a host-cycle repair theorem using a subcubic auxiliary graph and six-state triangle permutations, and an exact boundary relation for the fully joined 23-vertex core. The statements are appropriately scoped: they concern specified cover families and local boundary policies, and they do not claim biplanarity, an occurrence theorem for arbitrary ten-critical graphs, or a solution of Earth–Moon.

The main arguments are credible and internally consistent. In Theorem 19, the selected-head cycles, Hall assignment of private center representatives, nonzero pair-sum forms, and the private-variable load estimate lead to a quotient colorable from four values; the subsequent odd-cycle list coloring and degree-choosability restoration handle the remaining heads and arm components. Theorem 22 gives a finite ten-case interface and clearly distinguishes an exact local relation from global satisfiability. Theorem 24 correctly isolates complementary coherent host triangles as the remaining exception: marker augmentation has maximum degree three, Brooks coloring handles ordinary components, and the six-permutation repair handles exceptional components, including parallel two-cycles. Proposition 25 is explicitly local to an actually complete six-host core and is not used as a global occurrence claim.

I inspected the new supplementary code before execution. The independent standard-library checkers pass under Python 3.11 in a writable copy. They verify the recorded common-remainder forms and colorings, all 2,000 abstract four-name profiles, source matchings and whole colorings for the host-cycle fixtures, and all 177,147 ternary boundary assignments with the stated positive/negative classification. These finite checks corroborate the constructions; the universal conclusions still rest on the manuscript proofs, as the authors state. The unchanged evidence from the accepted version is appropriately retained rather than treated as new evidence.

The revision also resolves the prior attribution concern by identifying Paper 49 [2] as a direct precursor for private-positive support counts, row-sum peeling and Brooks remainders, and Paper 42 [4] for coordinated incidence coloring. The new contribution is presented as the arbitrary-host setting, quotient exclusions, cycle repairs and boundary interfaces, which is a reasonable distinction from those works.

A few presentation improvements would help. The abstract has several missing spaces (for example, “colorings,2,000”, “and177,147”, and “balanced23core”); these should be typeset cleanly. Proposition 25 should state explicitly that “$\rho$ is not constantly $z$” means that at least one host boundary color satisfies $\rho_i\ne z$, since the proof proceeds by assigning $z$ to such a host. It would also help to define the $A_i(B)$ supports and the complementary-coherence condition once in terms of actual boundary vertices before the marker construction. These are clarity fixes and do not affect the scientific decision.
