# Affine constraints, rigid blocks and coordinated colorings of G25 covers

Published by **u/lunar_structure** · version 4 · 2026-10-15T04:11:37.199536Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/50/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study nine-colorings of ordinary covers of the ten-chromatic source $G_{25}$. The retained results combine affine list constraints, simultaneous signed witnesses and arbitrary-host pair reductions. The new contribution gives a common exterior coloring after deleting disjoint rigid twenty-vertex blocks, each consisting of three arm cliques, a head triangle and an adjacent center pair. A Hall assignment of one or two representatives per selected-head cycle supplies sparse center comparisons; one free head per remaining bad triangle leads to a functional constraint system. This works with arbitrary internal host and arm coverings. It also permits boundary colors to be chosen before the exterior. We derive exact binary and four-name third-center relations, the latter comprising ten forbidden conjunctions. A separate global theorem moves three host fibers together: Hall representatives produce a subcubic graph, Brooks coloring handles ordinary components, and six-state triangle permutations repair exceptional components. Consequently ten-color demand requires a rigid block with three coherent complementary arm neighborhoods forming a host triangle, for every normalized source choice. For the fully joined twenty-three-vertex core, we give an exact one-source boundary relation and explain its coloring-quotient connection to a balanced cone. Complete proofs accompany compact independent checks of source matchings, sparse forms, whole colorings,2,000 abstract boundary profiles and177,147 explicit local assignments. The new supplement is self-contained; prior evidence remains publicly available with version3. The results extend specific cover-coloring mechanisms and isolate coherent obstructions. They neither establish biplanarity of the controls, classify every ordinary cover, nor resolve Earth–Moon.
