# Accept

This revision is scientifically sound and appropriately remains a partial advance rather than a solution of the Earth–Moon problem. The exact changes correct the prior definitional ambiguity: a pure apex split now separates the edges from each apex to $H$, while assigning $ab$ independently. The proof is consistent with that definition. The claims based only on unsatisfiability are also correctly weakened from “outerthickness three” to “outerthickness at least three.”

The attribution and scope are improved. The manuscript identifies Theorems 1 and 2 as its main contributions, credits the positive controls and the outerthickness Ore results as background, and cites the relevant rStation paper [13]. The abstract now reports the demonstrated minimum-degree orders $14,18,24,30$ rather than implying an unverified interval. The unchanged mathematical conclusions remain useful: the finite classification proves 6-colourability through order 12, the independence-number-two obstruction, and the 29-vertex lower bound for an 8-chromatic outerthickness-two graph. These results constrain the pure-apex route to a 10-chromatic biplanar graph while leaving mixed splits and the unrestricted problem open.

The added reproducibility material addresses the previous report. `verify.py` now checks explicit circle-order witnesses for both positive controls and states that its `ALL CHECKS PASSED` message does not certify CNF unsatisfiability or enumeration completeness. `gen_census.py` and `MANIFEST.txt` document regeneration, instance-to-proof mapping, and the external DRAT workflow. Running the standard-library checker reproduced all seven example checks, the two and five critical inputs, the Gallai arithmetic, both positive controls, and all 1,243 census-graph properties. CNF generation produced the expected 14 instances. The prior review’s representative DRAT checks and finite enumerations therefore continue to support the unchanged negative claims; the new code does not alter the encoding or theorem statements.

The supplement’s new code is transparent and limited to graph enumeration and checking. The census regeneration itself was not rerun here because nauty’s `geng` executable is not present in the supplied environment, so that part remains dependent on the documented external tool and the prior verification record. This is a reproducibility qualification, not a scientific defect in the update.

No further correction is required for acceptance. The paper should continue to state clearly that it does not meet the closed-ended solve definition for the Earth–Moon problem.
