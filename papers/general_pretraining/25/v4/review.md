# Accept

The revision adds useful, bounded support for the accepted contribution: sensitivity checks on the structure-only lookup and two additional training budgets for the existing local-global comparator. These additions concern the same models, features and scientific questions. They do not establish a new mechanism or introduce a separate substantive contribution. The central argument remains identifiable, and Table 6b and Figure 3 organize the intervention results effectively while the detailed prediction history stays in the supplement.

The distinction between training-time controls and evaluation-time interventions is now appropriately explicit. Thinning an own-document lookup and supplying a foreign-document lookup produce different losses in fixed checkpoints at approximately matched marginal no-match rates. This does not resolve the confounding in the separately trained foreign-document arm: match order, distance, placement and their relationship to the text still differ. The revised claim 3, Section 4.4 and limitations acknowledge this. Reporting order-0 targets separately from copies outside the chunk is essential: thinning retains about .69–.77 of the former gain, but only .03/.15 of the latter on dev-val and negative fractions on dev-held-out. Pooling these populations would conceal that difference.

The block-mask experiment supports the stated checkpoint-specific dependence on earlier fields. For order-0 targets whose own field is intact and whose nearest changed field is at least 1,024 positions earlier, the retained shares are .639 and .609, with document-bootstrap intervals [.554, .710] and [.465, .721]. The inspected bucket construction resets at chunk boundaries and excludes the current position from the preceding-history calculation. These results support sensitivity to distant input changes; they do not identify a circuit, establish training-time necessity, or allocate the original gain among mechanisms. Likewise, the dropout results warrant the manuscript's heterogeneous, seed-specific description rather than a pooled claim that the gain requires fields.

The added local-global runs qualify the matched-FLOPs comparison meaningfully. From the retained aggregates, I obtain LG512 margins over B + table of −.00143/−.00202 bpb at 477 updates and +.00946/+.01035 at 1,906 updates. Restricting claim 2 to the tested 953- and 1,906-update budgets, with the longer-budget result explicitly limited to one seed and development sets, is appropriate. This does not establish a crossover law or superiority at arbitrary longer budgets.

I reproduced the supplied swap, removal-sweep and block-mask summaries exactly, including Table 6b and the block-mask bootstrap intervals. The earlier run-result objects, learning-curve arrays, training-budget records and repacked stride-$T/2$ values are preserved. These checks establish consistency of the retained evidence and reported arithmetic; they do not reproduce training or checkpoint inference, independently verify the per-token classification, or authenticate the chronology of registrations. The bootstrap intervals are conditional on the checkpoints and sampled masks, and do not quantify variation across training or masking seeds.

Minor improvements:

- In the abstract and Section 4.11, prefer “the 477-update comparison is unresolved under the stated .003-bpb rule” to saying the lead is “absent.” A tolerance rule is not an equivalence test.
- Describe Figure 3a as showing no abrupt transition resolved on the sampled removal grid. The finite sweep does not exclude every possible threshold. In the preceding paragraph, restrict “every alteration” explicitly to the Table 6b interventions.
- Add the available uncertainty intervals to Figure 3c and mark curves that leave Figure 3a's plotting range. Keep the numerical summaries accessible as they are now.

The corrections to the abstract's training-length language and the completed attribution in reference [2] also address the previous accepted review's suggestions. The added evidence remains supporting material for the existing controlled comparison, with its limitations clearly stated.
