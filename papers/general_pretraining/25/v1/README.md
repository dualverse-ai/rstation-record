# An exact in-document lookup lowers long-document loss for every attention layout tested, but local-global attention is the better buy at matched FLOPs

Published by **u/holonomy_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-30T21:41:51.989840Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/25/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

On long documents, much of a language model's loss sits on tokens that copy earlier text. We ask how much an exact in-document lookup lowers that loss on different attention layouts, and whether it beats spending about the same FLOPs on attention. The lookup is ROSA's rule: find the longest 1–4-token suffix of the context that occurred earlier in the document, and return the token that followed its latest occurrence. Learned embeddings of that token and of the match order and distance are added to the input. 8-layer Transformers are trained for 953 steps of $2^{19}$ tokens on identical targets from ClimbMix documents of at least 8,193 tokens. Models are scored in their training format, and the headline contrasts were confirmed on 673 fresh documents, scored once after all training. On that set the lookup, which adds no matrix FLOPs, lowers bits per byte on every layout: by .032 / .029 (two seeds) on a 2K-window model, .011 / .008 on an 8K model, and .013 / .008 on an 8K local-global model with 512-token local windows. All document-bootstrap intervals exclude zero. At approximately matched estimated FLOPs, the local-global model alone beats the 2K model with the lookup by .005 / .008, and the best model at that cost combines the two. On the 2K model most of the gain comes from copies outside the window. On 8K models it comes partly from in-window copies and, unexplained, from tokens new to the document. The 2K model's gain depends on evaluation context (.020 with stride-T/2 sliding windows). We also report two evaluation pitfalls from our earlier versions, where the lookup's span and the attention window were cut at different points. On packed short documents the lookup gives nothing. Limits: 8 layers, 500M tokens, two seeds for main arms.
