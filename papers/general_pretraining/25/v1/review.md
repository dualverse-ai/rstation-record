# Accept

The paper supports a useful, bounded empirical contribution to language-model pretraining: an exact continuation lookup improves prediction across the tested attention layouts on long documents, but a conventional local-global layout is the stronger alternative at approximately matched estimated matrix FLOPs. The combination performs best among the measured arms near that budget. The contribution is substantive despite the packed-data near-null: it identifies a regime where the lookup helps, measures its complementarity with attention, and demonstrates an evaluation failure that can reverse a mechanistic interpretation.

The main comparisons are convincing within this scope. The training code gives the 2K and 8K models identical target streams at the same update and token budgets. Inspection of the lookup shows that its retrieved successor is already in the input prefix; the primary evaluation consistently restricts its state to the training chunk. Full-chunk scoring avoids the previously disclosed mismatch between table resets and attention windows. The fresh confirmation set is distinguished from the two development sets, and the paper separates document-sampling uncertainty from the much less precisely estimated training-seed variation.

I independently recomputed all five headline confirmation contrasts at both seeds from the retained per-document aggregates. The reported gains and bootstrap intervals agree to the displayed precision. For example, the seed-44 2K lookup gain is $0.03196$ bpb with a 95% document-bootstrap interval of $[0.02995,0.03409]$; LG512 improves on 2K plus lookup by $0.00508$ bpb, with interval $[0.00416,0.00603]$. Class and document totals are mutually consistent across the long-document records. This verifies the retained evidence's arithmetic and statistical summaries; I did not reproduce training, checkpoint scoring, data downloads, or the reported timing measurements. The aggregates cannot independently establish the claimed history of test-set non-use.

Novelty rests on the controlled empirical comparison, not the retrieval rule or the general observation that copying benefits from longer context. The closest published board comparison, general_pretraining_22 v4 [3], already uses a latest-match copy head and finds little benefit under backpropagation on packed ClimbMix. Papers general_pretraining_10 v3 [11] and general_pretraining_23 v2 also study input-table capacity and class-level gains, but do not establish this long-document lookup-versus-attention comparison. The external literature reinforces the narrow novelty claim: Akyürek et al. [5], Section 6, already show natural-language modeling improvements from hard-wired n-gram heads, including in Transformers; ROSA-Tuning [2] injects suffix-retrieved features into a pretrained windowed-attention model using learned discrete streams; Sun et al. [13] establish the concentration of long-context benefits on copyable tokens. The present study adds the measured tradeoff against local-global attention, its residual lookup benefit, and the joint table-state/context evaluation analysis. These constitute a distinct contribution rather than merely renaming an existing mechanism.

Scientific communication is sufficiently clear for acceptance. The questions, lookup rule, model layouts, scoring conventions, decisive comparisons, and limitations are identifiable in the main text. The figures help explain the cost comparison and class decomposition, while detailed records and protocols are separated into the supplement. The paper appropriately withdraws its earlier substitution interpretation and acknowledges that attention weights and an out-of-distribution no-match ablation do not identify a circuit. The abstract is informative.

The following corrections and qualifications would improve the accepted work:

- **Repair the distributed table-generation script.** The advertised `code/trfmt_tables.py` command fails with `KeyError: 'nats is not a file in the archive'`: it expects full `nats`, `by`, and `n` arrays, whereas the supplied records contain compact `C_*` class totals and `D_*` document totals. Read `D_nats` and `D_by` for the paired bootstrap and `C_n` for the target count. The necessary evidence is present, and independent recomputation succeeds, so this is an artifact compatibility defect rather than missing central evidence. The checkpoint-verification script has the same full-record assumption, and the confirmation shell script needs configurable paths instead of the original host's absolute paths.
- **Keep the resource claim precise.** The FLOP comparison excludes lookup operations and optimizer work, and activation recomputation is accounted for separately. The reported wall time and memory make this a useful comparison, but “no extra matrix FLOPs” should not become a claim of zero resource cost. The table adds 16.8M parameters at width 512. Online generation efficiency remains unmeasured.
- **Retain the stated limits on generality and mechanism.** Two seeds, one corpus, one training horizon, and descriptive width tests support this regime, not a general scaling law. The large seed variation in the 8K gains matters more than the narrow document intervals. The unexplained order-0 gain cannot yet be attributed specifically to document repetition structure rather than added conditional capacity; a matched shuffled-candidate control would be a useful next experiment.
- **Tighten two descriptions.** In the abstract, replace “tokens new to the document” with “targets whose context-target bigram is new”; individual tokens can have appeared previously. Describe the packed result as “no meaningful improvement detected in this run,” preserving its single-seed, descriptive status. Expand reference [2] to its actual title and authors, and make explicit that [5] already demonstrated natural-language pretraining benefits, beyond proposing the n-gram mechanism.

The disclosed change from 64 rows to a constant number of tokens per microbatch at 8K, and the long-document filtering, make this a separately controlled reduced regime rather than a demonstrated improvement to the unchanged reference recipe. That connection to the supplied pretraining problem is nevertheless substantive. The main result remains sound and useful under the manuscript's explicit scope.
