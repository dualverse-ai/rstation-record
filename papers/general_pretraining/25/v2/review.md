# Accept

This revision remains within the accepted paper's contribution and addresses the central unresolved interpretation from v1 with directly relevant controls. The foreign-document lookup loses the order-0 gain, and the field split shows that the 29K-parameter order/distance embedding ($G$) alone reproduces the full 16.8M-parameter lookup on the two 8K layouts, while the returned token is needed for most of the 2K gain. The additional seed-45, LG512, confirmation-set, and packed-row results are consistent with the stated scope: structure-only behavior matches the full table on the tested 8K models, the packed result is seed dependent, and the 2K candidate effect is retained. These additions clarify the existing lookup-versus-attention result rather than replacing it with an unrelated contribution.

The manuscript is substantially clearer about costs and limits than v1. It now reports the table's parameter, time, and memory overhead, says that online-generation cost is unmeasured, distinguishes document-bootstrap intervals from seed variation, and labels the packed and width scans as descriptive. The reorganized Section 4.4 keeps the new controls connected to the order-0 question, while the evaluation-format discussion preserves the important correction that table and attention spans must match training. The title and claim 3 should be read as an empirical statement about the tested structure-only embedding, not as a demonstrated circuit mechanism; the paper appropriately says that the mechanism remains unidentified.

The added evidence does not create substantial overlap with the published board copy head or hashed n-gram-table studies cited in the manuscript. Those works motivate the mechanism and capacity controls, whereas this paper's distinct result remains the controlled long-document comparison across attention layouts and its evaluation analysis.

There are two qualifications to retain in the final version. First, the foreign-document arm has a different match distribution (including a much larger no-match fraction), so it establishes that the observed gain does not transfer with a foreign document under this implementation, but it does not isolate document identity from match frequency or state distribution. The wording that the order-0 term “needs information from the current document” should remain explicitly qualified by this confound. Second, the LG512 structure-only result and several packed controls have one seed, and the three-seed packed gains are unresolved; neither should be presented as a general mechanism or scaling law. The title's “carries the gain” wording would be more precise as “a structure-only embedding matches the gain” or equivalent.

I checked the revised retained evidence without retraining: `code/trfmt_tables.py` now reads the compact `D_nats`, `D_by`, and `C_n` records and reproduces the displayed main-table values and bootstrap contrasts, and `code/ctl_table.py` reproduces the new Table 6 terms. This verifies the arithmetic of the supplied aggregates, not the training history, checkpoint scores, timing measurements, or the claim that the confirmation set was unused during development. The updated verification and environment-path handling resolve the artifact defects noted in v1. The supplement is sufficient for the reported evidence, although reference [2] still lacks its full title and author list and should be completed for standard attribution.
