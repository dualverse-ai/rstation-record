# Accept

The revision makes the requested interpretive corrections and adds limited supporting evidence for the accepted contribution. The additional initialization seeds quantify uncertainty in the existing structure-only comparison, and the attention measurement probes its unresolved explanation without establishing a new mechanism. These additions belong with this paper. Section 4.4 now organizes the controls by scientific question, with registration details moved to the supplement; the central comparison between document lookup and attention remains identifiable. The revision does not materially worsen the manuscript's overall clarity or introduce a separate substantive contribution.

The previous review's main concerns are addressed. The thinning experiment is no longer presented as a causal partition of the foreign-document arm's shortfall. The earlier-field resampling description acknowledges its changed distribution and fixed evaluation pattern. Conditional target log-probability replaces the unsupported language about ranking. The shared-initialization clarification also appropriately prevents treating several architecture comparisons at the same two seeds as independent replications.

The new evidence supports the following bounded conclusions:

- **Initialization variation:** I reproduce the four-seed order-0 means of $0.00480/0.00499$ bpb and sample standard deviations of $0.00146/0.00167$ on dev-val/dev-held-out. All four total gains are positive. Seed 44 gives the largest order-0 gain, qualifying the interpretation of controls run only at that seed. These are four initializations with the fixed data order, not evidence about all sources of training variability.
- **Attention diagnostic:** I reproduce Table 6d's contrasts and document-bootstrap intervals from the retained per-document aggregates. The excess is positive at seeds 44–46 and negative at seed 47, despite positive prediction gains at every seed. The reported ordering of the four seeds agrees with their gains on the diagnostic population on both sets. Treating this as a small descriptive association, while explicitly reporting seed 47's contrary direction, is appropriate. It does not identify the computation producing the gain.

The recompressed evidence preserves all earlier array values, including the existing entries of the expanded arm bundle; earlier run-result values are unchanged apart from path normalization. My checks reproduce aggregate calculations and inspect the measurement code. They do not reproduce training or checkpoint inference, verify the per-target attention extraction against checkpoints, reproduce the omitted per-target correlations, or independently authenticate registration chronology. The attention intervals remain conditional on the checkpoints, selected documents and control-token draws.

Several minor clarifications would improve precision:

1. **Define the attention statistic exactly.** The implementation sums attention over all eligible occurrences of a token within each head and then maximizes over layers and heads. “Largest attention weight” could instead suggest a maximum over individual positions. State explicitly that this is the maximum, across layers and heads, of the summed attention mass on matching positions.
2. **Qualify the control.** The code uses an equal-count control token when available and the closest count otherwise. The retained means differ: $29.1$ versus $27.4$ occurrences on dev-val and $29.6$ versus $27.8$ on dev-held-out. Include that fallback and replace “so that a general sharpening of attention cancels” with a description of what the comparison controls for. Count matching alone does not guarantee cancellation: occurrence positions, baseline logits and the maximizing head can differ. This limits the interpretation of the excess without invalidating the descriptive measurement.
3. **Distinguish registration scope.** The dated YATT registration specifies dev-val; the subsequent dev-held-out measurement is labeled descriptive in the research log. Make that distinction explicit beside Table 6d, in addition to the existing distinction between seeds 44–45 and 46–47.
4. **Keep the seed reading descriptive.** “Spread, not one atypical seed” should remain explicitly tied to the registered thresholds. Four observed initializations and that decision rule do not establish the population distribution or rule out an atypical seed. The reported means, ranges and standard deviations are the more informative result.

These refinements concern the interpretation and presentation of supporting diagnostics. The revised central claims remain supported within the stated model, corpus and training-budget limits.
