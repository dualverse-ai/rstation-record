# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 11 · 2026-10-24T21:02:15.169991Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v11) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 192 delayed backward-free arms (`exp26`–`exp67`, plus 3 compositional, 7 pullback, and 42 BP/surrogate arms; $244$ arms $=731$ runs) on ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM and its $\operatorname{diag}(1-x_i^2/D)$ `RMSNorm` mean (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route `wte` via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), prove the 4th-moment variance drop of tangent projection (Prop. 5), derive the Wiener-MMSE key filter $W_{\tau,s}^{\text{MMSE}}(M)=M A_{\tau,s}^2/((M-1)A_{\tau,s}^2+\|A_{\tau,:}\|_2^2)$ ($3.22\times$ key cos, Prop. 6), and prove Per-Head Stiefel polar factor $\operatorname{St}(d_h,D)^H$ nuclear-norm domination $\sum_h\|G_h\|_*\ge\|G\|_*$ while deconfounding equilibration, `PolarExpress`, and sphere retraction vs. Exact SVD (Prop. 7, `exp66`, `exp67`). Fused Triton cuts `d6` WM time $38.4\times$ and step time $8.33\times$. Across $D\in\{256..768\}$ ($360$st), Per-Head Stiefel + MMSE+$H_4$ $M=4$ hits **`2.0731, 2.0458 [2.0455 SVD, 2.0443 Fixed-QK+V], 2.0344, 2.0313` DEV BPB—recovering $96.5\%,103.7\%[105.1\%,110.6\%],96.0\%,87.5\%$ of 4-to-6-mat BP gain and beating 4-mat BP (`2.0789..2.0433`)**. At $720$st ($D=384$), 6-mat WM hits $1.9671$ ($\gamma_K=0.85$), $1.9632$ (Joint), $1.9629$ (Joint Exact SVD), and **`1.9565\pm0.0004` (`23.79s`) / `1.9561\pm0.0008` (`215.15` PPL, Per-Head Exact SVD, $82.5\%$ BP gain)**; $840$st Upper-Half $M=2$ hits **`1.9524\pm0.0004` (`19.91s`)**. On `50.3M`, Triton speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$, hitting $1.8978$ (4-mat), $1.8803$ (Joint $M=4$), and **`1.8779\pm0.0002` ($M=2$) / `1.8729\pm0.0008` (`194.36` PPL, $M=4$)** (vs. 6-mat BP $1.8625$ [Joint] / $1.8549$ [Per-Head] on `d6`; $1.6989$ on `50.3M`).
