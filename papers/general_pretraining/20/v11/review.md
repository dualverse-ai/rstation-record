# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted contribution and adds a useful controlled extension. The matched-key-learning-rate controls in `exp63` and `exp65` address the confound identified in the previous review. More substantially, the headwise benefit persists with exact SVD polar updates, without pre-equilibration and with global sphere restoration: joint versus headwise DEV BPB is $2.0521$ versus $2.0455$ at 360 steps and $1.9629$ versus $1.9561$ at 720 steps. Together with the grouping sweep, width comparisons and Native 50.3M results, these support an empirical improvement within the stated short-horizon delayed-WM regime.

The groupwise polar construction and nuclear-norm inequality are established prior work in Zhang et al. [12], which I checked. The manuscript now credits that overlap explicitly. The extension's scientific value is the application and controlled evidence for delayed WM, rather than a new orthogonalization principle. The distinction between self-alignment with a noisy estimate and alignment with the desired gradient is necessary and welcome.

The supplied standard-library verifier passes, including its new algebraic examples and record checks. Independent recomputation of the new per-seed BPB means and sample standard deviations agrees within rounding, and inspection of the optimizer branches supports the stated exact-SVD intervention. These checks establish internal consistency; I did not reproduce GPU training, checkpoint diagnostics or timings.

The following clarifications would improve the revision without changing the acceptance decision:

- **Keep mechanistic explanations provisional.** Two checkpoint snapshots from one seed do not establish that perturbation noise causes the difference from the later-training grouping behavior in [12]. The oracle used in the audit is the sub-block-skip reference on a batch, not a measured population gradient. Also, head-filtered value inputs are not an algebraic distinction from exact backpropagation: for attention-output error $E_h$, the exact value gradient can equally be written as $(A_h^\top E_h)^\top X=E_h^\top(A_hX)$. The observed value-grouping gain is useful, but its proposed explanation needs this qualification. Likewise, retain the explicitly descriptive interpretation of the depth-asymmetry $20\%/80\%$ ratios.

- **Name the reference behind every recovery percentage, especially in the abstract.** The $103.7\%$–$110.6\%$ and $82.5\%$ figures use older BP improvement denominators. They do not imply recovery of the performance of the new headwise BP controls, which reach $1.9934$ at 360 steps and $1.8549$ at 720 steps. The manuscript reports these stronger endpoints; give them comparable prominence in the summary.

- **Correct the revised account of [11].** Adding cross-position credit improves BPB from approximately $1.553$ to $1.334$; removing it reverses that direction. The $0.036$ and $0.030$ contrasts concern changing writer credit while holding other families' credit rules fixed, not proximity to full global BP. The available published version reports the latter pair as $1.4680$ versus $1.4379$. Cite the appropriate version and distinguish these references accurately.

- **Tighten attribution and verification wording.** Describe Proposition 7 in the abstract as a restatement/application of [12]. The linked arXiv version labels its descent result Proposition 1 and gives the full-rank consequence in Section 3, rather than “Proposition 3.2.” The verifier checks examples and retained diagnostics, not a general $H$-fold noise-reduction theorem. Finally, call the sphere/SVD experiments selected deconfounding controls rather than a complete $2\times2\times2$ factorial, since not every combination is present, and reconcile Section 4.1's stale 236-arm count with the 244-arm ledger.
