# Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Pre-Polar Equilibration, Superposed 1-Hop Highways, and 3-Stage Coordinate-Transport Bridges

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 15 · 2026-10-27T06:51:23.791436Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v15) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a global backward pass (`with torch.no_grad():`) while beating matched-subset node perturbation and scaling across depth $L\in\{6,12\}$? We study delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across $396$ multi-seed arms ($1,187$ runs: $279$ Delayed-WM, $13$ node-perturbation, $4$ head-only, $3$ compositional, $7$ pullback, $90$ BP/surrogate, `exp18`–`exp79`) on ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st), `d12` ($L=12,D=384$, $360$–$720$st), and `Native 50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). For 0-hop credit (`t-2`), we prove stream-partitioned WM and its `RMSNorm` leverage bias (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with causal attention ($+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route `wte` via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` kurtosis (Prop. 4), prove tangent-projection variance drop (Prop. 5), derive the Wiener-MMSE key filter $W_{\tau,s}^{\text{MMSE}}(M)$ ($3.22\times$ cos, Prop. 6), apply groupwise Stiefel factors $\operatorname{St}(gd_h,D)^{H/g}$ (Prop. 7), and prove linear (`PrePolar-Blend`, Prop. 8) and cubic Gram tapering $G_3=(G_nG_n^\top)G_n$ (`Gram3-PrePolar-Blend`, Prop. 9), reaching `1.9407` on `d6` ($720$st, vs. `1.9423` 4-mat BP) and `1.8583`/`1.8554` (`184.97` PPL) on `50.3M`. For cross-layer credit with zero reverse error matmuls through backbone weights ($W_{\text{fc}},W_{\text{proj}},W_{q,k,v}$), we establish two architectures: (i) **Proposition 10 (`exp77`, 4-slot `superposed_4stage`, zero extra forward passes):** superposing 0-hop credit at $(t-1,t-2)$ with 1-hop credit at $(t-3,t-4)$ (`1-Hop MLP` [Class I, zero $A_i^\top u_v$] and `Dual 1-Hop MLP+V` [Class II, cached $A_i^\top u_v$]) reaches **`1.9355`/`1.9362` (MLP) and `1.9247`/`1.9238` (`196.91` PPL, `73.17s`)** on `d6` ($720$st), **`1.9014` (`185.12` PPL)** on `d6` ($840$st), **`2.0606, 2.0291, 2.0157, 2.0082`** across $D\in\{256..768\}$ ($360$st), and **`1.9239` (`196.95` PPL, `141.59s`, vs. `1.9370` 0-hop)** on `d12` ($720$st); and (ii) **Proposition 11 (`exp78`–`exp79`, 3-stage `t-2`, zero backbone reverse matmuls, zero $A_i^\top u_v$):** a rank-$64$ bridge $B_l(h)=h+U_l(V_l^\top h)$ with a 2-call local bridge pullback $V_l(U_l^\top e_l)$, rescaled Step-1 seeding, and multi-horizon bridging reaches **`1.9370` (`34.44s`) / `1.9371` (`32.78s`)** on `d6` ($720$st) and **`1.9337` (`61.58s`) / `1.9335` (`202.21` PPL, `68.11s`)** on `d12` ($720$st, $2.08\times$ faster, `+0.0096` BPB tradeoff).
