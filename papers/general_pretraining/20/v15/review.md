# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds a substantive short-horizon extension. Delayed one-hop MLP/value transport improves on zero-hop WM across the tested widths and at depth 12. The learned forward-displacement bridges provide a smaller improvement while retaining the shorter credit pipeline. These are useful empirical advances in local credit assignment, although they do not establish competitive general pretraining efficiency.

I independently recomputed DEV endpoint means and sample standard deviations for all 84 added arms; they agree with the retained records within rounding. Selected paired comparisons also agree: at 720 steps, the dual highway improves over zero-hop WM by $-0.01683$ BPB on d6, with a descriptive three-seed $95\%$ paired-$t$ interval $[-0.02022,-0.01344]$, and by $-0.01307$ on d12, with interval $[-0.01493,-0.01120]$. The d12 multi-horizon Oja bridge improves over its matched-trainer zero-hop baseline by $-0.00487$, with interval $[-0.00717,-0.00256]$. I inspected the implementations and checked the supplied numerical identities; I did not reproduce GPU training, checkpoint alignments, or timings.

The additions extend the column-contraction and local-credit foundations of [5], the input-bus pooling of [10], and the credit-reference analysis of [11]. The learned forward-displacement target also gives a concrete distinction from the gradient-prediction experiments of Jaderberg et al. [14]. The contribution lies in the delayed transport constructions and their controlled empirical evaluation, rather than new Rademacher identities or new Stiefel geometry. The revised separation of fixed-state contraction diagnostics from deployed delayed training is appropriate.

The following qualifications and corrections matter:

1. **Correct the optimizer matching in Table 8 and Section 4.9.** The supplied `train_d6_exp77_surrogate_bp_arm` defaults to `use_gram3_qk=False`, and none of the 15 BP arm specifications enables it. Thus the bundled driver uses untapered per-head updates, despite Table 8 and Finding 1 describing these arms as using Gram3 tapering. Comparisons against the cubic zero-hop reference at $1.8941$ cannot isolate highway credit under a matched optimizer as currently documented. Use the appropriate untapered reference, already discussed elsewhere in the manuscript, or supply the actual configurations supporting the cubic comparison. The direct WM improvements above do not depend on that mislabeled comparison.

2. **Restrict zero-backward statements to the learning update.** The bridge trainers call `compute_layer_grad_cos_to_global_bp`, which explicitly enables autograd and executes `loss_bp.backward()` at diagnostic checkpoints. It restores the learner's gradients, so inspection does not indicate BP supervision of the WM update. Nevertheless, the complete experimental run contains global backward passes, and these diagnostics fall inside its measured learning-rule interval. Distinguish diagnostic operations from learner operations in the prose and counters. Continue explicitly identifying the attention transpose gather and the two-map auxiliary bridge pullback.

3. **Describe the bridge optimization literally.** In `oja_stiefel_bridge_step`, the encoder update combines the pre-update reconstruction residual with the newly updated decoder $U_l$. This is a stale-residual alternating update, not the exact encoder gradient of the displayed reconstruction objective evaluated at the updated decoder, nor an exact alternating least-squares solve. The conditional transport-variance identities remain valid. Likewise, the orthogonality bounds checked on a sampled random matrix are numerical examples, not bounds for every training iterate; the heuristic normalization does not guarantee the Newton–Schulz convergence condition for arbitrary inputs.

4. **Keep empirical attribution and efficiency claims narrow.** The scalar-versus-column-isolated bridge comparison changes seeding and learning rates as well as transport, as the revised text acknowledges. It cannot isolate variance reduction as the cause of the training difference. “Joint-head residual transport is required” is also stronger than these experiments establish. The confidence intervals describe selected three-seed comparisons after extensive development, not independent confirmatory tests. Strong full-BP controls remain substantially better. Larger-depth endpoints and the reported bridge/highway runtime ratios do not establish an equal-quality or equal-resource advantage.

5. **Qualify the timing evidence and retain its uncertainty.** The same named dual-highway configuration is reported at $73.17$ seconds for 720 steps but $47.53$ seconds for 840 steps. This substantial variation needs explanation before precise speed ratios are treated as reproducible properties. New artifacts retain arm-level timing, PPL and VAL-B means but omit their per-seed values, preventing independent checks of their dispersion. Correct the README's retention claims and provide stable links to the underlying records.

6. **Repair the verification entry points.** `python3 verify_paper5.py` stops at line 1588 because its source-string assertion requires double quotes while the audit source uses single quotes. The preceding numerical assertions in Check 4G succeed; separately invoking Check 4H and the artifact-check functions also succeeds. Static inspection additionally finds that `audit_exp77_mechanisms.py` uses `os.path.exists` without importing `os` in its consolidated-file fallback. These are repairable reproducibility defects, but the advertised all-checks-pass command currently fails. Use syntax-based checks rather than quote-sensitive source matching, and correct the manuscript's experiment-driver filenames.

Acceptance is for the supported developmental credit-assignment extension, with the operational and evidential limits above. A shorter abstract emphasizing the two additions, their strongest controlled results, and the remaining gap to full BP would make that contribution much clearer.
