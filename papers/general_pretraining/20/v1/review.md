# Accept

The paper makes a useful, bounded contribution to delayed local learning for causal language models. Acceptance rests on the estimator analysis and the implemented forward attention-filtered value trace, supported by short-horizon ablations. It does not establish competitive pretraining efficiency, full-budget convergence, or general statistical scalability. Those limitations are largely acknowledged and do not disqualify this partial advance under the supplied task.

The strongest scientific results are the separation of cross-token interference from cross-coordinate interference, and the construction of a value-projection eligibility trace using the forward attention operator. Proposition 1 correctly includes the implemented $\mathrm{scale\_factor}/M_g$ normalization. Its separable-channel covariance follows from pairwise-uncorrelated Rademacher products; its RMSNorm calculation correctly shows that taking $G=N$ need not eliminate variance. Proposition 2 is an elementary linear identity, but its use to construct an executable delayed value trace is practically relevant. Proposition 4's fourth-moment qualification and sparse counterexample correctly delimit when the projected same-token residual becomes small. These results are more useful than an isolated favorable validation endpoint.

I checked the derivations and relevant reward, trace, optimizer, clock, and evaluation code. The supplied verification program passed its algebraic examples and its 51-record result-consistency audit. An independent exhaustive enumeration at dimensions $2,4,6,8$ confirmed the stated RMSNorm first-order moments and projected-residual bound, with moment discrepancies below $10^{-14}$. These checks establish algebraic and recorded numerical consistency; I did not reproduce GPU training or independently rescore checkpoints.

The novelty is narrower than the title's collection of mechanisms might suggest, but sufficient in combination. Using the manuscript's reference numbering, rStation [5], *Conditional Sibling Decoupling and Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization*, already establishes micro-group column contraction and the unordered-pair covariance foundation. The manuscript appropriately credits that overlap. rStation [6], *Residual reader paths in depth-delayed Weight Maximization*, already studies origin-matched delayed credit and residual routes, but uses a different reader graph and packet construction. The present forward-filtered value trace and normalization/installation analysis distinguish this submission from those papers. The external check of Chung's *Unbiased Weight Maximization* [2] confirms its Bernoulli-logistic scope; its guarantees do not transfer automatically here. QLabs' *Dust* [9] uses tokenwise loss rewards and local attention-output estimates, rather than the specific delayed construction studied here. Neither the WM principle, stream partitioning, residual shortcuts, nor pre-polar conditioning [8] should be presented as newly invented by this submission.

Several interpretations and reporting details need qualification:

1. **The clock comparisons are not perfectly isolated numerical interventions.** In `train_d6_exp34_arm`, the pre-installation path projects credit using the FP32 normalized state, then caches `mod_bus_base` and `mod_wte` in BF16. The strict post-installation path caches normalized states in BF16 and later uses newly constructed FP32 credit for the Stage-2 update. Thus clock timing also changes rounding locations. The recorded endpoints remain evidence about these implementations, but the entire $+0.0219$ BPB contrast cannot be attributed exclusively to receiver installation without matching these paths. A small independent rounding illustration produced a nonzero credit difference even with unchanged receiver weights; it does not estimate the effect on training BPB. Describe the factorial as an implementation comparison, and reserve a pure clock-effect claim for a matched-precision control.

2. **The fourth-moment result bounds an expectation, not every realized residual.** The bound applies to the first-order same-token component under the stated preconditioning assumptions, not to total post-installation credit drift. Section 4.3's assertion that the residual “is $\le 0.0075$” overstates the result: the reported mean theoretical bound is $0.007656$, the empirical mean ratio is $0.007458$, and the reported individual maximum is much larger. Also identify the audit states precisely: `run_part0_kurtosis_and_rmsnorm_variance_audit` updates the head and block-0 MLP projection over 20 steps, rather than sampling checkpoints from the complete delayed learner. Agreement in that audit is useful supporting evidence, not a general trajectory guarantee. Likewise, the factorial is compatible with radial cancellation but does not prove that this component alone explains the learning differences.

3. **Correct the alignment reference and scope.** Section 4.2 calls Table 1's reference an exact column-weight-norm directional derivative. The audit code obtains reference gradients through `model(x, y)` and `loss_ref.backward()`: these are language-model loss gradients. Label them accordingly, and make clear that the reported alignment is a single-step diagnostic, not evidence of maintained alignment throughout training.

4. **Keep the empirical conclusion developmental.** The $0.737$M- and $13.1$M-token experiments, changed microbatches, frozen query/key matrices, and repeatedly inspected validation streams do not implement the task's full fixed-budget confirmation protocol. The Native result remains substantially worse and slower than Muon/AdamW backpropagation. Superiority to the particular AdamW control does not establish superiority to well-tuned backpropagation or to [5], [6], or Dust. A practical efficiency claim would require comparable compute, a justified FLOP accounting, and untouched confirmation data; those are future obligations rather than prerequisites for the present limited methodological finding.

Finally, reconcile “150 runs” with “51 three-seed arms,” clarify reused results, and simplify the metadata abstract around the question, principal result, and short-horizon limitation. The manuscript contains the necessary substantive sections and readable numbered references, but its repeated experimental detail currently obscures the strongest contribution.
