# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 10 · 2026-10-24T12:04:41.642655Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v10) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 162 delayed backward-free arms (`exp26`–`exp61`, plus 3 compositional, 7 pullback, and 36 BP/surrogate arms; $208$ arms $=623$ runs) on ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM and its $\operatorname{diag}(1-x_i^2/D)$ `RMSNorm` mean (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route `wte` via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), prove the 4th-moment variance drop of tangent projection (Prop. 5), and derive the Wiener-MMSE key filter $W_{\tau,s}^{\text{MMSE}}(M)=M A_{\tau,s}^2/((M-1)A_{\tau,s}^2+\|A_{\tau,:}\|_2^2)$ ($3.22\times$ key cos, Prop. 6). Fused Triton cuts `d6` WM time $38.4\times$ ($134.31\to3.50$ ms) and step time $8.33\times$ ($155.6\to18.68$ ms). Across $D\in\{256..768\}$ ($360$st), 6-mat $M=4$ (`2.0794, 2.0552, 2.0451, 2.0437`; **`2.0519\pm0.0009` Wiener-MMSE+$H_4$ at $D=384$**) attains **numerical proximity to 4-mat BP (`2.0789..2.0433`, $-0.0013$ to $+0.0020$ BPB; not 6-mat BP `2.0590..2.0153`)**. At $720$st ($D=384$), 6-mat WM hits $1.9756$ ($M=2$), $1.9672$ ($M=4, \gamma_K=0.85$), and **$1.9632\pm0.0006$ (`1.9284` `VAL-B`, $68.8\%$ of 4-to-6-mat BP gain; $1.9633$ in `24.36s` via sparse Top-$K=32$) with Wiener-MMSE $M=4$**, while $840$st Upper-Half $M=2$ hits $1.9609\pm0.0006$. On `50.3M`, Triton speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$, hitting $1.8978$ (4-mat), $1.8853$ (6-mat $M=2$), and **$1.8849\pm0.0003$ ($M=2$) / $1.8803\pm0.0007$ (`1.8668` `VAL-B`, `198.42` PPL, $M=4$) on Upper-Half `(4..7)` Wiener-MMSE+$H_4$**. Short-horizon results trail 6-mat BP ($1.8904$/$1.8625$ on `d6`; $1.6989$ on `50.3M`).
