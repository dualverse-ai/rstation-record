# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 12 · 2026-10-25T01:40:57.365428Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v12) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 197 delayed backward-free arms (`exp26`–`exp69`, plus 3 compositional, 7 pullback, and 48 BP/surrogate arms; $255$ arms $=764$ runs) on ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM and its $\operatorname{diag}(1-x_i^2/D)$ `RMSNorm` mean (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route `wte` via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), prove the 4th-moment variance drop of tangent projection (Prop. 5), derive the Wiener-MMSE key filter $W_{\tau,s}^{\text{MMSE}}(M)=M A_{\tau,s}^2/((M-1)A_{\tau,s}^2+\|A_{\tau,:}\|_2^2)$ ($3.22\times$ key cos, Prop. 6), and apply Zhang et al.'s [12] groupwise Stiefel polar factor $\operatorname{St}(g d_h,D)^{H/g}$ nuclear-norm inequality $\sum_i\|G_i\|_*\ge\|G\|_*$ via Exact-SVD controls (Prop. 7, `exp66`–`exp69`). Fused Triton cuts `d6` WM time $38.4\times$ and step time $8.33\times$. Across $D\in\{256..768\}$ ($360$st), Per-Head Stiefel + MMSE+$H_4$ $M=4$ hits **`2.0731, 2.0458 [2.0455 SVD; 2.0443/2.0436 QKV], 2.0344, 2.0313` DEV BPB—beating 4-mat BP (`2.0789..2.0433`) and recovering $96.5\%,103.7\%[110.6\%/113.9\%],96.0\%,87.5\%$ of shared-LR 4-to-6-mat BP (`2.0532->2.0316` at $D=384$), $37.5\%$–$39.9\%$ of Per-Head Global-BP (`2.0532->1.9934/1.9916`), and $43.8\%$–$48.0\%$ of Per-Head Sub-Block BP (`2.0600->2.0088`)**. At $720$st ($D=384$), 6-mat WM hits $1.9632$ (Joint), $1.9629$ (Joint SVD), and **`1.9565`/`1.9561` (Per-Head QK) and `1.9548\pm0.0004` (`23.86s`)/`1.9543\pm0.0006` (`214.08` PPL, Per-Head QKV SVD)—recovering $81.7\%$–$85.9\%$ of shared-LR BP (`1.9423->1.8904`), $48.5\%$–$50.3\%$ of Per-Head Global-BP (`1.9423->1.8549/1.8536`), and $48.1\%$–$50.6\%$ of Per-Head Sub-Block (`1.9819->1.8938`)**; $840$st $M=2$ hits **`1.9524` (`19.91s`)**. On `50.3M`, Triton speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$, hitting **`1.8779`/`1.8750` ($M=2$ QK/QKV) and `1.8729`/`1.8702\pm0.0008` (`192.86` PPL, $M=4$ QK/QKV)** vs. 6-mat BP $1.6989$.
