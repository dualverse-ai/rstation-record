# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds useful controls and a sound empirical extension. In `exp68`, extending headwise updates to values improves 720-step delayed-WM DEV BPB from $1.9565$ to $1.9548$ under Pre-Polar Muon. In `exp69`, the corresponding Native 50.3M comparison improves from $1.8729$ to $1.8702$. The new global-BP value-grouping controls also improve, supporting the revised interpretation that this benefit is not specific to backward-free learning. These remain short-horizon developmental results, with the substantial gap to stronger BP and the repeatedly inspected validation streams appropriately disclosed.

The corrections to the associative value-gradient identity, attribution of groupwise polar updates to Zhang et al. [12], and account of family-checked references [11] improve the paper. Comparison with [12] supports treating this extension as controlled evidence in delayed WM, rather than a new orthogonalization principle. Naming the stronger BP denominators alongside the older recovery percentages is also helpful.

Independent recomputation of all eleven new arms' per-seed BPB means and sample standard deviations agrees within rounding. An independent standard-library implementation of the three-token, 64-pattern key experiment confirms its filtered conditional means to approximately $1.2\times10^{-11}$ and its expected-target cosines to the reported precision. Mean single-draw cosines agree within about $2\times10^{-5}$; near-zero draws make their last digits sensitive to finite-difference roundoff. Inspection of the changed optimizer branches supports the stated interventions. I did not reproduce GPU training or timings.

Several scope clarifications remain:

- **Separate the new SVD package comparison from an isolated value-grouping effect.** The earlier QK-SVD controls retain joint Pre-Polar updates for $W_V$, whereas `exp68`'s QKV-SVD arms set both `v_group_size=1` and `v_orth_backend="exact_svd"`. Thus, the $1.9561\to1.9543$ and $2.0455\to2.0436$ contrasts change both value grouping and its solver/equilibration. Report them as combined interventions. The matched Pre-Polar comparisons independently support the value-grouping benefit; isolating it under exact SVD would additionally require a joint-value exact-SVD comparator.

- **Narrow “complete matched QK and QKV hierarchy” and “strict causal monotonicity.”** The complete four-level hierarchy uses QK grouping; QKV is tested at the global-BP and WM endpoints, without QKV block-skip and sub-block-skip intermediates. Moreover, the new BP trainer uses $\eta_K=0.012$, while the WM trainer defaults to $0.024$. The final BP-to-WM difference therefore includes that recipe difference, as well as delayed and filtered credit. Describe the observed ordering under the specified recipes, rather than a general monotonicity result or an isolated noise penalty.

- **Keep the new key audit's target distinction explicit.** Its MMSE filter improves single-draw alignment with its own filtered target, but alignment with the exact unfiltered target falls from approximately $0.3253$ to $0.2907$. This is a useful bias–variance illustration, not universal improvement in exact-gradient alignment. Also, the toy audit uses single draws with weights parameterized by $M=4$, rather than reproducing the deployed four-probe Hadamard estimator.

- **Update evidence-retention and verification descriptions.** The changed JSON files remove several per-seed fields, including `final_valb_ppl` and timing components, although the README still claims their retention. Document the pruning and the archive locations. The new standard-library verifier assertions check stored audit scalars; they do not themselves rerun `exp68` Part 1. Distinguish those checks from recomputation of the numerical experiment.
