# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 5 · 2026-10-23T06:28:35.184717Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v5) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a multi-layer causal Transformer be trained without a backward pass—with zero reverse pullbacks, zero VJP helpers, and zero same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while improving speed and short-horizon accuracy over matched-subset node-perturbation comparators? We study a 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) pipeline across 108 backward-free multi-seed arms (`exp26`–`exp48`, plus 7 local-pullback and 13 exact-backpropagation references; $128$ arms $=383$ runs) on development-only `DEV`/`VAL-B` splits of ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$720$ steps) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$ steps). We analyze stream-partitioned WM for separable channels and `RMSNorm` interfaces (Proposition 1), commute $W_v$ with causal attention during the forward pass to lift `attn.c_v` cosine alignment $8.2\times$ ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$, Proposition 2), route lexical credit via a diagonal skip surrogate (Proposition 3), and bound the expected same-token `RMSNorm` post-install residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Proposition 4). Fused Triton kernels cut the 13-matrix `d6` WM suite $38.4\times$ ($134.31\to3.50$ ms) and `fwd+rule` step time $8.33\times$ ($155.6\to18.68$ ms). On 4-matrix `d6` ($24.58\text{M}$ active parameters), co-designed `Pre-Polar Muon`, embedding velocity, and drift annealing reach $2.0682\pm0.0006$ `DEV` BPB (vs. $2.0532$ 4-matrix Backpropagation, $2.2603$ $K=16$ local node perturbation, and $2.2415$ 76-draw suffix-recomputed node perturbation). Extending to 5-matrix Spherical Active-$W_Q$ (`exp45`) reaches $2.0641\pm0.0006$, and full 6-matrix ($26.35\text{M}$, $100\%$ active) decoupled $(W_Q,W_K)$ with $\gamma_K=0.85$ future-query key credit (`exp47`) reaches $2.0631\pm0.0008$ ($M=1$) and **$2.0595\pm0.0007$ `DEV` BPB ($M=2$ amortized local probes)**. At fixed 100 steps on Native `50.3M`, isolated Triton substitution speeds up `fwd+rule` $1.44\times$ (`exp32`) and $6.33\times$ ($M_g=1$, `exp43`), reaching $1.8978\pm0.0007$ `DEV` BPB on 4 matrices, $1.8897\pm0.0008$ on 5-matrix Active-$W_Q$ (`exp46`, $9.80$ GiB), and **$1.8884\pm0.0007$ ($M=1$, $10.08$ GiB) and $1.8853\pm0.0013$ `DEV` BPB ($201.21$ PPL, $10.24$ GiB) on full 6-matrix Active-$(W_Q,W_K)$ at $M=2$ (`exp48`, $99.999\%$ active)**, vs. 6-matrix $1.6989$ backpropagation.
