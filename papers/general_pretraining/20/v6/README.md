# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 6 · 2026-10-23T14:05:16.004819Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v6) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a causal Transformer be trained without a backward pass—with zero reverse pullbacks, zero VJP helpers, and zero same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while improving speed and accuracy over matched-subset node-perturbation comparators? We study a 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) pipeline across 114 backward-free multi-seed arms (`exp26`–`exp49`, plus 7 local-pullback and 13 exact-BP references; $134$ arms $=401$ runs) on `DEV`/`VAL-B` splits of ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$720$ steps) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$ steps). We analyze stream-partitioned WM (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with causal attention to lift `attn.c_v` cosine $8.2\times$ ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$, Prop. 2), route lexical credit via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound expected same-token `RMSNorm` post-install residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), and prove the exact fourth-moment variance reduction of reward-side tangent projection (Prop. 5). Fused Triton kernels cut the `d6` WM suite $38.4\times$ ($134.31\to3.50$ ms) and step time $8.33\times$ ($155.6\to18.68$ ms). On 4-matrix `d6` ($24.58\text{M}$ active), co-designed `Pre-Polar Muon`, embedding velocity, and drift annealing reach $2.0682\pm0.0006$ `DEV` BPB (vs. $2.0532$ 4-mat BP, $2.2603$ $K=16$ `SR-LNP`, and $2.2415$ 76-draw `SR-NP`). 5-matrix Active-$W_Q$ (`exp45`) reaches $2.0641\pm0.0006$; 6-matrix decoupled $(W_Q,W_K)$ with $\gamma_K=0.85$ key credit (`exp47`, `exp49`) reaches $2.0631\pm0.0008$ ($M=1$), $2.0595\pm0.0007$ ($M=2$), **$2.0591\pm0.0002$ (+active `ve_gate`, $100\%$ matrix weights), $2.0552\pm0.0007$ ($M=4$, $60.2\%$ of 4-to-6-matrix BP gain), and $1.9763\pm0.0009$ ($227.41$ PPL in $21.67\text{s}$) at $720$ steps**. On 100-step Native `50.3M`, isolated Triton substitution speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$ ($M_g=1$), reaching $1.8978\pm0.0007$ (4-mat), $1.8897\pm0.0008$ (5-mat), and **$1.8853\pm0.0013$ `DEV` BPB ($201.21$ PPL, $10.24$ GiB VRAM) on 6-matrix $M=2$ (`exp48`, $99.9990\%$ active)**.
