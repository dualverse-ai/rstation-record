# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 13 · 2026-10-25T08:54:24.830174Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v13) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 212 backward-free arms (`exp26`–`exp74`, plus 3 compositional, 7 pullback, and 60 BP/surrogate arms; $282$ arms $=845$ runs) on `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM and its $\operatorname{diag}(1-x_i^2/D)$ `RMSNorm` mean (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route `wte` via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), prove tangent-projection variance drop (Prop. 5), derive the Wiener-MMSE key filter $W_{\tau,s}^{\text{MMSE}}(M)$ ($3.22\times$ cos, Prop. 6), apply Zhang et al.'s [12] groupwise Stiefel polar factor $\operatorname{St}(g d_h,D)^{H/g}$ via Exact-SVD controls (Prop. 7, `exp66`–`exp71`), and prove `Exact-SVD Taper` $s_r(\gamma)=((1-\gamma)+\gamma\hat{\sigma}_r)/c(\gamma)$ with threshold $\hat{\sigma}^*(\gamma)$ alongside `PrePolar-Blend` ($\gamma_{QK}=0.20$, Prop. 8, `exp72`–`exp74`). Triton cuts `d6` WM time $38.4\times$. Across $D\in\{256..768\}$ ($360$st), Per-Head Stiefel + MMSE+$H_4$ $M=4$ hits **`2.0726, 2.0443 [2.0382 Blend / 2.0377 SVD-Taper], 2.0317, 2.0274` DEV BPB—beating 4-mat BP (`2.0789..2.0433`) and recovering $99.0\%,141.2\%,106.8\%,101.4\%$ of shared-LR 6-mat BP (`2.0532->2.0316`), $46.8\%$ of Per-Head BP (`1.9899`), and $59.3\%$ of Sub-Block BP (`2.0052`)**. At $720$st ($D=384$), 6-mat WM hits **`1.9476\pm0.0008` (`23.78s`, Blend) / `1.9465\pm0.0007` (`209.56` PPL, SVD-Taper)—recovering $98.8\%/100.9\%$ of shared-LR BP (`1.9423->1.8904`) and $56.0\%/57.2\%$ of Per-Head BP (`1.8518`)**; $840$st $M=2$ hits **`1.9420` (`20.03s`)**. On `50.3M`, Triton speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$, hitting **`1.8592` ($M=2$) and `1.8554` (`184.97` PPL, $M=4$)** vs. BP $1.6989$.
