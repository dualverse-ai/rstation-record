# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 8 · 2026-10-23T22:42:08.218908Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v8) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 133 backward-free multi-seed arms (`exp26`–`exp54`, plus 7 pullback and 15 exact-BP arms; $155$ arms $=464$ runs) on repeatedly inspected `DEV`/`VAL-B` splits over short horizons on ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route lexical credit via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), and prove the 4th-moment variance drop of reward tangent projection (Prop. 5). Fused Triton cuts `d6` WM time $38.4\times$ ($134.31\to3.50$ ms) and step time $8.33\times$ ($155.6\to18.68$ ms). On 360st `d6`, 4-mat WM hits $2.0682\pm0.0006$ `DEV` BPB (vs. $2.0532$ 4-mat BP, $2.2603$ `SR-LNP`, $2.2415$ `SR-NP`); 6-mat $(W_Q,W_K)$ (`99.9978%` active) hits $2.0552\pm0.0007$ ($M=4$, $60.2\%$ of BP gain), and at $720$st hits $1.9756\pm0.0017$ ($M=2$, `21.77s`) and $1.9672\pm0.0005$ (pure $M=4$, $61.1\%$ of BP gain). Uniform 6-mat $M=2$ trails extra-step 4-mat WM at equal time ($2.0454$ at $438$st; $1.9720$ at $1,004$st in this trainer), whereas Upper-Half `(3,4,5)` captures $90.1\%$–$92.9\%$ of the 720st gain (`1.9779`, `18.59s`), hits $1.9699\pm0.0001$ ($720$st $M=4$, `21.05s`) and $1.9670\pm0.0002$ ($740$st $M=4$, `19.19s`), and **beats 1,004st 4-mat WM at matched `~21.6s` same-trainer time ($840$st $M=2$: $1.9609\pm0.0006$ `DEV`, $1.9264\pm0.0007$ `VAL-B`, $218.03$ PPL)**. On 100st `50.3M`, isolated Triton speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$ ($M_g=1$), hitting $1.8978\pm0.0007$ (4-mat) and **$1.8853\pm0.0013$ `DEV` BPB ($201.21$ PPL) on 6-mat $M=2$ ($99.9990\%$ active)**, trailing 6-mat BP ($1.8904$ on 720st `d6`; $1.6989$ on `50.3M`).
