# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 7 · 2026-10-23T18:22:39.165172Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v7) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 125 backward-free multi-seed arms (`exp26`–`exp51`, plus 7 pullback and 15 exact-BP arms; $147$ arms $=440$ runs) on repeatedly inspected `DEV`/`VAL-B` splits over short horizons on ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route lexical credit via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), and prove the 4th-moment variance drop of reward tangent projection in the pre-rotary basis (Prop. 5). Fused Triton cuts `d6` WM time $38.4\times$ ($134.31\to3.50$ ms) and step time $8.33\times$ ($155.6\to18.68$ ms). On 360st `d6`, 4-mat WM hits $2.0682\pm0.0006$ `DEV` BPB (vs. $2.0532$ 4-mat BP, $2.2603$ `SR-LNP`, $2.2415$ `SR-NP`); 6-mat $(W_Q,W_K)$ (`99.9978%` active) hits $2.0595\pm0.0007$ ($M=2$, `9.35s`) and $2.0552\pm0.0007$ ($M=4$, $60.2\%$ of BP gain `2.0532->2.0316`), and at $720$st hits $1.9763\pm0.0009$ ($M=2$, `21.67s`) and $1.9683\pm0.0007$ ($M=4+$`ve_gate`, $59.0\%$ of BP gain `1.9423->1.8904`). Full-depth 6-mat $M=2$ is unfavorable at equal time vs. extra-step 4-mat WM ($2.0454$ at $438$st; $1.9720$ at $1,004$st, `21.55s`), whereas Upper-Half `(3,4,5)` probing captures $92.9\%$ of the 720st gain (`1.9779`, `18.59s`) and **beats 1,004st 4-mat WM at matched `~21.6s` time ($840$st: $1.9609\pm0.0006$ `DEV`, $1.9264\pm0.0007$ `VAL-B`, $218.03$ PPL)**. On 100st `50.3M`, isolated Triton speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$ ($M_g=1$), hitting $1.8978\pm0.0007$ (4-mat) and **$1.8853\pm0.0013$ `DEV` BPB ($201.21$ PPL, $10.24$ GiB) on 6-mat $M=2$ ($99.9990\%$ active)**, trailing 6-mat BP ($1.8904$ on 720st `d6`; $1.6989$ on `50.3M`).
