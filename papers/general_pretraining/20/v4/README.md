# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 4 · 2026-10-23T02:25:46.803724Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a multi-layer causal Transformer be trained without a backward pass—executing zero reverse pullbacks, zero VJP helpers, and zero same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while improving speed and short-horizon accuracy over matched-subset node-perturbation comparators? We study a 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) pipeline across 97 backward-free multi-seed arms (`exp26`–`exp46`, plus 7 local-pullback and 13 exact-backpropagation references; $117$ arms $=350$ runs) on development-only `DEV`/`VAL-B` splits of ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$720$ steps) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$ steps). We analyze stream-partitioned WM for separable channels and `RMSNorm` interfaces (Proposition 1), commute $W_v$ with causal attention during the forward pass to lift single-step `attn.c_v` cosine alignment $8.2\times$ ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$, Proposition 2), route lexical credit via a diagonal skip surrogate (Proposition 3), and bound the expected same-token `RMSNorm` post-installation residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Proposition 4), verified across a 16-cell factorial and pure-`FP32` controls ($2.1359$ vs. $2.1579$ BPB). Fused register-tiled Triton kernels cut the 13-matrix `d6` WM suite $38.4\times$ ($134.31\to3.50$ ms/step) and `fwd+rule` step time $8.33\times$ ($155.6\to18.68$ ms/step). Co-designed `Pre-Polar Muon`, embedding velocity, and drift annealing reach $2.0682\pm0.0006$ `DEV` BPB on 4-matrix `d6` (vs. $2.0532$ 4-matrix Backpropagation, $2.2603$ $K=16$ `SR-LNP`, $2.2415$ 76-draw `SR-NP`), and Spherical Active-$W_Q$ (`exp45`, $W_K$ frozen) reaches $2.0641\pm0.0006$ ($2.0649\pm0.0006$ un-retracted; $2.0672\pm0.0006$ future-accumulated $W_K$). On Native `50.3M`, isolated Triton substitution cuts steady-state `fwd+rule` time $1.44\times$ (`exp32`) and $6.33\times$ ($M_g=1$, `exp43`), reaching $1.8978\pm0.0007$ `DEV` BPB on 4 matrices ($20.65$ GiB GPU-resident; $9.61$–$9.69$ GiB pinned-ring) and **$1.8897\pm0.0008$ `DEV` BPB ($1.8765\pm0.0013$ `VAL-B`, $203.72$ PPL) in $9.80$ GiB VRAM** with Spherical Active-$W_Q$ (`exp46`, $95.8\%$ active), trailing 6-matrix $1.6989$ backpropagation.
