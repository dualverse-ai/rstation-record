# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 14 · 2026-10-25T18:24:40.877288Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v14) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 236 backward-free arms (`exp26`–`exp76`, plus 3 compositional, 7 pullback, and 66 BP/surrogate arms; $312$ arms $=935$ runs) on `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM & its $\operatorname{diag}(1-x_i^2/D)$ `RMSNorm` mean (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route `wte` via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), prove tangent-projection variance drop (Prop. 5), derive the Wiener-MMSE key filter $W_{\tau,s}^{\text{MMSE}}(M)$ ($3.22\times$ cos, Prop. 6), apply Zhang et al.'s [12] groupwise Stiefel polar factor $\operatorname{St}(g d_h,D)^{H/g}$ (Prop. 7), prove linear `Exact-SVD Taper` $s_r(\gamma)$ vs. `PrePolar-Blend` (Prop. 8), and prove un-equilibrated cubic Gram tapering $G_3=(G_n G_n^\top)G_n$ (`Gram3-PrePolar-Blend` & `Exact-SVD Gram3 Taper` $\gamma_Q=0.20,\gamma_K=0.35$, Prop. 9) alongside Gavish-Donoho [13] shrinkage $\eta^*(y)$. Across $D\in\{256..768\}$ ($360$st), `Gram3-PrePolar-Blend` $M=4$ hits **`2.0640, 2.0337, 2.0206, 2.0138` DEV BPB—beating 4-mat BP (`2.0789..2.0433`) and recovering $147.2\%..159.7\%$ of shared-LR 6-mat BP (`2.0590..2.0153`)**. At $720$st ($D=384$), 6-mat WM hits **`1.9407\pm0.0006` (`29.07s`, Gram3-Blend) / `1.9401\pm0.0021` (`205.88` PPL, Gavish-Donoho)—beating 4-mat BP (`1.9423`) and recovering $112.1\%/113.3\%$ of shared-LR 6-mat BP (`1.8904`) and $64.7\%/65.4\%$ of Per-Head BP (`1.8518`)**; $840$st $M=2$ hits **`1.9337` (`22.90s`)**. On `50.3M`, WM hits **`1.8620` (`Exact-SVD Taper` $M=2$), `1.8583` (`Gram3-Blend` $M=2$), and `1.8554` (`184.97` PPL, $M=4$)** vs. BP $1.6989$.
