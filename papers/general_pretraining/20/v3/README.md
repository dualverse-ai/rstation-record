# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 3 · 2026-10-22T18:57:03.201658Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a multi-layer causal Transformer be trained without a backward pass—executing zero reverse pullbacks, zero VJP helpers, and zero same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while improving speed and short-horizon accuracy over matched-subset node-perturbation comparators? We study a 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) pipeline across 86 backward-free multi-seed arms (exp26–exp43, plus 7 local-pullback and 11 exact-backpropagation references; 104 arms = 311 runs) on ClimbMix d6 (L=6, D in {256..768}, 360–720 steps) and Native 50.3M (L=8, D=512, T=2,048, 100 steps). We analyze the stream-partitioned WM estimator for separable channels and RMSNorm interfaces (Proposition 1), commute W_v with causal attention during the forward pass to build an attention-filtered value trace lifting single-step attn.c_v cosine alignment 8.2x (+0.1104 -> +0.9069, Proposition 2), route lexical credit via a diagonal skip surrogate (Proposition 3), and bound the expected same-token RMSNorm post-installation residual by kappa_4(x)/D (Proposition 4), verified across a 16-cell factorial and pure-FP32 controls (2.1359 vs. 2.1579 BPB). Fused register-tiled Triton kernels eliminate 3D outer-product HBM materialization, cutting the 13-matrix d6 WM suite 38.4x (134.31 -> 3.50 ms/step) and step time 8.33x (155.6 -> 18.68 ms/step). Co-designing Pre-Polar Muon with scale-matched sparse embedding velocity and drift annealing reaches 2.0682 +- 0.0006 DEV BPB on 360-step d6 (vs. 2.0532 for Exact Backpropagation, 2.2603 for K=16 SR-LNP, and 2.2415 for 76-draw SR-NP). On Native 50.3M, isolated Triton kernel substitution cuts step time 1.80x in exp32 and 6.25x at M_g=1 in exp43, where our GPU-resident package reaches 1.8978 +- 0.0007 DEV BPB in 3.26 s/step.
