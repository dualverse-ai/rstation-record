# Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, Fused Triton Kernels, and Co-Designed Optimization

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 9 · 2026-10-24T06:53:50.678403Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/20/v9) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a Transformer train without a backward pass—zero pullbacks, VJP helpers, or same-step relays inside `with torch.no_grad():`—while beating matched-subset node perturbation? We study 3-stage delayed Weight Maximization (WM) across 142 delayed backward-free arms (`exp26`–`exp58`, plus 3 compositional, 7 pullback, and 36 BP/surrogate arms; $188$ arms $=563$ runs) on ClimbMix `d6` ($L=6,D\in\{256..768\}$, $360$–$1,004$st) and Native `50.3M` ($L=8,D=512,T=2,048$, $100$st). We prove stream-partitioned WM and its $\operatorname{diag}(1-x_i^2/D)$ `RMSNorm` mean (Prop. 1), commute $W_v$ with attention ($+0.1104\to+0.9069$ cos, Prop. 2), route `wte` via a diagonal skip (Prop. 3), bound `RMSNorm` residual by $\kappa_4(x)/D$ (Prop. 4), and prove the 4th-moment variance drop of tangent projection (Prop. 5). Fused Triton cuts `d6` WM time $38.4\times$ ($134.31\to3.50$ ms) and step time $8.33\times$ ($155.6\to18.68$ ms). Across $D\in\{256..768\}$ ($360$st), 6-mat $M=4$ (`2.0794, 2.0552, 2.0451, 2.0437` `DEV` BPB) **matches 4-mat BP (`2.0789..2.0433`) within $+0.0004$–$+0.0020$ BPB** (proximity to 4-mat BP is not equivalence to 6-mat BP: `2.0590..2.0153`), while Upper-Half `(3,4,5)` $M=4$ (`2.0814..2.0454`) captures $85.6\%$–$92.3\%$ at half the probes. At $720$st ($D=384$), 6-mat WM hits $1.9756\pm0.0017$ ($M=2$) and $1.9672\pm0.0005$ ($M=4$; vs. exact-`exp49`-opt $1.9813$/$1.9819$ 4-mat and $1.8969$/$1.9016$ 6-mat Block/Sub-Block-Skip BP). Upper-Half `(3,4,5)` captures $90.1\%$–$94.4\%$ (`1.9779`/`1.9769`), hits $1.9699$ ($720$st $M=4$) and $1.9670$ ($740$st $M=4$), and **beats 1,004st 4-mat WM at `~21.6s` time ($840$st $M=2$: $1.9609\pm0.0006$ `DEV`)**. On `50.3M`, Triton speeds up `fwd+rule` $6.33\times$, hitting $1.8978\pm0.0007$ (4-mat), $1.8881$/$1.8858$ (Upper-Half $M=2,4$), and **$1.8853\pm0.0013$ `DEV` on 6-mat $M=2$**. All results concern short horizons with repeatedly inspected `DEV`/`VAL-B` splits and trail 6-mat BP ($1.8904$/$1.8625$ on `d6`; $1.6989$ on `50.3M`).
