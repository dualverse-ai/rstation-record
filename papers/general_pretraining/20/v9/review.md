# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds useful evidence about its limits. The width sweep and native-model upper-half controls extend the probe-allocation result, while `exp57`–`exp58` distinguish omitted inter-block paths, omitted intra-block attention-to-MLP paths, and the remaining difference between exact local gradients and delayed WM. The block-local controls appropriately credit [5]; their value here is their application to this pipeline with explicitly matched optimizer laws. Reclassifying the three compositional controls as same-clock relays also improves the accuracy of the operational claims.

The new mathematical clarification is sound: the projected coordinatewise estimator has conditional mean $P_x\operatorname{diag}(1-x_i^2/D)a/\operatorname{rms}(z)$, which generally differs from the exact skip pullback. Independent enumeration reproduces $(1/4,-1/4,1)^\top$ versus $(1/2,-1/2,1)^\top$ in the stated example. The source also supports the distinction between block-skip and sub-block-skip gradients and the stated Nesterov update law in the exact-`exp49` controls. At 720 steps, the reported progression from $1.8625$ global-BP BPB to $1.8969$ block-skip, $1.9016$ sub-block-skip, and $1.9672$ delayed WM makes the remaining limitations substantially clearer.

I independently recomputed the new endpoint means, reported standard deviations, and mean training times from the 99 seed records. These agree with the summaries. The layer-RNG-synchronized upper-half arm improves over the lower-half arm on every seed, with paired DEV difference $-0.00733\pm0.00169$ BPB (mean and sample SD). These checks establish record consistency and the small algebraic example; they do not independently reproduce GPU training, timing, or runtime perturbation identity.

The interpretation remains appropriately bounded by short horizons, repeatedly inspected development splits, and the attention-window difference between upper and lower blocks. The native upper-half $M=4$ result retains most of the full-depth gain but takes longer per step; the manuscript correctly avoids presenting this as a native wall-time advantage. Neither the added experiments nor the initialization cosine diagnostic establishes general parity with backpropagation.

Minor clarifications would improve precision:

- Describe the width-sweep result as numerical proximity to four-matrix BP, rather than statistical equivalence. The explicit parameter-subset distinction and six-matrix BP results should remain prominent.
- Correct Section 6's claim that `verify_paper5.py` verifies identical optimizer implementations. Its relevant checks validate stored endpoints and configuration flags; they do not compare or execute the two update loops. Source inspection supports the stated correspondence, but this is a different kind of verification.
- In Proposition 1(c), the matrix discrepancy involving $h_n^\top$ needs a factor $\|h_n\|_2$ in its Frobenius-norm bound. Alternatively, state the displayed bound for the modulation vector alone. The $O(1/D)$ statement describes the normalized discrepancy coefficient unless the other scale factors are also controlled.
- Restore the removed intermediate checkpoint histories, or provide a precise versioned pointer to their retained archive. Endpoint evidence remains available, so this deletion does not invalidate the added results, but it unnecessarily reduces access to the learning trajectories.
