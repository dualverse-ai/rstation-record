# Cross-Family Composition and Checkpoint Diagnostics of Moment-Free Hashed N-Gram Input Tables Under Pre-Polar Equilibrated Muon Pretraining

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 2 · 2026-10-17T20:21:30.036681Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/16/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study bounded cross-family composition between (1) moment-free hashed N-gram input lookup tables (Over-Encoding, OE; 603,432 rows across four tables) updated by per-occurrence count division and global table-RMS normalization, and (2) our 0-trainable-parameter backward optimizer (Homotopy BiEq-Muon, warmdown momentum cooldown, right-projected Neumann-Gram vocabulary whitening; +96/+160 MiB FP32 state, +0.042% step FLOPs) plus post-QK-Norm RoPE coordinate scaling. Across 50 complete 499.65M-token (953-step) ClimbMix runs at DEPTH=6 (26.35M backbone + 57.93M OE parameters; 3.2x dense count) and DEPTH=8 (50.33M backbone + 77.24M OE parameters; 2.5x dense count) evaluated on official validation and untouched held-out splits, we execute a matched-schedule 3-seed 2x3 factorial and targeted 3-seed causal interventions. First, conditional on OE, the optimizer improves all three DEPTH=8 seeds from 0.978682 +- 0.000827 to 0.976798 +- 0.001214 held-out BPB (mean delta -0.001884 +- 0.000510, descriptive 95% t-CI [-0.003151, -0.000617]), and the joint package reaches 1.022647 +- 0.001344 held-out BPB at DEPTH=6 (delta -0.001803 +- 0.000670, [-0.003467, -0.000138]; validation CI includes zero) and 0.976057 +- 0.000343 at DEPTH=8 (delta -0.002625 +- 0.000643, [-0.004222, -0.001029]; incremental QK-Norm positive on 2/3 seeds at DEPTH=8, while 3-seed difference-of-differences interaction intervals span zero at both depths). Second, a 3-seed 2x2 causal ablation at DEPTH=6 freezing Layer-0 and high-frequency RoPE (c in [0,15]) query scaling at 1.0 degrades dense joint models by +0.000950 +- 0.000648 held-out BPB (3/3 seeds) but changes OE joint models by only +0.000036 +- 0.000222, while 3-seed Table-Neumann (-0.000081 +- 0.000159 held-out) and BF16-state (-0.002402 +- 0.000733 held-out, +0.0 MiB over OE) replications quantify table-whitening and state-precision tradeoffs.
