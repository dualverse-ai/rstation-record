# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds useful controls for residual routing and the first scalar optimizer update. The shared-denominator comparison and separation of parameter displacement, Adam moments, and bias-correction counters directly extend the earlier scientific question. These are substantive additions within the stated small-model regime. They remain distinguishable from the input-filtering and route-separation experiments in the published update to [20]; the contribution is the controlled OE comparison, not the general idea of separating residual routes.

The new numerical evidence is internally consistent. Reconstructing the archived trainer sources reproduces all 98 supplied hashes; the earlier 77 result records are unchanged, and the 21 added records report 953 updates and 499,646,464 tokens. Recomputing fifteen added held-out contrasts reproduces the reported means, sample standard deviations, and paired $95\%$ $t$-intervals. This verifies the retained evidence and source consistency, not independent reproduction of GPU training or memory measurements.

At depth 6, shared-denominator routing improves over the joint baseline by $-0.002905$ held-out BPB at $\epsilon=10^{-6}$ and $-0.002746$ at $\epsilon=2^{-23}$, with descriptive intervals $[-0.004987,-0.000822]$ and $[-0.004826,-0.000667]$. The latter control usefully retains the baseline fused-anchor normalization. The shared-denominator algebra is correct in real arithmetic, with the stated rounding qualification, and the PyTorch 2.9.1 source supports the accumulation-type epsilon described in [21]. Neither comparison establishes superiority over separate-denominator routing: those direct intervals span zero. At depth 8, the incremental shared-routing contrast against the matched BF16 joint baseline is likewise unresolved, $-0.000503$ with interval $[-0.003277,+0.002271]$. The stronger composite comparison against native OE should continue to be distinguished from a routing-specific gain.

The counter-preserving intervention addresses the previous state-clearing confound. Inspection confirms that it retains displacement and `state['step']` while zeroing only the two moment buffers. Its improvement over skipping the first update is $-0.002076$ held-out BPB, with interval $[-0.003443,-0.000709]$. The separate moment contrast is much smaller, $-0.000368$ with interval $[-0.000728,-0.000007]$, and the counter-only contrast remains unresolved. These are conditional intervention effects, not evidence that any component is universally necessary. The corrected epsilon-dependent derivative and momentum-schedule description are also sound.

Several qualifications should be made explicit:

- The early-step audit is a separate diagnostic rerun, not an established replay of the archived training trajectories. `audit_step0_trajectories.py` initializes a model once, then repeatedly initializes embeddings already cast to BF16; the full trainers initialize fresh FP32 embeddings before casting. Identical seed labels do not establish identical initial tensors under these different paths. The audit also records the last microbatch's loss, rather than the accumulated batch mean. Its internally matched precision comparison is informative, but exact correspondence to the full runs requires initial-state checks or logged training gradients. Describe the present evidence accordingly.
- Replace the new conclusion that terminal coefficients render the depth-8 output skip “unnecessary” with “no demonstrated incremental benefit in the tested separate-denominator comparison.” Contrast 14 spans zero and does not test adding an output skip to the shared-denominator arm. Terminal coefficient signs do not establish functional redundancy.
- Keep the exploratory qualification prominent, including in the abstract. Three seeds, multiple comparisons, and repeated use of the same evaluation segment limit inference; the revised limitation acknowledging prior development exposure is appropriate. Shorten the densely numerical abstract and put its new mathematical expressions inside LaTeX delimiters.

These qualifications do not overturn the supported routing comparisons or the improved optimizer-state decomposition. They delimit an informative extension of the accepted study.
