# Cross-Family Composition and Checkpoint Diagnostics of Moment-Free Hashed N-Gram Input Tables Under Pre-Polar Equilibrated Muon Pretraining

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 5 · 2026-10-18T14:34:05.153689Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/16/v5) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study bounded cross-family composition between (1) moment-free hashed N-gram input lookup tables (Over-Encoding, OE; 603,432 rows across four tables) updated by per-occurrence count division and global table-RMS normalization, and (2) our 0-trainable-parameter backward optimizer (Homotopy BiEq-Muon, warmdown momentum cooldown, right-projected Neumann-Gram vocabulary whitening; +96/+160 MiB FP32 state, +0.042% step FLOPs) plus post-QK-Norm RoPE coordinate scaling and decoupled 1-gram/N-gram residual routing. Across 98 complete 499.65M-token (953-step) ClimbMix runs (34 arms) at DEPTH=6 (26.35M backbone + 57.93M OE parameters) and DEPTH=8 (50.33M backbone + 77.24M OE parameters) on official validation and pre-frozen held-out splits, we execute a matched-schedule 3-seed 2x3 factorial and targeted 3-seed interventions. First, conditional on OE, the optimizer lowers held-out BPB on all three DEPTH=8 seeds from 0.978682 +- 0.000827 to 0.976798 +- 0.001214 (-0.001884 +- 0.000510, 95% t-CI [-0.003151, -0.000617]), and the joint package reaches 1.022647 +- 0.001344 held-out BPB at DEPTH=6 (-0.001803 +- 0.000670, [-0.003467, -0.000138]; validation CI spans zero) and 0.976057 +- 0.000343 at DEPTH=8 (-0.002625 +- 0.000643, [-0.004222, -0.001029]; interaction intervals span zero). Second, 9 causal masking runs freezing Layer-0 and high-frequency RoPE (c in [0,15]) query scaling at 1.0 increase BPB on all three dense DEPTH=6 seeds (+0.000950 +- 0.000648 held-out, CI spanning zero) with little mean cost under OE at DEPTH=6 (+0.000036 +- 0.000222) and DEPTH=8 (+0.000024 +- 0.000351). Third, across 36 decoupled routing runs (wte_lambdas, out_lambdas), the shared-denominator anchor x_{0,w} = w / s_{w+e}(eps) lowers DEPTH=6 held-out BPB under both eps=1e-6 (1.019742 +- 0.000825; -0.002905 vs. joint, CI [-0.004987, -0.000822]) and PyTorch's default CUDA eps_acc=2^-23 (1.019900 +- 0.001214; -0.002746 vs. joint, CI [-0.004826, -0.000667]), and reaches 0.975777 +- 0.001085 at DEPTH=8 (-0.002905 composite vs. native OE, CI [-0.004127, -0.001684]), while a float64/bfloat16 step-0 audit at alpha_0=1.6 and 15-run decomposition evaluate step-0 displacement, Adam moments, and step-counter progression.
