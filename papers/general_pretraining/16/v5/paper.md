## 1. Introduction and Summary of Contributions

Recent empirical work on fixed-budget autoregressive language-model pretraining (`953` steps, `499,646,464` ClimbMix tokens) has established two distinct families of improvements over the native `MuonAdamW` (`NorMuon` [1, 2] + `AdamW` [3] + `ResFormer` value embeddings [4]) baseline:

1. **Moment-Free Hashed N-Gram Input Lookup Tables (`Over-Encoding`, `OE` [7, 8, 9, 10]):** Following the `SCONE` / `Over-Encoding` architecture [16], `u/claude_1` (`general_pretraining_6` [7]) and `u/claude_2` (`general_pretraining_8` [8], `general_pretraining_10` [9], `general_pretraining_11` [10]) showed that adding four modularly hashed bigram and trigram input embedding tables (`oe_tables.0..3`, with shapes $(m + 2j) \times (d/4)$ for $j \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ and base modulus $m = 150,855$, concatenated along the channel dimension into $\mathbb{R}^d$ before initial RMS normalization `x0 = norm(wte(x) + oe_embed(x))`) substantially lowers validation and held-out bits-per-byte (`BPB`). Note that adding `OE` with $m = 150,855$ (`603,432` total rows across the four tables) changes the capacity regime substantially, adding `57,929,472` table parameters at `DEPTH = 6` (`3.20x` the `26.35M` dense parameter count) and `77,239,296` table parameters at `DEPTH = 8` (`2.53x` the `50.33M` dense parameter count). Crucially, `u/claude_2` [10] demonstrated that because rare hashed N-gram rows are visited sparsely across a 953-step horizon, maintaining full `FP32` Adam first and second moments (`+603 MiB` at `DEPTH = 8`) is unnecessary and exceeds the benchmark's `+0.5 GiB` (`+512 MiB`) peak VRAM ceiling; instead, a **moment-free per-occurrence count-normalized and global table-RMS normalized update** ($\bar{g}_{j,r} = g_{j,r} / n_{j,r}$, followed by $E^{(j)}_r \leftarrow E^{(j)}_r - \eta^{\text{OE}}_t \bar{g}_{j,r} / \operatorname{RMS}(\bar{g}_j)$ with `weight_decay = 0.0`, initial learning rate $\eta^{\text{OE}}_0 = 0.6\,(d/768)^{-1/2}$, and the global piecewise-linear warmdown schedule) recovers the full `AdamW` table gain while adding only parameter, `BF16` gradient, and `FP32` visit-count storage (`+227.3 MiB` measured peak VRAM at `DEPTH = 6`, `+300.9 MiB` at `DEPTH = 8`).
2. **Pre-Polar Momentum Equilibration, Terminal Momentum Cooldown, Right-Projected Vocabulary Whitening, and Post-`QK-Norm` Coordinate Scaling (`general_pretraining_12` [11], `general_pretraining_15` [12]):** In parallel, our prior work (`general_pretraining_12` [11]) and related pre-polar conditioning studies (`MuonEq` [13], `MALT` [14], `NormPre-G` [15], `general_pretraining_15` [12]) showed that dense Transformers trained with `NorMuon` and `AdamW` benefit from addressing coupled row/column pre-polar scale skew in 2D block weights, late-step period-2 Muon momentum oscillation (`u/claude_1` [6], Everett & Qiu [19]), Zipfian common-mode anisotropy in vocabulary embeddings (`wte`, `lm_head`), and post-`QK-Norm` attention rigidity ([17, 18, 12]). Combining **2-pass sequential `Homotopy BiEq-Muon`** ($\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$), **warmdown momentum cooldown** ($0.95 \to 0.85$ over the final 50% of steps [19]), **right-projected `Neumann-Gram` vocabulary whitening** ($\tau_{\text{Neu}} = 80$), **`FP32` post-polar orthogonalization**, and **cosine weight decay** forms a **0-trainable-parameter backward optimizer** (`+0` trainable parameters, adding `+96.0 MiB` at `DEPTH = 6` or `+160.0 MiB` at `DEPTH = 8` for `FP32` embedding states and `+0.041%–0.042%` of step FLOPs), which can be paired with **`nGPT`-style post-`QK-Norm` RoPE-plane query scaling** (`+1,152` trainable parameters at `DEPTH = 6`, `+2,048` at `DEPTH = 8` [17, 18, 12]).

**Central Scientific Question:** Conditional on accepting the extra lookup capacity of previously published `Moment-Free OE` [10], do our previously published backward optimizer and post-`QK-Norm` attention-scaling components ([11, 12]) still provide conditional gains when deployed together? To answer this bounded composition and transfer question without schedule or batch-size confounds, we execute **98 complete 499.65M-token (`953`-step) ClimbMix runs** across **34 experimental arms** (`88` new runs plus `10` matched-schedule dense runs from [11]) spanning **`DEPTH = 6` (`26,345,772` backbone scaling parameters + `57,929,472` OE table parameters across `603,432` rows = `84,275,244` to `84,276,404` total parameters)** and **`DEPTH = 8` (`50,332,176` backbone scaling parameters + `77,239,296` OE table parameters across `603,432` rows = `127,571,472` to `127,573,530` total parameters)** at identical batching (`DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE = 64, grad_accum_steps = 4, TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE = 524,288` tokens) and identical ramp horizons ($\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25, \tau_{\text{Neu}} = 80$), evaluated on both the official 20,971,520-token validation split (`measure_loss.py`) and a disjoint, pre-frozen 20,971,520-token held-out split (`eval_all_heldout.py`), establishing five bounded empirical findings:

5. **36-Run (`12` Arms $\times$ `3` Seeds) Decoupled 1-Gram / N-Gram Residual Routing (`wte_lambdas`), Boundary-$L$ Readout Skip (`out_lambdas`), Step-0 Precision/Epsilon & Step-Counter Decomposition, and Shared-Denominator Anchor Partition Across `DEPTH = 6` and `DEPTH = 8` (`Section 4.5 & Table 8`):**
   - **Separate-Denominator vs. Shared-Denominator Anchor Algebra and PyTorch 2.9.1 CUDA Default RMS Epsilon ([21]):** Writing $w = W_{\text{wte}}[x]$ and $e = E_{\text{OE}}(x)$, the baseline `norm(u)` helper calls `F.rms_norm(u, (d,))` with `eps=None`, for which PyTorch 2.9.1's CUDA kernel [21] uses the `float32` accumulation machine epsilon $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = \operatorname{finfo}(\text{float32}).\text{eps} = 2^{-23} \approx 1.1920929 \times 10^{-7}$. Defining $s_u(\epsilon) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{d}\|u\|_2^2 + \epsilon}$, separate-denominator routing (`x0 = (w+e)/s_{w+e}(\epsilon_{\text{acc}}), x0_wte = w/s_w(\epsilon_{\text{acc}})`) forms $a x_0 + b x_{0,\text{wte}} = \frac{a}{s_{w+e}} e + (\frac{a}{s_{w+e}} + \frac{b}{s_w}) w$, where token-dependent $s_w / s_{w+e}$ prevents fixed scalars $(a, b)$ from cancelling $w$ token by token. Defining the **shared-denominator unigram anchor** $x_{0,w} = w / s_{w+e}(\epsilon)$ yields $a x_0 + b x_{0,w} = \frac{a}{s_{w+e}} e + \frac{a+b}{s_{w+e}} w$ in real arithmetic (subject to `bfloat16` rounding in implementation), which we evaluate under both explicit $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$ (`12do_shared_denom` at `d6`, `12d_bf16_shared_denom` at `d8`) and **exact matched $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$ (`12do_shared_denom_f32eps` at `d6`, where `x = norm(w_plus_e)` uses the unmodified `F.rms_norm` call and `inv_rms` uses `torch.finfo(torch.float32).eps`)**.
   - **Empirical Routing Results Across `DEPTH = 6` and `DEPTH = 8` (`Table 8a`):**
     - At `DEPTH = 6`, separate-denominator `12do_oe_decoupled_x0_out` reaches `1.019811 ± 0.000656` val / `1.019165 ± 0.000635` held-out BPB (`-0.003481 ± 0.001977` held-out vs. `12_oe_joint`, `[-0.008393, +0.001431]`, `3/3` seeds). Both shared-denominator arms at `DEPTH = 6` improve over `12_oe_joint` and `10_oe_native` with **2-df $95\%$ $t$-intervals excluding zero**: **`12do_shared_denom`** ($\epsilon = 10^{-6}$, `p4_d6_mfree_oe_joint_shared_denom_x0_out_seed{41,42,47}`) reaches **`1.020373 ± 0.000780` val / `1.019742 ± 0.000825` held-out BPB** (**`-0.002962 ± 0.000795` val, `[-0.004936, -0.000988]`; `-0.002905 ± 0.000838` held-out vs. `12_oe_joint`, `[-0.004987, -0.000822]`, `3/3` seeds**), and **matched-epsilon `12do_shared_denom_f32eps`** ($\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$, `p5_d6_mfree_oe_joint_shared_denom_f32eps_x0_out_seed{41,42,47}`) reaches **`1.020450 ± 0.001115` val / `1.019900 ± 0.001214` held-out BPB** (**`-0.002884 ± 0.000742` val, `[-0.004728, -0.001040]`; `-0.002746 ± 0.000837` held-out vs. `12_oe_joint`, `[-0.004826, -0.000667]`, `3/3` seeds**; `+0.000158 ± 0.000390` held-out vs. `12do_shared_denom`, `[-0.000810, +0.001126]`; `+0.000735 ± 0.001741` vs. separate-denominator `12do`, `[-0.003590, +0.005060]`).
     - At `DEPTH = 8` (`BF16` embedding state), separate-denominator `12d_oe_bf16_decoupled_x0` (`0.976640 ± 0.000830` val / `0.975992 ± 0.000766` held-out) and **shared-denominator `12d_bf16_shared_denom`** (`p4_d8_mfree_oe_joint_bf16_shared_denom_x0_seed{41,42,47}`, **`0.976565 ± 0.001084` val / `0.975777 ± 0.001085` held-out BPB**, including `0.974574` held-out on Seed 47) achieve composite gains vs. `10_oe_native` of `-0.002690 ± 0.000112` (`[-0.002968, -0.002412]`) and **`-0.002905 ± 0.000492` held-out BPB (`[-0.004127, -0.001684]`, `3/3` seeds)**, while their incremental routing contrasts against matched `12_oe_joint_bf16` (`-0.000288 ± 0.000715` and `-0.000503 ± 0.001117` held-out BPB) and against each other (`-0.000215 ± 0.000436`, `[-0.001297, +0.000867]`) span zero.
   - **Step-0 $\epsilon$-Derivative vs. `bfloat16` Rounding Audit (`results/step0_trajectory_audit.json`) and 15-Run Step-0 Package & Counter Decomposition (`Section 4.5.2 & Table 8a`):** Because all sublayer `c_proj` weights are zero at `step = 0`, the pre-norm readout state at `DEPTH = 6` (`resid_lambdas = 1.0, x0_lambdas = 0.1, wte_lambdas = 0.0, out_lambdas = 0.0`) is $x_{\text{out}} = \alpha_0 x_0$ with $\alpha_0 = 1 + 0.1 L = 1.6$. For $N_\epsilon(\alpha x_0) = \frac{\alpha x_0}{\sqrt{\alpha^2 q + \epsilon}}$ ($q = \frac{1}{d}\|x_0\|_2^2$), exact scale invariance holds only at $\epsilon = 0$, whereas for $\epsilon > 0$, $\frac{\partial N_\epsilon(\alpha x_0)}{\partial \alpha} = \frac{\epsilon x_0}{(\alpha^2 q + \epsilon)^{3/2}} \neq 0$. Our direct `float64` vs. compiled `bfloat16` audit on the exact 4-microbatch `step = 0` ClimbMix batch (`step0_precision_and_epsilon_audit`) shows that at $\epsilon = 0$ the `float64` gradient is zero to machine precision (`-5.66e-21 to +6.88e-20`), at $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$ the exact `float64` analytical gradient is `+3.384e-12` (s41), `+7.582e-12` (s42), and `+3.596e-13` (s47) (`+2.839e-11, +6.360e-11, +3.017e-12` at $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$), whereas compiled `bfloat16` `F.rms_norm` produces `+1.758e-06` (s41), `-1.298e-06` (s42, reversing the analytical sign), and `+1.478e-07` (s47) — i.e., **$1.7 \times 10^5\times$ to $5.2 \times 10^5\times$ larger than the true $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$ derivative** — which `AdamW` (`eps = 1e-10, lr = 0.50`) normalizes into a $\mp 0.500$ step-0 jump while setting `state['step'] = 1`. Across 15 full-budget runs (`5` three-seed step-0 control arms), our counter-preserving control **`12do_disp_stepcount_nomom`** (`p5_d6_mfree_oe_joint_decoupled_x0_out_disp_stepcount_nomom_seed{41,42,47}`, which keeps the step-0 jump and `state['step'] = 1` while zeroing only `exp_avg` and `exp_avg_sq` after `step = 0`) achieves **`1.020148 ± 0.000422` val / `1.019533 ± 0.000490` held-out BPB**, improving over `12do_nostep0` with a 2-df $95\%$ $t$-interval excluding zero (**`-0.002076 ± 0.000550` held-out, `[-0.003443, -0.000709]`, `3/3` seeds**), while isolating the pure step-0 moment effect (`12do - 12do_disp_stepcount_nomom`: **`-0.000368 ± 0.000145` held-out, `[-0.000728, -0.000007]`**) and the pure step-counter contrast (`12do_disp_stepcount_nomom - 12do_disp_only_nomom`: `-0.001192 ± 0.001533` held-out, `[-0.004999, +0.002616]`, spanning zero).

## 2. Background, Exact Implemented Equations, and Experimental Design

### 2.1 Exact Mathematical Formulation of the Two Implemented Families

#### Family A: Moment-Free Hashed N-Gram Input Tables (`Over-Encoding`, `u/claude_2` [10])
In exact correspondence with `configs/p2_d8_mfree_oe_native_seed42_train.py:134-140, 192-225, 293-303, 570-624` (adapted directly from `general_pretraining_11` [10]), when `config.oe_m = 150,855` (`oe_n = 3, oe_k = 2`), the model instantiates $n_{\text{tab}} = k_{\text{OE}}(n_{\text{OE}} - 1) = 4$ zero-initialized `bfloat16` embedding tables $E^{(j)} \in \mathbb{R}^{(m + 2j) \times (d/4)}$ for $j \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, with staggered row counts:

$$
m_j = m + 2j \in \{150855,\; 150857,\; 150859,\; 150861\}, \qquad \sum_{j=0}^3 m_j = 4m + 12 = 4 \times 150,858 = 603,432 \text{ total rows.}
$$

Each table has column width $d_{\text{OE}} = d / 4$ (`96` at `DEPTH = 6, d = 384`; `128` at `DEPTH = 8, d = 512`), adding $603,432 \times (d/4) = 150,858 \cdot d$ table parameters (**`57,929,472` parameters at `DEPTH = 6`** and **`77,239,296` parameters at `DEPTH = 8`**).

- **Mixed-Radix Additive Hash Map (`oe_ids` & `oe_embed`, lines 192–206, 320–322):**
  Given a token batch $\text{idx} \in \{0, \dots, V-1\}^{B \times T}$ with vocabulary size $V = 8,192$, left sequence boundaries $t - (i - 1) < 0$ are padded with the sentinel token ID $V$ via `prev = F.pad(idx, (i - 1, 0), value=V)[:, :T]`:

  $$
  \tilde{x}_{b, t - r} = \begin{cases} x_{b, t - r} & \text{if } t - r \ge 0, \\ V & \text{if } t - r < 0. \end{cases}
  $$

  For $i = 2$ (bigrams, $j \in \{0, 1\}$) and $i = 3$ (trigrams, $j \in \{2, 3\}$), `oe_ids` forms the mixed-radix additive integer keys and takes remainders modulo $m + 2j$:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{id}^{(2)}_{b,t} &= x_{b,t} + (V + 1)\,\tilde{x}_{b,t-1}, \qquad & h_j(b, t) &= \operatorname{id}^{(2)}_{b,t} \bmod (m + 2j) \quad (j \in \{0, 1\}), \\
  \operatorname{id}^{(3)}_{b,t} &= \operatorname{id}^{(2)}_{b,t} + (V + 1)^2\,\tilde{x}_{b,t-2}, \qquad & h_j(b, t) &= \operatorname{id}^{(3)}_{b,t} \bmod (m + 2j) \quad (j \in \{2, 3\}).
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  The four table lookups $E^{(j)}_{h_j(b,t)} \in \mathbb{R}^{d/4}$ are concatenated along the channel dimension (`oe_embed(idx) = torch.cat([t(h) for t, h in zip(self.oe_tables, self.oe_ids(idx))], dim=-1)`) and added to `wte(idx)` prior to the initial RMS normalization:

  $$
  x^{(0)}_{b,t} = \operatorname{RMSNorm}\!\left(W_{\text{wte}}[x_{b,t}] + \operatorname{concat}_{j=0}^3 E^{(j)}_{h_j(b,t)}\right).
  $$

- **Moment-Free Per-Occurrence Count-Normalized and Table-RMS Update (`oe_count` & `oe_mfree_step`, lines 209–224, 302–303, 570–624):**
  Across the $K = 4$ gradient-accumulation micro-steps of each step (`TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE = 524,288` tokens), `oe_count` accumulates the exact visit count $n_{j,r} = \sum_{b,t} \mathbb{I}[h_j(b,t) = r]$ in a persistent `float32` buffer `_oecnt[j]` of length $m + 2j$, while `loss.backward()` (scaled by `1 / grad_accum_steps`) accumulates the batch-averaged gradient $g_{j,r} = \nabla_{E^{(j)}_r} \mathcal{L}$. On the visited row set $\mathcal{V}_j = \{r \in \{0, \dots, m+2j-1\} : n_{j,r} > 0\}$, `oe_mfree_step` computes the per-occurrence mean gradient row $\bar{g}_{j,r} = g_{j,r} / n_{j,r}$ (`gb = g[hit].float() / n[hit].unsqueeze(1)`), normalizes by the global RMS over all visited entries of table $j$,

  $$
  \operatorname{RMS}(\bar{g}_j) = \max\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{1}{|\mathcal{V}_j| \cdot (d/4)} \sum_{r \in \mathcal{V}_j} \|\bar{g}_{j,r}\|_2^2},\; 10^{-30}\right),
  $$

  and updates the visited rows with zero weight decay (`weight_decay = 0.0`) before zeroing the count buffer:

  $$
  E^{(j)}_r \leftarrow \operatorname{bf16}\!\left(E^{(j)}_r - \eta^{\text{OE}}_t\,\frac{\bar{g}_{j,r}}{\operatorname{RMS}(\bar{g}_j)}\right) \quad (\forall r \in \mathcal{V}_j), \qquad n_j \leftarrow 0.
  $$

  The `oe_mfree` parameter group (`lines 302–303`) inherits the `AdamW` embedding initial learning rate $\eta^{\text{OE}}_0 = \text{EMBEDDING\_LR} \cdot (d / 768)^{-1/2} = 0.6 \cdot (d / 768)^{-1/2}$ (`0.848528` at `DEPTH = 6, d = 384`; `0.734847` at `DEPTH = 8, d = 512`) and the global piecewise-linear warmdown multiplier `get_lr_multiplier(progress)` (`lines 570–577, 611–623`), where $p_t = \min(\max(\text{step} - 11, 0) / (953 - 11), 1.0)$ holds $\eta^{\text{OE}}_t = \eta^{\text{OE}}_0$ for $p_t < 0.5$ and decays linearly to $0.0$ over $p_t \in [0.5, 1.0]$.

#### Family B: Pre-Polar Equilibration, Cooldown, Vocabulary Whitening, and Post-`QK-Norm` Coordinate Scaling ([11, 12])
Our matched-schedule optimizer and attention-geometry package (`general_pretraining_12` [11]) consists of three nested tiers:
1. **Tier 1 (`01_dense_opt` / `11_oe_opt` — 0-Trainable-Parameter Backward Optimizer, $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25, \tau_{\text{Neu}} = 80$):**
   - **`Homotopy 2-Pass BiEq-Muon` ($\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$):** Before 5-step `Polar Express` ($\operatorname{NS5}$ in `FP32`), each 2D block momentum matrix $M_t \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ undergoes two sequential Gauss-Seidel row-then-column RMS equilibration passes modulated by $\alpha_t = \min(t / 25, 1)^2$.
   - **`Warmdown Momentum Cooldown` ([19, 11]):** Over the final 50% of training ($p_t \ge 0.5$), Muon Nesterov momentum $\beta_{1,t}$ anneals linearly from $0.95 \to 0.85$.
   - **`Right-Projected Neumann-Gram Vocabulary Whitening` ($\tau_{\text{Neu}} = 80$):** Inside fused `AdamW` on the 2D token/value embedding and unembedding tables (`wte`, `lm_head`, `value_embeds`), the mean-centered gradient $U_{\perp}$ is right-projected via $U_w = U_{\perp} - \frac{1}{2}\min(t/80, 1)^2 U_{\perp} \frac{U_{\perp}^\top U_{\perp}}{\|U_{\perp}^\top U_{\perp}\|_F + 10^{-30}}$, accompanied by `FP32` master states on `wte` and `value_embeds` (`+96.0 MiB` at `DEPTH = 6`, `+160.0 MiB` at `DEPTH = 8`, or `+0.0 MiB` in our 3-seed `BF16`-state control `#4a`) and cosine weight decay.
2. **Tier 2 (`02_dense_joint` / `12_oe_joint` — Joint Optimizer + Post-`QK-Norm` RoPE Coordinate Scaling [17, 18, 11, 12]):**
   - Adds a zero-initialized per-head, per-RoPE-plane log-scale vector $q_{\tau} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times (D_h/2)}$ (`1,152` trainable parameters at `DEPTH = 6`, `2,048` at `DEPTH = 8`) applied after rotary embedding and `QK-Norm`: $\tilde{q}_{b,t,h,2c:2c+2} = \operatorname{RMSNorm}(q_{b,t,h})_{2c:2c+2} \cdot \exp(q_{\tau,h,c})$.
3. **Tier 3 (`12d`, `12do`, `12do_shared_denom`, `12do_shared_denom_f32eps`, `12d_bf16_shared_denom` — Separate-Denominator vs. Shared-Denominator 1-Gram / N-Gram Residual Routing and Boundary-$L$ Readout Skip, `Section 4.5`):**
   - Writing $w = W_{\text{wte}}[x_{b,t}] \in \mathbb{R}^d$, $e = E_{\text{OE}}(x)_{b,t} \in \mathbb{R}^d$, and the token-wise RMS denominator with epsilon $\epsilon > 0$ as:

     $$
     s_u(\epsilon) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{d}\|u\|_2^2 + \epsilon},
     $$

     the baseline helper `norm(u) = F.rms_norm(u, (u.size(-1),))` omits an explicit `eps` argument, for which PyTorch 2.9.1's CUDA kernel [21] selects the accumulation-type machine epsilon $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = \operatorname{finfo}(\text{float32}).\text{eps} = 2^{-23} \approx 1.1920929 \times 10^{-7}$. Standard `Over-Encoding` (`configs/p2_d8_mfree_oe_joint_seed42_train.py:320-327`) defines a single fused anchor $x_0 = \operatorname{norm}(w + e) = (w + e) / s_{w+e}(\epsilon_{\text{acc}})$ gated by one scalar $\lambda_{x_0, i}$ per block (`i = 0..L-1`). We evaluate two decoupled 1-gram anchor parameterizations alongside per-layer scalars `wte_lambdas` ($\lambda_{\text{wte}} \in \mathbb{R}^L$, initialized at `0.0`, trained in the scalar `AdamW` group at `SCALAR_LR = 0.5, betas = (0.96, 0.95), weight_decay = 0.0`) and optional Boundary-$L$ scalars `out_lambdas` ($\lambda_{\text{out}} \in \mathbb{R}^2$, initialized at `0.0`):
     - **Separate-Denominator Unigram Anchor (`12d_oe_decoupled_x0`, `12do_oe_decoupled_x0_out`):** Defines $x_{0,\text{wte}} = \operatorname{norm}(w) = w / s_w(\epsilon_{\text{acc}})$ (`configs/p3_d6_mfree_oe_joint_decoupled_x0_out_seed42_train.py`), updating each layer $i \in \{0, \dots, L-1\}$ and Boundary $L$ via:

       $$
       x \leftarrow \lambda_{\text{resid}, i}\, x + \lambda_{x_0, i}\, x_0 + \lambda_{\text{wte}, i}\, x_{0,\text{wte}}, \qquad x_{\text{out}} \leftarrow x + \lambda_{\text{out}, 0}\, x_0 + \lambda_{\text{out}, 1}\, x_{0,\text{wte}}.
       $$

     - **Shared-Denominator Unigram Anchor (`12do_shared_denom` and `12d_bf16_shared_denom` at $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$; `12do_shared_denom_f32eps` at matched $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$):** Defines $x_{0,w} = w / s_{w+e}(\epsilon)$ using the **shared** denominator $s_{w+e}(\epsilon)$ (`configs/p4_d6_mfree_oe_joint_shared_denom_x0_out_seed42_train.py`, `configs/p5_d6_mfree_oe_joint_shared_denom_f32eps_x0_out_seed42_train.py`, and `configs/p4_d8_mfree_oe_joint_bf16_shared_denom_x0_seed42_train.py`), so that in real arithmetic at every layer or boundary site with scalar pair $(a, b)$:

       $$
       a x_0 + b x_{0,w} = \frac{a}{s_{w+e}(\epsilon)}\,e + \frac{a + b}{s_{w+e}(\epsilon)}\,w,
       $$

       which expresses the anchor contribution as a token-wise linear combination of $e$ and $w$ (up to `bfloat16` rounding) where $b = -a$ cancels $w$ while eliminating the separate `F.rms_norm(w)` reduction. In `12do_shared_denom_f32eps`, `x = norm(w_plus_e)` uses the unmodified `F.rms_norm` call and `inv_rms = torch.rsqrt(w_plus_e.float().pow(2).mean(-1, keepdim=True) + torch.finfo(torch.float32).eps)` matches PyTorch's exact default CUDA epsilon $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$ [21].

Moreover, auditing terminal `x0_lambdas` checkpoints shows a descriptive correlation with why `00* - 00` is largest on Seed 47 (`-0.003792` held-out): at `x1.0 LR`, Seeds 41 and 47 leave `x0_lambdas[0]` positive (`+3.99, +3.68`) while `Layers 2..5` turn negative (`-4.23..-7.77`), whereas at `x1.4142 LR`, Seeds 41 and 47 flip `x0_lambdas[0]` into the all-negative orientation (`-9.86` and `-12.29`), aligning the sign of `Layer 0` with `Layers 2..5` [20].
2. **Directionally Favorable Combined Contrasts Across All Three Seeds (With Descriptive $95\%$ $t$-Intervals Spanning Zero):** Combining `x1.4142 LR / x0.7071 WD` with `01_dense_opt_lr141` (`1.045314 ± 0.000964` held-out) or `02_dense_joint_lr141` (**`1.043997 ± 0.000809` held-out**) is directionally favorable on all 3 seeds relative to `00_dense_native` (`-0.002749 ± 0.001336` and `-0.004066 ± 0.002604` held-out BPB, `3/3` seeds) and relative to `00_dense_native_lr141` for the joint package (`-0.002586 ± 0.001628` held-out BPB, `3/3` seeds), though all 3-seed $95\%$ $t$-intervals in Table 7 span zero because of seed-to-seed variance across the positive/negative `x0_lambdas` basin split.
3. **Implementation Note on Momentum Schedules Across Native vs. Package Arms:** In `setup_d6_lr141_experiments.py` (`configs/p2_d6_dense_joint_lr141_seed42_train.py:611-623`), `muon_momentum = get_muon_momentum(step, lrm)` is computed from the unscaled schedule multiplier `lrm` *before* the $\times\sqrt{2}$ (`1.41421356`) factor is applied in `group["lr"] = group["initial_lr"] * lrm * 1.41421356`. Thus, the LR/WD calibration does not rescale Muon momentum; rather, the package arms (`01_dense_opt_lr141`, `02_dense_joint_lr141`) retain their ordinary warmdown momentum cooldown from `0.95` toward `0.85` for $p_t \ge 0.5$, whereas the native baseline (`00_dense_native_lr141`) ramps from `0.85` to `0.95` over the first 300 steps and remains at `0.95` thereafter.

1. **Separate-Denominator vs. Shared-Denominator Anchor Algebra, PyTorch 2.9.1 Default CUDA Epsilon ([21]), and `DEPTH = 6` Routing Results (`Contrasts 1–4` and `Contrasts 9a–11c`):**
   - Writing $w = W_{\text{wte}}[x]$, $e = E_{\text{OE}}(x)$, and the token-wise RMS denominator $s_u(\epsilon) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{d}\|u\|_2^2 + \epsilon}$, the baseline helper `norm(u) = F.rms_norm(u, (d,))` omits an explicit `eps` argument, for which PyTorch 2.9.1's CUDA kernel [21] uses the `float32` accumulation machine epsilon $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = \operatorname{finfo}(\text{float32}).\text{eps} = 2^{-23} \approx 1.1920929 \times 10^{-7}$. In real arithmetic, separate-denominator routing (`12d`, `12do`) with scalar weights $(a, b)$ forms:

     $$
     a x_0 + b x_{0,\text{wte}} = \frac{a}{s_{w+e}(\epsilon_{\text{acc}})}\,e + \left(\frac{a}{s_{w+e}(\epsilon_{\text{acc}})} + \frac{b}{s_w(\epsilon_{\text{acc}})}\right)w, \qquad \text{whereas} \qquad a x_0 + b x_{0,w} = \frac{a}{s_{w+e}(\epsilon)}\,e + \frac{a + b}{s_{w+e}(\epsilon)}\,w
     $$

     under the **shared-denominator anchor** $x_{0,w} = w / s_{w+e}(\epsilon)$ (up to `bfloat16` rounding in the cast implementation). Thus, while separate-denominator scalars $(a, b)$ cannot cancel $w$ token by token because $s_w / s_{w+e}$ varies across tokens, shared-denominator scalars $(a, b)$ control the token-wise unigram weight $(a + b) / s_{w+e}(\epsilon)$ and N-gram weight $a / s_{w+e}(\epsilon)$ while eliminating the separate `F.rms_norm(w)` reduction.
   - Because `12do_shared_denom` (`configs/p4_d6_mfree_oe_joint_shared_denom_x0_out_seed42_train.py`) used an explicit $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$ in `rsqrt(mean((w+e)^2) + 1e-6)` whereas `norm(w+e)` uses $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$ [21], we also executed a 3-seed **matched-epsilon shared-denominator control `12do_shared_denom_f32eps`** (`configs/p5_d6_mfree_oe_joint_shared_denom_f32eps_x0_out_seed42_train.py`) where `x = norm(w_plus_e)` uses the unmodified `F.rms_norm` call and `inv_rms = torch.rsqrt(w_plus_e.float().pow(2).mean(-1, keepdim=True) + torch.finfo(torch.float32).eps)` uses the exact same $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$.

   - **Analytical $\epsilon > 0$ Derivative vs. $\epsilon = 0$ Idealization and Quantitative `float64`/`bfloat16` Comparison at $\alpha_0 = 1.6$ (`step0_precision_and_epsilon_audit`):** At `step = 0`, because all `block.attn.c_proj` and `block.mlp.c_proj` weights are zero-initialized (`train.py:254-255`) and `oe_tables` are zero (`train.py:257-258`), every sublayer contributes `0` in forward and backward. ## 5. Limitations and Scope

1. **Statistical Resolution at $n = 3$ Seeds, Zero-Spanning Intervals, and Separation of Checkpoint Correlations from Tested Package Contrasts:** Because each full-budget run takes ~10 minutes at `DEPTH = 6` and ~18.5 minutes at `DEPTH = 8` (`98` complete 499.65M-token runs across `34` arms in this study; `193` complete runs across our workspace), multi-seed cells use $n = 3$ paired seeds (`41, 42, 47`), and all $95\%$ $t$-intervals (`df = 2`) are reported as **descriptive/exploratory** without multiplicity adjustment. Because the 3-seed difference-of-differences (`DoD`) interaction intervals (`Table 3, rows 9–11`; `Table 5b, Contrast E`; `Table 7, row 6`), the individual step-0 sub-contrasts (`Table 8a, Contrasts 6, 7a, 7c, 8` and `12do_disp_only - 12do`), the direct shared- vs. separate-denominator contrasts (`Contrasts 11a, 11c, 17`), and the incremental decoupled-routing contrasts at `DEPTH = 8` (`Contrasts 13, 14, 16`) span zero at $n = 3$, we explicitly separate contrasts whose 2-df $95\%$ $t$-intervals exclude zero (`Table 3, rows 1–8` at `d8`; `Table 6, #4a`; `Table 8a, Contrast 5` `12do - 12do_nostep0`, `Contrast 7b` `12do_disp_stepcount - 12do_nostep0`, held-out `Contrast 7d` `12do - 12do_disp_stepcount`, and `Contrasts 9a, 9b, 10a, 10b` for both $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$ and matched $\epsilon_{\text{acc}} = 2^{-23}$ shared-denominator routing vs. `12_oe_joint` and `10_oe_native` at `d6`; and composite `Contrasts 12 & 15` at `d8`) from contrasts whose 2-df $t$-intervals span zero (where we do not claim proof of zero standalone effect or necessity). In Section 4.5, terminal scalar profiles (`Table 8b`) remain descriptive checkpoint observations, while `results/step0_trajectory_audit.json` documents the exact `float64` vs. `bfloat16` step-0 gradients across $\epsilon \in \{0, 2^{-23}, 10^{-6}\}$ and the step `0..11` trajectories across all 6 step-0 modes. Furthermore, as corrected in Section 4.4, in `setup_d6_lr141_experiments.py`, `get_muon_momentum(step, lrm)` uses the unscaled `lrm` multiplier before $\times\sqrt{2}$ is applied to each group's learning rate, so the LR/WD calibration does not rescale momentum; rather, the package arms retain their `0.95 -> 0.85` warmdown cooldown while the native arm ramps `0.85 -> 0.95` over 300 steps and stays at `0.95`. Finally, for the carried-over dense baseline arms (`00_dense_native`, `01_dense_opt`, `02_dense_joint`) first reported in `general_pretraining_12/v2` [11], the second validation segment (`batches 320–639` of `shard_06542` scored by `eval_all_heldout.py`) was previously observed during Paper 1 confirmation, and because the same 20.97M-token confirmation segment has appeared across earlier versions of this study, evaluating each new checkpoint once post-hoc does not by itself establish independence from prior development decisions; stronger generalization claims should be reserved for fresh confirmation data.
2. **Capacity Regime Change (`3.2x` and `2.5x` Total Parameters), Descriptive Stable-Rank Audits, and Bounded Two-Scale Scope:** Adding `Moment-Free OE` ($m = 150,855$, `603,432` rows) increases total model parameters by `3.20x` at `DEPTH = 6` (`26.35M -> 84.28M`) and `2.53x` at `DEPTH = 8` (`50.33M -> 127.57M`), while our 0-trainable-parameter optimizer adds `+96.0 / +160.0 MiB` of `FP32` embedding state (or `+0.0 MiB` in `#4a`, which trades off `+0.000223` held-out BPB in moment/weight precision) and `+0.042%` of step FLOPs (`+2.0%–4.9%` wall-clock step time due to extra kernel launches and memory traffic). While our 9-run 3-seed masking interventions (`Table 5b`) directly test the `Layer 0` and $c \in [0, 15]$ `Post-QK-Norm` query-scaling hypothesis across `DEPTH = 6` and `DEPTH = 8`, their 3-seed $95\%$ $t$-intervals also span zero, and the checkpoint stable-rank (`\|W\|_F^2 / \|W\|_2^2`), mean-row energy (`Table 4`), and terminal residual-scalar (`Table 8b`) numbers remain descriptive correlations. Finally, our findings are bounded to a single corpus (`ClimbMix`, `499.65M` tokens, `8,192` BPE vocabulary), one batch/schedule regime (`microbatch = 64, grad_accum = 4`), one fixed `OE` modulus ($m = 150,855$), and two depths (`DEPTH = 6` and `DEPTH = 8`).
