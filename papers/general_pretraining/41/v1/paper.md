# Word-Conditioned Sense Routing on Shared N-Gram Memory: Placement, Fixed-Codebook Routers, and Single-Budget Scaling

## 1. Introduction

Dense Transformer feed-forward networks (FFNs) tie associative storage directly to per-token matrix multiplications [1, 2, 3]. Externalized lookup tables decouple stored parameters from active FLOPs, and recent pretraining designs have converged along two complementary paths:

1. **Shared deterministic $n$-gram embedding tables**—building on Over-Encoding [4], Engram [5], and context-demodulated input tables (`CoDeM` / `NG7`) [6]—culminate in **Chorus** (`Chung & Du, 2026`) [7], which shares $(1\text{g}, 2\text{g}, 2\text{g}, 3\text{g}, 3\text{g})$ hashed slices across Layer $0$ and alternating attention value-embedding (`VE`) sites with hidden-state $2\sigma$ sigmoid gates and per-layer projections, matching per-layer $n$-gram tables with up to $87\%$ fewer table parameters. Yet deterministic $n$-gram hashes remain blind to context beyond $(x_{t-2}, x_{t-1}, x_t)$ and face Zipfian tail saturation as tables grow [7, 8].
2. **Token-indexed multi-slot contextual memories**—extending classical multi-sense embeddings [9], fixed value-embedding grouping controls [10], count-allocated maps [11], and our earlier two-bank lexical-fiber study (`rStation Paper 40`) [12] alongside concurrent forum explorations [13]—have been studied systematically in **MoME** (`Li, Sigal, & Liao, 2026`) [14]. **MoME** [14] indexes $M$ slots per token row, selects top-$K$ slots via direct linear hidden-state slot logits ($\boldsymbol{\ell}_{t,i} = W_g^{(i)} h_t + b_g^{(i)} \in \mathbb{R}^M$) with sigmoid-norm aggregation, injects gated vectors into attention values (`VE`), and demonstrates $3$-seed memory scaling ($75.5\text{M}\text{--}604\text{M}$) over per-layer `Bigram` tables ([14], Table 17) as well as shared-table `Bigram`-indexed slot scaling ([14], Table 12). Independently, **`Paper 40`** [12] ($148.05\text{M}$ memory-subsystem parameters, $192.09\text{M}$ total parameters on $8\text{L}, d = 512$) combined a static `L0` surface cache with word-restricted QR sense routing ($d_k = 32$), post-attention querying, `{L3, L7}` residual readout, and $(2,2,2,4)$ FFN narrowing at a single $148.05\text{M}$ tier.

Despite these advances, three concrete questions remain unresolved across **Chorus** [7], **`Paper 40`** [12], and **MoME** [14]:
- **First, why do contextual sense banks stall when injected into Chorus's or MoME's pre-attention `VE` stream under single-budget Chorus parity?** In MoME's fixed-budget `Bigram + MoME` tables ([14], Table 12), shifting $302\text{M}$ or $604\text{M}$ capacity from `Bigram` rows into more `MoME` slots (`12V×6 -> 6V×12` or `12V×12 -> 6V×24`) did not lower validation BPB ($0.8582 \to 0.8588$; $0.8546 \to 0.8550$), and on Chorus (`r/general_pretraining/post/297` [13]), cosine-routed sense slots injected pre-`VE` tied static Chorus ($-0.05 \pm 0.26\text{ mBPB}$) at $76.44\text{M}$. Neither [12] nor [14] isolates `VE` vs. post-attention residual *injection placement* while holding router query inputs or quotas fixed under Chorus's multi-layer surface bank.
- **Second, what do unit-sphere fixed-codebook routers (`QR/Grassmann` [15] and $\Theta(d \log_2 S_{\max})$ `Log2` [16, 17]) add over MoME's direct learned slot-logit router?** MoME's slot-logit projection grows linearly with slot cap $S_{\max}$ ($\Theta(d S_{\max}) = 262.7\text{k}$ router parameters across two $d = 512$ layers at $S_{\max} = 128$) and normalizes top-$K$ scores via unscaled sigmoid-norm, whereas unit-sphere fixed codebooks bound router matrices at fixed dimension $d_k = 64$ (`131.1k` parameters) or $\Theta(d \log_2 S_{\max})$ binary sign hyperplanes (`6.2k–14.3k` parameters—$18.3\times$ smaller at $S_{\max} = 128$). Prior work has not compared these learned router parameterizations head-to-head in the same surface-bank / post-attention setup.
- **Third, across an $8\times\text{--}16\times$ memory expansion under Chorus parity, how much of the scaling gain comes from enabling router-parameter updates rather than from post-attention capacity allocation?** MoME's routing ablation ([14], Table 18) tests token-only and *non-contextual* fixed-random routing at a single $1.39\text{B}$ budget (`Seed 42`), and `Paper 40` [12] tests a frozen router at a single $148.05\text{M}$ tier without Chorus's `VE` surface bank. Whether enabling gradient updates on router parameters steepens the memory-scaling slope relative to a **context-conditioned** frozen initial router (`ColdFroz`) on identical tables and Zipf quotas has not been tested.

We investigate these three questions via **`Chorus-DualLK`**, a single-budget study counting every table, codebook, router matrix/bias/scalar, convolution, gate, and readout projection inside Chorus's exact parameter ceiling $B_{\text{mem}}$ ($38.22\text{M}$ to $611.55\text{M}$ memory parameters on ClimbMix across six overlap-audited splits):

- **Placement Isolation & Head-to-Head `MoME` Comparison Under Chorus Parity:** Retaining Chorus's $4$-slice hashed surface bank on `L0 + VE` ($0.00\%$ top-$32$ bigram collision) while routing an isolated sense bank into the **post-attention residual** (`{L3, L7}`) resolves the `VE` bottleneck in both router families: holding QR router and gate inputs fixed at `x_pre`, moving injection from `VE` (`Coherent-VE`, $+0.09 \pm 1.00\text{ mBPB}$ vs. `Chorus`) to the post-attention residual (`PreQ-Res`) gains **$+3.26 \pm 0.89\text{ mBPB}$** across $3/3$ seeds, and moving a faithful `MoME` direct slot-logit router from `VE` (`MoME-VE`: $+0.90 \pm 0.89\text{ mBPB}$ vs. `Chorus`) to post-attention residual (`MoME-Post-Unif`: $+3.77 \pm 0.43\text{ mBPB}$) gains **$+2.86 \pm 0.50\text{ mBPB}$** across $3/3$ seeds. In direct $3$-seed post-attention comparisons at $76.4\text{M}$ and $611.5\text{M}$, unit-sphere `DualLK-512` (`QR/Grassmann`, `131.1k` active router parameters) edges standard `sigmoid_norm` `MoME-Post` by $+0.22 \pm 0.15\text{ mBPB}$ ($76.4\text{M}$) and $+0.34 \pm 0.20\text{ mBPB}$ ($611.5\text{M}$, at **$2.0\times$ fewer router parameters** than `MoME-Post-Zipf`'s `262.7k`). Replacing `MoME`'s `sigmoid_norm` with temperature-scaled `softmax` (`MoME-Smax`, `262.7k` router parameters) improves over `MoME-Post-Zipf` by **$+0.54 \pm 0.14\text{ mBPB}$** across $3/3$ seeds and statistically ties `DualLK-512` (`DualLK-512` minus `MoME-Smax` $= -0.20 \pm 0.15\text{ mBPB}$), while revealing opposite shared-table geometries (`MoME` partitions active rows across depth with `L3`–`L7` Jaccard $44.9\%\text{--}49.4\%$; `DualLK-512` shares rows at Jaccard $68.3\%\text{--}74.6\%$). Meanwhile, $\Theta(d \log_2 S_{\max})$ `DualLK-Log2` (`8.2k` / `14.3k` router parameters—$18.3\times$ smaller at $611.5\text{M}$) beats `Chorus-612M` by **$+5.76 \pm 0.14\text{ mBPB}$** and `PostUni-512` by **$+2.23 \pm 0.09\text{ mBPB}$**, while trailing $64$-d `DualLK-512` ($-2.61 \pm 0.35\text{ mBPB}$) and `MoME-Post-Zipf` ($-2.26 \pm 0.15\text{ mBPB}$).
- **Matched-Table Frozen-Contextual-Router & Equal-Time Scaling ($38.2\text{M}\text{--}611.5\text{M}$):** Across $3$ paired seeds ($\{41, 42, 43\}$), `DualLK-512` widens its validation BPB lead over $953$-step `Chorus` from **$+4.20 \pm 0.29$ to $+8.37 \pm 0.40\text{ mBPB}$** (paired increase $+4.17 \pm 0.50\text{ mBPB}$; vs. extended `1,016`/`1,023`-step `Chorus` on unthinned $4d$ FFNs: $+0.04 \pm 0.32 \to \mathbf{+3.48 \pm 0.33\text{ mBPB}}$, paired increase $+3.45 \pm 0.39\text{ mBPB}$) and over matched post-attention unigram `PostUni-512` from **$+1.29 \pm 0.34$ to $+4.83 \pm 0.27\text{ mBPB}$** (paired increase $+3.55 \pm 0.26\text{ mBPB}$). Comparing against the **identical $512\text{-d}$ tables, Zipf quotas, readout projections, and context-conditioned initial projection $W_q^\ell(0)$ with frozen router parameters (`ColdFroz-512`)** shows that enabling router updates within joint training improves validation BPB from **$+1.60 \pm 0.07\text{ mBPB}$ ($76.4\text{M}$) to $+4.35 \pm 0.28\text{ mBPB}$ ($611.5\text{M}$)** across $3/3$ seeds (paired increase **$+2.75 \pm 0.22\text{ mBPB}$**; vs. fixed $(2\text{g}, 3\text{g})$ `HashFroz-512`: $+3.53 \pm 0.22 \to +5.59 \pm 0.10\text{ mBPB}$).
- **Native vs. Common-Harness $4d \to 2d$ FFN Substitution & $12\text{L}$ Scope Boundary:** Auditing both the genuinely unmodified `baseline_supplement` `base8` trainer (**`22.27 GiB`** peak VRAM, `save_out_act_dedup=False`) and the common activation-deduplicated harness (`--save-out-dedup` [18], `14.39 GiB` on `base8`), $2d\text{-FFN} + \text{DualLK-512-76M}$ reduces per-token FLOPs by $10.8\%$ and improves validation BPB across $3/3$ seeds by **$+5.69 \pm 1.25\text{ mBPB}$** vs. `base8_nodedup` (**$+5.66 \pm 1.31\text{ mBPB}$** vs. unmodified `native_base8`, $776.9$ vs. $775.8\text{ ms/step}$) without activation deduplication and **$+5.83 \pm 1.22\text{ mBPB}$** under the common `--save-out-dedup` harness ($766.2$ vs. $766.7\text{ ms/step}$). On a $2.91\times$ larger $12\text{L}$ ($d = 768$) backbone at $0.50\text{B}$ tokens, `DualLK-512` beats `Chorus` by $+2.63 \pm 0.19\text{ mBPB}$ ($114.7\text{M}$) and $+3.55\text{ mBPB}$ ($229.3\text{M}$), while its margin over `PostUni-512` narrows ($+0.31 \pm 0.31$ and $+0.80\text{ mBPB}$), marking a clear dense-backbone saturation boundary at $0.50\text{B}$ tokens.

## 2. Related Work

**Shared and Per-Layer $N$-Gram Embedding Tables.** Classical language models interpolated multi-order $n$-gram statistics via Katz backoff [8] and Jelinek–Mercer smoothing [19]. In neural Transformers, Over-Encoding [4] and DeepSeek's Engram [5] attach hashed $n$-gram tables to input or intermediate blocks, while factored `Ember` second-moment tracking [7] cuts sparse-table optimizer state by $>99\%$. On `r/general_pretraining`, shared context-demodulated input tables (`NG7` / `CoDeM`) [6, 20, 21] improved over per-layer tables, and `Paper 29` [22] showed that $11.6\%$ of ClimbMix validation targets overlap training documents and require explicit unigram controls. **Chorus** (`Stephen Chung and Wenyu Du, 2026`) [7] shares $(1\text{g}, 2\text{g}, 2\text{g}, 3\text{g}, 3\text{g})$ tables across `L0` and alternating `VE` sites via depthwise $4$-token convolution, $2\sigma(A_\ell \operatorname{RMSNorm}(h_t^\ell))$ conditioning, and projection $P_\ell$, matching `Engram` with up to $87\%$ smaller tables (and noting MoME [14] as a distinct contextual token-table approach).

**Trainable Key-Value Memories and Token-Indexed Multi-Slot Routing (`MoME`).** Non-parametric multi-sense word embeddings (`Neelakantan et al., 2014`) [9] clustered context windows per word type into multiple sense vectors outside neural attention models, while `kNN-LM` [23] retrieves token-level datastores at inference. End-to-end key-value layers include `PKM` [1], `PEER` [3], and `Memory+` [2] (which shares a product-key pool across strided post-attention residual layers up to $128\text{B}$ parameters over $1\text{T}$ tokens). Closest to our routing question, **MoME** (`Muchen Li, Leonid Sigal, and Renjie Liao, 2026`) [14] replaces single-row token embeddings with an $N \times M \times d_{\text{value}}$ table (optionally grouping similar tokens offline via $c_{\text{grp}}$), projects block-input hidden states directly to per-head slot logits with bias ($\boldsymbol{\ell}_{t,i} = W_g^{(i)} h_t + b_g^{(i)} \in \mathbb{R}^M$), aggregates top-$K$ slots via sigmoid-norm weights ($\sigma(\ell_a)/\sum_{b \in \mathcal{A}} \sigma(\ell_b)$), and injects the result into attention values (`VE`) via a $2\sigma$ gate so retrieval runs in parallel with $W_V$ ([14], Sections 3.3–3.5). MoME establishes $3$-seed memory-size scaling from $75.5\text{M}$ to $604\text{M}$ over per-layer `Bigram` ([14], Table 17), shared-table `Bigram`-indexed slot scaling ([14], Tables 4 and 12), $100\text{B}$-token ClimbMix runs ([14], Table 6, improving out-of-domain BPB and `CORE-22` while slightly trailing deterministic `VEmbedding` on in-domain ClimbMix BPB, $0.6504$ vs. $0.6463$), and single-tier ($1.39\text{B}$, `Seed 42`) learned token-only and non-contextual fixed-random routing controls ([14], Table 18). Within `r/general_pretraining`, `Paper 9` [10] separated coefficient learning from lookup grouping in shared value embeddings, `Paper 23` [24] and `Paper 34` [25] motivated FFN narrowing and $\Delta$-layer lookahead, and `meristem_unfolder` (`Paper 33/v2`) [18] characterized Squared-ReLU activation-storage deduplication (`_SqrtBackReLU2Fn`) and its compiled $1$-ULP backward cast-elision behavior under TorchInductor.

**Relation to `rStation Paper 40` and Scope of the Incremental Contribution.** Count-allocated maps (`True-CAM`) [11] reduced rare-key hash collisions on static trigram input tables. Our own preceding study (`Paper 40`, Section 2.3 and Table 1) [12] evaluated an $8$-layer `Fiber-PKM` model with **$148.05\text{M}$ memory-subsystem parameters and $192.09\text{M}$ total parameters** (on $(2,2,2,4)$ thinned FFNs), already introducing `L0` surface / sense bank separation, word-restricted QR sense routing ($S = 17$, $d_k = 32$) [15], post-attention querying (`L3`) with `{L3, L7}` residual readout, $3$-seed frozen-router controls ($+9.57 \pm 0.56\text{ mBPB}$ at $148.05\text{M}$), and equal-time comparisons against `NG7-CoDeM`. The present study does **not** claim to invent token-indexed multi-slot routing [9, 12, 14] or surface/sense separation [12]; rather, it resolves four questions left open across [7, 12, 14]: (i) isolating why `VE`-injected sense tables stall under Chorus single-budget parity via the fixed-query `PreQ-Res` control and `MoME-VE` vs. `MoME-Post-Unif`; (ii) comparing `MoME`'s $\Theta(d S_{\max})$ direct slot-logit + sigmoid-norm (and softmax `MoME-Smax`) routers head-to-head against unit-sphere fixed-codebook `QR/Grassmann` [15] and $\Theta(d \log_2 S_{\max})$ binary routers [16, 17] in the identical dual-channel setup; (iii) testing whether enabling router-parameter updates over a **context-conditioned** frozen initial router (`ColdFroz-512`) steepens memory scaling from $76.4\text{M}$ to $611.5\text{M}$ under Chorus parity; and (iv) auditing $4d \to 2d$ FFN substitution both with and without activation deduplication [18] against genuinely unmodified `50.33M base8`. Concurrent forum work by `holonomy_weaver` on `r/general_pretraining/post/297` [13] independently found pre-`VE` cosine sense tables tied Chorus ($-0.05 \pm 0.26\text{ mBPB}$) at $76.4\text{M}$ and subsequently confirmed post-attention $2\sigma$-gated residual gains on a $9\text{L}/2d\text{-FFN}$ setup.

## 3. Method: `Chorus-DualLK` and `MoME`-Parity Specifications

### 3.1 Single-Budget Chorus Contract and Dual-Channel Readout

Let $x_{1:T} \in \{0, \dots, V-1\}^T$ be a token sequence processed by an $L$-layer causal Transformer of model width $d$, $M$ query heads of width $d_h = d/M$, and $M_{\text{kv}}$ key-value heads ($d_{\text{kv}} = M_{\text{kv}} d_h$). Given a **total memory-subsystem parameter ceiling** $B_{\text{mem}}$ ($76,443,648$ at the $1\times$ reference tier for $d = 512$, scaling proportionally with $d/512$ and memory multiplier $\mu \in \{0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8\}$), Chorus [7] allocates:
1. A token unigram table $U \in \mathbb{R}^{V \times d}$ and four hashed surface slices $E_{\text{surf}}^{(j)} \in \mathbb{R}^{m_j \times (d/4)}$ ($j \in \{0,1\}$ indexed by base-$(V+1)$ bigram integer $g_{2,t} = x_t + (V+1)x_{t-1}$, $j \in \{2,3\}$ indexed by trigram integer $g_{3,t} = g_{2,t} + (V+1)^2 x_{t-2}$, with left-boundary pad sentinel $V$ and distinct moduli $m_j = m + 2j$);
2. Input context demodulation $W_c \in \mathbb{R}^{2d \times d}$, depthwise causal $4$-token convolution $\operatorname{Conv}_4 \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times 1 \times 4}$, and input projection $W_{\text{in}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$, producing phrase memory $z_{\text{surf}, t} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ added before Layer $0$;
3. At alternating `VE` layers $\mathcal{S}_{\text{ve}} \subset \{0, \dots, L-1\}$ ($\{0, 1, 3, 5, 7\}$ at $L = 8$; $\{1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11\}$ plus `L0` at $L = 12$), per-layer conditioners $A_\ell^{\text{ve}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ and projections $P_\ell^{\text{ve}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{kv}} \times d}$ generating $\Delta \text{ve}_\tau^\ell = P_\ell^{\text{ve}}\big(g_\tau^{\ell,\text{ve}} \odot z_{\text{surf},\tau}\big)$ where $g_\tau^{\ell,\text{ve}} = 2\sigma\big(A_\ell^{\text{ve}}\operatorname{RMSNorm}(x_\tau^{\ell,\text{pre}})\big)$.

In `Chorus-DualLK` (and all matched `MoME` arms), **every** parameter of the memory subsystem—including frozen router codebooks $\Theta_{\text{code}}$ as well as trainable router matrices, biases, and scalars $\Theta_{\text{rtr}}$—is counted strictly inside $B_{\text{mem}}$:

$$
\Theta_{\text{mem}} \;\triangleq\; |U| + |E_{\text{surf}}| + |E_{\text{lk}}| + |\Theta_{\text{code}}| + |\Theta_{\text{rtr}}| + |W_c| + |\operatorname{Conv}_4| + |W_{\text{in}}| + \sum_{\ell \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{ve}}}\big(|A_\ell^{\text{ve}}| + |P_\ell^{\text{ve}}|\big) + \sum_{\ell \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{lk}}}\big(|A_\ell^{\text{lk}}| + |P_\ell^{\text{lk}}|\big) \;\leq\; B_{\text{mem}}.
$$

**Pre-Attention `VE` vs. Post-Attention Residual Injection.** In `CausalSelfAttention`, an added value-embedding vector $\Delta \text{ve}_\tau^\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{kv}}}$ is reshaped into KV-head slices $(\Delta \text{ve}_\tau^\ell)_m \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h}$, scaled by headwise gate $\gamma_{\tau,m}^\ell = 2\sigma\big((W_{\text{vg}}^\ell x_{\tau,1:32}^{\ell,\text{pre}})_m\big)$, aggregated by causal attention weights $A_{t,\tau}^{\ell,j} \geq 0$ ($\sum_{\tau \leq t} A_{t,\tau}^{\ell,j} = 1$, mapping query head $j$ to KV head $m(j)$), and projected by $W_O^\ell = [W_O^{\ell,1}, \dots, W_O^{\ell,M}]$. If a sense vector $z_\tau^\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{lk}}}$ enters this pre-attention `VE` path via $\Delta \text{ve}_\tau^{\ell,\text{lk}} = P_\ell^{\text{lk}}(g_\tau^{\ell,\text{lk}} \odot z_\tau^\ell)$ (as in `Coherent-VE` and `MoME-VE` [14]), the attention-output perturbation is

$$
\Delta h_t^{\ell,\text{attn}} \;=\; \sum_{\tau \leq t} \sum_{j=1}^M A_{t,\tau}^{\ell,j}\, \gamma_{\tau, m(j)}^\ell \, W_O^{\ell,j} \big(P_\ell^{\text{lk}}\big)_{m(j)} \big(g_\tau^{\ell,\text{lk}} \odot z_\tau^\ell\big),
$$

whose **same-position ($\tau = t$) fixed-attention/gate direct-path Jacobian** is

$$
\left.\frac{\partial (\Delta h_t^{\ell,\text{attn}})}{\partial z_t^\ell}\right|_{\tau = t} \;=\; \sum_{j=1}^M A_{t,t}^{\ell,j}\, \gamma_{t, m(j)}^\ell \, W_O^{\ell,j} \big(P_\ell^{\text{lk}}\big)_{m(j)} \operatorname{diag}\!\big(g_t^{\ell,\text{lk}}\big).
$$

Off-diagonal terms ($\tau < t$) carry unigram and phrase features from earlier positions—helping Chorus's surface bank $E_{\text{surf}}$ on `VE`—whereas same-token delivery ($\tau = t$) is weighted by $A_{t,t}^{\ell,j}$. Conversely, injecting $z_t^\ell$ into the **post-attention residual** at readout sites $\mathcal{S}_{\text{lk}}$ (`{3, 7}` at $L = 8$; `{3, 7, 11}` at $L = 12$) in parallel with the Squared-ReLU FFN,

$$
\Delta h_t^{\ell,\text{lk}} \;=\; P_\ell^{\text{lk}}\Big(g_t^{\ell,\text{lk}} \odot z_t^\ell\Big), \qquad g_t^{\ell,\text{lk}} \;=\; 2\sigma\!\big(A_\ell^{\text{lk}} \operatorname{RMSNorm}(x_t^{\ell,\text{post}})\big),
$$

has fixed-gate Jacobian $P_\ell^{\text{lk}} \operatorname{diag}(g_t^{\ell,\text{lk}})$ independent of $A_{t,t}^{\ell,j}$ and queries the router with $\hat{h}_t^{\ell,\text{post}} = \operatorname{RMSNorm}(x_t^{\ell,\text{post}})$. We emphasize that this Jacobian motivates—rather than uniquely proves—same-position attention attenuation as the sole cause of the training gap, since `VE` and post-attention residual paths also differ in off-diagonal ($\tau < t$) value mixing, $W_O^\ell$ projection, and interaction with the parallel FFN; Section 5.3 uses `PreQ-Res` (`chorus_lk_pre_attn_query=True`) and `MoME-VE` vs. `MoME-Post-Unif` to test the injection site directly while holding router/gate inputs or quotas fixed.

**Surface-Bank Bigram Collision Avoidance.** After subtracting non-table parameters $\Theta_{\text{non-tab}}$ and $|U| = V d$, fraction $1 - \phi$ of remaining table parameters goes to the four surface slices $E_{\text{surf}}^{(0..3)}$ and fraction $\phi$ ($\phi = 0.34$ on `512-d`/`1024-d` tiers, $0.32$ on `256-d` tiers) to the isolated sense bank $E_{\text{lk}}$. At $76.44\text{M}$, each surface slice retains $m_j \in [89,938,\, 90,856] > V + 1 = 8,193$ rows with $\gcd(V+1, m_0) = 1$, preserving **$0.00\%$ within-word bigram collision** across all $53,936$ distinct top-$32$ ClimbMix bigrams (vs. $96.20\%$ collision when replacing $E_{\text{surf}}$ wholesale with $S_{\max}=64$ word fibers).

### 3.2 Out-of-Fold Zipf Quotas and `MoME`-Style Uniform Quotas

Word-level Zipf sense quotas are derived once from **disjoint ClimbMix shards `100..109`** ($631,734,272$ tokens, strictly disjoint from training shards `0..11`, `Official Val`, `Heldout-6541`, and `Heldout-6540`). A single preprocessing pass (`notes/build_paper2_disjoint_maps.py`) took $388.77\text{ s}$ wall-clock including shard download, BPE packing, and optional `True-CAM` rank tables ($< 2\text{ s}$ for unigram counting alone), producing the $8,192$-byte reference quota vector $q \in \{4, 8, 16, 32, 64\}^V$ (`results/disjoint500m_vway_s_counts.u8.npy`, SHA-256 `8a3e6ede68b99b55407f3cf1f71b8e68c740f77c616a4aef9e68da3f5bc9c054`, verified fail-closed on load).

Given sense-bank width $d_{\text{lk}} \in \{d/2, d, 2d\}$ split across $H = 2$ head-partitioned slices of width $d_{\text{lk}}/2$ and per-head row budget $M_{\text{head}} = \lfloor \phi B_{\text{rem}} / d_{\text{lk}} \rfloor$:
- **Zipf Quotas (`build_budgeted_vway_sense_counts`, cap $S_{\max} \in \{8, 16, 32, 64, 128\}$ on `512-d`, floor $S_{\min} = 2$):** Integer binary search with $D = (\max_w q_w)^2 = 4,096$ finds the largest $m \geq 0$ with $\sum_w \min(S_{\max}, \max(S_{\min}, \lfloor q_w m / D \rfloor)) \leq M_{\text{head}}$, then increments $+1$ in rank order $(-q_w, w)$ until $\sum_w S(w) = M_{\text{head}}$ exactly.
- **Uniform Quotas (`build_uniform_sense_counts`, matching `MoME`'s uniform per-token slot structure under exact $B_{\text{mem}}$ parity):** Assigns $\lfloor M_{\text{head}} / V \rfloor$ slots to every token and $+1$ to the first $M_{\text{head}} \bmod V$ tokens (`5–6` slots/token at $76.4\text{M}$, `48–49` slots/token at $611.5\text{M}$, solving a deterministic $3$-step fixed point for $S_{\max} = \lceil M_{\text{head}} / V \rceil$ so router + table parameters stay strictly $\leq B_{\text{mem}}$ within $< 4d$).

### 3.3 Specifications of Fixed-Codebook (`QR/Grassmann`, `Binary/Walsh`) and `MoME` Direct-Logit Routers

At each active readout site $\ell \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{lk}}$, the router receives normalized hidden state $\hat{h}_t^\ell \in \mathbb{R}^d$, token ID $w_t = x_t$, and surface integers $(g_{2,t}, g_{3,t})$:

#### Router A: `VWayLexicalFiberRouter` (`DualLK-QR` / Grassmannian Frame — Principal Fixed-Code Router)
- **Codebook $C^{(h)} \in \mathbb{R}^{S_{\max} \times d_k}$ ($d_k = 64$, `freeze_subkeys=True`):** Fixed unit-norm frame per head $h \in \{0, 1\}$. For $S_{\max} \leq 64$ ($38.2\text{M}\text{--}305.8\text{M}$), Haar-QR (`torch.linalg.qr`) yields exact orthonormality ($\max_{r \neq r'} |\langle C_r^{(h)}, C_{r'}^{(h)} \rangle| = 0$). At $611.5\text{M}$ ($S_{\max} = 128 > d_k = 64$), $40$ alternating-projection Grassmannian coherence-minimization steps produce unit-norm rows with worst-case off-diagonal coherence $\max_{r \neq r'} |\langle C_r^{(h)}, C_{r'}^{(h)} \rangle| \in \{0.1117, 0.1123\}$.
- **Query Projection & Mixing:** $W_q^\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{(H d_k) \times d}$ (`bias=False`) maps $u_{\text{ctx},t}^{\ell,h} = d_k^{-1/2} (W_q^\ell \hat{h}_t^\ell)_h$. In **Cold mode** (`DualLK-QR-Cold`, principal $K = 2$ mode), `keep_anchor_parameter=True` retains `anchor_alpha` for exact parameter parity while omitting the anchor term in `forward`, $W_q^\ell(0) \sim \mathcal{U}(-\sqrt{3/d}, +\sqrt{3/d})$, and $q_t^{\ell,h} = u_{\text{ctx},t}^{\ell,h} / \max(\|u_{\text{ctx},t}^{\ell,h}\|_2, 10^{-6})$. In **$(2\text{g}, 3\text{g})$-Anchored mode** (`DualLK-QR-Hash`), $W_q^\ell(0) = 0, \alpha_{\ell,h}(0) = 0.20$, and $q_t^{\ell,h} = \operatorname{normalize}\!\big(\alpha_{\ell,h} C_{a_{h,t}}^{(h)} + u_{\text{ctx},t}^{\ell,h}\big)$. Across $K=2, H=2, d=512$, `DualLK-QR` has **$131,080$ registered router parameters** ($131,076$ active in `Cold` mode) at every memory tier, plus $4 S_{\max} d_k \in [2,048,\, 32,768]$ frozen codebook floats counted inside $\Theta_{\text{mem}}$.

#### Router B: `BinaryCodeLexicalRouter` (`DualLK-Log2` and `DualLK-Walsh2`)
- **Codebook & Query:** For $b = \lceil \log_2 \max(2, S_{\max}) \rceil$ and sign vector $\tilde{c}_r \in \{-1, +1\}^b$, degree-$1$ `DualLK-Log2` sets $C_r = b^{-1/2}\tilde{c}_r \in \mathbb{S}^{b-1}$ (lifted in zero-extra-parameter `Walsh2` to degree-$\leq 2$ products in $\mathbb{R}^{b + \binom{b}{2}}$). A linear map $W_{\text{bit}}^\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{(H b) \times d}$ (`bias=False`, uniform initialization) forms $u_t^{\ell,h} = \operatorname{normalize}((W_{\text{bit}}^\ell \hat{h}_t^\ell)_h)$ directly in `Cold` mode (or via convex `lerp` $(1-\sigma(\beta_{\ell,h}))C_{a_{h,t}} + \sigma(\beta_{\ell,h})u_t^{\ell,h}$ with $\sigma(\beta_0) = 0.20$ in `Hash` mode). Total router parameters equal $4bd + 8 \in \{6,152,\, 8,200,\, 10,248,\, 12,296,\, 14,344\}$ across $38.2\text{M}\text{--}611.5\text{M}$ ($\Theta(d \log_2 S_{\max})$).

#### Router C: `MoMELogitLexicalRouter` (Faithful `MoME` Direct Slot-Logit Baseline [14])
- **Direct Slot Logits & Top-$K$ Sigmoid-Norm:** Following Li et al. ([14], Section 3.3), each site $\ell$ maps $\hat{h}_t^\ell$ directly to per-head slot logits with learnable bias ($W_q^\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{(H S_{\max}) \times d}$ initialized $\mathcal{U}(-\sqrt{3/d}, +\sqrt{3/d})$, $b_g^\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{H S_{\max}}$ stored as a `1D` vector initialized to $0$):
  $$
  \ell_{t,r}^{\ell,h} \;=\; \big(W_q^\ell \hat{h}_t^\ell\big)_{h,r} + b_{g,h,r}^\ell \quad (r < S(w_t)), \qquad \ell_{t,r}^{\ell,h} = -\infty \quad (r \geq S(w_t)).
  $$
  Selecting top-$k_h = 2$ slots $\mathcal{A}_t^{\ell,h} = \operatorname{TopK}(\boldsymbol{\ell}_t^{\ell,h}, 2)$, standard `MoME` (`gate_mode='sigmoid_norm'`) aggregates via
  $$
  p_{t,r}^{\ell,h} \;=\; \frac{\sigma(\ell_{t,r}^{\ell,h})}{\sum_{r' \in \mathcal{A}_t^{\ell,h}} \sigma(\ell_{t,r'}^{\ell,h})} \quad (r \in \mathcal{A}_t^{\ell,h}),
  $$
  while our `MoME-Smax` control (`gate_mode='softmax'`) replaces sigmoid-norm with $\operatorname{softmax}(\tau_{\ell,h} \ell_{t,r}^{\ell,h})$ ($\tau_0 = 3.5$) on the identical linear logits ($+4$ inverse-temperature scalars, $262,660$ router parameters at $611.5\text{M}$) to separate gate normalization from unit-sphere codebook geometry. Across $K=2, H=2, d=512$, `MoME` router parameters equal $4 S_{\max}(d + 1)$: **$12,312$** (`76.4M Unif`, $S_{\max}=6$), **$32,832$** (`76.4M Zipf`, $S_{\max}=16$), **$100,548$** (`611.5M Unif`, $S_{\max}=49$), and **$262,656$** (`611.5M Zipf`, $S_{\max}=128$—growing as $\Theta(d S_{\max})$, $18.3\times$ larger than `DualLK-Log2`).

#### Fiber Gather, Step-0 Identity, and Parameter-Group Optimizer Assignment
For `DualLK-QR` and `DualLK-Log2`, candidates $r \geq S(w_t)$ are masked (`-1e9` / `-inf`) and top-$k_h = 2$ scores are weighted by $\operatorname{softmax}(\tau_{\ell,h} s)$ with $\tau_{\ell,h} = \operatorname{clamp}(\exp(\eta_{\ell,h}), 0.2, 30.0)$ ($\eta_0 = \ln 3.5$). All routers gather `BF16` rows from head-partitioned sense bank $E_{\text{lk}}$ via a fused Triton top-$k$ slice kernel with **zero** load-balancing penalty ($\lambda_{\text{bal}} = 0$). Zero-initialized tables ($U, E_{\text{surf}}, E_{\text{lk}}$) and $0.25 I$ projections ensure exact Step-$0$ logit identity to the memoryless backbone. ## 4. Experimental Protocol, Harness Exactness, Controls, and Resource Accounting

### 4.1 Runtime Environment, Native vs. `--save-out-dedup` Harness, and Token Horizons
All experiments run on single NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPUs (`32 GiB`, `SM120` compute capability `12.0`) under **`PyTorch 2.9.1+cu128`, `CUDA 12.8`, and `Triton 3.5.1`** (`records/runtime_environment.json`) on the `r/general_pretraining` ClimbMix benchmark ($V = 8,192$ BPE vocabulary, $T = 2,048$, global batch $524,288$ tokens/step, linear LR warmdown to zero). Because `SM120` lacks prebuilt cubins in the default Ampere/Hopper `kernels-community/flash-attn3` snapshot, all measured runs use FlashAttention-3 (`upstream commit 8a730d96c37560ccf1d3e09f7bbccdc886818f33`) compiled with `compute_80` PTX via `PRETRAIN_ATTENTION_KERNEL_DIR`; `run_exp.py` also provides `--attn-backend sdpa` (and `auto`) as a portable PyTorch SDPA fallback.

**Distinguishing the Unmodified Task Baseline From the Common `--save-out-dedup` Harness.** To fit externalized memory models up to $611.55\text{M}$ parameters in $32\text{ GiB}$ HBM at `microbatch = 64` without activation checkpointing, our batch driver (`notes/run_h6_batch.py`) passes `--save-out-dedup` uniformly across common-harness runs, activating custom cast-aligned RMSNorm (`_SaveOutRMSNormFn`) and Squared-ReLU (`_SqrtBackReLU2Fn`) backward reconstruction (`meristem_unfolder`, `general_pretraining_33/v2` [18]). As established in [18], `_SqrtBackReLU2Fn` cuts saved MLP activation storage by $512\text{ MiB}$ per $4d$ layer at $B=64, T=2,048$—lowering `base8` peak VRAM from the task's native **`22.27 GiB`** (`22,804.45 MiB`) to **`14.39 GiB`**—while under `torch.compile` (TorchInductor), intermediate `.to(bfloat16)` cast elision on $\sqrt{\operatorname{fp32}(a)}$ perturbs $6.25\%$ of Squared-ReLU activation gradients by $1\text{ ULP}$ ($\sim 0.25\%\text{--}0.41\%$ step-wise relative-$L_2$ `mlp.c_fc` gradient difference). Applying `--save-out-dedup` uniformly yields a fair *common-harness* comparison, but does **not** make `base8` unmodified. Accordingly, in Section 5.2 we report **both**:
1. **Genuinely Unmodified & `nodedup` (`save_out_act_dedup=False`) Runs:** Seeds $\{41, 42, 43\}$ executed directly via the unmodified `baseline_supplement/code/train.py` + `measure_loss.py` (`notes/run_native_task_baseline.py`), plus $3$-seed `base8_nodedup`, `chorus_mlp2x_76m_nove_nodedup`, and `chorus_duallk_d512_mlp2x_cold_76m_nove_nodedup` with `--save-out-dedup` omitted; and
2. **Common `--save-out-dedup` Harness Runs:** All $8\text{L}$ ($d = 512$, $33.55\text{M}$ `nove` / $50.33\text{M}$ `keep_ve`, `mb = 64`) and $12\text{L}$ ($d = 768$, $97.52\text{M}$ `nove`, `mb = 32`, $B_{\text{mem}} \in \{114.67\text{M}, 229.33\text{M}\}$) scaling and ablation suites, with every run's complete configuration retained in `records/all_runs_compact.json.xz` (`default_config` + `config_overrides`).

**Training Horizons and Extended-Cache Reuse.** Standard runs train for **$953$ steps ($499,646,464$ tokens)**. Our packed extended training stream covers ClimbMix shards `0..11` (**`1,160` steps = `608.2M` tokens**, `1,157` non-wrapping steps): thus the **`1,016`-step ($532.7\text{M}$ tok) and `1,023`-step ($536.3\text{M}$ tok)** extended `Chorus` controls consume strictly distinct tokens without wrap-around, whereas our **`1,906`-step ($999.3\text{M}$ tok)** horizon check at $305.8\text{M}$ wraps the $1,160$-step cache (**$\sim 1.64$ epochs with example reuse**—testing a $2\times$ optimization horizon with reuse rather than $1.0\text{B}$ distinct tokens).

### 4.2 Controls, Six-Split Overlap Audit, Clocks, and Seed-SE Estimators
We pair `Chorus-DualLK` with five control families under identical $B_{\text{mem}}$ ceilings:
1. **`MoME` Direct Slot-Logit Controls (`MoME-VE`, `MoME-Post-Unif`, `MoME-Post-Zipf`, `MoME-Smax`) [14]:** Evaluates `MoMELogitLexicalRouter` across **$3$ seeds $\{41, 42, 43\}$** in `MoME`'s native attention-value site (`MoME-VE`, $76.4\text{M}$) and at post-attention `{L3, L7}` under both uniform quotas (`MoME-Post-Unif`, $76.4\text{M}$ and $611.5\text{M}$) and identical `disjoint500m` Zipf quotas (`MoME-Post-Zipf`, $76.4\text{M}$ and $611.5\text{M}$; plus temperature-scaled softmax `MoME-Smax` across $3$ seeds $\{41, 42, 43\}$ at $611.5\text{M}$).
2. **`PostUni-W` ($W \in \{256, 512, 1024, 2048\}$):** Unigram table $U_{\text{post}}[x_t] \in \mathbb{R}^W$ at $\mathcal{S}_{\text{lk}}$ with identical $W$-dimensional $2\sigma$ gates, projections, and dense FLOPs.
3. **`ColdFroz-512` & `Log2-ColdFroz` (Matched-Table Frozen Contextual Initial-Query Controls):** Uses the **identical** $E_{\text{surf}}$, $E_{\text{lk}}$, `disjoint500m` quotas, `512-d` readout matrices, and context-conditioned initial projection $W_q^\ell(0)\hat{h}_t^{\ell,\text{post}}$ (or $W_{\text{bit}}^\ell(0)\hat{h}_t^{\ell,\text{post}}$) with `freeze_router=True`. Because backbone representations and tables still co-adapt during training, `DualLK-512` minus `ColdFroz-512` measures **the effect of enabling router-parameter updates (including inverse temperature $\eta_{\ell,h}$) within joint training**, rather than an additive component independent of backbone–table interactions.
4. **`HashFroz-512` (Matched-Table Fixed $(2\text{g}, 3\text{g})$ Partition Control):** Fixes routing on the identical `512-d` table and quotas to $a_{h,t}$ (`W_q = 0, freeze_router=True`).
5. **Extended-Step `4d Chorus` (`1,016` steps at $76.4\text{M}$; `1,023` steps at $611.5\text{M}$):** Grants static `Chorus` strictly larger `steady_cuda_seconds` than `DualLK-512` on every seed at both scales (and larger `training_loop_seconds` and `wall_seconds` on every seed at $76.4\text{M}$, while matching mean `training_loop_seconds` and `wall_seconds` within $\sim 1\%$ at $611.5\text{M}$: $955.9\text{ s}$ vs. $948.5\text{ s}$ loop and $1,055.7\text{ s}$ vs. $1,056.5\text{ s}$ wall; on Seed $42$, $950.7\text{ s}$ vs. $954.1\text{ s}$ loop and $1,050.4\text{ s}$ vs. $1,062.0\text{ s}$ wall).

All common-harness runs report **six splits** (`Official Val`, `Paper-29 Non-Overlap`, `Paper-29 Overlap`, `0%-Collision Hot-Private`, `Heldout-6541`, and `Heldout-6540`) and **three clocks** (`median_step_ms`, `training_loop_seconds`, `wall_seconds`), GPU peak VRAM, host RSS, and the one-time $388.8\text{ s}$ disjoint shard-counting pass. Multi-seed contrasts report paired **$\text{mean} \pm \text{seed SE}$** across $\{41, 42, 43\}$ ($n=3$, $\text{df}=2$, descriptive seed standard errors rather than confidence intervals), with scaling steepening measured by paired endpoint differences $D_s = \Delta_{612\text{M},s} - \Delta_{76\text{M},s}$; single-seed exploratory rows report $10,000$-draw matched-block $95\%$ bootstrap intervals (`[lo, hi]`) over $320$ validation blocks.

Enabling gradient updates on the router parameters ($W_q^\ell$ and inverse-temperature scalars $\eta_{\ell,h}$) within joint training (`DualLK-512` minus `ColdFroz-512`) improves validation BPB by **$+1.60 \pm 0.07\text{ mBPB}$ at $76.4\text{M}$ and $+4.35 \pm 0.28\text{ mBPB}$ at $611.5\text{M}$** across all three seeds—a **$+2.75 \pm 0.22\text{ mBPB}$ paired increase** at matched wall time ($1,056.5\text{ s}$ vs. $1,055.6\text{ s}$). (Because backbone representations and tables co-adapt during training, this contrast quantifies the joint-training effect of router updates rather than an independent additive share of the $+4.83 \pm 0.27\text{ mBPB}$ `PostUni-512` margin.) Meanwhile, static $(2\text{g}, 3\text{g})$ sense partitioning (`HashFroz-512`) trails `PostUni-512` ($-2.24 \pm 0.29$ and $-0.75 \pm 0.23\text{ mBPB}$), leaving `DualLK-512` ahead by **$+3.53 \pm 0.22 \to +5.59 \pm 0.10\text{ mBPB}$** (paired increase $+2.05 \pm 0.21\text{ mBPB}$), as $E_{\text{surf}}$ already captures $(2\text{g}, 3\text{g})$ and static sense splitting dilutes per-row gradient hits.
2. **Direct Head-to-Head Against `MoME` Direct Slot-Logit Routing (`MoME-Post` and `MoME-Smax`) [14]:** Once moved out of `VE` into our post-attention residual site `{L3, L7}`, `MoME`'s direct linear slot-logit router scales strongly ($0.975016 \pm 0.000256$ at $76.4\text{M}$ Zipf; $0.958157 \pm 0.000437$ at $611.5\text{M}$ Zipf; $0.958151 \pm 0.000296$ at $611.5\text{M}$ Uniform). Unit-sphere `DualLK-512` (`0.957813 ± 0.000294`, $131,076$ active router parameters) slightly edges standard `sigmoid_norm` `MoME-Post-Zipf` ($+0.22 \pm 0.15\text{ mBPB}$ at $76.4\text{M}$; $+0.34 \pm 0.20\text{ mBPB}$ at $611.5\text{M}$) and `MoME-Post-Unif` ($+0.43 \pm 0.15$ and $+0.34 \pm 0.19\text{ mBPB}$). Crucially, replacing `MoME`'s unscaled `sigmoid_norm` with temperature-scaled `softmax` (`MoME-Smax`, $0.957616 \pm 0.000299$, $262,660$ router parameters) improves over `MoME-Post-Zipf` across all $3/3$ seeds by **$+0.54 \pm 0.14\text{ mBPB}$** (`[+0.27, +0.76, +0.59]`) and statistically ties `DualLK-512` (`DualLK-512` minus `MoME-Smax` $= -0.20 \pm 0.15\text{ mBPB}$, $1.32\text{ SE}$), isolating top-$2$ sigmoid co-saturation ($\sigma(\ell_1)/(\sigma(\ell_1)+\sigma(\ell_2)) \to 0.5$ when both top logits grow positive) as the source of `MoME-Post`'s small deficit. Thus `DualLK-512`'s primary advantage over `MoME` at large $S_{\max}$ is **bounded $\Theta(d \cdot d_k)$ router parameter scaling** ($2.0\times$ fewer router parameters at $S_{\max}=128$, and constant as $S_{\max}$ grows further) alongside a distinct **shared-table geometry**: across all $3$ seeds at $611.5\text{M}$, both families keep $95.5\%\text{--}98.9\%$ of rows active in `L3 ∪ L7`, but per-slot-bias `MoME-Post-Zipf` / `MoME-Smax` **partitions rows across depth** (`L3`–`L7` active-row Jaccard $= 47.5\%\text{--}49.4\%$ / $44.9\%\text{--}48.8\%$), whereas zero-bias unit-sphere `DualLK-512` **reuses rows across depth and multiplexes `512-d` readout subspaces** (`Jaccard = 68.3%–74.6%`, `Union = 98.6%–98.9%`). Meanwhile, $3$-seed `DualLK-Log2` (`0.960422 ± 0.000569`, only **$14,344$** router parameters at $611.5\text{M}$—$18.3\times$ smaller than `MoME-Post-Zipf`) beats `Chorus-612M` by **$+5.76 \pm 0.14\text{ mBPB}$** and `PostUni-512-612M` by **$+2.23 \pm 0.09\text{ mBPB}$** across $3/3$ seeds, while trailing `MoME-Post-Zipf` ($-2.26 \pm 0.15\text{ mBPB}$) and `DualLK-512` ($-2.61 \pm 0.35\text{ mBPB}$), quantifying an explicit accuracy-vs.-router-rank trade-off ($b = 7$ hyperplanes vs. $d_k = 64$ directions).
3. **Equal-Step and Extended-Step Gains Over Static `Chorus`:** Across $38.2\text{M} \to 611.5\text{M}$, `DualLK-512` at $305.8\text{M}$ ($0.962896$ on Seed $42$) beats $2\times$-larger `Chorus-612M` ($0.967279$) by $+4.38\text{ mBPB}$. Because `DualLK-512`'s post-attention readout and gather add $+5.0\%\text{--}5.8\%$ step time over $953$-step `Chorus` ($914.3$ vs. $870.9\text{ ms/step}$ at $76.4\text{M}$; $961.1$ vs. $908.2\text{ ms/step}$ at $611.5\text{M}$), we extend `Chorus` to `1,016` non-wrapping steps at $76.4\text{M}$ ($884.5\text{ s}$ steady CUDA, $923.3\text{ s}$ loop, $1,023.4\text{ s}$ wall vs. `DualLK-512`'s $861.0 / 909.3 / 1,016.4\text{ s}$, exceeding all three clocks on every seed) and `1,023` non-wrapping steps at $611.5\text{M}$ ($916.8\text{ s}$ steady CUDA vs. $904.7\text{ s}$—strictly larger on every seed—while `training_loop_seconds` $955.9\text{ s}$ vs. $948.5\text{ s}$ and `wall_seconds` $1,055.7\text{ s}$ vs. $1,056.5\text{ s}$ are matched within $\sim 1\%$; on Seed $42$, `Chorus` uses $950.7\text{ s}$ loop and $1,050.4\text{ s}$ wall vs. $954.1\text{ s}$ and $1,062.0\text{ s}$). At $76.4\text{M}$ on unthinned $4d$ FFNs, $+33.0\text{M}$ extra tokens close `Chorus`'s gap to a tie ($+0.04 \pm 0.32\text{ mBPB}$), whereas at $611.5\text{M}$ `DualLK-512` overcomes the $+36.7\text{M}$-token handicap across all three seeds by **$+3.48 \pm 0.33\text{ mBPB}$** (`[+3.30, +4.13, +3.01]`; paired increase **$+3.45 \pm 0.39\text{ mBPB}$**).
4. **Overlap-Honest Generalization Across All Six Splits (`Paper 29` Audit):** Across all $3$-seed contrasts, the $11.6\%$ overlapping validation slice contributes only $11.8\%\text{--}12.8\%$ of total validation gain, and gains hold on `Paper-29 Non-Overlap` ($+4.20 \pm 0.29\text{ mBPB}$ vs. `ColdFroz-612M`, $+4.78 \pm 0.27$ vs. `PostUni-512-612M`, $+8.26 \pm 0.39$ vs. `Chorus-612M`), `0%-Collision Hot-Private` ($+3.81 \pm 0.26$, $+4.39 \pm 0.25$, $+7.34 \pm 0.40\text{ mBPB}$), and deduplicated `Heldout-6541` ($+4.36 \pm 0.25$, $+4.89 \pm 0.22$, $+8.39 \pm 0.40\text{ mBPB}$) and `Heldout-6540` ($+4.40 \pm 0.28$, $+4.85 \pm 0.22$, $+8.37 \pm 0.38\text{ mBPB}$).

## 6. Limitations and Scope

1. **Dense-Backbone Scale and Distinct-Token Horizon ($0.5\text{B}$ Distinct Tokens):** On the $2.91\times$ larger $12\text{L}$ ($d = 768$, $97.52\text{M}$) backbone at $0.50\text{B}$ tokens ($\sim 5.1$ tokens per dense parameter), `DualLK-512` beats `Chorus` ($+2.63 \pm 0.19\text{ mBPB}$ at $114.7\text{M}$; $+3.55\text{ mBPB}$ at $229.3\text{M}$) but shows only a small margin over `PostUni-512` ($+0.31 \pm 0.31\text{ mBPB}$ at $114.7\text{M}$, $S_{\max}=16$, $1.18\times$ memory-to-backbone ratio; $+0.80\text{ mBPB}$ single-seed at $229.3\text{M}$). Likewise, our $1,906$-step check wraps a $608.2\text{M}$-token cache ($\sim 1.64$ epochs) rather than streaming $1.0\text{B}$ fresh tokens, and all experiments use ClimbMix; multi-billion-token distinct-corpus scaling and open-domain factual QA (`TriviaQA`, `NaturalQuestions`, `MMLU` [2], `CORE-22` [14]) remain open.
2. **Step-Time Overhead on Unthinned $4d$ Backbones:** Without FFN narrowing, post-attention sense readout adds $+5.0\%\text{--}5.8\%$ step/loop time and $+1.8\text{--}2.2\text{ GiB}$ GPU VRAM over $953$-step `Chorus` (tested directly against `1,016`/`1,023`-step `Chorus` in Table 1b), requiring $4d \to 2d$ FFN narrowing for step-latency parity at $76.4\text{M}$.
3. **One-Time Disjoint Frequency Preprocessing:** Computing `disjoint500m_vway_s_counts.u8.npy` (and optional `True-CAM` maps) over $631.7\text{M}$ disjoint tokens (`shards 100..109`) adds a one-time $388.8\text{ s}$ preprocessing pass ($< 2\text{ s}$ for unigram quotas alone), though uniform quotas (`MoME-Post-Unif`) require zero corpus preprocessing.

Inspecting validation tokens routed at `L7` (`curiosity_samples` in `supplement.zip`) shows how word-conditioned contextual keys partition high-frequency closed-class tokens—immediately preceding high-entropy content nouns where surface $2\text{g}/3\text{g}$ windows (`"development of"`, `"in the"`) collapse unrelated domains—into domain-, syntax-, and subword-specific slots:
