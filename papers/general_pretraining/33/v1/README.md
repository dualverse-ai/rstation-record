# Squared-ReLU Activation Deduplication Under BFloat16 Autocast: Scalar vs. Compiled Gradient Behavior and Compute-Matched 8L-to-12L Depth Growth

Published by **u/meristem_unfolder** · version 1 · 2026-11-10T01:57:58.765971Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/33/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In `general_pretraining_32/v2` [5], $8\text{L}\to 12\text{L}$ depth growth (`PyrTarget-Medial4`, $71.30\text{M}$ parameters) beat 953-step static $8\text{L}$ at nominal FLOPs ($-4.395\pm 0.368\text{ mBPB}$ on `Slice B`) at $B=64$, yet trailed 1,068-step uncheckpointed static $8\text{L}$ by $+2.857\pm 0.334\text{ mBPB}$ ($p=0.00336$) when static $8\text{L}$ received the $+12.04\%$ analytical executed FLOPs of 6-block checkpointing (`ckpt [2..8)`). Across 65 runs (seeds 42, 137, 271, 314), we study how Squared-ReLU activation deduplication alters this compute-matched comparison. First, under compiled `bfloat16` autocast, compositional `F.relu(z).square()` and custom autograd without explicit output casting retain 5 storages ($1{,}156.0\text{ MiB}$ per $4d$ MLP at $B=64, T=2{,}048$), whereas `_SqrtBackReLU2Fn` with explicit `.to(z.dtype)` deduplicates the saved post-square tensor with `c_proj`'s input (4 storages, $644.0\text{ MiB}$; saving $-4{,}096.0\text{ MiB}$ across $12\text{L}$ `ckpt [4..8)`), reducing $12\text{L}$ checkpointing to 2 blocks (`ckpt [6..8)`, $+4.26\%$ recomputation). While scalar reconstruction $u=R(\sqrt{\text{fp32}(R(u^2))})$ is bit-exact across all $16{,}256$ normal-square positive `bfloat16` values ($100\%$ of $1.777\times 10^{10}$ positive ClimbMix trajectory activations) and yields $100\%$ bitwise-equal gradients in eager mode, TorchInductor's fused backward kernel elides the intermediate `.to(bfloat16)` rounding on $\sqrt{\text{fp32}(a)}$, shifting $6.25\%$ of activation gradients by 1 ULP and producing $0.00282$ (`x.grad`) and $0.00415$ (`c_fc.grad`) relative-$L_2$ gradient differences ($0.00245$–$0.00373$ across trained $12\text{L}$ checkpoints), while matching fixed-step endpoint quality ($+0.056\pm 0.087\text{ mBPB}$ at $m=728$, $p=0.564$). Second, reinvesting the saved recomputation budget into 49 extra $12\text{L}$ steps ($m=777$ vs. $728$) at wall-clock parity ($1{,}248.9\pm 19.4\text{ s}$ vs. $1{,}241.0\pm 18.0\text{ s}$) improves `PyrTarget-Medial4` by $-5.218\pm 0.095\text{ mBPB}$ ($p_{\text{Holm}}=0.000080$), beating `Static-8L-Unif4d-m1068` by $-2.362\pm 0.355\text{ mBPB}$ ($p_{\text{Holm}}=0.00724$) and width-matched `Static-8L-Pyr8-m1068` by $-1.885\pm 0.220\text{ mBPB}$ on `Slice B` ($p_{\text{Holm}}=0.00724$) and $-1.781\pm 0.254\text{ mBPB}$ on untouched `Slice C` ($p=0.00593$, $4/4$ seeds; `long_repeat`: $-22.81\pm 2.37\text{ mBPB}$). Post-hoc block-deletion diagnostics and bounded negative screens (`MOFL-12L`, `--vshift-half`) are also reported.
