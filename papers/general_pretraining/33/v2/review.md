# Accept

This is an appropriate supporting-evidence revision of the accepted contribution. The four-seed, equally memory-optimized static 12-layer comparator addresses a specific limitation identified in the previous review. It strengthens the existing comparison between activation-storage savings, reduced recomputation, and depth-growth performance; it does not introduce a separate substantive contribution. The numerical qualifications, provenance corrections, separation of historical and confirmation runs, and relocation of secondary contrasts also improve the paper. Despite its dense tables, the resulting manuscript retains an identifiable central argument rather than becoming a collection of new experiments.

I independently recomputed the added paired comparisons from the retained endpoint records. Growth beats the optimized static comparator by $-6.807\pm0.613$ mBPB on historical Slice B, $-6.849\pm0.612$ mBPB on confirmation-run Slice B, and $-6.869\pm0.588$ mBPB on Slice C; the corresponding two-sided paired-test probabilities reproduce $0.00156$, $0.00153$, and $0.00135$. The static optimization comparison reproduces $-4.826\pm0.043$ mBPB. Here the uncertainties are standard errors over four paired seeds. The new static records specify the intended pyramidal widths, custom Squared-ReLU backward, and checkpoint range, with the additional RMSNorm, window, and embedding-filter options disabled. Their analytical executed budget is approximately $1.3392\times10^{17}$ FLOPs, consistent with the manuscript. These checks establish arithmetic and configuration consistency, not independent reproduction of GPU training or compiler measurements.

The revision preserves the accepted scope relative to depth-growth work [5, 6], output-based backward reconstruction [13], and memory-sensitive architecture comparisons [14, 15]. The added comparator supports this particular training recipe; it does not establish that growth is optimal across static schedules, architectures, scales, or corpora. Section 5 appropriately retains those limitations. Separating scalar reconstruction from compiled gradients, and replacing an equivalence implication with the fixed-step estimate and confidence interval, are useful corrections.

The following local corrections would improve accuracy and reproducibility without changing acceptance:

- **Qualify wall-time matching in Sections 2.3 and 5.** The reported full-loop means reproduce approximately $1248.9$ s for historical growth, $1266.4$ s for confirmation growth, and $1217.1$ s for the new static comparator. Thus confirmation growth takes approximately $4.05\%$ longer than static 12L, outside the stated $0.6\%$–$2.6\%$ range. Describe this comparison as matched analytical FLOPs with comparable measured time, and give the actual pairwise differences. Equal-wall-time superiority over static 12L has not been directly tested.
- **Finish reconciling the older static control.** The Table 1A note correctly identifies the missing `--s1-mlp-layer-ratios` flag in the single-seed Phase-8 run, but its claimed $74.45$M parameters and $3.628\times10^8$ FLOPs/token conflict with that record's $71{,}303{,}960$ parameters and approximately $3.460\times10^8$ nominal FLOPs/token. Uniform $4^{12}$ and the stated pyramidal profile both have total FFN expansion $48$, so redistribution alone does not explain the asserted parameter increase. Also relabel the corresponding Table B2 row and Section 4 comparison consistently as the uniform control. This does not affect the new four-seed pyramidal comparison.
- **Distinguish the status of Slice C comparisons throughout.** Section 3.3 usefully distinguishes the original frozen prospective comparison from the post-review static comparison. Apply that distinction to the conclusion and table headings: Slice C was already examined before the new comparator was added, so the latter is additional held-out evidence rather than a fresh untouched confirmation experiment. Its tests are also outside the original six-contrast Holm family.
- **Describe the retained trajectories precisely.** `principal_learning_curves` contains sampled, across-seed training-loss summaries, mostly at 20-update intervals, rather than every update, individual seed trajectories, or validation learning curves. Label them accordingly and document their extraction and smoothing. The retained kernel excerpts help inspect the cast-elision explanation; include the extraction procedure and execution configuration, since the excerpts show batch dimension $4$ while the principal isolated-MLP measurements use $64$.

Removing Table 0 and moving secondary tests to the appendix improves organization. Further shorten the metadata abstract around the storage mechanism, compiler caveat, and decisive comparison, and fix the overlapping annotations in the updated Figure 1. These are presentation improvements to an adequately communicated, bounded empirical contribution.
