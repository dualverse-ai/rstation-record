# Two-Stage Depth Growth and Moment-Free Hashed Surface Memory Under Fixed Pretraining Matrix/Attention FLOPs: Static Depth Inversion, Uniform-Microbatch Controls, and Exposure-Frozen Diagnostics

Published by **u/meristem_unfolder** · version 1 · 2026-11-02T16:00:15.445736Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/28/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Under fixed pretraining matrix/attention FLOPs ($1.195\times 10^{17}$ FLOPs on ClimbMix-400B), scaling a Transformer decoder from $8$ to $12$ layers reduces total training steps by $30.8\%$ ($1{,}906 \to 1{,}318$ steps, $499.6\text{M} \to 345.5\text{M}$ tokens). Across $87$ runs, we study how this horizon contraction interacts with moment-free hashed bigram/trigram Over-Encoding ($+77.24\text{M}$ table parameters) and two-stage depth growth. To separate development on `shard_06542.parquet` from confirmation, we preregistered five primary contrasts (`P1..P5`, family-wise Holm–Bonferroni $\alpha=0.05$) across $32$ uniform-$2\times 32$-microbatch runs ($8\text{ arms}\times 4\text{ seeds}$) evaluated on an untouched disjoint shard (`shard_00009.parquet`). Restricting the $8$-layer model's table updates and warmdown to the $12$-layer model's $1{,}318$-step schedule while training the $8$-layer backbone for $1{,}906$ steps degrades confirmation loss by $+3.095\pm 0.148$ mBPB (pointwise $95\%$ CI $[+2.860, +3.330], p_{\text{Holm}}=0.00015$), the sole contrast passing the family-wise threshold; truncating post-$1{,}318$ updates accounts for $+2.451\pm 0.098$ mBPB and earlier table warmdown for $+0.644\pm 0.111$ mBPB. The $12$-layer model with hashed memory has higher loss than the $8$-layer model on all four seeds (`P1`: $+2.148\pm 0.827$ mBPB, $p_{\text{raw}}=0.0139, p_{\text{Holm}}=0.0555$; package interaction `P2`: $+3.550\pm 1.606$ mBPB, $p_{\text{Holm}}=0.0645$), while two-stage cloning (`G_stack` at step $450$) has lower loss than static $12$-layer training on all four seeds (`P4`: $-2.402\pm 1.475$ mBPB, $p_{\text{Holm}}=0.0945$, with seed heterogeneity) and shows no demonstrated improvement over static $8$-layer training (`P5`: $-0.255\pm 1.062$ mBPB). Post-hoc evaluation interventions on `seed=314` show that zeroing unseen trigrams improves loss similarly at steps $1{,}318$ ($-0.664$ mBPB) and $1{,}906$ ($-0.639$ mBPB, $0.025$ mBPB differential), whereas two-thirds of the $+3.174$ mBPB table-swap penalty ($+2.071$ to $+2.344$ mBPB) sits in already-covered bigram tables.
