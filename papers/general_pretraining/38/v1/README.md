# Sub-Band-Aligned Attention Transport and Zero-Recompute Fused Kernels for Fixed-Clock Language Model Pretraining

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T22:17:05.932486Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/38/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In fixed-clock language model pretraining, architectural modifications must overcome their own step-time and memory overhead against extra-token baselines. While adjacent key-value shifting and post-attention gating are known primitives, naive combinations distort post-RMSNorm document-boundary keys, blur high-precision value channels, or forfeit per-step gains to activation-checkpoint recomputation. We present a selective, boundary-preserving attention-transport design (M21b) co-designed with zero-recompute fused Triton kernels (+8,256 parameters at 50.33M) so per-step quality gains translate directly into fixed-clock improvements, measured in milli-bits-per-byte (1 mBPB = 10^-3 bits-per-byte; negative values indicate lower loss). M21b restricts token-gated key shifting to low-frequency upper RoPE planes with BOS self-padding, aligns a four-sub-band log-odds value-shift bank with four-group sub-head output gating and straight-through RoPE query scaling, and fuses shifts, RoPE, and RMSNorm in Triton while aliasing FlashAttention's saved query buffer within 22,977 MiB on a 24 GiB RTX 4090. Across 358 ClimbMix-400B runs spanning four model sizes (26.35M-85.85M) and four disjoint held-out slices (84.51M tokens), five paired 50.33M seeds show Zero-Recompute Triton M21b improves validation loss over 1,095-step guarded B0 by -7.017+/-0.153 mBPB at matched wall time (1,019 steps, -0.8% steady time) and by -2.866+/-0.106 mBPB in 7.1% shorter time (953 steps) and outperforms iso-parameter and +2.10M-parameter combinations of established KV-shift and output-gating primitives by -17.405+/-2.375 mBPB and -5.342+/-0.085 mBPB, with -5.703+/-0.195 mBPB at 2x horizon. Single-criterion 2x2 contrasts show sub-band value shifting is approximately additive per update (-0.184+/-0.193 mBPB interaction) and brought to wall-time break-even by zero-recompute fusion, while 128-sequence unaliased probes separate intact stratum gains from pathway reliance.
