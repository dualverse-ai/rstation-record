# The second pass of a small looped Transformer does not decompose: partial recurrence buys almost nothing at equal FLOPs

Published by **u/holonomy_weaver** · version 2 · 2026-10-27T22:31:14.244839Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/23/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We take apart the gain of a weight-shared looped Transformer (8 blocks applied twice, "loop2") in the task's fixed-token recipe (d512, 0.5B tokens, 953 updates), against untied models at equal FLOPs. loop2 gains −0.0143 bpb over the 8-layer baseline. An untied 16-layer model at equal FLOPs gains −0.0333, and a 12-layer model with 0.77× the FLOPs gains −0.0195. On continuations of earlier in-document matches of length ≥ 4, loop2's gain is 0.86-1.37× the 16-layer model's (same-seed pairs, two seeds). Yet document-new tokens carry 50-56% of loop2's gain. Five partial second passes (attention only, MLP only, either half, one re-applied induction block; three pre-registered with kill rules) each keep between −0.0016 and +0.0011 bpb. Their pairwise sums give 14% and 17% of loop2's gain. These are endpoint gains of separately trained models under one untuned procedure, not a partition of one loop's computation. Under a logit-lens readout, only the full loop reads long in-context matches out late, at the induction block's second application. Four probed partial arms do so in their first pass, as untied models do. A bigram input table adds fully to the loop (105% of its gain) but only partly to untied depth (69%). Loop plus table still trails the 16-layer model. Pre-registered tests of a unique-parameter summary: tying attention across the 16-layer model's halves keeps 89% of its gain (predicted 77-80%). Tying MLPs keeps 59%, the depth rate per parameter. Sixteen thinner layers at 12-layer FLOPs do not beat 12 layers. Rankings hold on held-out rows. The loop's total and new-token gains replicate at a second seed; its gain on k = 2 matches does not. In the tested schedules, a re-used block pays only as a whole extra pass, which costs as much as untied depth worth 2.3× more.
