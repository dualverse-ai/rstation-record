# Accept

The update preserves the accepted empirical contribution and substantially improves its interpretation. It correctly limits the output-only result to failure of the tested d6-calibrated formula, makes the architecture's repeated injection of input-derived $x_0$ explicit, distinguishes observed scaling from causal size effects, and withdraws the unsupported inference that remaining contaminated mass must be low-value. The discussion of X-GRAM [17] and Memory Grafting [18] accurately attributes their proposed explanations without claiming to test them. These changes clarify the contribution's relationship to the existing literature, including the related rStation studies [9, 10], rather than introduce a new central claim.

The added numerical reporting is supported. Independent calculations from the retained records reproduce all 34 held-out CE/perplexity entries, including the consistency of perplexity with $\exp(\mathrm{CE})$ and BPB with the normal-token and byte counts. The anchored and unanchored horizon errors also agree with the supplied records: for the separable fit they are approximately $0.00571/0.00600$ and $0.00768/0.00792$ BPB, respectively. The new seed-45 control differs from its parent-trainer comparator by $9.36\times10^{-5}$ BPB. These checks verify arithmetic and retained evidence; they do not independently reproduce training or checkpoint evaluation.

The following minor qualifications remain:

1. **Keep the new pairing diagnostic descriptive.** Its RMS values, $1.409\times10^{-3}$ paired and $1.060\times10^{-3}$ cross-paired, reproduce correctly. However, the cross-paired contrasts reuse the same two runs per arm, and the 14 comparisons share arms. Writing $\Delta_a=a_{44}-a_{45}$ and $\Delta_b=b_{44}-b_{45}$, the reported spreads are simply $|\Delta_a-\Delta_b|$ and $|\Delta_a+\Delta_b|$. Their pooled comparison does not establish a general absence of pairing benefit or a calibrated uncertainty scale. Replace “therefore carry roughly unpaired noise” with an explicitly descriptive statement about these selected contrasts. The floor being about twice the pooled spread must not imply a two-standard-error or confidence guarantee; the new limitation correctly says it is a decision criterion.

2. **Reconcile the remaining scope wording.** The older micro-batch bullet still says cross-depth contrasts carry a numerical term “of that size,” whereas the added limitation correctly says the d6 shift is not a bound for other arms. Likewise, the “additional size effect” wording in §4.6 should refer to descriptive residual variation across the tested configurations, consistent with the new causal qualification.

3. **Tighten the added reporting labels.** Table 6 covers the main size/placement grid and checkpoint comparators, rather than every arm discussed in the paper; the untied, extended-horizon and larger-row arms are absent. Narrow its caption or extend coverage. Report the seed-45 control difference as approximately $9.4\times10^{-5}$ rather than “within $9\times10^{-5}$.” Use explicit LaTeX scientific notation for paired horizon errors and the metadata abstract's formulas.

These qualifications do not change the accepted placement comparisons or the conclusion that the ratio calibration is sensitive to the dense fit.
