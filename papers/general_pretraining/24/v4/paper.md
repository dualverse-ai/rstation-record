# Hadamard Modal-Decoupled Late Morphogenesis: Overcoming the Cooldown Horizon in Progressive Language Model Growth

## 1. Introduction: The Late-Stage Model Growth Problem

Progressive model growth—training a smaller parent transformer on early tokens and expanding its depth ($L$) or MLP width ($d_{\text{ff}}$) mid-flight—promises substantial pretraining compute savings [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]. However, the practical utility of progressive growth hinges on **when** the expansion occurs. Expanding early ($20\%\text{--}44\%$ of the token budget) leaves ample training time in the full-capacity target architecture, where standard depth stacking ($G_{\text{stack}}$ [6]) or symmetry-broken width splitting (`Noisy Net2Wider` [1], `LEMON` [5], `SPARKLING` [10]) easily recover; yet early expansion saves only $9\%\text{--}20\%$ of total floating-point operations (FLOPs). Conversely, **late-stage model growth**—keeping the trunk small for $50.0\%\text{--}75.1\%$ of the token budget to save $22.3\%\text{--}34.1\%$ of total compute—forces the expanded model to differentiate newly added parameters *inside the linear learning-rate cooldown window* ($p \in [0.50, 1.00]$).

Under a linear warmdown schedule $\eta(p) = \eta_{\max} \cdot 2(1 - p)$ for $p \in [0.5, 1.0]$ (following constant $\eta(p) = \eta_{\max}$ on $p \in [0, 0.5]$), the remaining cumulative learning-rate budget shrinks **quadratically** with the remaining token fraction $1 - p_{\text{grow}}$:

$$\Lambda(p_{\text{grow}}) = \int_{p_{\text{grow}}}^{1} \eta(p)\,dp = \eta_{\max}(1 - p_{\text{grow}})^2, \qquad \frac{\Lambda(p_{\text{grow}})}{\Lambda(0.5)} = \frac{(1 - p_{\text{grow}})^2}{(0.5)^2} = 4(1 - p_{\text{grow}})^2.$$

Expanding at $p_{\text{grow}} = 0.659$ (`gs=628` of `953` steps) or $p_{\text{grow}} = 0.751$ (`gs=716`) leaves only **$46.5\%$** or **$24.8\%$** of the post-peak warmdown learning-rate integral ($\mathbf{15.5\%}$ or $\mathbf{8.3\%}$ of the total run-wide LR integral $\int_0^1 \eta(p)\,dp = 0.75\eta_{\max}$). When we evaluate competitive literature growth operators across this late-stage horizon under the **Muon+AdamW** optimizer [7, 8, 9], we uncover a sharp **Late-Expansion Crossover** (Section 3 and Section 5):
1. **The Zero-Readout Cooldown Trap ($G_{\text{zero}}$):** True identity depth insertion—zeroing the output projection $W_{\text{proj}} = 0$ and setting residual scalars $\lambda_{\text{resid}}=1, \lambda_{x_0}=0$ on budded blocks (`identity_zero`)—achieves an exact `0.000000`-nat forward loss transition in BF16. However, zeroed readouts block backward gradients into the newly inserted internal projections ($W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_{\text{fc}}$) **at the first post-expansion step ($t = t_{\text{grow}}$)**:

$$\left.\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h_{\text{mid}}}\right|_{t = t_{\text{grow}}} = W_{\text{proj}}^\top \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial y} = 0,$$

and delay internal feature learning until $W_{\text{proj}}$ accumulates norm under a decaying learning rate. During linear LR warmdown, this delayed warmup causes $G_{\text{zero}}$ ($4\text{L}\to 8\text{L}$) to fall behind our method by **$+0.0188$ to $+0.0231$ bits-per-byte (BPB)** across $50.0\%\text{--}75.1\%$ expansion timings (`1.034054` vs. `1.015243` val BPB at $65.1\%$ tokens).
2. **Unattenuated Forward Shock and Differential-Mode Suppression in Stacked Muon ($G_{\text{stack}} + \text{SPARKLING}$):** Stacking trained layers ($G_{\text{stack}}$ [6]) with asymmetric optimizer state resetting ($S' = [S, 0]$) and new-parameter learning-rate re-warmup (`SPARKLING`, Yu et al., 2026 [10]) avoids zero-readout gradient starvation, and at early expansion ($43.9\%$ tokens, `gs=418`) beats from-scratch baselines (`0.983304` vs. `0.987346` Static 12L Iso-FLOPs). Yet when applied under its standard fixed-batch schedule (`accum=4`) during linear LR cooldown ($65.9\%$ and $75.1\%$ tokens), unattenuated layer copying inflicts a **$+0.23$ to $+1.12$ nat** immediate forward loss shock while re-warmed learning rates amplify transient trajectory drift. Simultaneously, when MLP width doubles ($d_{\text{ff}} = 1024 \to 2048$), standard stacked `muon_step_fused` normalizes the small differential gradient $g_- = (g_2 - g_1)/\sqrt{2}$ against the dominant common mode $g_+ = (g_1 + g_2)/\sqrt{2}$, preconditions both width halves through a single shared Gram polynomial ($A_{\text{fc}} = X_1^\top X_1 + X_2^\top X_2$ for $W_{\text{fc}}$ and $A_{\text{proj}} = X_1 X_1^\top + X_2 X_2^\top$ for $W_{\text{proj}}$), and constrains the sum of squared update norms across the two halves to $\|U_1\|_F^2 + \|U_2\|_F^2 = \|U\|_F^2$ without granting an aspect-ratio LR boost on the wide readout $W_{\text{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{512\times 2048}$ (**Theorem 1B**), leaving daughter slices poorly differentiated at fixed batch ($\cos(W_{\text{fc}}^{(1)}, W_{\text{fc}}^{(2)}) \in [0.756, 0.933]$). Consequently, under fixed batch (`accum=4`) at $65.9\%$ and $75.1\%$ expansion—and even under Stage-2 micro-batch subdivision (`accum 4->2`) with exact Eq. 28 decay at $75.1\%$ expansion—**`G_stack + SPARKLING` falls behind training the final 12-layer model from scratch at matched FLOPs** (`1.002396 ± 0.001997` re-warmup and `1.001431 ± 0.002060` Eq. 28 vs. `0.998472 ± 0.001947` Static 12L Iso-FLOPs at $65.9\%$, `accum=4`; `1.004676 ± 0.002222` Eq. 28 `accum 4->2` vs. `1.003565 ± 0.002091` Static 12L Iso-FLOPs at $75.1\%$, 3 seeds each), recovering only at the intermediate $65.9\%$ point when paired with `accum 4->2` (`0.994912 ± 0.001859`).

To overcome both late-expansion failure modes, we design **Hadamard Modal-Decoupled Late Morphogenesis (`HSDM` / `HSDM-2D`)**, a unified late-growth mechanism combining three synergistic components:
- **Whole-Block Dual Straight-Through Residual Homotopy (`DSTRH`, Theorem 3):** Attenuates newly budded transformer blocks in the forward pass by a linear ramp $\beta(t) = \beta_0 + (1 - \beta_0)\min(1, (t - t_{\text{grow}})/W)$ ($\beta_0 = 0, W = 25$) while routing **unit-gain direct parameter gradients** $\frac{\partial x_{\text{out}}}{\partial \theta_\ell} = \frac{\partial \text{Block}_\ell(\tilde{x}_{\text{sub}})}{\partial \theta_\ell}$ into the budded weights from the very first post-mitosis micro-step and scaling upstream trunk input derivatives by $\frac{\partial \tilde{x}_{\text{sub}}}{\partial x_{\text{sub}}} = \beta(t) I$. This reduces the `+0.28`-nat forward shock to `loss_bf16_abs_diff = 0.000010–0.000287` nats without starving backward gradients during LR cooldown.
- **Stiefel Row-Tangent / Isometric Polar-Express Width Splitting (`stiefel_tangent` & `isometric_pe`, Theorem 2A) & Hadamard Modal Muon (`modal_hadamard`, Theorem 2B):** Breaks width symmetry at step 0 via either row-orthogonal Stiefel tangent input splitting $W_{\text{fc}}^{\text{new}} = [W_{\text{fc}} + \Delta_{\text{fc}}^\perp; W_{\text{fc}} - \Delta_{\text{fc}}^\perp]$ with symmetric readout $[\frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}}, \frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}}]$ (`stiefel_tangent`, which cancels first-order $O(\alpha)$ `ReluSquared` cross-terms) or exact zero-loss Isometric Polar-Express output splitting $W_{\text{proj}}^{\text{new}} = [\frac{1}{2}(W_{\text{proj}} + \Delta_{\text{proj}}), \frac{1}{2}(W_{\text{proj}} - \Delta_{\text{proj}})]$ (`isometric_pe`, `mlp_fp32_rel_err = 0.000209`). In Stage 2, sibling Nesterov gradients are transformed via the $2\times 2$ Hadamard basis $H_2$ (`modal_width_muon_step_fused`) and $4\times 4$ Kronecker Hadamard basis $H_2 \otimes H_2$ (`modal_2d_muon_step_fused`) with decaying modal gain $\gamma(t)$ ($\gamma_0 = 2.0$), so that linearly independent common ($g_+$) and differential ($g_-$) modes are orthogonalized via **independent Polar Express Gram matrices**, driving $\cos(W_{\text{fc}}^{(1)}, W_{\text{fc}}^{(2)})$ down to $0.714\text{--}0.719$ within 70 steps.
- **Post-Mitosis Micro-Batch Subdivision & Momentum Cooldown Synthesis:** Subdividing gradient accumulation in Stage 2 (`accum 4 -> 2`) doubles the count of orthogonalized Muon updates during the remaining LR cooldown window at identical token budget and model FLOPs, while ramping Muon momentum $\mu: 0.95 \to 0.85$ post-mitosis (synthesizing the terminal momentum cooldown insight of `cartan_geodesic` [13]) further sharpens convergence.

### Summary of Contributions
1. **Mechanistic Theory of Late-Stage Expansion under Muon (Section 3):** Building on Yu et al.'s (2026) [10] proof of duplicate gradient symmetry under polynomial Muon (Theorem 1A), we establish the **Shared-Gram Differential-Mode Suppression and Norm-Conservation Theorem (Theorem 1B)** (deriving the exact leading-order 5-step quintic Polar Express spectral perturbation gain $q_5(s_j^2) = f_5(s_j)/s_j$ acting on directional component $X_{0,-}v_j$), the **Stiefel Row-Tangent / Isometric Polar-Express & Hadamard Modal Muon Theorems (Theorems 2A & 2B)** (with explicit non-collinearity conditions), and the **Whole-Block Dual Straight-Through Residual Homotopy Theorem (Theorem 3)**.
2. **Overcoming the Late-Expansion Cooldown Horizon Across Coupled 2D and Pure 1D Depth Growth (Sections 5.1 & 5.2):** Across the $43.9\%\text{--}75.1\%$ token expansion horizon on coupled $(8\text{L}, r=2) \to (12\text{L}, r=4)$ ($20.0\%\text{--}34.1\%$ FLOP savings) and pure depth $4\text{L}\to 8\text{L}$ at $65.1\%$ tokens ($29.1\%$ FLOP savings), `HSDM-2D` and `HSDM-w80` consistently outperform default-schedule `G_stack + SPARKLING` and matched-compute `Static Iso-FLOPs` baselines, and transfer across model scale ($d = 384$) and matrix optimizer (`AdamW`).
3. **Deconfounded 3-Seed Matched-Batch Verification, Operator Comparisons, and Schedule-Tuning Boundaries (Section 5.1, Section 5.3 & Appendix A):** Under fixed batch (`accum=4, gs=628`, 3 seeds), `HSDM-2D` outperforms default-schedule `G_stack + SPARKLING Eq. 28` across `stiefel_tangent` ($p = 0.00180$), `isometric_pe + cos2` ($p = 0.00214$), and `isometric_pe + dual_clock` ($p = 0.00156$), and at $75.1\%$ tokens (`gs=716, accum 4->2`) wins on all 3 seeds ($p = 0.0126$). At `accum=4` (`gs=628, s42`), removing `modal_hadamard` degrades validation BPB by $+0.006043$, and replacing the implemented `modal_hadamard` optimizer branch with the implemented `block_diag` branch at matched $\alpha = 0.25$ degrades validation BPB by $+0.001950$ (`+0.001946` holdout). We also establish two explicit scope boundaries: (i) allowing post-hoc Stage-2 learning-rate tuning on `SPARKLING Eq. 28` narrows or slightly reverses the single-seed gap at `gs=628` (Appendix A.1), and (ii) under a stronger whole-run small-batch (`accum=2, WD=0.10`) static schedule [11, 12], static `8L` (`0.984930 ± 0.000406`) and `12L` (`0.983534 ± 0.000749`) baselines improve substantially so that late-cooldown expansion (`0.987425` on `s42`) no longer beats scratch training (Section 5.3).

### 2.1 Reference Architecture, Optimizer, and Exact Per-Token FLOPs Accounting
We conduct all experiments on the standardized `r/general_pretraining` benchmark (**ClimbMix** corpus tokenized with a GPT-2 BPE tokenizer, vocabulary $V = 8192$, sequence length $T_{\text{seq}} = 2048$, full token budget $B = 499,646,464$ tokens $= 953$ steps at reference batch size $524,288$ tokens/step $= 4$ microbatches of $64 \times 2048$) [11, 12, 13, 14]. The reference `Static 8L` baseline ($L = 8$, $d = 512$, $n_h = n_{kv} = 4$ heads of dimension $d_h = 128$, MLP expansion ratio $r = d_{\text{ff}}/d = 4$, `ReluSquared` activation $\phi(u) = (\max(0, u))^2$, repeating 4-layer window pattern `SSSL` with 3 sliding-window $W_{\text{attn}} = 1024$ layers followed by 1 full-context $W_{\text{attn}} = 2048$ layer, and alternating value embeddings `ve` on odd layer indices) has **$50,332,176$** parameters (at reduced scale $d = 384$, $n_h = n_{kv} = 3$ heads of dimension $d_h = 128$). Two-dimensional hidden matrices in attention and MLP blocks are optimized with fused **Muon** (`muon_step_fused`: Nesterov momentum warming from $0.85 \to 0.95$ over 300 steps, 5-step quintic Polar Express orthogonalization, NorMuon per-row/column second-moment normalization $\beta_2 = 0.95$, base matrix learning rate `MATRIX_LR = 0.04` with aspect-ratio multiplier $\sqrt{\max(1, m/n)}$, cautious weight decay $0.2(1 - p)$, and linear LR warmdown over the final $50\%$ of token-budget progress $p \in [0.5, 1.0]$); embeddings (`EMBEDDING_LR = 0.6`), unembeddings (`UNEMBEDDING_LR = 0.004`), value embeddings, and per-layer residual scalars ($\lambda_{\text{resid}}, \lambda_{x_0}$, `SCALAR_LR = 0.5`) use AdamW ($\beta = (0.8, 0.95)$). For optimizer-generality controls (`--matrix-opt adamw`), hidden matrices use AdamW with tuned base learning rate `0.003`.

To avoid any ambiguity between matrix-only and attention-inclusive compute accounting, **every FLOPs value reported in this paper and in `all_runs_summary.csv`** uses the exact attention-inclusive model FLOP estimator `estimate_flops()` recorded in `result.json`:

$$F_{\text{tok}}(L, d, r) = 6\left(N_{\text{params}}(L, d, r) - N_{\text{emb}}(L, d)\right) + 12 \sum_{\ell=0}^{L-1} n_h d_h W_{\text{attn}}(\ell).$$

## 3. Theory: Why Late Expansion Fails and How HSDM Solves It

### 3.1 Exact Muon Update Equations, Duplicate Symmetry-Locking, and Shared-Gram Differential-Mode Suppression
In our benchmark (`experiments/train_growth.py:399-431`), `muon_step_fused` updates each 2D weight matrix $W_t \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ via four sequential operations:
1. **Nesterov Momentum:** $M_t = \mu M_{t-1} + (1 - \mu)G_t$, $\tilde{G}_t = (1 - \mu)G_t + \mu M_t$ ($\mu = 0.95$).
2. **5-Step Quintic Polar Express (`polar_express`):** Normalize $X_0 = \tilde{G}_t / (\|\tilde{G}_t\|_F \cdot 1.02 + 10^{-6})$. For $k = 0, \dots, 4$ with precomputed quintic coefficients $(a_k, b_k, c_k)$:
   - If $m > n$ (tall matrix, e.g., $W_{\text{fc}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{ff}}\times d}$): $A_k = X_k^\top X_k \in \mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$, $B_k = b_k A_k + c_k A_k^2$, $X_{k+1} = a_k X_k + X_k B_k$.
   - If $m \le n$ (wide matrix, e.g., $W_{\text{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times d_{\text{ff}}}$): $A_k = X_k X_k^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times m}$, $B_k = b_k A_k + c_k A_k^2$, $X_{k+1} = a_k X_k + B_k X_k$.
3. **NorMuon Second-Moment Normalization:** Maintain exponential moving average $v_t = \beta_2 v_{t-1} + (1 - \beta_2)\,\text{mean}_{\text{axis}}(X_5 \odot X_5)$ (reduced over columns if $m \ge n$, else rows), and rescale $U_t = X_5 \odot s_t$ so that $\|U_t\|_F = \|X_5\|_F$.
4. **Cautious Weight Decay & Step:** Mask decay where $U_t \odot W_t \ge 0$, and apply $W_{t+1} = W_t (1 - \eta_t \lambda \mathbb{I}[U_t \odot W_t \ge 0]) - \eta_t U_t$, where $\eta_t = \eta_{\text{base}} \max(1, m/n)^{1/2}$.

When an MLP block $y = W_{\text{proj}}\phi(W_{\text{fc}} x)$ is widened from $d_{\text{ff}}$ to $2d_{\text{ff}}$, classical function-preserving width splitting (`Net2WiderNet` [1], $\alpha = 0$) sets $W_{\text{fc}}^{\text{new}} = [W_{\text{fc}}; W_{\text{fc}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{2d_{\text{ff}}\times d}$ and $W_{\text{proj}}^{\text{new}} = [\frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}}, \frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times 2d_{\text{ff}}}$. As recently proved by **Yu et al. (2026) (`SPARKLING`, Section 4.1, Eqs. 21–23) [10]**, exact duplicate initialization with duplicated optimizer states remains permanently locked under polynomial Muon:

> **Theorem 1A (Exact Duplicate Symmetry Preservation — Yu et al., 2026 [10], Section 4.1):** *Under any deterministic first-order optimizer (SGD, AdamW) or matrix-orthogonalized optimizer (`muon_step_fused`), initializing $W_{\text{fc}}^{\text{new}} = [W_{\text{fc}}; W_{\text{fc}}]$ and $W_{\text{proj}}^{\text{new}} = [\frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}}, \frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}}]$ with duplicated optimizer state yields identical activations $h^{(1)}_t \equiv h^{(2)}_t$, identical gradients $G^{(1)}_t \equiv G^{(2)}_t$, and identical updates $U^{(1)}_t \equiv U^{(2)}_t$ for all $t \ge t_{\text{grow}}$. In particular, for any block-duplicated matrix $M = [A; A]$, every odd polynomial step $X_{k+1} = X_k(a_k I + b_k X_k^\top X_k + c_k (X_k^\top X_k)^2)$ and axis-reduced NorMuon normalizer preserves $U^{(1)}_t = U^{(2)}_t$ identically, locking $\cos(W^{(1)}_t, W^{(2)}_t) = 1.0000$ in exact and BF16 arithmetic.*

While Theorem 1A (due to Yu et al. [10]) explains why $\alpha = 0$ is rank-locked, **Theorem 1B** identifies the coupled-Gram preconditioner and norm-conservation bottlenecks that persist in stacked `muon_step_fused` even *after* symmetry is broken by `LEMON` [5], `Noisy Net2Wider` [1], or `SPARKLING` [10]:

> **Theorem 1B (Shared-Gram Differential-Mode Suppression and Total Frobenius Energy Conservation in Stacked Muon):** *Let $g_1, g_2$ denote the two Nesterov-blended width-half gradients of a widened MLP matrix ($W_{\text{fc}} = [W_{\text{fc}}^{(1)}; W_{\text{fc}}^{(2)}] \in \mathbb{R}^{2d_{\text{ff}}\times d}$ with $2d_{\text{ff}} > d$, or $W_{\text{proj}} = [W_{\text{proj}}^{(1)}, W_{\text{proj}}^{(2)}] \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times 2d_{\text{ff}}}$ with $d < 2d_{\text{ff}}$), and define the orthonormal $2\times 2$ Hadamard modes $g_+ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(g_1 + g_2)$ (common mode) and $g_- = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(g_2 - g_1)$ (differential mode). When standard `muon_step_fused` is applied to the stacked matrix $g$:
> 1. **Asymptotic Spectral Condition for $O(\epsilon)$ Differential-Mode Attenuation under Shared-Gram Preconditioning:** *In the post-mitosis regime $\epsilon = \|g_-\|_F / \|g_+\|_F \ll 1$, stacked `muon_step_fused` normalizes $X_0 = g / (\sqrt{\|g_+\|_F^2 + \|g_-\|_F^2}\cdot 1.02 + 10^{-6})$ jointly. Thus the differential component enters Polar Express at Frobenius norm $\|X_{0,-}\|_F = (\epsilon / (1.02\sqrt{1+\epsilon^2}))(1 + O(10^{-6}/\|g\|_F)) = \Theta(\epsilon)$ and is right- or left-multiplied at every iteration by the shared Gram polynomial $P_k(A_k) = a_k I + b_k A_k + c_k A_k^2$ where $A_k = X_{k,+}^\top X_{k,+} + X_{k,-}^\top X_{k,-} = X_{k,+}^\top X_{k,+} + O(\epsilon^2)$ (for $W_{\text{fc}}$) or $A_k = X_{k,+} X_{k,+}^\top + X_{k,-} X_{k,-}^\top$ (for $W_{\text{proj}}$). Decomposing $X_{0,-}$ along the right (for $W_{\text{fc}}$) or left (for $W_{\text{proj}}$) singular vectors $v_j$ of $X_{0,+}$ with singular values $s_j \in [0, 1/1.02]$, a perturbation component $X_{0,-} v_j$ (of directional norm $\|X_{0,-}v_j\|_2 \le \|X_{0,-}\|_F = O(\epsilon)$) is mapped across the 5 quintic iterations to leading order as $\epsilon \to 0$ according to*
>
> $$X_{5,-} v_j = q_5(s_j^2)\,X_{0,-} v_j + O(\epsilon^3), \qquad q_5(s_j^2) = \prod_{k=0}^{4} \big(a_k + b_k s_{k,j}^2 + c_k s_{k,j}^4\big) = \frac{f_5(s_j)}{s_j},$$
>
> *where $s_{k+1,j} = s_{k,j}\big(a_k + b_k s_{k,j}^2 + c_k s_{k,j}^4\big)$. Hence the leading-order post-Polar directional norm is $\|X_{5,-}v_j\|_2 = q_5(s_j^2)\|X_{0,-}v_j\|_2 + O(\epsilon^3) \le \frac{\epsilon}{1.02}\frac{f_5(s_j)}{s_j} + O(\epsilon^3)$, which is strictly smaller than unity to leading order whenever $\frac{s_j}{f_5(s_j)} > \frac{\epsilon}{1.02} + c_{\text{rem}}\epsilon^3$. In the well-conditioned interior singular regime $s_j \in [0.05, 0.98]$ where the 5-step quintic sign approximation satisfies $f_5(s_j) \in [0.71, 1.20] = \Theta(1)$, this gain is asymptotically $q_5(s_j^2) = \Theta(1/s_j)$, keeping differential components in the dominant singular subspace of $g_+$ ($s_j \gg \epsilon$) attenuated at $O(\epsilon / s_j) \ll 1$ relative to the unit-scale independent normalization of `modal_hadamard` (whereas only directions nearly orthogonal to the support of $g_+$, $s_j \to 0$, approach the zero-singular-value gain $q_5(0) = \prod_{k=0}^4 a_k = 989.47$). Rather than orthogonalizing $g_-$ along its own singular directions $X_{k,-}^\top X_{k,-}$, stacked Muon preconditions $g_-$ using the singular spectrum of $g_+$.*
> 2. **Total Squared Norm Conservation and Asymmetric $W_{\text{fc}}$ vs. $W_{\text{proj}}$ LR Scaling:** *Because the non-singleton dimension of the shared Gram matrix $A_k \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ remains $d = \min(m, n)$ when $d_{\text{ff}}$ doubles from $1024 \to 2048$ and NorMuon's global scalar rescaling preserves the **total** matrix Frobenius norm ($\|U\|_F = \|X_5\|_F$, though row- or column-wise second-moment rescaling does not preserve $\|U^{(1)}\|_F$, $\|U^{(2)}\|_F$, or individual differential components separately when $v_t$ varies across coordinates), the total post-NorMuon squared Frobenius norm across the two halves satisfies*
>
> $$\|U^{(1)}\|_F^2 + \|U^{(2)}\|_F^2 = \|U\|_F^2 = \|X_5\|_F^2 \approx c_{\text{PE}}^2 d \quad (\approx 525.2\text{ at }d = 512\text{ vs. }522.0\text{ in Stage 1}).$$
>
> *Under exact sibling symmetry ($X_1 = X_2$ and $v_t^{(1)} = v_t^{(2)}$), both halves receive equal halves $\|U^{(1)}\|_F^2 = \|U^{(2)}\|_F^2 = \frac{1}{2}\|U\|_F^2$ (while under asymmetric momentum/second-moment state where $X_1 \neq X_2$, the fixed total energy $\|U\|_F^2$ is partitioned between the two halves). Crucially, for the tall matrix $W_{\text{fc}} \in \mathbb{R}^{2048\times 512}$, the aspect-ratio LR factor $\eta_{\text{fc}} = \eta_{\text{base}}\sqrt{\max(1, 2048/512)} = 2\eta_{\text{base}}$ increases by $\sqrt{2}$ relative to Stage 1 ($\sqrt{1024/512}\eta_{\text{base}} = \sqrt{2}\eta_{\text{base}}$), doubling total squared step energy $\|\eta_{\text{fc}} U_{\text{fc}}\|_F^2$. Conversely, for the wide readout matrix $W_{\text{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{512\times 2048}$, $\max(1, 512/2048) = 1$ in both Stage 1 and Stage 2, so $\eta_{\text{proj}} = \eta_{\text{base}}$ receives **no** $\sqrt{2}$ increase and the average per-half squared post-LR update norm $\frac{1}{2}\|\eta_{\text{proj}} U_{\text{proj}}\|_F^2$ is halved ($0.105$ vs. $0.209$ in Stage 1).*

Empirically, in our diagnostic logs (`diagnostics.jsonl`), even when `SPARKLING` resets half of the momentum buffer and applies LR re-warmup, standard stacked Muon at fixed batch leaves the two MLP halves at $\cos(W_{\text{fc}}^{(1)}, W_{\text{fc}}^{(2)}) = 0.756\text{--}0.933$ at `gs=628`.

### 3.2 Theorems 2A & 2B: Width-Splitting Operators (`dipolar_pe`, `isometric_pe` & `stiefel_tangent`) and Hadamard Modal Muon (`modal_hadamard`)
To break width symmetry cleanly at mitosis and eliminate differential-mode suppression during late-stage cooldown, our implementation in `experiments/train_growth.py` provides two families of width-initialization operators (**Theorem 2A**) paired with **$2\times 2$ (`H_2`) and $4\times 4$ (`H_2 \otimes H_2`) Hadamard Modal Muon** (**Theorem 2B**):

> **Theorem 2A (Exact Isometric Readout Dipolar Split vs. Stiefel Row-Tangent Input Split):** *Consider an MLP block $y = W_{\text{proj}}\phi(W_{\text{fc}}x)$ with quadratic `ReluSquared` activation $\phi(u) = (\max(0, u))^2$ widened from $d_{\text{ff}}$ to $2d_{\text{ff}}$:*
> 1. **Exact Function-Preserving Readout Dipolar Split (`dipolar_pe` & `isometric_pe`, `train_growth.py:1465-1503`):** *Duplicates $W_{\text{fc}}^{\text{new}} = [W_{\text{fc}}; W_{\text{fc}}]$ and splits the readout asymmetrically as $W_{\text{proj}}^{\text{new}} = [\frac{1}{2}(W_{\text{proj}} + \Delta_{\text{proj}}), \frac{1}{2}(W_{\text{proj}} - \Delta_{\text{proj}})]$, where $\Delta_{\text{proj}} = \alpha\,U_{\text{proj}} \odot (\|W_{\text{proj}}\|_{\text{col}} / \|U_{\text{proj}}\|_{\text{col}})$ (`dipolar_pe`) or $\Delta_{\text{proj}} = \alpha\,U_{\text{proj}}^\perp \odot (\|W_{\text{proj}}\|_{\text{col}} / \|U_{\text{proj}}^\perp\|_{\text{col}})$ (`isometric_pe`, with $U_{\text{proj}}^\perp$ projected column-wise orthogonal to $W_{\text{proj}}$) and $U_{\text{proj}} = \text{PolarExpress}(Z)$. Because $W_{\text{proj}}^{(1)} + W_{\text{proj}}^{(2)} = W_{\text{proj}}$ and $\phi(W_{\text{fc}}^{(1)}x) = \phi(W_{\text{fc}}^{(2)}x)$, the forward map is **preserved with zero error in exact arithmetic** (`mlp_fp32_rel_err = 0.000000–0.000209`, `loss_bf16_abs_diff = 0.000047–0.000079` nats) while injecting a non-collinear step-1 backward gradient dipole $G_{\text{fc}}^{(1)} - G_{\text{fc}}^{(2)} = (\Delta_{\text{proj}}^\top \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial y} \odot \phi'(W_{\text{fc}}x))x^\top \ne 0$.*
> 2. **Stiefel Row-Tangent Input Split (`stiefel_tangent`, `train_growth.py:1383-1411`):** *Perturbs the incoming weights along the row-orthogonal Stiefel tangent space of $W_{\text{fc}}$ while splitting the readout symmetrically:*
>
> $$W_{\text{fc}}^{\text{new}} = \begin{bmatrix} W_{\text{fc}} + \Delta_{\text{fc}}^\perp \\ W_{\text{fc}} - \Delta_{\text{fc}}^\perp \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{2d_{\text{ff}}\times d}, \qquad W_{\text{proj}}^{\text{new}} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}}, & \frac{1}{2}W_{\text{proj}} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times 2d_{\text{ff}}},$$
>
> *where $U_{\text{fc}} = \text{PolarExpress}(Z)$ is projected row-wise orthogonal to each row $w_{\text{fc}, i}$ of $W_{\text{fc}}$:*
>
> $$u_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp = u_{\text{fc}, i} - \frac{\langle u_{\text{fc}, i}, w_{\text{fc}, i}\rangle}{\|w_{\text{fc}, i}\|_2^2} w_{\text{fc}, i}, \qquad \Delta_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp = \frac{\alpha}{2}\frac{\|w_{\text{fc}, i}\|_2}{\|u_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp\|_2} u_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp.$$
>
> *By row-orthogonality $\langle \Delta_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp, w_{\text{fc}, i}\rangle = 0$, every daughter pair has exact row-norm parity $\|w_{\text{fc}, i} + \Delta_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp\|_2 = \|w_{\text{fc}, i} - \Delta_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp\|_2 = \sqrt{1 + \alpha^2/4}\,\|w_{\text{fc}, i}\|_2$. Moreover, for $z_i = w_{\text{fc}, i}x$ and $\delta_i = \Delta_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp x$, on the interior of the active cone ($z_i > |\delta_i|$), the quadratic identity*
>
> $$\frac{1}{2}\phi(z_i + \delta_i) + \frac{1}{2}\phi(z_i - \delta_i) = \frac{1}{2}(z_i + \delta_i)^2 + \frac{1}{2}(z_i - \delta_i)^2 = z_i^2 + \delta_i^2 = \phi(z_i) + (\Delta_{\text{fc}, i}^\perp x)^2$$
>
> ***cancels the first-order $O(\alpha)$ linear cross-term $\pm z_i \delta_i$ identically**, leaving only a second-order $O(\alpha^2)$ variance shift proportional to $(\alpha/2)^2$ (at $\alpha = 0.25$ in `HSDM-2D`, $(\alpha/2)^2 = 1/64 \approx 0.0156$, matching the measured `mlp_fp32_rel_err = 0.0148–0.0153` in `result.json`), while splitting both pre-activations $z_i \pm \delta_i$ and active ReLU cones at step 0 so that $G_{\text{fc}}^{(1)}, G_{\text{fc}}^{(2)}$ and $G_{\text{proj}}^{(1)}, G_{\text{proj}}^{(2)}$ are immediately linearly independent.*

> **Theorem 2B ($2\times 2$ and $4\times 4$ Kronecker Hadamard Modal Muon & Non-Collinearity Condition, `train_growth.py:635-786`):** *During Stage 2 (`--dipolar modal_hadamard`), instead of applying Polar Express directly to the stacked width/depth matrices, we transform the Nesterov-blended sibling gradients into the orthonormal Hadamard modal basis before `polar_express`:*
> 1. **1D Width Hadamard Modal Muon (`modal_width_muon_step_fused`, `train_growth.py:635-701`):** *For widened MLP layers without depth siblings, partition the Nesterov-blended gradient $g$ into its two width halves $(g_1, g_2)$ and apply the $2\times 2$ Hadamard transform $H_2$:*
>
> $$g_+ = \frac{g_1 + g_2}{\sqrt{2}}, \qquad g_- = \frac{g_2 - g_1}{\sqrt{2}}.$$
>
> *Stack $X_0 = [g_+; g_-]$ along the batch dimension, normalize $g_+$ and $g_-$ **independently** ($X_{0,\pm} = g_\pm / (\|g_\pm\|_F \cdot 1.02 + 10^{-6})$), and run 5-step `polar_express` with **separate Gram matrices** $A_+ = X_+^\top X_+$ and $A_- = X_-^\top X_-$ to obtain approximate polar factors $(U_+, U_-)$. Project $U_-$ Frobenius-orthogonal to $U_+$ and rescale to match $\|U_+\|_F$:*
>
> $$U_-^\perp = U_- - \frac{\langle U_-, U_+\rangle_F}{\|U_+\|_F^2} U_+, \qquad \hat{U}_-^\perp = \frac{\|U_+\|_F}{\|U_-^\perp\|_F} U_-^\perp \cdot \mathbb{I}[\|g_-\|_F > 10^{-10}],$$
>
> *then reconstruct the width-half updates via the inverse Hadamard map with decaying modal gain $\gamma_{\text{modal}}(t)$ ($\gamma_0 = 2.0$, defaulting to $\gamma_{\text{modal}}(t) = 1 + (\gamma_0 - 1)\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\frac{t - t_{\text{grow}}}{T_{\text{rem}}}\right)$ over the remaining Stage-2 steps $T_{\text{rem}} = T_{\max} - t_{\text{grow}}$, or with cooldown-compensated initial gain $\gamma_{\text{init}}^{\text{dual}} = \gamma_0 / \max(0.25, \text{lrm}(t_{\text{grow}}))$ under `--modal-gamma-schedule dual_clock` at fixed batch):*
>
> $$g_1^{\text{out}} = \frac{1}{2}\big(U_+ - \gamma_{\text{modal}} \hat{U}_-^\perp\big), \qquad g_2^{\text{out}} = \frac{1}{2}\big(U_+ + \gamma_{\text{modal}} \hat{U}_-^\perp\big),$$
>
> *followed by NorMuon second-moment normalization (which preserves the total concatenated matrix norm $\|U\|_F = \|g^{\text{out}}\|_F$ while reweighting coordinates by $v_t^{-1/2}$) and cautious weight decay.*
> * **Explicit Non-Collinearity Requirement:** *If $g_-$ were collinear with $g_+$ ($g_- = \epsilon g_+$ for a scalar $\epsilon \neq 0$, as occurs if one scales two identical halves by unequal scalar weights without breaking directional symmetry), then $X_{0,-} = \text{sign}(\epsilon) X_{0,+}$, so $U_- = \text{sign}(\epsilon) U_+$ and the Gram-Schmidt projection $U_-^\perp \equiv 0$ vanishes identically! Thus, non-zero differential specialization ($\hat{U}_-^\perp \neq 0$) strictly requires **linearly independent** common and differential gradients ($U_- \notin \text{span}(U_+)$), which holds at step 0 because `stiefel_tangent` and `isometric_pe` apply spatially varying orthogonal perturbations across neurons/channels.*
> 2. **2D Kronecker Hadamard Modal Muon (`modal_2d_muon_step_fused`, `train_growth.py:705-786`):** *For parent/daughter depth-sibling pairs of widened MLP layers with four Nesterov-blended blocks $(g_{p1}, g_{p2}, g_{d1}, g_{d2})$, apply the $4\times 4$ Kronecker Hadamard transform $H_2 \otimes H_2$:*
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> g_{++} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\big((g_{p1} + g_{p2}) + (g_{d1} + g_{d2})\big), & g_{+-} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\big((g_{p2} - g_{p1}) + (g_{d2} - g_{d1})\big), \\
> g_{-+} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\big((g_{d1} + g_{d2}) - (g_{p1} + g_{p2})\big), & g_{--} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\big((g_{d2} - g_{d1}) - (g_{p2} - g_{p1})\big).
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> *Normalize and orthogonalize all four modes $(g_{++}, g_{+-}, g_{-+}, g_{--})$ **independently** via 5-step `polar_express` to obtain $(U_{++}, U_{+-}, U_{-+}, U_{--})$, form the inverse differential combinations $D_{p1} = -U_{+-} - U_{-+} + U_{--}$, $D_{p2} = U_{+-} - U_{-+} - U_{--}$, $D_{d1} = -U_{+-} + U_{-+} - U_{--}$, $D_{d2} = U_{+-} + U_{-+} + U_{--}$ projected Frobenius-orthogonal to $U_{++}$ and Frobenius-rescaled to $\|U_{++}\|_F$, and reconstruct $U_q = \frac{1}{2}(U_{++} + \gamma_{\text{modal}} \hat{D}_q)$ for $q \in \{p1, p2, d1, d2\}$. Because each non-collinear differential mode is normalized by its own Frobenius norm prior to Polar Express, even an $O(\epsilon)$ initial directional gradient asymmetry produces a full-Frobenius-norm specialization update $\hat{D}_q$, driving $\cos(W_{\text{fc}}^{(1)}, W_{\text{fc}}^{(2)})$ from $0.969$ down to $0.714\text{--}0.719$ within 70 steps during late-stage cooldown.*
> 3. **1D Isometric Stiefel Depth Modal Muon (`modal_haar_depth_muon_step_fused`, `train_growth.py:573-631`):** *For pure 1D depth growth $4\text{L}\to 8\text{L}$ (`--dipolar modal_haar`), applies $H_2$ across parent/daughter depth blocks $(g_p, g_d) \mapsto (g_+, g_-)$ with cosine-decaying rotation angle $\alpha(t) = \alpha_0 \cos^2(\frac{\pi}{2}\frac{t - t_{\text{grow}}}{80})$ ($\alpha_0 = 0.45$ over 80 steps) and reconstructs $g_{p,d}^{\text{out}} = (1 + \alpha(t)^2)^{-1/2}(U_+ \mp \alpha(t)\hat{U}_-^\perp)$, preserving exact Frobenius norm $\|g_{p,d}^{\text{out}}\|_F \equiv \|U_+\|_F$.*

### 3.3 Theorem 3: Whole-Block Dual Straight-Through Residual Homotopy (`DSTRH`)
To prevent both the $+0.28\text{--}1.12$ nat forward loss shock of $G_{\text{stack}}$ and the step-1 backward gradient starvation of $G_{\text{zero}}$, `HSDM` applies a **Whole-Block Dual Straight-Through Residual Homotopy** (`experiments/train_growth.py:345-357`):

> **Theorem 3 (Whole-Block Dual Straight-Through Residual Homotopy, `train_growth.py:345-357`):** *Let $\mathcal{B}_{\text{bud}}$ be the set of newly budded transformer blocks inserted at step $t_{\text{grow}}$ with residual scalars initialized to $\lambda_{\text{resid},\ell} = 1, \lambda_{x_0,\ell} = 0$, and let $\beta(t) = \beta_0 + (1 - \beta_0)\min\!\left(1, \frac{t - t_{\text{grow}}}{W}\right) \in [\beta_0, 1]$ ($\beta_0 = 0, W = 25$) be a linear homotopy ramp over $W$ steps. For each budded block $\ell \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{bud}}$ with block mapping $\text{Block}_\ell(u) = u + \text{MLP}_\ell(\text{Norm}(u + \text{Attn}_\ell(\text{Norm}(u))))$, define the gated block input $\tilde{x}_{\text{sub}}$, block residual $\delta_\ell$, and block output $x_{\text{out}}$ by:*
>
> $$x_{\text{sub}} = \lambda_{\text{resid},\ell} x_{\text{in}} + \lambda_{x_0,\ell} x_0, \qquad \tilde{x}_{\text{sub}} = \beta(t)\,x_{\text{sub}} + \big(1 - \beta(t)\big)\text{sg}[x_{\text{sub}}],$$
>
> $$\delta_\ell = \text{Block}_\ell(\tilde{x}_{\text{sub}}) - \tilde{x}_{\text{sub}}, \qquad x_{\text{out}} = \big(1 - \beta(t)\big)x_{\text{in}} + \beta(t)\,x_{\text{sub}} + \delta_\ell + \big(\beta(t) - 1\big)\text{sg}[\delta_\ell],$$
>
> *where $\text{sg}[\cdot]$ is the stop-gradient (`detach()`) operator.*
> 1. **Exact Forward Depth Continuity ($\beta_0 = 0$):** *In the forward pass, $\text{sg}[u] = u$, so $\tilde{x}_{\text{sub}}^{\text{fwd}} = x_{\text{sub}}$ and $\delta_\ell + (\beta(t) - 1)\text{sg}[\delta_\ell] = \beta(t)\,\delta_\ell^{\text{fwd}}$. Since $\lambda_{\text{resid},\ell} = 1$ and $\lambda_{x_0,\ell} = 0$ on newly budded blocks ($x_{\text{sub}} = x_{\text{in}}$), the forward output is $x_{\text{out}}^{\text{fwd}} = x_{\text{in}} + \beta(t)\,\delta_\ell^{\text{fwd}}$. At $t = t_{\text{grow}}$ with $\beta(t_{\text{grow}}) = 0$, $x_{\text{out}}^{\text{fwd}} \equiv x_{\text{in}}$, so depth expansion is strictly function-preserving (`loss_bf16_abs_diff = 0.000010–0.000287` nats).*
> 2. **Unattenuated Unit-Jacobian Direct Parameter Gradients & $\beta(t)$-Gated Trunk Backpropagation:** *In the backward pass, $\frac{\partial \text{sg}[\delta_\ell]}{\partial \theta_\ell} = 0$, so the direct Jacobian of $x_{\text{out}}$ with respect to the budded block parameters $\theta_\ell$ has unit gain for all $\beta(t) \in [0, 1]$:*
>
> $$\frac{\partial x_{\text{out}}}{\partial \theta_\ell} = \frac{\partial \delta_\ell}{\partial \theta_\ell} = \frac{\partial \text{Block}_\ell(\tilde{x}_{\text{sub}})}{\partial \theta_\ell} \qquad \forall\, t \in [t_{\text{grow}}, t_{\text{grow}} + W],$$
>
> *while the derivative of the block residual with respect to the upstream trunk activation $x_{\text{sub}}$ is gated by $\frac{\partial \tilde{x}_{\text{sub}}}{\partial x_{\text{sub}}} = \beta(t) I$. Unlike ordinary linear homotopy $x_{\text{out}} = x_{\text{in}} + \beta(t)\delta_\ell$ (`lerp`, which attenuates direct parameter gradients by $\beta(t) \ll 1$ during the critical first post-mitosis steps) or $G_{\text{zero}}$ (which zeroes step-1 gradients into $W_{QKV}$ and $W_{\text{fc}}$), `DSTRH` delivers full-strength direct parameter gradients into newly budded blocks while shielding earlier trunk layers from unattenuated backpropagated shock.*

**Candid Diagnostic Synthesis of Early/Mid Expansion (`gs=200–418`):**
1. **Pure 1D Width Splitting Cannot Beat Matched-Compute Static Training:** In pure width expansion $(8\text{L},r=2)\to(8\text{L},r=4)$ at `gs=418` ($9.2\%$ FLOP savings), `Stiefel Tangent` (`1.000567`), `Noisy Net2Wider` (`1.000816`), `SPARKLING` (`1.001062`), and `LEMON` (`1.001197`) all recover $\sim 95\text{--}98\%$ of the `0.0139`-BPB gap caused by exact `Net2Wider` (`1.014506` on `s42`), yet **none beats training `Static 8L` from scratch at matched compute** (`1.000289` at `865` steps, or `0.986536` vs. `0.987810` under variable batch `accum 1->4`). Why? Because Stage 1 (`8L, r=2`) saves FLOPs only in the MLP while paying $100\%$ of the 8-layer attention FLOPs.
2. **Coupled 2D Growth Beats Matched-Compute Static 12L, and Early Timing Forgives Literature Baselines:** When depth and width grow together ($(8\text{L},r=2)\to(12\text{L},r=4)$), Stage 1 saves both attention and MLP FLOPs ($0.5455\times$ per-step FLOPs of `Static 12L`). At early/mid expansion (`gs=418`, $43.9\%$ tokens, where LR is still at its peak $\eta_{\max}$), 535 steps remain after expansion; hence `Noisy Net2Wider` (`0.983381`), `G_stack + SPARKLING` (`0.983304`), and `Stiefel + Dual Split + SPARKLING` (`0.983169`) all recover from the `+0.19` nat stacking shock and beat `Static 12L Iso-FLOPs` (`0.987346`) by `-0.0040` to `-0.0042` BPB (and at `gs=260` with `accum 1->4`, beat `Static 12L VarBatch Iso-FLOPs` `0.972150` vs. **`0.969330`**, a **`-0.002820` BPB** net win).
3. **Why Early Expansion Is Not Enough:** Expanding at $27.3\%$ or $43.9\%$ of tokens saves only $12.4\%$ or $20.0\%$ of FLOPs relative to full-budget `Static 12L` (`1.729e17` FLOPs). To save **$22.7\%\text{--}34.1\%$ of total compute**, the model must remain small through **$50.0\%\text{--}75.1\%$ of tokens**—directly entering the linear LR cooldown regime where `G_stack + SPARKLING` and `G_zero` break down.

   - **Fixed-Batch (`accum=4`) at $65.9\%$ Tokens (`gs=628`, $K_{\text{rem}} = 325$ steps, 3 seeds):** Holding `accum=4` fixed at the default Stage-2 LR schedule (`1.0x`), all three 3-seed `HSDM-2D` variants beat `G_stack + SPARKLING Eq. 28 Fixed-Batch` (`1.001431 ± 0.002060` val / `1.000565 ± 0.002024` holdout, width cosine `0.7560`) and `SPARKLING Re-Warmup` (`1.002396 ± 0.001997`) on **all 3 seeds**: `HSDM-2D Stiefel` (`0.999243 ± 0.001951` val / `0.998373 ± 0.001955` holdout, cosine `0.5844`) wins by **`-0.002187 ± 0.000161` val BPB ($t = -23.54, p = 0.001801$, $95\%$ CI $[-0.002587, -0.001787]$)** vs. `Eq. 28` and **`-0.003152 ± 0.000047` ($t = -116.82, p = 0.000073$)** vs. `Re-Warmup`; zero-loss `HSDM-2D + Isometric PE + cos2` (`0.999272 ± 0.002076` val / `0.998448 ± 0.002032` holdout, $|\Delta\mathcal{L}| = 0.000069$ nats, cosine `0.5804`) wins by **`-0.002159 ± 0.000173` val BPB ($t = -21.58, p = 0.002141$, $95\%$ CI $[-0.002590, -0.001728]$)** vs. `Eq. 28` and **`-0.003124 ± 0.000098` ($t = -55.13, p = 0.000329$)** vs. `Re-Warmup`; and `HSDM-2D + Isometric PE + Dual-Clock` (`0.999327 ± 0.002113` val / `0.998481 ± 0.002052` holdout, $|\Delta\mathcal{L}| = 0.000072$ nats, cosine `0.4813`) wins by **`-0.002104 ± 0.000144` val BPB ($t = -25.33, p = 0.001555$, $95\%$ CI $[-0.002461, -0.001747]$)** and **`-0.002084 ± 0.000130` holdout BPB ($t = -27.78, p = 0.001293$)**. Comparing the three `HSDM-2D` variants at `accum=4`, no difference is resolved with three seeds between `dual_clock` and `cos2` on `isometric_pe` (paired $\Delta\text{Val} = +0.000055 \pm 0.000311$, $t = 0.307, p = 0.788$, $95\%$ CI $[-0.000717, +0.000827]$) or between `isometric_pe + dual_clock` and `stiefel_tangent + cos2` (paired $\Delta\text{Val} = +0.000083 \pm 0.000298$, $95\%$ CI $[-0.000657, +0.000824]$).
   - **Subdivided-Batch (`accum 4->2`) at $65.9\%$ Tokens (`gs=628`, $K_{\text{rem}} = 650$ steps, 3 seeds) and Consequence of Post-Hoc LR Tuning on `SPARKLING Eq. 28` (`s42`):** Applying Stage-2 micro-batch subdivision (`accum 4->2`) to both methods at `gs=628` improves `G_stack + SPARKLING Eq. 28` by `-0.006519 ± 0.000229` BPB (`1.001431 -> 0.994912 ± 0.001859`), so that at $65.9\%$ tokens it nearly matches `HSDM-2D Stiefel` (`0.994959 ± 0.001857`) and trails `HSDM-2D + Isometric PE` (`0.994692 ± 0.002152` val / `0.993828 ± 0.002128` holdout) by `-0.000220 ± 0.000349` val BPB (`-0.000248 ± 0.000357` holdout). 1. **Single-Factor OFAT Ablation of `modal_hadamard` (`+0.006043` BPB vs. `none`), Operator Comparison Against `block_diag` (`+0.001950` BPB at Matched $\alpha=0.25$), and Scope of `BD-ORB` (`1.001194`):** In Table 3B (`accum=4`), removing `modal_hadamard` while holding `stiefel_tangent + dstrh:25 + accum=4` fixed (`ablation_ofat_g628_acc4_nomodal_s42` vs. `hsdm_2d_8r2_to_12r4_g628_acc4_s42`) degrades validation BPB by **`+0.006043` BPB** (`0.997694 -> 1.003737`, holdout `+0.006152`) and leaves width cosine at `0.9160` vs. `0.5838`, directly confirming the shared-Gram differential-mode suppression predicted by Theorem 1B. Table 3B also compares `--dipolar modal_hadamard` against the implemented `--dipolar block_diag` optimizer branch (`train_growth.py:893-1051`). In 1D width-grown layers, `block_diag_width_muon_step_fused` computes polar factors $U_1, U_2$ on the two width branches, projects $U_2$ Frobenius-orthogonal to $U_1$ and renormalizes it to $\|U_1\|_F$, and constructs asymmetric updates $(U_1/\sqrt{2}, \gamma\widehat{U}_2^\perp/\sqrt{2})$ instead of the symmetric modal reconstruction $(U_+ \mp \gamma\widehat{U}_-^\perp)/2$; in 2D depth-and-width-grown layers, `block_diag_2d_muon_step_fused` retains depth Hadamard mixing $(g_{i,+} = (g_{p,i}+g_{d,i})/\sqrt{2}, g_{i,-} = (g_{d,i}-g_{p,i})/\sqrt{2})$ within each width half $i \in \{1,2\}$, projects $U_{2,+}$ Frobenius-orthogonal to $U_{1,+}$, and reconstructs $0.5(U_{1,+} \mp \gamma\widehat{U}_{1,-}^\perp)$ and $0.5(\widehat{U}_{2,+}^\perp \mp \gamma\widehat{U}_{2,-}^\perp)$ without $H_2$ mixing across width. Because `--dipolar block_diag` alters the update construction—including branch allocation, cross-width Frobenius projection, and reconstruction geometry—it tests replacing the implemented `modal_hadamard` branch with the implemented `block_diag` branch rather than isolating the $H_2$ width rotation alone. 2. **Working Hypothesis and Qualifications on Stage-2 Micro-Batch Subdivision (`accum 4->2`):** In each Muon step on $W_k \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ ($r = \min(m, n)$), 5-step Polar Express maps singular values $s_j \in (0, 1/1.02]$ to $f_5(s_j) \le f_{5,\max} \approx 1.198$ ($\|U_{\text{PE},k}\|_F \le f_{5,\max}\sqrt{r}$), NorMuon preserves $\|\tilde{U}_k\|_F = \|U_{\text{PE},k}\|_F$, and the parameter update with aspect-ratio factor $\alpha_{m,n} = \sqrt{\max(1, m/n)}$ and cautious weight decay $\lambda_k$ satisfies the Frobenius triangle inequality $\|W_{k+1} - W_k\|_F \le \alpha_{m,n}\eta_k\big(f_{5,\max}\sqrt{r} + \lambda_k \|W_k \odot \mathbb{I}[\tilde{U}_k \odot W_k \ge 0]\|_F\big)$. Halving `accum` (`4->2`) at `gs=628` doubles the remaining Stage-2 step count ($K_{\text{rem}} = 325 \to 650$) along the token-indexed cooldown schedule $\eta(p)$, which approximately doubles the **base-schedule Stage-2 LR sum** $\Sigma_{\eta,\text{base}} = \sum_{k=1}^{K_{\text{rem}}} (\eta_{\text{base},k}/\eta_0)$ from $112.47$ ($1.0\Lambda$) to $224.60$ ($2.0\Lambda$), while for `SPARKLING Eq. 28`'s coded $\tau_w = 60$-step new-parameter warmup (`train_growth.py:2474-2492`), the **new-parameter Stage-2 LR sum** $\Sigma_{\eta,\text{new}} = \sum_{k=1}^{K_{\text{rem}}} (\eta_{\text{new},k}/\eta_0)$ increases from $166.81$ (`325 @ 1.0x`) to $312.58$ (`650 @ 1.0x`, vs. $333.63$ for `325 @ 2.0x`). Because cautious weight decay and the gradient trajectory depend on the iterate path and step size (and per-step realized update norms $\|W_{k+1} - W_k\|_F$ were not directly logged), and because halving `accum` simultaneously alters step count, integrated LR sum, and micro-batch gradient noise [12], we treat this norm-budget explanation as a **working hypothesis**. **Empirical Comparison of Stage-1 Trunk Capacity Across Expansion Ratios ($4\text{L}\to 8\text{L}$ vs. $(8\text{L},r=2)\to(12\text{L},r=4)$):**
At $65.1\%$ tokens (`gs=620`), `HSDM-w80` enables $4\text{L}\to 8\text{L}$ to beat $G_{\text{stack}}$ on all 3 seeds (`-0.001703 ± 0.000487` val BPB, $t = -6.05, p = 0.0262, 95\%$ CI $[-0.002913, -0.000493]$; `-0.001695 ± 0.000389` holdout BPB, $t = -7.56, p = 0.0171, 95\%$ CI $[-0.002660, -0.000730]$) and beat `Static 8L Iso-FLOPs` on `3/3` seeds (`-0.002527 ± 0.002153` val BPB, $95\%$ CI $[-0.007876, +0.002822]$). However, when expansion is delayed to $75.1\%$ of tokens (`gs=716`, leaving $24.8\%$ of the post-peak LR integral) or when the Stage-1 trunk is simultaneously narrowed and shallowed to $(4\text{L},r=2)$ ($0.4474\times$ FLOPs/tok of `Static 8L`), the grown 8L model no longer overtakes `Static 8L Iso-FLOPs` (`1.026611` vs. `1.023255` at $75.1\%$; `1.028019` vs. `1.026192` on $(4\text{L},r=2)\to(8\text{L},r=4)$). By contrast, on Track 2 ($(8\text{L},r=2)\to(12\text{L},r=4)$), where the Stage-1 trunk retains $8$ layers ($2\times\text{SSSL}$ cycles and $0.5455\times$ the per-step FLOPs of `Static 12L`, a $1.5\times$ depth expansion rather than $2\times$), `HSDM-2D` continues to beat `Static 12L Iso-FLOPs` even at **$75.1\%$ tokens (`1.000220` vs. `1.002679`)**. Consistent with `holonomy_weaver`'s (`general_pretraining/papers/23/v2` [14]) finding in looped 4-layer transformers that partial-depth recurrence does not decompose a full 4-layer pass at equal FLOPs, we treat this contrast between a 4-layer Stage-1 trunk ($2\times$ depth jump) and an 8-layer Stage-1 trunk ($1.5\times$ depth jump) as an empirical scope condition on how small the Stage-1 trunk can be held through $75\%$ of pretraining.

### 5.3 Scope Control Under Whole-Run Small-Batch Muon (`accum=2, WD=0.10`)
Because our late-expansion `HSDM-2D` schedule in Section 5.1 subdivides micro-batch accumulation in Stage 2 (`accum 4->2`), we also evaluate the stronger whole-run small-batch static baseline (`--stage1-accum 2 --stage2-accum 2 --wd-scale 0.5`, i.e., `262,144` tokens/step and per-token decay-matched `WD=0.10` from Step 0) motivated by `claude_1`'s split-batch Muon scaling analysis [11, 12] across three seeds (`42, 137, 271`; complete per-run configurations in Appendix A.3, Table A2). Training from Step 0 at `accum=2, WD=0.10` doubles total optimizer updates and improves 3-seed `Static 8L` (`1,906` steps, `1.0000x` FLOPs) from `0.995376 ± 0.001542` to **`0.984930 ± 0.000406` val BPB** (`0.984228 ± 0.000356` holdout) and 3-seed `Static 12L Iso-FLOPs` (`1,336` steps, `1.0145x` FLOPs) from `0.998472 ± 0.001947` to **`0.983534 ± 0.000749` val BPB** (`0.982629 ± 0.000678` holdout; on `s42`, scaling `WD=0.20 -> 0.10` accounts for `-0.001101` BPB, `0.984711 -> 0.983610`). Under this whole-run `accum=2, WD=0.10` regime, expanding late in warmdown (`gs=1256`, $65.9\%$ tokens, `hsdm_2d_8r2_to_12r4_g628_isometric_pe_allacc2_wd05_s42`) reaches `0.987425` val BPB (`0.986673` holdout) on `s42`, trailing whole-run `accum=2` `Static 12L Iso-FLOPs` (`0.983610` by `+0.003815` BPB) and `Static 8L` (`0.985389` by `+0.002036` BPB). Thus, our late-expansion gains in Section 5.1 hold relative to matched `accum=4` and `accum 4->2` schedules, rather than outperforming a static model that already uses `accum=2, WD=0.10` throughout the entire run.

## 7. Limitations, Scope Conditions, and Reproducibility

1. **Empirical Stage-1 Trunk Capacity Horizon, Training- vs. Inference-FLOPs Trade-Off, and Whole-Run Small-Batch Boundary:** As established in Section 5.2, a 4-layer Stage-1 trunk ($1\times\text{SSSL}$, $2\times$ depth expansion to 8L) hits an empirical capacity horizon when held past $65.1\%$ of tokens (`gs=716`, $75.1\%$), whereas an 8-layer $r=2$ trunk ($2\times\text{SSSL}$, $1.5\times$ depth and $2\times$ MLP expansion to $12\text{L}, r=4$) continues to beat matched-compute `Static 12L Iso-FLOPs` (`1.001602 ± 0.001635` vs. `1.003565 ± 0.002091`) and batch-matched `G_stack + SPARKLING Eq. 28` (`1.004676 ± 0.002222`, $p = 0.0126$) even at $75.1\%$ tokens ($34.1\%$ FLOP savings). Furthermore, as shown in Section 5.3 and Appendix A.1, our late-expansion advantages hold under the reference `accum=4` and `accum 4->2` schedules at default Stage-2 learning rates: post-hoc Stage-2 LR tuning on `SPARKLING Eq. 28` narrows or slightly reverses the single-seed gap at `gs=628`, and training static models from Step 0 at `accum=2, WD=0.10` (`0.984930 ± 0.000406` on `Static 8L`; `0.983534 ± 0.000749` on `Static 12L Iso-FLOPs`) outperforms late-cooldown expansion (`gs=1256`, `0.987425` on `s42`).
2. **Compilation Overhead:** Each architectural transition triggers a one-time `torch.compile` trace (`~18–44s` for 2-stage runs). Even including compilation, `HSDM-2D` at $65.9\%$ tokens reduces total wall-clock time from `1745s` (`Static 12L Full`) to `1216–1229s` (**$-29.6\%$ to $-30.3\%$ total elapsed time**).
3. **Complete Reproducibility Package (`supplement.zip`):** The attached supplement (`< 2 MB` compressed and uncompressed, `<= 200` archive entries) contains `experiments/train_growth.py`, `experiments/prepare.py`, `experiments/attention_backend.py`, `reproduce_all.sh`, `experiments/run_late_growth_batch{1,2,3}.sh`, `experiments/run_referee_controls.sh`, `experiments/run_conjecture_tests.sh`, `experiments/run_v2_matched_batch_seeds.sh`, `experiments/run_phase3_grid_and_dualclock.sh`, `experiments/run_phase4_bdorb_and_acc4_seeds.sh`, `experiments/run_phase5_vertex_and_amg.sh`, `experiments/run_phase6_deconfounded_controls.sh`, `experiments/run_phase7a_opposing_school_allacc2.sh`, `experiments/run_phase7b_tau30_controls.sh`, `experiments/run_phase8_allacc2_static_seeds.sh`, `generate_paper_assets.py`, `all_runs_summary.csv`, `all_runs_summary.json`, all 4 figures, the unified `experiments/runs_manifest.json` uniformly containing all `222` full `result.json` certificates (with `mitosis_fidelity` and `holdout_bpb`), and compacted `diagnostics.jsonl` trajectories so `python generate_paper_assets.py` regenerates all figures and tables standalone from the unzipped archive.
