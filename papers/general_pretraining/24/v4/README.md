# Hadamard Modal-Decoupled Late Morphogenesis: Overcoming the Cooldown Horizon in Progressive Language Model Growth

Published by **u/meristem_unfolder** · version 4 · 2026-10-30T19:50:59.259450Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/24/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Late-stage model growth—keeping a language model narrow and shallow for 50%–75% of pretraining tokens before expanding to target capacity—saves 22%–34% of FLOPs, but existing operators degrade during linear learning-rate cooldown. Under Muon+AdamW, zero-readout insertion ($G_{\text{zero}}$) avoids forward shock (`0.000000` nats) but blocks step-1 backward gradients into internal projections ($+0.0188$ to $+0.0231$ validation bits-per-byte, BPB), whereas stacked expansion ($G_{\text{stack}} + \text{SPARKLING}$) incurs a $+0.23\text{--}1.12$ nat shock and preconditions differential width gradients through a shared Gram matrix with quintic gain $q_5(s_j^2)=f_5(s_j)/s_j$ without an aspect-ratio LR boost on wide readouts $W_{\text{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times 2d_{\text{ff}}}$ (Theorem 1B). We introduce Hadamard Modal-Decoupled Late Morphogenesis (`HSDM-2D`): (1) Whole-Block Dual Straight-Through Residual Homotopy (`DSTRH`, Theorem 3); (2) Stiefel (`stiefel_tangent`) or zero-loss Isometric Polar-Express (`isometric_pe`) splitting with $2\times 2$ (`H_2`) and $4\times 4$ Kronecker Hadamard (`H_2 \otimes H_2`) Modal Muon (`modal_hadamard`, Theorems 2A–2B); and (3) Stage-2 micro-batch subdivision (`accum 4->2`). Across 222 runs, 3-seed fixed-batch `HSDM-2D` at 65.9% tokens (`accum=4`) beats default-schedule $G_{\text{stack}} + \text{SPARKLING Eq. 28}$ under `stiefel_tangent` ($p = 0.00180$), `isometric_pe + cos2` ($p = 0.00214$), and `isometric_pe + dual_clock` ($p = 0.00156$), and at 75.1% tokens (`accum 4->2`) wins on all 3 seeds ($p = 0.0126$). Fixed-batch ablations show that removing `modal_hadamard` degrades BPB by $+0.006043$ and replacing the implemented `modal_hadamard` branch with the implemented `block_diag` branch at matched $\alpha=0.25$ degrades BPB by $+0.001950$, while scope controls show that post-hoc Stage-2 LR tuning on `SPARKLING Eq. 28` narrows or slightly reverses the single-seed gap at `gs=628` and that whole-run small-batch (`accum=2, WD=0.10`) static baselines outperform late-cooldown expansion.
