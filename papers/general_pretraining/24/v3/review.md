# Accept

This revision supplies limited supporting evidence and useful corrections to the accepted late-growth contribution. The additional learning-rate sweep, fixed-batch Isometric PE comparisons, and bounded alternative-operator control address the existing explanation rather than establish a separate substantive contribution. Separating Tables 3B and 3C and placing the detailed sensitivity grid and preliminary three-stage schedule in Appendix A preserves the main argument. These closely related diagnostics belong with this paper; they do not warrant separate submissions.

The new three-seed fixed-batch comparison is supported by the retained records. Independently recomputing Isometric PE plus `dual_clock` minus SPARKLING Eq. 28 gives $-0.002104\pm0.000144$ validation BPB, with paired $p=0.001555$ and $95\%$ confidence interval $[-0.002461,-0.001747]$. The dispersion is the sample standard deviation of paired differences. The summary endpoints agree with the manifest, and the prior records retain their existing fields and values. Verification here comprised record reconciliation, independent scalar/statistical calculations, and inspection of changed code; I did not reproduce GPU training.

The revised learning-rate discussion is appropriately cautious. The coded schedule gives base LR sums of $112.47$ and $224.60$ for 325 and 650 updates, and new-parameter sums of $166.81$ and $312.58$, respectively. Doubling coarse-step LR therefore approximately matches the fine-step base sum but does not match its new-parameter sum. The explicit qualifications about weight decay, trajectory dependence, gradient noise, and unequal static-versus-growth token horizons are scientifically necessary and consistent with the batch-size analysis in [12]. Likewise, the new BD-ORB result supports only the stated conclusion about one bundled alternative; it does not identify Hadamard mixing as the cause of that alternative's deficit.

Several local clarifications would improve the revision:

- **Carry the tuned-baseline consequence into the main interpretation.** Table A1's best tested SPARKLING settings reach $0.997205$ BPB at fixed batch and $0.992634$ with subdivision. On the same seed, these are slightly below the reported HSDM anchor values of $0.997694$ and $0.992815$. This does not overturn the three-seed comparisons at the specified schedules, but it prevents interpreting them as superiority over comparably optimized baselines. Describe the grid's endpoints as “best tested settings,” rather than established optima. Their validation difference is approximately $-0.004572$ BPB; it remains a single-seed descriptive contrast, not an isolated estimate of the effect of update count.

- **Replace the new equivalence wording.** Contribution 3 says the two HSDM variants perform equivalently within $\pm0.000083$ BPB. That number is the observed mean difference, not an equivalence margin supported by uncertainty. The correctly reported paired interval in Appendix A.2 is $[-0.000657,+0.000824]$. State that no difference is resolved with three seeds and give that interval. The comparison also changes both initialization and gain schedule, so it cannot isolate schedule sensitivity by itself.

- **Complete the asymptotic qualification in Theorem 1B.** The leading differential component is $q_5(s_j^2)X_{0,-}v_j$, not universally a component of amplitude $\epsilon q_5(s_j^2)$. Retain the actual directional amplitude, and make the below-unity statement explicitly leading-order or require a margin controlling the remainder. The added distinction between total Frobenius-norm preservation and preservation of individual components is helpful.

- **Align the new configuration descriptions with the artifacts.** Table 3B labels the fixed-batch Isometric PE run with $\alpha=0.5$, whereas its manifest and launcher specify $0.25$. Appendix A.2's three-stage run uses modal gain $\gamma_0=0.7$, rather than the standard HSDM value $2.0$; disclose this when describing that exploration. Distinguish planned launcher arms from completed retained runs: the phase-4 script includes two additional default-schedule Isometric PE seeds absent from the 207-run manifest.

The central contribution remains identifiable and supported within its accepted scope. Further presentation improvements should consolidate repeated numerical comparisons in the introduction and abstract, retaining the main schedule-dependent findings there and leaving detailed run identifiers in the tables and supplement.
