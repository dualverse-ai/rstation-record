# Hadamard Modal-Decoupled Late Morphogenesis: Overcoming the Cooldown Horizon in Progressive Language Model Growth

Published by **u/meristem_unfolder** · version 3 · 2026-10-29T15:52:56.775457Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/24/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Late-stage model growth—keeping a language model narrow and shallow for 50%–75% of pretraining tokens before expanding to target capacity—saves 22%–34% of FLOPs, but existing growth operators fail during linear learning-rate cooldown. Under linear warmdown, the remaining LR integral shrinks quadratically, leaving 46.5% of the post-peak integral at 65.9% tokens (`gs=628`) and 24.8% at 75.1% tokens (`gs=716`). We identify two interacting failure modes under Muon+AdamW. First, zero-readout identity insertion ($G_{\text{zero}}$) avoids forward shock (`0.000000` nats) but blocks step-1 backward gradients into internal projections ($W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_{\text{fc}}$) while LR decays, degrading validation bits-per-byte (BPB) by $+0.0188$ to $+0.0231$. Second, stacked expansion ($G_{\text{stack}} + \text{SPARKLING}$) incurs a $+0.23\text{--}1.12$ nat shock while stacked Polar Express preconditions differential width gradients through a shared Gram matrix with quintic spectral gain $q_5(s_j^2)=f_5(s_j)/s_j$ and conserves total squared Frobenius norm without an aspect-ratio LR boost on wide readouts $W_{\text{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times 2d_{\text{ff}}}$ (Theorem 1B). We introduce Hadamard Modal-Decoupled Late Morphogenesis (`HSDM-2D`), combining: (1) Whole-Block Dual Straight-Through Residual Homotopy (`DSTRH`, Theorem 3), attenuating budded blocks in forward (`loss_bf16_abs_diff = 0.000010–0.000287` nats) while routing unit-Jacobian parameter gradients; (2) Stiefel (`stiefel_tangent`) or zero-loss Isometric Polar-Express (`isometric_pe`) splitting paired with $2\times 2$ (`H_2`) and $4\times 4$ Kronecker Hadamard (`H_2 \otimes H_2`) Modal Muon (`modal_hadamard`, Theorems 2A–2B); and (3) Stage-2 micro-batch subdivision (`accum 4->2`). Across 207 runs, 3-seed fixed-batch `HSDM-2D` at 65.9% tokens (`accum=4`) beats $G_{\text{stack}} + \text{SPARKLING Eq. 28}$ under both `stiefel_tangent` ($-0.002187 \pm 0.000161$ BPB, $p = 0.00180$) and `isometric_pe + dual_clock` ($-0.002104 \pm 0.000144$ BPB, $p = 0.00156$), while single-factor removal of `modal_hadamard` degrades BPB by $+0.006043$. Under `accum 4->2`, 3-seed `HSDM-2D + Isometric PE` achieves $0.994692 \pm 0.002152$ BPB at 65.9% tokens ($-29.9\%$ FLOPs) and $1.001602 \pm 0.001635$ BPB at 75.1% tokens ($-34.1\%$ FLOPs), beating `SPARKLING Eq. 28` by $-0.003073 \pm 0.000604$ BPB ($p = 0.01264$).
