# TorchInductor Slice-View Storage Pinning, Factorial Separation of Pre-Sublayer vs. Post-QK-Norm Scaling, and Common-Backbone Pre-Polar Conditioning Diagnostics in Zero-Warmup Pre-Norm Transformers

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 2 · 2026-10-17T04:59:20.282598Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/15/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In parameter-free RMSNorm (`weight=None`) Pre-Norm Transformers trained at zero warmup (`WARMUP_RATIO = 0.0`, `MATRIX_LR = 0.04`) with `MuonAdamW` (`50.3M` Depth-8 and `26.35M` Depth-6 GPT, 953 updates = 499.6M ClimbMix tokens), concurrent paper `general_pretraining_12/v1` established 2-pass sequential `Homotopy-BiEq` (α_t = \min(t/80,1)^2), momentum cooldown, and vocabulary whitening. Across 52 runs (`51` hash-verified workspace runs + `reference_seed42`, `41/52` with holdouts), we report three non-overlapping diagnostic findings. First, in the measured PyTorch `2.9.1+cu126` / `TorchInductor` graph, naive unfused pre-sublayer scaling retains `97` vs. `93` full `(B,T,d)` `bfloat16` storages (`+256.0 MiB` at `B=32`, `+512.0 MiB` at `B=64`) because `ve_gate(x[..., :32])` aliases the unscaled `(B,T,d)` allocation on `L/2` layers; materializing `ve_gate(x[..., :32].contiguous())` (`exp67`) has `0.0` FP32/BF16 numerical difference while restoring `93` `(B,T,d)` storages (`+16.0/+32.0 MiB`, `0.993619` dev / `0.992905` holdout BPB), whereas online weight-column fusion ($W \odot \mathbf{1}\alpha^\top$, `exp17: 0.993561`) eliminates extra activation buffers (`+0.08 MiB`) and agrees to machine tolerance in FP32 (`4.14e-7` grad diff) but differs in single-step BF16 rounding (`0.0055` grad diff). Second, across Linear (`exp07, exp03, exp05`) and matched-LR Stopped-Exponential (`exp66, exp64, exp17`) $2 \times 2$ factorials on Seed 47, post-QK-norm scaling (`+2,048` scalars) yields `-0.001563` to `-0.002300` BPB while pre-sublayer gains (`+8,192` scalars) yield `-0.000572` to `-0.000580` BPB; we prove directly on discrete positions ($\det(R(2\theta_c)-I_2)=4\sin^2(\theta_c)>0$) that RoPE pair tying is necessary and sufficient for unit-shift stationarity, while 3-seed comparisons against `4,096`-scalar untied `nGPT` (`-0.000146 ± 0.000213` BPB) are unresolved, making the `50%` QK parameter reduction the defensible advantage. Third, in common-backbone 3-seed pre-polar adaptations, `Zeta` (`0.994766 ± 0.001892` BPB, `+96 MiB`) and `Turbo-Muon` (`0.993090 ± 0.000621` BPB) trail `Homotopy-BiEq`, while a 3-seed $2 \times 2$ matrix-scope factorial shows that rectangular MLP/gate matrices (`0.992488 ± 0.000401` BPB) beat square attention matrices (`0.992950 ± 0.000251` BPB) on every seed (`-0.000462 ± 0.000156` BPB) with a `+0.000301 ± 0.000096` BPB sub-additive interaction.
