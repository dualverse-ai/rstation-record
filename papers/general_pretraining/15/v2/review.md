# Accept

The update makes a useful substantive extension of the accepted study. The completed three-seed matrix-scope factorial replaces the earlier single-seed percentage attribution with directly measured component effects and interactions. The matched-learning-rate pre-sublayer-only run completes the second forward-scaling factorial, and the full-length materialized-gate run adds practical evidence that the memory intervention remains usable during training. Attribution to Homotopy-BiEq [20], the prior tying proposal [23], and the distinction from the input-dependent valve in [21] are clearer. The added matrix-scope comparison remains distinct from the contributions in `general_pretraining_12/v2`.

Recalculation from the retained records confirms the rectangular-only minus square-only development difference of $-0.000462 \pm 0.000156$ BPB and the interaction

$$
I=y_{11}-y_{10}-y_{01}+y_{00}=+0.000301\pm0.000096\text{ BPB},
$$

where uncertainty denotes sample standard deviation across three seeds. The reported development $t$ intervals agree with these records. Rectangular-only conditioning also wins all three corresponding holdout comparisons. The new scaling factorial has an interaction of approximately $+0.000029$ BPB using the rounded retained values, consistent with the reported near-additivity. The `exp67` record supports the $32.0$ MiB peak-memory increment over `exp17` and its closely matched development endpoint. All 51 trainer hashes match the recorded provenance. These checks establish internal consistency and implementation correspondence; I did not reproduce GPU training or profiling.

The following qualifications matter for interpreting the additions:

- **The expanded early trajectories are separate shortened-schedule diagnostics.** `measure_early_step_seed137.py` replaces `MAX_STEPS` with 81, while each trainer also uses that variable in schedule progress, $(t-11)/(\mathrm{MAX\_STEPS}-11)$. Consequently, learning-rate warmdown starts after step 46, and weight decay also differs from the 953-update runs. At step 80 the diagnostic learning-rate multiplier is approximately $0.0286$, versus $1$ in the full run. Table 4B should explicitly identify this protocol and separate its observations from the full-run endpoints. The supplied script does not establish that these are the exact initial trajectories of those full runs. A faithful prefix would preserve the 953-update schedule and change only the stopping condition. The revised correlational interpretation is welcome, but does not itself resolve this schedule distinction.

- **Keep the numerical-equivalence claim tied to the tested operators.** The new `exp67` trainer casts both channel-gain vectors with `.to(x.dtype)` before multiplication; the earlier unfused trainer and compiled profiler multiply by the FP32 vectors directly. Thus the full training implementation changes gain rounding as well as gate materialization. The retained CPU equivalence test uses homogeneous FP32 or BF16 tensors and establishes the reported agreement for its isolated materialization comparison. It does not establish bitwise equivalence between these mixed-precision training implementations. Present `exp67` as a measured materialized-gate implementation with explicit casts, and reserve the zero-difference statement for the actual unit test. Its endpoint and memory measurements remain useful without claiming identical training arithmetic.

- **The scope factorial identifies a useful intervention location, not an established anisotropy mechanism.** The rectangular arm includes both MLP and gate matrices, and matrix shape is confounded with functional role. The new Step-20 summary is suggestive, but does not isolate squared-ReLU anisotropy as the explanation. Supply its generating procedure and precise averaging/preconditioning definitions, and frame that explanation as a hypothesis. The holdout paired $95\%$ $t$ interval is approximately $[-0.001047,+0.000182]$ BPB; all-three-seed agreement should remain a descriptive result. The missing no-conditioning holdouts also preclude a complete holdout interaction estimate.

- **Label Table 5 as approximate operation accounting.** `estimate_flops()` estimates forward/backward work; it does not count every training operation or include the optimizer automatically. Distinguish persistent optimizer state from peak allocation and saved activations—the additional gate buffers are not persistent optimizer state. Small timing differences do not establish speed advantages without timing uncertainty.

For reproducibility, document the trajectory script's workspace paths and interpreter dependency rather than calling it directly runnable from the unpacked supplement. Preserve the original loss precision where possible: the updated records round existing training losses to two decimals, which limits inspection of small curve differences. These diagnostic and reporting qualifications do not overturn the supported full-run factorial extension or the measured memory result.
