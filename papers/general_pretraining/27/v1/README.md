# Context-Demodulated Multi-Pathway Phrase Memory and Ridge-Regularized L2 Readout Calibration for Low-Bit N-Gram Language Models

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T03:59:27.487038Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/27/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Hashed N-gram embedding tables expand pretraining capacity at O(1) lookup cost, yet narrow-slice tables suffer from severe hash collisions, static Layer-0-only injection, and 3.35-4.90 mBPB loss under 2-bit post-training quantization. Across 385 ClimbMix-400B runs at depths L in {6, 8} (widths d in {384, 512}, V=8192), we study Context-Demodulated Multi-Pathway Phrase Memory (CoDeM-Ngram) and Multi-Readout Ridge-Regularized L2 Calibration. CoDeM-Ngram modulates the raw 4-slice lookup with a gate driven by a channel-rolled 3-token unigram summary, applies a residual causal depthwise Conv1d(k=4), projects to Layer 0 via a post-gated Muon-trained matrix W_proj, and routes the pre-projection demodulated phrase vector into alternating attention Value-Embedding (VE) skips via Muon-trained adapters W_pve^(l) at zero extra lookups or table bytes. At exact memory-parameter parity (57.93M params at L=6; 77.24M at L=8), full-horizon 953-step (499.65M-token) CoDeM-Ngram improves validation BPB over OE-Ember by -6.273 mBPB at L=8 (0.970154 vs. 0.976427; -6.378 mBPB on an untouched 20.97M-token confirmation split) and -7.902 mBPB at L=6, while beating equal-time OE-Ember (-2.554 mBPB at L=8; -3.183 mBPB at L=6), parameter-matched FactorEngram-style basis gating (-2.964 and -3.438 mBPB), and gate-detachment-matched Unigram-VE (-1.619 and -2.316 mBPB). Finally, folding a closed-form L2 calibration matrix into W_proj and {W_pve^(l)} improves 2-bit BPB by -0.874 to -0.880 mBPB, enabling 2-bit CoDeM-Ngram (6.52-6.73x module compression) to beat 16-bit OE-Ember by -3.14 to -3.86 mBPB.
