# Accept

The paper establishes a useful, bounded improvement to hashed n-gram memory in the supplied pretraining setting. Its strongest contribution is the controlled evidence for combining context-dependent input injection with reuse of the resulting phrase features in attention-value pathways. The calibration study adds a practical application of ordinary ridge regression to these shared features. Acceptance rests on these empirical findings, rather than a new general theory of collision removal or quantization.

The evidence goes beyond an isolated favorable run. At both backbone sizes, the paper includes parameter-matched comparisons, three training seeds, full-horizon evaluation, learning curves, and longer-training OE controls. Independently recomputing differences from the retained V9 records gives improvements over the longer-training OE control of $-2.957$ mBPB at $L=6$ and $-2.538$ mBPB at $L=8$, with paired 95% Student-$t$ intervals $[-3.158,-2.755]$ and $[-3.478,-1.598]$. Against the gate-detachment-matched unigram pathway, the corresponding improvements are $-2.090$ and $-1.603$ mBPB. These comparisons substantively connect the contribution to the task's objective of improving prediction for comparable resources.

The main forward computation is understandable from Eqs. (7)–(11), including the important distinction between the pre-projection phrase features and the post-gated Layer-0 output. The full-space ridge solution and its folding into multiple linear readouts are mathematically sound. The retained records support the reported additional 2-bit calibration gains of approximately $0.874$ and $0.880$ mBPB. My verification consisted of code inspection, independent arithmetic checks on retained records, and literature comparison; I did not reproduce GPU training or checkpoint evaluation.

The following qualifications and corrections should accompany the contribution:

1. **Keep the novelty and competitor claims precise.** The relevant rStation papers [10]–[13] already establish low-bit table capacity, low-state writes, shared value embeddings, and unigram/n-gram routing. FactorEngram [6] already establishes componentwise contextual gating of retrieved coefficients and causal convolution. The present combination and its controlled measurements provide an incremental contribution beyond those results. However, `FE_basis_gate_isoparam` is a simplified, inspired comparator, not a reproduction of FactorEngram: the published method normalizes a projected backbone query, uses the reconstruction dictionary itself for gating, includes coefficient sparsity regularization, and studies internal insertion sites. The independent single-token gate used here omits those features. The reported advantage therefore establishes superiority to the specified control, not to the complete published method. Likewise, the joint change from that control to `NG6` does not separately identify the effects of channel rolls, additional context, and the second gate.

2. **Add the closest broader precedents.** MoVE [14] already shares memory across attention-value pathways with contextual routing; QwT [15] already uses closed-form linear regression to compensate quantization error. Neither establishes this paper's exact shared, demodulated n-gram architecture or its measured calibration decomposition, but both materially inform the attribution. The novelty of Section 2.4 is the application and simultaneous folding into existing readouts, not least-squares quantization compensation itself. References [6] and these precedents predate the submission well beyond the concurrency window.

3. **Correct the claimed “exact executable specification.”** Section 2.3 calls the matrix optimizer “Pre-Polar Equilibrated Muon,” but `ngram_bench.py:muon_step_fused` performs global normalization before Polar Express and adaptive rescaling afterward; it does not implement the pre-polar row/column equilibration described in [13]. The runners also ramp momentum from $0.85$ to $0.95$. The convolution inherits a separate AdamW group with $\beta=(0.9,0.95)$ and $\epsilon=10^{-8}$ from `NextGenGPT.setup_optimizer`, rather than the stated `x0_params` assignment and $\beta=(0.96,0.95)$. Section 2.4 should specify one codebook per table fitted to pooled normalized entries, with a sample cap of 500,000 and initialization by equally spaced points in $[-1.5,1.5]$; the code does not initialize at empirical quantiles or fit separate codebooks per row. These are localized specification corrections to the inspectable implementation underlying the comparisons.

4. **Separate evidence for efficacy from evidence for mechanism.** Shrinking the modulus changes capacity and predictor quality as well as collision density. Common-reference purity bins ensure comparable evaluation populations, but they do not measure each differently hashed model's own collision severity. The findings are consistent with collision mitigation and contextual fallback, as the limitations acknowledge; they do not uniquely establish demodulation as the cause. Similarly, average row-exposure ratios alone do not establish why phrase routing overtakes unigram routing. Keep that explanation explicitly hypothetical.

5. **Clarify confirmation and reproducibility.** Document deduplication establishes source separation, not independence from model selection. The same `fresh_rg42` split appears in both V8 and V9, so explain which decisions were frozen before its first use. Also, the released `heldout_dedup` iterator traverses all row groups, rather than explicitly enforcing the stated `0..41` restriction; record the actual consumed document range to substantiate that description. The supplement depends on `baseline/prepare.py` and `baseline/attention_backend.py`, so replace the self-contained claim with concrete setup instructions connecting these imports to the supplied task archive. Three-seed intervals should be described as exploratory, especially after extensive configuration screening.

Clarity is adequate for acceptance: the research question, computation, decisive comparisons, and limitations are identifiable. Nevertheless, the main text would be substantially easier to read with short descriptive arm names, one primary result table, and version-specific repetitions moved to an appendix. The abstract is informative but overcrowded. Preserve the distinction between module and whole-model compression, and between simulated packed storage and measured execution. The evidence supports two small backbone sizes on one corpus and recipe; it does not establish broader backbone transfer or native low-bit speedups. The disclosed microbatch change and bridge comparison also make the $L=8$ results an adaptation of the prescribed protocol, rather than a strict microbatch-64 comparison.
