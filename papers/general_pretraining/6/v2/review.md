# Accept

Version 5 makes a substantive and successful correction to the previous version's equal-byte accounting. The former mixed a time-matched table gain with a same-update dense step. The new analysis trains measured no-table intermediates for exactly the OE arm's updates and defines, consistently, $\mathrm{margin}=\Delta\mathrm{step}-G$. The retained decomposition is internally exact for every row. Its three mean validation margins are approximately $+0.00397$, $-0.00949$, and $-0.01452$ BPB for d6+OE/d8, d8+OE/d10, and d10+OE/d12, respectively; held-out margins agree within $0.0002$ BPB. The preregistered attribution test is met: the dense-step drop is $0.0292$ versus a $0.0107$ drop in $G$, so the claim that the sign change is driven mainly by the dense step is justified within this matching convention.

The new d8/d10 and d10/d12 equal-byte comparisons add useful scale evidence. Both use two seeds, the measured gaps are reproduced on the untouched shard, and the d8/d10 times are essentially equal. The d10/d12 ratios (1.006 and 1.013) are inside the stated preregistered 3% time gate. The stronger RMS-scale/Lloyd-Max post-training codec is a valuable control: it reduces the d8 int8-budget loss to a seed-sensitive tie while improving dense d8 more at low bytes, so it does not create an unreported OE win. These additions strengthen the paper's resource-allocation contribution without replacing it by a broad claim that tables scale better than depth.

The update also improves attribution and positioning. The new Tensorizing Engram and tokenizer-agnostic Engram references distinguish factorisation or hashing changes from the paper's row/bit/depth budget measurements. The supplement provides the prediction records, intermediate runs, scoring files, data-root check, codec outputs and held-out scores needed to inspect the new claims. The d10/d12 forecast is explicitly labelled heuristic and the raw measured gap is used for the conclusion, which is appropriate.

The conclusions remain bounded. Equal-time comparisons use a fixed total batch but different microbatches (32 for the OE/d10 arms and 16 for dense d12), and the longer arms read fresh deduplicated shards after the common 953-update prefix. Thus they are valid fixed-time observations under the reported harness, not a general architecture-only scaling law. The d6/d8 comparison still reuses dense seeds 44 and 42 against OE seeds 44 and 45, while the newer larger pairs use matching seed labels. Two seeds and the one-degree-of-freedom spread do not provide population confidence intervals. The d12 transfer guard remains failed and appropriately secondary; the broader result is still limited to this corpus, optimizer and relatively small set of model scales. Stored bytes remain estimates from post-training codecs, with no packed implementation, native low-bit execution, GPTQ-class quantizer or QAT result.

One minor presentation fix is warranted before publication: the new decomposition lines beginning “margin = OE...” and “Every term...” contain bare mathematical expressions. Put the identities and definitions in `$...$` or display `$$...$$` delimiters, and state in the abstract that the larger-scale “equal time” comparisons use the stated tolerance band. These are clarity improvements and do not affect the scientific validity of the update.
