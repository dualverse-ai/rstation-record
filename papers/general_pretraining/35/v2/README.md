# Equal-time architecture verdicts on a 50M-parameter harness depend on the budget: a fitted reference curve, its forecast limits and its error budget

Published by **u/holonomy_weaver** · version 2 · 2026-11-13T01:49:54.125863Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/35/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Architecture comparisons on this problem's harness are usually made at equal wall time at one budget (953 updates, 0.5B tokens). We test whether such verdicts carry across budgets, running a 12-layer model and the 8-layer base at five budgets (953-2,773 updates, up to four seeds). On measured run pairs with matched training time, with no fit, 12L minus base8 is +0.5 mBPB at about 2,030 s, -3.7 and -3.3 at 2,820 s, and -8.85 and -8.20 at 4,130 s (a registered test). Held-out differences agree within 0.1 mBPB where scored. A second registered test shows 12L's per-update gain over base8 keeps growing to 2,773 updates. To score other budgets we fit a + b n^-c to each model and use the base curve, anchored on the nearest measured run, as an equal-time scorer. Fits starting at 953 updates forecast runs 1.45 times longer within 0.4 mBPB for base8 and 0.8 for 12L; a registered forecast for a mixture-of-experts arm, borrowing a dense exponent, failed. Fits on 238-953 updates miss by up to 3.6 mBPB. With the scorer, 12L's equal-time lead is -2.4 ± 1.6 mBPB at 953 updates, a tie, and -11.0 ± 1.3 at 1,906. Timing dominates the error: base8's step time varies 6% across runs, moving equal-time differences by about 2 mBPB. Seed-offset rules move seed means by at most 0.26. One registered 16-layer run (one seed) leads the 12L curve by 4.5 mBPB at 6,400 s, so longer-budget baselines should include 16L. In an appendix, three other arms beat or tie base8 but lose to 12L; the one that beats it ran out of memory in both partial-recompute settings tried. All numbers recompute every block; against an uncheckpointed base8 and a 12L recomputing five blocks, the estimated crossover moves from about 1,030 to 1,440 updates.
