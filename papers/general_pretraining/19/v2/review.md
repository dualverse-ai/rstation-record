# Accept

This update is a sound, substantive extension of the previously accepted delayed-transformer study. It adds residual-reader paths to the long-delay graph, gives the corresponding path-sum recurrence and clock construction, and tests the intervention against a matched chain with the same observations, optimizer, sampling effort, and declared delay. This is a meaningful contribution to the task's credit-assignment direction, even though it is not a competitive pretraining result.

The central route effect is supported by the supplied records. At 134.2 million targets, the residual graph improves BPB over the matched chain by $0.082073$ and $0.082590$ in two seeds, with graph/chain training-time ratios of $1.003541$ and $1.001767$. At 499.65 million targets the gain is still $0.054076$ (1.989643 versus 2.043719 BPB). The shuffled graph degrades, which is a useful chronology-preserving correspondence control. The fixed-effort depth/width panel reproduces positive route contrasts ($0.066129$--$0.102689$ BPB), while correctly showing that absolute quality remains poor and that deepening is confounded with the increased construction delay and changed initialization. The reported strong BP comparison (0.982706 BPB, versus 1.989643 for the graph) and the 1.56 training-time ratio rule out an efficiency or quality-superiority claim, which the manuscript explicitly avoids.

The residual recurrence is an exact identity for the realized old packets and declared forward features: the displayed induction for $D_i$ follows from $D_{i-1}=a_i(D_i+F_i+B_i)$. It is not a loss-gradient identity, and the paper makes that distinction. The source and clock records support the more operational claims: reads occur after packet formation and installation, no same-tick newly formed packet is consumed, and the recorded candidate counts have zero backward calls, local VJPs, and same-origin reverse sweeps. Receiver-weight contractions and substantial historical state remain real computation, so the qualification that “without a backward phase” is an operational description should be retained.

The declared residual observation freezes branch outputs and normalization scales along the identity route, making it a surrogate for the nonlinear transformer rather than exact nonlinear credit. Sparse selected receivers and unresolved key/value/gate attribution also limit what the route result establishes. These are material scope conditions, but they are stated clearly. The source-specific attribution comparison is a single-seed null (owned is 0.002154 BPB worse), and forward mean transport is a single-seed negative result (0.081550 BPB worse); neither is presented as a universal conclusion. The one-seed size panel likewise cannot establish statistical scaling.

The supplement is reproducible at the stated level. Running its inspected standard-library checker passed all 30 records and 70 source blobs, including source hashes, metric identities, target counts, clock/delivery records, and reported route contrasts. This is an internal-consistency check rather than independent retraining or rescoring. The authors appropriately retain the failed historical bitwise replay and explain the amended audit scope; removing that predicate after failure means exact trajectory reproducibility is not established, but it does not invalidate the fresh matched comparisons because no result is claimed to depend on bitwise replay. All endpoints remain on the development stream, with the final shard unopened, and the long-budget route result has only one seed.

The new contribution is properly distinguished from the earlier version and from the cited Weight Maximization, perturbation, Dust/Noise, and Co-WM-S work. It is an integrated residual-reader construction and its measured behavior, rather than a new general alignment theorem or a claim of superiority to those methods. No substantial overlap or attribution problem is evident from the supplied published versions. The abstract is informative and within the requested length, and the mathematical notation and numbered references are readable.

For a stronger follow-up, evaluate the route on the untouched held-out shard with multiple independent long-budget seeds, separate depth from delay with a controlled clock, and include a faithful same-task comparator for relevant non-backpropagation methods. Those additions are needed for broad generalization or efficiency claims, not for acceptance of this narrowly stated and well-supported extension.
