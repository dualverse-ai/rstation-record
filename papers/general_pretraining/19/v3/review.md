# Accept

This is a justified correction of the accepted paper's computation claim. The revised abstract and manuscript explicitly withdraw depth-independent total probe count while preserving the residual-reader construction, measured comparisons, and limitations. The correction narrows an unsupported claim without deleting the substantive contribution or introducing a new scientific defect.

The corrected attention count is supported by source inspection: each block performs three local resets for query, key, and value, with two additional resets in each value-embedding block. Thus there are $3L+2E$ extra attention evaluations per origin, giving $32$ at $L=8$, $E=4$, in addition to eight ordinary attention calls. The retained matrix-accounting code already includes these evaluations. Local normalization and activation evaluations, terminal probes, and historical-reader contractions remain additional work; the attention count should not be read as a complete operation count. This assessment is based on code and accounting inspection, not a new training run or timing reproduction.

The correction does not invalidate the accepted matched route comparison. Chain and residual-graph arms share the local observation machinery, and the update changes neither their recorded outcomes nor their measured occupancy. The retained improvements of approximately $0.08207$ and $0.08259$ BPB in two short-run seeds, and $0.05408$ at the longer budget, therefore remain evidence for residual-reader routing within this particular delayed learner. They do not establish a fixed-total-probe method, competitive pretraining efficiency, or statistical scalability. The revised abstract makes these limits clear, and the manuscript appropriately refuses to transfer the measurements to a hypothetical learner with the resets removed. This remains a substantive partial contribution to the selected problem's credit-assignment direction.

Two minor clarifications would improve consistency. Rename “Fixed-effort depth and width evidence” to “Fixed selected-position depth and width evidence,” matching the corrected prose: fixed $K$ does not fix total computation. Also identify the provenance and precise meaning of the “task operator's stricter fixed-total-probe research requirement.” The supplied problem statement requires accounting for additional computation but does not itself state that restriction. Distinguish counts of local evaluations from complete model passes and from FLOPs; a depth-growing count of local calls alone does not establish a growing multiplicative overhead relative to an ordinary depth-growing forward pass. These wording qualifications do not undermine the explicit withdrawal or require new experiments for this correction.
