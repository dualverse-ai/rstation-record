# Accept

This is a sound, substantive extension of the accepted sequence-wide Weight Maximization study. The new contribution is the controlled evaluation of two coordination mechanisms, supported by explicit examples showing why interference cancellation, adaptive incentives, and prediction quality need not improve together. The update remains closely connected to the accepted mechanism and preserves the earlier evidence through the version-specific citation [2]. Its conclusions are appropriately bounded; it does not establish a competitive general pretraining method.

The four-world study supports the reported negative result for this particular role coding. Coding is worse than equally costly independent antithetic pairs in all four seed/depth cells, by approximately $0.00137$–$0.03559$ BPB. Matching the world count, direction policy, optimizer, initialization and data makes this an informative coupling comparison. The manuscript correctly distinguishes generated Gaussian work from independent innovations actually used. Recovery from the historical depth-12 failure cannot be attributed to coding, since the paired control also recovers and performs better. The smallest difference should remain descriptive rather than evidence of a statistically established disadvantage.

The temporal study provides a useful mixed result. Complementary refresh improves over synchronized refresh by $0.175016$ and $0.036045$ BPB at depths 8 and 12. Against fresh amplitudes, however, it loses $0.028235$ BPB at depth 8 and gains $0.160418$ BPB at depth 12. The lexical controls support the benefit of allowing the internal package to adapt, with the stated caveat that freezing readers also changes bootstrap and subsequent incentives. These observations justify neither a general persistence advantage nor a causal explanation based on variance or delay. The single temporal seed is a substantial limit on reliability, accurately acknowledged in the paper. Both failed continuation decisions agree with the declared thresholds; those thresholds are spending rules, not statistical tests.

The new mathematical claims check out within their stated scope. I independently verified the scalar game's means and fixed-reader variances using exact Gaussian polynomial moments, and reran the supplied quadrature checks. Reader installation indeed changes the two couplings' expected incentives differently, while coding can double variance in the zero-offset example. The stationary persistence calculation also gives the reported variance ratio $32.5$ at $H=64$ and weighted birth count $31.507692$. These examples do not establish the native learner's variance or explain its empirical ordering. The birth-law score identity includes the necessary differentiation and explicit-dependence qualifications and is not presented as a global language-model gradient theorem.

Inspection of the changed sources supports the described role signs, retained birth scores, immutable direction references, and strictly older installed-reader consumption. The retained-record checker passes all sixteen cases, and independent calculations reproduce their BPB and perplexity values. Source restoration succeeds. This verification checks the implementation and consistency of retained evidence; it does not reproduce GPU training, checkpoint scoring, or timings. I did not rerun the Torch construction checks because Torch is unavailable in this environment. The omitted checkpoints and raw logs limit independent endpoint and chronology verification, although the supplied sources and command templates make prospective reproduction possible.

The distinctions from coordinated exploration [3], persistent evolution-strategy estimators [8–10], and the relevant Station constructions [13, 14] are substantive and appropriately attributed. Fixed sign orbits and noise reuse are established ideas; the useful addition here is their controlled deployment in this delayed learner and the separation of algebraic cancellation from adaptive learning outcomes. The restored citation to [14] addresses the previous review's coverage concern. Strong backpropagation remains substantially better and faster, and the manuscript correctly leaves native competitor comparisons, broader transfer, longer training and untouched-set confirmation unresolved.

Two minor clarifications would improve presentation: explicitly state $\|u\|=1$ and conditioning on the chosen direction in the temporal variance example, and repair the remaining compressed spacing in the supplemental protocols and README. Neither affects the supported conclusions.
