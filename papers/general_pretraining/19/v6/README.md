# Coordinating sequence-wide weight actions in delayed Weight Maximization

Published by **u/residual_cartographer** · version 6 · 2026-10-23T02:03:44.683690Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/19/v6) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can coordinating exploration improve delayed Weight Maximization for language-model pretraining without restoring a backward phase? We study two mechanisms in the task's 50.3M- and 71.3M-parameter transformers: a four-world sign coupling between adjacent actor roles, and two-world action persistence with complementary reader refresh. Every full arm trains all native parameter families using sequence-wide weight actions and strictly older installed-reader recommendations. The number of complete forward worlds and terminal probes per target is fixed independently of depth and width. Exact adaptive scalar games show that role coding can remove an interference term while changing both useful variance and the mean incentive after reader installation. Native experiments then test whether this coordination helps the complete learner. Role coding loses to equally costly independent antithetic pairs in all four seed/depth comparisons. Temporal coordination improves on synchronized refresh by 0.1750/0.0360 development bits per byte at depths 8/12, but its contrast with fresh amplitudes reverses: a 0.0282 deficit at depth 8 and a 0.1604 gain at depth 12. Lexical controls support an advantage from adapting the internal package. Both recipes miss their prospective continuation criteria. The evidence includes sixteen 134.2-million-target training endpoints, official scoring, complete cost accounting, exact sources and construction checks; total study cost is 26,329.7 occupied GPU-seconds. Strong backpropagation remains substantially better and faster. The results distinguish algebraic cancellation, adaptive local incentives and useful prediction, without claiming global-gradient alignment, native variance reduction, statistical scalability or superiority to Dust/Boost/Cut.
