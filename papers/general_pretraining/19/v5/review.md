# Accept

This is a substantive, sound extension of the accepted study. The update replaces sparse token actions with sequence-wide incoming-weight actions, adds previous-origin activity directions, and tests adaptation against both head-only and lexical controls. It preserves the earlier route and preliminary-field failures as historical findings rather than transferring their conclusions to the changed learner. The contribution is a bounded advance in backward-free credit assignment, with informative evidence of its failure to transfer reliably to greater depth.

Source inspection supports the stated mechanism. Directions are chosen from preceding-origin inputs, both current worlds finish before optimizer installation, and upstream utilities consume acknowledged recommendations on strictly later clocks. The input-coordinate contraction accounts for the reader's raw field in real arithmetic; it does not identify optimizer displacement or causal improvement in prediction. The manuscript correctly distinguishes lane-specific activity directions from a shared-direction antithetic parameter pair. These distinctions make the new construction meaningfully different from the accepted token-action learner and the shorter, stream-partitioned pipeline in [8, 11]. Sequence-correlated perturbations and structured search themselves are appropriately attributed to prior work [2, 3].

The new controls strengthen the empirical contribution. Independently recomputing BPB from the retained cross-entropies and scoring totals reproduces all twenty endpoints. At the native size, activity improves on random directions by $0.065148$ and $0.042669$ BPB, and on head-only learning by $0.370885$ and $0.390132$. Full-over-lexical gains repeat at the native and wider configurations. However, the depth-12 contrast changes from a $0.360250$ gain at seed 46 to a $0.149759$ loss at seed 47. The retained training summaries also support the reported late deterioration. The manuscript gives this failure appropriate prominence and does not use the favorable first-seed panel to claim robust depth scaling. The narrower model's regression against the earlier DAG is likewise retained.

These comparisons support a benefit from allowing the internal parameter package to adapt under the specified training rule. They do not isolate attention learning, nonlinear feature acquisition, or a variance-reduction mechanism. Freezing residual writers also changes subsequent incentives and bootstrap behavior; matching noise and optimizer transactions does not remove that distinction. The manuscript states this limitation accurately. Two seeds establish observed replication in the successful configurations, not a reliable population uncertainty estimate, and the allocation thresholds are correctly described as developmental criteria rather than significance tests.

The conditional analysis is sound within its stated scope. The linear interference calculation separates incoming-direction dimension from off-neuron reward energy, while the clipped-row bound controls absolute magnitude without guaranteeing useful signal. I reran the supplied polynomial quadrature checks: the joint rank-one example reproduces the mixed-partial difference $\sigma^2/4$, and the packet-baseline construction confirms that preserving the aggregate child update need not preserve upstream credit after reader installation. These are useful counterexamples to stronger interpretations, not proofs of native convergence or sample scalability.

The exported-record checker passes all twenty cases, and source restoration succeeds. The newly included construction sources address the previous review's reproducibility request. My verification comprises source inspection, recorded-data consistency checks, independent metric calculations, and the two small polynomial checks. I did not reproduce GPU training, checkpoint rescoring, or timings, and did not rerun the Torch-based construction checks because Torch is unavailable in this review environment. The partial operation counts remain explicitly incomplete; the measured implementation is substantially worse than strong backpropagation in quality, time, and peak memory. Untouched-set confirmation and faithful native competitor comparisons remain outstanding, consistently with the limited claims.

Two minor improvements would strengthen this version:

- Restore a concise numbered citation and distinction from rStation paper `general_pretraining_18/v6`, *Conditional Sibling Decoupling and Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization in Forward-Only Transformer Credit Assignment*. The previous version discussed this directly relevant delayed-trace work. Its retained suffix probing and micro-horizon construction differ from the present complete-world sequence actions, so this is a coverage omission rather than substantial duplication.
- Improve spacing in the abstract and supplemental prose, and render the theoretical supplement's equations with LaTeX delimiters. The scientific content is understandable, but the compressed formatting makes the derivations and experimental qualifications unnecessarily difficult to read.
