# Accept

The paper establishes a useful, bounded advance in memory-augmented pretraining: a separate surface-phrase bank and a small, token-restricted contextual bank make learned addressing worthwhile against a static-memory comparator given additional training time. The strongest evidence is the matched learned-versus-frozen router comparison, followed by the equal-time static-table comparison. These address the supplied problem substantively. Acceptance is for this integration and its demonstrated behavior on two small ClimbMix backbones, not for general superiority over static memory or a new primitive of word-sense representation.

The central result is supported by the retained evidence. Independently recomputing the depth-8 paired differences gives $9.567\pm0.560$ mBPB over the frozen $k=2$ router and $3.541\pm0.291$ mBPB over static reuse at 984 updates, where uncertainty is the standard error across three seeds. The latter advantage is positive on all three seeds. Static reuse also receives slightly more total training-loop time on every paired run. Confirmation-shard results agree in direction and approximate magnitude. At depth 6, the $9.166\pm1.424$ mBPB advantage over NG7-CoDeM provides useful transfer evidence, although it remains within the same corpus and training horizon.

I independently decoded the 96 retained validation block arrays. Their reconstructed overall BPB agrees with the reported endpoints to within $1.34\times10^{-7}$ BPB, and their byte denominators sum correctly. Independent ratio bootstrapping reproduces the frozen-router interval, approximately $[9.501,9.631]$ mBPB, and the two-seed static-comparator interval, approximately $[3.684,3.872]$ mBPB. The latter describes seeds 41 and 42, not the three-seed mean of $3.541$; the manuscript identifies this subset. These checks validate numerical consistency of the retained evidence. They do not reproduce training, establish the chronology of held-out access, or independently measure CUDA performance.

The novelty is sufficiently distinct when stated as the paper now states it. Reference numbers here follow the manuscript. The same-problem CoDeM paper [11] already supplies phrase demodulation and deeper value-pathway reuse; CAM [20] already establishes the importance of separating rare keys from shared rows. External work establishes product-key addressing [1], shared and gated memory with optimized gathering [2], static conditional memory [4], token-based routing [23], and contextual selection among per-word senses [24]. The present paper credits these precedents. Its additional contribution is a particular, inexpensive contextual-address restriction integrated with a separate phrase bank, supported by frozen-router and resource-matched comparisons. This is more than renaming an existing static lookup or reporting another isolated favorable seed.

Clarity is adequate for acceptance. The research question, two-bank construction, decisive controls, and limitations are identifiable in the main text. The figures help expose the quality–time trade-off. The distinction between identical zero-table outputs and identical routing/gradient maps is especially useful. The operational interpretation of the locality strata is also appropriately narrower than a claim of demonstrated factual recall. The presentation remains unnecessarily dense with configuration names, but that does not prevent following the central argument.

Several qualifications and corrections should accompany the result:

1. **Keep the comparison and mechanism claims bounded.** The missing matched CAM/separate-tail control prevents concluding that contextual routing is better than a well-allocated static bank, or assigning the unseen-bigram improvement specifically to semantic sense selection. The learned-versus-frozen result remains informative without that stronger conclusion. Similarly, the uniform-quota crowding explanation in Sections 1 and 5.1 is plausible, but the single-seed configuration contrast does not isolate it causally. Describe it as an explanation consistent with the evidence. The cross-microbatch comparison of the two-dimensional router and Fiber-PKM also combines router, readout, and execution changes; the component timings support a bottleneck explanation without proving that the entire endpoint difference comes from removing Axis-1 search.

2. **Correct several executable-specification details.** In Section 2.3, the actual table learning rate is $0.20(d/768)^{-1/2}\,\mathrm{lrm}_t$, as shown in `models.py`, rather than $0.20\,\mathrm{lrm}_t$. At depth 6, `configs.py` selects global layers 3 and 5 and queries at layer 3, whereas the text says layers 2 and 5 and query layer 2. The stated global row formula interleaves banks by head, while the code stores all Bank-1 heads before Bank 2: the contextual row is $HM_{1,h}+hM_{2,h}+17w_t+j$. Also, `F.normalize(..., eps=1e-6)` divides by the maximum of the norm and epsilon, not their sum. These are localized corrections to an otherwise understandable implementation, but the blanket claim of line-for-line equation agreement should be removed until they are reconciled. Use “without first-moment momentum” or “factored-second-moment” to clarify what “moment-free” means here.

3. **Qualify the timing-suite description.** Section 3's statement that all 156 runs had at least 23,800 MiB free is inconsistent with the records: the maximum recorded value is 23,718.69 MiB, 11 early runs report 20,213.38 MiB, and 41 omit this field. Distinguish the primary matched timing runs from preliminary runs and identify the actual selection rule. This does not overturn the headline depth-8 comparison. “Approximately time-matched” is more accurate than “strict equal time”: seed 41's steady CUDA duration is 1082.187 s for Fiber-PKM versus 1081.316 s for static reuse, although the total training-loop comparison favors Fiber-PKM on that seed too.

4. **Preserve the uncertainty distinctions.** Three-seed Student-$t$ inference is fragile to the seed-effect distribution, and the regime-grouped Holm families do not account for every adaptive choice in developing 156 configurations. The consistent paired effects matter more than treating adjusted $p$ values near 0.05 as decisive thresholds. Retain the explicit distinction between seed variation and conditional evaluation-block variation. Where only a subset has retained arrays, place its point estimate beside its interval so readers cannot mistake that interval for uncertainty around the all-seed estimate.

5. **Complete the reproduction instructions.** Numerical summaries are directly inspectable, but the advertised training commands assume an unstated directory layout. Explain installation of the task baseline under `code/baseline_supplement/code`, placement of Paper 29's labels under the expected literature path, preparation of the additional evaluation shards, and the working directory or `PYTHONPATH` for module commands. Preserve the milestone logs supporting restored endpoints where available. Use separate notation for depth and width rather than alternating between $d=8$ and $d=512$; expand SSSL on first use and simplify the abstract's configuration-heavy wording.

These corrections and scope qualifications do not require reconstructing the central contribution: the submitted method and retained comparisons already support the narrower positive result.
