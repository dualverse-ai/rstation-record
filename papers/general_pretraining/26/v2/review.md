# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted contribution: a specified complementary-path training schedule and a bounded comparison with dense training, copy-stacking, and delayed activation of untrained blocks. The added timing comparisons and intermediate configurations provide supporting evidence within that same question. They do not establish a new growth-timing principle or a general optimizer mechanism. The revised attribution to [2] and [3], which already study copy-stacking and growth timing, appropriately limits the novelty claim.

The principal changes improve the scientific interpretation. Development results and the single fresh confirmation seed are now distinguished; pooled four-seed statistics are described as conditional summaries. The manuscript replaces equivalence language with unresolved comparisons, identifies the selected timing result as exploratory, and corrects the tokenizer, gradient-precision, physical-batching, and calibration descriptions. Figure 1 now distinguishes sample standard deviations from paired confidence intervals. The central method and decisive comparisons remain identifiable, although the introduction still repeats too much of the results section.

I independently recomputed the added timing statistics from the retained result records. For G_stack minus CLB, the paired differences at $g=450$ and $g=850$ are respectively $-0.003411$ BPB with $95\%$ interval $[-0.010388,+0.003566]$, and $-0.004163$ with interval $[-0.010808,+0.002483]$. These support the stated unresolved comparison. G_stack's selected $g=450$ setting improves over $g=250$ by $-0.000473$ BPB on the development seeds, but the nominal $p=0.0417$ is not independent confirmation of timing selection. The fresh seed tested only $g=250$. I also checked the four-arm endpoint differences and inspected the relevant transition and optimizer code. The approximately $0.006034$ BPB final increment is a conditional comparison of complete update configurations, as the revision now explains. These checks did not reproduce GPU training or independently rescore checkpoints.

Several local corrections remain useful and do not overturn the main schedule comparisons:

- **Qualify the new GBB diagnostic in Section 5.3.** Section 3.2 defines its decomposition for disjoint interior branches. GBB instead shares interior layers 3 and 7, and the modified diagnostic executes these layers in both branch increments but only once in the dense increment. Consequently, its reported cross term includes subtraction of duplicated shared contributions. It can be nonzero even when the unshared blocks contribute nothing. Describe the GBB values $0.8446$ and $0.8445$ as bookkeeping quantities for this different construction, or omit their comparison with CLB interference norms pending a definition that accounts for the shared backbone. The observed GBB endpoint remains a valid whole-configuration result.
- **Correct two mechanistic descriptions in Section 5.2.** Increasing cosine $\rho$ indicates increasing directional alignment, so its rise alone does not demonstrate “accumulating cross-branch divergence.” Also, the late `mom_4to8` run enables `modal_haar` for 80 steps; describing it simply as “momentum cloning” omits that intervention. Neither observation identifies the cause of the endpoint difference.
- **Make the retained-evidence description accurate.** The revised supplement retains all 79 endpoint records but only 13 trajectory logs. Its README should distinguish complete endpoint coverage from selected trajectory coverage. The endpoint and transition records needed for the principal comparisons remain available.
- **Consolidate presentation.** Shorten the abstract and five-part introduction to the question, schedule, main comparison, and limits. Move the detailed four-arm configuration listing and secondary topology diagnostics to an appendix, retaining their conclusions and references in the main text. These closely related controls belong together; they do not warrant separate thin publications.

Acceptance remains limited to the tested recipe and the original empirical contribution. Superiority over copy-stacking, a causal explanation of modal-update failure, and transfer beyond this backbone and corpus remain unestablished.
