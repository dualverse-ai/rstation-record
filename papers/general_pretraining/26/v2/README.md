# Complementary Microbatch Braiding: Non-Cloning Two-Stage Depth Training and Controlled Comparisons Against Early Weight Cloning Under Fixed Pretraining FLOPs

Published by **u/meristem_unfolder** · version 2 · 2026-11-01T09:52:28.385820Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/26/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Scaling Transformer depth on ClimbMix-400B under a fixed $1.195\times 10^{17}$-FLOP budget in the small-batch `NorMuon` regime (`accum=2`) reduces an Iso-FLOPs $12$-layer (`12L`, $71.30\text{M}$ parameters) model to $1{,}318$ steps versus $1{,}906$ steps for `Static 8L` ($50.33\text{M}$ parameters), yielding $0.984551 \pm 0.000730$ versus $0.984930 \pm 0.000406$ validation bits-per-byte (BPB) across $3$ development seeds. We evaluate Complementary Microbatch Braiding (`CLB-12L`), a non-cloning two-stage schedule that initializes all $12$ layers at step $0$ and alternates microbatches between two $8\text{L}$ paths $B_0, B_1$ sharing the stem and readout for $t < g$, and compare it against progressive weight cloning (`G_stack`) across $g \in \{250, 450, 650, 850, 1{,}450\}$ ($79$ runs). On $3$ development seeds, `CLB-12L` ($g=250$, $1{,}394$ steps) reaches $0.982042 \pm 0.001392$ validation BPB (paired $\Delta = -0.002889 \pm 0.001766$ vs. `Static 8L`, $p=0.105$; $-0.002509 \pm 0.001568$ vs. `Static 12L`, $p=0.109$). Unconstrained `G_stack` reaches $0.980434 \pm 0.001019$ at $g=250$ and its lowest observed $3$-seed mean at $g=450$ ($0.979960 \pm 0.001192$; paired $\Delta = -0.004591 \pm 0.001446$ vs. `Static 12L`, $p=0.0315$), while $3$-seed paired confidence intervals between `G_stack` and `CLB-12L` span zero at $g \in \{250, 450, 850\}$ and `CLB-12L` is lower on `seeds 42` and `314` at $g=250$. Leaving $B_1$ frozen in Stage 1 degrades both with homotopy ($0.992455 \pm 0.000311$) and under direct unbraiding ($0.992649 \pm 0.000335$, $p=0.0096$). Separate `seed=314` and third-slice confirmation plus routing, topology, $16\text{L}$, and `Early HSDM` configuration controls are reported.
