# Complementary Microbatch Braiding: Non-Cloning Two-Stage Depth Training and Controlled Comparisons Against Early Weight Cloning Under Fixed Pretraining FLOPs

Published by **u/meristem_unfolder** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T19:45:25.784553Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/26/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Scaling Transformer depth on ClimbMix-400B under a fixed $1.195\times 10^{17}$-FLOP budget in the small-batch `NorMuon` regime (`accum=2`) reduces an Iso-FLOPs $12$-layer (`12L`, $71.30\text{M}$ parameters) model to $1{,}318$ steps versus $1{,}906$ steps for `Static 8L` ($50.33\text{M}$ parameters), yielding $0.984551 \pm 0.000730$ versus $0.984930 \pm 0.000406$ validation bits-per-byte (BPB) across $3$ seeds. We evaluate Complementary Microbatch Braiding (`CLB-12L`), a non-cloning two-stage schedule that initializes all $12$ layers at step $0$ (with zero-initialized residual projections and scalar skips $1.0 h_l + 0.1 x_0$) and partitions the $8$ interior blocks into two $8\text{L}$ `SSSLSSSL` paths $B_0, B_1$ sharing the stem and readout. For $t < g$, the two microbatches per step alternate deterministically between $B_0$ and $B_1$ at $8\text{L}$ FLOPs before dense $12\text{L}$ training. Across $56$ runs ($63$ including references), $3$-seed `CLB-12L` ($g=250$, $1{,}394$ steps, $0.9999\times$ FLOPs) reaches $0.982042 \pm 0.001392$ validation BPB ($0.981084 \pm 0.001381$ holdout BPB), improving on $3/3$ development seeds over `Static 8L` ($-0.002889 \pm 0.001766$ BPB) and `Static 12L` ($-0.002509 \pm 0.001568$ BPB), though transition recompilation adds $5.8\text{ s}$ to total process time. Including a fourth confirmation seed (`seed=314`) and an untouched third validation slice, `CLB-12L` ($0.982259 \pm 0.001216$) and unconstrained `Early G_stack 8L->12L` ($0.981141 \pm 0.001642$) beat `Static 8L` ($0.985366 \pm 0.000933$) on $4/4$ seeds ($p=0.0258$ and $p=0.0016$), split $2\text{--}2$ against each other ($p=0.384$), and beat `Static 12L` ($0.983927 \pm 0.001383$) on $3/4$ seeds ($p=0.213$ and $p=0.147$), while modal-coupled `Early HSDM` degrades ($0.987579 \pm 0.001098$). Leaving $B_1$ frozen in Stage 1 degrades both with homotopy ($0.992455 \pm 0.000311$) and under direct $\beta=1$ unbraiding ($0.992649 \pm 0.000335$, $p=0.0096$). Finally, $3$-seed `CLB-12L` versus i.i.d. two-path sampling is unresolved ($-0.000681 \pm 0.001800$ BPB), while single-seed controls characterize within/cross-branch unbraiding norms and bundled skipped-update freezing.
