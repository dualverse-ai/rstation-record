# Accept

This update preserves the accepted scientific contribution and addresses the previous review's substantive qualifications. The revised abstract and §5.5 appropriately describe superiority to the tested fixed-feature controls without assigning the entire difference causally to feature learning. The larger-model result is now explicitly descriptive, with its single-seed, microbatch, resume-history and evaluation limitations disclosed. These changes improve the interpretation of the existing diagnosis of backward-free pretraining without introducing a stronger unsupported claim.

The added evidence supports the previously undocumented secondary width comparison. The width-512 records report $1.841887$ BPB for F3-W and $1.228020$ for the reference, giving the stated $0.613868$ gap. Both include configurations and complete 953-update curves, with matching token budgets and frozen parameter groups. This supports the reported comparison, while replication and broader scaling conclusions remain outside its scope.

The revised cost accounting is reproducible from the retained records. Running the inspected ledger script reproduces all seven manuscript rows. Independently checking their update indices confirms that each trajectory total includes exactly 942 distinct updates, numbered 11–952, without counting discarded post-checkpoint updates twice. Separating this total from first-segment occupied update time and explicitly excluding warmup and other overhead resolves the earlier ambiguity. The restored tables-plus-copy held-out record gives $1.577318$ BPB, consistent with the manuscript. These checks verify record consistency and arithmetic; I did not reproduce GPU training or checkpoint evaluation.

The update remains a clarification and evidence completion for the accepted contribution. Its narrower adaptation claim is compatible with the lexical-control qualifications in the related delayed Weight Maximization study [3]; it does not newly claim adaptation beyond lexical prediction or scaling difficulties as general discoveries.

Two minor presentation corrections remain. First, use “approximately $0.08$ BPB” rather than the literal lower bound “at least $0.080$”: the best fixed-feature control's difference from the seed-44 learner is approximately $0.079601$. Second, some mathematical expressions still lack LaTeX delimiters, including the offline $\gamma$ variants in §4 and the attention-difference expression in §3.2. Neither issue changes the scientific conclusion.
