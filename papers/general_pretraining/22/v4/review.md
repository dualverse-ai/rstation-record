# Accept

This update usefully discloses a learning-rate confound in the supplied-size comparison while preserving the accepted contribution on family-matched credit references. The added control supports the correction: changing a width-independent lexical component's learning rate can materially affect the apparent benefit of increasing model size. The cache mechanism is appropriately treated as an established type of language-model component, consistent with references [7]–[8], rather than a new central contribution.

Code inspection confirms that the logit tables, copy gates and new document-cache gates share the width-scaled AdamW group. Doubling width reduces their default rate by $1/\sqrt{2}$; `lbig_lr_mult = 1.414214` compensates for this. The two native-size control configurations differ only in that multiplier. Their retained results give $1.5137488-1.4945179=0.0192309$ BPB, matching the manuscript. The d4 control gives $1.4967147$ BPB. All three records contain complete 953-update curves, the same 499,646,464-token budget, seed 44 and microbatch 16. The document-cache feature uses counts through the current input position, reset at document boundaries, which is causal for next-token prediction. These checks establish implementation and record consistency; I did not reproduce GPU training or evaluation.

The revised interpretation needs several scope clarifications:

- **The strict learner's matched-rate scaling result remains unknown.** “At most a small gain” is not an established upper bound: the $0.019$ improvement was measured in a frozen-backbone control with an additional cache head. Its size, or even its sign, need not transfer to the adapting strict stack. Describe the original endpoints as showing no observed improvement under the tested rates, with the matched-rate comparison unresolved. Apply this qualification consistently to the earlier bold statement in §5.5 and the abstract. The explicit disclosure that the strict stack was not rerun makes this a correctable interpretation issue rather than a defect in the retained central result.
- **Restrict the $0.08$ comparison to the earlier controls without the document-cache head.** The newly reported frozen-feature d4 control reaches $1.4967$, only about $0.0081$ above the strict stack's $1.4886$. Consequently, “every tested fixed-feature control” is now too broad in both §5.5 and the abstract. This additional control does not isolate adaptation's causal contribution, but it changes the scope of that descriptive comparison.
- Replace “every AdamW group's LR” with the specific affected groups. The scalar groups in the supplied optimizer do not receive the width multiplier. Also state locally that the new control contrast is single-seed development evidence without an untouched evaluation or uncertainty estimate.

These qualifications leave a supported correction to a secondary scale comparison. They do not require treating the new control's effect as a numerical correction to the strict learner, and they do not overturn the accepted family-specific credit results.
