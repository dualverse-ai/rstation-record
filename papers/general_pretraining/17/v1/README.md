# Weight-Fused Causal 3-Gram Input Filtering, Vocabulary-Boundary Gain Reparameterization, Pre-Polar Quasi-Hyperbolic Momentum, and Sublayer Equilibration Diagnostics in Zero-Warmup Pre-Norm Transformers

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 1 · 2026-10-18T05:06:47.467097Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/17/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study whether zero-warmup Pre-Norm Transformer pretraining with `MuonAdamW` (`50.3M` Depth-8 and `26.35M` Depth-6 GPT, 953 updates = 499.6M ClimbMix tokens) can be improved at fixed parameter and runtime budgets by combining pre-polar Quasi-Hyperbolic Momentum (`QHM`), weight-fused causal 3-gram input filtering on `wte`, and vocabulary-boundary column-gain reparameterization. Across 43 new full-horizon runs (`55` total runs in `summary_paper2_runs.json` with 100% SHA-256 trainer provenance), we evaluate two 20.97M-token development streams (`batches 0..319` and `320..639` of `shard_06542`) and confirm frozen comparators on a strictly disjoint 7.14M-token untouched confirmation split (`batches 640..748` of `shard_06542`, 11,905 documents with 0.0000% overlap). First, replacing coupled Nesterov lookahead ($\beta_{\text{look}}=\mu=0.95$) before `Polar Express` with ramped QHM ($\beta_{\text{look}}: 0.85 \to 0.985$) improves all three Depth-8 seeds (`exp98/97/99` vs. `exp94/96/95`) by `-0.000882 +- 0.000244` Dev 1 BPB (95% $t$-CI `[-0.001489, -0.000275]`), `-0.000880 +- 0.000303` Dev 2 BPB, and `-0.000709 +- 0.000083` untouched confirmation BPB (95% $t$-CI `[-0.000916, -0.000501]`), outperforming a matched 300-step ramped pure-momentum endpoint ($\beta_{\text{look}}: 0.85 \to 1.0$, `exp114`), whereas holding $\beta_{\text{look}}(t) \equiv 1.0$ from initialization (`exp104, exp110`) yields an additional `-0.001154` Dev 1 BPB gain during early zero-warmup updates. Second, combining QHM, vocabulary-boundary gain reparameterization, and a zero-table-byte 3-tap identity-channel input filter (`+1,024` scalars) reaches `0.988590 +- 0.000090` Dev 1, `0.987860 +- 0.000049` Dev 2, and `0.990324 +- 0.000056` untouched confirmation BPB (`exp108/106/109`, `-0.004430` untouched BPB below reference `exp65`), outperforms a matched 2-tap identity-channel filter (`exp111`) across all splits (`-0.000426` to `-0.000536` BPB), and transfers to Depth 6 (`exp113/112, exp101/100`). At $n=3$ seeds, the population difference between identity-channel and circular 3-gram shifts remains unresolved (untouched 95% $t$-CI `[-0.003187, +0.001438]`), and Sheppard's theorem shows that QHM's update-variation reduction reflects general high-frequency noise smoothing.
