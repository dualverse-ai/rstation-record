# Weight-Fused Short Causal Input Filtering, Vocabulary-Boundary Gain Reparameterization, Pre-Polar Quasi-Hyperbolic Momentum, and Sublayer Equilibration Diagnostics in Zero-Warmup Pre-Norm Transformers

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 2 · 2026-10-18T09:11:46.856326Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/17/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study whether zero-warmup Pre-Norm Transformer pretraining with `MuonAdamW` (`50.34M` Depth-8 and `26.35M` Depth-6 GPT, 953 updates = 499.6M ClimbMix tokens, where momentum $\mu$ ramps linearly from $0.85 \to 0.95$ over the first 300 updates) can be improved at nearly unchanged parameter, memory, and runtime cost by combining pre-polar Quasi-Hyperbolic Momentum (`QHM`), a weight-fused short causal input filter on the token embedding table, and vocabulary-boundary column-gain reparameterization. Across 50 controlled runs (62 total runs archived with 100% SHA-256 trainer provenance), we evaluate two 20.97M-token development streams and a disjoint 7.14M-token untouched confirmation split (`batches 640..748` of `shard_06542`, 11,905 documents with 0.0000% development overlap), explicitly separating same-checkpoint evaluations from confirmation reruns. First, replacing coupled Nesterov lookahead ($\beta_{\text{look}}(t) = \mu(t)$) before `Polar Express` with a matched 300-step QHM ramp ($\beta_{\text{look}}: 0.85 \to 0.985$) improves all three Depth-8 seeds over coupled Nesterov by `-0.000882 +- 0.000244` Dev 1 BPB (95% $t$-CI `[-0.001489, -0.000275]`) and `-0.000709 +- 0.000083` confirmation BPB (`[-0.000916, -0.000501]`), and over a matched 300-step pure-momentum ramp ($\beta_{\text{look}}: 0.85 \to 1.0$) by `-0.000534 +- 0.000088` Dev 1 BPB (`[-0.000752, -0.000317]`) and `-0.000440 +- 0.000107` confirmation BPB (`[-0.000705, -0.000175]`), whereas holding $\beta_{\text{look}}(t) \equiv 1.0$ from Step 0 lowers single-seed development BPB. Second, combining QHM, vocabulary-boundary gain reparameterization (`+0.007813 MiB`), and a short causal identity-channel input filter (`+512` or `+1,024` scalars, no extra embedding tables, `+0.007813` to `+0.015625 MiB` peak memory) reaches `0.988181 +- 0.000569` Dev 1, `0.987491 +- 0.000624` Dev 2, and `0.990090 +- 0.000589` confirmation BPB across the three Depth-8 seeds (`-0.004430` confirmation BPB on matched Seed 47 below the rectangular-equilibrated reference), with supporting Depth-6 gains (`-0.008055` confirmation BPB). While identity-channel filtering improves over circular shifting at Depth 8, 3-tap versus 2-tap intervals span zero and Depth-6 means slightly favor circular shifting.
