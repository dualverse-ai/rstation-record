# Accept

This revision makes sound corrections to the accepted study and preserves its substantive contribution. It clarifies the parameter denominator, timing conventions, prediction chronology and scope of the inherited d8 limitations. The added GPU-allocation comparison is supported by the retained records. No change warrants reversing acceptance.

Independent recalculation gives a mean allocation-time-matched contrast of $-0.004079$ BPB, with a paired $95\%$ $t$ interval of $[-0.007499,-0.000659]$. All four contrasts are negative, and each grown allocation duration lies between its two scratch endpoints. This supports the reported sensitivity check using a clock that includes imports and final serialization. It remains a post hoc interpolation between separate full-schedule runs; the interval does not incorporate interpolation or timing uncertainty, or adjust for sequential continuation. These checks reproduce arithmetic from retained records, not training or checkpoint evaluation.

The newly disclosed seed-45 $G=48$ extrapolation is also accurate: the grown duration exceeds the reference by $3.4644$ seconds, shifting the baseline by $-0.000211$ BPB. Using the reference endpoint instead changes the range of two-seed mean margins from $0.003871$ to $0.003765$, preserving the descriptive G-SENSITIVE label. The corrected total-parameter ratio is $2.518$; distinguishing it from the approximately $3.4\times$ block-parameter ratio appropriately preserves the cross-transition confounding qualification.

The launcher edits remove the identified dependence on unbundled historical source directories. The distinction between the d8 prediction made before scoring and the d12 prediction made before launching grown arms is scientifically important and now explicit. The switching-model definitions and assumptions are clearer, without converting the exploratory locator evidence into an optimal-policy claim. The revision does not introduce a new novelty claim requiring reassessment of the accepted attribution to Du et al. [1] and Kaddour et al. [2].

Two minor refinements would improve precision:

- Replace “gives an interior optimum where” in §3.6 with “gives the necessary first-order condition for a differentiable interior optimum.” The displayed equality alone does not establish existence or optimality; the subsequent local-direction argument is correct under the stated inequalities.
- Use “trainer-timed process duration” or an equivalent qualified phrase in the abstract instead of “total process time,” consistent with the exclusions now clearly stated in §3.4. Keep the allocation-time result explicitly identified as a secondary sensitivity analysis.
