# Conditional Sibling Decoupling and Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization in Forward-Only Transformer Credit Assignment

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 7 · 2026-10-22T19:02:10.117514Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/18/v7) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Eliminating the global reverse-depth sweep of backpropagation is a central goal in neuromorphic computing, but conventional forward perturbation rules incur estimator variance scaling with parameter count $\mathcal{O}(|\Theta|/K)$ or hidden width $\mathcal{O}(M/K)$. Adapting Stephen Chung's Weight Maximization and Sylvester-Hadamard exploration, we study parameter-space column contraction over an $S$-token micro-horizon (`WM-S` and `Co-WM-S`). Under fixed downstream targets, column isolation replaces sibling-neuron cross-talk with $S$-token cross-talk, and sampling $K$ distinct Hadamard columns reduces conditional variance by $\frac{S-K}{K(S-1)}$, vanishing at $K=S$. We separate strict no-backward methods (`local_reverse_derivative_calls=0`, `hub_mode="dust_hub"`) from hybrid local-hub-pullback controls (`local_reverse_derivative_calls=L=6`, `hub_mode="co_wm_s"`, reconstructing $\widehat{E}_g = U_g W_O$ across $W_O$) and block-local reverse-credit controls. First, we establish a two-stage faithful baseline of published DUST (Stage 1: `max_grad_diff=0.0` suffix-cache equivalence, $1.75\times$ speedup; Stage 2: $2.37697\pm 0.06975$ holdout BPB under SGD $P=64$, $2.24921\pm 0.00044$ under Muon $P=64$, and $2.10883\pm 0.00138$ at $P=256$ on ClimbMix `d6`) alongside Fairly Optimized DUST (`sc+sh`, $2.04590\pm 0.00012$ at $P=64$). Second, we introduce Strict No-Backward `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S` (`delayed_trace_cowm_*`, `rev=0`), combining SRAM-fused outgoing-column contractions (`_outgoing_col_dot_all8_kernel`, $18.7\text{ ms}$ across 6 layers at $M=4096$) on `mlp.c_proj` $\to$ `mlp.c_fc` with full-hub covariance centering (`hub2`, `hubs=[2,2,2,2,2,2]`) and intra-block branch decoupling (`bd025`), using $0$ `hidden` suffix forwards and $0$ reverse/pullback matmuls. Across Regime I fixed-$D=256$ MLP-width scaling ($2$ seeds, $M \in \{256, 1024, 4096\}$) and Regime II full-model width scaling ($D \in \{128, 256, 384, 512\}, M=4D$), Strict No-Backward `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S` (`rev=0`) achieves descriptive gains over unmodified and Fairly Optimized DUST at all widths while running faster ($-0.01107\text{ BPB}$ and $92.7\text{ ms/step}$ faster at $M=4096$), and on 2-seed `d6` reaches $2.04412\pm 0.00014\text{ BPB}$ in $633.6\text{s}/635.2\text{s}$ (vs $2.04590\pm 0.00012$ at $648.0\text{s}/649.3\text{s}$ at $P=64$) and $2.08637\pm 0.01282\text{ BPB}$ in $294.9\text{s}/297.9\text{s}$ at $P=256$ (vs $2.11372\pm 0.00121$ at $302.0\text{s}/306.2\text{s}$). Checkpointed backpropagation retains superior efficiency ($1.45179\text{ BPB}$, $33.3\text{ ms}$).
