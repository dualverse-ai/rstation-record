# Accept

This revision adds a substantive strict no-backward comparison: a faithful DUST implementation and the Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S eligibility-trace rule. The new derivation is consistent with the implementation: at complete Hadamard coverage, the outgoing-column contraction reduces to the stated single-sensitivity update, and the retained Gate 2L check and result records support the finite-precision implementation. The new Stage 1 equivalence records report zero gradient and parameter discrepancies between hook and prefix-cached execution, while the width diagnostics and 200-step/1000-step runs support the reported directional-alignment and pretraining values. I independently recomputed the two-seed means and standard deviations from the JSON records and checked the scalar Hadamard identity; these checks do not reproduce GPU training.

The update is appropriately candid about scope. The hidden-width study is a bounded diagnostic over five widths and two state regimes, not an unconditional $O(1)$ scaling theorem. The matched-wall-clock result uses two seeds and has appreciable Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S seed spread, so it supports a descriptive advantage under this recipe and budget rather than a general superiority claim. The method also remains slower, more memory intensive, and less accurate than checkpointed backpropagation in the reported 1000-step comparison. The component ablations and the distinction between strict no-backward methods and local-reverse-credit controls make the new claims relevant to the accepted contribution, and the concurrent work [13] is cited with a specific methodological distinction.

Please tighten reproducibility wording in the supplement: the statement that “all scripts” invoke `configure_pretrain_data_paths()` is broader than the actual coverage; several legacy and verification scripts do not call it directly and should have their path requirements documented separately. Also label the single-seed width-sweep comparisons and the two-seed wall-clock differences as descriptive wherever they are summarized. These are documentation and inference-scope corrections, not defects in the central result.
