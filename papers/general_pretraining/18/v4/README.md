# Conditional Sibling Decoupling and Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization in Forward-Only Transformer Credit Assignment

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 4 · 2026-10-21T06:47:01.470522Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/18/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Eliminating the global reverse-depth sweep of backpropagation is a central goal in neuromorphic computing, but conventional forward perturbation rules incur estimator variance scaling with parameter count O(|Theta|/K) or hidden width O(M/K). Adapting Stephen Chung's Weight Maximization and Sylvester-Hadamard exploration, we investigate parameter-space column contraction over an S-token micro-horizon (WM-S and Co-WM-S). Under fixed downstream targets, column isolation replaces sibling-neuron cross-talk with S-token cross-talk, and sampling K distinct Hadamard columns reduces conditional variance by (S-K)/(K(S-1)), vanishing at K=S. We derive exact conditional weight-gradient covariances and counterexamples showing that width invariance requires non-cancellation and support alignment. Throughout this work, we strictly separate strict no-backward methods (zero reverse-depth sweeps and zero local reverse pullbacks) from reverse-depth-free local-reverse-credit controls (bwd_calls=0, retaining block-local reverse pullbacks). First, we establish a two-stage faithful baseline of published DUST: Stage 1 confirms bit-for-bit gradient equivalence (max_grad_diff=0.0) between hook reruns and prefix-cached suffix execution (1.75x speedup) and reproduces 8-layer reference learning (5.8565 vs 5.7486 test nats at P=256); Stage 2 evaluates faithful DUST on ClimbMix d6 (8.192M tokens), reaching 2.37697+/-0.06975 holdout BPB under SGD (P=64), 2.24921+/-0.00044 under Muon (P=64), and 2.10883+/-0.00138 at P=256. Second, we introduce Delayed / Eligibility-Trace Coordinated Weight Maximization (Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S), a strict no-backward eligibility-trace rule without direct hidden (mlp.c_fc) forward passes or local reverse pullbacks. Across M in {256..4096} at D=256, 34-draw DUST's mlp.c_fc cosine drops 4.43x on synthetic states (0.3060 to 0.0691) and 3.82x on 50-step ClimbMix states (0.2578 to 0.0675), whereas Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S stays bounded (0.3031 to 0.2582 and 0.2078 to 0.1884), beats dust_faithful_muon_P64 on 200-step multi-width pretraining at D in {128,384,512} under 1-step delay (d1), runs 1.48x faster at P=256 (1534.8 vs 2271.0 ms/step, 2.14471+/-0.00464 BPB), and wins the uninterrupted matched-wall-clock comparison with full LR cooldown (2.22478+/-0.01313 vs 2.24958+/-0.00005 holdout BPB at ~304s). Checkpointed backpropagation retains superior efficiency (1.45179 BPB, 33.3 ms).
