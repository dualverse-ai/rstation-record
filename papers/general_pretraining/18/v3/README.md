# Conditional Sibling Decoupling and Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization in Forward-Only Transformer Credit Assignment

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 3 · 2026-10-20T03:44:23.740692Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/18/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Eliminating the global reverse-depth sweep of backpropagation is a central goal in neuromorphic computing, but conventional forward perturbation rules face an unfavorable neuronal width-scaling bottleneck: global scalar rewards incur estimator variance proportional to the total parameter count O(|Theta|/K), while token-scalar perturbation incurs variance proportional to hidden width O(M/K). Adapting Stephen Chung's parameter-space Weight Maximization and Sylvester-Hadamard exploration, we investigate whether parameter-space column contraction over an S-token micro-horizon (WM-S and Co-WM-S) can decouple sibling neurons in language-model pretraining. Under fixed downstream targets, column isolation replaces sibling-neuron cross-talk with S-token cross-talk; sampling distinct Hadamard columns reduces conditional estimator variance by (S-K)/(K(S-1)), vanishing identically at K=S. We derive the exact conditional weight-gradient covariance and formulate counterexamples showing that width invariance is conditional on non-cancellation and support alignment rather than unconditional. In whole-network pretraining on ClimbMix (8.192M tokens, B=16, T=512, D in {128 ... 512}), streaming Co-WM-S (K=8) eliminates the reverse-depth sweep (bwd_calls = 0) and achieves 1.86518 +/- 0.00011 holdout BPB on d6, outperforming token-scalar perturbation by 0.15038 BPB and matching its sub-vocabulary surrogate (1.86466 BPB). Combining intra-block MLP-to-Attention residual pullback, Two-Level Window-Hadamard value transport, and Hadamard-QK attention probing reduces holdout BPB to 1.78546 +/- 0.00297 under sub-vocabulary Co-WM-S (fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_K8) and to 1.64719 +/- 0.00067 under full-vocabulary Co-WM-S (fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_fullvocab_K8, matching its analytic-linear surrogate fwd_streaming_oracle_coupled_fullvocab at 1.64800 +/- 0.00172), resolving 56.9% (56.4% analytic) of the intra-block surrogate gap to detached block-local autograd (1.57236 BPB). Checkpointed backpropagation retains superior efficiency (1.45179 BPB, 33.3 ms, 1731.4 MB); these findings define the theoretical scope and empirical boundaries of forward-only credit assignment.
