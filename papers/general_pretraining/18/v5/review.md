# Accept

The update makes a useful extension of the accepted contribution: it adds fixed-residual-width pretraining comparisons, an optimized DUST comparator, fused contraction timing, and explicit separation of attention-hub pullback controls from the methods retaining forward hub perturbations. The identity $\widehat E_g=H H^\top U_gW_O/S=U_gW_O$ correctly motivates the hybrid classification. The added experiments remain substantively connected to the existing estimator analysis rather than replacing it with an unrelated contribution. The discussion appropriately credits the overlapping attention-filtered trace identity in [14] and distinguishes the broader delayed-credit construction in [13].

I independently recomputed the principal two-seed summaries from the retained records. At fixed $D=256$, $M=4096$, the hub-balanced method reaches $2.13772\pm0.00264$ BPB versus optimized DUST's $2.14879\pm0.00153$, with mean step times of $859.7$ and $952.4$ ms. The reported time-budget comparisons likewise agree with the records: $2.04412$ versus $2.04590$ BPB at $P=64$, and $2.08637$ versus $2.11372$ at $P=256$. An independent exact-arithmetic check confirms the hub identity; source inspection supports the stated contraction structure. These checks do not reproduce GPU training or validate the measured Triton speedup independently.

Acceptance is for these bounded, descriptive results. The single-seed full-width sweep and two-seed comparisons do not establish general superiority or an asymptotic scaling guarantee. The manuscript correctly retains the worse fixed-1000-step result against optimized DUST and the substantial remaining gap to checkpointed backpropagation.

Please address the following qualifications:

- **Narrow the causal interpretation of Table 10.** The value-pooling comparison changes the estimator and also changes local query/key allocations from $(1,4)$ to $(4,7)$. Its $+0.03409$ BPB difference therefore does not isolate exponential recency weighting or demonstrate that reduced long-range credit variance causes the improvement. Report it as a comparison of the implemented configurations; a matched allocation and direct variance measurement would support the stronger explanation.
- **Restore the deleted live dependency.** `run_neuronal_scaling_pretrain.py` is still imported by `run_pure_forward_only_suite.py`, which the primary `run_dust_and_delayed_trace_suite.py` imports for evaluation. Thus the current archive alone cannot run the advertised suite even with external packages installed. The source remains inspectable in the accepted v4 archive, so this is a recoverable packaging regression rather than missing scientific evidence. Also correct the documented aliases: the parser accepts `mlp_width_sweep` and `screen_innovations`, but not `regime1_mlp_scaling` or `innovation_screen`.
- **Keep resource and attribution claims precise.** The time-budget winners combine trace substitution, allocation changes, branch decoupling and scalar traces; optimized DUST receives only the shared `sc+sh` improvements. These are package comparisons, not isolated estimates of the trace mechanism's benefit. “Complete” timing sums training steps and excludes evaluation and checkpoint-writing overhead; retain that distinction wherever summarizing wall time.
