# Conditional Sibling Decoupling and Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization in Forward-Only Transformer Credit Assignment

## 1. Introduction and Architectural Ledger

### 1.1 The Width-Scaling Variance Bottleneck in Forward-Only Credit
Eliminating the global reverse-depth sweep of backpropagation is a central pursuit in neuromorphic computing and biologically plausible credit assignment [1, 2, 8]. While standard reverse-mode automatic differentiation computes exact gradients in $\mathcal{O}(1)$ depth sweeps, it enforces global backward locking: no upstream layer can update its weights until the entire network forward graph has executed and downstream layers have finished their reverse-depth passes.

Forward perturbation methods avoid backward depth locking by perturbing hidden states during forward execution and correlating the resulting loss shifts with the injected noise [5, 6, 8, 12]. However, conventional forward perturbation rules face a severe **neuronal width-scaling bottleneck**:
- **Global Scalar-Reward Perturbation Control (`fwd_global_scalar_K2`)**: Simultaneous zero-mean perturbation across all parameters or activations correlated with a single global scalar loss shift $\Delta \mathcal{L}$ yields estimator variance that scales with the total parameter count $\mathcal{O}(|\Theta| / K)$. (Note: `fwd_global_scalar_K2` is a simplified global-scalar control, not published DUST [5]; see Section 2.6 and Section 3.5 for our two-stage faithful DUST baseline.) In a 6-layer Transformer at $D=512$ ($M=2048$), relative variance of `fwd_global_scalar_K2` at Layer 3 (`midMLP`) explodes to $3.26 \times 10^7$ (Table 3), stalling 200-step pretraining at $2.3898$ holdout BPB (Table 4) and 1000-step pretraining on `d6` ($D=384$) at $2.44794 \pm 0.11598$ holdout BPB (Table 5).
- **Token-Level Probing (`fwd_token_uwm_K2`)**: Evaluating per-token scalar rewards $\Delta \mathcal{L}_t \in \mathbb{R}$ isolates individual tokens, but shares a single scalar return across all $M = 4D$ sibling units in that sublayer. As Ren et al. [8] established, estimator variance scales as $\mathcal{O}(M / K)$, causing Layer 3 relative variance to grow from $26,968.01$ ($D=128$) to $103,931.10$ ($D=512$) (Table 3) and limiting 200-step holdout BPB to $2.1868$ at $D=512$ (Table 4) and 1000-step `d6` holdout BPB to $2.09816 \pm 0.00521$ (Table 5).

### 1.2 Stephen Chung's Foundations and Sylvester-Hadamard Coordination
To overcome this width bottleneck without a global backward pass, we adapt Stephen Chung's credit principles:
1. **Weight Maximization (`arXiv:2010.09770v3`) [2]**: Hidden units do not require an activation-space transpose circuit $(V^\top \delta_t)_i$. Instead, each unit $i$ observes only the plasticity displacement $\Delta \mathbf{v}^{(i)}$ of its own outgoing synaptic column $\mathbf{v}^{(i)} = V_{:, i}$, reinforcing activity when its outgoing column norm expands ($G^{(i)} = \mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \Delta \mathbf{v}^{(i)}$).
2. **Unbiased Weight Maximization (`arXiv:2307.13270`) [3]**: Centered and antithetic exploration cancels zero-order norm biases, and difference-quotient path averaging evaluates non-linear activation credit without analytical backward derivatives.
3. **Structured Coordination [4] and Sylvester-Hadamard Exploration [11]**: Multi-agent exploration noise coordinated via orthogonal Sylvester-Hadamard matrices eliminates cross-agent exploration interference. Following Choromanski et al. [11] (`arXiv:1804.02395`), who proved variance reduction for orthogonal and Hadamard perturbation directions, we adapt Sylvester-Hadamard exploration codebooks to the $S$-token micro-horizon of parameter-space column contractions. This is conceptually distinct from Chung's Structured Coordination [4] (`arXiv:2307.13256`), which investigated recurrent networks of stochastic Boltzmann units coordinated via multi-agent reinforcement learning.
4. **MAP Propagation (`arXiv:2010.07893v2`) [1]**: Layers iteratively settle post-activation states $H^1(n) = \mu^1 + e_1(n)$ to minimize output discrepancy, damping credit through maximum a posteriori (MAP) prior precision.

### 1.3 Strict No-Backward Credit vs. Global Reverse-Depth Removal with Local Reverse Credit Computations
We enforce a strict conceptual and experimental separation between **strict no-backward credit assignment** (`local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`) and **methods that remove the global reverse-depth sweep while retaining local reverse pullbacks**:
1. **Strict No-Backward Credit Assignment (Zero Reverse-Depth Sweeps AND Zero Local Reverse Pullbacks, `local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`)**:
   - **Published Faithful DUST (`dust_faithful_sgd_P64`, `dust_faithful_muon_P64`, `dust_faithful_muon_P256`) and Fairly Optimized DUST (`dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh`, `dust_faithful_muon_P256_sc_sh`) [5]**: Perturbs activation groups during forward execution (`writers`, `hidden`, `hubs` `o@layer`, `wte`, 256-column `lm_head` slabs) and correlates per-token centered loss rewards (and decayed future-token attention head rewards $\gamma=0.98$) directly with pre-synaptic states (`dust_faithful.py`, `delayed_trace_cowm.py`).
   - **Strict No-Backward Delayed / Eligibility-Trace Coordinated Weight Maximization (`Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S`, `delayed_trace_cowm_*`, `hub_mode="dust_hub"`, `local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`)**: Records local forward eligibility traces on `mlp.c_fc` during the clean forward pass using $S$-token Sylvester-Hadamard probes ($\xi_{g, s}^{(m)} = H_{s, c_m}$) and local forward activation evaluations $\phi(z \pm \sigma_{\text{act}})$, and modulates those pre-synaptic traces via 1-step cross-batch delayed (`d1`) or same-step (`d0`) outgoing-weight column contractions $c_g^{(i, m)} = \mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \Delta \mathbf{v}_g^{(i, m)}$ obtained from outgoing `mlp.c_proj` writer updates (`_outgoing_col_dot_all8_kernel` in `delayed_trace_cowm.py`). Critically, `delayed_trace_cowm_*` **never reconstructs an activation-space error vector** on `mlp.c_fc` and estimates attention hubs `o@layer` (`targets[layer]`) via DUST's own forward suffix perturbations (`site_kind="hub"`, `hub_mode="dust_hub"`), requiring **zero direct `hidden` (`mlp.c_fc`) forward passes** and **zero local reverse pullbacks (`local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`)**. Reallocating 4 of the 27 saved `hidden` draws at $P=64$ to `hubs` (`hubs = [2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2]`, `hub2`) and 23 to `writers` (`writers = [12, 10, 8, 7, 6, 6]`) unlocks full-block simplex covariance centering (`sc`, `draws >= 2`) across all 6 attention hubs while intra-block sibling branch decoupling (`bd025`), target-separated Hadamard readout probing (`sh`), and forward scalar traces (`st`) remain 100% forward-only.
2. **Global Reverse-Depth Removal with Local Reverse Credit Computations (Labelled Local-Reverse-Credit and Hybrid Controls)**:
   - **Hybrid Delayed-Trace + Local Attention-Hub Pullback Controls (`delayed_trace_full_*`, `delayed_trace_unified_*`, `hub_mode="co_wm_s"`, `local_reverse_derivative_calls = L = 6`)**: When `hub_mode="co_wm_s"` is enabled (`contract_hub_outgoing_columns`), micro-group $g$ forms $\Delta W_g = H^\top U_g$, contracts $C_g = \Delta W_g W_O$, and reconstructs the upstream activation-space tensor $\widehat{E}_g = \frac{1}{S} H C_g = U_g W_O \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times T \times D}$ at $K_{\text{wm}} = S = 8$ to supply `targets[layer]` to `local_errors` on the same step. Because $\widehat{E}_g = U_g W_O$ executes the 1-layer local linear reverse pullback of `attn.c_proj` ($Y = O W_O^\top$) in the Sylvester-Hadamard token basis, all `hub_mode="co_wm_s"` variants (`delayed_trace_full_*` and `delayed_trace_unified_*`) are explicitly classified and audited (`local_reverse_derivative_calls = 6`, `is_pure_forward_only = False`, `has_local_backward_phase = True`) as **hybrid local-hub-pullback controls** rather than strict no-backward methods.
   - **Streaming Stagewise Local-Reverse-Credit Controls (`fwd_streaming_cowm*`, `fwd_streaming_oracle*`, `fwd_lookahead_2b*`)**: `@torch.no_grad()` is enforced (`num_backward_calls = 0`, `num_depth_reverse_sweeps = 0`) and no global backward graph is instantiated. However, within each detached block, these methods retain **local reverse credit computations** (`d_relu2`, `_forward_rms_norm_directional_pull`, inverse rotary pullback `apply_rotary_emb(cos, -sin)`, and intra-block MLP-to-Attention residual pullback). Replacing analytical derivative formulas with central forward operator differences (`OpDiff`, `*_opdiff_*`) still evaluates local reverse sensitivity pullbacks and therefore does **not** remove backward credit computation. Accordingly, backpropagation (`backprop`, `backprop_ckpt`), block-detached autograd (`block_*_bp`), and all coupled local-error / analytic-surrogate / `OpDiff` / `Lookahead-2B` streaming variants are presented strictly as **labelled local-reverse-credit controls**.

We organize our study across three architectural paradigms:
- **Paradigm A: Two-Level Coordinated Weight Maximization (`fwd_two_level_cowm`)**: Combines parameter-space `Co-WM-S` column contraction on `lm_head` over micro-horizons of $S=8$ tokens, $2 K_{\text{net}}$ end-to-end antithetic full-network passes ($x_0 \to x_L$) with either depth-Hadamard carrier modulation (`net_carrier_mode="depth_hadamard"`) or independent per-probe carrier resampling (`net_carrier_mode="iid_resample"`), and intra-block parameter-space `Co-WM-S` on MLP projections ($W_1, V$) with head-decoupled forward FlashAttention probing on attention matrices ($W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O$).
- **Paradigm B: Streaming Stagewise Forward Co-WM-S and Local-Reverse-Credit Controls (`fwd_streaming_cowm*`, `fwd_lookahead_2b*`)**: Executes a single forward sweep through the network ($l = 0 \dots L-1$). At each block $l$:
  1. Forward output $x_l$ is evaluated and correlated with the language modeling target via an active sub-vocabulary projection ($V_{\text{sub}} = 256$ active tokens, or $V_{\text{sub}}=0$ for full vocabulary $V$);
  2. Block $l$'s parameters ($W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O, W_1, V$) compute gradients blockwise into `.grad` (either uncoupled in `fwd_streaming_cowm`, where both sublayers receive $\delta_{\text{block}}$; intra-block coupled in `fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled*`, where the MLP update runs first and pulls upstream credit across $W_1$ via parameter-space `Co-WM-S` into the attention sublayer; or 2-block cooperative lookahead in `fwd_lookahead_2b_orth_mv*`, where a sliding 2-block window $(l, l+1)$ transports block $l+1$'s zero-variance MLP + Attention-Value input credit projected onto $\delta_{\text{local}, l}^\perp$ into block $l$);
  3. **Block Deallocation**: All intermediate activations of block $l$ ($x_{\text{prev}}, x_{\text{attn\_in}}, u_{\text{attn}}, x_{\text{mlp\_in}}, u_{\text{mlp}}$) are **freed from GPU memory immediately** before block $l+1$ (or $l+2$ under 2-block lookahead) begins execution, maintaining $\mathcal{O}(1)$ activation memory in depth $L$;
  4. The optimizer updates all parameters only after `execute_pure_forward_step` returns.
- **Paradigm C: Strict No-Backward Credit Assignment (`dust_faithful_*`, `delayed_trace_cowm_*`) and Hybrid Local-Hub-Pullback Controls (`delayed_trace_full_*`, `delayed_trace_unified_*`)**: Detailed in Section 2.6 and evaluated in Section 3.5 across Stage 1 reference validation (`dust_stage1_reference_validation.json`), 5-component latency profiling across widths (`contraction_and_trace_width_profile.json`), pure hidden-neuron diagnostic scaling ($M \in \{256 \dots 4096\}$, `dust_and_delayed_trace_width_diagnostic.json`), Regime I 2-seed pure MLP-width pretraining scaling at fixed $D=256$ (`regime1_fixed_D256_mlp_width_pretrain_200.json`), Regime II full-model width pretraining scaling (`dust_and_delayed_trace_width_sweep_200.json`), 200-step component ablation screening (`innovation_screen_200_d6.json`), and 1000-step 2-seed `d6` pretraining under matched-token and matched-wall-clock budgets (`dust_and_delayed_trace_1000steps_d6.json`).

To evaluate the **incremental effects** of successive training interventions in one specified ordering between end-to-end backpropagation and streaming `Co-WM-S`, we construct a sequential ablation ladder:
1. **`block_fixed_top_discrepancy_bp` (Fixed Final-State Discrepancy + Detached Inter-Block Sublayers)**: Detaches residual stream inputs at each block boundary (`bwd_calls = 6`), evaluates the language modeling loss and `lm_head` update **exclusively at the final state $x_{L-1}$** (`w_stage = 1.0` on $L-1$, $0.0$ on $l < L-1$), and broadcasts the fixed final-state pre-norm discrepancy $\delta_{\text{final}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}(x_{L-1})}{\partial x_{L-1}}$ directly into each detached block $l$ via the residual identity skip connection (`x_l.backward(delta_final)`). Comparing `backprop` to `block_fixed_top_discrepancy_bp` holds the downstream supervision discrepancy fixed at $\delta_{\text{final}}$ while replacing all intervening non-identity sublayer Jacobians ($l' > l$) with the identity residual path.
2. **`block_local_greedy_bp_tophead` (Intermediate Local Supervision + Final-Only Readout Update)**: Retains the detached block boundaries (`bwd_calls = 6`), exact intra-block autograd, full vocabulary ($V$), and final-only `lm_head` update (`w_stage = 1.0` on $L-1$, $0.0$ on $l < L-1$) of `block_fixed_top_discrepancy_bp`, but replaces the broadcast final-state discrepancy $\delta_{\text{final}}$ with each block's own intermediate local-loss discrepancy $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}(x_l)}{\partial x_l}$.
3. **`block_local_greedy_bp` (Mixed-Readout Block-Detached Local Autograd [8])**: Identical to `block_local_greedy_bp_tophead`, except the shared `lm_head` accumulates $50\%$ of its gradient from the final block $L-1$ and $50\%$ uniformly across earlier blocks $l < L-1$ (`w_stage = 0.5 / (L-1)`), matching the readout update rule of `fwd_streaming_*`. (In Table 3, `block_local_greedy_bp_vsub256` additionally restricts the readout pullback to $V_{\text{sub}}=256$.)
4. **Incremental Intra-Block and 2-Block Local-Reverse-Credit Controls at Full Vocabulary ($V_{\text{sub}}=0$)**:
   - `fwd_streaming_oracle_fullvocab` (Uncoupled Baseline): Feeds $\delta_{\text{block}}$ into both MLP and Attention independently, with single-window `CM-Attn` ($K_v=1$) and pre-RoPE $K_{\text{qk}}=2$ attention probes.
   - `fwd_streaming_oracle_mlp_pull_only_fullvocab` (**+MLP-Pullback Only**): Adds only the upstream MLP-to-Attention residual pullback $\delta u_{\text{attn}} = \delta_{\text{block}} + \Delta_{\text{mlp}\to\text{attn}}$ (`intra_block_coupling=True`), keeping $K_v=1, K_{\text{qk}}=2$ and `f32_hadamard_qk=False`.
   - `fwd_streaming_oracle_mlp_whv_fullvocab` (**+MLP-Pullback + `CM-Attn-WH` $K_v=8$**): Additionally enables Two-Level Window-Hadamard value transport (`K_v=8`), keeping $K_{\text{qk}}=2$ and `f32_hadamard_qk=False`.
   - `fwd_streaming_oracle_coupled_fullvocab` (**+Combined `Hadamard-QK` Package, $K_{\text{qk}}=8$**): Jointly transitions Q/K probing from $K_{\text{qk}}=2$ pre-RoPE i.i.d. probes to the post-RoPE head-channel Sylvester-Hadamard Q/K package (`K_qk=8, f32_hadamard_qk=True`, $\sigma_{\text{qk}}=0.05$, `float32` perturbation construction/reward accumulation with `bfloat16` FlashAttention execution, and 16-lag scalar key lookahead), paired with `fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_fullvocab_K8` ($K=S=8$ parameter-space `Co-WM-S`).
   - `fwd_streaming_oracle_coupled_qproj_unbiased_fullvocab` (**+Bilinear Query-Subspace Projected Key Probing `Q-Proj-K`**): Holds $K_{\text{qk}}=8$, $\sigma_{\text{qk}}=0.05$, post-RoPE perturbation location, and `float32`/`bfloat16` precision handling strictly identical to `fwd_streaming_oracle_coupled_fullvocab`, replacing **only** the 16-lag scalar key correlation with bilinear rank-1 query-subspace projection (`qproj_k="unbiased"`), paired with `fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_qproj_opdiff_fullvocab_K8` ($K=S=8$ parameter-space `Co-WM-S` + `OpDiff`).
   - `fwd_lookahead_2b_orth_mv_a1.0_opdiff_qproj_fullvocab` (**+Adjacent 2-Block Orthogonal-Complement Cooperative Lookahead `Lookahead-2B-OrthMV` + `OpDiff`**): Adds sliding 2-block orthogonal-complement lookahead transport (`lookahead_2b="orth_mv", lookahead_alpha=1.0, opdiff=True`).
5. **Sub-Vocabulary Readout Truncation ($V_{\text{sub}}=256$)**: `fwd_streaming_oracle`, `fwd_streaming_oracle_coupled`, and their parameter-space `Co-WM-S` counterparts `fwd_streaming_cowm_K8`, `fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_K8`, `fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_qproj_opdiff_K8`, and `fwd_lookahead_2b_orth_mv_cowm_opdiff_qproj_K8` restrict the readout pullback to $V_{\text{sub}}=256$ active columns.

## 2. Theoretical Analysis and Scaling Laws

### 2.1 Micro-Horizon Parameter-Space Weight Maximization (`WM-S`)
In Chung's Weight Maximization framework [2, Eq. 1–3], hidden unit $i \in \{1, \dots, M\}$ does not possess an activation-space transpose circuit $(V^\top \delta_t)_i$. Instead, each unit $i$ observes only the local plasticity changes $\Delta \mathbf{v}^{(i)}$ of its own outgoing weight column $\mathbf{v}^{(i)} = V_{:, i} \in \mathbb{R}^{D_{\text{out}}}$, reinforcing its activity when its outgoing synaptic column grows in squared $L^2$ norm.

To adapt this principle to sequence modeling without per-token activation transposes ($S = 1$) or full-batch temporal cross-talk ($S = N_{\text{tok}}$), we introduce **Parameter-Space Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization (`WM-S`)**:
1. Partition the $N_{\text{tok}} = B \times T$ tokens into $G = N_{\text{tok}} / S$ disjoint micro-groups $\mathcal{S}_1, \dots, \mathcal{S}_G$ of size $S$ tokens ($S \in \{4, 8, 16, 64\}$).
2. For exploration draw $k \in \{1, \dots, K\}$, sample independent Rademacher perturbations $\xi_{g, s, i}^{(k)} \in \{-\sigma, +\sigma\}$ for each neuron $i \in \{1, \dots, M\}$.
3. Over each micro-group $\mathcal{S}_g$, the outgoing matrix $V \in \mathbb{R}^{D_{\text{out}} \times M}$ accumulates its parameter-space plasticity displacement:
   $$\Delta V_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(k)} = \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_g} \delta_{g, s} \big(\xi_{g, s}^{(k)}\big)^\top \;\in\; \mathbb{R}^{D_{\text{out}} \times M}.$$
4. Neuron $i$ evaluates its parameter-space inner product from its own column $\mathbf{v}^{(i)} = V_{:, i}$ and column update $\Delta \mathbf{v}_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(i, k)} = (\Delta V_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(k)})_{:, i}$ [2, Eq. 2]:
   $$G_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(i, k)} = \mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \Delta \mathbf{v}_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(i, k)} - \beta \left\|\Delta \mathbf{v}_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(i, k)}\right\|_2^2 \;\in\; \mathbb{R}.$$
5. Neuron $i$ computes its input credit for token $t \in \mathcal{S}_g$ [2, Eq. 3]:
   $$\hat{g}_{g, t, i}^{\text{WM-S}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K G_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(i, k)} \frac{\xi_{g, t, i}^{(k)}}{\sigma^2}, \qquad \nabla_{W_1} \mathcal{L} = \sum_{g=1}^G \sum_{t \in \mathcal{S}_g} \big(\hat{g}_{g, t}^{\text{WM-S}} \odot \phi'(a_{1, g, t})\big) x_{g, t}^\top.$$

**Proposition 1 (Conditional Coordinate Variance and Sibling Decoupling of `WM-S`)**:
*Conditioned on fixed downstream linear targets $\delta_{g, s}$ independent of $\xi$, with independent Rademacher perturbations $\xi_{g, s, i}^{(k)} \in \{-\sigma, +\sigma\}$ (so $(\xi_{g, t, i}^{(k)})^2 / \sigma^2 \equiv 1$) and $\beta = 0$, expanding $\hat{g}_{g, t, i}^{\text{WM-S}}$ yields:*
$$\hat{g}_{g, t, i}^{\text{WM-S}} = \big(\mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \delta_{g, t}\big) \underbrace{\frac{(\xi_{g, t, i}^{(k)})^2}{\sigma^2}}_{\equiv 1} + \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_g \setminus \{t\}} \big(\mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \delta_{g, s}\big) \frac{\xi_{g, s, i}^{(k)} \xi_{g, t, i}^{(k)}}{\sigma^2}.$$
*Because $\mathbb{E}[\xi_{g, s, i} \xi_{g, t, i}] = 0$ for $s \ne t$, the estimator is conditionally unbiased ($\mathbb{E}[\hat{g}_{g, t, i}^{\text{WM-S}} \mid \delta] = \mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \delta_{g, t}$) with conditional variance:*
$$\text{Var}\!\left[\hat{g}_{g, t, i}^{\text{WM-S}} \;\middle|\; \delta\right] = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_g \setminus \{t\}} (\mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \delta_{g, s})^2.$$

*Decoupling Mechanism*: In activation-space perturbation (`Cut`), the reward $R_t^{(k)}$ sums over all $M$ neurons at token $t$, yielding conditional variance $\frac{1}{K}\sum_{j \ne i} (\mathbf{v}^{(j)} \cdot \delta_t)^2$ that scales with $M-1$. In `WM-S`, parameter-space column isolation decouples all $M - 1$ sibling neurons $j \ne i$, replacing the sum over $M - 1$ neurons with a sum over $S - 1$ tokens in $\mathcal{S}_g$, which is conditionally independent of width $M$ for fixed per-unit targets.

### 2.2 Sylvester-Hadamard Temporal Coordination (`Co-WM-S`)
Because `WM-S` eliminates spatial sibling-neuron cross-talk under fixed $\delta$, the remaining conditional variance arises from the $S - 1$ cross-token terms within micro-horizon $\mathcal{S}_g$. In **`Co-WM-S`**, we adapt orthogonal Sylvester-Hadamard exploration [11] to token micro-horizons in sequence Transformers, structuring the $K \le S$ exploration probes across the $S$ tokens of micro-group $\mathcal{S}_g$ using $K$ distinct columns $\{c_1, \dots, c_K\}$ drawn uniformly without replacement from an $S \times S$ Sylvester-Hadamard matrix $H \in \{-1, +1\}^{S \times S}$ ($S$ a power of 2), modulated by an independent Rademacher carrier $r_{g, s, i} \in \{-1, +1\}$:
$$\xi_{g, s, i}^{(k)} = \sigma \, H_{s, c_k} \, r_{g, s, i}.$$

**Proposition 2 (Finite-Population Conditional Variance of `Co-WM-S`)**:
*Conditioned on fixed downstream targets $\delta_{g, s}$ and writing $c_s = \mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \delta_{g, s}$, uniform selection of $K \in \{1, \dots, S\}$ distinct Hadamard columns without replacement at $\beta = 0$ yields $\mathbb{E}[\hat{g}_{g, t, i}^{\text{Co-WM-S}} \mid \delta] = c_t$ and exact conditional variance:*
$$\text{Var}\!\left[\hat{g}_{g, t, i}^{\text{Co-WM-S}} \;\middle|\; \delta\right] = \frac{S - K}{K (S - 1)} \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_g \setminus \{t\}} c_s^2.$$
*Proof*: For distinct tokens $s \ne t$, $p_{s, t}(c) = H_{s, c} H_{t, c}$ has population mean $\frac{1}{S}\sum_{c=1}^S H_{s, c} H_{t, c} = 0$ by Hadamard orthogonality and unit variance $p_{s, t}(c)^2 \equiv 1$. Sampling $K$ distinct columns without replacement yields the classical hypergeometric variance reduction factor $\frac{S - K}{K(S - 1)}$. At $K = S$, the conditional variance vanishes **identically ($\text{Var} \equiv 0$)** for every width $M$.

### 2.3 Exact Covariance Identity, Sufficient Conditions, and Multi-Checkpoint Trajectory Audit
For row $i$ of the incoming weight matrix $W_1$, let $c_{g, s, i} = \mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \delta_{g, s} \in \mathbb{R}$ and $z_{g, t, i} = \phi'(a_{1, g, t, i}) x_{g, t} \in \mathbb{R}^D$, so the exact row gradient is $g_i = \sum_{g=1}^G \sum_{t \in \mathcal{S}_g} c_{g, t, i} z_{g, t, i} = \sum_{g=1}^G g_{g, i}$.

**Proposition 3 (Exact Conditional Frobenius Weight-Gradient Covariance and Relative Variance Law)**:
*Under `WM-S` exploration with $K$ independent Rademacher draws $\xi_{g, s, i}^{(k)} \in \{-\sigma, +\sigma\}$ and $\beta = 0$, the estimator error decomposes over unordered token pairs $\{s, t\} \subset \mathcal{S}_g$ ($s < t$):*
$$\widehat{\nabla}_{W_{1, i}} - g_i = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{g=1}^G \sum_{1 \le s < t \le S} \big(c_{g, s, i} z_{g, t, i} + c_{g, t, i} z_{g, s, i}\big) \frac{\xi_{g, s, i}^{(k)} \xi_{g, t, i}^{(k)}}{\sigma^2}.$$
*Because distinct pairs $\{s, t\} \ne \{s', t'\}$ are pairwise uncorrelated, the exact weight-gradient covariance is:*
$$\text{Cov}\!\left(\widehat{\nabla}_{W_{1, i}} \;\middle|\; c, z\right) = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{g=1}^G \sum_{1 \le s < t \le S} \big(c_{g, s, i} z_{g, t, i} + c_{g, t, i} z_{g, s, i}\big) \big(c_{g, s, i} z_{g, t, i} + c_{g, t, i} z_{g, s, i}\big)^\top,$$
*and the exact conditional Frobenius variance summed across all $M$ neurons is:*
$$\mathbb{E}\!\left[\left\|\widehat{\nabla}_{W_1} - \nabla_{W_1}\right\|_F^2 \;\middle|\; c, z\right] = \kappa(S, K) \sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{g=1}^G \sum_{1 \le s < t \le S} \left\|c_{g, s, i} z_{g, t, i} + c_{g, t, i} z_{g, s, i}\right\|_2^2,$$
*where $\kappa(S, K) = 1/K$ for `WM-S` and $\kappa(S, K) = \frac{S-K}{K(S-1)}$ for `Co-WM-S`.*

**Algebraic Cross-Token Identity**: By expanding the pair norm squared:
$$\sum_{1 \le s < t \le S} \left\|c_s z_t + c_t z_s\right\|_2^2 = \left(\sum_{s=1}^S c_s^2\right) \left(\sum_{t=1}^S \|z_t\|_2^2\right) + \left\|g_{\mathcal{S}_g}\right\|_2^2 - 2 \sum_{t=1}^S c_t^2 \|z_t\|_2^2.$$
Defining the **Within-Group Support Alignment Ratio** $\alpha(c, z)$, the **Global Gradient Coherence Ratio** $\gamma(c, z)$, and the **Group Gradient Coherence Ratio** $\gamma_{\text{grp}}(c, z)$:
$$\alpha(c, z) \triangleq \frac{\sum_{g, i} \left(\sum_{s=1}^S c_{g, s, i}^2\right) \left(\sum_{t=1}^S \|z_{g, t, i}\|_2^2\right)}{S \sum_{g, t, i} c_{g, t, i}^2 \|z_{g, t, i}\|_2^2}, \qquad \gamma(c, z) \triangleq \frac{\sum_i \|g_i\|_2^2}{\sum_{g, t, i} c_{g, t, i}^2 \|z_{g, t, i}\|_2^2}, \qquad \gamma_{\text{grp}}(c, z) \triangleq \frac{\sum_{g, i} \|g_{g, i}\|_2^2}{\sum_i \|g_i\|_2^2},$$
the exact conditional relative parameter-gradient variance is governed by the identity:
$$\text{RelVar}(\text{WM-S} \mid c, z) \triangleq \frac{\mathbb{E}\!\left[\left\|\widehat{\nabla}_{W_1} - \nabla_{W_1}\right\|_F^2 \;\middle|\; c, z\right]}{\left\|\nabla_{W_1}\right\|_F^2} = \frac{1}{K} \left[ \frac{\alpha(c, z) S - 2}{\gamma(c, z)} + \gamma_{\text{grp}}(c, z) \right].$$

**Analysis of Failure Modes and Counterexamples**:
Because $\alpha(c, z)$, $\gamma(c, z)$, and $\gamma_{\text{grp}}(c, z)$ depend on the empirical distribution of $c$ and $z$, Proposition 3 implies **conditional** width-invariance at fixed $(\alpha, \gamma, \gamma_{\text{grp}})$ rather than an unconditional guarantee across arbitrary network states:
1. *Gradient Cancellation Counterexample*: If token gradients cancel across the sequence ($\gamma \to 0$), relative variance diverges. For $S=8, K=1, D=1, M=4$, inputs $x = (1, 2, 0 \dots)$, targets $\delta = (4, -1+\varepsilon, 0 \dots)$, $z = (2, 8, 0 \dots)$, $c = (4, -1+\varepsilon, 0 \dots)$, the exact gradient is $g = 8\varepsilon$ and cross-term $c_1 z_2 + c_2 z_1 = 30 + 2\varepsilon$. The exact conditional relative variance is $\frac{(30+2\varepsilon)^2}{64\varepsilon^2}$, which equals $140{,}812.5625$ at $\varepsilon = 0.01$ (verified by exhaustive enumeration over all 256 Rademacher states).
2. *Cross-Token Support Mismatch Counterexample*: Non-cancellation ($\gamma > 0$) alone is insufficient if $c$ and $z$ have mismatched temporal support within a micro-group ($\alpha \to \infty$). For $c = (1, \varepsilon, 0 \dots)$ and $z = (\varepsilon, 1, 0 \dots)$, both nonzero token gradients equal $\varepsilon$ (reinforcing, $g = 2\varepsilon, \sum c_t^2 z_t^2 = 2\varepsilon^2$, so $\gamma = 2.0$), but $c_1 z_2 = 1 \gg \varepsilon^2$, giving $\alpha \approx \frac{1}{16\varepsilon^2}$. The exact conditional relative variance is $\frac{(1+\varepsilon^2)^2}{4\varepsilon^2} = 2500.500025$ at $\varepsilon = 0.01$ for $K=1$, $357.214289$ for $K=4$ `Co-WM-S`, and $0.0$ for $K=8$ `Co-WM-S`.

### 2.4 Whole-Network Probing: Sequence-Aware Variance, Sensitivity Thresholds, and Ablations
Propositions 1–3 establish conditional sibling-neuron decoupling given a fixed local output discrepancy $c$. Eliminating conditional local variance does **not** make the whole-network gradient unbiased with respect to end-to-end backpropagation. In a causal sequence Transformer, the downstream map $F^{(m)}: \mathbb{R}^{T \times D} \to \mathbb{R}^{T \times D}$ from injection site $m \in \{0, \dots, 2L\}$ ($S_{\text{sites}} = 2L + 1 = 13$ for $L=6$) to the final normalized hidden state has a block-lower-triangular sequence-channel Jacobian $J^{(m)} \in \mathbb{R}^{(TD) \times (TD)}$, with blocks $J_{u, v}^{(m)} \in \mathbb{R}^{D \times D}$ non-zero for all causal token pairs $u \ge v$ and zero for $u < v$. Given top sensitivity $c = (c_1, \dots, c_T) \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times D}$, the exact transpose sensitivity of site $m$ at token $t$ is $u_t^{(m)} = \sum_{u=t}^T (J_{u, t}^{(m)})^\top c_u \in \mathbb{R}^D$.

**Sequence-Aware Within-Site vs. Between-Site Variance of Depth-Hadamard vs. Independently Resampled Probing**:
Let $H_{\text{depth}} = 16$ be the smallest power of $2$ at least $S_{\text{sites}} = 13$, and let $\kappa(H_{\text{depth}}, K_{\text{net}}) = \frac{H_{\text{depth}} - K_{\text{net}}}{K_{\text{net}}(H_{\text{depth}} - 1)}$ ($\kappa = \frac{1}{15}$ at $H_{\text{depth}} = 16, K_{\text{net}} = 8$). Under **Depth-Hadamard Carrier Modulation (`depth_hadamard`)**, site $m$ draws one Rademacher tensor $r^{(m)} \in \{-1, +1\}^{T \times D}$ per $H_{\text{depth}}$-probe cycle and injects $\xi^{(m, k)} = \sigma h_{m, c_k} r^{(m)}$. Because $h_{m, c_k}^2 \equiv 1$, the within-site contribution ($m' = m$) is invariant to $K_{\text{net}} \le H_{\text{depth}}$ within a cycle, whereas the between-site contribution ($m' \ne m$) is weighted by $\kappa(H_{\text{depth}}, K_{\text{net}})$. Under **Independently Resampled Carriers (`iid_resample`)**, fresh i.i.d. tensors $r^{(m, k)}$ are drawn at every probe $k \in \{1, \dots, K_{\text{net}}\}$, dividing both within-site and between-site variances by $1 / K_{\text{net}}$.

For each return-aggregation mode, the linear-limit expectation and exact sequence-aware activation-credit variance $\operatorname{Var}(\hat{g}^{(m)}) = A_{\text{mode}}^{(m)} + \kappa_{\text{mode}} B_{\text{mode}}^{(m)}$ ($\kappa_{\text{mode}} = \kappa(H_{\text{depth}}, K_{\text{net}})$ for `depth_hadamard`, or dividing $A_{\text{mode}}^{(m)} + B_{\text{mode}}^{(m)}$ by $K_{\text{net}}$ for `iid_resample`) are:
1. **Full-Sequence Scalar-Dot Probing (`full_seq_dot`, $n = TD$)**:
   The scalar return sums over all tokens and channels: $R_{\text{seq}}^{(k)} = \sum_{m'=0}^{2L} \sum_{u=1}^T \langle u_u^{(m')}, \xi_u^{(m', k)} \rangle$.
   - *Linear-Limit Expectation*: Unbiased for the full causal transpose, $\mathbb{E}[\hat{g}_{\text{full}, t}^{(m)}] = u_t^{(m)} = \sum_{u=t}^T (J_{u, t}^{(m)})^\top c_u$.
   - *Exact Sequence-Wide Variance*: Writing $\|u^{(m)}\|_2^2 = \sum_{t=1}^T \|u_t^{(m)}\|_2^2$, the single-probe within-site and between-site variance energies summed over all $n = TD$ coordinates are:
     $$A_{\text{full}}^{(m)} = (TD - 1) \left\|u^{(m)}\right\|_2^2, \qquad B_{\text{full}}^{(m)} = TD \sum_{m' \ne m} \left\|u^{(m')}\right\|_2^2.$$
2. **Token-Local Channel-Dot Probing (`token_dot`)**:
   At token $t$, the token-local return sums only over the $D$ channels at position $t$, but because the perturbation $\xi^{(m', k)}$ is injected across the entire sequence, $(J^{(m')} \xi^{(m', k)})_t$ depends on perturbations at **all earlier tokens $v \le t$**:
   $$R_t^{(k)} = c_t^\top (\Delta x_{\text{final}}^{(k)})_t = \sum_{m'=0}^{2L} \sum_{v=1}^t \big\langle a_{t, v}^{(m')}, \xi_v^{(m', k)} \big\rangle, \qquad \text{where } a_{t, v}^{(m')} \triangleq \big(J_{t, v}^{(m')}\big)^\top c_t \in \mathbb{R}^D.$$
   - *Linear-Limit Expectation*: Multiplying by $\xi_t^{(m, k)} / \sigma^2$ eliminates $v < t$ in expectation ($\mathbb{E}[\xi_t \xi_v^\top] = 0$ for $v < t$), leaving only the diagonal token block and **truncating all future-token causal blocks $u > t$**:
     $$\mathbb{E}\!\left[\hat{g}_{\text{tok}, t}^{(m)}\right] = a_{t, t}^{(m)} = \big(J_{t, t}^{(m)}\big)^\top c_t \;\ne\; \sum_{u=t}^T \big(J_{u, t}^{(m)}\big)^\top c_u.$$
   - *Exact Sequence-Aware Token-Dot Variance (Including Earlier-Token Causal Cross-Talk)*: Multiplication by $\xi_t^{(m, k)}$ does **not** remove the variance of the earlier-token terms $v < t$! Under `depth_hadamard`, $\xi_t^{(m, k)} \xi_v^{(m, k)\top} / \sigma^2 = r_t^{(m)} r_v^{(m)\top}$ for all $v \le t$, so the single-probe within-site error energy $A_{\text{tok}, t}^{(m)}$ and between-site error energy $B_{\text{tok}, t}^{(m)}$ about the token-local mean $a_{t, t}^{(m)}$ at token $t$ are:
     $$A_{\text{tok}, t}^{(m)} = (D - 1) \left\|a_{t, t}^{(m)}\right\|_2^2 + D \sum_{v < t} \left\|a_{t, v}^{(m)}\right\|_2^2, \qquad B_{\text{tok}, t}^{(m)} = D \sum_{m' \ne m} \sum_{v \le t} \left\|a_{t, v}^{(m')}\right\|_2^2.$$
     Notice the second within-site term $D \sum_{v < t} \|a_{t, v}^{(m)}\|_2^2$: even at $D = 1$, where same-token channel cross-talk $(D-1)\|a_{t, t}^{(m)}\|_2^2 = 0$, earlier-token causal coupling ($v < t$) generates non-zero within-site variance that persists unattenuated across a fixed-carrier `depth_hadamard` cycle and divides by $1 / K_{\text{net}}$ under `iid_resample`!
   - *Explicit Causal Counterexample (Verified in Gate 2H)*: For $T=2, D=1$ (single site) with $J = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ and $c = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix}$, at token $t = 2$ we have $a_{2, 2} = 28$ and $a_{2, 1} = 21$. The second-token `token_dot` estimator is $r_2(21 r_1 + 28 r_2) = 28 + 21 r_1 r_2$. Its mean is $28$ (omitting future-token credit on token $1$, where $\mathbb{E}[\hat{g}_{\text{tok}, 1}] = 5 \ne 26$), and its exact variance about $28$ is $(1 - 1)(28^2) + 1(21^2) = 441.0$—which equals $441.0$ at every $K_{\text{net}} \in \{1, 2, 4\}$ under fixed-carrier `depth_hadamard`, and $441.0, 220.5, 110.25$ ($= 441.0 / K_{\text{net}}$) at $K_{\text{net}} = 1, 2, 4$ under `iid_resample` (verified in Gate 2H).
3. **Coordinate-Wise Diagonal Probing (`diag_jacobian`)**:
   Evaluates $\xi_{t, d}^{(m, k)} c_{t, d} (\Delta x_{\text{final}}^{(k)})_{t, d} / \sigma^2$, with expectation $\mathbb{E}[\hat{g}_{\text{diag}, t, d}^{(m)}] = c_{t, d} J_{(t, d), (t, d)}^{(m)}$ (verified in Gate 2G: for $J = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}, c = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix}$, $\mathbb{E}_{\text{diag}} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 28 \end{pmatrix} \ne J^\top c = \begin{pmatrix} 26 \\ 38 \end{pmatrix}$) and single-probe within-site and between-site variance energies:
   $$A_{\text{diag}}^{(m)} = \sum_{t, d} c_{t, d}^2 \left( \sum_{d' \ne d} \big(J_{(t, d), (t, d')}^{(m)}\big)^2 + \sum_{v < t} \sum_{d'=1}^D \big(J_{(t, d), (v, d')}^{(m)}\big)^2 \right), \quad B_{\text{diag}}^{(m)} = \sum_{m' \ne m} \sum_{t, d} c_{t, d}^2 \sum_{v \le t} \sum_{d'=1}^D \big(J_{(t, d), (v, d')}^{(m')}\big)^2.$$

**Sensitivity-Energy Condition for Depth-Hadamard vs. Independent Resampling ($B^{(m)} > 15 A^{(m)}$)**:
At $H_{\text{depth}} = 16, K_{\text{net}} = 8$, comparing $\operatorname{Var}_{\text{DH}} = A^{(m)} + \frac{1}{15} B^{(m)}$ against $\operatorname{Var}_{\text{iid}} = \frac{1}{8}(A^{(m)} + B^{(m)})$ yields the exact necessary and sufficient condition:
$$A^{(m)} + \frac{1}{15} B^{(m)} < \frac{1}{8}\big(A^{(m)} + B^{(m)}\big) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad B^{(m)} > 15 A^{(m)}.$$
Having $S_{\text{sites}} = 13$ sites does **not** by itself guarantee $B^{(m)} > 15 A^{(m)}$: if all 13 sites had equal sensitivity energy $\|u^{(m)}\|_2^2 = 1$, then $B_{\text{full}}^{(m)} / A_{\text{full}}^{(m)} = \frac{12 n}{n - 1} < 15$ for all $n \ge 6$ (for example, at $n = 512$ and unit site energy, $A = 511$ and $B = 6144$, so $\operatorname{Var}_{\text{DH}} = 920.6 > \operatorname{Var}_{\text{iid}} = 831.875$, making independent resampling better).
To test whether $B^{(m)} > 15 A^{(m)}$ holds on our initialized 6-layer Transformer (`c_proj_init_scale=0.5`, where `x0` and attention pathways carry larger sensitivity energy than individual MLP projections), `run_pure_forward_only_suite.py --mode diag` (`pure_forward_width_diagnostic.json`, `site_sensitivity_audit`) measures the exact 13-site sensitivity energies $E_m = \|\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial s^{(m)}}\|_F^2$:
- Across widths $D \in \{128, 256, 384, 512\}$, the measured ratio $B_{\text{full}}^{(m)} / A_{\text{full}}^{(m)}$ is **$20.08, 19.88, 20.05, 20.30$** at Layer 3 MLP ($m=8$) and **$26.40, 26.15, 25.67, 26.31$** at Layer 5 MLP ($m=12$). This ratio specifically governs the whole-sequence return in `full_seq_dot`, where $B_{\text{full}}^{(m)} > 15 A_{\text{full}}^{(m)}$ holds due to the larger sensitivity energy carried by `x0` and attention pathways relative to individual MLP projections; `token_dot` and `diag_jacobian` have their own distinct within-site and between-site propagation variance terms $A_{\text{tok}}, B_{\text{tok}}, A_{\text{diag}}, B_{\text{diag}}$ derived above. Because $B_{\text{full}}^{(m)} / A_{\text{full}}^{(m)} > 15$ at both MLP sites, the predicted DH-to-i.i.d. activation-credit variance ratio is $\frac{1 + 20.30/15}{(1 + 20.30)/8} = 0.8839 < 1$ at Layer 3 ($0.8068$ at Layer 5).
- In Table 3 ($D=512$), we report both **activation-credit relative variance** ($\operatorname{RelVar}(\widehat{\delta u}_{\text{mlp}}^{(3)})$) and **accumulated parameter-gradient relative variance** ($\operatorname{RelVar}(\widehat{\nabla}_{W_1}^{(3)})$): for `full_seq_dot`, `depth_hadamard` vs `iid_resample` gives $79,218.20$ vs $91,443.37$ on activation credit (ratio $0.866 \approx 0.884$) and $78,606.12$ vs $90,830.04$ on $\widehat{\nabla}_{W_1}^{(3)}$; for `token_dot`, $1191.29$ vs $1398.44$ on activation credit and $1180.58$ vs $1386.78$ on $\widehat{\nabla}_{W_1}^{(3)}$; and for `diag_jacobian`, $1.56$ vs $2.60$ on activation credit and $1.55$ vs $2.58$ on $\widehat{\nabla}_{W_1}^{(3)}$. Thus, while carrier reuse leaves within-site variance $A^{(m)}$ unaveraged within a 16-probe cycle, both `depth_hadamard` and `iid_resample` exhibit the same 4-order-of-magnitude variance separation across `diag_jacobian` ($\sim 1.6\text{--}2.6$), `token_dot` ($\sim 1.2\text{--}1.4 \times 10^3$), and `full_seq_dot` ($\sim 7.9\text{--}9.1 \times 10^4$).

**Intra-Block Coupled Forward-Only Credit Assignment (`Co-WM-S`, `CM-Attn-WH`, `Hadamard-QK`)**:
In our baseline uncoupled forward-only streaming surrogate (`fwd_streaming_oracle_fullvocab`), the block-local error $\delta_{\text{block}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}(x_l)}{\partial x_l}$ was fed into both MLP and Attention sublayers independently, omitting the downstream MLP pullback into Attention: in exact block-local backpropagation, $x_l = x_{\text{after\_attn}} + \text{MLP}(\text{norm}(x_{\text{after\_attn}}))$, so the true sensitivity of attention output is $\delta u_{\text{attn}} = \delta_{\text{block}} + J_{\text{mlp}\circ\text{norm}}^\top \delta_{\text{block}}$.
To close this gap strictly without a global backward sweep or autograd calls (`@torch.no_grad()`, `bwd_calls = 0`, fixed pass count independent of depth $L$ and width $M$), we introduce and ablate three coupled forward operators:
1. *Upstream MLP Input Credit via Parameter-Space `Co-WM-S` (`+MLP-Pullback`)*: Evaluating the MLP update first inside block $l$, let $\delta a_{1, g, s} = \hat{g}_{g, s}^{\text{Co-WM-S}} \odot \phi'(a_{1, g, s}) \in \mathbb{R}^M$ be the pre-activation discrepancy on $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times D}$. For each input channel $d \in \{1, \dots, D\}$ with column $\mathbf{w}_1^{(d)} = (W_1)_{:, d} \in \mathbb{R}^M$ and micro-group $\mathcal{S}_g$ ($S=8$), probe $k \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ injects $\xi_{g, s, d}^{(k)} = \sigma H_{s, c_k} r_{g, s, d}$, forms the probe-specific column displacement $\Delta \mathbf{w}_{1, \mathcal{S}_g}^{(d, k)} = \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_g} \delta a_{1, g, s} \xi_{g, s, d}^{(k)} \in \mathbb{R}^M$, contracts $G_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(d, k)} = \mathbf{w}_1^{(d)} \cdot \Delta \mathbf{w}_{1, \mathcal{S}_g}^{(d, k)}$ (`compute_linear_non_backprop_input_credit`), and reconstructs:
   $$\widehat{\delta x}_{\text{mlp\_in}, g, t, d} = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K G_{\mathcal{S}_g}^{(d, k)} \frac{\xi_{g, t, d}^{(k)}}{\sigma^2}$$
   without transposing $W_1$ in activation space or executing extra forward passes (exact at $K=S=8$, Gate 2J). Pulling $\widehat{\delta x}_{\text{mlp\_in}}$ across `norm(x)` ($\text{RMS}(x) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{D}\sum_{d=1}^D x_d^2 + \varepsilon}$, $\bar{x} = x / \text{RMS}(x)$) via `_forward_rms_norm_directional_pull` adds:
   $$\Delta_{\text{mlp}\to\text{attn}} = \frac{1}{\text{RMS}(x)} \left(I - \frac{1}{D}\bar{x}\bar{x}^\top\right) \widehat{\delta x}_{\text{mlp\_in}}$$
   to $\delta_{\text{block}}$. In our retained $T=512$ ($n_{\text{win}}=8$) diagnostic on `d6` (`t512_intra_block_ablation` in `pure_forward_width_diagnostic.json`), adding only this MLP pullback (`fwd_streaming_oracle_mlp_pull_only_fullvocab`) raises Layer 3 $W_O$ block-local cosine similarity from $0.93360$ to **$1.00000$** ($0.93314 \to 1.00000$ at $T=64$).
2. *Two-Level Window-Hadamard Forward Value Transport (`CM-Attn-WH`)*: At the pretraining sequence length $T=512$ ($n_{\text{win}}=8$ windows of $S_{\text{win}}=64$ tokens), single-window value probing ($K_v=1$) truncates cross-window causal value transport ($w_q > w_k$), causing Layer 3 $W_V$ block-local cosine to drop to $0.46853$ ($0.48951$ after MLP pullback). Multiplexing $S_{\text{win}}=64$ tokens per window via Sylvester-Hadamard matrix $H_{64}$ and $n_{\text{win}}=8$ windows via $H_8$ with causal window suffix summation over $w_q \ge w_k$ ($K_v = n_{\text{win}} = 8$, verified in Gate 2J) in `fwd_streaming_oracle_mlp_whv_fullvocab` restores $T=512$ Layer 3 $W_V$ block-local cosine from $0.48951$ to **$1.00000$**.
3. *Post-RoPE Head-Channel Sylvester-Hadamard Query/Key Probing (`Hadamard-QK`)*: Constructing perturbations on post-RoPE normalized $Q/K$ using $H_{d_h}$ channel coordination ($d_h=64, K_{\text{qk}}=8$, $\sigma_{\text{qk}}=0.05$) and accumulating rewards in `float32` (while converting perturbed $Q, K$ to `work_dtype` = `bfloat16` inside CUDA FlashAttention) with fused causal future-query lookahead ($L_k=16, \rho=0.85$) raises $T=512$ Layer 3/5 $W_Q$ block-local cosine from $0.14129 / 0.13918$ (`rel_var = 56.12`) to **$0.35023 / 0.35241$ (`rel_var = 6.94`)** in `fwd_streaming_oracle_coupled_fullvocab` (and **$0.34973 / 0.35366$** in full-vocabulary `Co-WM-S` `fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_fullvocab_K8`).

### 2.5 MAP Settling: Single-Step Equivalence vs. Multi-Step Spectral Damping
In Post-Activation MAP Settling [1, Eq. 1, 5, 6, 7], post-activation discrepancy $e_1 = H^1 - \phi(W_1 x)$ minimizes:
$$E(e_1) = \frac{\rho}{2} \|e_1\|_2^2 + \frac{1}{2} \|e_2(e_1)\|_2^2, \qquad e_2(e_1) = -\epsilon_{\text{clamp}} \hat{\delta}_y - e_1 V^\top, \quad e_1(0) = \mathbf{0}.$$

**Proposition 4 (GS-MAP Spectral Filter and Settling Provenance)**:
*Under the recurrence $e_1(n+1) = e_1(n) - \eta (\rho e_1(n) - e_2(n) V)$ with non-oscillatory step size $0 < \eta \le \frac{1}{\rho + \|V\|_2^2}$, the exact $N$-step credit operator is:*
$$\hat{g}_h^{\text{GS-MAP}(N)} = (\delta_y V) \, \mathcal{F}_N(V^\top V), \qquad \text{where } \mathcal{F}_N(V^\top V) = \frac{\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \big(I - \eta(\rho I + V^\top V)\big)^k}{\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (1 - \eta \rho)^k}.$$
1. **$N=1$ Equivalence**: At $N = 1$, $\mathcal{F}_1(V^\top V) = I$, recovering exact local credit $\delta_y V$ ($\text{RelBias} \le 0.00022$ in Table 2).
2. **Multi-Step Spectral Damping ($N \ge 2$)**: Rather than improving local credit accuracy, $N \ge 2$ applies a contractive spectral filter that damps top singular modes ($\text{RelBias} = 0.129\text{--}0.163$ in Table 2).

### 2.6 Strict No-Backward Credit Assignment: Faithful DUST vs. Delayed / Eligibility-Trace Coordinated Weight Maximization (`Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S`)
While our streaming algorithms (`fwd_streaming_cowm*`, `fwd_lookahead_2b*`) eliminate the global reverse-depth sweep (`num_depth_reverse_sweeps = 0`), they still execute block-local reverse sensitivity pullbacks across activation/`RMSNorm` derivatives and intra-block sublayers. A **strict no-backward** algorithm must compute parameter updates without either a global reverse-depth sweep or local reverse derivative pullbacks.

**Published Faithful DUST (`dust_faithful.py`) [5]**:
In published DUST (`https://qlabs.sh/research/dust/` and `literature/dust/dust.py`), parameter updates are estimated by perturbing activation groups during forward execution (`writers`: `attn.c_proj` and `mlp.c_proj`; `hidden`: `mlp.c_fc`; `hubs`: `o@layer` attention head outputs; `wte`; and 256-column `lm_head` slabs; plus 2-sided `scalar_gradients` on `resid_lambdas, x0_lambdas, ve_gate`) with draw-chunk centered per-token loss rewards $\tilde{\ell}_{b, t}^{(p)} = \ell_{b, t}^{(p)} - \frac{1}{|\mathcal{C}|}\sum_{q \in \mathcal{C}} \ell_{b, t}^{(q)}$ (and future-token decayed per-head rewards $\gamma = 0.98$ inside `local_errors` for $W_Q, W_K, W_V$). Crucially:
1. *Non-Zero `c_proj` Initialization*: Because DUST perturbs `mlp.c_fc` (`hidden`) during forward execution and observes the loss shift through `mlp.c_proj`, zero-initializing `c_proj` (as in `baseline_code/train.py`) blocks `hidden` signals at step 0; published DUST initializes `attn.c_proj` and `mlp.c_proj` with $\text{std} = 1.5/\sqrt{d_{\text{in}}}$.
2. *Prefix-Cached Suffix Execution vs. Hook Reruns*: While the minimal reference script (`literature/dust/dust.py`) registers forward hooks and reruns the full network from `wte`, the DUST paper [5, §2.3] specifies prefix-cached suffix evaluation (`blocks[l:]`), which evaluates only blocks downstream of the perturbed block $l$ with bit-for-bit identical gradients (`max_grad_diff = 0.0` at $P=256$, verified in Stage 1A).
3. *Hidden-Width Scaling Bottleneck of Direct `hidden` Probing*: When DUST perturbs all $M$ hidden neurons of `mlp.c_fc` simultaneously with $\xi_{b, t} \in \mathbb{R}^M$ ($\sigma = 0.2$), neuron $i$'s estimator correlates $\xi_{b, t, i}$ with a token reward containing the summed perturbation of all $M-1$ sibling neurons $\sum_{j \ne i} (\mathbf{v}^{(j)} \cdot \delta_{b, t}) \xi_{b, t, j}$. Consequently, DUST's `mlp.c_fc` gradient variance grows linearly with hidden neuron count $\mathcal{O}(M / P_{\text{hidden}})$.

**Delayed / Eligibility-Trace Coordinated Weight Maximization (`Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S`, `delayed_trace_cowm.py`)**:
To eliminate DUST's $\mathcal{O}(M / P_{\text{hidden}})$ sibling-neuron interference on `mlp.c_fc` **without** direct `hidden` forward passes and **without** any local reverse/pullback step, we transfer parameter-space `Co-WM-S` into a three-phase eligibility-trace rule:
1. **Phase 1 — Clean-Pass Local Forward Eligibility-Trace Recording (`record_mlp_forward_eligibility_traces`)**:
   During the single clean forward pass at block $l$, let $x_{g, s} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ be the pre-synaptic input to `mlp.c_fc` ($W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times D}$) and $z_{g, s} = W_1 x_{g, s} \in \mathbb{R}^M$ the clean pre-activation in micro-group $g \in \{1, \dots, G\}$ ($G = BT / S, S=8$). Using only local forward evaluations of the activation function $\phi(z) = \max(0, z)^2$:
   $$d_{g, s, i} \triangleq \delta h_{0, g, s, i} = \frac{\phi(z_{g, s, i} + \sigma_{\text{act}}) - \phi(z_{g, s, i} - \sigma_{\text{act}})}{2\sigma_{\text{act}}}, \qquad \tilde{h}_{g, s, i}^{(m)} = d_{g, s, i} \, \xi_{g, s}^{(m)},$$
   where $\xi_{g, s}^{(m)} = H_{s, c_m} \in \{-1, +1\}$ ($m = 1, \dots, K_{\text{wm}}, K_{\text{wm}} \le S$) are shared Sylvester-Hadamard micro-horizon probes (`64 KB` per layer, since each neuron is already spatially decoupled from sibling neurons by its own outgoing column $\mathbf{v}^{(i)}$). The local pre-synaptic input eligibility trace for micro-group $g$ and probe $m$ is $e_g^{(m)} = \sum_{s=1}^S \xi_{g, s}^{(m)} x_{g, s} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ (retaining $d_{g, s, i}$ inside $\tilde{h}_{g, s, i}^{(m)}$ so the activation sensitivity enters the weight update exactly once).
2. **Phase 2 — Outgoing Writer Forward Perturbation (Same-Step `d0` or 1-Step Cross-Batch Delayed `d1`)**:
   The outgoing projection $V = \text{mlp.c\_proj} \in \mathbb{R}^{D \times M}$ obtains its forward writer reward vector $u_{g, s} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ from DUST's `writers` perturbation draws (either from the current batch `d0` or from the previous step's writer reward buffer `d1` before overwriting). Zero direct `hidden` (`mlp.c_fc`) forward passes are executed (`n_hidden_draws = 0`).
3. **Phase 3 — Outgoing-Column Contraction and Trace Modulation (`contract_delayed_outgoing_columns_with_trace`)**:
   For each micro-group $g$ and Hadamard probe $m$, neuron $i$ forms its outgoing weight-column plastic displacement $\Delta \mathbf{v}_g^{(i, m)} = \sum_{s'=1}^S \tilde{h}_{g, s', i}^{(m)} u_{g, s'} = \sum_{s'=1}^S d_{g, s', i} \, \xi_{g, s'}^{(m)} u_{g, s'} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ (computed across all $M$ neurons simultaneously via Tensor-Core `torch.bmm`), contracts its own outgoing column $\mathbf{v}^{(i)} = V_{:, i} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ with $\Delta \mathbf{v}_g^{(i, m)}$:
   $$c_g^{(i, m)} = \mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \Delta \mathbf{v}_g^{(i, m)} = \sum_{s'=1}^S d_{g, s', i} \big(\mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot u_{g, s'}\big) \xi_{g, s'}^{(m)} \;\in\; \mathbb{R},$$
   and accumulates its `mlp.c_fc` row update by modulating the pre-synaptic input trace $e_g^{(m)}$ (`mod_accum.T @ x_g` in `delayed_trace_cowm.py` lines 171–174):
   $$\widehat{\nabla}_{W_{1, i}} = \frac{1}{K_{\text{wm}}} \sum_{m=1}^{K_{\text{wm}}} \sum_{g=1}^G c_g^{(i, m)} e_g^{(m)} = \sum_{g=1}^G \sum_{s=1}^S \left(\frac{1}{K_{\text{wm}}} \sum_{m=1}^{K_{\text{wm}}} c_g^{(i, m)} \xi_{g, s}^{(m)}\right) x_{g, s}.$$
   *Exact Conditional Recovery vs. Finite Precision and Writer-Reward Noise*:
   - **Exact Algebraic Recovery ($K_{\text{wm}} = S$)**: By Sylvester-Hadamard completeness ($\frac{1}{S}\sum_{m=1}^S \xi_{g, s'}^{(m)} \xi_{g, s}^{(m)} = \mathbf{1}[s' = s]$), the reconstructed pre-activation modulation satisfies the exact single-sensitivity identity:
     $$\frac{1}{S}\sum_{m=1}^S c_g^{(i, m)} \xi_{g, s}^{(m)} \equiv d_{g, s, i} \big(\mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot u_{g, s}\big), \qquad \widehat{\nabla}_{W_{1, i}}\Big|_{K_{\text{wm}}=S} \equiv \sum_{g=1}^G \sum_{s=1}^S d_{g, s, i} \big(\mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot u_{g, s}\big) x_{g, s}.$$
     (For example, at $S = K_{\text{wm}} = 8$ with scalar $x_s = u_s = v = 1$ and $z_s = 1 \implies d_s = 2$, both the displayed formula and `contract_delayed_outgoing_columns_with_trace` evaluate to $\sum_{s=1}^8 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 16.0$, verified in Gate 2L.)
   - **Finite-Precision Tensor-Core Agreement**: In `delayed_trace_cowm.py`, $x_g, \tilde{h}_0, V_{\text{snapshot}}, u_g$ are stored in `bfloat16` for Tensor-Core `torch.bmm` throughput and accumulated in `float32`, yielding conditional cosine similarity `0.999997` against the `bfloat16` reference in Gate 2L (`1.000000` rounded to 6 decimals in Table 7's conditional panel).
   - **Remaining Writer-Reward Estimation Noise**: When $u_{g, s}$ is estimated from a finite number $P_{\text{writers}}$ of forward writer draws rather than the exact local target $\delta_{g, s}$, $K_{\text{wm}} = S = 8$ eliminates the internal MLP column-contraction variance ($\text{Var}[\widehat{\nabla}_{W_1} \mid u] = 0$), while the remaining whole-network variance is governed solely by the $D$-dimensional outgoing writer estimate $u_{g, s}$ (independent of hidden neuron count $M$).
4. **Phase 4 — Strict No-Backward Attention Hub Centering (`hub2`, `hub_mode="dust_hub"`, `rev=0`) vs. Hybrid Local Attention-Hub Pullback (`Hub-Co-WM-S`, `hub_mode="co_wm_s"`, `rev=6`)**:
   - *Strict No-Backward Hub-Balanced Allocation (`hub2`, `delayed_trace_cowm_*`, `hub_mode="dust_hub"`, `local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`)*: In published DUST [5] at $P=64$ (`n_layer=6`), the attention hub allocation `hubs = [2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1]` assigns only $1$ suffix forward draw (`draws = 1`) to attention hubs `o@2, o@3, o@4, o@5`. Because `draws = 1` cannot be sample-mean centered across draws (`delayed_trace_cowm.py` line 696), four of the six attention hubs in `dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh` fall back to uncentered 1-sample REINFORCE. Because `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S` eliminates all $27$ `hidden` (`mlp.c_fc`) suffix forward draws (`[9, 8, 4, 3, 2, 1]`), reallocating just $4$ of those $27$ saved draws to `hubs` (`hubs = [2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2]` $= 12$ draws, `hub2`) and $23$ to `writers` (`writers = [12, 10, 8, 7, 6, 6]` $= 49$ draws; total $49 + 12 + 3 = 64$ direct draws) in `delayed_trace_cowm_P64_hub2_bd025_sc_sh_d1` ensures every attention hub `o@0..5` has $\ge 2$ draws—activating full-block covariance centering (`sc`) across all $6$ hubs while retaining DUST's exact forward hub perturbations (`site_kind="hub"`) and $\gamma$-decayed ($\gamma = 0.98$) local attention perturbations (`causal_v_trace=False`), with **zero** reverse pullbacks (`local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`).
   - *$W_O$-Free Forward Causal Value Pooling (`record_attn_v_forward_pooled_input`) and Concurrent Work [14]*: Because $v_{b, \tau}^{(h)} = W_V^{(h)} x_{\text{in}, b, \tau}$ is linear, Fubini's theorem commutes $W_V^{(h)}$ with forward causal attention:
     $$o_{b, t}^{(h)} = \sum_{\tau \le t} A_{b, h, t, \tau} \big(W_V^{(h)} x_{\text{in}, b, \tau}\big) = W_V^{(h)} \underbrace{\left(\sum_{\tau \le t} A_{b, h, t, \tau} x_{\text{in}, b, \tau}\right)}_{\triangleq \, \bar{x}_{b, t}^{(h)} \in \mathbb{R}^D}.$$
     This forward attention-filtered value trace identity was also independently derived in Proposition 2 of concurrent work `general_pretraining_20/v1` [14] (published on rStation approximately six hours prior to our initial v5 submission within the concurrency window). Under `hub_mode="dust_hub"` (`record_attn_v_forward_pooled_input`), $\bar{x}_{b, t}^{(h)}$ can be reassembled directly with DUST's forward suffix hub error $\hat{u}_{\text{hub}, b, t}^{(h)} = \texttt{targets[layer]}_{b, t}^{(h)}$ at `o@layer` (upstream of $W_O$) via $\sum_{b, t} \hat{u}_{\text{hub}, b, t}^{(h)} (\bar{x}_{b, t}^{(h)})^\top$ (`local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`, zero $W_O$ involvement). However, our controlled 200-step ablation on `d6` (Table 10) reveals that at $P=64$, retaining DUST's recency-weighted ($\gamma^{t-\tau}, \gamma = 0.98$) local value perturbation (`delayed_trace_cowm_P64_hub2_bd025_sc_sh_d1`, `2.12965` holdout BPB) outperforms unweighted ($\gamma = 1$) causal value pooling (`delayed_trace_cowm_P64_hub2_vtrace_bd025_sc_sh_d1`, `2.16374` holdout BPB) by `-0.03409 BPB`; we therefore keep `causal_v_trace=False` in all primary strict no-backward `delayed_trace_cowm_*` pretraining arms.
   - *Hybrid Local Attention-Hub Pullback Control (`Hub-Co-WM-S`, `hub_mode="co_wm_s"`, `local_reverse_derivative_calls = L = 6`)*: In `contract_hub_outgoing_columns` (`hub_mode="co_wm_s"`), micro-group $g$ forms $\Delta W_g = H^\top U_g$ from the current `attn.c_proj` writer rewards $U_g \in \mathbb{R}^{S \times D}$, multiplies $C_g = \Delta W_g W_O$, and reconstructs the $(B, T, D)$ upstream activation-credit tensor $\widehat{E}_g = \frac{1}{K_{\text{wm}}} H C_g$ to supply `targets[layer]` to `local_errors` on the same step (and similarly in `contract_delayed_outgoing_columns_with_attn_v_trace`). At the implemented complete Hadamard basis $K_{\text{wm}} = S = 8$, $\frac{1}{S} H H^\top = I_S$ yields the exact identity:
     $$\Delta W_g = H^\top U_g, \qquad C_g = \Delta W_g W_O, \qquad \widehat{E}_g = \frac{1}{S} H C_g = U_g W_O.$$
     For the forward linear map $Y = O W_O^\top$, $U_g W_O$ is the exact 1-layer local linear reverse pullback of the output credit $U_g$ across $W_O$, executed in the Sylvester-Hadamard token basis (`CosSim = 0.999998` against `u_attn @ W_O` in Gate 2M). Accordingly, all `hub_mode="co_wm_s"` variants (`delayed_trace_full_*` and `delayed_trace_unified_*`) record `local_reverse_derivative_calls = L = 6` (`is_pure_forward_only = False`, `has_local_backward_phase = True`) and are presented strictly as **hybrid local-hub-pullback controls** quantifying the effect of replacing forward `o@layer` hub suffix passes with a 1-layer local linear pullback across $W_O$.
5. **SRAM-Fused Tensor-Core Outgoing-Column Contraction Kernel (`_outgoing_col_dot_all8_kernel`)**:
   In a naive PyTorch implementation, forming $\Delta V_g^{(m)} = u_{g, m} \tilde{h}_0^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{G \times D \times M}$ via `torch.bmm` and contracting $(V \odot \Delta V_g^{(m)}).\text{sum}(\text{dim}=1)$ across $K_{\text{wm}}=8$ probes materializes $8 \times G \times D \times M$ intermediate tensors in HBM (`247.4 ms` across 6 layers at $D=256, M=4096$). In `delayed_trace_cowm.py` (lines 125–244), `_outgoing_col_dot_all8_kernel` fuses the outer-product displacement and column dot product across all $K_{\text{wm}}=8$ Hadamard probes inside a single Triton kernel: each program loads a $(B_D \times B_M)$ tile of $V_{\text{snapshot}}$ into SRAM registers once, computes $\Delta V_g^{(1\dots 8)}$ via `tl.dot` on Tensor Cores, multiplies by $V_{\text{snapshot}}$ in SRAM, and accumulates the $8$ scalar column contractions without ever writing $(G, D, M)$ to HBM—reducing 6-layer contraction latency at $M=4096$ from `247.4 ms` to **`18.7–19.1 ms` ($13.0\times$ faster, `cos = 0.9999986`)**.
6. **Fairly Optimized Shared Backward-Free Mechanisms (`sc`, `sh`, `bd025`, `st`)**:
   To ensure complete experimental fairness when improving residual-writer and readout estimation, we implement four strictly backward-free mechanisms in `delayed_trace_cowm.py` (`local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`) and apply the shared writer/head improvements (`sc+sh`) identically to **both** Fairly Optimized DUST (`dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh`, `dust_faithful_muon_P256_sc_sh`) and Strict No-Backward `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S` (`delayed_trace_cowm_*_sc_sh_*`, as well as the hybrid `delayed_trace_unified_*` controls):
   - *Full-Block Sample Covariance Centering (`simplex_centering=True`, `sc`)*: Centers per-token loss rewards $\tilde{\ell}_{b, t}^{(p)} = \ell_{b, t}^{(p)} - \frac{1}{K_w}\sum_{q=1}^{K_w} \ell_{b, t}^{(q)}$ across all $K_w \ge 2$ draws at a site rather than $n=2$ pairs, eliminating finite-chunk baseline variance.
   - *Target-Separated Antithetic Hadamard Vocabulary Probing (`stratified_head=True`, `sh`)*: Evaluates an exact 1D forward finite difference on the observed target coordinate $y_{b, t}$ (`logits.scatter_add_`) alongside vectorized antithetic Sylvester-Hadamard probes on non-target 256-column vocabulary slabs (`0` softmax derivatives), raising `lm_head` cosine from `0.214` to `0.946` and `wte` cosine from `0.156` to `0.625`.
   - *Intra-Block Attention-MLP Branch Decoupling (`branch_decouple_alpha=0.25`, `bd025`)*: In standard DUST `writers` draws, `attn.c_proj` noise $\xi_{\text{attn}}$ and `mlp.c_proj` noise $\xi_{\text{mlp}}$ are summed into the same residual stream $h_{\text{mid}} + \xi_{\text{attn}} + \text{MLP}(\text{norm}(h_{\text{mid}} + \xi_{\text{attn}})) + \xi_{\text{mlp}}$, creating anti-correlated common-mode variance between $\hat{u}_{\text{attn}}$ and $\hat{u}_{\text{mlp}}$. Setting `branch_decouple_alpha=0.25` routes `h_mid_pert = h_mid + xi_attn` **only** into the forward MLP branch `block.mlp(_norm(h_mid_pert))` while keeping the direct skip connection at unperturbed `h_mid_clean`, then recombines $\hat{u}_{\text{attn}} = \hat{u}_{\text{mlp}} + \alpha \hat{u}_{\text{via-mlp}}$ (`0` backward calls, `0` local reverse pullbacks), lifting Layer 3 `mlp.c_fc` cosine by **`+71.6%` (`0.0775 -> 0.1330`)**.
   - *Forward Residual-Stream Scalar Eligibility Trace (`scalar_trace=True`, `st`)*: Replaces the $16$ full-network ES forward passes for `resid_lambdas` and `x0_lambdas` with local forward inner products between clean block states $(x_{\text{in}, l}, x_0)$ and `writer_errors_attn[l]`, saving $16$ full forward passes per step (`~110–180 ms/step`).

### 3.1 Whole-Transformer Initialization Diagnostic and Unbiased Bias/Variance Decomposition
Table 3 reports gradient fidelity against analytical backpropagation across widths $D \in \{128, 256, 384, 512\}$ ($L=6, B=4, T=64, V=4096$) over $R = 32$ independent random trials (`pure_forward_width_diagnostic.json`).

**Unbiased Squared Relative Bias vs. Plug-In Sample-Mean Bias**:
For any stochastic gradient estimator $\hat{G}^{(r)}$ ($r = 1, \dots, R$) with sample mean $\bar{G}_R = \frac{1}{R}\sum_{r=1}^R \hat{G}^{(r)}$, expectation $\mu = \mathbb{E}[\hat{G}]$, and reference gradient $G_{\text{bp}}$, the naive plug-in squared relative bias $\widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{plug}} = \frac{\|\bar{G}_R - G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}{\|G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}$ has expectation:
$$\mathbb{E}\!\left[\widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{plug}}\right] = \frac{\|\mu - G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}{\|G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2} + \frac{1}{R} \text{RelVar}(\hat{G}), \qquad \text{where } \widehat{\text{RelVar}} = \frac{1}{R - 1} \sum_{r=1}^R \frac{\|\hat{G}^{(r)} - \bar{G}_R\|_F^2}{\|G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}.$$
When $\text{RelVar}$ is large, $\widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{plug}}$ is dominated by residual sample-mean variance $\text{RelVar}/R$ rather than true systematic bias. To eliminate this contamination:
1. We compute **Total Relative MSE** directly from the $R=32$ individual trial errors (with standard error of the mean across trials):
   $$\widehat{\text{TotalMSE}} = \frac{1}{R} \sum_{r=1}^R \frac{\|\hat{G}^{(r)} - G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}{\|G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}.$$
2. We report the **unbiased bias-corrected squared relative bias estimator** (with Tukey leave-one-out jackknife standard error across the $R=32$ trials):
   $$\widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{unb}} = \widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{plug}} - \frac{1}{R} \widehat{\text{RelVar}}, \qquad \mathbb{E}\!\left[\widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{unb}}\right] = \frac{\|\mu - G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}{\|G_{\text{bp}}\|_F^2}.$$
   By algebraic identity, $\widehat{\text{TotalMSE}} = \frac{R-1}{R}\widehat{\text{RelVar}} + \widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{plug}} \equiv \widehat{\text{RelVar}} + \widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{unb}}$. When $\widehat{\text{RelBias}}^2_{\text{unb}}$ is comparable to or smaller than its jackknife standard error (as in `full_seq_dot` and `token_dot`), no nonzero bias is statistically resolved, and its square root should not be interpreted as a resolved bias magnitude.

## 4. Discussion, Prior Work, and Honest Boundaries

### 4.1 Comparison with Related Non-Backpropagation Methods
1. **Published Faithful DUST [5] vs. Fairly Optimized DUST (`sc+sh`) vs. Strict No-Backward `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S` (`rev=0`) and Hybrid Local-Hub-Pullback Control (`rev=6`)**:
   Our evaluation separates five distinct regimes: (i) the simplified global-scalar control `fwd_global_scalar_K2` ($2.44794 \pm 0.11598$ BPB), which perturbs all parameters with a single scalar reward and is never used to represent DUST; (ii) **Published Faithful DUST** (`dust_faithful.py`), which reproduces published DUST learning on its reference 8-layer architecture (Stage 1: `5.8565` vs `5.7486` test nats at $P=256$, bit-for-bit suffix caching speedup $1.75\times$) and reaches $2.37697 \pm 0.06975$ (SGD, $P=64$), $2.24921 \pm 0.00044$ (Muon, $P=64$), and $2.10883 \pm 0.00138$ (Muon, $P=256$) on ClimbMix `d6`; (iii) **Fairly Optimized DUST** (`dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh`, `dust_faithful_muon_P256_sc_sh`), which equips DUST with our shared backward-free full-block covariance centering (`sc`) and target-separated Hadamard readout probing (`sh`), improving 1000-step $P=64$ holdout BPB from `2.24921 ± 0.00044` to `2.04590 ± 0.00012`; (iv) **Strict No-Backward `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S`** (`delayed_trace_cowm_*`, `hub_mode="dust_hub"`, `local_reverse_derivative_calls = 0`), which eliminates all 27 direct `hidden` (`mlp.c_fc`) suffix forward passes via SRAM-fused outgoing-column contractions (`_outgoing_col_dot_all8_kernel`, `19.1–21.3 ms` across 6 layers at $M=4096$), retains DUST's strict no-backward `o@layer` direct suffix perturbation with full-hub covariance centering (`hub2`, `hubs=[2,2,2,2,2,2]`), and executes zero reverse or local-pullback matrix multiplications; and (v) **Hybrid Local-Hub-Pullback Controls** (`delayed_trace_full_*`, `delayed_trace_unified_*`, `hub_mode="co_wm_s"`, `local_reverse_derivative_calls = L = 6`), which additionally replace the 8 `o@layer` suffix forwards by contracting $\Delta W_g^{\text{attn}}$ with $W_O$ (`attn.c_proj`) to reconstruct a local activation error tensor $\widehat{E}_g = U_g W_O$ at the attention hub. Across our two-seed Regime I pure MLP-width scaling at fixed $D=256$ (Table 8A: where Strict No-Backward `delayed_trace_cowm_P64_hub2_bd025_sc_sh_d1` (`rev=0`) achieves a descriptive two-seed advantage over `dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh` across $M \in \{256, 1024, 4096\}$ that reaches **`-0.01107 BPB`** (`2.13772 ± 0.00264` vs `2.14879 ± 0.00153`) while **`92.7 ms/step` faster** at $M=4096$), our single-seed Regime II full-model width sweep across $D \in \{128, 256, 384, 512\}$ (Table 8B: where Strict No-Backward `delayed_trace_cowm_P64_hub2_bd025_sc_sh_d1` (`rev=0`) achieves a descriptive single-seed advantage over `dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh` at all four widths while `18.1–42.4 ms/step` faster), and our two-seed `d6` benchmarks (Table 9: where Strict No-Backward `delayed_trace_cowm_P64_fast_w22_sc_sh_st_d1_wc648s` (`rev=0`) reaches **`2.04412 ± 0.00014` BPB** in `633.6 s` timed / `635.2 s` complete vs 1000-step `dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh` `2.04590 ± 0.00012` at `648.0 s` timed / `649.3 s` complete, and Strict No-Backward `delayed_trace_cowm_P256_bd025_sc_sh_st_d1_wc307s` (`rev=0`) reaches **`2.08637 ± 0.01282` BPB** in `294.9 s` timed / `297.9 s` complete at $P=256$ vs `dust_faithful_muon_P256_sc_sh_wc307s` `2.11372 ± 0.00121` at `302.0 s` timed / `306.2 s` complete), these single-seed and two-seed comparisons establish a consistent descriptive performance and efficiency gain under strict no-backward execution (`rev=0`).
2. **Local-Reverse-Credit Controls (Ren et al., ICLR 2023) [8]**:
   Among labelled local-reverse-credit controls (which eliminate the global reverse-depth sweep, `bwd_calls = 0`, while retaining block-local reverse sensitivity pullbacks), our sequential and component ablation ladder in Table 5 isolates each intervention on `d6`: (a) omitting inter-block sublayer Jacobians under fixed final-state supervision discrepancy (`backprop` $1.45172 \to$ `block_fixed_top_discrepancy_bp` $1.62152$, $+0.16980$ BPB); (b) replacing fixed final-state discrepancy with intermediate block-state supervision (`block_local_greedy_bp_tophead` $1.58085$, $-0.04067$ BPB); (c) mixing intermediate-block losses into `lm_head` (`block_local_greedy_bp` $1.57236$, $-0.00849$ BPB); (d) uncoupled forward surrogate at $V=8192$ (`fwd_streaming_oracle_fullvocab` $1.74582$, $+0.17346$ BPB); (e) isolating `+MLP-Pullback` ($1.73609$, $-0.00973$ BPB), `+CM-Attn-WH` ($1.71756$, $-0.01853$ BPB), and `+Hadamard-QK` ($1.64800$, $-0.06956$ BPB); and (f) replacing analytic linear transposes with $K=S=8$ parameter-space `Co-WM-S` (`fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_fullvocab_K8` $1.64719 \pm 0.00067$, `fwd_streaming_cowm_coupled_K8` $1.78546 \pm 0.00297$, `fwd_streaming_cowm_K8` $1.86518 \pm 0.00011$).
3. **Randomized Automatic Differentiation (Oktay et al., ICLR 2021) [12]**:
   RAD inserts random projections $P = R R^\top$ into reverse-mode derivative chains, whereas `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S` (`hub_mode="dust_hub"`, `rev=0`) contracts neuron $i$'s own outgoing column $\mathbf{v}^{(i)} \cdot \Delta \mathbf{v}_g^{(i, m)}$ against local forward eligibility traces without any reverse-depth or local backward pass.
4. **Concurrent Residual Reader Paths in Depth-Delayed Weight Maximization [13]**:
   Concurrent work on this benchmark (*Residual reader paths in depth-delayed Weight Maximization*, `general_pretraining_19/v3` [13], which updated its scope from `v1` and withdrew its earlier fixed-probe scalability claim) studies depth-delayed weight-change packet transport across a 33-tick residual credit graph without activation perturbations (`no_bwd_lr_scaled_d1`, reaching $2.393591$ holdout BPB at $1000$ steps on `d6`). Whereas [13] propagates credit through multi-step packet transport across the entire network graph without direct writer suffix perturbations, Strict No-Backward `Delayed-Trace-Co-WM-S` (`delayed_trace_cowm_*`, `rev=0`) retains prefix-cached suffix forward perturbations on residual-stream writers (`mlp.c_proj`, `attn.c_proj`, `wte`, `lm_head`) and `o@layer` hubs while replacing all 27 `hidden` (`mlp.c_fc`) suffix forwards with delayed $d_{\text{delay}}=1$ outgoing-column trace contractions using $S$-token Sylvester-Hadamard probe codebooks, reaching **`2.06189 ± 0.00021` BPB** (`1000` steps, $P=64$, `rev=0`), **`2.04412 ± 0.00014` BPB** (`633.6 s` timed / `635.2 s` complete matched wall-clock, $P=64$, `rev=0`), and **`2.08637 ± 0.01282` BPB** (`294.9 s` timed / `297.9 s` complete matched wall-clock, $P=256$, `rev=0`) on `d6`.
5. **Concurrent Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization [14]**:
   Concurrent work published within the concurrency window (*Three-Stage Delayed Eligibility-Trace Weight Maximization for Backward-Free Transformer Pretraining: Stream Partitioning, Forward Attention-Filtered Value Traces, and Post-Installation Credit Debiasing*, `general_pretraining_20/v1` [14]) independently formalizes delayed outgoing-column trace contraction across two-layer compositions together with stream partitioning, a forward attention-filtered value eligibility trace $\widetilde{X}_{v,t} = \sum_{s \le t} A_{t,s} X_{v,s}$ (their Proposition 2), and post-installation credit debiasing. Our work complements and differs from [14] in five specific respects: (i) **Audit of Local-Hub-Pullback vs. Strict No-Backward Attention Credit**: we explicitly prove and audit that contracting $\Delta W_g^{\text{attn}}$ with $W_O$ (`Hub-Co-WM-S`, `hub_mode="co_wm_s"`, `rev=6`) algebraically reconstructs the local activation error pullback $\widehat{E}_g = U_g W_O$, and we directly ablate unweighted ($\gamma=1$) $W_O$-free forward causal value pooling (`record_attn_v_forward_pooled_input`, `rev=0`, `2.16374` holdout BPB in Table 10) against DUST's $\gamma=0.98$ exponentially decayed local value perturbation (`causal_v_trace=False`, `rev=0`, **`2.12965` holdout BPB**), showing that exponential recency weighting suppresses long-range attention credit variance under $P=64$; (ii) **Full-Hub Covariance Centering (`hub2`)**: we identify that DUST's default 6-layer `P=64` schedule (`hubs=[2,2,1,1,1,1]`) leaves deeper attention hubs `o@2..5` with $1$ draw where covariance centering is inactive, and show that reallocating 4 of the 27 saved `hidden` draws to `hubs=[2,2,2,2,2,2]` (`hub2`) improves 200-step `d6` holdout BPB from `2.14226` to **`2.12965`** (`-0.01261 BPB`) under strict no-backward execution (`rev=0`); (iii) **Intra-Block Sibling Branch Decoupling (`bd025`) and Target-Separated Hadamard Readout Probing (`sh`)**: we derive conditional sibling decoupling (Proposition 1) and demonstrate its empirical utility across both strict no-backward (`delayed_trace_cowm_*`) and local-reverse-credit (`fwd_streaming_*`) families; (iv) **SRAM-Fused Triton Contraction Kernel (`_outgoing_col_dot_all8_kernel`)**: we eliminate intermediate memory materialization to achieve $19.1\text{--}21.3\text{ ms}$ contraction overhead across 6 layers at $M=4096$ (Table 7B); and (v) **Controlled Multi-Width Scaling (Regime I Fixed-$D$ MLP-Width & Regime II Full-Model Width) Against Faithful & Fairly Optimized DUST**: we validate our strict no-backward (`rev=0`) and hybrid (`rev=6`) estimators against both published Faithful DUST (`dust_faithful.py`, validated on Stage 1) and Fairly Optimized DUST (`dust_faithful_muon_P64_sc_sh`, `dust_faithful_muon_P256_sc_sh`) across $M \in \{256, 1024, 4096\}$, $D \in \{128, 256, 384, 512\}$, and matched-wall-clock $P \in \{64, 256\}$ budgets.

### 4.2 Resource and Task Boundaries
1. **No General Resource Advantage Over Checkpointed Backpropagation**:
   We do not claim a general memory or throughput victory over backpropagation. In 1000-step pretraining on `d6`, activation-checkpointed backpropagation (`backprop_ckpt`) achieves lower validation loss ($1.45179$ vs $1.64719\text{--}2.37697$ BPB), lower step latency ($33.3\text{ ms}$ vs $163.9\text{--}2271.0\text{ ms}$), and lower peak GPU memory ($1731.4\text{ MB}$ vs $1763.0\text{--}5128.7\text{ MB}$).
2. **Reduced-Horizon Controlled Study vs. Full-Horizon Pretraining**:
   Our ClimbMix experiments use micro-batch shape $B=16, T=512$ ($8,192$ tokens/step, $8.192\text{M}$ tokens over 1000 steps and $16.384\text{M}$ tokens over 2000 steps) to permit controlled multi-width and 2-seed ablations across strict no-backward methods (`dust_faithful_*`, `delayed_trace_cowm_*`, `rev=0`), hybrid local-hub-pullback controls (`delayed_trace_full_*`, `delayed_trace_unified_*`, `rev=6`), and block-local reverse-credit controls (`fwd_streaming_*`), alongside our Stage 1 reference validation at $B=8, T=2048$ (`dust_stage1_reference_validation.json`).
