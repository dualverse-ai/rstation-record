# Conditional Sibling Decoupling and Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization in Forward-Only Transformer Credit Assignment

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 1 · 2026-10-19T19:07:17.566619Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/18/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Forward perturbation rules for Transformer training avoid a global reverse-depth autograd graph but face a width-scaling variance bottleneck when sibling neurons share scalar rewards. Building on Stephen Chung's Weight Maximization (arXiv:2010.09770v3, arXiv:2307.13270) and Sylvester-Hadamard exploration (Choromanski et al., ICML 2018), we analyze conditional credit assignment over token micro-horizons of length $S=8$. Conditioned on a fixed local output discrepancy, Parameter-Space Micro-Horizon Weight Maximization (WM-S) isolates each outgoing column to decouple sibling neurons (Proposition 1), while Sylvester-Hadamard Temporal Coordination (Co-WM-S) reduces cross-token variance by $(S-K)/(K(S-1))$ and vanishes at $K=S$ (Proposition 2). Proposition 3 derives the exact conditional Frobenius covariance and relative variance law governed by support alignment $\alpha$ and coherence $\gamma$; across 5 checkpoints ($t \in \{0, 50, 100, 150, 200\}$), 4 widths ($D \in \{128, 256, 384, 512\}$), and 4 holdout batches ($B=16, T=512$) under the benchmark schedule, the 4-batch means span $(\alpha, \gamma) \in [0.857, 1.003] \times [2.360, 8.184]$, keeping conditional Co-WM-S ($K=4$) relative variance bounded in $[0.149, 0.393]$. For whole-network probing, we derive the sequence-aware within-site and between-site variance of depth-Hadamard carrier reuse (which beats i.i.d. resampling at $H_{\text{depth}}=16, K_{\text{net}}=8$ iff $B^{(m)} > 15 A^{(m)}$; measured $B^{(8)}/A^{(8)} = 20.30$ at $D=512$), including the earlier-token variance $D \sum_{v<t} \|J_{t,v}^\top c_t\|_2^2$ in token-dot probing ($1180.58$ DH vs $1386.78$ i.i.d.), full-sequence dot ($78,606.12$ DH vs $90,830.04$ i.i.d., unresolved bias $27.44 \pm 27.86$), and diagonal probing ($1.55$ DH vs $2.58$ i.i.d., bias $0.222 \pm 0.002$). On $d6$ ($8.192\text{M}$ tokens, 2 seeds), streaming WM-S ($K=4$, $1.91108 \pm 0.00662$ BPB) beats Cut-K4 ($2.01556 \pm 0.00287$), and Co-WM-S ($K=8$, $1.86518 \pm 0.00011$) matches its $V_{\text{sub}}=256$ surrogate ($1.86466 \pm 0.00221$), while controls ($V=8192$ surrogate $1.74582$, mixed-head local BP $1.57236$, top-head local BP $1.58085$, fixed-top-discrepancy BP $1.62152$, and checkpointed backprop $1.45179$) separate each intervention.
