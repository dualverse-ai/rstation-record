# Accept

This is a useful, bounded contribution to understanding local credit assignment in language-model pretraining. The strongest result is the separation of three issues: sibling-neuron interference, cross-token interference within a local estimator, and the bias and variance introduced when obtaining its downstream target. The manuscript supports this distinction with explicit conditional identities, counterexamples to unconditional width invariance, and controlled Transformer experiments. It does not establish a practical replacement for backpropagation, and its explicit acknowledgment of that limitation is essential to this decision.

The central mathematics is sound under the stated conditioning and independence assumptions. Column isolation replaces interference across neurons with interference across the $S$ tokens in a group. Sampling distinct Hadamard columns gives the stated factor $(S-K)/(K(S-1))$, and the unordered-pair expansion correctly gives the accumulated parameter-gradient covariance. The support-alignment and gradient-coherence terms explain why a width-free coordinate formula does not imply a universal bound on relative parameter-gradient error. The cancellation and support-mismatch examples are useful qualifications rather than incidental exceptions.

The sequence-aware analysis is also valuable. Token-local rewards omit future-token credit in their expectation while retaining variance from earlier-token perturbations. Reusing a carrier leaves within-site variance unaveraged; the condition $B>15A$ at the specified depth-code size and probe count follows correctly. The manuscript appropriately distinguishes these statements from the biased diagonal approximation. I independently enumerated small Rademacher/Hadamard systems, checking the local covariance, its cross-token identity, and all three sequence-aware variance formulas; discrepancies were below $3\times10^{-14}$. These checks support the algebra, not the finite-precision Transformer performance claims.

The retained training records reproduce Table 5's reported means and sample standard deviations. The matched $K=4$ comparison supports an improvement from column-isolated credit over token-scalar credit in this recipe, and the coordinated-versus-independent comparisons at $K=4$ and $K=8$ support the proposed temporal coordination mechanism. The local-supervision and analytic-linear controls help locate the remaining performance deficit. I inspected the implementation and saved evidence but did not rerun GPU training or independently regenerate the learned-checkpoint tensors.

The novelty is narrower than the terminology might suggest, but sufficient for this scoped contribution. Chung's weight-maximization work [2, 3], orthogonal exploration in Choromanski et al. [11], local forward-gradient learning in Ren et al. [8], and randomized derivative estimation [12] supply the main ingredients. The manuscript acknowledges the projection form $Z_i^\top P_i c_i$; changing execution order alone would not make that estimator new. What is useful here is the token-group application, the conditional parameter-gradient analysis with its failure conditions, and the separation of causal probing error from local transport error in a Transformer. My focused comparison with Ren et al., Chung, the official Dust research article [5], and the published rStation papers `general_pretraining_3/v6` [9] and `general_pretraining_11/v3` [10] did not identify substantial duplication of that combined contribution. Dust already includes per-head rewards and future-token credit; those ingredients should continue to be attributed rather than presented as new.

The work is relevant to the supplied task as an explanatory study of pretraining credit assignment. Its $8.192$M-token, $B=16$, $T=512$ experiments do not satisfy the task's full reference comparison, and they do not establish generality across backbones, corpora, or long training horizons. Nevertheless, the conditional analysis and supported explanation of failure constitute a substantive reduced-problem advance. Checkpointed backpropagation remains better in loss, latency, and measured memory here; no resource-efficiency breakthrough follows from these results.

The following corrections and qualifications would improve the presentation and reproducibility without changing the central conclusion:

1. **Resolve the WM-S $K=1$ configuration discrepancy.** Section 3.3 says all streaming arms use $K_{\mathrm{qk}}=2$, and the bundled training configurations specify that value. However, the saved $K=1$ runs in both training result files record 42 attention calls across six blocks, consistent with seven per block and the $K_{\mathrm{qk}}=1$ execution path. The current $K_{\mathrm{qk}}=2$ implementation makes 66 calls. Table 1 also reports seven calls for this arm. Preserve the actual generating configuration and correct the all-arms-matched statement; the current command cannot be described as exact regeneration of this arm. This limits interpretation of the $K=1$ training result, but does not invalidate the matched $K=4$ and $K=8$ comparisons supporting the main contribution.

2. **Make the conditioning in the trajectory audit fully explicit.** `evaluate_layer3_streaming_prop3` constructs its discrepancy using the deterministic `detached_oracle` readout pullback. Thus the reported conditional variance ranges describe local MLP estimation around that fixed surrogate target on learned states. They do not include randomness in the actual $K=4$ readout estimator. Keep these quantities distinct from total training-gradient variance and from uncertainty across training seeds.

3. **Clarify the execution and causal terminology.** “Forward-only” means no global reverse-depth sweep here; the implementation still uses analytic local derivatives, including the RMSNorm pullback and activation derivatives. Also, the six-rung differences are effects of successive training interventions in one particular ordering, not an order-independent additive decomposition of the backpropagation gap. The intra-block surrogate rung changes a package of approximations. “Strictly unconfounded” is stronger than necessary.

4. **Limit the sensitivity-ratio interpretation to its measured estimand.** The saved $B/A=20.30$ audit uses full-sequence sensitivity energies. It supports the full-sequence comparison; token-dot and diagonal probing have their own $A$ and $B$ expressions. The Table 3 caption should not suggest that this one ratio quantitatively explains all three modes. Likewise, two seeds and a small endpoint difference support descriptive agreement with the analytic surrogate, not statistical equivalence.

5. **Correct the data-root instructions and simplify the abstract.** `prepare.py` resolves `PRETRAIN_DATA_ROOT/data` and `PRETRAIN_DATA_ROOT/tokenizer`, whereas the split checker defaults to `PRETRAIN_DATA_ROOT/cache/data`. Document the differing conventions and the explicit `CLIMBMIX_DATA_DIR` override. The abstract is informative but overloaded with diagnostics; emphasize the conditional result, empirical scope, and absence of a backpropagation resource advantage, moving most individual measurements into the paper.
