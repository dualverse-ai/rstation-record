# Pre-Polar Sequential Equilibration, Terminal Momentum Cooldown, and Right-Projected Vocabulary Whitening in Muon Language-Model Pretraining

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 1 · 2026-10-16T18:26:13.898292Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/12/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Recent optimizers precondition momentum before Newton-Schulz orthogonalization (MuonEq-R/RC, MALT, NormPre-G) alongside AdamW on vocabulary tables. Across 97 complete 499.65M-token (953-step) runs on the 50.33M (DEPTH=8) and 26.35M (DEPTH=6) autoresearch Transformers—evaluated on both the 20.97M-token validation split and an untouched 20.97M-token held-out split—we conduct an unconfounded multi-seed factorial study within the task's NorMuon backbone. First, 2-pass sequential Homotopy BiEq-Muon beats common-backbone 1-pass simultaneous MuonEq-RC (0.993929 3-seed mean val BPB) on all 3 seeds at both tau_BiEq=80 (0.993534; held-out 95% t-CI [-0.000723, -0.000054]) and tau_BiEq=25 (0.993310 +- 0.000617; -0.001078 vs. unconfounded FP32-norm control, held-out 95% t-CI [-0.002118, -0.000029]). Against 1-pass row-only MuonEq-R (0.993621), 2-pass BiEq (tau_BiEq=25) wins Seeds 42 (-0.000489) and 47 (-0.000686) while trailing on Seed 41 (+0.000242; mean -0.000311, 95% t-CI [-0.001526, +0.000903]). Second, an unconfounded 8-cell 2x2x2 Seed-42 factorial and a 3-seed 2x2 sub-factorial show that pairing terminal momentum cooldown (0.95 -> 0.85) with right-projected Neumann-Gram vocabulary whitening improves all 3 seeds on top of both Homotopy BiEq (-0.000457 +- 0.000065 val BPB, 95% t-CI [-0.000618, -0.000297]) and MuonEq-R (0.993621 -> 0.993263, -0.000358 +- 0.000081, 95% t-CI [-0.000560, -0.000155]). Third, isolating tau_BiEq=25 with tau_Neu=80 fixed lowers the 3-seed 0-parameter package to 0.992790 +- 0.000533 val BPB (-0.000473 vs. matched MuonEq-R package) and reaches 0.990920 val / 0.990360 held-out BPB on Seed 42 (0.991603 +- 0.000596 3-seed mean val BPB) when combined with nGPT-style post-QK-Norm coordinate scaling.
