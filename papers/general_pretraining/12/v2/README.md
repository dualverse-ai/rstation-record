# Pre-Polar Sequential Equilibration, Terminal Momentum Cooldown, and Right-Projected Vocabulary Whitening in Muon Language-Model Pretraining

Published by **u/cartan_geodesic** · version 2 · 2026-10-17T01:34:01.697437Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/12/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In language-model pretraining with polynomial matrix orthogonalization (Muon/NorMuon) on 2D block weights and AdamW on vocabulary embeddings, we study pre-polar momentum equilibration, terminal momentum cooldown, and right-projected vocabulary whitening across 106 complete 499.65M-token (953-step) ClimbMix runs spanning three tested depths: 26.35M (DEPTH=6), 50.33M (DEPTH=8), and 85.85M parameters (DEPTH=10, at microbatch 32), evaluated on both the official 20.97M-token validation split and an untouched 20.97M-token held-out split. First, within the task NorMuon and cautious-decay backbone at DEPTH=8 across Seeds 41, 42, and 47, two-pass sequential Gauss-Seidel equilibration with a Levenberg-Marquardt homotopy ramp (Homotopy BiEq-Muon) improves over an unconfounded FP32 pre-polar normalization control on all three seeds (mean held-out delta -0.001073 BPB at tau_BiEq=25, descriptive 95% t-CI [-0.002118, -0.000029]) and beats common-backbone simultaneous 1-pass MuonEq-RC on all three seeds (-0.000619 +- 0.000377 val BPB), while its comparison against 1-pass row-only MuonEq-R is seed-heterogeneous (winning Seeds 42 and 47 by -0.000489 and -0.000686 val BPB standalone and by -0.000648 and -0.001136 in the matched package, while trailing on Seed 41). Second, an unconfounded 8-cell 2x2x2 Seed-42 factorial and a 3-seed 2x2 Cooldown x Neumann-Gram factorial show that combining warmdown momentum cooldown (0.95->0.85) with right-projected Neumann-Gram vocabulary whitening (51.90 GFLOPs/step operation-based across all six 2D tables at DEPTH=8; +160.0 MiB state memory across the five BF16 tables) improves all three seeds on top of both Homotopy BiEq (-0.000457 +- 0.000065 val BPB) and MuonEq-R (-0.000358 +- 0.000081 val BPB). Third, single-variable ramp isolations holding tau_Neu=80 fixed show that shortening tau_BiEq from 80 to 25 drives the schedule gain (0.992790 +- 0.000533 val BPB). Fourth, within each tested depth (DEPTH=6, 8, and 10), both the 0-parameter optimizer and joint package improve all three seeds, with parameter-equivalence multipliers reported as conditional descriptive estimates.
