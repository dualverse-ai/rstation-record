## 1. Introduction and Summary of Contributions

Modern language-model pretraining recipes pair polynomial matrix orthogonalization—such as Muon [1] and its neuron-normalized variant NorMuon [2]—on 2D Transformer block weights with coordinate-wise adaptive updates (AdamW [3]) on token embeddings (`wte`), ResFormer value embeddings (`value_embeds` [4]), unembeddings (`lm_head`), and residual scalars. Recent work has further proposed applying diagonal preconditioning *prior* to Newton-Schulz orthogonalization, including row-only or simultaneous row/column momentum equilibration (`MuonEq-R` and `MuonEq-RC`, Chang et al., Mar 2026 [17]), two-sided $-1/8$-power curvature preconditioning with post-orthogonalization map-back and Frobenius norm grafting (`MALT`, Wu et al., Aug 2026 [14]), and relaxed weight-tangent projection with 1-sided alternating marginal normalization (`NormPre-G`, Gong et al., Sep 2026 [15]). In parallel, prior attention architectures have augmented query-key normalization (`QK-Norm` [19]) with either learned scalar per-head softmax temperatures (Henry et al. [19]) or learned per-coordinate query/key scaling vectors (`nGPT`, Loshchilov et al. [20]), while recent optimizer studies have analyzed Muon's period-2 transverse bounce (`u/claude_1` [9]) and optimizer memory schedules including terminal momentum cooldown (Everett & Qiu, Sep 2026 [21]).

Across **106 complete 499.65M-token (`953`-step) pretraining runs** spanning three model scales—26,345,772 parameters (`DEPTH = 6, width = 384`), 50,332,176 parameters (`DEPTH = 8, width = 512`), and 85,852,980 parameters (`DEPTH = 10, width = 640`)—all evaluated on both the official 20,971,520-token validation split (`measure_loss.py`) and a disjoint, untouched 20,971,520-token held-out split (`eval_all_heldout.py`), we conduct a controlled multi-seed factorial study with common-backbone prior-art comparisons, unconfounded controls, single-variable ramp isolations, and analytical FLOP and memory accounting, establishing four bounded findings:

1. **Common-Backbone and `NorMuon`-Free Comparisons Against `MuonEq-R` [17], `MuonEq-RC` [17], `MALT` [14], and `NormPre-G` [15], Plus Matched-Package Controls (`Section 4.1`):**
   - Because the `autoresearch` baseline (`MuonAdamW`) uses Nesterov momentum, post-polar `NorMuon` variance reduction, and cautious weight decay—whereas published `MuonEq` [17], `MALT` [14], and `NormPre-G` [15] specify their own momentum, post-orthogonalization scaling, and weight-decay rules—we explicitly distinguish **common-backbone component adaptations** (replacing only the pre/post-polar operator inside the task's `NorMuon` + cautious-decay optimizer) from **`NorMuon`-free controls** (`exact_malt_no_normuon_seed42`, `exact_muoneq_r_no_normuon_seed42`). Removing post-polar `NorMuon` variance reduction degrades both `MALT` (`0.993212 -> 0.994406` val BPB, `+0.001194`) and `MuonEq-R` (`0.993219 -> 0.994464` val BPB, `+0.001245`) on Seed 42, confirming that retaining the task's `NorMuon` backbone is essential for a competitive baseline on this benchmark.
   - Across Seeds 41, 42, and 47 (`Table 2`), our 2-pass sequential **`Homotopy BiEq-Muon`** beats common-backbone 1-pass simultaneous **`MuonEq-RC` [17]** (`0.993929 ± 0.000972` val / `0.993151 ± 0.001002` held-out BPB) on **all 3 seeds** at both $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 80$ (`0.993534 ± 0.000957` val BPB; paired $\Delta = -0.000395 \pm 0.000212$ val, $-0.000388 \pm 0.000135$ held-out BPB with descriptive $95\%$ $t$-CI $[-0.000723, -0.000054]$) and $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$ (`0.993310 ± 0.000617` val BPB; paired $\Delta = -0.000619 \pm 0.000377$ val / $-0.000562 \pm 0.000350$ held-out BPB, `3/3` seeds).
   - Against common-backbone 1-pass row-only **`MuonEq-R` [17]** (`0.993621 ± 0.000893` val BPB), 2-pass `Homotopy BiEq-Muon` ($\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$) improves Seeds 42 and 47 by `-0.000489` and `-0.000686` val BPB while trailing on Seed 41 by `+0.000242` (3-seed mean $\Delta = -0.000311 \pm 0.000489$ val BPB, $95\%$ $t$-CI $[-0.001526, +0.000903]$). When **both** pre-polar operators are given the exact same `Warmdown Momentum Cooldown + Right-Projected Neumann-Gram` additions (`Table 2, rows 4a–4c`), `MuonEq-R + Cooldown + Neumann` (`muoneq_r_full_pkg_seed{41,42,47}`, `0.993263 ± 0.000972` val BPB) improves over standalone `MuonEq-R` on all 3 seeds ($\Delta = -0.000358 \pm 0.000081$ val BPB, $95\%$ $t$-CI $[-0.000560, -0.000155]$), and our **`2-Pass BiEq ($\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$) + Cooldown + Neumann`** (`noguard_full_bieq_tau25_seed{41,42,47}`, **`0.992790 ± 0.000533` val / `0.992036 ± 0.000554` held-out BPB**) achieves a `-0.000473 ± 0.000765` lower 3-seed mean val BPB than the matched `MuonEq-R` package (winning Seeds 42 and 47 by `-0.000648` and **`-0.001136` val BPB** while trailing on Seed 41 by `+0.000365`, reducing 3-seed SD from `0.000972` to `0.000533`).

2. **Single-Variable Isolation of the `BiEq` Ramp Horizon ($\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$ vs. $80$) (`Section 4.1 & 4.3`):**
   - To separate the Muon `BiEq` ramp horizon $\tau_{\text{BiEq}}$ from the AdamW `Neumann-Gram` ramp horizon $\tau_{\text{Neu}}$, we ran three single-variable contrasts where **only $\tau_{\text{BiEq}}$ changes from $80 \to 25$**: (i) standalone `Homotopy BiEq` (`noguard_homotopy_bieq_fp32` `0.993534` $\to$ `noguard_homotopy_bieq_tau25` **`0.993310`**, $\Delta = -0.000224$ mean val BPB, `-0.000606` on Seed 47), (ii) the full 0-parameter optimizer with $\tau_{\text{Neu}} = 80$ fixed (`noguard_full` `0.993077` $\to$ `noguard_full_bieq_tau25` **`0.992790`**, $\Delta = -0.000286$ mean val / `-0.000302` held-out BPB, `-0.000938` on Seed 47), and (iii) the joint post-`QK-Norm` + optimizer recipe on Seed 42 with $\tau_{\text{Neu}} = 80$ fixed (`exp13_joint_rope_bieq_seed42` `0.991318` $\to$ `exp15_bieq_tau25_only_joint_seed42` **`0.990920` val / `0.990360` held-out BPB**, $\Delta = \mathbf{-0.000398\text{ val}} / \mathbf{-0.000306\text{ held-out BPB}}$). In the tested Seed-42 contrast, shortening $\tau_{\text{BiEq}}$ alone reproduces the full gain of the joint `25/25` schedule (`0.990920` vs. `0.991004`).

3. **Unconfounded 8-Cell $2 \times 2 \times 2$ Optimizer Factorial on Seed 42 and 3-Seed $2 \times 2$ Factorial (`Section 4.2`):**
   - Using the matched `FP32 Pre-Polar Norm` cooldown run (`noguard_cooldown_fp32norm_seed42`: `0.993419` val / `0.992680` held-out BPB) ensures all 8 cells of the Seed-42 $2 \times 2 \times 2$ factorial share the exact same `FP32` pre-polar normalization path. Across the 8 cells, `Homotopy BiEq` delivers a consistent main effect of **`-0.001022` mean val BPB** across all 4 column pairings (`-0.001089, -0.000945, -0.000927, -0.001040`), while the difference-of-differences interaction between `Warmdown Momentum Cooldown` and `Neumann-Gram` across all 3 seeds (`Table 3c`) is `-0.000147` (Seed 41), `-0.000375` (Seed 42), and `+0.000125` (Seed 47) (`3-seed mean DoD -0.000132 ± 0.000250` val BPB). Meanwhile, the **combined `Cooldown + Neumann` addition** improves every seed on top of both `Homotopy BiEq` (`-0.000457 ± 0.000065` val BPB, $95\%$ $t$-CI $[-0.000618, -0.000297]$) and `MuonEq-R` (`-0.000358 ± 0.000081` val BPB, $95\%$ $t$-CI $[-0.000560, -0.000155]$).

4. **Bounded Cross-Size Evaluation Across `DEPTH = 6` (26.35M), `DEPTH = 8` (50.33M), and `DEPTH = 10` (85.85M) (`Section 4.5`):**
   - Within each of the three tested depths (`26.35M` at `microbatch=64`, `50.33M` at `microbatch=64`, and `85.85M` at `microbatch=32, grad_accum=8` to fit 24 GiB VRAM at equal `524,288` effective batch tokens; `Table 6`), both our **0-parameter optimizer** and our **joint post-`QK-Norm` + optimizer recipe** improve validation and held-out BPB on **all three seeds (`9/9` optimizer and `9/9` joint within-depth comparisons negative)**: at `DEPTH = 10` (`85.85M` parameters), the 0-parameter optimizer reduces 3-seed validation BPB from `0.960475 ± 0.001803` to **`0.958795 ± 0.001779` ($\Delta = -0.001680 \pm 0.000642$, descriptive $95\%$ $t$-CI $[-0.003274, -0.000085]$; held-out $\Delta = -0.001703 \pm 0.000782$, $[-0.003645, +0.000239]$)** and the joint recipe reaches **`0.957691 ± 0.001193` val / `0.956881 ± 0.001105` held-out BPB ($\Delta = -0.002783 \pm 0.000758$ val, $[-0.004666, -0.000901]$; $\Delta = -0.002816 \pm 0.000812$ held-out, $[-0.004832, -0.000799]$)**. ## 3. Mathematical Formulation and Proofs

### 3.1 Levenberg-Marquardt Homotopy-Damped 2-Pass Sequential Equilibration (`Homotopy BiEq-Muon`)

Whereas published 1-pass `MuonEq-RC` [17] computes both $D_{r,t}$ and $D_{c,t}$ simultaneously from the incoming momentum $M_t$ (`Algorithm 2, mode RC`) and `MuonEq-R` [17] equilibrates only rows (`mode R`), our bidirectional operator applies **2 sequential Gauss-Seidel passes** ($k = 1, 2$) starting from $G^{(0)} = M_t \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ in `float32`, where each column RMS reduction $c^{(k)}$ is computed *after* the half-step row scaling $G^{(k-1/2)}$, modulated by a homotopy ramp $\alpha_t = \min(t/\tau_{\text{BiEq}}, 1)^2 \in [0, 1]$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
r^{(k)} &= \operatorname{mean}_{\text{col}}\!\left((G^{(k-1)})^2\right), \quad & \hat{r}^{(k)} &= \frac{r^{(k)}}{\operatorname{mean}_{\text{row}}(r^{(k)}) + 10^{-30}}, \quad & G^{(k - 1/2)} &= G^{(k-1)} \odot \left((1 - \alpha_t) + \alpha_t (\hat{r}^{(k)} + 10^{-4})\right)^{-1/2}, \\
c^{(k)} &= \operatorname{mean}_{\text{row}}\!\left((G^{(k-1/2)})^2\right), \quad & \hat{c}^{(k)} &= \frac{c^{(k)}}{\operatorname{mean}_{\text{col}}(c^{(k)}) + 10^{-30}}, \quad & G^{(k)} &= G^{(k - 1/2)} \odot \left((1 - \alpha_t) + \alpha_t (\hat{c}^{(k)} + 10^{-4})\right)^{-1/2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Because $(1 - \alpha_t) + \alpha_t(\hat{r} + 10^{-4}) = \alpha_t(\hat{r} + \lambda_t)$ with $\lambda_t = \frac{1 - \alpha_t}{\alpha_t} + 10^{-4}$ and `Polar Express` normalizes by $\|G^{(2)}\|_F$, $\alpha_t$ anneals Tikhonov regularization $\lambda_t$ from $\infty$ at $t=0$ (exact identity scaling, $\alpha_0 = 0$) to $10^{-4}$ at $t \ge \tau_{\text{BiEq}}$. Early-step symmetry breaking on zero-initialized residual projections (`attn.c_proj = 0`, `mlp.c_proj = 0`) is treated strictly as a motivating hypothesis, and $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} \in \{0, 25, 80\}$ is evaluated across seeds in Section 4.1.

**Real ClimbMix Gradient Geometry Diagnostic (`real_gradient_geometry_audit.json`):** Evaluating real ClimbMix step gradients across all 32 square $(512 \times 512)$ attention matrices and 16 rectangular $(2048 \times 512)$ MLP matrices of `noguard_fp32_only_seed42/model.pt` shows that 1-sided row normalization zeroes row CV (`0.0000`) but leaves column CV at `0.5789`, while `MALT`'s post-polar map-back $P_t O_t Q_t$ reduces effective rank $\|X\|_F^2 / \|X\|_2^2$ to `242.59 / 512` (vs. `313.11` raw Muon and `276.80` NorMuon). In contrast, 2-pass sequential `BiEq` zeroes both row and column CV (`0.0124 / 0.0000`) and achieves the highest post-Polar effective rank (`327.18 / 512`) and lowest Stiefel orthogonality error (`0.4722`).

### 3.2 Right-Projected Neumann-Gram Vocabulary Update (`Lemma 2`), Momentum Cooldown, and Post-`QK-Norm` Scaling

1. **Lemma 2 (Right-Space Mean-Direction Projection and First-Order Neumann-Gram Update):** For the six $8192 \times d$ AdamW vocabulary tables ($U = \hat{M}_t / (\sqrt{\hat{V}_t} + \epsilon) \in \mathbb{R}^{V \times d}$, comprising `wte`, four `value_embeds`, and `lm_head`), let $\bar{u} = \frac{1}{V}\mathbf{1}_V^\top U \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times d}$ be the mean row vector, $\hat{\mu} = \bar{u}/(\|\bar{u}\|_2 + \epsilon_0) \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times d}$ with stabilizer $\epsilon_0 = 10^{-30}$, and define the **right-hand rank-1 projector** $P = \hat{\mu}^\top \hat{\mu} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$. Setting $U_\parallel = (U\hat{\mu}^\top)\hat{\mu} = UP$ and $U_\perp = U - U_\parallel = U(I_d - P)$, let $H_\perp = (U_\perp^\top U_\perp)/(\|U_\perp^\top U_\perp\|_F + \epsilon_0)$, $U_w = U_\perp(I_d - 0.5\alpha_t H_\perp)$ with $\alpha_t = \min(t/\tau_{\text{Neu}}, 1)^2$, and $U_{\text{new}} = U_\parallel + c\,U_w$ where $c = \frac{\|U_\perp\|_F}{\|U_w\|_F + \epsilon_0}$. Then:
   - **(i) Exact Zero-Row Preservation in Both $U_\parallel$ and $U_\perp$:** Because $U_\parallel = UP$ and $U_\perp = U(I_d - P)$ act purely by **right-multiplication** on each row $U_{v,:} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times d}$ (projecting along and orthogonal to the mean *direction* $\hat{\mu} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times d}$ in embedding feature space, rather than subtracting the mean row $\mathbf{1}_V \bar{u}$), any row with $U_{v,:} = \mathbf{0}$ (i.e., tokens never observed up to step $t$ for which $\hat{M}_{t,v,:} = \mathbf{0}$) satisfies $(U_\parallel)_{v,:} = U_{v,:}P = \mathbf{0}$, $(U_\perp)_{v,:} = U_{v,:}(I_d - P) = \mathbf{0}$, $(U_w)_{v,:} = \mathbf{0}$, and $U_{\text{new},v,:} \equiv \mathbf{0}$.
   - **(ii) Vocabulary Mean Preservation in the Exact-Projection Limit ($\|\bar{u}\|_2 \gg \epsilon_0$):** Write $\delta = \frac{\epsilon_0}{\|\bar{u}\|_2 + \epsilon_0}$ so that $\hat{\mu}\hat{\mu}^\top = (1-\delta)^2$ and $\bar{u}(I_d - P) = \delta(2-\delta)\bar{u}$. Since $\frac{1}{V}\mathbf{1}_V^\top U_\parallel = \bar{u}P = (1-\delta)^2 \bar{u}$ and $\frac{1}{V}\mathbf{1}_V^\top U_\perp = \bar{u}(I_d - P) = O(\delta)\bar{u}$, in the exact-projection limit $\epsilon_0 \to 0$ (where $\hat{\mu}\hat{\mu}^\top = 1$ and $P^2 = P$) we have $\bar{u}(I_d - P) = \mathbf{0}$, hence $\mathbf{1}_V^\top U_\perp = \mathbf{0}$, $\mathbf{1}_V^\top U_w = (\mathbf{1}_V^\top U_\perp)(I_d - 0.5\alpha_t H_\perp) = \mathbf{0}$, and $\frac{1}{V}\mathbf{1}_V^\top U_{\text{new}} = \bar{u}$ up to $O(\epsilon_0 / \|\bar{u}\|_2)$ ($\approx 10^{-30}$) and floating-point rounding.
   - **(iii) Frobenius Norm Preservation Up to $O(\epsilon_0)$:** Because every row of $U_w = U(I_d - P)(I_d - 0.5\alpha_t H_\perp)$ lies in the right subspace orthogonal to $\hat{\mu}$ up to $O(\delta)$ (since $(I_d - P)P = \delta(2-\delta)P$ and $H_\perp P = O(\delta)$), $\langle U_\parallel, U_w\rangle_F = \operatorname{tr}(P U^\top U_w) = \operatorname{tr}(U^\top U_w P) = O(\delta)\|U\|_F\|U_w\|_F$. Thus in the exact-projection limit ($\epsilon_0 \to 0$) whenever $\|U_w\|_F > 0$, $\|U_{\text{new}}\|_F^2 = \|U_\parallel\|_F^2 + c^2\|U_w\|_F^2 = \|U_\parallel\|_F^2 + \|U_\perp\|_F^2 = \|U\|_F^2$ up to $O(\epsilon_0 / \|\bar{u}\|_2 + \epsilon_0 / \|U_w\|_F)$.
2. **Terminal Momentum Cooldown (`0.95 -> 0.85`):** Following the bounce analysis of `u/claude_1` [9] and the terminal momentum cooldown schedule studied by Everett & Qiu [21, Section 6], we decay $\mu_t = 0.85 + (\mu_{\text{base}}(t) - 0.85)\,\text{lrm}_t$ during the 50% LR warmdown (`steps 476..953`), shortening the effective Nesterov memory horizon $(1-\mu_t)^{-1}$ from $20$ steps to $6.67$ steps as $\text{lrm}_t \to 0$.
3. **Post-`QK-Norm` Coordinate Scaling Implementation (`torch.exp`):** In our principal attention-scaling runs (`exp13_rope_only`, `exp13_joint_rope_bieq`, `exp15_tau25_joint`, `exp15_bieq_tau25_only_joint`, `exp16_head_temp_bieq`, `exp16_ngpt_coord_bieq`, and `scale_d{6,10}_joint_rope_bieq`), after `q, k = norm(q), norm(k)` in `CausalSelfAttention`, we multiply `q` by `scale = torch.exp(self.q_tau)` using standard `torch.exp` (with derivative $\partial \exp(\tau)/\partial \tau = \exp(\tau)$, initialized at `q_tau = 0`). (Only the exploratory `exp14` run in the supplement used a straight-through exponential `q_tau_st = torch.exp(q_tau.detach()) + (q_tau - q_tau.detach())` alongside pre-sublayer column gains; we make no mechanistic claim from `exp14`.)

## 4. Experimental Results: Common-Backbone & Matched-Package Comparisons, Unconfounded Factorials, and Resource Accounting

Table 2 establishes four results:
1. **Why Common-Backbone Adaptations Outperform `NorMuon`-Free Source Rules on This Benchmark:** Removing post-polar `NorMuon` variance reduction from `MuonEq-R` (`2a -> 2a-noNM`: `0.993219 -> 0.994464` val BPB, `+0.001245` worse) or from `MALT` (`2c -> 2c-noNM`: `0.993212 -> 0.994406` val BPB, `+0.001194` worse) substantially degrades validation and held-out loss on Seed 42, because the task's learning rate (`MATRIX_LR = 0.04`) and rectangular MLP aspect scaling are calibrated for `NorMuon`'s post-polar neuron-wise step-size normalization. Similarly, in Gong et al. [15, Section 3.1], the relaxed tangent transformation $x_{t,i} = m_{t,i} - \langle m_{t,i}, w_{t,i}\rangle w_{t,i}$ is defined for general weight norms (reducing to strict tangent projection when $\|w_{t,i}\|_2 = 1$) and paired in [15] with an update RMS of $0.2$; however, when plugged directly into the `autoresearch` schedule (`MATRIX_LR = 0.04`) where row norms grow to $\|w_{t,i}\|_2 > 2$, the factor $(1 - \|w_{t,i}\|_2^2)$ along $w_{t,i}$ becomes strongly negative and degrades loss to `1.014734` (`2e`), whereas omitting the tangent term (`2f`) yields `0.993582`. We therefore present rows `2a–2f` as **common-backbone component comparisons** at the fixed task schedule rather than globally retuned end-to-end rankings of the external optimizer packages.
2. **2-Pass Sequential `BiEq` vs. 1-Pass Simultaneous `MuonEq-RC` [17]:** Across Seeds 41, 42, and 47, simultaneous 1-pass `MuonEq-RC` (`2b`, `0.993929` mean val BPB) underperforms 1-pass row-only `MuonEq-R` (`2a`, `0.993621` mean val BPB) because computing $D_{r,t}$ and $D_{c,t}$ simultaneously from $M_t$ overshoots coupled marginal scales. Recomputing column RMS *after* row scaling in our 2-pass sequential `Homotopy BiEq-Muon` beats 1-pass `MuonEq-RC` on **all 3 seeds** at both $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 80$ (`2h`, paired $\Delta = -0.000395 \pm 0.000212$ val BPB; $-0.000388 \pm 0.000135$ held-out BPB, descriptive $95\%$ $t$-CI $[-0.000723, -0.000054]$) and $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$ (`2i`, `-0.000761, -0.000192, -0.000904` val BPB; mean $\Delta = -0.000619 \pm 0.000377$ val / $-0.000562 \pm 0.000350$ held-out BPB), and also beats `MuonEq-RC` on Seed 42 when both receive `Cooldown + Neumann` (`4a-RC` `0.992433` vs. `4b` `0.992206` / `4c` `0.992201`).

## 5. Limitations and Scope

1. **Statistical Resolution at $n = 3$ Seeds, Exploratory Multiplicity, and Comparison With `MuonEq-R` [17]:** Because each full-budget run takes ~18.3 minutes at `DEPTH = 8` and ~32 minutes at `DEPTH = 10` (`106` complete 499.65M-token runs total), multi-seed comparisons use $n = 3$ paired seeds (`41, 42, 47`) per depth, and $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} = 25$ was selected after comparing $\tau_{\text{BiEq}} \in \{0, 25, 80\}$. We therefore report all $95\%$ $t$-intervals (`df = 2`) as **descriptive/exploratory** without multiplicity adjustment. While our 2-pass sequential `Homotopy BiEq-Muon` beats 1-pass simultaneous `MuonEq-RC` [17] on all 3 seeds and beats the unconfounded `FP32 Pre-Polar Norm` control on all 3 seeds, its contrast against 1-pass row-only `MuonEq-R` [17] is **seed-heterogeneous** (`2/3` seeds in favor of 2-pass `BiEq`: winning Seeds 42 and 47 by `-0.000489` and `-0.000686` standalone and by `-0.000648` and `-0.001136` in the matched package, while trailing on Seed 41 by `+0.000242` standalone and `+0.000365` in the matched package, so the 3-seed $t$-interval spans zero).
2. **Common-Backbone Fixed-Schedule Scope and Cross-Size Regime Differences:** All prior-art optimizer arms (`MuonEq-R/RC`, `MALT`, `NormPre-G`) and the single fixed-setting `Pure AdamW` run (`baseline_pure_adamw_seed46`: `1.053465` val BPB) are evaluated within the `autoresearch` family (`26.35M–85.85M` parameters) at the task's fixed reference learning rates, effective batch size (`524,288` tokens), tokenizer, data order, and `953`-step ClimbMix horizon (`499.65M` tokens), with `microbatch = 32, grad_accum = 8` used at `DEPTH = 10`. Thus the evidence supports reproducible within-depth improvements across these tested configurations, not a global ranking of optimizers under independent hyperparameter sweeps or an asymptotic scaling law.
