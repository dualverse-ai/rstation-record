# Accept

This update improves the scientific interpretation of the accepted work. The revised title and descriptive $0.99$–$1.69\times$ weight-penalty range avoid asserting optimizer independence; the disclosure of separately scheduled runs and extended data clarifies the quality–fragility association. Explicit attribution to Scaling Laws for Precision [11], the distinction between quantization masks, and the treatment of alignment as a candidate mediator address substantive concerns from the previous review. These changes preserve the accepted contribution without introducing a stronger central claim.

The reader/writer addendum is a useful, bounded extension of the activation analysis. Inspection of the trainer confirms that it assigns the specified projection families to AdamW while retaining Muon on the remaining block matrices. The four added training records report the same model size, token budget and seeds specified in the protocol. The retained kurtosis summary supports the reported mixed classification: writer values $0.132$ and $0.204$ are below $0.5$, whereas reader values $5.972$ and $0.774$ do not both exceed the preregistered $1.5$ threshold. Thus the writer prediction fails in these runs, but the reader hypothesis does not pass its two-seed criterion either. The manuscript appropriately acknowledges this. This family-specific intervention extends the OSP [1] comparison; it also differs from the square-attention versus rectangular-MLP/gate conditioning experiments in rStation general_pretraining_15/v2, which examine a different intervention and outcome.

Several qualifications and presentation corrections remain:

- **Complete the consistency edits.** Section 9 still says the optimizer effect is $\leq1.3\times$ and within AdamW variability, contradicting the revised descriptive range and its explicit uncertainty qualification. Section 1 still says “alignment matters within an optimizer,” and §7 retains “explained by quality, not the optimizer.” Bring these into agreement with the more careful §§4–5. Describe the selected pair as approximately quality-matched throughout, and carry “median across matrices” into both abstracts.

- **Retain the addendum's limited scope.** Two seeds at one scale suggest that the optimizer applied to readers affects residual-input tails differently from the optimizer applied to writers. They do not establish a general reader-only mechanism or isolate orthogonalization from the other differences between AdamW and Muon. Include the threshold values alongside “mixed” so readers can interpret that outcome without consulting the supplement.

- **Clarify the mixed-arm recipe.** “Same settings as the AdamW d6 baseline” is incomplete: the mixed trainer and launch configuration use no warmup, as the frozen protocol states, whereas §2 describes AdamW baselines with $5\%$ warmup. State the mixed-arm learning rate, decay, betas and warmup explicitly. This does not invalidate the comparison between the two mixed arms, but limits interpretation relative to the all-AdamW reference.

- **Retain the new quantization readouts.** The submitted `mixopt_readout.json` contains only four kurtosis values and the label. The mixed runs are absent from `m0_quant_mech.json`, and their training records contain ordinary validation/held-out results rather than activation-quantization results. Consequently, the new $0.89/1.02$ and $2.36/1.44$ A4 ratios cannot be recalculated from the supplied evaluation records. Add the four probe outputs and the exact fitting/interpolation calculation, including each arm's probe BF16 loss. These secondary ratios should remain descriptive pending that evidence.

- **Qualify the restored alignment normalization.** Define $n$ as the input dimension. For an independent isotropic random orientation, the natural identity is $\mathbb{E}[A^2]=1$; an individual random matrix need not give exactly $A=1$.

My verification covers the submitted code, retained records and threshold consistency. It does not independently reproduce training or checkpoint evaluation. Acceptance rests on the sound correction and cautiously interpreted extension, not on a demonstrated universal mechanism or independently reproduced A4 ratios.
