# Apical-Pyramidal FFN Allocation, Interior vs. Apical Depth-Splicing Topologies, and Per-Block Sequence Coarsening Diagnostics in Fixed-Budget Pretraining

Under a fixed pretraining compute budget (1.1945e17 nominal FLOPs, wd_scale=1.0), we evaluate vertical feed-forward network (FFN) allocation, depth-expansion splice topology, and per-block sequence coarsening across 242 runs on disjoint shard_00009.parquet slices (development Slice A and confirmation Slice B, 20.97M target tokens each). First, in 16-layer models at matched parameters, FLOPs, and tokens, placing wide 6d FFNs at the apex beats basal placement by -3.799+/-0.201 mBPB on Slice A (p=0.000324; I1), persisting under all-global attention (-3.716+/-0.497 mBPB on Slice B, p=0.00494; T1) and at reduced width d=384 (-3.143+/-0.594 mBPB, p=0.0132; T2). A smooth linear OpenELM FFN ramp (r=(1^4,2^4,3^4,4^4)) beats basal placement by -4.328+/-0.268 mBPB (p=0.000516; I1b) and uniform 2d by -1.656+/-0.337 mBPB (p=0.0161; I1d), performing comparably to step-apical placement (-0.528+/-0.368 mBPB, p=0.246; I1c); uncalibrated Logit-Lens and SVD stable-rank profiles are reported strictly as exploratory descriptive associations. Second, under native B=64 microbatching across 8 seeds, a shared-checkpoint fork at fixed {3,7,11} global-attention depths shows interior period-preserving growth beats contiguous apical G_stack by -2.384+/-0.773 mBPB on Slice B (p=0.0177, p_Holm6=0.0530; U6_true_8s), while the two-stage interior-pyramidal package beats apical G_stack by -3.317+/-0.661 mBPB (p=0.00153, p_Holm6=0.00614; U7_true_8s) and 953-step static 8L by -4.395+/-0.368 mBPB (p_Holm6=0.000040; U0_true_8s). However, when static 8L is granted the +12.04% analytical executed FLOPs consumed by 6-block activation checkpointing at 12L (1,068 uncheckpointed steps, 1.3387e17 FLOPs), static 8L leads checkpointed 12L growth overall by +2.857+/-0.334 mBPB (p=0.00336; U0_exec_true) while trailing by -16.073 mBPB on long-distance bigrams. Third, across 4 seeds in matched-configuration coarse-to-fine continuations (3.0145e16 FLOPs), retaining the 2-tap Haar input filter improves sh9A by -17.187+/-1.370 mBPB over an outer-gain-only control (p=0.00109; C2F_2_pure_filter, 89.6%+/-3.6% of the -19.188+/-1.061 mBPB recovery over naive unfolding).
