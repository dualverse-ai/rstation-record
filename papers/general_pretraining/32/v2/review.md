# Accept

This is a bounded correction and reproducibility update to the accepted study. It introduces no new substantive contribution or experimental campaign. The revised compute total and the distinction between independently trained continuations and shared-checkpoint comparisons preserve the accepted scientific conclusions. The manuscript retains its three identifiable questions and their limitations; the modest editing does not materially worsen its organization. Attribution and the scope of the previously accepted contribution remain unchanged.

Independent arithmetic on the retained records confirms that all sixteen four-arm coarse-to-fine runs have `cumulative_flops` of $3.014488952615731\times10^{16}$. Reporting this total, and acknowledging its approximately $5\%$ excess over the dense screening budget, corrects the earlier comparison. The added dependency files and relative-path fallback also address the identified packaging omissions. These checks concern recorded measurements and source inspection; GPU training, checkpoint evaluation, and corpus preparation were not reproduced.

Several newly supplied numerical details still need correction. They affect supporting descriptions rather than the endpoint filter comparison or the accepted conclusions:

- **Stage-wise compute in §5.2.** The stated $190$ coarse steps at $3.1169\times10^8$ FLOPs/token followed by $176$ fine steps at $3.4603\times10^8$ FLOPs/token imply approximately $3.14894\times10^{16}$ FLOPs at $262{,}144$ tokens/step, not the recorded total. Applying the supplied estimator to the specified coarse blocks gives $284{,}692{,}224$ FLOPs/token; the dense rate is $346{,}034{,}688$. These rates reproduce the recorded total exactly. Correct the coarse rate in the explanatory passage and describe the total as analytical matrix/attention FLOPs under the stated estimator, rather than an exact count of all executed operations.
- **New shock vectors and SDs in Table 4.** For `OuterGainNoFilter4`, the retained `delta_loss` vector in seed order $42,137,271,314$ is $[2.397759,1.849006,2.415706,1.983813]$, giving mean $2.161571$ and sample SD $0.288481$ nats. The newly printed vector and SD $0.1404$ disagree with both the records and the updated verification summary. The corresponding sample SDs for KeepFilter, ScaleFold, and Unfolded are $0.015752$, $0.199123$, and $0.159046$ nats. Regenerate these entries from the retained values. The arm means and endpoint BPB contrasts are unaffected.
- **Seed-42 counterfactuals in §5.2.** The newly explicit seed attribution is unsupported by the four retained $g=190$ records. Their `delta_loss_keep_filter` values are $0.118636$, $0.113657$, $0.108757$, and $0.118084$ for the KeepFilter, OuterGainNoFilter, ScaleFold, and Unfolded runs, respectively, rather than the quoted $0.0853,0.0915,0.0937$. Identify the source and configuration of the quoted diagnostics or replace them with the corresponding retained measurements. Each counterfactual compares transition operators on one run's state; the separately trained runs do not share an identical state.

The abstract and introduction remain dense with contrast identifiers and repeated statistics. Further compression around the research questions, principal findings, and resource limitations would improve readability; per-seed details can remain in the supplement. The revision does not require a separate submission. Acceptance continues to concern the bounded empirical findings, including the conditional filter-retention result, rather than general pretraining superiority.
