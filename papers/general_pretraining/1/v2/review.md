# Accept

This update preserves the accepted source-exclusion theorem and original confirmation results, addresses the previous review’s statistical qualifications, and adds useful evidence about their practical interpretation. The additions remain connected to the accepted contribution: they distinguish finite source inclusion, conditional tail prediction, and aggregate prediction quality. They do not introduce an unsupported general improvement claim.

The natural-context follow-up substantively strengthens the interpretation of the tail results. Its construction compares fixed target regions with natural preceding tokens, and the retained source losses reproduce all eight favorable middle-long tail contrasts at context 2048. This supports an effect extending beyond adaptation to an artificial interior BOS in this assay. The manuscript appropriately identifies these as exploratory development results, reports the shorter-context reversal, and avoids claiming consistent absolute gains for the 37-source longest stratum. The revised explanation of source bootstrapping correctly distinguishes hypothetical exchangeable-source inference from finite-census sampling error and training-seed uncertainty.

The whole-source census provides an important qualification: segmentation remains worse in aggregate at both seeds, while random spans have small effects of opposite signs. The preparation and scoring code implement document-local strided evaluation with explicit target accounting and duplicate exclusions. These findings support the stated distinction between conditional tail benefits and overall prediction improvement. The paper correctly avoids attributing differences between official and census scores solely to source coverage, since context and exclusions also change.

The source-order controls preserve the intended finite row multisets, and the reported sequence-shuffling improvement fails the declared $0.002$ BPB threshold. This supports closing that particular candidate, not a general negative result about shuffling. The initialization measurements are appropriately presented as an obstacle to a simple causal explanation rather than an explanation of the final scores. The stronger ordinary comparator improves both aggregate scores and all 12 nonempty census cells; its one-seed, joint batch/decay/clock interpretation is suitably limited. Existing batch-size research [14] supports treating such controls as conventional comparisons, not a new optimization contribution. It does not establish a universally valid Muon scaling rule.

Inspection of the pinned public TRL, Axolotl, Megatron, and Mosaic components supports the stated reasons that they fall outside this theorem’s hypotheses. These are useful applicability boundaries, not certifications of complete coverage. The update also credits the external crop replication and stronger-recipe antecedent rather than claiming their ideas as novel. No new novelty claim requiring rejection emerges from these additions.

I reproduced the compact verifier’s natural-context bootstrap summaries, ten census scores, original census contrasts, order-control gate, and resource arithmetic. An additional calculation confirmed the stronger comparator’s improvement in every nonempty census cell. These checks establish consistency of the retained statistics; I did not rerun training, corpus preparation, or checkpoint evaluation. The public revision-1 records for [8] and [9] match the retained responses. Thus the protocol’s public timestamp is now supported, while its declaration that confirmation had not yet run remains an author statement rather than an independent execution audit. Correcting the timing column to include compilation also improves the resource comparison.

One reproducibility correction is needed: `runs/valuespan_ordinary_s43/run_baseline.py` and `run_eval.py` change directory to an absolute path in the author’s private workspace. The regeneration helper copies these launchers without relocating that path, so the documented comparator command will fail or target the wrong directory elsewhere. Resolve the run directory relative to the launcher, or document an explicit relocation step. This is a localized portability defect with an inspectable remedy; it does not undermine the retained scientific evidence. Also render the newly added FLOP estimate in Section 8 with LaTeX delimiters for consistency.

Acceptance remains for the bounded theorem and empirical audit, now with better-supported limits on its prediction consequences. It does not establish a generally superior packing or pretraining method.
