# Residual-Highway Input-Bus Pooling and Shared Cross-Slab Hadamard Value Multiplexing for Global-Backward-Free Perturbation Pretraining of Reduced-Scale Transformers

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 2 · 2026-10-24T11:42:08.668179Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/21/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Global-backward-free Transformer perturbation pretraining—using zero autograd sweeps across depth, zero inter-block reverse cascades, and zero dense weight-transpose multiplications while permitting within-module local pullbacks—suffers two variance bottlenecks in existing estimators [1], [6]: sparse input embeddings use only 3 direct perturbations out of width D=384, while local attention value and value-embedding probes collapse 64 head channels into a scalar score. Building on delayed-trace Weight Maximization [1], [16] and moment-free Over-Encoding [2], [15], we study two incremental additions across 102 two-seed GPU runs on ClimbMix d6 (D=384, T=512, 26.35M dense parameters), wider scale (D=512, M=2048, 39.85M dense parameters), and longer context (T=2048, B=4): (1) we show that tokenwise writer rewards estimate the same-position token-loss derivative, prove the Diagonal-Attention DAG Theorem and its Three-Level Causal Credit Hierarchy, verify the cut-l input-gradient identity on a 6-layer GPT Transformer, and show descriptively on a 50-step warmed state (R=32 trials with unclipped plug-in moment estimates and resampling dispersion diagnostics) that pooling 49 writer estimates along scalar highway gains trades estimated checkpoint-specific bias (plug-in cosine 0.9187 vs. 0.9878 for 3-draw direct) for a 49.9x raw-variance reduction, lifting single-step wte cosine by 2.86x and split-half cosine from 0.1196 to 0.5724; and (2) we share a 1-call cross-slab Rademacher-Hadamard value-multiplexed estimate across value, value-embedding, and gate updates (lifting conditional value-embedding cosine by 21.5x over [1] and reaching plug-in cosine 1.0004 on c_v and 0.9998 on value_embeds at gamma=1.00), while a truncated-lag window-banded variant (W_band=256) cuts coupling FLOPs by 7.97x at T=2048. Driving Over-Encoding with pooled input-bus credit lowers 200-step fresh-test BPB from 2.13484 to 2.07883 (0 extra parameters) and 2.06198 (+OE) on d6, 2.13402 to 2.06742 at D=512, and 2.08635 to 2.01977 at T=2048.
