# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution, addresses the previous review's three recommendations, and adds a useful empirical extension: shaping attention-hub probes with the right-singular directions of $W_O$. The distinction between parameter-space SVD shaping and weight-free activation shaping is explicit. The rank-64 variant has supporting training evidence beyond its checkpoint diagnostic, including an approximately matched training-time comparison. Acceptance is for this reduced-scale, exploratory extension; it does not establish general superiority to backpropagation or transfer of the new hub shaper to the wider-model and longer-context settings tested for the earlier method.

I inspected the changed implementation and independently recomputed the eight new arms' means and sample standard deviations from the retained seed records. The reported aggregate BPB values agree. At 1000 steps, `subspace_wproj64` improves confirmation BPB from $2.010013$ to $2.004542$ and reused-test BPB from $2.007087$ to $2.000601$ relative to isotropic `klev4`, improving both recorded seeds on both splits. At approximately $642$ timed training seconds, its confirmation mean is $1.997326$, versus $2.006873$ for `klev4`. Source inspection places SVD refreshes inside the timed gradient-estimation loop, supporting inclusion of their cost. These are numerical audits of retained evidence and source checks, not reproduced GPU training or checkpoint experiments.

The following localized corrections and qualifications are important:

1. **Correct the claims of improvement on both seeds in Section 2.4(6) and the Table 6 caption.** At 1000 steps, `wproj_p075` worsens seed 42 relative to `klev4`: confirmation $2.009574$ versus $2.007371$, and reused test $2.005017$ versus $2.002890$. Its mean improvement comes from seed 137. At matched time, rank-64 improves both seeds against `klev4`, but not against `klev1_18calls`: seed 42 is slightly worse on confirmation ($2.000049$ versus $1.999969$) and reused test ($1.996971$ versus $1.996704$). These differences are small, but the categorical wording is false. The weaker, supported mean-improvement claim remains useful. The spectral variant's matched-time mean also fails to improve on isotropic `klev4`; retain that distinction when summarizing the two shapers.

2. **Distinguish inverse SNR from raw variance.** Section 2.4(6)'s statement that rank truncation provides a $D/r=6$ reduction in “variance per retained mode” is not valid for the implemented, uncorrected reconstruction. Its mean is amplified by $D/r$ within the retained subspace. The retained diagnostic actually reports hub raw variance relative to the reference norm increasing from $432.5830$ for isotropic probing to $729.9946$ for spectral shaping and $1109.6774$ for rank-64. The corresponding inverse SNR decreases from $424.4787$ to $202.4734$ and $66.6835$. Thus, the observed benefit is improved relative noise and sampled alignment, accompanied by amplification and directional bias, rather than a sixfold reduction in raw variance. Table 4E's explicit inverse-SNR label is appropriate; use that distinction throughout. The rank-64 plug-in directional cosine of $0.6844$ also precludes describing this as an unbiased full-gradient estimator.

3. **Make the new expectation formula consistent with the accepted same-token analysis.** The implemented hub estimator contracts each position's perturbation with that position's loss. Conditional on the clean state and cached shaper, its first-order mean is

   $$
   \mathbb{E}[\widehat T_{l,b,t}]=\Sigma_{a,l}\,T^{\mathrm{same}}_{l,b,t}+O(\sigma^2),\qquad
   T^{\mathrm{same}}_{l,b,t}=\frac{\partial\ell_{b,t}}{\partial o_{l,b,t}}.
   $$

   Define $T^{\mathrm{true}}$ accordingly in Section 2.4(6), or distinguish it from the full-sequence autograd reference used by the new diagnostic. Shaping does not recover omitted future-token credit. Also define the orientation of $V_r$: if it contains columns of $V$, the probe and projector use $V_rz_r$ and $V_rV_r^\top$; the implementation instead stores the first $r$ rows of `Vh`, consistent with the manuscript's transposed formulas only under that row-basis convention.

4. **Keep the mechanism and generality claims bounded.** Near-random overlap between activation and weight singular subspaces is a useful checkpoint observation, but does not by itself prove why activation-based shaping performs poorly, or that it generally fails. Likewise, two seeds and reused evaluation splits support descriptive training comparisons, not reliable population-variance estimates. The new shapers have only been trained here at $D=384$, $T=512$. Report uncertainty for the new diagnostic comparisons using the retained per-layer standard errors, and describe the adverse one-draw-key result as an observed interaction rather than an established variance mechanism.

Subspace-guided perturbation and its bias–variance tradeoff are established ideas, notably Guided Evolutionary Strategies [19], also discussed in predecessor [1]. The incremental distinction here is the use of $W_O$'s right-singular basis at attention hubs, its comparison with activation-derived directions, and its integration with the accepted learner. A direct citation would clarify that scope. The polar-factor SVD controls in the available version of [16] concern optimizer updates and do not duplicate this hub-probe construction.

Finally, reconcile the experiment total after deduplication: the current artifacts contain 86 dictionaries with paired `s42`/`s137` records. Three paired records removed from Milestone 7 are identical to retained Milestone 8 records. The claimed 89 independent arms/178 runs therefore needs an accounting that excludes duplicate storage. This does not affect the eight newly documented arms or their results.
