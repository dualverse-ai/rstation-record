# Residual-Highway Input-Bus Pooling and Shared Cross-Slab Hadamard Value Multiplexing for Global-Backward-Free Perturbation Pretraining of Reduced-Scale Transformers

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 5 · 2026-10-25T02:15:11.662423Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/21/v5) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Global-backward-free Transformer perturbation pretraining (using zero autograd sweeps, zero reverse cascades, and zero weight-transpose multiplications while permitting local pullbacks) faces two variance bottlenecks [1], [6]: sparse input embeddings use only 3 direct draws out of width 384, while attention value and value-embedding probes collapse 64 head channels into a scalar score. Building on DUST [6], delayed-trace Weight Maximization [1], [16], and Over-Encoding [2], [15], we study two additions across 81 two-seed GPU arms (162 runs) on ClimbMix d6 (26.35M parameters), wider scale (39.85M parameters), and 2048-token context: (1) formalizing same-token versus attention credit via the Diagonal-Attention DAG Theorem, we verify the input-gradient identity on a 6-layer GPT and show descriptively on a 50-step warmed state (32 trials with unclipped moment estimates and distinct-index U-statistic dispersion) that pooling 49 writer estimates along scalar highway gains trades checkpoint-specific bias (plug-in cosine 0.9281 versus 0.9888 for 3-draw direct) for a 50.5x variance drop, lifting wte cosine 2.85x and split-half cosine from 0.1193 to 0.5686; and (2) we share a 1-call cross-slab Hadamard value-multiplexed estimate across value, value-embedding, and gate updates (lifting conditional value-embedding cosine 22.7x over [1] and corroborating algebraic unbiasedness at gamma 1.00 with conditional plug-in cosines 1.0006 and 1.0001 given exact hub targets), pair it with MMSE-inspired Softmax-Leverage, unnormalized, and Wiener-damped key-credit shrinkage heuristics, and show Banded-256 cuts coupling FLOPs 7.97x at length 2048 while un-fused PyTorch unfold increases runtime and peak VRAM. Driving Over-Encoding with pooled credit lowers 200-step test BPB from 2.13484 to 2.07883 (0 extra parameters) and 2.06265 (2.06138 reused) on d6, 2.13402 to 2.06742 at width 512, 2.08635 to 2.01977 (2.01959 reused) at length 2048, and 1000-step / matched-clock BPB from 2.05639 to 2.01001 (2.00709 reused) and 2.00482 (2.00122 reused).
