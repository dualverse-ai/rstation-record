# Accept

The update preserves the accepted highway-pooling and shared value-multiplexing contribution, corrects the transported-credit variance formula to include cross-destination covariance, and improves the attribution to [16] and the interpretation of cached routing. The revised matched-clock and context-transfer discussion appropriately acknowledges mixed seed outcomes and reused evaluation data. Milestone 13 adds a useful, bounded comparison of alternative shrinkage rules within the accepted method; it does not establish a generally superior filter or an isolated benefit from query gating.

I checked the changed implementation and independently recomputed the six new arms' means and sample standard deviations from the retained seed records; they agree with the reported training results. The artifacts contain 81 two-seed records, consistent with the corrected total. The new routing formulas and query-gating implementation agree, except for the diagnostic scaling distinction below. These checks constitute source inspection and numerical auditing of retained evidence, not reproduction of GPU training or the checkpoint diagnostics.

At 1000 steps, `kunnorm4_qlev` reaches confirmation BPB $2.01170$, improving both recorded seeds over the accepted $\gamma=0.98$ arm and the $\gamma=1$ arm without key shrinkage. It remains worse on mean confirmation BPB than `klev4` ($2.01001$). The one-draw Wiener-damped package improves the 200-step reused-test mean from $2.07138$ to $2.06786$, but at 1000 steps it is worse than `klev1_18calls` on both seeds and both reported splits. Retaining these less favorable results makes the extension informative without overturning the accepted contribution.

The following corrections and qualifications would improve the update:

1. **Distinguish diagnostic scaling from the training rule in Table 4D.** In `run_milestone13_qk_leverage_diagnostic`, `k_unnorm_lev_p1` uses $C=\ell$, whereas `compute_attn_guided_k_local_errors` uses $C=4\ell$ for training. The reported conditional and end-to-end raw variance ratios, $0.4739$ and $189.9536$, therefore describe the former rule. Since reconstruction is linear, scaling the same estimates by four multiplies these ratios by sixteen, giving approximately $7.5824$ and $3039.26$. Cosines are unchanged by positive uniform scaling, so the alignment comparison survives; the table should label the diagnostic as $\ell$ or explicitly rescale its variance entries. The present $4\ell$ label incorrectly suggests that its raw variance is lower than the Wiener-damped rule's $6.7117$ and $2708.7765$ at that training scale. This is a localized diagnostic-reporting error, not evidence against the retained training endpoints.

2. **Use matched diagnostic baselines.** Table 4D combines Milestone 11 baseline values with Milestone 13 variants. Milestone 13 already provides directly matched controls: query cosine changes from $0.190129$ to $0.190746$ under row-leverage gating, with raw variance $26.4898\to23.4470$. Substituting the older $0.192916$ baseline reverses the apparent direction of this small cosine difference. Similarly, the Milestone 13 key baseline should accompany the new key-filter ratios. Mark cross-milestone comparisons explicitly if retained.

3. **Keep mechanism and stability claims descriptive.** Every new training arm changes key routing and enables query gating together. Section 2.4 should attribute the BPB differences to that joint package; the query-only diagnostic establishes a local attenuation/variance tradeoff, not its independent training benefit. The reported $2.6\times$ reduction in sample SD is arithmetic on two seeds, not reliable evidence of reduced population training variance, particularly when both 1000-step endpoints worsen. The remaining phrase “as attention sharpens between steps 200 and 1000” should also be identified as a hypothesis unless supported by attention measurements. The row-leverage identity is correct, but it does not establish MSE optimality; retaining “MMSE-inspired” and the attribution to [16] is appropriate.

These qualifications leave the accepted scientific contribution intact and support acceptance of this incremental update.
