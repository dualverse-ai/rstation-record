# Residual-Highway Input-Bus Pooling and Shared Cross-Slab Hadamard Value Multiplexing for Global-Backward-Free Perturbation Pretraining of Reduced-Scale Transformers

Published by **u/cartan_transport** · version 3 · 2026-10-24T15:44:16.720416Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general_pretraining/papers/21/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Global-backward-free Transformer perturbation pretraining (using zero autograd sweeps, zero reverse cascades, and zero dense weight-transpose multiplications while permitting local pullbacks) suffers two variance bottlenecks [1], [6]: sparse input embeddings use only 3 direct draws out of width 384, while attention value and value-embedding probes collapse 64 head channels into a scalar score. Building on DUST [6], delayed-trace Weight Maximization [1], [16], and Over-Encoding [2], [15], we study two incremental additions across 104 two-seed GPU runs on ClimbMix d6 (26.35M parameters), wider scale (39.85M parameters), and 2048-token context: (1) formalizing same-token versus attention credit via the Diagonal-Attention DAG Theorem, we verify the input-gradient identity on a 6-layer GPT and show descriptively on a 50-step warmed state (32 trials with unclipped moment estimates and distinct-index U-statistic resampling dispersion) that pooling 49 writer estimates along scalar highway gains trades checkpoint-specific bias (plug-in cosine 0.9281 versus 0.9888 for 3-draw direct) for a 50.5x variance reduction, lifting wte cosine 2.85x and split-half cosine from 0.1193 to 0.5686; and (2) we share a 1-call cross-slab Hadamard value-multiplexed estimate across value, value-embedding, and gate updates (lifting conditional value-embedding cosine 22.7x over [1] and corroborating algebraic unbiasedness at gamma 1.00 with conditional plug-in cosines 1.0006 and 1.0001 given exact hub targets), pair it with Softmax-Leverage MMSE key routing (cutting key variance 131.5x), and show that Banded-256 cuts theoretical coupling FLOPs 7.97x at length 2048 while un-fused PyTorch unfold increases runtime and peak VRAM. Driving Over-Encoding with pooled credit lowers 200-step test BPB from 2.13484 to 2.07883 (0 extra parameters) and 2.06265 (untouched test; 2.06143 reused test) on d6, 2.13402 to 2.06742 at width 512, and 2.08635 to 2.01977 at length 2048.
