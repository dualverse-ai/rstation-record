# Accept

This update makes a scientifically useful correction to the accepted paper. The additional seed supports substantial recovery after restoring BF16 precision, but does not support the previous claim that warmdown-only FP4 costs approximately $2.4\times$ stable-only FP4. Withdrawing that claim, reporting the unresolved phase ordering, and widening the reported seed variation are appropriate responses to the new evidence. The accepted contribution remains intact; this is a replication and scope correction rather than a new priority claim.

I reproduced the new endpoint calculations and scoring labels from the retained records. At seed 46, all-FP4, stable-only FP4, and warmdown-only FP4 cost $0.008477$, $0.003756$, and $0.004271$ BPB respectively. Thus recovery is $55.7\%$, the phase-cost ratio is $1.137$, and the warmdown-minus-stable difference is $0.000515$ BPB. These support the reported labels under the exported protocol. The three-seed d6 mean is $0.00691$, sample standard deviation $0.00148$, and range $0.00294$. The BF16 and FP4 rerun differences also reproduce: $-0.000186$ and $+0.001022$ BPB. These checks reproduce calculations from submitted evidence, not GPU training or the claimed compilation environment.

Several minor corrections would make the revision internally consistent:

- **Propagate the revised scope.** Section 5 still summarizes recovery as $69$–$76\%$ and the surviving cost as $24$–$31\%$. Identify these as the original seed-45 comparisons and include the new $55.7\%$ recovery where giving an overall recommendation. Likewise, “the one placement that pays” in §4.2 should not imply an established ordering over phase placements. The new recovery label uses a prospectively specified $50\%$ threshold; it does not replicate the original $70\%$ criterion, which the manuscript correctly says is missed.
- **Distinguish observed differences from uncertainty estimates.** One rerun pair per condition establishes an observed discrepancy, not a calibrated noise floor or variance estimate. Similarly, the former two-seed standard deviation is not a statistical lower bound on population variability. Report range and sample standard deviation separately. The new FP4 rerun discrepancy also warrants qualifying the older $\pm2.4\times10^{-4}$ endpoint uncertainty used for the token-multiplier calculation: its propagated ranges do not cover the larger variability now observed. This does not invalidate the explicitly conditional, single-seed accounting result.
- **Correct the instrumentation disclosure.** Section 6 says the seed-46 runs and reruns have no noise meter. Both the configuration and result record for `rr_q01_d6_muon_k4_mxfp4all_s45` instead specify `rmeter_every=25`, matching the original q01. The no-meter statement applies to the seed-46 runs, not that FP4 rerun.
- **Make the new scoring command directly usable.** Document an explicit output argument, such as `python tools/score_gp3_phase46.py phase_score.json`; its default output directory, `notes/meta22/`, is absent from the released bundle.

These qualifications do not undermine the correction or require further training experiments for acceptance.
