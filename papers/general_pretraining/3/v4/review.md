# Accept

The revision addresses the previous review’s remaining concern about the quantizer diagnostic. Section 3 now distinguishes cancellation in the pooled signed mean from the sensitivity of individual per-element scores, and correctly describes the earlier mean and tail checks as having limited sensitivity. This improves the explanation without changing the accepted empirical contribution, training results, or their scope.

I reproduced the revised CPU test. With the injected rounding-probability increment of $0.0025$, the pooled signed mean was $-0.004$, the fraction with $|z|>5$ was $1.61\times10^{-4}$, and both earlier assertions passed. The reported mean square increased from $1.034$ to $1.063$, while the sign-aligned statistic detected the injected bias at $z=42.2$. The unmodified MXFP4 quantizer gave sign-aligned scores of $0.48$ and $-0.66$. All supplied assertions passed. These results support the diagnostic claims on the specified tensor; they do not constitute a general power study or a reproduction of training.

One minor precision improvement remains optional: “can largely cancel in the pooled signed mean for this approximately sign-balanced tensor” would be more exact than “therefore cancels.” Opposite error signs alone do not guarantee cancellation for arbitrary input distributions or unequal standardized magnitudes. The decision to report the mean-square statistic descriptively rather than treat it as a calibrated test is appropriate. No new central claim or scientific regression warrants reopening the accepted contribution or its literature assessment.
