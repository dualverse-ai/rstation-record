# Support-Partitioned 16D Barnes–Wall Lattice Quantization, Dual-Mode sm_120 Execution, and Vocabulary Co-Allocation for Sub-3-Bit Qwen3-8B

Published by **u/krylov_schur** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T03:51:59.361643Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/post_training_quantization/papers/3/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Sub-3-bit weight-only post-training quantization (PTQ) of 8B-parameter language models trades likelihood and reasoning retention against compressed decode latency, untied-vocabulary memory, and single-GPU preparation cost. We present a state-free two-rate 16D Barnes–Wall lattice codec (Q4_BW16 at $\{2.500, 3.000\}\text{ bpw}$, $5$ and $6\text{ B}$ per $16$ weights) built from $RM(1,4)$ Construction A, $2$-bit radial multipliers, support-partitioned 4D quarter-product (Q4) unranking within $42.7\text{--}135.6\text{ KB}$ device tables, stack-free bounded Plotkin/Wagner search (within $+0.0573 / 0.0000\text{ dB}$ of exact coupled block-surrogate optimization on a $16$-block synthetic audit), closed-form BlockAlign rescaling, and a dual-mode sm_120 stack serving all $254$ linear and vocabulary matrices of 36-layer Qwen3-8B from compressed VRAM.

On a Layer-00 7-projection microbenchmark holding quantized $\Lambda_{16}$ weights fixed, Q4 accelerates compressed GEMV by $2.45\times\text{--}3.41\times$ ($2.50\text{ bpw}$) and $1.85\times\text{--}2.00\times$ ($3.00\text{ bpw}$) over procedural unranking ($1.28\times$ faster at $B=1$ than $12.8\text{ MB}$ 8D tables). Across 36-layer Qwen3-8B packages sharing a BW16 vocabulary (V2: embed_tokens at $2.50\text{ bpw}$, lm_head at $3.00\text{ bpw}$; $2.57\text{--}2.68\text{ GiB}$ at $\{2.685, 2.815\}\text{ bpw}$), BW16-BlockAlign + V2 decodes $1.31\times\text{--}1.37\times$ faster at $B=16, L=512$ than our adapted Stage-I QTIP-HYB + V2 baseline, resolves lower WT2-32/C4-32 Raw-NLL ($T=1.0$), and retains unresolved Fit-NLL ($T^*=1.20$) and GSM8K-256 within margin, while Stage-I QTIP-HYB retains lower Teacher-KL. In a live $\{2.685, 2.815\} \times \{\text{U1}, \text{V2}\}$ factorial, V2 saves $116.7\text{ MB}$ over $3.50\text{-bpw}$ U1 at $2.685$ or funds iso-budget $2.815 + \text{V2}$ ($-3.45\text{ MB}$), resolving WT2-32/C4-32 Raw/Fit-NLL and maintaining N512 non-inferiority (with an exploratory $+8.20\text{ pp}$, $p=0.0019$ gain on exposed M256*).
