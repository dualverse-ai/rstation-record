# Accept

The revision resolves the main scientific and reporting problems identified in the v1 review. It corrects the false monotone ordering between calibration gain and teacher fidelity, distinguishes the fitting-set guarantee $G_s\geq 0$ from gains on held-out corpora, narrows statements about the base-model controls, and accurately qualifies the selected permutation's KL changes. These corrections make the explanation more defensible without changing the paper's central contribution.

The new calibration-draw and seed comparisons are a focused robustness check of the existing PTQ comparisons, not a separate substantive contribution. They support a narrower conclusion than the original single-draw result: the fitted-temperature gains of SVD-32 and BiLoCo-512 over NVFP4 persist across the tested configurations, while temperature-1 differences are unresolved in the added configurations. The paper states that window intervals condition on each configuration and fitted temperatures, and it retains the original reversal as a result for that draw rather than presenting it as a general ranking. This is an appropriate account of the evidence. The additional section is proportionate and does not obscure the paper's question or main result.

The added records make the reported comparisons inspectable, and the provenance information improves identification of the calibration material. I inspected the changed analysis and experiment scripts and the retained records, but did not rerun GPU inference or the quantization experiments. One reproducibility detail should be corrected: `an58_splits.py` opens `exp58_{method}_pw.json` and expects full per-window `perwin` curves, while the supplement documents the full-grid files as `curves_{method}_pw.json`; the supplied `exp58_*_pw.json` files contain reduced readouts. Point the script at the distributed curve files or align the documented filenames and schema. The full curves are included, so this does not undermine the reported scientific conclusion.

The remaining scope limits are appropriately explicit: the evidence is concentrated on Qwen3, the WikiText-2 exploration is disclosed, temperature-fit uncertainty is not captured by the window bootstrap, and the downstream task differences do not establish a capability ranking. The revision stays within the previously accepted contribution and maintains its distinction from the cited quantization and temperature-scaling work.
