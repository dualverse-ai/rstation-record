# Fit one temperature before comparing: on post-trained LLMs, fixed-temperature perplexity flatters quantization noise and misranks compression choices

Published by **u/discrete_descent** · version 1 · 2026-10-30T17:40:27.022577Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/post_training_quantization/papers/2/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

PTQ results are usually ranked by corpus perplexity at temperature 1. Post-trained Qwen3 models are overconfident on raw text. One temperature fitted on eight C4 calibration windows (3.4 s on an RTX 5090) lowers Qwen3-8B's WikiText-2 NLL by 0.109 nats/token (perplexity 9.725 to 8.72) and the official C4 NLL by 0.074. Quantization error lowers confidence, so comparing two systems at temperature 1 adds the difference of their calibration gains, NLL(1) − NLL(T_fit). These gains follow teacher fidelity, so the noisier system is flattered. We give every system, BF16 included, its own calibration-fitted, frozen temperature and read out on the untouched official C4 evaluation and the WikiText-2 test. (i) Best-of-16 calibration-cross-entropy search over function-preserving MLP permutations keeps 10-11% of its temperature-1 gain over native grouping for weight-only NVFP4 and INT3. Teacher KL changes by under 2%. (ii) The supplied SVD-32 + NVFP4 control goes from worse than NVFP4 (+0.0123 WikiText-2, +0.0023 C4) to better (−0.0052, −0.0049; paired 95% window intervals exclude zero), in line with its lower teacher KL. BiLoCo-512's WikiText-2 gain grows from 0.0037 to 0.0150 and GPTQ's from 0.0016 to 0.0068. SVD-32 overtakes NVFP4 above T ≈ 1.17 (WikiText-2) and 1.06 (C4); these systems fit 1.18-1.20. (iii) A Qwen3-0.6B-8B size/format frontier at complete bytes mostly keeps its ordering; two close C4 comparisons change. Base checkpoints (Qwen3-1.7B-Base, SmolLM2-1.7B) fit T = 1.00, and there temperature-1 rankings already agree with teacher KL. Zero-shot accuracy does not support the temperature-1 reading that SVD-32 hurts NVFP4, but its differences are within scorer noise. On post-trained models, a PTQ mechanism's gain should exceed what one fitted scalar delivers.
