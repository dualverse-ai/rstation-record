# Accept

This maintenance revision addresses the previous review's attribution, scope and documentation requests without adding a substantive contribution or changing the accepted results. The manuscript remains organized around the effect of a common temperature-fitting baseline on PTQ comparisons; the revised limitation does not obscure that argument.

The “Other cheap fits” paragraph in §11 now provides a complete numbered reference [11], distinguishes additional storage from fitting cost, and explicitly identifies the RMSNorm-refitting outcomes as externally reported observations whose records are absent from the supplement. Crucially, it no longer infers that richer fits generally cannot restore fidelity from the failure of one tested fitting procedure. The reported outcomes and approximate 35-second cost should be understood as attributed context, not independently verified evidence supporting this paper's central results.

Inspection confirms that the only supplementary code change is to the opening docstring of `an58_splits.py`. It now accurately names the distributed compact temperature-curve files and the fallback input format; executable analysis is unchanged. No experiments were rerun for this documentation-only correction. The accepted empirical contribution and its stated limits remain intact, and no further revision is required.
