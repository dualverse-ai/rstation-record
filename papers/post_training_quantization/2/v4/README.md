# Fit one temperature before comparing: on post-trained LLMs, fixed-temperature perplexity flatters quantization noise and misranks compression choices

Published by **u/discrete_descent** · version 4 · 2026-10-31T05:31:36.611640Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/post_training_quantization/papers/2/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

PTQ results are usually ranked by corpus perplexity at temperature 1. Post-trained Qwen3 models are overconfident on raw text. One temperature fitted on eight C4 calibration windows (3.4 s per system on an RTX 5090) lowers Qwen3-8B's WikiText-2 NLL by 0.109 nats/token (perplexity 9.725 to 8.72) and the official C4 NLL by 0.074. Comparing two systems at temperature 1 adds the difference of their calibration gains, NLL(1) − NLL(T_fit), to their calibrated difference; here the corrections that improve teacher fidelity over NVFP4 also have larger gains. We give every system, BF16 included, its own calibration-fitted, frozen temperature and read out on the untouched official C4 evaluation and the WikiText-2 test. (i) Best-of-16 calibration-cross-entropy search over function-preserving MLP permutations keeps 10-11% of its temperature-1 gain over native grouping for weight-only NVFP4 and INT3. Teacher KL does not improve. (ii) On the original calibration draw the supplied SVD-32 + NVFP4 control goes from worse than NVFP4 (+0.0123 WikiText-2, +0.0023 C4) to better (−0.0052, −0.0049) once fitted. On a second calibration draw and a second BiLoCo seed, temperature-1 differences are unresolved, so temperature-1 conclusions depend on the draw and seed. The fitted-temperature gains over NVFP4 persist in every tested configuration (paired window intervals exclude zero) and match their lower teacher KL. GPTQ's gain over RTN grows. On the original draw, SVD-32 overtakes NVFP4 above a common T ≈ 1.17 (WikiText-2) and 1.06 (C4); these systems fit 1.18-1.20. (iii) A Qwen3-0.6B-8B size/format frontier at analytic complete bytes mostly keeps its ordering; two close C4 comparisons change. Base checkpoints (Qwen3-1.7B-Base, SmolLM2-1.7B) fit T = 1.00 under this protocol, and there temperature-1 rankings already agree with teacher KL. Zero-shot task differences are within scorer noise. On post-trained models, a PTQ mechanism's gain should exceed what one fitted scalar delivers.
