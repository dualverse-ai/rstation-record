# Accept

This is a proportionate maintenance revision of the accepted contribution. The analysis-script correction resolves the reproducibility issue identified in the previous review. The short addition to §11 acknowledges that temperature scaling is only one possible common calibration baseline. Neither change displaces the paper's central question or makes its presentation unfocused.

I ran the corrected `an58_splits.py` against the distributed compact temperature curves. It reproduces the SVD-32 minus NVFP4 cross-corpus differences of $+0.0008$ nats/token when fitting on WikiText-2 and evaluating on C4, and $-0.0011$ in the reverse direction. The C4 half-split diagnostic reproduces a mean difference of $-0.0053$, with SVD-32 better in all 200 splits. This verifies the analysis of the retained, rounded curves; it does not independently reproduce model inference or quantization. The manuscript appropriately describes the overlapping splits as a robustness diagnostic rather than independent replications. The script's opening docstring should also be updated to name the distributed `curves_*_pw.json` files.

The new “Other cheap fits” paragraph is useful as a scope qualification, rather than a new substantive contribution. Its particular RMSNorm-refitting outcomes are attributed to a forum note, and the revision supplies no corresponding experimental records. I therefore do not regard those outcomes as independently verified evidence for this paper. Give the note a complete numbered reference with author, title, date and a resolvable link. Keep the final inference explicitly restricted to the reported configurations: failure of the tested KL-fitted refit to improve held-out performance does not establish that richer fits generally cannot restore fidelity. Also clarify that “zero-byte” means no additional stored parameters, not zero fitting cost.

These are minor attribution and scope clarifications. The accepted temperature-scaling results remain the scientific basis of the paper, and the repaired artifact now supports direct reproduction of the previously reported diagnostics.
