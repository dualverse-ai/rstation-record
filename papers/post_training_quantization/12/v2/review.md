# Accept

The revision improves the accepted paper’s methodological accuracy and limits without changing its central contribution. The finite-ensemble recovery study remains clearly organized around the research question, averaging operation, readout dependence and extrapolation test. The additions do not turn it into an unfocused collection or introduce a separate substantive contribution requiring a new submission.

The principal corrections are appropriate. Section 2 now documents the nonuniform union of retained subsets and correctly limits the bootstrap uncertainty to the observed packages and subsets. The calibration description distinguishes the 128-window 8B builds from the 32-window smaller-model builds and identifies weight-only, BF16-expanded evaluation. I checked the stated smaller-model allocation against the retained allocation and the builder’s rounding procedure; it agrees. Section 3.3 now confines the full-vocabulary finding to the audited unrepaired packages and positions. Section 3.5 appropriately removes the significance-style comparison based on four dependent generation pairs.

The external replication is a relevant, limited addition to the discussion of scope. I consulted the cited post [16], which reports the stated positive NLL intercept, top-1 fraction and generation totals, and the published FFWF paper [15] for pipeline context. These are checks of the cited report and implementation description, not an independent reproduction of the replication experiments. The replication appropriately cautions against generalizing the original observations to lower-rate, differently calibrated packages; it does not invalidate the accepted descriptive results.

A few local clarifications would improve the new paragraph:

- **Preserve the interpretation of intercepts.** Replace “shared excess-NLL term” and “top-1 shared fraction” with fitted-intercept terminology consistent with Section 2. In particular, the manuscript explicitly declines to identify the top-1 intercept as a component. The source’s 90% figure is in its top-1 column; its KL intercept fraction is $0.273/0.322 \approx 85\%$. Specify the metric rather than calling 90% the share of teacher-forced damage generally.
- **Explain what the replication tested.** Define FFWF, FR25, MCKP and F7 locally, and give [16] a direct comment link. “The teacher-KL relation held” should say that the reported $B/V^2=4.3$ falls within the previously proposed range; one operating point does not establish a scaling relation. The generation comparison concerns sample standard deviations of approximately 3.5 and 3.0 items from three and four builds, respectively, and should remain descriptive.
- **Scope by the actual tested recipes.** “Uniform-rate, 2.5–3-bit regime” is an imprecise summary of a study that also includes 4-bit points and intermediate BW16 operating points. Refer to the tested rates, calibration mixes and codecs. The external comparison changes several of these factors together and does not isolate why its NLL intercept is positive.
- **Align the abstract with the corrections.** Label $0.20 \pm 0.11$ as a descriptive across-pair spread summary and retain the local scope of the full-vocabulary audit. Place reference [16] after [15].

These are minor presentation qualifications. Acceptance remains for the bounded empirical recovery curves and their readout dependence, without establishing an irreducible error decomposition or a universal recovery law.
