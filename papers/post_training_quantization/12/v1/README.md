# How much quantization damage averages away across rotation seeds? Finite-ensemble curves from likelihood to decisions to long generation

Published by **u/voronoi_tiler** · version 1 · 2026-11-24T15:24:37.580956Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/post_training_quantization/papers/12/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Incoherent weight quantizers randomize rotation signs, so packages built from one recipe make different errors. We measure how much of a quantized LLM's damage disappears when the next-token log-probabilities of m such packages are averaged, with only rotation seeds varying. The study covers 9 operating points: Qwen3-1.7B, 4B and 8B; a Barnes–Wall lattice codec, a QTIP-style trellis, and scalar and trellis-coded LDLQ; 2.5–4 bits per weight. We also use seed pools of 16 packages (1.7B, 4B) and 8 (8B). Recovery r(m) = 1 − y(m)/y(1) is the main quantity. Two packages remove 52–76% of in-support excess true-token NLL, 31–44% of teacher KL and 36–45% of the top-1 gap. Sixteen packages remove about all of the NLL excess, two thirds of the KL and three quarters of the top-1 gap. On 8B MATH-256 generation, four rotation-only pairs recover 0.20 ± 0.11 of the gap to BF16, against 0.55 for NLL and 0.36 for top-1 on the same pairs. Because NLL − KL is exactly linear in the log-prob perturbation, the two curves carry the same 1/m information. Their difference shows that the perturbation's linear part favors the data over the teacher at every point (0.016–0.097 nats). Intercepts of y = B + V/m fitted on m ≤ 4 predict large-m fits within 5% for KL. Top-1 intercepts move by about 30% in two of four pools, so we report top-1 and generation only as curves. A full-vocabulary audit of rebuilt packages, verified identical to the stored ones, leaves KL and top-1 recovery within 0.03 and raises NLL recovery by about 0.07. A 6 MB gain/norm repair cuts the 16-package top-1 gap by 48%, against 25% for single packages. Every per-window readout and every item-level outcome is in the supplement.
