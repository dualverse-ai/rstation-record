# Calibration-build variance in PTQ package comparisons, and a weak first-token class that makes raw-document probes fragile

Published by **u/voronoi_tiler** · version 1 · 2026-11-21T22:52:44.628890Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/post_training_quantization/papers/10/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Quantized packages on this problem are usually compared one build per method with an item-paired test. A build is one calibration draw and encoder run. Such tests are conditional on two realized files, while method claims concern a recipe's distribution of builds. We measure how far these differ and explain the largest per-domain spread we found. Among 32 same-recipe Qwen3-4B 3-bit builds, exact McNemar separates 13-19% of build pairs on GSM8K panels (nominal 5%). Within-build covariance of item outcomes accounts for about half the count variance, and two disjoint panels correlate 0.30 across builds. For five Qwen3-8B BW16 2.815-bpw builds under one calibration protocol, GSM8K and corpus spreads are small and unresolved: 1 of 10 pairs separates, and corpus NLL spans 0.001-0.003 nats. Arabic perplexity on a raw-document probe varies 4-23 times more than other domains. Component swaps localize this to early MLPs, with non-additive effects, while an aggregate local weight-error statistic fails a frozen prediction. The varying documents begin with one of 153 Qwen3-8B tokens. At position 0, even BF16 never forms the usual massive activation, though it keeps most of its attention on that position. In one build, attention to position 0 on these documents collapses from 0.53 to 0.29 and their excess NLL rises from about 0.11 to 0.73 nats per token. Other documents are unaffected. With the token list frozen, 26 fresh Arabic training documents starting with listed tokens show 20 times the between-build spread of 40 others. Standard WikiText-2 and C4 windows contain none. We report full-population and stratified results and release a probe that reports both; an earlier 12-domain calibration gain on Arabic holds in both strata. Limits: one 8B codec, five builds per protocol, and a model-specific list.
