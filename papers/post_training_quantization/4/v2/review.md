# Accept

This revision improves the precision and interpretation of the accepted contribution without introducing a new substantive result or obscuring the central argument. The manuscript remains focused on predicting calibration-Hessian layer-error optimism, with the unsuccessful quantizer examples serving as evidence of its practical relevance and limits.

The principal corrections are scientifically appropriate. The opening now uses the damped Hessian to define $U$, states the zero-mean, uncorrelated, equal-variance noise assumptions, and distinguishes a ratio of expected errors from an identity for each realized quantizer. The revised discussion correctly limits agreement across element codes to the three tested codes and treats positive optimism as an empirical observation rather than a universal inequality. Explicitly distinguishing fresh WikiText windows from document-disjoint C4 data, and identifying the 12 shared covariance estimates underlying the 21 matrices, improves interpretation of the descriptive prediction checks.

The corrected scale-offset uncertainty agrees with the retained results: the paired C4 differences are approximately $0.019608$, $0.023843$, and $0.057693$, giving mean $0.033714$ and sample standard deviation $0.020873$. The revised claim of an observed disagreement with the layer proxy, without population-level inference from three seeds, is justified. This verification recalculates retained measurements; it does not independently reproduce Hessian collection or whole-model experiments.

The added discussion of [14] and [15] appropriately distinguishes the prediction target from block-relative format distortion and acknowledges the limitations of fixed-temperature perplexity. Attribution of the error-feedback model and trace identity to prior work also clarifies the existing contribution. The one-model, input-side-Hessian, simulated-quantization scope remains explicit.

Document consistent data placement and the necessary portable path adjustments. These packaging limitations do not prevent inspection of the retained evidence, but the advertised regeneration workflow still needs adjustment.

Rename Section 4.1's unqualified “code-independent” heading to indicate agreement across the tested codes.

Label these as outside the present paper and distinguish completed from planned runs. Narrow “every whole-model result” in Section 6 to results supporting this manuscript. These ledger entries do not establish a new damping method or extend the accepted scientific claims.
