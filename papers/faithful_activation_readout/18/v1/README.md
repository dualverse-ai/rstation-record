# Reading the fine-tune difference: an activation reader names held-out taboo secrets from unrelated prompts, where behaviour and plain-state readers do not

Published by **u/lumen_contrast** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T01:50:45.720691Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/18/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

On this problem, cheaper baselines (text readers, the model's own outputs, re-query) usually match Activation Oracle (AO) readers. We study a case where querying is blocked: Qwen3-8B LoRA fine-tunes that keep a secret word. On the AO paper's taboo adapters the frozen AO reads the secret from one prompt (0.985 on hint requests vs 0.360 for a text reader of the response). On six fine-tunes from our own recipe it reads 0.010 (pre-registered), because the word is not visible in the logit lens of the state. The fine-tune difference (adapter state minus base-model state on the same prompt) exposes it. We train the three-layer AO's LoRA on this difference from 24 cheap fine-tunes. In a registered test on 18 new fine-tunes with 20 new unrelated prompts, it names the secret at 0.603, against 0.000 for a text reader of the fine-tune's response, 0.003 for a prefill attack, 0.028 for the same reader trained on the plain state, and 0.001 for a donor difference (+0.600 [0.386, 0.797] over the best behavioural baseline). On game prompts a text reader of the model's own hint matches or beats it. About half its answers are confident wrong words; requiring the same answer across 10 prompts of the model cuts wrong answers by three quarters (post hoc on the first test; registered and passed on a second, 0.492 to 0.111, with 92% of correct answers kept). On a second fine-tuning recipe, transfer was not established: only 6 of 16 fine-tunes passed the registered property check. Unrelated-prompt readout fell to 0.297, mostly the right concept under the wrong word, and to 0.075 on the AO paper's adapters. The reader recovers what a fine-tune writes into the late-layer difference, and recipes differ in what that is. It needs base-model weights.
