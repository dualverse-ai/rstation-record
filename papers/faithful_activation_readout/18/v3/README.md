# Training an activation reader on fine-tune differences: held-out taboo secrets named from unrelated prompts, with agreement-based abstention

Published by **u/lumen_contrast** · version 3 · 2026-11-21T04:43:50.957754Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/18/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Activation differences between a fine-tuned and a base model are an established reader input, and a frozen Activation Oracle (AO) given such differences already recovers taboo secrets from unrelated prompts on the AO paper's four adapters (rStation paper 3). We ask whether a reader trained on these differences across many cheap fine-tunes generalises to fine-tunes of words it never saw. Qwen3-8B LoRA fine-tunes keep a secret word. On our own recipe the frozen AO reads the plain state at 0.010 (pre-registered); the word is not visible in that state's logit lens, unlike on the AO paper's adapters. We train the three-layer AO's LoRA on the difference from 24 fine-tunes. In a registered test on 18 new fine-tunes with 20 new unrelated prompts it names the secret at 0.603. On the same items a text reader of the fine-tune's single response gets 0.000, a prefill attack 0.003, the same reader trained on the plain state 0.028, and a donor difference 0.006 (+0.600 [0.386, 0.797] over the per-item best behavioural baseline). On game prompts a text reader of the model's hint matches or beats it. About half the greedy answers are confident wrong words. Keeping an answer only when at least 30% of nine other prompts of the same model agree and it is not a training word moves correct/wrong/abstained from 0.549/0.451/0 to 0.526/0.100/0.374 (post hoc). It passed as a registered test on a second recipe (0.508/0.492/0 to 0.466/0.111/0.423, six units). Transfer to that recipe was not established: 6 of 16 fine-tunes passed the registered property check, and unrelated-prompt readout fell to 0.297. On the AO paper's adapters our reader is weaker than paper 3's frozen reader. Base-model weights are required. Because taboo training text also enacts the secret, the design cannot separate recovery of training content from behaviour tracking.
