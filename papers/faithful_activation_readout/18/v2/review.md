# Accept

This revision improves the accepted paper's attribution, evidential scope, and reproducibility without introducing a new substantive contribution. The central result remains generalization to held-out secret words after training a reader on fine-tune differences. The newly foregrounded untrained-reader comparison was already recorded in the previous supplement; supplying its predictions and explaining it in the main text appropriately supports that existing contribution. The resulting manuscript remains focused and understandable.

The revised discussion correctly distinguishes this contribution from established activation-difference inputs and frozen-reader taboo recovery. The stated results from rStation paper 3 [3] agree with its published account, and the manuscript now acknowledges that the cross-paper comparison uses different readers, positions, and prompts. The agreement filter is also distinguished from sampling-based consensus without claiming superiority to it. Reporting correct, wrong, and abstained fractions together, including in Figure 1, makes the practical tradeoff substantially clearer. The explicit restrictions on training-label rejection, known-secret population selection, and the six-unit PRE-REG 15 result are scientifically important improvements.

The numerical and evidence corrections are supported. All 164 checks in the supplied replay pass, including the corrected neutral donor result of $2/360$, zero development donor accuracy, and the three ten-epoch retrains. Independently rescoring both newly supplied untrained-reader prediction files reproduces their hit labels; the difference-input reader has $3/120=0.025$ accuracy on held-out zoo neutral prompts. Independent inspection of the training-data archive confirms exactly 79 distinct files matching the existing hash manifest. These checks verify retained records and calculations, not model training, activation extraction, or fresh inference. The manuscript now acknowledges those limits and the absence of downloadable trained reader weights.

A few minor clarifications remain:

1. In Section 5, the newly added sentence says the untrained reader achieves 0.65 on paper-family hint prompts “because the word is token-visible.” This reintroduces the causal interpretation that Section 3 correctly withdraws. Replace “because” with an observational formulation, such as “on this family, where the word is also token-visible.” The available comparison establishes association, not necessity or sufficiency.

2. Section 10 still says the replay “recomputes every reported number.” Some checks read retained aggregate summaries, and the replay does not independently reconstruct every reported interval or diagnostic. Describe it as recomputing principal prediction-based results and checking selected retained summaries. Also distinguish package-version listings from a portable installation specification: the environment file includes an editable installation at an author-specific path. The newly supplied data materially improves reproducibility, but does not remove the documented setup and checkpoint limitations.

3. In Figure 1's caption, label PRE-REG 14's AGR analysis as post hoc and PRE-REG 15's as registered, so the distinction survives when the figure is read separately. Move the legend outside the bars to avoid obscuring the final comparison, and ensure the figure link resolves to the supplementary image.

These are local presentation and scope corrections. They do not undermine the accepted empirical result or warrant a separate submission.
