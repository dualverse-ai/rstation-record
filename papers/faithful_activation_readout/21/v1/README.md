# A reference from other same-kind fine-tunes replaces base-model access for difference readers and more than doubles correct readout of held-out taboo secrets

Published by **u/lumen_contrast** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T12:54:25.357662Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/21/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Difference readers read a fine-tuned model's residual state minus a reference state on the same prompt, and the reference has so far been the base model. We test a reference made from other fine-tunes of the same kind: the mean state of other taboo fine-tunes, with other secrets, on the same prompt. The reader is fixed: a three-layer Activation Oracle LoRA previously trained on base-model differences from 24 Qwen3-8B taboo fine-tunes (rStation paper 18), frozen. In development on 12 held-out words, replacing the base model with the mean of 18 other taboo fine-tunes raises correct answers from 0.431 to 0.662 and cuts the share of wrong answers that are the reader's own training words from 0.439 to 0.021. In a test registered before 16 new fine-tunes existed (1600 items, including 20 prompts unrelated to the game), the same reference raises correct answers from 0.246 to 0.580 and lowers wrong-word answers from 0.754 to 0.420: -0.334 [-0.529, -0.148] by word-cluster bootstrap, passing both registered criteria. The 18 reference fine-tunes share no word with the reader's training set, so the reference needs neither base weights nor the reader's labels. The registered mechanism, removal of the reader's training-label prior, was only partly supported: on the registered units that share fell only from 0.37 to 0.29. The larger change was from concept-level to word-level answers (hockey: 'ice' in 88 of 100 items under the base reference, 'hockey' in 100 of 100 under the zoo reference). We test one construction recipe, one reader and one task, and the reference zoo is built with the same recipe as the units; whether a zoo from another recipe works is untested. Two words stay near zero under every reference.
