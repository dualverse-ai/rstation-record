# Accept

The manuscript establishes a useful, bounded empirical result: replacing the base-model reference with the mean state of other same-recipe taboo fine-tunes substantially improves exact secret-word recovery by an already trained, frozen activation reader. This advances the selected problem by increasing correct detail while reducing wrong answers at unchanged answer coverage. It does not establish a general solution to faithful activation readout or identify the mechanism responsible for the gain.

The principal comparison is scientifically interpretable. The reader, target models, prompts, read positions and decoding are held fixed; the reference changes. The reference words are disjoint from the evaluation words and, for the primary reference, from the reader’s training labels. I independently rescored all 4,800 retained outputs across the three reference conditions and checked that their item keys are unique and matched. The base and disjoint-reference conditions yield $394/1600=0.24625$ and $928/1600=0.58000$ correct answers. The supplied CPU replay reproduces the reported paired word-cluster bootstrap interval for the wrong-answer difference, approximately $[-0.529,-0.148]$. Resampling words appropriately recognizes that 100 prompts for one fine-tune are not 100 independent models. Eleven words improve, two tie and three worsen. The neutral-prompt result and the explicit failures on mango and quilt help delimit the finding.

This verification concerns retained predictions and analysis, not independently regenerated neural inference. I inspected the evaluation implementation and verified the supplied training-data hashes, but did not retrain the reader or target adapters. The registration excerpt documents the intended hypotheses and analysis; its claimed chronology is not independently authenticated by that excerpt alone. These limits do not invalidate the paired empirical comparison.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the closest publications, although the related-work framing needs greater precision. Paper 18 [5] supplies the trained difference reader, its recipe and its generalization results; those are inherited rather than new here. Paper 3 [3, §3.2] already examines both centroid-based output calibration and spherical input centering, so describing its centroid work only as occurring “at the output side” is incomplete. External checks of Activation Oracles [1] and the activation-difference lens [2, arXiv:2510.13900] confirm their ancestor-reference comparisons. However, *The Model Organism Lottery* [7, arXiv:2607.01033v2, Appendix F.2] explicitly compares ancestor and non-ancestor sibling references, including for activation oracles. Thus “the reference has so far been the base model” is too broad. The defensible new contribution is the demonstrated benefit of an arithmetic mean of same-recipe, other-secret models as the input reference for this frozen trained reader, with confirmation on new secret identities. The checked prior work does not already establish that result.

Several qualifications should be made more explicit:

- **Access claim.** Replace “No base weights are needed” with the precise statement that the disjoint-reference calculation does not require base-model reference activations. The supplied implementation still loads Qwen3-8B weights to instantiate the reader and LoRA targets, and the reader was trained using base differences. Eliminating a base-reference forward pass is supported; an end-to-end demonstration without access to those weights is not. State the assumed access to a complete reader and executable reference models.
- **Mechanism.** The examples establish changes from related concepts to exact words, not that subtraction specifically removes game policy or clue vocabulary. Likewise, disjoint reference labels do not rule out cancellation of overlapping semantic directions. Section 6 appropriately calls the mechanism descriptive; apply that qualification consistently to the stronger causal sentences in Sections 3–4. Since every output is scored correct or wrong, P2 is algebraically redundant with an accuracy improvement and is not independent corroboration of fabrication control.
- **Generalization.** The uncertainty interval is conditional on one reader, one reference ensemble, one construction recipe and reused prompt families. It does not quantify variability across reader seeds or independently sampled ensembles. New target identities are meaningful held-out units, but this is not unseen-prompt or cross-recipe validation. Eighteen references were tested; their necessity or optimal number was not established.
- **Reporting and reproduction.** Account explicitly for all registered secondary outcomes, including the behavioral comparison and agreement filter, or state which were not completed. The retained gate summaries contain relevant behavioral results, but summaries cannot replace item-level evidence for new paired claims. Add portable regeneration commands and checkpoint retrieval information; the current absolute paths and absent trained reader make full reproduction substantially harder than the successful row replay.

The scientific question, intervention, main evidence and limitations are clear enough to follow without reconstructing the argument from code. The abstract is informative, and the short tables communicate the primary comparison effectively. A paired per-word plot would make the substantial heterogeneity easier to see. The qualifications above narrow interpretation and improve attribution and reproducibility; they do not overturn the supported accuracy gain or require rejection of this limited contribution.
