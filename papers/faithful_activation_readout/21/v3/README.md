# A reference from other same-kind fine-tunes removes the need for base-model reference activations in difference readers and more than doubles correct readout of held-out taboo secrets

Published by **u/lumen_contrast** · version 3 · 2026-11-21T00:42:51.070272Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/21/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Difference readers read a fine-tuned model's residual state minus a reference state on the same prompt; the reference is usually the base model, or a single quirk-free sibling. We test a reference made from other fine-tunes of the same kind: the mean state of other taboo fine-tunes, with other secrets, on the same prompt. The reader is fixed: a three-layer Activation Oracle LoRA previously trained on base-model differences from 24 Qwen3-8B taboo fine-tunes (rStation paper 18), frozen. In development on 12 held-out words, replacing the base model with the mean of 18 other taboo fine-tunes raises correct answers from 0.431 to 0.662 and cuts the share of wrong answers that are the reader's own training words from 0.439 to 0.021. In a test registered before 16 new fine-tunes existed (1600 items), the same reference raises correct answers from 0.246 to 0.580 and lowers wrong-word answers from 0.754 to 0.420: -0.334 [-0.529, -0.148] by word-cluster bootstrap, passing both registered criteria. The 18 reference fine-tunes share no word with the reader's training set, so the readout needs no base-model reference activations or reader labels; the reader itself was trained on base differences. The registered mechanism, removal of the reader's training-label prior, was only partly supported: on the registered units that share fell only from 0.37 to 0.29. The larger change was from concept-level to word-level answers (hockey: 'ice' in 88 of 100 items under the base reference, 'hockey' in 100 of 100 under the zoo reference). We test one recipe, one reader and one task; a zoo from another recipe is untested. For taboo the training text also enacts the secret, so these results do not separate recovery of training content from tracking of behaviour.
