# Accept

This revision appropriately clarifies the accepted free-choice forecasting study. It preserves the research question, methods and central evidence while correcting the interpretation of the earlier-option subgroup, disclosing the donor fallback, qualifying the statistical conclusions and improving attribution. The matched-only analysis is a limited sensitivity check of the existing intervention, not a new substantive contribution requiring a separate submission. The resulting manuscript remains focused and understandable.

The operational definition of an “earlier different-option match” is a substantial improvement over interpreting regex matches as changes of mind. Stating that read points precede the last qualifying phrase, rather than necessarily the first expression of the choice, also makes the forecasting endpoint clearer. The added explanation correctly distinguishes failure of a lower-confidence-bound criterion from evidence that the effect is below $0.10$, and distinguishes predictive accuracy contrasts from a decomposition of information.

I independently recomputed the added matched-only contrasts from the retained patch records using the stated 2,000-draw, seed-zero cluster bootstrap. They reproduce the reported results: $0.018\;[-0.032,0.073]$ for all 273 matched rows and $0.015\;[-0.044,0.079]$ for the 204 earlier-match rows; option-set clustering gives $[-0.043,0.085]$ for the latter. The signed median offset is $-10$ tokens. These checks support the conclusion that the fallback does not explain the unresolved patch advantage. They verify analysis of retained outputs, not renewed model inference.

A few minor inconsistencies remain:

- In §5, the earlier-match A1 table row says that 52 rows use the fallback. That is the count across all 325 rows; the 245-row earlier-match subgroup has 41 fallback rows. Correct the parenthetical or explicitly identify its population.
- Carry the improved operational wording into the abstract and summary passages. “Scores the same” should mean no resolved difference, and the reported zero against the token-matched donor includes fallback rows. In §6, “does not carry the rollout-specific choice” should say that the tested patch does not transfer detectable predictive information. An earlier hypothetical option mention also does not establish that a commitment itself spans multiple phrases. These qualifications are already explained elsewhere and should be consistent throughout.
- The reproduction instructions improve transparency but do not yet make the inference commands portable merely by editing `ao_lib.py`: for example, `prereg29_descriptive.py` and `p12_optionset_sensitivity.py` retain independent absolute paths. Document those substitutions too. The checkpoint hash prefixes and model index hash do not identify all model weights or a complete upstream revision. Preserve the explicit admission that revisions were not recorded, and provide full file hashes or immutable revisions where recoverable.

These are localized reporting and reproducibility improvements. The revision strengthens the interpretation of the existing bounded empirical result without introducing a serious scientific regression or an unfocused expansion.
