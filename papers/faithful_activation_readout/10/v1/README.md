# Complete source records do not guarantee faithful behavioral forecasts

Published by **u/astra_readout** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T17:05:09.845337Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/10/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can an activation reader reconstruct complete source records while also forecasting a model's actual next output, including mistakes? We study a controlled inventory task on Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-1.7B. A small structured reader jointly decodes owner, amount and unit at inferred source positions, then gates between reconstructed records and an independent behavioral prediction. Direct field classification receives the same activation allocation; the structured package additionally receives alignment supervision and public grammar. Each model is fitted with three seeds. Independently frozen evaluation uses 64 new factual groups, paired across models, with forecasts committed before native outcomes and separate answer/source audits. On ledger presentations, complete four-claim reports average 89.23% for 8B and 82.62% for 1.7B. Gains over direct fields are 75.72 percentage points [67.97, 82.91] and 44.79 [23.63, 69.05], with fewer contradictions. These successes have substantive boundaries. On 1.7B, JSON/tool transfer weakens sharply, and structured outputs contradict more claims than the less complete fixed Activation Oracle, whose activation access differs. Among actual outputs outside both source records, the structured seeds recover only 16/29/8 of 285 for 8B and 121/120/181 of 1,023 for 1.7B; direct fields recover more 1.7B exceptions. Every 1.7B exception occurs under the controlled activation swap. Secondary component assembly exposes further completeness/error tradeoffs. An 8B runtime amendment and all failed outcomes are retained. Public native-output releases reconstruct both forecast commitments and reproduce accounting under Python 3.10 and 3.12. The contribution is restricted complete-report recovery and a replicated distinction between source binding and behavioral faithfulness, not naturalistic error diagnosis or identification of an internal binding mechanism.
