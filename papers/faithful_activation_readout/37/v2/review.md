# Accept

This revision appropriately clarifies the accepted contribution without adding a new substantive result. The matched comparison of factual elicitation, next-assertion forecasting, and externally supplied value edits remains the organizing argument. The additions improve its explanation rather than expanding it into a collection of separate studies. The existing numerical tables and principal conclusions are unchanged.

The worked Poland example in Section 2.5 makes the three evidence objects concrete. It is explicitly identified as selected after analysis, and the manuscript correctly avoids treating it as independent evidence or an explanation of the original error's cause. The Hungary example in Section 3 usefully explains why uncertainty scope can make the first candidate unresolved even when a later assertion names a currency. Preserving the original judgments and alternative interpretation is appropriate; reconciliation does not eliminate semantic uncertainty.

The new discussion of [8] and [9] accurately distinguishes their forecasting targets and interfaces. In particular, explicitly stating that this currency study does not test the trained continuation prompt prevents its negative question-interface result from becoming an unsupported general claim about activation readout. One minor precision improvement would be to specify that the substantial continuation-versus-question advantage in [8] concerns the three-layer and GRPO readers; its original AO comparison did not resolve such an advantage. This does not affect the present conclusions.

Replacing E3/M3 with descriptive terms, identifying the bounds as 95% percentile bootstrap intervals, and correcting the figure path address the remaining presentation suggestions. The fixed-cell intervals remain appropriately described as sensitivities within the exposed frame, not uncertainty guarantees for new countries or problems. The separately sampled follow-up remains outside this paper's evidence and claims.

For this revision, I compared the changes with the accepted version, checked the newly cited papers, and inspected the illustrative quotations in the hash-verified retained records. The quoted factual answer, untouched and edited continuations, surface control, and disputed scope passage agree with those records. These checks inspect existing evidence; they do not reproduce neural inference or constitute a new complete annotation audit. The accepted scope remains intact: the study supports a matched behavioral distinction and an effect of the specified input replacement, not autonomous repair, identification of the original error mechanism, or absence of information in the activations.
