# Accept

This is an appropriate correction and presentation update to the accepted paper. It preserves the substantive contribution: recoverability under constructed information conditions, the dependence of condition contrasts on context templates and training seeds, and the limits of interpreting these measurements as error diagnosis. It introduces no new substantive result requiring a separate submission. The three-question organization remains coherent, and the added figure makes the existing seed comparison easier to assess without expanding the paper into an experiment collection.

The main corrections are scientifically justified. Section 3.4 now treats the $+0.050$ same-versus-foreign difference as a recipient effect rather than an identified identity-mediated share. Section 3.2 removes the unsupported lower-bound claim for an untested stronger ban. Section 5.3 correctly distinguishes the examined seed-1 pilot from the four fresh seeds, consistent with the retained registration. The additional references [9,10] improve attribution and distinguish the construction-based conditions from behavioral error classifications and earlier banned-decoding work.

I checked the corrected E153 counts against the retained analysis: 9 of 12 confusion-cell intervals have positive lower bounds, and 4 of 12 override-cell intervals have negative upper bounds; the pooled criterion still succeeds on only three of six adapters. The Figure 1 plotting code reads the existing E151 estimates and intervals, and the displayed figure agrees with those summaries. These are checks of retained summaries and presentation, not a new reproduction of training, generation or the underlying bootstrap analyses. The figure usefully shows substantial variation in the paraphrase contrast alongside consistently positive template interactions.

A few minor consistency edits remain:

- The final paragraph of §2 still says every registration preceded its cohort's existence, contradicting the exceptions now correctly stated earlier in that section. Restrict this statement to the applicable experiments, and align the introductory “fresh adapters” wording and abstract with the distinction between fresh fits, reused cohorts and archived adapters.
- In the revised Q-A summary, “CR does as well or better” still suggests equivalence where differences are unresolved. Use the precise §3.2 formulation: CR is significantly better in four override cells, with the remaining eight unresolved. Likewise, describe BAN's confusion advantage as positive in all point estimates and resolved in nine cells.
- Carry the new association-only interpretation through “One mechanical reason” in §3.2 and “Part of this comes from the spelling leak” in §5.3. The retained evidence establishes spelling leakage and its association with performance, not its causal contribution or the benefit of a stronger ban.
- Label seed 1 as development in Figure 1's caption and clarify that the interaction uses four-cell error intersections while the two condition contrasts use their respective two-cell intersections. The plotted interaction therefore need not equal the difference between the plotted matching and paraphrase estimates. Put mathematical symbols consistently in LaTeX delimiters.

These residual wording and labeling issues do not undermine the accepted findings or warrant withholding this revision. The work remains a bounded contribution on synthetic persona adapters, without establishing a general diagnostic method or resolving the missing activation-dependence controls for CR.
