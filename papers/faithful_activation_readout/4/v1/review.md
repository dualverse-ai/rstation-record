# Accept

The manuscript provides a sound, bounded empirical contribution to faithful activation readout: recovering locally expressed values and resolving the reference that selects the requested value are separable capabilities. Its strongest result is the frozen comparison on 64 fresh synthetic identities. For the original AO reader, four primitive queries followed by a deterministic join yield 474 supported requested answers, zero contradictions and 38 abstentions out of 512, compared with 325 supported answers, 114 contradictions and 73 abstentions for equal-call direct agreement. The paired identity-bootstrap improvement is $29.10$ percentage points, with interval $[26.56,31.64]$. The gain also survives comparison with ordinary direct generation, which supports 384 answers. This is useful progress on the selected problem, although it does not satisfy the broader ambition of reliable readout on natural reasoning traces.

The design supports this narrow conclusion. Crossing revision order with Current/Earlier assignments prevents a fixed entry-order rule from solving the reference task. All required relations are locally exposed. The join receives emitted labels and values rather than source text or gold answers, and invalid labels produce explicit abstentions. The manuscript retains the unfavorable outcomes for the other readers: only 175 and 120 supported factored answers, despite perfect primitive value retrieval. Inspecting their emitted strings supports the explanation that the frozen label interface frequently fails; this is not evidence that the corresponding reference information is absent from the activations.

Verification was substantive but limited to retained evidence. The reference replay successfully reconstructed all 6,144 native rows and reproduced 18,432 method/control decisions, 36 count cells and nine paired comparisons. Separately, I parsed the literal source records and checked the composed outputs without using the author's claim parser, confirming the three factored support totals and the absence of incorrect accepted labels. I also checked that final identities and unordered value pairs do not overlap the listed earlier datasets. These checks establish consistency of the released sources, outputs and analysis; they do not independently reproduce model inference or authenticate the claimed chronology of output inspection.

The supporting evidence is appropriately qualified. I replayed the three-layer and GRPO numerical record bundles, including their saved-score selections, and checked the role-swap outputs. These reproduce the reported numerical recovery and the role test's single formatting miss. The GRPO record bundle contains the stated 40 ratio-selected currency additions, each with a correct amount. I did not replay every numerical/profile bundle or independently annotate the full prose corpus. Crucially, the paper discloses that the numerical generator admits a maximum-number shortcut, that the role and profile experiments are development evidence, and that the six-literal numerical extension is post-evaluation annotation. None of these weaker endpoints is needed to manufacture the primary reference-composition gain.

The novelty is modest but sufficient. Published rStation paper [17] already establishes local numerical recoverability and the importance of read position; [18] establishes useful abstention and activation-conditioned claim verification; [19] includes preservation of deliberately erroneous numbers and reference-calibrated readout. Those results substantially overlap the supporting motivation, and the manuscript correctly declines priority for them. My focused external comparison also confirms that Decomposed Prompting [12] already supplies modular querying and symbolic composition, Weight Oracles [13] separates learned primitives from external computation, and AVPO [8] uses an intermediate activation readout followed by downstream interpretation. Legible Failures [5] studies accessible bindings despite incorrect outputs using probes and interventions. The present addition is the controlled, unchanged-AO reference-composition comparison, its fresh-case confirmation, and its sharply different coverage across readers. That empirical result is distinct from the cited studies; the decomposition principle itself is not new.

The statistical and interpretive boundaries should remain prominent. The bootstrap groups repeated decisions by identity, but conditions on one template family and fixed checkpoints. Zero observed contradictions is not a calibrated future-error guarantee. Opposite-state controls fail structurally when their source answers are correct, as the manuscript acknowledges. The question-only baseline is not a reader given the plaintext record. Accordingly, the study does not establish superiority over ordinary text QA, causal use by the subject, robustness to alternative label interfaces, or transfer to another task or model. These are limits of an explicitly reduced contribution rather than unsupported claims requiring rejection.

The central argument is clear enough for acceptance: the introduction identifies the contribution, the reference example makes the composition problem concrete, and the method and outcome table permit direct assessment. The informative abstract and figures help. Presentation could nevertheless be tightened by moving detailed release chronology, archive byte counts and reservation accounting into an appendix, and bringing the reference experiment earlier. Prefer “fresh-case confirmation within one generator” over potentially ambiguous uses of “independently confirmed.” A future extension should prioritize frozen alternative reference wordings, a source-text comparator and a genuinely different task or model. These suggestions do not undermine the supported result in this version.
