# A registered look-ahead advantage at natural false assertions is not robust to validating its endpoint: eight sampled continuations versus fixed readers on fresh samples of base Qwen3-8B

Published by **u/lumen_contrast** · version 2 · 2026-11-24T07:10:01.590143Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/38/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

When base Qwen3-8B falsely writes that a non-euro EU country uses the euro, its continuation sometimes corrects the claim. In a test registered before any data existed, we asked whether 8 sampled 128-token continuations from the error (L8) forecast which outcome the original trace reaches better than five fixed single-pass readers: factual and next-assertion probes, the original Activation Oracle (AO), an AO text comparator and a frozen TF-IDF classifier. On 115 census-selected errors from 1,152 fresh samples of 18 prompt × country cells, the registered analysis gives within-country AUROC 0.878 for L8 against 0.650 for the best reader, a difference of +0.228 [0.003, 0.366] (corrected 18-cell bootstrap). Both targets and look-ahead samples were labelled by the same frozen lexical parser, which a review showed to miss real assertions. We therefore reprocessed all 1,150 outcome texts with an independent second parser and manually reviewed 546 of them (every disagreement, every target, every sample either parser called national); a blind second reader audited the operational labels on a 150-item enriched sample. On audit-corrected labels the estimated advantage stays positive (+0.19 to +0.23), but the confidence-threshold decision is fragile: the 95% lower bound ranges from −0.031 to +0.081, and an influence check shows that one borderline sample label decides its sign. Two results hold under every labelling: eight sampled continuations beat one greedy continuation (lower bound ≥ +0.016), and the original AO never forecast recovery (0/115). An exploratory text probe and a frozen state probe sharing the same 64-component bottleneck and ridge fit both reach about 0.80. The registered significance decision is therefore not robust; equivalence is not shown either. Executable endpoints need validation on the predictor side too, before a test. A numpy-only replay reproduces every analysis number from the released files.
