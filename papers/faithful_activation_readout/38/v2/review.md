# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects and qualifies the accepted study. It preserves the contribution: a registered forecasting comparison whose confidence-threshold decision is sensitive to how predictor-side and target-side assertions are labelled. The changes introduce neither a separate substantive contribution nor an unfocused expansion. The resulting manuscript still explains the question, decisive example, evidence and limitations clearly.

The most consequential correction is Section 2's disclosure that eligibility information had reached the census auditor for 15 of 72 attempts. I checked the retained comparison records: 57 attempts are marked unexposed, with three-way agreement on 56, eligibility agreement on all 57, and six shared eligible cases. Thus the overall agreement cannot be presented as a wholly blind audit. The revised wording makes that distinction explicit. This reduces the strength of the independent census validation, but does not invalidate the narrower endpoint-sensitivity finding or change the selected cuts. The unexposed subset provides useful, limited corroboration; it should not be interpreted as comprehensive validation of census completeness.

The revisions to the abstract and Sections 5–6 improve scientific precision. They distinguish a consistently positive estimated advantage from a fragile significance decision, state that intervals condition on the annotation set, avoid claiming equivalence, and correctly distinguish eight sampled continuations versus one greedy continuation from the single-sample aggregation comparison. The replacement of “equal capacity” by the actual shared bottleneck and fitting procedure is also appropriate. These qualifications preserve the accepted evidence without strengthening its scope.

The added compute account and reproducibility documentation are useful supporting additions. Two minor qualifications remain:

- Section 7 reports grouped execution costs, rather than costs for each comparator in the primary analysis. The approximately sixfold ratio is arithmetically consistent with the reported totals, but its numerator includes the greedy continuation and its denominator includes additional probe/AO conditions and feature extraction. Call this a ratio of the reported execution groups, define Q4 and the five AO arms, and specify whether loading and fitting are excluded. It is not an exact L8-versus-best-reader efficiency comparison.
- Section 9 now distinguishes retained-score replay from feature regeneration. I inspected the new map-check script; it compares supplied feature-derived predictions with retained scores using a $10^{-9}$ threshold. The activation features are absent, so I did not execute that check or independently verify the stated $3\times10^{-16}$ discrepancy. Adding the helpers improves provenance, but their original absolute paths still require adaptation for regeneration. Document those path substitutions and retain the distinction between replaying released scores and reproducing neural inference.

These are reporting refinements, not grounds for rejecting the update. The correction of the audit's exposure status and the narrower interpretation of the statistical results materially improve the paper.
