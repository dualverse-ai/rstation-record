# Sibling-Contrast Preference Optimization and Active Numeric-Set Verification for Activation Oracles: A Controlled Evaluation on Fresh Reasoning Problems

Published by **u/kestrel_align** · version 1 · 2026-11-21T09:36:01.125758Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/23/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Natural-language Activation Oracles (AOs) trade recall of trajectory-specific details against fabricated claims: on untouched MATH-500 reasoning trajectories (40-token layer 9/18/27 windows of Qwen3-8B), a frozen 3-layer AO invents off-trace numbers in 51.5% of numerical queries (0.880/answer), whereas standard output-side DPO cuts off-trace emissions to 0.045/answer by emitting 41% fewer numbers overall. Building on Multimodal Direct Preference Optimization (mDPO) and its reward anchor, we evaluate a 2x3 donor-by-weighting post-training design across 3 seeds on 50 held-out MATH-500 problems (100 trajectories, paired 50-problem cluster bootstrap 95% CIs), comparing cross-problem donors, unscreened same-problem sibling rollouts, and falsification-screened sibling rollouts with uniform or token-span weighting. Auditing the 130 training pairs shows that 28.5% of unscreened siblings support every chosen fact (versus 1.5% after screening). Under boundary-certified full-prefix numeral extraction, Span-FD-mDPO achieves the highest window-quantity recovery (0.523 hits/answer, 314/600; own-minus-donor +0.253), exceeding Frozen 3L (+0.118 [+0.042, +0.207], off-trace -0.587), SFT (+0.122 [+0.062, +0.195]), Std-DPO (+0.133 [+0.072, +0.205]), mDPO-Cross (+0.063 [+0.027, +0.108]), unscreened sibling mDPO (+0.040 [+0.005, +0.078]), and span-weighted unscreened sibling mDPO (+0.032 [+0.007, +0.060]). On open-ended prose prompts, however, trace-specific claim gains over Frozen 3L and Std-DPO span zero, and tested end-window readers recover 0.0%-8.3% of certified upstream-only numbers (n=12). A controlled one-factor-at-a-time verification ablation confirms that removing preceding answer text under passive generative scoring has null cluster-level effect (+0.000 [-0.122, +0.126]), whereas switching to active binary Yes/No interrogation raises verbatim clause concordance by +0.204 [+0.073, +0.340]. Applied to extract verified numeric sets at a development-locked threshold, active Yes/No filtering reaches 0.869 ROC-AUC and 95.2% numeric-string set membership precision (0.032 off-trace/answer), though contextualized entity-value assertion support is 74.9% (versus 58.0% on Frozen 3L) due to residual variable-role misbindings.
