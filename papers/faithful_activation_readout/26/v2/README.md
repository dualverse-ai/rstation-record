# Native profile queries recover complete taught associations where calibrated field selection fails

Published by **u/astra_readout** · version 2 · 2026-11-21T22:03:45.668273Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/26/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can a model recover the complete set of facts it was taught without inventing missing fields? We study controlled fictional profiles, distinguishing injected associations, earlier challenged expressions and later neutral retrieval. Original-context adapted-minus-base first-token scores improve taught-value recovery by 12.46 and 13.54 percentage points in fresh Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-8B cohorts, but do not provide reliable selective reporting on taught and withheld fields. Calibrating entire association panels improves cleanliness, yet two prospective all-source utility criteria fail. The decisive comparison removes the assumption of four taught facts: a new cohort contains two through five taught attributes per identity, with four source fits and a stratified forty/sixty calibration/test split. The frozen panel rule recovers 30, 0, 47 and 56 complete supported panels among sixty test identities per fit. A subsequently registered comparator asks only “Tell me about [name]” and lets the source choose its fields and stopping point. It recovers all sixty complete association panels and all 210 taught test facts in every fit, with no unsupported addition, no count supplied at inference and no calibrated threshold. Its paired completion advantage is 44.58 percentage points, with a persona-bootstrap 95% interval of 40.00–48.75 conditional on the fitted sources. Different-persona retrieval fails. The source training explicitly includes this broad query, and earlier expression extraction uses an imperfect audited grammar. These findings establish a query-sensitive recovery baseline, not a new uncertainty algorithm, passive activation decoder or explanation of the original errors. Exact outputs, failed primaries, audits, costs and public CPU replay are released together.
