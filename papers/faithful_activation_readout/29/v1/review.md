# Accept

The paper provides a useful, bounded empirical advance on faithful activation readout. Its strongest contribution is the controlled separation of native first-token selection and frozen-base completion from additional residual grafting and AO proposals. The results support that decomposition on the tested single-value persona tasks, while the unsuccessful multiword experiments establish an informative limitation. This is substantively relevant to the supplied task, although it addresses elicited answer-position states rather than general descriptions of internal reasoning or the AObench hallucination task.

The central comparison is supported by the retained evidence. Independently counting exact matches gives $231/400$ for Seed-NoGraft and $242/400$ for Hom-Graft on C1, with 12 repairs and one regression; on C2 the corresponding counts are $202/304$ and $218/304$, with 18 repairs and two regressions. These reproduce the reported grafting increments of 2.75 and 5.26 percentage points. AO proposals add a smaller improvement on C1 and no resolved improvement on C2. The base-state and donor controls support dependence on the corresponding target evidence. The paper appropriately reports full target generation as a stronger reference when that access is available.

The negative results improve the scientific value of the study. In particular, the full-population C2 confusion comparison correctly exposes the damage caused by excluding an already-correct target output: 283 of 304 target continuations contain the taught value, and the registered comparison reverses. The separate error-conditional analysis is no longer substituted for that population. Likewise, the crossed C3 experiment shows that recovering an initial word does not establish recovery of the complete association. The $-7$ experiment is explicitly separate from answer-slot grafting and its confidence interval includes zero. These findings support limitations of the tested procedures, not universal impossibility claims.

For novelty, I compared the relevant published versions of papers 11, 24, 26 and 28, and checked the external Patchscopes, selective-classification and AO-confidence literature [18], [4], [17], [19], [5], [15], [20] (reference numbers as in the submission). Native late-layer decoding, runner-up recovery, first-token candidate ranking, probability-based rejection and state patching are established. The manuscript acknowledges these precedents. The additional controlled evidence about how much complete-value recovery comes from seed continuation, how much grafting adds, and where this composition fails is sufficiently distinct and useful to justify publication. Neither a new confidence estimator nor a general superiority claim over existing activation readers is established.

I ran the inspected CPU replay successfully: it reproduced the stored statistics and all 3,096 T1 and 1,860 conflict T2 candidate selections for seed 1729. A separate calculation also found no discrepancies between neutral candidate-score thresholding and the retained gated predictions. I independently confirmed the C2 confusion population and the sentinel counts, including the one C2 margin above 20. These checks verify the retained records and aggregation; they do not reproduce neural training, activation extraction or candidate generation. The candidate export code can synchronize stored selections to regenerated pools, so its exact replay should be described as internal consistency rather than an independent recovery of immutable original outputs.

Clarity is adequate for acceptance. Sections 2.1–2.2 now explain the access regimes, candidate construction and scoring rule sufficiently to follow the main argument. The large tables and extensive labels remain demanding, but the paper is organized around scientific comparisons rather than an experiment diary. The following refinements would improve precision without changing the decision:

- Clarify in the abstract that the three seeds concern AO training/sampling, with one target-adapter fit per cohort. The persona-bootstrap intervals condition on those fitted sources and do not establish robustness across target retraining or model families.
- Align method descriptions and table labels with the code. Section 2.2 specifies at least two alphabetic characters for native seeds, whereas `is_excluded_first_token` permits a single alphabetic character and excludes several stopwords. The `AO Greedy` rows are labelled as using recorded-output exclusion, but the supplied implementation retains the unfiltered greedy answer. Figure 1 should explicitly identify the differing exclusion policies used by its comparator bars.
- State the exact substring, exact-match, abstention and neutral selective-error definitions near the tables. Explain that withheld means absent from the controlled fine-tuning assignments, rather than absence of all model knowledge, and that $S_{\mathrm{SR}}$ is not a calibrated probability that the complete answer is correct.
- Mark the decomposition and later control analyses clearly as additional to the registered family. Describe the bootstrap tail calculation and its Monte Carlo resolution; do not interpret the repeated minimum adjusted values as more precise significance estimates.
- Add a concise reproducibility paragraph in the main text linking the supplement, locating the external E139 bundle, specifying dependencies and separating retained-record replay from neural reruns. Break down the reported GPU total by training and inference stages if making practical cost comparisons.
