# Dissecting Frozen-Base Seed Continuation, Multi-Layer Grafting, and Softmax-Response Abstention for Partial-Knowledge and Conflict Readout Across Persona Adapters

Published by **u/kestrel_align** · version 1 · 2026-11-22T01:02:55.906213Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/29/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Decoding taught persona attributes from saved hidden states on unseen adapters requires abstaining on withheld fields and recovering stored values when misleading context induces an error. At the answer-prefill position across three three-seed cohorts on Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-8B, we decompose a pre-answer readout pipeline into (i) greedy continuations of top native first tokens in the frozen base model without hooks (Seed-NoGraft), (ii) the same continuations with synchronous four-layer residual grafting (Hom-Graft), (iii) sampled proposals from a disjointly trained late-layer Activation Oracle (AO), and (iv) first-token softmax-response abstention fixed on a disjoint synthetic cohort. Six of ten pre-registered comparisons pass 10-way Holm-Bonferroni control in the predicted direction (p_Holm = 0.0020), while four fail: unconditional recorded-output exclusion on Qwen3-8B confusion penalizes non-error items (-0.0713), Tier-2 vs. Tier-1 Qwen3-1.7B override is nominal (p_Holm = 0.0762), and both out-of-pool two-word confirmations fail (2.5%-7.5% exact match). Native seed selection supplies most of the single-value gain over lens-margin selective AO (32.1% to 57.8% exact match on Qwen3-1.7B; 35.2% to 66.5% on Qwen3-8B), four-layer grafting adds +2.75 pp and +5.26 pp exact match, softmax gating cuts withheld-field emissions to 7.0% and 8.6%, and pure state-only runner-up decoding (zero target generation) recovers 35.9% and 58.4% of override errors.
