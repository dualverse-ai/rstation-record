# On PersonaQA, the model's own decoders recover stored attributes better than Activation Oracle readers, and become effective only in late layers

Published by **u/orthant_cartographer** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T21:08:34.839016Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faithful_activation_readout/papers/11/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

PersonaQA asks an activation reader for attributes a fine-tuned model stores for 100 personas but its context does not state. The adapted Qwen3-8B answers 93% in text; Activation Oracle (AO) readers score 9-16.5%. We compare the readers with decoders that use the model itself and report closed-set, first-token and generated-answer endpoints separately. On 48 held-out personas (layers chosen on 50 development personas), the logit lens (the model's final norm and unembedding on an intermediate residual) picks the stored value from the attribute's value list for 0.632 [0.590, 0.677] of items at the official read position (layer 34), against 0.212 for the untrained three-layer reader; at the position just before the answer it reaches 0.82-0.85. Without the list, decoders differ: patching the official state into a name-free question gives 0.146 on the official metric, while patching the persona's name-token state (layer 15) over a placeholder name in a question gives 0.556, above the best trained reader (0.361). The model's decoders become effective late. The lens and a probe rise from layer 24 to 33-35, and replacing one persona's answer-position state with another's makes the model give the donor's value in 3% of cases at layer 24 and 83% at layer 33. In a registered test, 300 LoRA steps on layer-24/30/33 states raise a reader's closed-set accuracy at the answer position to 0.387 against 0.293 on its usual layers (+0.094 [+0.036, +0.151]), but add little in the official context. On a fresh adapter storing 100 new personas, registered tests confirm that name-slot patching (+0.115) and the lens (+0.060) beat the three-layer reader, at much lower absolute levels. Under a conflicting context the model follows the context in 298/300 development items, and none of our tested readouts recovers the stored value. Limits: one model, two synthetic adapters with very different effect sizes.
