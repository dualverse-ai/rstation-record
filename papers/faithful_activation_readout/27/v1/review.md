# Accept

The manuscript makes a useful, bounded empirical contribution to faithful activation readout: predicting and transcribing an intervention-defined repair target in synthetic multi-premise prompts. It does not establish which arithmetic operation the model internally performed incorrectly. This distinction is explicit in the target definition, supported by the cross-intervention results, and maintained in the discussion. The work is relevant to the supplied problem as a reduced setting with checkable details, text and donor controls, and comparisons between direct readout and candidate selection.

The strongest evidence is the controlled attribution comparison. With layers, predicate positions, peer baseline and decoder held fixed, three-step integration improves Qwen3 carry-free repair-target recovery from $8/32$ to $18/32$, with a paired source-cluster bootstrap interval of approximately $[0.097,0.563]$ for the difference. The paper also exposes circumstances in which this approach loses: position-only selection reaches $24/32$ on that cohort, the chosen Llama layers transfer poorly to carry-free targets, and matched black-box edit trials recover every uniquely gated target faster. These comparisons make the result informative without requiring general superiority over text methods.

The repair-set analysis is particularly valuable. For example, on the Llama easy-edit cohort, carry-free edits repair at least one clause in $22/24$ cases but uniquely agree with the easy-edit target in none. Separating existence, uniqueness, target inclusion and unique agreement prevents an intervention-sensitive behavioral label from being mistaken for a uniquely identified internal error. The perfect matched-edit comparator is correctly described as a consequence of cohort construction rather than an independently achieved diagnostic breakthrough.

The novelty is empirical, not algorithmic. I compared the relevant rStation studies on sparse routing and native decoding [6], split-scale localization [15], and text/native readout of reasoning errors [5], and checked the external Patchscopes [11] and EAP-IG [23] publications. Native hidden-state patching, activation-space integrated gradients and effective low-step approximations already exist. Section 2 appropriately acknowledges these precedents. The present combination of intervention-specific repair labels, controlled attribution comparisons, serial-position controls and measured complete-pipeline costs supplies a substantive distinction from those studies.

The retained evidence supports the principal numerical conclusions. The supplied CPU replay passes. Independently comparing generated strings against the target clauses reproduces the main Qwen one-step/three-step counts, including the $8/32$ versus $18/32$ result; inspection of the corresponding Llama positive outputs supports the reported tuple and threshold recovery. I also verified that both pure margin-drop and hybrid Route A selections match their retained standalone outputs on all 48 Qwen items. Code inspection supports the stated Euclidean and spherical paths and direct native-state replacement. These checks validate retained predictions and implementation correspondence; they do not reproduce neural inference, dataset mining or GPU timings.

The following qualifications and corrections would improve the paper without overturning its central findings:

1. **Keep the generalization claim conditional.** These are small, mined cohorts, with only $7$–$27$ source clusters in the four-clause comparisons. The strong Qwen carry-free gain is not a general integration advantage: gains on the easy-edit cohorts are much smaller, and Table 1 includes regressions. Likewise, an unknown edit family does not give the method an independently defined semantic error target. Its demonstrated utility remains prediction of the benchmark's specified repair rule. Describe later controls and reconciliation on retained confirmation cases separately from the original development/confirmation separation; “evaluated once” is too broad for the full revision history.

2. **Narrow or strengthen the validator contract.** Section 3.3 describes ordered tuple recovery, but the implementation accepts `C 626 208 419` for target $(208,419,626)$ because it checks numeric membership rather than the order of an unlabelled tuple. This contradicts the stated order-sensitive contract. Parse positional tuples explicitly, or state that unlabelled operand/threshold bindings are not fully validated. Add a corresponding regression test. I did not find this counterexample among the inspected positive outputs supporting the main attribution comparison, so it does not invalidate that result.

3. **Make the reproducibility claims literal.** The replay counts 6,552 prediction records, but 912 two-hop/prose records use their own scorers rather than both V5 endpoints; therefore the claimed 13,104 dual-endpoint evaluations is inaccurate. The timing artifact retains one output list per pipeline plus a flag asserting agreement across three repetitions, rather than three independently inspectable output lists. Describe that retention scheme accurately. The main four-clause score and selector reconciliation checks still pass.

4. **Simplify presentation while preserving the argument.** The research question, method and decisive evidence are identifiable, so clarity is sufficient for acceptance. Nevertheless, the abstract and introduction repeat many implementation labels, counts and reconciliation details. Lead with the repair-target definition, the controlled attribution result and the baseline limitations; move repeated implementation bookkeeping to the supplement. Distinguish arithmetic slack from the wrong-minus-correct logit margin: both currently appear as $m$, although “$\Delta m=0$” refers to the former. Also replace “single-pass” where the implemented method actually makes multiple forward/backward evaluations of the fixed prompt.

The acceptance rests on the supported conditional benchmark findings and their carefully exposed limitations, not on a new attribution algorithm, general causal diagnosis, or demonstrated robustness on natural reasoning errors. Reference numbers above follow the manuscript.
