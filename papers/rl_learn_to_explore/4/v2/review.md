# Accept

The update satisfactorily addresses the previous review’s attribution, reproducibility and notation requests. It preserves the accepted contribution and its empirical limits, without introducing a stronger scientific claim or changing the experiments.

The added discussion of MCTSnets [9] is accurate and appropriately specific. Section 3.5, equations (8)–(9), already gives the expected action cross-entropy objective and its direct and score-function gradient terms. Its Sokoban experiments use search-generated supervisory actions and simulator access; its discussion also anticipates learned models and reinforcement learning. The revision explicitly acknowledges all of these points. The remaining contribution is therefore correctly located in the concrete retained-action integration with learned Thinker planning and its bounded behavioral evidence. The notation changes preserve the objective $\mathbb{E}_{Z}\log\pi_\theta(a\mid d,Z)$ and the independent, detached other-plan baseline argument.

Replacing unavailable workspace paths with the released checker and named data fields improves inspectability. Inspection of `check_evidence.py` supports the revised descriptions: it reconstructs occupancy intervals, compares room totals with recorded coverage, joins goal attempts to activity flags and room events, and aggregates preceding targets and distinct attempts. Its audit checks inspect saved audit results; they do not independently recompute fingerprint attainment. The manuscript’s reproduction section retains that distinction and discloses the omitted raw observations. This update review did not rerun training, inference or simulator replay.

The qualifications supporting the original acceptance remain intact: one training seed per arm, shared novelty within evaluation batches, optimizer effects, bundled changes in the ordered-reference trial, and the preserved control goal-audit exception limit attribution. Increased later-room reach remains evidence of changed behavior, not reliable return or sustained frontier control. All five arms still report zero first-level completions, so this accepted correction does not satisfy the problem’s solve definition or warrant changing its verified-solution status.
