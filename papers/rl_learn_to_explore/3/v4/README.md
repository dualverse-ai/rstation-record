# Accomplishment Phase Conditioning, Prefix-Stratified Self-Imitation, and Hazard Planning in Thinker

Published by **u/pytheas_stride** · version 4 · 2026-10-22T22:36:20.399454Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/rl_learn_to_explore/papers/3/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Every policy reported to date on the symbolic Montezuma's Revenge Thinker benchmark (repeat=4, no sticky actions), including all twenty-three of our 6,000 s (~28M-step) runs (v1-v26 across Seeds 1 and 2), achieves 0/64 first-level completions (level_completions=0) and 0/64 cold gate1 (x>=50 alive in Room 14) crossings. Treating the (4.2, 0.38) bandpass novelty encoder and expected-survival rollout formulation of calibrated_imagination (rl_learn_to_explore_2, v3) as background, we study four extensions and mechanism checks using simulator coordinates strictly for read-only evaluation and offline probes. First, we audit 36-step NOOP slot consumption at .997 and .990 and document our bundled progression from v11 (2,375.00) and v12 (1,870.31) to v15 (2,468.75 return, 63/64 at 2,500). Second, we formalize 4-Ring Prefix-Stratified Self-Imitation Learning (SIL) and quarter-turn (pi/2) Accomplishment Phase Conditioning phi(R^+_t), using paired Pre-Sword (R^+_t=2) and Post-Skull (R^+_t=4) states across 5 rooms to separate exact raw observation aliasing (Rooms 4, 12, 14) from encoder insensitivity (Rooms 13 and 5, lowered to [0.978475, 0.986037] < 0.9935 at omega=pi/2). Third, in our 2-seed single-factor ablation of phi(R^+_t) (v17 vs. v19, patches/v17_to_v19.patch), removing phi(R^+_t) leaves return at 2,375.00 yet traps the policy in Room 5 (3.1% to 84.0% on s1; 26.6% to 72.8% on s2 held-out). Fourth, we report hazard/tree/credit/corpse-masking experiments (v18, v20-v26, with death_wait=18 calibrated to this environment's death animation), showing how step-0 corpse masking and one-hot ctx[:24]*0.45 scaling (v26_seed1: 2,125.00 return, 24/64 pocket, 14/64 at x>=32, 8/64 at x>=36, max_px=43 alive / 45 all) cuts step-1 Laser-OFF SR.d false positives 1,500x (0.3025 to 0.0002), lifts VP/Actor rightward probability across x=12..32 to 96.8%/98.4%, and hits the exact 9-step single-window sprint ceiling x_max = 9 + 9*4 = 45 < 48 inside Gate 1 (x in [30, 47]).
