# Accept

This update is acceptable and preserves the scientific status of the previously accepted study. It adds `v26_seed1`, the `v23_to_v26` source patch, new retained corridor-floor and Room-14 policy arrays, and the corresponding receipt and manifest updates. The added run is correctly reported as unsolved: it has 0/64 first-level completions and 0/64 gate1 crossings, despite 64/64 first-room exits and improved frontier diagnostics (24/64 pocket entries, 14/64 reaching $x\ge 32$, and `max_px=43` alive).

The new mechanism discussion is appropriately scoped. The step-0 corpse masking and one-hot room-context scaling are a bundled, single-seed intervention, and the lower return than `v23` makes clear that the result is a diagnostic tradeoff rather than a demonstrated improvement. The retained arrays support the stated changes in frozen-model behavior: the corridor-floor Laser-OFF step-1 `SR.d` rate changes from $0.3025$ to $0.0002$, the Laser-ON five-step hazard mean changes from $0.2976$ to $0.7095$, and the real-root VP/Actor rightward probabilities reach 96.77%/98.44%. These are useful evidence about model and policy representations, but they do not establish that the learned agent can cross Gate 1 or that either factor is causally responsible in isolation.

The added geometric argument, $x_{\max}=9+9\times4=45<48$ for a single nine-step Laser-OFF sprint from the entrance, is a valid environment-specific ceiling under the stated starting-position, speed, and laser-phase assumptions. It usefully explains why a single-window attempt cannot reach the post-Gate-1 corridor, while the manuscript correctly presents the observed two-step continuation and the remaining failure to complete the level. The calibration of `death_wait=18` to this simulator and the absence of transfer evidence are now explicit.

I inspected the changed patch and scripts before execution. The pure-NumPy verifier completed successfully: all 158 manifest files and 22 patches passed digest and size checks; paired Pre-Sword/Post-Skull aliasing, Stage-4 probes, all 23 run receipts, held-out evaluations, and retained action traces matched the reported values. This verifies the packaged evidence and frozen-array calculations; it does not reproduce the expensive training runs or provide an independent live JAX replay of `v26`.

The update should retain its current qualifications in any publication: v26 changes multiple mechanisms at once, most post-v17 comparisons remain single-seed, and the corridor and policy numbers are checkpoint probes rather than completion-rate evidence. Future work would benefit from factorized v26 ablations and another training seed, but these are not required for acceptance of this bounded update.
