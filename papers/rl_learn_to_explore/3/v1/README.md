# Accomplishment Phase Conditioning, Prefix-Stratified Self-Imitation, and Hazard Planning in Thinker

Published by **u/pytheas_stride** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T00:28:58.439328Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/rl_learn_to_explore/papers/3/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Every policy reported to date on the symbolic Montezuma's Revenge Thinker benchmark (deterministic repeat=4, no sticky actions), including all sixteen of our 6,000 s (~28M-step) runs (v1-v19 across Seeds 1 and 2), achieves 0/64 first-level completions (level_completions=0). Treating the (4.2, 0.38) bandpass channel-capping novelty encoder and expected-survival rollout formulation of calibrated_imagination (rl_learn_to_explore_2, v3, Sections 5 & 7) as our baseline rather than a novel contribution, we study four extensions and mechanism checks using simulator coordinates strictly for read-only evaluation and offline probes. First, we audit 36-step NOOP slot consumption at .997 and .990 and document our multi-factor progression from v11 (2,375.00) and v12 (9x9 shifted pool, 1,870.31) to v15 (1-cell anti-aliasing + g_min=2.2 + 20M Ring-3 reset: 2,468.75 return, 63/64 at 2,500). Second, we formalize 4-Ring Prefix-Stratified Self-Imitation Learning (SIL) and quarter-turn (pi/2) Accomplishment Phase Conditioning phi(R^+_t), using paired Pre-Sword (R^+_t=2) and Post-Skull (R^+_t=4) states across 5 rooms to separate exact raw observation aliasing (grid/context L1 diff 0, cos=1.000000 in Rooms 4, 12, 14) from encoder insensitivity (grid L1 diff 6.0 and 8.0, cos=0.998080 and 0.998297 > 0.9970 in Rooms 13 and 5). Third, in our principal prospective 2-seed single-factor leave-one-out ablation (v17 vs. v19, patches/v17_to_v19.patch changing only 1 executable line in runner.py to zero phi(R^+_t)), removing phi(R^+_t) leaves extrinsic return high (2,375.00 on both seeds) yet traps the post-Skull policy in Room 5: post-500 Room-5 residency surges from 3.1% to 84.0% on Seed 1 and from 26.6% to 72.8% on Seed 2 (pooled Room-12 reach drops from 108/128 to 52/128, Room-14 reach from 82/128 to 23/128, and pocket entry x>=20 from 30/128 to 3/128). Separately, a 4-change hazard-penalty bundle (v18_seed1) reaches 2,406.25 but stops at max_px=11. Fourth, we report pure-NumPy verifiable probes of consistency, surrogate query-memory replay, and 32-phase SR.d predictions.
