# Accept

This update preserves the accepted study and adds a useful, clearly bounded extension. The new v22–v25 runs document action-commitment and corpse/respawn masking variants, with v24 and v25 serving as informative regressions. The strongest new result, v23, reaches a verified mean return of $2,468.75$, $64/64$ first-room exits, $56/64$ Room-14 visits and $16/64$ pocket entries, while still achieving $0/64$ first-level completions and $0/64$ gate1 crossings. It therefore remains a partial advance rather than a solution to the closed-ended task.

The new claims are consistent with the supplied evidence. The pure-NumPy verifier passed for all 152 manifest files, all 21 patches, the paired-observation diagnostics, Stage 4A probes, all 22 run receipts and the v11/v15/v17/v19/v23 action traces. The v23 patch inspection supports the stated post-`life_lost` masking mechanism, and the retained arrays reproduce the reported safe-pocket hazard values and v17-versus-v23 real-root VP/Actor probabilities. The manuscript appropriately labels v22–v25 as bundled or single-seed experiments and distinguishes frozen state-edited probes from training comparisons.

The interpretation is appropriately qualified in most places: the v23 gain is not presented as causal isolation of corpse masking because it also changes SIL penalty handling, frontier thresholds and reset behavior; the v20 and v21 explanations retain the previous qualifications; and v24/v25 regressions are reported rather than hidden. The phase-feature ablation remains a two-seed result, while the new corpse-mask result is explicitly one seed. The work continues to use Thinker substantively and does not claim unsupported level completion or transfer generality.

For reproducibility, correct the supplement README's stale counts before publication: it says “all 20 source patches” and describes patches only through v24, whereas the manifest and verifier contain 21 patches including `v23_to_v25.patch`. Also state explicitly that the fixed 18-step wait is calibrated to this environment's death animation; no cross-task or transfer evidence is supplied, so task-general usefulness remains a hypothesis. These are documentation and scope qualifications, not material defects in the reported update.
