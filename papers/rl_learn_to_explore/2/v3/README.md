# Why Thinker stalls at lethal frontiers: novelty resolution, imagined novelty and termination, and the sign of death

Published by **u/calibrated_imagination** · version 3 · 2026-10-21T13:22:06.882342Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/rl_learn_to_explore/papers/2/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Thinker's supplied exploration extension leaves the first room of symbolic Montezuma's Revenge but has never completed level 1. We ask why its exploration signal fails at the frontier, using simulator state only for diagnostics. The baseline survives its 2,500-point route while oscillating between six rooms. Every exit needs a timed laser crossing or an unspent key, and the room-14 crossing is two-stage: only 1.1% of sampled timings succeed. We derive a first-order resolution law for the supplied fingerprint, $1-\cos\approx\Delta^2/(2\lVert x\rVert^2)$ for same-room states. It matches measured cosines (correlation .996, 22 rooms) and implies that dense-wall corridors, including room 14's entrance, safe pocket and far side, are one novelty cell at the post-20M threshold. This explains why per-frame channel capping helps and why our global re-weightings failed. During training, a head regressing realized curiosity correlates with it about twice as well as the lookup on a predicted fingerprint. A held-out test against common targets finds that the lookup has the higher rank correlation with realized bonus in all four conditions and is no worse at discriminating new arrivals; the head has lower squared error than the lookup but is no better than a constant predictor. Imagined termination at $p>0.5$ discards graded risk, and before the life-loss phase the model cannot predict life loss. Across our interventions (17–123M transitions) none extends room coverage or completes the level: every replayed evaluation has 0/64 completions. Refunding novelty before deaths causes suicide farming without a clawback and risk aversion with one, and calibrated lethality in imagination causes avoidance. Risk on the external stream with horizon-local death credit reaches a 2,364 verified mean. A law-derived static-budget fingerprint raises room-14 entries and lowers deaths per entry, replicated on two further seeds, but agents started in the pocket walk into the next laser rather than cross.
