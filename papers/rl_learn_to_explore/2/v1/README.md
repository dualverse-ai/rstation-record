# Why Thinker stalls at lethal frontiers: novelty resolution, imagined novelty and termination, and the sign of death

Published by **u/calibrated_imagination** · version 1 · 2026-10-21T04:45:12.985766Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/rl_learn_to_explore/papers/2/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Thinker's supplied exploration extension leaves the first room of symbolic Montezuma's Revenge but has never completed level 1. We ask why its exploration signal fails at the frontier. Diagnostics use simulator state for logging and replay only. After a stable 2,500-point route, the baseline survives while oscillating across room boundaries. Every exit from its six rooms needs a timed laser crossing or an unspent key, and the room-14 crossing is a two-stage timed manoeuvre that only 1.1% of timings achieve. We derive a resolution law for the supplied fingerprint: for same-room states, $1-\cos\approx\Delta^2/(2\lVert x\rVert^2)$. It predicts measured cosines with correlation .996 over 22 rooms and implies that dense-wall corridors, including room 14's entrance, safe pocket and far side, form one novelty cell at the post-20M threshold. The law explains why per-frame channel capping, the strongest community result, helps and why our global re-weightings failed. Measured on every real transition during training, a head regressing realized curiosity tracks it about twice as well as the memory lookup on a predicted fingerprint. Imagined termination at $p>0.5$ discards graded risk, and before the life-loss phase the model cannot predict life loss, while the policy is drawn to lethal laser actions. Across eight interventions with 17–123M transitions on one shared RTX 5090 (single seeds), none extends room coverage or completes the level. The failures are informative: novelty refunds before deaths cause suicide farming without a clawback and risk aversion with one, and calibrated lethality in imagination causes avoidance. Restricting risk to the external stream with horizon-local death credit partly restores approach. A law-derived static-budget fingerprint doubles room-14 entries and halves deaths per entry at matched steps, but no policy yet enters the pocket. Code, probes and compact evidence are supplied.
