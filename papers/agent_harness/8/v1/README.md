# Checkpoint Attribution of Inconclusive Differential Self-Repair and Streaming Termination Diagnostics in an Agent Harness

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/agent_harness/papers/8/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Candidate-blind differential adjudication across two independent trajectories improves workspace selection on Harness-Bench (`gpt-6-luna`), motivating a diagnostic question: what happens when a single-trajectory harness synthesizes a seed-masked counter-implementation (`ACK-MCDC`) or runs zero-LLM inline checks (`Inline-Verify`) to repair its own draft? Across `120` counterbalanced `development` episodes, paired pre/post-repair checkpoints, and a `90`-workspace audit, neither intervention demonstrates a statistically reliable score improvement (`+6.27` all-attempt points, `95% CI [-0.90, +13.44]` for specialized `ACK-MCDC` at `2.09x` recorded cost, worst-case billing-ratio interval `[0.60x, 5.76x]`; `+1.20` points, `[-3.70, +6.10]` for `Inline-Verify` at `1.20x` recorded cost, `[0.24x, 2.23x]`), leaving cost-frontier dominance inconclusive. Checkpoint diffs across all `12` committed `ACK-MCDC` repairs explain why within-trajectory gains stall (`+1.10` all-attempt points; `0.00` completed median): `7/12` commits fix sub-clauses or input cohorts while sibling defects keep conjunctive checks false, `3/12` reflect false-alarm or incomplete oracle witnesses, `1/12` rescues missing deliverables after a stream drop (`+63.0` points), and `1/12` introduces a `-30.0`-point ordering regression. Separately, tracing why upstream `HTTP/1.1` `IncompleteRead` drops reach the container as clean `HTTP/1.0` EOF via `ModelBudgetProxy` explains `6/60` `tools=None` finalization collapses (`0/60` under a stream-termination guard, `p = 0.0274`), while synthetic probes expose and fix a `response.incomplete` handling defect in the initial guard.
