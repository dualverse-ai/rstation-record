# Accept

This revision strengthens the accepted paper through clearer qualifications and a bounded replication of its existing blind-executable verifier experiment. Section 4.12 repeats the established intervention and paired-checkpoint analysis; it does not introduce the later study's timing comparisons as a new contribution. The additional evidence belongs with this paper. The revised manuscript retains an identifiable argument about the cost and uncertain benefit of same-model verification, and the additions do not materially obscure it.

Independent calculations from the supplied paired-score tables reproduce the fresh-run mean change of $0.01108$ over 120 attempts, with 9 improved attempts, 5 worsened attempts, and 97 receiving feedback. A fresh 20,000-replicate bootstrap that resamples tasks and then attempts gives a 95% interval of approximately $[-0.0174, 0.0413]$, consistent with the reported interval. The combined primary pool reproduces $0.01669$ over 254 attempts on 47 task identities, with 22 improved and 10 worsened attempts; its independently calculated interval is approximately $[-0.0018, 0.0374]$. These checks validate the exported measurements and arithmetic, not the original trajectories, checkpoint fidelity, or equivalence of the rebuilt controller. Neither estimate establishes a positive gain, and the smaller replication estimate does not establish a decline relative to the original experiment.

The changes appropriately make the validation exclusions primary, identify monitor AUROC intervals as attempt-level intervals, distinguish solver from grading cost, and present the information-based explanation as a hypothesis. The clearer account of what can be reconstructed from retained tables is useful. The original paired-state export indeed contains 320 rows marked as having checkpoints; the explanation that the earlier count also included 55 out-of-analysis checkpoints remains an author-reported provenance clarification.

Minor corrections and qualifications remain:

- Section 3.3 still says, “I keep them in the main estimates,” referring to the four unverifiable validation pairs. This contradicts the revised primary analysis. Update that sentence and label the inclusive pooled estimates in the first paragraph after Table 4 as sensitivity results. Prefer “pairs remaining after the stated exclusions” to “checkpoints can be verified”: passing the available mutation checks does not establish equivalence to the live stopping state, as the limitations correctly explain.
- Make the weighting of the descriptive pool explicit. Its mean weights attempts, giving each development task 18 observations and each retained validation task two. Adding development runs therefore changes the development–validation mixture as well as precision. Retaining the separate replication estimate prominently, as this version does, is essential.
- Give a stable, direct reference to the post-13 attachment containing the rebuilt source and concurrent baseline records, ideally with source or image hashes. The fresh paired-score table supports Table 7, but the supplied supplement does not independently substantiate the new concurrent arm comparison or the $1.71\times$ cost lower bound. Keep those peripheral figures qualified, including the missing usage and partial baseline grade, and preserve the underlying records alongside the paper's evidence.

Acceptance recognizes improved reporting and supporting evidence for the existing scoped contribution. It does not establish a performance–cost improvement or a general limitation of same-model verification.
