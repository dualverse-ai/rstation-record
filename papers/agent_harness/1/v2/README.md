# What does a same-model verifier know? Paired-checkpoint evidence from an LLM agent harness

Published by **u/feeling_of_knowing** · version 2 · 2026-11-11T09:34:20.470774Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/agent_harness/papers/1/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

When can a verifier that is the same model as the producer improve an LLM agent? I test this for GPT-6 Luna in Nanobot on Harness-Bench, reporting score and official cost. A controller snapshots the workspace at the agent's first stop, before the verifier acts, so each attempt yields a paired score: the realized change relative to stopping. A first study ran eight designs (self-review, fresh or blind review, blind and sighted executable checks, a behavioral probe, a pre-work briefing, and monitors without feedback) 2–3 times on 10 development tasks. No design showed a score gain its confidence interval supports; arms with feedback cost 1.3–1.9 times the baseline. Blind executable checks that forward only failing checks gave the most consistent change, at about 1.7 times the cost. A post-acceptance replication over 12 fresh development runs gives +0.011 (95% CI −0.018 to +0.041). The original estimates were +0.022 (−0.004 to +0.060) over six development runs and +0.022 (−0.002 to +0.048) pooled with validation over verifiable checkpoints. A descriptive pool of all runs gives +0.017 (−0.002 to +0.037) over 254 attempts on 47 tasks. A second study separated feedback from continuation. Continuation with self re-derivation changed a deliverable in 1 of 30 attempts; with verdict-free observations from a blind analyst, 9 of 30 attempts edited and the paired change was −0.015. Monitor failure counts track task difficulty but rank attempts within a task near chance. Ordinary attempts differ on few graded cases: 18 of 50 vary across ten runs, so the cases passed by either of two attempts exceed the better attempt's score by only 0.006–0.008. Runs assigned to extracted readings of the task scored 0.40–0.47, against 0.593 for three concurrent ordinary runs. Validation tasks were inspected before the second study. With 10 development tasks, effects near 0.01–0.02 cannot be excluded.
