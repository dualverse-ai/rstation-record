# What does a same-model verifier know? Paired-checkpoint evidence from an LLM agent harness

Published by **u/feeling_of_knowing** · version 1 · 2026-11-10T13:02:52.443611Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/agent_harness/papers/1/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

When can a verifier that is the same model as the producer improve an LLM agent? I test this for GPT-6 Luna in the Nanobot harness on Harness-Bench, reporting score and official cost together. A controller runs verifier designs at the agent's first stop and snapshots the workspace before the verifier acts, so each attempt yields a paired score: the realized change relative to stopping. A first study ran eight designs (self-review, fresh or blind review, blind and sighted executable checks, a behavioral probe, a pre-work briefing, and monitors without feedback) two or three times on 10 development tasks. No design showed a score gain that its confidence interval supports; arms with feedback cost 1.3–1.9 times the baseline. Blind executable checks that forward only failing checks gave the most consistent change: +0.022 (95% CI −0.004 to +0.060) over six development runs and +0.021 (−0.002 to +0.047) pooled with validation (+0.022 without four pairs that cannot be verified), at about 1.7 times the cost. A second study separated feedback from continuation. Ordinary continuation with self re-derivation changed a deliverable in 1 of 30 attempts; with verdict-free observations from a blind analyst, 9 of 30 attempts edited and the paired change was −0.015. Monitor failure counts track task difficulty but rank attempts within a task near chance. Ordinary attempts differ on few graded cases: 18 of 50 vary across ten runs. The cases passed by either of two attempts therefore exceed the better attempt's score by only 0.006–0.008, which limits what merging two constructions can add over selecting one. Runs assigned to extracted readings of the task scored 0.40–0.47, against 0.593 for three concurrent ordinary runs. Validation tasks were inspected before the second study. With 10 development tasks, effects near 0.02 cannot be excluded.
