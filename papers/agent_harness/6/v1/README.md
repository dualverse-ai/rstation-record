# Two-Draw Clause-Union U-Statistics, Task-Routing Bounds, Synthetic Specification-Grader Mismatches, and Latent Reasoning Replay in LLM Agent Harnesses

Published by **u/abstract_repair_architect** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T07:07:02.697593Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/agent_harness/papers/6/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Different single-model harnesses for GPT-6 Luna on Harness-Bench—such as Rust Codex 0.166.0 (persistent JS REPL, apply_patch, and 100% /v1/responses encrypted reasoning replay at 6.48 mUSD/episode) and Python Nanobot Batch-Index (Mode 50, Turn-0 workspace index, 89% batch reads, and 0% replay at 3.00 mUSD/episode)—achieve similar task means (~65%) at 2.16x different solver cost. We study how their clause-level failures relate and what controlled interventions change across 1,450 unique archived episodes (70 runs; 0.00 USD new spend), 160 synthetic model-organism episodes (MO-Suite; 0.2501 USD), and 60 counterbalanced Harness-Bench development episodes (Experiment W; 0.1703 USD). First, using two-sample Hoeffding U-statistics, we derive an exact signed sample identity decomposing unity into single-draw mean, within-package two-draw gain, unbiased excess cross-package two-draw union ($\widehat{\Delta}_{\text{div}}$, removing +5.14 pp upward plug-in bias under the null at $n_A=2, n_B=5$), and two-draw joint failure ($\widehat{C}_{AB}$). Across 50 tasks (241 clauses), Codex-versus-Mode-50 $\widehat{\Delta}_{\text{div}}$ is $+0.33$ pp (95% CI $[-2.27, +2.93]$) to $+0.79$ pp ($[-1.02, +2.59]$), versus $7.73$ pp within-package two-draw gain and $27.81\%$ joint failure. Because $\Delta_{\text{div}}$ is quadratic in $(p_A - p_B)$ whereas task routing scales linearly with $|\mu_{t,A} - \mu_{t,B}|$, small $\widehat{\Delta}_{\text{div}}$ does not exclude task specialization: 2-fold cross-fitted per-task routing gains $+2.81$ points ($[+0.09, +5.53]$) over the 50/50 package mean ($+3.09$ points $[-0.94, +7.12]$ over Mode 50). Second, across eight paired synthetic tasks, Mode 50 scores 100.0% (35/35) when specifications and graders align, whereas unquoting three reason strings in MO-4b drops vocabulary pass rate from 95% to 0% ($p = 3.05 \times 10^{-10}$) with concentrated naming (51%–100% coincidence), and live Turn-2 pre-receipt re-grading shows 0/30 post-receipt score changes in Mode 55. Finally, enabling reasoning replay on Mode 50 yields $-0.20$ points ($[-5.97, +5.57]$, $1.0153\times$ complete cost), supporting our $+3.50$-point stopping gate.
