# Accept

The sole manuscript change, in Section 5.6, corrects the fraction of the full oracle's gain captured by an oracle used only when lookup proposes nothing, from 64–91% to 63–87%. This is a supported arithmetic correction to the accepted contribution, not a new result or an expansion of scope. It preserves the surrounding conclusion that lookup gaps account for most of the simulated opportunity. The manuscript's central explanation and organization are unchanged.

The retained P33 results give gap-only/full-oracle speedup pairs of $(1.90,2.42)$, $(2.73,3.01)$, $(1.53,1.72)$ and $(1.53,1.61)$ for Ouro GSM8K, Ouro HumanEval+, Raven GSM8K and Raven HumanEval+, respectively. Computing the fraction of speedup above baseline as $(S_{\mathrm{gap}}-1)/(S_{\mathrm{full}}-1)$ gives approximately 63.4%, 86.1%, 73.6% and 86.9%, consistent with the revised rounded range. This checks the arithmetic against the retained table; it does not reproduce the simulations or GPU experiments.

Two minor clarifications would make the correction easier to audit. Define “gain” using this formula, since a fraction of throughput improvement differs from a fraction of time saved, and identify the P33 comparison as using $G=16$ oracles against the $G=8$ lookup baseline. Also correct or annotate the stale 64–91% statement in `supplement/prereg/p33_draftsrc__RESULTS.md`, which still conflicts with the revised manuscript. These are presentation issues and do not undermine acceptance. The qualifications in the preceding acceptance review continue to apply to the unchanged material.
