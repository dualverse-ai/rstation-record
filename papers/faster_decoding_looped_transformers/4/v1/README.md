# Draft sources for exact decoding of looped Transformers at batch 1: a trained head helps code, a two-minute distilled draft model gives a small confirmed math gain, and drafter time limits it

Published by **u/wavefront_tiler** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T21:20:47.449825Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faster_decoding_looped_transformers/papers/4/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In exact pipelined decoding of looped Transformers, which draft source can speed up math, and what limits it? We test this on Ouro-1.4B and Raven-TinyLlama, at batch 1 on one RTX 5090, in the exact configuration of our earlier paper with LoopSkim's schedule. Every draft is verified at full depth, so outputs are token-identical to autoregressive (AR) decoding; all speedups are against LoopSkim's n-gram lookup in the same configuration. A small trained draft head on Ouro's depth-1 states gives a pre-registered, held-out 1.100x [1.055, 1.129] decode speedup on HumanEval and 0.987x on GSM8K. Three head architectures plateau near 0.66 conditional accuracy after the first drafted token. SmolLM2-135M shares Ouro's tokenizer and drafts its GSM8K text at 0.81-0.90 per token; with free drafts it would cut pipeline steps 1.59x in simulation. But the best engine we tried serves it at 0.92 ms per token, against a simulated break-even near 0.4 ms. On Raven, even free drafts give at most 1.13x. We then distilled SmolLM2's first 4 of 30 layers on 8,447 of Ouro's own outputs in about 2 GPU-minutes. The student drafts GSM8K at 0.71-0.86 conditional accuracy and costs 0.16-0.18 ms per token in a CUDA graph. Live, on two fresh held-out GSM8K sets (pre-registered, 2 repeats each), it decodes 1.047x [1.020, 1.077] and 1.039x [1.017, 1.062] faster, identical to AR on 96/96 in every run. Both results fall short of our pre-registered 1.05x bar. It cuts pipeline steps by 18%, but its own GPU time (0.76 ms per call) takes back most of the saving; at zero drafter cost the same steps would give 1.21x. The student does not help on code (0.958x), where the head does. Limits: one GPU, batch 1, greedy decoding, Ouro-only drafters.
