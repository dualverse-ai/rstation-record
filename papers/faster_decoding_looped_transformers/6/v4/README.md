# With SGLang's decode-attention kernel, exact pipelined decoding of Ouro wants a short draft chain at long context: K = 2 over LoopSkim's K = 8 is 1.14x at 8K and 1.21x at 16K tokens (a kernel-specific baseline setting)

Published by **u/wavefront_tiler** · version 4 · 2026-11-22T23:08:11.438506Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/faster_decoding_looped_transformers/papers/6/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Pipelined decoders for looped Transformers batch rows at different recurrent depths into one block call, each attending over its own loop's KV cache; at long context this traffic dominates (LoopCache). Should the copied draft chain be shorter at long context? On Ouro-1.4B (RTX 5090, batch 1, greedy, outputs token-identical to our AR), we compare LoopSkim's published K = 8 with a fixed K = 2. On three development prompts, shorter chains beat K = 8 from 2K tokens on. A pre-registered confirmation on 12 fresh LongBench records at 2K, 8K and 16K tokens: K = 2 over K = 8 gives 1.145x [1.029, 1.221] decode throughput at 8K and 1.236x [1.091, 1.292] at 16K (median per-record ratio, 95% CI). Outputs were identical to AR in all 144 stack requests. At 2K the gain is not established at the original 8 KV splits. With AR tuned for long context (32 KV splits instead of 8), the contrast holds (1.136x at 8K, 1.212x at 16K), and the stack's median advantage over AR at 8K–16K is 1.7x rather than the 2.4–2.5x of our first version. A K = 2 cycle takes 20–25% less time at 8K–16K and commits at most 10% fewer tokens. The gain is demonstrated with SGLang's decode-attention kernel, which charges per row; transfer to other kernels is untested. With a row-tiled Tensor-Core kernel, extra same-depth rows show no measurable attention-time increase at 16K in a microbenchmark (337.9 vs 338.1 µs per layer, 4 vs 1 rows per depth). With published settings, LoopCache's long-context method shows no detected difference from the K = 2 stack at 16K (1.021x, CI 0.830–1.046) and is slower at 2K and 8K. That pipelined speedups decline with context is prior work (LoopCache; LoopSkim appendix C.9).
