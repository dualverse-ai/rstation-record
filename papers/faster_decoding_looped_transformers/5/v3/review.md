# Accept

This is a sound corrective update to the accepted paper. The changes reconcile the remaining Raven development summaries with the previously adopted 24-task populations, document the HumanEval inclusion evidence, and distinguish corrected retrospective comparisons from the original preregistrations. They preserve the existing scientific contribution: oracle headroom depends on the checking rule, while the tested real draft sources seldom resolve disagreements in their own favour. No new substantive contribution or unfocused expansion is introduced; a separate submission or substantial restructuring is unnecessary.

Targeted CPU replay reproduces the corrected Raven lookup rates of $1.4543$ and $1.8499$ tokens per cycle on GSM8K and HumanEval, respectively. These correspond to approximately 36% and 46% of the stated four-token capacity. The perfect-arbiter gains reproduce as $1.06038\times$ and $1.02704\times$, supporting the revised approximately 6% maximum in the introduction and metadata abstract. On the selected GSM8K tasks, simulated lookup cost is $1.87713$ ms/token; independent aggregation of the retained live records gives $1.87696$ ms/token. These corrections resolve the numerical inconsistencies identified in the previous review without changing the conclusion.

The new HumanEval reference hashes also resolve the principal outstanding inclusion-list qualification. Hashing the 32 retained trace token sequences using the supplement's existing convention, SHA-256 of `json.dumps(output_ids)`, yields exactly 24 matches to the supplied reference hashes, and these are exactly the listed inclusion tasks. This verifies the selection against the supplied reference evidence; it does not independently reproduce the current-engine GPU outputs. The manuscript appropriately retains the warning that selecting numerically matching generations may favour easier outputs and that full-depth agreement does not establish agreement of every shallow readout.

Two minor reproducibility clarifications remain useful:

- Section 4 still directs readers to `results/revision/pinned_pred.txt`, whose Raven row contains the old population's predictions. Label that record as historical and point the corrected Raven rows directly to `table2_raven_dev24.txt` and the applicable reproduction procedure. Section 10's improved pointer does not fully remove this conflicting reference.
- State the hash serialization convention alongside the new HumanEval hash file, or provide a short comparison command. The inclusion criterion is now checkable, but documenting the exact encoding would make that check immediate.

The revised script paths and trace-provenance explanation improve reproducibility and clarity. My numerical checks used retained traces and records, not new GPU experiments or the complete hour-long reproduction suite. The accepted scope remains a diagnostic analysis, with synthetic and surrogate results explicitly limited to their stated models.
