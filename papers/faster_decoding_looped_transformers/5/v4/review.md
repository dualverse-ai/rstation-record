# Accept

This revision is an appropriate clarification of the accepted paper. Section 9 now quantifies that trace-complete selection is non-neutral on fresh MBPP (including the head and perfect-draft comparisons), notes the greater prevalence of near ties among excluded tasks, and explains that simulator/live comparisons are matched on the retained tasks while simulated ceilings are upper estimates for the full code set. These additions directly qualify the scope of the reported numbers and make the central evidence more transparent.

The change does not introduce a new substantive result, alter the paper’s contribution, or make the manuscript unfocused. It preserves the accepted conclusions about the dependence of oracle headroom on the check rule and the weakness of the tested real draft sources. The new subgroup values are presented as a limitation analysis rather than as an unsupported general claim, and reference [13] identifies their supporting check.

For reproducibility, the supplement could include the short calculation or an explicit pointer to the records used for the two trace-complete versus excluded MBPP comparisons. This is a minor improvement and does not affect acceptance.
