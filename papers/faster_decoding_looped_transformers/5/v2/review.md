# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective update to the accepted diagnostic contribution. The aggregate interpretation of the row-work bound, narrower head-policy claim, explicit distinction from the stronger lookup baseline in [4], and added discussion of LoopCache [11] and LoopFill [12] improve precision and attribution. The additional draft-length comparisons provide limited support for the existing check-rule finding rather than a new substantive contribution. The resulting manuscript remains organized around that finding; a separate submission or major restructuring is unnecessary.

The important scientific correction is the Raven development population mismatch. Comparing simulator and live results for different generated sequences was inappropriate. Restricting the comparison to identical reference outputs is justified, provided the resulting selection limitation remains explicit. I independently compared the retained Raven GSM8K trace outputs with the current AR records: exactly 24 of 32 match, and these are exactly the tasks in the new inclusion manifest. Targeted CPU replay reproduces the revised oracle ratios, $1.604\times$ under readout-wins and $2.059\times$ under trust. Aggregating the retained live records on these tasks reproduces approximately $1.530\times$ and $1.939\times$ in the first repeat. The four live/predicted tokens-per-cycle ratios are approximately $0.9823$, $0.9717$, $0.9937$, and $0.9994$. Thus the corrected comparison supports the claimed approximately 2.8% agreement without removing the central oracle effect. These checks reproduce calculations from retained evidence, not GPU generation.

Independent aggregation also reproduces the revised Table 3 Raven disagreement counts and point estimates: 670 disagreements with draft accuracy $0.0507$ on GSM8K, and 559 with accuracy $0.0394$ on the listed HumanEval subset. This continues to support the explanation that these lookup drafts seldom rescue incorrect shallow predictions. The smaller priced refinement-lane effects remain scoped surrogate results, not evidence of a practical speedup or an impossibility theorem.

Several residual corrections would make the update internally consistent without changing its scientific conclusion:

1. **Propagate the new population through the remaining summaries.** Section 3 still gives Raven development lookup rates of 1.32 and 1.76 tokens/cycle. Replay on the listed subsets gives 1.454 and 1.850, corresponding to about 36% and 46% of the stated four-token capacity; update the introductory capacity range accordingly. Section 5 retains perfect-arbiter gains of $1.052\times$ and $1.026\times$, whereas replay gives $1.0604\times$ and $1.0270\times$. The abstract's “1–5%” and Section 1's 5.4% maximum should reflect the approximately 6% maximum. Section 2's Raven cost-validation pair, 2.057 versus 2.070 ms/token, also uses the older population; the corrected GSM8K baseline is about 1.877 ms/token both simulated and live.

2. **Make the HumanEval inclusion provenance inspectable.** The new manifest lists 24 matching HumanEval tasks, but the retained `step4.txt` explicitly says that comparison was not checkable because this paper lacks a HumanEval live AR run. Identify and provide the current F80 reference outputs or hashes used to construct this list, together with the comparison procedure. I reproduced calculations conditional on that list, but could independently verify its output-matching criterion only for GSM8K. This qualification concerns the supporting HumanEval analysis, not the verified GSM8K correction or unchanged fresh-MBPP comparison.

3. **Point readers to the corrected reproduction artifacts.** Section 10 still names the old `lane_sens.py` and `matched_live.py`; the updated lane results use `lane_sens_24.py`. The cited `pinned_pred.txt` still contains the old Raven population. Provide archive-relative commands, distinguish historical records from current results, and retain the original preregistrations while labeling corrected retrospective comparisons explicitly. Section 9 should also preserve Section 2's distinction between served-engine Raven development traces and HF traces.

These are localized reporting and provenance qualifications. The accepted contribution remains intact: oracle headroom depends on the checking policy, while the tested real draft sources do not exhibit the disagreement accuracy needed to exploit that diagnostic headroom.
