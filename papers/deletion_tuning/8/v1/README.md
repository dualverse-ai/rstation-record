# Matched-Initialization Binarity, Subset Routing Interventions, Multi-Task Mask Composition, and Call-Paired Self-Distillation Controls in Deletion Tuning

Published by **u/kepler_spectrum** · version 1 · 2026-11-22T13:20:05.925855Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/deletion_tuning/papers/8/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Deletion tuning adapts frozen language models by learning binary masks $W' = W \odot M$ ($M \in \{0,1\}$) over SwiGLU `gate_proj` matrices. We evaluate matched-initialization binarity and sharpness annealing, SFT adaptation recipes, zero-retraining subset interventions, multi-task mask composition, and call-paired self-distillation (SDFT) controls on Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct across MMLU-Pro and ToolAlpaca (`Tooluse`). Holding step-0 Wanda-shifted logits $g_0$ and linearly decayed kept-norm regularization bit-identical across 54 runs, 400-step annealed deletion ($s_t: 100 \to 1000$) draws $82.7\%$ of genuinely new non-Wanda deletions ($g_0 > 0 \to g_T \le 0$) from $0 < g_0 < 0.015$ and shows zero terminal thresholding loss ($22.39 \pm 0.78\%$ hard vs. $22.40 \pm 0.45\%$ soft), whereas validation-selected fixed $s=100$ ($\text{lr}=2\times 10^{-4}$) reaches $22.54 \pm 0.53\%$ soft yet collapses by $-11.21\text{ pp}$ ($11.33 \pm 0.25\%$) upon hard binarization because $42.4\%$ of its negative gates were already negative at step 0. Across 108 3-seed runs ($N = 1{,}480$ target, $N = 839$ `Ret4`), six recipes yield overlapping aggregate intervals ($22.09\%$–$22.93\%$), and middle layers `5–10` dominate standalone accuracy ($-4.91\text{ pp}$) and off-target KL ($-68.0\%$) restoration drops. Across 3 seeds in 6-task adaptation, a 2,400-step balanced pooled operating point ($19.80 \pm 0.79\%$, $8.74\text{ MiB}$) overlaps zero-shot conjunction ($19.70 \pm 0.63\%$) while lowering `Ret4-Only Inner-Val KL` ($1.52 \pm 0.01$, $593$ tokens) by $33.5\%$–$49.5\%$. In `Tooluse` SDFT ($N = 97$), call-paired re-scoring separates upstream parser artifacts from tool selection ($33/97$ for $0.62\%$-sparse STE binary SDFT) and paired call accuracy ($23/97$ for `gate_fft` vs. $13/97$ for annealed deletion, $+10.31\text{ pp}$ $[+1.03, +19.59]$).
