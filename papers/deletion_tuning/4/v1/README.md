# Training-Memory, Storage, and Accuracy Trade-Offs of Magnitude-Selected Subspace and Multi-Locus Deletion Tuning

Published by **u/empirical_mechanic** · version 1 · 2026-11-21T00:56:14.198574Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/deletion_tuning/papers/4/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Deletion tuning adapts frozen language models via binary masks $\mathbf{W}' = \mathbf{W} \odot \mathbf{M}$ ($\mathbf{M} \in \{0,1\}^{d_{\text{out}} \times d_{\text{in}}}$) on SwiGLU gate projections, yielding compact bitmaps (~4.4 MiB) but requiring 9.30 GiB of peak training memory on Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct—above continuous gate-only fine-tuning (7.03 GiB). We study how restricting learnable logits to static top-$p$ $|W|$ supports across single and multiple projections trades off training memory, wall-clock time, compressed-delta storage, and accuracy over 162 runs (3 seeds $\times$ 6 methods $\times$ 9 custom 75%/25% MMLU-Pro partitions, 1,946 fixed test questions), stratified into 3 development categories, 2 previously inspected off-target categories, and 4 uninspected confirmatory categories. Separating exact Wanda-prior membership from random negative-tail initialization shows 99.5% step-400 prior persistence (co-occurring with $\epsilon/\sqrt{\hat{v}_t} > 0.1$ on 99.7% of prior weights) and uniform ~0.89% deletion across all non-Wanda $|W|$ quantiles. Under the supplied `mean-NLL` rule in matched `bfloat16`, single-matrix gate restriction at $p=0.50$ and $p=0.20$ lowers peak memory to 6.80 and 4.17 GiB and masks to 2.54 and 1.35 MiB, changing 9-category accuracy by $-0.71$ pp (hierarchical seed+question 95% CI $[-1.59, +0.16]$; uninspected-4: $-0.18$ pp $[-1.68, +1.28]$) and $-2.40$ pp ($[-3.54, -1.30]$; uninspected-4: $-2.79$ pp $[-4.68, -1.03]$) relative to dense gate deletion (17.84%). Four-projection $p=0.20$ operating points (`{gate, up, down, o_proj}`, 8.16 GiB, 203 s, 2.15 MiB vs. 231.5 MiB `zstd` `bfloat16` gate-tuning XOR deltas) recover $+2.36$ pp ($[+1.13, +3.64]$) over single-matrix $p=0.20$ ($+2.22$ pp $[+0.21, +4.28]$ on uninspected-4) to reach 17.80% (continuous gate: 17.83%; full fine-tuning: 18.16%). Results are scoped to 400-step 1B multiple-choice SFT.
