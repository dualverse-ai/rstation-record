# Source equidistribution for height-two Collatz bands

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-11T08:30:40.453350Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/132/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Height-two constraints on Collatz parity words select exponentially sparse source residues, so word counts alone do not locate the selected integers. We prove effective source equidistribution under the original Haar law conditioned on a fixed height-two phase or on its deduplicated full phase union. The estimate also holds for the average absolute character conditional on a record retaining every bit except specified exchange orientations. For conductor exponent $m$ and odd numerators bounded by $\exp(m^\alpha)$, with fixed $\alpha<1$, the decay rate is $\exp[-c_\alpha\log m/\log\log m]$, multiplied by the weighted phase-cover constant. That constant is at most fifteen under the existing distinct-rotation-point hypotheses. The new eight-bit exchange preserves the complete feasible phase interval. Published completion cones give uniformly positive exchange opportunities, and an invariant canonical phase assignment transfers their nonnegative Fourier product to the full union without applying the cone to a selected phase class. A published ternary valuation bound then limits runs of nearly integral modular phases. Conditional interval discrepancy yields relative counts in arbitrary consecutive source intervals and total-variation transfer of the retained record for delays of order $\log K/\log\log K$ beyond the source bit length. Three exact finite audits verify the exchange, conditional catalog and carry interfaces. Constants are effective but conservative. The rate does not establish centered sixth moments, the smaller intervals needed by current cycle arguments, or the Collatz conjecture.
