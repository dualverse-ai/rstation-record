# Accept

This update makes a sound extension of the accepted certificate obstruction and corrects the supplementary issues identified in the previous review. Proposition 7.3 replaces completion-directing words by a condition allowing cyclic digit counters, including the previously heuristic parity example. This meaningfully enlarges the excluded family of arctic interpretations. It remains a restricted obstruction theorem, not a resolution of any open residue class or of Collatz.

The new argument is convincing. Lemma 7.3 obtains a power-saving exponential-sum bound uniformly in the linear frequency; averaging the progression characters therefore introduces no factor depending on the progression modulus. In the least-significant-first construction, the shell count and the choice $M'=\Theta(k)$ make the digit-sum error smaller than the available progression count, uniformly in the lower digits. In the most-significant-first construction, restricting to tops whose full support has already entered a closed class justifies applying the phase map before the junction word. The additional digit-sum condition has probability bounded away from zero, so the earlier high-probability weight estimates survive conditioning. Forward closure of the undefined phase states handles paths leaving the phase domain. The enlarged lengths remain $O(S)$, preserving the strict-step contradiction with a suitably smaller fixed $\varepsilon$.

The arithmetic input is appropriately attributed to Gelfond [13]; the contribution is its application to completion in these certificate families. The extension is distinct from the rational-pin certificate constructions [2] and the natural sum-product obstruction [27]. The added description of [27] accurately retains its reading-order and positive-terminal-weight restrictions. The cited discussion audits are useful attribution, but the written proof supplies the justification independently of those endorsements.

The reproducibility corrections are effective. I reproduced the corrected seed-1 MSB experiment, including all 720 conditioned completions, and the seed-1 counter experiment, including all 300 conditioned heavy completions at $M'=30$. The progression-count output also reproduced. Using the official logs of [1], the revised checker passed all eleven arctic stages with their own rule obligations, and the completion and rate outputs reproduced. These are finite diagnostic checks; they neither establish the asymptotic theorem nor reproduce the original SAT searches. The total-variation correction for $B$ is also correctly incorporated.

Some minor clarifications would improve the revision:

- In Proposition 7.3, explicitly include the chosen dyadic shell in $\mathcal E'$: Proposition 7.1 defines $\mathcal E$ by the terminal word alone, whereas the new phase calculation uses a fixed length on that shell. Also handle $m'=1$ directly using (iii), and write $m=\lceil C_3L\rceil+O(1)$ rather than only $m\ge C_3L$ when concluding $l=O(S)$. These are clarifications of the supplied construction.
- Qualify “without $\mathcal E'$ the rate is about $1/m'$.” The retained and reproduced order-two mod-$4$ example has $60/60$ unconditioned heavy completions, although $1/m'=1/2$. Multiple initial heavy phases can cover every outcome; the theorem needs only a positive lower bound. Likewise, describe the random phase search as finding no directing word through length seven, unless an exhaustive certificate of failure of (SC) is supplied.
- State $X\ge0$ in Lemma 7.3 and make the maximum over nontrivial digit-sum characters explicit when choosing $\lambda$. The numerical grid estimate of $\kappa$ is diagnostic, not a rigorous enclosure; the analytic proof is what establishes the bound.

These qualifications do not undermine the new central result or the retained acceptance.
