# Normal witnesses in the open Yolcu-Aaronson-Heule classes, and a limit for arctic certificates

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 1 · 2026-10-26T14:53:24.382044Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/40/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule reduced Collatz residue-avoidance statements to relative termination of string rewriting systems and left the classes $\{1\},\{5\},\{7\},\{5,7\}$ mod 8 open. We prove that two i.i.d. block measures on $\mathbb Z_2$, supported on points whose Syracuse words stay in these classes, have Fourier decay $O(m^{-\delta})$ at every frequency of dyadic level $m$. The proof transposes Tao's pairing argument: a constant commutator between two inverse branches, a cosine product over swapped pairs, and a local limit theorem for a 3-adic unit. By Davenport-Erdos-LeVeque, each open class therefore contains a 2-adic point with normal binary digits and positive liminf drift, along whose orbit every block frequency exists. Next we show that, for each open class, no arctic (max-plus) interpretation that reads numbers from the least significant end and satisfies an end-readability condition (C-end) proves relative termination, in one stage or with rule removal. The proof compares the interpretation on two integers built from a normal witness. Below the leading block, both integers look random to the automaton, but the image is longer by about $k\log_23-S$ bits and must still receive a smaller value. Removing (C-end) is reduced to an end-completion problem for automata reading the progression $3^ky+e$, which we state with the known obstructions. For certificates of zero-entropy type we prove two exact lemmas. The resolution needed to exclude one rational cycle by residue-graph certificates is $D(C)+2$. Arctic interpretations whose binary part consists of labelled simple cycles cannot prove any open class. Integer survival counts up to $2^{32}$, and in residue trees up to $2^{72}$, match the 2-adic Markov model within a factor $1.3$ in all four classes. No open class is settled. The results identify normal points of positive drift as the obstruction that a certificate must separate from the integers.
