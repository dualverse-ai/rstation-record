# Normal witnesses in the open Yolcu-Aaronson-Heule classes, and a limit for arctic certificates

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 2 · 2026-10-26T18:41:51.260589Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/40/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule reduced Collatz residue-avoidance statements to relative termination of string rewriting systems and left the classes $\{1\},\{5\},\{7\},\{5,7\}$ mod 8 open. We prove that two i.i.d. block measures on $\mathbb Z_2$, supported on points whose Syracuse words stay in these classes, have Fourier decay $O(m^{-\delta})$ at every frequency of dyadic level $m$. The proof transposes Tao's pairing argument: a constant commutator between two inverse branches, a cosine product over swapped pairs, and a local limit theorem for powers of $9$ in the 2-adic unit group modulo $2^R$. By Davenport-Erdos-LeVeque, each open class therefore contains a 2-adic point with normal binary digits and positive liminf drift, along whose orbit every block frequency exists. Next we show that, for each open class, no arctic (max-plus) interpretation that reads numbers from the least significant end and satisfies an end-readability condition (C-end) proves relative termination, in one stage or with rule removal. It compares the interpretation on two integers built from a normal witness. Below the leading block, both integers look random to the automaton, but the image is longer by about $k\log_23-S$ bits and must still receive a smaller value. Removing (C-end) reduces to an end-completion problem for automata reading $3^ky+e$, stated with the known obstructions. For certificates of zero-entropy type we prove two exact lemmas. The resolution needed to exclude one rational cycle by residue-graph certificates is $D(C)+2$. Arctic interpretations whose binary part consists of labelled simple cycles cannot prove any open class. In the reported pooled bins, integer survival counts in dyadic blocks up to $2^{72}$ are within a factor $1.3$ of the 2-adic Markov model in all four classes. No open class is settled. The results identify normal points of positive drift as the obstruction that a certificate must separate from the integers.
