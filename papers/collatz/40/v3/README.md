# Normal witnesses in the open Yolcu-Aaronson-Heule classes, and a limit for arctic certificates

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 3 · 2026-10-27T12:07:12.903127Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/40/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule reduced Collatz residue-avoidance statements to relative termination of string rewriting systems and left the classes $\{1\},\{5\},\{7\},\{5,7\}$ mod 8 open. We prove that two i.i.d. block measures on $\mathbb Z_2$, supported on points whose Syracuse words stay in these classes, have Fourier decay $O(m^{-\delta})$ at every frequency of dyadic level $m$. The proof transposes Tao's pairing argument: a constant commutator between two inverse branches, a cosine product over swapped pairs, and a local limit theorem for powers of $9$ in the 2-adic unit group modulo $2^R$. By Davenport-Erdos-LeVeque, each open class therefore contains a 2-adic point with normal binary digits and positive liminf drift. Next we show that, for each open class, no arctic (max-plus) interpretation proves relative termination, in one stage or with rule removal, if each closed class of its support automaton has a completion-directing word. This holds in both reading directions. The most-significant-first case is the shape of the Yolcu-Aaronson-Heule arctic certificates, and all four of their arctic certificates for solved subclasses satisfy the condition. The proof compares two integers built from a normal witness: below the leading block both look random to the automaton, but the image is longer by about $k\log_23-S$ bits and must still receive a smaller value. Classes whose completion depends on a phase (a mod-3 counter) remain open. For zero-entropy certificates we prove two exact lemmas: excluding one rational cycle by residue-graph certificates needs resolution $D(C)+2$, and arctic interpretations whose binary part consists of labelled simple cycles cannot prove any open class. In reported pooled bins, integer survival counts up to $2^{72}$ are within a factor $1.3$ of the 2-adic Markov model. No open class is settled. Normal points of positive drift are the obstruction that a certificate must separate from the integers.
