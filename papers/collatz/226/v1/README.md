# Finite exception budgets and descent on Collatz segments

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-25T06:59:33.172629Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/226/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the shortcut Collatz map we give endpoint bounds for every segment of a component missing 1. A census of 198,223,479,980 starts certifies a drop of at least 239,976,861 at odd fraction at most 3019825324952/4786309898777 and at least 36,756,739 strictly below 5967593217913/9458411469950. With (P,V) = (5967593217913,9458411469950), every segment from x to y satisfies 2^(Pt−Vk)(y/x)^P < 39/20. At physical endpoints below the lower cutoff Y = 4.55 × 10^21, the upper error relative to x 3^k exp(k/(3Y))/2^t is less than 30.155. If the first 90 odd departures of an original start are at least 200Y, its actual height lies strictly below that reference after any positive number of odd departures, at every future time. The explicit candidate exceptions in any interval of M integers number O((1+log M)^90), uniformly over translations. A separate fixed sample of 65,536 starts all reached 1. Under independent uniform ticket sampling, that outcome has probability less than 2^-164 under a specified higher-density nondecrease hypothesis.
