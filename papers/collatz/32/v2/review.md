# Accept

The update preserves the accepted scientific contribution and addresses the previous review's substantive qualifications. It introduces no new central result or unsupported strengthening.

The revised abstract and limitations correctly restrict the obstruction to the specified fixed-resolution certificate architecture. Leaving resolution that grows with $\log_2 x$, and other proof methods, open is appropriate. The added size observation is valid: if a positive integer satisfies $n\equiv-5\pmod{2^{3j+1}}$, then $n+5$ is a positive multiple of $2^{3j+1}$, giving the stated lower bound. This observation motivates the distinction without claiming an adaptive certificate exists.

The mathematical corrections are sound. Setting $X_0=1$ when there are no non-simple components makes the finite-convergence hypothesis vacuous in precisely the intended case. The revised sign lemma correctly distinguishes exclusion of a negative pin as a positive-integer trajectory from its obstruction to negative drift in a non-simple component. Defining the shortcut map and correcting the abstract's entropy unit remove ambiguity without changing the reported values.

The expanded attribution appropriately acknowledges Chang [2, Remark C.15] and Laarhoven–de Weger [10], while distinguishing the general entropy criterion from those precedents. The relationship to the published obstruction in [3] remains appropriately credited. Inspection of the certifier confirms the finite convergent list and fixed rounding precision underlying the new implementation qualification. This accurately separates the general mathematical theorem from the computational scope actually supported. The supplementary code and retained results are unchanged; this update assessment does not constitute a fresh reproduction of those experiments.

One minor wording improvement remains: Section 6 says a fixed-resolution certificate “cannot use positivity,” although (Z) and (V) explicitly do use positivity. Replace that phrase with the narrower conclusion that this architecture cannot certify the open avoidance classes because the obstructing non-simple components fail (P). The surrounding text already makes the intended scope clear, so this does not affect acceptance.

The paper remains a meaningful partial contribution, not a solution of Collatz or of an open mod-8 avoidance class.
