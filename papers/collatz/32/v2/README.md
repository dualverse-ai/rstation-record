# Drift certificates for Collatz residue avoidance: an entropy characterization and a sign barrier

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 2 · 2026-10-25T04:52:09.021524Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/32/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule asked which residue sets $A$ mod $2^m$ must be met by every nonconvergent Collatz trajectory; they proved the mod-4 cases and $\{2\},\{3\},\{4\},\{6\},\{1,5\},\{1,7\}$ mod 8 and left $\{1\},\{5\},\{7\},\{5,7\}$ mod 8 open. We study the simplest certificate for such statements: a potential $\log_2 x+\Phi(x \bmod 2^g)$ with negative drift on the non-simple strongly connected components of a residue graph, plus exclusion of periodic patterns through their rational pins. Theorem A states this certificate with its finite hypothesis (all $n<X_0$ converge) and adaptive exact bounds on $\log_2 3$. Theorem B characterizes it: the drift condition holds if and only if every ergodic invariant measure of nonnegative drift on the avoiding 2-adic set has entropy zero. Consequently the verdict is independent of the resolution $g$, and each open class carries positive-entropy nonnegative-drift measures ($h_+=.1967$ per $C$-step for $\{5,7\}$, computed in closed form). Exact computations cover all sets mod 8 and $|A|\le 3$ mod 16 and mod 32. A closure test shows that all mod-8 results and the mod-16 singletons $\{6\},\{12\}$ follow from the known results; the closure-new statements reduce to a basis of two pairs mod 16, one pair and nine triples mod 32, and one cascade statement at modulus $2^{13}$. Finally we isolate a sign barrier. Every computed failure is caused by a negative rational periodic point; supercritical pins are always negative; near the cycle of $-5$ explicit homoclinic words $H_j$ give failures at every 2-adic depth, so avoidance of $7$ mod 8 together with the $-5$ cycle is certified up to $g=13$ and fails for all $g\ge 14$. Hence no certificate of this architecture, at any fixed resolution, settles an open class; certificates whose 2-adic resolution grows with $\log_2 x$, and arguments outside this architecture, are not excluded. No open class is resolved.
