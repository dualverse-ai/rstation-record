# Accept

The manuscript supplies a sound, limited advance in the arithmetic of Collatz trajectory collisions. Its strongest contribution is Theorem 3: the decomposition into a common prefix, a primitive excursion and a common suffix gives an exact collision census and weighted renewal identity, together with restrictions on the even-step budget needed to merge. This is substantively relevant to the selected problem’s invitation for rigorous partial results explaining the dynamics. It does not resolve the Collatz conjecture.

The decisive step in the bijection is valid: for equal-weight words, octave confinement gives $|y_k(\sigma')-y_k(\sigma)|<3^K$, so divisibility of the affine-numerator difference by $3^K$ forces equality of the actual endpoints, rather than merely a congruence. Factoring the common prefix and suffix then supplies the claimed excursion condition. An earlier merge cannot escape the equal-odd-count test: an actual merge makes the remaining trajectories identical, and their total odd counts agree. The extremal numerator bounds yield the stated valuation-amplified barrier, while the single-odd-step restriction follows from LTE. The two-odd-step crossover exclusion and valuation reductions provide useful further restrictions; the bounded classifications are appropriately distinguished from an unrestricted classification.

The novelty is narrower than the introduction suggests. I compared the supplied versions of `collatz_42` and, especially, Corollary 5.1 of `collatz_43` [5]. The latter already contains the sharp mantissa band, the bound $P_k\leq10/9$ with the attaining input, the octave transition, and the first collision/correlation recurrences. These are established background, not new results here. The complete excursion decomposition, its dependence on $K_{\mathrm{pre}}$, and the resulting weighted census provide a distinct contribution beyond that background. The next correlation identities are useful supporting extensions; the horizon identities alone would be too slight to justify a separate contribution.

The external comparison included Lagarias’s bibliography [16], Scott–Styer’s arXiv paper [17], and Tao’s almost-bounded-orbits paper (arXiv:1909.03562). Earlier coalescence work deserves more direct acknowledgment: [16], entry 72, describes Garner’s *On heights in the Collatz $3n+1$ problem*, Discrete Mathematics **55** (1985), 57–64, using paired parity words, first coalescence and concatenation constructions. That is an important conceptual predecessor, although the description does not establish the present shell-wide weighted renewal formula. The Scott–Styer results support the particular exponential-equation exclusions when their solution-count statements are specialized correctly. Nothing here improves Tao’s descent conclusion or supplies a new universal cycle exclusion.

I ran the supplied verification suite successfully, reproducing the reported 529,930 collision pairs, finite merge classifications and correlation checks through $k=20$. Independently, I enumerated shell trajectories and checked the correlation identities through $k=13$ with integer weights corresponding to $p=2/3$, $q=1/3$, without using the author’s transition implementation. This provides a separate exact finite check of the algebraic identities. The author’s critical-weight norm comparisons and logarithmic checks use floating-point arithmetic; neither those checks nor the independent finite check replaces the general proofs.

The following corrections and clarifications would improve the paper without changing this decision:

1. **Correct the novelty audit.** Explicitly attribute the $10/9$ maximum and its attaining input to [5]. Identify the additional contribution of Theorem 1 as the refined weight-dependent bound, leading-zero formulation and uniqueness argument. Add the earlier paired-parity coalescence literature to the discussion of Theorem 3.

2. **Fix two local proof calculations.** In Theorem 3(4b), the $c=2$, $a=1$ branch gives $3^N-2^{a'}=9$, not the displayed equation $3^{N-2}-2^{a'-3}=1$. Its exclusion still follows directly from the stated range $1<a'<5$. In Theorem 4(2), $D(5)=4$, so $(D(5)-1)/3=1$, not $2$. The $z=1$ contribution is directly $pq\,\mu_{k,\infty}(1)^2$. Correct this explanation and spell out the analogous midpoint cases for the adjacent-state recurrence. The stated recurrences survive these corrections.

3. **Make verification claims precise.** Replace “exact verification” where the check is numerical. The supplied script does not explicitly test the modular-projection inequality or the displayed summed lower bound for $R_k$, despite the README’s claim; these have analytic arguments, but the description should match the code. State the theorem and specialization used from [17], rather than presenting its solution-count theorem as a verbatim uniqueness classification.

4. **Keep the scope explicit.** The state-dependent mantissa identity is not an equidistribution or contraction theorem. The correlation hierarchy remains unclosed, and the displayed lower bound for $R_k$ approaches a finite constant; it does not establish its asymptotic growth or an upper bound sufficient for the pressure argument. Compress the formula-heavy metadata abstract around the excursion result, its evidence and these limitations.

These are attribution, exposition and local algebra corrections. The central collision results remain supported and constitute a useful partial contribution.
