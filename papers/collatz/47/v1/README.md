# Two-Horizon Trajectory Merge Regimes, Universal Suffix-Divisibility Automatic Primitivity, Complete \Delta K = 2 and \Delta K = 3 Excursion Valuation Oracles, Twisted Archimedean Phase Coherence, and Iterated Composition Norms in the Collatz Map

We study five structural questions on the shortcut Collatz map $C(n)$ and its dyadic horizon map $H(n) = C^{\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor}(n) \in \mathcal{O}_K = [3^K, 2\cdot 3^K - 1]$. First (Theorem 1), we classify all two-horizon trajectory merges $H^2(x_0) = H^2(x'_0)$ with $H(x_0) \neq H(x'_0)$ into four regimes: cross-octave same-shell merges (Regime I) are unconditionally empty, intra-octave merges (Regime II) obey a two-horizon pullback band, seam-straddling merges (Regime III) satisfy a weighted seam-pinning inequality and integer barrier, and cross-octave cross-shell merges (Regime IV) obey a deterministic irrational torus shift law. Second (Lemma 1 and Theorem 2), we establish the Pascal Prefix Split (proving odd-integer injectivity on $I_1, \dots, I_6$), prove via $2$-adic injectivity of $C_L$ that every endpoint-distinct equal-weight excursion merge is automatically primitive for all $\Delta K \ge 1$, and derive exact $O(1)$ valuation oracles for $K_1 = 1$-plateau-exit and terminal-inverted $\Delta K = 3$ excursions. Third (Theorem 3), we establish a complete 4-branch $O(1)$ valuation oracle and multiplicity-$\le 2$ law for general-$m$ ($\Delta K = 2$) merges, unconditional 2-term factorizations at $m = 1$, and a certified $K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 2{,}000$ classification. Fourth (Theorem 4), we prove intra-fibre phase coherence and a Fej\u00e9r-kernel aliasing decomposition for twisted Archimedean octave measures. Fifth (Theorem 5), for the composition operator $(Uf)(n) = f(C(n))$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z}_{>0})$, we establish $3$-adic branch-word coalescence identities, the Haar lower bound $r(U) \ge 2/\sqrt{3}$, exact positive-integer Collatz-Wielandt certificates on $(\mathbb{Z}/3^r\mathbb{Z})^\times$ tightening to $1.154109 \le r(U) \le 1.155516$ at $r = 9$, and cycle-free $\ell^2$ eigenvectors of $U^*$ across $|\lambda| < 2^{1/8}$.
