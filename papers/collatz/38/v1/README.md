We establish exact symbolic theorems across four Collatz interfaces, verified by a stdlib-only Python suite (86,989 exact checks in 1.16 s). First, for the negative-cycle cascade H_j=(1,2)^j 2 (q_j=3^{2j+1}-2^{3j+2}>0 for j>=3), we derive closed-form cycle numerators and prove that for all j>=6, all positive basin candidates D_{i,h}=p_i-2q_j h>0 partition into four disjoint families (FamI-FamIV): FamIV falls <=0 in <=9 steps (<=4 steps for j>=12) and FamI-FamIII merge into the floor(j/2)+1 funnel states M_{j,m}=(q_j+3^{2j+2m+2})/4 without hitting H_j. We prove the all-step iterate formula F_{q_j}^t(M_{j,m})=(3^{2j+2m+2+t}-B_t q_j)/2^{E_t+2}, polynomial word mergers for 3j>=E, the 28-trunk collapse of 1<=m<=100 for 3j>E_0(m), and the unconditional all-m phase overshoot proving (m,L,j)=(2,2,9) is the unique C_{139} breakout hit. Second, combining maximal-run reserve consumption with [4, Lemma 3.2], we obtain J_{m-1}=(L_m-1)(r_m+s_m)+t_m+Delta S_m>=t_m>=1 and derive the Laurent PGF, PMF, variance 68/9, long-run variance 4, conditional switch mean E[W_i|A_i]=1/3, and all-lag negative covariance Cov(W_0,W_h)<0 of W_i=J_i-2s_{i+1}. Third, using a paired degree-16 positivity identity Q_9(v)>0, we prove the constant-shift guard 2^{9S}(6n-c_n)<3^{9k}(6n+18k-c_n) (c_n in {8,11,17}) and the uniform 4C deficit clock. Fourth, we prove the w=6 arctic valuation formula max(1,v_2(V+5)-3), stage-1 window obstructions and telescoping laws, the Run-Transposition Identity, the Ree-Radford-Lyndon dimension law H(t)=(1-t)^2/(1-2t), the universal 2-adic pin-shift identity, and on {2,12}^* the dual-shifted excess laws, entry-state Coupled Bezout Tower, unconditional m<=14 macro-run cycle exclusions, and L_p p-state Group-Extension DFA.
