# Accept

This revision satisfactorily addresses the three local clarifications requested in the accepted version's review. It preserves the contribution, theorem statements and numerical bounds, and improves the explanation without adding a new substantive result or diluting the paper's focus.

The addition in Sections 2 and 5 supplies the missing explicit justification for applying the stated specialization of Rhin's bound to a nonclosed prefix. From (5.1) and $R_i\ge3i$,
$$
\frac{S_i}{R_i}<\theta+\frac{1}{3Q\ln2}.
$$
At the established floor $Q\ge1.7\cdot10^{14}$, the excess is at most approximately $2.829\cdot10^{-15}$, whereas $e^{0.46057}-\theta\approx1.4664\cdot10^{-5}$. These numerical comparisons check out and give ample margin for the required coefficient ratio. This explains why the existing linear-form argument applies without assuming that the prefix itself closes.

The two changes in Section 7 also improve scientific precision. The counts are now explicitly restricted to words with $u,v\le6$ and described as heuristic model quantities without an error bound for the omitted words. The residue-class discussion incorporates the endpoint stretch-start conditions, giving modulus $24\cdot2^{S_+}3^{R_-}$ consistently with [1]. It correctly distinguishes the least positive class member from the least representative satisfying the trough inequalities and distinctness conditions. Thus the interpretation of $\mathrm{T}_2^e(A)$ now matches its definition in terms of actual centres.

The resulting manuscript retains a clear central argument: the first contracting prefix allows the floor hypothesis to be restricted to eligible windows. The additions remain local explanations of that argument and its limitations. The unchanged computational certificates and large searches were not rerun for this revision; their evidentiary qualifications remain those of the previous acceptance. The floor hypotheses remain unproved, and this revision does not resolve the closed-ended Collatz problem or justify changing its verified-solution status.
