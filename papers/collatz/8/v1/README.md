# The Bezout–Syracuse Seed Identity, Dual Fixed-Point Homogenization, Coupled Block-CRT Renormalization, and Deterministic Functional-Graph Obstructions for {1, 2}-Valuation Collatz Cycles

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-10-20T02:24:01.962727Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/8/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study positive integer cycles of the accelerated Syracuse map $U(x)=(3x+1)/2^{v_2(3x+1)}$, focusing primarily on the restricted binary valuation alphabet $\mathcal{A}_{\{1,2\}}(k,S)$ ($u=2k-S$ ones and $v=S-k$ twos) alongside general identities on unrestricted compositions $\mathcal{A}(k,S)$. First, for all $a\in\mathcal{A}(k,S)$, we prove that the least positive forward $2$-adic seed $r_2(a)<2^S$ maps under $(3^k x+C(a))/2^S$ directly to the least backward $3$-adic seed $r_3(a)<3^k$ without modulo-$3^k$ reduction ($2^S r_3(a)-3^k r_2(a)=C(a)$ in $\mathbb{Z}$), identifying $(r_3(\sigma^t a)-r_2(\sigma^t a))/(2^S-3^k)$ with $\lfloor x_t(a)/2^S\rfloor=\lfloor x_t(a)/3^k\rfloor$ for positive integer cycles. Second, reindexing the fixed-point excess identities of [25] by step positions yields $u=1$ and $v=1$ coprimality $\gcd(C(a),|2^S-3^k|)=1$, mod-$5$/$7$ index-parity collapse ($N_5^{\mathrm{unres}}(0;k,k+2)=0$ for odd $k\ge 3$ and $N_7(0;2m,4m-3)=2\binom{m}{3}$), and reduced gap divisors. Third, grouping $a\in\mathcal{A}(k,S)$ ($k<S<2k$) into $m=\#\{t:a_t\ge 2\}$ blocks $(1^{u_j},d_j)$ yields a descent-exponent-independent product identity and a bound $B_{\mathrm{univ}}(k,S,m)\le B_{\mathrm{univ}}(k,S,S-k)<(2^{S/k}-3)^{-1}$ strictly sharpening the Crandall–Garner–Eliahou bound. Fourth, on the binary alphabet $\mathcal{A}_{\{1,2\}}(k,S)$, grouping $r$-run words $(1^{u_1},2^{v_1},\dots,1^{u_r},2^{v_r})$ yields an $r$-run minimum-element bound improving on Simons–de Weger's published $v/\Delta$ bound by a factor of at least $3v/(2u)>2.114\times$ (and on the coarse auxiliary bound $B_{\mathrm{aux}}$ by $\ge 3v/u>4.228\times$), together with a Block-CRT recurrence $2^{S_{i+1}} P_{i+1}-3^{u_i+v_{i-1}} P_i=\Omega_i$. We prove that the total-defect zero-quotient graph $\mathcal{G}_{\Omega=0}^{\mathrm{block}}$ has out-degree $\le 1$ over $\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^3$, and—combining an outside-box Bezout bound with a $61,151$-state inside-box certificate—establish $z_i\ge 733,339$, $r>1.1\times 10^6(S\ln 2-k\ln 3)$, $S/k<1.58496305$, and $k\ge 2,966$ ($r\ge 933$ at $(2966,4701)$) for $\{1,2\}$-cycles without external $2^{68}$ searches.
