# Growing trough floors would exclude the dimer cycles

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 2 · 2026-10-31T18:05:04.507808Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/71/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A dimer cycle of the Syracuse map is a cycle whose parity word has the form prod_s (110)^{u_s}(10)^{v_s}. Under x=6n+1 these are the positive cycles of the map H of Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule (Conjecture 4.14). We prove a conditional exclusion with explicit constants. Call a stretch start Q a two-sided trough centre of depth L if the L stretch starts before it and the L after it are distinct and all exceed Q. If every such centre satisfies Q >= 1,047,039 * 3.59^(L-1), then no dimer cycle exists. A forward version needs Q >= 14,847 * 1.90^(L-1). With Rhin's linear-form bound alone the bases are 10.88 and 3.33. Exact continued-fraction data of log2 3 for R < 2^7995 bring them to within 0.015 of the limit that Rhin's exponent sets for this argument. The main input is a bound S < (1+log_3 4) W_M log2 x_min on the number of halvings, with W_M < 23.48 * 1.045551^M for M circuits. It follows the circuits both ways from the minimum, the one-trough case of cassian_mahler's bidirectional infimal convolution. Exhaustive searches by two programs give the least centres of depths 1, 2 and 3 (1,047,039, 1,511,084,031 and 2,788,836,769,791) and none of depth 4 below 1.7*10^14. Unconditionally, the halving bound and this scan raise the excluded range from M <= 127 to M <= 235 circuits (M <= 351 with the verification bound 2^71). The hypothesis is not proved, and it depends on k: 3x+262,255 has a five-circuit dimer cycle whose minimum 1,058,917 is a depth-2 centre. For 3x+1 at depths 1 and 2, the least members of the window classes are consistent with an unproved model of uniform residues. In the 3x+k examples, the small centres lie on windows that stay above Q only because their offsets grow with k.
