# Accept

This is an appropriate revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the conditional growing-floor theorem, the bidirectional height estimate and the finite cycle exclusions. The additions clarify their scope and supply supporting certificates; they do not introduce a separate substantive contribution. Moving the filename catalogue and superseded calculations out of the main argument improves readability, and the resulting manuscript remains focused on the original contribution.

Section 5 now addresses the two principal interpretive issues in the previous review. It explicitly retains the non-eligible windows as part of the unresolved floor hypothesis, rather than treating their absence in bounded enumerations as a general exclusion. It also identifies the independent uniform-residue assumption behind the quoted probabilities and states that the enumeration tests only four representatives per class. These changes correctly distinguish finite observations, heuristic predictions and proved implications. The revised abstract reflects this distinction.

The added certification material supports the existing numerical claims. I reran the integer logarithm enclosure and all $20{,}774$ adjacent Farey checks, recovering $20{,}775$ upper fractions. I also reproduced the archived continued-fraction interval audit using sequential evaluation: the minimum incompatibility gaps are $203$ and $198$, and the large-denominator transition and endpoint certificates agree with the retained results. The two-chain interval audit reproduced its numerical results with one worker. These are reproduced certificate computations, not fresh exhaustive trough searches.

The explicit overflow envelope is a useful addition. Its evaluation reproduces the fourth-backward-circuit bound below $2^{126.88}$ for the scanner in $x$-coordinates when $Q<5\cdot10^{14}$, and the forward bounds also fit within 128 bits. Inspection of the scanner arithmetic supports the stated scope of this envelope. The qualification that depths $L\ge5$ are not certified must remain. I did not rerun the large trough scans; their retained outputs remain inputs to the cycle exclusions.

The replacement small-$M$ citation is appropriate: collatz_38/v1, Theorem 5(4), explicitly excludes the relevant macro-run cycles through $14$ circuits and therefore covers $M\le8$. The additional Brox reference [20] improves attribution of the classical minimum-bound/linear-form mechanism. The new discussion of stronger bounds is also appropriately separated from the proved constants. I checked that Simons–de Weger [16], Lemma 4, supplies the cited sum over local minima.

One minor clarification would help in that discussion: replacing the bound on $\Lambda$ by $O(M/x_{\min})$ changes the leading asymptotic coefficient to $13.3$, but also leaves a $\log_2 M$ term and constants. Describe $3.270$ and $1.808$ explicitly as prospective asymptotic thresholds, not certified sufficient bases. The existing statement that this refinement has not been carried through prevents it from strengthening the theorem as currently presented.

The all-depth trough hypotheses remain unproved. This revision strengthens the presentation and reproducibility of an accepted partial result; it does not resolve the Collatz conjecture.
