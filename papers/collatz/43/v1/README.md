# Commuting $(a,c)$-Lattice Transfer DAGs, All-Order $3$-Adic Stopping-Barrier Laws, and Hurwitz–Resolvent Decompositions of Critical Collatz Dirichlet Series

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-10-27T07:18:51.332849Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/43/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We develop an exact analytical and algorithmic framework for two coupled transfer-operator problems governing Collatz dynamics across the dyadic parity horizon $2^m$: (i) the discrete Fourier spectrum $\Phi_m(b/2^m)$ of stationary two-block Collatz renewal laws on $\mathbb{Z}/2^m\mathbb{Z}$, and (ii) the entropy-saddle-normalized Dirichlet series $Q_t(s) = \lambda^{-t} \sum_{n \ge 1} \alpha^{J_t(n)} q^{t - J_t(n)} n^{-s}$ of the deterministic shortcut Collatz map $C(n)$. Formulating the two-block Fourier recursion on the dyadic character group $\mathbb{Z}[1/2]/\mathbb{Z}$, we prove (Theorem 1) that the frequency maps $T_A(\xi) = 4 \cdot 3^{-1}\xi$ and $T_B(\xi) = 8 \cdot 9^{-1}\xi$ commute and preserve lower-depth projections, collapsing the $2^\ell$-leaf recursion tree onto a 2D lattice DAG $\mathcal{L}_m = \{(a, c) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^2 : 2a + 3c \le m\}$ of Ehrhart size $|\mathcal{L}_m| = ((m+3)^2 + 3)/12$ ($6 \mid m$). Evaluating all odd $b < 2^{\lfloor m/3 \rfloor}$ up to $m = 48$ ($L = 16$, $32{,}768$ frequencies), we prove an Exact No-Wraparound Convex Combination Law for $9 \mid b$ (Theorem 2), the Exact $3$-Torsion Ejection Identity for $v_3(b) = 1$, and the All-Order $v_3(b) = r$ Stopping-Barrier Theorem (Theorem 3) with renewal probabilities $P_{\mathrm{I}}(r) = (1 + p(-p)^r)/(1 + p)$ and $r$-independent error bound $\frac{2\pi(3+2p)}{4p-3}\frac{b}{2^m}$ for $p > 3/4$. For $Q_t(s)$ and $D_t(s)$, we prove an exact $2^t$-periodic Hurwitz–zeta reduction, two-sided tail sandwich, telescoping ternary discrepancy tail bound, and shift-remainder condensation (Theorem 4), a uniform $O(1)$ bound on absorbed and descended trajectories isolating post-horizon upward excursions (Theorem 5), and an exact pre/post-horizon dyadic identity for the Collatz resolvent $W(n) = \sum_{t \ge 0} \lambda^{-t}\alpha^{J_t(n)}q^{t-J_t(n)}$ with asymptotic constant $C_\infty = \frac{2}{2 - 2^\theta} \approx 29.331150$ verified over $1 \le n \le 2^{20}$ (Theorem 6).
