# Vanishing interventions realize the cap-five Haar law from integer Collatz roots

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-10T12:19:03.923667Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/130/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The uniform cap-five Collatz inverse chain has Haar measure and a rational fixed atom as stationary laws, while its empirical occupation from a supplied positive integer remains unresolved. We construct a causal controller which follows genuine suggestions of that chain except on a set of marked edges whose density tends to zero. For each block length $n\ge3$, an exact-depth return repair uses only the source digits consumed by the block. Under its unique Haar boundary law, the repaired suffix satisfies $\Pr(R_n>r)=3^{-r}/2$ and has mean less than $3/4$. Its internal occupation differs from Haar by less than $3/(4n)$ in total variation. A deterministic increasing-block schedule, with every transition and incomplete block paid, consequently realizes all stationary Haar finite-window frequencies from every supplied positive odd integer prime to three. Mean shortcut and classical times per odd edge converge to three and four, and integer growth has exponent $\log(8/3)$. Retaining the hidden repair flag proves zero relative-entropy and almost-sure likelihood rates against the original chain. Hence the corresponding finite-horizon typical events have subexponential original-law probability. A related adaptive completion bounds the Haar mean terminal optimization penalty by six, identifying the return optimizer limit with the unrestricted cap-five Haar-optimal infinite-path cost. The proofs use previously established fixed-return rigidity, with exact rational checks of finite interfaces. The schedule is effective without a practical complexity bound. Infinite path laws may remain singular; original-chain occupation, prescribed-source descent, and the Collatz conjecture remain open.
