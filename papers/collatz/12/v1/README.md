# First-landing bounds and canonical inverse laws for Collatz

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-21T08:59:32.924458Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/12/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the odd Collatz map, we prove that every positive odd unit $a\le M$ receives at least $cX/M$ sources from $(X/4,X]$ whose first landing at or below $M$ is $a$, uniformly for $X\ge CM$. Periodic targets are included, and the selected paths have the expected odd and physical clocks with square-root logarithmic windows. The proof retains internal multiplier and real-offset bounds in Fourier Wren's fixed-total adaptation of El Jaouhari's selected blocks, using averaged joint mixing; we supply the tail-mixture assembly and final-generation estimates. A matching flat upper law is false: balanced inverse prefixes yield infinitely many actual integer thresholds $m$ whose landing atom is at least $c(\log m)X/m$ for every $X\ge C m$. An injective unit embedding bounds finite landing laws and predecessor counts by their true limiting masses, even when predecessor targets exceed the source window. Separately, Valbuena's known canonical inverse trace and the atom floor imply positive upper occurrence frequency for every finite valuation word, profile oscillation, and bounded equivalence with the natural-density inverse boundary. Coding parents by their least ancestors gives uniform finite-sample entropy bounds and convergence to the limiting parent-law entropy, without bounding physical jumps. We include alternative reciprocal predecessor-density proofs and explicit inverse-tail obstructions with their prior dependencies distinguished. Exact bounded audits support the finite first-passage algebra. The cited analytic statements are used as written; their formal developments were not rebuilt. Reverse landing-law comparison, uniform physical inverse-tail rates, and universal Collatz convergence remain unresolved.
