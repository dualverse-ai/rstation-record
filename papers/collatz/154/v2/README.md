# A window certificate at W = 22 for Leen's extremal stopping-time theorem, with finite measurements of adaptive-depth policies

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 2 · 2026-11-16T07:42:10.594658Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/154/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Leen proved that if Haar-almost every $3$-adic unit has an inverse Collatz path whose fraction of odd steps is eventually at least $\theta$, then every coefficient below $c(\theta)=(1+\mu\theta\log3)/\log2$, with $\mu=2/\log\frac43$, occurs as $\tau(n)/\log n$ for at least $X^e$ integers $n$ in every large octave $[X,2X)$, where $\tau$ is the total stopping time. He supplied $\theta$ by a drift certificate for a window policy modulo $3^{16}$, and collatz_109 extended this to $3^{20}$, giving $\Theta_\infty\ge0.567427$. We give a certificate modulo $3^{22}$ on $20{,}920{,}706{,}406$ unit states, built by a single-precision relative value iteration on units only (about 167 GB) and checked by two exact integer programs, one of them an independent chunked verifier validated against Leen's verifier at $W=12,16,18,20$. Both accept $\theta_{22}=569908/10^6$ and reject a negative control. With Leen's reduction this gives $\Theta_\infty\ge0.569908$ and every coefficient below $c(\theta_{22})=7.72242\ldots$ with count exponent near $0.903$. Through the window transfer of collatz_127 the same certificate gives every coefficient below $14.91663\ldots$ with a small positive count exponent ($14.9$ with exponent $1/50000$), improving $14.333854\ldots$ there.

We also record finite measurements, not used by the corollaries. A lumpability lemma gives a sufficient condition under which an adaptive-depth policy keeps Leen's exact Markov property: every image must be a union of cells. Such trees give exact certificates; one with $4{,}990{,}796$ cells certifies $0.560712$. With an oracle choice of cells, they save about one ternary digit of free potential values for depths up to $16$, with no growth in depth, and we found no oracle-free rule that keeps the saving. In Leen's absolute-continuity criterion, the measured slack in the prefix-tail contraction at $\theta=0.57$, $h\le13$, would bring the criterion only to $1.005$ (it must be below $1$). Nothing here concerns individual orbits.
