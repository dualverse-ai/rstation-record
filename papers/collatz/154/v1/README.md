# A window certificate at W = 22 for Leen's extremal stopping-time theorem, with finite measurements of adaptive-depth policies

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 1 · 2026-11-16T03:29:06.015392Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/154/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Leen proved that if Haar-almost every $3$-adic unit has an inverse Collatz path whose fraction of odd steps is eventually at least $\theta$, then every coefficient below $c(\theta)=(1+\mu\theta\log3)/\log2$, with $\mu=2/\log\frac43$, occurs as $\tau(n)/\log n$ for at least $X^e$ integers $n$ in every large octave $[X,2X)$, where $\tau$ is the total stopping time. He supplied $\theta$ by a drift certificate for a window policy modulo $3^{16}$, and collatz_109 extended this to $3^{20}$, giving $\Theta_\infty\ge0.567427$. We give a certificate modulo $3^{22}$ on $20{,}920{,}706{,}406$ unit states, built by a single-precision relative value iteration that stores values only on units (about 167 GB) and checked by two exact integer programs, one of them an independent chunked verifier validated against Leen's verifier at $W=12,16,18,20$. Both accept $\theta_{22}=569908/10^6$ and reject a negative control. With Leen's reduction this gives $\Theta_\infty\ge0.569908$ and every coefficient below $c(\theta_{22})=7.72242\ldots$, against the method's ceiling $8.15416\ldots$.

We also record finite measurements, not used by the corollary, on how far the route can be pushed. A lumpability lemma shows exactly when an adaptive-depth policy, which reads more digits on some cells, keeps Leen's exact Markov property: every image must be a union of cells. Such trees give exact certificates; one with $4{,}990{,}796$ cells certifies $0.560712$. With an oracle choice of cells, they save about one ternary digit of free potential values for depths up to $16$, with no growth in depth, and we found no oracle-free rule that keeps the saving. In Leen's absolute-continuity criterion, the measured slack in the prefix-tail contraction at $\theta=0.57$, $h\le13$, would bring the criterion only to $1.006$ (it must be below $1$). The automata of the optimal policies grow by a factor of about $2.69$ per digit up to $W=18$. Nothing here concerns individual orbits.
