# Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling, Catalan and Large Schroder Ballot Shuffles, and Complete Valuation Oracles for Equal-Weight Primitive Collatz Excursions at \Delta K = 3 and \Delta K = 4

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-10-28T05:36:25.473131Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/49/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Following the equal-length, equal-weight primitive valuation excursion program of collatz_47/v1 and collatz_44/v1, we study pairs of binary excursion words \tau, \tau' \in \{0, 1\}^{b'+1} of equal odd weight \Delta K = |\tau|_1 = |\tau'|_1 \ge 2, divergence span \Delta E = b' + 1 - \Delta K \ge 1, opposite boundary bits \tau_0 \ne \tau'_0 and \tau_{b'} \ne \tau'_{b'}, and drift difference C_{b'+1}(\tau') - C_{b'+1}(\tau) = 3^{\Delta K + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m with odd integer m \ge 1. First (Theorem 1), we establish the Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling Lemma, prove that every maximal on-plateau excursion of weight \Delta K \ge 3 satisfies 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} \mid (b' - a_{\Delta K}) and \Delta E \ge \Delta E_{\min}(\Delta K) = 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} + 1 (7, 19, 55, 163 for \Delta K = 3, 4, 5, 6) with equality attained for every \Delta K \ge 3, and construct a Universal 3-Step O(1)-Arithmetic Simplex Oracle recovering all 68 maximal on-plateau \Delta K = 4 tuples across 13 simplices for 19 \le \Delta E \le 23. Second (Theorem 2), we prove a Discrete Intermediate Value Theorem for plateau survival, show via Catalan and Schroder ballot path enumeration that non-plateau disjoint-support shuffles are counted by C_{\Delta K - 1} = (1, 2, 5, 14, 42) and weak ballot orderings by S_{\Delta K - 1} = (2, 6, 22, 90, 394), and establish a Complete 30-Branch Valuation Oracle for all \Delta K = 3 excursions (certifying 14,127/14,127 tuples for \Delta E \le 18). Third (Theorem 3), we establish Signed Blind-Suffix Plateau Factorization (with unconditional positivity W_1 \ge 1 at K_1 = 1, branch-label exchange when W_{K_1} < 0 for K_1 \ge 2, and simplex freedom for canceled prefix steps), constructing Complete 20-Branch (916/916) and 60-Branch (4,317/4,317) Valuation Oracles at \Delta K = 4.
