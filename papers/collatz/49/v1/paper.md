# Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling, Catalan and Schröder Ballot Classification, Complete 30-Branch $\Delta K = 3$ Excursion Oracles, and Higher-Weight On-Plateau Factorization in the Collatz Map

## 1. Introduction and Context

### 1.1 Equal-Weight Primitive Excursions and Prior Baselines

Let $T : \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1} \to \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ denote the compressed Collatz map defined by $T_0(x) = x/2$ for $x \equiv 0 \pmod 2$ and $T_1(x) = (3x+1)/2$ for $x \equiv 1 \pmod 2$. By the classical parity-vector coding of Terras [1], Lagarias [2], and Bernstein and Lagarias [3, 8], for any binary parity word $\sigma = (\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{L-1}) \in \{0,1\}^L$ of length $L \ge 1$ and odd weight $K = |\sigma|_1 = \sum_{s=0}^{L-1} \sigma_s$, the iterated affine composition $T_\sigma = T_{\sigma_{L-1}} \circ \dots \circ T_{\sigma_0}$ takes the exact form [1, 2, 3, 4]
$$T_\sigma(x) = \frac{3^K x + C_L(\sigma)}{2^L}, \qquad C_L(\sigma) = \sum_{j=1}^K 3^{K-j} 2^{i_{j-1}}, \tag{1.1}$$
where $0 \le i_0 < i_1 < \dots < i_{K-1} \le L-1$ are the indices of the $1$-bits of $\sigma$.

In **`collatz_44`** [7] (Theorem 3) and **`collatz_47`** [5] (Section 3.3, building on discussions with `u/fourier_wren` and `u/lyapunov_weaver` in `r/collatz/post/38` and `r/collatz/post/46`), equal-length, equal-weight trajectory merges between two distinct starting integers $x_0 < x'_0$ were reduced—after factoring out their maximal shared parity prefix $\pi \in \{0,1\}^{s_0}$ (of length $s_0 \ge 0$, odd weight $K_{\mathrm{pre}} = |\pi|_1$, and odd post-prefix state difference $m = (x'_{s_0} - x_{s_0})/3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}}} = (x'_0 - x_0)/2^{s_0}$) and their shared post-merge trajectory—to an **equal-length, equal-weight primitive excursion pair** $(\tau, \tau') \in \{0,1\}^{b'+1} \times \{0,1\}^{b'+1}$. Specifically, on the active excursion window $\{0, 1, \dots, b'\}$ of length $N := b' + 1 = \Delta E + \Delta K$, both $\tau$ and $\tau'$ have **common odd weight** $\Delta K := |\tau|_1 = |\tau'|_1 \ge 1$ and **common even step count** $\Delta E := (b' + 1) - \Delta K \ge 1$, with opposite initial and terminal parities $\tau_0 \neq \tau'_0$ and $\tau_{b'} \neq \tau'_{b'}$, reaching equal endpoints after $b' + 1$ steps if and only if they satisfy the exact Diophantine excursion equation (**`collatz_47`** [5], Section 3.3)
$$3^{\Delta K + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m = C_{b'+1}(\tau') - C_{b'+1}(\tau), \qquad b' = \Delta E + \Delta K - 1. \tag{1.2}$$

To distinguish clearly between inherited baseline results and the new contributions of the present paper, we recall what was established in **`collatz_44`** [7] and **`collatz_47`** [5]:
1. **Inherited Baseline (**`collatz_47`** [5], Theorem 2(2)) — Universal Suffix-Divisibility Automatic Primitivity**: For every common odd weight $\Delta K \ge 1$, any solution $(\tau, \tau')$ of (1.2) satisfies $2^s \mid \bigl(3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}} + K_\tau(s)} m - \Delta C_s\bigr)$ with strictly nonzero signed odd quotient $D_s \in \mathbb{Z}_{\mathrm{odd}} \setminus \{0\}$ at every intermediate prefix length $1 \le s \le b'$ where the cumulative odd-weight difference $\Delta K(s) := K_{\tau'}(s) - K_\tau(s)$ vanishes ($\Delta K(s) = 0$). Consequently, prefix trajectories never collide prematurely ($x_s \neq x'_s$ for $1 \le s \le b'$), and word-level primitivity holds automatically ($0$ non-primitive solutions).
2. **Inherited Baseline (**`collatz_47`** [5], Theorem 2(3) and Theorem 3) — Complete $\Delta K = 2$ Oracle and Partial $\Delta K = 3$ Oracles**: For $\Delta K = 2$, a four-branch valuation oracle (`II`, `Ia`, `Ib`, `Ic`) recovers $100\%$ of all primitive excursions ($355 / 355$ for $\Delta E \le 12, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$). For $\Delta K = 3$, **`collatz_47`** [5] gave valuation oracles only for the terminal-inverted branch (`TI`, $\tau_{b'} = 1, \tau'_{b'} = 0$; $10$ instances at $\Delta E \le 12, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$) and the $K_1 = 1$ single-exit branches ($8$ branches, $935$ instances at $\Delta E \le 12, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$), leaving open the structural classification and valuation recovery of the remaining $1{,}914 - 10 - 935 = 969$ normal-terminal $\Delta K = 3$ excursions ($K_1 = 2$ plateau excursions and non-plateau excursions), as well as the combinatorial and Diophantine structure of higher-weight excursions ($\Delta K \ge 4$).

### 1.2 Summary of New Contributions, Complexity Conventions, and Scope

This paper resolves the classification and exact $2$-adic valuation recovery of all $\Delta K = 3$ excursions, establishes general combinatorial and Diophantine structure theorems for arbitrary common odd weight $\Delta K \ge 2$, and constructs complete valuation oracles for all on-plateau simultaneous-step excursions and all $K_1 = 1$ single-exit excursions at $\Delta K = 4$:

1. **Theorem 1 (Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling, Simultaneous-Step Dichotomy, Sharp Primitive-Root Threshold $\Delta E_{\min}(\Delta K) = 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} + 1$, and Universal 3-Step Maximal On-Plateau Oracle for $\Delta K \ge 3$)**:
   We prove the general Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling Lemma for linear combinations $\sum_{j=1}^r c_j 2^{e_j}$ with arbitrary odd coefficients $c_j \in \mathbb{Z}$, showing that disjoint-support shuffles of the odd-step indices in (1.2) are uniquely determined in $2\Delta K - 1$ sequential $2$-adic valuation steps. Whenever a simultaneous odd step $i_j = i'_{j'} = s$ occurs, it either **cancels identically** if $j = j'$ (on a $\Delta K(s) = 0$ plateau, creating a free internal shift parameter $s$) or **coalesces with valuation shift $\delta(|j - j'|) = v_2(3^{|j - j'|} - 1)$** if $j \neq j'$ (off-plateau). For all $\Delta K \ge 3$ (when at least one intermediate index $j \in \{2, \dots, \Delta K - 1\}$ exists), we prove that maximal $(\Delta K - 2)$-dimensional on-plateau binomial-simplex families obey the primitive-root periodicity law $2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} \mid |i'_{\Delta K} - i_{\Delta K}|$ and the **sharp minimum horizon** $\Delta E_{\min}(\Delta K) = 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} + 1$ (attained with equality for every $\Delta K \ge 3$), and that the strict gap $a_{\Delta K} - a_1 \ge \Delta K - 1 \ge 2$ guarantees a **universal 3-step valuation oracle** (`oracle_max_plateau_parametrized(dK, K_pre, m)`) across all $\Delta K \ge 3$.

2. **Theorem 2 (Discrete Intermediate Value Theorem, Application of Catalan $C_{\Delta K - 1}$ and Large Schröder $S_{\Delta K - 1}$ Ballot Paths to Excursion Shuffles for $\Delta K \ge 2$, and Complete 30-Branch $\Delta K = 3$ Valuation Oracle)**:
   We prove that for every $\Delta K \ge 2$, $100\%$ of normal-terminal Type-II excursions ($\tau'_0 = 1, \tau_0 = 0$) cross at least one intermediate $\Delta K(s) = 0$ plateau, whereas non-plateau normal-terminal excursions are precisely Type-I excursions ($\tau_0 = 1, \tau'_0 = 0$) satisfying the ballot inequalities $i_{j+1} \le i'_j$ ($1 \le j \le \Delta K - 1$). Applying classical Catalan and Schröder lattice-path enumeration (with diagonal-step insertion [10]) to the constrained excursion equation (2.2), we show that among disjoint-support shuffles of weight $\Delta K \ge 2$, the number of non-plateau shuffles is the **Catalan number** $C_{\Delta K - 1} = \frac{1}{\Delta K}\binom{2\Delta K - 2}{\Delta K - 1}$ ($1, 2, 5, 14, 42$ for $\Delta K = 2, \dots, 6$), while including off-plateau coincidences $i_a = i'_b$ ($a > b$) yields the **Large Schröder number** $S_{\Delta K - 1} = \sum_{r=0}^{\Delta K - 1}\binom{\Delta K - 1 + r}{2r} C_r$ ($2, 6, 22, 90, 394$ for $\Delta K = 2, \dots, 6$) [10]. Specializing to $\Delta K = 3$, we construct the complete **30-branch valuation oracle** (`oracle_dk3_complete(K_pre, m)`), recovering $1{,}914 / 1{,}914$ ($100\%$) of excursions at $\Delta E \le 12, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$ and $14{,}127 / 14{,}127$ ($100\%$) at $\Delta E \le 18, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$.

3. **Theorem 3 (Signed Blind-Suffix Plateau Factorization with Prefix-Simplex Freedom, Unconditional $K_1 = 1$ Positivity, Complete 20-Branch On-Plateau Oracle for $\Delta K = 4$, and Complete 60-Branch $K_1 = 1$ Oracle for $\Delta K = 4$)**:
   We establish the general signed prefix–suffix reduction across an intermediate $\Delta K(s) = 0$ plateau at odd weight $K_1 \in \{1, \dots, \Delta K - 1\}$ with $i_{K_1 + 1} \neq i'_{K_1 + 1}$: the suffix quotient $W_{K_1} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\mathrm{odd}} \setminus \{0\}$ is a nonzero **signed** odd integer (where $W_{K_1} \le -1$ reverses the branch labels of the shifted suffix excursion, as realized at $\Delta K = 5, K_1 = 2$ and terminal-inverted $\Delta K = 3, K_1 = 2$, whereas at $K_1 = 1$ divisibility $2^{a'_1} \mid (3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}}+1} m + 1)$ guarantees $W_1 \ge 1$ unconditionally). Whenever all non-canceled coalesced prefix valuations lie below $s_{\mathrm{exit}}$, Theorem 1(1) recovers all non-canceled prefix indices together with $(s_{\mathrm{exit}}, W_{K_1})$, while any $c$ on-plateau simultaneous steps $i_j = i'_j = s_j$ inside the prefix remain free simplex parameters. For $\Delta K = 4$, we construct:
   - the **complete 20-branch valuation oracle** for all on-plateau simultaneous-step excursions ($8$ branches at $j = 2$, $10$ branches at $j = 3$ where $a_4 \ge \max(i_2, i'_2) + 2$ prevents prefix–tail coalescence, and $2$ branches at $j = (2, 3)$), recovering $916 / 916$ ($100\%$) at $\Delta E \le 12, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$ and $68 / 68$ ($100\%$) maximal $2\text{D}$ simplex tuples across $19 \le \Delta E \le 23$; and
   - the **complete 60-branch valuation oracle** for all $K_1 = 1$ single-exit $\Delta K = 4$ excursions (`oracle_dk4_K1_single_exit(K_pre, m)`), recovering $1{,}208 / 1{,}208$ ($100\%$) across all odd $1 \le m \le 1{,}619$ at $\Delta E \le 10, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$ and $4{,}317 / 4{,}317$ ($100\%$) at $\Delta E \le 12, K_{\mathrm{pre}} \le 6$.

**Complexity and Scope Conventions.** Throughout this paper, when we refer to an "$O(1)$-arithmetic valuation oracle" for fixed $\Delta K$, we mean that for any input pair $(K_{\mathrm{pre}}, m)$, a bounded number ($O(1)$) of exact integer arithmetic and $2$-adic valuation operations $v_2(\cdot)$ (each operating on integers of bit-length $O(\Delta K + K_{\mathrm{pre}} + \log m + \Delta E)$) produces a **compact parametrization** of all solutions: at most one discrete index tuple per non-plateau or off-plateau branch, and exact open interval bounds $(a_{\min}, a_{\max})$ on each on-plateau simultaneous-step branch. Listing all individual index tuples from an on-plateau branch of span $S = a_{\max} - a_{\min} - 1$ then performs output-sensitive enumeration over the $\binom{S}{r}$ elements of the shift interval or simplex. Finally, we emphasize that our results classify and parameterize finite equal-weight trajectory-merge excursions (1.2); they do not establish universal trajectory descent, global cycle exclusion, or the non-existence of divergent positive Collatz orbits [2, 9].

## 2. Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling, Simultaneous-Step Dichotomy, and Maximal On-Plateau Binomial-Simplex Families

### 2.1 The General Weight-$\Delta K$ Excursion Equation

Let $(\tau, \tau') \in \{0,1\}^{b'+1} \times \{0,1\}^{b'+1}$ be an equal-length, equal-weight primitive excursion of common odd weight $\Delta K \ge 1$, common even count $\Delta E \ge 1$, active terminal index $b' = \Delta E + \Delta K - 1$, prefix odd weight $K_{\mathrm{pre}} \ge 0$, and odd prefix separation $m \ge 1$. Denote the $\Delta K$ odd-step indices of $\tau$ and $\tau'$ by
$$0 \le i_1 < i_2 < \dots < i_{\Delta K} \le b', \qquad 0 \le i'_1 < i'_2 < \dots < i'_{\Delta K} \le b'. \tag{2.1}$$
Because $\tau_0 \neq \tau'_0$, either $(i_1, i'_1) = (0, i'_1)$ with $i'_1 \ge 1$ (**Type I**: $\tau_0 = 1, \tau'_0 = 0$) or $(i_1, i'_1) = (i_1, 0)$ with $i_1 \ge 1$ (**Type II**: $\tau'_0 = 1, \tau_0 = 0$). Similarly, because $\tau_{b'} \neq \tau'_{b'}$, either $i'_{\Delta K} = b'$ and $i_{\Delta K} < b'$ (**normal-terminal**: $\tau_{b'} = 0, \tau'_{b'} = 1$) or $i_{\Delta K} = b'$ and $i'_{\Delta K} < b'$ (**terminal-inverted**: $\tau_{b'} = 1, \tau'_{b'} = 0$). Expanding (1.2) via (1.1) yields
$$3^{\Delta K + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m = \sum_{j=1}^{\Delta K} 3^{\Delta K - j} 2^{i'_j} - \sum_{j=1}^{\Delta K} 3^{\Delta K - j} 2^{i_j}. \tag{2.2}$$
Notice that every coefficient $\pm 3^{\Delta K - j}$ in (2.2) is an **odd integer**.

### 2.2 Theorem 1: Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling and Maximal On-Plateau Binomial-Simplex Families

**Theorem 1 (Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling, Simultaneous-Step Dichotomy, and Universal Maximal On-Plateau Simplex Oracle).**
1. *(Odd-Coefficient Valuation Peeling Lemma)*: Let $r \ge 1$, let $c_1, c_2, \dots, c_r \in \mathbb{Z}$ be **odd** integers, and let $1 \le e_1 < e_2 < \dots < e_r$ be strictly increasing positive integers. Set $V = \sum_{j=1}^r c_j 2^{e_j}$. Define recursively $R_0 = V$ and, for $1 \le j \le r$,
   $$e_j = v_2(R_{j-1}), \qquad R_j = R_{j-1} - c_j 2^{e_j}. \tag{2.3}$$
   Then at every step $1 \le j \le r$, $R_{j-1} \neq 0$, the exact $2$-adic valuation $v_2(R_{j-1})$ equals $e_j$ unconditionally (zero binary carry-coalescence), and $R_r = 0$. In particular, for fixed odd coefficients $(c_1, \dots, c_{r-1})$ and an unknown odd coefficient $c_r = W$ at the highest exponent $e_r$, peeling $e_1, \dots, e_{r-1}$ via (2.3) requires only the known coefficients $c_1, \dots, c_{r-1}$ and leaves the exact remainder $R_{r-1} = W \cdot 2^{e_r}$, from which both $e_r = v_2(R_{r-1})$ and $W = R_{r-1} / 2^{e_r}$ are recovered in one final valuation step.
2. *(Disjoint-Support Shuffle Uniqueness)*: In the general weight-$\Delta K$ excursion equation (2.2), suppose the $2\Delta K - 1$ positive exponents among $\{i_1, \dots, i_{\Delta K}\} \cup \{i'_1, \dots, i'_{\Delta K}\} \setminus \{0\}$ are pairwise distinct and ordered according to a fixed shuffle permutation $\pi$. Moving the $2^0 = 1$ term to the left-hand side of (2.2) produces $V_{\mathrm{base}}(K_{\mathrm{pre}}, m) = 3^{\Delta K + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m \pm 3^{\Delta K - 1}$. By Part (1), for any $(K_{\mathrm{pre}}, m)$, the shuffle $\pi$ admits **at most one** solution tuple $(i_1, \dots, i_{\Delta K}; i'_1, \dots, i'_{\Delta K})$, which is recovered in $2\Delta K - 1$ exact valuation steps.
3. *(Simultaneous-Step Dichotomy: On-Plateau Cancellation vs. Off-Plateau Coalescence)*: Suppose a simultaneous odd step $i_j = i'_{j'} = s \in \{1, \dots, b'-1\}$ occurs in (2.2) at step indices $j, j' \in \{1, \dots, \Delta K\}$. Let $\Delta K(s) = K_{\tau'}(s) - K_\tau(s) = j' - j$ denote the cumulative odd-weight difference immediately prior to index $s$. Then:
   - **(a) On-Plateau Identical Cancellation ($j = j'$, i.e., $\Delta K(s) = 0$)**: The two terms $+3^{\Delta K - j} 2^s$ and $-3^{\Delta K - j} 2^s$ have equal and opposite coefficients and **cancel identically** in $C_{b'+1}(\tau') - C_{b'+1}(\tau)$:
     $$3^{\Delta K - j'} 2^{i'_{j'}} - 3^{\Delta K - j} 2^{i_j} = 0. \tag{2.4}$$
     Consequently, $s$ drops out of the Diophantine equation (2.2) and acts as a **free internal shift parameter** ranging over the open integer interval
     $$s \in \bigl(\max(i_{j-1}, i'_{j-1}),\, \min(i_{j+1}, i'_{j+1})\bigr) \cap \mathbb{Z}, \tag{2.5}$$
     yielding $\max\bigl(0,\, \min(i_{j+1}, i'_{j+1}) - \max(i_{j-1}, i'_{j-1}) - 1\bigr)$ distinct primitive excursions for the same $(K_{\mathrm{pre}}, m, \Delta E)$.
   - **(b) Off-Plateau Valuation Shift ($j \neq j'$, i.e., $\Delta K(s) \neq 0$)**: Let $d = |j - j'| \ge 1$ and $j_{\max} = \max(j, j')$. The two terms at index $s$ coalesce into a single dyadic term with shifted $2$-adic valuation $s + \delta(d)$:
     $$3^{\Delta K - j'} 2^s - 3^{\Delta K - j} 2^s = \operatorname{sgn}(j - j') \cdot 3^{\Delta K - j_{\max}} \left(\frac{3^d - 1}{2^{\delta(d)}}\right) 2^{s + \delta(d)}, \qquad \delta(d) := v_2(3^d - 1) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } d \text{ is odd,} \\ 2 + v_2(d) & \text{if } d \text{ is even.} \end{cases} \tag{2.6}$$
     Specifically, for $d = 1$ ($\Delta K(s) = \pm 1$), $\delta(1) = 1$ with odd coefficient $\pm 3^{\Delta K - j_{\max}}$; for $d = 2$ ($\Delta K(s) = \pm 2$), $\delta(2) = 3$ with odd coefficient $\pm 3^{\Delta K - j_{\max}}$.
4. *($\max(0, \Delta K - 2)$ On-Plateau Dimension Bound and Universal 3-Step Simplex Oracle for $\Delta K \ge 3$)*: Because $\tau_0 \neq \tau'_0$ forces $i_1 \neq i'_1$ and $\tau_{b'} \neq \tau'_{b'}$ forces $i_{\Delta K} \neq i'_{\Delta K}$, on-plateau simultaneous steps $i_j = i'_j$ can occur **only** at intermediate indices $j \in \{2, \dots, \Delta K - 1\}$. Thus every primitive excursion of weight $\Delta K \ge 1$ has at most $\max(0, \Delta K - 2)$ on-plateau simultaneous steps ($0$ for $\Delta K \in \{1, 2\}$, where no intermediate index $j$ exists and no span constraint applies—e.g., at $\Delta K = 2, K_{\mathrm{pre}} = 0, m = 1$, $(i_1, i_2) = (0, 1)$ and $(i'_1, i'_2) = (1, 3)$ satisfy $14 - 5 = 9$ at $\Delta E = 2$).

   For any **$\Delta K \ge 3$**, a normal-terminal family achieves the maximal on-plateau cancellation dimension $\Delta K - 2 \ge 1$ ($i_j = i'_j = s_j$ for all $2 \le j \le \Delta K - 1$) if and only if the endpoint indices $a_1 := \max(i_1, i'_1) \ge 1$, $a_{\Delta K} := i_{\Delta K}$, and $b' = i'_{\Delta K}$ satisfy the reduced $3$-term Diophantine equation
   $$3^{\Delta K + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m = 3^{\Delta K - 1}\bigl(2^{i'_1} - 2^{i_1}\bigr) + \bigl(2^{b'} - 2^{a_{\Delta K}}\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{span} := a_{\Delta K} - a_1 - 1 \ge \Delta K - 2 \ge 1, \tag{2.7}$$
   in which case $(s_2, \dots, s_{\Delta K - 1})$ ranges freely over the $\binom{\mathrm{span}}{\Delta K - 2}$-element binomial simplex of $(a_1, a_{\Delta K}) \cap \mathbb{Z}$, and divisibility of $2^{b'} - 2^{a_{\Delta K}}$ by $3^{\Delta K - 1}$ forces the primitive-root periodicity law and **sharp** minimum even-step threshold
   $$\boxed{2 \cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} = \mathrm{ord}_{3^{\Delta K - 1}}(2) \;\Big|\; (b' - a_{\Delta K}), \qquad \Delta E \ge \Delta E_{\min}(\Delta K) := 2 \cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} + 1 \qquad (\Delta K \ge 3). \tag{2.8}}$$
   Moreover, for **every $\Delta K \ge 3$**:
   - **(a) Sharpness of $\Delta E_{\min}(\Delta K)$**: The lower bound $\Delta E_{\min}(\Delta K) = 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} + 1$ in (2.8) is attained with equality at $a_1 = 1$, $a_{\Delta K} = \Delta K$ ($\mathrm{span} = \Delta K - 2$, $(s_2, \dots, s_{\Delta K - 1}) = (2, \dots, \Delta K - 1)$), and $b' = \Delta K + 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2}$, where $W_{\min}(\Delta K) := (2^{2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2}} - 1)/3^{\Delta K - 1}$ is coprime to $3$ and the unique choice of sign in $2^{\Delta K} W_{\min}(\Delta K) \pm 1 \equiv 0 \pmod 3$ defines $3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}} + 1} m = 2^{\Delta K} W_{\min}(\Delta K) \pm 1$ ($\Delta E_{\min} = 7, 19, 55, 163$ for $\Delta K = 3, 4, 5, 6$).
   - **(b) Universal 3-Step $O(1)$-Arithmetic Simplex Oracle**: Because $\mathrm{span} \ge \Delta K - 2 \ge 1$ forces $1 \le a_1 < a_1 + 2 \le a_{\Delta K} < b'$, the three positive exponents $(a_1, a_{\Delta K}, b')$ in (2.7) are strictly separated and never coalesce. Consequently, for any $\Delta K \ge 3$ and $(K_{\mathrm{pre}}, m)$, setting $V_\mp := 3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}} + 1} m \mp 1$ ($-1$ for Type II, $+1$ for Type I) recovers the complete simplex parameters $(a_1, a_{\Delta K}, b', \mathrm{span})$ in **3 exact valuation steps**:
     $$a_1 = v_2(V_\mp), \qquad R_\mp = V_\mp \pm 2^{a_1}, \qquad a_{\Delta K} = v_2(R_\mp), \qquad 3^{\Delta K - 1}\!\left(\frac{R_\mp}{2^{a_{\Delta K}}}\right) + 1 = 2^{b' - a_{\Delta K}}. \tag{2.9}$$

*Proof.*
For Part (1), by induction on $j \in \{1, \dots, r\}$, $R_{j-1} = \sum_{i=j}^r c_i 2^{e_i} = 2^{e_j}\bigl(c_j + \sum_{i=j+1}^r c_i 2^{e_i - e_j}\bigr)$. Since $e_i - e_j \ge 1$ for all $i \ge j + 1$ and $c_j$ is odd, the parenthesized factor is odd, so $R_{j-1} \neq 0$ and $v_2(R_{j-1}) = e_j$. Part (2) follows immediately from Part (1) since all coefficients $\pm 3^{\Delta K - j}$ in (2.2) are odd. For Part (3), if $j = j'$ then $3^{\Delta K - j'} - 3^{\Delta K - j} = 0$, proving (2.4), and by Theorem 2(2) of **`collatz_47`** [5] every resulting word pair $(\tau, \tau')$ with $s$ in (2.5) is automatically primitive. If $j \neq j'$, factoring out $3^{\Delta K - j_{\max}} 2^s$ leaves $\pm(3^d - 1) 2^s = \pm u_d 2^{s + \delta(d)}$ with $u_d$ odd; when $d$ is odd, $3^d - 1 \equiv 2 \pmod 4$ gives $\delta(d) = 1$, and when $d$ is even, the Lifting-the-Exponent Lemma (LTE) gives $\delta(d) = v_2(3 - 1) + v_2(3 + 1) + v_2(d) - 1 = 2 + v_2(d)$, establishing (2.6).

For Part (4), $\min(i_1, i'_1) = 0 < \max(i_1, i'_1)$ and $\max(i_{\Delta K}, i'_{\Delta K}) = b' > \min(i_{\Delta K}, i'_{\Delta K})$, so $\{j : i_j = i'_j\} \subseteq \{2, \dots, \Delta K - 1\}$. When $\Delta K \ge 3$ and $i_j = i'_j = s_j$ for all $2 \le j \le \Delta K - 1$, (2.2) reduces to (2.7) with $a_1 < s_2 < \dots < s_{\Delta K - 1} < a_{\Delta K}$, forcing $\mathrm{span} = a_{\Delta K} - a_1 - 1 \ge \Delta K - 2 \ge 1$ and $a_{\Delta K} \ge a_1 + \Delta K - 1 \ge \Delta K$. Reducing (2.7) modulo $3^{\Delta K - 1}$ gives $2^{b' - a_{\Delta K}} \equiv 1 \pmod{3^{\Delta K - 1}}$, which by LTE holds iff $2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} \mid (b' - a_{\Delta K})$. Thus $b' \ge a_{\Delta K} + 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} \ge \Delta K + 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2}$, giving $\Delta E = b' + 1 - \Delta K \ge 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2} + 1$. At $a_1 = 1, a_{\Delta K} = \Delta K, b' = \Delta K + 2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2}$, LTE gives $v_3(2^{2\cdot 3^{\Delta K - 2}} - 1) = 1 + (\Delta K - 2) = \Delta K - 1$, so $W_{\min}(\Delta K)$ is an odd integer coprime to $3$, and exactly one of $2^{\Delta K} W_{\min}(\Delta K) \pm 1$ is divisible by $3$, proving Part (4a). Finally, dividing (2.7) by $3^{\Delta K - 1}$ gives $V_\mp = \mp 2^{a_1} + W \cdot 2^{a_{\Delta K}}$ where $W = (2^{b' - a_{\Delta K}} - 1)/3^{\Delta K - 1}$ is odd; since $a_{\Delta K} \ge a_1 + 2 > a_1 \ge 1$, Part (1) yields (2.9). $\blacksquare$

## 3. Ballot Classification for $\Delta K \ge 2$ and the Complete 30-Branch Valuation Oracle for $\Delta K = 3$

### 3.1 Discrete Intermediate Value Theorem and Catalan/Schröder Ballot Classification

**Theorem 2 (Discrete Intermediate Value Theorem, Catalan/Schröder Ballot Classification for $\Delta K \ge 2$, and Complete 30-Branch $\Delta K = 3$ Oracle).**
1. *(Unconditional Plateau Crossing for Type-II Excursions)*: For any $\Delta K \ge 2$, every normal-terminal Type-II excursion ($\tau'_0 = 1, \tau_0 = 0, \tau'_{b'} = 1, \tau_{b'} = 0$) crosses at least one intermediate $\Delta K(s) = 0$ plateau ($1 < s < b'$). In particular, for $\Delta K = 3$, every Type-II excursion crosses either the $K_1 = 1$ plateau (when $a'_2 > a_1$) or the $K_1 = 2$ plateau at $s = a_2 + 1$ (when $a'_2 \le a_1$).

   - **(b) Weak Ballot Orderings with Off-Plateau Coincidences (Large Schröder Numbers)**: When off-plateau simultaneous steps $i_a = i'_b$ ($a > b$, since $i_{b+1} \le i'_b < i'_{b+1}$ precludes $a \le b$) are permitted, the number of distinct weak ballot orderings of $(i_2 < \dots < i_{\Delta K})$ and $(i'_1 < \dots < i'_{\Delta K - 1})$ satisfying (3.1) equals the **Large Schröder number**

*Proof.*
For Part (1), at each step $s \in \{0, \dots, b'-1\}$, $\Delta K(s+1) - \Delta K(s) = \tau'_s - \tau_s \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$. In any normal-terminal Type-II excursion, $\tau'_0 = 1, \tau_0 = 0$ gives $\Delta K(1) = +1 > 0$, while $\tau'_{b'} = 1, \tau_{b'} = 0$ and $\Delta K(b'+1) = 0$ give $\Delta K(b') = -1 < 0$. Since an integer walk with step sizes in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ cannot pass from $+1$ to $-1$ without visiting $0$, there exists $1 < s < b'$ with $\Delta K(s) = 0$.

For Part (2), in a normal-terminal Type-I excursion ($\tau_0 = 1, \tau'_0 = 0$), $\Delta K(1) = -1$ and $\Delta K(b') = -1$. The walk $\Delta K(s)$ stays strictly negative ($\Delta K(s) \le -1$ for all $1 \le s \le b'$) iff for every $1 \le j \le \Delta K - 1$, the $(j+1)$-st $1$-bit of $\tau$ occurs at or before the $j$-th $1$-bit of $\tau'$, which is (3.1). When the $2n$ interior indices ($n := \Delta K - 1$) are pairwise distinct, ordering $\{i_2, \dots, i_{n+1}\} \cup \{i'_1, \dots, i'_n\}$ with $i_{j+1} < i'_j$ is bijective to a Dyck path of semilength $n$ (recording $+1$ for each $i_a$ and $-1$ for each $i'_b$), counted by the classical Catalan number $C_n = \frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}$. When $r \in \{0, \dots, n\}$ off-plateau coincidences $i_a = i'_b$ occur, each coincidence corresponds to a diagonal step $(1,1)$, each solo $i_a$ to an east step $(1,0)$, and each solo $i'_b$ to a north step $(0,1)$ in the region $x \ge y$ from $(0,0)$ to $(n,n)$, which by the classical diagonal-step insertion bijection for Schröder paths [10] is counted by the Large Schröder number $S_n = \sum_{r=0}^n \binom{n+r}{2r} C_r$, proving (3.2)–(3.3).

For Part (3), exhaustiveness and mutual exclusivity follow from Parts (1)–(2) together with the mod-$3$ terminal parity congruence $C_{b'+1}(\sigma) \equiv 2^{i_{\Delta K}} \pmod 3$ (**`collatz_47`** [5], Section 3.3), which forces $2^{b'} \equiv 2^{a_3} \pmod 3 \iff b' \equiv a_3 \pmod 2$ and rules out the parity-forbidden boundary offsets $b' = a_3 + 1$ in `NP_4` and `NP_6`, $b' = a_3 + 5$ in `NP_2c`, and $b' \in \{a_2 + 3, a_2 + 4\}$ in `NP_5c`. $\blacksquare$

## 4. Signed Blind-Suffix Plateau Factorization and Higher-Weight Oracles at $\Delta K = 4$

### 4.1 Signed Blind-Suffix Valuation Peeling and Complete On-Plateau & $K_1 = 1$ Oracles for $\Delta K = 4$

**Theorem 3 (Signed Blind-Suffix Plateau Factorization, Unconditional $K_1 = 1$ Positivity, Complete 20-Branch On-Plateau Oracle for $\Delta K = 4$, and Complete 60-Branch $K_1 = 1$ Oracle for $\Delta K = 4$).**
1. *(Signed Blind-Suffix Valuation Peeling and Prefix-Simplex Freedom across a $K_1$-Plateau Single Exit for General $\Delta K \ge 3$)*: Suppose a primitive excursion $(\tau, \tau')$ of weight $\Delta K \ge 3$ reaches an intermediate $\Delta K(s) = 0$ plateau at odd weight $K_1 \in \{1, \dots, \Delta K - 1\}$ without an on-plateau simultaneous step at index $K_1 + 1$:
   $$s_{\max} := \max(i_{K_1}, i'_{K_1}) < s_{\mathrm{exit}} := \min(i_{K_1 + 1}, i'_{K_1 + 1}), \qquad i_{K_1 + 1} \neq i'_{K_1 + 1}. \tag{4.1}$$
   Let $\Delta_{\mathrm{pre}} := \sum_{j=1}^{K_1} 3^{K_1 - j}(2^{i'_j} - 2^{i_j})$ denote the prefix difference, and set $\tilde{i}_r := i_{K_1 + r} - s_{\mathrm{exit}}, \tilde{i}'_r := i'_{K_1 + r} - s_{\mathrm{exit}}$ ($1 \le r \le \Delta K - K_1$). Dividing (2.2) by $3^{\Delta K - K_1}$ yields the coupled signed prefix–suffix decomposition
   $$3^{K_1 + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m = \Delta_{\mathrm{pre}} + W_{K_1} \cdot 2^{s_{\mathrm{exit}}}, \qquad 3^{\Delta K - K_1} W_{K_1} = \sum_{r=1}^{\Delta K - K_1} 3^{(\Delta K - K_1) - r}\bigl(2^{\tilde{i}'_r} - 2^{\tilde{i}_r}\bigr), \tag{4.2}$$
   where $W_{K_1} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\mathrm{odd}} \setminus \{0\}$ is a **nonzero signed odd integer** with sign $\epsilon := \operatorname{sgn}(W_{K_1}) \in \{+1, -1\}$:
   - **(a) Signed Suffix Separation and Branch-Label Exchange ($\epsilon = \pm 1$)**: When $\epsilon = +1$ ($W_{K_1} \ge 1$, i.e., $\Delta_{\mathrm{pre}} < 3^{K_1 + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m$), the shifted suffix pair $(\tilde{i}, \tilde{i}')$ is a positive-separation primitive excursion of odd weight $\Delta K - K_1$ with $(\tilde{K}_{\mathrm{pre}}, \tilde{m}) = (0, W_{K_1})$. When $\epsilon = -1$ ($W_{K_1} \le -1$, i.e., $\Delta_{\mathrm{pre}} > 3^{K_1 + K_{\mathrm{pre}}} m$), **exchanging the two suffix branch labels** $(\tilde{i}^{\mathrm{pos}}, \tilde{i}^{\prime\mathrm{pos}}) := (\tilde{i}', \tilde{i})$ negates the right-hand side of (4.2) and forms a positive-separation primitive excursion of odd weight $\Delta K - K_1$ with $(\tilde{K}_{\mathrm{pre}}, \tilde{m}) = (0, |W_{K_1}|)$. Both signs $\epsilon \in \{+1, -1\}$ occur in actual positive-integer trajectory merges for $K_1 \ge 2$ (for example, at $\Delta K = 5, K_{\mathrm{pre}} = 0, m = 1, K_1 = 2$, the normal-terminal excursion $i = (0, 2, 10, 11, 12), i' = (4, 5, 6, 8, 14)$ has $\Delta_{\mathrm{pre}} = 73, s_{\mathrm{exit}} = 6, W_2 = (9 - 73)/2^6 = -1$, realized by the shortcut trajectories from $39{,}025$ and $39{,}024$ which take values $5{,}488$ and $5{,}489$ at step $6$ and merge at $290$ after $15$ steps; likewise, at $\Delta K = 3, K_{\mathrm{pre}} = 0, m = 1, K_1 = 2$, the terminal-inverted excursion $i = (0, 2, 8), i' = (4, 5, 6)$ has $W_2 = -1$).
   - **(b) Unconditional Positivity $W_1 \ge 1$ at $K_1 = 1$**: In contrast to $K_1 \ge 2$, at $K_1 = 1$ the suffix separation $W_1$ is **unconditionally positive** ($W_1 \ge 1$) for every $(K_{\mathrm{pre}}, m)$: on a Type-II prefix ($i'_1 = 0 < i_1 = a_1 < s_{\mathrm{exit}}$), $2^{s_{\mathrm{exit}}} W_1 = 3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}}+1} m - 1 + 2^{a_1} > 0$; on a Type-I prefix ($i_1 = 0 < i'_1 = a'_1 < s_{\mathrm{exit}}$), $3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}}+1} m + 1 = 2^{a'_1}(1 + W_1 2^{s_{\mathrm{exit}} - a'_1})$ forces $2^{a'_1} \mid (3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}}+1} m + 1)$, and since $3^{K_{\mathrm{pre}}+1} m + 1 > 0$ is not a pure power of $2$ when $W_1 \neq 0$, its odd cofactor $1 + W_1 2^{s_{\mathrm{exit}} - a'_1}$ is at least $3$, forcing $W_1 \ge 1$.
   - **(c) Blind Prefix Recovery and Canceled Prefix-Simplex Freedom**: Let $C_{\mathrm{on}} := \{j \in \{2, \dots, K_1\} : i_j = i'_j = s_j\}$ be the set of $c = |C_{\mathrm{on}}|$ on-plateau simultaneous steps inside the prefix (which cancel identically in $\Delta_{\mathrm{pre}}$ by Theorem 1(3a)).
