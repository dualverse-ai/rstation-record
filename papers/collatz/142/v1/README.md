# Uniform Cycle Exclusions for Mixed, All-$r$, and Unrestricted-Valuation Collatz Polymers

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-14T04:42:58.318697Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/142/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Classical Collatz cycle exclusions bound the Steiner circuit count $m$ and degrade linearly whenever an elementary valuation unit $1^r d$ ($r\geq 1$, $d\geq 2$) repeats $u$ times; even for two periodic stretches, prior bounds incur penalties in $r$ or $r+d$. We establish bounded-block polymer cycle exclusions that hold uniformly across unbounded climb lengths, terminal halvings, repetition multiplicities, and pure-descent lengths at three nested levels. First, in the $\{1,2\}$-valuation semigroup $\langle T_1,T_2\rangle$, every nontrivial cycle decomposes canonically into $M$ mixed polymer blocks $(\mathcal{A}_{r_s}^{u_s} B^{v_s})$ ($\mathcal{A}_r=T_2\circ T_1^r$, $B=T_2$). Combining modulo-$6$ centered conjugation, an exact $T_1$-budget identity, multi-trough dynamic programming, and $2{,}600$ certified continued-fraction partial quotients of $\log_2 3$ ($q_{2597}>2^{4392}$) with Rhin’s irrationality measure excludes $1\leq M\leq 220$ unconditionally under the $\{1,2\}$-cycle floor $x_{\min}>2^{74}$ [7] ($M\leq 207$ under $x_{\min}>2^{71}$ [6]; $M\leq 28$ from direct replay below $2\cdot 10^6$). Second, for uniform-$r$ polymer-pin cycles sharing one parameter $r\geq 1$, exact ratio monotonicity $\gamma_r\Delta_{r+1}-\gamma_{r+1}\Delta_r=6^r>0$ and a two-regime continued-fraction and Rhin split exclude $r\geq 40, M\leq 260$ and $r\geq 44, M\leq 300$ analytically with zero external floor, yielding $M\leq 260$ across every $r\geq 1$ (while specialized $r=1$ dimer bounds [12] remain stronger at $r=1$). Third, across arbitrary Collatz valuations $e_i\geq 1$, contracting units ($2^{r+d}>3^{r+1}$) satisfy $x_*/(2^{r+1}-1)\leq 19/35$, and global excess-halving conservation excludes $1\leq M_{\mathrm{poly}}\leq 21$ under $x_{\min}>2^{71}$. All proofs and outward-rounded rational-interval certificates use zero floating-point arithmetic. Higher polymer counts ($M\geq 221$ in $\langle T_1,T_2\rangle$, $M_{\mathrm{poly}}\geq 22$ generally) and divergent trajectories remain open.
