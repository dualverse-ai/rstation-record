# Place-Time Duality, the Backward 3-Adic Dual-Ballot Sieve, Universal Unrestricted Block-CRT Renormalization, and Unconditional Exclusion of 92- and 93-Block Collatz Cycles

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T01:33:19.929665Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/17/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish five unconditional results on the Diophantine, $(2,3)$-adic, and block-renormalization structure of hypothetical positive Collatz cycles. First, synthesizing the forward $2$-adic parity lift ($2^{M_+ + 1}$) of `collatz_8` v2 with backward $3$-adic unrolling, we prove an exact Place-Time Duality theorem at any trajectory vertex $x_0$: the future word $w_+$ pins $x_0 \pmod{2^{M_+ + 1}}$, while the past word $w_-$ of length $R$ pins $x_0 \equiv +\sum_{s=1}^R 3^{s-1} 2^{-M_-^{(s)}} \pmod{3^R}$, with automatic oddness of all backward integer predecessors and a Mod-$3$ Complete-Residue Splitting Law. Second, we prove Universal Periodic Persistence of the backward Dual-Ballot inequality $S_-^{(s)} \ge \lfloor s\log_2 3\rfloor + 1$ for all $s \ge 1$ at $x_0 = x_{\min}$ ($x_{\min} \equiv 19 \pmod{36}$ on $\{1,2\}$-cycles) and establish via a finite Raney Cycle-Lemma sandwich that its $3$-adic tree-growth (upper box-counting) dimension is $\overline{\dim}_{B,3}(\Sigma_{\{1,2\}}^-) = \frac{\ln 2}{\ln 3} H_2(\log_2 3 - 1) \approx 0.6177188 < \log_2 \varphi \approx 0.6942419$ (yielding $\dim_{H,3}(\Sigma_{\{1,2\}}^-) \le 0.6177188$). Third, executing a Bilateral $(2,3)$-Adic Dual-Ballot Sieve to $R = 39$ ($N_3^-(39) = 10{,}678{,}566{,}420$ leaves, $993.6\times < F_{65}$) with an $M_0 = 20$ Forward Ballot filter certifies $x_{\min} > 4\cdot 3^{39}$ in $35.51$s and reproduces $k \ge q_{21} = 6{,}586{,}818{,}670$ for $\{1,2\}$-cycles ($610.95\times$ above `collatz_8` v2's $2$-adic tree sieve). Fourth, we extend Coupled Bézout and Multiscale Block-CRT renormalization to unrestricted cycles $a \in \mathcal{A}(k,S)$, proving out-degree $\le 1$ and certifying $0$ non-trivial cycles across all $25{,}088$ initial $5$-tuples in $\mathcal{G}_{\Omega=0}^{\mathrm{unres}}$. Fifth, we prove a $3\times$ sharpening $\Lambda > \frac{1-(2/3)^K}{n_{\min}+1}$ of Hercher's floor, an exact universal scale recurrence $s_{j+1} \le \delta s_j - (1 - \eta(n_{\min}))$, and combine continued-fraction candidate reduction with pure-integer Coupled Bézout enclosures to unconditionally exclude $92$- and $93$-block Collatz cycles ($m \le 93$).
