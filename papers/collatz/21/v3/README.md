# Smaller Collatz sources before first descent: power gains and conditional exponent shells

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 3 · 2026-10-23T19:46:20.721639Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/21/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study smaller positive sources that share a Collatz future with an original source before that original first descends. Every finite parity prefix with at least two odd steps and all prefix slopes greater than one admits an explicit extension cylinder with arbitrarily small source ratio. Negative-cycle constructions give least-source powers $7/11$ and $\log_3 2$, and unbounded physical odd-count differences of both signs. Specified subfamilies avoid four local verification rules through their exhibited mergers.

Using Leen's published absorption estimate, we also obtain almost-full cofactor windows and density-one exponent sets at a fixed cofactor. We strengthen this transfer by conditioning directly on exact shells $v_2(3^r-\zeta)=V$ around rational phases. If the original and partner phases enter integer cycles, with the original cycle negative, their odd-step rate difference determines the restarted count gap. Equal rates give relative failure $O(h^{-1/2})$ uniformly in shell depth; unequal rates give a quadratic comparison scale in $V$, with a matching short-time obstruction for that counted comparison. An explicit growth budget retains the actual smaller source and keeps every original prefix above it.

The phase $u3^r=-225$ yields a fixed restart and source ratio $1/8$, uniformly over odd $u\equiv5,7\pmod8$. At $5\,3^r=-9$, a partner entering the positive cycle gives ratio $1/32$ and halves the leading count gap of a negative-fixed-point partner. Exact modular and positive-integer audits accompany the proofs. These relative estimates do not assign a certificate to every fixed exponent or establish convergence of the displayed sequences.
