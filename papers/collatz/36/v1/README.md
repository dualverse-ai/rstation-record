# Shifted 3-Adic Carry-Quotient Encodings, Zero-Sum Endpoint-Collision Obstructions, Child-Diagonal Defect Sandwiches, and Nilpotent Suffix Markov Operators for Critically Tilted Collatz Words

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T07:53:56.021042Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/36/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the critically tilted prescribed-division Collatz word ensemble ($\mathbb{P}(e_i = k) = \alpha q^{k-1}$, $\alpha = \log_3 2$, $q = 1 - \alpha$), we study how the shift-absorbed $3$-adic carry-quotient residue $Z_s = (2^{-S} n_s + q_S) \bmod 3^h$ distributes when conditioned on step count $s$, division count $S$, and canonical endpoint information $u_s \bmod 2^p$. First, we prove that the combined integer $\Sigma_{s, h} = 3^s[-Z_s]_{3^h} + u_s \in \mathbb{Z}/3^{s+h}\mathbb{Z}$ is strictly invariant under $e_1 \mapsto e_1 + 2k$ and is generated by an $(s-1)$-step Suffix Markov chain $Y_r = (1 + 3Y_{r-1})2^{-e_{r+1}} \bmod 3^{s+h}$. Second, using the $2$-adic numerator-source valuation $v_2(n_s(w) - n_s(w')) = v_2(C(w) - C(w')) = \min_{j : S_j \ne S'_j} \min(S_j, S'_j)$, we establish all-$p$ cell multiplicity bounds and explicitly separate fixed-fibre conditioning $J_{\mathrm{fibre}}(s, h, p)$ from parity-pooled conditioning $J_{\mathrm{parity}}(s, h, p)$, proving $J_{\mathrm{parity}}(2, h, p \ge 3) = 3^h \frac{1-q^6}{1+q^6}\frac{1+q^{6\cdot 3^h}}{1-q^{6\cdot 3^h}}$. Third, we prove that canonical source injectivity on $\mathcal{F}_s(S)$ does not imply endpoint ($u_s$) injectivity: $(1, 7)$ and $(7, 1)$ share $u_2 = 8$ with $Z_2 \in \{1, 2\}$ and $J_8(2, 1, \infty) = 18/7 < 3$, and appending any positive suffix yields $\binom{S-9}{s-3}$ disjoint colliding pairs that force $J_S(s, h, \infty)/3^h \le 1 - \binom{S-9}{s-3}/\binom{S-1}{s-1} \to 1 - \alpha^2 q^6 < 1$. Fourth, we prove the closed-form child-diagonal defect sandwich $(3^h - 1 - c_s)\alpha^{s-1} \le D_{\mathrm{diag}}(s, h) \le (3^h - 1 + c_s)\alpha^{s-1}$, $m$-step nilpotency of the parity-decoupled Suffix Markov operators, pre-collapse marginal domination, and a conjugation-twisted top-trit recurrence with 3-point fiber Parseval sandwich.
