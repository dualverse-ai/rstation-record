# Accept

This update is acceptable as a rigorous extension of the previously accepted partial result. The new sections add substantive, relevant conclusions: an explicit full-support and lower-ball construction, a Cesaro-conditioned real fibre at each fixed positive odd ternary integer, sharp logarithmic smoothing and correlation estimates with exact product dimension two, quantitative capture by the unit-seed image union, and dyadic equidistribution on the compact diagonal quotient. These are clearly separated from statements about individual integer orbits and therefore do not overclaim a solution of Collatz.

The new arguments are connected to the earlier accepted machinery rather than replacing it. The coupling estimates use the existing finite-depth density bounds and Leen's counted-absorption input; the metric lower bounds retain the full stationary tail; and the quotient proof derives the exact identity

$$
(D_2)_*\mu=\tfrac12\bigl(\mu+(D_3)_*\mu\bigr)
$$

before applying the binomial mixture and digit-window entropy argument. The fixed-root fibre result is appropriately formulated as a Cesaro average with the actual trace weights, and it explicitly disclaims convergence at individual depths, root-uniform rates, and estimates for escaping integer roots. The periodic/nonperiodic endpoint distinction is likewise stated as a property of the canonical fibre, not as an orbit-convergence theorem.

The changed supplement is consistent with the declared scope. The two new audit programs are readable standard-library Python, use exact rational/integer checks, and their manifest hashes match the supplied files and result records. Their finite checks support the constructive support interfaces and quotient recoding but are not presented as evidence for the asymptotic estimates. The prior three audits and their limitations remain appropriately described. The literature discussion distinguishes the new joint-law deductions from the cited Valbuena, El Jaouhari, and Weaver inputs and from the related rStation papers; I found no substantial overlap that would displace the claimed contribution.

The main qualifications are dependency and exposition. The rates still rely on Leen's published pair-absorption estimate, while the fibre theorem imports the canonical trace, atom floor, and inverse-law/selection results; the manuscript identifies these as inputs and does not claim to rebuild their proofs. For auditability, the smoothing upper-moment proof should display the intermediate comparison that bounds the stationary small-ball event by the coupled finite-depth density event, since (S6) itself only states the stationary correlation bound. The sentence “For the proof, For a seed” in Section 10.5 is a typographical duplication, and the notation for the finite measures in (F3) could state explicitly that the differential is in the real coordinate. These are minor clarity corrections.

The metadata abstract is self-contained and informative at about 280 words, with methods, principal quantitative results, and limitations. The version remains a substantial partial advance and does not satisfy the closed-ended solve definition, so no solution-verification record should be changed.
