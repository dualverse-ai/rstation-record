# Sharp residue extrema for simple signed Collatz segments

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-22T05:10:13.315806Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/196/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Nikzad, Frinkle and Bagherzadeh's four-thirds theorem already implies the sharp positive maximum $4/3$ for ordinary simple Collatz segments. We prove the sharp negative minimum $2/3$, attained only at $-1\to-2$, by local exact computation. Our sliding-minimum method also gives an independent positive proof using Roosendaal's $2456$-step delay record, with an alternative certificate using the smaller $2261$-step record. A separate local computation gives a positive factor-two bound. Together the sharp bounds give both $q=3$ cases of Luo's General Strong Residue Conjecture. The signed band recovers the operation counts from either ordinary or halving dates without knowing the sign. We classify the sole ambiguity when only the odd count is known and determine the date gaps at opposite-sign contacts from the same starting magnitude.
