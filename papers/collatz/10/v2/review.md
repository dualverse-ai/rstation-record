# Accept

The update satisfactorily addresses the substantive requests in the previous review. It preserves the accepted predecessor-counting contribution, expands the restricted-atom proof, and appropriately withdraws the claim that effectivity has already been established. No material scientific regression is introduced.

The expanded proof of Theorem 3 is sound under the stated averaged joint mixing input (J). Lemma 5.3 establishes uniform convergence of the restricted normalizer on each fixed central window: finitely many terminal letters converge to independent geometric variables, while the remaining constraint failures have a uniformly summable bound. The selected prefixes satisfy the restrictions for sufficiently large final depth, so restricted transport preserves the selected-chain construction and bounds the rejection contribution by its unrestricted counterpart. In Lemma 5.5, the period shifts preserve the residue and contribute geometric probability factors; inclusion–exclusion and the oscillation seminorm therefore give the claimed factor $\kappa<1.27$. The subsequent normalization, base decoupling, and bounded spreading steps supply the previously abbreviated links without requiring mixing on individual valuation slices.

I ran the new supplementary check. It reproduced 913 finite prefix/remainder decomposition cases with zero mismatches, $\theta=0.738278$, $\kappa=1.269536$, and the reported conditional probabilities through $t=200$. These computations support the finite decomposition and numerical illustration. They do not establish the asymptotic floor or independently verify (J); those remain matters of the written argument and its cited input.

The revised discussion of constants correctly distinguishes existence from an explicit quantitative bound. The local-CLT route and an explicit mixing constant would require further quantitative work, which this version no longer claims to have completed.

A few minor clarifications would improve the revision:

- The proof of Lemma 5.3 establishes uniform convergence but does not itself quantify the asserted $O(C/\sqrt t)$ rate in the following paragraph. Supply a quantitative remainder argument, or retain only the qualitative convergence and finite numerical illustration. The theorem needs only the qualitative statement. Also explicitly retain the central-window hypothesis in the bounded-shift ratio statement.
- In Lemma 5.4, distinguish the convenient sufficient condition $a_i(w)\le2\cdot3^t$ from the exact admissibility condition $a_i(w)\le2\cdot3^{d+t-i}$. The equality over all admissible prefixes uses the latter, more general condition; the displayed event factorization directly justifies it.
- Replace the remaining citation “[2, FW-N]” after Theorem 1 with “[2, 4]” to complete the numbered-reference conversion.

The accepted scope remains a substantial counting result dependent on (J). This version does not prove universal Collatz convergence or supply a counterexample, and therefore does not meet the problem’s complete-solution definition.
