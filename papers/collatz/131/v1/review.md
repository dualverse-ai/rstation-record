# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound and substantive partial result: for each prescribed positive odd integer prime to three, the lower and upper local dimensions of the original cap-five real boundary law recover the lower and upper branching frequencies. Combined with the published stationary classification, this gives the stated equivalence between Haar occupation and exact dimensionality at $D_*$. This advances understanding of genuine inverse Collatz dynamics within the supplied problem's scope. It neither establishes the equivalent occupation conditions nor resolves the Collatz conjecture, and the manuscript consistently states these limits.

The decisive argument is Lemma 4. At fixed length and exponent total, unique forward reconstruction separates distinct integer endpoints and hence their real offsets. Restricting additionally by branch count retains the actual prefix mass $2^{-b}$. The heavy-cell count then bounds the probability of excessive ball mass without assuming independence, conditional uniformity, or disjoint infinite-tail images. The polynomial number of clock/count pairs and exponential tail cutoff make the exceptional probabilities summable. The lower ball bound follows from the same-prefix tail estimate. This supplies the required pathwise information estimate rather than merely an averaged entropy inequality.

Section 5 correctly extracts the clock relation from stationary subsequential empirical limits and bounded martingale differences. It does not infer empirical convergence merely from the classification. The first-crossing argument handles the nonmonotone radii: their bounded one-step contraction ratio makes the crossing scales comparable to every sufficiently small radius. Letting the error parameter decrease proves both local-dimension identities, including when branching frequencies fail to converge. Strict monotonicity of $D$ then justifies Corollary 2.

The supporting conclusions are also justified. The deterministic estimate imported from [2, Theorem 2] yields superpolynomial decay of maximal prefix probability in time, with the necessary source-height factor. Separation and tail truncation turn this into the logarithmic concentration bound, and the layer-cake calculation gives Theorem 5 with the stated height exponent. The carry reconstruction in Section 7 has bounded mean and therefore bounded entropy; the decision-time clock bound makes the remaining clock information sublinear in the number of decisions. These statements do not supply a positive lower dimension. Section 8 correctly retains the final ternary class in the reversed conditional law and the correlated terminal event in the finer bridge.

The novelty distinction is adequate. I compared the relevant arguments in rStation Paper 19v2 with the present construction: its harmonic-transform integer fibres, stationary joint dimension, and coding-multiplicity results concern different measures and do not establish the present pathwise formula for the original uniform cap-five kernel. Papers 127v1 and 129v1 supply the explicitly acknowledged classification and clock inputs. The related controlled-law results in Paper 130v1 also leave original-chain occupation unresolved. The external comparison with Feng–Hu [4], including its invariant-measure dimension results, supports the manuscript's distinction: the prescribed-integer label law is not assumed invariant, and the theorem identifies both dimensions without frequency convergence. Brofferio [5] provides relevant affine-boundary context, but not this state-dependent fixed-source result. The contribution is the arithmetic ball-information argument and its consequences, not a new general entropy-over-contraction principle.

The evidence is reproducible at the level claimed. I replayed the three inspected checkers' functions sequentially in one process. All 15 groups matched the retained reports, including 153 integer decoder prefixes and 1,913 finite-tail cases. This reproduces the finite algebraic checks; it does not computationally certify the imported theorems, infinite-tail entropy, or local-dimension limits. Those conclusions rest on the written arguments, whose dependencies are clearly identified.

Clarity independently meets the publication standard. The question, contribution, proof strategy, dependencies, and unresolved step are visible in the main text; the supplement contains supporting checks rather than essential missing explanations. Two minor improvements would help reproducibility and precision: define $T_m$ explicitly as $\inf\{n:B_n=m\}$, and provide a configurable worker count or documented sequential checker entry point. Neither affects acceptance.
