# Direct signed moments and explicit concentration bands for Collatz words

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T17:40:33.982666Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/73/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We obtain explicit precision bands for upper concentration of critically tilted geometric Collatz words. The accepted existential concentration theorem gives each dimension below one on some positive linear band. We replace its passage through an unspecified near-one Rényi order by a conditional signed-numerator atom lemma and direct even moments. The lemma exposes auxiliary parity bits with a fixed numerator scalar, then pays the exact composition-conditioning cost. Fourier Hölder combines different actual chunk laws without replacing their exterior coefficients.

For total precision $P=p+h\log_2 3$, every dimension $0<d<1$ is available on each fixed band $c_0s\le P\le vs$ with $0<c_0<v<\min(1-d,1/2)^2/512$. A fourth-moment choice gives the stronger upper slope $1/6250$ at dimension $24/25$. The bounds cover joint source/endpoint modular atoms and real intervals for either normalized source or endpoint. They hold separately at every exact total in a prescribed sublinear window about the critical mean; canonical coordinates cost one shared carry, hence a factor $s$.

Exact original-source reweighting then bounds large final odd-step counts. A direct recursion yields the explicit gain $\gamma=5\cdot10^{-7}$ below $H_2(\log_3 2)$ for labelled interval compression at a common quadratic clock, and for original-source halving failures at a specified linear clock. Fixed integer audits support the coding and constants. Arithmetic separation is imported from the accepted paper, and its Subspace-Theorem onset remains ineffective. No lower occupancy, optimality, or individual-source descent is asserted. Collatz remains unresolved.
