The update supplies a sound, substantial extension of the accepted result. Theorem A improves the uniform labelled image bound from the critical power with a logarithmic saving to

$$
G_{k^2}(m)\le K m^{\theta-\gamma},\qquad
\theta=H_2(\log_3 2),\quad k=\lceil\log_2m\rceil,
$$

for some fixed $\gamma>0$. It preserves arbitrary interval translations, the common clock, and the odd-step label. The original-source halving bounds and convergence of distinct-state orbit zeta sums below $\theta$ are meaningful consequences. The accepted logarithmic-bound proof is retained; the judgment here concerns the new arguments and their consequences. None of these results resolves the Collatz conjecture.

The strict entropy gain in Theorem B is supported by the proof. At fixed coarse totals, triangular parity coding makes each next numerator bit a parity bit or its complement. Conditioning on all lower input prefixes fixes the signed addition carry, so the XOR entropy calculation in (M9) applies without assuming independent ordinary binary digits. The hypergeometric calculation gives the stated limiting budgets. The arithmetic transport in (M12)–(M18) retains the actual residue quotients and decodes signed numerator sums, rather than claiming injectivity of tuples. I checked the integral formulation of the Subspace Theorem in Bilu [10, Theorem 2.3]: it applies to these integer vectors without a primitiveness assumption. The fixed forms, exponential height bound, and growing valuation gap justify the exclusion of exceptional rational lines.

The subsequent entropy steps preserve the required probability law. Conditioning on the earlier word and separate coarse totals gives an actual convolution of independent internal words. The entropy triangle and balanced recursion yield the stated $D_r$, including the strict gain $D_1>\theta$. The passage to a fixed Rényi order uses bounded coarse alphabets after paying for the removed factors; it does not incorrectly apply a bounded-alphabet assertion to whole chunks. The product-space concentration argument and convexity control exceptional environments. For prescribed critical totals, centering the comparison geometric law at that total incurs only a polynomial conditioning loss, and the environment already determines the total. These points support uniformity at each individual total, rather than merely an averaged conditional statement.
