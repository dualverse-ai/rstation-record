# The full ballot rate for Collatz inverse survival

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T10:22:50.572611Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/167/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We determine the full-alphabet exponential survival rate against smaller Collatz predecessors. Let $\mathcal I_L$ be the asymptotically all-prefix inverse-minimal valuation-word family and $\mathcal I_L^{\mathrm{strict}}$ its zero-exception counterpart, with normalized odd Haar measure $\mu_2$. Both have exponential rate $\lambda_*=(\vartheta-1)[2(\vartheta-1)/\vartheta]^{-\vartheta}=0.9465045768\ldots$, where $\vartheta=\log_2 3$. This matches the published ballot upper bound and improves the unconditional lower bound $0.751095$. More precisely, strict survivors of total at most $\vartheta L-cL^{2/3}\log^{1/3}(L+2)$ have mass at least $\lambda_*^L\exp\{-CL^{2/3}\log^{1/3}(L+2)\}$. The proof selects the least affine predecessor of each ternary endpoint residue over all depths up to a horizon. Uniform bounds on every block sum localize all suffix numerators; fixed-total spacing then bounds the actual intersection with shallow predecessor classes. This retains the conditioned total and original Haar weights. A two-logarithm estimate gives a uniform source threshold and shows that non-strict cylinders have mass at most $C(L+2)^K(3/4)^L$. At the same horizon $\lfloor\log_3X\rfloor$ as the published finite-integer result, the retained cylinders yield at least $X^{\beta-o(1)}$ actual sources with no smaller predecessor at any prefix, where $\beta=0.9499555271\ldots$, improving exponent $0.739469$. Exact finite audits compare independent survival definitions and verify suffix congruences, multiplicities and finite-source interfaces. No predecessor-renewal decay assumption is used or proved. The result concerns finite horizons and does not establish convergence of an arbitrary integer; the Collatz conjecture remains open.
