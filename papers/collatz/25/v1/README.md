# Universal Canonical-Root Width, the Defect-Free Eulerian Farey Digraph, Complementary-Factor Chebyshev Decomposition, and Fixed-Slope Q_d-Fibre Census for Collatz Cycle Words

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-10-24T09:18:33.855672Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/25/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Building on the baseline canonical-root width inequality $W\le m+\delta-2$ (and its $\delta=1,m\ge 2$ refinement) and sum-unbalanced half-word resultants in `collatz_22/v3` [1] and the coprime plateau correspondence in `collatz_24/v1` [2], we study two structural problems for positive Collatz cycle valuation words $a\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^k$ ($k<S<2k$): eliminating the $+g$ ($g=\gcd(k,S)$) noncoprime width offset via dual-parent Farey windows, and characterizing the cyclotomic fibres $\{a\in\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{prim}}(dr_0,ds_0):Q_d\mid C(a)\}$ for fixed reduced slopes $s_0/r_0$, where $D_0=2^{s_0}-3^{r_0}>0$ and $Q_d=(2^{ds_0}-3^{dr_0})/D_0$. First, we prove that for every upper window $\delta=kq-hS\ge 1$ and $m\ge 2$, the strict refinement $W\le m+\delta-3$ holds (with analogous lower-window and sign-split sparse-numerator bounds). Second, for $k_0=k/g\ge 2$, avoiding the disjoint exceptional sets $J\cap J_-=\emptyset$ of the reduced Farey parents $(h_+,q_+)$ and $(h_-,q_-)$ defines a strongly connected Eulerian digraph $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{Farey}}$ with zero floor defect on every edge; for all words with $W\ge 1$, this yields $\{0,\dots,W\}=\{z_y:y\in(I_+\setminus J)\cup(I_-\setminus J_-)\}$ and the $g$-free bound $W\le m_++m_--3$, path-saturation bounds, Fubini-Farey incompatibility $h_-m_++h_+m_-\ge k$, and $g$-free cycle bounds $S\le C\min(m_+,m_-)(m_++m_-)\log(k+1)$ and $k\le C(m_++m_-)^{A+1}\log(k+1)$. Third, we establish the dynamic-range identity $|\log_2(C_{\max}/C_{\min})-W|<1+(S-k\log_2 3)$, the subword spacing bound $\mathrm{mult}(w)\le 1+\lfloor(M-n)/(2\max(3^L,2^{S(w)}))\rfloor$, balanced-shift $\{2,3\}$-free half-word resultants, and the Chebyshev decomposition $d\cdot 2^{\delta_{s,-}}C(a^{(s)})=\Sigma_s^+(a)Q_d+D_0L_s^{(d)}(a)$ retaining $g_d=\gcd(d,D_0)$. Specializing the classical $3x+c$ cycle correspondence [12, 13] to $c=D_0$ on $q_i=C(a^{(i)})/Q_d$ establishes fixed-slope all-$d$ finiteness and certifies the complete all-$d$ census across 11 reduced pairs $(r_0,s_0)$ up to $(10,16)$ (while leaving arbitrary-slope growth $r_0\to\infty$ and divergent orbits outside its scope).
