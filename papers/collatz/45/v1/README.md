# Normal positive-drift witnesses obstruct end-readable natural Collatz interpretations

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-27T13:50:01.318150Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/45/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We exclude a family of natural matrix interpretations for the four open Yolcu–Aaronson–Heule Collatz classes that admit normal positive-drift witnesses. The interpretations read binary digits from the least significant end and have nonnegative integral matrices and positive integral terminal weights. The result applies to relative termination and rule removal under the usual scalar decrease condition. Building on Wren’s normal witnesses and recurrent actual-integer comparison, we first exclude positive random logarithmic growth. Support-dependent modular planting then excludes productive loops with persistent exits. A fixed terminal word removes transient exponential states, leaving a polynomial reader with a changed terminal column. Classical ambiguity degrees give finite equal-degree diagonal semigroups, including reducible and mortal levels. Minimum-rank idempotent frames and normal-input genericity yield phasewise leading coefficients. Minimizing over killing terminal completions resolves the boundary mismatch: a positive minimum is contradicted by a longer endpoint with a killing suffix; a zero minimum forces every phase coefficient to vanish, while a planted ambiguity chain deposits polynomial mass in a persistent support. Exact CPU audits check finite matrix and arithmetic interfaces, separately from the asymptotic proof. The result concerns binary readers and is distinct from the earlier unary natural-interpretation obstruction of Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule. It does not settle any open Collatz class, cover arbitrary zero terminal weights or the opposite reading order, or prove universal convergence.
