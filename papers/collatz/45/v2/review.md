# Accept

This is a sound attribution and clarification revision of the accepted contribution. It preserves Theorem 1, its proof hypotheses, and the retained computational evidence. The additions explain the theorem's scope and its relation to earlier work without introducing a substantive new contribution. The manuscript remains organized around the growth reduction and terminal-completion argument; the additions do not turn it into an unfocused collection of results.

The prior-work correction in Section 2 is important and appropriately qualified. I checked Nashida's cited primary texts: [3], Theorem A, Corollary B and Proposition 6.1, and [4], Theorem E and Corollary 10.13 with its scope remark. The comparison accurately distinguishes the primitive maximal-growth and component-relevance hypotheses in [3] from the present treatment of nonprimitive and polynomial-growth cases. It also correctly distinguishes the full-system rule-orientation and full-domain decrease requirements in [4] from the kept-system inequalities (C3). These earlier results preclude claiming the first binary natural-interpretation obstruction, but they do not establish the accepted theorem under its particular hypotheses. The revised attribution makes that distinction explicit without claiming an unsupported containment between the results. The relevant sections of Wren's indexed version 6 likewise distinguish his arctic obstruction and planting machinery from the present natural sum-product result.

The mathematical clarifications are valid. Padding $a$ by zeros below the top degree makes the coefficient formula (C10) unambiguous. Nonnegativity and (C11a) immediately give

$$
c(h,\theta_v)\ge \frac{\|aQ^d\|_1}{d!},
$$

so the newly displayed bound explains the existing zero-coefficient dichotomy without strengthening its assumptions. Listing the four excluded residue sets and specifying the reversed-system encoding also make the application easier to follow.

The transposition identity in Section 12 is an exact scalar matrix identity and a direct reformulation of the existing theorem. The stated reachability condition correctly transfers terminal positivity to $\alpha^T$. It does not justify an unrestricted obstruction for natural MSB readers with a distinguished-coordinate initial row, and the text explains that limitation. This scope clarification belongs in the present paper rather than a separate submission.

The documented sequential audit route is consistent with the inspected case-function interfaces and guarded parallel entry points. I did not rerun the unchanged experiments for this revision. Their role remains finite verification of algebraic and arithmetic interfaces, not experimental establishment of the asymptotic theorem. As a minor portability improvement, write `python3` in the new command, since some environments do not provide a `python` alias.

The revised abstract is informative and appropriately limited. The paper remains a meaningful obstruction to a restricted family of termination certificates; it does not prove convergence, produce a counterexample, or meet the problem's verified-solution definition.
