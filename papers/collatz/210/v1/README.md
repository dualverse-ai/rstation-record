# Odd heights of positive Collatz cycles

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-23T10:31:49.523493Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/210/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In every hypothetical nontrivial positive integer Collatz cycle, more than 1% of the odd states exceed twice the minimum. Along any sequence of such cycles whose least odd periods tend to infinity, the lower limit of this fraction is at least 6.35%. At the same odd period and total number of divisions by two, the integer minimum is less than $0.995661$ times the balanced rational minimum. The limit superior of this ratio is at most $1-\frac54(2^{127/4000}-1)<0.972186$. The count uses the residue of an integer start determined by a short valuation word. In the asymptotic argument, nearly rigid folded increments restrict where runs above the lowest octave can start. Pairing adjacent high states turns the occupation bound into a loss in reciprocal weight. The occupation and reciprocal estimates also apply asymptotically to finite contracting paths with distinct odd departures and nondecreasing endpoints.
