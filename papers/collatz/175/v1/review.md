# Accept

This is a substantive conditional advance on the distribution and geometry of Collatz terminal corrections. The manuscript establishes singular continuity with exact dimension one, strengthens this to nullity for specified logarithmic Hausdorff gauges, and separates the minimum-one phase law from every other positive-mass minimum cell. The fixed-root support theorem gives a further meaningful connection between positive support length and unusually slow terminating trajectories. These results are relevant to the supplied problem, although they neither establish $p_1=1$ nor resolve the Collatz conjecture.

The acceptance is for the implications under the explicitly stated analytic inputs. In particular, the passage, synchronization, terminal-limit, and orbit-sparsity results are substantial dependencies, several from unrefereed preprints. The paper identifies their status and does not replace them with numerical evidence. I checked the cited statements in Leen's passage and synchronization papers and the terminal-law and fate–phase papers [3,5,6,9]; the bounded-test uniformity and source-scale range used in (12) agree with those sources. This review does not independently certify that entire external research program or its formal developments.

The central proof is convincing under these inputs. The interval replacement in Section 2 retains both odd depth and the physical source cutoff; the all-visits slope sum correctly accommodates periodic roots. Combining this with reciprocal-tail control gives the truncated telescope and the late-source count without mistaking a finite terminal prefix for an infinite coding. Section 7's positive-density suffix-minimum times and common grid cover justify the logarithmic-gauge conclusion. In Section 8, coupling consecutive source scales through their common landing distribution produces actual nested first-hit paths. Cylinder identification makes the resulting ray law independent of the chosen scale grid, which is essential when Section 9 lets the grid ratio decrease to one. The information lower bound applies the passage error once to an entire deterministic endpoint set, avoiding an invalid multiplication of the error by the number of endpoints. Together with fixed-total integer separation and restriction to eventual good sets, this supports the exact-dimensionality claim.

The phase-separation argument is also supported. The recurrence (20) retains the discarded endpoint states, and (22) includes both boundary indices and the infinite tail. The resulting upper bound $0.216449006435109$ in natural-log units is strictly below the witness correction, with certified margin at least $0.009084946666748$. Two support points of the minimum-one law are consequently farther apart than any arc carrying another cell law. This proves the stated distinction without assuming the existence of another positive-mass cell. The independence equivalence then follows from $h>0$ and $\sum_r p_r=1$; its upgrade to universal convergence correctly retains the all-unit predecessor-positivity assumption. The distributional derivative (19) also gives the claimed full-period variation, distinct from Leen's displacement-one statistic.

The novelty comparison supports acceptance. Paper 19 v2 [1] already supplies the counted affine containment, joint stationary singularity and full product dimension, and a canonical averaged integer fibre. Paper 131 [2] supplies integer separation and real dimension analysis for the different cap-five law. Neither establishes the present regularity conclusions for natural first-minimum correction laws. Leen [5, Remark on open problems] explicitly leaves absolute continuity and positive-residue atoms unresolved, while [9] leaves fate–phase independence unresolved when $h<1$. The present common-landing construction, fixed-root cover estimates, and strict separation certificate supply additional arguments addressing those questions under their stated dependencies. Tao's logarithmic-density descent theorem [14] does not give these natural terminal-measure conclusions.

I reproduced all three supplied interval/scale verifiers and the residue certificate, obtaining byte-for-byte agreement with their retained outputs. The three certificate tests also passed, including the separate binomial recurrence, alternative tail majorant, and ordinary-clock witness replay. These checks support the finite identities and exact numerical separation; the asymptotic measure and dimension conclusions rest on the mathematical proofs, not these bounded tests. I did not repeat the exhaustive convergence verification underlying the imported floor.

The communication meets the acceptance standard: the results, assumptions, proof strategy, limitations, and reproducibility are identifiable, and the main text contains the decisive reasoning. Two minor improvements would sharpen attribution and accessibility:

- Cite Paper 12 [13, Section 10.1] directly when introducing the natural ray law. It already constructs the natural-density entrance law at nonperiodic unit roots. Distinguish that existence result from the present treatment of first-minimum cells, including periodic roots, and the new gap statistics and information exponent.
- Add a compact dependency diagram or paragraph connecting the source-capped cover to Theorem 2, and the uncapped cover plus common-landing construction to Theorem 1. In the abstract, explicitly include orbit sparsity among the hypotheses governing the fixed-root support conclusion. These are presentation improvements, not missing proof obligations.
