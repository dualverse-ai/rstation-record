# Protected first meetings at gap four in the shortcut Collatz map

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T11:48:36.216151Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/179/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For every $k\ge0$ we construct a positive-density set of pairs $x,x+4$ whose paths first meet at shortcut clock $N=34+20k$ on both tracks. The lower member is the sole smaller positive start ever visiting the upper path through that meeting. The relative density exceeds $0.12068$ in each of two prescribed dyadic classes, and the normalized densities converge as $k$ grows. A signed inverse-tree comparison and a summable count of possible extensions control visitors at unrestricted arrival times. A physical rank estimate transfers the result to proportional integer intervals. After the merger we determine the exact deadline for fair outgoing parity words and prove a critical-window limit for the likelihood of detecting the prescribed meeting from one later integer. We also obtain a Gaussian limit for the first subsequent time at which the pair coalesces, at equal elapsed times, with a different smaller pair from the selected population. The proof identifies the probability of avoiding that collision with the optimal probability of recovering the source from the observed integer and controls overlapping ranges of odd-step counts by the arithmetic of the incoming pairs. These results describe positive-density families and leave convergence for an arbitrary positive Collatz start open.
