# Large multiplicative Sidon subsets in odd Collatz segments

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-16T22:49:56.788718Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/156/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $B$ consist of $N$ distinct consecutive positive odd Collatz states, and let $V$ count its internal edges with division exponent one. We prove that $B$ contains a multiplicatively Sidon subset after deleting at most $12V+C\log(2N)$ states, with an effective absolute constant $C$. Decreasing-edge exponents are unrestricted. When $V=0$, only a terminal segment of logarithmic length is removed. Consequently $E_\times(B)=O(N^2+V^3)$; families with $V=o(N)$ have asymptotically maximal pairwise product counts, and those with $V=o(N^{2/3})$ have asymptotically minimal energy. The same subset controls arbitrary samples. For every prescribed order $k$, we also construct multiplicative $B_k$ subsets of positive density whenever $V\le\eta N$ with fixed $\eta<2-\log_2 3$, giving $|B^{(k)}|\asymp_{k,\eta}N^k$. The sparse-rise selection yields $E_k(B)=O_k(N^k+V^{2k-1})$. Combining the Sidon theorem with Stevens--Warren's published shifted-product bound gives $|BB|\ge cN^{49/38}/\log(2N)^{C_1}$ uniformly over all distinct odd segments, improving the previous unrestricted exponent $6/5$. The new mechanism orders exact logarithmic corrections at selected original indices. Simultaneous running maxima and suffix minima give a large Sidon subset; amortized first crossings of a capped clock give the larger fixed-order density range. A classical logarithmic-form estimate aligns product collisions above a polynomial height, supplied by drift. We retain the large effective constant and the classical shifted-product logarithmic loss. Exact audits check the order, original-index and product-count interfaces. These structural restrictions neither impose the required rise density on a counterexample nor prove convergence.
