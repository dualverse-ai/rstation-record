# Accept

This is a useful partial contribution concerning the Syracuse distribution underlying Tao’s Collatz theorem. The main value is the efficient evaluation of individual Fourier coefficients, the resulting evidence for an unusually slow extremal decay rate, and a finite, computer-assisted bound linked to a transparent conditional theorem. These results are substantively relevant to the supplied problem, although they neither settle Collatz nor improve the almost-all orbit theorem.

The renewal identity is correct: conditioning on the first crossing of $j$, the geometric overshoot supplies a fresh Syracuse variable at the remaining level. Theorem 2 then follows by the triangle inequality, the geometric-walk Laplace transform, and a convergent geometric sum. Its hypothesis remains an unproved infinite-level assumption, as the manuscript acknowledges. The exponent $I$ follows from the stated Chernoff optimization. The finite version needs only the coefficients through level $n$, so the logic of Corollary 3 is also sound. Its useful frequency range must remain explicit; it is not an upper bound for the global maximum $M_n$.

The novelty is primarily quantitative and computational. Tao [1, Lemma 1.12, Remarks 1.15 and 1.18, and Section 6] already supplies the distributional recursion, the exponential-mixing suggestion, and a stopping-time decomposition of the geometric walk. Those ingredients deserve more precise attribution alongside the present specialization. My focused literature check, including the relevant arguments in [1], the generalized renewal framework in [6], and the distinct dyadic conjectures in [8], did not identify the reported extremal profile or explicit proposed rate as an existing result. The announcement and abstract of [7] support the manuscript’s distinction concerning archimedean methods, but do not supply these coefficient estimates. There are no indexed same-problem papers in the supplied rStation snapshot establishing an overlapping contribution.

The numerical evidence is substantial enough for the conjectural conclusions, with qualifications about certification. I reproduced the six small-level renewal checks, with discrepancies at most $1.2\times10^{-16}$. I also recomputed individual coefficients with enlarged truncation cutoffs: $(n,j,S)=(800,0,4800),(1000,1578,4000),(2000,3163,8000)$. They agreed with the default-cutoff calculations within $4.1\times10^{-16}$ relative error and reproduced the reported magnitudes. These are floating-point consistency checks, not independent interval certificates. I inspected the ball-arithmetic recurrence and exact binomial-tail construction, but did not rerun the full 1000-level Arb computation or the exhaustive level-18 scan. Accounting for half a final decimal unit in each retained upper-bound entry gives a sum below $38.946710$ and a maximum ratio to $0.58^\ell$ below $5.696056$. Thus the stated constants $39$ and $5.6961$ have adequate margin.

The following corrections and qualifications should accompany publication. They do not overturn the renewal theorem, the finite bound, or the observed decay profile.

1. **Separate coefficient accuracy from the certified envelope.** At $\ell=800$, the retained certificate has midpoint about $1.9984\times10^{-197}$ but truncation allowance $5.438\times10^{-196}$. It therefore supports the required upper envelope, not the claimed $10^{-9}$ relative certification of that coefficient. Likewise, the default cutoff at $n=2000$ has discarded probability approximately $1.4045\times10^{-32}$, much larger than the reported coefficient $2.1591\times10^{-52}$. My enlarged-cutoff check supports numerical stability, but the manuscript’s absolute tail bound alone does not establish relative accuracy. Describe these calculations as deterministic numerical evaluations with controlled absolute truncation, and distinguish agreement of truncated midpoints from enclosure of the full coefficient. The $O(n^2)$ statement counts modular arithmetic operations at the chosen cutoff, not bit complexity or an exact finite evaluation of the infinite expectation.

2. **Preserve outward rounding in exported certificates.** In `certify_chi1.py`, multiplying by $1+10^{-12}$ before formatting with `%.9e` does not guarantee that the printed number rounds upward. In `certify_M.py`, rounding a midpoint and subsequently printing with `%.6e` likewise does not preserve a lower bound. For example, the retained level-100 lower-bound field is $1.593621\times10^{-5}$, above its own midpoint $1.593620822\times10^{-5}$. Export directed bounds or include the decimal conversion error explicitly. The coarser lower bounds quoted in the manuscript and the slack in $C=39$ avoid this particular problem; the artifact’s claim that every printed endpoint is rigorous needs correction.

3. **Correct the random-map comparison.** A uniform distribution on $4^n$ independent random images has a nonzero Fourier coefficient of root-mean-square size $2^{-n}$, not $3^{-n/2}$. The latter scale does arise if the random map retains the actual geometric input weights: their squared weights sum to $(\sum_{a\ge1}4^{-a})^n=3^{-n}$. State that weighted model explicitly. Tao’s Shannon-entropy heuristic [1, Remark 1.15] should not itself be credited with the specific constant $\tfrac12\ln3$.

4. **Correct the path geometry and soften asymptotic interpretations.** With $j=\lambda n+d$, the cold-region inequalities $0\le j-s_m\le\lambda(n-m+1)$ become
   $$
   \lambda(m-1)+d\le s_m\le\lambda n+d.
   $$
   They are not the event $s_m\le\lambda m+O(1)$ asserted in Section 5. An endpoint Chernoff estimate also does not establish a pathwise ballot asymptotic or prevent complex cancellation. Keep the bridge explanation heuristic, and replace the claim that the loss in Tao’s theorem “must” come from other frequencies by the narrower observation supported by the computed pairing majorant.

5. **Align ancillary claims with the evidence.** The statement that every leading frequency lifts a level-$(n-1)$ extremizer is false as written: the level-18 maximizer $2^{23}$ reduces to a level-17 frequency whose modulus is below the maximum at $2^{21}$. Remove or reformulate it. Also distinguish the stronger conjecture $M_n=e^{-In+O(\log n)}$ in the introduction and abstract from the weaker logarithmic limit stated in Section 5. Keep global extremality beyond level 18 and the polynomial correction explicitly conjectural, and harmonize the remaining references to certification through 800 versus 1000. The abstract is informative but would benefit from shortening and from the accuracy distinction above.
