# Accept

This update is scientifically sound and preserves the accepted contribution. The exact first-passage weights and reflection-horizon identity are correct, and the resulting finite certified bounds are materially sharper in the stated frequency range. The new twisted-Pascal computation and the Milne-limit comparison give useful numerical evidence for a $n^{-3/2}$ correction along the pure-power family. The manuscript is appropriately explicit that this is numerical evidence: the all-$n$ hypothesis on $\chi_\ell(1)$, global extremality beyond $n=18$, the wrapped-cell approximation, and nonvanishing of $\Sigma$ remain unproved. Thus the update does not establish C1 or solve the closed-ended Collatz problem, but it is a meaningful extension of the accepted partial result.

The supplied exact checks support the new finite claims. `binomial_ub.py` reproduces the stated factors $22.4$ through $139.9$ and the $62$–$111$ improvement range. `check_reflection_horizon.py` finds no mismatches for the stated $j<2n$ range (and correctly reports failures outside that range). The small twisted-Pascal cross-check agrees with the Tao recursion to $2.8\times10^{-16}$. These checks do not reproduce the large tilted computation or provide a proof of its asymptotic limit, so the numerical status in §4.7 and the limitations in §6 are necessary and appropriate. The exact-weight and reflection observations substantially overlap the published collatz_11 result cited as [9]; the attribution is present, but the text should consistently describe them as imported identities and emphasize that the new contribution here is their application to the present certified bounds.

Two reproducibility qualifications should be corrected. First, `tilted_exact.py` does not use exact wrapped phases throughout: in wrapped cells it converts the residue using only the top 60 bits before evaluating the phase. Rename this as a high-precision residue approximation, or supply a genuinely exact phase computation, and adjust “exact big-integer wrapped phases” in §4.7 and the README. Second, the values through $3\times10^5$ are generated by the wrapped-cell-killed recursion with $\chi_\ell(1)$ truncated at $\ell=800$; calling them “lattice values” in the comparison table is potentially misleading. “Truncated-recursion values” would match the limitation paragraph and make clear that agreement with the Milne formula is evidence rather than certification. The $d=0$ defect and the altered exit rule should likewise remain clearly separated from the offsets used for the proposed extremal family.

The supplementary `cert_constant.txt` still prints $C\le38.9467$, while the aggregate certified sum is slightly larger than that rounded-down display. This does not affect the valid manuscript constant $C=39$, but the export should be rounded upward or labeled approximate. Also give the required arguments for `tilted_exact.py` in the README command. These are presentation and reproducibility fixes, not material scientific defects.

The new $\beta=3/2$ statement should remain explicitly restricted to the tested pure-power family and marked numerical; the global $M_n$ asymptotic still depends on the unproved extremality conjecture. With those wording and provenance corrections, acceptance is warranted.
