# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted contribution and adds a sound conditional nonvanishing certificate in §4.7. The distinction between truncated-recursion evidence and certified bounds is clearer, withdrawing the unsupported pointwise remainder rate is appropriate, and the corrected constant $38.9468$ supports the unchanged bound $C=39$. The explicit attribution of the imported identities to [9] is also an improvement. These changes do not establish the asymptotic formula or resolve the Collatz problem.

The new certificate is valid as an implication from (C0), ($\Psi$), and (H$'$). Splitting the series at levels $250$ and $1000$, retaining the signed initial sum, and bounding the remaining terms by the triangle inequality gives the claimed nonvanishing threshold. I reproduced `sigma_cert.py` and independently recalculated the bound using the accepted, directed-rounding modulus table throughout. At $\alpha=1/2$ and $r_c=20$, the latter calculation gives a threshold approximately $117.216118$ and a conditional lower bound $|\Sigma|>0.36$ even at $C_\psi=117$. Sampled signed enclosures at levels $1,20,131,250$ reproduced exactly. This checks the aggregation and selected coefficient computations; it does not reproduce every coefficient or the large-$n$ experiments.

The conditional status needs to remain explicit. Numerical solver values do not establish the universal inequalities (C0) and ($\Psi$), and the finite envelope through level $1000$ does not establish (H$'$). Replace “the solver values … satisfy” with wording that they *suggest* these assumptions, and qualify “what remains … is … a bound for $h^*$” by also retaining (H$'$). The displayed implication itself is sound; “This is PROVED” should refer explicitly to that implication. Including the short error-budget inequality and the relation $h_0^*=\lambda^{-1}e(1/3)h^*(\lambda-1)$ in the manuscript would make the argument easier to audit without consulting code.

Three minor reproducibility and attribution corrections remain:

- `sigma_cert.py` overwrites accepted bounds with `certify_chi1_801_1000_raw.txt`; 98 entries are slightly smaller than their accepted directed-rounding counterparts. Use the accepted table throughout, or document rigorous outward rounding for the raw file. The independent calculation above confirms that this correction preserves the manuscript's threshold $117$. Also round sufficient thresholds downward in `c21_sigma_cert.txt`, or label them approximate: the printed $117.22$ exceeds the computed threshold.
- Propagate the corrected terminology to §5 (“exact data” through $3\times10^5$), §6 (“validated against exact phases”), and §7 (“exact wrapped phases”). Add commands for the two new certificate scripts to the README.
- Remark 3.1 should acknowledge that [9, Theorem 3.1 and Tables 2–3] already includes applications to these certified bounds, as well as the identities. Present this material as an incorporated result, rather than claiming the application itself as new. The new conditional nonvanishing calculation is a distinct addition.

These qualifications do not undermine the accepted finite bounds or the new conditional result. The all-level tail hypothesis, harmonic-function estimates, and local-limit argument remain open, so the $n^{-3/2}$ asymptotic retains its numerical status.
