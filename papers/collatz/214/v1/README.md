# Weighted Prefix-Stopping Trees, Kakutani Column-IET Piece Covers, and Perturbed Mechanical Windows for Collatz Trajectories

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-23T23:34:23.542802Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/214/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study how the order of valley blocks limits the occupation of positive-integer shortcut Collatz trajectories. A weighted prefix-stopping identity expresses the initial-letter mass of the stopping leaves through right-special factors. Backward propagation bounds the contribution of positive bilateral multiplicity, which vanishes for interval-exchange and episturmian codings. A discrete Kakutani tower over an interval exchange gives a linear bound on the binary parity complexity with coefficients independent of the block lengths. Together with an explicit state-height estimate and parity injectivity, this yields a linear occupation bound for fixed interval-exchange piece covers. For universal two-size mechanical windows with up to $t$ flips, summing dyadic slope bands before block sizes removes one logarithmic factor from the upper estimate. Windows with an index-zero flip have sharp weighted growth of order $N^{3+t}$ for each fixed ray. Under the Bondareva–Luchin–Salikhov exponent, the one-flip occupation exponent satisfies $\sigma_1 < 3$. Finally, primitive-root decoding for prefix-free superblock dictionaries gives a cycle bound in terms of factor complexity and cyclic discrepancy. Exact rational arithmetic certifies a largest ceiling of 478,993 shortcut steps, below the classical lower bound of 17,087,915 for nontrivial integer cycles, in six dictionaries drawn from rational control families under the specified complexity and discrepancy hypotheses. Four of the dictionaries encode the grouped upper controls, including all 56 golden schedules. The piece-cover implications require a bound on total valley gain, and the excluded families have restricted symbolic schedules.
