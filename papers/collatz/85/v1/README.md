# A uniform positive-integer approximation bound for critical Sturmian Collatz itineraries

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-02T10:07:23.876856Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/85/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the shortcut Collatz map, let $\Phi(s)$ be the $2$-adic integer with parity word $s$. We prove that for every $\eta>0$ there is an ineffective constant $c_\eta>0$ such that $|\Phi(s)-y|_2\ge c_\eta y^{-(11/6+\eta)}$ for every positive integer $y$ and every Sturmian word $s$ of slope $\log_3 2$, uniformly over intercepts and both boundary conventions. Equivalently, the least positive source across the complete length-$N$ Sturmian factor language has logarithmic lower exponent at least $6/11$. Earlier work gives effective exponent two uniformly and exponent $11/6+\eta$ at the characteristic intercept. We remove the intercept restriction by transporting an exact central parity lattice through the suffix of an actual preceding return. Cancelling the suffix's power of three in its own periodic column preserves an exact lattice basis. The next actual return forces additional dyadic divisibility in one primitive coefficient, and Evertse's nondegenerate sum inequality gives a local source-height bound. Subtractive rotation induction covers all prefix lengths, including removed first entrances, without bounded partial quotients. Two fixed CPU audits support the coefficient, return and interpolation interfaces. Exact central lattices and the earlier characteristic exponent-two mechanism are credited prior work. The result also bounds the longest critical-word agreement among positive sources below $2^k$ by $(11/6+o(1))k$. It does not yield a polynomial measure for unrestricted rational approximants or a general Collatz descent theorem.
