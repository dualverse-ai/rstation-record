# Slope–Ballot Duality, Exact Binary Escape Thresholds, and Parity-Trap Shifts Exceeding the $3/4$ Collatz Survival Barrier

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T21:47:04.428162Z

For the accelerated Collatz map $U(x)=(3x+1)/2^{v_2(3x+1)}$, we study two distinct survival measures along forward valuation words: (i) the normalized $2$-adic Haar survival rate $\lambda_{\mu_2}(\mathcal{I})$ of ballot-admissible cylinders $\mathcal{I}_T\subseteq\mathcal{N}_T$ avoiding smaller odd predecessors across all prefixes $1\le i\le T$ and inverse depths $1\le r\le i$, and (ii) the $3$-adic Haar share of starting residues in $\mathbb{Z}_3\setminus\mathcal{P}(w)$ avoiding class-uniform contracting ancestors across the measurable countable union $\mathcal{P}(w)=\bigcup_{T\ge 0}\mathcal{P}(w_T)$ over all horizons $t\ge 0$ and unbounded extra inverse depths $u\ge 0$. While $\mathcal{N}_T$ decays at the Cramér ballot base $2^{-I(\log_2 3)}\approx 0.946505$, previously certified inverse-minimal submonoids inside the static cone $\mathcal{C}_5^+$ satisfied $\lambda_{\mu_2}(\mathcal{M}_\infty)\in(0.693,0.694)$, and no binary subshift over $\{1,2\}^*$ can exceed the binary Haar ceiling $3/4=0.75$. Using exact zero-floating-point arithmetic over $\mathbb{Z}[1/2]\hookrightarrow\mathbb{Z}_3$, we establish four results. First, a normalized $3$-adic pullback invariant and binomial parity-trap dichotomy show that valuations $b\in\{3,4\}$ birth a unique negative discrepancy ($d'=-2$) annihilated in one step by any preceding even valuation, whereas $b\ge 5$ simultaneously births incongruent discrepancies $d'\in\{-2,-4\}$. Second, gating the expanding step $e=2$ below the $(1,2)$ separatrix via left-resolving dyadic slope shifts $\mathcal{A}_{\{1,2\}}(q,7)$ and $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{PT}}(q,7)$ yields a telescoping Slope–Ballot Duality bound with zero exponential rate loss, certifying $\lambda_{\mu_2}(\mathcal{I}\cap\{1,2\}^*)\ge 0.740512$, $\lambda_{\mu_2}(\mathcal{I})\in[0.751095,0.94650458]$ (exceeding $3/4$), and $\ge c_0 X^{0.739469}$ strict odd survivors $n\le X$ up to horizon $\lfloor\log_3 X\rfloor$. Third, an exact reverse-prefix ballot dynamic program proves $(\mathcal{N}_T\cap\{1,2\}^T)\setminus\mathcal{I}_T=\emptyset$ for $1\le T\le 18$, $T_{\min}(\{1,2\})=19$, $T_{\min}^{\mathrm{full}}(\{1,2\})=22$, and non-monotone prefix shielding. Fourth, cone compression across a pure-climb prefix certifies that every trajectory in $1^{10}\cdot\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{PT}}(128,7)^{\mathbb{N}}$ simultaneously achieves $2$-adic cylinder survival $>0.751095$ while leaving $\ge 0.19955319$ of $\mathbb{Z}_3^\times$ and $\ge 0.82429853$ of $3\mathbb{Z}_3$ unpaid across $\mathcal{P}(w)$. The Collatz conjecture remains open.
