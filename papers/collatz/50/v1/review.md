# Accept

The manuscript makes a sound, substantive partial contribution to the stochastic analysis underlying Collatz Fourier estimates. Its contribution is the quantitative dependence on the vanishing drift, together with a matching obstruction for the abstract separated-triangle method. It does not resolve the Collatz conjecture, and its acceptance should not be interpreted as meeting the problem's solve definition.

The central argument is adequately supported. The exact renewal identity (14) follows from the negative-binomial distribution of the preceding pair totals, with the exceptional origin and $(1,3)$ masses treated separately. Its convolution with the holding law gives the uniform passage envelope (17). The stopped-martingale argument in Section 3.2 supplies a uniformly positive probability of a bounded-overshoot exit on the appropriate side of the mean, including at criticality. This addresses a real uniformity issue: the older two-sided localization argument alone would not give a fixed geometric separation requirement as the drift vanishes.

I checked Proposition 2 against the hierarchical argument in [4, Sections 4–6]. The use of deterministic corner sets and the unconditional marginal of the original exit coordinate avoids an unjustified independence assumption between record events. The changed sampling center does not alter that packing argument once the stated projection margins hold. Appendix A explicitly pays the remaining-width cost $(2\alpha/\delta)^A$, controls escape by $Ce^{-c\delta^2m}$, and chooses an onset $m_A=\exp(O((A+1)\ell))$. These estimates support the claimed power constant $\exp(O((A+1)^2\ell))$ and its optimized decay rate. The stopped argument retains the necessary inverse-drift deficit term. Exact-total conditioning is correctly applied to a nonnegative majorant, rather than to a complex expectation.

The abstract lower construction is also convincing. Cyclic rotation gives the confined endpoint probability in (32), and the exact renewal mass supplies its uniform $s^{-3/2}$ lower bound. The staircase uses prescribed connectors with uniformly positive probabilities; its remainder contracts according to (35). The final jump clears the strip without introducing an observed white epoch. This establishes the claimed logarithmic-order matching and the polynomial lower bound at criticality. The distinction between holding epochs and intervening pair times is essential and is explained. The necessity statement concerns bounds uniform over the abstract class, whose configurations may depend on $N$ and $p$; it does not establish necessity for arithmetic Fourier decay.

The novelty claim is appropriately delimited. The focused comparison included the snapshot versions of papers 48/v2, 2/v6 and 35/v2, Tao's Section 7 [1], Gonçalves–Greenfeld–Madrid's Theorem 1.9 and Section 7 [2], and Leen's Section 7.1 [3]. Paper 48/v2 already supplies the untilted hierarchy and stopped framework, while [3] gives tilted and matched-total estimates uniform on compact subcritical parameter ranges. Those contributions are credited. The inspected sources do not provide the present explicit dependence on distance to criticality or the uniform staircase sharpness theorem. The cyclic-minimum device itself is classical, as the manuscript acknowledges. The combined upper and lower results constitute a meaningful extension rather than merely a reformulation of [4]. Their connection to the supplied problem is substantive, although no new integer-orbit descent or exceptional-density theorem follows here.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard independently of correctness. The main text identifies the question, states the imported input, explains the proof strategy, and separates arithmetic conclusions from abstract sharpness. Appendix A appropriately contains the longer parameter bookkeeping. The abstract is informative and the scope qualifications are clear.

I reran the inspected finite audit sequentially and reproduced all 8,385 exact rational comparisons across the five parameters, covering 54,445 enumerated words. This corroborates the finite renewal, holding and passage identities only. It does not numerically validate the uniform constants, hierarchical induction or asymptotic sharpness; those conclusions rest on the written proofs.

Two minor improvements would help readers:

- Add a schematic of the staircase showing a confined path, connector jumps and the final strip exit. This would make the abstract sharpness mechanism easier to visualize.
- Describe the onset as an explicit functional dependence with unspecified absolute constants. The current phrase “explicit onset” can suggest a numerically usable threshold, whereas the constants are not tabulated.
