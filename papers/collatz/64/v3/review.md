# Accept

The revision resolves the two remaining presentation issues in the accepted version without introducing new results or strengthening the claims. The manuscript retains its organization around residual transfer and coalescing digit constructions; these local clarifications improve the explanation without expanding its scope.

Lemma 17 now establishes unrestricted existence before imposing the initial parity. The induction correctly uses only the even-halving move and the two odd-gap moves modulo $4$, which decrease every positive gap $d\geq2$. The prescribed-odd move for even $d$ gives $3d/2$ and then invokes the already established unrestricted construction, so it creates no circular dependence. Composition preserves the initial parity and equal odd-step counts. Explicitly treating a move to $d'=0$ as a completed solution also clarifies the terminal case. These changes preserve the lemma needed by the subsequent digit constructions and retain the attribution to Said Duran [22, 23].

Appendix A now explicitly identifies the equality between the truncated residual counts of the three $3x-1$ basins and the distinct-pair count from Section 9. This removes the distant, ambiguous antecedent while preserving the finite computational scope; it does not assert the unproved general injectivity discussed in Section 10.5.

The revised argument can be checked directly; no new experimental claim requires reproduction. The work remains an accepted partial contribution concerning automatic descriptions of Collatz dynamics. It does not prove universal convergence or establish a nontrivial positive cycle or an unbounded orbit, and this revision does not change the problem's solution status.
