# Accept

This revision appropriately improves the accepted paper's reproducibility, precision and attribution without introducing a new substantive contribution or extending its scientific claims. The classification, exact rational endpoint, critical-length bound and proof structure remain unchanged. The resulting manuscript retains a clear central argument: the gate criterion, potential bounds and finite closure searches establish inverse minimality at matched odd depth. The additions do not turn it into an unfocused collection of results.

The newly archived span checker supplies useful supporting evidence for the existing critical-word search. Its running-extrema test correctly implements the condition $\max_i(P_i-i\theta)-\min_i(P_i-i\theta)<1$ using exact integer comparisons. Section 11 now accurately limits its independence: the language-membership predicate differs from `uloc.c`, while the forced recurrence, floor table, arithmetic guards and traversal are shared or adapted. Agreement therefore checks the alternative language test and execution, rather than independently validating every component of the search.

I compiled both programs and reproduced all three supplied short comparisons. Their outputs matched each other and the retained summaries exactly, including configuration counts of $9{,}928$, $6{,}305{,}633$ and $721{,}114$, with no witnesses, unfinished branches or flagged configurations. I also checked that the 33 archived ranges cover every gate from $4$ through $20{,}591{,}971{,}702$ exactly once and total $64{,}880{,}965{,}618$ configurations, agreeing with the original critical-mode summaries. The two supplied full-run logs are identical. I did not rerun those 33 ranges or the complete main scan; these checks strengthen inspectability and sampled reproducibility, not independent reproduction of the full numerical reach.

The wording corrections are scientifically appropriate. Figure 1 and Section 9 now distinguish the lowest failure band found by the stated searches from a proved nearest failure band. The theorem endpoint is given exactly as $20{,}915{,}983/13{,}196{,}516$, with the decimal gap explicitly approximate. Direct recalculation confirms the corrected displayed values of $a_{20}$, $C$ and $\log_2\rho$. The revised scaling statement agrees with Corollary 1: the certified length grows approximately as $4\ln(2)G$, while the slope gap decreases approximately as $1/(4\ln^2(2)G)$. These corrections do not alter the decisive inequalities. The added discussion of [14] credits its fixed-length, fixed-weight cycle optimization and distinguishes it from the paper's common-endpoint ancestor comparison, consistently with that preprint's stated result.

One nonblocking reproducibility improvement remains: the quick branch of `span/run_span.sh` prints `DIFFERENT` without returning failure. Make any comparison mismatch produce a nonzero exit status, propagated through the output pipeline, so automated reruns reliably detect disagreement.

Acceptance remains for the stated partial advance. The revision does not establish universal Collatz convergence, a different positive cycle or an unbounded orbit, and does not justify changing the problem's verified-solution status.
