# Height-sensitive multiplicative energy of odd Collatz trajectories

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-17T13:23:55.216180Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/160/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study how actual state heights restrict multiplicative coincidences in odd Collatz trajectories. An $L$-step distinct segment is multiplicatively Sidon whenever its minimum exceeds $8L9^L$; the simpler threshold $2^{6L}$ also suffices. This improves the previous $2^{8L^2}$ threshold uniformly over every division exponent. The proof retains the established balanced-collision identity but estimates the last endpoint in the near-cancellation case, allowing its division denominator to cancel the large factor. A backward partition then gives, for every $N$-state distinct segment $B=\{q_i\}$, the unconditional height-sensitive estimate $E_\times(B)\le2592(\sqrt N+\sum_i g(q_i)^{-1/2})^4$, where $g(q)=\max\{1,\lfloor\log_2(q+1)/16\rfloor\}$. The partition explicitly pays abrupt downward jumps and all cross-block energy. Consequently an eventual lower bound $\log q_i\ge ci^\beta$, $0<\beta\le1$, gives $E_\times(B_N)=O(N^{4-2\beta})$; an eventual positive lower exponential growth rate gives quadratic energy and quadratic pair-product growth. Separately, record logarithms are bounded by the source logarithm plus $O(\sqrt{|BB|})$, so exponential record growth forces quadratic product growth along those prefixes without a pointwise height hypothesis. We credit the analogous additive partition and record arguments and distinguish the existing height-free and sparse-rise multiplicative bounds. Exact CPU audits check frozen orbit fixtures, both endpoint branches, and 220,872 bounded capacity walks. The all-size results are analytic. No lower growth hypothesis is established for a hypothetical divergent orbit, and neither a height-free fixed-power energy bound nor Collatz convergence follows.
