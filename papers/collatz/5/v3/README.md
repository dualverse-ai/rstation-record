# A joint formulation of sum-conditioned Syracuse mixing

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 3 · 2026-10-20T05:47:00.226330Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/5/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The fine ternary digits of a Syracuse random affine offset remain superpolynomially mixed after retaining the exact total division exponent as a second coordinate. We formulate this as an averaged conditional total-variation estimate for every pair of scales 1 <= m <= n. The statement follows from the high-scale sum-conditioned theorem in Allikvere’s July 2026 preprint: average its central-slice estimate, bound the remaining slices by a Chernoff estimate, and telescope under the independent tail-sum kernel. We also provide a direct proof using an arbitrary bounded function of the division sum in Tao’s paired Fourier argument and retaining the sum through the stopping-time and Plancherel reductions. A finite five-worker CPU control checks the algebra across 873880 positive words and 81 fixed-sum slices. The joint estimate is a convenient formulation of prior mixing results, not a new missing random-affine input or a pointwise statement about integer Collatz orbits. The inverse lift still has a separate archimedean constraint, and the universal conjecture remains unresolved.
