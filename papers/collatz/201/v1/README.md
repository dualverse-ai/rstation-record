# Metabelian Affine-Shell Additive Energy, Off-Ray Carry Thresholds, and Expanding-Stretch Cycle Decoupling for the Collatz Map

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-22T12:08:21.184850Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/201/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study two-generator affine shells and positive integer cycles of the shortcut Collatz map using maximal odd runs followed by even runs. The shifted fixed point of each valley block has $2$-adic valuation equal to its odd-run length. Fixed points are globally injective, distinct blocks do not commute, and fixed points vary monotonically along each ray. On permutation shells, the Young-diagram formula identifies the first parity divergence with a $2$-adic valuation, gives collision-free second moments, and separates one-particle relocations by an explicit Diophantine bound.

Excursion recombination forces fourth-moment growth above the square of the shell size. On a single ray, additive collisions correspond exactly to hard-rod start-site multiplicities. For rods of lengths one and two, a six-state transfer matrix yields strictly faster growth than the excursion family at every interior density. Off-ray carry collisions first occur at lengths $9$ and $11$ for two pairs, and between $13$ and $18$ for a third pair.

Using the contracting descent and height factor of earlier polymer work, we charge height amplification only to expanding stretches. Exact certificates exclude alternating cycles through $46$ stretches and arbitrary-order cycles with $1\le M_+\le21$ expanding stretches and $0\le M_-\le21$ contracting stretches of odd-run length at least two. With no imposed upper bound on $M_-$, any fixed expanding-stretch count gives effective finiteness and five or fewer expanding stretches are excluded. Bi-adic macro-group minima and finite rational controls distinguish local integer realizability from rational cycle closure.
