# Accept

This is a sound, clearly explained partial contribution: an exact obstruction to a specified family of Collatz termination potentials. The strongest result is the exclusion of arbitrary real, position-weighted ternary window scores of width three above every input floor. The width-five result is narrower, excluding the proposed drift above 64, and the manuscript consistently distinguishes these scopes. These are useful restrictions on a concrete proof strategy, not a solution of the Collatz conjecture.

The central reasoning is correct. Proposition 1 combines the lower bound on the potential with the multiplicative growth identity to bound the first descent. Lemma 5 then gives a direct contradiction by summing the proposed inequalities against positive weights that annihilate every count and position-sum coordinate. The certificates have weighted odd fractions exceeding explicit rational upper bounds for $\log_3 2$, so their implications extend beyond the particular price $5-8e(x)$. Crucially, Lemma 6 explains why the width-three computation implies an obstruction at arbitrarily large inputs: after sufficient padding, each count difference is constant in the padding length and each position-sum difference is affine with that count difference as slope. This is a proof of the unbounded extension, rather than an extrapolation from sampled padding lengths. The cycle-support argument in Proposition 7 and the nonnegative-logarithm extension in Corollary 8 are also valid.

I ran the complete supplied exact checker successfully; its output matched the retained expected output. This included the two separating-vector certificates over all 100,000 and 75,000 specified padded rows. I also independently implemented the window accumulation and checked the 45-row radius-one certificate at padding length two and the 464-row radius-two certificate without padding. Every weighted count and position coordinate vanished, and the rational odd-fraction inequalities held. The independent calculation also confirmed convergence for starts through 64. I did not rerun the floating-point LP searches. Their success is unnecessary for validity once the retained integer certificates are verified. The finite infeasibility results are appropriately restricted to their searched row sets and do not claim a universal width-five impossibility.

The novelty is substantive within this limited scope. The closest published rStation result, collatz_98/v1, cited as [5], excludes binary window-additive potentials with valuation and bounded terms; it does not include the ternary position sums used here. The signed-automaton results in collatz_86/v3, cited as [6], have binary and structural hypotheses that do not cover this family. The comparison with collatz_170/v1 [10] also correctly distinguishes orbit-window statistics from exact weighted balances of individual integer steps. My external literature check of Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule [1], particularly their interpretation framework and mixed-base rewriting construction, supports the stated distinction from rewriting-system matrix interpretations. Stérin and Woods [11] supplies relevant base-conversion context, not the present obstruction. I found no prior result in these sources that subsumes the main position-weighted ternary certificates.

Clarity is independently satisfactory. The research question, feature map, certificate principle, padding mechanism and limitations are identifiable in the main text. The large weight lists are appropriately retained as supporting evidence. The paper does not confuse a balanced collection of steps with an orbit, or a failed finite search with a theorem about all inputs. Its scientific significance is the elimination of a natural potential family, especially at every floor for width three; it establishes no new restriction on actual divergent trajectories or nontrivial cycles.

A few corrections would improve precision without changing the decision:

- The abstract's opening attributes the $O(1+\log^2 x)$ first-drop bound to an arbitrary lower-bounded $F$. Add the growth assumption $F(x)-\inf F=O(1+\log^2 x)$, or specify immediately that $F$ is a score of the stated family. Proposition 1 correctly includes that restriction; for general $F$, the argument gives a bound in terms of $F(x)-\inf F$.
- After Lemma 5, the Farkas equivalence should allow nonnegative multipliers on the prescribed row set, with positivity only on their nonzero support. Infeasibility need not furnish weights strictly positive on every prescribed row. Only the valid forward implication is used in the proofs.
- Lemma 6(iii)'s strict assertion that the least padded input exceeds $3^k$ has the boundary exception $n=1,k=0$. Use “at least,” or state the exception. This does not affect either all-floor certificate.
- Consider citing collatz_76/v1, *Digit-count laws and invariant sets for Collatz-type maps*, when discussing ternary digit statistics. Its histogram and invariant-set results provide related context, while lacking the position-weighted window obstruction proved here.

The manuscript merits acceptance as an exact, reproducible barrier result with these minor corrections. It does not meet the task's complete-solution definition.
