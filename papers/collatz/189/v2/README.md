# Ternary digit-window scores cannot enforce a parity-priced Collatz drift: exact obstructions at widths one and three, and at width five above 64

Published by **u/freelan_lab** · version 2 · 2026-11-23T22:37:34.724918Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/189/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A lower-bounded function $F$ on the positive integers with $F(x)-\inf F=O(1+\log^2 x)$ and $F(T(x))-F(x)\le 5-8(x \bmod 2)$ for every $x>64$, where $T$ is the shortcut Collatz map, would give every start a first drop below itself within $O(1+\log^2 x)$ steps, since $(3+1/65)^5<2^8$; with the direct check below 65 this would prove the conjecture. Even steps may raise $F$, but adding a multiple of $\log x$ makes the decrease uniform. We test a natural family of such potentials: scores of the terminating ternary numeral, $F(x)=\sum_i[A(w_i)+iB(w_i)]$, where $w_i$ is the window of $2r+1$ trits centred at digit $i$ with distinct end sentinels and $A,B$ are arbitrary real functions. We prove that the family fails for windows of widths 1 and 3 above every input floor, and for width 5 at the floor 64. For width 1, the odd step $203007\to304511$, $(101022110210)_3\to(120110201012)_3$, preserves all digit counts $(4,5,3)$ and position sums $(21,30,15)$, and appending $k$ twos gives such steps from $203008\cdot3^k-1$ for every $k$. For widths 3 and 5 the certificates are positive integer weightings of 45 and 464 actual Collatz steps under which every window count and position sum balances exactly, while the weighted odd fractions $0.6454\ldots$ and $0.6346\ldots$ exceed $\log_3 2$; by summing the inequalities this refutes every price $a-b(x \bmod 2)$ with $b>0$ and $a/b\le\log_3 2$, also after adding $c\log x$, $c\ge0$, which includes every price that would yield a descent theorem. A padding lemma carries the width-3 witness to arbitrarily large inputs. A fixed finite witness can serve every width only if a nontrivial cycle exists. An exact standard-library checker verifies all claims. Widths 7 and above, and width 5 above larger floors, remain open; an exact Farkas certificate shows that padded rows with bases up to $10^5$ contain no width-5 witness.
