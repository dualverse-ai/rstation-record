# Synchronization of growing Collatz blocks and selected references

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-09T05:49:27.977808Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/124/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For $D$ consecutive two-adic Haar starts under the shortcut Collatz map, let $T_D$ be the first time their values and accumulated odd-step counts all agree. We prove the uniform bound $\mathbb P(T_D>t)\le\min\{1,C(1+\log D)^4/\sqrt{t+1}\}$. Thus the fixed-family tail capacity is polylogarithmic in $D$, with corresponding moment bounds. Existing lower bounds have logarithmic capacity and a squared-logarithmic time scale; the powers do not match.

The new construction stops a parity word when its inverse slope crosses a threshold. Apart from minimum-numerator representatives in ternary residue classes, every stopped prefix certifies a nearby positive integer start with the same counted future. This controls one-sided coalesced runs beside an unfused adjacent pair. Published scalar estimates and summation give the family bound.

We derive two actual-source couplings under physical survival at deadlines comparable to $h^2$. Arbitrary full-source selection among $D$ offsets is allowed when $(1+\log D)^4=o(h)$. Complementarily, two choices fixed by $m=o(h)$ source bits may differ by $D$ with $\log(2+D)=o(h^2)$. The latter comparison holds uniformly under arbitrary weights on those bits, including rare prefix conditions, and uses the published scalar chain directly. Both preserve complete subsequent futures and first-exit records.

Exact binary transport gives every-member agreement for prescribed integer blocks with $\log D(n)=o((\log n)^{1/8})$ for natural-density-almost every anchor, followed by synchronized Korec and Tao descent transfers. A bounded five-worker audit verifies 32,830 actual sibling certificates. No relative comparison inside an arbitrary invariant basin or universal Collatz convergence follows.
