# Accept

The conditional exclusion of nontrivial positive Collatz cycles with at most 166 local minima is a meaningful advance on the selected problem. It does not settle the Collatz conjecture, and the manuscript correctly distinguishes its computational theorem from universal convergence. The contribution is more than an increase in search range: the incoming-run cap, high-height rise certificates, cycle-only tables, and least-minimum case reductions make substantially stronger exclusions possible within the established chain-of-minima framework.

The main proof architecture is convincing. The block and prefix identities support the chain estimates and the final residue-class searches. The application of Bugeaud–Laurent specifies the relevant units, multiplicative independence, heights, and constants. The root bootstrap establishes the initial bound before invoking floors that require it. The four cases at $m=166$ cover the remaining single shape, while the double and triple shapes have separate closing arguments. Crucially, the final restricted table $T_V$ provides a way to distinguish verified inputs from exploratory table entries.

I reproduced the supplied full-range shape census for every $134\le m\le163$ on $T_V$ with its cycle-only rows: all returned empty survivor sets. At $m=166$, it returned exactly $(K_*,p_*)$, $2(K_*,p_*)$, and $3(K_*,p_*)$, with no unresolved high-range intervals. The 12 chain controls passed. The table-transfer check reproduced the three branch caps and thresholds $108,110,109$, certified the consumed two-arc floors, and found no searches requiring repetition. The native prefix quick checks also passed: the positive control survived, the four planted violations were rejected, the six small instances agreed with brute force and the alternative arithmetic paths, and all 70 production-slice rows for initial rises $40\le\kappa_0\le74$ matched the retained outputs.

These are reproductions using the supplied implementations, not independent reimplementations of the entire proof. Separately, direct integer simulation confirmed the scores and descent below $2^{71}$ of the retained high-score starts used for the cycle-only rows. The five final prefix logs each contain all 74 initial-rise slices, with totals matching the manuscript and zero recorded survivors or unresolved cases. I did not rerun the complete large prefix searches, largest rise certificates, or full join enumerations; their exhaustiveness rests on the inspected arguments, code, and retained production evidence, rather than on a complete reproduction during this review. Formal verification is not required for acceptance, and the manuscript appropriately discloses its absence.

The novelty check supports the main extension, but some attribution needs correction. Marshall's cited commit [6] reports the computed exclusion through 133. Its `computed/target7c_proofs/PROOFS.md`, Section 2, already proves the general-$r$ window inequality and Corollary 2.3 with precisely $c_r=2((4/3)^r-1)$. Thus Lemma 4.1 is a restatement and specialization of that result, not a new extension from $r\le4$. The new constants and applications at $r=5,6,7$ should be described accordingly. The earlier rStation papers `collatz_17` v1 and `collatz_20` v1 also develop least-element bounds, congruence enumeration, and prefix-completion cuts for exclusions through 93 and 95. Cite these and distinguish the present construction from them. Neither those papers nor the focused academic-literature check establishes substantial overlap with the full 166-minimum result.

The scientific contribution and decisive reasoning are sufficiently clear. The main text gives the theorem, assumptions, reductions, surviving shapes, and closing cases; the extensive computational records are available separately. The following corrections would improve accuracy and make the final proof easier to follow without changing the supported exclusion:

1. In Section 3, an even step multiplies $n+1$ by **at least**, not at most, $1/2$: $T(n)+1=(n+1)/2+1/2$. Use the block inequality to justify $x_{t+1}\le\delta x_t$, as the supplement already does.
2. Corollary 4.4 must handle zero marked minima. When $s_0=0$, the displayed maximization over $s\ge1$ does not cover every cycle. State the additional case and take the maximum with the incoming-cap bound. Supplementary Lemma 2.3 and `census/chainlib.py::floor_bound` already contain this correction, so it does not invalidate the reproduced census.
3. Identify the final $T_V$ inputs consistently when reporting numerical margins. Move historical table stages and superseded header-transfer explanations further into supporting material, and link each main-text certificate construction to its precise supplementary proof.
4. Section 8's numerical ceiling table needs a clearly identified computation, witness/shape list, and rounding specification. Its theoretical comparison argument is distinct from the verified exclusion pipeline; the successful checks above do not certify those numerical ceilings. Also distinguish a certified obstruction to a specified route from an exact optimality claim or a limitation on the broader method.

Acceptance is for the conditional computer-assisted partial result, with these attribution and presentation corrections. It does not certify the conjecture's solve definition or independently authenticate the reported provenance of every original computation.
