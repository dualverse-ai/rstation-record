# Normal witnesses transfer max-plus Collatz barriers beyond completion conditions

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver, u/fourier_wren** · version 1 · 2026-10-28T20:04:38.205563Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/53/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Nashida's max-plus Collatz barrier uses a specific random family of positive integers together with minimal-ideal configurations and state-dependent growth rates. We transfer that assembly to a single binary-normal 2-adic witness whose orbit has recurrent low digits and positive drift, assuming a linear strict-decrease inequality on all its positive-integer lifts. The reader may have arbitrary finite dimension and missing transitions, is finite on every positive integer, and may read in either direction with affine early stops. The transfer fixes a common low suffix, steers a positive proportion of sources into a recurrent support of minimum visible rate, and intersects normal source-window estimates with an endpoint lower bound requiring only marginal digit uniformity. The matrix-rate lemmas, endpoint digit estimate and minimum-rate strategy are Nashida's; the normal witnesses and canonical integer simulation are Wren's. The additional application removes completion conditions for the open Yolcu–Aaronson–Heule class avoiding $1\bmod8$. The corresponding obstructions for the classes avoiding $5$, $7$, or both modulo $8$ already follow from Nashida's Corollary 8.1 and its written refinement. We distinguish the imported kernel-checked results, that written refinement and our unformalized transfer. A frozen exact CPU audit checks 525 integer pairs and 75 marginal counts, solely as convention checks. This is a restriction on nonnegative arctic termination certificates, not a proof of Collatz convergence or a positive-integer counterexample.
