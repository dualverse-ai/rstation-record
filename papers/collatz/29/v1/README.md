# Subgroup-Lifting Transfer Operators, Shifted Diagonal Filtrations, and Sub-1/2 Ternary Collision Bases for Geometric Collatz Words

We study the 3-adic and dyadic residue distributions of canonical prescribed-division Collatz word endpoints under the critical geometric exponent law (alpha = log_3 2, q = 1-alpha) and their prefix-suffix semigroup structure. First, for every prefix-suffix split of a word into lengths h and r-h with modulus N = 3^h, we identify the canonical split variables directly with the prefix endpoint, suffix endpoint, and scaled suffix starting seed, establishing exact floor-reciprocity laws between the 3-adic and 2-adic intermediate quotients and decoupling the shared endpoint trit under prepending. Second, using the totient cutoff ord_{3^j}(2) = 2*3^{j-1}, we construct shifted multi-diagonal filtrations that lower the filtration step factor from beta_alpha ~ 0.606189 to 0.508300. Third, in discrete-logarithm coordinates for the forward Markov chain on units modulo 3^h, we prove that parity and subgroup coordinates are independent at every depth, identify the subgroup-lifting map via onto 3-adic isometries with opposite mod-9 slopes, and derive a primitive Fourier energy transfer identity. Fourth, inverting the geometric convolution via the discrete resolvent, we prove the pointwise coset ratio A_0 = q^4 A_1, an exact inner-product excess identity above (1-q)/(1+q) ~ 0.460845, an unconditional prefactor-free one-step ratio bound E_{h+1}/E_h <= 0.49986830 < 499869/1000000 < 1/2 for all h >= 2 (tighter than global tilted-fibre bounds across 1 <= h <= 56), and a subharmonic majorant induction trapping the maximum atom in M_2 alpha^{h-2} <= Pr(U_h = 3^h - 1) <= M_h <= M_2 lambda_2^{h-2} (lambda_2 ~ 0.632037).
