# Additive-to-Multiplicative Parseval Isometry, Quarter-Plane Twisted Pascal Green's Functions, and m-Step Horizontal Plaquette Majorants for the Syracuse Characteristic Function

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-10-21T08:53:04.787817Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/11/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the $n$-step Syracuse random variable $\mathrm{Syrac}_n = \sum_{m=1}^n 3^{m-1} 2^{-s_m} \pmod{3^n}$ with characteristic function $\chi_n(\xi)$ and primitive mean square $E_n$, we establish four results on its fine-scale Fourier analysis and stopping-shell correlations. First, along the Hull--Dobell orbit $y_t = (4^t - 1)/3 \bmod 3^{n-1}$, we prove an exact Parseval isometry equating $\frac{2}{3} 3^{n-1} E_{n-1}$ to the primitive Fourier energy of $q_{n-1}(t) = \mathbb{P}(\mathrm{Syrac}_{n-1} \equiv y_t)$, giving $3^2 E_2 = 5/7$ and self-contained Cauchy--Schwarz bounds $E_n/E_{n-1} \ge 7/27$ ($n \ge 3$) and $E_n/E_{n-1} \ge 18581/69905$ ($n \ge 4$). Second, for $\tau_j = \min\{m \ge 1 : s_m > j\}$, we prove $\tau_j - 1 \sim \mathrm{Binomial}(j, 1/2)$; evaluated in exact rationals on the certified $\overline{|\chi_\ell(1)|}$ table ($\ell \le 1000$) of `collatz_2/v2`, this sharpens [2, Corollary 3] by $62.2\times$--$110.7\times$ and extends [2, Corollary 3] (with constant $40$) to $n \le 2394$ for $j \ge \max(0, 2n - 1001)$ via binomial reflection symmetry. In the coordinate $t_r = j - s_{n-r+1}$, the renewal kernels satisfy a Quarter-Plane Twisted Pascal PDE with a dual backward adjoint recurrence and a Skip-Free Cold-Triangle Defect Formula for $d_0 \le 0$. Third, unrolling $\chi_r(2^{-s}\xi)$ by $m$ horizontal steps with terminal boundary $1$ yields a nonnegative majorant hierarchy $\widetilde{\eta}_r^{(m)}(s; \xi) \ge |\chi_r(2^{-s}\xi)|$ whose continuum polynomial has circle-average $L^2(\mathbb{T})$ saddle rate $1/\sqrt{3} < \log_2(3/2)$ at slope $3/4$ by Tao's injectivity lemma, while an exact phase-transition formula $I_{\mathrm{eff}}(\rho)$ analyzes the constant-base model convolution $\mathcal{R}_n(\rho)$. Fourth, we prove exact top-shell cancellation $\Sigma_{\mathrm{diag}}^{(P)}(f) = \mathcal{B}_P(f) - \mathcal{Q}_P^-(f)$ and an unconditional $O(1/m)$ modular-injectivity bound on $\mathcal{Q}_P^-(f)$. These results do not establish universal Collatz convergence, deterministic plaquette re-pinning, or an all-$\xi$ exponential bound on $|\chi_n(\xi)|$.
