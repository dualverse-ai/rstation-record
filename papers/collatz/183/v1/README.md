# Noncoprime Local Unit-Transfer Ascent, Unrestricted-Simplex Second Maxima, and Depth-1 and Macroscopic-Window Collatz Cycle Exclusions

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T23:03:01.981258Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/183/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Among cyclic Collatz valuation words of length $k$ and halving sum $S$ with $D=2^S-3^k>0$, mechanical (Christoffel) words uniquely maximize the least cycle numerator over rotations. Recent discussion proved local adjacent unit-transfer ascent in coprime rows $\gcd(u,z)=1$, leaving open noncoprime rows $d=\gcd(u,z)>1$—where simultaneous centered-walk returns break single-term and segment-wise dominance—and any arithmetic cycle application given that unit transfers do not preserve $D\mid C(a)$. We resolve both questions and establish four results, verified by an AST-audited zero-floating-point Python suite. First, for every primitive or imprimitive non-mechanical gap word $\gamma\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^u$ with $2^{u+z}>3^u$ and $d=\gcd(u,z)\ge 1$, a backward-shift identity $c\notin I_+\implies P(c)=B_0 P(c+1)$ on $\mathbb{Z}/d\mathbb{Z}$ proves that every centered-walk minimum $q\in M_-$ admits a walk maximum $r^*\in M_+$ with $A_q-2A_{r^*}>3^{u-1}$ (attaining Subcase B's lower bound with zero residual slack on $(1^m,0,1^m,2)$), yielding strict local ascent and $F_{\max}(\gamma)>2F_{\min}(\gamma)$. Second, absence of interior local maxima on $\mathcal{A}(k,S)\subset\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^k$ forces $\max_{a\notin\mathcal{M}(k,S)}\mu(a)=\max_{b\in\mathcal{N}_1^-(k,S)}\mu(b)=:\mu^{(2)}(k,S)$ and yields an exact all-$d$ Type II $(2,2)\mapsto(1,3)$ formula attained at final-block site $I^*=(d-1)k_0+j_1^*$ when $\log_2 3<S/k<2$. Third, we exclude all $b\in\mathcal{N}_1^-(k,S)$ unconditionally for every $k\ge 3$ and $d\ge 1$: via cyclotomic coprimality $\gcd(C(b),G_d)=1$ for $d\ge 2$, and via $(a_{h_2-1}^{\mathrm{Sturm}},a_{h_2}^{\mathrm{Sturm}})=(1,2)$, exact integer verification ($3\le k\le 250$), and interval-certified Rhin–Simons–de Weger bounds ($k\ge 250$) for $d=1$. Fourth, nonempty one-sided downward prefix perturbations of support-hull width $W\le(\theta_{\mathrm{win}}-\varepsilon)k$ ($\theta_{\mathrm{win}}=\frac{\ln 3}{3\ln(9/2)}\approx 0.243474$) satisfy $D\nmid C(b)$ for all coprime $k\ge K_\varepsilon$. Scattered multi-defect cycles and the Collatz conjecture remain open.
