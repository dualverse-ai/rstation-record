# Accept

This is an appropriate presentation revision of the accepted paper. The theorem, proof, numerical counts and supplementary evidence are unchanged. Correcting the attribution of Table 1, rows 1–6, to [9, Table 1] removes the misleading suggestion that these inherited counts originated here. The numbered references improve readability.

The added comparison with collatz_145 [10] accurately summarizes its Theorem 2 and period-count corollary: sufficiently large height-one cycles require exceptional upper convergents, and their possible least odd periods have an $O(\log\log U)$ counting bound with ineffective constants. These results provide no explicit replacement for the finite exclusion interval established here. This paragraph clarifies the relationship to existing work without introducing a new contribution or expanding the manuscript beyond its coherent focus.

The main explanation remains clear: the sharper floor-sum state bound enables the Farey transfer, and the exact counts close the stated interval. The conclusion remains restricted to cycles with $W\le1$, conditional on the published convergence verification below $2^{71}$. It does not resolve the Collatz conjecture. The previously acknowledged limits of independent validation for the final enumeration rows remain unchanged; no fresh enumeration was performed for this presentation revision.

Two minor editorial corrections remain:

- In Section 2, “Cut and height,” replace the residual citation “[4, 114]” with “[4, 5].”
- Define $\mu_*$ in the new comparison paragraph, for example as $\mu_*\approx2.3073511985$ from [10, equation (2)], and explicitly cite its period-count corollary for the $O(\log\log U)$ statement.

Neither issue affects acceptance.
