# Accept

Accept this revision as a priority and scope correction to the accepted paper. It withdraws overstated novelty claims, credits earlier results, and identifies the remaining methodological contribution without adding new theorems or changing the accepted arguments. The resulting manuscript retains a coherent explanation: smoothing the starting distribution permits a rapid renewal estimate and a starting-scale-independent main term, which improve the comparison error in Tao’s iteration. The added comparisons do not turn it into an unfocused collection of results.

The correction concerning Nashida [12] is supported by the cited manuscript. Its Theorem 1.1 gives $E(X,N_0)\le KXN_0^{-c'}$ uniformly in the endpoint, with $c'\approx3.506\times10^{-6}$. This asymptotically supersedes the density bounds of Theorems 1 and 1′ and Corollary 2 here. Nashida’s Sections 3 and 7 nevertheless distinguish the fixed-power one-step comparison from a prospective stronger comparison estimate. Thus the stronger final density theorem does not itself subsume this paper’s smooth-start passage formula or its quantitative renewal argument. Retaining those arguments with corrected attribution is scientifically useful; a separate submission is unnecessary for this correction.

The comparison with Shaik [13] is also substantially accurate. Theorem 1.1 supplies the stated shell exceptional bound and simultaneous two-sided first-passage estimates for targets between the constructed landing and the source. It additionally controls the odd-step count, supporting conversion from the shortcut clock to the Syracuse clock with the same order of error. Its moving target range does not directly supply this manuscript’s fixed-$N_0$, endpoint-uniform exceptional estimate. Conversely, Theorem 7 does not supply Shaik’s full simultaneous trajectory and height conclusions. The revised acknowledgment of overlap, rather than priority for two-sided passage estimates, is appropriate. These comparisons are based on the cited mathematical statements and arguments; I did not independently replay their Lean formalizations.

Minor clarifications would improve the revised presentation:

- In the paragraph following Theorem 7, define $A_M$ and $\Delta_M$, or state the comparison without unexplained source notation. Briefly identify Shaik’s odd-step-count estimate as the justification for changing clocks; halving the leading constant alone would not justify the error bound.
- In Section 1’s proposed comparison with Nashida’s assembly, mention compatibility of starting distributions and the final conversion to counts as well as uniformity in $N_0$. Nashida’s Theorem B uses logarithmically weighted windows, whereas Proposition 6′ uses smooth dyadic starts. Keeping this as an unproved compatibility question is appropriate.
- Replace the retained phrases “new in this version” referring to version 2 results. Consolidate the version-history and novelty paragraphs into a present-tense account of the contribution, retaining a short priority correction. The abstract could likewise be shortened while preserving its explicit acknowledgment of superseded bounds.

The revision remains a partial result about exceptional densities and passage times. It does not establish convergence for every positive integer or otherwise meet the problem’s complete-solution definition.
