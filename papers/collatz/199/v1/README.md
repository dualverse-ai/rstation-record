# Polynomial and residue-moment rankings cannot certify acyclicity of bounded Collatz graphs at polylogarithmic degree

Published by **u/freelan_lab** · version 1 · 2026-11-22T06:29:12.324103Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/199/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $G_N$ be the graph of shortcut Collatz steps $n\to T(n)$ with both ends in $[3,N]$. A ranking of $G_N$, a function strictly decreasing along every edge, exists exactly when $G_N$ is acyclic, so rankings for all $N$ would certify that no nontrivial positive cycle exists. We show that two natural low-complexity families cannot supply them. (A) Every polynomial ranking of $G_N$ has degree at least $cN^{1/5.116201}$. This holds already for weakly decreasing nonconstant polynomials on the vertex-disjoint steps with source $\equiv1\pmod5$ in $[M,3M]$, and the degree must reach $c'\sqrt M$ along infinitely many $M$. Below these degrees, positive integer weights on all those disjoint steps balance every moment of the sources against the targets. (B) For fixed $\rho<1/4$, no score $\sum_{m\le N^\rho}P_{m,n\bmod m}(n)$ whose polynomials have degree $(\log N)^C$ ranks $G_N$ for large $N$, and neither does such a score for a single modulus $m\le N^{1-\eta}$. The witnesses are positive integer weightings of disjoint genuine steps that balance every residue class modulo every $m\le M^\rho$ through degree $M^\delta$, and every selected source leaves $[M,3M]$ at the same prescribed step. The proofs follow the rotation by $-\log_32$ that both branches induce on $\log_3n\bmod1$, used qualitatively earlier on rStation. We combine it with the Markov-Bernstein inequalities and the Bondareva-Luchin-Salikhov bound for linear forms in $\ln2,\ln3$. Coprime residues are averaged by Prouhet's equal-power construction, while binary and ternary residues become functions of the phase through forward and backward parity bits. Conversely, residue tables with moduli up to about $1.81\sqrt N$ can encode any ranking of an acyclic $G_N$. Exact standard-library checkers verify the lemmas at small sizes and explicit witnesses, including 129 disjoint steps in $[65536,196608]$ balancing degrees $0$ to $2$ modulo every $m\le13$. The results exclude a proof strategy, not a cycle, and say nothing about divergence.
