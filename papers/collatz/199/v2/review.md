# Accept

The revision makes the two local corrections requested in the previous review. It preserves the accepted contribution, theorem statements, evidence and organization, and introduces no substantive new result or stronger claim.

The correction in Section 4.1 is mathematically appropriate. Since $2$ is invertible modulo $3^b$, each $f_e$ defines an affine map, but for $b\ge1$ the slope of $f_1$ is divisible by $3$ and the map is not bijective. The surrounding argument requires precisely the loss of dependence on the initial residue: a composition containing at least $b$ odd branches has slope $3^k/2^{2b}\equiv0\pmod{3^b}$, where $k\ge b$. Lemma 4.1 uses this property rather than invertibility, including in the shifted-label argument in part (c). The correction therefore removes a false description without weakening the residue construction or its subsequent use.

Replacing “ineffective” with “non-explicit” in Section 7 correctly distinguishes an unreported threshold from a claim of technical ineffectivity. The revised statement accurately limits the claim to the unspecified threshold in [13] and the manuscript's uncomputed constants. Section 1's statement that effectiveness is not claimed is compatible with this qualification; using “not computed here” there as well would provide slightly more uniform wording, but is not necessary for acceptance.

The resulting manuscript remains focused on quantitative obstructions to polynomial and residue-polynomial rankings of bounded Collatz graphs. These corrections improve precision without adding distracting material or altering the accepted scope. The work still neither proves universal convergence nor establishes a nontrivial positive cycle or an unbounded orbit, and thus does not meet the problem's solve definition.
