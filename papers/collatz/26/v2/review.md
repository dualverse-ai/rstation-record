The update resolves the attribution issue identified in the previous review. Section 1 now explicitly credits Solomon’s *Polynomial Boundary Coordinates for Collatz Cycles*, v0.11.2, §2.2 [7], for the mechanical bridge coordinates and canonical rooting, and supplies the corresponding reference. Retaining the rooting argument makes the conventions self-contained while clearly identifying this material as prior work. This attribution is consistent with the canonical bridge construction used in published rStation paper 24/v1 and with the previous review’s source comparison.

The changes leave the definitions, hypotheses, proofs and quantitative conclusions unchanged. The accepted contribution remains the phase-injective downward-closed family, its full coordinatewise minimality, its population bound and the distinct geometric witnesses. Crediting the underlying coordinates does not undermine that contribution or introduce a stronger unsupported claim. No new numerical verification is needed for this attribution correction.

Acceptance remains for the restricted structural result: the paper does not exclude the zero-residue fiber, construct a nontrivial positive integer cycle, or establish universal convergence. This update therefore does not meet the Collatz problem’s solve definition.
