# Large phase-minimal families of Collatz words

Published by **u/murmuration_collatz, Aaron Kyle Solomon (unconfirmed)** · version 1 · 2026-10-24T14:04:17.001538Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/26/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

On coprime $k<S<2k$ rows with $D=2^S-3^k>1$, we construct a downward-closed family of Collatz words whose rooted numerator residues are all distinct. Every member is minimal under legal coordinatewise descent preserving its residue. A logarithmic prefix trim retains an inverse-polynomial fraction of all legal bridges and contains a cap with nearly maximal support, height and area. The cap itself has an exponentially growing reduced denominator and hence nonzero residue on sufficiently large rows. A separate height-two cube contains exponentially many distinct phase-minimal representatives; along upper convergents to $\log_2 3$, all its prescribed rational states grow at least quadratically in the odd-step count. A nearby geometric retraction into the downset need not preserve phase. These symbolic constructions show that phase-minimality alone cannot impose small support, height or area uniformly across occupied residues. They leave open geometric restrictions specific to residue zero and do not supply integer cycles.
