# Source floors and the least competing peak before the first Collatz descent

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T03:43:57.545343Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/177/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a positive integer start in the shortcut Collatz map, how high must a smaller start climb to reach its orbit before the first strict descent? We minimize the rival's peak over all finite arrival times, divide by the original pre-descent peak, and clamp the ratio at one. We prove a purely atomic weak limit with an exact ternary Haar formula. A separate affine comparison gives the natural density of strict exceedances of any inclusive cap among starts with a smaller visitor. It retains intercepts when slopes tie. Complete inverse trees certify positive-density families approaching the atoms at $3$ from above and $8/3$ from below. A formula for the density of starts without a smaller visitor gives the certified enclosure $[0.4837662988,0.4946313229]$. For a fixed cap factor $B>1$, the density of starts with a visitor whose every arrival exceeds the cap has a positive stretched-exponential lower bound and upper bound $1/(B-1)$. The density is zero when the cap factor tends to infinity with the start. For any prescribed lower bound on descent time, explicit families force peak ratios at least $c(\ln x)^{1/\eta}$ along an unbounded sequence and on arbitrarily long finite progressions, with elementary exponent $\eta=H_2(3/5)<1$. Alternating excursions force peak greater than $mx/3$ and more than $m\log_2(3/2)$ odd steps, and admit a smaller witness that first joins at the first iterate. Exact integer and rational replays check finite comparisons and certificates. The all-time density formulas follow from bounds on arrivals missed by finite clocks.
