# Joint Dual-Anchor Marked-Run Rigidity, Conditional Trough-Floor Base Collapse to $1^+$, and Bi-Adic Cylinder Realization Through Depth $9$ on Dimer Collatz Trajectories

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-21T15:58:17.723803Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/190/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Under the odd-accelerated Collatz map $T(z)=(3z+1)/2^{v_2(3z+1)}$, a dimer trajectory from odd $x_0 \ge 3$ concatenates circuits $(1,2)^{u_c}(2)^{v_c}$ ($u_c,v_c \ge 1$), realizing the $\{5,7\}\bmod 8$-avoidance dynamics equivalent to termination of Yolcu, Aaronson, and Heule's partial map $H$ and its $11$-rule rewriting system. Prior work excluded positive dimer cycles directly from eligible trough floors at base $1.82$ (forward) or $3.29$ (two-sided), near elementary propagator barriers. We establish four unconditional structural and conditional finiteness results, backed by an AST-audited zero-floating-point Python suite. First, coupling anchors $-5$ on dimer runs and $+1$ on standalone falls across unboundedly many circuits yields a subsum-nondegenerate integer identity proving $(\log_2 9)A+2B\le \log_2(x_0+5)+\log_2(9/8)U+o_{r,s}(\log_2(x_0+5)+U)$ and cycle concentration caps $5.3605\%$ and $20.7519\%$ (recovering the known $\log_8 9$ fixed-circuit excursion exponent as a credited corollary). Second, adapting Quantitative Subspace meshes to whole-circuit dimer windows yields start-independent exceptional dyadic dimer-count bins and $R^{1-o(1)}$ residual cycle dimers after sublogarithmic circuit deletions. Third, combining open-prefix slope-dip bounds with periodic-dictionary duration estimates collapses both critical exponential bases for conditional cycle finiteness to every $A>1$: $\mathrm{T}_1^e(A)$ forces bounded-horizon descent above $Q_*(A)$, excludes divergent dimer orbits, and yields a halting cycle search, whereas $\mathrm{T}_2^e(A)$ bounds $Q_{\min}\le Q_{*,2}(A)$ via an early/late slope-dip split. Fourth, unimodular minimal-shell coordinates give $Q_{\min}^e(L)<24\cdot 2^{S_{\min}(L)}$, Cramér–Legendre tilting proves $\Sigma_L<122.359^{-L}$, and branch-and-bound certifies $Q_{\min}^e(1\ldots 9)$, uncovering minimal-shell jumping at $L=8$. Unconditional trough floors and the Collatz conjecture remain open.
