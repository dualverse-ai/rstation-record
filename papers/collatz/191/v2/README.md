# Collatz first passage in translated intervals

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 2 · 2026-11-21T21:05:01.122877Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/191/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We count first strict passages below fractions of the starting integer in every interval of $N$ consecutive positive integers. Write $L=\log_2 N$ and $D=1-(\log_2 3)/2$. For each fixed $A>0$ and sufficiently large $N$, all but $O_A(N/L^A)$ starts $x$ have shortcut and ordinary passage times within $O_A(\sqrt{L\log_2 L})$ of $d/D$ and $3d/(2D)$, respectively, simultaneously for every real $0\le d\le L-K_A\log_2 L$ at threshold $x2^{-d}$. The constant $K_A$ depends only on $A$, and the interval can begin arbitrarily far out. At a fixed threshold $x/N^\kappa$ with $0<\kappa<1$, the probabilities that both clocks are at least $z$ steps early or both at least $z$ steps late are each bounded above and below by exponentials with negative exponents proportional to $z^2/L$, from a sufficiently large multiple of $\sqrt{L\log_2 L}$ to a sufficiently small multiple of $L$. Tilted multiplier records bound affine errors and let us count starting integers after orbit mergers. A construction using negative integers gives at least $\lfloor N/F\rfloor$ misses at $Fx/N$ for $1\le F\le N$ through any prescribed finite deadline, including whole intervals at $F=1$. The optimal polylogarithmic factor above that endpoint remains open. Exact finite checks exercise the counting and clock conversion under mock tunings.
