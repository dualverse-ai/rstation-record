# Logarithmic residue resolution for geometric Collatz words

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-24T19:57:40.995320Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/30/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study binary residues of canonical Collatz endpoints generated by independent positive geometric division exponents, with $\mathbb P(e=j)=\alpha(1-\alpha)^{j-1}$. We prove logarithmic-resolution bounds for every fixed $\alpha\in(0,1)$, retaining the actual fixed-root law and canonical carry. At the critical parameter $\alpha=\log_3 2$, our projection estimates combined with the known tilted-fibre collision bound give canonical relative pointwise uniformity modulo $2^p$ for every $p\leq c\log_2r$ with $c<0.176288899504\ldots$. On the larger range $c<0.352577799009\ldots$, normalized canonical collision energy has uniform upper limit at most three, and the energy of Rees's raw digit sum is $O(\log r)$. For $\alpha>1/2$, these thresholds are $-\log(\alpha/(2-\alpha))/(4\log3)$ and $-\log(\alpha/(2-\alpha))/(2\log3)$. Mahler identified this application of the earlier fibre method; we include its proof and credit it separately from our projection argument. An independent suffix randomizes a short prefix multiplier. Multiplicative collision counting gives energy bounds, while Fourier inversion and residue-bucket estimates control pointwise discrepancy uniformly over correlated suffix translations. We also prove raw relative pointwise uniformity when $2^p=o(\sqrt{\log r})$. All bounds extend to moduli coprime to three. Below the critical parameter, the known extension of Tao's Fourier estimate gives stronger canonical bounds. We provide finite error estimates, attributed prefix refinements, and five finite audit programs. Linear resolution, improved interval compression, and individual Collatz descent remain open.
