# Negative-Cycle Shadows, Non-Stationary Binary Splicing, and Analytic Obstructions to Collatz Predecessor Renewal

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-17T22:55:33.014670Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/162/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the accelerated Collatz map $U(x)=(3x+1)/2^{v_2(3x+1)}$, parity-trap shifts over $\{1,2,3,4\}$ recently crossed the $3/4$ survival rate ($\lambda_{\mu_2}(\mathcal{I})\ge 0.751095$), leaving open on the binary alphabet $\{1,2\}^*$ whether the continuum limit of dyadic slope shifts $\mathcal{A}_{\{1,2\}}(q,\beta_{\max})$ or any shift-invariant binary subshift can reach $3/4$, and how $2$-adic survival couples to the $3$-adic Syracuse density $\rho$. Using AST-audited zero-floating-point arithmetic over $\mathbb{Z}[1/2]\hookrightarrow\mathbb{Z}_3$, we establish four results. First, identifying $\beta(d,c)=(c+1)/2^d$ at trunk and Conway–Lagarias cycle points $-1,-5,-7$ yields a universal pullback step identity, exact unconstrained binary escape horizons ($r_{\min}^{\mathrm{le0}}=14$, $r_{\min}^{\mathrm{strict}}=16$), and a three-tier sub-$3/4$ impossibility hierarchy: $\sup_{q,\beta_{\max}}\lambda_{\mu_2}(\mathcal{A}_{\{1,2\}}(q,\beta_{\max}))\le 0.74791353$ (resolving Open Question 1a of collatz_152) and $\lambda_{\mu_2}(\Sigma)\le\rho_{\mu_2}(\Sigma_{\neg w_{22}})\le 0.74999998<3/4$ for every shift-invariant binary subshift surviving at any horizon $T\ge 22$ under any header $1^{M_0}$. Second, an automatic $(5/7,7/5)$ tube bound, a left-resolving SFT lower bound ($\theta_0>0.98735053$), and a zero-overshoot Ville martingale tail ($\ge 127/128$) build a non-stationary splicing architecture $1^{M_0}\cdot H\cdot U$ reducing $\lim_{L\to\infty}\mu_2(\mathcal{I}_L\cap\{1,2\}^L)^{1/L}=3/4$ unconditionally to $\limsup_{r\to\infty}Z_r^{\mathrm{coupled}}(x)^{1/r}<\theta_0$ ($Z_{10}(1/2)\approx 0.08252$, $Z_9^{\mathrm{coupled}}(11/30)\approx 0.02306$). Third, a telescoping collision identity $1+\sum_{r=1}^R Z_r^{(u)}(\sqrt{u})=\bigl(\frac{1+\sqrt{u}}{3(1-\sqrt{u})}\bigr)^R E_R^{(\sqrt{u})}$ bridges critical linear collision energy $E_R=\Theta(R)$ at $u=1/4$ via $Z_r^{(1/4)}(1/2)=E_r-E_{r-1}$, alongside an exact binary fiber-miss law. Fourth, we prove three unconditional analytic obstructions—weak-$L^2$ rearrangement ($\sqrt{32/27}>1$) and universal Hölder bases $>51/50=1.02$, multiplicative $\chi(4)$-phase-blindness ($\ge\frac{1}{4}U_r\ge 1/3$), and non-existence of continuous $3$-adic sub-invariant functions—while showing primitive additive $L^4$ conductor shells $\Delta_m$ break phase-blindness and contract to $\Delta_{10}<0.02769218$. The Collatz conjecture remains open.
