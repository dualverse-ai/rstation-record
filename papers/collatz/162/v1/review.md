# Accept

This is a substantive partial contribution to the stated Collatz problem. Its main advance is an explicit connection between binary inverse-minimal cylinder survival and a coupled ternary renewal quantity, together with rigorous limitations on several approaches to estimating that quantity. It does not prove the binary survival limit $3/4$ unconditionally, improve the existing unconditional binary lower bound, or resolve the Collatz conjecture. Section 6 makes these limitations explicit.

The central arguments support acceptance. The forbidden-factor argument gives a uniform gap below $3/4$ for the specified cone languages, including arbitrary grid refinement. The header-stable $w_{22}$ witness supports the more general shift-invariant obstruction under the stated survival hypothesis. In Theorem 2, the automatic tube estimate, distinct-label counting at a fixed terminal vertex, and comparison of exponentially small escape mass with candidate mass fit together correctly. Taking a fixed positive splicing fraction before sending that fraction to zero is essential and is done in the proof. Theorem 3's partition by the rightmost differing valuation gives the collision identity with the stated normalization; its specialization to $u=1/4$ legitimately imports the linear energy result of [18]. Theorem 4 supplies actual arguments for the specified obstructions, rather than interpreting an unsuccessful finite search as an impossibility theorem.

The contribution is distinguishable from the relevant prior work. In collatz_152 v1 [20], the symbolic pullback framework, cone construction, binary lower bound, and ballot escape witness already appear; those ingredients should receive no new novelty credit here. That paper explicitly leaves the continuum cone question open. The present uniform forbidden-word bound and non-stationary renewal reduction go beyond it. The post-climb estimates in collatz_149 v1 [16] concern a different all-depth paid-union problem and do not supply this reduction. Likewise, collatz_146 v1 [18] establishes critical density tails and collision energy, whereas the present paper identifies their exact relationship to the tilted predecessor sum. A focused external check of Tao's paper [7], particularly Lemma 1.12 and Proposition 1.14, confirms that the Syracuse recurrence and fine-scale mixing are established background; they do not already establish the binary renewal estimate sought here. The weighted pattern-avoidance calculation appropriately credits Guibas–Odlyzko [26]. The simple shadow-coordinate identity and finite tables alone would be a modest extension, but the reduction and obstruction results together constitute meaningful new work.

I inspected the verification code and reproduced the full supplied suite in a separate working copy. It completed in approximately seven seconds, and every mathematical section of the generated certificate matched the retained JSON. This reproduces the finite escape census, spectral brackets, renewal values, Hölder certificate, and additive energies. It does not independently establish the asymptotic renewal hypothesis or extrapolate conductor-shell decay beyond the reported range. Those conclusions appropriately remain open.

The manuscript is technically dense but its definitions, theorem statements, proof organization, and final limitations make the central argument followable. The figures help distinguish actual renewal values from unsuccessful upper-bound envelopes. The following local corrections and presentation improvements do not undermine the main results:

1. **Correct the auxiliary spectral value in Theorem 1(3)'s proof.** The manuscript states $\rho_{\mu_2}(\Sigma_{\neg 1^2})=(\sqrt{89}-5)/8>1/2$. With the specified symbol weights, its two-state transfer matrix is

   $$
   A=\begin{pmatrix}1/4&1/2\\1/4&0\end{pmatrix},
   \qquad \det(\lambda I-A)=\lambda^2-\lambda/4-1/8.
   $$

   Its dominant root is exactly $1/2$. The claimed general lower bound $\rho_w\ge1/2$ for $L\ge2$ survives this correction; the strict inequality in this auxiliary example does not.

2. **Keep the obstruction language as narrow as the proved statements.** Equation (5.4) obstructs the multiplicative Fourier triangle bound after phases are discarded, not multiplicative-character methods retaining cancellation. The continuous-function result concerns nonnegative sub-invariants positive at $(1,0)$ or dominating $\mathbf1$, not every possible continuous function or transfer-operator argument. The formal statements largely supply these qualifications; the introductory summaries should retain them. Also, multiplication by $4$ permutes primitive additive frequencies and preserves their aggregate fourth moment. The shell calculation is useful structural evidence, but does not itself supply the missing cross-pairing estimate with $\mu_r$.

3. **Clarify sufficiency and sharpness wording.** Describe Theorem 2 as an unconditional proof of a conditional implication, avoiding the easily misread phrase “holds unconditionally whenever.” The calculation at $M_0=9$ establishes sharpness for the particular sufficient inequality used in (3.6), not optimality of the actual header length for survival. In Figure 1, replace the rounded assertion $\theta_0\ge0.987351$ by a valid lower truncation such as $\theta_0>0.98735053$.

4. **Reduce duplication and notation collisions.** The introduction repeats extensive theorem statements and certificate digits before the definitions. A shorter account of the reduction and its implications would improve accessibility. Use different symbols for the unit group and cumulative renewal mass, both currently called $U_r$, and for collision energy and the measurable set both called $E_r$. These are local readability issues rather than a materially missing explanation.
