# Affine slices and additive expansion in Collatz trajectories

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T06:04:45.448272Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/165/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove uniform additive expansion for actual Collatz trajectories. Let $B$ consist of $n$ distinct consecutive positive shortcut-Collatz states. For a translated generalized arithmetic progression $P$ of rank at most $r$ and parameter volume at most $Cn$, we show that $\log n\ge(Kr)^{10}\delta^{-1}\log(2C/\delta)$ implies $|B\cap P|<\delta n$, with absolute $K$. Consequently the concentration at fixed rank and volume is $O_{r,C}(n\log\log n/\log n)$, and $E_+(B)\le C_0n^3/(\log n)^c$ for absolute $c>0$. Thus $|B+B|\ge c_0n(\log n)^c$. No source-height or selected-gap restriction is imposed. The new argument forms a rational space of bounded defects directly from the actual return edges, without requiring proper initial coordinates. Its transverse affine forms satisfy homogeneous return equations. Primitive normalization forces odd denominators, and a deep binary congruence either counts the remaining chain heads or places all of them in one lower-dimensional affine slice. Discrete John parametrization costs only a rank-dependent factor; the same equations restrict to successive slices without renewed word transfer. A terminal zero fibre has bounded physical diameter by an integer-minor estimate, so established interval sparsity finishes the proof. Classical BSG, weak polynomial Freiman--Ruzsa after a proper coordinate lift, and lattice-section bounds give the energy consequence. Prescribed exact CPU audits check the new oblique-slice, defect, normalization and determinant interfaces on frozen trajectories and declared controls. The asymptotic conclusions are analytic. No fixed-power energy saving in $n$, opposing structure estimate for a counterexample, or descent theorem follows. Collatz convergence remains open.
