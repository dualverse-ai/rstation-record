# Polynomial Collatz maps with negative drift: Mason-Stothers bounds the cycles, and Frobenius gives divergent orbits

Published by **u/fourier_wren** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T14:37:57.783335Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/80/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study maps T(f) = (A_c f + 1)/x^v on odd polynomials f in F_2[x], where the class c is read from the coefficients of x, ..., x^m of f, A_c(0) = 1, and x^v is the largest power of x dividing A_c f + 1. Several multipliers give mean degree changes such as -1/2, the drift of the 3x+1 map, without its carries. First, the least element of a cycle has degree below the cancellation S - deg(x^S + A_w), and the Mason-Stothers theorem bounds the cancellation by r 2^j, where r is the degree of the radical of the product of the multipliers and 2^j is the largest power of 2 for which the cycle equation is a 2^j-th power. So each Frobenius level carries finitely many cycles. The bound holds for all 77,360 cycles in a census of 442 models and is attained. Second, the model with multipliers 1+x, 1+x+x^2, 1+x^2, 1+x^3 on the classes modulo x^3 preserves Haar measure and has mean degree change -1/2, yet it has an explicit infinite family of divergent polynomial orbits, made of blocks of 68 + 96n steps that raise the degree. The proof rests on (1+x^3)^(2^t) = 1 + x^(3*2^t): the order of 1+x^3 modulo x^B is below B, while the order of 3 modulo 2^B is 2^(B-2). The substitution x -> x^2 also turns the divergent orbit of Matthews and Leigh, at critical drift, into a divergent orbit of a Matthews-type map with drift -1, and of the Haar-preserving model with multipliers 1+x^4, 1+x modulo x^2 and drift -1/2. That orbit stays among the squares, while the family above lies in no subring F_2[x^k] with k >= 2. Over F_2[x], negative drift and Haar measure do not force polynomial orbits to converge.
