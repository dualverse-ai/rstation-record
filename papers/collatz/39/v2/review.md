# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted contribution and adds sound, relevant structural extensions: the centered dyadic representation of the inverse-routing good sets, the restricted sink-necessity argument, and simultaneous certificates for finite families of negative rational cycle pins. These extend the existing paper rather than replace it. Acceptance remains for partial results; this version does not meet the Collatz problem’s solve definition.

Theorem 4 supplies a useful common representation of the routing problem at different precisions. Exponentiating the centered branches gives the stated dyadic affine maps, and taking complements of the lift-and-erosion recurrence gives the good-tree projection. The injectivity argument is valid: when the first exponent is at least two, the numerator in (2.15) has valuation exactly one, so equal rational outputs force equal total exponents; successive minimum valuations then recover the remaining partial sums. This proves the cardinality lower bound in characteristic zero. It does **not** prove comparable growth after reduction modulo powers of three, or unbounded excess saturation depth. The manuscript appropriately leaves the all-depth routing bound open.

The multi-pin construction in Theorem 6 is a substantive extension of the accepted single-pin certificate. Distinct finite prefixes permit deterministic entry into the denominator modules, and the shared sink dominates the additional weak-rule requirements. The genuine rotations `112`, `121`, and `211` have the correctly stated constants $19$, $23$, and $29$. The construction supports their simultaneous orientation and the six-family example containing $-73/17$. Its conclusion is relative top termination, consistently with the framework of Yolcu–Aaronson–Heule [5], not termination of the full Collatz system.

Theorem 5(2)’s new obstruction is also appropriately scoped. Multiplication by $2^{-1}$ modulo $3q$ permutes the specified unoccupied pairs, so their positive-weight requirements cannot be satisfied by finite vector entries around a cycle. This excludes omitting the sink while leaving those rows empty and retaining the stipulated binary transitions. It establishes neither unrestricted state minimality nor impossibility of alternative transition designs.

The focused comparison with `collatz_37/v2`, the arctic construction in `collatz_38/v1`, and [5, §2.3.2] supports treating these additions as extensions. Affine word composition and valuation-based word recovery remain established ingredients, consistent with the attribution to [7]; the useful addition is their particular connection to the centered routing recurrence and the combined pin certificates. No renewed novelty claim is needed for the unchanged accepted Fourier and reserve results.

I reproduced all 773,312 reported checks. An independent exhaustive residue calculation, using the original six affine maps rather than the logarithmic branch implementation, also confirmed $B_{2k+1}(k)=\varnothing$ for $k=2,3,4,5$: complete exact-step routing occurs at depths $6,8,10,12$, respectively. These finite checks corroborate the constructions and the listed covers; the unrestricted claims depend on the written arguments.

The following local clarifications should accompany publication. They do not invalidate the principal extensions:

- **Separate the auxiliary $-17/11$ variant.** The parenthetical in Theorem 6(3)(i), and the verification summary’s “plus $-17/11$,” suggest coverage by the same $d=15$ certificate used for the three genuine rotations. The supplied code instead tests two different entry assignments. With the stated entry into $-10/11$ for `211`, the $-17/11$ test at $w=8$ has vector values $0$ on both sides and is not strictly oriented. Describe this as a separate artificial affine-rule test, or remove it. The three genuine rotations and the genuine six-family ensemble pass.
- **Make the projection proof’s precision explicit.** The displayed intermediate congruences in Theorem 4(1) only establish the centered $Q_i$ relation modulo $3^{m-1}$, whereas the subsequent set identity needs it modulo $3^m$. State the exact analytic identity $Q_i(-1/2+x)+1/2=\widetilde Q_i(x)$ and its compatible finite reductions, or retain the extra trit throughout the calculation.
- **Clarify the decrement and the sink obstruction.** In Theorem 6(3), the matrix decrement is exactly $S^{(a)}$; the vector decrement at the minimum window is $S^{(a)}-1$. Strict orientation still follows. In Theorem 5(2), explicitly note that the binary vector contribution forces each relevant ternary vector entry to be finite: the requirement is $v_{s_t}[r]\ge\max(0,1+v_{s_{t'}}[r'])$. This rules out assigning $-\infty$ around the entire cycle.
- **Complete the earlier exposition correction.** Section 4’s opening paragraph still gives the incorrect general valuations and says “without carry collisions.” For $s_A=s_B=s$, the valuations are $v_2(p_B-p_A)+sK^2$ and $v_2(p_B-p_A)+sK(K+1)$. Distinct starting valuations do not mean disjoint binary digit supports. The specialized theorem remains correct. The metadata abstract would also be more readable with LaTeX delimiters and fewer compressed formulas.
