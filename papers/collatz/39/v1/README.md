# Bijective 3-Adic Step Maps for Inverse Collatz Routing, Universal Rational-Pin Arctic Automata, Two-Center 2-Adic Tschakaloff Decompositions, and Finite-Horizon Tilted Reserve Lambert Laws

We establish structural, algebraic, and harmonic-analytic results across four Collatz interfaces, accompanied by an exact integer and rational verification suite (763,812 checks, zero floating-point operations). First, for the 3-adic inverse-routing branch maps Q_0, Q_1 and cross-branch conjugacies Phi_d on Z/3^m Z, we prove that the cyclic step maps and inverse step maps are bijections from Z/3^{m-1} Z onto their mod-3^m cosets, establish the higher-order inverse difference valuation law k + v_3(k!) bounding contiguous cyclic runs, prove an affine homothety conjugacy in multiplicative coordinates 4^s with simultaneous fixed point s_* = -1/2 and multi-step extinction of all proper mod-3 coset unions under the inter-layer lift-and-erosion operator, and determine the complete 2D Fourier spectrum via leading-3-adic-coefficient linearity on 3-coset orbits, yielding O(m^2 3^{m/2}) single-step interval discrepancy. Second, for every odd denominator q > 1 coprime to 6, we construct a closed-form (q+1)-state rational-pin weak YAH arctic automaton whose shifted boundary rules form an acyclic (3/2, 3/4)-forest solved by 1 + v_2(m+q), and give d = 12 and d = 15 relative top-termination certificates orienting all windows w >= 8 for the -19/11 loop. Third, we decompose two-center non-stationary 2-adic schedules into disjoint quadratic Tschakaloff valuation progressions, realizing exact Syracuse words modulo 2^{2N(N+1)+1}. Fourth, we derive a closed-form finite-horizon rational Lambert sum for exponentially tilted macro-block reserves with certified interval enclosures at the critical Cramer zero-drift tilt. Iterable multi-layer O(k) routing bounds and the full Collatz conjecture remain open.
