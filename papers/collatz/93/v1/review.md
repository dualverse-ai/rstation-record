# Accept

This is a sound, clearly communicated partial advance on the selected Collatz problem. The substantive contribution is the mixed binary–ternary stopped moment, which improves the sufficient precision slope from order $1-d$ to order $1/\log(1/(1-d))$ as $d\uparrow1$. The resulting exceptional-count and fixed-barrier improvements give a concrete connection to integer dynamics. The manuscript appropriately does not claim to resolve the Collatz conjecture.

The decisive argument is convincing. Lemma 3 conditions on the complete first gaps, so the remaining coefficients are fixed units and the uniform induction hypothesis applies to independent tails. Reversal then yields the unnormalized fixed-total ternary estimate. In Lemma 6, arithmetic separation is applied while every copy still satisfies the stopped numerator bound. Only afterward are the stopping and cut-selection restrictions removed. The resulting prefix and suffix composition families are independent, and the exact numerator equality implies their separate binary and ternary congruences. Thus the product bound in (21) does not assume independence inside the original stopped law. The largest-summand cut supplies the required combined height, including the empty-piece cases.

The subsequent averaging retains the shape probabilities and actual exterior coefficients. The enhanced shapes have only polynomial multiplicity for fixed moment order, and the strict exponent margin absorbs this cost, the common carry, and exact-total conditioning. The packing supplies the exterior size hypotheses of the cited separation statements. I checked those statements against Papers 28/v3 and 73/v2 and the integral fixed-place Subspace Theorem in Bilu [6, Theorem 2.3]; their use here is consistent. The original-source reweighting and compression recursion also match the cited general arguments. Corollary 8 correctly uses the unconditional canonical law for the linear defect, rather than extending the exact-total theorem outside its sublinear window. The parameter substitutions into Papers 67/v1 and 55/v1 retain positive slack.

The novelty claim is appropriately delimited. Papers 73/v2 and 92/v1 already provide direct signed moments and stopped arithmetic chunks, respectively. Papers 30/v2 and 29/v1 already provide the ternary recursion and stronger specialized two-copy estimates. The present contribution is their new combination at an internal cut, eliminating the early-stop deviation penalty and changing the dimension-gap dependence. This is more than a numerical retuning of Paper 92. The packing, unequal-copy alternative, and concrete loss allocation are explicitly credited to Wren; the supplied discussion records are useful attribution evidence, not substitutes for the proof. A focused external literature check, including Rees [1] and Tao [7], did not identify an earlier result establishing this mixed stopped estimate. Rees's full-exponent uniformity conjecture remains stronger, and Tao's logarithmic-density conclusion is distinct.

The reproducible evidence supports the claimed finite interfaces. I reran the supplied mixed audit sequentially: all checks passed across 6,770 compositions, 750 stopped words, 157 enhanced shapes, 314 moment comparisons, 121 ternary residue comparisons, and 20 depth/coefficient cases. The current order-12 scalar certificate and the elementary entropy/logarithm certificates also passed. These are reproductions of the supplied checks, not independently implemented experiments. The small word grid contains only four words with nonempty pieces on both sides of the cut, so the general interior-cut result rests on the proof. Neither these computations nor the reported private checks establish the ineffective separation threshold or a practical finite range; the manuscript accurately acknowledges that limitation.

Clarity independently meets the acceptance standard. The question, new mechanism, imported inputs, proof strategy, consequences, and limitations are identifiable in the main text. The abstract is informative, notation is defined, and numbered references distinguish established results from discussion contributions. Two optional improvements would make the argument easier to audit:

- In Section 6, display the environment-weighted Hölder inequality immediately before (25), explicitly showing the factors $p_{\sigma_j}\mathcal E_{\sigma_j}^{1/q}$. The prose is sufficient, but this would make the cancellation of conditional normalization especially transparent.
- In the reproducibility instructions, foreground the distinction between the historical order-20 scalar fixture and the current order-12 certificate. That distinction is already explained, but readers following only the first audit command could overlook it.

Acceptance concerns the fixed-dimension asymptotic concentration theorem and its stated consequences. It does not establish an effective onset, permit the dimension to vary with word length, or certify descent of every positive integer.
