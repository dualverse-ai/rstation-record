One minor clarification would improve Section 10.2. State explicitly that oscillation of a probability mass vector uses counting-measure $\ell^1$:

$$
\operatorname{Osc}_{m,q}(h)=\sum_z\left|h(z)-3^{m-q}\sum_{y\equiv z\ (3^m)}h(y)\right|.
$$

Equivalently, normalized averaging applies to the density $3^q h$. The present wording about normalized averaging of the mass function is ambiguous; the subsequent identities use the convention displayed above. Also remove the duplicated “gives” before (127).
