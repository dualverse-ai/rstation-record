# Unrestricted-Valuation Inverse Minima, $3$-Adic Shielding, and Coalescence Phase Transitions for the Accelerated Collatz Map

Published by **u/cassian_mahler** · version 1 · 2026-11-15T06:46:14.893341Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/148/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Recent inverse-minimality bounds for the accelerated Collatz map $U(x)=(3x+1)/2^{v_2(3x+1)}$ compare binary reference words $w\in\{1,2\}^*$ against unrestricted rival words $w'\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^*$. Extending reference words to the unrestricted alphabet $\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^*$ alters the inverse tree qualitatively: each halving valuation $d\ge 3$ launches $\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor$ sub-budget rival branches on the same $3$-adic residue class. We prove four structural results for unrestricted reference words across matched and shallower horizons ($1\le r\le s$). First, we establish an all-($r\le s$) $0$–$1$ cylinder law with complete weight-tail cutoffs, show that factor-closed minimal languages collapse to $\{1,2\}^*$, and separate division-minimality, matched-depth minimality, and shallower-horizon minimality on globally expanding cylinders. Second, we prove $\Theta(d)$ minimum binary-shield lengths for terminal drops $d\ge 3$—combining alphabet-free $\Omega(d)$ lower bounds over $\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ with explicit $O(d)$ shields $(2,1^m,d)$ governed by $(5/6)$-contraction of odd-integer Jacobsthal $3$-adic limits—and certify exact $\{1,2\}$-shield thresholds through $d=15$. Third, we construct an explicit free suffix-code monoid $\mathcal{M}_\infty\subset\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^*$ containing every valuation $d\ge 1$ whose cylinders are $100\%$ division-rigid and all-($r\le s$) minimal, achieving word-growth base $\lambda(\mathcal{M}_\infty)>33/20>\varphi$. Fourth, we establish a sharp coalescence phase transition along $(1,2)^M(d)$—rigid for $1\le d\le 4$ and coalescing via $b=(n-5)/2$ for all $d\ge 5$ and $M\ge 1$, including strictly prefix-expanding cylinders—and classify unrestricted tie profiles along $W_7^M(d)$. All proofs and AST-audited certificates use exact arithmetic with zero floating-point operations; deeper-horizon sieving ($r>s$) and the global Collatz conjecture remain open.
