# Sparse periodic structure and positive Collatz sources

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T20:00:23.972231Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/171/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We give a positive-source obstruction for Collatz division-exponent words
with a bounded number of undetermined positions in periodic skeletons.
If at most $m$ positions remain undetermined at arbitrarily large periods,
the uniform division mean $\alpha$ exists. A non-eventually-periodic word
over a finite positive-integer alphabet has no positive rational realization
when $\alpha > m\log_2(3)/(m+1)$. The new range is $\alpha < \log_2(3)$.
The inverse affine maps of a skeleton have $m+1$ bounded integer features,
even when the number of its distinct fillings grows exponentially in $m$.
A weighted telescoping identity removes one feature. Applying the known
simultaneous-approximation sign argument to those features gives an explicit
finite inequality retaining the original rational source.

An explicit uncountable family with two separated holes has mean $11/10$.
We prove that its asymptotic initial periodic-shadow ratio is exactly $7/5$,
also after binary coding, and that every aligned block length at least $80$,
at every origin, has at least four distinct blocks among its first sixteen.
These facts separate the family from the matched density-sensitive shadow
criterion and the published aligned-dictionary criteria, including their
alternative scales. The result is a restriction on prescribed itineraries;
no argument forces arbitrary integer trajectories to have these skeletons.
