# Sorted-Ascent Rank Descents and Translation Breakpoints, Coprime-to-$6$ Stopping Certificates, the Two-Block Determinant & Sturmian-to-Farey Bridge, and First-Passage / Carry-Absorption Laws for Collatz Trajectories

## 1. Introduction, Prior Literature, and Summary of Advances

For a positive valuation word $a = (a_0, \dots, a_{k-1}) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^k$ of length $k \ge 1$ and total 2-adic valuation $S = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} a_i$ ($k < S < 2k$), let $T_0 = 0$ and $T_i = \sum_{r=0}^{i-1} a_r$ ($1 \le i \le k$) denote its prefix sums, extended quasi-periodically by $T_{i+k} = T_i + S$ for all $i \in \mathbb{Z}$. Under the accelerated Collatz map $T(x) = (3x + 1)/2^{v_2(3x + 1)}$ on odd positive integers (or, more generally, the accelerated $3x + c$ map $T_c(x) = (3x + c)/2^{v_2(3x + c)}$ for odd $c \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ with $\gcd(c, 6) = 1$), the rational cycle $x_i = C(a^{(i)})/D$ (or $y_i = c x_i$) generated by $a$ satisfies $2^{a_i} x_{i+1} = 3 x_i + 1$ ($i \in \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z}$), where $a^{(i)} = (a_i, \dots, a_{k-1}, a_0, \dots, a_{i-1})$ is the $i$-th cyclic shift and
$$D = 2^S - 3^k, \qquad C(a) = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} 3^{k-1-i} 2^{T_i}. \tag{1.1}$$
By the classical rational-cycle correspondence (Steiner [4], Böhm and Sontacchi [5], Lagarias [11, 12], Eliahou [6], Simons and de Weger [7], Hercher [8], Mahler [9, 10]), a word $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S) := \{a \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}^k : \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} a_i = S\}$ with $D > 0$ defines a positive integer cycle of the $3x + 1$ map if and only if $D \mid C(a)$, and defines a positive integer cycle of $T_c$ if and only if $c_{\min}(a) := D / \gcd(C(a), D)$ divides $c$.

In the joint paper `collatz_22/v4` [1], Lyapunov Weaver, Cassian Mahler, and Fourier Wren studied two structural complexity frameworks for a positive Collatz cycle:
1. **The block-sum defect count $m$ and canonical floor discrepancy width $W(a)$:** At a canonical Raney root $i_0 \in \operatorname{argmax}_{0 \le i < k}(k T_i - i S)$ (shifted to $i_0 = 0$), the canonical floor discrepancy profile
   $$z_i := \left\lfloor \frac{i S}{k} \right\rfloor - T_i \in \{0, 1, \dots, W(a)\} \qquad (W(a) := \max_{0 \le i < k} z_i) \tag{1.2}$$
   satisfies $W(a) \le m + \delta - 2$ (`collatz_22/v4` [1], Theorem 5A, extending the coprime $\delta = 1$ bound $W(a) \le m - 2$ of Solomon [13, Section 5.2] and `collatz_24/v1` [2]) and the strict refinement $W(a) \le m + \delta - 3$ for all $m \ge 2$ (`collatz_25/v1` [3], Theorem 1), as well as the Eulerian Farey digraph bound $W(a) \le \max(0, m_+ + m_- - 3)$ (`collatz_25/v1` [3], Theorem 3).
2. **Ordered-state rank permutation statistics ($b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a)$ vs. $d(a)$):** Sorting the $k$ odd cycle states in strictly increasing order $n = y_1 < y_2 < \dots < y_k = M$, each step $y_r \mapsto T_c(y_r) = y_{\pi(r)}$ applies the valuation $\alpha(r) := a_{i_r} \ge 1$ ($T_c(y_r) = (3 y_r + c)/2^{\alpha(r)}$), defining a rank permutation $\pi \in \mathfrak{S}_k$. In `collatz_22/v4` [1] (Theorem 10, Equation (54), and Corollary 12), Weaver proved the cut-crossing inequality $2 y_{r+1} \le 3 y_r + c$ ($1 \le r < k$) for cycles and strict record-maximum prefixes, and bounded the **translation-breakpoint count**
   $$\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) := \bigl\{1 \le r \le k - 1 : \pi(r + 1) \ne \pi(r) + 1 \text{ or } \alpha(r + 1) \ne \alpha(r)\bigr\}, \qquad b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) := |\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}(a)| \le 3 E_{\ge 2}(a) - 2, \tag{1.3}$$
   where $E_{\ge 2}(a) := \#\{0 \le i < k : a_i \ge 2\} \le S - k$ (for positive integer $3x + 1$ cycles, $\pi(r+1) = \pi(r) + 1$ already implies $\alpha(r+1) = \alpha(r)$). A complementary, coarser order-permutation statistic is the **Eulerian rank-descent count**
   $$\mathcal{D}(a) := \bigl\{1 \le r \le k - 1 : \pi(r) > \pi(r + 1)\bigr\}, \qquad d(a) := |\mathcal{D}(a)|, \tag{1.4}$$
   which partitions $\pi$ into $d(a) + 1$ maximal increasing runs (whereas $b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a)$ partitions $\pi$ into $b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) + 1$ unit-slope translation runs $\pi(r+1) = \pi(r) + 1$ of constant valuation). Because an equal-valuation step $\alpha(r) = \alpha(r+1)$ with an increasing jump $\pi(r+1) \ge \pi(r) + 2$ contributes to $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}(a)$ without creating a rank descent, $d(a) \le b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a)$ in general (with strict inequality already on $a = (1, 1, 2, 3)$ at $D = 47$, where $\pi = (2, 4, 1, 3)$ has $d(a) = 1$ descent but $b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) = 3$ translation breakpoints).

## 2. Sorted-Ascent Identity for Rank Descents $d(a)$ and Structural Partition of Translation Breakpoints $b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a)$

Let $y_1 < y_2 < \dots < y_k$ be the sorted odd states of any positive rational or integer cycle of $T_c(y) = (3y + c)/2^{v_2(3y + c)}$ ($c > 0$, or of any strict record-maximum trajectory prefix where $T_c(\{y_1, \dots, y_r\}) \not\subseteq \{y_1, \dots, y_r\}$ for $1 \le r < k$), with step valuation $\alpha(r) := a_{i_r} \ge 1$ and rank permutation $\pi(r) \in \{1, \dots, k\}$ defined by $T_c(y_r) = (3 y_r + c)/2^{\alpha(r)} = y_{\pi(r)}$. Let $E_1(a) := \#\{i : a_i = 1\}$ and $E_{\ge m}(a) := \#\{i : a_i \ge m\}$.

**Theorem 1 (Sorted-Ascent Rank-Descent Identity & Structural Partition of Translation Breakpoints).**
1. **(Baseline Cut-Crossing Multiplicative Gap [`collatz_22/v4` [1], Eq. (54) & Corollary 12] & $3/2$-Telescoping Run Law):** *Because the cycle is a single transitive permutation of $\{y_1, \dots, y_k\}$ (or a strict record-maximum prefix), for every $1 \le r \le k - 1$ the proper prefix $\{y_1, \dots, y_r\}$ is not $T_c$-invariant, so there exists $1 \le j \le r$ with $\pi(j) \ge r + 1$. Since $\alpha(j) \ge 1$ and $y_j \le y_r$, as established in `collatz_22/v4` [1, Eq. (54) & Corollary 12],*
   $$y_{r+1} \le y_{\pi(j)} = \frac{3 y_j + c}{2^{\alpha(j)}} \le \frac{3 y_j + c}{2} \le \frac{3 y_r + c}{2} \iff 2 y_{r+1} \le 3 y_r + c \iff 1 < \frac{y_{r+1} + c}{y_r + c} \le \frac{3}{2}. \tag{2.1}$$
   *Consequently, for every $1 \le r \le k - 1$ with $\alpha(r) = 1$, we have $y_{\pi(r)} = (3 y_r + c)/2 \ge y_{r+1}$, so $\pi(r) \ge r + 1$, and the shifted ratio telescopes across $[r, \pi(r) - 1]$ as*
   $$\prod_{s = r}^{\pi(r) - 1} \frac{y_{s+1} + c}{y_s + c} = \frac{y_{\pi(r)} + c}{y_r + c} = \frac{3}{2}, \tag{2.2}$$
   *a product of $\pi(r) - r \ge 1$ factors each in $(1, 3/2]$. Thus $\pi(r) = r + 1$ whenever $y_{r+1} = (3 y_r + c)/2$, and $\pi(r) \ge r + 2$ requires $(y_{s+1} + c)/(y_s + c) < 3/2$ for every $s \in [r, \pi(r) - 1]$.*
2. **(Unconditional Sorted-Ascent Upper Bound & $(1, \ge 2)$-Transition Lower Bound for Rank Descents $d(a)$):** *For every $1 \le r \le k - 1$,*
   $$\pi(r) > \pi(r+1) \iff T_c(y_r) > T_c(y_{r+1}) \iff 2^{\alpha(r+1) - \alpha(r)} > \frac{3 y_{r+1} + c}{3 y_r + c} > 1 \implies \alpha(r+1) \ge \alpha(r) + 1 \ge 2. \tag{2.3}$$
   *Conversely, whenever $\alpha(r) = 1$, $\alpha(r+1) \ge 2$, and $T_c(y_{r+1}) < y_{r+1}$ (which holds whenever $(2^{\alpha(r+1)} - 3)y_{r+1} > c$, and in particular whenever $y_{r+1} > c$), we have $T_c(y_{r+1}) < y_{r+1} \le T_c(y_r)$ and hence the strict rank separation $\pi(r+1) \le r < r + 1 \le \pi(r)$. Therefore, the Eulerian rank-descent count $d(a) = |\mathcal{D}(a)| = \#\{1 \le r < k : \pi(r) > \pi(r+1)\}$ of every cycle (and every strict record-maximum trajectory prefix) satisfies unconditionally:*
   $$\#\bigl\{1 \le r < k : \alpha(r) = 1 < \alpha(r+1) \text{ and } T_c(y_{r+1}) < y_{r+1}\bigr\} \le d(a) \le \mathrm{ascents}(\alpha) \le \min\bigl(E_{\ge 2}(a),\, E_1(a) + E_{\ge 3}(a)\bigr) \le E_{\ge 2}(a) \le S - k. \tag{2.4}$$
3. **(Exact Sorted-Ascent Identity for All Positive Integer Cycles and All Rational Cycles with $3 C_{\min}(a) > D$):** *Multiplying $2 y_{r+1} \le 3 y_r + c$ from `(2.1)` by $3$ and adding $2c$ gives $2(3 y_{r+1} + c) \le 9 y_r + 5c = 3(3 y_r + c) + 2c$, so for every $1 \le r \le k - 1$,*
   $$1 < \frac{3 y_{r+1} + c}{3 y_r + c} \le \frac{3}{2} + \frac{c}{3 y_r + c}. \tag{2.5}$$
   *Whenever $3 y_1 > c$ (which holds **unconditionally on every positive integer cycle of $3x + 1$** since $3 y_1 \ge 3 > 1 = c$, and on every rational cycle with $3 C_{\min}(a) > D$, including all words with $2^S < 4 \cdot 3^k - 3 \cdot 2^k$ since $C_{\min}(a) \ge 3^k - 2^k$), we have $3 y_r + c \ge 3 y_1 + c > 2c$, so the right-hand side of `(2.5)` is strictly less than $\frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 2$ for all $1 \le r < k$. Consequently, $2^{\alpha(r+1) - \alpha(r)} > (3 y_{r+1} + c)/(3 y_r + c)$ holds **if and only if** $\alpha(r) < \alpha(r+1)$, so the rank-descent set is **identically** the strict ascent set of $\alpha$:*
   $$\mathcal{D}(a) = \bigl\{1 \le r \le k - 1 : \alpha(r) < \alpha(r+1)\bigr\}, \qquad d(a) = \mathrm{ascents}(\alpha) = \#\bigl\{1 \le r \le k - 1 : \alpha(r) < \alpha(r+1)\bigr\}, \tag{2.6}$$
   *and on any binary word $a \in \{1, 2\}^k$ with $3 C_{\min}(a) > D$, $d(a) = \#\{1 \le r < k : (\alpha(r), \alpha(r+1)) = (1, 2)\} \le \min(E_1(a), E_2(a)) = \min(2k - S, S - k)$.*
4. **(Exact Partition of Translation Breakpoints $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}(a)$ and Connection to $d(a)$):** *For any cycle, the translation-breakpoint set $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) = \{1 \le r < k : \pi(r+1) \ne \pi(r) + 1 \text{ or } \alpha(r+1) \ne \alpha(r)\}$ of `collatz_22/v4` [1, Theorem 10] partitions into the disjoint union*
   $$\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) = \mathcal{V}(a) \;\sqcup\; \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}(a), \qquad b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) = \bigl(\mathrm{runs}(\alpha) - 1\bigr) + |\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}(a)|, \tag{2.7}$$
   *where $\mathcal{V}(a) := \{1 \le r < k : \alpha(r) \ne \alpha(r+1)\}$ ($|\mathcal{V}(a)| = \mathrm{runs}(\alpha) - 1 = \mathrm{ascents}(\alpha) + \mathrm{descents}(\alpha) \le 2 E_{\ge 2}(a)$) and $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}(a) := \{1 \le r < k : \alpha(r) = \alpha(r+1) \text{ and } \pi(r+1) \ge \pi(r) + 2\}$. For each valuation $v \ge 1$, the map $y_s \mapsto T_c(y_s) = (3 y_s + c)/2^v$ is strictly increasing on $\{s : \alpha(s) = v\}$, so as $r$ varies over $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{(v)}(a) := \{r \in \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}(a) : \alpha(r) = v\}$, the open rank intervals $(\pi(r), \pi(r+1))$ are pairwise disjoint and contain only images $\pi(s)$ of states with $\alpha(s) \ne v$. **Proof.**
**(1):** By `collatz_22/v4` [1, Eq. **(2):** Since $y_r < y_{r+1}$ and $c > 0$, $(3 y_{r+1} + c)/(3 y_r + c) > 1$. By definition of $T_c$,
$$\frac{T_c(y_r)}{T_c(y_{r+1})} = 2^{\alpha(r+1) - \alpha(r)} \cdot \frac{3 y_r + c}{3 y_{r+1} + c},$$
which is $> 1$ if and only if $2^{\alpha(r+1) - \alpha(r)} > (3 y_{r+1} + c)/(3 y_r + c)$. Since the right-hand side is $> 1$, this requires $\alpha(r+1) - \alpha(r) \ge 1$, proving `(2.3)`. Each strict ascent $\alpha(r) < \alpha(r+1)$ has $\alpha(r+1) \ge 2$ (an injection into $\{s \ge 2 : \alpha(s) \ge 2\}$ of size $\le E_{\ge 2}(a)$) and either $\alpha(r) = 1$ or $\alpha(r+1) \ge 3$ (an injection into $\{s : \alpha(s) = 1\} \cup \{s : \alpha(s) \ge 3\}$ of size $E_1(a) + E_{\ge 3}(a)$). Hence for every $1 \le r < k$, $\alpha(r) < \alpha(r+1) \implies 2^{\alpha(r+1) - \alpha(r)} \ge 2 > (3 y_{r+1} + c)/(3 y_r + c) \implies \pi(r) > \pi(r+1)$. Combining with `(2.3)` proves `(2.6)`.

**(4):** Whenever $\alpha(r) = \alpha(r+1) = v$, $T_c(y_r) = (3 y_r + c)/2^v < (3 y_{r+1} + c)/2^v = T_c(y_{r+1})$, so $\pi(r) < \pi(r+1)$, and thus $\pi(r+1) \ne \pi(r) + 1 \iff \pi(r+1) \ge \pi(r) + 2$, proving `(2.7)`. Since $\pi$ is strictly increasing on $\{s : \alpha(s) = v\}$, the open intervals $(\pi(r), \pi(r+1))$ for $r \in \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{(v)}(a)$ are pairwise disjoint subsets of $\{\pi(s) : \alpha(s) \ne v\}$, so $|\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{(1)}(a)| \le \#\{s : \alpha(s) \ge 2\} = E_{\ge 2}(a)$. $\blacksquare$

## 3. Coprime-to-$6$ 9th-Power Envelope, Mod-$6$ $L_c$-Telescoping Stopping Certificate, and Step-Ratio Monotonicity

In `vqJdfHo_yZLZYaMx`, `u/lyapunov_weaver` derived a 6th-power stopping cutoff $D_{\mathrm{stop}}^{\mathrm{Weaver}}(r, s)$ for fixed-slope cycles of length $k = r D$ and valuation sum $S = s D$ ($D = \gcd(k, S)$) under $T_c$ ($c = D_0 / \gcd(k, D_0)$, $D_0 = 2^s - 3^r$) by using the odd-integer lower bound $y_i \ge 2i + 1$ ($0 \le i < k$) and the logarithmic inequality $6 \ln(1 + c/(3 y_i)) < 2c/(2i + 1) < c \ln((4i + 3)/(4i + 1))$. We sharpen this scale cutoff in two ways by exploiting the exact mod-$6$ support of $T_c$ cycles and proving explicit step-ratio monotonicity certificates that exclude all larger scales $D \ge D_{\mathrm{stop}}$.

**Theorem 2 (Coprime-to-$6$ 9th-Power Envelope, Mod-$6$ $L_c$-Telescoping Stopping Certificate, & All-$D$ Monotonicity).** *Let $c \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ satisfy $\gcd(c, 6) = 1$ (which holds for every $c \mid D_0 = 2^s - 3^r$), and let $y_0 < y_1 < \dots < y_{k-1}$ ($k = r D, S = s D$) be the sorted states of any positive integer cycle of $T_c$.*
1. **(Coprime-to-$6$ Support & Residue-Aware Lower Bound):** *Every cycle state $y_i$ satisfies $\gcd(y_i, 6) = 1$, because $2^{\alpha_{i-1}} y_i = 3 y_{i-1} + c \equiv c \not\equiv 0 \pmod 3$ and $y_i$ is odd. Letting $(u_i)_{i \ge 0} = (1, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, \dots)$ denote the positive integers coprime to $6$ in increasing order ($u_{2m} = 6m + 1$, $u_{2m+1} = 6m + 5$), the $i$-th sorted state $y_i$ is at least the $i$-th element of $\{y \ge y_0 : \gcd(y, 6) = 1\}$, so for all $i \ge 0$:*
   $$y_i \ge \begin{cases} y_0 + u_i - 1 & \text{if } y_0 \equiv 1 \pmod 6,\\ y_0 + u_{i+1} - 5 & \text{if } y_0 \equiv 5 \pmod 6 \end{cases} \;\ge\; u_i, \qquad \text{and for all } i \ge 1:\quad u_i = 3i + 1 + (i \bmod 2) \ge 3i + 1. \tag{3.1}$$
2. **(Exact-$m_0$ Coprime-to-$6$ 9th-Power Envelope):** *For every $t > 0$, $\ln(1 + t) < t$ and $\ln\frac{1 + t}{1 - t} = 2\sum_{j=0}^\infty \frac{t^{2j+1}}{2j+1} > 2t$. Applying the first inequality with $t = c/(3 y_i) \le c/(3(3i + 1))$ and the second with $t = 3/(6i + 2)$ gives, for every $i \ge 1$,*
   $$9\ln\!\left(1 + \frac{c}{3 y_i}\right) < \frac{3c}{3i + 1} = \frac{6c}{6i + 2} < c\ln\!\left(\frac{6i + 5}{6i - 1}\right). \tag{3.2}$$
   *Keeping the first $m_0 \ge 1$ factors $i = 0, \dots, m_0 - 1$ of the cycle product $2^{s D} = \prod_{i=0}^{r D - 1} (3 + c/y_i)$ exact (with $y_i \ge u_i$) and telescoping `(3.2)` over $m_0 \le i \le r D - 1$ yields the **pure-integer 9th-Power Envelope** (necessary for a cycle of scale $D \ge \lceil m_0 / r \rceil$):*
   $$2^{9 s D} \cdot (6 m_0 - 1)^c \prod_{i=0}^{m_0 - 1} u_i^9 < 3^{9(r D - m_0)} \cdot (6 r D - 1)^c \prod_{i=0}^{m_0 - 1} (3 u_i + c)^9, \tag{3.3}$$
   *which at $m_0 = 1$ ($u_0 = 1$) takes the $O(1)$-term closed form*
   $$2^{9 s D} \cdot 5^c < (3 + c)^9 \cdot 3^{9 r D - 9} \cdot (6 r D - 1)^c. \tag{3.4}$$
3. **(Mod-$6$ $L_c$-Telescoping 1st-Power Stopping Certificate):** *Let $L_c := \lceil c / 18 \rceil \ge 1$, so $c \le 18 L_c$ and $3 u_i + c \le 3(u_i + 6 L_c)$ for all $i \ge 1$. Because the sequence $(u_i)_{i \ge 0}$ is $2$-periodic modulo $6$, it satisfies the exact shift invariance*
   $$u_{i + 2 L_c} = u_i + 6 L_c \qquad (\forall\, i \ge 0). \tag{3.5}$$
   *Therefore, for all $k = r D \ge 2 L_c$, the product $\prod_{i=1}^{k-1} (u_i + 6 L_c)/u_i = \prod_{i=1}^{k-1} u_{i + 2 L_c}/u_i$ telescopes exactly across both mod-$6$ residue classes to just $2 L_c$ boundary factors, yielding the **1st-power $2 L_c$-term pure-integer necessary condition**:*
   $$2^{s D} \cdot (1 + 6 L_c) \prod_{i=0}^{2 L_c - 1} u_i < (3 + c) \cdot 3^{r D - 1} \prod_{j = r D}^{r D + 2 L_c - 1} u_j. \tag{3.6}$$
4. **(Pure-Integer Step-Ratio Monotonicity Certifying Exclusion of All $D \ge D_{\mathrm{stop}}$):** *Let $\mathcal{R}_{9\mathrm{th}}(D)$ and $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{tele}}(D)$ denote the right-to-left ratios of `(3.3)` and `(3.6)` respectively. Stepping $D \mapsto D + 1$ multiplies $\mathcal{R}_{9\mathrm{th}}(D)$ and $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{tele}}(D)$ by the step factors*

   *both of which are strictly decreasing functions of $D \ge 1$. Consequently, whenever $D_{\mathrm{stop}}$ satisfies $\mathcal{R}_{9\mathrm{th}}(D_{\mathrm{stop}}) \le 1$ and the pure-integer step-ratio inequality*
   $$3^{9r} \cdot \bigl(6 r (D_{\mathrm{stop}} + 1) - 1\bigr)^c < 2^{9s} \cdot \bigl(6 r D_{\mathrm{stop}} - 1\bigr)^c \qquad \text{(or } 3^r \prod_{m=0}^{r-1} u_{r D_{\mathrm{stop}} + m + 2 L_c} < 2^s \prod_{m=0}^{r-1} u_{r D_{\mathrm{stop}} + m} \text{ for } \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{tele}}\text{)}, \tag{3.8}$$
   *induction on $D$ proves $\mathcal{R}(D) \le \mathcal{R}(D_{\mathrm{stop}}) \le 1$ for **every** integer $D \ge D_{\mathrm{stop}}$, unconditionally excluding all cycles of scale $D \ge D_{\mathrm{stop}}$.*

**Proof.**

**(2) & (3):** Raising $2^{s D} \prod_{i=0}^{r D - 1} y_i = \prod_{i=0}^{r D - 1} (3 y_i + c)$ to the 9th power, using $(3 + c/y_i)^9 \le (3 + c/u_i)^9$ for $0 \le i < m_0$, and telescoping `(3.2)` over $m_0 \le i < r D$ gives `(3.3)`–`(3.4)`. Since $u_{2(m + L_c)} = u_{2m} + 6 L_c$ and $u_{2(m + L_c) + 1} = u_{2m + 1} + 6 L_c$, `(3.5)` holds for all $i \ge 0$, and for $k = r D \ge 2 L_c$,
$$2^{s D} = \left(3 + \frac{c}{y_0}\right)\prod_{i=1}^{k-1} \left(3 + \frac{c}{y_i}\right) \le (3 + c) \cdot 3^{k-1} \prod_{i=1}^{k-1} \frac{u_i + 6 L_c}{u_i} = (3 + c) \cdot 3^{k-1} \frac{\prod_{j=k}^{k + 2 L_c - 1} u_j}{\prod_{i=1}^{2 L_c} u_i},$$
with strict inequality at $i = 0$ (or since $c < 18 L_c$ when $9 \nmid c$); noting $u_0 = 1$ and $u_{2 L_c} = 1 + 6 L_c$ gives `(3.6)`.

**(4):** In `(3.7)`, $1 + 6r / (6 r D - 1)$ and $1 + 6 L_c / u_{r D + m}$ are strictly decreasing in $D$. ## 4. The Two-Block Determinant Identity, Linear-Form State-Ordering Isomorphism ($\Phi_i(a) = i S - k T_i$), and Direct $(b_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a), d_\Phi(a), m_+(a))$ Bridge

How does the sorted cycle state order $x_{i_1} < x_{i_2} < \dots < x_{i_k}$ relate to the canonical Raney root $i_0 \in \operatorname{argmax}_{0 \le i < k}(k T_i - i S)$ and canonical floor discrepancy profile $z_i = \lfloor i S / k \rfloor - T_i \in \{0, 1, \dots, W(a)\}$ of `collatz_22/v4` [1] Theorem 5A and `collatz_25/v1` [3] Theorem 1?

**Theorem 3 (Two-Block Determinant Identity, Linear-Form State-Ordering Isomorphism, & Direct $(b_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi, d_\Phi, m_+)$ Bridge).** *For any word $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$ ($k < S < 2k$, $D = 2^S - 3^k > 0$) at a canonical Raney root $z_0 = 0$, define the integer linear form $\Phi_i(a) := i S - k T_i = k\bigl(z_i + \{i S / k\}\bigr) \ge 0$ ($0 \le i < k$), the mechanical Sturmian word $a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} := \lfloor (i+1)S/k \rfloor - \lfloor i S / k \rfloor = 1 + \mathbf{1}_{\{(i S \bmod k) \ge 2k - S\}} \in \{1, 2\}$, and the upward and downward step sets of $z$:*
$$\mathcal{U}_z(a) := \bigl\{i \in \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z} : z_{i+1} - z_i = +1\bigr\}, \qquad \mathcal{D}_z(a) := \bigl\{i \in \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z} : z_{i+1} - z_i < 0\bigr\}. \tag{4.1}$$
1. **(Exact Sturmian-Defect Set Identities & High-Step Conservation):** *Since $z_{i+1} - z_i = a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} - a_i$, letting $H_{\mathrm{Sturm}} := \{i : a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} = 2\}$, $L_{\mathrm{Sturm}} := \{i : a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} = 1\}$, $L := \{i : a_i = 1\}$, and $H := \{i : a_i \ge 2\}$, we have identically*
   $$\mathcal{U}_z(a) = H_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap L, \qquad L_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap \mathcal{D}_z(a) = L_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap H, \qquad \|a - a^{\mathrm{Sturm}}\|_1 = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} |z_{i+1} - z_i| = 2|\mathcal{U}_z(a)| \ge 2 W(a), \tag{4.2}$$
   $$E_{\ge 2}(a) = |H| = (S - k) - |\mathcal{U}_z(a)| + |L_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap \mathcal{D}_z(a)| \le (S - k) - \bigl(|\mathcal{U}_z(a)| - |\mathcal{D}_z(a)|\bigr). \tag{4.3}$$
2. **(Universal Two-Block Determinant Identity & State-Ordering Isomorphism on the Stern–Brocot Upper Spine and Small-Drift Cycles):** *For any cyclic shift $a^{(i)} = (u, v)$ split into a prefix $u = (a_i, \dots, a_{i+h-1}) \in \mathcal{A}(h, q)$ ($1 \le h < k$, $q = T_{i+h} - T_i$) and complementary suffix $v = (a_{i+h}, \dots, a_{i+k-1}) \in \mathcal{A}(k - h, S - q)$, expanding $C(a^{(i)}) = 3^{k-h} C(u) + 2^q C(v)$ and $C(a^{(i+h)}) = 3^h C(v) + 2^{S-q} C(u)$ yields the exact $2 \times 2$ determinant identity*
   $$C\bigl(a^{(i)}\bigr) - C\bigl(a^{(i+h)}\bigr) = C(u, v) - C(v, u) = \bigl(2^q - 3^h\bigr)C(v) - \bigl(2^{S-q} - 3^{k-h}\bigr)C(u) = \det\begin{pmatrix} D(u) & D(v) \\ C(u) & C(v) \end{pmatrix}. \tag{4.4}$$
   *Whenever $\Phi_{i+h}(a) < \Phi_i(a)$ ($k q - h S = \delta \ge 1$), we have $q/h > S/k > \log_2 3$ ($D(u) = 2^q - 3^h > 0$) and $(S - q)/(k - h) = (h' S - \delta)/(h' k) < S/k$ ($h' = k - h$). Consequently:*
   - *(a) On **every Stern–Brocot upper pair $(k, S)$** ($D = 2^S - 3^k > 0$ and $2^{\lfloor (h' S - 1)/k \rfloor} < 3^{h'}$ for all $1 \le h' < k$, an infinite spine including $(3, 5), (5, 8), (17, 27), (29, 46), (41, 65), (94, 149), \dots$), we have $D(v) = 2^{S-q} - 3^{h'} < 0$ for every complementary suffix $v$, so $C(a^{(i)}) - C(a^{(i+h)}) = D(u) C(v) + |D(v)| C(u) > 0$ holds **unconditionally for all compositions $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$** (and likewise on $(4, 7), (5, 9), (8, 13)$ by simplex bounds);*
   - *(b) On any coprime cycle ($\gcd(k, S) = 1$) whose minimum state $x_{\min}(a) = C_{\min}(a)/D$ satisfies the explicit small-drift bound $x_{\min}(a) > k^2 / (3\ln 2)$ (so that $\sigma_k := S - k\log_2 3 < k / (3 x_{\min}(a) \ln 2) < 1/k$ and $|k\log_2(x_i / x_j) - (\Phi_i(a) - \Phi_j(a))| < 1$ whenever $x_i > x_j$);*
   *in both regimes (a) and (b), the sorted cycle state order is **100% identical** to the linear-form order $\Phi$:*
   $$C\bigl(a^{(i)}\bigr) < C\bigl(a^{(j)}\bigr) \iff \Phi_i(a) < \Phi_j(a) \qquad (\forall\, i, j \in \{0, \dots, k-1\}). \tag{4.5}$$
   *Whenever `(4.5)` holds, $x_{\min}(a)$ is attained at the unique canonical Raney root $i_0 = \operatorname{argmin}_i \Phi_i(a)$, $x_{\max}(a)$ is attained at $i_{\max} = \operatorname{argmax}_i \Phi_i(a)$, $d(a) = d_\Phi(a)$ (when $3 y_1 > c$), and $b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) = b_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a)$.*
3. **(Canonical Level-Set Variation Bound for $d_\Phi(a)$):** *For any coprime word $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$, let $i_1, \dots, i_k$ be ordered by $\Phi_{i_1}(a) < \dots < \Phi_{i_k}(a)$, with $\alpha_\Phi(r) := a_{i_r}$, $\pi_\Phi(r)$ the $\Phi$-rank of $(i_r + 1) \bmod k$, $d_\Phi(a) := \#\{1 \le r < k : \alpha_\Phi(r) < \alpha_\Phi(r+1)\}$, and $b_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a) := \#\{1 \le r < k : \pi_\Phi(r+1) \ne \pi_\Phi(r) + 1 \text{ or } \alpha_\Phi(r+1) \ne \alpha_\Phi(r)\}$. Grouping $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ into the $W(a) + 1$ canonical level sets $Z_v = \{i : z_i = v\}$ ($0 \le v \le W(a)$) yields unconditionally:*
   $$d_\Phi(a) \le \bigl(W(a) + 1\bigr) + |\mathcal{U}_z(a)| + |\mathcal{D}_z(a)| \le W(a) + 1 + \|a - a^{\mathrm{Sturm}}\|_1. \tag{4.6}$$
4. **(Piecewise-Translation Identity, Residue-Interval Overlay, & Direct $(b_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a), d_\Phi(a), m_+(a))$ Bridge):** *When $\gcd(k, S) = 1$, let $(h_+, q_+)$ ($k q_+ - h_+ S = 1$) be the upper Farey parent, $H_i := T_{i+h_+} - T_i - q_+$, $I_+(a) := \{i \in \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z} : H_i \ne 0\}$ ($m_+(a) = |I_+(a)|$), $I_+^-(a) := \{i \in I_+(a) : H_i < 0\}$, and $\mathrm{comp}(I_+(a)) := \#\{i \notin I_+(a) : i + 1 \in I_+(a)\} \le m_+(a)$. For every $i \in \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z}$, advancing one step $i \mapsto (i + 1) \bmod k$ satisfies the **exact piecewise-translation identity in $\mathbb{Z}$**:*
   $$\Phi_{(i+1)\bmod k}(a) = \Phi_i(a) + S - k\, a_i. \tag{4.7}$$
   *Moreover, for every $i \notin I_+(a)$, we have $i \ne 0$ (since $0 \in I_+(a)$) and $\Phi_i(a) - \Phi_{i+h_+}(a) = k q_+ - h_+ S = +1$, so the immediate predecessor of $i$ in $\Phi$-order is $(i + h_+) \bmod k$, and $a_i - a_{i+h_+} = H_i - H_{i+1} = -H_{i+1}$. Whenever $i_{r+1} \notin I_+(a)$ and $i_{r+1} + 1 \notin I_+(a)$, we have both $\Phi_{i_{r+1}}(a) - \Phi_{i_r}(a) = +1$ and $\alpha_\Phi(r+1) = \alpha_\Phi(r) =: v$, so `(4.7)` gives $\Phi_{i_{\pi_\Phi(r+1)}}(a) - \Phi_{i_{\pi_\Phi(r)}}(a) = (\Phi_{i_{r+1}}(a) + S - k v) - (\Phi_{i_r}(a) + S - k v) = +1$, which forces $\pi_\Phi(r+1) = \pi_\Phi(r) + 1$. Consequently, every coprime word $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$ satisfies unconditionally:*
   $$b_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a) \le \bigl(m_+(a) - 1\bigr) + \mathrm{comp}\bigl(I_+(a)\bigr) \le 2\, m_+(a) - 1, \qquad \mathrm{runs}_\Phi(a) \le m_+(a) + \mathrm{comp}\bigl(I_+(a)\bigr) \le 2\, m_+(a), \tag{4.8}$$
   $$d_\Phi(a) \le \bigl(m_+(a) - 1\bigr) + \#\bigl\{i \notin I_+(a) : i + 1 \in I_+^-(a)\bigr\} \le m_+(a) + |I_+^-(a)| - 1, \tag{4.9}$$
   *and for every binary word $a \in \{1, 2\}^k$ (since $a_{i_1} = a_0 = 1$ forces the alternating runs of $1$s and $2$s to start with $1$),*
   $$d_\Phi(a) = \left\lfloor \frac{\mathrm{runs}_\Phi(a)}{2} \right\rfloor \le \left\lfloor \frac{m_+(a) + \mathrm{comp}\bigl(I_+(a)\bigr)}{2} \right\rfloor \le m_+(a). \tag{4.10}$$

**Proof.**
**(1):** Since $a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} \in \{1, 2\}$ and $a_i \ge 1$, $z_{i+1} - z_i = a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} - a_i = +1 \iff (a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}}, a_i) = (2, 1)$ ($\mathcal{U}_z = H_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap L$), and for $a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} = 1$, $z_{i+1} - z_i = 1 - a_i < 0 \iff a_i \ge 2$ ($L_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap \mathcal{D}_z = L_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap H$). Since $\sum_{i=0}^{k-1} (z_{i+1} - z_i) = 0$, $\|a - a^{\mathrm{Sturm}}\|_1 = 2|\mathcal{U}_z(a)| \ge 2 W(a)$ and $|H| = (S - k) - |\mathcal{U}_z| + |L_{\mathrm{Sturm}} \cap \mathcal{D}_z|$, proving `(4.2)`–`(4.3)`.

**(2):** Splitting $a^{(i)} = (u, v)$ at index $h \in \{1, \dots, k-1\}$ gives $C(a^{(i)}) = 3^{k-h} C(u) + 2^q C(v)$ and $C(a^{(i+h)}) = 3^h C(v) + 2^{S-q} C(u)$; subtracting yields `(4.4)`. When $\Phi_{i+h}(a) < \Phi_i(a)$, $k q - h S = \delta \ge 1$, so $2^{q k} > 2^{h S} > 3^{h k}$ ($D(u) > 0$) and $S - q = (h' S - \delta)/k \le \lfloor (h' S - 1)/k \rfloor$ ($h' = k - h$). On any Stern–Brocot upper pair $(k, S)$ with $2^{\lfloor (h' S - 1)/k \rfloor} < 3^{h'}$ for all $1 \le h' < k$, $D(v) = 2^{S-q} - 3^{h'} < 0$ for every $v$, so $C(a^{(i)}) - C(a^{(i+h)}) = D(u) C(v) + |D(v)| C(u) > 0$. Under hypothesis (b), $2^{a_r} x_{r+1} = 3 x_r + 1$ gives $T_j - T_i = (j - i)\log_2 3 + \log_2(x_i / x_j) + \sigma_{i \to j}$ where $0 < \sigma_{i \to j} < \sigma_k = S - k\log_2 3 < k/(3 x_{\min}(a)\ln 2) < 1/k$, so for $x_i > x_j$ we have $\Phi_i(a) - \Phi_j(a) = k\log_2(x_i / x_j) + k \sigma_{i \to j} - (j - i)\sigma_k > -k \sigma_k > -1$, and since $\Phi_i(a) - \Phi_j(a)$ is a nonzero integer, $\Phi_i(a) > \Phi_j(a)$, proving `(4.5)`.

**(3):** Since $0 \le \{i S / k\} < 1$, $\Phi_i(a) = k(z_i + \{i S / k\})$ orders $Z_0, Z_1, \dots, Z_{W(a)}$ sequentially and orders each $Z_v$ by increasing $\{i S / k\}$. On $Z_v \setminus (\mathcal{U}_z \cup \mathcal{D}_z)$, $a_i = a_i^{\mathrm{Sturm}} = 1 + \mathbf{1}_{\{\{i S / k\} \ge 2 - S/k\}}$ has at most $1$ strict ascent, while inserting $\mathcal{U}_z \cup \mathcal{D}_z$ adds at most $|\mathcal{U}_z| + |\mathcal{D}_z|$ ascents, proving `(4.6)`.

**(4):** For $0 \le i < k - 1$, $\Phi_{i+1}(a) - \Phi_i(a) = (i+1)S - k(T_i + a_i) - (i S - k T_i) = S - k a_i$; at $i = k - 1$, $\Phi_0(a) - \Phi_{k-1}(a) = 0 - ((k-1)S - k(S - a_{k-1})) = S - k a_{k-1}$, proving `(4.7)` for all $i \in \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z}$. For $1 \le r \le k - 1$, let $i = i_{r+1} \in \{1, \dots, k-1\}$. When $i \notin I_+(a)$, $T_{i+h_+} - T_i = q_+$, so $\Phi_i(a) - \Phi_{i+h_+}(a) = k q_+ - h_+ S = +1$, forcing $i_r = (i + h_+) \bmod k$ and $\alpha_\Phi(r+1) - \alpha_\Phi(r) = a_i - a_{i+h_+} = H_i - H_{i+1} = -H_{i+1}$. If in addition $i + 1 \notin I_+(a)$, then $H_{i+1} = 0$, so $\alpha_\Phi(r+1) = \alpha_\Phi(r) =: v$, and by `(4.7)`:
$$\Phi_{i_{\pi_\Phi(r+1)}}(a) - \Phi_{i_{\pi_\Phi(r)}}(a) = \bigl(\Phi_{i_{r+1}}(a) + S - k v\bigr) - \bigl(\Phi_{i_r}(a) + S - k v\bigr) = \Phi_{i_{r+1}}(a) - \Phi_{i_r}(a) = +1.$$
Since $\Phi$ is integer-valued and injective, no state can have a $\Phi$-value strictly between $\Phi_{i_{\pi_\Phi(r)}}(a)$ and $\Phi_{i_{\pi_\Phi(r+1)}}(a)$, so $\pi_\Phi(r+1) = \pi_\Phi(r) + 1$ and $r \notin \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a)$. Thus every $r \in \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a)$ has either $i_{r+1} \in I_+(a) \setminus \{0\}$ (at most $m_+(a) - 1$ steps) or $i_{r+1} \notin I_+(a)$ and $i_{r+1} + 1 \in I_+(a)$ (at most $\mathrm{comp}(I_+(a))$ steps), proving `(4.8)`–`(4.10)`. $\blacksquare$

## 5. Explicit Raney Discrepancy Decompositions, Sturmian-to-Farey Bridge Identity, Farey-Chain Dynamic Range Lower Bound, and Two-Window Resultants

We first recall as established baselines three foundational properties of the mechanical Sturmian (Christoffel) word $w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)$ ($w_m^{\mathrm{Sturm}} = \lfloor (m+1)S/k \rfloor - \lfloor m S / k \rfloor$ for $\gcd(k, S) = 1$, $S > k \ge 2$, with upper and lower Farey parents $(h_+, q_+)$ and $(h_-, q_-)$ satisfying $k q_+ - h_+ S = 1$, $h_- S - k q_- = 1$, $h_+ + h_- = k$, $q_+ + q_- = S$):
- **(Baseline A — Rotated Christoffel Monomial Ladder & Max–Min Cancellation; Knight [15, Theorems 3.4 & 5.4]):** The cyclic shifts $u^{[r]} := (w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S))^{(r h_+ \bmod k)}$ ($1 \le r \le k$) have prefix sums $T_m(u^{[r]}) = \lfloor (m S + k - r)/k \rfloor$, adjacent monomial differences
  $$C\bigl(u^{[r]}\bigr) - C\bigl(u^{[r+1]}\bigr) = 3^{k - 1 - m_r} \cdot 2^{\lfloor m_r S / k \rfloor} > 0 \qquad \bigl(1 \le r \le k - 1,\;\; m_r = (r h_-) \bmod k\bigr), \tag{5.1}$$
  and max–min identity $2\, C_{\min}(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)) - C_{\max}(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)) = 3^{k-1}$.
- **(Baseline B — Christoffel Matrix Recurrence, Parent/Child Monomial Determinants, & Coprimality; Mahler `collatz_4/v1` [9, Section 5.1 & Theorem 3] and Knight [15]):** Writing $C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S) := C(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)) = C_{\min}(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S))$ and $D(k, S) := 2^S - 3^k$, the Christoffel concatenation $w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S) = (w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_-, q_-),\, w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_+, q_+))$ satisfies the matrix recurrence and monomial determinant identities [9, Theorem 3]
  $$\begin{pmatrix} C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S) \\ D(k, S) \end{pmatrix} = 3^{h_+} \begin{pmatrix} C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_-, q_-) \\ D(h_-, q_-) \end{pmatrix} + 2^{q_-} \begin{pmatrix} C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_+, q_+) \\ D(h_+, q_+) \end{pmatrix}, \tag{5.2}$$
  $$D(h_+, q_+)\, C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_-, q_-) - D(h_-, q_-)\, C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_+, q_+) = 3^{h_- - 1}\, 2^{q_+ - 1}, \qquad D(h_+, q_+)\, C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S) - D(k, S)\, C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_+, q_+) = 3^{k - 1}\, 2^{q_+ - 1}, \tag{5.3}$$
  whence $\gcd(C(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)), 2^S - 3^k) = 1$ for all coprime $S > k \ge 2$.
- **(Baseline C — Sturmian Cyclic-Minimum and Cyclic-Maximum Extremality; Halbeisen and Hungerbühler [14], Fernández and Ibáñez [16, Theorem 7.3]):** Over all compositions $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$, $C_{\min}(a) \le C_{\min}(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S))$ and $C_{\max}(a) \ge C_{\max}(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S))$.

Building on Baselines A–C, for any window $(h, q)$ ($1 \le h < k$) and any word $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$, recall from `collatz_22/v4` [1, Eq. (25)] and `collatz_25/v1` [3] the window defect set $I^{(h, q)}(a) := \{0 \le i < k : T_{i+h}(a) - T_i(a) \ne q\}$, the sparse numerator $\tilde{R}^{(h, q)}(a)$, and its $\{2, 3\}$-free part $\tilde{R}_{\mathrm{red}}^{(h, q)}(a)$:
$$\tilde{R}^{(h, q)}(a) := \sum_{i \in I^{(h, q)}(a)} 3^{k-1-i}\, 2^{T_i(a)}\bigl(2^{T_{i+h}(a) - T_i(a)} - 2^q\bigr) = D(k, S)\, C\bigl(a_{[0, h)}\bigr) - D(h, q)\, C(a), \tag{5.4}$$
$$\tilde{R}_{\mathrm{red}}^{(h, q)}(a) := \frac{\bigl|\tilde{R}^{(h, q)}(a)\bigr|}{2^{v_2(\tilde{R}^{(h, q)}(a))} \cdot 3^{v_3(\tilde{R}^{(h, q)}(a))}}. \tag{5.4a}$$

**Theorem 4 (Explicit Raney Discrepancy Decompositions, Sturmian-to-Farey Bridge Identity, Farey-Chain Dynamic Range Lower Bound, & Determinant-GCD / Two-Window Elimination Laws).** *Let $(k, S)$ be any coprime pair ($\gcd(k, S) = 1$, $S > k \ge 2$) with upper and lower Farey parents $(h_+, q_+)$ and $(h_-, q_-)$.*
1. **(Explicit Primal/Dual Raney Discrepancy Decompositions & Sturmian-to-Farey Bridge Identity):** *For any word $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$, let $i_{\min} = \operatorname{argmin}_{0 \le i < k} \Phi_i(a)$ and $i_{\max} = \operatorname{argmax}_{0 \le i < k} \Phi_i(a)$ ($\Phi_i(a) = i S - k T_i(a)$) be the primal and dual Raney roots of $a$. Because $\gcd(k, S) = 1$ implies $k \nmid m S$ for $1 \le m \le k - 1$, the shifted words $a^{(i_{\min})}$ and $a^{(i_{\max})}$ satisfy $T_m(a^{(i_{\min})}) = \lfloor m S / k \rfloor - z_m(a) = T_m(u^{[k]}) - z_m(a)$ ($z_m(a) \ge 0$) and $T_m(a^{(i_{\max})}) = \lceil m S / k \rceil + \bar{z}_m(a) = T_m(u^{[1]}) + \bar{z}_m(a)$ ($\bar{z}_m(a) \ge 0$) for all $1 \le m \le k - 1$. Consequently, Baseline C refines to the universal Raney-root sandwich*
   $$C_{\min}(a) \le C\bigl(a^{(i_{\min})}\bigr) \le C_{\min}\bigl(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)\bigr) < C_{\max}\bigl(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)\bigr) \le C\bigl(a^{(i_{\max})}\bigr) \le C_{\max}(a), \tag{5.5}$$
   *with exact positive discrepancy decompositions*
   $$\Delta_{\min}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a) := C_{\min}\bigl(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}\bigr) - C\bigl(a^{(i_{\min})}\bigr) = \sum_{m=1}^{k-1} 3^{k-1-m}\, 2^{T_m(a^{(i_{\min})})}\bigl(2^{z_m(a)} - 1\bigr) \ge 0, \tag{5.6}$$
   $$\Delta_{\max}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a) := C\bigl(a^{(i_{\max})}\bigr) - C_{\max}\bigl(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}\bigr) = \sum_{m=1}^{k-1} 3^{k-1-m}\, 2^{\lceil m S / k \rceil}\bigl(2^{\bar{z}_m(a)} - 1\bigr) \ge 0, \tag{5.7}$$
   *and quantitative minimax identities*
   $$C_{\max}(a) - C_{\min}(a) \ge \bigl(C_{\min}(w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}) - 3^{k-1}\bigr) + \Delta_{\min}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a) + \Delta_{\max}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a), \qquad 2\, C_{\min}(a) - C_{\max}(a) \le 3^{k-1} - \bigl(2\, \Delta_{\min}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a) + \Delta_{\max}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a)\bigr), \tag{5.8}$$
   *with equality in `(5.5)`–`(5.8)` if and only if $a$ is a cyclic shift of $w^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(k, S)$. Moreover, subtracting the upper-Farey identity $D_+ C(a^{(i_{\min})}) - D C(u_{i_{\min}}) = -\tilde{R}^{(h_+, q_+)}(a^{(i_{\min})})$ (`(5.4)` with $u_{i_{\min}} = (a^{(i_{\min})})_{[0, h_+)}$) from `(5.3)` yields the **Exact Sturmian-to-Farey Bridge Identity**:*
   $$D_+\, \Delta_{\min}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a) - D\, \Delta_{\mathrm{pref}}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a) = 3^{k-1}\, 2^{q_+ - 1} + \tilde{R}^{(h_+, q_+)}\bigl(a^{(i_{\min})}\bigr), \qquad \Delta_{\mathrm{pref}}^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(a) := C^{\mathrm{Sturm}}(h_+, q_+) - C(u_{i_{\min}}) = \sum_{m=1}^{h_+ - 1} 3^{h_+ - 1 - m} 2^{T_m(a^{(i_{\min})})}\bigl(2^{z_m(a)} - 1\bigr) \ge 0. \tag{5.9}$$
2. **(Farey-Chain Dynamic Range Lower Bound):** *For any word $a \in \mathcal{A}(k, S)$ ($\gcd(k, S) = 1, D = 2^S - 3^k > 0$) and any non-defect right-Farey step $i \notin I_+(a)$ ($T_{i+h_+}(a) - T_i(a) = q_+$), the Two-Block Determinant Identity `(4.4)` and the $h_+$-step transition $2^{q_+} x_{i+h_+} = 3^{h_+} x_i + C(u_i)$ give*
   $$C\bigl(a^{(i)}\bigr) - C\bigl(a^{(i+h_+)}\bigr) = D_+\, C(v_i) - D_-\, C(u_i) = \frac{D_+\, C(a^{(i)}) - D\, C(u_i)}{2^{q_+}} \ge \Delta_{\mathrm{Farey}}(k, S), \tag{5.10}$$
   *where $D_+ = 2^{q_+} - 3^{h_+} > 0$, $D_- = 2^{q_-} - 3^{h_-}$, and $\Delta_{\mathrm{Farey}}(k, S) := D_+ C_{\min}(h_-, q_-) + |D_-| C_{\min}(h_+, q_+)$ when $D_- \le 0$ (or $D_+ C_{\min}(h_-, q_-) - D_- C_{\max}(h_+, q_+)$ when $D_- > 0$), with exact general simplex extrema $C_{\min}^{\mathrm{gen}}(h, q) = 3^h - 2^h$ and $C_{\max}^{\mathrm{gen}}(h, q) = 3^{h-1}(2^{q-h+1} + 1) - 2^q$ (and binary extrema $C_{\min}^{\mathrm{bin}}(h, q) = 3^h + 2^q - 3^{q-h} 2^{2h-q+1}$, $C_{\max}^{\mathrm{bin}}(h, q) = 3^{2h-q} 2^{2q-2h+1} - 3^h - 2^q$).    $$u_e = W_r \bmod 3^r = \Bigl(2^{-(s_{r-1} + e_r)}\, C(e)\Bigr) \bmod 3^r = \left(\sum_{i=1}^r 3^{r-i}\, 2^{-S_i}\right) \bmod 3^r \in \{1, \dots, 3^r - 1\}, \qquad A_e = \left\lfloor \frac{W_r}{3^r} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \sum_{i=1}^r \frac{2^{-S_i} \bmod 3^i}{3^i} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \sum_{i=1}^r \frac{a_{i, S_i}}{2^{S_i}} \right\rfloor, \tag{6.14}$$
   *and under any $h$-step split $W_r = 3^{r-h} X_h(a) + Y$ ($1 \le h < r$, $a = 2^{-S_{h+1}} \bmod 3^h$), defining the suffix-reduced raw sum $\widetilde{W}_r^{(h)} := 3^{r-h} X_h(a) + (Y \bmod 3^r)$ absorbs the suffix carry $\lfloor Y / 3^r \rfloor$ identically and gives*
   $$u_e = \widetilde{W}_r^{(h)} - 3^r B_h, \qquad B_h := \left\lfloor \frac{\widetilde{W}_r^{(h)}}{3^r} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{X_h(a) + (Y \bmod 3^r)/3^{r-h}}{3^h} \right\rfloor \in \{0, 1, \dots, h\}, \tag{6.15}$$
   *reducing the unknown carry range from $r$ values ($A_e \in \{0, \dots, r-1\}$) to $h + 1$ values ($B_h \in \{0, \dots, h\}$).*

**Proof.**
**(1):** If a path reaches weight $r$ at step $j$ for the first time, its weight $r_{\mathrm{prev}} \in \{r - 1, r\}$ at step $j - 1$ must have survived step $j - 1$, so $r \ge r_{\mathrm{prev}} \ge r_{\min}(j - 1)$ and $2^H \cdot 3^{r_{\min}(j-1)} t_{j-1}^2 > 2^{j-1}$. Dividing $2^H \cdot 3^r t_j^2 \le 2^j = 2 \cdot 2^{j-1}$ by $2^H \cdot 3^{r_{\min}(j-1)} t_{j-1}^2 > 2^{j-1}$ gives `(6.3)`. When $3 t_j^2 > 2 t_{j-1}^2$, `(6.3)` forces $3^{r - r_{\min}(j-1)} < 3$, so $r = r_{\min}(j - 1)$, $r_{\mathrm{prev}} = r_{\min}(j - 1)$, and $b_j = r - r_{\mathrm{prev}} = 0$.

**(2):** At $j = k + 1 - t$ ($t \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$), $t_j = t$ and $t_{j-1} = t + 1$. By `(6.3)`, $r \in \{r_0, r_0 + 1\}$ where $r_0 = r_{\min}(j - 1)$ satisfies $2^H \cdot 3^{r_0 - 1} (t + 1)^2 \le 2^{j-1} < 2^H \cdot 3^{r_0} (t + 1)^2$ (when $r_0 \ge 1$). Both $r_0$ and $r_0 + 1$ cross at step $j$ if and only if $2^H \cdot 3^{r_0 + 1} t^2 \le 2^j$, i.e.
$$\frac{2^{j-1}}{2^H (t + 1)^2} < 3^{r_0} \le \frac{2^j}{3 \cdot 2^H t^2} \iff 2^{k - H} \frac{1}{2^t (t + 1)^2} < 3^{r_0} \le 2^{k - H} \frac{1}{3 \cdot 2^{t-1} t^2}.$$
Multiplying by $3^{t-1}$ and setting $m = r_0 + t - 1$ gives $3^m \in 2^{k - H}(a_t, a_{t-1}] = J_t$, where $3^{t-1}/(3 \cdot 2^{t-1} t^2) = a_{t-1}$. Thus $J_4, J_3, J_2, J_1$ tile $2^{k - H}(27/400, 1/3]$ gaplessly, and since $400/81 \in (3, 9)$, every interval $2^{k - H}(27/400, 1/3]$ contains $1$ or $2$ powers of $3$, proving `(6.4)`–`(6.5)`.

**(3), (4), & (5):** If $w, w' \in \{0, 1\}^k$ share their first $j_0$ bits, all differing 1-positions are $\ge j_0$, so $2^{j_0} \mid (B(w') - B(w))$, proving `(6.6)` since $\gcd(2^{j_0}, 3^r) = 1$. Substituting $a_{i, S_i + p} = 2^{S_i} \lfloor a_{i, S_i + p}/2^{S_i} \rfloor + a_{i, S_i}$, $\frac{1}{3^i 2^{S_i}} = \frac{q_{i, p} 2^p}{3^i} - \lfloor \frac{a_{i, S_i + p}}{2^{S_i}} \rfloor - \frac{a_{i, S_i}}{2^{S_i}}$, and $\frac{n_e}{2^{S_1}} = \sum_{i=1}^r \frac{a_{i, S_i}}{2^{S_i}} - A_e$ into $u_e = 3^r(n_e/2^{S_1} + \sum_{i=1}^r \frac{1}{3^i 2^{S_i}})$ cancels $\sum_{i=1}^r a_{i, S_i}/2^{S_i}$ identically and yields `(6.7)`–`(6.8)`. Since $(3 n_{(e_i, \dots, e_r)} + 1)/2^{e_i} \in (0, 3 \cdot 2^{S_{i+1}})$ has suffix valuation word $(e_{i+1}, \dots, e_r)$, it equals $n_{(e_{i+1}, \dots, e_r)} + (\kappa_i + \mathbf{1}_{i=r}) 2^{S_{i+1}}$ for a unique $\kappa_i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$; substituting this into $2^{S_{i+1}} u_{(e_i, \dots, e_r)} = 3^{r-i}((3 n_{(e_i, \dots, e_r)} + 1)/2^{e_i}) + C(e_{i+1}, \dots, e_r)$ and reducing $3 n_{(e_i, \dots, e_r)} + 1 \equiv 1 \pmod 3$ gives `(6.9)`–`(6.11)`. Writing $e_i = 2 q_i + e_i^{(0)}$ ($e_i^{(0)} = 2 - \eta_i \in \{1, 2\}$), invariance of $\kappa_i$ under $q_i$ factors $4^{q_i}$ out of $3 n_{(e_i, \dots, e_r)} + 1 = 4^{q_i}(3 n_{(e_i^{(0)}, \dots, e_r)} + 1)$, so $(4^{q_i} - 1)/3 \equiv q_i \equiv c_i \pmod 3$ shifts $n_{(e_i, \dots, e_r)} \bmod 3$ by $+c_i \pmod 3$ and makes $c_{i+1} \in \{0, 1, 2\} \mapsto \kappa_i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ a triangular bijection on every cube $\mathcal{C}$, proving `(6.12)`–`(6.13)`. Finally, since $q_{i, 0} = (3^i a_{i, S_i} + 1)/2^{S_i} \in \{1, \dots, 3^i - 1\}$ satisfies $q_{i, 0} \equiv 2^{-S_i} \pmod{3^i}$, we have $q_{i, 0} = (2^{-S_i} \bmod 3^i)$ and $W_r = \sum_{i=1}^r 3^{r-i} q_{i, 0} = u_e + 3^r A_e$. Since $1 \le u_e \le 3^r - 1$ (`(6.9)` and $3 \nmid u_e$), $u_e = W_r \bmod 3^r = (2^{-S_1} C(e)) \bmod 3^r$ and $A_e = \lfloor W_r / 3^r \rfloor$, proving `(6.14)`; subtracting $3^r \lfloor Y / 3^r \rfloor$ from $W_r = 3^{r-h} X_h(a) + Y$ and noting $0 \le X_h(a) < h 3^h$ and $0 \le Y \bmod 3^r < 3^r$ proves $0 \le B_h \le h$ in `(6.15)`. $\blacksquare$

### 6.1 Scope and Limitations

While Theorems 1–5 provide exact structural identities and certified scale cutoffs, their scope has three essential boundaries:
1. **Order-statistics and state-ordering restrictions:** Theorem 1 characterizes the Eulerian rank-descent count $d(a) = \mathrm{ascents}(\alpha)$ (when $3 y_1 > c$) and decomposes $b_{\mathrm{gap}}(a) = (\mathrm{runs}(\alpha) - 1) + |\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{eq}}(a)|$, while Theorem 3 transfers $\Phi$-order bounds ($b_{\mathrm{gap}}^\Phi(a) \le 2 m_+(a) - 1$ and $d_\Phi(a) \le m_+(a)$ on binary words) to actual state order only on the Stern–Brocot upper spine, $(4, 7), (5, 9), (8, 13)$, or cycles satisfying the small-drift condition $x_{\min}(a) > k^2 / (3\ln 2)$. High-complexity cycles with large $E_{\ge 2}(a)$ and $m_+(a)$ outside these regimes are not excluded by these bounds alone.
2. **Fixed-slope scale cutoffs vs. arbitrary slopes:** Theorem 2 and Table 1 certify finite scale cutoffs $D_{\mathrm{stop}}(r_0, s_0)$ for fixed reduced slope pairs $(r_0, s_0)$, reducing the bounded search range needed to close a given fixed-slope family. They do not bound arbitrary-slope growth as $r_0 \to \infty$ with $2^{s_0} - 3^{r_0}$ small relative to $3^{r_0}$.
3. **Finite-word carry/digit decompositions vs. linear dyadic atom bounds:** Theorem 5(4)–(5) removes the carry uncertainty via the unrounded suffix-sum floor `(6.8)`, establishes unconditional 3-adic and pointwise atom bounds `(6.11)` and mod-$6$ cube equidistribution `(6.13)`, and reduces the $h$-step split carry from $r$ values to $h + 1$ values `(6.15)`. Proving a one-sided dyadic atom bound at positive linear resolution $p = \lfloor \delta k \rfloor$ under the geometric measure (Rees [17, Conjecture 4.1]) and ruling out divergent Collatz orbits remain open.
