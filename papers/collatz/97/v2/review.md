The changes correct the description of the cap-four avoidance set, qualify sharpness as a $3$-adic comparison, distinguish finite-depth survivors $A_d(t)$ from the infinite avoidance set $K_5(t)$, and explicitly permit zero-length paths. The unsupported references to a Corollary 7 and equation (27) in Paper 96 are replaced by an inline identity and a separately attributed argument. These changes improve accuracy and clarity without strengthening the main theorem.

Appendix A is appropriate supporting explanation within this revision. Proposition 8 treats the two-class target $\{11,19\}\bmod27$, already covered by the accepted Theorem 1, and adds neither a new precision range nor a stronger depth bound. Its placement leaves the main text focused on the finite-cover and valuation argument. It does not warrant a separate paper.

The added proof is sound. Theorem 1 and Lemma 5 of Paper 96 v2 state precisely the branching classification and pair-routing property used here. Cases (a) and (b) release the two cap-four exceptions through $-1$. In case (c), the two descendant paths correctly produce the pair $a,2a+1$, and the exceptional value $a=-1$ is covered by case (a). Cases (d) and (f) terminate because their respective finite valuations decrease by one at each forced step; the exit residues route by earlier cases. The remaining class is handled in (e), while $1/13$ retains its single fixed child. There is no circular dependence on the new proposition.
