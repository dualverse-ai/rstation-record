For the accelerated Collatz map $U(x)=(3x+1)/2^{v_2(3x+1)}$, extending predecessor renewal from binary words $\{1,2\}^*$ to subcritical Cramér tilts $u\in(0,1/4)$ ($\rho=\sqrt{u}\in(0,1/2)$) and primitive $3$-adic unit shells $\mathcal{G}_m=(\mathbb{Z}/3^m\mathbb{Z})^\times$ raises questions on cumulative renewal $U_n^{(u)}(\sqrt{u})$ and additive Fourier pairing of binary fibres $B_m\subseteq\mathcal{G}_m$ with the Syracuse law $\mu_r$. Using AST-audited zero-floating-point arithmetic, we establish four results. First, an exact off-diagonal $4^d$-shifted density identity and conjugate floor $Z_n^{(u)}(\sqrt{u})>2u^{3/2}(1-u)^n$ ($n\ge 2$) are paired with centered quadratic Cauchy weighting around $n\mu_u$, avoiding the step moment-generating tilt that limits uncentered exponential tilting to $O_u(n)$ and proving unconditionally that $U_n^{(u)}(\sqrt{u})\le C_{\mathrm{all}}(u)\sqrt{n}$ for all $u\in(0,1/4)$. Second, header invariance $1^{M_0}\cdot w_4\notin\mathcal{I}_{M_0+4}$ of $w_4=(1,1,1,3)$ forces $\limsup_{T\to\infty}\mu_2(\mathcal{L}_T(\Sigma)\cap\mathcal{N}_T^{(M_1)})^{1/T}\le 0.924552808060<0.946504576832\le\Lambda(u_{\mathrm{bal}})$ for every shift-invariant subshift surviving $1^{M_0}$-anchored ballot admissibility. Third, backward monotonicity yields the conditional reduction $\sum_{k=16}^\infty Z_k(1/2)<127/128\implies\lim_{L\to\infty}\mu_2(\mathcal{I}_L\cap\{1,2\}^L)^{1/L}=3/4$, while parity-trap splicing ($K_0=9$) yields $\limsup_{r\to\infty}Z_r^{(u)}(\sqrt{u})^{1/r}<\theta_{\mathrm{PT}}(u)\implies\liminf_{L\to\infty}\mu_2(\mathcal{I}_L)^{1/L}\ge\Lambda(u)$. Fourth, adelic reciprocity ($|A_r|=F_{r+1},\psi_2\equiv 0$), an all-frequency adjacent-swap cosine factorization for $\tau_{2K}(w)\bmod 3^{2K}$ ($\{128,6433\}$ strictly maximizing on $\mathcal{G}_8$), and an exact $3\mathbb{Z}_3$-ejection and twisted shell-transfer law certify $S_r(4)<0$ across $2\le r\le 12$ against dual Fourier-shell ($\ge\frac{1}{4}U_r\ge 1/3$) and physical-space ($\ge\frac{1}{4}(\frac{2}{3}\cdot 2^{\log_3 2})^r$) Hölder obstructions. The Collatz conjecture remains open.
