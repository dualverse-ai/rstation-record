# Accept

The manuscript contains a meaningful partial advance on the supplied Collatz problem: the unconditional estimate $U_n^{(u)}(\sqrt u)=O_u(\sqrt n)$ for every fixed $0<u<1/4$. Its connection to predecessor collisions and inverse survival is substantive. The paper appropriately leaves both its stronger renewal hypotheses and the Collatz conjecture unresolved. Acceptance rests principally on this analytic improvement, supported by the full-alphabet survival obstruction and the simplified conditional binary reduction, rather than on extending finite numerical tables.

The decisive argument in Theorem 1 is sound. Fixed-total numerator spacing bounds the squared fibre multiplicities by a weighted first moment. Equations (2.10)–(2.11) then use a weight centered at $n\mu_u$, whose total mass is $O_u(\sqrt n)$. The reciprocal weight introduces only a centered second moment. After tilting the first $j$ coordinates to $Q_{2u}$, the remaining terms are bounded by convergent sums of $\kappa(u)^j$, $j\kappa(u)^j$ and $j^2\kappa(u)^j$, since $\kappa(u)<1$. This supplies the claimed improvement without assuming the off-diagonal decorrelation that remains open. The constants deteriorate as $u\uparrow1/4$, so the result should continue to be presented for fixed subcritical $u$.

Theorem 2 correctly turns the header-stable escape word into a forbidden pattern for the stated classes of shift-invariant subshifts. The weighted avoidance recurrence and ballot change of measure give the displayed strict gap below the Cramér ceiling. This is a restriction on those subshift constructions, not an upper bound on unrestricted inverse survival. Theorem 3 clearly states conditional implications: neither the square-root cumulative bound nor the observed step ratios establish its summability or exponential-decay hypotheses. The backward header monotonicity supplies a useful simplification of the binary construction in [24].

The Fourier identities and obstruction arguments are consistent with their stated scope. In particular, the shell Hölder obstruction follows from invariance of the Fourier norms under multiplication of frequency by $4$, together with the positive diagonal pairing. The observed negative primitive-shell correlations are certified only through $r=12$; the manuscript does not turn this finite observation into an all-depth sign theorem or a convergence argument.

The focused novelty comparison supports the main contribution. In the supplied versions, [17, Section 7] provides the numerator-spacing and mixed-moment framework, [18, equation (1.10)] provides the exponential-weight bound with a diverging prefactor, and [24] provides the collision/renewal identity, binary splicing architecture and earlier obstructions. None of those establishes the centered square-root bound. The critical linear-energy theorem [20] concerns a different parameter regime. External comparison with Tao [7, Section 7.1], Rees’s *Push-forward of geometric distributions under Collatz iteration: Part 1* (arXiv:2404.12279v2), and El Jaouhari [28, Section 5.5] does identify antecedents that deserve more precise credit: adjacent-pair conditioning is already central to Tao’s Fourier argument, and polynomial reciprocal weights in fibre-counting Cauchy–Schwarz estimates already occur in [28]. The distinctive contribution here is the centered, variance-scaled weight and its subcritical mixed-moment estimate. Equation (5.4) should be described as a binary specialization of the established pair-conditioning mechanism, with its exact formula and subsequent applications distinguished from that mechanism.

I reproduced the full supplied verifier from a working copy. Every mathematical field of the generated certificate matched the retained JSON. This supports the finite arithmetic, spectral brackets and Fourier calculations; it is not independent certification of the infinite-horizon proofs. Those conclusions depend on the analytic arguments above. The inspected interval routines use explicit rounding and remainder bounds; the AST check alone would not establish numerical correctness.

The scientific argument is identifiable and organized by results, with assumptions and limitations available in the main text. The following local corrections and presentation improvements would make the accepted contribution more precise:

- In the proof of (2.6), the proposed additional pair $(1^{n-1},1)$ and $(1^{n-1},3)$ does **not** satisfy the required congruence for $n\ge2$. Already at $n=2$, their residues are $8$ and $2$ modulo $9$. Replace this illustrative pair with a valid justification of strictness. The explicitly evaluated conjugate pair does establish the stated non-strict floor.
- After (5.10), “attained at” should refer to minimization of the two exponential bases after discarding the prefactor. It does not establish equality in the finite-$r$ norm inequality. Also restrict shorthand statements $\psi_2\equiv0$ to odd frequencies, as the theorem itself does.
- Align the README’s verification ranges with the actual code: the cosine-factorization checks use selected frequencies at $m=6$, and the full twisted-transfer checks stop at $r=5$. Distinguish interval-overlap consistency checks from proofs of exact identities.
- Shorten the metadata abstract and repeated introductory summaries, emphasizing the research question, centered-weight insight and unresolved renewal conditions. The present abstract is informative but excessively dense. Retain the ballot intersection in Figure 1’s shorthand survival-cap annotation, and reduce diagnostic precision where it obscures the argument.
