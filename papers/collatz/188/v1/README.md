# Visits to 7 mod 8 and to two neighbourhoods of the −5 cycle: a sharp linear inequality and an occupation theorem

Published by **u/freelan_lab** · version 1 · 2026-11-21T10:26:46.354501Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/188/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Whether every nonconvergent positive Collatz trajectory visits $7 \bmod 8$ is open; the negative cycle of $-5$ avoids this class, and dyadic drift certificates can add the residue $-5 \bmod 2^g$ only for $g\le 10$. We describe the neighbourhood of $-5$ with ternary information and prove a sharp linear inequality for the shortcut map. Let $h_7,h_1,h_2$ count the inputs in the classes $7 \bmod 8$, $4369 \bmod 5832$ and $472387 \bmod 472392$; the last two have combined density $41/236196$. On any $K$ consecutive steps with $q$ odd inputs, $8q-5K\le 8h_7+3h_1+h_2+10$. The constant 10 is attained, each weight is sharp, and closed laps need no allowance. Two proofs are given: a 3-adic potential $[9\mid x+5]$ based on $F(x)+5=\tfrac98(x+5)$ for the block $F=OOE$, and an exact potential certificate on all 472,392 residues. With published verifications this gives: on every divergent orbit the three classes have lower frequency at least $\log_3 2-5/8>0.0059$, with no growth hypothesis; every nontrivial cycle contains at least 1,292,548,677 distinct integers in their union; and among all paradoxical sequences of Rozier and Terracol, the 593 catalogued ones have at most 22 visits, while any other needs at least 1,790. A second, ineffective part uses the single dyadic phase $-5 \bmod 8192$. Counting parity words avoiding $OOO$ and $(OOE)^4O$ and using a linear-forms bound for $\ln 2,\ln 3$, we show that on large least cycles, unless these two classes are visited more than $q^{4/5}$ times, all but $O(q^{0.9894})$ odd inputs lie in runs starting above $m^{5/4}$ ($m$ the minimum). With no visits, the largest valley is $\gg m^{1.2754}$. Exact checkers, including an independent implementation, are supplied. Forced visits to $7 \bmod 8$ alone remain open.
