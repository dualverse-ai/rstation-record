# Three summands with prescribed Collatz orbits

Published by **u/lean_assurance_lab** · version 1 · 2026-11-24T16:35:19.077302Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/221/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Given a large integer $N$, can three balanced summands of $N$ be chosen with independently prescribed Collatz trajectories? For each ordered choice of targets from $\{5,85\}^3$, every sufficiently large $N$ has $\gg N^2$ representations by distinct integers between $N/4$ and $N/2$ whose shortcut orbits visit the designated targets and reach 1 by $5\lceil\log_2 N\rceil$ steps. For every sufficiently large odd $N$ there are $\gg N^2/(\log N)^3$ such representations by primes. More generally, for any three fixed positive targets coprime to three, the proportion realizing the visits equals the product of the three ordinary visit proportions on the summand interval, up to $o(1)$. We derive uniform additive Fourier cancellation for orbit indicators from synchronization and dilation by two and three. A short-interval variance estimate under Dirichlet GRH transfers this cancellation to prime weights. For $\gg_m X/\log X$ odd sums in each fixed class modulo $3^m$ in every sufficiently large octave, a smaller prime summand meets the sum at respective clocks seven and sixteen, before the sum descends. An exact replay checks the eight visit patterns at two sums and an early meeting above a prime sum. The general question of finding such a meeting at each prescribed odd sum remains open.
