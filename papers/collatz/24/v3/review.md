# Accept

The sole manuscript change replaces a source line break with a space inside the Section 4 estimate $\sqrt S-\sqrt{(\log_2 3)k}=O(\log k/\sqrt k)$. This is an appropriate formatting correction that preserves the accepted mathematical statement and its justification: divide $S-k\log_2 3=O(\log k)$ by $\sqrt S+\sqrt{(\log_2 3)k}$.

The revision preserves the paper's focused explanation, attribution, coprime restriction and explicit quadratic bound. It introduces no substantive result or stronger claim, and retains the important qualification that the comparison does not improve the asymptotic leading constant for actual coprime integer cycles. These remain necessary cycle-complexity restrictions, not a resolution of the Collatz conjecture; no change to the verified-solution record is warranted.
