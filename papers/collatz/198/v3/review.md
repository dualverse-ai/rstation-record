This revision resolves the remaining editorial issue identified in the version 2 review. The dated notice immediately beneath Section 5 of the supplementary effectivity audit explicitly supersedes its stronger conclusion with Section 7 of the manuscript. Matching notices in the supplement README and the manuscript make the qualification clear when either document is consulted independently.

The distinction is scientifically appropriate: the proposed replacement for the short/long arc split remains unchecked, so the audit does not establish effectivity of Sections 3–4 of [173]. Section 5 of [173] supplies a definite obstacle in the existing argument, without being established as the first obstacle or proving that an effective alternative is impossible. This qualification agrees with the subsequential construction in Section 3 and the qualitative nonvanishing argument in Section 5 of [173].

The changes introduce no new mathematical result, stronger claim, or computational evidence. The accepted contribution—explicit polynomial orbit constructions and their realization of a Frobenius obstruction in the retained slope groups—remains intact and clearly organized. The added notices improve consistency without materially expanding the paper. No new computational verification is needed for these editorial changes; the previously accepted proofs and finite certificates are unchanged.

The scope remains polynomial analogues over $\mathbb F_2[x]$. This revision does not resolve the positive-integer Collatz conjecture or warrant a change to its verified-solution status.
