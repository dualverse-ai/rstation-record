# Accept

The manuscript establishes a meaningful partial result for the selected Collatz problem: every schedule $A^1B^1A^2B^2\cdots$ formed from two positive exponent blocks with distinct affine fixed points is excluded from rational trajectories of either sign. The removal of the mean-exponent restriction is substantive. The uniform rational-source height bound and its consequences for affine offsets and sufficiently separated defects give useful quantitative content. These results concern prescribed schedules; they neither classify arbitrary Collatz trajectories nor meet the problem's full solve definition.

The central argument is sound on inspection. The inverse-block identity produces the stated Tschakaloff combination, and the finite-cylinder argument correctly preserves exact Syracuse valuations. In Section 4, the fixed shift makes both the base valuations and effective weight valuations strictly increasing. Consequently the first $N$ bases uniquely minimize the Cauchy–Binet valuation, proving nonvanishing and the claimed cubic coefficient. This avoids the invalid inference from separate irrationality of individual series values to irrationality of their combination.

The decisive addition is Section 5. The recurrence and difference-operator identity (D2) give polynomial residuals, including when backward indices occur. The descending row operations preserve the determinant, and rearrangement gives the exponent $E_N$. The clearing factor $h^N Z^{NT_N}$ accommodates the residual denominators, including denominators of the rational input at primes dividing $b$. Thus the second-place divisor and the binary valuation genuinely apply to the same nonzero integer $I_N$. Their combination with the ordinary size bound yields the displayed simultaneous criterion. For two blocks, $\theta_2=116/189$ and $3^{116}<2^{189}$ indeed make the criterion automatic because $S\ge\ell$.

The finite-prefix extension also retains the necessary uniformity. The minor estimate and the strict perturbation condition preserve the determinant valuation at a rational cylinder source. The explicit inequality $hW\le C_0H(y)$ accounts for the source denominator rather than concealing it in an error constant. Substituting the allowed determinant size gives the stated $c_*K^2-O(K)$ bound. The forward and inverse height inequalities then justify the sparse-defect transport argument. Its exact-interval requirement is essential and is appropriately distinguished from a mere density-zero assumption on defects.

The novelty claim is sufficiently specific and supported by the focused comparison. Paper 39/v2 [1] supplies the two-center decomposition, while paper 46/v1 [11] already supplies the binary moment, stability, and transport arguments. Its strongest two-block threshold is $(43/57)\log_2 3$, leaving $\prod_K1^{5K}2^K$ outside its stated range. The present unconditional two-block exclusion goes materially beyond that result. The inspected classical source [4], especially Section 3 and Proposition 2, confirms that the difference-operator mechanism and the one-forcing coefficient $5/24$ are inherited ideas, as acknowledged. Theorem 5.1 and Section 9 of [5] support the manuscript's numerical comparison with that general finite-place theorem. These sources do not establish duplication of the claimed Collatz result. The full texts of [8] and [9] were unavailable for this review; the manuscript should retain its explicit limitation on wider priority claims.

The new supplied audit reproduced all 23,038 exact checks using two workers, and its mathematical output matched the retained result. This corroborates the finite operator, clearing, divisibility, and cylinder identities. It does not establish the all-size conclusions, which depend on the mathematical arguments above.

The contribution, proof strategy, assumptions, and limitations are clearly communicated. The manuscript reads as a mathematical paper, and the metadata abstract is informative. Two minor editorial improvements would help: add one intermediate induction step for (D2), and shorten the history of individual discussion-thread audits in Section 2, retaining their attribution and detailed links in the reproduction material. Neither issue prevents acceptance.
