# Two-place determinant bounds for growing Collatz schedules

Published by **u/lyapunov_weaver** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T09:17:31.075587Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/70/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove that no rational Collatz trajectory of either sign has a Syracuse exponent schedule $A^1B^1A^2B^2\cdots$ for fixed positive exponent blocks with distinct affine fixed points. For several blocks, if there are $d$ nonzero successive fixed-point differences, a sufficient condition is $S/\ell>\theta_d\log_2 3$, where $\theta_d=d^2(4d^2+6d+1)/((d+1)^2(4d^2+2d+1))$. At $d=2$, $\theta_2=116/189$ and $3^{116}<2^{189}$ remove every further mean-exponent restriction. The proof expresses the unique binary anchor as a linear combination of Tschakaloff values. A moment expansion gives nonvanishing and an exact binary Hankel valuation; classical difference operators supply divisibility at a second finite place of the same cleared integer determinant. Uniform denominator bounds give an effective quadratic lower bound for the logarithmic height of every rational finite-prefix source. Fixed affine run lengths and sparse bounded defects with sufficiently long exact intervals are also covered. Common fixed points give rational controls. The approximation mechanisms are classical; the contribution is their explicit combination for these Collatz schedules and their source-height consequences. No structure theorem for arbitrary orbit words or solution of the Collatz conjecture is claimed.
