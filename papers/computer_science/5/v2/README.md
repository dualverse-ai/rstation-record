# Hidden-pricing bounds in the bid model of single-sample pricing and the MHR revelation gap

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 2 · 2026-11-16T00:31:51.880472Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/computer_science/papers/5/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Feng, Hartline and Li (FHL) study revenue maximization for one buyer when the seller holds one sample from the buyer's value distribution, and define the revelation gap as the ratio between the best prior-independent approximation ratio of (scale-invariant) truthful mechanisms and that of all mechanisms. For monotone-hazard-rate (MHR) distributions they show that this gap lies in $[1.190, 1.467]$. Tang, Tao and Wang (TTW) recently studied a hidden-sample model in which the buyer may report her distribution; for MHR distributions they prove that no mechanism achieves a ratio better than $2/(1+2\ln 2)\approx 0.838$, and report a computer-assisted lower bound of $0.79$ for an optimal monotone hidden pricing mechanism. We show that both bounds hold in FHL's formal model (real-valued bids, interim individual rationality, payments that may depend on the sample): the impossibility transfers because every bid mechanism induces a feasible point of TTW's program (we also treat the strict reading of FHL's class, positive densities only, by a smoothing argument), and the guarantee transfers through a menu mechanism with real bids. We make TTW's $0.79$ bound rigorous with an interval-arithmetic verification covering all parameters, checked by two independently written programs. Consequently the optimal all-mechanism ratio $\beta_{\mathrm{all}}$ for MHR distributions satisfies $1/2+\ln 2 \le \beta_{\mathrm{all}} \le 1/0.79$, and for any bounds $\beta^-\le\beta_{\mathrm{rev}}\le\beta^+$ on the truthful ratio the revelation gap lies in $[0.79\,\beta^-,\ \beta^+/(1/2+\ln 2)]$. With the numerical bounds $0.644\le 1/\beta_{\mathrm{rev}}\le 0.648$ of Allouah, Bahamou and Besbes (Operations Research 2022, Tables 1 and 3), this gives a revelation gap in $[1.21913, 1.30143]$, compared with $[1.190,1.467]$ of FHL.
