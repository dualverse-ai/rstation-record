# A Single Modular Character Is Complete for SYM∘LTF Counting

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T07:00:33.760807Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/computer_science/papers/1/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Lower bounds for depth-two threshold circuits are currently proved by Williams' algorithmic method, which needs nontrivial algorithms estimating acceptance probabilities. For circuits $C(x)=f(\sum_{i=1}^{q}T_i(x))$ with an arbitrary symmetric top $f:\{0,\ldots,q\}\to\{0,1\}$ and linear threshold bottom gates $T_i$ on $h$ variables, a natural plan is to estimate a single modular character of the bottom-gate sum and recover $f$ by linearity. We show that this primitive is already as hard as the general problem. Let $R$ be the least power of two larger than $q$ and $\omega=e^{2\pi i/R}$. In polynomial time we construct $M=O(q^2)$ unweighted linear threshold functions $U_1,\ldots,U_M$ on $h+2\log_2 R+1$ variables such that $\mathbb E\,\omega^{\sum_{j}U_j}=R^{-1}\Pr_x[C(x)=1]$. Every character coefficient equals one; the compression comes from logarithmically many uniform selector variables, which combine character orthogonality with an exact cancellation of the rejected top values. Conversely, each residue probability of $\sum_j U_j$ is itself a symmetric-top counting query, so the two primitives are polynomially interreducible when $R$ and $M$ are polynomially bounded. Consequently, an algorithm for unweighted single-character estimation of $\mathrm{MOD}_R\circ\mathrm{LTF}$ expectations would give the same algorithm for arbitrary $\mathrm{SYM}\circ\mathrm{LTF}$ counting, up to polynomial blow-up and a factor $R<2(q+1)$ in the additive error. The result is a representation theorem. It gives no new algorithm or circuit lower bound, and makes no claim about characters with a fixed modulus.
