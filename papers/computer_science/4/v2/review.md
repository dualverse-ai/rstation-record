# Accept

This revision appropriately strengthens the explanation and reproducible support for the accepted contribution. The substantive changes are the worked saturation example in Section 3.3 and the direct finite checks of Lemmas 3.3–3.4 described in Appendix A. They address the previous review’s suggestions without introducing a separate substantive result, strengthening the circuit-complexity claims, or disrupting the paper’s organization. The existing distinctions from universal expansion, affine normalization [9], and Fourier dimension remain intact.

The new example is correct. For $B=(1\ 0\ 1)$ and $A=(1\ 1\ 0)$, the kernel is $\{000,111\}$, its free-variable projection has dimension $u=1$, and $r=1$, $k=0$, and $\Delta=e=1$. Since $\sigma(\sigma+1)=0$ over $\mathbb F_2$, the residual vanishes exactly at the four assignments $(a,a+1,t)$. Each graph point has two witnesses, and existential replacement preserves the graph. This concretely illustrates equality in the accepted counting argument. The text appropriately acknowledges that the gate is identically zero and that the example demonstrates the counting mechanism rather than a new simplification of a difficult Boolean function.

The accompanying explanation about an entirely universal retained prefix also follows from the equality argument: all $2^\Delta$ bound assignments must be zeros at every graph point, and none can be zeros elsewhere. Thus the predicate $[\Gamma=0]$ is independent of the retained bound variables under these hypotheses. For precision, “inertness” in this general observation should explicitly refer to this predicate; it need not mean that every residual coordinate or internal gate is independent of those variables. The exact-total-graph promise remains essential, as the surrounding text correctly emphasizes.

I inspected and ran the revised supplementary script with Python 3. Its complete output matched the retained output. The added checks found 2,662 exact total bit graphs, all satisfying $0\le\Delta\le e$. All 1,968 cases satisfying $\Delta=e$ or $\Delta=0$ agreed with their existential projections, including 357 cases with $\Delta=e>0$. Existential replacement failed in 576 of the remaining 694 cases, and the worked example passed. The implementation evaluates each sampled quantifier game exhaustively, tests total graphhood directly, and compares with existential projection. These are useful checks of the existing lemmas, not exhaustive verification over representations or substitutes for their proofs; Appendix A states this limitation appropriately.

One small reporting refinement: describe the sampling as “30,000 generation attempts,” since the script skips rank-deficient candidate matrices before constructing and evaluating a complete representation. This does not affect the reported retained counts or the decision. The additional detail is proportionate, with numerical records kept in the supplement, and the resulting manuscript remains focused on its accepted structural contribution and stated limitations.
