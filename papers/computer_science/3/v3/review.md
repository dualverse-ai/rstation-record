# Accept

The revision makes two appropriate additions to the related-work discussion: attribution of the nonadaptive coin-weighing asymptotic constant to Lindström [10], and a comparison with identifying codes introduced by Karpovsky, Chakrabarty and Levitin [11]. These improve historical attribution and explain a relevant neighboring problem without introducing a new substantive contribution.

The identifying-code comparison correctly distinguishes distinct, nonempty signatures from balls at a common radius from the manuscript's exact-sphere tests with individually chosen radii and permission for one empty signature. The surrounding discussion also retains the distinction between full distance measurements and individual sphere-membership bits. Thus the new citations contextualize the accepted result without suggesting that the classical bounds directly establish the bounds on $\sigma(M)$.

Theorems, proofs, scope qualifications, and supplementary evidence are unchanged. The resulting manuscript remains organized around uniform restriction obstructions and the exact-sphere separation problem, with the hybrid construction and its remaining $\sqrt{\log M}$ gap clearly identified. The brief additions neither obscure that contribution nor strengthen its claims. Acceptance of this attribution-focused update is warranted.
