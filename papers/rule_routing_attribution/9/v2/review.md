# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects and clarifies the accepted diagnostic contribution. It introduces no new substantive result and preserves a coherent argument: paired completion interventions distinguish answer-dependent ranking from preference for the documents actually used in training. The added figure presents existing evidence rather than expanding the paper into additional experiments.

The principal corrections are scientifically sound. Sections 1 and 4.2 now distinguish a negative answer-even contrast from the original MaxSim scores: $D_y=E+O$, so the reported S1-correct means imply approximately $-0.25+0.85=0.60$, favoring trained documents. Restricting “almost entirely answer-odd” to the summed and preconditioned scores properly accommodates MaxSim's larger even component on S1 errors. The explicit completion-specific standard deviations agree with the checker's separate standardizations. Acknowledging that 400 queries reuse training attribute assignments corrects the evaluation's scope without undermining the paired intervention. The revised membership summary appropriately recognizes reversed rankings and surface-form confounding rather than claiming zero information.

Figure 1 visually agrees with Table 2 and makes the distinction between the score families easier to see; the numerical values and uncertainty intervals remain available in the table. Inspection of the checker diff shows that the paired-analysis formulas are unchanged, while superseded analyses are now gated behind `--legacy`. This review checks the revised explanation, figure and code changes; it does not independently repeat the previous review's reproduction of the score matrices or rerun model training.

Specify summed and preconditioned scores there too. In Section 5, qualify “does not distinguish” and “none ... ranks trained documents above” with the same consistent-preference language now used in the contribution list.
- Complete the new reproduction sequence by explicitly obtaining `cto_T2_part00_core`, which is listed in the evidence URLs but omitted from the T2 steps, and identifying where its answer file is obtained. Use an isolated T2 working directory so replacing the answer mapping cannot inadvertently apply T2 correctness labels to retained T conflicting-pool matrices. Reconcile the README's parts 1–3 instruction with Section 7's parts 1–4, and provide a part-to-directory mapping.

These are minor documentation improvements. The revision strengthens the accepted paper's interpretation and readability while retaining its bounded scope: a two-data-order RR diagnostic, not a general membership-identification result or a demonstrated improvement across the official RR/FB-distr evaluation.
