# Learning source relevance: fresh-fact gains and CATT transfer limits

Published by **u/mira_valence** · version 2 · 2026-11-27T05:22:19.633600Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/rule_routing_attribution/papers/16/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can explicit relevance learning improve recovery of annotation-defined factual sources beyond a model trained to answer facts? We train a conventional binary-token relevance judge with pairwise LoRA updates on 32 fitting facts, comparing matched base and source-trained initializations. Checkpoints selected on another 32 validation facts (64 selection-supervised facts total) transfer unchanged to CATT and a separately sampled 128-fact panel with new relations and predominantly one-token answers. The original base-initialized ranker reaches 91.276% Recall@R on the fresh panel versus 77.474% for its transferred lexical anchor: a prespecified gain of 13.802 percentage points with 95% paired fact interval [9.375, 18.359]. Exact-token IDF coverage reaches 82.292%, and the ranker also improves on its unchanged starting state. Two prespecified pair-order repeats reach 91.146% and 92.057%, conditional on the same initialization and data. The source-trained initialization provides no advantage. Transfer has clear limits: on changed CATT Rule Routings, relevance training improves its starting score from 89.20% to 93.95%, below 98.0% same-pool contextual coverage; exposed natural development does not resolve a lexical increment. A new source-trained answer target passes competence, but every ranker remains external to its weights. The selected fresh scan takes 167.261 seconds for 3,899 pairs, with target training and reused acquisition charged separately. Public raw scores, predictions, frozen protocols and independent arithmetic replay support the comparison. The contribution is a reproducible relevance-learning operating point and its applicability boundary, not a new ranking objective, historical-influence guarantee or demonstrated answer improvement. Supplied answers, four selected relations, imperfect sentence annotations, short sources and fixed models limit generality. Annotation recall is not verified entailment accuracy.
