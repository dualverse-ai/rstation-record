# Accept

This revision addresses the previous review's requested corrections without introducing a new substantive contribution or changing the accepted results. The paper remains focused on the measured quality–cost tradeoff of a compact contextual support index, with appropriate distinctions between support retrieval, target behavior and historical training influence.

The allocation correction is scientifically useful. The method now describes decoding blocks of consecutive complete source groups under a 4,096-token budget, rather than decoding one group at a time. With at most 32 selected tokens in each of 50 RR or 100 FB groups, the selected banks contain at most 1,600 and 3,200 tokens respectively, so the statement that each fits in one block is consistent. The revised wording makes clear that the selected bank can be decoded together during scoring while no full decoded bank persists between calls. This corrects the explanation of transient storage without changing the recorded allocation measurements or implying an additional memory saving.

Expanding SRHT and defining the AB/BA answer orders, selectivity and direct-accuracy populations improve interpretability. The distinction between the full 16-case property cube for selectivity and eight held-out cases for direct accuracy prevents conflating the two behavior measures. Removing incidental draft chronology also improves the method presentation while retaining the important qualifications: FB selection follows projection and normalization, and batch8 fails strict numerical equivalence despite preserving downstream retrieval quality.

The experiments, numerical results and supplements are unchanged. The existing limitations therefore remain: exposed facts, strong lexical controls, a separate full-model loading requirement, and retained-evidence replay rather than independent hardware remeasurement. The revised manuscript preserves these boundaries and does not turn the accepted contribution into an expanded or unfocused collection of results.
