# Accept

This revision provides useful, limited supporting evidence for the accepted contribution. Section 6.1 tests the existing frozen kernels on additional rules and matched author-trained adapters; Section 8 controls a specific confound in the existing membership audit. Neither introduces a separate method or an independent substantive contribution requiring another paper. The manuscript remains organized around improving rule-support retrieval and explaining its limits as evidence of training-example influence. The added table and control do not materially obscure that argument.

The new retrieval comparisons are supported by the released matrices. Independently recomputing Recall@10 and paired fact-bootstrap intervals from archives M1 and M2 reproduces Table 2b to within 0.05 percentage points in recall, consistent with the documented float16 storage. In particular, A′ reproduces 83.15% summed and 88.95% position-resolved recall, resolving the previous review's distinction between the 500-candidate evaluation and the membership-corpus metric fit. On the additional rules, the summed gains over the retained baselines are approximately 32.35 and 34.65 points, with intervals approximately $[26.60,38.20]$ and $[30.60,38.70]$. The position-resolved recalls reproduce 89.25% and 89.80%. The supplied per-category validator counts also reproduce the reported accuracy and selectivity for A2, C and C2. These checks validate retained evidence and its aggregation; they do not independently reproduce model training or inference.

The count-matched FB analysis is a useful strengthening of the existing interpretation. Using archive J and the source corpus, I independently recover 786 retained constant documents across all 50 functions and equal mean counts of approximately 11.845. The unnormalized kernel's membership AUC changes from approximately $0.55936$ to $0.47290$, with a matched-subset interval of approximately $[0.43462,0.51103]$. The cosine kernel gives approximately $0.50600$ with interval $[0.46918,0.54135]$, and the count heuristic gives exactly $0.5$. These agree with the manuscript within storage precision. This evidence supports a count-confounded interpretation of the original positive membership discrimination.

The revision also addresses the previous presentation concerns: A′ appears in the swap table; Section 7 distinguishes persistent high retrieval from a replicated loss-restriction ablation; and the official-checkpoint limitation is distinguished from the additional author-trained adapters. Inspection of the revised archive L checker confirms explicit score-file selection, reconstruction of groups and restoration of scales before subtraction. The discussion of [3] correctly acknowledges prior evaluation of rules 51–100 and explains why its 100-rule target is not a matched numerical comparator.

A few minor qualifications would make the revision more precise:

- In Section 6.1, replace “the same” gain with “similar observed gains,” and restrict “does not depend on having selected them on rules 1–50” to the demonstrated persistence of an advantage on these additional rules. Two data orders with one initialization and the same grammar do not establish equivalence of gains or general independence from development choices. The existing distinction from official-run validation should remain explicit.
- In Section 8, describe the fall after matching as consistent with count imbalance explaining the original positive discrimination. Dropping unmatched documents changes the evaluated population and leaves other surface differences uncontrolled; it does not isolate a causal effect of count. Also retain the small positive activation/output effects and their intervals rather than implying that every score is exactly at chance.
- Make the FB analysis portable from the cited release. The new script expects local `scores.e22_*.npz` filenames, whereas archive J supplies `fbm_kron_dot.npz` and `fbm_kron_cos.npz`; it also expects three activation/output matrices absent from J. Provide an explicit mapping or command-line paths and identify the evidence location for those secondary rows. My independent reproduction covers the two Kronecker rows and the count control, not those three secondary rows.

These qualifications do not undermine the central findings or warrant rejection of the update.
