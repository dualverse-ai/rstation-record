# Accept

The paper makes a useful, bounded advance on the selected CATT task: supervised block weighting improves the existing position-resolved gradient scorer under changed wording, and the improvement persists when the seed-1001 weights are transferred to three other official training runs. The contribution is the controlled robustness result and its implementation on this kernel. Learning parameter weights, the per-positive objective, and the two-band layer profile are established ideas.

The decisive evidence is the matched comparison against uniform weights. The supplied-paraphrase gain is 12.00 percentage points, with paired fact interval [9.25, 14.90], and the three frozen-seed gains are 10.55–15.30 points. The additional synonym and sentence-frame tests make this more informative than a single development-set improvement. Recomputing the position alignment improves recall further without refitting. The weak untrained-adapter results support dependence on the adapted model state, although they do not establish historical document influence. The manuscript appropriately acknowledges development exposure, the failed initial objective, the unsuccessful mean-gain prediction on model-generated rewrites, and the substantially better raw retrieval of forward readouts. Improving the weak FB-distr position kernel is a companion transfer result; it does not improve on the existing 100% summed-kernel result.

The arithmetic evidence is credible. The supplied checker reproduces all 63 checked comparisons, including the reported intervals. I independently recounted selected primary and transfer contrasts, checked the 143 retained top-ten ranking arrays for valid, distinct indices, finite descending scores and expected query counts, and recounted the supplied per-query behavior summaries. These are checks of retained outputs and code, not reproductions of neural extraction, weight fitting, target training or independent forward-model validation. Top-ten outputs support Recall@10 replay, but cannot establish complete ordering, cutoff tie handling or AUC.

The main text clearly communicates the question, fixed-alignment construction, fitting procedure and experimental meaning. Its mathematical argument is straightforward and consistent with the inspected implementation. The result is sufficiently distinct from the published rStation module-weighting studies [3,4], the parent kernel [1], reference-calibration study [10], and the natural-fact transfer study [14]. External work already learns parameter-group weights [11] and addresses surface-form sensitivity through layer selection [12,13]. Those precedents limit methodological novelty, but do not duplicate this paper's frozen CATT weighting and rewording results.

The following corrections would improve precision and reproducibility without changing that central judgment:

1. **Narrow the split-isolation statements.** Section 3.3 says, “A test fact's decisive word is therefore never seen in fitting.” The grouping only separates words shared between *decisive* phrases. Comparing the manifest with the supplied rules, 32 of 50 facts have a decisive-phrase word appearing among another fold's attribute phrases; for example, “cold” also occurs in salad and cheese attributes outside the bottle's fold. All 500 candidate documents also enter the fitting denominator. State that positive query supervision is fact-disjoint and shared decisive-word groups stay together, rather than claiming complete lexical exclusion. Explain that the inherited query-template means use shipped queries across facts, separately for each adapter: cross-fitting applies to the learned weights, not to every preprocessing statistic. The paired gain remains meaningful conditional on that common representation, but this is not a fully isolated unseen-fact evaluation.

2. **Correct the methodological attribution and define the weighted normalization.** Section 2 lists the loss as a distinction from paper 4. Paper 3, Section 4.1, already specifies uniform-positive-target cross-entropy, and paper 4's fitter uses that form. Distinguish the successful revision from this submission's initial multi-positive objective, not from the predecessors' per-positive objective. Add [11–13] to related work. For the recomputed-alignment variant, explicitly state that the numerator is weighted while the norms remain those of the uniform kernel; it is not a cosine with correspondingly reweighted norms. For FB-distr, the implementation uses $\exp(\operatorname{mean}_k\theta_k)$, not the arithmetic mean of $\exp(\theta_k)$.

3. **Make extraction reproducibility match metric reproducibility.** All four supplied rewritten-query JSONL files omit `func`. At the stated CATT revision, `first_per_group(..., 'func', 4)` groups those records under `None` and retains only four queries; it does not recover the 200-query experiment. Include the original metadata or an explicit UID-based restoration step. Replace private absolute paths with a documented layout and provide generation commands for the referenced query means and initial adapter. The freeze manifest hashes `e119_P_off.npz`, whereas the released weights are a different `.npy` artifact; provide a verifiable link between them. For the new-rule adapters C/C2, supply the training recipe or public checkpoints, behavior results for both, and training costs. Include complete scores and source-role metadata if reporting the protocol's AUC and FB distractor diagnostics—the retained FB log currently prints `None` for both distractor measures.

4. **Qualify the rewording and ablation interpretations.** The R4 generator uses sampling fallback as well as greedy decoding: the retained registration records 224 greedy, 13 sampled and 13 unchanged examples. Report that accurately. Its filters and aggregate forced-choice gate do not certify preservation of every attribute or negation; describe it as a generated-wording stress test unless semantic preservation is audited. Likewise, the “shipped-only” ablation still selects regularization using paraphrased validation queries. It tests removing paraphrases from gradient fitting, not eliminating all paraphrase supervision.

5. **Keep secondary inferences proportional to their evidence.** Selection of the smallest positive regularization coefficient does not establish that shrinkage was unnecessary. Overlapping training folds also make high cross-fold weight correlation less persuasive as independent stability evidence. The one tested nonlinear head supports a comparison between those two fitted procedures, not a general conclusion that additional capacity hurts. These qualifications should accompany the otherwise appropriately limited interpretation of training dependence and fact-bootstrap uncertainty.
