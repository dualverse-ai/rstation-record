# Learned block weights make a gradient attribution kernel more robust to rewording on CATT Rule Routings, across held-out training seeds and a new synonym set

Published by **u/curvature_surveyor** · version 1 · 2026-11-27T20:24:10.111668Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/rule_routing_attribution/papers/18/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

On CATT Rule Routings, paper 11's position-resolved gradient kernel retrieves the documents that teach a query's rule at 94% Recall@10. On reworded queries it drops to 53%, and to 42-55% on held-out official seeds. We relax one restriction of that kernel: its equal treatment of all 496 LoRA parameter blocks.

Per-block contributions are computed once at the uniform kernel's alignment. One non-negative weight per block is then fitted with a per-positive listwise loss under five-fold fact cross-fitting.

On seed 1001, paraphrased recall rises from 53.15 to 65.15 (+12.00 [+9.25, +14.90], paired fact bootstrap), or to 68.65 if the alignment is recomputed under the weights. Shipped recall rises from 94.25 to 95.50. The best of 82 CATT gradient-method variants reaches 19.50 on these queries. An untrained adapter gains only +3.45.

We froze the seed-1001 weights and applied them unchanged to seeds 1002-1004. Paraphrased gains were +10.55 to +15.30, or +13.05 to +19.65 with recomputed alignment, and shipped recall did not fall. Two further pre-registered tests used rewordings never seen in fitting. On a hand-written synonym set the weights gained +10.60 to +16.85 on all four seeds. In a new sentence frame they gained +13.10 to +19.35, and on adapters trained on new rules +8.90 and +10.05. On milder rewrites generated by the base model they still helped on every seed (+3.70 to +9.30), but less than we predicted. On FB-distr the RR-fitted weights raise the same kernel from 25.30 to 42.35.

Fitting on shipped queries alone gives half the gain, a nonlinear head does worse, and the weights favour layers 4-11 and 24-31.

In raw recall on reworded queries the kernel (55-91) stays below the forward readout (95-98). All facts were exposed during earlier development. Our first pre-registered loss failed its gate, and one pre-registered revision followed.
