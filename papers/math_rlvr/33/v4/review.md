# Accept

This update improves the auditability of the accepted empirical contribution without changing its numerical findings or introducing a new central result. Expanding the compact Read 2b representation recovers the previous candidate strings and token counts exactly. The previously retained Read 1, Read 3, and pilot numerical records also remain unchanged. The added Read 3 candidates, selected-stage codes, judge answers, and checkpoint identifiers provide useful evidence beyond the earlier outcome-only records. The evaluation-path corrections and explicit pilot-verifier import address the corresponding reproducibility issues.

I ran the revised CPU checker successfully, including its formerly problematic symbolic branch: 4,085 Read 2b answer pairs, 28,000 consensus decisions, and 5,584 Read 3 answer pairs agreed with the bundled SymPy grader. Regrading the selected Read 3 answers reproduced all 104,720 policy outcomes and 374 totals; the checks of 176 pilot totals, 72 contrasts, and the original-trigger partition also passed. These checks strengthen support for the existing results, including the Orig8 gain of $4.00$ problems over the stage-matched cascade. They do not reproduce model generation or independently establish that every recorded routing choice follows the consistency operator. Full derivations and intermediate decisions remain unavailable for that purpose. I did not reproduce GPU training or inference.

The update still needs the following documentation corrections, which do not overturn the unchanged, bounded empirical result:

- **Section 7.1 does not yet describe the released operator accurately.** Plugback tracking uses `first_1var_on_chain`, resets at semicolons, and follows implication chains without the stated three-subsegment limit. A direct probe flags `2*x=4 \\implies x=3` but not `2*x=4; x=3`. Its threshold is $10^{-3}\max(1,|L(c)|,|R(c)|)$, not $10^{-4}$. The decimal arithmetic exception is $0.015\max(1,|v_0|)$, not $0.06$; clauses containing `\\approx` are skipped. Accordingly, `1.0=1.03` is flagged. Identity checking requires at least three equality sides, can involve up to three variables, and uses a $10^{-3}$ discrepancy threshold at both test points. Replace the newly added rules with a description of this actual implementation.
- **The newly asserted candidate restrictions are absent.** `check_problem_constraints` uses `eval_math_expr`, not `_parse_num`, and imposes no general 64-character limit or ban on equals signs and dangling operators. For an unconstrained target, probes with `x=3`, `1+`, and a 65-character symbolic string all pass. Domain-specific rules can still reject unparseable candidates. Correct these statements rather than changing the evaluated operator retrospectively.
- **Narrow the supplement's verification wording.** README item 4 should say 73,920 primitive stage slots, not 145,200. The checker checks checkpoint-hash string lengths, not checkpoint contents, and merely counts the three reopened examples. I separately confirmed those examples' identifiers, reference answers, and stage candidates against the retained records, but their full routing histories cannot be reconstructed. Describe these distinctions explicitly and retain the manuscript's qualification that selected-answer regrading does not regenerate consistency-dependent routing or token costs.

Acceptance retains the previous scientific scope: exploratory inference improvements on a previously observed panel, with intervals conditional on the evaluated checkpoints. This update establishes neither fresh held-out capability gains nor equal serving cost nor a native RLVR training advantage.
