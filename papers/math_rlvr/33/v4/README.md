# Compact Algebraic State Continuation, Multi-Projection Cascades, and Horizon Backtracking in Small Mathematical Models

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 4 · 2026-10-25T12:58:37.202426Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/33/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Under tight token budgets, small mathematical reasoning models (Qwen3-0.6B-Base, 751M untied parameters) frequently truncate mid-derivation or terminate early after an intermediate algebraic slip. Across 26 RLVR training runs, 10 RLVR checkpoints plus Base, and evaluations on Val210 (N=210) and Confirm350 (N=350), backed by a 1,935.7 KiB reproducibility archive retaining per-item stage candidates and routing decisions, we report five bounded findings. First, for truncation-triggered @512 continuation on Confirm350, deterministic 3-projection cascading across 16-line, 7-line, and setup-only algebraic prefixes solves 169.00/350 problems (Orig8, Read 2b), outperforming 2-attempt (+13.38+-2.66 (SE), z=+5.02) and 4-attempt (+7.12+-2.43 (SE), z=+2.93) escape fallback and 4-attempt majority voting (+5.50+-2.23 (SE)). Second, extending @1024 continuation into a 5-stage cascade gains +4.50+-0.94 (SE) over Pass@1(@1024) on Confirm350, with full-prefix last-state continuation accounting for +3.62+-0.85 (SE) and @512 backtracking adding +0.88+-0.37 (SE). Third, on the previously observed Confirm350 panel (Read 3), gating the 5-stage @1024 cascade with deterministic arithmetic, plugback, and target-domain consistency checks solves 183.12/350 problems (Orig8, 573.5 simulated output tokens), gaining +4.00+-1.21 (SE) (95% CI [+1.63, +6.37], z=+3.31) over the stage-matched unverified cascade and +5.62+-1.40 (SE) ([+2.88, +8.37], z=+4.02; exact +5.625) over single-pass Pass@1(@2048) (177.50, 573.0t), with 31/32 of the net cascade gain occurring on originally untriggered rollouts. Fourth, single-pass repair (+2.38+-1.55 (SE)) and costly pairwise arbitration (806.7t, +0.62+-1.17 (SE) over 3-stage gating) yield unresolved incremental gains, and multi-stage cascades incur prefill, KV-reset, and worst-case latency overhead not captured by output-token averages. Fifth, state-transition RLVR training established no native training advantage over standard controls.
