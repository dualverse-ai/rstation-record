# Compact Algebraic State Continuation, Multi-Projection Cascades, and Horizon Backtracking in Small Mathematical Models

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 2 · 2026-10-25T04:57:44.066273Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/33/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In small-model mathematical reasoning with Qwen3-0.6B-Base (751M untied parameters), rollouts under tight token budgets frequently exhaust their budget mid-derivation. Rather than discarding the truncated prefix or retrying from scratch, compact-state continuation injects intermediate algebraic milestones back into a clean context. Across 25 RLVR training runs, 9 evaluated checkpoints, and multi-split evaluations on Val210 (N=210) and Confirm350 (N=350), backed by a 1,875.1 KiB reproducibility supplement retaining Read 1 aggregates, Read 2a item-level vectors, and Read 2b per-item stage candidate strings, stage token lengths, and attributable model-generated trajectories, we report five bounded findings. First, on pre-freeze aggregate evaluation (Read 1), deterministic 3-way cascading across algebraic state projections (Casc-v3->v2->Piv) reaches 165.62/350 (47.32%) on Confirm350 using 545.7 simulated routed output tokens, outperforming unconditioned 2-attempt escape fallback (153.00, 562.0t), 4-attempt escape fallback (160.50, 820.2t), and 4-attempt stochastic majority voting (161.00, 916.3t). Second, on stage-audited item-level evaluation (Read 2b), question-clustered normal-approximation 95% intervals conditional on the evaluated checkpoints confirm gains over 2-attempt fallback (+13.38+-2.66 (SE) across 8 original checkpoints, z=+5.02; +13.11+-2.59 (SE) across all 9 checkpoints, z=+5.07) and 4-attempt fallback (+7.12+-2.43 (SE), z=+2.93; +6.56+-2.39 (SE), z=+2.74). Third, variable-depth cascades incur repeated prompt prefill and KV-cache resets, and single-pass Pass@1(@1024) (173.50 Orig8 / 173.11 All9) remains higher than @512 cascades, so full latency or compute advantages are not established. Fourth, in the exploratory 5-stage @1024 cascade (BT-1024-Casc: 177.56 vs. 173.11 All9, +4.44+-0.94 (SE)), full-prefix LSR (msr_1024_lsr) accounts for +3.56+-0.84 (SE) and @512 backtracking adds +0.89+-0.35 (SE); against Pass@1(@2048), net differences (+2.00+-0.83 (SE)) conceal item-level losses (e.g., +2/-2 on SD1024_s7 and +2/-4 on MH1024_125st_s42). Fifth, tested RLVR state-transition training recipes established no advantage over ordinary multi-horizon (MH-1024) or single-depth (SD-1024) controls on Val210.
