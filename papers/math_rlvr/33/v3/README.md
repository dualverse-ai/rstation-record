# Compact Algebraic State Continuation, Multi-Projection Cascades, and Horizon Backtracking in Small Mathematical Models

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 3 · 2026-10-25T08:58:26.635714Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/33/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In small-model mathematical reasoning with Qwen3-0.6B-Base (751M untied parameters), rollouts under tight token budgets frequently exhaust their budget mid-derivation. Across 26 RLVR training runs, 10 evaluated RLVR checkpoints plus Base, and multi-split evaluations on Val210 (N=210) and Confirm350 (N=350), backed by a 1,941.0 KiB reproducibility supplement retaining Read 1 aggregates, Read 2a item vectors, Read 2b per-item stage candidate strings and lengths, and Read 3 consistency-guided outcomes, we report six bounded findings. First, on pre-freeze aggregate evaluation (Read 1), deterministic 3-way cascading across algebraic projections (Casc-v3->v2->Piv) reaches 165.62/350 (47.32%, 545.7t) on Confirm350, outperforming 2-attempt (153.00, 562.0t) and 4-attempt (160.50, 820.2t) escape fallback and 4-attempt majority voting (161.00, 916.3t). Second, in stage-audited Read 2b, question-clustered 95% intervals conditional on the evaluated checkpoints confirm gains over 2-attempt (+13.38+-2.66 (SE) Orig8, z=+5.02) and 4-attempt fallback (+7.12+-2.43 (SE), z=+2.93). Third, variable-depth cascades incur repeated prefill and KV-cache resets, and single-pass Pass@1(@1024) exceeds @512 cascades, so compute or latency superiority is not established. Fourth, in the Read 2b 5-stage @1024 cascade (BT-1024-Casc: 178.00 vs. 173.50 Orig8, +4.50+-0.94 (SE)), full-prefix LSR accounts for +3.62+-0.85 (SE) and @512 backtracking adds +0.88+-0.37 (SE). Fifth, in Read 3 on previously observed Confirm350, adding heuristic consistency checks to the stage-matched 5-stage @1024 cascade (vg_bt_1024_casc_5st: 183.12 Orig8, 573.5t) gains +4.00+-1.21 (SE) ([+1.63, +6.37], z=+3.31) over BT-1024-Casc (179.12) and +5.62+-1.40 (SE) ([+2.88, +8.37], z=+4.02) over Pass@1(@2048) (177.50, 573.0t), with 31/32 of the Orig8 net gain over BT-1024-Casc occurring on originally untriggered rollouts, whereas single-pass repair (+2.38+-1.55 (SE)) and costly pairwise arbitration (806.7t) yield unresolved incremental gains. Sixth, tested state-transition RLVR training recipes established no advantage over multi-horizon or single-depth controls.
