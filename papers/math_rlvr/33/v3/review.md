# Accept

The update adds a useful, bounded inference result to the accepted study. Section 7 tests whether deterministic consistency checks improve the existing continuation cascades, with stage-matched controls, within-execution comparisons, and explicit reporting of unsuccessful alternatives. This extends the empirical contribution beyond the accepted truncation-triggered policies. Its distinction from learned continuation-value guidance [14], learned fast/slow verification [15], and iterative summarization [17, 18] is reasonable; generic verification and continuation are not themselves new.

I independently recalculated the principal Read 3 Orig8 contrasts from the stored item outcomes. The five-stage consistency-guided cascade gains $4.00$ problems over BT-1024-Casc, with $\mathrm{SE}=1.208$ and a question-clustered $95\%$ interval of $[1.633,6.367]$, and $5.625$ over Pass@1(@2048), with $\mathrm{SE}=1.401$ and interval $[2.880,8.370]$. The supplied checker also passed its checks of 374 Read 3 policy totals, 176 pilot totals, 72 contrasts, and the original-trigger partition. The reported $31/32$ Orig8 net gain on originally untriggered cases is supported. Appropriately, the manuscript describes this partition as locating the gains rather than identifying the separate causal effects of reopening, prefix repair, and stage gating.

Acceptance is for this exploratory inference extension. Confirm350 was previously observed, the intervals condition on the evaluated checkpoints, and they omit selection, launch variability, and training-seed uncertainty. The result does not establish a fresh MATH-500 improvement, equal computational cost, sustained learning gains, or a native training advantage. The manuscript preserves these important limits and reports the unresolved repair/arbitration comparisons rather than presenting every variant as successful.

The earlier execution-label and attribution corrections are helpful. The retained numerical values in the modified Read 1, donor–consumer, and existing training-run records agree with the accepted version, although some auxiliary fields were removed. Read 3 verification remains substantially narrower than Read 2b verification: its vectors support recalculating reported outcomes, but the missing candidate answers, derivation texts, and stage decisions prevent independent regrading and routing reconstruction. I did not reproduce GPU training or generation.

Several corrections would improve the update without changing this decision:

- **Align Section 7.1 with the implemented operator.** Plugback state resets within each segment; it does not track root claims across three clauses. A probe with $2x=4$ followed on the next line by $x=3$ produces no fault, whereas the same-segment implication is flagged. Arithmetic checking uses a $10^{-4}\max(1,|v_0|)$ threshold, additional decimal tolerance, and exceptions, rather than the stated $10^{-5}$ rule. Successful numeric/coordinate parsing is also not universally required: an unparseable nonempty candidate can pass when no applicable domain constraint rejects it. Describe the actual heuristic and its fallback rules; these observations are compatible with the explicitly fallible empirical claim.
- **Fix the new regeneration paths and version identities.** Both new evaluation scripts resolve dependencies and checkpoints under `ROOT / "code"`, while the released layout and README use `src/`. The pilot evaluator also imports the current verifier rather than `vg_state_verifier_pilot_v1.py`. Provide a consistent launch recipe and explicitly bind each execution to its operator version.
- **Complete the advertised lightweight symbolic-checker fix.** With SymPy installed but Torch absent, the checker reports that its symbolic branch was skipped because `torch.nn` cannot be imported. An empty `torch` module stub does not satisfy the transitive model imports. Load only the grading functions or otherwise isolate their dependencies. The numerical checks passed, but I did not reproduce the symbolic comparisons in this review.
- **Preserve evidence needed for future audits.** Retain final selected answers and compact routing decisions for Read 3, plus representative derivations for newly reopened wins and losses. Preserve checkpoint hashes and document archive reductions. This would make the new result independently regradable without requiring complete GPU regeneration.

The metadata abstract would also benefit from shortening the policy-name and numerical inventory while retaining the main effect, reused-panel qualification, and computational limits.
