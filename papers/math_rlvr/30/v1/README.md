# Retrofitting Pretrained Transformers With Under-Relaxed Recurrent Depth for RLVR: Production-Path Diagnostics, Compute-Matched Controls, and Dual-Depth Routing on Qwen3-0.6B-Base

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 1 · 2026-10-23T09:24:02.637517Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/30/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We present a controlled, fully regradable study (15 runs, 58,773 SymPy-regraded candidate answers, 0 grading discrepancies) testing middle-block recurrence (R=2 passes over layers [12:20], 36 effective layers) on untied Qwen3-0.6B-Base (751,632,384 base parameters) under GRPO (L_train=1024, K=4). Separating Val210 (used at Step 0 to select gamma_0=0.05) from untouched HeldOut740 = Ext240 (200 GSM8K + 40 AMC23) + MATH-500 across Seeds 42, 101, and 7, we establish four findings. First, on the production 28-layer BF16 model across all 210 Val210 prompts, un-gated recurrence (gamma=1.0) shifts the Coda-entry representation h_20 by 85.57% in relative L2 norm (logit KL=0.3057) and collapses 50-step GRPO to 201.00 +- 7.55 / 740 on HeldOut740 Fast@2048 (-168.33 +- 14.57 vs. 50-step Single-Depth), whereas LayerScale-style gating (GRD, gamma_0=0.05, +9,216 parameters) bounds Coda-entry shift to 5.09% (KL=0.00237) and recovers 376.33 +- 10.02 / 740 (R_eval=2) and 379.33 +- 9.87 / 740 (R_eval=1 bypass_loop=True). Second, an on-policy Step-3 GRPO gradient decomposition shows the extra-pass core parameter gradient is only 2.25% of the primary core gradient (cos=0.999770), refuting a Sobolev-regularization mechanism, and frozen gamma=0.05 matches trainable GRD on HeldOut740 Fast@512 (338/740 vs. 337/740, Seed 42). Third, while 50-step GRD (R_eval=1) exceeds 50-step Single-Depth on HeldOut740 (+10.00 +- 6.08 at @2048; +9.33 +- 5.03 at @512), a 65-step compute-matched Single-Depth control (1,820 vs. 1,800 layer-steps) reaches 380.67 +- 6.81 / 740 at Fast@2048 (-1.33 +- 4.04), closing the long-horizon single-pass gap. Fourth, we validate deployed online EscFB batching (346/740 on HeldOut740 Fast@512) and show that 2-attempt stochastic Single-Depth retry matches 3-seed GRD EscFB accuracy (386.67 +- 4.93 vs. 386.00 +- 7.55 / 740 at @2048) at comparable prefill+decode layer-token FLOPs.
