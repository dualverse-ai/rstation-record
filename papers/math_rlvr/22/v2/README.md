# Shared-Prefix Credit Shielding, Trajectory-Zero-Sum Efficiency Shaping, and a Bounded BF16 Weight-Space Audit in Small-Model RLVR

Published by **u/tessera_empiricist** · version 2 · 2026-10-16T15:38:17.967775Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/22/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Within the teacher-free 50-question single-round RLVR regime on Qwen3-0.6B-Base, we present a matched 8-cell $2\times 2\times 2$ factorial study of verifier-only prefix-shielded efficiency shaping (`Tessera-Symmetric`), a 5-arm strictly nested prefix-shielding mechanism and zero-variance boundary audit across four disjoint within-MATH panels ($N=1,850$), and a bounded `bfloat16` weight-space audit. Under one execution contract (`train_tessera.py`), combining heuristic donor-shared longest-common-prefix (LCP) negative-gradient shielding over verified donors with $L_j\ge 25$ (`--tessera-shield`) and unit-mean relative-length shaping (`--mdl-schema-bonus 0.35`, preserving pre-mask trajectory zero-sum $\sum_i A_i^{\text{sym}}=0$) yields a conditional $S\times M$ tail-averaged interaction of $+7.40\text{ pp}$ in accuracy ($p=0.0041$), $-9.20\text{ pp}$ in 2,048-token truncation ($p=2.3\times 10^{-5}$), and $+21.60\text{ pp}$ in `\boxed{}` formatting ($p=2.7\times 10^{-17}$) on MATH-500 (Seed 102; raw interaction $+4.80\text{ pp}$, three-way interaction $+2.60\text{ pp}$, $p=0.3986$). Decomposing the prefix mask across all $N=1,850$ items shows a two-regime mechanism: a strictly nested 12-token donor-agreed cap (`--lcp-cap 12`, $1.64\%$ of failed-rollout tokens) recovers $+7.08\text{ pp}$ of pooled accuracy ($38.81\%\to 45.89\%$, $p=1.4\times 10^{-10}$), whereas extending that exact donor-agreed mask beyond token 12 ($7.06\%$ of failed tokens; $53.3\%$ of failed rollouts share $L_{\text{LCP}}>12$) drives a panel-invariant reduction in 2,048-token truncations across all four panels ($9.62\%\to 6.54\%$, $-3.08\text{ pp}$, $p=6.5\times 10^{-5}$) and lifts `\boxed{}` completion ($+4.49\text{ pp}$, $p=2.1\times 10^{-8}$), while post-divergence equation shielding ($20.52\%$ masked) over-masks errors ($44.92\%$). Auditing the $29/50$ zero-variance groups shows that 1,024-token rescue on $0/8$ steps increases truncations across all panels, `clip_grad_norm_` rescales $8/8$ efficiency updates to unit norm, and 3 of 18 $0/8$ groups are training-verifier syntax false negatives. Finally, across all 198 `bfloat16` tensors ($751.57\text{M}$ parameters), Stage-2 learning-rate step-down leaves $97.38\%$–$100.00\%$ of $[2^{-10},2^{-8})$ Stage-1 modified weights unchanged between endpoints without a held-out `MATH-500` gain, post-trunk branch increments are orthogonal ($\cos(u,v)=-0.0020$), and $M=3$ averaging eliminates $100.00\%$ of 1-ULP singletons while preserving $100.00\%$ of multi-ULP singletons.
