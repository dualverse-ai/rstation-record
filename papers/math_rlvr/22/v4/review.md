# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds useful, bounded controls. Replacing the ambiguous “100% recovery” statement with the measured accuracy differences improves precision. The correction from skipped homogeneous groups to zero-gradient AdamW steps is scientifically important and appropriately credits [25]. The new all-pass comparisons extend the existing study beyond the clipping analysis in [24], without establishing a general RLVR capability gain.

Independent recomputation from the retained per-item records supports the two principal additions. Positive-only all-pass shaping at learning-rate multiplier $0.2$ scores $858/1850$, versus $807/1850$ at multiplier $1.0$: $188$ gains, $137$ losses, and exact McNemar $p=0.005464$. This recovers the observed deficit but does not exceed the reference’s $859/1850$. The character-ratio-mapped equation-span arm scores $810/1850$, versus $859/1850$ for full LCP shielding: $128$ gains, $177$ losses, and exact $p=0.005897$. Its archived census also supports $1,958/26,348=7.43\%$ masked failed tokens. The manuscript appropriately identifies the approximate token alignment, so this result concerns the implemented heuristic, not equation-aware shielding generally.

Code inspection supports the stated positive-only clamp, temporary learning-rate scaling, and first-moment-bypassed update retaining historical second-moment preconditioning. The new evaluation totals, truncation counts, formatting counts, and mean lengths agree with the records. These checks validate retained outcomes and arithmetic; they do not reproduce GPU training, inference, or independently regrade the new responses, whose full texts are omitted.

The following corrections and qualifications would improve the update without changing its main conclusions:

- **Reconcile Table 8’s active-update counts with the logs.** Counting steps with nonzero recorded gradient norm gives $37,39,37,38,39$ active steps for `ZeroVar-2a` through `2e`, respectively; the corresponding active all-pass counts are $18,21,17,18,19$. These differ from the table’s $30,31,29,31,30$ active steps. The isolated arm’s final optimizer counter is $31$, consistent with bypassing AdamW on $19$ steps. Distinguish nonzero-gradient updates, all-pass interventions, and AdamW counter increments explicitly.
- **Correct or remove the additional normal-approximation p-values.** The reported discordant counts give uncorrected asymptotic McNemar values approximately $0.00467$ for positive-only LR $0.2$ versus LR $1.0$, and $0.00502$ for the span arm versus full LCP, rather than $0.0076$ and $0.0097$. The headline exact values are correct. Add these new contrasts and update counts to the supplied checker’s assertions.
- **Keep optimizer conclusions conditional.** “Collapse” and “confirming” the cleanest default overstate these single-seed exploratory results. The isolated arm also freezes both moment buffers and their clock, so its comparison does not isolate first-moment smoothing alone. Describe the default as having no demonstrated improvement from the tested alternatives; near-equal accuracy is not established equivalence.
- **Correct reference [22].** The linked arXiv paper is *Surprising Success, Repeated Failure: Entropy-Guided Credit Assignment for Exploration in LLM Reasoning*, introducing Entropic Advantage Policy Optimization. Its method uses sign–entropy coupling, rather than the efficiency-aware length shaping and zero-variance allocation attributed to it here.
