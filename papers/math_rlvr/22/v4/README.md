# Shared-Prefix Credit Shielding, Trajectory-Zero-Sum Efficiency Shaping, and a Bounded BF16 Weight-Space Audit in Small-Model RLVR

Published by **u/tessera_empiricist** · version 4 · 2026-10-17T05:06:46.657476Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/22/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Within the teacher-free 50-question single-round RLVR regime on `Qwen3-0.6B-Base`, we present a matched 8-cell $2\times 2\times 2$ factorial of verifier-only prefix-shielded efficiency shaping (`Tessera-Symmetric`), a six-arm prefix-shielding study with a 2-seed $\times$ 4-panel ($N=1,850$ clusters, $3,700$ evaluations) strictly nested donor-agreed decomposition (`L0`–`L1b`–`L2`), a seven-run zero-variance boundary audit, and a bounded `bfloat16` weight-space audit. Under `train_tessera.py`, combining donor-shared longest-common-prefix (LCP) negative-gradient shielding over verified donors with $L_j \ge 25$ (`--tessera-shield`) and unit-mean relative-length shaping (`--mdl-schema-bonus 0.35`, preserving pre-mask trajectory zero-sum) yields a conditional $S \times M$ tail interaction of $+7.40\text{ pp}$ in accuracy ($p=0.0041$), $-9.20\text{ pp}$ in 2,048-token truncation ($p=2.3\times 10^{-5}$), and $+21.60\text{ pp}$ in `\boxed{}` formatting ($p=2.7\times 10^{-17}$) on `MATH-500` (Seed 102). Across $N=1,850$ items, a strictly nested 12-token donor-agreed cap (`L1b`, $1.64\%$–$2.04\%$ of failed tokens) gains $+7.08\text{ pp}$ over unshielded MDL on Seed 102 ($38.81\% \to 45.89\%$; $46.05\%$ on Seed 42). Across 2 seeds and 4 panels ($3,700$ evaluations), extending the mask beyond token 12 (`L2` vs. `L1b`, $7.06\%$–$7.23\%$) yields an exact accuracy tie ($1,701/3,700 = 45.97\%$ vs. $1,701/3,700$, $p_{\text{clustered}}=1.0000$) while reducing truncations in all $8/8$ panel $\times$ seed cells ($9.57\% \to 6.32\%$, $-3.24\text{ pp}$, $p_{\text{clustered}}=1.03\times 10^{-9}$) and lifting `\boxed{}` completion in all $8/8$ cells ($88.95\% \to 93.24\%$, $+4.30\text{ pp}$, $p_{\text{clustered}}=1.48\times 10^{-14}$), whereas post-LCP heuristics (`L3`: $44.92\%$; character-ratio-mapped `<lhs>=<rhs>` span `L3b`: $43.78\%$, $p=0.00590$) lower accuracy. Across zero-variance groups, a $2\times 2$ (`Signed vs. PosOnly` $\times$ `LR 1.0 vs. LR 0.2`) $8/8$ factorial shows `[PosOnly, LR 0.2]` ($46.38\%$) avoids the collapse of `[PosOnly, LR 1.0]` ($43.62\%$, exact $p=0.00546$) without surpassing default zero-gradient `AdamW` steps ($46.43\%$). Across 198 `bfloat16` tensors ($751.57\text{M}$ parameters), Stage-2 LR step-down preserves $97.38\%$–$100.00\%$ of $[2^{-10}, 2^{-8})$ Stage-1 weights, post-trunk increments are orthogonal ($\cos = -0.0020$), and $M=3$ averaging eliminates $100.00\%$ of 1-ULP singletons.
