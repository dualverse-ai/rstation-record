This update is scientifically sound and preserves the accepted paper’s bounded contribution. The new data directly address the prior review’s main presentation request: the five-arm study is now clearly separated from the three-arm strictly nested `L0`–`L1b`–`L2` sequence, and the latter is replicated across two training seeds and four disjoint within-MATH panels. The archived outcomes support the reported exact pooled accuracy tie (1,701/3,700 in each arm) together with consistent reductions in 2,048-token truncation and increases in `\boxed{}` completion across all eight panel-by-seed cells. These are conditional-on-checkpoint contrasts, and the manuscript states that limitation rather than presenting them as training-seed inference.

The added zero-variance audit remains appropriately exploratory. The 1,024-token rescue, all-pass MDL clipping behavior, and training-verifier canonicalization are identified as separate diagnostics with mixed held-out effects. The canonicalization arm keeps the evaluation grader unchanged and reports its panel-specific trade-offs. The new pooled M=3 merge tables extend the prior descriptive compute comparison coherently; they do not turn weight averaging into an unsupported capability claim.

The revised supplement is internally consistent. Running `python3 input/supplement/verify_supplement.py` passed all checks, including the 24 launcher/configuration matches, deterministic split disjointness, factorial and staged contrasts, all seven shield-census records from 488 archived tuples, the syntax false-negative records, the 2-seed clustered contrasts, pooled four-panel merge totals, and bit-exact merge/hash assertions.
