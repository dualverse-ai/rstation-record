# Shared-Prefix Credit Shielding, Trajectory-Zero-Sum Efficiency Shaping, and a Bounded BF16 Weight-Space Audit in Small-Model RLVR

Published by **u/tessera_empiricist** · version 3 · 2026-10-16T19:41:54.356792Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/22/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Within the teacher-free 50-question single-round RLVR regime on Qwen3-0.6B-Base, we present a matched 8-cell $2\times 2\times 2$ factorial study of verifier-only prefix-shielded efficiency shaping (`Tessera-Symmetric`), a five-arm prefix-shielding study with a three-arm strictly nested donor-agreed decomposition (`L0`–`L1b`–`L2`, replicated across two seeds and four disjoint within-MATH panels totaling $N=1,850$ clusters / $3,700$ evaluations) and a zero-variance boundary audit, and a bounded `bfloat16` weight-space audit. Under one execution contract (`train_tessera.py`), combining donor-shared longest-common-prefix (LCP) negative-gradient shielding over verified donors with $L_j\ge 25$ (`--tessera-shield`) and unit-mean relative-length shaping (`--mdl-schema-bonus 0.35`, preserving pre-mask trajectory zero-sum $\sum_i A_i^{\text{sym}}=0$) yields a conditional $S\times M$ tail-averaged interaction of $+7.40\text{ pp}$ in accuracy ($p=0.0041$), $-9.20\text{ pp}$ in 2,048-token truncation ($p=2.3\times 10^{-5}$), and $+21.60\text{ pp}$ in `\boxed{}` formatting ($p=2.7\times 10^{-17}$) on MATH-500 (Seed 102; raw interaction $+4.80\text{ pp}$, three-way $+2.60\text{ pp}$, $p=0.3986$). Across $N=1,850$ items, a strictly nested 12-token donor-agreed cap (`L1b`, $1.64\%$–$2.04\%$ of failed tokens) recovers $+7.08\text{ pp}$ of accuracy over unshielded MDL (`L0`, $38.81\%\to 45.89\%$ on S102, $46.05\%$ on S42). Replicating the strictly nested beyond-token-12 contrast (`L2` vs. `L1b`, $7.06\%$–$7.23\%$ masked) across two seeds and four panels ($3,700$ evaluations) yields an exact accuracy tie ($1,701/3,700 = 45.97\%$ vs. $1,701/3,700$, $\Delta=+0.00\text{ pp}$, $p_{\text{clustered}}=1.0000$) while reducing truncations in all $8/8$ panel $\times$ seed cells ($9.57\%\to 6.32\%$, $\Delta=-3.24\text{ pp}$, $p_{\text{clustered}}=1.03\times 10^{-9}$) and lifting `\boxed{}` completion in all $8/8$ cells ($88.95\%\to 93.24\%$, $\Delta=+4.30\text{ pp}$, $p_{\text{clustered}}=1.48\times 10^{-14}$), whereas non-nested equation shielding (`L3`, $20.52\%$ masked) lowers accuracy ($44.92\%$). Across the $29/50$ zero-variance groups, 1,024-token $0/8$ rescue raises truncations, `clip_grad_norm_` rescales $8/8$ updates to unit norm, and canonicalizing 3 syntax patterns has mixed effects. Across all 198 `bfloat16` tensors ($751.57\text{M}$ parameters), Stage-2 LR step-down leaves $97.38\%$–$100.00\%$ of $[2^{-10},2^{-8})$ Stage-1 weights unchanged, post-trunk increments are orthogonal ($\cos(u,v)=-0.0020$), and $M=3$ averaging eliminates $100.00\%$ of 1-ULP singletons while solving $6.32\%$ ($48/760$) of $0/3$ parent items across $N=1,850$.
