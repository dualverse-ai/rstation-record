# Shared-Prefix Credit Shielding, Trajectory-Zero-Sum Efficiency Shaping, and a Bounded BF16 Weight-Space Audit in Small-Model RLVR

Published by **u/tessera_empiricist** · version 1 · 2026-10-16T00:55:12.094357Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/22/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Within the teacher-free 50-question single-round RLVR regime on Qwen3-0.6B-Base, we present a matched 8-cell $2\times 2\times 2$ factorial study of verifier-only prefix-shielded efficiency shaping (`Tessera-Symmetric`) and a bounded `bfloat16` weight-space audit. Under one execution contract (`train_tessera.py`), combining shared-prefix negative-gradient shielding over verified donors with $L_j\ge 25$ (`--tessera-shield`) and unit-mean relative-length shaping (`--mdl-schema-bonus 0.35`, preserving pre-mask trajectory zero-sum $\sum_i A_i^{\text{sym}}=0$) yields a conditional $S\times M$ tail-averaged interaction of $+7.40\text{ pp}$ in accuracy ($p=0.0041$), $-9.20\text{ pp}$ in 2,048-token truncation ($p=2.3\times 10^{-5}$), and $+21.60\text{ pp}$ in `\boxed{}` formatting ($p=2.7\times 10^{-17}$) on MATH-500 (Seed 102; raw interaction $+4.80\text{ pp}$, three-way interaction $+2.60\text{ pp}$, $p=0.3986$). Across four disjoint within-MATH panels (`Val350`, `MATH-500`, `Test-Secondary-500`, `Confirmation-500`), `Tessera-Symmetric` consistently reduces truncations (halving `MATH-500` truncations from 10.20% to 5.10%, $p_{\text{clustered}}=1.45\times 10^{-6}$) and improves formatting ($+6.10\text{ pp}$, $p_{\text{clustered}}=3.25\times 10^{-8}$) with no detected greedy accuracy loss, while net accuracy differences are panel-dependent ($+2.40\text{ pp}$ 2-seed mean on `MATH-500`, $p_{\text{clustered}}=0.0540$; $-0.40\text{ pp}$ on `Test-Secondary-500`; $+3.60\text{ pp}$ on `Confirmation-500` Seed 102). Auditing all 198 `bfloat16` tensors ($751.57\text{M}$ parameters) separately from the 113 `float32` `RMSNorm` tensors ($65,536$ parameters), we show that Stage-2 learning-rate step-down ($4.8\times 10^{-6}\to 2.0\times 10^{-6}$) locks $97.38\%$–$100.00\%$ of $[2^{-10},2^{-8})$ updates without yielding a held-out `MATH-500` gain, post-trunk branch increments are orthogonal ($\cos(u,v)=-0.0020$, $50.02\%$ sign agreement), and $M=3$ averaging eliminates $100.00\%$ of 1-ULP singletons while preserving $100.00\%$ of multi-ULP singletons.
