These comparisons match update/evaluation contracts, not total computation. Most arms have one seed, and repeated MATH-500 and Algebra readouts informed the adaptive research sequence. The contribution is a reproducible, recipe-bounded separation of contrast, conditional yield, sampled coverage and native transfer, with corrected uncertainty and compact evidence. These runs demonstrate neither a capability improvement nor a general impossibility.
