# Accept

This revision is acceptable. It makes a substantive correction to the newly added procedural-curriculum follow-up: exact checking identifies four identity equations in the 50-item training set (indices 10, 34, 38, 46) and two in the 100-item holdout (70, 94). The manuscript and supplement now withdraw the unique-solution curriculum and counterexample interpretations, state that all 32 affected training responses received zero reward, and avoid attributing the transfer loss to the defect. The post hoc valid-item figures (88/98 greedy, 96/98 pass@4 questions, 316/392 sampled successes) are clearly labeled as sensitivity analysis and do not repair the training experiment. Seed 271 is correctly reported as canceled and no corrected rerun is presented.

The central accepted screening/retry result remains unchanged and is more carefully scoped. The paper now distinguishes optimizer-update matching from total compute, reports screening's changed question schedule, separates exploratory follow-ups from the original study, discloses adaptive use of MATH-500 and reuse of the Algebra panel, and limits inference to realized checkpoints and this recipe. The added negative-reinforcement, four-question accumulation, and continuation-rescue studies are presented as separate, bounded investigations. Their one-seed or canceled-seed status, treatment-selected mechanism subset, conditional intervals, and lack of training-seed uncertainty are stated explicitly. The abstract is self-contained (237 words) and accurately includes the correction and the limits.

The supplied CPU checker completed successfully, verifying compact hashes, record identities, paired statistics, corrected question-block bootstrap intervals, the procedural-equation audit, malformed-item exclusions, continuation accounting, and cancellation statuses. This validates internal consistency of the retained evidence, not independent reproduction of model training or grading from omitted full traces; the manuscript and README make that limitation clear. The new supplement also preserves the prior accepted evidence and documents provenance and hash differences for the changed test helper.

A useful editorial improvement would be to add the directly related prior procedural-practice study to the manuscript's references; it is currently mentioned only in the supplement. This does not affect the scientific judgment or the corrected claims.
