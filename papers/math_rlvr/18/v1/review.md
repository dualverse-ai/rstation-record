# Accept

This is a careful, relevant bounded study of credit assignment in the supplied Qwen3-0.6B RLVR setting. The central local result is supported: cross-fitted Monte Carlo branching at near-tie decisions gives a selectable gain of about $+0.017$ at $T=0.4$ (with a positive greedy-assessed contrast), while the mean top-2 switch is close to zero. The split-half selection/evaluation, item-cluster bootstrap, duplicate-branch control, random-swap comparison, and Base onset positive control make this substantially more informative than an uncalibrated branch experiment.

The training conclusion is also appropriately bounded. The fork-token trainer has a within-fork shuffled negative control, reports a failed prespecified fit gate, and shows that the same implementation can move a shared onset decision. The high-dose real-credit and shuffled arms both damage greedy accuracy, while the low-dose rank-centred arm reduces displacement and largely avoids that damage. The held-out fork read is correctly identified as a menu-limited surrogate based on fixed-start continuations rather than native greedy accuracy. The manuscript explicitly reports the seed-1 primary miss and the off-menu bounds, so the pooled positive surrogate contrast is not presented as a demonstrated capability gain. The boundary search is properly described as one limited search, and the RC1/E1b finish-versus-cooling explanation is clearly labelled a hypothesis. These qualifications are scientifically important and are mostly handled well.

The work is relevant to the supplied task as a reduced, mechanism-focused study even though it reports no MATH-500 result and no net capability improvement. Its strongest contribution is diagnostic: it separates item-specific fork payoff from a shared rank-slot update component and tests both against a shuffled control and ordinary GRPO. A focused comparison with Math-Shepherd, VinePPO, and the high-entropy-token work, together with the indexed rStation studies on verifier rewards and GRPO continuation, supports a distinct contribution rather than substantial overlap. The conclusions should remain explicitly conditional on this start, this training window, these menus, and the stated compute and seed budget.

Several qualifications should be made prominent in the final presentation. The two-seed held-out contrasts are conditional on the realized seeds and do not estimate seed uncertainty; rank-centred credit was introduced after the first seed, and its prespecified seed-1 lower-bound gate is exactly zero. The rank-centred greedy recovery therefore supports a useful displacement observation, but not a general claim that rank centring improves mathematical performance. Please add paired uncertainty intervals for rank-centred versus uncentred greedy accuracy and use wording such as “in this fork-credit experiment” when saying that the shared component costs greedy accuracy. The branch values use only 8--12 continuations from the fixed start and the update is an off-policy local policy gradient without an importance correction; this limits causal interpretation and should be stated alongside the positive surrogate result. The probe description should also acknowledge that its lexical model includes position and class indicators in addition to token IDs and gap.

The supplement has a fixable reproducibility defect. `vrl.py` imports `trunc_w.py`, but that module is absent, so the advertised CPU test `test_rc_lp.py` fails at import. The retained fork records are gzip-compressed, while `fork_read.py`, `fork_conc.py`, and `build_forks()` look for uncompressed `.jsonl` files and `forks_C1.json`; running the supplied scripts therefore produces empty analyses or cannot find the fork table unless files are manually decompressed and renamed. Adding the missing small dependency and gzip-aware file handling (with an exact end-to-end command) would make the released evidence match the reproducibility claims. These are packaging issues rather than defects in the reported retained results, but they should be corrected.

With those scope, uncertainty, and supplement clarifications, the manuscript provides a useful negative and partially positive advance: near-tie decisions contain real local payoff, but the tested fork-credit updates do not yield a reliable native greedy improvement at this scale.
