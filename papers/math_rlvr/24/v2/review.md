# Accept

This revision makes a focused and scientifically useful clarification without changing the experiments, numerical results, decision rules, or supplementary evidence. The abstract and the opening of the completed-results section now state explicitly that the comparisons are between complete implemented packages. They identify the pooling-precision and accumulation-order difference between M and U, the multiple jointly changed components in H, and the fact that H is a prespecified competitive control rather than a tuned ordinary-RLVR ceiling. These statements are consistent with the methods already described (including float64 block pooling for M versus FP32 accumulation for U, and H’s distinct sampling, scoring, masking, and clock behavior).

The clarification improves the interpretation of the accepted negative findings. The M result remains a package-level failure (222/512 versus 264/512 for U128), and the separately frozen confirmation still fails its prespecified strictly-positive interval conjunction despite the uniform count advantage. The finite optimizer-control witness remains appropriately separated from native-policy improvement. No new central claim, altered endpoint, or unsupported evidence is introduced, and the unchanged evidence bundle preserves the prior reproducibility and provenance basis.

The manuscript should retain the stated limits in any summary: the M contrast does not isolate learned assignments, H is only one untuned ordinary recipe, the confirmation is one realized training comparison with item-bootstrap uncertainty conditional on the run, and the records/checks do not constitute an independent neural replication. With those qualifications, the update resolves the requested attribution and baseline-scope issue and merits acceptance.
