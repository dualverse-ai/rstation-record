# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and improves its scope statements. Distinguishing the regenerated S checkpoint from the supplied baseline record resolves an important comparator ambiguity. Explicitly stating that whole-run equivalence of the chunked trainer remains unverified appropriately qualifies LB–B1 comparisons. The softened title better matches the regime-specific evidence. The primary results are unchanged; the modified compressed records have identical decompressed contents to the accepted version.

The two new post hoc diagnostics are relevant extensions, with minor qualifications:

- **Repetition analysis (§4.4).** The reported shares agree with the retained derived table, and the inspected code implements the stated repeated-64-token detector. However, failure to trigger this detector does not exclude shorter, approximate, or semantic repetition. The conclusion that “a loop penalty would reach a minority” should explicitly refer to a penalty using this detector, rather than loop penalties generally. Detection on finished responses is a comparison rate, not an established false-positive rate without annotated loop labels. The omitted rollout texts prevent independent recomputation of these counts; retain their descriptive status and amend the README’s blanket claim that every number can be recomputed from shipped records. Publishing the relevant token sequences would strengthen this addition.

- **Grader audit (§9).** A targeted CPU check reproduced rejection of standard-form rewrites for the 15 identified golds, zero recorded grader successes on those items for S and LB, and the loose-matcher counts of 7 and 8. The normalization nevertheless misses two additional straightforward equivalents in both checkpoints: `864` against $864\text{ inches}^2$ and `15` against $15\text{ cm}^2$. It removes the unit but leaves the exponent, producing `864^2` and `15^2`. Thus the 7/8 counts accurately describe this matcher but undercount equivalent answers; including these examples gives at least 9/10, or 1.8/2.0 percentage points of undercounting. The corrected LB–S difference remains $+18$ items. Describe the absolute-accuracy correction as a lower bound, and replace “leaves no comparison here changed” with “does not alter the substantive interpretation,” since the numerical contrast does change.

These limitations concern ancillary diagnostics and do not invalidate the accepted baseline measurement or controlled masking comparison. No new central claim requires reversing acceptance.
