# Accept

This update preserves the accepted scientific contribution and directly addresses the three material issues in the previous review.

The coverage analysis is now correctly described. The manuscript withdraws the v4 “truth-0 calibration” interpretation for the seed-24 masked arm, identifies the shared question stream, and uses the seed-25 arm as the different-stream comparator. The revised claims are appropriately bounded: the horizon statistic is negative and does not meet the preregistered GO criterion, while the comparator is near zero with a wide interval. The amendment to the horizon preregistration is also disclosed clearly: the update-120 MATH-500 result was obtained under a score-only gate before the later monotonicity failure, so it is reported as descriptive rather than as a compliant preregistered endpoint. These changes remove an interpretation and gating ambiguity without changing the underlying result.

The added evidence is inspectable and internally consistent. The portable coverage implementation uses a stable candidate ordering and the shipped question streams and per-question counts. Running it on a temporary reconstruction reproduced the reported horizon values (0, −3 and −9 items), the solved-only value, the seed-25 comparator (−1 [−16, 15]) and the overlapping seed-24 comparison. The new vector converter likewise reproduces the soup comparison, +1.2 percentage points with 95% interval [−2.6, 5.0], from the compressed per-item records. The per-10-update summaries support the newly stated masking and truncation trajectories. These checks verify retained numerical evidence, not a fresh training or grading run; the manuscript makes that limitation clear.

The new supplement launchers remove private paths and expose the relevant arguments. The DAPO attribution for masking overlong samples is a useful clarification, and reference [36] is supplied. No new central claim is introduced that would require a different evidentiary standard. The conclusions remain appropriately cautious: masking removes a collapse failure mode, the horizon run declines after its descriptive peak, the soup does not resolve a gain, and forced-onset training does not outperform its matched control. The abstract, scope statements, limitations and numbered references remain adequate.

For reproducibility, add one explicit sentence to the supplement instructions showing that `analysis/restore_items.py` must first restore `splits/split_v1.json` (and giving the expected baseline-pool path) before invoking `v5/coverage.py`; the script currently assumes those files even though they are packed or supplied externally. This is a minor packaging clarification and does not affect the scientific validity of the update.
