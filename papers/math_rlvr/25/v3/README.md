# Homogeneous-Group AdamW Stepping, Pre-Boxed Derivation Gating, and Branched Rollout Credit in Small-Model Mathematical RLVR

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 3 · 2026-10-17T05:32:41.926058Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/25/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We present a twelve-arm, three-seed (42, 101, 7) diagnostic study of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) on Qwen3-0.6B-Base across 43 primary training runs (2,352 steps; plus 12 external-evaluation retraining runs) and 119 evaluations (41,120 independently regraded responses, including 2,700 sampled-k=3 rollouts) on an untouched 240-item external benchmark (GSM8K-200 + AMC23-40), a 210-item validation split, and MATH-500. First, on the 54%-62% of steps with homogeneous binary rewards, replacing zero-advantage backward passes with explicit O(P) zero-gradient AdamW updates is bitwise equivalent on any fixed trajectory sharing a common active-step loss while cutting analytical output-token dense FLOPs by 43.2% and padded full-sequence FLOPs by 41.3% (3.5% same-host wall-clock reduction). Second, in two-stage (4x2) branched rollouts, we contrast an F_t-measurable stopped tree preserving capped sampling marginals against an outcome-dependent root-restart sampler. Under both samplers at a 512-token cap, a sibling-disagreement prefix bonus (beta=0.25 vs. 0.0) shows no detected benefit within two-level 95% CIs, and a descriptive pair-ledger census shows that reward disagreement occurs on 7.5%-8.7% of pairs with 48.9%-66.7% of divergent pairs having their R=0 leaf at the 512-token cap. In a single-factor 1,024-token stopped-tree contrast (Arm 12 vs. Arm 5, split fixed at 160), cap hits drop 4.2x (24.4% to 5.8%), Both<Cap divergent pairs triple (5.0 to 15.0/run), and MATH-500 rises on all three seeds from 44.20% +/- 0.53% to 46.47% +/- 0.81% (+2.27 pp; pooled item 95% CI [+0.13, +4.47] pp, two-level 95% CI [-0.73, +5.13] pp). Finally, a 100-question accumulation recipe (Arm 11) reaches 46.20% +/- 0.35% on MATH-500, and shortest-completed tie-breaking (MDL-Maj@3) improves 3-seed greedy voting over MaxLen-Maj@3.
