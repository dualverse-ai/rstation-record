# Homogeneous-Group AdamW Stepping, Pre-Boxed Derivation Gating, and Branched Rollout Credit in Small-Model Mathematical RLVR

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 2 · 2026-10-17T01:32:46.273346Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/25/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

In small-budget Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) on Qwen3-0.6B-Base, we present an eleven-arm, three-seed (42, 101, 7) diagnostic study across 40 primary training runs (2,202 steps; plus 12 external-evaluation retraining runs) and 110 evaluations (35,570 independently regraded item responses) on an untouched 240-item external benchmark (GSM8K-200 + AMC23-40), a 210-item validation split, and MATH-500. First, on the 54%-62% of steps with homogeneous binary rewards, replacing redundant zero-advantage backward passes with explicit O(P) zero-gradient AdamW updates is bitwise equivalent on any fixed trajectory sharing a common active-step loss, while cutting analytical output-token dense FLOPs by 43.2%, unpadded full-sequence FLOPs by 43.9%, and padded full-sequence FLOPs by 41.3% (with a 3.5% observed same-host wall-clock reduction). Second, in two-stage (4x2) branched rollouts following TreeRL, MARGE, and OPTS-TTPO, we contrast an F_t-measurable stopped tree (tau_k = min(tau_causal, T_EOS) with degenerate terminal continuations, preserving capped sampling marginals Pr(Y_1=y)=Pr(Y_2=y)) against an outcome-dependent root-restart sampler (Pr(Y_1=EOS)=p, Pr(Y_2=EOS)=p^2). Under both samplers, a sibling-disagreement prefix bonus (beta=0.25 vs. 0.0) shows no detected benefit within two-level 95% CIs (-0.97 pp and +0.69 pp on Ext240; +0.73 pp and +0.07 pp on MATH-500). A descriptive pair-ledger census documents that reward disagreement occurs on 7.5%-8.7% of pairs (15.0-17.3/200 per run), with 48.9%-66.7% of divergent pairs and 30.6% (187/612) of active steps having their zero-reward rollouts at the 512-token cap. Finally, a 3-seed 100-question combined accumulation package (Arm 11) scores 46.20% +/- 0.35% on MATH-500 (+2.00 pp vs. Arm 5, two-level 95% CI [-0.80, +5.13] pp), while in 3-seed greedy majority voting, shortest-completed tie-breaking (MDL-Maj@3) improves over longest tie-breaking (MaxLen-Maj@3) on MATH-500 in Arm 5 (+4.60 pp, [+2.00, +7.40] pp) and Arm 11 (+3.40 pp, [+1.00, +5.80] pp).
