# Homogeneous-Group AdamW Stepping, Pre-Boxed Derivation Gating, and Branched Rollout Credit in Small-Model Mathematical RLVR

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 4 · 2026-10-17T15:25:22.884861Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/25/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We present a thirteen-arm, three-seed (42, 101, 7) diagnostic study of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) on Qwen3-0.6B-Base across 46 primary training runs (2,502 steps; plus 12 external retraining runs) and 128 primary evaluations (43,970 independently regraded responses, including 2,700 sampled-k=3 rollouts) on an untouched 240-item external benchmark (GSM8K-200 + AMC23-40), a 210-item validation split, and MATH-500. First, on the 54%–62% of steps with homogeneous binary rewards, replacing zero-advantage backward passes with explicit O(P) zero-gradient AdamW updates is bitwise equivalent on any fixed trajectory sharing an active-step loss while cutting analytical output-token dense FLOPs by 43.2% (3.5% same-host wall-clock reduction). Second, in two-stage (4x2) branched rollouts, we contrast an F_t-measurable stopped tree preserving sampling marginals against an outcome-dependent root-restart sampler. In a complete 2x2 (512 vs. 1,024 tokens x beta = 0.0 vs. 0.25) stopped-tree factorial (Arms 5, 6, 12, 13), sibling-disagreement prefix credit (beta=0.25 vs. 0.0) yields no detected accuracy benefit at either horizon (two-level 95% CIs span zero; MATH-500 interaction -1.80 pp, [-6.93, +3.47] pp). A descriptive census and prefix audit (gt512_prefix_audit) document that expanding the horizon to 1,024 tokens reduces cap-associated active steps from 29.3% to 5.6%, tripling completed Both<Cap divergent pairs, with 87/90 and 107/118 of >512-token successful solutions achieving correctness only after token 512, raising MATH-500 from 44.20% +/- 0.53% to 46.47% +/- 0.81% (+2.27 pp; two-level 95% CI [-0.73, +5.13] pp). Finally, a 100-question accumulation recipe (Arm 11) reaches 46.20% +/- 0.35% on MATH-500, and shortest-completed tie-breaking (MDL-Maj@3) improves 3-seed greedy voting over MaxLen-Maj@3.
