# Homogeneous-Group AdamW Stepping, Pre-Boxed Derivation Gating, and Branched Rollout Credit in Small-Model Mathematical RLVR

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 8 · 2026-10-19T03:28:46.170104Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/25/v8) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We present a nineteen-arm, three-seed (42, 101, 7) study of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) on Qwen3-0.6B-Base across 64 primary training runs (3,422 steps; plus 12 external retraining runs) and 182 primary evaluations (66,142 independently regraded responses across PASS 1 and PASS 4) on GSM8K-200 + AMC23-40 (N=240), a 210-item validation split, and MATH-500. First, on the 54%–62% of steps with homogeneous binary rewards, replacing zero-advantage backward passes with O(P) zero-gradient AdamW updates is bitwise equivalent on any fixed trajectory while cutting analytical output FLOPs by 43.2% (3.5% same-host wall-clock reduction). Second, in two-stage (4x2) rollouts, an F_t-measurable stopped tree preserving sampling marginals contrasts with outcome-dependent root-restart sampling. In a 2x2 (512 vs. 1,024 tokens x beta = 0.0 vs. 0.25) stopped-tree factorial (Arms 5, 6, 12, 13), sibling-disagreement prefix credit (beta=0.25 vs. 0.0) shows no detected benefit at either horizon (MATH-500 interaction -1.80 pp), while raising horizon to 1,024 tokens cuts cap-associated active steps from 29.3% to 5.6% (87/90 and 107/118 of >512-token correct completions fail at token 512 before passing) and lifts MATH-500 from 44.20% to 46.47%. Third, on 98 discordant items where Arm 12 seed=42 succeeds and seed=7 fails, teacher-forcing shows 96.83% argmax agreement and 85.25% top-2 containment at mismatches; across all 950 items, tie-gated progressive low-margin forking (TieGated-FG05@3.48) resolves 60.6% of 3-way ties, reaching 514/950 = 54.11% (+3.68 pp; [+2.00, +5.37] pp). Finally, across six 3-seed 1,024-token Online Counterfactual Fork-Pair GRPO package variants (Arms 14–19; 920 steps replayed in PASS 4), Rescue-Gated CF-GRPO (Arm 15) eliminates 4/4 corruptions (0 vs. 13 in Arm 14), reaching 47.67% +- 0.83% greedy (61.60% Pass@3) on MATH-500 and 52.56% +- 0.75% on Pool950, while Arms 17–19 reach 58.11% Pool950 MDL-Maj@3 and 63.00% MATH-500 Pass@3 (70.00% 6-model union) with single-policy 95% CIs spanning zero.
