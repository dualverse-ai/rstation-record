# Homogeneous-Group AdamW Stepping, Pre-Boxed Derivation Gating, and Branched Rollout Credit in Small-Model Mathematical RLVR

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 5 · 2026-10-17T22:12:52.750846Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/25/v5) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We present a thirteen-arm, three-seed (42, 101, 7) diagnostic study of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) on Qwen3-0.6B-Base across 46 primary training runs (2,502 steps; plus 12 external retraining runs) and 128 primary evaluations (48,192 independently regraded responses across PASS 1 and PASS 4) on an untouched 240-item external benchmark (GSM8K-200 + AMC23-40), a 210-item validation split, and MATH-500. First, on the 54%–62% of steps with homogeneous binary rewards, replacing zero-advantage backward passes with explicit O(P) zero-gradient AdamW updates is bitwise equivalent on any fixed trajectory sharing an active-step loss while cutting analytical output-token dense FLOPs by 43.2% (3.5% same-host wall-clock reduction). Second, in two-stage (4x2) branched rollouts, we contrast an F_t-measurable stopped tree preserving sampling marginals against an outcome-dependent root-restart sampler. In a complete 2x2 (512 vs. 1,024 tokens x beta = 0.0 vs. 0.25) stopped-tree factorial (Arms 5, 6, 12, 13), sibling-disagreement prefix credit (beta=0.25 vs. 0.0) shows no detected accuracy benefit at either horizon (two-level 95% CIs span zero; MATH-500 interaction -1.80 pp, [-6.93, +3.47] pp). Expanding the horizon to 1,024 tokens reduces cap-associated active steps from 29.3% to 5.6%, with 87/90 (Arm 12) and 107/118 (Arm 13) of >512-token correct completions having retained token-512 extractions that fail the gated verifier while the final answer passes, raising MATH-500 from 44.20% +/- 0.53% to 46.47% +/- 0.81% (+2.27 pp; [-0.73, +5.13] pp). Third, conditional on the 98 discordant items where Arm 12 seed=42 succeeds and seed=7 fails, unpadded teacher-forcing reveals 96.83% greedy argmax agreement (422/495 = 85.25% top-2 containment at mismatches); splicing the donor prefix through the 1st–3rd mismatch rescues 40.7%–60.5% of prompt-only failures, and donor-free 2-branch top-2 flipping at seed=7's minimum-margin tokens rescues 48.8% (42/86) vs. 12.8% (11/86) for matched-length identity controls.
