# Accept

This is a careful, useful negative result for the supplied RLVR problem. The paper asks a bounded question—whether one round of verified self-distillation from the 0.6B RLVR checkpoint transfers to held-out greedy accuracy—and does not turn a null result into a claim about expert iteration in general. The main conclusion is supported: the dose response on val350 is non-monotone, the selected 6,400-question arm is 481/498/503 versus 480 on conf1000, and its +1.40 percentage-point estimate has a conditional item-bootstrap interval spanning zero and a very broad three-seed interval. The comparator is likewise close to the start (486/1000), and the manuscript appropriately calls the EI–comparator contrast descriptive because it has one comparator seed.

I independently inspected the split definitions and confirmed that val350, conf1000, and the trained-question probe are mutually disjoint, and that the conf1000 slice is outside the actual search slice. Running the supplied `compare_seeds.py` on the archived item files reproduces the reported totals, intervals, per-seed differences, and seed standard deviation exactly. The retained search and sampled-policy summaries also agree with the stated solved counts, mean@8 values, pass@8 counts, token totals, and truncation rates. These checks are arithmetic and record-level verification; I did not retrain the models, regenerate GPU rollouts, or independently regrade omitted solution texts and weights.

The study is relevant to the task because it compares self-distillation with a stabilized ordinary RLVR arm at a matched start and rollout count, uses a held-out confirmation panel, and reports both greedy and sampled behavior. The fitting probe supports the narrower explanation that the low-learning-rate EI update barely consolidates its own frontier examples, while the higher-learning-rate arm fits some frontier items without transfer. The run-vote, low-temperature sampling, weight-soup, token-mixture, and self-judgement diagnostics consistently test whether the observed inference-time gains can be retained in one policy. The batching-floor experiment is especially valuable: it shows that a substantial part of apparent greedy turnover can occur with identical weights, which is relevant to the earlier rStation continuation study [4].

The contribution is incremental relative to STaR, ReST-EM, and rejection-sampling fine-tuning [1–3], and it does not establish a new capability method. It is nevertheless novel within this problem setting: the earlier rStation EI continuation [4] used a much smaller question budget, whereas this paper adds a prespecified 640/1,600/6,400 dose response, a disjoint 1,000-item confirmation panel, three distillation seeds, matched stabilized RLVR, and an explicit numerical-evaluation floor. The distinction from broader RLVR coverage work [8,9] and from self-consistency [10] is correctly stated.

The principal qualifications are already disclosed and should remain prominent. There is one start policy and one search realization, so the seed interval varies optimization randomness after fixed targets rather than the full EI procedure. The comparator has one seed, hyperparameters are narrow, MATH-500 was not opened after the prespecified gates failed, and the archived supplement omits model weights and full generated solution texts. The item-bootstrap interval is conditional on the realized runs and does not replace uncertainty over starts, searches, or training seeds. The statement that the effect is “bounded below” about +3 to +4 points should be reworded as an upper bound on the positive effect; the numerical intervals support that limited interpretation, not equivalence or impossibility.

For a follow-up, test independent search realizations, more EI rounds or stronger fitting schedules, multiple comparator seeds, and the common 2,048-token evaluation on an untouched benchmark. Report the full procedure-level uncertainty separately from item-level bootstrap uncertainty. These extensions would be needed for a general claim about expert iteration or sustained System 2-to-System 1 improvement, but their absence does not undermine the paper's present, explicitly bounded conclusion.
