# Accept

This revision makes appropriate corrections and scope clarifications to the accepted contribution. It introduces no new substantive result and preserves the central argument: a causal survivor baseline retains the on-policy pass@k gradient, with exact limits on per-depth centering and comparator-dependent variance tradeoffs.

The changes resolve the principal ambiguities identified in the previous review:

- Section 1 now separates the opposite noise-factor crossovers against the sequence-centered and positive-only estimators from the strict noise-factor improvement against Walder and Karkhanis's Section 4.2 estimator [8]. This agrees with Equations (26)–(28b). The equal within-class variance assumption and the distinction between a noise factor and total gradient variance remain explicit.
- Theorem 2(6) now restricts its impossibility statement to fixed sample-only weight maps independent of $(\sigma,u)$. The added Bernstein-basis independence argument correctly explains why unbiasedness across survival probabilities forces coefficient matching on each survivor-count stratum. The degree mismatch establishes that no such estimator can have zero mass throughout a stratum $1\le M\le k$; it does not prescribe the residual's placement for every estimator. This use of the equal-length specialization of Proposition 1 in [7] is appropriate. For $M=1$, the obstruction follows directly because zero mass forces the sole weight to vanish.
- Section 7 correctly distinguishes the single-$k$ guarantee from the general count-stratum lift. The sufficient threshold $m_t\ge k_{\max}(\mu)+1$ follows immediately from Equation (23), and the stated odd-$G$ binary GRPO-std consequence follows from the existing coefficient cancellation.

The shortened abstract and revised introductory catalogue retain the construction, scientific meaning and limitations. The paper remains dense, but these changes do not obscure its contribution or expand it into an unfocused collection. Its significance remains theoretical credit assignment relevant to RLVR; it supplies no new evidence of improved Qwen3-0.6B-Base mathematical accuracy. The supplementary computations are unchanged. My verification of this revision was analytical; I did not rerun the checker previously reproduced in the acceptance review.

Two minor wording refinements remain useful. In Theorem 2(6), replace “single realization $\{c_t=1\}$” with “event $\{c_t=1\}$,” which generally contains several reward realizations. In Section 7, define $k_{\max}$ only for a nonzero multiplier, or explicitly handle $\mu\equiv0$, whose lifted weights and mass vanish identically. Further shortening of repeated theorem summaries and detailed empirical discussion would improve readability, but is not required for acceptance of this correction-focused revision.
