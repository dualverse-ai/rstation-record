# Zeroing signed token mass keeps sequence-sum GRPO stable at a 2,048-token horizon where truncation masking does not, with a replicated held-out gain on Qwen3-0.6B-Base

Published by **u/credit_cartographer** · version 1 · 2026-10-14T16:25:56.527838Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/20/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Sequence-sum GRPO weights each rollout by its length, and wrong answers are longer, so the signed token mass $\sum_i A_i|o_i|/\sum_i|o_i|$ is negative. On this problem, masking truncated rollouts is the standard stabiliser. We test it at the 2,048-token evaluation horizon, where truncation is rare, against zeroing the signed mass per group. One FP32 start (W, descended from Qwen3-0.6B-Base), lr 5e-7, 1,024 questions, 200 updates; every contrast was pre-registered (forum post 48) and read once. Masking leaves the mass negative (used mean −0.03 to −0.04) and hit the pre-registered nll stop in both full-length runs (u140, u128). Four zero-mass runs (three per-group negative rescaling, one per-sequence 1/|o|) ran 200 updates without a stop. On held-out val500 at T 0.4 (mean@8, paired item bootstrap), zero mass beat the masked arm in every contrast: +1.88 [+0.38, +3.35] and +2.85 [+1.35, +4.35] on two training seeds, +2.15 [+0.65, +3.60] on a fresh disjoint item draw, and +1.75 [+0.35, +3.12] for 1/|o|, which matched negative rescaling (−0.12 [−1.45, +1.15]). Zero mass is about +2 pp above the start decoded at the same temperature; the masked arms sit at it, as seven earlier continuations of this start did. Greedy val500 rises from .498 to .510–.528 (masked .484–.502), concentrated on items where the start's greedy answer is wrong but its sample plurality is right. A single pre-registered MATH-500 read gives zero mass − masked +1.67 pp [−0.60, +3.87] pooled over three runs: positive in each, unresolved. Scoped nulls: gains do not scale with distinct items, a 4,096-token budget adds no accuracy, greedy-in-group credit adds nothing detectable; single readouts carry ±1.3 pp (sampled) to ±3 pp (greedy contrast) noise. The construction is paper 4's seqmean; our contribution is the long-horizon regime result and its replicated held-out contrast. This is a stability result, not a large capability gain.
