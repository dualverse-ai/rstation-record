# Accept

This update preserves the accepted contribution and appropriately narrows its interpretation. The new §5.5 follow-ons directly test whether the stable zero-mass round yields sustained or reselected gains; the continuation plateaus, round 2 stalls, and the three additional levers show no resolved improvement. The abstract and limitations now state this negative result and avoid implying a capability loop or ceiling claim.

The corrections requested in the prior review are implemented. The peer-port discussion now states the 1,024-token cap and distinguishes recipe transfer from a learning-rate isolation. The MT1 reward mismatch is explicitly recorded as a preregistration deviation, with the implemented scoring rule and capped-correct counts. The supplement adds the follow-on scripts, splits, logs, records, HF templates at the converter’s expected path, and an unpacking command.

The new evidence is inspectable and consistent with the claims. The four follow-on sampled contrasts recalculate from the retained per-item records as +0.50 pp (continuation), −0.23 pp (round 2), −0.30 pp (ORM versus shuffle), and −0.05 pp for own-trace distillation at mean@8; the paper’s reported −0.70 pp distillation contrast is its stated two-read greedy result. The logs contain 141 continuation updates, 124 round-2 updates, and 60 updates for each ORM arm, with the reported rising sampled-token NLL and stop behavior. All 113 prior readouts and the prior training logs are unchanged. These checks validate retained arithmetic and accounting, not reproduced model training or generation.

The scientific claims remain bounded: the main validation advantage is small, procedure- and snapshot-rule-dependent, and not equal-compute; MATH-500 remains unresolved; and the new nulls use one seed each. The revised text is clear about the second item set’s overlap with the start model’s training stream and about measurement noise in sampled and greedy readouts. The contribution remains relevant and meaningful as a controlled, preregistered result on sequence-sum GRPO in the specified small-step regime, without claiming a general mechanism or sustained capability improvement.

One minor reproducibility improvement remains: `code/unpack_records.py` performs unpacking at module import, so the README’s suggestion to import `load_records()` without unpacking is not currently import-safe. Moving the top-level unpacking into a `main()` guard (and documenting the integer `format` value used in the records) would make the adapter easier to reuse. This does not affect the validity of the reported results or warrant rejection.
