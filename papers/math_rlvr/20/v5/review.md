# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds a useful, directly relevant replication. The new §5.6 comparison starts from the task’s regenerated 50-update checkpoint (S50) and uses the same item split, seed, optimizer settings, and stopping procedures for the zero-mass (NS) and masked (MSK) arms. The retained records reproduce the reported validation values (.445, .476, .513), MATH-500 counts (221, 229, 254), and all three new paired-bootstrap intervals, including NS − MSK of $+5.0$ percentage points with $[+1.4,+8.6]$. The logs also support the stated MSK stop at update 124 and the 200-update NS run. This strengthens the claim that the observed contrast transfers to the supplied-start lineage.

The inference remains appropriately bounded. The new replication has one training seed per arm, and the primary comparisons include the procedures’ stop and snapshot rules: NS receives 200 updates while MSK is read at update 100 after stopping at 124. The MATH-500 result is therefore evidence for this complete procedure comparison, not an equal-compute estimate or a general claim about zero signed mass. The manuscript discloses these limitations, keeps the earlier unresolved three-run MATH-500 result, and does not claim sustained or large capability improvement. The wording “MT1 minus the first 50” should be clarified as removal of the training-pool indices in the first 50-question baseline (only two MT1 entries), since it could otherwise be read as removing 50 MT1 entries.

The correction to $\bar W$ is scientifically sound. It removes the earlier independence assertion, documents the seed overlap and common-random-number comparisons, and states that intervals treating $\bar W$ as fixed do not estimate new engine-seed or training-realization uncertainty. This makes the uncertainty scope more accurate without changing any result.

The added loop/depth, minority-weight, FP32-delta, layer-inference, and resume-guard code paths are inactive in the reported configurations and do not alter the retained evidence. For reproducibility, the supplement should add the two new post-48 preregistration records identified in the change summary to `prereg/preregistrations_post48.json`; that file currently does not include the §5.6 S50/replication entries despite the README saying it contains every preregistration and readout. This is a documentation improvement rather than a scientific defect.
