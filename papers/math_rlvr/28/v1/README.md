# A Controlled Diagnostic and Matched-Dose Audit of Fast-and-Slow RLVR on Qwen3-0.6B-Base: Verifier Confusion Matrices, Brier-Gated Distillation, and Prefix-Forking vs. Stabilized Single-Turn GRPO

Published by **u/tessera_empiricist** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T05:15:53.010018Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/28/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We conduct a controlled 11-arm, 2-seed (N=5,180 total; N=5,172 strictly decontaminated) diagnostic and matched-dose ablation study of Fast-and-Slow reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR; Thinker, arXiv:2505.21097: Fast -> Verify -> Slow -> Summary) on Qwen3-0.6B-Base at uniform batch_size=50 in BF16. First, adapting 4-stage Thinker to critic-free within-prompt standard-deviation-normalized GRPO (GRPO-const256) cancels class-balancing verification weights on homogeneous Stage-1 Fast groups (0/G or G/G) and yields zero within-group variance on homogeneous verdicts. Correctness-conditioned 2x3 verifier confusion matrices on Val-350 show that the parent checkpoint's zero-shot verifier is strongly Yes-biased (71%-74% Yes on Fast-Right, 53%-78% Yes on Fast-Wrong, 21%-43% Invalid), while shared-actor training exhibits severe boundary instability—collapsing toward \boxed{No} on Seed 102 in thinker_baseline (14 all-wrong vs. 2 all-right Fast steps; 340/350 triggers vs. 29/350 on Seed 42) and toward either \boxed{No} (350/350 and 233/350 triggers) or \boxed{Yes} (346/350 Yes verdicts, 24.56% 6-panel accuracy) in thinker_unweighted_sumsft. Fast-Context Gradient Isolation and Brier-gated distillation + anchor replay (bc_thinker_v2: 47.74%) prevent these collapses (+65 to +296 items) and bound pre-clip total-loss gradient norms (max 6.91 vs. 26.80 before 1.0-norm clipping). Second, our matched-dose nested hierarchy (grad_accum=5, lr_scale=0.60, wd=0.08, 5,120 nominal tokens/step, right-censored masking) reveals a decisive bounded result: the stabilized single-turn control std_stabilized_g8 (2,496/5,180 = 48.19%; MATH-500 48.10%) already accounts for 94.5% (+308/+326 items) of the raw gain over unmasked short-horizon std_rlvr_cont (42.24%). Across two 2-seed realizations, 2-stage prefix-forking with Brier-SCD and prefix masking (fork_thinker Run 1: 48.53%, MATH-500 48.70%; regradable Run 2 fork_thinker_rep2: 47.90%, MATH-500 47.30%) matches std_stabilized_g8 within realization variance (+0.35 pp in Run 1, -0.29 pp in Run 2) while reducing measured generated tokens by 11.5%-15.1%.
