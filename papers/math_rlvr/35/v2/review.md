# Accept

This update appropriately corrects and documents the accepted contribution. It retains the distinction between rescuing a continuation with an externally supplied arithmetic correction, changing a conditional likelihood margin at a fixed prefix, and improving complete unaided solutions. The changes clarify supervision, representation effects, statistical scope and reproducibility. The additional arm-relative novelty analysis is a limited sensitivity check on the existing result, suitable for this revision rather than a separate submission.

The principal presentational change is the promotion of the previously reported full-checker-v3 exclusion analysis to the M0 headline. The retained outcomes reproduce repair-minus-own of $+8.68$ percentage points, with the reported item-bootstrap interval $[+4.49,+13.47]$, on $167$ items. An independent aggregation by item also gives $8.6826$ percentage points. The manuscript and metadata now place this beside the frozen all-item estimate of $+6.05$ points $[+1.57,+10.76]$ on $223$ items and explicitly disclose that the exclusion was chosen after outcomes were known. This presentation is acceptable with those qualifications. The larger estimate describes the retained item population; it is not a new prospective validation of checker v3. Its bootstrap interval is conditional on that exclusion and does not account for the analysis-selection process. The original batch-dependent continuation allowance and item-level pairing limitations remain relevant to both estimates. The revised description of the no-allowance-stop subset as descriptive is appropriate.

The new R/NR novelty check resolves a concrete ambiguity in the accepted paper. From the retained claim evidence, I confirmed that the $42$-claim R bank shares no expression with the $215$ form-matched, main-split-novel held-out claims, but shares correct values with two claims on two items. Excluding those claims leaves $146$ items. Independent key-based alignment of the two arms gives a mean margin contrast of $1.22178$; the supplied analysis reproduces $+1.222$ with interval $[+0.818,+1.670]$. Thus the reported conditional-margin effect survives this arm-relative exclusion. The text correctly distinguishes novelty relative to the main $490$-claim bank from novelty relative to the separate R bank. This remains expression-string and correct-value novelty, not a demonstration of generalization to new arithmetic algorithms.

Several wording changes improve the scientific interpretation. R/NR source selection now acknowledges final-answer grading, and stored repaired trajectories are correctly described as replayed, off-policy examples. The surface-form audit now explicitly measures the model's wrong-value rendering and treats a corresponding mismatch for the correct target as an untested possibility. The margin definition makes the measurement clearer. Labeling the cost table as online training cost also removes an unwarranted implication of comparable total efficiency. The added discussion of symbolic token-level feedback in RLSF [19] and activation-based arithmetic-error detection with selective re-prompting in Sun et al. [20] appropriately credits relevant precedents. It preserves the accepted, narrower distinction from the related rStation studies [13]–[15].

The reproducibility changes are useful. In a clean working copy, the revised v3 analysis, R-novelty analysis, form-matched readout, native readout and dose readout reproduced their shipped JSON results; the headline recomputation also passed. Comparison of the decoded old and new packs found no alterations to previously retained values: the changes add match fields and additional supporting records. These checks establish consistency of the retained evidence and reported estimators. They do not reproduce training, regenerate completions, recompute neural likelihoods or independently regrade the text-free outcomes. Section 7 now communicates that distinction substantially better.

Two minor documentation qualifications remain:

1. Section 7 and the README say that the nonportable builders' outputs are shipped. This is too broad for the bank builder: the release includes compact claim evidence and span counts, but not the full `claims.jsonl`, `span_train.json` or `span_train_fm.json` files. Identify which outputs are retained and which require regeneration.
2. `src/cvlt_banks.py` recomputes and checks form-match flags, but assumes that each input claim already has a `formmatch` field; its `--write` path does not add that field to the claim file. Describe this stage as validation/reconstruction from prepared inputs, or document the missing flag-writing preparation step. I could inspect this logic but could not reproduce full-bank equality without the unshipped pool texts and prepared bank inputs.

The resulting manuscript remains organized around a coherent scientific argument. The added sensitivity and documentation support that argument without introducing a new substantive contribution or materially obscuring it. Acceptance continues to apply to the bounded empirical findings for the tested model, checker-derived training packages and doses. The revision supplies no new evidence of sustained native mathematical-reasoning gains and does not strengthen the negative comparisons into a general impossibility claim.
