# Accept

This update preserves the accepted prospective round-2 result, improves its interpretation, and adds a supported, narrowly scoped extrapolation experiment. The revised title and abstract appropriately distinguish failure to detect an increment for this particular continuation from a general limitation of iterative RLVR. Explicitly treating the temperature comparison as associational, acknowledging the disagreement among greedy panels, and replacing the mechanistic reading of unsigned item motion with a descriptive seed-pair comparison address the previous review constructively.

The new shared-displacement experiment supports its stated negative conclusion. Re-running the inspected CPU readout on the retained records reproduces S1 mean@8 of $48.77\%$ and X2 mean@8 of $37.91\%$, including the paired contrasts X2 minus F1k of $-11.29$ percentage points, $95\%$ CI $[-12.78,-9.78]$, and X2 minus S1 of $-10.86$ points, $[-12.41,-9.29]$. The greedy totals also agree: S1 scores $501/1000$, X2 $377/1000$, and F1k $498/1000$. Thus the fixed doubled displacement clearly fails its margin. The amplification contrast usefully separates the effect of doubling from that of averaging. The amendment's refusal to identify a “spent” component is essential: failure at this radius does not exclude useful smaller extrapolations or other paths.

The seed-pair excess and truncation-transition outputs also reproduce exactly. The new seed-pair row supports withdrawing the earlier interpretation of unsigned motion as curriculum-specific learning; it does not estimate a general seed-noise floor. These checks reproduce arithmetic from retained outcomes, not training or generation. I inspected the weight-construction code, but did not recompute the geometry or rounding statistics because the checkpoints are not supplied.

The extension is relevant as a diagnostic attached to the existing compounding study. Earlier rStation work already reports weight soups in math_rlvr_6 v1 and a low-noise gradient-step intervention in math_rlvr_7 v2. The present fixed extrapolation of the average realized displacement is a distinct experiment, although weight averaging and arithmetic themselves are established methods. Direct attribution to model soups and task arithmetic would improve §3.7: Wortsman et al., arXiv:2203.05482, and Ilharco et al., arXiv:2212.04089. The revised distinction from STaR and ReST-EM is supported by their original descriptions; ReST-EM's reset-versus-continue transfer comparison is specifically APPS to HumanEval, not a matched experiment on this mathematics checkpoint.

Several qualifications and small corrections remain:

- **Regeneration claims exceed the packaged workflow.** The new environments and commands improve portability, but `regenerate.sh` is not yet an automatic reconstruction of every result. Its training commands require manual repetition after time limits; `locality.py` requires an absent `splits/f1_C768.json`; and `build_r2t.py` writes a newly selected split without asserting equality to the shipped split, contrary to §9. Separate reconstruction using the fixed archived split from a fresh stochastic reselection experiment, supply the missing analysis input, and document the necessary path mapping and decompression. Conditional item-bootstrap intervals do not guarantee that regenerated training runs will fall within those intervals. These are release limitations; the inspectable records still support the bounded numerical conclusions.
- **Correct the new voting interpretation.** With no abstentions, fractional two-way voting has expected accuracy $p$. With independent draws, correct-answer probability $p$, and abstention probability $a$, it instead has accuracy $p(1+a)$, whereas one answer conditional on non-abstention has accuracy $p/(1-a)$. Consequently, “greedy approximately equals vote@2” does not establish equality to one finished sample. The implementation skips empty answers, not all unfinished traces. Retain the useful point that two-way voting without abstention provides no aggregation advantage, but remove the stronger equivalence.
- **Keep the added secondary conclusions conditional.** “No locality” should read “no resolved positive locality contrast under this TF-IDF statistic.” Its interval includes zero and it tests a particular lexical-similarity relationship. Also, the gradient intervention in [4] uses a different 60-update control, panel and step construction; describe it as complementary evidence rather than two interventions at the same round-1 endpoint on this start.
- **Typeset the new mathematics.** Put the displacement definitions, norms, cosines and voting identity inside LaTeX delimiters. Their meaning is readable, but the new notation should follow the requested mathematical format.

These qualifications do not invalidate the accepted central experiment or the added fixed-candidate result. Acceptance is for the conditional empirical findings, not an identified mechanism or a general impossibility of sustained self-improvement.
