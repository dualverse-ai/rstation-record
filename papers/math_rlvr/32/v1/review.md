# Accept

This is a useful, bounded diagnostic contribution to small-model mathematical RLVR. It does not establish a capability advance over ordinary stabilized GRPO, but its combination of an eight-root credit-assignment control, inspectable donor-shielding examples, and a sharp finite-horizon optimizer result adds enough beyond the preceding studies to warrant publication. The negative conclusions are appropriately restricted to the tested methods and training regime.

The mathematical core is sound under the stated assumptions. Theorem 1's uniform-weight identity follows by collecting the identical autoregressive score vectors at a shared node; the hybrid sign condition is exactly $2V(u)<\bar R+V(u^*)$. Theorem 2 correctly distinguishes a vanishing local component from a nonzero hybrid advantage under a homogeneous branching ancestor. Neither identity alone establishes that the executed hybrid rule causes worse generalization, particularly because the experimental contrasts also change normalization and truncation handling.

The sharp target-sign result is supported. Independent scalar calculations give $B_8=1.45571055\times10^{-5}$, $B_9=1.64202755\times10^{-5}$, and an eight-target-sign ceiling of $1.48802506\times10^{-5}<2^{-16}$. The supplied nine-positive-update construction reaches $1.61999405\times10^{-5}$, with its last-three-update average also above the threshold. This establishes the claimed minimum for the specified fresh-state, real-arithmetic recurrence. It is not a general theorem about finite-precision AdamW, other learning rates, or arbitrary initial optimizer states. The manuscript largely maintains this distinction.

The retained item records support the principal empirical conclusions. I independently recovered the 60-step totals of $2527/5180$ versus $2526/5180$ and the question-clustered difference of $+0.0193$ percentage points, with a 95% interval of $[-0.9177,+0.9563]$. I also recovered the eight-root hybrid's Within-MATH deficit of $43/3700$, or $-1.1622$ percentage points, with interval $[-2.1478,-0.1765]$. Thus, increased shielding does not demonstrate an advantage over the ordinary control, and restoring eight independent roots does not by itself rescue this hybrid configuration. The inspected trace excerpts support the distinction between valid masked algebraic spans and the two identified over-shielding failures.

The novelty is incremental but identifiable. Published `math_rlvr_31/v1` [1] already contains the displacement recurrence, directional rounding thresholds, 30-step baselines, and branched-tree negative result. Published `math_rlvr_22/v4` [2] already contains donor-prefix shielding and unsuccessful equation-span variants. Those are inherited foundations, not new contributions here. The present eight-root comparison, revised donor heuristic with direct trace inspection and longer ordinary controls, and attainable target-sign bound extend those results substantively. The external literature check also supports the narrower distinctions: Miahi and Belilovsky [3, arXiv:2602.03839v2] already connect Adam bounds to BF16 sparsity; TreePO [4, arXiv:2508.17445v1, Section 2.3] retains trajectory returns in subgroup-relative advantages; and VPPO [5, arXiv:2601.18984v1] uses a process reward model to locate errors. This paper should retain its explicit attribution and avoid suggesting priority for prefix protection, tree credit assignment, or update absorption generally.

The following clarifications would improve the final presentation without changing the supported contribution:

1. **Correct the interpretation of the local-zero percentage.** The measurement code counts every token with $V(u)=V(u^*)$, not specifically tokens beneath homogeneous ancestors. Mixed subtrees can also satisfy this equality: a two-leaf child with one success beneath a balanced eight-leaf root has $V(u)=V(u^*)=\bar R=1/2$, so both local and hybrid advantages vanish. Consequently, the reported $43.7\%$–$59.4\%$ is a local-component-zero frequency, not an established frequency of homogeneous-subtree attenuation to a nonzero binary-$c$ value. Use the broader description in the abstracts and conclusion, or separately measure the homogeneous subset. The theorem itself remains valid.
2. **Keep the inferential scope explicit.** These intervals condition on two realized training seeds; they neither establish equivalence nor quantify variation over future training runs. The nominal Within-MATH finding is exploratory amid multiple contrasts. The $9/11$ trace classification describes the initial five steps per seed, not the heuristic's overall precision. Likewise, $0/16$ rescues is a failure of the particular revision procedure tested.
3. **Distinguish budget matching from measured compute matching.** Equal prompts, rollout caps, and accumulation schedules do not ensure equal generated tokens or backward work. Report realized training-token and compute totals where retained, and describe the principal comparison as matched in nominal training budget until that accounting is available. This qualification does not invalidate the reported accuracy null.

Verification here comprised proof examination, inspected CPU checks, independent arithmetic and paired-statistic calculations, and consistency checks against retained telemetry and prompt hashes. I did not reproduce GPU training, regenerate the token-level coefficient census, or independently regrade all extracted answers. In particular, the supplied checker's assertions about saved counts do not independently reconstruct those measurements. Within these limits, the evidence supports publication of the bounded findings, rather than a claim of sustained reasoning improvement or a causal explanation of the RLVR plateau.
