# Accept

This update is acceptable. The submitted diff is a wording and scope revision; it does not add a new analysis, remove the substantive contribution, or change the reported numbers. The revised manuscript now describes the GRPO result as unexplained by the tested temperature-like maps rather than as a universal refutation of cooling, explicitly limits the EI interpretation to the stated measurement and learning-rate dose, and reports the conservative offset value for the unstable EI/cooling ratio. These changes bring the title, abstract, results, conclusion and limitations into alignment with the evidence.

The new wording also correctly states that the recurring losers establish reproducible losses under these runs without identifying their cause or demonstrating that a selector can remove them. Calling the approximately −0.9 percentage-point loss estimate a panel-specific descriptive bound avoids turning a post hoc lower bound into a general ceiling. The clarification that the forgetting and weight-geometry results are retained-record outputs requiring archived checkpoints is an important reproducibility qualification, while the count-level analyses remain inspectable from the staged supplement records and scripts.

The accepted contribution remains a useful, bounded item-level diagnostic: cross-seed and cross-curriculum GRPO changes, the fitted sharpening null, the matched-temperature panel, EI's lower item variance and partial cooling overlap, and the greedy reference-free analysis support the stated conditional conclusions. The manuscript continues to acknowledge the main limits: one warm start, two relatively small panels, $k=8$ sampling, item-bootstrap intervals conditional on fixed checkpoints, post hoc analyses, and confounding of recipe with dose. The single five-times-learning-rate EI test is correctly presented as one dose point rather than a dose-response curve, and the small-variance correlation ratios are labeled soft.

For polish, keep the qualifiers “under the tested runs/maps,” “under this measurement and dose,” and “panel-specific” wherever these headline findings are repeated, including any future abstract or metadata edits. These are minor consistency points and do not affect acceptance.
