# Conditioning Without Copying: State-Masked Suffix Distillation, Spliced Prefix Interference, and Multi-Round Retention Boundaries in Qwen3-0.6B-Base

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 1 · 2026-10-27T05:25:37.216662Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/34/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

When Qwen3-0.6B-Base (751M parameters [1]) fails to finish a derivation within L1=1024 tokens, test-time continuation from extracted intermediate states or native prefixes rescues 50-60 problems per 640-question slice [2, 3, 13, 14]. We test whether consolidating these rescues into single-pass policy weights works better under token-0 spliced Expert Iteration (Compact_Spliced_EI, supervising y_spliced = s_compact + b_bridge + y^(2)) or State-Masked Suffix Distillation (Suffix_Only_Spliced, SOS), which places s_compact + b_bridge in the causal context (labels = -100) and supervises only y^(2). Across three converged RLVR parents (MH1024_s42, SD1024_s101, MH1024_s7), Compact_Spliced_EI fails to improve single-pass accuracy (Pool1300 @2048 mean 679.7/1300 vs. Parent 681.7/1300, 3-seed net Delta = -6; Pool800 @2048 mean 442.0/800). Teacher-forced NLL and initial parameter-gradient audits at theta_parent show that spliced prefixes (s_compact + b_bridge) incur 0.9200 nats/token (4.71x the 0.1973 nats/token of y^(2)), account for 0.6021 (s42), 0.6721 (s101), and 0.6649 (s7) of per-item summed component gradient norms, and align strongly with initial full-trace gradients (cos = 0.8083/0.8936/0.8825 vs. 0.5227/0.3720/0.4331 for SOS). Budget-matched 5-attempt native-prefix (Prefix64_Matched5: 60/54 rescues vs. 54/52 compact-state) and contiguous sub-derivation (Coherent_SubDeriv: 51/51; Fluent: 57/51) controls lower prefix NLL to 0.0839-0.1605 nats yet still do not beat 1-pass Raw_EI_1Pass on Pool1300. Across all three seeds, SOS improves Pool800 @2048 over Parent (455.0 vs. 441.7, 3-seed Delta = +40, +13.3/seed, 95% CI [+6.0, +74.0]) and over Compact_Spliced_EI (Delta = +39, +13.0/seed, 95% CI [+12.0, +67.0]), shows no statistically resolved difference from Parent or Raw_EI_1Pass on Pool1300 @2048 (688.0 vs. 681.7 and 683.7; MATH-500 233.0 vs. Parent 240.0 and Raw_EI_1Pass 237.0), and leads the 5-stage deliberative cascade on MATH560 (320/315 in Ledger B, 309 on s7). Exact 375/375-item matched interventions (Bridge_Supervised_SOS, Content_Free_State_SOS, Delta = -29 on Pool800), 3-seed layer-partitioned/orthogonal-subspace synthesis tests, and exploratory Round-2 parent-reset replay complete the study.
