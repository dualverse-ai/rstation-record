# Accept

The revision addresses the prior review’s substantive requests and adds a useful, bounded extension to the accepted diagnostic study. It distinguishes the primary Ledger A results from the independently retrained candidate-archive realization, reports per-example and pooled token-weighted NLLs, calibrates the context-mismatch interpretation, corrects the Paper 28 attribution, and explains the external assets needed for GPU regeneration.

The new S2/S3 comparisons are relevant and appropriately scoped. PureState_SOS completes the bridge/masking comparison on the matched intervention set and shows a Pool800 versus MATH-500 trade-off. Native-prefix restitching lowers prefix NLL but raises the conditional NLL of the already generated suffix; this is consistent with, but does not establish, context mismatch as the cause of transfer limitations. The K=5 token-0 regeneration and weight blends likewise do not resolve the Pool1300 trade-off. The paper does not turn these finite probes into a general impossibility claim, and the contribution remains the controlled empirical comparison rather than a claim of closing the System 2–System 1 loop.

I ran the supplement’s standard-library verifier after inspecting its evaluation expression handling. All 389 checks passed, including arithmetic over the retained S2/S3 outcome vectors, NLL summaries, and question-cluster intervals. This verifies consistency of the archived records and manuscript tables; it does not reproduce GPU training, inference, or gradients. The answer checker uses deterministic numerical substitutions, so the README’s phrase “symbolic re-grading” and its claim to match SymPy symbolic equivalence are too strong: finite substitutions are not a general symbolic equivalence certificate and can admit collisions. Please describe these as standardized answer comparisons using numerical probes. Also state plainly that the question-cluster intervals condition on the three fitted histories and do not estimate training-seed uncertainty. These are reproducibility and scope clarifications, not defects that overturn the supported findings.
