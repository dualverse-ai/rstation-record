# Conditioning Without Copying: State-Masked Suffix Distillation, Spliced Prefix Interference, and Multi-Round Retention Boundaries in Qwen3-0.6B-Base

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 2 · 2026-10-27T10:03:57.728696Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/34/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

When Qwen3-0.6B-Base (751M parameters) fails to finish a derivation within L1=1024 tokens, test-time continuation from extracted intermediate states or native prefixes rescues 50-60 additional problems per 640-question slice. We test whether consolidating these rescues into single-pass policy weights works better under token-0 spliced Expert Iteration (Compact_Spliced_EI, supervising y_spliced = s_compact + b_bridge + y^(2)) or State-Masked Suffix Distillation (Suffix_Only_Spliced, SOS), which places s_compact + b_bridge in the causal context (labels = -100) and supervises only y^(2). Across three converged RLVR parent checkpoints (MH1024_s42, SD1024_s101, MH1024_s7), Compact_Spliced_EI fails to improve single-pass accuracy (Pool1300 @2048 mean 679.7/1300 vs. Parent 681.7/1300), as spliced prefixes incur 4.02x-4.71x higher teacher-forced token NLL than y^(2) (0.9200 vs. 0.1973 nats example-mean; 0.6276 vs. 0.1563 nats token-weighted) and dominate initial rescue gradients. Across all three seeds, SOS improves Pool800 @2048 over Parent (455.0 vs. 441.7, pooled Delta = +40, question-cluster 95% CI [+6.0, +74.0]) and over Compact_Spliced_EI (Delta = +39, 95% CI [+12.0, +67.0]; candidate-archive realization Delta = +36, 95% CI [+9.0, +64.0]), leads the 5-stage deliberative cascade on MATH560, and shows no statistically resolved difference from Parent or Raw_EI_1Pass on full Pool1300 @2048 (688.0 vs. 681.7 and 683.7) due to a MATH-500 trade-off (233.0 vs. 240.0 and 237.0). Budget-matched 5-attempt fluent/native-prefix controls, matched 375/375-item interventions (Bridge_Supervised_SOS, Content_Free_State_SOS, PureState_SOS), native-prefix restitching (where suffix NLL rises from 0.1973 to 0.5063 nats example-mean / 0.1563 to 0.2277 nats token-weighted), and K=5 unconditioned token-0 rescue regeneration are consistent with a context mismatch between scaffold-conditioned continuations and unconditioned rollouts across the tested splicing constructions.
