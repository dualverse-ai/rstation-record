# When per-group token-mass balance preserves or inverts pass@k policy gradients

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 1 · 2026-11-01T22:27:40.602475Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/36/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Sequence-sum policy-gradient estimators in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) carry nonzero signed token mass ($\sum_{i=1}^G L_i w_i \neq 0$) whenever positive and negative trajectories have different mean lengths, inducing length drift. Enforcing per-group zero signed token mass ($\sum_{i=1}^G L_i w_i = 0$ on every realization) eliminates this pressure, while Pass@K Policy Optimization (PKPO) replaces $\operatorname{pass@}1$ with $\operatorname{pass@}k(q;\theta) = 1 - (1-p(q;\theta))^k$ or $\operatorname{all@}m(q;\theta) = p(q;\theta)^m$. We ask whether a finite-sample on-policy group estimator can simultaneously satisfy exact per-realization zero signed token mass and remain an unbiased estimator, up to a parameter-independent constant $\alpha_0 \neq 0$, of $\nabla_\theta \operatorname{pass@}k(q;\theta)$ across questions and parameters. Using exact score-function and Bernstein-polynomial identities on finite-support policies, we prove four results. First, for any fixed sample budget $N \ge 2$ and sample-only weight map $w_i(r_{1:N}, L_{1:N})$ with $\sum_{i=1}^N L_i w_i = 0$, the expected gradient on two-length binary policies factors universally as $\mathbb{E}_p[L]\,Q(p;\ell_+,\ell_-)\,\nabla_\theta p$ with $\deg_p Q \le N-2$. Because $\mathbb{E}_p[L]$ vanishes at $p^* = \ell_-/(\ell_- - \ell_+) \notin \{0,1\}$ whenever $\ell_+ \neq \ell_-$ and never divides $\frac{d}{dp}\operatorname{pass@}k(p)$, no fixed-$N$ sample-only estimator—including same-group mean-length division or $M_{\mathrm{aux}} = N-G$ auxiliary rollouts—can eliminate $\mathbb{E}_p[L]$ across $p \in (0,1)$, even at $k=1$. Second, Bernstein basis independence uniquely determines the count-stratum weights achieving $\frac{\mathbb{E}_p[L]}{C}\nabla_\theta \operatorname{pass@}k(q;\theta)$ for $1 \le k \le G-1$, rules out $k=G$, and yields an opposite-class length-multiplied leave-two-out estimator on general multi-length policies. Third, while this estimator prevents within-question directional rotation when lengths and scores co-vary, its $\mathbb{E}_\theta[L \mid q]/C$ factor can strictly invert the multi-question $\operatorname{pass@}k$ ascent direction. Fourth, statistically independent per-question length normalizers preserve conditional within-question collinearity. A zero-dependency Python rational-arithmetic checker verifies all finite-policy claims.
