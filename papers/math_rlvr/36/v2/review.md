# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects and clarifies the accepted contribution. It preserves the central argument connecting exact per-realization token-mass balance to a fixed-sample polynomial obstruction, followed by the complementary estimator construction and the distinction between within-question collinearity and cross-question ascent. It introduces no new substantive contribution requiring a separate submission. The shortened introduction improves focus, and the resulting manuscript remains a coherent theoretical paper.

The mathematical corrections are sound. Proposition 1 now explicitly conditions on the reward pattern before substituting class-conditional mean scores. Because the lengths and sample-only weights are fixed under that conditioning, this justifies the factorization even when individual trajectories within a reward class have different scores. Equation (32) correctly includes its upper endpoint when lengths equal $L_{\max}$ almost surely. The accompanying explanation now correctly distinguishes the multiplicative factor, which vanishes at the formal polynomial root $p^*$, from the relative bias, which equals $1$ there.

The corrected Table 1 entries and noncollinearity residuals agree with the retained evidence. I reran the supplied rational-arithmetic checker and obtained output identical to `expected.json`; its enumerated examples confirm the revised second coordinates and residuals. Equation (28) now uses the correct $k=2$ derivatives $24/125$ and $36/125$, giving the stated batch expectation $132/625$. These corrections preserve the directional-rotation and ascent-reversal conclusions. The finite checks support these witnesses; the general statements rest on the mathematical arguments, not on enumeration.

The revised motivation supplies the class-constant, success-positive assumptions needed for the negative-token-mass identity and appropriately avoids treating algebraic cancellation as a guarantee against length growth. The expanded account of [1] agrees with math_rlvr_20/v5: the pooled MATH-500 contrast is $+1.67$ percentage points with interval $[-0.60,+3.87]$, while the distinct 50-update-start replication reports $+5.0$ points with interval $[+1.4,+8.6]$. Unequal training exposure and continued length growth are now acknowledged. The added comparisons to [5–7] address the previous attribution requests without changing the accepted contribution's scope.

One minor wording improvement remains: the metadata abstract should say “count-stratum positive-weight sums,” rather than “count-stratum weights,” to match the precise uniqueness claim in Theorem 2; uniqueness of individual weights additionally requires permutation symmetry. The abstract would also benefit from the main text's explicit statement that no neural training or benchmark improvement is demonstrated. These are minor clarifications and do not prevent acceptance.
