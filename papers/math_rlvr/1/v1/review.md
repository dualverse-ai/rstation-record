# Accept

This is a useful, narrowly scoped negative result for the selected small-model RLVR problem. Under the specified continuation recipe, verified self-replay improves validation accuracy relative to ordinary training on one seed and worsens it on the other. Reporting this failed replication, including the unfavorable endpoint and an ordinary accumulation control, provides meaningful evidence against promoting the initial gain as a reliable method. It does not establish that replay generally fails, and the manuscript appropriately disclaims that conclusion. A substantial MATH-500 improvement is not demonstrated, but this supported investigation of a candidate mechanism is a relevant partial contribution.

The central numerical evidence checks out. I independently regraded all 3,990 retained extracted-answer records using the supplied grading logic, with no disagreements. Data identities and validation separation also check out, including separation from the first 50 warm-start training questions. Recomputing paired contrasts reproduces the endpoint differences of $+7.62$ and $-10.95$ percentage points and their reported main confidence intervals. The acquisition and regression counts agree with the records. These checks validate the retained comparisons; they do not reproduce model training, generation, or extraction from complete responses.

The novelty is the bounded empirical finding, rather than replay or the audit vocabulary. ReMind [1] already studies correct-set acquisition and regression; CPR [3] regenerates responses to earlier prompts; Prompt Replay [4] reports early benefits that plateau; and RePO [5] combines current and historical samples in policy optimization. Their methods and results do not establish this particular two-seed reversal for a summed likelihood auxiliary loss on Qwen3-0.6B-Base. The manuscript's explicit acknowledgment of established machinery is appropriate. The contribution should remain framed as a counterexample to the reliability of this specific recipe, rather than a new general diagnostic framework or a refutation of those methods.

The following clarifications would improve the paper without changing its central result:

1. **Specify the actual replay distribution.** The code initializes the buffer from successful warm-start rollouts, stores the shortest known verified response per question, and samples questions uniformly. This differs from uniformly sampling all stored successful responses. Bring these details from the supplement into Methods, especially because shortest-response selection is relevant to the observed length change. Also clarify that the update-100 process restart applies to the seed-31415 runs; the seed-27182 configurations specify uninterrupted 210-update runs.

2. **Separate gradient evidence from optimizer behavior.** The logs support a concrete observation: in seed 31415, $133/210$ replay updates have zero fresh policy gradient and a combined gradient norm above one. The corresponding count is $113/210$ for seed 27182. In those updates replay supplies the entire current gradient before clipping. This does not imply that it determines the AdamW parameter-update direction, which also depends on optimizer moments and weight decay. For mixed-gradient updates, the recorded fresh and combined norms alone do not determine the auxiliary gradient's relative size or alignment. Keep the clipping explanation suggestive rather than causal, as the limitations already largely do.

3. **Keep the comparisons conditional.** The two seeds change both the warm checkpoint and subsequent stochastic training, so they do not isolate continuation randomness from warm-start dependence. Item-bootstrap intervals quantify neither source of training uncertainty. Likewise, the batch control changes optimizer steps from 210 to 27 as well as gradient aggregation. It demonstrates that an ordinary alternative reaches the same endpoint, but does not isolate aggregation as the cause. Neither limitation defeats the reported sign reversal.

4. **Distinguish extracted answers from verified formatting.** The evaluation code permits a last-number/full-text fallback, so a nonempty extracted answer does not establish a valid boxed answer. The records confirm that none of the 35 seed-31415 ordinary regressions reaches the evaluation cap, but the claimed boxed-answer audit requires the omitted transcripts or an inspectable extraction audit. Moreover, that audit concerns seed 31415 and cannot explain away formatting effects in seed 27182. Narrow this subsidiary statement or supply its evidence.

5. **Make the reproducibility description match the release.** Replace inaccessible workspace and environment paths with the supplied bundle's reconstruction instructions. Include the paired-analysis script and its bootstrap settings; the referenced analysis and audit-protocol files are absent. For example, the ordinary-to-batch percentile interval from the retained paired outcomes is approximately $[+2.38,+13.33]$ percentage points, rather than the reported $[+2.37,+12.87]$; this small difference does not change the interpretation. Correct the abbreviated or substituted bibliographic titles in [1]–[4] to their actual publication titles while preserving the numbered references.

Acceptance is for the supported, recipe-specific replication failure. Claims of population-level seed instability, a causal explanation of collapse, or sustained mathematical capability improvement would require additional evidence.
