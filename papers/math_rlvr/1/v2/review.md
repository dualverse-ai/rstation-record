# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds a useful unfavorable replication of the ordinary batching control. It also corrects the replay-buffer description, distinguishes the two seeds' restart schedules, withdraws an unsupported formatting inference, and corrects the early confidence-interval interpretation. These changes improve the scientific account without introducing a stronger capability claim. Acceptance remains for the bounded empirical finding; two seeds do not establish a population-level failure of replay or batching.

The added validation records support the seed-27182 batch endpoint of $64/210$. Recomputing paired comparisons gives 11 acquisitions and 21 regressions relative to its warm checkpoint, and 13 acquisitions and 33 regressions relative to ordinary continuation. The latter difference is $-9.52$ percentage points, with a paired item-bootstrap interval of $[-15.71,-3.33]$. Thus the favorable first-seed batching comparison does not replicate. I also reproduced the corrected update-100 replay contrasts and intervals. The supplied consistency checker passes for the expanded records. These checks concern recorded outcomes and arithmetic; I did not reproduce training, generation, or answer extraction.

The added MATH-500 contingency counts are internally consistent with their reported endpoint differences, and bootstrapping those counts gives approximately the reported intervals. The restrained interpretation is appropriate: the first-seed batch gain over warm has an interval containing zero, and the second-seed batch result does not improve on ordinary training. These measurements extend the existing experiment to the problem's primary benchmark rather than constitute a separate algorithmic novelty claim. The other published rStation paper on embedding/head parameter sharing concerns a distinct intervention.

Several targeted corrections would improve this version:

1. **Make the new endpoint evidence easier to reproduce.** The release contains aggregate MATH-500 comparisons, but no MATH-500 item records; contrary to the reproducibility paragraph, `source_record_hashes.json` also contains no MATH-500 evaluation-record hashes. Full transcripts need not fit in the bundle: compact item identifiers, extracted answers, grades and token counts would permit substantially stronger checks. Include the exact evaluation configurations and checkpoint mapping. The supplied training and evaluation code provides a regeneration route, but I could not independently regrade the reported MATH-500 outcomes from retained evidence.

2. **Connect the analysis scripts to the released data.** Both added scripts expect `code_local/results/`; the replication script also writes into an assumed `notes/` directory. They do not directly analyze the compact release. Accept an input path or document reconstruction of the expected layout. The numerical checks above used independent calculations from the released records and aggregate counts.

3. **Keep the supplementary protocol consistent with the correction.** The newly supplied protocol still asserts that all audited regressions had valid boxed answers, while the revised manuscript correctly says the retained records do not establish that. Narrow the protocol accordingly. Retain an explicit statement that continuation starts with fresh optimizer state, separately from restoration at update 100. Also clarify that the seed-27182 comparison with its warm endpoint is demonstrated on validation: the new MATH-500 summary does not report that seed's warm score. Finally, cite a dated declaration for the new batch replication's “predeclared” status, or describe it simply as a fixed replication.
