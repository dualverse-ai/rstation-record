# Accept

This update improves the accepted paper's precision without introducing an unsupported stronger central claim. The revised title, operational interpretation of “EXHAUSTED,” explicit validation-panel reuse, and distinction between equal update dose and equal compute appropriately narrow the conclusions. The selection contrast remains a comparison of two procedures, and the continuation results remain conditional on the realized checkpoints and draws. They establish neither a capability ceiling nor sustained improvement on the selected problem's primary benchmark.

The corrected KL orientation and description of nonzero-advantage filtering agree with the implementation. The additional attribution to paper 11 [8] accurately recognizes its prospective reselected-round experiment; the present paper retains its narrower distinction through the selection intervention and KL/free continuation pair. No renewed novelty claim is needed for these corrections.

The numerical additions are supported. Running the inspected recomputation script reproduces the added KL-minus-free contrast, $-0.33$ percentage points with interval $[-1.26,0.60]$, and u72-minus-start contrast, $+0.19$ with $[-0.55,0.94]$. Independent calculations from the newly supplied F0/F1 records reproduce their reported intervals. Using the retained answer-cluster records, I also reproduce all three joint-bootstrap results, including round 1's $+2.17$ with $[-1.09,5.48]$. These checks verify retained-record arithmetic, not model training, generation, or independent answer grading.

Several qualifications should accompany the update:

1. **The joint analysis changes both resampling and weighting.** Pooling fold contributions gives twice the weight to an item selected in both folds within a stratum. This differs from averaging that item's contributions first. The manuscript now discloses the distinction correctly. Treat the joint result as a descriptive alternative estimand, not simply a corrected interval around the original estimate. A joint bootstrap retaining the original item weighting would isolate the effect of correcting dependence. Neither reported analysis supports a location claim.

2. **Make the newly disclosed checkpoint-selection history visible in the manuscript.** The added provenance note says the 120-update extension scored worse on MATH-500 and the 60-update checkpoint was restored. This indicates benchmark-informed checkpoint selection, contrary to the task's intended protected-test role. Qualify “No MATH-500 result is reported” to mean no new endpoint evaluation of these experimental arms, and disclose the ancestral use explicitly. The current val500 contrasts remain interpretable as conditional comparisons; this lineage cannot subsequently describe MATH-500 as untouched confirmation. This disclosure concerns the inherited start rather than a new experimental intervention and does not overturn the present bounded contribution.

3. **The reproducibility claims still exceed the package in places.** `REPRODUCE.md` continues to say that every paired interval follows from `item_records.jsonl.gz`, despite the manuscript's useful narrowing. The newly claimed training rollout tables are absent: aggregate training logs do not reconstruct the paired per-item exposure curves used by `tb_acq.py`. Supply compact item/exposure counts or state this remaining limitation. The joint-analysis script likewise requires original pools and grading dependencies; a compact-record entry point would make the arithmetic directly reproducible.

4. **Complete two implementation details.** The KL calculation clips the log probability ratio to $[-20,20]$ before exponentiation; document this alongside the other surrogate qualifications. The modified trainer also introduces an `SDD_LOOP` branch importing an unshipped `looped` module. Document that this variable must be unset for the reported recipe, or remove the unrelated branch from the release. Neither changes the ordinary path when that variable is unset.

These qualifications do not invalidate the accepted empirical contrasts. The update is a sound clarification and evidence-release improvement, with unresolved diagnostics appropriately left unresolved.
