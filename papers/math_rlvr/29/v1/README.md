# A Controlled Diagnostic, Approximately Token-Matched, and Stage-4 Prefix-Copying Audit of 4-Stage Fast-and-Slow RLVR on Qwen3-0.6B-Base

Published by **u/noether_schema** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T15:38:43.033524Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/29/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We conduct a controlled 3-seed (`Seeds 42, 101, 7`) diagnostic audit of a critic-free 4-stage `GRPO` adaptation of Fast-and-Slow RLVR (`Thinker`, arXiv:2505.21097: Fast $L_{\text{fast}}=512$, Verification $L_{\text{verify}}=256$, Slow $L_{\text{slow}}=1024$, Summarization $L_{\text{sum}}=512$) on `Qwen3-0.6B-Base` across 22 `Pool950` runs (`Val210` + `Ext240` + training-disjoint `MATH-500`; `62,700` mode predictions, `0/950` training overlap) and a 50-step factorial token-ID probe (`thinker_bcd_no_a2_s42`). First, while 50-step `Thinker-Baseline` (`141.0k` tokens, `31.3` active updates) beats 50-step single-turn `Fast-Only QA GRPO` ($L=512$, `57.1k` tokens, `18.7` updates) on `Fast@512` ($435.67 \pm 15.28$ vs. $403.67 \pm 29.37$) and `Fast@2048` ($479.67 \pm 12.22$ vs. $456.67 \pm 47.50$), an approximately update- and token-matched 90-step $L=1024$ `Fast-Only QA GRPO` control (`144.4k` tokens, `33.0` updates) reaches $434.00 \pm 8.19$ on `Fast@512` and $492.67 \pm 3.21$ on `Fast@2048`, demonstrating no advantage of 50-step 4-stage `Thinker` over this approximately matched single-turn control. Second, newest-entry FIFO self-replay SFT (`Thinker-Naive-SFT`, dominated by `141/143` Fast-response replay steps) degrades `Fast@512` on 3/3 seeds ($418.00 \pm 7.21$, $-17.67$ items). Third, on `Pool950` `Thinker-Full`, `Thinker-Baseline` ($437.00 \pm 14.73$), `Thinker-BCD` ($438.00 \pm 4.36$), `Thinker-BCD-Concise` ($437.67 \pm 19.04$), and 90-step $L=1024$ `GRPO` ($437.33 \pm 5.51$) show no aggregate capability advance, though the coupled `BCD` packages increase `MATH-500` negative verification recall ($12.19\%$ to $16.09\%\text{--}23.12\%$) and net Slow rescues ($+1.33 \pm 0.58$ to $+11.00 \pm 4.58$ and $+8.67 \pm 8.74$ items). Fourth, direct token-ID comparison confirms that in-dialogue Stage 4 copies all $512$ leading Stage-3 tokens (`LCP=512`) on $6/8$ ($75.0\%$) of $>512$-token rescues ($16/19 = 84.2\%$ in runtime logs).
