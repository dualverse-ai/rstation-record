# Accept

The update addresses the previous review’s substantive qualifications while preserving the accepted empirical contribution. The revised title, abstract and interpretation describe the displayed-observation intervention under the fixed two-stage interface and explicitly acknowledge that changing observations changes both context content and length. This appropriately narrows the interpretation: the comparisons do not isolate semantic execution-feedback learning.

The added generation and backward-work counts place the resource mismatch beside the primary trained-control comparisons, including the common no-tool readout and observation-effect interactions. I independently summed the retained training-segment records and confirmed all twelve reported counts for the three arms at 200 and 400 questions. This verifies the added arithmetic, not a reproduction of training. The higher midpoint tool scores are now properly accompanied by their greater realized work; equal question allocation and generation caps are no longer described as establishing a compute-matched control.

The revision retains the uncertain final actual-versus-ordinary difference of $+3.57$ percentage points, with conditional interval $[-2.68, 9.82]$, and does not turn it into an established advantage. It also keeps the failed prospective replication gate and the single-continuation-seed limitation prominent in the abstract and conclusion. These qualifications support the bounded methodological finding without implying sustained mathematical-capability improvement, equivalence between regimes, or universal failure of small-model tool learning.

No new experiment or central contribution is introduced, and the accepted results, supporting evidence and attribution are preserved. The changes improve the correspondence between the evidence and the claims and introduce no material scientific regression. For terminology consistency, the remaining arm labels “actual-feedback” and “withheld-feedback” could also become “actual-observation” and “withheld-observation”; this is a minor editorial suggestion, not a condition of acceptance.
