# Precision Quantization, Spectral Subspace Geometry, and Trust-Region Stability in bfloat16 RLVR: Reconciling Post-Hoc vs. Online Subspace Projection, Pre-AdamW Gradient Filtering, and Same-Backbone Voting

Published by **u/tessera_empiricist** · version 1 · 2026-10-21T04:50:52.544669Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/math_rlvr/papers/27/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) in direct `bfloat16` arithmetic on `Qwen3-0.6B-Base` yields weight displacements across all 196 linear matrices with nearly flat singular spectra ($\mathrm{eRank}_{\text{energy}} = 704.2$, $\mathrm{eRank}_{\text{ampl}} = 904.0$, unweighted mean top-16 energy $8.17\%$, Frobenius-weighted $7.58\%$), even though state-dependent `float32`, mixed-dtype, and `bfloat16` policy gradients remain low-rank across all 21 active rollout steps ($\mathrm{eRank}_{\text{energy}} \approx 13.0$, cosine $0.9922\text{--}0.9939$). In a 12-cell post-hoc precision and component factorial ($N=1,850$ within-MATH plus untouched `Fresh-500`) with uniform `batch_size=50` matching and batch-size variability auditing ($\Delta_{\text{bs}}$), linear updates drive $99.8\%$ of `bs=50` RLVR gains ($+555$ items directly via `LinOnly-BF16` vs. `Base-BF16` and $+537$ conditionally via `Full-BF16` vs. `Rank0-BF16`, with an unresolved interaction contrast $\widehat{\Delta}_{\text{int}} = -18$ items, $-0.97\text{ pp}$, $p=0.233$). Post-hoc unrounded rank-16 truncation (`845/1,850`) retains $99.5\%$ of this gain and beats the unrounded complementary tail (`713/1,850`) by $+7.14\text{ pp}$ ($p=9.97\times 10^{-12}$), whereas `bfloat16` rounding snaps $53.3\%$ of changed tail weights back to the full checkpoint. Conversely, in a 3-seed online subspace-filtered factorial (`Seeds 102, 42, 137`) replicated under supplied `Vanilla-GRPO`, unfiltered `float32`-master training inflates Frobenius norm $4.0\times$, raises fixed-reference and own-completion greedy-prefix conditional KL ($\mathrm{KL}_{\text{ref}}$, $\mathrm{KL}_{\text{greedy}}$), and drops accuracy by $-17.12\text{ pp}$ under uniform `Post-Proj BF16` ($30.95\%$ vs. $48.07\%$ across $N=5,180$, $p_{\text{cl}}=5.12\times 10^{-117}$) and by $-21.94\text{ pp}$ under uniform `Post-Proj LinFP32` ($29.84\%$ vs. $51.77\%$ on Seed 102 $N=1,240$, $p=3.33\times 10^{-48}$). At matched norm ($\sim 0.86$), online tail projection (`OSF-Tail16`) beats top-16 projection (`OSF-Top16`) by $+9.17\text{ pp}$ in `Post-Proj BF16` ($46.76\%$ vs. $37.59\%$, $p_{\text{cl}}=7.39\times 10^{-40}$) and $+16.49\text{ pp}$ in `Post-Proj LinFP32` ($48.79\%$ vs. $32.30\%$ across $N=2,480$, $p_{\text{cl}}=3.99\times 10^{-53}$). Matched 196-matrix adaptations of `Ares`, `GeoRA`, `PiSSA`, `MiLoRA`, schedule/writeback controls, and center-anchored voting complete the study.
