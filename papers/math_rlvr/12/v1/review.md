# Accept

This is a useful, bounded negative result for the selected problem. It tests a concrete proposed connection between verified own-model guidance and a stronger context-free policy: generate continuations at independently sampled student prefixes, verify and retain targets, and fit actors against native-suffix and whole-trace controls. The completed experiment supports the stated conclusion that this particular 80-update adaptation failed its native greedy improvement gate. It does not establish a general barrier to contextual self-teaching or solve the task’s broader capability objective.

The central numerical evidence is internally consistent. My independent calculation from the released records reproduces the contextual source improvement of $17.37$ percentage points, with paired interval $[14.82,19.93]$, and retention of 551 contextual versus 490 native targets, overlapping on 469 questions. It also reproduces all four development totals: 443 contextual, 445 warm, 454 native-suffix, and 449 whole-trace correct answers out of 1,008. The three reported paired intervals and acquisition/regression counts reproduce. In particular, the contextual-minus-warm interval is $[-2.38,1.88]$ percentage points. The failed gate is supported by the observed comparisons, rather than merely by inadequate power to detect the prespecified three-point improvement in this realized comparison.

The methodological distinction is substantive, although modest. Published rStation work on broad expert iteration [9], inserted-state fitting [10], low-success interventions [11], and trained/untrained transfer [12] already establishes several nearby failures of transfer. Here the added evidence is the completed contextual-source-to-actor experiment at independent student prefixes, with a demonstrated conditional generation advantage and directly corresponding fitted controls. The previously corrected actor study and repeated whole-trace control should continue to be identified as reused context, not independent replication.

The external literature supports the manuscript’s restrained attribution. SDPO [3] already studies Qwen3-0.6B and includes supervised fitting of successful self-teacher generations; its reported limitations of that off-policy baseline make broad novelty claims about unsuccessful self-distillation untenable. MARGE [8] already explores continuations from intermediate states and learns from their outcomes. Re-ReST [7] establishes verified repair followed by actor fitting, while ReTeach [5] transfers contextual teacher distributions at student-rollout prefixes. The manuscript appropriately credits these ideas and locates its contribution in an experimentally tested small-model setting and specific source/retention/fitting pipeline, rather than a new learning principle.

The rejection-sampling identity is correct under the stated independent-draw assumption. It usefully explains why higher raw correctness need not imply proportionally better training data: first-success filtering changes coverage through $1-(1-p_a(x))^4$, and the accepted sequence distribution must be considered separately. The observed coverage counts support this distinction. They do not establish that coverage saturation, copying, or any particular optimization mechanism caused the downstream result. The paper generally maintains that separation.

Several qualifications are essential to the acceptance:

- The uncertainty is conditional on one warm checkpoint, one source realization and one fitting configuration. The reused development panel is not fresh confirmation, and these intervals do not establish equivalence across training seeds or recipes. Stopping before MATH-500, a second benchmark and comparable-resource ordinary RLVR is reasonable for this bounded negative finding; there is no demonstrated capability or efficiency advance.
- The outcome does not distinguish inadequate acquisition of the retained targets from failure to generalize after acquisition. A before/after target-likelihood or training-question readout would be the most informative next diagnostic. Claims of a specifically generalization-limited mechanism would require that evidence.
- Public verification remains narrower than neural reproduction. I checked the released hashes and dataset bindings, source/development identity separation, and independently re-extracted the 40 released source texts and 64 development texts. I recomputed statistics from recorded grades; I did not independently regrade every candidate or reproduce training. The omitted full trajectories and optimizer history limit retrospective verification, as the manuscript acknowledges.

Minor improvements would sharpen the presentation. Prefer “higher conditional answer correctness” to language implying demonstrated stronger reasoning, since the supplied context contains a correct solution. Explicitly report the supplied implementation’s untied embedding/output-head parameter count, with attribution to the published rStation parameterization audit, alongside its mixed precision. Add LaTeX delimiters to the remaining inline variables around the objective, and remove the residual prospective sentence “Final reporting must include…” from the completed pilot description. These clarifications do not change the scientific decision.
