# Accept

This revision makes appropriate corrections and reproducibility improvements to the accepted item-level analysis. It introduces no new substantive result and preserves the paper's focused question: where the observed RL and SFT gains lie relative to the base model's sampled vote. The main tables and their finite-panel interpretation remain unchanged.

The changes clarify several scientifically important qualifications. Table P now distinguishes the four training arms with documented 2,048-token caps from R-AlphaRand, whose cap is unconfirmed. The methods explicitly distinguish greedy checkpoint readouts from stochastic, onset-fixed reference pools. The uncertainty statement correctly conditions on the realized pools, strata and checkpoints, and distinguishes the 16-sample comparison on $K_n$ from the 64-sample vote defining that stratum. Identifying the historical positive control as using different item membership removes an unwarranted implication about power in the current analysis. The structural-zero explanation also makes the gained/lost decomposition easier to interpret.

Targeted inspection confirms that the S0/S1 producer protocol states greedy decoding and `max_tokens=2048` and contains the four named training arms and plain base, but not R-AlphaRand. The new grading module preserves every function definition from the vendored original; its changes concern documentation and optional imports. The table-analysis changes leave the numerical computation intact, and the retained numerical tables are byte-identical to the accepted version. The new requirements resolve the previously identified dependency-pin conflict and separate table analysis from historical generation. These are source and retained-output checks; I did not independently reinstall the environment or rerun grading, generation or training for this revision.

Two minor presentation issues remain:

1. Carry the R-AlphaRand qualification through the surrounding prose and table labels. Section 1 still says unconfirmed-cap checkpoints are reported separately, although R-AlphaRand remains in Table 1; Section 2 says “fully matched protocol,” Section 5 says “Under one protocol,” and supplementary Table A says “strictly matched.” Label that row as provisional wherever it appears and restrict confirmed cap-matching statements to the other four arms. Its observed scores remain reportable, but its equal-cap interpretation is unverified. The explicit disclosure in Table P makes this a wording inconsistency rather than a new unsupported central result.
2. Use $S_{\mathrm{mode}}$, $S_{\mathrm{vote}}$, $K_n$ and $K_z$ consistently throughout, rather than only in the revised rung-table heading.

Acceptance retains the original scope: a conditional behavioral comparison on one panel and checkpoint lineage. The results do not establish equivalence on $K_n$, impossibility on $K_z$, or superiority at equal inference cost. The revision improves those boundaries without expanding or obscuring the accepted contribution.
