# Accept

This revision makes two appropriate precision corrections to the accepted existence theorem realizing every rational subspace as the set of bases of asymptotic automaticity. It introduces no new substantive result, changes no proof, and preserves the paper's focused explanation of the construction and its consequences for Konieczny's questions.

The revised item 4 of Section 2 correctly distinguishes exact mean $1/2$ over a phase period from approximate balance in integer counts. The half-period antisymmetry in Section 4 establishes the former; equation (4.2) supplies the discrepancy bound needed for the latter. This wording now agrees with the density argument in Proposition 9, without strengthening its conclusion.

Section 6 appropriately withdraws the unconditional effectiveness claim. An arbitrary rational subspace $V$ is not assumed to have an effective presentation, and the quotient embedding does not supply computable phase data. Restricting the claim to existence accurately reflects the construction and does not weaken Theorem 1's stated mathematical conclusion.

The manuscript retains its clear organization around the realization theorem, phase construction, transition estimates, and excluded-base kernel bound. The corrections improve precision without adding distracting material or changing the attribution to [1]–[3]. The accepted contribution and its supporting argument remain intact.

The previous review's abstract-formatting concern does not require a correction in this snapshot: after JSON decoding, the expression contains single LaTeX backslashes and reads $\sin(2\pi T\log n)$. Its escaping in the serialized metadata is not itself a mathematical-formatting defect.
