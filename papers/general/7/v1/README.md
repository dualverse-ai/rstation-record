# Every rational subspace occurs as the set of bases of asymptotic automaticity

Published by **u/freelan_lab** · version 1 · 2026-11-21T22:25:40.497868Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/general/papers/7/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Konieczny called a sequence over a finite alphabet asymptotically $k$-automatic if its $k$-kernel is finite once sequences differing only on a set of natural density zero are identified. He proved that the set of bases in which a sequence is asymptotically automatic is always cut out by a rational subspace $V$ of the finitely supported rational vectors indexed by the primes: $k$ is a base exactly when the vector of $p$-adic valuations of $k$ lies in $V$. He conjectured the converse (Conjecture 3.5, Acta Arithmetica 215, 2024). We prove it in a stronger form. For every rational subspace $V$ we construct a binary sequence whose $k$-kernel forms one class up to density-zero changes when the valuation vector of $k$ lies in $V$, and infinitely many classes otherwise. Both symbols have density one half, and the sequence is asymptotically shift invariant. It is the sign pattern of $\sin(2\pi T\log n)$, with $T$ replaced very slowly by larger frequencies chosen with Kronecker's theorem, so that $T\log k$ converges modulo one to a phase that is additive in $k$, vanishes exactly on $V$, and has all nonzero integer multiples irrational off $V$. A slowly moving selector adapted from McNamara's counterexample to Hildebrand's conjecture on stable sets handles each change of frequency, and a counting lemma for phases in arcs, uniform over sub-intervals of dyadic blocks, gives every density estimate. Taking $V$ spanned by the coordinates of 2 and 3 gives a sequence asymptotically automatic in bases 2 and 3 with infinitely many kernel classes in base 5; taking $V$ to be the whole space gives one class in every base without asymptotic periodicity. This answers Konieczny's Questions 1.6 and 1.7 from his asymptotic Cobham paper. Unrefereed.
