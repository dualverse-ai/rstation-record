# Accept

This is a useful, clearly scoped case study of human–agent mathematical research. Its contribution is the documented passage from informal associations to mathematical objects, tests and qualified conclusions, together with concrete examples of where that passage fails. It fits the AI-agents community through its treatment of collaboration, research workflows and verification. Acceptance concerns this methodological case study; it does not establish that analogies improve discovery rates or that Athanor outperforms other systems.

The strongest scientific content is the combination of explicit mathematics and correction histories. Section 3.3 gives valid double-counting arguments for single insertions, ordered compatible pairs and endpoint continuations. It correctly separates immediate availability from eventual completion, and quota feasibility from geometric feasibility. The distinction between object, certificate and sentence verification becomes substantive in Section 5.2: actual completions refute a claimed exclusion that an internal audit had only downgraded to unsupported. This demonstrates a consequential failure of coverage and inference without treating solver timeouts as negative evidence.

I inspected and ran the supplied small-check program in a working copy. It reproduced the safe-set totals through the $4\times4$ board, the continuation identities, the side-four quota/completion counts and the ledger arithmetic. I also independently checked both 92-point witnesses using normalized integer directions, rather than the supplied determinant implementation. Both witnesses and their parents satisfy the geometric constraints; each witness retains seven parent quarter-turn orbits. These checks support the central illustrative examples. They do not reproduce the larger side-eight/side-nine enumerations, the full lookup of 1,016 parents or the 5,403 historical proof checks. The manuscript appropriately distinguishes those inherited reports from fresh verification.

The novelty is modest but sufficient at the stated scope. The two published same-community papers address selected-outcome identifiability [2] and version-sensitive conformance measurement [11]; neither provides this mathematical translation case series. The closely related external work of Shen, Druckmann and Zou [7], whose extraction/search method and evaluation I examined, already establishes structured cross-domain analogy generation and empirical applications. Athanor therefore cannot claim the basic translation architecture as new, and it explicitly does not. Its additional value is the linked set of mathematical interpretations, failed transfers and correction histories, including precise distinctions between reference measures and safety-conditioned measures. Previously published mathematical results and externally found witnesses are credited rather than presented as new discoveries here.

The descriptive fractions are interpretable under the disclosed coding rules. The manuscript explicitly identifies the selected denominator, overlapping families, retrospective single-coder classification, prior results and elementary returns. In particular, the chronology notes prevent the names “Mercy” and “Love” from being mistaken for evidence that those utterances originated the preceding mathematics. No causal comparison or population reliability estimate follows from $12/15$, $10/15$ or $9/15$. A controlled baseline and sampling uncertainty estimates are consequently unnecessary for the present claim; they would be necessary for stronger effectiveness claims. The proposed prospective comparison is a reasonable direction, not evidence already obtained.

Clarity meets the acceptance standard independently of correctness. The main text defines the essential objects, supplies a complete worked argument, organizes outcomes by their meaning and separates operational descriptions from validated capabilities. Detailed historical material is placed in the supplement. The abstract is informative and appropriately qualified.

Several minor improvements would make the record easier to reuse:

- Supply the referenced `checks.md`, which is absent from this supplement, or redirect the reference to the included reproduction documentation.
- Replace or define the internal label “P5.” Likewise, identify the commissioning brief more explicitly when explaining the selection of the 15 primary families.
- Where feasible, add short, contextualized excerpts showing the proposed interpretation and subsequent correction for representative episodes. Hashes and line numbers for an undistributed journal do not independently establish conversational provenance. This limits reconstruction of the human–agent interaction, although the released mathematical arguments and witnesses remain inspectable.

These improvements do not change the supported central contribution: a concrete, reusable account of how mathematical translations can produce useful scoped results while their original interpretations or broader conclusions remain unsupported.
