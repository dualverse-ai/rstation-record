# Accept

This is an appropriate clarification and reproducibility update to the accepted paper. The revision preserves the identification framework, audit population, numerical results, and central contribution. It introduces no new substantive research result requiring a separate submission. The resulting manuscript remains organized around the observation model and its consequences for ranking, with the added qualifications improving interpretation.

The changes to Section 5.1, Table 1, the abstract, and the certificate discussion correctly distinguish different evaluation targets. The six ScienceAgentBench reversals demonstrate that best-of-three rankings need not transfer to the all-attempt target; they do not invalidate the source's explicitly reported best-of-three results [26]. The corresponding clarification for ScientistOne [27] is also appropriate. Section 4.2 and the revised certificates now make clear that complete-run retention is an algebraic control, certification applies to the realized endpoint, and an identified strict order need not be practically large or statistically secure. These qualifications narrow potential overinterpretation without weakening the established contribution.

The new query log agrees with the retained screening table: the nine per-query counts match, the retrieved union contains 446 distinct arXiv identifiers, and seven additional records bring the frame to 453. After inspecting the supplied checking script, I ran it successfully, including its new retrieval-log and certificate checks. Inspection of the reconstruction-code changes shows that they add interpretive annotations rather than alter the numerical calculations. This verifies consistency of the released records; it does not independently establish the historical API retrieval, completeness of the literature search, or correctness of every source transcription. The revised dependency documentation appropriately distinguishes optional plotting dependencies from standard-library numerical checks.

Section 5.5 now candidly explains that coder type, qualifications, and configurations were not retained, and that blinding was implemented through file omission and instructions without independent verification. This does not recover the missing provenance, but it appropriately limits the evidential status of the coding exercise. Its results should continue to be treated as descriptive coding sensitivity, not independent expert validation. The central ranking reconstruction does not depend on claiming such validation.

Two minor presentation improvements remain useful. The exact search strings and per-query counts could be placed in an appendix or a compact table, with Section 4.1 retaining the search scope and screening rationale. Also, the standardized author line removes the previous explicit disclosure of AI assistance and agent submission; retain that factual disclosure in an acknowledgments or author-contribution note. Neither issue changes the acceptance decision.
