# Calibrated Geometry of Weil Classes, Double-Monomial $\mathrm{SU}(m)$ Designs, Chebyshev Endomorphism Projectors, and Closed-Form Jacobians of Entangled and Sporadic Complete-Intersection Cycles

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 2 · 2026-10-16T13:35:39.368690Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/4/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study differential-geometric calibration and correspondence projection of Weil classes on abelian $2m$-folds alongside explicit complete-intersection realizations of exceptional Hodge classes on Fermat varieties $X_d^4 \subset \mathbb{P}^5$, $X_{12}^2 \subset \mathbb{P}^3$, and $X_6^{2p} \subset \mathbb{P}^{2p+1}$. First, via the principal-angle decomposition on $\mathrm{Gr}(m,2m)$, we prove that every graph $m$-plane $\Gamma_B$ satisfies $|\langle P_{\Gamma_B},\Psi_\pm \rangle| = |\det B|/\det(I_m+B^\dagger B) \leq 2^{-m}$ (with equality iff $B \in \mathrm{U}(m)$) and obeys the Maclaurin inequality $\mathrm{Tr}_{E_k}(P_W)+\mathrm{Tr}_{E_{m-k}}(P_W) \geq 2\binom{m}{k}|\langle P_W,\Psi_\pm \rangle|$. Second, averaging sign-adjusted graphs $\Gamma_{-g} = \{w+gz=0\}$ over the special monomial group $G_m \subset \mathrm{SU}(m) \cap \mathrm{SL}_m(\mathbb{Z}[i])$ of order $4^{m-1}m!$ plus coordinate subtori yields an effective cycle $\mathcal{Z}_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(m)} \geq 0$ on $E_i^{2m}$ ($m \geq 2$) whose Hodge locus is the $m^2$-dimensional Weil PEL domain $\mathfrak{H}_{\mathrm{Weil}}(\phi)$. Third, we prove $i\partial\bar{\partial}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Weil}}(B(z)) = \gamma_Z^*(\omega_{\mathrm{Pl\ddot{u}cker}})$, forcing a qualitative calibration deficit $\Delta_{\mathrm{curv}}(Z)>0$ on simple Weil varieties, and construct an $(m+1)$-point rational Chebyshev-Weil projector $\Pi_{\mathrm{Cheb}}^{(m)} \in \mathbb{Q}[\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Q}}(A_\tau)]$ onto $W_{\mathrm{Weil}}(A_\tau)$, reducing the Hodge conjecture for $W_{\mathrm{Weil}}(A_\tau)$ on a simple fiber to a seed cycle with non-vanishing Weil period. Fourth, building on the Catalecticant rank theorem of Dan, Villaflor Loyola, and Kloosterman and the sextic realizations of Braun et al., we derive closed-form factored $6 \times 6$ Jacobians for general even-degree ($d=2m \geq 6$) double-Aoki-Shioda surfaces $Z^{(2m)} \subset X_{2m}^4$ and $Z_{\mathrm{nonic}} \subset X_9^4$. Fifth and sixth, at $d=12$ we construct weighted Brieskorn-Pham curves $C_{\mathrm{spor}}, C_{S_1,S_4} \subset X_{12}^2$ and entangled surfaces on $X_{12}^4$ (ranks $1605, 2653, 3018$) directly realizing $S_4, S_1$, and all $7$ pair-free orbits on $X_{12}^4$, and prove the Mod-3 Block Decomposition Law on $X_6^{2p}$.
