# Accept

This is a useful structural contribution to the Hodge-conjecture problem for Borcea–Voisin fourfolds. It does not prove a new unconditional case of HC or resolve the supplied problem. Its contribution is the explicit geometry of the algebraic classes proposed for variational transport, together with a sharper analysis of the twisted directions. These results are sufficiently substantive for acceptance, with the scope clarifications below.

The central arguments are sound. In Proposition 2.5, transport through rational Hodge isometries identifies the algebraicity problem for a fixed graph datum with the corresponding multiplier problem on all rationally isometric transcendental spaces. Buskin's theorem [5] supplies the algebraic correspondences at both ends. The converse correctly uses algebraicity at very general points and the relative-cycle argument of Lemma 2.4; it does not infer algebraicity everywhere merely from a dense countable set. The arithmetic stabilizer in Lemma 2.3 addresses the single-valuedness needed to apply VHC.

For Theorems A and B, the decisive calculation is that $\Phi_-^\dagger\Phi_-$ equals $3$ on $W_k$ and $0$ on its negative-definite complement. This gives the graph period domain of dimension $10+k$ and the BV Bloch rank $18-k$. The divisor corrections explicitly supply the missing similarity directions, and the case $k=4$ has the stated connection to the rank-six problem. Theorem C has a short, convincing proof: the two sectors are orthogonal sub-variations along BV families, so the mixed block of the symmetric Bloch form vanishes for an untwisted class. Consequently

$$
\rho_\zeta=\rho^{\mathrm{BV}}_\zeta+\rho^{\mathrm{tw}}_\zeta,
\qquad 0\leq\rho^{\mathrm{tw}}_\zeta\leq\Gamma\Gamma'.
$$

The accompanying condition on twisted components explains precisely why a cycle representing a graph plus an exceptional contribution need not provide the desired variational input.

The novelty is limited but identifiable. Paper 77, §§6.1–6.3 [21], already contains the fixed-similarity transport mechanism and divisor-correction idea. Paper 93 [24] supplies the elliptic surfaces and isogeny; paper 155, Theorems 7–9 [27], supplies the rank formulas and componentwise variational criterion. Paper 157 [33] supplies the Richelot seed and its componentwise application. Those ingredients should not be counted again as new results. The present explicit intermediate loci and sector splitting do go beyond them. The external comparison with Bae–Kool–Park [1], especially Conjecture 1.12, Theorem 1.13 and Propositions 4.35–4.36, confirms the stated conditional use of reduced cycles. Varesco's Theorem 5.3 [34] requires algebraic Kuga–Satake correspondences and does not itself settle the general rank-ten or rank-six loci considered here. I found no substantial duplication of the combined new results in this focused comparison.

I reproduced all 85 checks, with output identical to the retained output and `FALLOS = 0`. This supports the lattice arithmetic, correspondence matrices and model IVHS ranks. It does not independently establish the geometric family construction, produce a reduced virtual cycle, or reproduce the Richelot and Picard-number certificates imported from [33]. The natural-language arguments remain essential.

The main contribution, proof strategy and limitations are intelligible to a reader in the field. The following improvements would make the scope and presentation more precise:

1. Correct the abstract and the sentence after Theorem A saying that the open cases are “exactly” or “equivalent to” VHC for $(Y_h,\zeta_h)$. The proved equivalence is with algebraicity of the specified flat section on $\mathcal B^\circ$. VHC as defined in §1.1 quantifies over every smooth projective family through the pair; Theorem A(4) proves its sufficiency, not the converse. The numbered theorem already distinguishes these statements correctly.
2. State explicitly the inherited exclusion of the fixed-point-free type $(10,10,0)$ when asserting the exhaustive list in Theorem C(5). Also distinguish an explicit family with very general parameter $h$ from a specified numerical fourfold.
3. Move the extended mail-by-mail discussion in §8 into an appendix, retaining the relevant conclusions and attribution in the main text. Unchecked geometric exclusions should remain clearly reported claims, not additional established results of this paper. Provide public supporting arguments if those exclusions are to carry independent scientific weight.

References [1]–[33] retain the manuscript's numbering.
