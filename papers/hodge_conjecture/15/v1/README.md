# Generic Algebraic Correspondences and the Rational Hodge Conjecture for Very General $\mathfrak{S}_3$-Symmetric Cyclic Quintic Surface Squares, Calabi–Yau Quintic Threefold Squares, and Split Quintic Fourfolds and Sixfolds

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 1 · 2026-10-18T01:17:22.769404Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/15/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for four interrelated geometric families at very general parameters in the three-parameter $\mathfrak{S}_3$-symmetric family of ternary quintics $g_B(x_0, x_1, x_2) = \sum x_i^5 + B_1 m_{320} + B_2 m_{311} + B_3 m_{221}$: (1) general-type cyclic quintic surface squares $\Sigma_B \times \Sigma_B \subset \mathbb{P}^3 \times \mathbb{P}^3$ ($p_g = 4, b_4 = 2{,}811$, $47$ rational Hodge classes); (2) split quintic fourfolds $X_B^{(4)} = V(g_B(x) - g_B(y)) \subset \mathbb{P}^5$ ($h^{3,1} = 120, b_4 = 821$, $30$ Hodge classes); (3) Calabi–Yau quintic threefold squares $Y_B \times Y_B \subset \mathbb{P}^4 \times \mathbb{P}^4$ ($h^{3,0} = 1, b_3 = 204$, level $3$, $b_6 = 41{,}620$ with $220$ Hodge classes, including $41{,}616$ in $H^3(Y_B)^{\otimes 2}$ with $216$ Hodge classes); and (4) Hodge-level-$4$ split quintic sixfolds $X_B^{(6)} \subset \mathbb{P}^7$ ($h^{5,1} = 36, b_6 = 13{,}109$, $137$ Hodge classes). Under $\mu_5 \times \mathfrak{S}_3$, $H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}(\Sigma_B, \mathbb{Q})$ ($\dim = 52$) splits over $K = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$ into Type I ($\dim_K = 1$, pure $(1,1)$, algebraic on $\Sigma_B$ by Lefschetz $(1,1)$), Type II (two blocks of $\dim_K = 4$, ball $\mathbb{B}^3$), and Type III ($\dim_K = 4$, balls $\mathbb{B}^3$). Closed-form Macaulay determinants $\det M_{5,\mathrm{III}}$, $\det M_{6,\mathrm{III}}$, $\det M_{6,\mathrm{II}}$ establish open period images in $\mathbb{B}^3$. Using the countability of rational homomorphisms $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Q}}(W_0, W'_0)$ on the universal cover $\widetilde{U}$, open-set irreducibility of $W_{\chi_1}|_{U_0}$, and Galois rank-invariance $\tau_t(f_a \pi_m) = f_a \pi_{tm \bmod 5}$ of the rational branch projectors $f_a \in \mathbb{Q}[\mu_5]$, we eliminate all cross-branch/cross-type Hodge morphisms, prove $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{Q}-\mathrm{Hdg}}(W_B) = K$ and generic $\mathbb{Q}$-Hodge simplicity ($P_S^2 = P_S \in K \implies P_S \in \{0, 1\}$, without assuming $K$-stability), and realize all transcendental Hodge classes by explicit group-ring correspondences of verified matrix ranks $20$ and $100$.
