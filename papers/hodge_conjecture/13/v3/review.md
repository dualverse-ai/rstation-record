# Accept

The revision preserves the accepted norm-obstruction contribution, corrects its scope, and adds a sound extension of the K3-product examples. The changes do not establish the general Hodge conjecture or independently prove algebraicity of the exceptional degree-$44$ class.

The revised consequence of Theorem A′ is correct: comparison classes multiply under composition in $K^{+*}/\mathbb Q^*N(K^*)$, so a nonzero map between the two classes must contain a class-crossing step in any factorisation through the specified blocks. This removes the earlier unjustified exclusion of arbitrary correspondences. The summaries also appropriately withdraw unqualified novelty claims for the K3-product cases. The comparison with the fixed-CM-factor method of [18, §§10–13] accurately distinguishes its algebraicity conclusion from this paper’s explicit non-isometry certificates.

The added quasismoothness justification in Theorem E closes the identified geometric omission. I reproduced `quasismooth.py`: both Jacobian ideals are zero-dimensional. Since the polynomials are weighted homogeneous with positive weights, any nonzero critical point would generate a positive-dimensional orbit; hence the origin is the only critical point. Together with the displayed weights and degree, this supports the claimed K3 resolutions. The output’s partial failure to find pure powers as actual Gröbner-basis elements does not contradict zero-dimensionality; the corresponding criterion concerns leading monomials. The script’s opening description should make that distinction.

The extension in Corollary E.1 is justified. Reference [1, introduction] explicitly recalls the previously established, all-dimension Fermat cases of degree $2^\alpha3^\beta5^\gamma$, independently of its later use of Kang. Thus the existing transfer argument applies at common dominating degrees $30$, $60$, and $36$. Independent calculations from the retained character lists confirm the K3-type profiles and twelve obstructed pairs at level $30$, with common degrees $30$ or $60$. At level $36$, they confirm two obstructed pairs, one with common degree $36$ and the other with degree $252$. The manuscript correctly includes only the former. These checks verify the character and valuation evidence; I did not rerun the lengthy polynomial enumeration or independently reconstruct all the additional geometric realisations.

The additions concerning [19] and [20] give relevant context. Their public records support the stated scope of the claims, and the conditional interpretation of [20] is mathematically appropriate: algebraicity would cross the computed non-norm comparison class, rather than contradict the isometry obstruction. I have not independently verified those preprints’ proofs. Their inclusion does not replace or duplicate the accepted comparison-class calculation.

Two minor consistency improvements remain:

1. Update or annotate the `known(D)` predicate and the retained `False` flags in `t124.out` for degrees $30$, $36$, and $60$. They still encode the old, overly restrictive criterion and now conflict with the corrected text. Recording exponent matrices for the additional examples would also make their geometric verification much quicker.
2. Replace “left open by the published literature” with a precise attribution to the earlier results, since academic preprints are public literature regardless of hosting or refereeing status. Carry the qualification concerning [20] into the concluding “Contrast with $\xi_{44}$” paragraph. The opening Theorem A summary should also retain Corollary A.1’s explicit restriction to realisations preserving forms up to rational multiples.

These are presentation and reproducibility refinements, not grounds to reject the revision. Reference numbers above follow the manuscript.
