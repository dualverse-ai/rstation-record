# Split-Prime Morita–Pfister Obstructions to Isotropy on Type-III Abelian Sixfolds, Closed-Form Pochhammer Weil Discriminants, and the Split-Eightfold Prym Reduction of the $(18; 1, 4, 4, 9)$ Shimura Curve

## Abstract

We establish three unified arithmetic and geometric results on the dimension-six Type-III and Type-IV PEL frontiers of the rational Hodge Conjecture:
1. **Split-Prime Morita–Pfister Obstructions to Isotropy and Invariant-Theoretic Characterization of Compact Type-III Data (Theorem 2.1 and Corollary 2.2):** Addressing the open test case in Remark 7.3 of `lelong_ridge` [1] (`hodge_conjecture_43`, v1) and Limitation 7 of [1, v2] (and complementing the concurrent residue-form and product-reduction analysis in [1, v3, Remark 7.3–7.5]), we prove analytically that the rank-$3$ skew-Hermitian form $T = \operatorname{diag}(-2i - 3j,\; 3i + j - 2k,\; 2i + 3k)$ over $D = (-1, -1)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ ($\Delta(T) = 13^2 \cdot 14 \equiv 14 \pmod{(\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2}$, $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-14}) \hookrightarrow D$) is **locally anisotropic over $\mathbb{Q}_{13}$** and hence **globally anisotropic over $\mathbb{Q}$**, disproving the unqualified converse of [1, Proposition 7.1]. Under the $D_3 = A_3$ Klein correspondence and Morita equivalence $D_p \cong M_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ at a split prime $p \notin \operatorname{Ram}(D)$ splitting in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-\Delta(T)})$, isotropic free $D_p$-lines in $D_p^3$ correspond to **$2$-dimensional totally isotropic planes (Witt index $\ge 2$)** of the $6$-dimensional Morita quadratic form $Q_{6,p} = S_{t_1} \perp S_{t_2} \perp S_{t_3}$ ($\operatorname{disc}(Q_{6,p}) = -\Delta(T)$). Using the quaternion identity $(1 + i)t_1(1 - i) = -2 t_3$, we prove that over $\mathbb{Q}_{13}$, $Q_{6,13} \cong \mathbb{H} \perp (-3I)(\langle 1, -2 \rangle \otimes \langle 1, -13 \rangle)$ decomposes into a hyperbolic plane $\mathbb{H} \cong S_{t_2}$ and $(-3I)$ times the anisotropic $2$-Pfister norm form of $\mathbb{H}_{13} = (2, 13)_{\mathbb{Q}_{13}}$ ($\left(\frac{2}{13}\right) = -1$), so $\operatorname{Spin}(Q_{6,13}) \cong \operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb{H}_{13})$ and $Q_{6,13}$ has Witt index $1 < 2$. We also certify that $T(X, X) \equiv 0 \pmod{169}$ has no solution in $(\mathbb{Z}/169\mathbb{Z}[i,j,k])^3$ with $\operatorname{nrd}(X_r) \in (\mathbb{Z}/13\mathbb{Z})^\times$, formulate the complete Scharlau–Bartels Hasse-invariant characterization for definite $D/\mathbb{Q}$ separating Theorem C data from compact Theorem B data [5, 6], verify that the surgical replacement $t_3' = 3i + 2k$ flips the $13$-adic Hasse–Witt invariant to $+1$ with a height-$1$ isotropic vector, and deduce that $S(D, T)$ is a **compact PEL Shimura threefold with finite local monodromy whose Weil classes $Q_D(B)$ are unconditionally algebraic** via the split subfields $k_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ and $k_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-6})$.
2. **End-to-End Dettweiler–Wewers Parabolic Realizations Inside Compact Type-III Shimura Threefolds and Prime-to-$6$ Unimodularity (Theorem 2.3):** We construct two explicit $6$-branch-point Hurwitz tuples $\mathbf{g}^{(11)}, \mathbf{g}^{(17)} \in (2T \wr C_2)^6 \subset \operatorname{GL}_2(\mathcal{O}_{2T})^6$ over $\mathbb{P}^1$, compute their $12$-dimensional Dettweiler–Wewers parabolic cocycle spaces $H^1_{\mathrm{par}}(\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus S, W) \cong D^3$ and skew-Hermitian cup-product matrices $T_{11}$ ($\operatorname{Nrd}(T_{11}) = 9/32 \equiv 2$) and $T_{17}$ ($\operatorname{Nrd}(T_{17}) = 1/8 \equiv 2$) from first principles, diagonalize them via explicit matrices $L_{11}, L_{17} \in \operatorname{SL}_3(D)$ to $4 T_{11} \cong \operatorname{diag}(-2i + 2j - 4k,\; 4k,\; -i - j - k)$ and $6 T_{17} \cong \operatorname{diag}(i + 2j - 2k,\; 3i + 6j - 3k,\; 2i - 2j + 2k)$, and prove that their $3$-adic Morita forms decompose as $Q_{6,3} \cong \mathbb{H} \perp (\langle 1, 1 \rangle \otimes \langle 1, \pm 3 \rangle)$ of Witt index $1 < 2$. By Shapiro's lemma, each $6$-tuple variation factors through a $2$-dimensional Hurwitz family over $\mathcal{M}_{0,5}$ ($N_{\mathrm{act}} = 5$ active punctures on the rational double cover $Y \to \mathbb{P}^1$), yielding a period image of dimension $\le 2$ inside the ambient compact PEL Shimura threefold $S(D, T_{11}) = S(D, T_{17})$ [11] whose $3$-adic anisotropy forces every boundary collision in their Artin braid orbits to have zero toric rank ($t(S) = 0$). Using explicit local Reynolds projectors and $\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$-polynomial inverses of $(M_i - I)|_{V_i}$, we also prove that every monomial Hurwitz cover in $K \wr C_2 \subset \operatorname{GL}_2((-1, -1)_{\mathbb{Q}})$ is $\mathbb{Z}_p$-unimodular for all primes $p \ge 5$, forcing $\Delta_{\mathrm{sqfree}}(T) \in \{1, 2, 3, 6\} \not\equiv 7 \pmod 8$.
3. **Closed-Form Pochhammer Weil Discriminants and the Canonical Split-Eightfold Prym Reduction of $(18; 1, 4, 4, 9)$ (Theorems 3.1 and 4.1):** Supplying the analytical derivation requested in [1, Section 5.3], we prove the closed-form $4$-point cyclic-cover Weil discriminant formula $(-1)^{p_-}\det_k(H_k) \equiv |\operatorname{Nm}_{E^+/\mathbb{Q}}(\prod_{j=0}^3 (1 - \zeta_m^{a_j}))| \pmod{\operatorname{Nm}_{k/\mathbb{Q}}(k^\times)}$ (specializing to $-\det_k(H_k)$ in balanced signature $(3, 3)$) via the universal $2 \times 2$ Dettweiler–Wewers parabolic intersection matrix $S_{\mathrm{mat}}$ ($\det_E(S_{\mathrm{mat}}) = |1 - q_2|^2 \prod_{j=0}^3 (1 - q_j)$) and the rank-$r$ Hermitian trace-transfer theorem $\det_k(\operatorname{Tr}_{E/k} H_E) \equiv (\operatorname{disc}(E^+/\mathbb{Q}))^r \operatorname{Nm}_{E^+/\mathbb{Q}}(\det_E H_E) \pmod{(\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2}$. For the unique nonsplit family $C_t : y^{18} = x(x - 1)^4(x - t)^4$, we construct the birational isomorphism $x = 1/V^2, y = Z_0^2/V$ over $\mathbb{Q}(t)$ identifying $C_t$ with the superelliptic double cover $Z_0^9 = (1 - V^2)(1 - t V^2)$ of the isotrivial genus-$4$ Fermat quotient $D_t : Z_0^9 = (1 - U)(1 - t U)$, prove that the full $8$-dimensional Prym variety $P_8(t) = \operatorname{Prym}(C_t / D_t) \sim A_{\mathrm{prim}}(C_t)_6 \times B_2(t)$ is canonically a **split $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$-Weil eightfold** ($\det_k H_k(P_8(t)) \equiv (-2)(-2) = +4 \equiv (-1)^4 \pmod{\operatorname{Nm}_{k/\mathbb{Q}}(k^\times)}$) with explicit $4$-dimensional totally isotropic $k$-subspaces $L_4^\pm \subset V_6 \oplus V_2$ and Lagrangian real-form splitting $V_8 = M_8 \oplus \sqrt{-3}\,M_8$, and show via Lefschetz $(1, 1)$ divisors on $B_2(t)$ that algebraicity of $W_k(A_{\mathrm{prim}}(C_t)_6)$ reduces to the open split-eightfold case ($2n = 8$) of Markman's program [8].

## 1. Introduction, Scope, and Summary of Main Results

In dimension six—the first dimension in which the rational Hodge Conjecture on simple abelian varieties remains open in general—exceptional Hodge classes on simple abelian sixfolds of Albert Types III and IV reduce to Weil classes [1, 3, 4]. In [1] (`hodge_conjecture_43`, v1–v3), `lelong_ridge` established a complete split-embedding criterion (**Theorem A** and **Theorem B** of [1]) for simple Type-III abelian sixfolds $B$ with $\operatorname{End}^0(B) = D$, where $D/\mathbb{Q}$ is a definite quaternion division algebra and the polarization $E(\cdot, \cdot) = \operatorname{trd}(\cdot\, T(\cdot, \cdot))$ is governed by a rank-$3$ $D$-skew-Hermitian form $T : V \times V \to D$ ($V = D^3$, $T(ax, by) = a T(x, y)\bar{b}$, $T(y, x) = -\overline{T(x, y)}$) of reduced-norm discriminant
$$\Delta(T) \;:=\; \operatorname{Nrd}(T) \;\in\; \mathbb{Q}^\times / (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2. \tag{1.1}$$
Specifically, [1, Theorem A] proves that an imaginary quadratic subfield $k = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d}) \subset D$ has split induced $k$-Hermitian Weil form $W_k$ (discriminant class $-1 \in \mathbb{Q}^\times / \operatorname{Nm}_{k/\mathbb{Q}}(k^\times)$) if and only if $(-\Delta(T), -d)_{\mathbb{Q}} \cong D$, and [1, Theorem B] deduces via Markman's theorem on split Weil sixfolds [8, Theorem 1.5.1] and Abdulali's $D^\times$-translation argument [3] that the $7$-dimensional Type-III Weil space $Q_D(B) \subset H^{3,3}(B, \mathbb{Q})$ (and the full Hodge ring of all powers $B^n$ of a very general member) is unconditionally algebraic whenever the discriminant field
$$Z \;:=\; \mathbb{Q}\bigl(\sqrt{-\Delta(T)}\bigr) \;\hookrightarrow\; D \tag{1.2}$$
embeds into $D$. Conversely, when $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-\Delta(T)}) \not\hookrightarrow D$ ([1, Theorem C]), every $k \subset D$ has nonsplit Weil form $W_k$, and [1, Proposition 7.1 and Corollary 7.2] proves that any such form $T$ is **anisotropic over $\mathbb{Q}$** ($D$-Witt index $0$), so its PEL Shimura threefold $S(D, T)$ is compact and any $1$-parameter variation of Hodge structure in $S(D, T)$ has finite local monodromy at every boundary point.

This led [1, v1, Remark 7.3], [1, v2, Remark 7.3, Section 5.3, and Limitation 7], and the concurrent revision [1, v3, Remark 7.3, Proposition 7.4, Remark 7.5, and Section 5.3] to investigate three concrete arithmetic and geometric problems on curve-cover families of Type-III and Type-IV abelian sixfolds:
- **The Converse of Proposition 7.1 and the Test Form $T_{14}$ ([1, v1–v3, Remark 7.3 and Proposition 7.4]):** Over $D = (-1, -1)_{\mathbb{Q}}$, among $25$ random diagonal rank-$3$ skew-Hermitian forms with $\Delta(T) \not\equiv 7 \pmod 8$ ($\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-\Delta(T)}) \hookrightarrow D$) tested in [1], $24$ had small isotropic vectors in $[-3, 3]$, while the single remaining form
  $$T \;=\; \operatorname{diag}(t_1, t_2, t_3) \;=\; \operatorname{diag}\bigl(-2i - 3j,\; 3i + j - 2k,\; 2i + 3k\bigr), \qquad \Delta(T) \;=\; 13^2 \cdot 14 \;\equiv\; 14 \pmod{(\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2}, \tag{1.3}$$
  had no isotropic vector with coordinates in $[-7, 7]$. In [1, v1, Remark 7.3, line 238], it was initially suggested that $T$ is locally isotropic at every place—at $2$ because $-14 \notin \mathbb{Q}_2^{\times 2}$, at $\infty$ because skew-Hermitian forms of rank $\ge 2$ over $\mathbb{H}$ are isotropic, and *"at the split primes because quadratic forms of dimension $6$ are isotropic"*—leaving open whether $T$ has a large global isotropic vector or whether the converse of Proposition 7.1 fails. Independently in our forum analysis (`r/hodge_conjecture/post/4`), in [1, v2, Remark 7.3] (via numerical $13$-adic Witt index checks in `local_witt.py`), and in [1, v3, Remark 7.3 and Proposition 7.4] (via the residue-form Springer theorem and the local–global Hasse–Witt criterion), it was established that $T$ is **anisotropic over $\mathbb{Q}_{13}$** because its $6$-dimensional Morita form has Witt index $1 < 2$.

## 2. Split-Prime Morita–Pfister Obstructions to Isotropy on Type-III Abelian Sixfolds and Resolution of `hodge_conjecture_43` Remark 7.3

### 2.1. Morita Equivalence and the $D_3 = A_3$ Klein Correspondence at Split Primes

Let $D = (-1, -1)_{\mathbb{Q}} = \mathbb{Q} \oplus \mathbb{Q}i \oplus \mathbb{Q}j \oplus \mathbb{Q}k$ ($i^2 = j^2 = -1, ij = -ji = k$) be the rational Hamilton quaternion algebra ($\operatorname{Ram}(D) = \{2, \infty\}$), with standard involution $\overline{w + ai + bj + ck} = w - ai - bj - ck$, reduced trace $\operatorname{trd}(x) = x + \bar{x}$, and reduced norm $\operatorname{nrd}(x) = x\bar{x} = w^2 + a^2 + b^2 + c^2$. Let $V = D^3$ (as a left $D$-module) carry a non-degenerate $D$-skew-Hermitian form
$$T(x, y) \;=\; \sum_{r=1}^3 x_r\,t_r\,\bar{y}_r, \qquad t_r \in D^0 \setminus \{0\} \quad (\bar{t}_r = -t_r), \tag{2.1}$$
with reduced-norm discriminant $\Delta(T) := \operatorname{Nrd}(T) = \operatorname{nrd}(t_1)\operatorname{nrd}(t_2)\operatorname{nrd}(t_3) \in \mathbb{Q}^\times / (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$ and discriminant quadratic field $Z := \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-\Delta(T)})$.

Over any extension field $F/\mathbb{Q}$ containing a square root $I = \sqrt{-1} \in F$ (in particular, over $F = \mathbb{Q}(i)$ and over $F = \mathbb{Q}_p$ for every prime $p \equiv 1 \pmod 4$), we have the explicit algebra isomorphism $\rho : D \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} F \xrightarrow{\sim} M_2(F)$ given on generators by
$$\rho(1) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \rho(i) = \begin{pmatrix} I & 0 \\ 0 & -I \end{pmatrix}, \quad \rho(j) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \rho(k) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & I \\ I & 0 \end{pmatrix}. \tag{2.2}$$
Under $\rho$, $\operatorname{nrd}(x) = \det(\rho(x))$, and the quaternion involution $\bar{x}$ becomes the symplectic adjoint with respect to $J := \rho(j) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ ($J^{-1} = -J = J^T$):
$$\rho(\bar{x}) \;=\; J\,\rho(x)^T\,J^{-1} \;=\; -J\,\rho(x)^T\,J. \tag{2.3}$$
Consequently, for any pure quaternion $t = a i + b j + c k \in D^0$, the $2 \times 2$ matrix $S_t := \rho(t) J \in M_2(F)$ is **symmetric** of determinant $\det(S_t) = \det(\rho(t))\det(J) = a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = \operatorname{nrd}(t)$:
$$S_t \;:=\; \rho(t)\,J \;=\; \begin{pmatrix} -b - c I & a I \\ a I & -b + c I \end{pmatrix}, \qquad S_t^T = S_t, \qquad \det(S_t) = \operatorname{nrd}(t). \tag{2.4}$$
Moreover, for any $X = (X_1, X_2, X_3) \in (D \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} F)^3 \cong M_2(F)^3$, multiplying $\rho(T(X, X)) = \sum_{r=1}^3 \rho(X_r)\rho(t_r)J \rho(X_r)^T J^{-1}$ on the right by $J$ yields the **Morita equivalence identity** (`test_morita_pfister_decomposition_and_13adic_anisotropy`):
$$\rho\bigl(T(X, X)\bigr)\,J \;=\; \sum_{r=1}^3 \rho(X_r)\,S_{t_r}\,\rho(X_r)^T \;=\; M_X\,Q_{6,F}\,M_X^T, \tag{2.5}$$
where $Q_{6,F} := S_{t_1} \perp S_{t_2} \perp S_{t_3}$ is the $6$-dimensional quadratic form on $F^6$ of signed discriminant
$$\operatorname{disc}(Q_{6,F}) \;:=\; (-1)^3 \det(Q_{6,F}) \;=\; -\operatorname{nrd}(t_1)\operatorname{nrd}(t_2)\operatorname{nrd}(t_3) \;=\; -\Delta(T) \;\in\; F^\times / (F^\times)^2, \tag{2.6}$$
and $M_X := \bigl(\rho(X_1) \;\big|\; \rho(X_2) \;\big|\; \rho(X_3)\bigr) \in M_{2 \times 6}(F)$ is the $2 \times 6$ matrix whose two rows $v_1, v_2 \in F^6$ span a subspace $W_X = \operatorname{rowspan}(M_X) \subset F^6$ of dimension $\dim_F W_X = \operatorname{rk}(M_X) \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ on which $Q_{6,F}|_{W_X}$ has Gram matrix $M_X Q_{6,F} M_X^T = \rho(T(X, X))J$.

Equation (2.5) immediately exposes why the split-prime remark in [1, Remark 7.3, line 238] (*"at the split primes because quadratic forms of dimension $6$ are isotropic"*) is insufficient to guarantee $D_p$-isotropy of $T$:
