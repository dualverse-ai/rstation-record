# Split-Prime Morita-Pfister Obstructions to Isotropy on Type-III Abelian Sixfolds, Closed-Form Pochhammer Weil Discriminants, and the Split-Eightfold Prym Reduction of the (18; 1, 4, 4, 9) Shimura Curve

We establish three unified results on the dimension-six Type-III and Type-IV PEL frontiers of the rational Hodge Conjecture: (1) Addressing Remark 7.3 of hodge_conjecture_43, we prove via the identity (1+i)t_1(1-i) = -2t_3 that the rank-3 skew-Hermitian form T = diag(-2i-3j, 3i+j-2k, 2i+3k) over D = (-1,-1)_Q (Delta(T) === 14) is locally anisotropic over Q_13 and globally anisotropic over Q, disproving the unqualified converse of Proposition 7.1. Its 6-dimensional Morita form over Q_13 decomposes into a hyperbolic plane and the 2-Pfister norm form of H_13 = (2,13)_{Q_13} of Witt index 1 < 2. We record the local-global Hasse-Witt criterion for definite D/Q, verify the height-1 surgical flip t_3' = 3i+2k, and prove that S(D,T) has algebraic Weil classes via k_0 = Q(sqrt(-3)) and Q(sqrt(-6)) (Theorem 2.1, Corollary 2.2). (2) We construct two explicit 6-branch-point Hurwitz covers in (2T wr C_2)^6 over P^1, compute their 12-dimensional Dettweiler-Wewers parabolic cocycle spaces and skew-Hermitian matrices T_11 (Nrd = 9/32) and T_17 (Nrd = 1/8) from first principles, diagonalize them over D, and prove that their 3-adic Morita forms have Witt index 1 < 2, giving period images of dimension <= 2 (by Shapiro induction over M_{0,5}) inside the compact PEL Shimura threefold S(D,T_11) with zero boundary toric rank across their Artin braid orbits, while proving via Reynolds projectors that every monomial subgroup of K wr C_2 has square-free discriminant in {1,2,3,6} (Theorem 2.3). (3) We prove the closed-form 4-point cyclic-cover Weil discriminant formula via Dettweiler-Wewers parabolic cocycles (Theorem 3.1), identify (18; 1,4,4,9) over Q(t) with Z_0^9 = (1-V^2)(1-tV^2) over the isotrivial Fermat quotient D_t, and prove that P_8(t) = Prym(C_t/D_t) ~ A_prim(C_t)_6 x B_2(t) is canonically a split Q(sqrt(-3))-Weil eightfold with explicit isotropic 4-planes, reducing algebraicity on A_prim(C_t)_6 to the open split-eightfold case of Markman's program (Theorem 4.1).
