# All-Genus K-Linear Seifert-Bruhat Determinant Law, the Foster-Greenwood Dichotomy Between G_24, G_29 and G_31, and Hurwitz-Hodge Dominance for Q(i)- and Q(sqrt(-7))-Weil Sixfolds

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/58/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For an imaginary quadratic field K = Q(sqrt(-d_K)) and a positive-definite d-dimensional K-Hermitian space (V, h_V), we prove an all-genus K-linear Seifert-Bruhat determinant formula for the parabolic K-Hermitian form H_par of any generating tuple in U_d(K, h_V) with V^Pi = 0 and even dimension 2m: (-1)^m det_K(H_par) is congruent modulo N_{K/Q}(K^*) to the product of local Wall invariants Theta(C_j). For arbitrary finite-order elements with det_K(g) in {+1, -1}, we prove an unconditional Hodge-balance character law (-1)^k Theta(g) = (-sqrt(-d_K))^delta q(g) with positive rational q(g), whose complex phases cancel on every balanced (m, m) family in any genus. Across all conjugacy classes of G_24 in U_3(Q(sqrt(-7))) and G_29 in U_4(Q(i)), q(C) lies in N_{K/Q}(K^*), making every balanced Hurwitz family split. Conversely, in G_31 in U_4(Q(i)), three regular classes with Foster-Greenwood reflection-length excess 6 > 4 have q(C) = 3 outside N_{Q(i)/Q}(Q(i)^*) and avoid all proper reflection subgroups: (R^10, C_34) is 100% generating with volume 45,147,375/16 > 0, immersing an 8-dimensional divisor of non-split Q(i)-Weil sixfolds with algebraic Weil classes while codimension bounds preclude dimension 9. Finally, exact Ramanujan-trace Mobius inversion yields I_gen(G_29, R^11, C_12) = 13,097,700 and I_gen(G_24, R^12) = 5,322,240 on M_{0,12}, proving Markman-free algebraicity of W_K(A) on split sixfolds, all fourfold discriminant classes, and simple split powers over Q(i) and Q(sqrt(-7)).
