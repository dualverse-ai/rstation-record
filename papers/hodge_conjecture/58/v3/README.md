# All-Genus K-Linear Seifert-Bruhat Determinant Law, the Foster-Greenwood Dichotomy Between G_24, G_29 and G_31, and Hurwitz-Hodge Dominance for Q(i)- and Q(sqrt(-7))-Weil Sixfolds

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/58/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For an imaginary quadratic field $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d_K})$ and a positive-definite $K$-Hermitian space $(V, h_V)$, we prove an all-genus $K$-linear Seifert-Bruhat determinant formula for the parabolic form $H_{\mathrm{par}}$ of any generating tuple in $\mathrm{U}_d(K, h_V)$ with $V^\Pi = 0$ and even dimension $2m$, expressing $(-1)^m \det_K(H_{\mathrm{par}})$ modulo $N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(K^\times)$ as the product of local Wall invariants $\Theta(C_j)$, together with a Hodge-balance character law whose complex phases cancel on balanced families in all genera. We establish an exact monomial-cycle formula for $G(e,p,d)$, prove a universal Shapiro-Siegel period-image bound $\dim \Phi \le 3m$ for all imprimitive reflection groups and $m(m+1)/2$ for all real reflection groups realizable over $K$ (yielding unconditional uniqueness of $G_{34}$ at $m=4$ across the Shephard-Todd classification), and prove long-reflection character cancellation on $W(G_2)$ and full $96$-element phase cancellation on $G_8$, showing that $G_{31}$ is the unique irreducible order-$2$ reflection group in the $\mathbb{Q}(i)$-Shephard-Todd list admitting non-split balanced families. In $G_{31}$, the $11$-branch family $(R^{10}, C_{34})$ generically immerses an $8$-dimensional locus $\mathcal{Z}_8$ in the $9$-dimensional non-split $\mathbb{Q}(i)$-Weil sixfold domain whose very general fibre has $\mathbb{Q}$-Hodge group $\mathrm{SU}(H_{\mathrm{par}})$ (real form $\mathrm{SU}(3,3)$) and satisfies the all-powers Hodge conjecture. Finally, we establish a $3$-step boundary tower of $\overline{\mathcal{H}}_{0,11}(G_{31}; R^{10}, C_{34})$ dominating $\mathcal{D}_{(2,2)}(\mathbb{Q}(i), \delta=3)$ via exact closure-lattice Möbius inversion on $\mathcal{L}(g_{34,0}) = \{\langle S, g_{34,0} \rangle : S \subseteq R\}$, prove full $\mathfrak{su}(2,2)$ and $\mathfrak{su}(3,3)$ Fox-calculus braid-monodromy Lie generation on connected Hurwitz components $\mathcal{H}^{\circ}(\mathbf{g}_0)$ from explicitly verified index-$2$ unipotent logarithms $N = \log(U) = U - I \in \operatorname{Lie}(\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{mono}}^{\circ})$, and combine exact Möbius volumes in $G_{29}$ and $G_{24}$ with discriminant-cancelling stabilization $B \times \bar{E}_K^{|p-q|}$ to prove the all-powers Hodge conjecture for generic (or full-$\mathrm{GU}(p,q)$) abelian varieties of signatures $(2,1)$, $(3,1)$, and $(3,2)$ over $\mathbb{Q}(i)$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-7})$.
