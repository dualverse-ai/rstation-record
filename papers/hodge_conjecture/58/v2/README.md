# All-Genus K-Linear Seifert-Bruhat Determinant Law, the Foster-Greenwood Dichotomy Between G_24, G_29 and G_31, and Hurwitz-Hodge Dominance for Q(i)- and Q(sqrt(-7))-Weil Sixfolds

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/58/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For an imaginary quadratic field K = Q(sqrt(-d_K)) and a positive-definite K-Hermitian space (V, h_V), we prove an all-genus K-linear Seifert-Bruhat determinant formula for the parabolic form H_par of any generating tuple in U_d(K, h_V) with V^Pi = 0 and even dimension 2m, expressing (-1)^m det_K(H_par) modulo N_{K/Q}(K^*) as the product of local Wall invariants Theta(C_j). For finite-order elements with det_K(g) in {+1, -1}, we prove the Hodge-balance character law (-1)^k Theta(g) = (-sqrt(-d_K))^delta q(g) with positive rational q(g) and reflection-length parity l_R(g) - k(g) = delta(g) mod 2, whose complex phases cancel on balanced families in all genera. We establish an exact monomial-cycle formula for G(4, p, d) over Q(i), evaluate q(C) across G_24, G_29, G_31, and crystallographic Weyl groups, and prove long-reflection parity character cancellation on W(G_2), showing that G_31 is the unique irreducible order-2 reflection group in the Q(i)-Shephard-Todd list admitting non-split balanced families. In G_31, three regular classes with reflection-length excess (two of order 12 with l_R = 5 and one self-inverse class of order 6 with l_R = 6) have q(C) = 3 outside N_{Q(i)/Q}(Q(i)^*) and avoid all proper reflection subgroups. The 11-branch family has volume 45,147,375/16 > 0, generically immersing an 8-dimensional image Z_8 in the 9-dimensional non-split Q(i)-Weil sixfold domain while codimension bounds preclude dimension 9. Since every proper Hermitian subdomain of SU(3, 3) has dimension at most 6 < 8, a very general fibre of Z_8 is simple with Hodge group SU(3, 3), satisfying the rational Hodge conjecture on all powers. Finally, exact Mobius volumes in G_29 and G_24 give alternative Hurwitz-Brauer proofs for split sixfolds and fourfolds over Q(i) and Q(sqrt(-7)).
