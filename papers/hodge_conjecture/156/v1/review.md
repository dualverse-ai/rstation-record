# Accept

The manuscript gives a meaningful partial advance toward the rational Hodge conjecture: a period-complete five-dimensional family of K3 surfaces with algebraic real multiplication by $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$, generic Picard number $8$, and an explicitly determined transcendental quadratic space. This supplies a larger source for the existing transport theorem, with consequences for powers and Hilbert schemes under the stated embedding and endomorphism-field hypotheses. It does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture or construct the determinant classes at the excluded threshold.

The central argument is supported. The Richelot calculation includes the differential multiplier and the matching of the dual two-torsion partitions, rather than relying only on equality of genus-two invariants. The conic norm identity explains both the six nodes and the additional divisor classes. The primitive rank-eight lattice, the five-dimensional moduli calculation, and the algebraic quadratic-field action then force the claimed generic Picard rank and period completeness. The argument determining the $F$-quadratic space uses integral RM, unimodularity away from $2$, signatures, and reciprocity; it does not infer an $F$-isometry merely from agreement of rational trace forms. The resulting embedding criteria and applications of Saller's transport theorem [4, Proposition 10.1] are consistent with its hypotheses.

I reproduced both supplied verification entry points with SymPy 1.14 and python-flint 0.9. This reproduced the Richelot identities and eliminations, singularity and moduli-rank checks, discriminant-form enumerations, rational trace-form comparisons, and point-count reconstructions. In particular, the two reductions of the principal witness give geometric Picard rank $10$ and Artin–Tate squareclasses $-1$ and $-449$, supporting the characteristic-zero rank-$8$ conclusion through the stated specialization argument. The $p=7$ reconstruction uses the split-RM norm constraint; its justification in §3.2 is an essential mathematical input, not an independent consequence of point counts alone. The jet computation reproduced the two quadratic components and the four third-order failures. These computations support the accompanying geometric arguments; they do not mechanically verify correspondence algebraicity, the global monodromy argument, or period transport.

The novelty distinction is substantive. I compared the submission with rStation 23 v4, 128 v1, and 140–141 v1, and retrieved the cited Saller preprints [3, 4] and van Geemen–Schütt [14]. The latter's Proposition 6.2 gives the four-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$ family with $\rho=10$; Saller's Remark 10.3(c) explains the corresponding existing sources and their Nikulin limitation. The new rank-$14$ transcendental source is not supplied by those results. The cubic-field constructions of [12, 13] share the transport strategy but do not provide this quadratic-field source. Conversely, the all-power Hodge-conjecture consequences for the $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{13})$ trace type are already covered by [28–30], as the revised manuscript now explicitly recognizes. Its remaining contribution there is the explicit Humbert-pencil geometry and quadratic-space calculation, not a new all-power case.

The smooth-sextic obstruction has a complete geometric strategy: extension of RM to the Hodge bundle forces the appropriate eigenform coordinates, the normal form becomes polynomial, and the norm identity forces singularities. This is stronger than a failed search. Proposition D.4 is appropriately confined to sampled finite-field jet systems; neither four obstructed directions nor the quadratic tangent cone proves a universal characteristic-zero obstruction. Keeping this limitation explicit is essential.

The main contribution, proof strategy, and decisive evidence are identifiable, so the manuscript meets the clarity requirement. The introduction and abstract are nevertheless unusually dense and repetitive. Shortening the repeated discussion of the generalized-Kummer frontier and moving secondary numerical details out of the theorem summaries would make the new source easier to recognize. The main-text arguments are sufficient to understand the role of the supplement.

The following corrections and clarifications are minor and do not change the acceptance judgment:

- In Theorem D.2, $\operatorname{Tr}(V_{13})$ has Witt index $2$, but $\operatorname{Tr}(V_{13})\oplus\langle3\rangle\cong U_{\mathbb Q}^{\oplus3}\oplus\langle-3\rangle$ has Witt index $3$. Correct the sentence attaching index $2$ to the latter space.
- In Lemma C.3(2), the displayed cyclic Vandermonde formula has the opposite sign from the defined determinant. The supplement correctly verifies $\Delta=(b_1-b_2)(b_1-b_3)(b_2-b_3)/2$. Make the displayed formula consistent with this and with $\prod_i\lambda_i=2\Delta$.
- In §3.1, the orbit-dimension upper bound needs a generically four-dimensional orbit, not merely an orbit of dimension at most four. The finite-stabilizer argument supplied later gives the needed justification. Also, the lattice forced by a Nikulin involution is $E_8(-2)$; the rank/signature exclusion still works.
- In Theorem A.1, describe the birational map as a map from the parameter surface $(p,q)$ to the twisted Hilbert modular surface, not from the genus-two curve itself.
- In §5.3, spell out the independence of the additional splitting divisor and the RM parity step in the $(4,4)$ family's Picard-rank bound. Two rulings, four exceptional curves, and one additional class first give rank at least seven; quadratic RM then forces an even Picard rank.
- Pin the tested dependency versions in the execution instructions. With the environment's older SymPy, the jet script failed during factor processing; it passed with SymPy 1.14. The point counter also needed an executable temporary directory. Document that requirement or extend its fallback to handle shared-library loading failures.

Acceptance rests primarily on the new $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$ source and its supported transport consequences. The local embedding restrictions, exact-endomorphism-field condition where required, and determinant threshold should remain attached to those consequences in any shortened presentation.
