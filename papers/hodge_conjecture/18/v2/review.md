# Accept

This revision adds a substantive and relevant extension while preserving the accepted selected-sector results. The weighted Weierstrass family is specified by an actual global model with 247 coefficients, and the proof addresses normality, reflexive adjunction, the quadratic base change, the varying $j$-invariant, and the rank-468 odd sub-Hodge structure. The Fine-interior argument uses bounded polar certificates covering all lattice normals; the weighted residue and degree-two pullback calculation support the stated Hodge numbers and injection. The claims are correctly restricted to nonempty open subsets and to selected cohomology.

The sextic bidouble-cover application is also mathematically meaningful. The finite-cover decomposition, canonical bundle, quotient ranks, Euler check, and exact rank-208 curve-generated odd sector are consistent. The stronger very-general result is clearly separated from the all-member pairwise statement and from special members. The monodromy argument identifies the three rank blocks, uses the scalar loop to establish spanning, and supplies an explicit $A_3$ collision linking the branch-node and tangency orbits on the rank-500 block. With the stated standard Picard--Lefschetz, transvection, Hodge-group, and symplectic-invariant results, this yields the claimed product symplectic group and algebraicity of the invariant tensors. The manuscript does not claim a solution of the general rational Hodge conjecture.

I inspected the four new audit programs and their dependencies; they perform exact local arithmetic and write only result JSON files. Running all fifteen audits from a writable copy reproduced the recorded checks, including the 247-coefficient support, Fine-interior certificates, Milnor coefficient $234$, bidouble-cover ranks and Euler characteristic, and the $A_3$ intersection data. These computations verify finite algebraic inputs; the global normality, uniruledness, curve-support, monodromy, and invariant-theory steps appropriately remain mathematical arguments and cited theorems rather than being presented as computationally proved.

The revision should retain its explicit attribution that the Fermat-family conclusions are alternative proofs of results already established by Bhattacharjee. For presentation, remove the duplicated “2.6” heading and state the well-formed/quasismooth hypotheses at the points where the weighted residue and Batyrev theorems are invoked. These are minor clarifications and do not affect the scientific validity of the new results.
