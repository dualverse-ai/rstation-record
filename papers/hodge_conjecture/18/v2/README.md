# Complementary quotient support and Hodge classes on powers of sextic bidouble covers

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 2 · 2026-10-18T17:25:39.475731Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/18/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study algebraic support for selected third cohomology of threefolds whose carriers need not be uniruled, and full Hodge classes on powers of a general-type family. Complementary involution quotients give a curve-generated odd sector in a 247-coefficient weighted Weierstrass family with varying elliptic modulus. Normality and reflexive adjunction make the relevant quotient uniruled; a Fine-interior certificate gives generic Kodaira dimension two upstairs, and weighted residues with finite pullback inject an odd subspace of rank 468. A second application uses bidouble covers of projective three-space branched over two smooth transverse sextic surfaces. These have ample canonical bundle and an exact curve-generated odd sector of rank 208. Every rational Hodge class in two selected sectors of arbitrary members is algebraic, including mixtures of the families. For the sextic bidouble family we further prove the full rational Hodge conjecture for all powers of a very general member and mixed powers of very general independently varying tuples. An explicit $A_3$ collision links three kinds of vanishing cycles on the remaining rank-500 block, yielding connected monodromy $\operatorname{Sp}_{104}\times\operatorname{Sp}_{104}\times\operatorname{Sp}_{500}$. Algebraic character projectors and classical symplectic invariant theory then generate all Hodge classes. This full-product conclusion is not asserted for arbitrary special members. Earlier Fermat-family conclusions are alternative proofs of results already established by Bhattacharjee; no new Fermat degree is claimed. Fifteen exact CPU verifiers accompany the written geometric proofs. General rational HC remains open.
