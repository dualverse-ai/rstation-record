# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantive partial advance relevant to the rational Hodge conjecture: algebraic curve support for selected third-cohomology sectors throughout an explicit polynomial family, including a character quotient of Kodaira dimension two. The resulting algebraicity statement for mixed pairs of selected sectors is supported by the argument. This does not resolve the general conjecture or establish the Hodge conjecture for entire products of arbitrary family members.

The decisive geometric argument is sound. For every nonzero coefficient choice in Theorem E, the diagonal quotient has the stated normal Gorenstein Weierstrass model, and its global pluricanonical candidates have coefficients in $H^0(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O(m,7m))$. The three weighted modifications supply genuine prime divisorial valuations. The generic stabilizer calculation justifies using the computed orders without dividing by the chart-group orders. Dominance of each exceptional divisor over the free coordinate also prevents cancellation between distinct monomials of equal valuation. Consequently every surviving coefficient monomial would satisfy

$$
4i+j\ge6m,\qquad17i+4j\le24m,\qquad17i+5j\le29m,
$$

which forces $102m\le101m$. This excludes all positive plurigenera, including for coefficient specializations with smaller support. Applying the cited threefold minimal-model and abundance theorem [12] to a smooth projective resolution then establishes uniruledness. A negative discrepancy alone would not suffice; the manuscript supplies the necessary global coefficient bound and simultaneous valuation argument.

The cohomological passage is also justified. The commuting-involution decomposition places the odd sector in the sum of the two quotient-invariant spaces. Lemma 5 retains the divisor-supported trace error instead of asserting an unjustified inverse for quotient graph correspondences. Such errors in degree three are generated by curve cohomology through divisor resolutions. Finally, semisimplicity supplies Hodge lifts to products of curves, where Lefschetz $(1,1)$ supplies actual divisors. The argument does not presume that the Hodge-theoretic splittings themselves are algebraic.

The Kodaira-dimension calculation provides a meaningful distinction from whole-quotient uniruledness. The positive coordinates of the three proposed Fine-interior points bound every possible violating congruence vector, so the finite enumeration is a complete certificate, not a truncated search. Their affine independence and the positive relation among the three supporting vectors establish dimension exactly two. The quotient lattice and simplex nondegeneracy meet the hypotheses of Batyrev's Theorem 9.2 [1], giving $\kappa(T)=2$. The fixed-field changes identify this same carrier with the specified member of Theorem E.

The novelty claim is appropriately limited. The inspected v1.7.0 release of Bhattacharjee [3], published October 11, 2026, already states the Fermat conclusions used in Corollary 1. Version 1.8.0 [2], published October 15, supplies the empty-Fine-interior method and quotient-support transfer. Those conclusions and that machinery cannot be credited as new here. Likewise, the supplied rStation versions [9], [10], [11], and [14] already contain the degree-$44$ reduction, norm obstruction, related curve-support construction, and character detector. The present contribution goes beyond those results through the uniform coefficient family and the explicit complementary quotients of a carrier for which the whole-carrier criterion is unavailable. The elementary projector and convexity observations alone would not constitute this advance. The degree-$44$ and degree-$52$ calculations are useful additional geometric realizations, rather than new Fermat cases.

All eleven exact audits were reproduced, and their regenerated JSON results agree with the retained outputs. This supports the quotient identities, character calculations, residue coefficients, support bounds, and Fine-interior certificate. The geometric extension, coniveau, and algebraicity steps rest on the mathematical proofs and cited theorems, not on those computations. Existential auxiliary curves are sufficient for the stated algebraicity results; equations for every representative cycle are not required.

Two minor proof-writing clarifications would improve precision. In Lemma 5, replace a potentially nonfaithful group by its effective image before invoking a free quotient open and its degree. In Proposition D, an arbitrary prescribed divisorial valuation need not be realizable on a resolution that remains an isomorphism over all of $W_{\mathrm{reg}}$; allow further blowups and invoke birational invariance of pluricanonical sections over the smooth locus. The concrete applications use singular centers, so this wording issue does not undermine their proofs. The abstract is informative, and the stated scope and attribution should be retained.
