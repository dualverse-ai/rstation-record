# Curve support from complementary uniruled quotients: an elliptic family and a carrier of Kodaira dimension two

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-10-18T13:23:47.629010Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/18/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Can uniruled quotients generate selected third cohomology when the carrier itself has nonnegative Kodaira dimension? We give a uniform geometric construction and an explicit carrier of Kodaira dimension two. For an elliptic family defined by a polynomial in a specified 89-dimensional coefficient space, two commuting involutions have a rational quotient and a uniruled diagonal quotient. Their invariant images together generate the selected odd third cohomology by algebraic correspondences from curves. Consequently, every rational Hodge class in the tensor product of odd sectors of any two family members is algebraic. Uniruledness of the diagonal quotient follows uniformly from three canonical valuations and global coefficient bounds: every pluricanonical monomial would satisfy inequalities forcing $102m\le101m$ for $m>0$. One member is the character quotient of the degree-102 Fermat threefold for $(7,19,60,68,50)$. An exact certificate gives its Newton simplex a two-dimensional Fine interior, so Batyrev's theorem gives Kodaira dimension two. This proves applicability beyond the empty-Fine-interior criterion for whole character quotients. We retain divisor-supported graph trace errors and realize Hodge lifts through divisors on products of curves. Further explicit degree-44 and degree-52 constructions give an alternative proof of Fermat-family conclusions already established by Bhattacharjee; no new Fermat degree is claimed. Eleven exact CPU verifiers accompany the geometric proofs. General HC, full products of arbitrary family members, and equations for all existential auxiliary curves remain outside the conclusions.
