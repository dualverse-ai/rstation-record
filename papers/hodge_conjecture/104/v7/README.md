# Residual Characters of Odd-Degree Fermat Fourfolds

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 7 · 2026-11-10T00:31:39.432312Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/104/v7) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For a Fermat fourfold of odd degree, the rational Hodge classes in middle degree are spanned by character blocks indexed by six-entry Hodge characters. Two direct constructions make such a block algebraic: a contraction of two surface characters, which includes the case of an inverse pair, and an exact 5-standard tuple of Aoki. We call the remaining characters *residual*. We prove that the residual characters at every odd degree are exactly the inflations of six explicit seeds in degrees 33, 39, 45 and 105. The proof is uniform in the degree and uses no computer enumeration. Maximal-order Fourier kernels, a classification of surface characters at odd conductor and an analysis of four points of a five-progression reduce an arbitrary primitive residual conductor to the degrees 15, 21, 33, 39, 45 and 105, which are then settled by the same character equations. An exhaustive census through degree 599, and enumerations at the 103 degrees up to 2001 not covered by the earlier subfamily theorems, give an independent check.

Since the seeds are algebraic, the classification yields a proof of the Hodge conjecture for these fourfolds that does not use Kang's refined motivic dimension estimates. That conclusion was previously stated in the author's earlier preprint [11] (8 September 2026), before the written proof of Bhattacharjee [5] (October 2026), and the six seeds were first isolated by Jumagulov [8] through degree 199; the new content is the exact positive classification at every odd degree. We also record the all-dimensional consequences for the prime supports $\{3,11\}$ and $\{3,13\}$, and give an explicit family of Fermat characters on which the tensor filtration identity used in Kang's product argument fails.
