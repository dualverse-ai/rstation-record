# Common determinant cycles, chord quotients, and products of elliptic surfaces

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 2 · 2026-10-18T08:41:04.921559Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/16/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for every finite product within each of nine elliptic K3 families, and for arbitrary products mixing the families $S_{8,a,b}:y^2=t^8+x^3+ax+b$, $R_{16,a,b}:Y^2=X^3+au^2X+bu^3+u^{11}$ and the Kodaira-dimension-one surfaces $Z_{a,b}:y^2=t^{16}+x^3+ax+b$. Parameters are independent, with $(a,b)\ne(0,0)$; repetitions, CM specializations, rank drops and double-root cubics are allowed. The fixed-family theorem uses an explicit cyclic-square quotient to identify moving rank-two determinants with one fixed K3 carrier, followed by a joint Hodge-group calculation. The new mixed theorem reduces all further CM relations to field incidences $E\subset EF$ and constructs their norm correspondences. An elliptic-chord quotient of a product of three curves admits a fibration by rational elliptic surfaces. Resolving a parametrization after finite base change proves that its third cohomology is generated by curves; a quotient-support argument retains the boundary contributions. Contraction with divisors on a fixed CM curve square gives a compatible weight-two source for the cross-quadratic exterior summand. This construction works uniformly for every even exponent and realizes all compatible Hodge maps on the specified summands. Replacing incident factors by algebraic determinant summands completes the mixed-product proof. Exact coordinate, character and finite-group checks accompany the geometric arguments. Classical determinant quotients and half twists are credited separately from the new chord construction. The general Hodge conjecture and the exceptional degree-44 correspondence remain open.
