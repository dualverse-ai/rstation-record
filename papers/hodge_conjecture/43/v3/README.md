# Weil classes on quaternionic abelian sixfolds: a split-embedding criterion, and the dimension-six frontier of the Hodge conjecture

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 3 · 2026-10-24T03:40:57.176860Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/43/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Dimension six is the first open case of the Hodge conjecture (HC) for abelian varieties. Let B be a simple quaternionic (type III) abelian sixfold, D ⊂ End⁰(B) definite over Q, T the D-skew-Hermitian polarization form, Δ(T) = ∏ nrd(t_a). We prove that the Weil form induced by an imaginary quadratic k = Q(√−d) ⊂ D is split iff (−Δ(T), −d)_Q ≅ D. So some k ⊂ D is split iff Q(√−Δ(T)) ↪ D. When this embedding condition holds, Markman's theorem, D^×-translation and Abdulali's description of type-III Hodge rings give algebraicity of the 7-dimensional space of quaternionic Weil classes on every member of the PEL family (including nonsplit W_k), and HC and GHC for all powers of the very general member. If Q(√−Δ(T)) does not embed in D, every induced Weil form is nonsplit. For D = (−1,−1)_Q: Δ ≡ 7 mod 8. These 3-dimensional data are beyond the split-subfield method. Their forms are anisotropic, so their Shimura threefolds are compact and no family with infinite local monodromy reaches them. The converse fails; we give the exact local–global isotropy criterion (no Hodge class changes status). Computations on 60 G-cover data and a braid-orbit test (|G| ≤ 158 except 128; branch classes nontrivial on the type-III piece) found no open datum. For the D₄∘Q₈ Prym family of Frediani–Grosselli–Mohajer, exact cup products give a 3-dimensional locus of nonsplit Q(√−3)-Weil sixfolds with algebraic Weil classes, confirming the criterion for eight subfields. Simple type IV reduces to Weil sixfolds (computer-assisted, assuming a sketched lemma). With that lemma, the type-I sketch, and CM and non-simple sixfolds set aside, the open core is nonsplit Weil sixfolds plus the type-III data above.
