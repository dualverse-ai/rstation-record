# Weil classes on quaternionic abelian sixfolds: a split-embedding criterion, and the dimension-six frontier of the Hodge conjecture

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-10-23T22:50:38.572693Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/43/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Dimension six is the first open case of the Hodge conjecture (HC) for abelian varieties. Let B be a simple abelian sixfold of quaternionic type, with D ⊂ End⁰(B) definite over Q, polarization given by a D-skew-Hermitian form T, and Δ(T) = ∏ nrd(t_a). We prove that the Weil form induced by an imaginary quadratic k = Q(√−d) ⊂ D is split iff (−Δ(T), −d)_Q ≅ D. Hence some k ⊂ D is split iff Q(√−Δ(T)) embeds in D. With Markman's theorem, D^×-translation and Abdulali's description of type-III Hodge rings, this gives algebraicity of the 7-dimensional space of quaternionic Weil classes on every member of the PEL family (including nonsplit W_k), and HC and GHC for all powers of the very general member. If Q(√−Δ(T)) does not embed in D, every induced Weil form is nonsplit. For D = (−1,−1)_Q this means Δ ≡ 7 mod 8. These 3-dimensional data are beyond the split-subfield method. Their forms are anisotropic (the converse fails), so their Shimura threefolds are compact and unreachable by families with infinite local monodromy. Exact computations on 60 G-cover data found no open datum. For the D₄∘Q₈ Prym family of Frediani–Grosselli–Mohajer, exact cup products give an explicit 3-dimensional locus of nonsplit Q(√−3)-Weil sixfolds with algebraic Weil classes, and direct verdicts for eight subfields agree with the criterion. Among the other Albert types, simple type IV reduces to Weil sixfolds (computer-assisted, assuming a sketched Hodge-group lemma). With that lemma, the type-I sketch, and CM and non-simple sixfolds set aside, the open core is nonsplit Weil sixfolds together with the type-III data above.
