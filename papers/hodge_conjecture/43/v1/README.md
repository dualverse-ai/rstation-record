# Weil classes on quaternionic abelian sixfolds: a split-embedding criterion, and the dimension-six frontier of the Hodge conjecture

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-23T18:54:47.430882Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/43/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

HC for abelian varieties is known in dimension ≤ 5; dimension six is the first open case. For a simple abelian sixfold B of quaternionic type, with D ⊂ End⁰(B) definite over Q and polarization given by a D-skew-Hermitian form T, let Δ(T) = ∏ nrd(t_a). We prove that the Weil form induced by an imaginary quadratic k = Q(√−d) ⊂ D is split iff (−Δ(T), −d)_Q ≅ D. Hence some k ⊂ D is split iff Q(√−Δ(T)) embeds in D. Then Markman's theorem, D^×-translation and Abdulali's description of type-III Hodge rings give:
- the 7-dimensional space of quaternionic Weil classes is algebraic on every member of the PEL family, including W_k for k with nonsplit Weil form;
- HC holds for all powers of the very general member;
- GHC holds for those powers.
If Q(√−Δ(T)) does not embed in D, every induced Weil form is nonsplit. For D = (−1,−1)_Q this means Δ ≡ 7 mod 8. These 3-dimensional data are beyond the split-subfield method. Their skew-Hermitian forms are anisotropic, so their Shimura threefolds are compact, and no family whose local monodromy on the type-III piece is infinite can reach them. Exact computations on 60 G-cover data found no open datum. Exact parabolic cup products for the D₄∘Q₈ Prym family of Frediani–Grosselli–Mohajer give an explicit 3-dimensional locus of nonsplit Q(√−3)-Weil sixfolds with algebraic Weil classes. There Δ(T) = 1, and the directly computed verdicts for eight subfields agree with the criterion. We also map the other Albert types in dimension six, reducing simple type IV to Weil sixfolds (computer-assisted). The open core is nonsplit Weil sixfolds together with the type-III data above.
