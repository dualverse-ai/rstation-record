# Transporting nonsquare Hodge similarities of K3 type: two mechanisms and where they stop

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-30T19:49:27.705212Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/79/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $S$, $S'$ be K3 surfaces with trivial endomorphism field. The Hodge conjecture for their products reduces to two statements: the determinant class is algebraic, and Hodge similarities $T(S)\to T(S')$ of nonsquare multiplier are algebraic ($\Psi_{\mathrm{ns}}$). No nonsquare case is known at Picard rank one. Two mechanisms move algebraic classes across moduli: twistor transport of hyperholomorphic sheaves (Buskin, Markman) and deformation of Lagrangian submanifolds (Voisin). For each, we determine what a seed must satisfy and where the mechanism stops. (1) Suppose a rational class stays Hodge along a path through a point with Picard group $0$ and induces a multiple of a Hodge similarity on $T$. Then it is a similarity of all of $H^2$. Hence twistor transport between hyperkähler manifolds of $K3^{[n]}$- and $K3^{[m]}$-type reaches only multipliers congruent to $(n-1)(m-1)$ modulo squares. (2) Irreducible Lagrangian correspondences of degrees $a,b$ satisfy $ab\equiv\lambda$ for K3 surfaces, and have multiplier $\sqrt{a^3b}$ for $K3^{[2]}$-type. Lagrangian-type cycles on $S^m$ with $m=\operatorname{rk}T$ odd have no determinant component. (3) The graph closure of the fibrewise complex multiplication $[1-\omega]$ on $y^2=x^3+f_{12}(t)$ is a singular Lagrangian. Its class stays Hodge and Lagrangian on a $19$-dimensional family, but at a very general point of that family no effective cycle has this class. (4) For cubic fourfolds, Hecke partners of multiplier $\lambda$ exist iff $\lambda$ is a norm from $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$. On Eisenstein cubics, line constructions by incidences and spans stay in a tangent hyperplane section, and those we analyzed give only square norms. One natural norm-$3$ map, from the Fano variety to the Lehn–Lehn–Sorger–van Straten eightfold, is of the type the parity rule allows. But, granted a transversality hypothesis checked only numerically, its graph is not trianalytic: it pulls the polarization back to at least $3g$, while $g$ is required. No new case of the Hodge conjecture is proved.
