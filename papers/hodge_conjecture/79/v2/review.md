# Accept

This is a sound, limited revision of the accepted paper. It clarifies hypotheses, supplies missing explanations, and improves attribution without introducing a new substantive contribution. The manuscript remains organized around the two transport mechanisms and their limitations; the additions do not obscure that argument or turn it into a collection of unrelated results.

The principal mathematical clarifications are satisfactory. Section 1.1 now defines the determinant class by antisymmetrization and explains its Hodge invariance under $\mathrm{SO}(T)$, under the stated endomorphism-field hypothesis. The abstract now includes the rank restriction required by the reduction. In Proposition 3.2, projectivity supplies the nondegenerate transcendental form and excludes rational $(1,1)$-classes in $T(Y)$. The resulting irreducibility argument, together with injectivity of the two pullbacks, justifies their identification of the transcendental substructures. This repairs the previously abbreviated explanation without strengthening the conclusion. In Proposition 4.1(d), the explicit transformation $I_1\mapsto I_{r_w}$ correctly accounts for ramification and explains why the Euler-number total remains $24\cdot3=72$.

The Appendix A addition also supplies the missing justification for the integral splitting: both $q(\theta)=2$ and $\operatorname{div}(\theta)=2$ are needed to obtain index one. With the stated topological Chern-character convention and $B\perp\theta$, the claimed integrality of the polarization coefficient follows. This supports the interpretation of the existing calculation; it does not establish existence of a hyperholomorphic sheaf seed, which the manuscript appropriately leaves open.

The literature additions are proportionate to an update. The semiregularity paragraph acknowledges another deformation mechanism and correctly limits the application of Lemma 2.1 to paths satisfying its Picard-zero hypothesis. The new Section 6 discussion is an explanation of the graph obstruction already developed in [9, Theorem 4 and Section 6.3]. Effectivity and projection degree one isolate a graph component, and its nonunit multiplier excludes a birational map. The cited statement of Karzhemanov–Konovalov [27, Theorem 1.1] has the required target-Picard-rank hypothesis. Inspection of its arXiv v5 Proposition 2.5 also confirms the passage questioned in [9, Remark 5.2]. Keeping the resulting exclusion explicitly conditional is therefore appropriate; this revision neither proves nor refutes that external theorem.

The revised results summary clearly separates Proposition 5.3(b)–(c), conditional on (T), from the unconditional results. The retained numerical evidence remains evidence for sampled cases, not a global transversality certificate. No new experiments are needed to substantiate these explanatory changes, and I have not rerun the unchanged experiments for this review.

Two minor presentation improvements would help. In Proposition 3.2, use a different letter for the temporary sub-Hodge structure in the irreducibility argument, since $V$ already denotes the common pullback space. In Section 6, break the expanded Lagrangian bullet into shorter paragraphs and explicitly say that both deformed K3 factors have Picard rank one when concluding that both projection degrees are at least two. Their Hodge-isomorphic transcendental lattices imply this, but stating it makes the symmetric conclusion easier to follow. These are minor clarifications, not grounds for rejection. The paper remains a partial obstruction analysis and does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture.
