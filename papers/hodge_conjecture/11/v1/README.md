# The Hodge conjecture for Thom–Sebastiani cubic tenfolds (and quartic sixfolds)

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-17T12:43:55.790372Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/11/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Cubic tenfolds are the first cubic hypersurfaces for which the rational Hodge conjecture is open in the middle degree. For Thom–Sebastiani tenfolds X=V(g(x)+h(y)) in P^11, with g,h cubic forms in six disjoint sets of variables and T_g,T_h the transcendental parts of H^4 of the cubic fourfolds V(g),V(h), we prove that HC(X) holds if and only if every Hodge morphism T_g→T_h is algebraic, i.e. HC(X) ⟺ HC(V(g)×V(h)). The proof uses the Katsura–Shioda inductive structure Bl X ≅ Bl(X'_g×X'_h)/μ3 with X'_g=V(g+u^3), which we prove for arbitrary g,h; HC for cubic fourfolds; and a level-one lemma (Paranjape–Kollár, Bloch–Srinivas) making the μ3-twisted part (522 of 924 dimensions of H^{5,5}_prim) unconditionally algebraic. Combining Beauville–Donagi with Markman's theorem on rational Hodge isometries of K3^[n]-type varieties, every Hodge isometry T_g≅T_h is algebraic, with no associated K3 needed. Hence HC holds for X unless T_g≅T_h and the Hodge morphisms are not spanned by isometries, i.e. for non-isometric similitudes and real multiplication, the cases open for K3×K3. This covers very general pairs, g=h, and Fourier–Mukai partners. For pairs of triple covers V(F+x5^3), the van Geemen–Izadi Kuga–Satake correspondence makes all Hodge morphisms algebraic, including non-isometric similitudes on special members, so HC holds on the whole 20-dimensional family V(F(x)+x5^3+F'(y)+y5^3). The similitude case is nonempty over every very general cubic fourfold. The same method gives HC(V(g+h)) ⟺ HC(S_g×S_h) for quartic sixfolds built from quartic K3 surfaces. Bearing on HC: new unconditional cases in the first open cubic dimension; general cubic tenfolds are not addressed.
