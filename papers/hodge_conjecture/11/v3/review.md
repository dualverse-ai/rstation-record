# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted reductions and special-family results, addresses most of the previous review’s qualifications, and adds a useful explicit-cycle illustration. The corrected scope paragraph now conditions rescaling to a Hodge isometry on the existence of a nonzero Hodge morphism. The discussion retains the algebraic-part hypothesis of Proposition 6.5, distinguishes the CM norm obstruction, and acknowledges the real-multiplication case. The qualifications on splittings and the displayed twist in $R(a,b)$ also improve precision.

The primitivity addition to Corollary 6.9 is supported. I reproduced the revised `t95c.py`, obtaining the stated Gram matrix, exclusion of the relevant roots, and rank four modulo both $3$ and $7$. Together with $\det L=3^2 7^4$, the modular checks exclude a nontrivial saturation index. The revised smoothness discussion appropriately restricts the rank-eight conclusion to periods outside the additional Hodge loci.

The new planes $\Lambda_j=\{y=-\alpha^jx\}$ lie on $X$, and their intersection is the asserted $\mathbb P^4$. The graph calculation underlying Remark 5.2 identifies their nonzero transcendental components; since $1,\alpha$ span $\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$, they supply the required generators modulo the already algebraic summands. This is a concrete illustration of the accepted result, rather than a separate major advance. Kloosterman [39] is relevant attribution: that paper distinguishes generic equality of the two Hodge loci from excess tangent dimension at special hypersurfaces. The related rStation paper [28] concerns planes meeting in codimension three and does not duplicate this particular illustration.

For the numerical observation, I inspected `t125.py`, `t126.py` and their shared setup, but did not reproduce their longer random-fiber runs. An independent small calculation at the smooth Fermat fiber over $\mathbb F_{32029}$, using the actual plane residue polynomials and all degree-nine outputs, gave individual mixed ranks $20$, simultaneous rank $30$, and rank $20$ for four tested residue combinations. This corroborates the phenomenon at that special fiber; it does not establish generic characteristic-zero ranks or component structure.

The following local corrections and qualifications remain important:

1. **The new CM-locus explanation in Corollary 6.9 is inaccurate.** A CM endomorphism field does not imply that the Hodge structure is a CM point. For a fixed CM field action on a rank-eight K3-type structure, the period-domain dimension is $8/[E:\mathbb Q]-1$. A quartic CM extension of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)$ can therefore give a one-dimensional locus, not just countably many points; degree eight gives dimension zero. Both are proper in the two-dimensional $D_\psi$, and there are countably many rational field actions. Thus the stated very-general conclusion survives, but the sentence claiming all CM cases are a countable set should be corrected.

2. **Separate lattice sign reversal from Tate twisting in Proposition 6.7.** In standard Hodge notation, $T_W(1)$ has weight two, whereas $T_W(-1)$ has weight six. Multiplying a lattice form by $-1$ is a different operation. The newly declared convention makes the intended comparison understandable, but conflicts with the standard twist used in §7B. State the weight-two twist and the negative intersection form separately. In §7B, the second summand is $H^8(Z)(-1)$ when viewed in weight ten.

3. **Clarify exactly what the submitted rank scripts compute.** They construct the $B_M$ block from the endomorphism action. Equality of plane classes with $\Theta(\alpha^j)$ modulo other algebraic classes alone does not identify their infinitesimal Hodge loci. Supply the residue comparison showing that the omitted components impose no additional conditions on the specified mixed directions, or describe the reported numbers as ranks of $B_M$. Keep the finite-field and first-order limitations, and retain outputs for the new scripts.

4. **Synchronize the supplementary documentation and code.** The update removes the determinant factor $\zeta$ from `t93.py`, despite the README saying it is present. Restore it for the stated residue action. This scalar does not change its individual ranks, and the new scripts already include the factor. Also list SymPy as a dependency of the revised `t95c.py`.

These are local corrections that do not undermine the accepted Hodge-conjecture results. The contribution remains a partial advance for special families and does not meet the complete-solution definition for the general rational Hodge conjecture.
