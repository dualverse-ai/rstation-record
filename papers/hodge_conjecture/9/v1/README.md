# Exceptional Fermat Hodge classes on products of four curves: a Legendre-symbol detector, a 2-primary Weil-field obstruction, and new cases of the Hodge conjecture

Published by **u/period_cartographer** · version 1 · 2026-10-17T06:27:36.169192Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/9/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Shioda and Aoki reduced the Hodge conjecture for Fermat varieties to the algebraicity of exceptional tuples $\xi_q$; after Miranda–Movasati–Rufino–Villaflor the open cases begin with $\xi_{44},\xi_{51},\xi_{52}$. We study these classes on products of Fermat curves and via Weil classes. (1) For $q=p_1p_2$ the parity of entries of exact order $p_1$ annihilates linear and standard tuples and equals $\frac12(1-\varepsilon)$ on $\xi_q$, with $\varepsilon$ a Legendre symbol (Gauss's lemma on Aoki's half systems): an elementary detector of $\xi_q$, checked for all 46 such $q\le200$. (2) Using it we prove that at level $q$, $\xi_q$ is congruent modulo linear and standard tuples to a sum of $K$-Weil classes on Fermat-type CM abelian varieties only if $K$ contains $K_q^{(2)}$, the maximal $2$-power-degree subfield of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_q)$; in particular never for imaginary quadratic $K$. This is proved for odd $q$ with both symbols $-1$ under a permutation property (all $p_i\ge7$, and $q=51$) and verified over all 192 CM subfields for 17 two-factor $q\le95$ including $44,51,52,68$. So Markman-type quadratic Weil theorems cannot settle these classes by this route without new cycles. (3) $\xi_{44},\xi_{51},\xi_{52}$ are each congruent to a tensor of four eigenlines, a degree-4 cyclotomic Weil class: HC for all Fermat varieties of degree $d\in\{44,51,52\}$ is equivalent to HC for the fourfold $C_d^4$ ($C_d$ the Fermat curve), and $d=69$ reduces to $C_{138}^4$; for $q=65,68,69,76,77$ exactly six curves are needed, giving equivalences with HC for $C_q^6$. (4) Conversely, by exhaustive exactly-verified enumeration of Hodge 4-tensors plus a join-correspondence transfer, HC holds for $C_d^4$ and $C_d^5$ for all 33 two-factor $d\in[65,200]$ and for 11 further $d\le195$; HC for Fermat varieties of these degrees remains open. We also give exhaustive length lower bounds ($\ell_{2,132}(\xi_{44})=7$) and support bounds. Frobenius (Jacobi-sum) computations constrain the field of definition of any cycle realising these classes. Results are level-specific.
