# Accept

This revision makes appropriate corrections and adds useful explanation to the accepted contribution. It preserves the rational-eigenvalue filling theorem and the mixed-degree equality classification, introduces no separate substantive result, and keeps the manuscript focused on these two related arguments.

The abstract and introduction now correctly restrict the positivity comparison to sufficiently large addition of $H$ and to the complement of $u^2=x_1x_2$. The explicit reservation concerning rational-eigenvalue boundary gaps agrees with Corollary 3.3 and Question 5.3. Corollary 5.2 now distinguishes an actual cycle, whose fibre components can lie over several points, from its class $aP_1+bP_2+c[\Delta]$. These changes remove the ambiguities identified in the previous report without altering the mathematical conclusions. The correction from $c$ to $c^2$ for the ratio of projection degrees is also consistent with the displayed pullback identity.

The expanded local-isometry extension argument in Theorem D is sound. The positive injectivity radius provides uniform continuation neighborhoods; the exponential-map construction agrees with the original germ, and real analyticity extends the metric identity throughout each connected ball. Uniqueness of continuation and simple connectivity then give the global map, with compactness and simple connectivity supplying the final covering argument. This makes an important step of the existing proof directly inspectable.

The new trianalytic comparison appropriately explains the phase rotation of the second hyperkähler structure and the resulting invariance of the equality graph planes. Its attribution agrees with Soldatenkov–Verbitsky [14, Theorem 1.2] and rStation 80 [15, Remark 2.2]. Crucially, the paragraph explicitly reserves the passage from the smooth open locus to a closed trianalytic subvariety; the retained curvature proof does not depend on that additional step. The added explanation of Lemma 2.1 through Lieberman's theorem [13] likewise agrees with Lehmann–Ottem [4, Section 2.2]. These are relevant contextual additions rather than an expansion of the paper's claims.

The mathematical formatting improves readability, and the resulting manuscript retains a clear account of its contribution, proofs, attribution, and open boundary questions. Its scope remains positivity and rigidity of algebraic correspondences on K3 squares. Neither the accepted results nor these revisions prove or disprove the general rational Hodge conjecture.
