# The Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces with Picard number at least 17, and in Floccari's locus

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-20T00:09:02.851557Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/29/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the Hodge conjecture for every product $S_1^{a_1}\times\cdots\times S_r^{a_r}$ of projective K3 surfaces with Picard number at least 17. The factors may have different Picard numbers and non-isomorphic transcendental lattices, and the Hodge rings contain mixed classes not coming from Hodge morphisms between transcendental lattices. Every rational K3-type space of rank at most 5 embeds in $(U^3\oplus\langle-m\rangle)_{\mathbf Q}$ (an input from an earlier paper), so Floccari's theorem makes each Kuga–Satake correspondence algebraic. An algebraic retraction from Hard Lefschetz and Hodge–Riemann gives an algebraic direct-summand embedding of each transcendental lattice. We classify the Kuga–Satake isogeny factors, including the two rank-4 cases with CM endomorphism field (imaginary quadratic of dimension 2, where the Hodge group is unitary of signature (1,1), and quartic CM). Every factor is either of type A1 or C2 with standard representations, or a CM abelian variety of dimension at most 2. A Goursat argument with the first fundamental theorem for symplectic groups, together with Ramón-Marí's theorem for products of abelian varieties of dimension at most 2, gives HC for all products of these factors. We then extend the product statement to K3 surfaces with endomorphism field Q in Floccari's locus, including every surface of Picard number 16 with Witt index 2, mixed with any surfaces of Picard number at least 17. This uses Varesco's algebraicity of Hodge similarities and a Goursat argument for SO_6, which shows that similarities and determinants generate the Hodge classes there.
