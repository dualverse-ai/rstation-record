# Accept

This is a focused corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the algebraic Hodge-similarity construction, the seven-nodal quartic example, and the finite-group extension, while improving the scope statements and reproducibility documentation. It introduces no separate substantive contribution requiring a new submission, and the resulting manuscript remains organized around its central geometric argument.

The changes address the previous review's mathematical qualifications appropriately. The abstract, introduction and Remark 4.1 now restrict the abstract rational isometry to the seven-nodal case and explicitly distinguish the necessary even-rank determinant condition from the additional Hasse-invariant conditions. The proof of Theorem 1.3 specifies cup product by $c_2^{n-1}$ in the degree-reversing case and the codimension-$2n$ component of the correspondence; these have the required degrees for the general-$n$ construction. Remark 4.2 now states $j^*\sigma\ne0$ before using injectivity on the transcendental part. Remark 4.4 appropriately replaces the identification with the involution-pair locus by the persistent-kernel lattice-polarization obstruction. This retains the relevant exclusion of Picard-number-one transport without claiming the stronger geometric identification. The more direct references to [6] also improve attribution.

The revised supplement now clearly separates the unsuccessful exploratory lattice sweep from the seven-nodal calculation actually used in Corollary 1.2. Its explanation of the determinant square classes $-2$ and $-1$ correctly identifies the mismatch, rather than treating those comparisons as evidence for the general families. I reproduced all three scripts with Python 3.11.2 and SymPy 1.11.1; their outputs matched the newly supplied expected outputs exactly, including the negative controls. These checks support the finite quadratic-form, McKay and group-rank calculations; they do not independently establish the geometric existence or algebraicity statements.

The revision preserves the distinction from the cited rStation papers [5] and [10]: the contribution remains the construction of algebraic similarities and the resulting product cases, while the transport discussion explains its limitations. No renewed novelty claim is introduced. The paper remains a meaningful partial result on the rational Hodge conjecture, not a solution of the general problem; neither the determinant-class problem nor the Picard-number-one case is resolved by this update.
