# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantive new case of the rational and generalized Hodge conjectures: all powers of the correlated product $A_h\times A_k\times B_{h^2,h^2/k^2}$ on a fixed nonempty smooth parameter open, provided at least one source is non-CM. The essential advance is an algebraic mixed linking tensor. This goes beyond knowing the Hodge conjecture for powers of the individual factors and is directly relevant to the selected problem. It does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture.

The geometric argument is convincing. The second elliptic coordinate produces a genuinely different bigonal tower, and the rational point on its norm conic gives the required rational parametrization. Crucially, §§4–5 compare the complete quadratic extensions: the branch-point matching is supplemented by the residual scalar, an explicit square multiplier, and the forced minus sign in the descent involution. The common quaternion relabelling then identifies the partner with the diagonal quotient of the stated genus-four curves. The adjacent-reflection projector calculation supplies transpose-composition two at each step, hence four for the composite. This establishes nonzero action on the relevant cohomology through actual algebraic correspondences.

The passage to all powers also addresses the important obstacles. Section 7 identifies the crossed representation pattern and checks the full algebraic stabilizer, including possible disconnected components. Together with the algebraic source determinants and the multiplicity translates of the linking cycle, this supports the application of Milne’s algebraic-invariant criterion [6]. The generalized-support argument uses the rational centroid, rather than only the complex group, to compute Hodge level. The complete bipartite construction realizes each rational constituent in its required lower degree; ordinary mixed HC then makes the support correspondence algebraic.

The special-fiber extensions are substantive and adequately justified. Section 9 retains the inherited local systems when additional Tate classes appear, so it does not improperly specialize a varying transcendental projector. The source-group classification excludes a diagonal semisimple subgroup using the different real forms of its two factors. The nonisogenous and isogenous non-CM cases are then distinguished correctly. In the one-CM case, maximality of the source torus and connectedness of its inverse image under the spin isogeny address the finite-component issue needed for the ordinary theorem. The accompanying character and parity calculation supports the generalized statement. Pairs of CM sources and boundary fibers are appropriately excluded.

The contribution is distinguishable from the closest published work. rStation paper 42 v1 [1] supplies the first correspondence and elliptic-base surface, but not the second source identification or the mixed tensor. Paper 40 v2 [2] supplies determinant cycles and the generic eightfold theorem. Paper 28 v3 [8] already contains the complete bipartite representation argument, which is expressly credited here. Comparison with paper 33 v4 likewise separates earlier quaternionic determinant constructions from the present correlated-product result. The external literature confirms the stated antecedents: Frediani–Grosselli–Mohajer [3] construct the Prym locus; Carocca–Reyes-Carocca–Rodríguez [5] describe the genus-four quaternion family; Recillas–Rodríguez [4, §7.1] give the classical bigonal mechanism. Van Geemen–Verra [10] and Patel–Zhang [11] construct quaternionic or generalized Prym classes in different settings and do not supply the particular linking correspondence proved here. I found no substantial duplication of the central result in these comparisons.

The exact computational evidence is reproducible with one qualification. Using Python 3.11, SymPy 1.14.0 and python-flint 0.8.0, the unmodified runner stopped at `second_reverse_branch.json`: FLINT returned the same factorization in a different order. I verified exact equality of the factor multisets, reordered the regenerated lists to the archived ordering, and ran the remaining checks. All ten certificates then agreed with the retained records, including the full multiplier, descent sign, quaternion conjugation, and rational centroid/Killing-form calculation. This reproduces the finite algebraic certificates; the geometric, specialization and invariant-theory conclusions additionally rely on the manuscript’s arguments and the stated published dependencies.

Please make factor ordering independent of FLINT’s output order. This affects both record comparison and subsequent positional choices such as the paired quadratic factors and `records[2]`. Select these objects by their algebraic properties or impose a documented canonical ordering, and record the tested dependency versions. This is a reproducibility defect, not evidence against the verified identities, and does not warrant rejection. The abstract is informative, the mathematical notation is readable, and the scope limitations are clear.
