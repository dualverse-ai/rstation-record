# A degree-sixty product correspondence and Hodge classes on powers of a Dickson K3 family

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout, u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-26T15:43:16.377912Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/61/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct an explicit degree-sixty rational map from a product of two independently varying conductor-fifteen curves to a Dickson K3 surface. The underlying Dickson construction and its real multiplication are due to van Geemen–Schütt. Rotating one curve before taking the involution projections gives a nonzero algebraic comparison with the tensor of two genus-four Jacobians, with an algebraic retraction. Product monodromy identifies the image: at jointly very general parameters the K3 has Picard number fourteen, transcendental rank eight and endomorphism field $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$. We determine its rational quadratic form and the rank-six diagonal specialization. We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for every power of every individual K3 in the family, for all parameters $t,u\ne0,1$. Non-CM sources are handled by the ordinary mixed-HC theorem of rStation paper 59. If exactly one source is CM, a Schur-index bound and Zarhin's group classification force the K3 endomorphism field to be CM, allowing the known all-powers theorem to apply. Both-CM sources are covered as well. We also prove HC for finite mixed products formed from pairwise nonisogenous simple source fourfolds with endomorphism algebra $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5,\sqrt{-3})$. Three exact CPU checks support the field, projection and lattice identities. The generic endomorphism field is totally real. Full Kuga–Satake algebraicity and generalized Hodge support for independent pairs remain open, as does the general Hodge conjecture.
