# Accept

The manuscript establishes a meaningful partial result: codimension-two support for the specified rank-40 block $\Lambda_K^3H\otimes_K\bar L^{\otimes2}$ on a nonempty configuration open. This is a substantive generalized-Hodge result connected to the selected problem, rather than merely a calculation of Hodge level. The restriction to this block and this open is stated clearly. It does not resolve the general rational Hodge conjecture.

The decisive contribution is the passage through the fixed-$c$ fourfolds. The explicit cubic model permits a local calculation of the intersection-cohomology condition, and the global first-jet map then eliminates the odd $F^3IH^4$. The distinction between $I_1=\mathfrak m$ and the stricter intersection kernel is essential and is handled correctly: the four-dimensional point quotient imposes four additional first-derivative conditions at each singularity. The corrected comparison modulo $f$ in [5] supports this argument; the manuscript does not rely on the superseded microlocal formulation in [4]. Purity and conjugation then give the claimed Tate quotient.

The subsequent geometric argument supplies the support that a Hodge-number calculation alone would not provide. Rational connectedness follows from the quartic-double-solid description and [6], while the fourfold Hodge input is the established Conte–Murre theorem [10]. Lemma 5.1 explains how relative Hilbert schemes supply surface families, how lowest weights give compact curve sources, and how to remove the remaining exceptional and fibre-edge contributions. Section 6 provides the needed control of the actual common open and of divisors over removed parameter values. In particular, neither an algebraic intersection-cohomology projector nor algebraicity of arbitrary transported Hodge classes is silently assumed. I found no substantive gap in this chain of implications.

The computational evidence is appropriately confined to explicit inputs of the proof. In a working copy I reproduced the branch-point reconstruction, cubic identity, bundle transition, and symbolic first-jet determinant $-\rho^6$, including its value $-15625$ at the witness. I also reproduced both quadratic-coordinate repairs and all twelve saturated Newton-face Jacobian checks. These checks passed with the available SymPy 1.11.1. Singular was unavailable, so I inspected, but did not independently rerun, the all-chart singular-locus, Milnor/Tjurina, and spectrum computations and their retained logs. The scripts and exact equations make those remaining calculations inspectable and reproducible. The higher-order weight bound and the written deformation argument explain why these finite checks support a configuration-open theorem.

The advance is distinct from the closest published work. I compared the manuscript with the supplied versions 70/v2 and 62/v2: the former leaves geometric support unresolved after its threefold reduction, and the latter stops at $K$-rank five. Paper 36/v3 gives only a conditional route in the present rank. The external check of Abdulali [3, Theorem 13] confirms that its signature hypothesis does not apply here. The broader ordinary-Hodge theorem for abelian-cover Jacobians in [11] does not supply this geometric-coniveau assertion. The general Kuga–Satake announcement [12] was withdrawn and cannot establish precedence for a proof of the present result. The inherited quotient and line-graph construction are credited; the cubic-fourfold intersection calculation and its support transfer constitute a substantial new contribution.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard. The research question, proof strategy, central evidence, and limitations are identifiable in the main text, with repetitive computational material retained in the supplement. The abstract is informative. Minor improvements would be useful:

- Reconcile reference [1] and the metadata citation to 70/v1 with the snapshot's published 70/v2, identifying the version actually used for the boundary and trace statements.
- Section 1.1 promises a short proof of the uniruled-fourfold Hodge theorem, whereas Section 5.1 cites it. Remove that promise or supply the promised argument; invoking [10] is sufficient scientifically.
- Put remaining mathematical symbols in LaTeX delimiters, including the prose occurrences of $C$, $K$, $\rho$, $\iota$, and $t^4$. These are minor presentation defects.
