# The Rational Hodge Conjecture for W(B_4)-Symmetric General-Type Sextic Surface Squares, Universal 12-Line Divisors, and Calabi–Yau Sectors of Cyclic Threefold Squares and Split Sixfolds

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/17/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For very general parameters $(B, C) \in U$, we prove the rational Hodge conjecture for the two-parameter family of $W(B_4)$-symmetric general-type sextic surface squares $V_{B,C} \times V_{B,C} \subset \mathbb{P}^3 \times \mathbb{P}^3$ ($b_2 = 106, h^{2,0} = 10$) and for the Order-$1$ and level-$3$ Calabi–Yau sectors of the associated cyclic threefold and split sixfold tower, where $W(B_4) = (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^4 \rtimes \mathfrak{S}_4$ (order $384$). Writing $u_r = x_r^2$, the identity $f_{B,C} \in (e_1, e_3)$ implies that $V(e_1, e_3) \subset \mathbb{P}^3$ consists of $12$ universal projective lines $L_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}^{(p)} \subset V_{B,C}$. Their $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(i)/\mathbb{Q})$-invariant $\det(g)$-alternating divisor $D_{\det}$ satisfies $I_{\mathrm{lines}} v_{\det} = -10 v_{\det}$ and $D_{\det}^2 = -120 \ne 0$, defining a nonzero flat rational Betti $(1, 1)$-class $[D_{\det}] \in W_{\mathrm{I}}$ orthogonal to $\nabla_B^a \nabla_C^b \omega_{\mathrm{I}}$ for all orders $(a, b) \ge (0, 0)$. Combined with $49$ pure $(1, 1)$ representation dimensions, this gives $\rho(V_{B,C}) = 51$ and splits the $55$-dimensional transcendental lattice via the Klein four-group $V_4 \subset \mathfrak{S}_4$ into $10$ K3-type blocks ($W_{\mathrm{I}}^{\mathrm{trans}}$ of dimension $4$, three Type S-II blocks of dimension $5$, and six Type S-III blocks of dimension $6$). Exact Griffiths pole-reduced determinants at $(0, 0)$ ($\det_{H^{1,1}}(W_{\mathrm{I}}^{\mathrm{trans}}) = 2$, $\det M_{\mathrm{S-II}}^{\mathrm{PR}} = 8/3$, $\det M_{\mathrm{S-III}}^{\mathrm{PR}} = -5/36$) prove $\operatorname{NS} = 0$, $\mathbb{Q}$-Hodge simplicity, and $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{Q}-\mathrm{Hdg}} = \mathbb{Q}$ on each block, establishing via the $46$ group-ring matrix units that all $2{,}649$ rational Hodge classes in $H^4(V_{B,C}^2, \mathbb{Q})$ ($2{,}546$ primitive) are algebraic. On $Y_{B,C} \subset \mathbb{P}^4$ ($b_3 = 520 = 66 + 454$), full-block pole-reduced determinants ($\det M_{\mathrm{CY3}, 5\times 5}^{\mathrm{PR}} = 48$, $\det M_{\mathrm{CY6}, 7\times 7}^{\mathrm{PR}} = 40/9$) prove $\operatorname{Hdg}^3((M_{\mathrm{CY}} \otimes M_{\mathrm{CY}})^{\mu_6}) \cong K \oplus M_4(K)$ ($\dim_{\mathbb{Q}} = 34$, all algebraic via deck-commuting matrix units), reducing the remaining $454$-dimensional level-$1$ sector to geometric curve support.
