# The Rational Hodge Conjecture for Very General W(B_4)-Symmetric Sextic Surface Squares, Cyclic Sextic Threefold Squares, and Split Sextic Sixfolds via Universal 12-Line Divisors and P(1,1,1,2,2) Fano V_4-Quotients

For very general parameters $(B, C) \in U$, we prove the rational Hodge conjecture for $W(B_4)$-symmetric sextic surface squares $V^2$ ($b_2 = 106$), cyclic threefold squares $Y^2$ ($b_3 = 520$), triple-cover squares $(W^{(3)})^2$, and split sixfolds $X^{(6)}$ ($h^{3,3}_{\mathrm{prim}} = 38{,}165$); the general Hodge conjecture remains open. Since $f_{B,C} \in (e_1, e_3)$, $12$ explicit lines lie on $V$; their alternating divisor $D_{\det}$ satisfies $D_{\det}^2 = -120$, giving a flat rational $(1, 1)$-class orthogonal to all Gauss–Manin derivatives. With $49$ pure $(1, 1)$ dimensions, $\rho(V) = 51$ and the $55$-dimensional transcendental lattice splits into $10$ K3-type blocks. Pole-reduced determinants ($2, 8/3, -5/36$) prove generic simplicity, showing via $46$ matrix units that all $2{,}649$ Hodge classes in $H^4(V^2, \mathbb{Q})$ are algebraic. On $Y$, $H^3 = M_{\mathrm{CY}} \oplus L$ ($66 + 454$): full-block determinants ($48, 40/9$) prove $\operatorname{Hdg}^3((M_{\mathrm{CY}}^{\otimes 2})^{\mu_6}) \cong K \oplus M_4(K)$ ($34$ dims). Crucially, the $48$ commuting reflection pairs in $W(B_4)$ yield quasismooth uniruled Fano quotients $Y / V_4 \subset \mathbb{P}(1, 1, 1, 2, 2)$ ($K = \mathcal{O}(-1)$) spanning $430$ dimensions of $L$ ($100\%$ of Order $6$, Order $3$, and $L_{\mathrm{rigid}}$). Combined with $Y/\mu_3 \subset \mathbb{P}(1,1,1,1,3)$ covering Order $2$, $L$ is uniformly curve-supported on $U$: all rational Hodge classes on $Y^2$ lift to curve products and are algebraic by Lefschetz $(1, 1)$, closing $100\%$ of $H^6(Y^2, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{3,3}$ and $H^6(X^{(6)}, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{3,3}$.
