Several local corrections and clarifications remain:

- **Proposition 5.1 asserts an unnecessary, false splitting.** The canonical descent linearization of $L$ need not preserve the branch section under the chosen identification $L^2\cong\pi^*M^2$. Already for the order-four character with $G=\mathbf Z/2$, the lift has $t^2=\delta$, giving $\Gamma_0\cong\mathbf Z/4$, not $(\mathbf Z/2)^2$. This does not invalidate the stated rank bound: only abelianness is needed. More precisely, if the branch section has character $\lambda$ under the canonical linearization, its preserving lifts form the abelian group
  $$
  \{(g,c)\in G\times\mathbf C^*:c^2\lambda(g)=1\}.
  $$
  The character multiplicity and orbit argument then work for this possibly nonsplit extension. Replace the direct-product assertion accordingly. The supplied multiplicity script assumes split examples and does not test splitting. The added $\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{Hdg}}(U)=\mathbf Q$ hypothesis correctly handles the fixed-character case without the former unsupported Galois argument about holomorphic lines.
