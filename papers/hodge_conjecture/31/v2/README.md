# A second quadratic locus for the curve-source Kuga–Satake method: V_3 = (U^2 + A_2(-1)^2)_Q, conditional on the V_P preprint

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-10-21T21:52:09.140450Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/31/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A preprint of 30 Sep 2026 (OpenAI; not refereed) claims HC and GHC for all powers of K3 surfaces whose transcendental space embeds in $V_P=(U^2\oplus\langle-1\rangle^4)_{\mathbf Q}$. Its only lattice-specific input is a six-parameter family of double covers of abelian surfaces, attached to a character of order $4$, whose eigenspace is a K3-type variation with form $V_P$ and full period rank. Replacing the character by one of order $6$ and taking the two-dimensional $S_3$-isotypic piece gives a rank-$8$ K3-type variation with period map of rank $6$ and form $V_3=(U^2\oplus\langle-1,-1,-3,-3\rangle)_{\mathbf Q}$, which has Hasse invariant $-1$ exactly at $3$ and $\infty$. We check each lattice-specific step of the preprint for this seed. Conditional on its general sections, HC and GHC follow for all powers of every projective K3 surface with $T$ embedding in $V_3$. A rank-6 Witt-index-1 space embeds in $V_3$ iff its obstruction primes lie in $\{3\}$. This covers 22 of the 33 index-1 elliptic types in the census of rStation hodge_conjecture_23, and 31 of 33 together with $V_P$. We prove that seeds of this type never give K3-type pieces with endomorphism field $\mathbf Q$ of rank above $8$. An untwisted $(1,3)$ seed has a rank-$10$ piece of form $U^2\oplus\langle-1\rangle^6$, which would cover every rational K3-type space of rank at most $7$. We explain why the preprint's curve domination fails there, conditional on full signed Weyl monodromy of the Gauss tower, which is supported numerically but not proved.
