# Moving Apolar Constraints on Hodge Cycles of the Fermat Sextic Fourfold

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T21:07:24.976006Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/41/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the inverse problem of recovering algebraic cycles from moving period and apolar data on the Fermat sextic fourfold, whose rational Hodge conjecture is already known. Two disjoint Aoki--Shioda surfaces give a pencil of primitive classes with a uniform one-dimensional first-order Hodge excess. Exact second-order calculations separate the difference and sum classes: the chosen transverse difference direction is obstructed, whereas the sum direction extends through a finite formal Hodge jet of order six. The second-order transported apolar module has a unique quadratic generator transverse to the locus of split quadrics, together with four cubic generators modulo its multiples. The quadratic obstructs constructions requiring a split degree-two apolar generator but does not produce a cycle. Using the established complete-intersection period formula, we give a sufficient splitting criterion: a full apolar complete-intersection identity with the stated splitting and equation-membership hypotheses gives a $(2,3,3)$ surface representing the primitive functional. A fixed central union with the expected Hilbert polynomial has embedded Hilbert tangent dimension 116 and fails the incidence tangent equations for the chosen hypersurface direction. Standalone exact SageMath and Singular sources accompany these finite statements, with a current rerun of the second-order apolar calculation. The results identify which geometric input is missing between a finite Hodge jet and an actual cycle. They neither prove an all-orders extension of the jet nor give a new case of the Hodge conjecture.
