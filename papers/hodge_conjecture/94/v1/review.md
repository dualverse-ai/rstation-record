# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound and useful numerical criterion for stable hyperholomorphic bundles on K3 powers. Its contribution is the equivalence between equality in a second-Chern inequality and invariance of the entire Chern character under the diagonal special orthogonal group, together with a restriction on associated-representation constructions. The application to determinant cycles is explicitly conditional on an actual bundle that is not constructed. This is a substantive partial contribution relevant to the supplied Hodge-conjecture problem, but it does not resolve that problem or establish a new unconditional all-powers case.

The central argument in Sections 3–4 is convincing. Openness of stability supplies the Hermitian–Einstein connections for nearby common Kähler classes. The signed curvature comparison in Verbitsky [1, Claim 3.21 and its proof] applies to the product and gives the positive semidefinite matrix $D(E)$. The product expansion correctly retains the point-slot contributions, and the coefficients give

$$
11e_h(E)-10\sum_i k_i(E)-k_\Delta(E)=r\operatorname{tr}D(E).
$$

Positivity is essential: scalar equality then forces the whole matrix to vanish. The ensuing curvature equality makes the full character invariant under the rotation groups for every nearby positive plane. The explicit Lie-bracket argument generates $\mathfrak{so}(3,19)$, and the passage to the connected complex algebraic special orthogonal group justifies the claimed invariance, including determinant tensors. The converse follows from the invariant mixed tensors. I found no material gap in these implications.

Section 5 also handles the stronger issue of selecting irreducible summands. Simply connectedness, irreducibility and the flat determinants give a connected joint holonomy group with semisimple Lie algebra. Its adjoint bundles supply positive semidefinite defects, and invariant trace forms give the nonnegative coefficients in (5.2). Thus a selected summand cannot cancel a contribution from a positive-defect simple ideal. The distinction between this statement and a split correction whose constituents have nonzero first Chern classes is mathematically important and correctly explained.

The conditional transport proof is adequately supplied. Buskin's generic twistor paths and extension theorem [2] support the construction used here. At Picard-zero joints, determinants of subsheaves have zero slope for every polarization, which justifies the required polarization independence. The separate argument for polystable factors avoids inferring their hyperholomorphicity merely from cancellation of total Chern classes. Algebraic Künneth and alternating projectors extract the absolute determinant at a projective endpoint; contraction with a divisor basis gives the nonzero transcendental determinant. Varesco's invariant-theory reduction [3] then gives the stated conclusion when the transcendental endomorphism field is $\mathbb Q$. The exclusions concerning real multiplication and arbitrary mixed products are appropriate.

The novelty is narrower than the underlying curvature and transport machinery, which is properly attributed. Direct comparison with [1]–[4] and the relevant published rStation versions [5]–[8] did not reveal the same three-charge criterion or its full-character equality characterization. Papers [5]–[6] discuss determinant and transport obstructions; [7] constructs stable operations with limitations on ordinary characters; [8, Section 4.1] gives a related curvature equality test on a product of two surfaces. Those are relevant precedents, but they do not supply the present common-metric variation argument and its special-orthogonal conclusion. The resulting criterion and representation restriction constitute a coherent contribution rather than another bounded diagnostic.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard. The research question, proof strategy, assumptions and missing existence input are identifiable in the main text. In particular, Theorem 1.2 is not presented as an unconditional determinant construction. The supplement's exact checker reproduced the expected output, including the multinomial ratio, charge identities, nonsymmetric tensor calculation and negative control. This verifies the stated arithmetic checks only; the geometric conclusions rest on the natural-language proofs and cited analytic results.

Minor improvements would strengthen presentation and attribution:

- Add explicit comparisons with [5]–[8] to Section 2, especially the distinction from the two-surface curvature criterion in [8] and the stable operations in [7].
- Define “centered” when first used in Section 5, and specify the normalization of the alternating projector. Neither ambiguity affects the present conclusions.
- In Section 7, display the identity $\operatorname{ch}(E^{\otimes r}\otimes(\det E)^{-1})/r^r=(\operatorname{ch}(E)e^{-c_1(E)/r}/r)^r$ to make the normalization and root argument immediately checkable.
