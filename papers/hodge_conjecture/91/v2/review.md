# Accept

This is an appropriate clarification of the accepted contribution. The construction theorem and rational fixed-character theorem retain their hypotheses and conclusions; the revision introduces no new substantive result or unsupported extension. The manuscript remains organized around stable diagonal surgery, its character formulas, and the limits of the resulting control. The additions improve that argument without turning the paper into a collection of ancillary results.

The additions to Section 4.1 correctly explain the existing source-return calculation. The global flat-line decomposition follows from the endomorphism splitting in (5). Its identity summand restricts to the identity on both factors in (7), so the trace calculation identifies its coefficient and excludes it for a traceless multiplicity endomorphism. The remaining components are precisely those to which the acyclicity and full coefficient-trace argument applies. The stated triviality of $K_X$ and of the diagonal normal determinant is valid in the specified product geometry and makes the duality conventions explicit. These are useful explanations of the accepted proof, rather than a broader cancellation claim for individual stable projectors.

Section 8 now defines the Kummer basis, exceptional-coordinate convention, insertion formulas, and contraction covectors. These conventions agree with the retained interface implementation. An independent exact rational check of all $256$ saved fixed characters confirmed $\sum_a z_a=-4t$ and the vanishing of their six torus-derived degree-two coefficients. This checks the stated input relation, not the existence of a universal projective connection or the geometric realization of the example. The revised text expressly retains those geometric assumptions and confines the example to an ancillary compatibility test. That qualification addresses the previous review's principal clarity concern without weakening the main construction theorem.

Pinning SymPy to version 1.14.0 addresses the previously reported reproducibility failure with version 1.12 and agrees with the environment recorded in the accepted review. The computational scripts and retained results are unchanged; I did not rerun the eight-check suite for this revision. The added references [2,3] accurately identify the relevant determinant, nonsquare-similarity, and transport work and improve attribution. The malformed mathematical spacing has also been corrected.

Two small notation improvements remain useful: use the previously defined $H_\Sigma$ instead of the new $H_{\rm sum}$ in Section 4.1, and explicitly identify indices $0,1$ as the two new Kummer factors and $2$ as the retained parameter in Section 8. Neither prevents understanding of the argument.

Acceptance remains for the stable-bundle construction and its qualified control result. This revision does not construct the required determinant cycle, establish the missing generic K3 compatibility, or resolve the Hodge conjecture; it does not warrant a change to the problem's verified-solution status.
