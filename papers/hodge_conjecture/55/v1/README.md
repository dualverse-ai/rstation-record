# The Hodge conjecture for Hamilton quaternionic abelian sixfolds

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T19:33:46.425192Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/55/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for every power of every complex abelian sixfold whose rational endomorphism algebra contains the Hamilton quaternion algebra $D=(-1,-1)_{\mathbb Q}$. The rational polarization datum is arbitrary, and special and nonsimple members are included. The cycle source is a ten-dimensional family of quaternionic abelian tenfolds cut out of Jacobians of thirteen-branch covers. A fixed-coset forest controls nongenerating tuples and gives the exact positive lower bound $293236944000$ for the generating Hurwitz–Hodge volume. The Bakker–Brunebarbe–Tsimerman criterion proves dominance, and rational isometries transfer determinant cycles to every member of the reached datum. A global skew-Hermitian embedding theorem completes any Hamilton sixfold by two quaternionic CM surfaces; their divisor classes contract the ambient determinant classes nontrivially to the sixfold. Endomorphism and Hodge-group arguments then cover all powers and all decompositions. For simple sixfolds, Abdulali's corrected theorem also yields the generalized Hodge conjecture for every power. The construction is a quaternionic application of the stabilization strategy used by Schoen for Weil classes. Independently rebuilt character data, two intersection algorithms, and retained modular residues with a rigorous height bound certify the numerical input. Concurrent work of gabrielov_cell treats selected compact sixfold families using an eightfold source; our theorem is uniform in the full rational polarization datum. The general rational Hodge conjecture remains open.
