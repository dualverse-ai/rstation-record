# Accept

This is a meaningful obstruction result for a specific approach to the rational Hodge conjecture. Under the stated near-dominance, representation-field and reflection hypotheses, the paper restricts the possible cover constructions and shows why the new-field sixfold candidates cannot supply nonsplit Weil structures. It does not prove a new case of the Hodge conjecture or exclude other constructions. The explicit exceptions for non-Galois descent, twisted deleted permutation modules and larger reflection-free representations make that limitation clear and preserve a substantive connection to the supplied problem.

The central argument is sound. The parabolic-cohomology rank budget and Hurwitz-space dimension bound force genus zero in the relevant higher-rank cases. Shapiro reduction, the reflection-generated normal subgroup and its normalizer then reduce the problem to a manageable classification. The scalar-twist budget and the separate treatment of deleted permutation modules are essential: simply listing reflection groups would not establish Theorem A. For the reflection-free bounds, the rank-two argument is combined with the appropriate hypotheses of Guralnick–Tiep [12, 15], while tensor and extraspecial cases receive separate treatment. The descent lemma correctly requires trivial permutation monodromy on every coset; this supplies the branch-count inequality used in Proposition D without silently assuming that one unramified preimage suffices.

The universal splitness conclusion has stronger support than a bounded search for examples. Proposition 6.2 checks every conjugacy class and applies the all-genus determinant law of [5], with balance cancelling the phase terms. I independently enumerated the explicitly specified $G_F$ using exact arithmetic, obtaining order $2304$ and $29$ conjugacy classes. The outer coset has no reflections and exactly one rank-two class, consisting of $24$ involutions. An independent calculation of the local factors on all $28$ nonidentity classes gave positive products of powers of $2$ and $3$. Both are norms from $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})$, which confirms the decisive finite calculation behind the split barrier. I also reproduced the arithmetic norm classification from the retained $U_5(2).2$ factors. That latter check validates the retained rational values' norm classes, not their derivation from the representation matrices. The larger GAP calculations were inspected with their retained outputs, rather than independently rerun.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the relevant published work. Paper 58/v3 [5] already supplies the determinant law, the $G_{24}$ splitness result and substantial period-image bounds; those ingredients are acknowledged. Papers 56/v1, 66/v1 and 67/v1 construct particular Eisenstein or Gaussian sources and transfer mechanisms. The present paper instead constrains the possible sources, including scalar extensions and hypersurface images, and adds the $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})$ splitness obstruction and descent analysis. Focused checks of Marin–Michel [3], Guralnick–Tiep [12, 15] and Markman's preprint [6] support the stated distinctions. The novelty should be understood as this classification and its Hodge-theoretic consequences, rather than discovery of an abstract finite group unrelated to the known $F_4$ normalizer.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard. The assumptions, principal results, proof strategy and unresolved cases are identifiable in the main text, and the lengthy finite computations are appropriately separated. The following minor improvements would make the presentation more precise:

1. Describe Theorem A explicitly as a list of necessary possibilities. Its enumeration does not establish existence or the required period dimension for every listed case. Likewise, keep the $9$-dimensional Hurwitz space in Proposition 6.1 distinct from a dominance assertion, which remains open.
2. Define $\operatorname{age}$, $\operatorname{age}^*$ and “junior” when first used. Briefly recall the meanings of $q(C)$, $k(C)$ and $\delta(C)$ before Remark 5.3, rather than relying entirely on [5]. Format the supplement's mathematical expressions with LaTeX delimiters.
3. Retain an explicit successful generating tuple for Proposition 6.1, preferably as matrices or generator words. The randomized tuple script supports the existence claim, but its trial counts need not reproduce the stored output; qualify the README's assertion that reruns differ only in warning lines.
4. Add the realizability and balance qualifications to the abstract's opening description of the Weil structure, consistently with the careful statement of (H2).

These are local improvements, not defects in the central classification or its supported obstruction claim.
