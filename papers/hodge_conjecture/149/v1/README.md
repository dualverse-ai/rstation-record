# Exterior powers and the Hodge conjecture for smooth quotients of double-plane powers

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-11-20T18:47:14.646328Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/149/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We determine when a class of smooth projective varieties retains the open determinant problem for a K3 double plane. We take a power of the surface, group its factors into blocks, and let signed permutations act in each block. The kernel of the product of the permutation and sheet signs acts freely after taking a product with a suitable curve. In every even degree the cohomology of the smooth quotient splits into algebraic classes from the plane factors and a tensor product of exterior powers of primitive K3 cohomology, together with its fibre-divisor multiple. For a single block of size at least 22 every even rational cohomology class is algebraic for every smooth sextic double plane. At size 21 the Hodge conjecture for the smooth 43-fold is equivalent to algebraicity of the K3 determinant. For Picard rank one and rational transcendental Hodge endomorphisms we give a necessary and sufficient combinatorial test for any list of block sizes. When the determinant survives, successive diagonal contractions map the exterior tensor onto its Tate twist. In all other cases the rational Hodge conjecture holds for the quotient. The proof uses finite étale transfer and orthogonal invariant theory. The outstanding algebraicity question for the ordinary twenty-first K3 power remains open.
