# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects attribution, clarifies the accepted argument, and supplies missing supporting documentation. It introduces no new substantive contribution or material scientific regression. The manuscript remains organized around the variable-length fixed-surface construction and its consequences for multiplier classes $[2]$, $[3]$, and $[6]$, with uniform Picard-number thresholds $9$ and $13$.

The principal correction, Remark 6.6, is sound. Since $V_5\cong U^3_{\mathbb Q}$, Lemma 6.2 identifies the $p=5$ hypothesis exactly with an embedding

$$
T(S)_{\mathbb Q}\hookrightarrow U^3_{\mathbb Q}\oplus\langle-m\rangle,
\qquad m>0.
$$

I checked Floccari’s Theorem 5.11 [27] and Varesco’s Theorem 5.3 [4]: the former establishes algebraicity of the Kuga–Satake correspondence on this locus, and the latter establishes algebraicity of similarities between surfaces with algebraic Kuga–Satake correspondences. Positive rational scaling preserves the locus; a rational $m>0$ can also be replaced by an integer in its square class. Thus every similarity partner remains covered. The comparison with [8, Lemma 6A.1] and [19, Proposition 3.1] confirms the stated prior coverage and the rank-six Witt-index interpretation. Presenting Corollary C(3) as an alternative construction of a known case is the correct resolution of the previous review’s qualification. The abstract and principal comparison tables now reflect this distinction.

The two proof clarifications are valid. In Theorem A, replacing $Z$ by $c^{-1}Z$ preserves algebraicity with rational coefficients and changes the multiplier from $pc^2$ to exactly $p$. In Proposition 4.1, Step 3, choosing common generic periods in nonempty overlaps of consecutive deformation charts, then using uniqueness of the marked fibre and injectivity in (T5), supplies the required identification of the group actions. This clarifies the existing deformation argument without strengthening its conclusion.

The added verification transcript contains 137 successful checks and ends with `FALLOS = 0`, consistent with the previous review’s recorded reproduction. The executable supplements are unchanged. I inspected the new transcript but did not rerun the Python or Maple computations in this revision review. These finite arithmetic and partition checks support the examples; the general conclusions continue to rest on the mathematical proofs. Including the transcript and recording software versions resolves the missing-output issue. The reported Maple execution remains an author-reported check.

Two minor editorial adjustments would improve consistency: qualify the closing sentence of Section 2 about the absence of prior statements of “Corollaries C, D” to exclude the already known content of C(3), and move the explicitly “not new” bullet outside the list introduced as “The new points.” These residual wording issues do not undermine the correction, which is otherwise explicit and well explained.

The additions remain proportionate and preserve the paper’s focus. This remains a partial advance for specified K3 surfaces and multiplier classes, not a resolution of the full rational Hodge conjecture; acceptance of this revision does not change that status.
