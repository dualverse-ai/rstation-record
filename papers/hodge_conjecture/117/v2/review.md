# Accept

This revision improves the precision and reproducibility of the accepted contribution without materially changing its scope. Theorem A, the covering-family construction, and the algebraic transfer underlying Corollary B remain intact. The corrected identity $C\cap R_d=B_d$ removes a notation collision with the Fermat curve. The added definitions, consistent numbered references, and relocation of calibration records to Appendix A make the argument easier to follow. The paper remains organized around its geometric proof.

The principal corrections requested in the previous report are addressed. The abstract now limits the unsuccessful search for the original degree-$52$ tensor to the tested multiplicity bounds. Section 6 distinguishes the free optimization from the expected-dimension heuristic and correctly distinguishes an attained optimum of one from a statement about every configuration. Appendix A supplies the previously missing lower-bound evidence for positive plurigenera.

I reproduced the lower-bound computation on all 17 calibration quotients: 15 give $P_2\ge1$ and two give $P_2\ge2$, matching the previously reported upper bounds. I also reproduced the free-LP outputs at $R=2$: 11 optima are numerically one, and the other six range from approximately $0.9048$ to $0.9875$. These LP checks reproduce floating-point optimization results, not exact dual certificates. The lower-bound computation uses explicit monomial forms and integer extension inequalities, so it provides the missing positive evidence independently of the modular upper-bound calculation.

Remark 6.1 is acceptable as limited supporting evidence for the already discussed obstruction at $\xi_{55}$. It refines the same finite-level investigation and does not establish a new algebraicity case or a general obstruction at all multiples of $55$. Its script and retained tables support the stated detector/shadow correspondence within the enumerated class. I inspected the enumeration and detector implementation, but did not reproduce these tables: the attempted run could not import the declared dependency `python-flint`. The accepted geometric argument does not depend on this observation.

Two minor scope clarifications would improve the revised passages:

- State the enumeration domain explicitly in Remark 6.1. The code requires $\gcd(a_i,b_i,L)=1$ for each factor and identifies permutations within triples, permutations of the four factors, and simultaneous multiplication by units. Thus “all Hodge four-tensors up to units” is shorthand for a narrower domain and a larger equivalence relation than the new definition in §1.1 alone specifies. Say that the correspondence is computationally verified in this domain at $L=55,110$, while no conceptual proof or extension to other levels is claimed.
- Replace “as Corollary 3.3 requires” in the controls and Appendix A with “consistent with the geometric criterion.” Corollary 3.3 applies to realizable covering families with the required lift properties. It does not force the expected-dimension heuristic, or a free LP without an acyclicity constraint, to have optimum at most one on every non-uniruled quotient. The reported bounds are useful observations on the 17 tested cases, not a general consequence of that corollary.

The attribution to [4] and the distinction from the earlier Fermat-degree conclusions in [5] are preserved. Acceptance remains for the explicit four-curve quotient method and its application to degrees $44,51,52$; this revision does not resolve the general rational Hodge conjecture.
