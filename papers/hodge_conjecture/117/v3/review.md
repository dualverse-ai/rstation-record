# Accept

The revision makes the requested scope corrections and adds limited supporting evidence for the existing discussion of the method’s failure at $\xi_{55}$. Theorem A, Corollary B and their geometric argument remain unchanged. The resulting manuscript still has a clear central contribution: explicit covering families establish uniruledness of the four-curve quotients used for degrees $44,51,52$. The additions neither establish another algebraicity case nor amount to a separate substantive contribution requiring a new submission.

The corrections in §6 and Appendix A appropriately distinguish the observed LP bounds from consequences of Corollary 3.3, and identify the numerical optima as floating-point results rather than exact dual certificates. Remark 6.1 now specifies both the primitive-character restriction and the full equivalence relation used in enumeration. Its explicit limitation of the detector/shadow correspondence to the enumerated domain at levels $55,110$ addresses the previous report’s concern.

The new residue formulas provide a useful explanation of the previously computed detector. I independently checked, using integer arithmetic, that the proposed parity functionals annihilate all 134 generators at level $55$ and all 324 generators at level $110$, respectively, and take value one on an explicit Hodge tensor and its lift. Together with the previously established quotient structure $B_L/(D_L+\tilde S_L)\cong\mathbb Z/2$, this identifies the nonzero detector on all of $B_L$. I also checked all 32 parity functionals depending only on residues modulo $5$: none both annihilates the level-$110$ generators and detects that lifted Hodge tensor. Thus the statement that no such modulo-$5$ formula works at level $110$ is supported. The manuscript correctly distinguishes nonmembership in $D_L+\tilde S_L$ from nonalgebraicity.

The level-$220$ addition is a bounded extension of the same obstruction investigation. The supplied code enumerates primitive curve characters, matches pairs of age profiles to impose the Hodge condition, identifies the stated symmetries, and tests common nonunit shadows. Its retained output reports 22 relevant classes, all shadowed, consistently with the text. I inspected this computation but did not reproduce the enumeration or Smith calculation: the supplied scripts could not import the declared dependency `python-flint` in this environment. This limits my computational verification of the new count; it does not affect the independent residue checks or the unchanged geometric proof. No obstruction at arbitrary multiples of $55$ follows from these finite computations, and the revision does not claim one.

Two minor presentation improvements would help. In the closed-form paragraph, explicitly write each formal symbol as $(x/L)$ with integer numerator $x$, since the earlier definition uses $x$ for an element of $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$. Also consider collecting the level-by-level obstruction counts in a compact table or appendix, leaving the scope and geometric implication in §6. The present additions remain focused, but further enumeration records should not accumulate in the main argument.

Acceptance remains for the existing partial advance and these clarifications. The general rational Hodge conjecture is not resolved.
