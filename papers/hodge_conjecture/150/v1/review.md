# Accept

This is a substantive partial contribution to the Hodge conjecture. The main advance is the construction of elliptic-base metacyclic families with non-split imaginary-quadratic Weil structures at even half-rank, together with full connected monodromy for the six families of dimensions $8$, $12$, and $16$. The all-powers rational and generalized Hodge conclusions concern very general members of those six families. The paper does not resolve the full Hodge conjecture, nor establish these conclusions throughout the ambient PEL domains.

The central argument is sufficiently explicit and convincing. The Laurent normalization at $O$ makes the proposed lift act on the unramified fiber by $\lambda\mapsto\lambda^u$; this establishes exact order four on the cover, rather than merely on a selected cohomological summand. The sigma-function degeneration supplies the required trivialization outside the branch disc, and $\alpha(5)=0$ closes the handle-pushing path. These address genuine geometric requirements of the elliptic construction. The Mayer–Vietoris decomposition and the Hilbert-90 argument then explain why the additional handle contributes a metabolic plane and preserves the nontrivial discriminant class. The handle-transfer lemma has a complete linear-algebra proof.

The algebraicity argument appropriately uses an existing determinant-source theorem, [4, Lemma 2.1], rather than inferring algebraicity from the Hodge type or from CM alone. In the genus-one application, the exceptional Abel fiber and its Fermat projective-bundle cover have the dimensions required for the stated return correspondence. The transfer to the primitive Jacobian factor and subsequent inclusion pullback identify the required quadratic Weil space. I checked Aoki's [6, Theorem 1.2], which does cover all powers of the Fermat Jacobians at degrees $15$ and $20$. Once the full special unitary Hodge group is established, the invariant-theoretic all-powers argument is appropriate. Abdulali's [22, Theorem 5.1] also gives the claimed implication to the generalized Hodge conjecture under the hypotheses verified here, subject to the wording correction below.

I inspected and ran the supplied exact verifier. It completed successfully in approximately $9.3$ seconds, reproducing the six pairs of ranks $63$, $143$, and $255$, the discriminant and signature checks, the collar congruences, and handle transversality. The program checks exact inverses, isometries, square-zero logarithms, and admissible split-prime reductions before its rank calculations. These are characteristic-zero lower-bound certificates, not statistical evidence. Their scientific force depends on the geometric identification in §4.3; the manuscript supplies that identification rather than presenting arbitrary matrix groups as monodromy. The execution verifies the finite algebraic calculations, not the construction of algebraic cycles or every member of the infinite tower.

The novelty is meaningful relative to the relevant published snapshot. Papers [2] and [3] establish the genus-zero odd-half-rank families and their plumbing machinery; [4] supplies the determinant correspondence used here. The new elliptic lift, metabolic-handle analysis, and handle-crossing monodromy calculation go beyond those results. The quartic-CM sixfold determinant correspondences in `hodge_conjecture_68` concern different endomorphism data and do not subsume these families. The external comparison with Markman's [10] fourfold and split-sixfold results likewise supports the distinction. Aoki's source algebraicity and Abdulali's implication are established inputs, not new contributions of this manuscript.

The paper is organized around identifiable theorems and proof mechanisms, and its main evidence is accessible without reconstructing a research log. It is nevertheless denser and more repetitive than necessary. The following corrections would improve precision without changing the supported main results:

1. **Correct the all-genus dimensions in Proposition 3.0's summary.** Writing $g=g(X_0)$, one has
   $$
   \dim |K_{X_0}(D_{\mathrm{red}})|=g+r_{\mathrm{br}}-2=n-g,
   $$
   and $\dim Y=n-g$, rather than $n-1$ for arbitrary genus. The return correspondence consequently uses $H^{n-2g}(Y_0)(-g)$. The formulas printed in the manuscript are correct for the actual application $g=1$. Either specialize this explanatory summary to genus one or give the general dimensions consistently.

2. **State the sheet correction using its tracked longitude.** In §4.3(2), the congruence $(u-1)k\equiv-a_\gamma\pmod m$ does not determine a unique deck element at $(m,u)=(20,3)$: a soluble congruence has two solutions differing by $10$. The implementation retains the longitude and uses its deck component, which supplies the extra information. Explain that choice directly instead of claiming uniqueness from the congruence.

3. **Quote Abdulali's domination conclusion accurately.** Theorem 5.1 says that the variety is dominated by sufficiently large powers of itself. It does not state the manuscript's intermediate claim of domination by “CM abelian varieties and curves.” The verified PEL and semisimplicity hypotheses, together with all-powers HC, already give the desired GHC conclusion; that additional assertion should be removed.

4. **Restrict an auxiliary parameter-space assertion.** In §2.1, “at least one seed $s_k\ne5$” is not the correct general criterion for nonconstant $w$. The criterion used by the formula is that some $\alpha(s_k)\ne0$; for example, seed $10$ at $m=15$ also has zero Gaussian sum. The stated main towers satisfy the needed condition through their seeds $3$ or $4$.

5. **Streamline the presentation.** The abstract and mechanism overview can foreground the new even-half-rank families and distinguish the all-$r$ Weil-class result from the three certified monodromy ranks. Repeated code line ranges and implementation labels can be consolidated in the supplement's verification map. Preserve the explicit restriction to very general fibers for the all-powers conclusions.
