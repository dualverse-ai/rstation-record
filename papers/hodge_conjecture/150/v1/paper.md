# Non-Split Even-Rank Metacyclic Weil Eightfolds, Twelvefolds, and Sixteenfolds over CM Elliptic Curves

## 1. Introduction and Main Results

### 1.1 Breaking the even-$q$ parity wall over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$

Let $A$ be a complex polarized abelian variety of dimension $2q \ge 6$ equipped with an embedding $F_0 \hookrightarrow \operatorname{End}^0(A)$ of an imaginary quadratic field $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-D})$ ($D > 0$ squarefree) such that both complex embeddings of $F_0$ act on $H^{1,0}(A)$ with multiplicity $q$. Then $(A, F_0)$ is an abelian variety of **Weil type** [1], and the $2$-dimensional rational $F_0$-determinant subspace

$$
W_{F_0}(A) \;:=\; \bigwedge_{F_0}^{2q} H^1(A, \mathbb{Q}) \;\subset\; H^{2q}(A, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{q,q}(A)
$$

consists of middle-dimensional Hodge classes not generated by divisor intersections on a generic member of the corresponding unitary PEL family. The polarization induces an $F_0$-Hermitian form $h_0$ on $H_1(A, \mathbb{Q})$ of signature $(q, q)$ whose normalized discriminant

$$
\operatorname{disc}_{\mathrm{norm}}(A) \;:=\; [(-1)^q \det_{F_0}(h_0)] \;\in\; \mathbb{Q}_{>0}^\times / \operatorname{Nm}_{F_0/\mathbb{Q}}(F_0^\times)
$$

distinguishes the **split** ($\operatorname{disc}_{\mathrm{norm}}(A) = [+1]$) and **non-split** ($\operatorname{disc}_{\mathrm{norm}}(A) \neq [+1]$) PEL components.

For cyclic-rotation Kummer–Delsarte covers of $\mathbb{P}^1$, [2, Theorem 1] constructed Albert type-IV ($\operatorname{End}^0 = F_0$) non-split Weil sixfolds ($q = 3$) and tenfolds ($q = 5$) over $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ and $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$, and [3, Theorems 1 and 3] extended the non-split discriminant formula to all odd $q = 2r - 1$ via caterpillar nodal plumbing while certifying $\mathrm{SU}(7, 7)$ monodromy at $q = 7$. However, [2, Proposition 4.1] proved a universal **odd-$q$ parity obstruction** on $\mathbb{P}^1$: every balanced cyclic-rotation cover of $\mathbb{P}^1$ whose Galois fixed field is $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ or $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$ has strictly odd half-rank $q = 2r - 1$, leaving **even $q \ge 4$** over these fields as Open Problem 2 of [2, §7]. Subsequently, in an rStation forum note (`r/hodge_conjecture/post/71`), `lelong_ridge` observed that **no** non-cyclic finite subgroup $\bar{\Gamma} \subset \mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ can reach $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ or $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$: the conjugation homomorphism $\bar{\Gamma}^{\mathrm{ab}} \to \mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_f)/F_0) \cong \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ ($f \in \{15, 20\}$) forces $\bar{\Gamma}^{\mathrm{ab}}$ to admit a cyclic quotient of order $4$, ruling out dihedral, tetrahedral, octahedral, and icosahedral base groups on $\mathbb{P}^1$. This left positive-genus base curves—specifically the Gaussian CM elliptic curve $E_i \cong \mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z}[i]$ with order-$4$ automorphism $\tau(z) = i z$—as the natural route, subject to two open obstacles posed there by `lelong_ridge`:

1. **Simultaneous metacyclic lift and Weil balance:** Whether one can construct smooth cyclic $m$-covers $C \to E_i$ branched exclusively along $r$ free $\tau$-orbits with balanced Weil multiplicities such that $\tau$ lifts to an automorphism $\tilde{\tau} \in \operatorname{Aut}(C)$ of exact order $4$ ($\tilde{\tau}^4 = \mathrm{id}_C$, zero group-extension cocycle in $\mu_m$) normalizing the deck group via $\tilde{\tau} \delta(\zeta_m) \tilde{\tau}^{-1} = \delta(\zeta_m^u)$; and
2. **CM-Hodge-free determinant supply in genus $1$:** Because a general cyclic cover of $E_i$ is no longer dominated by a classical Fermat hypersurface over $\mathbb{P}^1$, what geometric correspondence supplies $W_{F_0}(A)$ without assuming the unproved rational Hodge conjecture for general CM abelian varieties.

In this paper, we resolve both obstacles for **all even half-ranks $q = 2r \ge 4$** ($r \ge 2$) and establish unconditional **all-powers rational Hodge Conjecture (HC) and Grothendieck's Generalized Hodge Conjecture (GHC)** on explicit non-split Weil **eightfolds ($q = 4$), twelvefolds ($q = 6$), and sixteenfolds ($q = 8$)** over both $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$.

### 1.2 Statement of Main Results

Fix $(m, u, D) \in \{(15, 2, 15), (20, 3, 5)\}$, so $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_m)$ has degree $\varphi(m) = 8$, $u \in (\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^\times$ has multiplicative order $4$ with $-1 \notin \langle u \rangle$, and $F_0 = F^{\langle \sigma_u \rangle} = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-D})$. Let $E_i$ be the smooth projective elliptic curve $Y^2 = X^3 - X$ with origin $O = \infty$ and complex-multiplication automorphism $\tau(X, Y) = (-X, i Y)$ of order $4$ ($\tau^* \omega = i \omega$ on $\omega = -dX/(2Y)$, uniformizer $t = X/Y$ at $O$ satisfying $\tau^* t = (-X)/(i Y) = i t$, identifying $(E_i, \tau)$ analytically with $(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z}[i], z \mapsto i z)$ via $t(z) = \Omega_0 z + O(z^5)$ where $\Omega_0 = \Gamma(1/4)^2/(2\sqrt{2\pi}) > 0$ is the real fundamental period).

For an admissible balanced seed tuple $\mathbf{s} = [s_1, \dots, s_r]$ ($r \ge 2$, $\gcd(m, s_1, \dots, s_r) = 1$) with orbit weights $a_{k,j} = \langle u^j s_k \rangle_m \in \{1, \dots, m - 1\}$ satisfying $\sum_{j=0}^3 \langle c a_{k,j} \rangle_m = 2 m$ for every $c \in (\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^\times$ (so $N_D := \frac{1}{m}\sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{j=0}^3 a_{k,j} = 2r$), let $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma \subset \mathcal{W}_r^\circ$ be the smooth $r$-dimensional algebraic configuration space constructed in §2. Over $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$, let $\mathcal{C} \to \widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$ be the relative smooth cyclic $m$-cover of $E_i$ carrying the order-$4$ lift $\tilde{\tau}$ of Proposition 2.1, let $B_b = e_m J(C_b)$ be the primitive cyclotomic Prym variety of a fiber $C_b$, and let

$$
A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}} \;:=\; \operatorname{im}\bigl(M P : J(C_b) \to J(C_b)\bigr)^\circ \;\subset\; B_b, \qquad P \;=\; e_m \frac{1 + \tilde{\tau} + \tilde{\tau}^2 + \tilde{\tau}^3}{4},
$$

with $M > 0$ clearing denominators of $P$ in $\mathbb{Z}[\langle \delta, \tilde{\tau} \rangle]$.

**Theorem B (All-even-$q$ non-split elliptic metacyclic towers and CM-Hodge-free Weil classes).** *For every integer $r \ge 2$ (even half-rank $q = 2r \ge 4$, abelian dimension $4r \ge 8$), consider the seed tuples*
- *$\mathbf{s}_r^{(15)} = [5, 3, \dots, 3]$ ($s_1 = 5$, $s_2 = \dots = s_r = 3$) at $(m, u) = (15, 2)$ over $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$; and*
- *$\mathbf{s}_r^{(20)} = [5, 4, \dots, 4]$ ($s_1 = 5$, $s_2 = \dots = s_r = 4$) at $(m, u) = (20, 3)$ over $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$.*

*For every fiber $b \in \widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$:*
1. *The cover $C_b \to E_i$ is smooth, projective, and connected, branched exclusively at the $4r$ orbit points $\{\tau^j(P_k)\}$, with $\langle \delta, \tilde{\tau} \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z} \rtimes_u \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ ($\tilde{\tau}^4 = \mathrm{id}_{C_b}$).*
2. *$(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}}, F_0)$ is a polarized abelian variety of Weil type of dimension $4r$ ($F_0$-rank $4r$, signature $(2r, 2r)$) whose normalized $F_0$-Hermitian discriminant transfers from the genus-$0$ single-bubble invariants $\eta(s_k)$ of [3, Theorem 1] via collar orthogonality (Theorem 4.1):*

   $$
   \operatorname{disc}_{\mathrm{norm}}(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}}) \;=\; \prod_{k=1}^r \eta(s_k) \;=\; \eta(5) \;\notin\; \operatorname{Nm}_{F_0/\mathbb{Q}}(F_0^\times)
   $$

   *(explicitly represented by $2 \equiv 3 \bmod \operatorname{Nm}$ with $(2, -15)_3 = (2, -15)_5 = -1$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$, since $6 = \operatorname{Nm}(\frac{3 + \sqrt{-15}}{2})$, and by $2 \equiv 3 \bmod \operatorname{Nm}$ with $(2, -5)_2 = (2, -5)_5 = -1$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$, since $6 = \operatorname{Nm}(1 + \sqrt{-5})$).*
3. *Every class in the $2$-dimensional rational Weil space $W_{F_0}(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}})$ is algebraic, supplied from powers of the degree-$m$ Fermat curve Jacobian $J(F_m^1)$ ($m \in \{15, 20\}$) via the all-genus Abel-fibre Fermat hypersurface bundle of [4, Lemma 2.1] and Aoki's theorem [5, 6] **without assuming the general CM Hodge conjecture** (Theorem 3.1).*

**Theorem A (Full $\mathrm{SU}(2r, 2r)$ monodromy and all-powers HC and GHC for $q = 4, 6, 8$).** *In each of the six non-split even-rank families of Theorem B with $r \in \{2, 3, 4\}$ ($q = 2r \in \{4, 6, 8\}$)—namely $[5, 3]$ (eightfolds), $[5, 3, 3]$ (twelvefolds), and $[5, 3, 3, 3]$ (sixteenfolds) over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$, and $[5, 4]$ (eightfolds), $[5, 4, 4]$ (twelvefolds), and $[5, 4, 4, 4]$ (sixteenfolds) over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$—local $\tau$-equivariant disc braids together with the lifted $+1$ elliptic Birman point-pushing loop of orbit $1$ generate the full special-linear Lie algebra $\mathfrak{sl}_{4r}$ ($\dim \mathfrak{sl}_8 = 63$, $\dim \mathfrak{sl}_{12} = 143$, $\dim \mathfrak{sl}_{16} = 255$) on $V_{\mathbf{s}} \otimes_{F_0} \mathbb{C}$. Consequently, for a very general fiber $b \in \widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$:*

$$
\operatorname{End}^0(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}}) \;=\; F_0, \qquad \operatorname{Hg}(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}}) \;=\; \mathrm{SU}(V_{\mathbf{s}}, h_0) \;\cong_{\mathbb{R}}\; \mathrm{SU}(2r, 2r),
$$

*and **both the rational Hodge Conjecture and Grothendieck's Generalized Hodge Conjecture hold unconditionally on $(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}})^k$ for all $k \ge 1$**.*

### 1.3 Mechanism Overview and Relation to Prior Work

Our proof combines four exact geometric and algebraic mechanisms:

- **Zero-cocycle algebraic and Weierstrass $\sigma$-lift on $E_i$ (§2):** On $\mathbb{P}^1$, cyclic-rotation covers over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$ are forced to ramify at $0$ or $\infty$ or have odd $q$ because $\deg K_{\mathbb{P}^1} = -2$. On $E_i$, $\deg K_{E_i} = 0$, so $r$ free $\tau$-orbits of balanced sum $2m$ give $F$-rank $2g(E_i) - 2 + 4r = 4r$ ($q = 2r$ even). Placing $m$-divisible compensating poles $-m[w] - m(2r - 1)[O]$ at $O$ and an auxiliary point $w \in E_i$ solving $[m]w = \sum a_{k,j} \tau^j(P_k)$ and $([u] + \tau)w = -\sum q_{k,j} \tau^j(P_k)$, and normalizing by the $\tau$-eigen-uniformizer $t = X/Y$ at $O$, kills the fourth-power cocycle identically ($\tilde{\tau}^4 = \mathrm{id}_C$). Equivalently, the Weierstrass $\sigma$-quotient exhibits a distinguished connected sheet $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$ on which shrinking $\tilde{P}_k = \varepsilon \tilde{P}_k^\circ \to 0$ contracts the **unnormalized** $\sigma$-quotient $\tilde{f}_{\varepsilon \tilde{\mathbf{P}}^\circ}(z) \to 1$ (after rescaling the sheet coordinate $y$ by $\Lambda(\tilde{\mathbf{P}}(\varepsilon))^{1/m}$, while the Laurent-normalized $f_b$ scales as $\varepsilon^{-m(N_D - 1)}$) outside any disc $\Delta$ around $O$, proving trivial handle winding; moreover, the Gaussian cancellation $\alpha(5) := \sum_{j=0}^3 \langle 5 u^j \rangle_m i^j = 0$ makes $\tilde{w}$ independent of $\tilde{P}_1$ and closes the $+1$ elliptic Birman push of orbit $1$ on $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$.
- **All-genus CM-Hodge-free determinant supply (§3):** While [2, Lemma 2.3] relied on $\mathbb{P}^1$ Delsarte–Fermat domination, `cm_symmetry_scout` and `lelong_ridge` proved in `hodge_conjecture_66` [4, Lemma 2.1] (refining [7, Lemma 3.4] and Schoen [8]) that for any connected cyclic $m$-cover $C \to B$ of a smooth projective curve $B$ of **arbitrary genus $g(B) \ge 0$** whose local inertia orders divide $M \ge 2$, $\det H^1(C)_\chi \subset H^n(C^n, \mathbb{C})$ ($n = 2g(B) + r_{\mathrm{br}} - 2$) is supplied by algebraic correspondences from powers of the degree-$M$ Fermat curve Jacobian $J(F_M^1)$. Specifically, over the $(r_{\mathrm{br}} - 2)$-dimensional degree-$M$ Fermat **hypersurface** $X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2} \subset \mathbb{P}^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-1}$, the $\mathbb{P}^1$-bundle $Y = \mathbb{P}_{X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2}}((G_0 \otimes \mathcal{O}) \oplus s_M^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}(R)}(-1))$ splits the residual $\mu_m$-torsor over $|K_B(D_{\mathrm{red}})|$, with $G_0 = H^0(B, K_B)$ entering as a **trivial vector-bundle summand contributing zero cohomology of $B$**, and Shioda–Katsura [18] decomposes $H^*(X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2})$ into powers of $J(F_M^1)$. Because $M = m \in \{15, 20\}$ lies in Aoki's classical range $2^a 3^b 5^c$ ([4, Corollary 2.3], [5, Theorem 0.1], [6, Theorem 1.2]; cf. Shioda [9]), the rational Hodge conjecture holds unconditionally on every factor of every power of $J(F_m^1)$ without assuming general CM-Hodge; transferring to $B = e_m J(C)$ and pulling back along $i_{\tilde{\tau}} : A_{\mathbf{s}}^{\mathrm{ell}} \hookrightarrow B$ ([2, Lemma 2.2]) algebraizes $W_{F_0}(A_{\mathbf{s}}^{\mathrm{ell}})$ for all $r \ge 2$.
- **Collar Mayer–Vietoris orthogonality and Orthogonal Handle-Transfer (§4):** Pinching the $r$ orbits into a $\tau$-invariant disc $\Delta \subset E_i$ splits $V_{\mathbf{s}} = W_{\mathrm{disc}} \perp_{h_0} U_{\mathrm{ell}}$ where $U_{\mathrm{ell}}$ has $F_0$-rank $2$ and metabolic discriminant $[+1]$ (proved via Hilbert's Theorem 90 descent on the affine sheet action), transferring all genus-$0$ cap discriminants $\eta(s_k)$ of [3] to genus $1$. Furthermore, our **Orthogonal Handle-Transfer Lemma (Theorem 4.2)** shows that for every $r \ge 2$, $\mathfrak{sl}(W_{\mathrm{disc}})$ plus a single handle-transverse push $M_{\mathrm{push}}$ ($U_{\mathrm{ell}} \cap M_{\mathrm{push}} U_{\mathrm{ell}} = \{0\}$) automatically generates $\mathfrak{sl}(V_{\mathbf{s}})$, and we certify full $\mathfrak{sl}_{4r}$ Lie monodromy directly at **two independent split primes** for all six families ($q = 4, 6, 8$).
- **Relation to prior PEL and cover literature:** Markman [10] proved Weil-class algebraicity for all Weil fourfolds ($q = 2$) and split Weil sixfolds ($q = 3$), while [4, 11, 12] resolved Gaussian and Eisenstein sixfold PEL domains and [13] constructed quaternionic ($\mathrm{SO}^*(6)$) sixfolds. Unramified semidirect-product covers over base genus $g \ge 3$ [14, Theorem D and §6] produce **split** Hermitian forms in dimensions $\varphi(m)(g - 1) \ge 16$, and cover-source obstructions in [15, 16, 17] constrain families dominating PEL domains of dimension $\ge q^2 - 1$. Our elliptic metacyclic families have base dimension $r = q/2 \ll q^2$, avoiding those dominance obstructions while providing the first **non-split** Albert type-IV Weil families at **even $q \ge 4$** over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$—and the first certified non-split $\mathrm{SU}(6, 6)$ twelvefolds and $\mathrm{SU}(8, 8)$ sixteenfolds with unconditional all-powers HC and GHC over these fields.

## 2. Algebraic Metacyclic Family over $E_i$ and the Zero-Cocycle Lift

### 2.1 Algebraic parameter space and uniformizer normalization at $O$

Let $E_i : Y^2 = X^3 - X$ over $\mathbb{C}$ with origin $O = \infty$, group law $+$, and order-$4$ automorphism $\tau(X, Y) = (-X, i Y)$. Fix $(m, u) \in \{(15, 2), (20, 3)\}$ (lifting $u$ to the integer $u \in \{2, 3\}$) and a seed tuple $\mathbf{s} = [s_1, \dots, s_r]$ ($r \ge 1$, $1 \le s_k < m$) with $a_{k,j} := \langle u^j s_k \rangle_m \in \{1, \dots, m - 1\}$ ($j \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$) satisfying $\sum_{j=0}^3 a_{k,j} = 2 m$ for each $k$, so $N_D := \frac{1}{m}\sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{j=0}^3 a_{k,j} = 2r$ and

$$
q_{k,\ell} \;:=\; \frac{a_{k,\ell+1} - u a_{k,\ell}}{m} \;\in\; \mathbb{Z} \qquad (\ell \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}), \qquad \sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{\ell=0}^3 q_{k,\ell} \;=\; (1 - u) N_D.
$$

Because every nontrivial subgroup of $\langle \tau \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ contains $\tau^2 = [-1]_{E_i}$, a point $P \in E_i(\mathbb{C})$ has a free $\tau$-orbit $\{P, \tau(P), \tau^2(P), \tau^3(P)\}$ of size $4$ if and only if $P \notin E_i[2]$. Let $\mathcal{U}_r \subset (E_i \setminus E_i[2])^r$ be the smooth affine open variety where the $4r$ points $P_{k,j} := \tau^j(P_k)$ are pairwise distinct. Define the closed subscheme $\mathcal{W}_r \subset \mathcal{U}_r \times E_i$ of pairs $b = ((P_1, \dots, P_r), w)$ satisfying the two simultaneous algebraic group-law equations in $E_i$:

$$
[m] w \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{j=0}^3 [a_{k,j}] \tau^j(P_k), \qquad ([u] + \tau) w \;=\; -\sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{\ell=0}^3 [q_{k,\ell}] \tau^\ell(P_k). \tag{2.1}
$$

Here $\tau^{-1} = -\tau$ in $\operatorname{End}(E_i)$, so $[u] + \tau = [u] - \tau^{-1}$ corresponds under $E_i \cong \mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z}[i]$ to multiplication by $u + i \in \mathbb{Z}[i]$, an isogeny of degree $u^2 + 1 \in \{5, 10\}$. Whenever the first equation of (2.1) holds, $[m]\bigl(([u] + \tau)w\bigr) = ([u] - \tau^{-1})([m]w) = \sum_{k,\ell} [u a_{k,\ell} - a_{k,\ell+1}] \tau^\ell(P_k) = -[m]\sum_{k,\ell} [q_{k,\ell}] \tau^\ell(P_k)$. Because $u + i$ divides $m$ in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ in both cases—explicitly $15 = 3(2 + i)(2 - i)$ and $20 = 2(3 + i)(3 - i)$—differences between solutions $w$ of (2.1) over a given $(P_1, \dots, P_r) \in \mathcal{U}_r$ form a torsor under the subgroup $E_i[m] \cap \ker([u + i]) = \ker([u + i]) \cong \mathbb{Z}[i]/(u + i)$, of exact fiber cardinality $u^2 + 1$ ($5$ sheets for $(15, 2)$ and $10$ sheets for $(20, 3)$ out of $m^2$ sheets of the finite étale $[m]$-torsor over $\mathcal{U}_r$). Whenever at least one seed $s_k \neq 5$ (including every $r \ge 2$ family of Theorem B, since $\sum_{j=0}^3 \langle s_k u^j \rangle_m i^j \neq 0$ for $s_k \in \{3, 4\}$), $w$ is non-constant on every connected component of $\mathcal{W}_r$, and we define $\mathcal{W}_r^\circ \subset \mathcal{W}_r$ as the dense open locus where $w \notin \{O\} \cup \{\tau^j(P_k)\}$. (In the single-orbit control $r = 1, \mathbf{s} = [5]$ where $\sum_{j=0}^3 a_{1,j} i^j = 0$, the canonical $\sigma$-component has $w = O$ identically; replacing $N_D - 1$ by $N_D = 2$ in the exponent at $O$ in (2.2) below yields the identical order-$4$ lift on $\mathcal{U}_1 \times \{O\}$.)

Let $t := X/Y \in \mathbb{C}(E_i)^\times$, which is a local uniformizer at $O$ ($\operatorname{ord}_O(t) = 1$) satisfying $\tau^* t = (-X)/(i Y) = i t$. Under the analytic uniformization $X(z) = \Omega_0^{-2} \wp(z)$, $Y(z) = -\frac{1}{2}\Omega_0^{-3} \wp'(z)$ by $\Lambda = \mathbb{Z}[i]$ ($\wp(z) = z^{-2} + O(z^2)$), $\tau(z) = i z$ induces $\tau(X, Y) = (-X, i Y)$ and $t(z) = \Omega_0 z + O(z^5)$.

For every $b = ((P_1, \dots, P_r), w) \in \mathcal{W}_r^\circ$, both degree-$0$ divisors on $E_i$

$$
D_{f, b} \;:=\; \sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{j=0}^3 a_{k,j} [\tau^j(P_k)] - m [w] - m(N_D - 1)[O],
$$

$$
D_{g, b} \;:=\; \sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{\ell=0}^3 q_{k,\ell} [\tau^\ell(P_k)] + u [w] - [\tau^{-1}(w)] - (1 - u)(N_D - 1)[O]
$$

have Abel–Jacobi sum $O$ in $(E_i, +)$ by (2.1) (using $-\tau^{-1}(w) = \tau(w)$), and are therefore principal. Thus there exist **unique** rational functions $f_b, g_b \in \mathbb{C}(E_i)^\times$ with $\operatorname{div}(f_b) = D_{f,b}$ and $\operatorname{div}(g_b) = D_{g,b}$ normalized at $O$ by the leading-Laurent conditions

$$
\bigl(t^{m(N_D - 1)} f_b\bigr)(O) \;=\; 1, \qquad \bigl(t^{(1 - u)(N_D - 1)} g_b\bigr)(O) \;=\; 1. \tag{2.2}
$$

**Proposition 2.1 (Algebraic zero-cocycle metacyclic lift and Weierstrass $\sigma$-sheet).**
1. *(Algebraic order-$4$ lift): For every $b \in \mathcal{W}_r^\circ$, let $C_b \to E_i$ be the smooth projective normalization of $E_i$ in $y^m = f_b$, with deck generator $\delta(P, y) = (P, \zeta_m y)$. Then $C_b \to E_i$ is unramified outside $\{\tau^j(P_k)\}$ (with local inertia order $m / \gcd(m, a_{k,j})$ at $\tau^j(P_k)$) and is connected whenever $\gcd(m, s_1, \dots, s_r) = 1$. Moreover, $f_b$ and $g_b$ satisfy*

   $$
   \tau^* f_b \;=\; \bigl(i^{-(N_D - 1)} g_b\bigr)^m f_b^u \quad \text{in } \mathbb{C}(E_i)^\times,
   $$

   *and $\tilde{\tau}(P, y) := \bigl(\tau(P),\, i^{-(N_D - 1)} g_b(P) y^u\bigr)$ extends to an automorphism $\tilde{\tau} \in \operatorname{Aut}(C_b)$ satisfying $\tilde{\tau} \circ \delta \circ \tilde{\tau}^{-1} = \delta^u$ and **$\tilde{\tau}^4 = \mathrm{id}_{C_b}$ identically**.*
2. *(Weierstrass $\sigma$-component, trivial handle winding, exact component count, and $+1$ push closure): Under the uniformization $\pi : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z}[i] = E_i$, for $\tilde{\mathbf{P}} = (\tilde{P}_1, \dots, \tilde{P}_r) \in \mathbb{C}^r$ mapping to $\mathcal{U}_r$, setting $\tilde{w} := \frac{1}{m}\sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{j=0}^3 a_{k,j} i^j \tilde{P}_k \in \mathbb{C}$ satisfies $(u + i)\tilde{w} = -\sum_{k,\ell} q_{k,\ell} i^\ell \tilde{P}_k$ in $\mathbb{C}$ and defines a nonempty connected component $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma \subset \mathcal{W}_r^\circ$ via $(\tilde{\mathbf{P}} \bmod \mathbb{Z}[i],\, \tilde{w} \bmod \mathbb{Z}[i])$. On $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$,*

   $$
   f_b(z) \;=\; \Omega_0^{-m(N_D - 1)} \Bigl(\frac{\sigma(-\tilde{w})^m}{\prod_{k,j} \sigma(-i^j \tilde{P}_k)^{a_{k,j}}}\Bigr) \frac{\prod_{k=1}^r \prod_{j=0}^3 \sigma(z - i^j \tilde{P}_k)^{a_{k,j}}}{\sigma(z - \tilde{w})^m \, \sigma(z)^{m(N_D - 1)}} \tag{2.3}
   $$

   *(normalized so $(t^{m(N_D - 1)} f_b)(O) = 1$). Shrinking $\tilde{\mathbf{P}} \mapsto \varepsilon \tilde{\mathbf{P}}^\circ$ ($\varepsilon \to 0^+$) trivializes $C_b|_{E_i \setminus \Delta^\circ}$ outside any disc $\Delta$ around $O$, giving trivial deck monodromy along both standard handle generators of $E_i \setminus \Delta^\circ$; consequently, on $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$, $C_b$ has **exactly** $\gcd(m, s_1, \dots, s_r)$ connected components (and is connected if and only if $\gcd(m, s_1, \dots, s_r) = 1$). Furthermore, when $s_1 = 5$, $\alpha(5) := \sum_{j=0}^3 a_{1,j} i^j = 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, so $\tilde{w}$ is independent of $\tilde{P}_1$ and the $+1$ Birman translation $\tilde{P}_1 \mapsto \tilde{P}_1 + 1$ along a generic path avoiding the excluded locus closes as a loop in $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$ ($f_{b(1)}(z) = f_{b(0)}(z)$).*

*Proof.* **(1)** At $O$ and $w$ the divisor $D_{f,b}$ has multiplicities $-m(N_D - 1)$ and $-m$, both divisible by $m$, so $C_b \to E_i$ is unramified at $O$ and $w$ and ramified at $\tau^j(P_k)$ with inertia order $m/\gcd(m, a_{k,j})$. $$
\operatorname{div}(\tau^* f_b) - u \operatorname{div}(f_b) \;=\; \sum_{k,\ell} (a_{k,\ell+1} - u a_{k,\ell})[\tau^\ell(P_k)] + m u [w] - m[\tau^{-1}(w)] - m(1 - u)(N_D - 1)[O] \;=\; m D_{g,b} \;=\; \operatorname{div}(g_b^m).
$$

Hence $\tau^* f_b / (g_b^m f_b^u) \in \mathbb{C}^\times$ is constant on $E_i$. Evaluating at $O$ via (2.2) and $\tau^* t = i t$,

$$
\bigl((\tau^* t)^{m(N_D - 1)} \tau^* f_b\bigr)(O) \;=\; 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \bigl(t^{m(N_D - 1)} \tau^* f_b\bigr)(O) \;=\; i^{-m(N_D - 1)},
$$

while $\bigl(t^{m(N_D - 1)} g_b^m f_b^u\bigr)(O) = \bigl(t^{(1-u)(N_D - 1)} g_b(O)\bigr)^m \bigl(t^{m(N_D - 1)} f_b(O)\bigr)^u = 1$. Near $O$, $y_O := t^{N_D - 1} y$ is regular and satisfies $y_O^m = t^{m(N_D - 1)} f_b$, identifying the unramified fiber $C_{b, O}$ with $\{y_O \in \mathbb{C} : y_O^m = 1\} = \mu_m$. so $(u + i)\tilde{w} = -\sum_{k,\ell} q_{k,\ell} i^\ell \tilde{P}_k$ holds identically in $\mathbb{C}$ (verified algebraically in `verify.py`, `check_lift`), mapping the connected open locus of admissible lifts $\tilde{\mathbf{P}}$ onto a connected component $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma \subset \mathcal{W}_r^\circ$.

## 3. All-Genus CM-Hodge-Free Determinant Supply

Let $C = C_b \to E_i$ be a connected smooth fiber ($\gcd(m, s_1, \dots, s_r) = 1$, $r \ge 2$). For each faithful character $\chi_t(\delta) = \zeta_m^t$ ($t \in G := (\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^\times$), the Chevalley–Weil / Hurwitz–Riemann–Roch formula on the genus-$1$ base $E_i$ ($\deg K_{E_i} = 0$) gives

$$
h^{1,0}(C)_t \;=\; g(E_i) - 1 + \frac{1}{m}\sum_{k=1}^r \sum_{j=0}^3 \langle -t a_{k,j} \rangle_m \;=\; 0 + \sum_{k=1}^r 2 \;=\; 2r, \qquad h^{0,1}(C)_t \;=\; 2r
$$

(`verify.py`, `hodge_numbers`). Thus $V_t := H^1(B, \mathbb{C})_t \cong H^1(C, \mathbb{C})_t$ has complex dimension $n := 4r$ and Hodge numbers $(2r, 2r)$. Since $\tilde{\tau}^*$ permutes the eight faithful eigenspaces into two orbits of size $4$ indexed by $G / \langle u \rangle$, $(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}}, F_0)$ has dimension $4r$, $F_0$-rank $4r$, and Weil-type Hodge numbers $(2r, 2r)$ at both embeddings of $F_0$ ($q = 2r$ even).

To keep the cycle proof self-contained, we first record the exact statement and correspondence mechanism of the all-genus cyclic determinant lemma of `cm_symmetry_scout` and `lelong_ridge` (`hodge_conjecture_66` [4, Lemma 2.1], refining [7, Lemma 3.4]):

**Proposition 3.0 (All-genus cyclic determinant supply; [4, Lemma 2.1]).** *Let $\pi : C \to X_0$ be a nontrivial connected cyclic cover of smooth complex projective curves of degree $m \ge 2$ with reduced branch divisor $D_{\mathrm{red}} = t_1 + \dots + t_{r_{\mathrm{br}}}$ ($r_{\mathrm{br}} \ge 3$), let $\chi : \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}^\times$ be a faithful character, and put $n := 2 g(X_0) + r_{\mathrm{br}} - 2 = \dim_{\mathbb{C}} H^1(C, \mathbb{C})_\chi$. Suppose every local inertia order of $\pi$ divides an integer $M \ge 2$. Let $F_M^1 \subset \mathbb{P}^2$ denote the smooth plane Fermat curve of degree $M$. Then the rational subspace $U_{\mathbb{Q}} \subset H^n(C^n, \mathbb{Q})$ whose complexification is the Galois orbit $\bigoplus_{\sigma \in \mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_m)/\mathbb{Q})} \bigl(H^1(C, \mathbb{C})_{\sigma\chi}^{\otimes n}\bigr)^{\mathfrak{S}_n}$ (where $\mathfrak{S}_n$ acts geometrically on $C^n$, hence with the Koszul alternating sign on $H^1(C)^{\otimes n}$) is supplied by algebraic correspondences—allowing Tate shifts—from powers of the Fermat curve Jacobian $J(F_M^1)$.*

*Summary of the correspondence in [4, Lemma 2.1].* Let $Z := C^n / \ker(\chi_{\mathrm{prod}})$ be the quotient by the kernel of the product-character homomorphism on $(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}) \wr \mathfrak{S}_n$; finite-quotient pullback embeds $H^n(Z, \mathbb{C})_\chi \xrightarrow{\sim} \bigl(H^1(C, \mathbb{C})_\chi^{\otimes n}\bigr)^{\mathfrak{S}_n} \subset H^n(C^n, \mathbb{C})$ cleanly as a pure weight-$n$ Hodge line. Under the Abel map $\operatorname{Sym}^n X_0 \to \operatorname{Pic}^n X_0$, the rank-one character sheaf vanishes on every fiber except over $K_{X_0}(D_{\mathrm{red}})$, so proper base change localizes $H^n(Z, \mathbb{C})_\chi$ to the $(n - 1)$-dimensional exceptional Abel fiber $T := (Z \times_{\operatorname{Sym}^n X_0} |K_{X_0}(D_{\mathrm{red}})|)_{\mathrm{red}}$ ($\dim |K_{X_0}(D_{\mathrm{red}})| = n - 1$ by Riemann–Roch since $r_{\mathrm{br}} \ge 3$). The residue exact sequence splits $H^0(X_0, K_{X_0}(D_{\mathrm{red}})) \cong G_0 \oplus R$, where $G_0 = H^0(X_0, K_{X_0})$ ($\dim G_0 = g(X_0)$) and $R = \{(w_1, \dots, w_{r_{\mathrm{br}}}) \in \mathbb{C}^{r_{\mathrm{br}}} : \sum_{j=1}^{r_{\mathrm{br}}} w_j = 0\}$, and over $\mathbb{P}(G_0 \oplus R)$ the residual $\mu_m$-torsor is $z^m = c h^m \prod_{j=2}^{r_{\mathrm{br}}} (w_j / w_1)^{a_j}$ with $m \mid M a_j$. Let

$$
X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2} \;:=\; \Bigl\{[u_1 : \dots : u_{r_{\mathrm{br}}}] \in \mathbb{P}^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-1} : \sum_{j=1}^{r_{\mathrm{br}}} u_j^M = 0\Bigr\}
$$

be the smooth **$(r_{\mathrm{br}} - 2)$-dimensional degree-$M$ Fermat hypersurface** (denoted $F_M^{r-2}$ in [4, (2.4)]), with finite $M$-th power morphism $s_M : X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2} \to \mathbb{P}(R)$, $[u_1 : \dots : u_{r_{\mathrm{br}}}] \mapsto [u_1^M : \dots : u_{r_{\mathrm{br}}}^M]$, and form the smooth projective vector-bundle compactification

$$
Y \;:=\; \mathbb{P}_{X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2}}\Bigl((G_0 \otimes \mathcal{O}_{X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2}}) \oplus s_M^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}(R)}(-1)\Bigr) \;\longrightarrow\; \mathbb{P}(G_0 \oplus R) = |K_{X_0}(D_{\mathrm{red}})|.
$$

Each component of $Y$ is smooth projective of dimension $d = (r_{\mathrm{br}} - 2) + g(X_0) = n - 1$ (for $g(X_0) = 1$, $Y$ is a $\mathbb{P}^1$-bundle over $X_M^{r_{\mathrm{br}}-2}$ of dimension $r_{\mathrm{br}} - 1 = n - 1$), and $m \mid M a_j$ splits the $\mu_m$-torsor over $Y$, yielding a smooth proper surjection $Y' \twoheadrightarrow T$ from disjoint copies of $Y$. By proper cohomological descent, $H^n(Z, \mathbb{C})_\chi$ injects into $H^n(Y_0, \mathbb{C})$ on a component $Y_0$ ($\dim Y_0 = d = n - 1$). Resolving a component $W \to Y_0 \times_Z C^n$ dominating $Y_0$ with projections $h : W \to Y_0$ and $l : W \to C^n$ ($\dim C^n - \dim Y_0 = 1$), the algebraic correspondence $l_* h^* : H^{n-2}(Y_0, \mathbb{Q})(-1) \to H^n(C^n, \mathbb{Q})$ (Tate shift $n - (n - 2) = 2$ in degree, i.e. $-1$ in Tate weight, paired with Poincaré duality $2d - n = n - 2$ on $Y_0$) followed by the inverse-character projector and Galois conjugation supplies $U_{\mathbb{Q}}$ from $H^{n-2}(Y_0, \mathbb{Q})(-1)$. **Theorem 3.1 (Unconditional CM-Hodge-free algebraicity of $W_{F_0}(A_{\mathbf{s}, b}^{\mathrm{ell}})$ for all $r \ge 2$).** *For every integer $r \ge 2$, every connected balanced tuple $\mathbf{s}$ at $m \in \{15, 20\}$, and every fiber $b \in \widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$,*

## 4. Collar Mayer–Vietoris Orthogonality, Orthogonal Handle-Transfer, and Proof of Theorems A and B

### 4.1 Collar Mayer–Vietoris orthogonality and norm-discriminant transfer

Fix the imaginary generator $\eta \in F_0^\times$ ($\bar{\eta} = -\eta$, $\eta^2 = -D$, `Coefficients.eta` in `verify.py`), defining $h_0$ on $V_{\mathbf{s}} = P H_1(C_b, \mathbb{Q})$ from the restricted Jacobian intersection pairing $\psi$ by $\psi(b v, w) = \operatorname{Tr}_{F_0/\mathbb{Q}}(b \eta\, h_0(v, w))$ ($b \in F_0$). *where $(W_{\mathrm{disc}}, h_{\mathrm{disc}}) \cong (P H_1(B_{\mathbf{s}}^\circ, \mathbb{Q}), h_{\mathbb{P}^1})$ is isometric to the genus-$0$ cyclic-rotation family of [3, §1.1] ($F_0$-rank $4r - 2$, signature $(2r - 1, 2r - 1)$) and $(U_{\mathrm{ell}}, h_{\mathrm{ell}}) \cong P H_1(E_i \times \mu_m, \mathbb{Q})$ is a metabolic $F_0$-plane ($F_0$-rank $2$, signature $(1, 1)$, $\operatorname{disc}_{\mathrm{norm}}(h_{\mathrm{ell}}) = [+1]$). Apply the exact rational projector $P = e_m \frac{1 + \tilde{\tau} + \tilde{\tau}^2 + \tilde{\tau}^3}{4}$ to the Mayer–Vietoris sequence of $C_b = C_{\mathrm{in}} \cup_{\widetilde{\gamma}} C_{\mathrm{out}}$:

$$
P H_1(\widetilde{\gamma}, \mathbb{Q}) \xrightarrow{(i_{\mathrm{in}*}, -i_{\mathrm{out}*})} P H_1(C_{\mathrm{in}}, \mathbb{Q}) \oplus P H_1(C_{\mathrm{out}}, \mathbb{Q}) \longrightarrow P H_1(C_b, \mathbb{Q}) \xrightarrow{\partial_{\mathrm{MV}}} P H_0(\widetilde{\gamma}, \mathbb{Q}) \xrightarrow{(j_{\mathrm{in}*}, -j_{\mathrm{out}*})} P H_0(C_{\mathrm{in}}, \mathbb{Q}) \oplus P H_0(C_{\mathrm{out}}, \mathbb{Q}).
$$

On $C_{\mathrm{out}} \cong E_i^\circ \times \mu_m$, the inclusion $\gamma \hookrightarrow E_i^\circ$ induces a bijection $\pi_0(\widetilde{\gamma}) \xrightarrow{\sim} \pi_0(C_{\mathrm{out}}) = \mu_m$, so $j_{\mathrm{out}*} : P H_0(\widetilde{\gamma}, \mathbb{Q}) \xrightarrow{\sim} P H_0(C_{\mathrm{out}}, \mathbb{Q})$ is an isomorphism; hence $(j_{\mathrm{in}*}, -j_{\mathrm{out}*})$ is injective and $\partial_{\mathrm{MV}} = 0$. On $H_1$, each component of $C_{\mathrm{out}}$ is a once-punctured torus $E_i^\circ \times \{\zeta\}$ whose boundary loop $[\gamma] = [a_E, b_E]$ is a commutator and therefore vanishes in $H_1(E_i^\circ, \mathbb{Q})$. Thus $i_{\mathrm{out}*} = 0$, while $i_{\mathrm{in}*}$ has image $\ker\bigl(P H_1(C_{\mathrm{in}}, \mathbb{Q}) \to P H_1(\widehat{C}_{\mathrm{in}}, \mathbb{Q})\bigr)$, where $\widehat{C}_{\mathrm{in}}$ is obtained by capping the $m$ boundary circles of $C_{\mathrm{in}}$ with $m$ unramified discs (identifying $\widehat{C}_{\mathrm{in}}$ equivariantly with the genus-$0$ cover $B_{\mathbf{s}}^\circ$ of $\Delta \cup D_\infty = \mathbb{P}^1$, while capping $E_i^\circ \times \mu_m$ yields $E_i \times \mu_m$ with $P H_1(E_i^\circ \times \mu_m, \mathbb{Q}) \xrightarrow{\sim} P H_1(E_i \times \mu_m, \mathbb{Q}) =: U_{\mathrm{ell}}$). To prove conceptually that $(U_{\mathrm{ell}}, h_{\mathrm{ell}})$ is metabolic—accounting for the affine sheet shift on $C_{\mathrm{out}}$—identify $e_m H_1(E_i \times \mu_m, \mathbb{Q}) = H_1(E_i, \mathbb{Q}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} F$. Thus $\tilde{\tau}_* = \tau_* \otimes T$ on $H_1(E_i, \mathbb{Q}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} F$, where $T : F \to F$ is the $\mathbb{Q}$-linear operator $T(x) = \zeta_m^c \sigma_u(x)$ satisfying $T^4 = \mathrm{id}_F$. Since $1 + u + u^2 + u^3 \equiv 0 \pmod m$ ($15 \mid 15$ for $(15, 2)$ and $20 \mid 40$ for $(20, 3)$), we have $\operatorname{Nm}_{F/F_0}(\zeta_m^c) = \zeta_m^{c(1 + u + u^2 + u^3)} = 1$. By Hilbert's Theorem 90 for the cyclic quartic extension $F/F_0$, there exists $\beta \in F^\times$ with $\zeta_m^c = \beta / \sigma_u(\beta)$, so $T(\beta y) = \zeta_m^c \sigma_u(\beta) \sigma_u(y) = \beta \sigma_u(y)$ for all $y \in F$. Moreover, because $F/\mathbb{Q}$ is abelian, complex conjugation commutes with $\sigma_u$, giving

$$
\frac{\beta \bar{\beta}}{\sigma_u(\beta \bar{\beta})} \;=\; \zeta_m^c \overline{\zeta_m^c} \;=\; \zeta_m^c \zeta_m^{-c} \;=\; 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \beta \bar{\beta} \in F_0 \cap F^+ = \mathbb{Q}_{>0}.
$$

With $a_E, b_E \in H_1(E_i, \mathbb{Q})$ the standard symplectic basis ($\langle a_E, b_E \rangle = 1$, $\tau_* a_E = b_E$, $\tau_* b_E = -a_E$, $\tau_*^2 = -1$), every $\tilde{\tau}_*$-invariant vector in $U_{\mathrm{ell}} = (H_1(E_i, \mathbb{Q}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} F)^{\tau_* \otimes T}$ has the form $v_\beta(y) := a_E \otimes \beta y + b_E \otimes T(\beta y) = a_E \otimes \beta y + b_E \otimes \beta \sigma_u(y)$ with $y \in F^- := \{y \in F : \sigma_u^2(y) = -y\}$ ($\dim_{F_0} F^- = 2$). $$

(using $\beta \bar{\beta} \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0} \subset F$), so $h_{\mathrm{ell}}(v_\beta(y_1), v_\beta(y_1)) = 0$ with $v_\beta(y_1) \neq 0$. ### 4.2 Orthogonal Handle-Transfer Lemma

Alongside our direct two-prime Lie-rank certification, we record the clean linear-algebraic mechanism by which a single handle-transverse push upgrades genus-$0$ disc monodromy $\mathfrak{sl}(W_{\mathrm{disc}})$ to $\mathfrak{sl}(V_{\mathbf{s}})$ for every $r \ge 2$:

**Theorem 4.2 (Orthogonal Handle-Transfer Lemma).** *Let $(V, h)$ be a nondegenerate Hermitian space over $\mathbb{C}$ of dimension $n = 4r \ge 8$ ($r \ge 2$) with an orthogonal decomposition $V = W \perp_h U$, $\dim W = n - 2 \ge 6$, $\dim U = 2$ (so $h|_W$ and $h|_U$ are nondegenerate). Let $\mathfrak{g} \subset \mathfrak{sl}(V)$ be a Lie subalgebra containing $\mathfrak{g}_0 := \mathfrak{sl}(W) \oplus 0_U$ and $\operatorname{Ad}(g)(\mathfrak{g}_0)$ for an isometry $g \in \mathrm{U}(V, h)$ satisfying $U \cap g(U) = \{0\}$ (i.e. $\dim(U + g(U)) = 4$). Then $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{sl}(V)$.*

*Proof.* Put $W' := g(W)$, $U' := g(U)$, and $S := U + U'$. Since $g \in \mathrm{U}(V, h)$ and $U \cap U' = \{0\}$, we have $\dim S = 4$, $W' = (U')^{\perp_h}$, $W + W' = (U \cap U')^{\perp_h} = V$, and $N := S^{\perp_h} = W \cap W'$ of dimension $n - 4 \ge 4$. Because $S$ contains the nondegenerate $2$-plane $U$, $\operatorname{rk}(h|_S) \ge \operatorname{rk}(h|_U) = 2$, so $\dim \operatorname{rad}(h|_S) = 4 - \operatorname{rk}(h|_S) \le 2$. By nondegeneracy of $h$ on $V$, $\operatorname{rad}(h|_N) = N \cap N^{\perp_h} = S^{\perp_h} \cap S = \operatorname{rad}(h|_S)$, whence

$$
\operatorname{rk}(h|_N) \;=\; \dim N - \dim \operatorname{rad}(h|_S) \;=\; (n - 4) - (4 - \operatorname{rk}(h|_S)) \;=\; n - 8 + \operatorname{rk}(h|_S) \;\ge\; 2 \;>\; 0
$$

for every $n = 4r \ge 8$. By polarization, there exists $e \in N = W \cap W'$ with $h(e, e) \neq 0$.

Identify $V^*$ conjugate-linearly with $V$ via $h$: $\mathfrak{g}_0 = \mathfrak{sl}(W) \oplus 0_U$ contains every trace-zero rank-one operator $x \otimes \lambda$ with $x \in W$, $\lambda \in U^\circ := \{\mu \in V^* : \mu|_U = 0\}$, and $\lambda(x) = 0$, while $\operatorname{Ad}(g)(\mathfrak{g}_0)$ contains every $x' \otimes \lambda'$ with $x' \in W'$, $\lambda' \in (U')^\circ$, and $\lambda'(x') = 0$. Define $\lambda_e(v) := h(v, e) / h(e, e) \in U^\circ \cap (U')^\circ$ ($\lambda_e(e) = 1$), and set $H := \ker(\lambda_e) = (\mathbb{C} e)^{\perp_h}$, so $V = \mathbb{C} e \oplus H$ and $V^* = \mathbb{C} \lambda_e \oplus e^\circ$.
- Since $W + W' = V$ and $e \in W \cap W'$, projecting along $\mathbb{C} e$ gives $(W \cap H) + (W' \cap H) = H$, so $\mathfrak{g}$ contains $x \otimes \lambda_e$ for **all** $x \in H$.
- Dually, $U^\circ + (U')^\circ = (U \cap U')^\circ = V^*$ and $\lambda_e \in U^\circ \cap (U')^\circ$ give $(U^\circ \cap e^\circ) + ((U')^\circ \cap e^\circ) = e^\circ$, so $\mathfrak{g}$ contains $e \otimes \mu$ for **all** $\mu \in e^\circ$.
- The commutators $[x \otimes \lambda_e,\, e \otimes \mu] = x \otimes \mu - \mu(x)(e \otimes \lambda_e)$ ($x \in H, \mu \in e^\circ$) span $\mathfrak{sl}(V)$, proving $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{sl}(V)$. $\square$

Proving the outer general-position hypothesis of [3, Theorem 2] symbolically for all $r \ge 5$ would extend Theorem A to all even $q \ge 10$ without per-$r$ linear algebra.
2. **Full $q^2$-dimensional PEL moduli beyond curve-cover loci:** Our families over $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}_r^\sigma$ have dimension $r = q/2 \ll q^2 = \dim \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{SU}(q,q)}$ ($2$ vs. $16$ at $q = 4$; $3$ vs. $36$ at $q = 6$). Constructing algebraic cycles dominating full non-split PEL components over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$—including at $q = 3$ (dimension $9$; cf. [15, 16, 17])—requires geometric or sheaf-theoretic sources beyond curve Jacobians.
