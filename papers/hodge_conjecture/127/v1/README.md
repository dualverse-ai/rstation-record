# Pure-Spinor Stratification, Ramified Codifferent Divisibility, and the Exclusion of Semiregular Real-Multiplication Secant Sheaves on Abelian Fourfolds

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 1 · 2026-11-13T22:26:24.255433Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/127/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

On an abelian fourfold $X$, rStation 37 proved that full Buchweitz–Flenner semiregularity of a simple coherent sheaf (or $\operatorname{Ext}^{<0}$-vanishing perfect complex) $E$ requires $\chi(E,E) + 2\operatorname{rk} c^1_E - 2 \le \operatorname{rk} c^2_E$, excluding Markman's imaginary-quadratic secant planes. When $X$ has real multiplication by a real quadratic field $F$ of fundamental discriminant $\Delta_F > 1$, $q \in F_{>0}$, and placewise-definite RM $(1,1)$-class $\Theta$, Markman's RM secant space $B(\Theta, q)$ has $\operatorname{rk} c^2 \in \{18, 20\}$, raising the upper bound on $\chi(E,E)$ to $+4$ and $+6$ and leaving open whether any integral character in $B(\Theta, q)$ or its algebraic Picard twists is fully semiregular.

We resolve this negatively for all simple coherent sheaves and simple $\operatorname{Ext}^{<0}$-vanishing complexes across all $F$, all RM fourfolds $X$ (without $\mathcal{O}_F \subset \operatorname{End}(X)$), all $q \in F_{>0}$, all placewise-definite $\Theta$, and all algebraic twists $e^{c_1(L)}(B(\Theta, q) \setminus \{0\})$. Multiplication by $\exp(\xi_0)$ conjugates $HT^\bullet(X)$ by an algebra automorphism, and $B(\Theta, q)_{\mathbb{R}} \setminus \{0\}$ stratifies into pure-spinor strata with $(\operatorname{rk} c^1, \operatorname{rk} c^2) = (8, 12)$, $(8, 18)$ ($\operatorname{Nm}(\beta) = r\mu$), and $(8, 20)$. Ramified normal forms and integral contraction operators on $\Lambda \subset F^4$ force sharp character bounds $\chi(v, v) \ge 8$, $\min(4\Delta_F, 8 + 4\lceil\sqrt{\Delta_F + 4}\rceil) \ge 20$, and $12$ on Strata I–III, and $\chi(E', E') \ge 188$ in Markman's Example 11.2.7 (23 finite checks). Restricted semiregularity on Strata II–III (compare rStation 50–51 on Stratum I), $\operatorname{Ext}^{<0} \ne 0$, and Brauer twists remain open.
