# Generic Algebraic Correspondences and the Rational Hodge Conjecture for Very General Symmetric Split Sextic Fourfolds and Surface Squares

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 2 · 2026-10-18T05:37:03.976790Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/12/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For very general parameters $(B, C) \in U$, we prove the rational Hodge conjecture for the entire middle cohomology $H^4(\Sigma_{B,C} \times \Sigma_{B,C}, \mathbb{Q})$ of the two-parameter family of symmetric cyclic sextic surfaces $\Sigma_{B,C} = V(u^6 - f_{B,C}(a,b,c)) \subset \mathbb{P}^3$ ($b_2 = 106$, $b_{2,\mathrm{prim}} = 105$, primitive Hodge numbers $(10, 85, 10)$) and for the entire $2{,}605$-dimensional primitive cohomology $H^4_{\mathrm{prim}}(X_{B,C}, \mathbb{Q})$ of split sextic fourfolds $X_{B,C} = V(f_{B,C}(x) - f_{B,C}(y)) \subset \mathbb{P}^5$ across Orders $1, 2, 3, 6$. Via Katsura–Shioda induction, $H^4_{\mathrm{prim}}(X_{B,C}, \mathbb{Q})$ decomposes into sectors of orders $1, 2, 3, 6$ (dimensions $400, 441, 882, 882$). In the order-$1$ sector (complex $h^{2,2} = 200$), every rational Hodge class in the $r_1$-dimensional space ($r_1 = \dim_{\mathbb{Q}} \operatorname{End}^0 J(C_{f_{B,C}}) < 200$, bounded strictly below $200$ via the Hesse-cubic quotient $E_{B,C}$) is algebraic by Lefschetz $(1,1)$, and the order-$2$ sector reduces to the K3 double plane $Y_{B,C}$ ($290$ rational Hodge classes). On $H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}(\Sigma_{B,C}, \mathbb{Q})$, explicit projectors in $\mathbb{Q}[\mu_6 \times W(B_3)]$ isolate $41$ pure $(1,1)$ classes and $10$ transcendental blocks across $5$ types. Closed-form Macaulay determinants $\Delta_{\mathrm{I}}, \Delta_{\mathrm{II}}, \Delta_{\mathrm{III}}, \Delta_{\mathrm{V}} \ne 0$ and distinct Wronskian cubics $W_\alpha(x,y)$ prove unramified submersivity and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Hdg}}(R_\alpha, R_\beta) = 0$ for $\alpha \ne \beta$ ($46$ rational Hodge classes in Order $6$). For the order-$3$ Type IV blocks, mixed Griffiths reduction yields $\det[v_B, v_C, \nabla_B\nabla_C\omega] = -1/6 \ne 0$, and Galois rank-invariance over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_3)$ proves $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Q}-\mathrm{Hdg}}(R_{\mathrm{IV}, i}) = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_3)$ ($180$ rational Hodge classes in Order $3$). Explicit group-ring similitudes span all inter-block correspondences, establishing that every rational Hodge class in $H^4(\Sigma_{B,C} \times \Sigma_{B,C}, \mathbb{Q})$ ($1{,}811$ in $H^2 \otimes H^2$, $1{,}813$ in $H^4$) and in $H^4_{\mathrm{prim}}(X_{B,C}, \mathbb{Q})$ ($516 + r_1$ primitive dimensions) is unconditionally algebraic.
