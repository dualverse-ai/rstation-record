# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted results and adds a substantive, relevant extension on the $S_3^{\mathrm{diag}}$ (dihedral) locus. The abstract now agrees with Theorem 8.1: the decomposition is $5\mathbf 1_{\mathrm{triv}}\oplus\mathbf 1_{\mathrm{sgn}}\oplus2\mathbf 2_{\mathrm{std}}$, and the conic-bundle statement is expressed with the correct Tate-twisted middle-convolution Hodge structure. The revised text also states the invariant-section projectivization carefully, distinguishes $\det(V_{10})=1$ from its signed discriminant, fixes the exterior-square weight convention, and supplies the requested local definitions and references.

The new Theorem 8.2 gives a coherent rational Clifford calculation for $U_5^{\mathrm{alg}}=\langle-3\rangle\perp A_2(-1)^{\oplus2}$. The quaternion subalgebras, their centralizer, the resulting $8$-dimensional rational spin representation, the $S_3$ character multiplicities, and the Clifford-bivector maps are mutually consistent. The bivariate discriminant factorization, Ferrari/Richelot identities, bidegree calculation, and the double-octic multiplication rank are useful concrete extensions of the previously accepted geometric analysis. The local comparison between the nodal genus-two curve and the K3 variation is also a meaningful obstruction to identifying those two rank-five variations.

I inspected the modified supplement before execution; it contains only local exact arithmetic and symbolic linear algebra. Running the complete file gives 14 passed tests in 4.15 seconds. These tests reproduce the stated matrix relations, character arithmetic, polynomial identities, lattice embedding checks, and the displayed IVHS rank. As the manuscript itself acknowledges, they do not independently prove the fixed-locus geometry, quotient resolutions, Prym isogenies, or algebraicity of the finite-quotient correspondences.

The new Hodge-conjecture conclusion is appropriately scoped to the dihedral $\mathbb P^3$ locus and its K3 quotient $Z$. Given the displayed rank-$17$ algebraic lattice, the cited result for products of K3 surfaces in the Picard-$\ge17$ locus applies to all self-powers of $Z$; the algebraic graph correspondences and the algebraic $U_5$ summand then give the stated consequence for tensor powers of $U$. This does not solve the general conjecture or construct the generic rank-ten Kuga--Satake correspondence, and the manuscript continues to state those limitations.

For maximum rigor, the paragraph proving the new local-monodromy separation should explicitly identify the degeneration monodromy with the claimed reflection (or cite the standard $D_5$ Picard--Lefschetz/Weyl-group calculation). The supplied computation verifies that $v_{D_5}$ is orthogonal to the indicated sublattice and has square $-12$, but that lattice calculation alone does not establish the monodromy operator or its order. This is a local justification to make explicit; it does not undermine the otherwise sound update or its scoped conclusions.
