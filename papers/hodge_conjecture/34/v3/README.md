# Double Covers of Odd-Polarized Abelian Surfaces: Multiplicity-vs.-Isotypic Rank Laws, Clifford-Spinor Structure, Parity-Selection Pryms, and Product-Locus Quotients

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 3 · 2026-10-22T15:56:11.854651Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/34/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

A central problem for K3-type Hodge structures is to determine when the anti-invariant sub-Hodge structure U in H^2_-(X, Q) of a double cover X -> B of a (1, d)-polarized abelian surface, branched along a smooth symmetric divisor D in |2L|_+, is realized by algebraic cycles on abelian varieties, and what monodromy obstructions govern the rank-ten (1, 3) seed V_10. First, we prove odd-d character-orthogonality laws separating multiplicity-space and isotypic ranks under semidirect symmetries, showing why (1, 3) is the minimal odd polarization yielding a K3-type piece of rank at most 10 in the tau = +1 eigenspace. Second, for V_10 and its Kummer double cover, we prove a Fock-space D_5 spinor plethysm and Kulikov nilpotent criterion, a gonality dichotomy on D_bar = D/tau (gonality 5 generically via the 32-codeword Kummer code versus 2 on the product locus), and a Universal k-Tuple Quotient Theorem for D^k/W(D_k). Third, we prove a single-reflection fixed-part criterion and the cubic nodal blow-up law showing that the Gauss parity-selection Prym has non-unipotent stable reduction across nodal degenerations, whereas the conic-bundle Prym A_12 satisfies H^1(A_12, Q) = MC_{-1/2}(U)(1). Fourth, on the product and A_2-polarized E^2 loci, we prove the Klein splitting U = 7 + 3, dihedral enhancement to P^3 with U = 5*1_triv + 1_sgn + 2*2_std, Cl^+(W_Z) = M_4(Q) versus Cl^+(U_5^alg) = M_2((-1,-3)_Q), rational dihedral spinor branching bridged over Q by algebraic Clifford bivectors, interior local monodromy separation along P_cusp = 0, the bidegree-(6, 7)/(6, 6) fibration hierarchy on S, and the unconditional rational Hodge Conjecture in all degrees for all self-powers Z^k and tensor powers U^k on P^3. Constructing a generic rank-ten Kuga-Satake correspondence remains open.
