# Double Covers of Odd-Polarized Abelian Surfaces: Multiplicity-vs.-Isotypic Rank Laws, Clifford-Spinor Structure, Parity-Selection Pryms, and Product-Locus Quotients

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 1 · 2026-10-21T15:57:37.891735Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/34/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study K3-type anti-invariant Hodge structures U subset H^2_-(X, Q) on double covers X -> B of (1, d)-polarized abelian surfaces branched along smooth symmetric divisors D in |2L|_+. First, we prove that the mod-2 Weil pairing on B[2] is nondegenerate if and only if d is odd (fixing an even symmetric characteristic of Arf invariant 0 gives the 10-even / 6-odd split and Tr(tau | H^2_-(X)) = -4), and establish the odd-d multiplicity-vs.-isotypic rank-to-h^{2,0} laws dim M_rho = 8 h^{2,0}(M_rho) + 2 c_rho and dim U_rho = 4 h^{2,0}(U_rho) + (2 d d_rho^2)/|N| for G = N rtimes <tau>. Second, for the (1, 3) seed V_{10}, we prove the fixed-B period rank lower bound >= 3, the Cardano cube-norm 3-torsion identity on D_s, and explicit Fock-space D_5 spinor plethysm and Kulikov nilpotent matrices. Third, on the Kummer double cover Y_tilde -> K, we prove the gonality dichotomy between NS(B) = Z[L] (gonality 5 via the 32-codeword Kummer code) and the Humbert product locus B = E_1 x E_2 (where w^2 = A(t)F(t)Q(t) has gonality 2), alongside the Universal k-Tuple Quotient Theorem on D^k/W(D_k). Fourth, we prove the single-reflection fixed-part monodromy criterion, the cubic nodal blow-up attachment law for Gauss stable reduction, and the equivariant pencil Leray / middle-convolution calculation H^1(A_{12, s}, Q) =~ MC_{-1/2}(U)_s(1) on Z_s. Finally, we prove the Humbert Klein four-group splitting U = 7 oplus 3 and the A_2-polarized E^2 S_3-decomposition with iota_u-anti-invariant class D_{17}^- = P_+ - P_-, Shioda projection sigma(P_+) = D_{17}^- - 2F (h(P_+) = 4), degree-12 pullback similarity U^{S_3^{diag}} =~ W_Z(12) =~_Q U_{hyp}^2 oplus <-3>, and unconditional fixed-E period rank 3 on |2L|_+^{S_3^{diag}} =~ P^3.
