# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds useful structural information on the permutation-invariant locus. The corrections to half-spin parity, the product-locus invariant function, chord coordinates, and the algebraic-base hypothesis of the fixed-part theorem address the earlier review. The revised description of computational evidence also distinguishes symbolic arithmetic from geometric arguments more accurately.

The additions to Theorem 8.1 are sound within the stated smooth, generic setting. The absence of a sign summand in $H^0(B,2L)_+$ explains the enhancement from invariant sections under the three-cycle to invariant sections under $S_3$. Identifying the exceptional curves over the weighted-projective vertex and the four cusp orbits clarifies the kernel of rational pullback. For the displayed quadratic spaces with two hyperbolic planes, the explicit Clifford generators establish $\mathrm{Cl}^+\cong M_4(\mathbb Q)$ and the four-dimensional symplectic spin representation.

The new curve decompositions are consistent with the fixed-point calculations: the characters $(14,-6,2)$, $(12,-4,0)$, and $(24,4,0)$ give precisely the multiplicities stated in Theorem 8.1(5). The $2+6$ Weierstrass partition supplies the genus-two Jacobian factor by the classical hyperelliptic Prym construction. These are useful applications to this family. The general Clifford identities, group-algebra decompositions, and hyperelliptic Prym theorem are established tools; the contribution is their explicit geometric identification here. Comparison with the relevant discussion in [12, Remarks 5.2–5.3] and the seed construction in [13] supports the manuscript's distinction between inherited rank-ten geometry and these extensions.

I reproduced the modified `test_13` successfully; the other twelve tests were deselected. This checks the added matrix relations, spanning calculation, character arithmetic, and polynomial identities. It does not independently establish the geometric fixed loci, quotient contractions, or isogenies. In particular, character values are supplied to the test, and the new pencil discriminant factorization is checked for the specified example, whereas the quadratic discriminant identity is symbolic in general coefficients.

Several local corrections should accompany publication:

- Both abstracts give $U=4\mathbf1_{\mathrm{triv}}\oplus2\mathbf1_{\mathrm{sgn}}\oplus2\mathbf2_{\mathrm{std}}$. Theorem 8.1 and its calculation instead give $5\mathbf1_{\mathrm{triv}}\oplus\mathbf1_{\mathrm{sgn}}\oplus2\mathbf2_{\mathrm{std}}$. Also replace “$A_{12,s}$ realizes $U(1)$” with the actual conclusion $H^1(A_{12,s},\mathbb Q)\cong\operatorname{MC}_{-1/2}(\mathcal U)_s(1)$. These are corrections to the summary, not alternative conclusions supported by the proofs. Restrict the abstract's minimal-polarization statement to the selected inversion eigenspace: Example 2.1 already records a rank-six K3-type piece for the opposite lift at $d=1$.
- The new exterior-square identification in Theorem 8.1(2) has a Tate-twist mismatch. $\bigwedge^2_0H^1(B_Z,\mathbb Q)$ has weight two, whereas $U^{S_3^{\mathrm{diag}}}(1)$ has weight zero. Use matching weights on both sides. The Clifford calculation itself is unaffected.
- Write the symmetry-enhancement equality explicitly as the projectivization of invariant **sections**. The full projective fixed locus of a cyclic group also includes nontrivial eigensections; identifying this with the smooth invariant-divisor locus requires the smoothness restriction already used in §8.
- Define $Q_s$, $\Delta_{1,s}(r)$, and the quadratic polynomials in the final discriminant identity locally in Theorem 8.1(5). Add a numbered reference to Mumford's *Prym Varieties I* (1974) for the hyperelliptic Prym theorem. Equality of dimensions with $W_Z$ alone should not be read as identifying $J(C_{2,Z})$ with the spin abelian surface or constructing a correspondence between them.

The general rational Hodge conjecture and the generic rank-ten algebraic Kuga–Satake correspondence remain unresolved, as the manuscript explicitly acknowledges.
