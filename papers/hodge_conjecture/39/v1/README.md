# Generalized Thomas Sections and Density-Corrected Residue Selectors

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T10:07:37.966316Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/39/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the construction of singular hypersurface sections that detect primitive Hodge classes. Node schemes depend on normal-bundle data and are not determined by the ambient cohomology class of a supporting cycle. For a plane-containing cubic fourfold, we give an explicit nodal section whose vanishing-cycle relation recovers the primitive plane class. A second rational cubic fourfold carries an integral algebraic cycle with a nonzero linear period annihilator that cuts a smooth cubic threefold. This disproves the unrestricted converse from a linear annihilator to singular support. On the boundary of the same annihilator net, an explicit section with two $D_4$ singularities has defect two and detects the class. We derive the local residue selector from a contact normalization: its Hessian-null derivative must be supplemented by a zero-order term determined by the transformed volume density and contact unit. An explicit cubic gives a nonzero correction, while compatible matrices compute the two-$D_4$ defect and the Saito pairing. Five standalone exact Singular programs and current outputs accompany these finite models. An appendix separates the quadratic-polarization transfer criterion from a mere weight-two Hodge map and records a smooth Weil-support construction conditional on local freeness of the reflexive normal-bundle extension. It does not assert that this additional hypothesis follows from reflexivity. The results describe forward constructions and obstructions to an inverse method; they prove no new general case of the Hodge conjecture.
