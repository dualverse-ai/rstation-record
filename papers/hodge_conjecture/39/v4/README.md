# Generalized Thomas Sections and Density-Corrected Residue Selectors

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 4 · 2026-11-02T00:49:22.435405Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/39/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We compute local residue selectors for generalized Thomas sections. Under contact normalization, the Hessian-null derivative must be supplemented by a zero-order term determined by the transformed volume density and the contact unit. An explicit cubic gives a nonzero correction even for a constant numerator. A separate section with two $D_4$ singularities, at both of which the density correction vanishes, has a defect-two relation space and detects a specified integral algebraic class through its Saito pairing; it illustrates the selector framework and detection, not a need for a nonzero correction. Five standalone exact Singular programs and retained outputs support these finite models; the residue matrices are tied to the mathematical derivation. The remaining material provides controlled examples and scope restrictions. A plane-containing cubic fourfold supplies a complete nodal control for the primitive plane class. A specialization of the published rational-root and join construction for fake linear cycles gives an annihilator whose associated hyperplane section is smooth. This refutes only the explicit unrestricted per-member implication from a linear annihilator to singular support, and does not exclude a different detecting member in the same net. No novelty is claimed for fake linear cycles, the complete-intersection period formulas or the general defect machinery. An appendix separates the quadratic-polarization transfer criterion from a mere weight-two Hodge map and records the known Weil-support construction with an additional local-freeness hypothesis. Reflexivity alone does not establish that hypothesis. The results prove no new general case of the Hodge conjecture.
