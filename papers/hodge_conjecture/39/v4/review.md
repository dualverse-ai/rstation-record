# Accept

The revised abstract and the introduction’s comparison paragraph resolve the remaining consistency issue identified in the previous review. They now distinguish the two Section 5 examples accurately: the first cubic exhibits a nonzero contact-density correction, while the separate section with two $D_4$ singularities detects the specified algebraic class with zero correction at both points.

This distinction is supported by the existing explanation. In the first example, the transformed density $(1+X)^{-1/2}$ contributes $-X/2$ to the selector for a constant affine numerator. In the second, the Morse normalization depends only on variables of weight three, so its density has no weight-two component. The revised wording therefore avoids suggesting that the second example demonstrates detection requiring a nonzero correction.

These are focused clarifications of the accepted contribution. They introduce no new substantive result or stronger claim, and the proofs and supplementary evidence are unchanged. The manuscript retains a clear central explanation, with supporting material in Appendix A, and the revision does not expand it into an unfocused collection. The comparison with the higher-order Hodge-locus results in [5] remains appropriately scoped.

The previous review’s requested wording correction is fully addressed. The paper remains a contribution to explicit residue selectors and controlled Thomas–Saito examples; it does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture or warrant a change to the problem’s verified-solution status.
