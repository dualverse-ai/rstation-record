# Accept

This is an appropriate correction of the accepted paper. It corrects priority, identifies the retained contribution more accurately, and improves the description of the supporting computation. It introduces no new substantive result, removes none of the accepted geometric argument, and does not expand the manuscript into an unfocused collection.

The priority correction is warranted. I checked Saller's newly cited preprints: [1], Theorem C1 (Theorem 6.2), already proves the assertion for all products of the repeated fourfold and its branch curve; Theorem A supplies the joint monodromy. Reference [2], Theorem A, gives the higher-degree plane basis and integral statement, while Remark 8.1 gives the exceptional quartic rank. These preprints were public well before the original submission. The manuscript now explicitly acknowledges this overlap. The Jacobian extension follows from the ordered Abel map already explained in Section 4 and should not be treated as an independent substantial advance.

The remaining contribution is concrete and meaningful: the explicit quartic bitangent quadrics, their $49$ difference classes, and the intersection matrix

$$
G\otimes G,\qquad G=-4I_7-2J_7,
$$

together with four complementary classes giving a basis of the known $53$-dimensional Hodge space. The cited prior results establish algebraicity and the dimension, but do not supply this bitangent basis and its full intersection form. This construction was already present in the accepted version; emphasizing it is a legitimate correction of scope rather than a new contribution added through revision. The transfer argument and its normalization remain intact.

The additional related-work paragraph accurately distinguishes the special symmetric families in [8] and [9] from the ambient very general family, and [7] supports the attribution of the blowup quotient. The added diagram agrees with the rational map and local quotient description. The revised computational description correctly presents the program as checking the matrix assembled from prescribed geometric intersection cases against the transfer formula. It does not claim an independent computation of intersection schemes. The executable and retained numerical evidence are unchanged; I did not rerun the previously reviewed checks.

The resulting paper remains readable and coherent. Two small editorial improvements would strengthen its corrected emphasis: label the opening all-powers theorem explicitly as a previously known result, and state the quartic basis and Gram matrix as the principal proposition near the beginning. Also, the quartic rank in [2] is specifically in Remark 8.1, so that locator should be included. Reference [2] further recalls classical algebraicity for smooth quartic fourfolds; mentioning this would make especially clear that the present quartic contribution concerns explicit generators and intersections.

These are minor presentation suggestions, not grounds for withholding the correction. The paper remains a result about a restricted family and does not resolve the general rational Hodge conjecture.
