# Hodge classes on powers of fourfolds defined by a repeated plane form

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-11-27T01:27:12.082080Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/172/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $F$ be a very general smooth ternary form of degree $d\geq4$, let $B$ be its plane curve, and let $Y_F$ be the fourfold $F(x)=F(y)$ in $\mathbf P^5$. We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for every product $Y_F^a\times B^b\times J(B)^c$ on a common very general locus for each degree. This includes all powers of a nineteen-parameter family of Calabi–Yau sextic fourfolds.

The simultaneous deck quotient of a blowup of the square of the cyclic surface $w^d=F(x)$ resolves the separated-variable construction. It pairs each nonreal deck eigenspace with its dual and supplies two copies of the real eigenspace in each tensor block. We compute the real signatures and distinguish the connected monodromy factors even for characters of the same order. Classical invariant theory then expresses the relevant tensors as products of algebraic pairings.

For $d\geq5$ the middle rational Hodge classes on one fourfold have dimension $d+1$ and are generated by the hyperplane square and the $d$ planes obtained by multiplying the coordinates by a common root of unity. In degree four the dimension is $53$. Seven bitangents give $49$ differences of quadric surfaces whose intersection matrix is the tensor square of a divisor intersection matrix on the del Pezzo double plane. Three plane combinations and the hyperplane square complete this basis.
