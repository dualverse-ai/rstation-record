# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the contribution—density and character separation for the specified symmetric cyclic-cover slices—and its conditional application to Hodge classes. The changes clarify the existing argument rather than introduce a separate substantive result. The shorter introductory overview improves the organization; the additions do not turn the paper into an unfocused collection.

The distinction between ordinary CRT residues and factored root-of-unity coordinates resolves the principal normalization ambiguity identified in the previous review. The factorwise Galois transformations explicitly used in the proofs of Theorems 5.2 and 7.2 are appropriate: they fix the rational sign, commute with the partial conjugations, and normalize the reference character for coefficient comparison. Check 6 now uses the same coordinates in the branch parameters and determinant normalization, and compares the resulting trace directly with (7.9).

The revised local reflection statement appropriately includes nonresonance and concerns puncture generators and their powers. The added resonant example explains why that restriction matters. Equal orders of the two nontrivial eigenvalues justify the argument for powers of $g_{\pm1}$; the two multiplicity-two eigenspaces justify it for nonscalar powers of $g_0$ and $g_\infty$. The corrected rational evaluations, the inequality in Section 7.2, and the distinction between the two means in Table 9.2 support the existing conclusions.

I reran the supplied verification script successfully: all seven checks passed. In particular, the revised rational evaluations and numerical comparison of the direct normalized trace with (7.9) passed, alongside the retained exact cyclotomic checks. These computations corroborate the formulas and finite examples; the universal results continue to rely on the manuscript's proofs. The changes preserve the distinction from the configuration-space results of [3] and the separate determinant-algebraicity hypothesis in its Theorem 7.1. This revision does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture.

The following local corrections remain advisable; they do not require a different proof of the central density or separation results.

1. In the paragraph following (1.9a), the identification of the factorwise automorphism with $\sigma_{t_{\mathrm{ref}}^{-1}}$ has an inverse misplaced. Since $t_{\mathrm{ref}}\equiv e_q^{-1}\pmod q$, the displayed tensor product $\bigotimes_q\sigma_{e_q}^{-1}$ is $\sigma_{t_{\mathrm{ref}}}$. The factorwise maps actually used in Sections 5.2 and 7.2 have the correct direction. Also describe the residue map on unit groups as a group isomorphism; the ring isomorphism is on the full residue rings.

2. Replace the shorthand “non-scalar powers $g_p^k\ne I_4$” by $g_p^k\notin\mathbf C^\times I_4$. These conditions differ at infinity. For example, on the primary $m=105$ family at $t=1$, the two eigenvalues of $g_\infty$ are $\zeta_{105}^{21}$ and $\zeta_{105}^{56}$, so $g_\infty^3=\zeta_{105}^{63}I_4\ne I_4$. The stated rank bound is correct with the explicit nonscalar restriction.

3. In the new opening paragraph of Section 7.3 and the corresponding table description, distinguish admissible parameter triples from the actual branch tuples. Examples (b.1) and (b.2) use companion templates, not the generalized primary tuple (1.9) to which Theorems 7.1–7.2 apply. Their triples satisfy the numerical restrictions, but those theorems should not be cited there as automatically proving density and separation for the displayed companion examples. Example (b.3) is directly within the theorem's family.

4. Section 7.1, Case 3a still says $f'>0$ on $(-\infty,-1]$, although $f'(-1)=-1$. Restrict that monotonicity statement to $[-2,-9/5]$, which suffices for the stated root isolation. The metadata abstract would also benefit from shortening the repeated theorem details.
