# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the contribution—monodromy density and character separation on the specified symmetric cyclic-cover slices—and the conditional application to Hodge classes. The changes improve the existing explanation without introducing a new substantive result or expanding the paper into an unfocused collection.

The four principal corrections are sound:

1. Following (1.9a), the identification of the factorwise automorphism with $\sigma_{t_{\mathrm{ref}}}$ now agrees with $t_{\mathrm{ref}}\equiv e_q^{-1}\pmod q$. The distinction between the CRT ring isomorphism and its restriction to unit groups is also correct. These changes align the introductory convention with the normalization actually used in Sections 5.2 and 7.2.
2. The explicit restriction $g_p(t)^k\notin\mathbf C^\times I_4$ correctly excludes nonidentity scalar powers, for which the asserted minimum rank would be zero. The revised statement matches the eigenspace argument in Lemma 2.1: nonscalar powers of the two multiplicity-two operators retain two-dimensional eigenspaces, while the equal-order argument handles the other punctures.
3. Section 7.3 and Table 9.2 now distinguish the companion-template examples (b.1) and (b.2) from the generalized primary family (1.9). Admissibility of a parameter triple alone does not put its branch tuple within Theorems 7.1–7.2. Example (b.3) is within that family, whereas (b.4) violates the stated restriction on $N$. The revision removes the previous unsupported implication that the primary-family theorems automatically cover the companion examples.
4. In Section 7.1, Case 3a, $f'(X)\ge103/25$ on $[-2,-9/5]$ is correct: $f''(X)=6X+2<0$ there, and $f'(-9/5)=103/25$. Together with the stated endpoint values of $f$, this supplies the required root isolation and preserves the subsequent trace lower bound. No monotonicity assertion on the larger interval is needed.

Two small consistency edits remain advisable. The opening paragraph of Section 7 still describes (b.1)–(b.3) collectively as lying “within Theorem D(1)'s regime”; align it with the precise distinction now made in Section 7.3. The Section 1.2 summary of Theorem A should likewise say “non-scalar powers” when summarizing the rank bound. The detailed statements resolve these ambiguities, so they do not prevent acceptance. The abstract remains informative but would read more easily with fewer formulas and repeated theorem details.

I checked the changed mathematical statements against their surrounding arguments. The supplementary files are unchanged, and I did not rerun their computations for this revision. The retained finite checks remain corroboration of formulas and examples, rather than proofs of the universal claims.

The revision preserves the distinction from the configuration-space results in [3]. Its balanced all-powers application still requires the separate determinant-algebraicity hypothesis explicitly present in [3, Theorem 7.1]. It does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture.
