# Braid Monodromy Rigidity on One-Dimensional Symmetric Cyclic Prym Slices: Adjoint $\mathfrak{sl}_4$-Dichotomy, Ramanujan-Sum Trace Averages, and Length-Three Cyclotomic Separation

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 4 · 2026-11-07T20:14:29.147633Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/114/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $m\ge 3$ be odd and let $S=\mathbf{P}^1_\lambda\setminus\{0,\infty,1,-1\}\subset\mathcal{M}_{0,6}$ be the one-dimensional symmetric slice of degree-$m$ cyclic covers $C_\lambda\to\mathbf{P}^1$ branched at $(-1,-\lambda,0,\lambda,1,\infty)$ with a $(2,2,1,1)$-symmetric weight tuple $\mathbf{a}$ under an involution $h\in(\mathbf{Z}/m\mathbf{Z})^\times$ ($H=\{1,h\}$). Unlike on $\mathcal{M}_{0,6}$, under the local non-resonance conditions of Lemma 2.1 (satisfied by all units $t\in(\mathbf{Z}/m\mathbf{Z})^\times$ on our CRT families), every puncture of $S$ is a simultaneous multi-point collision whose local monodromy generator $g_p(t)$ ($p\in\{1,-1,0,\infty\}$) on $V_t=H_1(C_\lambda,\mathbf{C})_t$ and non-scalar powers $g_p(t)^k\notin\mathbf{C}^\times I_4$ have $\operatorname{rank}(g_p(t)^k-cI_4)\ge 2$ for all $c\in\mathbf{C}^\times$, excluding rank-$1$ pseudoreflections and transvections; moreover, for composite $m=a\cdot m'$ ($\gcd(a,m')=1$), the multiplier $v\equiv(-1,+1)\pmod{(a,m')}\notin\pm H$ is locally isospectral with $t=1$ and collides on the length-$2$ braid words $g_1g_0$ and $g_1g_{-1}$.

We establish an unconditional global braid-monodromy rigidity and cyclotomic separation theory on $S$: (1) cyclotomic linear disjointness in the $g_1(t)$-eigenbasis yields the adjoint dichotomy $\mathfrak{g}\in\{\{0\},\mathfrak{sl}_4(\mathbf{C})\}$ (Theorem A); (2) for $m=15N$ ($\gcd(N,30)=1$), Ramanujan-sum trace averages of $(g_1g_0)^2$ prove factorwise Zariski density in $\operatorname{SU}(J(t))$ up to $\mu_{15N}$ scalars, and dual-basis expansion of $g_1^2g_0$ over $\mathbf{Q}(\zeta_N)$ separates all non-trivial cosets $[t]\neq[\pm 1]$ (Theorem B); (3) $g_1^2g_0$ uniformly separates $v$ from $\pm H$ for coprime odd $a,m'\ge 3$ (Theorem C); and (4) Galois involution projections extend density and length-$3$ separation to three-factor conductors $m=a\cdot b\cdot N$ ($\gcd(N,30)=1$), alongside four method-boundary examples (Theorem D). By Goursat's lemma, this determines the generic derived Mumford–Tate group $\operatorname{Hg}(B_{\eta,\mathrm{prim}})_{\mathrm{der}}=\prod_{[t]}\operatorname{SU}(J(t))$ and endomorphism algebra $\mathbf{Q}(\zeta_m)^H$, and on characterwise $(2,2)$-balanced subfamilies ($d_t=3$) gives $\operatorname{Hg}(B_{\eta,\mathrm{prim}})=\prod_{[t]}\operatorname{SU}(2,2)$, reducing all-powers Hodge algebraicity to the separate determinant-cycle input on individual factors (Corollary E).
