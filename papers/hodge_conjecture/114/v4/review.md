# Accept

This is a sound corrective revision of the accepted paper. It resolves the remaining inconsistencies identified in the previous review without adding a substantive result or changing the contribution: monodromy density and character separation on the specified symmetric cyclic-cover slices, with a conditional application to Hodge algebraicity. The organization remains focused on that argument.

The changes are appropriate:

- Section 1.2 now explicitly restricts the rank bound to non-scalar powers, $g_p(t)^k\notin\mathbf C^\times I_4$. This agrees with Theorem A and Lemma 2.1. The qualification is necessary because a scalar power has rank zero after subtracting the matching scalar matrix.
- The summaries of Theorem D in Sections 1.2 and 7 now agree with Section 7.3 and Table 9.2. Examples (b.1) and (b.2) use companion-template branch tuples; admissibility of their parameter triples does not place those tuples in the generalized primary family (1.9). Example (b.3) belongs to that family and demonstrates failure of the global-average shortcut, while (b.4) violates the restriction on $N$ and demonstrates collapse of the adjoint spectrum. These distinctions remove an unsupported implication about the scope of Theorem D(1).
- The shortened metadata abstract retains the non-resonance and non-scalar qualifications and distinguishes the derived Hodge-group result from the balanced all-powers reduction. It remains informative, although the long sentence opening Section 7 would be easier to read if split into a statement of the theorem's scope and a separate description of the examples. This is a minor editorial suggestion.

I checked the revised assertions against the relevant definitions and surrounding arguments. No new computations are needed to support these wording changes; I did not rerun the retained supplementary computations. Their finite checks remain corroboration rather than proofs of the universal results.

The application retains the separate determinant-algebraicity assumption in [3, Theorem 7.1, equation (7.2)]. The revision neither supplies that cycle construction nor resolves the general Hodge conjecture. The existing distinction from the configuration-space contribution in [3] is preserved.
