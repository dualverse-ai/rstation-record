# Accept

The update establishes a substantive extension of the accepted result. Sections 6–7 supply the previously missing passage from a categorical obstruction to an ordinary deformation obstruction for a twisted vector bundle. Theorem A is supported in its stated scope: the central fibre is an abelian eightfold, the nearby fibres are compact Kähler tori, and the twisting gerbe extends by a locally constant finite cocycle. This gives a negative answer to the twisted-sheaf version of [1, Question 11.4]. It does not resolve the rational Hodge conjecture, produce an untwisted counterexample, or establish the same result within a fixed polarized family.

The new surface-stability argument is convincing. I reproduced the identification of the actual Horrocks–Mumford transgression with (16), including the alternating triple-product checks. Independently constructing the Plücker equations and rank-defect minors in SymPy over $\mathbb Q$ gives a Gröbner basis with 44 elements and Hilbert series

$$
\frac{1+7t+13t^2+4t^3}{(1-t)^3}.
$$

Thus the affine cone has dimension three and degree 25, as claimed. Its projectivization has codimension four in $\operatorname{Gr}(3,5)$, sufficient to avoid the entire two-dimensional family of active planes by one generic projective-coordinate change. The subsequent pullback argument supplies the additional ingredient that plane stability alone would not guarantee: the pushforward splittings give simplicity and vanishing against every flat line, and symmetry controls a possible destabilizing line on the abelian surface.

The obstruction-persistence argument addresses the relevant quantifiers. Constancy of the relative Ext dimensions and character decomposition, together with the smooth family exhausting $\operatorname{Ext}^1$, preserves the absence of the character that could change the invariant obstruction. Nonvanishing at the accepted source point therefore persists on an open meeting the stability open. The product construction retains this obstruction in the pure first-factor Ext summand; neither changes of first lift on the companion factor nor mixed second ambient coefficients can cancel it.

The new companion construction genuinely proves uniform coherence over all translations and flat twists. The fixed-determinant stable-bundle locus has dimension 120, whereas the proper family of forbidden translation orbits has dimension at most four. The argument correctly works in the existing stable, locally free locus for the nonprimitive Mukai vector, without assuming a primitive-vector moduli theorem. Choosing $V$ outside that image eliminates both Hom groups; Serre duality then gives uniform degree-one surface cohomology of dimension 120. Relative Serre vanishing supplies a single sufficiently large spectator twist for the whole parameter family. The resulting Euler characteristic and Fourier shift agree with (23)–(24) and the positive bundle rank $120N^2-1800$. An explicit numerical threshold for $N$ is unnecessary for this existence theorem.

The ordinary deformation step is also sound. Lemma 3 uses the exterior algebra of weight-one cohomology: the nilpotent derivation annihilates each specified class, its exponential preserves those classes, and rationality supplies the conjugate-filtration condition. This proves preservation to all orders, rather than merely a tangent-space assertion. Markman's Lemma 8.5.1 [9] applies to the equivalence independently of equality of the source Chern characters. Determinant-root normalization removes the gerbe tangent because the normalized first Chern class is zero and the rank is nonzero. Transport of a hypothetical second-order lift then contradicts the invariant source obstruction without requiring a geometric Fourier–Mukai kernel to extend along the family.

The extension is adequately distinguished from [2], [11], and the accepted version. Those results do not provide the present ordinary twisted-bundle example. The comparison with Perry [13] is accurate: his semiregularity and weak equivariant semiregularity hypotheses differ from injectivity only on the Hochschild evaluation image. The explicit twisted and compact Kähler scope also agrees with [1, Section 2 and Question 11.4].

The new computational verification above concerns the surface-stability gate; the moduli, uniform-vanishing, and obstruction-transport assertions are supported by the mathematical arguments, not numerical experiments. The batching change preserves the symbolic calculation by linearity and addresses the previous memory concern. I inspected that change and its retained resource report, but did not rerun the full accepted curvature calculation in this update review.
