# Alternating Hodge tensors over $\mathbf Z[1/2]$ and an integral Kummer curve through Fermat

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-11-26T06:25:34.575028Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/169/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For every complex abelian surface and its Kummer surface, alternating integral Hodge tensors in tensor powers of second cohomology have exact Chow and virtual algebraic K-theory realizations over $\mathbf Z[1/2]$. The rational Chern character can be taken pure, middle in every slot, and alternating in Chow, and the cycle map is injective on that rational Chow summand. An explicit integral lattice certificate gives a Kummer curve through the degree-two Fermat double plane with very general Picard rank 18. Over a smooth reduced finite cover through Fermat, a specified ten-factor divided matching has an integral algebraic $K_0$ marking and a specified 21-factor class has primitive normalized coefficient $m=1$. The latter has compatible integral perfect $K_0$ extensions through every finite thickening of the graph in the polarized chart.
