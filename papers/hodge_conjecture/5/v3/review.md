# Accept

This revision appropriately narrows the interpretation of the evidence while preserving the accepted obstruction theorem and cone-family result. It addresses the previous review’s substantive presentation requests without introducing a new central claim or weakening the established contribution.

The revised abstract and §§1, 8.6 now distinguish sampled finite-field evaluations from polynomial identities. In particular, the proposed formula $c_\lambda l^2\det A$ and its characteristic-zero multiplicity consequences are no longer presented as established results. Likewise, the polarised-adjugate span calculation is correctly limited to the fitted quadratic model. These qualifications are essential: the accepted nonvanishing certificates support the dimension bounds, but do not certify the stronger leading-form identities.

The revision also correctly distinguishes a component of $W'_e$ contained in $U_\lambda$ from a component of $U_\lambda$ itself. Proposition 8.3 continues to establish the former, with dimension $84$ in the transverse slice; the dimension and structure of the containing Hodge-locus component remain open. The corrected double-cone coordinates are consistent with the Fermat equation: imposing $y_3=y_4=0$ cancels the two shared cubic pairs, whereas imposing $x_8=x_9=0$ leaves a smooth Fermat sixfold.

The changes to §9 appropriately identify the cited Chow-group theorem [10–12], rather than an expected-dimension count, as the justification. The distinction between an explicit cone correspondence and the existence of its sixfold input cycles is now clear, and Question 9.1(2) asks the relevant remaining construction question. These changes preserve the attribution and scope of the accepted application of known Hodge-conjecture results.

Two minor wording inconsistencies remain:

- In §8.6’s caveat, replace “the dimension $84$ of every irreducible family” with “the upper bound $84$ for the families described here.” The cone-family components have dimension $84$, while §8.2 bounds the split family by $83$.
- Carry the sampled-model qualification into the final paragraph of §9, where “every leading form is divisible by $l^2$” still reads as an established identity. Refer instead to the observed or fitted leading forms.

Neither issue affects acceptance. The revision does not establish the proposed characteristic-zero identities or resolve the general rational Hodge conjecture.
