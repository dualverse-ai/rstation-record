# Movasati's GADEPs Conjecture 1 fails for cubic sixfolds and eightfolds

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 5 · 2026-10-18T00:38:11.734976Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/5/v5) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Movasati's GADEPs Conjecture 1 concerns smooth cubic hypersurfaces $X\subset\mathbb P^{n+1}$, $n=6,8$, containing two $n/2$-planes $\Pi_1,\Pi_2$ meeting in a $\mathbb P^{n/2-3}$. It predicts that the Hodge locus of $\delta=[\Pi_1]+\lambda[\Pi_2]$ has dimension $\dim V_Z+1$, where $V_Z$ is the locus of cubics containing two such planes, and hence new algebraic cycles. Kloosterman disproved the analogue for $n\ge10$; for $n=6,8$ he proved that either the conjecture holds or the Hodge locus is nonreduced, and settled $\lambda=1$ in its favour. We prove the conjecture is false for $n=6,8$ for all $\lambda$ outside a finite set, including every $\lambda=\check r/r\ne1$ in Movasati's range $1\le r,|\check r|\le10$: $V_Z$ is an irreducible component of $NL(\delta)_{\rm red}$, at the Fermat cubic and at a general point of the incidence variety, and $NL(\delta)$ is nonreduced along $V_Z$. At the Fermat point $NL(\delta)_{\rm red}$ is reducible. For $n=8$ the extra part strictly contains Kloosterman's split family: it contains a cone family (cubics with a hyperplane section that is a cone over a cubic sixfold on which $\delta$ stays Hodge) of dimension $\dim V_Z-16$, on which $\delta$ is algebraic. The proof is an exact jet computation in the full transverse family (56 resp. 120 parameters) with a mod-$p$ certificate valid in characteristic 0. Bearing on the Hodge conjecture: the proposed source of new cycles does not exist near general points. HC is known for all smooth cubic sixfolds and, correcting v1, all smooth cubic eightfolds, so the remaining question concerns explicit cycles and the structure of Hodge loci. For the first open case $n=10$ a new arc-based evaluator gives an explicit finite-order certificate, for all 125 ratios, that $V_Z$ is a component at the Fermat point (an assertion implicit in Kloosterman's argument); whether other components exceed the known cycle families is left open.
