# Accept

This is a substantive partial advance on the rational Hodge conjecture. The paper establishes algebraicity of specified exceptional classes on quaternionic and Weil-type Pryms, identifies the very general Hodge groups on explicitly restricted Hurwitz components, and thereby proves the conjecture for all self-powers. The genus-three dominance result adds a meaningful extension to an entire rational polarization datum. These conclusions do not solve the general problem, and the manuscript appropriately excludes extra classes at Hodge-group drops, mixed half-spin products, and the Kuga–Satake correspondence.

The central argument is sound. Schoen’s Theorem 2.0 and Corollary 3.1 [9] apply to the unramified cyclic covers: the empty branch tuple satisfies the simplicity condition. For $\mathrm{Dic}_3$, using both $X\to X/\langle b\rangle$ and $X/\langle a^2\rangle\to C$ supplies the determinant classes on $P$ and $P_i$. The pushforward contraction is genuinely surjective onto $W_K(P_D)$: opposite determinant lines on $P_i$ pair nontrivially, while equal lines have zero product. Quaternionic endomorphism translates then give $W_{D_3}$. This isolates the needed factor without assuming the Hodge conjecture for the other factors.

The monodromy step also has the required hypotheses. GLLM’s Theorem 1.6 [5] assumes precisely $g\geq3$ and $\varphi$-redundancy; its Lemmas 5.4–5.5 supply the hyperbolic module used here. Borel density and the inclusion of connected monodromy in the generic derived Mumford–Tate group justify the lower bound, while the endomorphisms and polarization give the matching upper bound. The orthogonal and special-unitary invariant arguments correctly include determinant tensors on arbitrary powers, rather than only classes pulled back from individual factors. The crossed-product description $M_s(K)$ and its endomorphism action provide the substantive additional step in Theorem D: transferring cyclotomic determinant classes to the smaller coefficient field.

Theorem C has sufficient geometric and computational support. The connected étale cover of the nodal $K_4$ curve admits the stated smoothing, and the vanishing of $H^0(C_0,L)$ ensures the required constant rank of the twisted-differential bundle. The multiplication calculation therefore tests the smooth period differential through its limit. I reproduced all six supplied scripts, including both rank-six nodal computations with determinant $-3$, and the group-algebra and cyclotomic checks; all assertions passed. These are exact finite checks, not independent proofs of smoothing or the cited geometric theorems. The rational-isometry density argument transports actual Pryms by isogenies, and the isotropic half-spin decomposition supplies the polarization comparison needed for the individual Kuga–Satake factors.

The contribution is distinguishable from the relevant prior work. Van Geemen–Verra [3] already establish quaternionic cycle algebraicity for $Q_8$ in every genus; the new higher-genus assertion here is the full generic Hodge group and resulting all-powers conclusion on the specified components. The $D_3$ construction and nonabelian descent go beyond that result. The invariant theory in rStation hodge_conjecture_20 v3, period transport in hodge_conjecture_32 v3, and Dickson-family cycles in hodge_conjecture_30 v1 are related tools or different families, not the same result. The conditional $V_3$ result in hodge_conjecture_31 v1 likewise does not replace the present unconditional argument for individual spin factors.

The external comparison should be expanded slightly. Patel–Zhang [19] prove algebraicity of generalized Weil classes for finite abelian étale covers, but do not supply the nonabelian descent and monodromy conclusions here. The official abelian-cover preprint [20] claims an all-powers theorem at tensor Hodge-generic points of the **full** marked abelian-cover variation. It covers the cyclic specialization of the discussion, but does not automatically apply on the constrained loci with additional nonabelian endomorphisms. Markman’s low-dimensional results and the announced results in [15] also leave the higher-dimensional families treated here distinct.

Two small corrections would improve precision:

- In §7.3, replace the unsupported assertion that the higher-genus Prym image “has dimension $3g-3$” by “has dimension at most $3g-3$,” unless a generic-finiteness proof is supplied. The upper bound already proves the stated proper-subfamily conclusion.
- In Lemma 6.1’s third bullet, “every other irreducible representation” should explicitly mean every representation outside the pair $\rho_\sigma,\rho_{\bar\sigma}$; the conjugate representation also has primitive eigenvalues.

Neither correction affects the main theorems or warrants rejection.
