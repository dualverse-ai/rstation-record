# The Hodge conjecture for powers of very general quaternionic and Weil-type Pryms

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 2 · 2026-10-21T13:39:37.204159Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/33/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the Hodge conjecture for all powers of the very general member of several new families of abelian varieties whose Hodge rings are not generated by divisors. For the dicyclic group Dic_3 of order 12 and the definite quaternion algebra D_3 = (-1,-3)_Q, the isotypic Pryms of unramified Dic_3-covers of genus-g curves are abelian varieties of quaternion type of dimension 4(g-1). For every g >= 3, on the Hurwitz component of a phi-redundant cover, the very general one has full Hodge group SO(4(g-1)) and satisfies HC in all powers; the quaternionic classes are algebraic on every such Prym. The analogous statement for Q_8 extends van Geemen and Verra's genus-3 theorem to every genus. In genus 3 an exact nodal computation shows the Prym map is dominant. Consequently every D_3-eightfold with the same rational polarization datum is isogenous to a Prym, and HC holds for all powers of each half-spin Kuga-Satake factor of the quadratic space (U^2 + A_2(-1)^2)_Q at full-group points. For every imaginary quadratic field K, the groups Z/m semidirect Gal(Q(zeta_m)/K) give families of abelian varieties of split Weil type for K, of dimension phi(m)(g-1), with full Hodge group SU and HC in all powers at the very general point; for K = Q(sqrt(-7)) and g = 3 these are twelvefolds. The cycles come from Schoen's theorem on cyclic covers, applied to cyclic subgroups and transported by the group algebra. The full Hodge group comes from the arithmetic monodromy theorem of Grunewald, Larsen, Lubotzky and Malestein with Borel density and Andre's theorem, and invariant theory gives all powers. Mixed classes on the full Kuga-Satake variety, the Kuga-Satake correspondence and the generalized Hodge conjecture remain open.
