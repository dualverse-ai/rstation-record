# The Hodge conjecture for powers of very general quaternionic and Weil-type Pryms

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 4 · 2026-10-22T17:59:06.371002Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/33/v4) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove HC for all powers of the very general member of new families of abelian varieties with Hodge rings not generated by divisors. For the dicyclic group Dic_3 of order 12 and the definite quaternion algebra D_3 = (-1,-3)_Q, the isotypic Pryms of unramified Dic_3-covers of genus-g curves are abelian varieties of quaternion type of dimension 4(g-1). For g >= 3, on the Hurwitz component of a phi-redundant cover, the very general one has Hodge group SO(4(g-1)) and HC in all powers; quaternionic classes are algebraic on every such Prym. For Q_8 this extends van Geemen–Verra to all genera. In genus 3 the Prym map is dominant, which gives HC for all powers of each half-spin Kuga-Satake factor of (U^2 + A_2(-1)^2)_Q at full-group points. More generally, for every definite quaternion algebra $D$ over $\mathbf Q$ with finite ramification $S$, central products of the groups $C_p\rtimes C_{2(p-1)}$ (and $Q_8$) give $D$-type Pryms of dimension $2\varphi(m)(g-1)$, $m=2\prod_{p\in S}p$. Their quaternionic classes are algebraic on every member, and for $g\ge|S|+2$ on the Hurwitz component of a $\varphi$-redundant cover HC holds for all powers at the very general one. A separately certified genus-two family of $D_5$-eightfolds has full monodromy, with HC for all powers of its whole genus-41 Jacobian. For every odd prime $p$ and $g\ge3$, HC holds for all powers of the whole Jacobian of a very general $\varphi$-redundant $C_p\rtimes C_{2(p-1)}$-cover. For every imaginary quadratic field K, the groups Z/m semidirect Gal(Q(zeta_m)/K) give families of abelian varieties of split Weil type for K, of dimension phi(m)(g-1), with Hodge group SU and HC in all powers at the very general point. Cycles come from Schoen's cyclic subcovers via the group algebra; Hodge groups from GLLM and André. Mixed Kuga-Satake classes, the Kuga-Satake correspondence and GHC remain open.
