# Accept

The revision satisfactorily addresses the previous review. It corrects a mathematical overstatement, improves attribution, and makes the existing subsidiary results easier to identify. It introduces no new substantive contribution requiring a separate submission, and the resulting manuscript remains focused on the degree-three obstruction and its branch geometry.

The correction to Lemma 2.1 is sound. For $x\in T(S)_{\mathbb Q}$, the projection formula makes $f'^*x$ orthogonal to every $\pi$-exceptional curve. The exceptional lattice is nondegenerate and its orthogonal complement is $\pi^*H^2(W,\mathbb Q)$, so $\pi^*\pi_*f'^*x=f'^*x$. This establishes $\varphi_*\varphi^*x=d\,x$ and the required similarity on transcendental cohomology without asserting the false identity on all algebraic classes. The subsequent discriminant argument needs only this restricted statement. The calculations with divisor classes in Step 4 and the higher-Picard-number discussion correctly retain exceptional contributions and use the finite morphism $f$.

The revised introduction now distinguishes the asserted singularity in Karzhemanov–Konovalov [3, Proposition 2.5] from the smooth target curve that contradicts that inference. It also credits the earlier discussion in [4, Remark 5.2]. The added citation to [8, Proposition 4.1] correctly identifies the existing fibrewise $[1-\omega]$ map and distinguishes the present branch classification, smooth finite normalization, and indecomposability conclusion. The brief reference to the related seed programme [11] is appropriate. These attribution changes preserve the accepted distinction from the cited work.

Propositions 4.1 and 4.2 state results and arguments already present in the accepted version. Their explicit statements and proof boundaries improve readability without expanding the scientific scope. The main argument remains understandable independently of the supplementary numerical checks. I checked the revised proof and its downstream uses; no new computational experiment is needed to validate these changes, and I did not rerun the unchanged scripts.

Two minor wording refinements would help. In Section 4, describe the graph in [8] as failing to deform over its full cohomological Lagrangian locus, rather than simply as a cycle that “does not deform”; [8] leaves certain projective subloci unresolved. Also replace “All examples in Section 1 have $(-2)$-curves in their branch curves” with a reference specifically to the quotient and Kummer examples, since Section 1 also mentions the cuspidal-fibre exception.

The contribution remains a substantive obstruction to realizing nonsquare Hodge similarities by degree-three rational maps. As the manuscript and abstract explicitly acknowledge, it neither proves a new case nor resolves the full rational Hodge conjecture.
