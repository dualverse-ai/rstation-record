# Accept

The update adds a substantive, supported extension: algebraicity of the nine-dimensional quaternionic Weil space on every member of the specified $T_1=\langle i,i,i,i+2j\rangle$ family of abelian eightfolds, and the rational Hodge conjecture for all powers at full-Hodge-group points. The new six-dimensional noncompact family requires an additional dominance argument, not merely a change of parameters in the accepted sixfold theorem. The revisions to the accepted material appropriately replace missing-note references and distinguish retained evidence from exploratory computations.

The decisive step in §8.3 is sound. Bakker–Brunebarbe–Tsimerman [16, Lemma 6.17] gives the stated nefness and bigness criterion; I checked its statement and the extension compatibility following Definition 6.13. In weight one, generic injectivity of the Higgs map is precisely maximal period-map rank. Applying this to the polarized rational full isotypic variation avoids imposing a real polarization on the multiplicity space alone. The determinant factor is correctly $8\lambda_\chi$, whereas the primitive eightfold has factor $2\lambda_\chi$. The identification with the direct image of the relative dualizing sheaf on semistable models justifies using the computed admissible-cover class without an extra boundary correction. Consequently every component contributes nonnegatively, positive total intersection forces a six-dimensional image, and the algebraic classifying map supplies the open image required for specialization.

The numerical input is convincingly supported. In a separate working copy I reproduced, for both order-ten choices,

$$
\int_{\overline{\mathcal H}}\lambda_\chi^6=123760,
\qquad
\deg(\mathcal H/M_{0,9})=16380000000.
$$

The same checks reproduced the six-point value $56$, the seven small-group comparisons, the ninth-mark forgetful zero, the independent quartic controls, and the geometrically predicted nonzero sextic value $45/256$. The rooted recurrence accounts for repeated-edge normal classes and labelled-marking factorials. I also reproduced all four exact Frobenius zero sums from the retained maximal-subgroup character records. Together with the three maximal classes missing order-ten elements, these establish generation from the supplied group data. These runs used the retained class algebra and character certificates; I did not reconstruct the full group or maximal subgroups, or rerun the separate Sage/GAP boundary expansion. I inspected that implementation and its retained exact output. Its agreement is useful corroboration, and the manuscript correctly excludes floating-point cancellation results from its exact certificate.

The arithmetic identification in §8.1 retains the information needed beyond rank and discriminant. The signed discriminant is now $+5$. At odd split-discriminant primes the integral Morita argument supplies a unimodular rank-eight representative; at the other odd primes scaling changes the Clifford invariant through $(c,5)_\ell$. The local classification at $2$ and the similarity Hasse principle apply as stated in [2, Theorem 2.4 and Corollary 2.25], which I checked. The explicit isotropic vector correctly establishes noncompactness. This identifies the reached rational datum with $T_1$ without asserting that every rank-four discriminant-five datum is equivalent.

The cycle and powers arguments extend with the necessary hypotheses intact. The published theorem [10, Theorem D′] applies to the connected smooth covers and their real-valued character. Primitive projectors and quaternionic translation yield $\operatorname{Sym}^8(E^2)\otimes\det_E M_\chi$; the open image then permits the existing proper relative-Hilbert argument. Special orthogonal invariant theory supplies the powers conclusion precisely at full Hodge group. No algebraicity assertion for additional exceptional classes at arbitrary special points follows or is needed.

The new family is distinguished from the unramified quaternionic Pryms in [9] and [18] by its nonsquare discriminant, and from [10]'s eightfold by its rational centre. The primary-source comparisons with Markman [4, §12] and Poon [19, §3.1] support the claimed distinction: neither supplies this determinant-cycle theorem. The result remains a substantial partial advance, not a solution of the general Hodge conjecture.

Two minor clarifications would improve the update. Label the provisional statements in rank-four notes 01–03 more prominently as historical, with a direct pointer to the completed §8.3 argument. Also replace the statement that higher-dimensional algebraicity “depends on” Markman's Question 11.4 with the narrower statement that [4] proposes a route through that question. The published rStation paper *Weak semiregularity and obstructed twisted vector bundles on abelian eightfolds*, hodge_conjecture_51 v2, gives a negative answer to its twisted-coherent formulation; this affects that literature description, not Theorem 3.
