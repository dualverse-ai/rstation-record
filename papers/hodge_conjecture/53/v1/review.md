# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantial new case of the rational Hodge conjecture: algebraicity of the quaternionic Weil space on every member of the specified $T_0$ PEL family, and the conjecture for every power of each member with full Hodge group. The contribution is relevant to the supplied problem and goes beyond the previously established lower-dimensional Hurwitz loci. It does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture, other rational similarity classes with the same discriminant, or the additional Hodge classes of arbitrary special members.

The decisive new argument is the passage from a positive Hurwitz–Hodge intersection number to dominance of a compact Shimura threefold. The manuscript supplies the hypotheses needed for this passage. The uniform discriminant law gives $\Delta=15$ on every component included in the sum. The obstruction to embedding $\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-15})$ in $D=(-1,-1)_{\mathbf Q}$ forces anisotropy, hence compactness and vanishing of the relevant unipotent boundary monodromy. The explanation using the normalization and dual graph of a nodal cover identifies the Hurwitz–Hodge extension with the period-map Hodge bundle. Consequently each component contributes nonnegatively, and positive total volume forces some component to dominate. This avoids the invalid inference from full monodromy alone to dominance, and does not assume that the explicitly tested tuple belongs to a dominant component.

The intersection calculation is adequately supported. I checked the degree-one formula against Jarvis–Kimura [1], including the dual-bundle sign and the conversion from node inertia to the pulled-back boundary divisor. The treatment of repeated boundary factors and the stable-tree contraction is consistent with that normalization. Running the supplied quick certificate in a separate working copy reproduced

$$
\int_{\overline{\mathcal H}_c}\lambda_\chi^3=56,
\qquad \deg(\mathcal H_c/M_{0,6})=36000
$$

for all four class choices, including the four stated expansion contributions and the control examples. The same run reconstructed the integral reflections and the explicit tuple's parabolic pairing, obtaining rational dimension $12$, reduced norm $960$, and square class $15$. This reproduction uses the retained class algebra and character records. I inspected the GAP construction and maximal-subgroup exclusion, but did not independently rebuild the full group, character table, or maximal-subgroup classification. The five retained fixed-coset rows do imply generation for every prescribed branch choice; the provided reconstruction code makes that remaining computational input publicly reproducible.

Proposition 1.3 is an essential substantive part of the result. Rank and discriminant alone would not identify the target, as the supplied counterexample illustrates. The integral Morita argument instead supplies a unimodular representative at every relevant odd split prime. Together with the local classifications at the other primes, this gives local similarity to $T_0$. I checked the cited source [2]: Corollary 2.25 does give the required similarity Hasse principle when $|\Omega_D|=2$, and the local statements used here agree with its Theorems 2.1–2.4. The distinction between similarity and isometry, and the treatment of negative scalars by complex conjugation, are appropriately retained.

The cycle transfer also addresses the important logical steps. The hypotheses of the published real-character determinant theorem [10, Theorem D′] are satisfied. Rational primitive projectors, coefficient-field matrix units, quaternionic translation, and faithful scalar extension carry its cycle classes to $Q_D(B)$. Dominance supplies an actual open image; the proper relative Hilbert-scheme argument then extends these classes to every fibre. Finally, special orthogonal invariant theory gives the all-powers assertion precisely under the stated full-Hodge-group hypothesis. Specialization is not used to claim algebraicity of new exceptional tensors at special points.

The novelty distinction is convincing. Papers [14] and [15] establish cycle and monodromy results on Hurwitz loci of dimension at most two and leave the ambient compact threefold outside those loci open. This manuscript supplies dominance and the arithmetic identification needed to reach that ambient family. Paper [11] supplies the split-embedding obstruction, not this resolution of its non-embedding case. The external comparison with Markman's split-sixfold theorem [4] and van Geemen–Verra's quaternionic Prym constructions [9] likewise supports the distinction: neither establishes the present $T_0$ result. The imported determinant-cycle theorem, discriminant law, and translation mechanism are explicitly credited.

One minor reproducibility improvement is advisable: replace the references to unbundled `notes/literature.md` and `notes/theorem_R.md` with the corresponding bundled proof notes where applicable, and clearly separate any unavailable exploratory checks from the retained certificate. The central arguments do not depend on those missing notes. The informative abstract and the explicit limitations otherwise describe the contribution accurately.
