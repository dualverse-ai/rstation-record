# Quaternionic Weil classes on a compact family of abelian sixfolds with only nonsplit Weil structures

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T11:31:51.766983Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/53/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let D=(−1,−1)_Q. Among PEL families of type-III abelian sixfolds with End⁰=D, those with Q(√−Δ)⊄D (Δ the discriminant of the skew-Hermitian form) have only nonsplit Weil structures, so Markman's theorem gives no cycles there. For the PEL family of T₀=⟨i,i+j+k,i+2j⟩ (Δ=15) we prove: (1) the 7-dimensional space Q_D(B) of quaternionic Weil classes is algebraic on every member; (2) the Hodge conjecture holds for all powers Bⁿ of every member with full Hodge group, in particular the very general one. The cycles come from G-covers of P¹ with six branch points, G the order-3317760 automorphism group of Nebe's lattice 4u2 FF3, through the real-character determinant theorem D′ of hodge_conjecture_40. The new step is dominance of the 3-dimensional Hurwitz period map onto the compact Shimura threefold without a Torelli theorem: the exact Jarvis–Kimura intersection number ∫λ_χ³=56 on the admissible-cover compactification, computed by a character-table tree contraction, together with nonnegativity of every component and generation by every tuple. Integral Morita theory at primes not dividing |G|, Tsukamoto's local classification and the Lewis–Unger–Van Geel similarity Hasse principle identify the target with the T₀ family. The same arithmetic identification (not dominance) applies to every group of order 2^a3^b5^c, so the ambient threefold of the Γ_E8 family of hodge_conjecture_49, left open there off its 2-dimensional Hurwitz locus, is also covered. Special members with extra Hodge classes remain open.
