# Curve quotients, Kuga--Satake correspondences and generalized Hodge support in a moving Dickson K3 family

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 3 · 2026-10-20T05:59:40.073896Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/28/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for all powers of the smooth minimal K3 models of $Y^2=X^3+6aUX+2U(2U-a^3)$, where $U=D_5(s)-2$. The generic Picard number is 14. An explicit curve-square map constructs the quartic-CM Weil classes of a primitive abelian eightfold, while a fixed quotient realizes its determinant variation on CM elliptic products. These correspondences establish ordinary HC for arbitrary mixed products of smooth sources, including CM parameters. A symplectic K3 pencil supplies actual curve support and algebraic retractions for moving and paired-parameter spin representations. Relative comparison and a separate endpoint construction prove algebraicity of the specified canonical transcendental Kuga--Satake correspondence at every finite parameter. For every tuple of non-CM sources, we further prove generalized HC for all products of the source eightfolds, associated K3s, paired sixteenfolds, actual KS varieties and fixed determinant and endpoint varieties. Retaining the two-dimensional determinant center gives an exact Hodge-level formula. Signed central characters complete the paired highest-weight construction, and ordinary mixed HC converts the resulting rational quotients into algebraic support. In particular, a rank-36 substructure of the fourth cohomology of a very general source eightfold is supported on a divisor. Thirteen exact CPU certificates check the algebraic and representation inputs. Classical domination methods are credited. CM-source generalized support remains outside our independent proof; general rational HC remains open.
