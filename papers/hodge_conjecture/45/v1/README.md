Let E/Q be a smooth elliptic curve and B = {(z_1,z_2,z_3) in E^3 : z_1+z_2+z_3=O} =~ E^2 carry the symmetric (1,3)-polarization L = O_B(D_0). Extending hodge_conjecture_34 (v3) from the S_3-invariant K3 quotient locus P^3 across the swap-invariant locus |2L|_+^{g=+1} =~ P^5 (where for [D] in (P^5)_sm, S = X_tilde/<g,tau> is a minimal surface of general type with p_g=1, q=0, K_S^2=1, b_2(S)=21) and |2L|_+ =~ P^7, we establish: (1) Identifying H^0(B,2L)_+ =~ H^0(E,2O_E)^{tensor 3} with multilinear polynomials in (x_1,x_2,x_3) and constructing translation operators J_{a,f}(s_D) = 2(D_a f)s_D - f(D_a s_D) in H^0(B,3L)_-, the fixed-E period map on (|2L|_+)_sm subset P^7 attains sharp generic rank 7, its restriction to (P^5)_sm is generically etale dominant of full rank 5 onto D_IV(W_S) for W_S =~_Q U_hyp^{oplus 2} oplus <-1,-1,-3>, and its restriction to (P^4)_sm has generic rank 4, establishing generic rho(S)=14 and rho(X)=9 on P^5 when End(E)=Z (Theorem 2.1). (2) Along any codimension-1 Noether-Lefschetz locus NL_m subset (P^5)_sm where v_alg in <-1,-1,-3> (v_alg^2=-m<0) becomes algebraic, W_6 = v_alg^perp has rank 6, Witt index 2, and Cl^+(W_6) =~ M_4(Q(sqrt(-3m))), making the Weil classes on the Kuga-Satake abelian fourfold A_m (and its square A_m x A_m) algebraic with rational coefficients (Proposition 3.1). (3) Replacing the x_3^2 proxy of hodge_conjecture_34 by x_1 x_2 = A_0/A_2, every [D] in P^5 corresponds bijectively to a projective linear triple [L_2 : L_1 : L_0] determining Q_conic(x_3) = L_1^2 - L_0 L_2, exhibiting S across (P^5)_sm as u^2 = Phi_true(r,x_3)Delta_12(r,x_3) of bidegree (6,6), splitting P(D/swap -> D_bar/swap) ~ E x E_tilde_D, giving the Kummer square-class criterion for C_bar_L, and constructing a 6-hyperplane 2-torsion simplex in P^5 (Theorem 4.1). (4) For all k>=2 and 1<=m<=k, Lambda^m V_std|_{W(D_k)} =~ Ind_{H_m}^{W(D_k)}(lambda_m) has zero virtual negative terms, with star^2 = (-1)^n on W(D_{2n})^+ (Proposition 5.1).
