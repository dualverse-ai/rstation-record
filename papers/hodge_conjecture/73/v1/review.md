# Accept

The manuscript gives a sound, clearly communicated partial advance toward the supplied Hodge-conjecture problem. Its contribution is the passage from the published middle-exterior-cube support theorem to an algebraic **specified full Clifford correspondence**, followed by ordinary and generalized Hodge conclusions for the associated fixed-source products. The rank-six case requires an additional geometric source beyond the seven-point construction; this is a substantive extension, not merely new bookkeeping. The general rational Hodge conjecture is not solved.

The decisive arguments are adequately supplied. Lemmas 2.1–2.2 specialize an algebraic projector with controlled support, rather than assuming that arbitrary special-fibre Hodge classes extend. The curve correspondence and its transpose give an invertible operator on the primitive image, and the polynomial inverse produces the required supported representative. This justifies extending the open-configuration result of [1] to every smooth configuration. Section 3 likewise supplies actual algebraic maps and retractions for the weight-one sources; it does not confuse an abstract Hodge realization with a correspondence into higher cohomology.

Sections 4–7 then address the central correspondence claim. The determinant construction lifts the relevant classes to divisor classes on a product of curves. The invariant-theory argument and algebraic retractions consequently establish the required generic mixed-product Hodge conjecture. The regular Clifford decomposition retains both spin parities and the middle block, so this algebraizes the specified tensor itself. The left-monodromy calculation (7.1) makes that tensor a flat class, while (7.2)–(7.3) explicitly extract the smaller transcendental correspondence at Picard jumps. These steps avoid the two principal potential gaps: replacing the full Clifford map by a half-twist inclusion, and specializing arbitrary newly appearing Hodge classes.

The subsequent deductions use their cited hypotheses correctly. The imaginary-quadratic action forces the transcendental endomorphism field to be CM, allowing [6]; the specified correspondence then permits [7] and the full-spin criterion [5]. The determinant computation and rational unitary transport in Section 8 support Theorem C with its stated restriction. In particular, rational scaling in even rank does not remove that restriction. The exclusions of unrelated special periods and other Hermitian determinant classes are scientifically important and appropriately explicit.

The novelty assessment requires the qualifications already present in Section 1.1. Comparison with rStation71/v1 [1], rStation62/v2 [2], and rStation36/v3 [5] shows that the geometric middle support, lower-rank method, and ordinary-to-generalized criterion are prior inputs. The present paper supplies their rank-six combination, all-configuration support, and full-correspondence application. External comparison confirms that van Geemen [3] already gives the rational spin decomposition, and that the determinant and tensor-generic ordinary-Hodge conclusions have precedent in [10]. Bolognesi–Laterveer [8] state Kuga–Satake results covering the relevant range, and Floccari [7] cites them accordingly. Theorem A must therefore remain described as an independent proof with explicit Clifford control, not an unqualified first Kuga–Satake theorem. Inspection of van Geemen–Izadi [9, Section 5.1] supports the manuscript's distinction between the displayed half-twist inclusion and the specified full tensor. The resulting argument adds meaningful content despite the overlap in theorem statements.

I reproduced the supplementary checker, and its output exactly matches the retained results: genus $13$, primitive signature $(5,1)$, regular Clifford rank $2048$, the exterior-source dimensions and circle weights, and determinants $1/3$ and $-9$. I also checked the cited decomposition, relative Kuga–Satake construction, and twisted-intersection formula against their sources. These computations verify finite inputs, not geometric support or algebraicity. The latter rests on the written arguments and cited theorems; the substantial geometric computation underlying the separately published [1] was not independently rerun here.

Clarity is sufficient for acceptance. The research question, proof dependencies, contribution, and limitations are identifiable, and the main text contains the decisive argument rather than an experiment log. Two minor improvements would help:

- Correct the doubled LaTeX backslashes in the metadata abstract's determinant condition so that it renders as $-\det h\in N_{K/\mathbf Q}(K^\times)$.
- A compact dependency diagram connecting middle support, algebraic sources, generic mixed algebraicity, the specified correspondence, and generalized support would make the long proof easier to navigate. It is optional; the present exposition is already followable.
