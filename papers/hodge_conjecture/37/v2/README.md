# A Chern-character test for semiregularity on abelian fourfolds, and the failure of Markman's imaginary-quadratic secant sheaves

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 2 · 2026-10-22T09:56:42.700692Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/37/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Markman's secant-sheaf programme proves algebraicity of Weil classes on abelian $2n$-folds once a sheaf on an abelian $n$-fold, with Chern character on a secant line of the spinor variety, is semiregular. It works for genus-three Jacobians. Specializing the Calabi–Yau adjunction between evaluation and trace, we note that on abelian fourfolds the Buchweitz–Flenner map on $\mathrm{Ext}^2$ is the transpose of Hochschild evaluation. This gives a test on the Chern character alone: a simple fully semiregular sheaf satisfies $\chi(F,F)-2+2\,\mathrm{rk}\,c^1\le\mathrm{rk}\,c^2$, with equality for line bundles and the ideal sheaf of a point. The contraction ranks have a Hankel structure in the moments $j!\,\mathrm{ch}_j$. For Markman's imaginary-quadratic secant characters, $\chi=8d^2|c|^2\Theta^4/4!>0$. So on every abelian fourfold, for every ample $\Theta$, no simple sheaf with such a character is fully semiregular, and Perry's $G$-semiregularity for free translation groups does not rescue them. For the weaker condition that Markman's argument actually uses (injectivity on the image of evaluation, i.e. $\ker\mathrm{ob}=\ker\mathrm{ch}$), both of his genus-four families fail as well. They cannot deform to first order transversally to the Jacobian locus: for the theta-divisor sheaves, a non-Cartier surface does not survive the smoothing of the nodes of $\Theta$; for the union ideals, Brill–Noether loci are rigid (Lombardi–Tirabassi). By Künneth the failure lifts to his product objects on $X\times\hat X$, along a direction tangent to the Weil period domain. So the mechanism with Brill–Noether seeds is special to genus three, where the Jacobian locus is open. A variant covers his real-multiplication construction, e.g. for genus-four curves with an automorphism of order five. A second, noncommutative obstruction holds on every principally polarized fourfold: any secant ideal sheaf with a reduced component of codimension at least three, or with two surface components meeting transversally, fails, which also excludes Abel–Prym seeds. No new case of the Hodge conjecture is proved.
