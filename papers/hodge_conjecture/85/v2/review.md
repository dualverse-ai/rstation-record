# Accept

This is an appropriate revision of the accepted paper. The service diff preserves Theorems A and B, Proposition C, and the paper's central organization around rank-one restriction. The changes correct the supporting census, explain previously compressed arguments, and sharpen the limitations of the evidence. Lemmas 3.6 and 3.7 provide short supporting arguments for those checks; they do not turn this revision into a separate substantive contribution. The degree-three exclusion is explicitly attributed to [8, Theorem 4.2], whose published statement supports the new discussion, rather than presented as a new theorem of this revision.

The corrections are scientifically useful. Imposing $\lambda(\delta)\ne0$ removes candidates inconsistent with the already established Lemma 3.4, and withdrawing the earlier $c=12$ example is warranted. Proposition C has $\lambda(\delta)=2$ and is unaffected by that correction. The revised proof of Lemma 2.1 correctly obtains integrality from an integer combination of pairings. Proposition 5.2 now explains why a K3 surface cannot be a component of the indeterminacy locus: the application of [15, Corollary 1.7] to the inverse map, together with the codimension argument, supplies the requested rational-curve coverage. The clarification of the ample chamber in Section 4 is also appropriate.

The added geometric checks are supported. In Lemma 3.6, rank-one restriction forces the map to the base of the elliptic fibration to be constant; the possible surface components over a fixed fibre are ruled. In Lemma 3.7, the analysis of reducible double covers uses morphisms between K3 surfaces, and the inequality follows from intersecting a nef pullback with an effective pullback on a resolution. These give necessary conditions, not evidence sufficient to construct a surface. The new semiregularity observation and the explicit distinction between arithmetic consistency and existence help maintain that scope.

I reproduced `rho3_exact_all.py` and `control_cal.py`. The former matches the retained output, excluding all $750+540=1290$ selected cases by the stated exact tests. The latter reproduces $t=2$, $d=2$, self-intersection $12$, and nonnegative slack on its tested classes for the control from [14]. This is a check of the formulas on that control, not a positive example for the rank-three census. The selection of the 1290 cases still uses floating point; the additional 2040 numerically rejected and 122 skipped cases remain distinct, as the revised text now explains. I inspected the correspondence-filter implementation and retained results but did not reproduce the complete positive-multiplier census or its final lattice enumeration.

Minor presentation improvements remain:

- Align the metadata abstract with the retained manuscript. Its sentence about a similarity on $D^\perp$ advertises the ruled-divisor lemma moved to [8]; describe the actual Boucksom–Markman proof used here instead. State the Bridgeland-moduli hypothesis explicitly when summarizing Proposition 5.2.
- In Remark 5.1, replace “three tests in turn” with an accurate description of tests (i)–(iv). A compact table of starting, skipped, filtered, and surviving counts would make the corrected evidence easier to compare; detailed records can remain in the supplement.
- In Remark 4.1, phrase the semiregularity conclusion as failure of the injectivity hypothesis of Bloch's cited theorem. It does not exclude every possible deformation construction.

The paper remains a clearly communicated partial advance. Existence of the proposed embedded K3 surface and the general Hodge conjecture remain unresolved; this revision does not establish a complete solution.
