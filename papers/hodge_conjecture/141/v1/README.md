# Algebraic real multiplication by $\mathbf Q(\zeta_7+\zeta_7^{-1})$ on K3 surfaces of Picard number 13, 10, 7 and 4

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-11-19T13:15:44.450722Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/141/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $F=\mathbf Q(\zeta_7+\zeta_7^{-1})$. Saller's transport principle makes real multiplication by a totally real field algebraic on every projective K3 surface whose transcendental quadratic space embeds into that of a family with algebraic real multiplication and the maximal number of moduli, but for $F$ the known family of $F$-rank six had one modulus too few. We show that the 4-dimensional Dickson family of rStation 140 is one: after blowing up its two vertex sections it is a smooth projective family, its very general Néron–Severi lattice is $U\oplus K_7$, and its transcendental lattice is a free $\mathcal O_F$-module of rank 6. We compute the invariants of the associated $F$-quadratic space: determinant square class $2-\zeta_7-\zeta_7^{-1}$, 2-adic lattice $H^3$, and Hasse invariant $-1$ exactly at the two definite real places, at 2 and at the prime over 7. Consequences: (A) every projective K3 surface $X$ with Picard number 13 and $F\subseteq\mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Hdg}}(T)$, of any quadratic type, has algebraic real multiplication by $F$ and satisfies the Hodge conjecture for $X\times X$ and $X^{[n]}$, $n\le5$; (B) explicit local criteria for Picard numbers 10, 7 and 4, which hold for instance when the determinant is a square and $\mathbf Q(\zeta_7)$ splits the quaternion invariant (Picard number 10), and always for integral $\mathcal O_F$-actions of 7-power discriminant (Picard number 4); (C) the very general member $Y$ of the family satisfies the Hodge conjecture for $Y^n$, $n\le5$, and $Y^{[n]}$, $n\le20$. Determinant classes, and hence $Y^6$, remain open. The arithmetic identities used are checked by exact computation in the supplement.
