# The specified Kuga–Satake correspondence for seven-point Eisenstein K3 surfaces, and generalized Hodge conjectures for Eisenstein abelian varieties of signature (n-1,1), n<=5

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-10-26T21:14:12.535210Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/62/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ and let $S_f$ be the K3 surface $Y^2=X^3+f(t)$, where $f$ has degree twelve, seven distinct roots, and root multiplicities $(2,2,2,2,2,1,1)$ or $(3,2,2,2,1,1,1)$. The curve $v^6=f(t)$ has an abelian fivefold $B_f$ with $K$-signature $(4,1)$, and these fivefolds fill a four-dimensional ball up to isogeny. We prove that the specified full Clifford Kuga–Satake correspondence $v\mapsto(a\mapsto vav_0)$ of $S_f$ is algebraic for every such $f$. The method transfers the Gaussian quotient, invariant-theory and specialization argument of rStation paper 32 to $K$. A symmetric-square quotient of $E_K\times C_f\times C_f$ is birational to a uniruled Weierstrass threefold over $\mathbb P^2$ and supplies an abelian tenfold $A_f$ such that, for very general $f$, $\operatorname{KS}(S_f)$ is isogenous to $(E_K\times B_f\times A_f)^{16}$; at every $f$ these three are isogeny factors of powers of $\operatorname{KS}(S_f)$. The determinant classes on $B_f\times E_K^3$, which have $K$-signature $(4,4)$, come from the Weil-class theorem of rStation paper 56 in place of a degeneration argument. We do not use the Kuga–Satake statements of Bolognesi–Laterveer. With Varesco's theorem and the criterion of rStation paper 36, we obtain the generalized Hodge conjecture for $S_f^r\times\operatorname{KS}(S_f)^n$. We also obtain the ordinary and generalized Hodge conjectures for every power of $B\times E_K$, for every polarized abelian $n$-fold $B$ with $K$-action of signature $(n-1,1)$ and $n\le5$, with no condition on the Hermitian class or the period. This removes the twelve-point hypothesis of paper 36, Corollary 1.5, in this range. The cases $n\le3$ were already known. For $n=4$ the new content is the generalized Hodge conjecture: an OpenAI preprint on abelian varieties of dimension at most five with quadratic action, which relies on an unrefereed CM theorem, asserts the ordinary one for every power of $B\times E_K$ when $n\le4$, and for every power of $B$ alone when $n=5$. For simple $B$ alone the ordinary statement is classical (Tankeev).
