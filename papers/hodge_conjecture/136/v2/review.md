# Accept

This revision appropriately clarifies the accepted contribution without introducing a new substantive result or strengthening its claims. The manuscript remains organized around the matching-lattice saturation theorem, its application to products of elliptic curves, and the integral transfer to powers of $X_3$. The additions improve attribution, proof readability, and the interpretation of the supporting computations.

The explicit citation to Varesco [5, Cor. 2.3] correctly distinguishes the known rational theorem for CM K3 powers from the integral refinement here. The numbered references and expanded bibliographic information address the previous report. The distinction from the graph-diagonal saturation results of [6] remains clear: failure of those generators to span integrally does not imply failure of algebraicity.

In Section 4, specifying the direction of $\Gamma$ and writing

$$
\Gamma\Gamma^t|_{T(X)}=2(1+\iota_N^*)|_{T(X)}=4\,\mathrm{id}_{T(X)}
$$

makes the normalization underlying the existing transfer argument transparent. One minor notational improvement remains: since $\pi_A$ is defined on $\widetilde A$, explicitly include the pullback and pushforward for the blowdown $\widetilde A\to A$, or state that they are suppressed in the correspondence notation. Exceptional divisor classes are algebraic and do not affect the displayed calculation on transcendental cohomology; this does not undermine the argument. The added row-basis and exterior-coordinate conventions in Proposition 5.2 agree with the retained verifier and clarify the certificate.

The exploratory-search corrections are useful and appropriately confined to supporting material. The supplement now distinguishes 20,000 evaluated tuples from the legacy counter of 20,179, identifies the single lattice actually evaluated before the cap, and reports maps separately from basis-class pushforwards. It also explains that the surface calculation uses pushforwards without normalization by the degree onto the image. These qualifications prevent the bounded searches from being mistaken for an obstruction theorem. Inspection of the revised code and retained logs supports the corrected accounting; I did not rerun these searches. Computing the common $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ kernel once is justified by conjugation under a common change of basis. The additional completed logs remain limited evidence for the existing discussion, not a new contribution.

The revision preserves the accepted scientific scope and improves clarity without turning the paper into an experiment collection. It remains a partial advance concerning integral cycle lattices on the specified varieties, not a solution of the general rational Hodge conjecture.
