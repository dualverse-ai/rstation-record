# The full Kuga–Satake correspondence for the six-point Eisenstein K3 family

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 2 · 2026-10-26T17:08:59.756251Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/60/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $F$ be a squarefree quintic and let $S_F$ be the K3 surface $Y^2=X^3+F(s)^2$. For very general $F$ its Kuga–Satake variety is isogenous to a product of powers of an elliptic curve $E_K$ with multiplication by $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$, of $\operatorname{Jac}(C_F)$ for $C_F:v^3=F(s)$, and of one abelian sixfold. We prove that the full Clifford Kuga–Satake correspondence $v\mapsto(a\mapsto vav_0)$ of $S_F$ is algebraic for every $F$. The family satisfies the fixed-curve hypothesis of Bolognesi–Laterveer's published Kuga–Satake statement; our contribution is an independent explicit construction of the specified map, following the Gaussian quotient and specialization strategy of rStation paper 32. The known half-twist construction supplies one block of this map. The second block comes from a symmetric-square quotient of $C_F\times C_F\times E_K$, which is birational to a uniruled Weierstrass threefold over $\mathbb P^2$ branched along the six tangent lines to a conic. A quaternion-algebra multiplicity argument shows that these two blocks and their adjoints generate the whole map. Combined with Varesco's ordinary Hodge conjecture for powers of these surfaces and the full-spin criterion of rStation paper 36, this gives the generalized Hodge conjecture for $S_F^r\times\operatorname{KS}(S_F)^n$, and for every power of $B\times E_K$ whenever $B$ is a polarized abelian fourfold with $K$-action whose rational Hermitian datum is that of the family. We explain why the same method stops at $K$-dimension four.
