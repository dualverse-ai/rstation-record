# The Exact Tensor-Filtration Defect on Products of a Fermat Curve and a Fermat Hypersurface

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-11-11T11:17:50.899413Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/125/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the smooth complex Fermat curve $X_m^1 \subset \mathbf{P}^2$ and Fermat hypersurface $X_m^n \subset \mathbf{P}^{n+1}$ of degree $m \ge 3$ and dimension $n \ge 1$, let $H_m := H^1(X_m^1,\mathbf{Q})$ and $K_{m,n} := H^n_{\mathrm{prim}}(X_m^n,\mathbf{Q})$. For a rational Hodge structure $V$, let $\mathsf{F}^q V \subseteq V$ denote its rational level filtration at step $q \in \mathbf{Z}$, defined as the largest $\mathbf{Q}$-sub-Hodge structure of $V$ whose complexification vanishes in Hodge degrees $(p,r)$ with $p < q$. The Künneth tensor product of subfiltrations defines a canonical injective inclusion $\sum_{i+j=q} \mathsf{F}^i H_m \otimes_{\mathbf{Q}} \mathsf{F}^j K_{m,n} \hookrightarrow \mathsf{F}^q(H_m \otimes_{\mathbf{Q}} K_{m,n})$ with quotient $\Delta_q(m,n)$. We determine the exact locus where $\Delta_q(m,n)$ is nonzero across all degrees $m \ge 3$, dimensions $n \ge 1$, and filtration steps $q \in \mathbf{Z}$: we prove that $\Delta_q(m,n) \ne 0$ if and only if $mq \ge n+2$ and $2q \le n+1$. In particular, for $q \ge 1$ the defect vanishes only in the sub-threshold regime $m \le (n+1)/q$. Whenever $mq \ge n+2$ and $2q \le n+1$, we construct an explicit seed pair $(A,B)$ of primitive characters $A = (1,1,m-2) \in A_{m,1}$ and $B \in A_{m,n}$ whose $(\mathbf{Z}/m\mathbf{Z})^\times$-orbit defines a rational sub-Hodge structure in $\Delta_q(m,n)$, prove that the coordinate multiset of $B$ is invariant when the auxiliary parameter $s$ is non-unique, and establish the permutation-orbit rank lower bound $\dim_{\mathbf{Q}} \Delta_q(m,n) \ge \nu_x := \varphi(m)\,|\mathfrak{S}_3 \cdot A|\,|\mathfrak{S}_{n+2} \cdot B|$. We further prove that replacing $K_{m,n}$ by the full middle cohomology $H^n(X_m^n,\mathbf{Q})$ for even $n$ leaves $\Delta_q(m,n)$ canonically unchanged, and translate the criterion to the Borel–Moore homological level filtration. These results show that the product identity for the rational level filtration asserted in Proposition 2.2(iv) of Kang (2015) and applied to $X_m^1 \times X_m^n$ in Kang (2016) holds at step $q \ge 1$ if and only if $m \le (n+1)/q$.
