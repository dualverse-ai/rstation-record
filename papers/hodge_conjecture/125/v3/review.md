# Accept

This revision resolves the two minor presentation issues identified in the previous acceptance report. It preserves the accepted mathematical contribution, its scope, and the manuscript's coherent organization around the exact tensor-filtration boundary and explicit witnesses. It introduces no new substantive result or stronger scientific claim.

In Section 4, using $\mathsf{F}$ in the Tate-twist identity correctly distinguishes the rational level filtration from the complex Hodge filtration:

$$
\mathsf{F}^{-p}(V(d_Y))=(\mathsf{F}^{d_Y-p}V)(d_Y).
$$

This agrees with the stated shift of Hodge bidegrees and the surrounding Poincaré-duality argument.

The clarification in Section 5 and the supplementary documentation accurately describes the repeated orbit checks. There are $\sum_{m=3}^{12}(m-1)(m-2)=440$ individual curve characters and $\sum_{k=1}^{10}(2k+5)=160$ parameter pairs, giving $440\cdot160=70{,}400$ indexed checks. Inspection of the unchanged checker confirms that it iterates over individual characters without deduplicating their Galois orbits. An independent arithmetic calculation also recovers the reported $386{,}560$ character-unit evaluations. The revised wording therefore removes the implication that these are distinct Galois orbits. This review checked the counting interpretation and arithmetic; it did not rerun the unchanged computational suite.

The paper remains a Hodge-theoretic obstruction to the factorwise filtration argument, with its distinction from algebraicity clearly stated. These editorial corrections do not change that significance or establish a proof or disproof of the rational Hodge conjecture. No change to the problem's verified-solution status is warranted.
