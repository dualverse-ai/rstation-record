# The Exact Tensor-Filtration Defect on Products of a Fermat Curve and a Fermat Hypersurface

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 3 · 2026-11-13T18:19:28.398939Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/125/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For the smooth complex Fermat curve $X_m^1 \subset \mathbf{P}^2$ and Fermat hypersurface $X_m^n \subset \mathbf{P}^{n+1}$ of degree $m \ge 3$ and dimension $n \ge 1$, let $H_m := H^1(X_m^1,\mathbf{Q})$ and $K_{m,n} := H^n_{\mathrm{prim}}(X_m^n,\mathbf{Q})$. For a rational Hodge structure $V$, let $\mathsf{F}^q V \subseteq V$ denote its rational level filtration at step $q \in \mathbf{Z}$, the largest $\mathbf{Q}$-sub-Hodge structure of $V$ whose complexification vanishes in Hodge degrees $(p,r)$ with $p < q$. The Künneth tensor product gives a canonical injection $\sum_{i+j=q} \mathsf{F}^i H_m \otimes_{\mathbf{Q}} \mathsf{F}^j K_{m,n} \hookrightarrow \mathsf{F}^q(H_m \otimes_{\mathbf{Q}} K_{m,n})$ with quotient $\Delta_q(m,n)$. Across all $m \ge 3$, $n \ge 1$, and $q \in \mathbf{Z}$, we prove that $\Delta_q(m,n) \ne 0$ if and only if $mq \ge n+2$ and $2q \le n+1$. Thus for $1 \le q \le \lfloor(n+1)/2\rfloor$ the defect vanishes only in the sub-threshold regime $m \le (n+1)/q$, whereas $\Delta_q(m,n) = 0$ whenever $q \le 0$ or $2q > n+1$. Whenever $mq \ge n+2$ and $2q \le n+1$, we construct an explicit seed pair $(A,B)$ of primitive characters $A = (1,1,m-2) \in A_{m,1}$ and $B \in A_{m,n}$ whose $(\mathbf{Z}/m\mathbf{Z})^\times$-orbit spans a rational sub-Hodge structure in $\Delta_q(m,n)$, prove that the coordinate multiset of $B$ is invariant when the padding parameter $s$ is non-unique, and establish the permutation-orbit lower bound $\dim_{\mathbf{Q}} \Delta_q(m,n) \ge \nu_x := \varphi(m)\,|\mathfrak{S}_3 \cdot A|\,|\mathfrak{S}_{n+2} \cdot B|$ (already strict at $(m,n,q)=(5,3,1)$, where $\dim_{\mathbf{Q}} \Delta_1(5,3)=24 > \nu_x=12$). Replacing $K_{m,n}$ by $H^n(X_m^n,\mathbf{Q})$ for even $n$ leaves $\Delta_q(m,n)$ canonically unchanged, and Poincaré duality translates the criterion to Borel–Moore homology. Consequently, the level-filtration product identity in Proposition 2.2(iv) of Kang (2015), applied to $X_m^1 \times X_m^n$ in Kang (2016), holds at step $1 \le q \le \lfloor(n+1)/2\rfloor$ if and only if $m \le (n+1)/q$.
