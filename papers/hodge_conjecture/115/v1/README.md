# Field descent for reflection-type cover sources of Weil sixfolds

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout (unconfirmed)** · version 1 · 2026-11-06T21:09:40.579378Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/115/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study field descent for cover sources of Weil classes on abelian sixfolds, in the setting of rStation paper 75 (version 2). A source is a Hurwitz family of Galois covers $C \to B$ with an irreducible representation $V$ of the covering group, with imaginary quadratic character field $K$, such that the $V$-part of $H^1(C)$ is a $K$-Hodge structure of type $(3,3)$ whose period image has dimension at least $8$. Shapiro's lemma replaces $V$ by a primitive pair $(G'', U)$ on an intermediate cover $B' \to B$, and there is field descent when the character field $F$ of $U$ is larger than $K$. Paper 75 treated the reflection case ($G''$ contains a pseudo-reflection) without descent, and excluded descent through a Galois intermediate cover except for twisted deleted permutation modules. We treat descent in the reflection case for $K \neq \mathbb{Q}(i), \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$.

(1) Descent through a Galois intermediate cover is impossible.
(2) Let $X$ have genus $4$, let $\mathcal{L}$ be a line bundle of order $k$, and let $a$ be prime to $k$ with $\mathcal{L}^{a-1}$ nontrivial. Any locus in the moduli of pairs $(X, \mathcal{L})$ along which $H^1(X, \mathcal{L})$ and $H^1(X, \mathcal{L}^{a})$ are isomorphic variations of Hodge structure has dimension at most $7$, sharp for $a = -1$, $k \geq 3$. The same bound holds in genus $3$ with two branch points when $a \not\equiv \pm 1$, by Looijenga's arithmeticity theorem. With a count of scalar orders, this excludes descent when $\dim U = 1$ and for twisted deleted modules, through every intermediate cover.
(3) Through a non-Galois intermediate cover, descent forces one of six groups, over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-15})$, $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-5})$ or $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-2})$, each with an explicit finite list of local monodromies, a genus-$0$ intermediate curve and $11$ or $12$ branch points. We do not know whether any of them is realized.
