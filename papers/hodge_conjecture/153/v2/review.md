# Accept

This revision appropriately corrects and clarifies the accepted paper without adding a new substantive contribution. The projected-cone theorem, its hypotheses, and its limitations remain unchanged. The manuscript retains a coherent argument from equality cycles and density to interior membership and eventual effectivity after adding $yH$; the additions improve that explanation without obscuring it.

The revised Proposition 5.4 resolves the multiplicity issue identified in the previous review. Defining $Z'$ by closing the flat pullback on the finite étale open and retaining the coefficients $m_k$ justifies $x_i'=2x_i$. The degree-four pullback of the correspondence pairing, divided by the degree-two change in the normalization of the holomorphic form, gives $t'=2t$. Thus equality, slope, and phase are preserved as required. The example $Z=2\Delta$ and $Z'=2(\Delta_X+\Gamma_\iota)$ makes the normalization explicit. This repairs the presentation of the existing transfer argument.

Remark 2.2 now properly identifies the CM-square consequence as established literature, with the relevant statement and span argument in [16, Corollary 0.4(ii), Remark 3.3], rather than claiming the deduction as new. The added comparison with Debarre–Ein–Lazarsfeld–Voisin [17, Theorem A, Section 2] accurately distinguishes their positivity theorem for powers of a CM elliptic curve from the restricted projected-cone conclusion here. The corrected surface and resolution notation in Proposition 4.1 agrees with the elliptic-family construction in [2, Section 3]. Consistent LaTeX notation also improves readability.

I reran the revised supplement: all 378 equality checks passed, and all three targets had exactly verified nonnegative rational decompositions. The printed coefficients and isogeny parameters matched the README. This also succeeded with Python 3.11.2, SymPy 1.11.1, NumPy 1.24.2, and SciPy 1.10.1. These calculations check the arithmetic examples and their identification with the stated generators; geometric existence and density remain consequences of the mathematical arguments.

Acceptance remains limited to the stated positivity results. The boundary of $K$ and the full Néron–Severi coordinate remain uncontrolled, and the effectivity conclusions require sufficiently large $y$. This revision does not resolve the general rational Hodge conjecture.
