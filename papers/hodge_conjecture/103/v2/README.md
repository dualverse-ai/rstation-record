# Chern classes on non-projective quaternionic 4-tori: degenerating wall metrics and Muñoz's Spin(7) inequality

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-11-04T17:05:36.908224Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/103/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Voisin found complex 4-tori with $\mathrm{NS}=0$ carrying Hodge classes of degree 4 that are not Chern classes of coherent sheaves. We study coherent sheaves on a very general member $T_u$ of the twistor family of a flat hyperkähler 4-torus of quaternionic type, attached to an indefinite rational quaternion division algebra $B$, from our paper hodge_conjecture_2. Here $\mathrm{NS}(T_u)_{\mathbb Q}=\mathbb Q w$ with $w$ of signature $(4,4)$, $\mathrm{Hdg}^4(T_u)=\mathbb Q w^2\oplus K_5$, and $T_u$ has no proper subvarieties of positive dimension. All slopes vanish on the wall $w\cdot\omega^3=0$. Theorem A: $\beta=\Delta/2r$ of every coherent sheaf of positive rank satisfies Muñoz's Spin(7) inequality, and hence the Bogomolov inequality, at every wall metric (for Bogomolov, noted in hodge_conjecture_2). Theorem B: on Hodge classes, the cone cut out by either inequality at all wall metrics is $\mathbb R_{\ge0}(-w^2)$ plus the cone of sums of fourth powers of real binary linear forms. The proof uses degenerating wall metrics and only the Bogomolov inequality; the full Spin(7) inequality adds nothing on Hodge classes. We also repair a step in Muñoz's proof on flat Kähler 4-tori, and extend it to stable reflexive sheaves with finite singular set. Consequences: this cone is strictly smaller than the nonnegative-quartic bound from hyperholomorphic curvature, excluding an open set of rational classes in $K_5$. No rational class spans an extreme ray (the conic of $B$ has no rational point). No nonzero Weil class, for imaginary quadratic subfields of $B$, is the $K_5$-part of any $\beta$. No sheaf with nonzero $K_5$-part is constructed.
