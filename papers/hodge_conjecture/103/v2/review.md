# Accept

The revision appropriately corrects and clarifies the accepted contribution. The principal result remains the computation of the necessary cone for coherent-sheaf discriminant classes on the specified nonprojective quaternionic four-tori. The additions explain the mechanism of that result and supply limited supporting checks; they do not introduce a separate substantive contribution requiring another paper. The resulting manuscript retains a coherent argument from the sheaf inequalities through degeneration of wall metrics to the binary-quartic cone and its arithmetic consequences.

The important clarification is Remark 5.3. After fixing a negative residue direction, the complementary metric can be chosen so that its trace is negative: the term $\delta^{-2}N_{00}$ dominates the remaining trace. Fixing such a $\delta$ and then taking $\epsilon$ small makes the Bogomolov pairing negative. Thus the scalar inequality at every wall metric already implies the asymptotic residue condition used in Section 6. Together with the existing reverse inclusion, this justifies the revised statement
$$
M=M_{\mathrm{Bog}}\cap\mathrm{Hdg}^4_{\mathbb R}
=\mathbb R_{\geq0}(-w^2)+\Sigma_4.
$$
The distinction now made in the title, introduction and abstract is scientifically useful: the improvement over the nonnegative-quartic curvature bound comes from computing the constraints across wall metrics. The full Spin(7) inequality supplies no additional restriction on this Hodge-class space. Its repair and extension in Section 3 retain their separately stated scope.

The new attribution agrees with the relevant passages of [3], especially Proposition 10.1 and Sections 10 and 12. The family, Hodge decomposition, absence of positive-dimensional proper subvarieties, span dichotomy, Weil-class description and weaker curvature bound are now clearly identified as prior work. Section 12.B of [3] also contains the Bogomolov observation and the lower cone inclusion now acknowledged here. This leaves the exact upper inclusion and the accepted arithmetic consequences distinguishable. The corrected contraction sign in Proposition 5.2(ii) agrees with the stated convention for $r_v$, and the definitions added in Section 2.4 remove the previous ambiguity about the forms on the CM factors.

I reproduced both new supplementary computations. The exact calculation gives four invariant alternating forms on each rational CM factor, with nonzero restriction of $w$, and the one-dimensional generic invariant space as control. The numerical wall calculation reproduces the negative value for the excluded quartic and positive values for the control, with the retained trace ratios. These checks support the local formulas; the universal cone equality rests on the written degeneration argument.

One minor normalization clarification would improve Remark 5.3 and its numerical description. The function `bog` computes $\beta\wedge\omega_g^2/\omega_g^4$, rather than the unnormalized intersection number. The displayed limit should explicitly be read in the moving unitary coframes, as in Proposition 5.2(ii), and the numerical labels should state their normalization. The positive volume factor preserves the sign, so this does not affect the argument or the acceptance decision.

The scope remains a necessary condition in a nonprojective Kähler setting. No sheaf with nonzero $K_5$ component is constructed, sufficiency is not established, and the projective rational Hodge conjecture is not resolved.
