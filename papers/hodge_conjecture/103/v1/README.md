# Spin(7)-instantons and Chern classes on non-projective quaternionic 4-tori

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-11-04T08:51:13.130658Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/103/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Voisin found complex 4-tori with $\mathrm{NS}=0$ carrying Hodge classes of degree 4 that are not Chern classes of coherent sheaves. We study coherent sheaves on a very general member $T_u$ of the twistor family of a flat hyperkähler 4-torus of quaternionic type, attached to an indefinite quaternion division algebra $B$ over $\mathbb Q$. Here $\mathrm{NS}(T_u)=\mathbb Q w$ with $w$ of signature $(4,4)$, $\mathrm{Hdg}^4(T_u)=\mathbb Q w^2\oplus K_5$ with $\dim K_5=5$, and $T_u$ has no proper subvarieties of positive dimension. All slopes vanish on the wall $w\cdot\omega^3=0$ of Kähler metrics. We prove Muñoz's Spin(7) inequality for polystable bundles on every flat Kähler 4-torus, repairing one step of his proof, and extend it to stable reflexive sheaves with finite singular set. Theorem A: $\beta=\Delta/2r$ of every coherent sheaf of positive rank lies in the cone $M$ of classes that satisfy the inequality at every wall metric. Theorem B: $M$ is $\mathbb R_{\ge0}(-w^2)$ plus the cone of sums of fourth powers of real binary linear forms, spanned by classes of linear foliations by quaternionic 4-planes. The proof uses metrics that degenerate along the wall. Consequences: $M$ is strictly smaller than the bound from the curvature of hyperholomorphic bundles, excluding an open set of rational classes in $K_5$; no rational class spans an extreme ray, since the conic of $B$ has no rational point; and $K_5$ is spanned by Weil classes for imaginary quadratic subfields of $B$, but no nonzero Weil class is the $K_5$-part of any $\beta$. No sheaf with nonzero $K_5$-part is constructed.
