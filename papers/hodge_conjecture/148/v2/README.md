# Strongly positive classes that are not pseudoeffective: a Galois-place obstruction on K3 squares and abelian fourfolds

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-11-21T07:41:48.579905Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/148/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Demailly's Hodge conjecture for strongly positive currents (HC+) asks that every closed strongly positive current with class in the real span of integral Hodge classes be a weak limit of nonnegative combinations of integration currents. Babaee and Huh disproved it on a toric fourfold with an extremal current in an effective class. HC+ also fails if a rational Hodge class with a smooth closed strongly positive representative is not pseudoeffective. We give such classes. (A) Let X be a projective K3 surface such that the totally real part E0 of the Hodge endomorphism field of its transcendental lattice is not Q. Then H^4(X × X, Q) contains a Hodge class in the interior of the strongly positive cone that is not pseudoeffective. It is algebraic on the families of rStation 140 and 141 and on the Kummer surface of E_i × E_√−2. (B) On B = (E_i × E_√−2)^2 there is a class in N^2(B)_Q with a constant strongly positive representative that is not pseudoeffective. So Psef^2(B) ≠ Strong^2(B), a negative answer to a question of Debarre, Ein, Lazarsfeld and Voisin, in the smallest dimension and codimension where a gap can occur. Strong positivity sees a Hodge correspondence only through its eigenvalue at the real place carrying H^{2,0}. An effective surface acting on the transcendental part by u, with projections of degrees x1 and x2, satisfies s(u)^2 ≤ x1 x2 at every real place s, by the Hodge index theorem or by conjugating the cycle under Aut(C). The second proof shows that pseudoeffective classes have strongly positive Aut(C)-conjugates and the strongly positive cone of B is not Aut(C)-stable. We ask when the two cones agree; in odd degree, finitely many surfaces cannot reach the mixed-sign vertices of the bound. The computations on B are checked in exact arithmetic.
