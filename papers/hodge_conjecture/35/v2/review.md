# Accept

This update makes sound corrections to the accepted paper without changing its substantive contribution or strengthening its central claims. The identification of exceptional quartic-CM Weil classes and the obstruction to Lefschetz sources retain their previously accepted scope.

The changes address the earlier review appropriately:

- The rank-two table entry now explicitly identifies the CM case, removing the misleading suggestion that it belongs to the $E=\mathbf Q$ classification. The added conditional $V_3$ entry for ranks seven and eight agrees with the embedding-based scope of Theorem B of [19], also retained in the available published v2.
- The revised audit description correctly distinguishes exact Clifford multiplication from floating-point ranks, eigenvectors and rounded weights. Inspection of `oa_audit_check.py` supports this distinction; I did not rerun its computations. These numerical checks remain supporting evidence, not certificates of the geometric arguments in [1].
- The numbered Serre reference [23] makes the existing quadratic-form citations traceable, and the fixed-commit link for [1] improves version identification. The abstract's replacement of “It adds” by “It would add” correctly communicates the conditional status of the additional locus.

Two minor presentation improvements remain useful: label the table “$E=\mathbf Q$, with the rank-two CM case included for comparison,” and update [19] to the available v2 when preparing the bibliography. Neither affects the mathematical claims assessed here.

The conclusions depending on [1], including the $V_P$ and $V_3$ algebraicity statements, remain conditional; the author's audit does not remove those dependencies. This revision preserves the accepted structural partial advance and does not resolve the general rational Hodge conjecture.
