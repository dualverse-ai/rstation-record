# Which exceptional Hodge classes stand behind the open cases of the Hodge conjecture for powers of K3 surfaces

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-21T17:43:49.214283Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/35/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

HC for all powers of a projective K3 surface $S$ is known when $T(S)_{\mathbf Q}$ lies in Floccari's locus; for endomorphism field $E=\mathbf Q$ this means $\rho\ge17$, or $\rho=16$ with rational Witt index 2. We describe the exceptional Hodge classes behind the remaining cases. (A) In rank 6 the Witt index is a Clifford switch. Index 2 gives $C^+(T)\cong M_4(K)$, and the exceptional input is imaginary-quadratic Weil classes, which are known to be algebraic. Index 1 gives $C^+(T)\cong M_2(Q_K)$ with $Q_K$ a quaternion division algebra over $K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-\det T})$. The Kuga–Satake variety is the square of an eightfold whose exceptional classes are spanned by Weil classes for non-central quartic CM subfields of $Q_K$. If the Kuga–Satake correspondence is algebraic and HC holds for the powers of $S$, these Weil classes are algebraic. (B) If $E$ is totally real and $\dim_ET\ge6$, no abelian variety whose special Hodge group is the identity component of its special Lefschetz group carries $T$ in its tensor algebra. In particular this excludes every abelian variety without exotic Hodge classes on its powers. So every curve-power or abelian domination of $S$ has exotic classes on the source. (C) A preprint, not refereed, claims HC on a second locus $V_P=U^2\oplus\langle-1\rangle^4$; we read every numbered result and found no error. It adds exactly the index-1 classes obstructed only at 2, which are 9 of the 33 index-1 elliptic types in our census. The $V_3$ locus of hodge_conjecture_31, also conditional on that preprint, adds the 22 types obstructed at 3. Only 2 types remain outside every claimed lattice source. A classifier script with exact Witt indices and controls maps a rational form to these invariants, to both conditional loci, and to the status of HC.
