# Accept

This is an appropriate clarification of the accepted paper. The revision makes the conditional status of the sixfold computations substantially clearer without introducing a new substantive contribution or obscuring the existing argument. The accepted results on trianalytic seeds, the exclusion of $K3^{[2]}$ seeds, and the fibre-preserving Eisenstein calculation remain intact. The paper remains a useful partial investigation of nonsquare Hodge similarities; it constructs no seed and proves no new case of the Hodge conjecture.

The main improvement is Section 5.2. Proposition 5.2 now explicitly assumes (I1) and (I2), separates the cited cohomological structure results from the author-computed inputs, and explains the proposed representation-theoretic origin of the $H'$-term. The linearity and Schur's-lemma argument explains the form of (I1) under the stated equivariance assumption; it does not independently establish that assumption for the universal class being used. Likewise, (I2) remains an assumed projection formula whose geometric intersection calculation has not been justified independently. These limitations are now visible where the proposition is stated, rather than left to a qualification after its proof.

The revised Table 1 correctly distinguishes absence of an arithmetic obstruction from existence of an algebraic cycle. The dependency statements in Section 1.3, Proposition 6.2, the incidence discussion, and the abstract also improve the interpretation of the congruences and numerical windows. The unchanged scripts supply checks of the resulting arithmetic, not verification of the equivariance or transversality inputs. This revision should not be understood as upgrading those inputs to proved results.

A few minor consistency edits would complete the clarification:

- In Section 1.2's $K3^{[3]}$ bullet and Section 8's “Where they can be” bullet, explicitly repeat “assuming (I1) and (I2)” for the integrality conclusion. In the final sentence of Section 8, the assertion $b \equiv 6 \pmod 7$ should retain the multiplier-$75$ setting, the assumption $W\not\subset E$, and the two inputs of Proposition 6.2(b).
- After Proposition 5.2, replace “It does not construct a class” with “It does not construct an algebraic cycle or an effective surface.” The proposition does assert existence of an integral cohomology class under its hypotheses; the unresolved issue is geometric realization.
- When documenting (I1) and (I2) further, specify the exact normalization and equivariance statement and the actual transversality condition. The current formulas are sufficiently explicit as conditional inputs, but their proposed geometric justification is not yet independently reproducible from the explanation alone.
- Convert the author-key bibliography labels to consistent numbered references as required by the journal format.

These are local presentation issues, not grounds to overturn acceptance. The revised status distinctions preserve the paper's scientific value and make its limitations easier to assess; no separate submission or expansion is warranted for these explanatory changes.
