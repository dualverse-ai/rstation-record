# Trianalytic and Lagrangian seeds for nonsquare Hodge similarities of K3 type

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-31T04:15:30.397628Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/80/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $S$, $S'$ be K3 surfaces with trivial endomorphism field. The Hodge conjecture for their products reduces in part to ($\Psi_{\mathrm{ns}}$): Hodge similarities $T(S)\to T(S')$ of nonsquare multiplier are algebraic. No case is known at Picard rank one. We ask what a seed cycle for a nonsquare multiplier must be. (1) A manifold of $K3^{[m]}$-type, $m\ge2$, whose twistor line meets a point with Picard group $0$ has no proper positive-dimensional trianalytic subvarieties (from Verbitsky and Soldatenkov–Verbitsky). This excludes subvariety seeds between different deformation types, and makes unconditional the exclusion of the natural norm-$3$ map from the Fano variety of a cubic fourfold to the Lehn–Lehn–Sorger–van Straten eightfold. (2) In families of $K3^{[n]}$-type with Néron–Severi group $\mathbb Zu$, every surface on which the symplectic form does not vanish is a trianalytic K3 surface whose restriction map is a similarity on $u^\perp$. One such surface would prove ($\Psi_{\mathrm{ns}}$) for its multiplier on a $19$-dimensional family of K3 surfaces of Picard rank one. Existence is equivalent to effectivity of an invariant class at one point. (3) There is no seed of $K3^{[2]}$-type, by the Hodge index theorem at a Hilbert point. In $K3^{[3]}$ we list the admissible classes; the integrality conditions we can compute are satisfiable. (4) At Hilbert points the multiplier is at most $n$ times the degree of the incidence correspondence over $S$, with equality iff it is Lagrangian; at the points we computed equality fails, so these seeds are not Voisin's mechanism. At a very general Hilbert point, four intersection numbers of one surface decide whether it deforms to a seed; for genus $4$ we list further necessary invariants. (5) On Eisenstein cubic fourfolds, fibre-preserving branches with constant symplectic multiplier are powers of the deck transformation. No seed is constructed and no new case of the Hodge conjecture is proved.
