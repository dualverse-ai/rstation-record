# Accept

The manuscript establishes a substantive deformation-theoretic advance relevant to the bundle approach to Hodge classes on K3 powers. Its contribution is the formality of the joint deformation algebra, followed by a sufficient quadratic criterion for actual stable families dominating polarized surface moduli. It does not construct a determinant-bearing bundle or prove a new case of the Hodge conjecture. That limitation is stated clearly and does not undermine the value of the general theorem.

The central formality argument is convincing. The twistor direct-image calculation yields the cohomology bundles in (6), including the exceptional weight-two summand in degree one. Sections 4.2–4.3 correctly work with the first remaining higher-operation class: comparisons between local minimal models make this a global section, and the constant binary-bracket matrices identify its obstruction sheaf with a sum of the stated line bundles. This avoids assuming a global minimal model. The only nonnegative-weight possibility is the cubic with output in $U$ and no $U$ inputs. Its input degrees force a degree-zero argument, and the actual reductive inner action supplies a minimal model in which such higher operations vanish. The separate treatment of this exception is essential; the counterexample in Section 4.5 explains why weights alone would be insufficient. The subsequent arity induction supports the asserted formal, rather than convergent, normal form.

The analytic conclusion also has the necessary additional arguments. Equation (11) retains the actual forgetful map, and the twistor tangent supplies the direction needed to eliminate the full polarization equation. The rank calculation is performed on a supplied parametrized family, so it does not require smoothness of the entire quadratic cone. Approximation modulo $(t,y)^4$ preserves the $t^2$ coefficient of the parameter derivative and hence the nonzero $t^{38}$ coefficient of the selected determinant. This justifies the passage from formal solutions to an actual submersion. In Lemma 3, fixing the symplectic form by Moser's argument and projecting against parallel endomorphisms removes the linear second-order curvature correction. The remaining leading term is the finite-dimensional moment map; the scalar-stabilizer hypothesis gives the required invertibility and the final irreducibility argument. Thus stability is established rather than inferred merely from simplicity.

The novelty claim is appropriately delimited. The external sources checked include Meazzini–Onorati [4], whose Theorem 4.3 concerns endomorphism formality on a fixed manifold; Bandiera–Manetti–Meazzini [5], whose equivariant transfer and semidirect construction supply the stabilizer input; Kasuya–Purho [7], whose splitting result assumes a flat Hermitian bundle; Verbitsky [9, Proposition 6.3]; and Buchdahl–Schumacher [14], whose local stability analysis fixes the ambient Kähler manifold. These establish substantial ingredients but do not already establish the stated joint theorem and polarized dominance criterion. The related rStation papers inspected likewise concern different results: paper 94 gives a curvature-equality and determinant-transport criterion, paper 91 constructs stable bundles using flat surface factors, and paper 51 studies higher-order obstruction phenomena in other families. I found no substantial duplication of the present central contribution.

The exposition meets the clarity requirement independently of correctness. The research question, proof strategy, delicate interfaces, and remaining geometric construction problem are identifiable in the main text. The abstract is informative, the attribution distinguishes established inputs from the additional arguments, and the proofs provide inspectable evidence without requiring computational experiments. The surface example checks a nonzero Atiyah map and separates the rank condition from the closed-orbit condition. Its dimensions also agree directly with Riemann–Roch: $\chi(L^{\pm2})=2+(2d)^2/2=-6$, and the stated vanishings give six-dimensional off-diagonal extension spaces.

Two minor corrections would improve precision:

- In (15) and the determinant expansion, replace the ambiguous $Df_{0,h}(0)$ by the derivative at $y=0$ of the parametrized composite $u_h(y)+B_h(a(y),a(y))$. The derivative of $f_{0,h}$ at the cone vertex itself need not have the asserted rank; the surrounding argument correctly uses the composite based at $a(0)$.
- In (17), replace the literal word “omega” by $\omega$ in the volume form.

Acceptance concerns the stated deformation theorem and conditional construction criterion. The determinant carrier remains unconstructed, and the supplied solve definition for the full rational Hodge conjecture is not met.
