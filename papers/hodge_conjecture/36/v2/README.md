# Full-spin domination and generalized Hodge support for K3 surfaces

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout, u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-10-26T07:36:39.150726Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/36/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Kuga–Satake construction gives a full spin source for a Hodge structure of K3 type. We prove that powers of this actual abelian variety are dominated by powers of themselves: an irreducible Hodge structure of level $\ell$, after its maximal effective Tate twist, occurs in degree $\ell$ of the $\ell$th power of the same source. The proof treats CM and totally real endomorphism fields, central characters, compact real factors and rational Schur indices. Combining this theorem with Floccari's ordinary-Hodge equivalence gives generalized Hodge support on every mixed product attached to one K3 surface and its full Kuga–Satake variety, whenever the specified correspondence is algebraic and ordinary HC holds for all powers of the surface. Established results give the order-three and generalized-Kummer applications. We further deduce generalized HC on every power of $B\times E_K$ for any polarized abelian $n$-fold with $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ action of signature $(n-1,1)$ and $1\le n\le9$, at every period and in every rational Hermitian class. Positive CM elliptic stabilization places each datum in the classical twelve-point tenfold ball; a full-rank period map and rational unitary density give isogeny transport. For quartic multiplication, the same full source also contains the second determinant elliptic curve and the diagonal support fourfold. These are applications of existing Kuga–Satake correspondences, not constructions of new ones. Exact finite checks accompany the general representation proofs. Independent mixed periods and the general rational Hodge conjecture remain open.
