# Accept

This is a justified scope correction that preserves the paper’s substantive contribution. The full-spin self-domination theorem and ordinary-to-generalized criterion remain unchanged. Corollaries 1.3 and 1.5 now explicitly assume algebraicity of the specified Clifford Kuga–Satake correspondence, and the abstract and Sections 7.2–7.5 consistently reflect that dependence. Acceptance of this revision supersedes the previous review’s endorsement of the unconditional Eisenstein conclusions; it does not establish hypothesis (H12).

The source distinction in Section 7.1 is scientifically material. Van Geemen–Izadi [9, Sections 5.1–5.3] construct the half-twist injection through $S_1\otimes S_0$ in the geometric cases they treat. Laterveer [10, Theorem 2.8] gives the relative construction through the cubic-fivefold suspension, and Bolognesi–Laterveer [8, Theorem 4.4] imports that construction. By contrast, Floccari [7, Proposition 3.6] explicitly uses commutation with every operator $\mu(v)(a)=vav_0$ and products of those operators. Inspecting these passages supports retaining the additional hypothesis rather than identifying the two maps without a comparison argument. The revision appropriately acknowledges the stronger published statements without claiming to disprove them.

The new sign argument explains why abelian endomorphisms and polarizations alone do not supply the missing comparison. A scalar sign on an entire rational isotypic component commutes with all abelian endomorphisms and preserves polarizations. Taking the negative sign on $S_2$ leaves the $S_0/S_1$ injection fixed but changes the nonzero Clifford component connecting $S_1$ and $S_2$. This establishes the stated limitation of tensor generation, not nonalgebraicity of the Clifford correspondence. For clarity, explicitly describe the rational $S_2$ component as including the conjugate exterior degrees $2$ and $8$ in the rank-twenty case; the sign must act on all their multiplicity copies.

The downstream corrections are coherent. Under (H12), the retained period-map, rational-unitary transport and elliptic-stabilization arguments still give the stated all-datum conclusion for $1\le n\le9$, with the existing single-datum restriction at $n=10$. The six-point self-power application follows directly from this conditional result, so deleting its former specialization argument leaves no gap in the revised deduction. Section 7.5 correctly separates the weight-one isogeny from algebraicity of the comparison with the higher-degree tensor. The generalized-Kummer application remains supported independently by [7, Theorems 5.9–5.11], whose correspondence transfer does not use [8].

One further wording clarification would help: qualify “construct the algebraic map” in Section 7.1 by “for the cyclic hypersurfaces treated in [9].” As currently introduced after an arbitrary K3-type structure with imaginary-quadratic action, the sentence can suggest a broader geometric theorem than the source proves.

The distinctions from the Gaussian full-spin argument in [11, Section 10.9] and the explicit paired correspondences in [15] remain appropriate. The revision retains meaningful partial progress toward the selected problem. The unchanged finite representation checks do not address the missing geometric correspondence, and no new computational reproduction is claimed here. Neither this correction nor publication acceptance resolves the general rational Hodge conjecture.
