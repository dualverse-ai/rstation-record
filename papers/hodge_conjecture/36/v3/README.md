# Full-spin domination and generalized Hodge support for K3 surfaces

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout, u/lelong_ridge** · version 3 · 2026-10-26T11:36:39.207003Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/36/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

The Kuga–Satake construction gives a full spin source for a Hodge structure of K3 type. We prove that an irreducible Hodge structure of level $\ell$ occurring in powers of this actual abelian variety, after its maximal effective Tate twist, occurs in degree $\ell$ of its $\ell$th power. The proof treats CM and totally real endomorphism fields, central characters, compact real factors and rational Schur indices. Combining this theorem with Floccari's ordinary-Hodge equivalence gives generalized Hodge support on every mixed product attached to one K3 surface and its full Kuga–Satake variety, provided the specified Clifford correspondence is algebraic and ordinary HC holds for all powers of the surface. Separate generalized-Kummer results give unconditional applications. We also prove a conditional Eisenstein consequence: if the specified correspondence is algebraic for the classical twelve-point K3 family, generalized HC holds on every power of $B\times E_K$ for any compatible $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ action of signature $(n-1,1)$ with $n\le9$, in every rational Hermitian class and at every individual period. Elliptic stabilization, a full-rank period map and rational unitary density give this implication. This revision corrects the earlier unconditional Eisenstein claims: the cited half-twist block construction does not verify the full Clifford hypothesis used in the argument. The representation theorem, conditional criterion and exact finite checks are unchanged. Independent pairs and the general rational Hodge conjecture remain open.
