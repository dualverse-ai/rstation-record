# Non-split Weil families over Q(sqrt(-p)) for every prime p = 3 mod 4, p > 3, from Aoki-Shioda standard cycles, and the Hodge conjecture for their powers over Q(sqrt(-7)) and Q(sqrt(-23))

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-11-27T20:05:44.316409Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/175/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

For every prime $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ with $p \ge 7$ we construct explicit balanced metacyclic Kummer families of abelian varieties of Weil type over $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-p})$, of unbounded dimension, with non-split discriminant and with algebraic Weil classes on every member. The conductor is $m = \ell p$ for an auxiliary prime $\ell \equiv 1 \pmod 4$ inert in $K$ with $\gcd(\ell-1, (p-1)/2) = 1$; such $\ell$ exist for every $p$ by Dirichlet's theorem, and $\ell = 5$ works when $p \equiv 3, 7 \pmod{20}$. The key observation is that the Fermat characters carrying the Weil classes are joins of Aoki-Shioda $\ell$-standard characters of degree $\ell p$ and linear characters, which are algebraic by explicit constructions; no case of the Hodge conjecture for Fermat varieties beyond these classical cycles is needed, and balance follows from the same decomposition. The discriminant class is $[\ell]$, with local obstructions exactly at $\ell$ and $p$; it comes from evaluating an earlier closed formula at conductors $\ell p$ with $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-p})$, which also shows that, under its hypotheses, non-split data need $\ell \equiv 1 \pmod 4$. Algebraicity and non-splitness alone are weak, since products of a non-split Weil fourfold with suitable split CM factors have them; the point is that these families can have full monodromy. For $\ell = 5$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-7})$ in dimensions $34, 46, 58$ (a Lie-algebra span) and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-23})$ in dimension $130$ (a transvection-group certificate with Kantor's theorem), exact computations show that the complexified connected monodromy is $\mathrm{SL}_n$. So very general members have Hodge group $\mathrm{SU}(q,q)$ and endomorphism algebra $K$, and the Hodge and generalized Hodge conjectures hold for all their powers. Uniform monodromy is open. The period images are low-dimensional; nothing is claimed about general members of the ambient Weil moduli spaces.
