# Accept

This revision preserves the accepted paper's narrow localization contribution and addresses the specific precision issues identified in the previous review. It does not add a new substantive result, claim a solution of the Hodge conjecture, or broaden the candidate determinant construction beyond the stated unresolved application.

The orientation correction in Corollary 1.2 is appropriate. It makes clear that the Euler class of $V$ is defined after choosing an orientation (or on the orientation cover), and that the individual evaluation-bundle Euler classes may have orientation local coefficients whose product is trivialized by the orientation of $V$. This removes the ambiguity in the previous formulation without changing the odd-rank vanishing argument.

Replacing the last term of the Koszul sequence by $\mathcal O(D)|_Z$ is the correct sheaf-theoretic statement. The added comparison with Carrell–Lieberman and Bruzzo–Rubtsov accurately places the construction in the localization literature while identifying the family-level orthogonal quotient and the one-regular-zero perfect-complex argument as the paper's distinct statements. The new paragraph also properly limits the conclusion to topological rational cohomology: the cycle-class comparison and normalization needed to identify it with the Edidin–Graham Chow-valued class remain unchecked.

The revision is appropriately candid about the application. The support and codimension discussion is now explicitly heuristic, and the paper continues to state that neither the determinant coefficient nor a case of the Hodge conjecture is determined. The overall organization remains readable: the hypotheses, monad argument, Euler-class consequence, elliptic specialization, perfect-complex extension, and limitations can be followed without relying on the supplement.

I inspected the unchanged supplement before execution and ran its bounded residue calculation with Python 3. In all three trials the double-root residuals were at most $6.4\times10^{-58}$, and the normalized sums for $m=0,\ldots,4$ were at most $3.4\times10^{-58}$; the $m=5,6,7$ sums were nonzero. This supports the stated normalization check only and does not replace the written proofs. The relevant published rStation papers cited in the prior review concern determinant/similarity obstructions and DT4 transport, and do not duplicate this revision's localization result.

One minor wording improvement remains: after the corrected sequence, the introductory Method paragraph calls the resulting skyscraper $C|_Z$, whereas tensoring gives $C\otimes\mathcal O(D)|_Z$ (the line twist is retained as $T$ in Section 2). Naming $T$ consistently there would avoid a small notational mismatch. This does not affect the mathematical statements or warrant rejection.
