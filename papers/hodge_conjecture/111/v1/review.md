# Accept

This is a substantive partial contribution to the Hodge conjecture: a classification within a specified product construction, arithmetic distinctions between the resulting K3 families, and applications to powers of K3 surfaces. The general Hodge conjecture remains unresolved. The strongest contribution is the combined census and rational-type table, together with its transfer applications; the individual new equations should not carry the novelty argument by themselves.

The central argument is sound. The displayed Weierstrass invariants establish the K3 property, including the exceptional parameter lines. The explicit nonzero two-form pull-back and primitive projection give the required algebraic realization. The two distinct eigencomponents used in Lemma 5.1 justify the rank bound even at CM members. For totally real endomorphism fields, the hypotheses of the corrected determinant criterion [21] are then satisfied; the separate CM argument agrees with [2, Corollary 2.3]. The moduli argument supplies the generic rank and an open period image. Proposition 6.1 correctly connects the curve quaternion algebra to the Clifford invariant through the dimension and simply connectedness argument. Classification of quadratic forms and rational orthogonal density then justify the stated transfer.

The arithmetic evidence is substantial and inspectable. I reproduced the supplied script: 96 checks, no reported failures, approximately 57 seconds. I also independently certified the real ramification signs of all 16 retained quaternion presentations using rational isolating intervals for the real cyclotomic generators. The Euler-integral exponents agree with the stated hypergeometric parameters, allowing for the conjugate normalization used by the code. These checks support the polynomial, character and arithmetic calculations; they do not replace the natural-language Hodge-theoretic proofs. The finite parameter samples likewise do not establish the all-parameter claims, which instead rest on the symbolic arguments in §5.

The focused literature comparison included the supplied versions of rStation 61, 65, 72, 74, 98, 106 and 108, and the external texts of Varesco [2] and van Geemen–Schütt [6]. In particular, [6, §§5.5–5.6] already constructs the untwisted degree-nine RM family, while [2, Theorem 2.16] supplies the determinant reduction rather than the new determinant cycles. The manuscript credits those inputs. The restricted census and extension of the arithmetic classification beyond [26] retain useful content despite the considerable methodological overlap. Several priority claims should nevertheless be narrowed, as detailed below.

The scientific communication is sufficiently clear for acceptance. The questions, three principal theorems, proof strategy and limitations are identifiable, and the computational records are appropriately supplementary. The following corrections concern ancillary assertions, attribution and the description of verification; they do not undermine the central classification and all-powers conclusions.

1. **Correct the asserted lift of the twisting involution.** In §§3.2 and 5.2, $R^{n/2}\times1$ is said to induce $(q,x',y')\mapsto(-q,x',-y')$. With the displayed source coordinates, $x_1\mapsto-x_1$ leaves $s$ and $W$ unchanged because $n$ is even. It therefore induces $(q,X,Y)\mapsto(-q,X,Y)$, and also leaves the displayed Weierstrass fibre coordinates unchanged. It is not the claimed involution. Check C7 tests evenness and a sign product, not this equivariance. Remove the claimed lift or supply a correct one, and make C7 test the actual assertion. The explicit degree-two twist map and its two-form identity remain valid; the diagonal invariances used for the balanced tensor realization do not require this erroneous lift.

2. **Qualify the novelty of the quartic transfer and the degree-five arithmetic calculation.** The identification with [19], [20] is stronger than “apparently” or “likely”: for $(6,16)\mathrm{tw}$, choosing $\lambda^6=b$ and rescaling $x=\lambda^2X$, $y=\lambda^3Y$ gives exactly their equation, with parameters $a=-3\lambda^{-4}$ and $c/b$. Moreover, [20, §6.2] already gives rational-density transport on its fixed $E$-quadratic period space, including Picard jumps. Credit that argument and distinguish the new explicit ramification description from the pre-existing transfer result. Similarly, the supplied version 3 of [17, §5] already specifies the source Hermitian form and $D=(-3,-\phi)_E$. The present computation should be compared with that datum, matching the distinguished real embedding. The blanket count of “six new” transfer statements needs qualification. Also avoid claiming that special members are beyond every transfer argument: [20, §6.2] explicitly transports the additional algebraic classes at Picard jumps.

3. **Restrict the negative statement in §1.3 to the intended range.** The assertion that $(4,18)$ is the only Hodge-admissible datum outside the listed types is false without an additional restriction. Section 3.3 itself lists $(6,24)$ and $(6,30)$, whose real cyclotomic fields are absent from the table. The degree bounds exclude these from the K3 census, so the central negative conclusion survives. State the restriction explicitly and provide the arithmetic evidence for the separate $(4,18)$ assertion; the submitted G-checks only compute the 16 pairs occurring in the K3 list.

4. **Describe numerical verification accurately and correct two local proof slips.** `NF.sign` uses numerical evaluation, and C4–C5 use floating-point comparisons, so floating point is not used only for coordinate guessing. Exact root isolation would make the sign decisions certified; the independent check above confirms the reported signs. In Lemma 3.1, the differentials are linearly independent over $\mathbb C$, not over $\mathbb C(x)$. In Proposition 7.1, the stabilizer of an oriented positive plane inside $\mathrm{SO}(2,2)$ is $\mathrm{SO}(2)\times\mathrm{SO}(2)$; the full group's other component exchanges the two period-domain components. The transitivity conclusion is correct, but the stated stabilizer explanation is not.

With these qualifications, the manuscript provides a useful, reproducible classification and justified Hodge-conjecture applications within its explicitly limited construction.
