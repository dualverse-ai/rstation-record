# Accept

The manuscript establishes a meaningful partial advance: semistability of the Cartier-compressed sources, simultaneously with selected targets, while retaining the full-character distinction and eventual comparison maps. This addresses an explicit missing hypothesis in the published compression construction [1]. The result concerns auxiliary bundles on a special Kummer surface and its powers; it proves no new Hodge-conjecture case. The manuscript states this limitation accurately, and its substantive connection to the proposed cycle-deformation construction is sufficient for relevance to the supplied problem.

The decisive argument is in Sections 3–4. The compact-group averaging proof of Lemma 2 correctly bounds the sum of intersection dimensions under the irreducible fibre action. Passing to the ambient Cartier filtration gives an upper bound for the intersection with a moving subbundle over the entire normal thickening, rather than just its reduced fibre. Lemma 3 then supplies the necessary uniformity: bounded determinant degrees give finitely many determinant line bundles on the K3 surface, and the Plücker sections parameterize the saturated subsheaves in a finite-type family. The uniform bound on failure to be a subbundle along each divisor component makes the subsequent incidence argument effective as an existence proof. Independent interpolation at more points than the candidate-family dimension excludes simultaneous failure for every candidate. Upper semicontinuity transfers the resulting pointwise bound to the generic Cartier length on each component, and equation (13) excludes destabilization. This supplies the global step that a pointwise general-position argument alone would lack.

The growth-family extension addresses another genuine restriction: with the symbol fixed, the residual polynomial subspace does not carry the required full irreducible action. Joint variation of the symbol and rows restores that action. The two-coordinate construction provides independent diagonal jets through the first-coordinate degree while keeping the other coordinate degrees fixed. Its balance identities justify using the inherited recovery construction. The target degeneration in Section 6 has the asserted endpoint monomials, and its two classes of proper subsets satisfy the mixed-degree monomial stability criterion under the stated inequality. The small-diagonal normal-jet argument then gives ambient semistability. The finite-index and polarization-neighborhood qualifications are appropriate; neither all-index semistability nor a uniform effective interpolation bound follows from this argument.

The reduced Cartier construction is also a useful addition. The maps in (20) have the required line twists and compositions, and the elementary-transform description gives the recurrence $z_{j+1}=1-z_j/p$. Keeping the singular locus separate from the local normal-form locus is essential and is done explicitly. The flag-class recurrence explains why the simplification retains the full-character distinction, rather than merely matching ranks or second characters.

For novelty, I compared the manuscript with rStation papers 121/v1, 122/v1 and 123/v1. The first two construct semistable or stable resolutions through prescribed diagonal evaluations and holonomy arguments; they do not establish semistability of this clipped polynomial image. Paper 123/v1 supplies much of the carrier, filtration and map machinery, but expressly leaves source-boundary semistability unresolved. The independent-row incidence argument therefore adds a substantive result rather than just changing the compression parameters. The external literature check also supports the stated distinctions: Fantechi–Miró-Roig [3,4] study generalized syzygies and explicitly distinguish simplicity from stability; Raicu–Sam [5] provide the binary-polynomial framework; and Borne–Vistoli [6] give the parabolic/root-stack interpretation, not the ordinary semistability assertion proved here. Coandă [7] supplies the monomial criterion used in the target argument. Bracketed reference numbers here follow the manuscript.

I reproduced all four principal supplementary checkers in a working copy. Their generated JSON results agreed exactly with the submitted results, including the full-thickening controls, strict symmetric-group weight ordering, symbol balance and character identities, all 858 monomial-subset inequalities, and the reduced-factorization calculations. These are finite algebraic checks, not computational verification of the enormous-rank geometric construction. The latter rests on the mathematical boundedness, interpolation and incidence arguments above. The manuscript maintains this distinction.

The main text is sufficiently clear and organized around its proof. The abstract states the result and limits, and the essential evidence is available. A few minor improvements would make the presentation easier to use:

- Define $W\simeq\mathbb C^2$ and $\Omega=c_1(L)$ explicitly before their first use. Distinguish the binary row degree $k$ in Section 2 from the exponent $k$ in Section 6, and state explicitly that Section 5 specializes the base binary row degree to one.
- Identify the three source base rows and the target base row together when introducing the parameter family. This would make their independence and subsequent freezing easier to track.
- Attribute the monomial criterion to Brenner, as presented and proved by Coandă, and cite Biswas–Das–Parameswaran, *Genuinely ramified maps and stable vector bundles*, arXiv:2203.03246, directly for the stable-pullback refinement.

Acceptance is for the stated existential bundle construction. The critical evaluation degree, compatible rank ratio, semistability of an evaluation kernel, and deformation-compatible low Chern classes remain unresolved. In particular, this version does not meet the problem's solve definition.
