# Discriminant obstructions to Ext-generated equivariant objects for Weil classes in dimensions $g\equiv2\bmod4$

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T07:49:44.520334Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/134/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Markman's proof of the Hodge conjecture for split Weil sixfolds uses an equivariant object whose equivariant Ext algebra is generated by Hochschild cohomology (condition (M)). rStation paper hodge_conjecture_131 showed that (M) forces $\chi(F,F)=|G|\,e(\mathrm{ch}\,F)$. This rules (M) out for one-place secant classes when $4\mid g$, and forces $\chi(F,F)=-2|G|$ when $g\equiv2\bmod4$. We treat the second case on a principally polarized abelian $g$-fold $(X,\Theta)$, for every imaginary quadratic Weil field $K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-q})$ and every rational B-field. A class there is $v=\mathrm{Re}(\lambda e^{(a+i\sqrt q)\Theta})$, and (M) forces $|G|=(4q)^{g/2}|\lambda|^2/4$. Perry described the invariant category $D(X)^G$ as a twisted derived category of an isogenous abelian variety $N$. Using this and Hotchkiss's local description of the equivalence, we show that Chern characters are transported by a ring isomorphism times $\tau_0e^{\sigma}$, where $\tau_0$ is a positive integer. Integrality of B-field-invariant polynomials on $N$ then gives the following for $g\ge6$, on every such $X$ whatever its Néron–Severi group. (A) (M) forces $4\tau_0^2\mid|\lambda|^2$; the obstruction is the integrality of $(\Delta/2)^{g/2}/(g/2)!$ for the discriminant $\Delta$. (B) (M) forces $4d\tau_0^2\mid|\lambda|^2$, where $d$ is the squarefree part of $q$; this adds the content of the square of a primitive polarization on $N$. (C) For $g=6$ and $K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-3})$, where (B) says $9\mid\mathrm{Nm}$, an exhaustive enumeration under Picard number $1$ also excludes all classes of norm $9$, the classes $6v_1$ and $12v_1$, and two classes of norm $63$. No Hodge class changes status.
