# All-Genus Parabolic Determinant Laws, Connected SO*(6) Monodromy in G_2592 over D_3, and Hurwitz Non-Dominance in Gamma_{E_8}, G_2592, and 160#84

We investigate the reach of finite-group Hurwitz curve families into the compact non-embedding locus of Theorem C for polarized abelian sixfolds of Albert Type III with quaternionic multiplication by a definite quaternion algebra D over Q. First, for every base genus gamma >= 0 and unitary representation on W = D^d, we prove a universal Bruhat factorization of handle blocks, showing handles contribute a square to the parabolic reduced norm. Under D-linear splittings and Study reduced-norm positivity, this yields the all-genus parabolic discriminant law Delta(T_par) = prod nu(c_j) mod squares. Since sum r_D(c_j) = 3 - 2(gamma - 1)d < 0 excludes gamma >= 2 for all d >= 2 with m = 3 and W^Pi = 0, no Hurwitz family in Gamma_{E_8} in U_2(D_2) can dominate a Theorem C PEL threefold in any base genus gamma >= 0. Second, we classify the degree-600 pure-braid orbit of the Gamma_{E_8} Hurwitz surface over M_{0,5} and its 148 irreducible boundary components under the boundary stabilizers P_{D_{i,j}}, proving the Mayer-Vietoris toric defect vanishes identically and establishing potential good reduction across all boundary components. Third, over D_3 = (-1,-3)_Q, we construct G_2592 = G_648 x| C_4 in U_3(D_3) and a 5-branch-point Hurwitz family with Delta_sqfree = 2, realizing the odd-prime Theorem C locus with connected monodromy SO*(6) (31-adic Lie rank 15) and proving the rational and general Hodge conjectures for all powers of a very general member. Finally, every odd-coset element of G_2592 has r_D = 3 via invertibility of bar(h)*h + I_3, while kappa|_{G_1296} = (det_K)^3 is a character, bounding r <= 5 in G_2592 and proving strict rigidity r = 3 in 160#84 over D_5.
