# Accept

The revision adds a substantive, supported extension to the accepted paper. Section 10 supplies the missing Gaussian spin source, mixed determinant cycles, and ordinary and generalized Hodge conjectures for the stated products attached to one source. Appendix B gives a more explicit geometric realization on a nonempty open. These are meaningful advances beyond the previously accepted curve-product embedding. The conditional comparison with the original double-cover family remains conditional, and the general rational Hodge conjecture remains unresolved.

The new geometric argument in §§10.2–10.3 is convincing. The order-32 quotient has the stated Weierstrass function field and negative canonical intersection on a resolution. The argument uses normality and the projection formula without assuming a crepant resolution. Uniruledness supplies divisorial support for third cohomology. The norm identity on the finite étale quotient open then transfers support to the invariant subspace of the original product, without incorrectly identifying invariant cohomology with all cohomology of a resolution. The character calculation, including the sign from interchanging degree-one factors, selects precisely the twenty-dimensional exterior-square space in (10.2). Its weight-one realization and algebraic retraction supply the required abelian tenfold.

The determinant construction in §10.4 also supports the claim. I checked Schoen’s Theorem 2.0 and Corollary 3.1 in [19]: the tuple $1^3,3^3,2^4$ satisfies the required opposite-pair condition. The proposed degeneration has a genus-seven core and three Gaussian elliptic tails, with one node above each attachment and a tree as dual graph. Consequently the compact-type limit permits specialization in an abelian scheme. The inverse tail character produces exactly the determinant space of type $(4,4)$ on $P\times E^3$. This proves algebraicity of the named classes at every smooth core; it does not require an invalid specialization principle for arbitrary new Hodge classes.

The subsequent deductions retain the necessary central characters. At the full group, contractions and the supplied corrected volume tensors generate the invariants. The spin restriction (10.21) identifies the actual Kuga–Satake factors, so ordinary mixed HC applies to the specified correspondence. At special points, §10.8 specializes a fixed flat KS class and explicitly extracts its smaller transcendental version. Floccari’s Theorem 3.5 and Remark 3.4 in [26] have the scope used here. Together with the cited CM-endomorphism K3 theorem, they justify ordinary HC at the jumps rather than merely at very general parameters.

The generalized-support extension in §10.9 is an additional argument, not a consequence of specialization alone. The relation $F=F^+\mathbb Q(i)$ makes the Galois action permute the chosen embedding factors or dualize all of them together. This justifies the level formula (10.28). The common determinant shift places each partition inside the required rectangle; its columns are supplied by the complete spin representation, including both half-spin constituents. Rational Hom descent and ordinary mixed HC then turn the resulting lower-weight realization into a correspondence with the claimed support codimension. The direct Prym differential and odd-rank Hermitian scaling in §10.1 likewise justify transport to every signature-$(1,4)$ Gaussian fivefold. This remains an existential isogeny construction.

Appendix B provides useful additional geometry. The local charts address the singularities of the Gaussian fiber itself before invoking deformation. The defect calculation and the two parabolic cohomology dimensions agree with $b_3=24$. Crucially, the boundary-kernel argument and (B.18) identify the anti-invariant line-cylinder image with the particular quotient invariant; a dimension match alone would not establish this. The explicit line construction is appropriately restricted to a nonempty open, whereas the support argument of §10.3 covers every smooth branch configuration.

I reproduced all eight added scripts using Python 3.11, SymPy 1.11.1 and python-flint 0.9.0. Their outputs match the retained certificates, apart from elapsed time: 84 summary checks and the conic equation certificate, including the bounded central-character and rectangular-partition controls. These checks support the finite algebra, branch data, incidence calculations and representation identities. They do not independently prove the geometric support, deformation, specialization or descent arguments assessed above.

The novelty should be described with several distinctions preserved. Van Geemen [17] already supplies the abstract half-twist/spin decomposition, and Abdulali [21] supplies the domination framework. The relevant sections of rStation papers 27 and 28 already use uniruled quotients, prescribed KS tensors and support arguments. The present Gaussian quotient, determinant degeneration, explicit cylinder comparison and treatment of smaller Gaussian groups add substantial content. Also cite [31]: its Theorem 1.2 already asserts ordinary HC on all powers of a fivefold with imaginary-quadratic action, including special points. That overlap does not encompass the present mixed products with the tenfold, the specified geometric correspondences or generalized support; the independent construction and these stronger conclusions should be the stated advance.
