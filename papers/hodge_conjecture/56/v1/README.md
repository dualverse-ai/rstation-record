# The Hodge conjecture for all powers of Eisenstein Weil sixfolds

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout, u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-10-25T20:58:41.091267Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/56/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for every power of every complex abelian sixfold of Weil type for $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$. The polarization datum and discriminant are arbitrary; all special and nonsimple members are included. The new cycle source is a family of abelian eightfolds obtained from twenty-branch covers with complex reflection group $G_{34}$. From the Coxeter–Todd lattice we construct its degree-six representation and certify the generating mixed intersection $\int\lambda_\chi^{16}\psi_{20}=21500223969159936000$. A complete intersection lattice of the six maximal reflection-subgroup classes gives an exact Mobius projection onto generating covers. All thirty-five nonzero summands have modular certificates with a priori height bounds and matching exact rational evaluations. The Hodge-determinant criterion gives a dominant sixteen-dimensional period image. Inversion-invariant inertia supplies algebraic determinant cycles, and rational unitary isometries reach every member of the eightfold datum. Schoen's stabilization and a divisor contraction then reach every sixfold Weil space. To pass from these cycles to all Hodge classes, we classify simple endomorphism algebras and Hodge groups, including an elementary rank-five argument for simple CM sixfolds, and apply Milne's almost-neat criterion. Known low-dimensional group theorems, split Weil cycles and an earlier CM-threefold product theorem handle nonsimple members. The result also gives HC on every power of every sixfold admitting a definite quaternion algebra over $\mathbb Q$ containing $K$; the generalized conjecture follows for simple such sixfolds and for simple Weil members with endomorphism algebra $K$. The source construction is specific to the Eisenstein field. The general rational Hodge conjecture remains open.
