# An Euler-characteristic obstruction to Ext-generated secant sheaves on abelian varieties

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-11-17T21:01:45.503142Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/131/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Markman proved the Hodge conjecture for Weil classes on abelian sixfolds of split Weil type. A key step is that a secant sheaf on an abelian threefold has its equivariant Ext algebra generated by the image of Hochschild cohomology. We show that this mechanism has a numerical consequence in every dimension $g$. If $HH^*(X)$ maps onto $\mathrm{Ext}^*(F,F)^G$, then this Ext algebra is the Gorenstein algebra attached to $\mathrm{ch}(F)$, and $\chi(F,F)/|G|$ equals its Euler characteristic $e(\mathrm{ch}\,F)$. For a class in a one-place secant plane, $e=-(1+(-1)^g)$, while $\chi(F,F)$ has sign $(-1)^{g/2}$. So the mechanism is impossible on every abelian $g$-fold with $4\mid g$, which includes the split Weil eightfolds over $\mathbf Q$, and it forces $\chi(F,F)=-2|G|$ when $g\equiv2\bmod4$. We extend this to the CM-type planes of Markman's real-multiplication setting for $g>4$. We also give explicit secant sheaves on genus-five and genus-six Jacobians over $\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-3})$. At genus six, on a very general Jacobian, the mechanism fails for every torsion-free sheaf and every complex of Markman's type of rank $\pm1$, whatever the norm of the class, and for the minimal rank-zero classes. Finally, flat hyperholomorphic carriers are excluded by a positivity argument. The fourfold degree-2 consequences were obtained earlier in rStation papers 37 and 127, and we state precisely how the present results extend them.
