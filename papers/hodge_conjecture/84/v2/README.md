# Multipliers of smooth K3 surfaces in hyper-Kähler fourfolds of K3^[2]-type

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-11-03T17:05:38.159865Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/84/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $X$ be a projective hyper-Kähler fourfold of $K3^{[2]}$-type whose transcendental Hodge structure has endomorphism field $\mathbb Q$, and let $\Sigma\subset X$ be a smooth K3 surface on which the symplectic form does not vanish. Restriction to $\Sigma$ multiplies the Beauville–Bogomolov form on $T(X)$ by a positive rational number $c$, the multiplier of $\Sigma$, which is an integer when $\rho(X)\le2$. When $T(X)$ is the transcendental lattice of a K3 surface $S$, a non-square multiplier gives an algebraic Hodge similarity $T(S)_{\mathbb Q}\to T(\Sigma)_{\mathbb Q}$ that is not a scaled isometry, and the Hodge conjecture for $S\times\Sigma$. Generalized Nikulin surfaces give $c=2$ on families of dimension $12$. We show that this mechanism gives only squares at small Picard number. If $\rho(X)=1$, then $X$ contains no smooth K3 surface. If $\rho(X)=2$, then $c\in\{1,4,16\}$. For Hilbert squares $S^{[2]}$ with $\rho(S)=1$, $c=4$. The proofs combine the self-intersection formula $[\Sigma]^2=\int_\Sigma c_2(X)-24$ with Fujiki's relations, an explicit description of $H^4(X,\mathbb Z)$, a determinant identity for the restricted Néron–Severi form, and one positivity condition: the restricted form minus $c$ times the Beauville–Bogomolov form is non-negative on nef classes. The cohomological conditions alone leave $c=19$ on moduli spaces $M_S(2,h,28)$ over K3 surfaces of degree $114$. There we construct an integral Hodge class and a K3 surface of degree $6$ that satisfy all of them. The positivity condition, with the Bayer–Macrì description of nef cones, excludes this case. For immersed surfaces with double points the bound on $c$ disappears.
