# Accept

This is an appropriate corrective revision of the accepted paper. It preserves the central contribution—the mixed-curvature identity, dense-leaf rigidity, and their obstruction to invariant hyperholomorphic carriers—without introducing a new substantive result or an unfocused expansion. The changes improve the accuracy of the motivation, scope, and presentation.

The restored genericity and nonzero-rank qualifications in §1.1 agree with Markman [1, Proposition 10.2.1 and Theorem 1.1.2]. The revised §1.2 correctly distinguishes producing a carrier along twistor lines from establishing transport through the required Weil family. These changes remove an overstatement of the connection to the Hodge conjecture while retaining the substantive motivation for the obstruction.

The odd–odd summand added in §2.1 corrects the even-cohomology decomposition. Proposition 2.2(a) now specifies the normalized top forms used in its product model, addressing the previous omission concerning integration conventions. The revised references to reflexive sheaves with admissible hyperholomorphic connections better match Theorem 5.3. In §6.2, support of the double-dual quotient in codimension at least three does preserve the first two Chern classes; this is a useful qualification of the earlier discussion, rather than a new carrier theorem.

The numerical results are now consistently described as diagnostics. Their evidence and the theorem proofs are unchanged; I have not rerun the computations for this revision. Moving the search chronology to the supplement, shortening the abstract, and adding the full reference to Floccari–Fu [12] improve readability and attribution. The resulting manuscript remains organized around a coherent mathematical argument, with the distinction from the already discussed one-place constructions intact. The map $A_W\to M$ in §6.4 gives the appropriate direction for a prospective pullback construction and remains explicitly an open route.

Two minor clarifications remain advisable:

1. Define the newly introduced $d$ in §1.1. In the notation of [1], $d=\dim_K H^1(A,\mathbb Q)=4$ here, so $d>2$ is automatic in this setting.
2. In §6.2, explicitly say that applying the theorem through $\mathcal E^{\vee\vee}$ also requires that this reflexive hull satisfy the connection and invariance hypotheses. Equality of $c_2$ alone does not supply those hypotheses or imply that the original non-reflexive sheaf is a flat bundle. Likewise, replace §7’s shorthand “the only hyperholomorphic carriers are flat” with the precise general conclusion that their endomorphism bundles are flat; flatness of $\mathcal E$ itself has the additional hypotheses of Theorem 5.3(2).

Acceptance remains for the restricted obstruction proved in the paper. The revision neither establishes algebraicity of the relevant Weil classes nor meets the supplied solve definition for the rational Hodge conjecture.
