# Two-place rigidity: hyperholomorphic carriers of norm-type Hodge classes on Weil eightfolds are flat

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-11-16T18:06:05.964293Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/130/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Markman reduced the Hodge conjecture for Weil classes on abelian eightfolds $A = X\times\hat X$, with $X$ an abelian fourfold with real multiplication by a real quadratic field $F$, to the algebraicity of a flat deformation of a class $\kappa(E)$ satisfying a genericity condition. We test a second route, used by Markman for Weil fourfolds of discriminant 1: deforming a Cayley class with a hyperholomorphic sheaf in Verbitsky's sense, here on the eightfold itself. For decomposable classes $w$ in Markman's secant space, the classes $k_w=\kappa_2(\Phi(F_1\boxtimes F_2^\vee))$ are invariant under a product $G_w$ of two Spin(3,4)'s, one per real place, and are nonzero at both places for every $F$, $q$ and $w$. The main results are proved. For a bundle with $\mathrm{Sp}(1)$-invariant curvature on a flat hyperkähler torus preserving the two places, the $(2,2)$ place component of $c_2(\mathrm{End}\,E)$, paired with $\omega_1^3\omega_2^3$, is a positive multiple of $\int|F^0_{12}|^2$. If $F_{12}=0$ and both lattice projections are dense, the bundle is an orthogonal parallel sum of projectively flat bundles. Consequence, with Verbitsky's and Bando-Siu's results as recalled inputs: on a very general Weil eightfold, a reflexive (or twisted) sheaf with an admissible hyperholomorphic connection for a flat hyperkähler structure through its complex structure, whose $c_2(\mathrm{End})$ is $G_w$-invariant, has flat $\mathrm{End}$. So no $k_w$ has such a carrier. No case of the Hodge conjecture is proved; complexes, non-reflexive sheaves, carriers on non-flat hyperkähler manifolds and non-invariant classes remain open.
