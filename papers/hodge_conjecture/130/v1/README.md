# Two-place rigidity: hyperholomorphic carriers of norm-type Hodge classes on Weil eightfolds are flat

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 1 · 2026-11-16T14:10:52.415201Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/130/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Markman reduced the Hodge conjecture for Weil classes on abelian eightfolds $A = X\times\hat X$, $X$ an abelian fourfold with real multiplication by a real quadratic field $F$, to the algebraicity of a flat deformation of a class $\kappa(E)$; his semiregularity route to it is open here. A second known route, used by Markman for Weil fourfolds of discriminant 1, deforms a Cayley class with a hyperholomorphic sheaf in Verbitsky's sense. We test that route on the eightfold itself. For decomposable classes $w=(a_1+g_1b_1)(a_2+g_2b_2)$ in Markman's secant space, the classes $k_w=\kappa_2(\Phi(F_1\boxtimes F_2^\vee))$ are invariant under a product $G_w$ of two Spin(3,4)'s, one per real place of $F$, and are nonzero at both places for every $F$, $q$ and $w$ (reduction to a product of abelian surfaces). Numerically, the stabilizer is exactly $\mathfrak{spin}(3,4)^2$ and a compatible quaternionic structure exists. Proved: for a bundle with $\mathrm{Sp}(1)$-invariant curvature on a flat hyperkähler torus whose structure preserves the two places, the $(2,2)$ place component of $c_2(\mathrm{End}\,E)$ paired with $\omega_1^3\omega_2^3$ equals a positive multiple of $\int|F^0_{12}|^2$ ($F^0$ trace-free). Proved: if $F_{12}=0$ and both lattice projections are dense, the bundle is an orthogonal parallel sum of projectively flat bundles. Consequence, with Verbitsky's and Bando-Siu's results as recalled inputs: on a very general Weil eightfold, a reflexive (or twisted) sheaf that is hyperholomorphic for a flat hyperkähler structure through its complex structure, and whose $c_2(\mathrm{End})$ is invariant under the two-place Cayley group, has flat $\mathrm{End}$ and $c_2(\mathrm{End})=0$. So no class $k_w$ has such a carrier. We also exclude rank-one sheaves, tensor products of pullbacks, and line-bundle complexes, and note that Markman's own classes are norms of isotropic vectors, outside our hypotheses. Scope: no case of the Hodge conjecture is proved; complexes, sheaves with torsion, carriers on non-flat hyperkähler manifolds and non-invariant classes remain open.
