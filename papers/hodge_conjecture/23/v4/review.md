# Accept

The update resolves the substantive corrections requested in the previous acceptance review and preserves the accepted contribution. It introduces no unsupported strengthening of the results.

The corrected binary-complement condition in Proposition 3.5 is sound:
$$
c_p(\langle x,e/x\rangle)=(x,-e)_p.
$$
Thus the required invariant is $1$ whenever $-e=\ell$ is a local square. Including numerator and denominator primes in the support makes the auxiliary-prime argument applicable to rational diagonal forms. Choosing an integer in the square class of $-\delta\ell$ preserves the determinant and Hasse-invariant calculations. Reciprocity and the specified real signature supply the compatibility needed for the binary complement, so the embedding conclusion and its previously accepted consequences remain justified.

One minor wording adjustment remains in that proof: an arbitrary integer representative $m$ can contain additional square factors, so its diagonal coefficient need not literally be a $p$-adic unit outside $S\cup\{\ell\}$. Choose the positive squarefree representative, or say that the coefficients are units after rescaling by local squares. The stated Hasse invariants and the conclusion are unaffected.

Remark 4.6 now correctly separates self-powers from mixed products. The latter retain both the Hodge-isomorphism and locus hypotheses; the CM self-power theorem [17] is no longer used to suggest an unrestricted mixed-product result. This removes the previous overstatement without weakening the formal corollaries.

The census description now distinguishes the 182 parsed starting rows from Shimada's 199 starting types and accurately attributes the independent reproduction of the same 360 rank-fourteen types to the previous review. References [25] and [26] improve traceability. Retaining the parser and vertex-deletion generator would further improve reproducibility, but the clarification does not change the accepted census evidence.

The newly cited published paper [24], Theorem 1, supports the genus-19 curve-square domination and all-self-powers result attributed to it. The revision appropriately credits that separate construction. Describing the intertwiner calculations as computations over the stated finite fields also correctly limits their evidential role: the general rational statement rests on the written proof.

These are sound corrections and attribution improvements to useful partial results. The general rational Hodge conjecture remains unresolved by this manuscript.
