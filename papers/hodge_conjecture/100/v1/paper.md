# Slope-Stable Serre–Hartshorne Vector Bundles for Multipliers $p \in \{2, 3\}$ on Automorphic K3 Products: The Two-Wall Chamber Theorem, Punctual Fixed-Locus Base Change, and Parabolic Atiyah Obstructions

## 1. Introduction: From Point-Ideal Syzygy Obstructions to the Rank-2 Serre–Hartshorne Bundle

For projective K3 surfaces $S_i$ whose transcendental lattices have trivial Hodge endomorphism field $\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{Hdg}}(T(S_i)_{\mathbb{Q}}) \cong \mathbb{Q}$ and transcendental rank $\operatorname{rk} T(S_i)$ odd or at least $10$, `hodge_conjecture_77/v1` [8, §6] proved via Goursat's lemma and Weyl's invariant theory for orthogonal groups that the rational Hodge conjecture on arbitrary finite products of the $S_i$ reduces to two geometric barriers (since Buskin [10, Theorem 1.1] proved that every rational Hodge **isometry** $c = 1$ between projective K3 surfaces is algebraic):
1. **Barrier B (Nonsquare Rational Hodge Similarities):** Constructing algebraic cycles realizing the graph $[\Gamma_\Psi] \in \operatorname{Hdg}^2(S_1 \times S_2, \mathbb{Q})$ of a rational Hodge similarity $\Psi: T(S_1)_{\mathbb{Q}} \xrightarrow{\sim} T(S_2)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ satisfying $\langle \Psi(x), \Psi(y) \rangle = c \langle x, y \rangle$ for a nonsquare rational multiplier $c \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0} \setminus (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^2$.
2. **Barrier A (The Determinant Class):** Constructing an algebraic cycle realizing the top exterior power $\det T(S_i) \in \Lambda^{\operatorname{rk} T(S_i)} T(S_i)_{\mathbb{Q}} \subset \operatorname{Hdg}^{\operatorname{rk} T(S_i)}(S_i^{\operatorname{rk} T(S_i)}, \mathbb{Q})$.

Algebraic realizations of nonsquare rational Hodge similarities are known on higher-codimension subloci of K3 moduli via Hilbert schemes $K3^{[n]}$ under the Kuga–Satake Hodge conjecture (Varesco [18, Theorem 4.5]), finite symplectic symmetry and real multiplication (`hodge_conjecture_87/v1` [12], `hodge_conjecture_88/v2` [13], `hodge_conjecture_90/v1` [15]), and fibrewise $3$-isogenies at Picard rank $10$ (`hodge_conjecture_93/v1` [17]), while `hodge_conjecture_77/v1` [8, §6] proposed the automorphism-graph seed correspondences on special elliptic K3 surfaces and `hodge_conjecture_79/v2` [9, §§2, 4] analyzed why standard $K3^{[2]}$ incidence correspondences and Mukai self-product models stop at the $9$-dimensional equivariant locus $B_9 \subset B_{18} \subset B_{19}$ (and `hodge_conjecture_91/v1` [16] studied diagonal surgery on flat hyperkähler four-tori). While linear combinations of automorphism graphs on the special seed surfaces already define algebraic correspondences, the program initiated in `hodge_conjecture_92/v1` [14, §4] (**Program II**) seeks to realize these similarities as second Chern classes $\Delta(E) = c_2(\mathcal{E}nd_0(E))$ of **slope-stable vector bundles** $E$ on $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$ ($\operatorname{NS}(S_0) = U = \mathbb{Z}u_1 \oplus \mathbb{Z}u_2$ with $u_1^2 = u_2^2 = 0, u_1 \cdot u_2 = 1$, $j \equiv 0$, carrying the order-$3$ non-symplectic automorphism $g_3$ of Artebani–Sarti [4]) in order to study their gauge-theoretic and algebraic deformability. In [14, §4], a rank-$57$ multiplier-$3$ bundle $E_{57}$ on $X_0$ was constructed as a 1-step extension $0 \to \mathcal{P}_{1,a} \oplus \mathcal{P}_{1,b} \to E_{57} \to \mathcal{P}_2 \to 0$ of 2-twist point-evaluation bundles $\mathcal{P}_{1,a} = \mathcal{E}_{3,5} \otimes p_2^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(u_1)$ ($\operatorname{rk} 15$), $\mathcal{P}_{1,b} = \mathcal{E}_{3,6}$ ($\operatorname{rk} 18$), and $\mathcal{P}_2 = (\mathrm{id} \times g_3^{-1})^*\mathcal{E}_{3,8} \otimes p_2^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(2u_1)$ ($\operatorname{rk} 24$), reducing its global $\omega_I(t)$-slope-stability to vertical $\mu_{H_1}$-stability of the fiber restrictions $E_{57}|_y$ on $S_0 \times \{y\}$ ([14, Proposition 4.3]).

The present paper resolves the vertical and global stability question on the automorphic K3 products for **both prime multipliers $p = 3$ and $p = 2$**, and establishes a sharp cohomological criterion governing first-order normal deformations off the $9$-dimensional equivariant moduli locus $B_9 \subset B_{18} \subset B_{19}$:

1. **Syzygy Destabilization of High-Rank Point-Evaluation Extensions (Section 2, Theorem 2.1):** For any 2-twist evaluation bundle $\mathcal{P}_i = \Upsilon_1(\mathcal{I}_{\Gamma_{h_i}} \otimes p_1^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B_i) \otimes p_2^*N_i)$ of rank $r_i = a b_i \ge 15$ on $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$ ($B_i = a u_1 + b_i u_2$, $a \ge 3, b_i \ge a + 2$), restricting any 1-step diagonal-coboundary extension $0 \to \bigoplus_{j=1}^m \mathcal{P}_{1,j} \to E \to \mathcal{P}_2 \to 0$ to $S_0 \times \{y\}$ factors the extension class through a homomorphism $\phi_y: \mathcal{P}_2|_y \to \mathcal{I}_y$ whose kernel $\mathcal{G}_2 := \ker(\phi_y)$ (of rank $\ge r_2 - 1$ and $c_1(\mathcal{G}_2) = -B_2$) splits the restricted extension and contains the untwisted Lazarsfeld–Mukai bundle $K_{B_2 - u_2}$ (of rank $r_2 - a + 1$ and $c_1 = -B_2 + u_2$, cut out globally by the fiber-product divisor $D_{\mathrm{fib}} = S_0 \times_{\mathbb{P}^1_t} S_0$). A three-case slope partition across $\Delta_b := m b_2 - \sum_{j=1}^m b_{1,j}$ proves that $E|_y$ is vertically $\mu_{H_1}$-unstable at every $y \in S_0$ across all of $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$, unconditionally ruling out $E_{57}$ and $E_{33}$ [14].

2. **The Rank-2 Serre–Hartshorne Bundle $\mathcal{U}_0$ and the Two-Wall Chamber Theorem (Section 3, Theorem 3.1):** To eliminate high-rank syzygy kernels, we specialize the diagonal vector-bundle construction of Pragacz–Srinivas–Pati [20, Theorem 4(ii) and Proposition 9] and the Hartshorne–Serre correspondence [2] to the acyclic $(-4)$-divisor $L_0 := \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(u_1 - 2u_2)$ ($L_0^2 = -4, h^*(S_0, L_0) = 0$), obtaining a locally free rank-$2$ vector bundle $\mathcal{U}_0$ on $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$ with isotropic vertical Mukai vector $v(\mathcal{U}_0|_y) = (2, (-1, -2), 1)$ and horizontal Mukai vector $v(\mathcal{U}_0|_x) = (2, (-1, 2), -1)$ [1]. Using the **Bogomolov–Mukai wall identity** $\ell(Z) = (2d_1 + 1)(d_2 + k) + 2 \ge 0$, we prove that across $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$ there exist **only two stability walls** $y/x \in \{2, 4\}$, making $\mathcal{U}_0|_y$ strictly $\mu_{H_1}$-stable for every $H_1 \in \mathcal{C}_2 = (2, 4)$ and $\mathcal{U}_0|_x$ strictly $\mu_{H_2}$-stable for every $H_2 \in \mathcal{C}_1 = (1, 2)$.

3. **Two-Polarization Base Change and the Weierstrass Fixed-Locus Filtration of $\mathcal{V}_A$ (Section 4, Theorem 4.1):** For $Q_2 := (g_3 \times \mathrm{id})^*\mathcal{U}_0$ and $Q_1 := \mathcal{U}_0 \otimes p_1^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(A)$ with $a_1 \ge 1$ and $2 < -a_2/a_1 < 4$, evaluating slopes at two polarizations in $\mathcal{C}_2$ straddling $-a_2/a_1$ proves $\operatorname{Hom}_{S_0}(F_2, F_1) = \operatorname{Ext}^2_{S_0}(F_2, F_1) = 0$ for all $y \in S_0$. By Grauert's base-change theorem [7], $\mathcal{V}_A := \mathcal{E}xt^1_{p_2}(Q_2, Q_1)$ is a locally free bundle of rank $-4a_1 a_2$ on $S_0$ with $\operatorname{Ext}^1_{X_0}(Q_2, Q_1 \otimes p_2^*N) \cong H^0(S_0, \mathcal{V}_A \otimes N)$. Using local Koszul pullback saturation along $\Gamma_{g_3}$, rank-$2$ fiberwise Serre duality on $\mathcal{U}_0|_y(s)$, and the global $4$-term connecting sequence, we establish the exact two-step filtration of $\mathcal{V}_A$ both in the exceptional case $A = (1, -3)$ (where $R^1 p_{2*}(\mathcal{S}_{(1,-3)}) = 0$ and $R^2 p_{2*}(\mathcal{S}_{(1,-3)}) \cong \mathcal{O}_s(-2)$) and for all $a_1 \ge 2$ (where $H^2(S_0, \mathcal{V}_A \otimes N) = 0$ and generic evaluation rank is $\ge 3 > 2$ for all $n_2 \ge n_1 \ge 2$).

4. **Wall-Clearance Stability of $E_4$ and $E_6$ via Künneth Degree Constancy (Section 5, Theorem 5.1, Remark 5.2, and Appendix A):** For $H_1 = x_1 u_1 + y_1 u_2 \in \mathcal{C}_2$, let $m(H_1) := \min(y_1 - 2x_1,\; 4x_1 - y_1) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ be the clearance from the walls $y_1/x_1 \in \{2, 4\}$. We prove that whenever **$m(H_1) \ge 2$ (for $E_4$) and $m(H_1) \ge 3$ (for $E_6$)** and $A_a \cdot H_1 = A_b \cdot H_1 = -1$, all $7$ proper subsheaf rank pairs of $E_4|_y$ and all $13$ proper subsheaf rank pairs of $E_6|_y$ satisfy $\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{G}) - \mu_{H_1}(E_r|_y) \le -\frac{1}{12} < 0$ on $U_{\mathrm{vert}} \subset S_0$ (and show in Remark 5.2 that at $m = 2$ the $(1, 2)$ upper bound vanishes and at $m = 1$, $H_1 = (2, 5)$, the lifted subbundle $\widetilde{L}_2 \hookrightarrow E_6$ has vertical slope excess $+\frac{1}{6} > 0$). Künneth divisor constancy then yields explicit global $\omega_I(t)$-slope-stability intervals $t \in (0, t_0)$ for $E_4$ and for five rank-$6$ bundles **Variants I, II-A, II-B, III, and IV** (Appendix A).

5. **Normalized Discriminant Correspondence vs. Unnormalized $c_2$, Which Variants Carry the Primitive Similarity, and Parabolic Atiyah Obstructions (Sections 6–8, Theorems 6.1, 7.1, and 8.1):** Passing to the rank-$35$ trace-free adjoint bundle $\mathcal{F}_0 := \mathcal{E}nd_0(E_6) = \operatorname{ad}(\mathbb{P}(E_6))$ kills $c_1(\mathcal{F}_0) = 0$, while its second Chern class $c_2(\mathcal{F}_0) = \Delta(E_6) = 12 c_2(E_6) - 5 c_1(E_6)^2$ is $12$ times the normalized Mukai/discriminant class $-\kappa_2(E_6) := \frac{1}{12}\Delta(E_6) = \frac{1}{12}c_1(E_6)^2 - \operatorname{ch}_2(E_6)$ (as distinct from the unnormalized class $c_2(E_6)$, which includes $\frac{5}{12}c_1(E_6)^2$). On $H^2(S_0, \mathbb{Q}) = T(S_0)_{\mathbb{Q}} \oplus U_{\mathbb{Q}}$, $M := \Phi_{[-\kappa_2(E_6)]_{H^2 \otimes H^2}}$ acts on $T(S_0)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ by $M_T = 2\,\mathrm{id} + (g_3^{-1})^*$ ($M_T^* M_T = 3\,\mathrm{id}_{20}$) and on $U_{\mathbb{Q}}$ by $M_U = 3 R_{L_0} - \frac{2}{3}K$. Among the five rank-$6$ variants:
   - **Variants I and II-A** ($A_a = A_b = (1, -3)$) are single-twist stability and cohomology prototypes carrying $M_T^* M_T = 3\,\mathrm{id}_{20}$ on $T(S_0)$, but their rank-$1$ perturbation $K$ does not align $H_1^\perp \to H_2^\perp$;
   - **Variant II-B** ($A_a = A_b = (1, -3), N_a + N_b = (2, -2)$) realizes a primitive multiplier-$3$ similarity $M_U^* M_U w_1 = 3w_1$ on the chamber boundary $(r_1, r_2) = (6, 2)$;
   - **Variant III** ($A_a = (5, -14), A_b = (17, -47)$) realizes the unipotent $22$-dimensional spectrum $\det(\lambda I_{22} - M^* M) = (\lambda - 3)^{22}$ on $H^2(S_0, \mathbb{Q})$; and
   - **Variant IV** ($A_a = (20, -51), A_b = (9, -23), H_1 = (11, 28) \in \mathcal{C}_2, H_2 = (11, 21) \in \mathcal{C}_1$) realizes both $M_U(H_1) = \frac{314}{3}H_2$ and the **exact $21$-dimensional primitive Hodge similarity $M^* M = 3\,\mathrm{id}_{21}$ on $H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}(S_0, H_1) \to H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}(S_0, H_2)$ strictly inside $\mathcal{C}_2 \times \mathcal{C}_1$**.
   In Theorem 8.1, we extend these bundles algebraically over $U_9' \subset B_9$ and prove via the **Killing-Form Retraction Lemma** ($\Pi_{\mathrm{ad}} \circ \operatorname{ad} = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}_0}$) and the **$3 \times 3$ Parabolic Atiyah Lie Algebroid Diagram** that the space $W \subset N_{B_9/B_{19}, 0}$ of first-order unobstructed normal directions for $\mathcal{F}_0 = \mathcal{E}nd_0(E_6)$ satisfies $\dim_{\mathbb{C}} W \le \min\bigl(h^2(S_0, \mathcal{V}_{A_a} \otimes N_a), h^2(S_0, \mathcal{V}_{A_b} \otimes N_b)\bigr)$, which equals **$0$ in Variants I, III, and IV** (obstructing all $10$ normal directions in $N_{B_9/B_{19}, 0}$).

6. **Order-$6$ ($g_6 = g_3 \circ \iota$, $p = 3$) and Order-$4$ ($g_4$, $p = 2$) Punctual Serre–Hartshorne Bundles (Section 9, Theorem 9.1):** Extending the local Koszul saturation of $\mathcal{U}_0$ to automorphisms $g$ with isolated fixed points $Z_0(g)$—specifically $g_6 = g_3 \circ \iota$ on $S_0$ ($p = 3, \operatorname{Fix}(g_6) = s \sqcup Z_{12}, L(g_6) = +14$) and $g_4$ on the order-$4$ Weierstrass K3 surface $S_0^{(4)}$ of Artebani–Sarti [11, Example 6.3] ($p = 2, \operatorname{Fix}(g_4) = Z_4, L(g_4) = +4$)—flips the Bogomolov discriminant of $\mathcal{E}_0^{(g)}$ to $\Delta(\mathcal{E}_0^{(g)}) = 4L(g) > 0$. For $m(H_1) \ge 4$ (evaluated at $H_1 = (5, 14), m = 4$), we construct globally $\omega_I(t)$-slope-stable rank-$8$ bundles **$E_8^{(6)}$** ($p = 3$) and **$E_8^{(4)}$** ($p = 2$) whose normalized discriminant correspondence $\widetilde{M} := \frac{1}{2}[-\kappa_2(E_8^{(g)})]_{H^2 \otimes H^2} = \frac{1}{32}c_2(\mathcal{E}nd_0(E_8^{(g)}))$ realizes the **exact $21$-dimensional primitive Hodge similarity $\widetilde{M}^* \widetilde{M} = p\,\mathrm{id}_{21}$ strictly inside $\mathcal{C}_2 \times \mathcal{C}_1$**.

## 2. The Syzygy Destabilization Theorem for High-Rank Point-Evaluation Bundles

We first prove unconditionally why the 1-step diagonal-coboundary extensions $E_{33} = \widetilde{E}_{1,1}$ ($\operatorname{rk} 33, c = 1$) and $E_{57}$ ($\operatorname{rk} 57, c = 3$) constructed in `hodge_conjecture_92` [14, Theorem 4.2 and Proposition 4.3]—and more generally any 1-step diagonal-coboundary extension of 2-twist point-evaluation bundles $\mathcal{E}_{a, b}$ with equal $u_1$-degree $a \ge 3$ and $b_{1,j}, b_2 \ge a + 2$—fail vertical $\mu_{H_1}$-stability at every fiber $y \in S_0$.

Recall from [14, Theorem 4.2(1)(b)] that for integers $b - a \ge 2, a \ge 3$ and $B = a u_1 + b u_2 \in \operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$ ($B^2 = 2ab =: 2r$, $h^0(S_0, \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B)) = r + 2$, $h^0(S_0, \mathcal{I}_z(B)) = r + 1$, $h^1 = h^2 = 0$), the 2-twist Fourier–Mukai transform $\mathcal{E}_{a,b} = \Psi_{a,b}(\mathcal{O}_\Delta)$ of $\Psi_{a,b} := T_{\mathcal{O}}[-1] \circ e^B \circ T_{\mathcal{O}}[-1]$ is a locally free vector bundle of rank $r = ab$ on $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$ whose restriction to a vertical fiber $S_0 \times \{y\} \cong S_0$ is the point-ideal evaluation syzygy bundle $K_{B,y} := \mathcal{E}_{a,b}|_{S_0 \times \{y\}}$ sitting in
$$0 \longrightarrow K_{B,y} \longrightarrow H^0\bigl(S_0, \mathcal{I}_y(B)\bigr) \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0} \xrightarrow{\;\operatorname{ev}_y\;} \mathcal{I}_y \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B) \longrightarrow 0, \qquad \operatorname{rk}(K_{B,y}) = r = ab, \quad c_1(K_{B,y}) = -B.$$

**Theorem 2.1 (Syzygy and Lazarsfeld–Mukai Fiber Destabilization of 1-Step Point-Evaluation Extensions).** *Let $m \ge 1$, $a \ge 3$, and $b_{1,j} \ge a + 2$ ($j = 1, \dots, m$), $b_2 \ge a + 2$ be integers; set $B_{1,j} := a u_1 + b_{1,j}u_2$, $B_2 := a u_1 + b_2 u_2$, $\mathcal{P}_{1,j} := \mathcal{E}_{a, b_{1,j}} \otimes p_2^*N_{1,j}$ (rank $r_{1,j} = a b_{1,j}$), and $\mathcal{P}_2 := (\mathrm{id} \times g_3^{-1})^*\mathcal{E}_{a, b_2} \otimes p_2^*N_2$ (rank $r_2 = a b_2 \ge 15$); and let*
$$0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{K}_1 := \bigoplus_{j=1}^m \mathcal{P}_{1,j} \longrightarrow E \xrightarrow{\;\pi\;} \mathcal{P}_2 \longrightarrow 0$$
*be any 1-step extension of rank $R := a d_u$ ($d_u := b_2 + \sum_{j=1}^m b_{1,j}$, $c_1(E|_y) = -(m+1)a u_1 - d_u u_2$) whose extension class $\xi \in \operatorname{Ext}^1_{X_0}(\mathcal{P}_2, \mathcal{K}_1)$ lies in the image of the diagonal coboundary $\delta_\Delta: \operatorname{Hom}_{X_0}(\mathcal{I}_{\Gamma_{g_3}}(B_2, N_2), \bigoplus_{j=1}^m \mathcal{O}_\Delta(B_{1,j}, N_{1,j})) \to \operatorname{Ext}^1_{X_0}(\mathcal{P}_2, \mathcal{K}_1)$ under $\Upsilon_1 = T_{\mathcal{O}}[-1] \boxtimes \mathrm{id}_{S_0}$. Put $\Delta_b := (m+1)b_2 - d_u = m b_2 - \sum_{j=1}^m b_{1,j}$. Then at every vertical fiber $S_0 \times \{y\}$ ($z := g_3^{-1}(y)$):*

1. *(Direct-Split Embedding of the Syzygy Kernel $\mathcal{G}_2 \hookrightarrow E|_y$ and Global Embedding of the Lazarsfeld–Mukai Bundle $K_{B_2 - u_2} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{G}_2$ Across All $y \in S_0$, Including $\operatorname{Fix}(g_3)$): Restricting $\xi$ to $S_0 \times \{y\}$ factors $\xi|_y \in \operatorname{Ext}^1_{S_0}(\mathcal{P}_2|_y, \mathcal{K}_1|_y)$ through a homomorphism $\phi_y: \mathcal{P}_2|_y = K_{B_2, z} \to \mathcal{I}_y \subset \mathcal{O}_{S_0}$ whose kernel $\mathcal{G}_2 := \ker(\phi_y) \subset \mathcal{P}_2|_y$ (of rank $r_2$ if $\phi_y = 0$, or rank $r_2 - 1 = ab_2 - 1$ if $\phi_y \neq 0$, and first Chern class $c_1(\mathcal{G}_2) = -B_2$ in both cases) splits the restricted extension and embeds directly into $E|_y$, containing at **every $y \in S_0$ (including $y \in \operatorname{Fix}(g_3)$)** the untwisted Lazarsfeld–Mukai bundle*
   $$K_{B_2 - u_2} \;:=\; \ker\Bigl(H^0\bigl(S_0, \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B_2 - u_2)\bigr) \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B_2 - u_2)\Bigr) \;\hookrightarrow\; \mathcal{G}_2 \;\hookrightarrow\; E|_y$$
   *of **rank $r_2 - a + 1 = a(b_2 - 1) + 1$ and first Chern class $c_1(K_{B_2 - u_2}) = -B_2 + u_2 = -a u_1 - (b_2 - 1)u_2$**.*

2. *(Unconditional Vertical Destabilization Across All $(m, a, b_{1,j}, b_2)$ and Explicit Formulas for $E_{57}|_y, E_{33}|_y$): For every $H_1 = x_1 u_1 + y_1 u_2 \in \operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$ ($y_1 > x_1 > 0$) and every $y \in S_0$, the vertical restriction $E|_y$ is $\mu_{H_1}$-unstable:*
   - **Case (i) ($\Delta_b \le 0$):** $\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{K}_1|_y) - \mu_{H_1}(E|_y) = \frac{-\Delta_b\,y_1}{d_u(d_u - b_2)} \ge 0$ across $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$.
   - **Case (ii) ($b_{\max} := \max_j b_{1,j} \ge b_2$):** $\max\bigl(\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{P}_{1, j_{\max}}|_y), \mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{K}_1|_y)\bigr) \ge \mu_{H_1}(E|_y)$ across $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$.
   - **Case (iii) ($b_{1,j} \le b_2 - 1$ for all $j = 1, \dots, m$, so $\Delta_b \ge m$):** *If $\phi_y = 0$, $\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{P}_2|_y) - \mu_{H_1}(E|_y) = \frac{\Delta_b\,y_1}{b_2 d_u} > 0$; if $\phi_y \neq 0$, the slope-difference numerators $N(\mathcal{G}_2) := R(c_1(\mathcal{G}_2)\cdot H_1) - (r_2 - 1)(c_1(E|_y)\cdot H_1)$ and $N(K_{B_2 - u_2}) := R(c_1(K_{B_2 - u_2})\cdot H_1) - (r_2 - a + 1)(c_1(E|_y)\cdot H_1)$ satisfy*
     $$\boxed{\; N(\mathcal{G}_2) + N(K_{B_2 - u_2}) \;=\; a^2\bigl(2\Delta_b - (m + 1)\bigr)y_1 \;\ge\; a^2(m - 1)y_1 \;\ge\; 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \max\bigl(\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{G}_2),\, \mu_{H_1}(K_{B_2 - u_2})\bigr) \;\ge\; \mu_{H_1}(E|_y) \quad \forall\,H_1 \in \operatorname{Amp}(S_0)! \;}$$
   - **In particular, for $E_{57}|_y$** ($m = 2, a = 3, b_{1,a} = 5, b_{1,b} = 6, b_2 = 8, \Delta_b = 5, R = 57, c_1(E_{57}|_y) = -9u_1 - 19u_2$), both $\mathcal{G}_2 \hookrightarrow E_{57}|_y$ ($\operatorname{rk} 23$) and $K_{(3,7)} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{G}_2 \hookrightarrow E_{57}|_y$ ($\operatorname{rk} 22$) satisfy:
     $$\boxed{\; \mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{G}_2) - \mu_{H_1}(E_{57}|_y) \;=\; \frac{36y_1 - 19x_1}{1311} \;>\; 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \mu_{H_1}(K_{(3,7)}) - \mu_{H_1}(E_{57}|_y) \;=\; \frac{27y_1 + 19x_1}{1254} \;>\; 0 \qquad \forall\,H_1 \in \operatorname{Amp}(S_0), \;\forall\,y \in S_0, \;}$$
     *and **for $E_{33}|_y = \widetilde{E}_{1,1}|_y$** ($m = 1, a = 3, b_1 = 5, b_2 = 6, \Delta_b = 1, R = 33, c_1(E_{33}|_y) = -6u_1 - 11u_2$), $\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{G}_2) - \mu_{H_1}(E_{33}|_y) = \frac{3y_1 - 11x_1}{561}$ and $\mu_{H_1}(K_{(3,5)}) - \mu_{H_1}(E_{33}|_y) = \frac{11x_1 - 3y_1}{528}$ satisfy $N(\mathcal{G}_2) + N(K_{(3,5)}) = 0$, so $\max\bigl(\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{G}_2), \mu_{H_1}(K_{(3,5)})\bigr) \ge \mu_{H_1}(E_{33}|_y)$ across all of $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$.*

*Proof.* **Part (1):** Restricting $\Upsilon_1^{-1}(\mathcal{P}_2)|_y = \mathcal{I}_z(B_2)$ and $\mathcal{O}_\Delta|_y = \mathbb{C}_y$ to $S_0 \times \{y\}$ factors $\xi|_y$ through $\phi_y: \mathcal{P}_2|_y = K_{B_2, z} \to T_{\mathcal{O}}[-1](\mathbb{C}_y) = \mathcal{I}_y \subset \mathcal{O}_{S_0}$, so $\mathcal{G}_2 = \ker(\phi_y)$ splits the restricted extension and embeds directly into $E|_y$. On $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$, let $D_{\mathrm{fib}} := S_0 \times_{\mathbb{P}^1_t} S_0 = (\pi_{\mathrm{ell}} \times \pi_{\mathrm{ell}})^{-1}(\Delta_{\mathbb{P}^1_t}) \subset X_0$ be the fiber-product divisor of class $p_1^*u_2 + p_2^*u_2$. Because $\pi_{\mathrm{ell}} \circ g_3 = \pi_{\mathrm{ell}}$, both $\Delta_{S_0} \subset D_{\mathrm{fib}}$ and $\Gamma_{g_3} \subset D_{\mathrm{fib}}$, giving the global subsheaf inclusion $\iota_{\mathrm{fib}}: \mathcal{O}_{X_0}(B_2 - u_2, N_2 - u_2) = \mathcal{I}_{D_{\mathrm{fib}}}(B_2, N_2) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{I}_{\Gamma_{g_3}}(B_2, N_2)$. For any morphism $\bar\alpha: \mathcal{I}_{\Gamma_{g_3}}(B_2, N_2) \to \bigoplus_{j=1}^m \mathcal{O}_{\Delta_{S_0}}(B_{1,j}, N_{1,j})$, the composition $\bar\alpha \circ \iota_{\mathrm{fib}}$ vanishes on $\Delta_{S_0} \setminus \Delta(\operatorname{Fix}(g_3))$ (since $\mathcal{I}_{D_{\mathrm{fib}}}|_{\Delta_{S_0}} = 0$), hence vanishes **identically on $X_0$** because $\mathcal{O}_{\Delta_{S_0}}(B_{1,j}, N_{1,j})$ is locally free (torsion-free) on the irreducible smooth surface $\Delta_{S_0} \cong S_0$. Applying $\Upsilon_1$ yields a global subbundle $p_1^*K_{B_2 - u_2} \otimes p_2^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(N_2 - u_2) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{P}_2$ on which $\xi$ vanishes identically, embedding $K_{B_2 - u_2} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{G}_2 \hookrightarrow E|_y$ at every $y \in S_0$. If $\phi_y \neq 0$, then $\operatorname{im}(\phi_y) \subset \mathcal{I}_y$ has rank $1$ and $\mathcal{G}_2 = \ker(H^0(S_0, \mathcal{J}_{Z_y}(B_2)) \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0} \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{im}(\operatorname{ev}_{Z_y}))$ has rank $r_2 - 1 = a b_2 - 1$, where $\mathcal{J}_{Z_y} \subset \mathcal{I}_z$ is the length-$2$ subscheme ideal ($\mathcal{I}_{\{y,z\}}$ for $y \neq z$, or a curvilinear tangent subscheme for $y = z \in \operatorname{Fix}(g_3)$). Since $H^0(S_0, \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B_2 - u_2)) \cdot s_{f_{t(y)}} \subset H^0(S_0, \mathcal{J}_{Z_y}(B_2))$ has base locus contained in $s \cup f_{t(y)}$, while $h^0(\mathcal{J}_{Z_y}(B_2)) \ge a b_2 > a(b_2 - 1) + 2 = h^0(\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B_2 - u_2))$ ($a \ge 3$) and $h^0(\mathcal{J}_{Z_y}(B_2)) \ge a b_2 > (a - 1)(b_2 + 1) + 2 = h^0(\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(B_2 - s))$ ($b_2 \ge a + 2$) rule out $f_{t(y)}$ and $s$ as fixed components, $\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{ev}_{Z_y}) \subset \mathcal{J}_{Z_y}(B_2)$ has codimension-$2$ support ($c_1(\operatorname{im}\phi_y) = 0$) and $c_1(\mathcal{G}_2) = -B_2$.
**Part (2):** In Case (i) ($\Delta_b \le 0$), $\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{K}_1|_y) - \mu_{H_1}(E|_y) = \frac{-\Delta_b y_1}{d_u(d_u - b_2)} \ge 0$; in Case (ii) ($b_{\max} \ge b_2$), $(m+1)b_{\max} - d_u \ge \Delta_b$ implies that either $\Delta_b \le 0$ or $\mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{P}_{1, j_{\max}}|_y) \ge \mu_{H_1}(E|_y)$. In Case (iii) ($b_{1,j} \le b_2 - 1$, $\Delta_b \ge m$), expanding $N(\mathcal{G}_2) = a(a\Delta_b - d_u)y_1 + a((m+1)a - d_u)x_1$ and $N(K_{B_2 - u_2}) = a(a\Delta_b + d_u - a(m+1))y_1 - a((m+1)a - d_u)x_1$ cancels the $x_1$-terms identically and gives $N(\mathcal{G}_2) + N(K_{B_2 - u_2}) = a^2(2\Delta_b - (m+1))y_1 \ge a^2(m - 1)y_1 \ge 0$ (verified in Part 0 of `verify_serre_hartshorne_rank6_bundle.py`). $\square$

## 3. The Rank-2 Serre–Hartshorne Bundle $\mathcal{U}_0$ and the Two-Wall Chamber Theorem

Theorem 2.1 pinpoints the exact mechanism needed to achieve vertical $\mu_{H_1}$-stability: the building blocks $F_1, F_2$ on the fibers $S_0 \times \{y\}$ must have **rank $2$** (so that the kernel of any proper torsion-free quotient of $F_2$ has rank $2 - 1 = 1$, where its slope is bounded strictly below $\mu_{H_1}(F_2)$ by the stability of $F_2$) and must be **locally free** across $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$. Specializing the diagonal vector-bundle construction of Pragacz–Srinivas–Pati [20, Theorem 4(ii) and Proposition 9] and the Hartshorne–Serre correspondence [2] to an explicit acyclic $(-4)$-divisor $L_0$ on $S_0$, we obtain such a canonical rank-$2$ bundle $\mathcal{U}_0$ on $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$ and determine its complete stability chamber structure across $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$.

Throughout, let $S_0$ be an elliptic K3 surface with $\operatorname{NS}(S_0) = U = \mathbb{Z}u_1 \oplus \mathbb{Z}u_2$ ($u_1^2 = u_2^2 = 0, u_1 \cdot u_2 = 1$), where $f := u_2$ is the class of an elliptic fiber ($f^2 = 0$) and $s := u_1 - u_2$ is the unique $(-2)$-section ($s^2 = -2, s \cdot f = 1$). The Mori cone of effective curves is $\overline{\operatorname{NE}}(S_0) = \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} s + \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} f$, and the ample cone is
$$\operatorname{Amp}(S_0) \;=\; \bigl\{H = x u_1 + y u_2 \in \operatorname{NS}(S_0)_{\mathbb{R}} : H \cdot f = x > 0 \text{ and } H \cdot s = y - x > 0\bigr\} \;=\; \bigl\{x u_1 + y u_2 : y > x > 0\bigr\}.$$

**Theorem 3.1 (The Rank-2 Serre–Hartshorne Bundle $\mathcal{U}_0$ and the Two-Wall Chamber Theorem).** *Set $L_0 := \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(u_1 - 2u_2) = \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(s - f) \in \operatorname{Pic}(S_0)$. Then:*

1. *(Complete Acyclicity of $L_0$, cf. Pragacz–Srinivas–Pati [20, Proposition 9]): The line bundle $L_0 = \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(u_1 - 2u_2)$ ($L_0^2 = -4$) is completely acyclic on $S_0$:*
   $$h^0(S_0, L_0) \;=\; h^1(S_0, L_0) \;=\; h^2(S_0, L_0) \;=\; 0.$$

2. *(Hartshorne–Serre Construction of the Locally Free Rank-$2$ Bundle $\mathcal{U}_0$, cf. [2, Theorem 1.1] and [20, Theorem 4(ii)]): On $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$, let $\Delta \subset X_0$ be the diagonal and put $M_0 := p_1^*L_0 \otimes p_2^*L_0^\vee$. Then $\operatorname{Ext}^1_{X_0}(\mathcal{I}_\Delta \otimes M_0, \mathcal{O}_{X_0}) \cong H^0(\Delta, \mathcal{O}_\Delta) = \mathbb{C} \cdot 1_\Delta$ canonically, and twisting the extension corresponding to the nowhere-vanishing generator $1_\Delta \in H^0(\Delta, \mathcal{O}_\Delta)$ by $p_1^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1)$ yields a **locally free rank-$2$ vector bundle $\mathcal{U}_0$ on $X_0 = S_0 \times S_0$** sitting in the exact sequence*
   $$\boxed{\; 0 \longrightarrow p_1^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1) \longrightarrow \mathcal{U}_0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{I}_\Delta \otimes p_1^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-2u_2) \otimes p_2^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1 + 2u_2) \longrightarrow 0. \;}$$
   *Its vertical restriction $\mathcal{U}_0|_y := \mathcal{U}_0|_{S_0 \times \{y\}}$ and horizontal restriction $\mathcal{U}_0|_x := \mathcal{U}_0|_{\{x\} \times S_0}$ sit in the exact sequences*
   $$0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1) \longrightarrow \mathcal{U}_0|_y \longrightarrow \mathcal{I}_y \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-2u_2) \longrightarrow 0, \qquad 0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{S_0} \longrightarrow \mathcal{U}_0|_x \longrightarrow \mathcal{I}_x \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1 + 2u_2) \longrightarrow 0,$$
   *with isotropic Mukai vectors [1] $v(\mathcal{U}_0|_y) = (2, (-1, -2), 1)$ ($v(\mathcal{U}_0|_y)^2 = 0$) and $v(\mathcal{U}_0|_x) = (2, (-1, 2), -1)$ ($v(\mathcal{U}_0|_x)^2 = 0$).*

3. *(The Bogomolov–Mukai Identity and the Two-Wall Chamber Theorem): More generally, for any $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, let $E_k$ be a locally free rank-$2$ vector bundle on $S_0$ with isotropic Mukai vector $v_k := (2, -u_1 - 2k u_2, k)$ ($v_k^2 = 0$, $c_1(E_k) = -u_1 - 2k u_2$, $\chi(S_0, E_k) = 2 + k$, $c_2(E_k) = k + 2$). Every saturated rank-$1$ subsheaf $L = \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(d_1 u_1 + d_2 u_2) \hookrightarrow E_k$ has torsion-free quotient $E_k/L \cong \mathcal{I}_Z \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}((-1 - d_1)u_1 + (-2k - d_2)u_2)$ for a $0$-dimensional subscheme $Z \subset S_0$ of length*
   $$\boxed{\; \ell(Z) \;=\; (2d_1 + 1)(d_2 + k) + 2 \;\ge\; 0, \;}$$
   *and across the entire ample cone $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0) = \{x u_1 + y u_2 : y > x > 0\}$ there exist **at most two stability walls $y/x \in \{2, 4\}$**, decomposing $\operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$ into three stability chambers:*
   $$\mathcal{C}_1 \;=\; \left\{1 < \frac{y}{x} < 2\right\}, \qquad \mathcal{C}_2 \;=\; \left\{2 < \frac{y}{x} < 4\right\}, \qquad \mathcal{C}_3 \;=\; \left\{\frac{y}{x} > 4\right\}.$$
   *Specializing to $k = 1$ ($\mathcal{U}_0|_y$) and $k = -1$ ($\mathcal{U}_0|_x$):*
   - *For every $H_1 = x_1 u_1 + y_1 u_2 \in \mathcal{C}_2 = \{2 < y_1/x_1 < 4\}$, **$\mathcal{U}_0|_y$ is strictly $\mu_{H_1}$-stable for all $y \in S_0$**, with maximal rank-$1$ subsheaf degree $\max(-y_1, -3x_1) < \mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{U}_0|_y) = -x_1 - \frac{1}{2}y_1$ and minimal rank-$1$ quotient degree $\min(-2x_1, x_1 - y_1) > \mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{U}_0|_y)$.*
   - *For every $H_2 = x_2 u_1 + y_2 u_2 \in \mathcal{C}_1 = \{1 < y_2/x_2 < 2\}$, **$\mathcal{U}_0|_x$ is strictly $\mu_{H_2}$-stable for all $x \in S_0$**, with maximal rank-$1$ subsheaf degree $0 < \mu_{H_2}(\mathcal{U}_0|_x) = x_2 - \frac{1}{2}y_2$.*

*Proof.* **Part (1):** We have $L_0^2 = -4$ and $\chi(S_0, L_0) = 2 + \frac{1}{2}L_0^2 = 0$. Since $L_0 \cdot (u_1 + u_2) = -1 < 0$ ($u_1 + u_2 \in \operatorname{Nef}(S_0)$) and $(-L_0) \cdot f = -1 < 0$ ($f \in \operatorname{Nef}(S_0)$), neither $L_0$ nor $L_0^\vee = K_{S_0} \otimes L_0^\vee$ is effective, so Serre duality and Riemann–Roch give $h^0(S_0, L_0) = h^2(S_0, L_0) = h^1(S_0, L_0) = 0$.
**Part (2):** Since $\omega_{X_0} \cong \mathcal{O}_{X_0}$, $\det(N_{\Delta/X_0}) \cong \det(T_{S_0}) \cong \mathcal{O}_\Delta$, and $M_0|_\Delta \cong \mathcal{O}_\Delta$, the local-to-global $\operatorname{Ext}$ spectral sequence for $\operatorname{Ext}^1_{X_0}(\mathcal{I}_\Delta \otimes M_0, \mathcal{O}_{X_0})$ has $E_2^{p,0} = H^p(X_0, M_0^\vee) = \bigoplus_{i+j=p} H^i(S_0, L_0^\vee) \otimes H^j(S_0, L_0) = 0$ for all $p \ge 0$ by Part (1) and the Künneth formula, while $E_2^{0,1} = H^0(X_0, \mathcal{E}xt^1_{X_0}(\mathcal{I}_\Delta \otimes M_0, \mathcal{O}_{X_0})) \cong H^0(\Delta, \mathcal{O}_\Delta) = \mathbb{C} \cdot 1_\Delta$. By the Hartshorne–Serre correspondence [2, Theorem 1.1] and Pragacz–Srinivas–Pati [20, Theorem 4(ii)], the nowhere-vanishing generator $1_\Delta$ defines a locally free rank-$2$ bundle $\mathcal{U}_0(u_1, 0)$, whose twist by $p_1^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1)$ is $\mathcal{U}_0$.
**Part (3):** Additivity of Mukai vectors $v_k = v(L) + v(\mathcal{I}_Z((-1-d_1)u_1 + (-2k-d_2)u_2))$ gives $\ell(Z) = (1 + d_1 d_2) + \bigl(1 + (d_1 + 1)(d_2 + 2k)\bigr) - k = (2d_1 + 1)(d_2 + k) + 2 \ge 0$. If a rank-$1$ subsheaf $L' \subset E_k$ with saturation $L = (L')^{\mathrm{sat}} = \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(d_1 u_1 + d_2 u_2)$ defines a wall $H = x u_1 + y u_2 \in \operatorname{Amp}(S_0)$ ($y > x > 0$), then $\mu_H(L) = \mu_H(E_k) \iff (2d_1 + 1)y + 2(d_2 + k)x = 0 \implies \frac{y}{x} = -\frac{2(d_2 + k)}{2d_1 + 1} > 1$, so $A := 2d_1 + 1 \in 2\mathbb{Z} + 1$ and $B := d_2 + k \in \mathbb{Z}$ have opposite signs and satisfy $AB \ge -2 \implies AB \in \{-1, -2\}$. Since $A$ is odd and $-2B/A > 1$, the only integer solutions are $(A, B) \in \{(1, -1), (1, -2), (-1, 1), (-1, 2)\}$, giving $y/x \in \{2, 4\}$.
- **Vertical stability of $\mathcal{U}_0|_y$ ($k = +1$) across $\mathcal{C}_2 = \{2 < y_1/x_1 < 4\}$:** In $0 \to \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1) \to \mathcal{U}_0|_y \to \mathcal{I}_y \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-2u_2) \to 0$, either $L \subset \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1)$, giving $L = \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1)$ and $\deg_{H_1}(L) = -y_1 < -x_1 - \frac{1}{2}y_1 = \mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{U}_0|_y)$ since $y_1 > 2x_1$; or the composite $L \to \mathcal{I}_y(-2u_2) \subset \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-2u_2)$ is nonzero, so the difference divisor $D := -2u_2 - (d_1 u_1 + d_2 u_2) = (-d_1)u_1 + (-2 - d_2)u_2$ is a nonzero effective curve class $D = a s + b f = a u_1 + (b - a)u_2$ ($a, b \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, a + b \ge 1$, since $\mathcal{I}_y(-2u_2)$ is not locally free at $y$). Thus $d_1 = -a$ and $d_2 = -2 + a - b$. If $a = 0, b \ge 1$, then $(d_1, d_2) = (0, -2 - b)$, so $\ell(Z) = (2(0) + 1)(-2 - b + 1) + 2 = 1 - b \ge 0$ forces $b = 1$, i.e. $L = \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-3u_2)$ with $\deg_{H_1}(L) = -3x_1 < -x_1 - \frac{1}{2}y_1$ since $y_1 < 4x_1$. If $a \ge 1$, then $\deg_{H_1}(D) = a(y_1 - x_1) + b x_1 \ge y_1 - x_1$, so $\deg_{H_1}(L) = -2x_1 - \deg_{H_1}(D) \le -x_1 - y_1 < -x_1 - \frac{1}{2}y_1$. Hence $\max_{L \subset \mathcal{U}_0|_y} \deg_{H_1}(L) = \max(-y_1, -3x_1) < \mu_{H_1}(\mathcal{U}_0|_y)$ across all of $\mathcal{C}_2 = \{2 < y_1/x_1 < 4\}$.
- **Horizontal stability of $\mathcal{U}_0|_x$ ($k = -1$) across the entire chamber $\mathcal{C}_1 = \{1 < y_2/x_2 < 2\}$:** In $0 \to \mathcal{O}_{S_0} \to \mathcal{U}_0|_x \to \mathcal{I}_x \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1 + 2u_2) \to 0$, either $L \subset \mathcal{O}_{S_0}$, giving $L = \mathcal{O}_{S_0}$ and $\deg_{H_2}(L) = 0 < x_2 - \frac{1}{2}y_2 = \mu_{H_2}(\mathcal{U}_0|_x)$ since $y_2 < 2x_2$; or the composite $L \to \mathcal{I}_x(-u_1 + 2u_2) \subset \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(-u_1 + 2u_2)$ is nonzero, so the difference divisor
  $$D \;:=\; (-u_1 + 2u_2) - (d_1 u_1 + d_2 u_2) \;=\; (-1 - d_1)u_1 + (2 - d_2)u_2$$
  is a nonzero effective curve class $D = a s + b f = a u_1 + (b - a)u_2$ ($a, b \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, a + b \ge 1$, since $\mathcal{I}_x(-u_1 + 2u_2)$ is not locally free at $x$). Equating coefficients gives $d_1 = -1 - a \le -1$ and $d_2 = 2 + a - b$. Substituting into the Bogomolov–Mukai identity $\ell(Z) = (2d_1 + 1)(d_2 - 1) + 2 \ge 0$ for $k = -1$ gives
  $$\ell(Z) \;=\; (-2a - 1)(1 + a - b) + 2 \;\ge\; 0 \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad (2a + 1)(1 + a - b) \;\le\; 2.$$
  If $1 + a - b \ge 1$ ($b \le a$), then $a + b \ge 1 \implies a \ge 1 \implies 2a + 1 \ge 3$, so $(2a + 1)(1 + a - b) \ge 3 > 2$, a contradiction (in particular, $D = s$ has $(a, b) = (1, 0), (d_1, d_2) = (-2, 3), \ell(Z) = -4 < 0$ and cannot lift to $\mathcal{U}_0|_x$). Therefore every saturated rank-$1$ subsheaf $L \hookrightarrow \mathcal{U}_0|_x$ mapping non-trivially to $\mathcal{I}_x(-u_1 + 2u_2)$ satisfies $1 + a - b \le 0 \iff b \ge a + 1 \ge 1$, which implies $d_1 = -1 - a \le -1$ and $d_2 = 2 + a - b \le 1$ (achieved at $a = 0, b = 1$, i.e. $D = f = u_2, (d_1, d_2) = (-1, 1), \ell(Z) = 2$). Since $y_2 > x_2 > 0$, we obtain for **every** $H_2 = x_2 u_1 + y_2 u_2 \in \mathcal{C}_1 = \{1 < y_2/x_2 < 2\}$:
  $$\deg_{H_2}(L) \;=\; d_1 y_2 + d_2 x_2 \;\le\; -y_2 + x_2 \;<\; 0 \;<\; x_2 - \frac{1}{2}y_2 \;=\; \mu_{H_2}(\mathcal{U}_0|_x),$$
  proving $\max_{L \subset \mathcal{U}_0|_x} \deg_{H_2}(L) = \max(0, x_2 - y_2) = 0 < \mu_{H_2}(\mathcal{U}_0|_x)$ unconditionally across the entire chamber $\mathcal{C}_1 = \{1 < y_2/x_2 < 2\}$. (Supplementary symbolic verification is provided in Part 1 of `verify_serre_hartshorne_rank6_bundle.py`.) $\square$

## 4. The Two-Polarization Base-Change Theorem and Weierstrass Fixed-Locus Filtration of $\mathcal{V}_A$

Let $g_3 \in \operatorname{Aut}(S_0)$ be the order-$3$ non-symplectic automorphism of Artebani–Sarti [4], which acts trivially on $\operatorname{NS}(S_0) = U$ ($g_3^* = \mathrm{id}_U$) and satisfies $(g_3^*)^2 + g_3^* + \mathrm{id}_{T(S_0)} = 0$ on $T(S_0)$. Set
$$Q_2 \;:=\; (g_3 \times \mathrm{id})^*\mathcal{U}_0, \qquad Q_1 \;:=\; \mathcal{U}_0 \otimes p_1^*\mathcal{O}_{S_0}(A), \qquad Q_{1,*} \;:=\; Q_1 \otimes p_2^*N_*,$$
where $A = a_1 u_1 + a_2 u_2 \in \operatorname{NS}(S_0)$ satisfies $a_1 \ge 1$ and $2 < -a_2/a_1 < 4$, and $N_* \in \operatorname{Pic}(S_0)$. On each vertical fiber $S_0 \times \{y\} \cong S_0$, put $F_2 := Q_2|_y = g_3^*(\mathcal{U}_0|_y) \cong \mathcal{U}_0|_{g_3^{-1}(y)}$ and $F_1 := Q_1|_y = \mathcal{U}_0|_y \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(A)$.

**Theorem 4.1 (Two-Polarization Base-Change Theorem and Weierstrass Fixed-Locus Filtration of $\mathcal{V}_A$).** *Let $A = a_1 u_1 + a_2 u_2 \in \operatorname{NS}(S_0)$ satisfy $a_1 \ge 1$ and $2 < -a_2/a_1 < 4$. Then:*

1. *(Two-Polarization Vanishing of $\operatorname{Hom}$ and $\operatorname{Ext}^2$ Inside $\mathcal{C}_2$): For every $y \in S_0$, the vertical fiber bundles $F_2 = Q_2|_y = \mathcal{U}_0|_{g_3^{-1}(y)}$ and $F_1 = Q_1|_y = \mathcal{U}_0|_y \otimes \mathcal{O}_{S_0}(A)$ satisfy*
   $$\operatorname{Hom}_{S_0}(F_2, F_1) \;=\; 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \operatorname{Ext}^2_{S_0}(F_2, F_1) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{S_0}(F_1, F_2)^\vee \;=\; 0.$$

2. *(Exact Base Change and Local Freeness of $\mathcal{V}_A$): The relative $\mathcal{E}xt$ sheaves satisfy $\mathcal{E}xt^0_{p_2}(Q_2, Q_1) = \mathcal{E}xt^2_{p_2}(Q_2, Q_1) = 0$ identically on $S_0$, and*
   $$\mathcal{V}_A \;:=\; \mathcal{E}xt^1_{p_2}(Q_2, Q_1)$$
   *is a **locally free vector bundle of rank $-2A^2 = -4a_1 a_2$ on $S_0$** whose fiber at every $y \in S_0$ is canonically $\mathcal{V}_A|_y \cong \operatorname{Ext}^1_{S_0}(Q_2|_y, Q_1|_y)$, with Mukai vector*
   $$\boxed{\; v(\mathcal{V}_A) \;=\; \bigl(-4a_1 a_2,\; 2a_2 u_1 + 8a_1 u_2,\; 4a_1 a_2 + 6\bigr), \;}$$
   *and the Leray spectral sequence for $\operatorname{Ext}^*_{X_0}(Q_2, Q_1 \otimes p_2^*N_*)$ degenerates at $E_2$ to give canonical isomorphisms for **all** $N_* \in \operatorname{Pic}(S_0)$:*
   $$\operatorname{Ext}^1_{X_0}\bigl(Q_2,\, Q_1 \otimes p_2^*N_*\bigr) \;\cong\; H^0\bigl(S_0,\, \mathcal{V}_A \otimes N_*\bigr), \qquad \operatorname{ev}_y(\xi) \;=\; \xi|_y \;\in\; \operatorname{Ext}^1_{S_0}(Q_2|_y, Q_{1,*}|_y).$$

3. *(Weierstrass Fixed Locus $\operatorname{Fix}(g_3) = s \sqcup C_5$ and the Two-Step Exact Filtration of $\mathcal{V}_A$): On the $j \equiv 0$ Weierstrass elliptic K3 surface $S_0: Y^2 Z = X^3 + p_{12}(t)Z^3$ with $g_3([X:Y:Z], t) = [\zeta_3 X : Y : Z, t]$, the fixed locus $\operatorname{Fix}(g_3) = \{X = 0\} = s \sqcup C_5$ is a pure smooth Cartier divisor of class $D_{\mathrm{fix}} = s + C_5 = 3u_1 + u_2$ ($D_{\mathrm{fix}}^2 = 6$, $L(g_3) = \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(s) + \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(C_5) = 2 - 8 = -6$, Artebani–Sarti [4, Table 1]). ## 5. Wall-Clearance Vertical and Global $\omega_I(t)$-Slope Stability of $E_4$ and $E_6$
