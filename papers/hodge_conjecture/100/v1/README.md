# Slope-Stable Serre–Hartshorne Vector Bundles for Multipliers p in {2, 3} on Automorphic K3 Products: The Two-Wall Chamber Theorem, Punctual Fixed-Locus Base Change, and Parabolic Atiyah Obstructions

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 1 · 2026-11-04T05:55:44.786236Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/100/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We resolve vertical and global slope stability for 1-step extensions on automorphic K3 products and construct globally slope-stable vector bundles realizing primitive rational Hodge similarities of nonsquare prime multipliers p = 3 and p = 2.

First, we prove the Syzygy Destabilization Theorem: any 1-step diagonal-coboundary extension of 2-twist evaluation bundles with equal u_1-degree a >= 3 and b_i >= a + 2 (including E_33 and E_57) is vertically unstable on every fiber via a two-point syzygy subsheaf and a Lazarsfeld-Mukai subbundle. Second, applying the Pragacz-Srinivas-Pati diagonal construction and Hartshorne-Serre correspondence to the acyclic (-4)-divisor L_0 = O(u_1 - 2u_2), we construct a rank-2 bundle U_0 on X_0 = S_0 x S_0 and prove the Two-Wall Chamber Theorem: across Amp(S_0), the Bogomolov-Mukai identity yields only two walls y/x in {2, 4}, making U_0|_y stable in C_2 = (2, 4) and U_0|_x stable in C_1 = (1, 2). Third, under wall clearance m(H_1) >= 2 (E_4) and m(H_1) >= 3 (E_6), we establish Two-Polarization Base Change, a 3-step sheaf-theoretic base-change proof at A = (1, -3), the Filtration Subsheaf h^0-Bound Lemma, global slope stability of E_4 and E_6 (Variants I-IV), and primitive similarity M^* M = 3 id_21 in Variant IV.

On the coupled tower B_9 subset B_18 subset B_19, Grauert base change gives the relative algebraic family over U_9' subset B_9, while the Parabolic Atiyah Algebroid Diagram bounds first-order normal unobstructed directions by min_bullet h^2(V_{A_bullet} tensor N_bullet) (= 0 in Variants I, III, IV). Finally, local Koszul pullback saturation for g_6 = g_3 o iota (p = 3) and g_4 on S_0^{(4)} (p = 2) yields globally slope-stable bundles E_8^{(6)} and E_8^{(4)} for m(H_1) >= 4.
