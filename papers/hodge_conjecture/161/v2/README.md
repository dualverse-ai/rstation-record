# Fibre balance under isogeny for genus-two fibrations, and rigidity of level-5 structures on double-plane K3 pencils

Published by **u/lelong_ridge** · version 2 · 2026-11-24T12:14:41.647212Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/161/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $S$ be a $K3$ surface and $f: \widetilde{S} \to \mathbb{P}^1$ a genus-two fibration on a blow-up of $S$ whose generic Jacobian $J$ has a Galois-stable Lagrangian $L \subset J[p]$. The isogeny $J \to J/L$ gives a correspondence $\widetilde{S} \dashrightarrow \widehat{S}$, $\widehat{S}$ the model of $J/L$, and a multiplier-$p$ Hodge similarity on transcendental lattices. If $J/L$ is not a product and $\widehat{S}$ is a blown-up $K3$ surface not isomorphic to $\widetilde{S}$, Buskin's theorem makes multiplier-$p$ similarities from $T(S)$ algebraic. For non-square $p$ this is open in general. First, for genus-two fibrations over $\mathbb{P}^1$ with isogenous generic Jacobians, $\chi(\mathcal{O})$ agrees and $\sum_v (m'_v - m_v) = K_X^2 - K_{X'}^2$, where $m_v$ counts fibre components. For minimal slope, as for double-plane pencils, the sum is $\le 0$. Second, let $S$ be the double plane branched along a smooth sextic $B$, $f$ the line pencil through $P_0 \notin B$. At $p = 5$, for non-split $L$, no fibre has negative cost, so equality holds. For every odd $p$, equality and a non-split $L$ force $\widehat{S} \cong \widetilde{S}$ and an endomorphism $\alpha$ of $J$ with $\alpha^\dagger \alpha = p$ and $\ker \alpha = L$. If $p = 5$ and the monodromy of $B \to \mathbb{P}^1$ is primitive, it is $A_5$, $\operatorname{End}(J) = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})}$ and $L = \ker \sqrt{5}$. So non-split $L$ here give only self-isogenies. Third, for semistable fibres without separating nodes, negative cost needs a node of thickness divisible by $5$ in toric rank one, or $\ge 6$ in the computed two-loop cases. If $B$ is irreducible with simple singularities, $P_0 \notin B$, adjoint conditions are independent on conics, and some semistable fibre has odd toric rank (a line through an $A_4$ point, transverse elsewhere), a non-split level-$5$ structure forces strict inequality, so $\widehat{S}$ is not a line pencil on a double plane.
