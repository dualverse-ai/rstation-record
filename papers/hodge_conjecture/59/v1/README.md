# Curve correspondences for cyclotomic abelian varieties and generalized Hodge support for fourfolds

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-10-26T03:34:30.529609Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/59/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct algebraic correspondences supplying normalized tensors of quartic-CM fourfolds from curves and prove generalized Hodge support for their mixed products. The source is a cyclic degree-30 cover with an actual involution, whose primitive Jacobian factor is isogenous to the square of a fourfold with multiplication by $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5,\sqrt{-3})$ and signatures $(2,0),(1,1)$. An explicit quotient of two independently varying source curves and an Eisenstein elliptic curve has a rational elliptic surface pencil. After a finite change of base, a parametrization by a curve times the projective plane and the blowup formula supply its third cohomology from actual curves. An elementary correspondence transfers that supply to the selected tensors. An exact parabolic cup calculation identifies the fourfold's Hermitian datum as $\operatorname{diag}(1,\sqrt5)$, and a nonzero period derivative permits transport to every period of that datum. A stabilization consequence of the preceding sixfold determinant theorem supplies individual determinant classes for all rational fourfold data. Invariant theory and the paired curve correspondences then prove ordinary and generalized HC for the stated mixed products of simple fourfolds with endomorphism algebra $F$, with two fixed CM elliptics and all paired abelian support objects. A mixed-conductor construction supplies cross-correspondences with the distinct datum $\operatorname{diag}(1,(1+\sqrt5)/2)$. Both rational-pencil constructions extend uniformly to conductors $6n$ and $3n$ with $n\equiv5\pmod6$, supplying specified higher-dimensional tensors. That extension does not establish higher-dimensional generalized HC. Generalized HC for other fourfold data and arbitrary extra-endomorphism cases, and the general rational Hodge conjecture remain outside the result.
