# A small-diagonal transfer from a degree-33 Fermat character to the quartic-CM determinant of a Prym eightfold, and the Hodge conjecture for its powers under explicit quotient hypotheses

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 3 · 2026-10-19T06:37:44.756391Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/20/v3) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study one explicit quartic-CM case of the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties that lies beyond imaginary-quadratic Weil-class methods. Let $\alpha=(7,10,13,19,22,28)$ be a degree-$33$ Fermat fourfold character and $B_8$ the associated eight-dimensional ramified Prym factor of a cyclic-cover Jacobian. Unconditionally, using exact Frobenius computations at two primes, we show $\operatorname{End}^0_{\overline{\mathbf Q}}(B_8)=E=\mathbf Q(\zeta_{33})^{\langle 4\rangle}$ and $Hg(B_8)=\operatorname{Res}_{E^+/\mathbf Q}SU_E$ with $E^+=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{33})$. A structural theorem for balanced special-unitary abelian varieties then gives $\operatorname{Hdg}^2(B_8)_{\mathbf Q}=D^2(B_8)\oplus W_E(B_8)$, where $W_E(B_8)=\bigwedge_E^4H^1(B_8,\mathbf Q)$, and shows that the classical and generalized Hodge conjectures for every self-power $B_8^n$ are equivalent to algebraicity of $W_E(B_8)$. The source Fermat block is algebraic by Aoki's level-$66$ identity, and alternatively by a cubic-to-Fermat correspondence elaborating the cubic-scroll strategy of Favero–Kelly. Our main contribution is an explicit transfer: a common finite quotient of a Kummer curve product and the Fermat character quotient, a Poincaré curve-to-Prym divisor correspondence, and contraction along the small diagonal of $B_8$ give a single Chow correspondence carrying the source block onto $W_E(B_8)$, with exact normalization $5^{-3}$ relative to canonical Galois descent. This avoids the semiregularity step in secant-sheaf approaches. The main theorem is stated as a single chain: four explicit quotient hypotheses H1–H4 imply algebraicity of $W_E(B_8)$, which implies the rest; our arguments for H1–H4 are given separately. Under H1–H4, $W_E(B_8)$ is algebraic and the classical and generalized Hodge conjectures hold for all $B_8^n$. The exact computations are supplied. We do not claim the Hodge conjecture for $X_{33}^4$ or in general.
