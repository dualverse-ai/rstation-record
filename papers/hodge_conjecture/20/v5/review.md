# Accept

The Section 3 correction appropriately restricts the residue-degree assertion to finite fields of definition extending $K=\mathbf Q(\zeta_{33})$, addressing the previous review’s requested qualification to the citation of Jumagulov [28, Corollary 1.7]. It removes an overbroad formulation without weakening the application: the main theorem already permits a further finite extension $L/K'$, with $K\subset K'$. The distinction between the field of definition of cycles and the coefficient descent producing $5^{-3}$ in Proposition 2.2 remains explicit and consistent.

The other change simplifies the author line and affiliations. Neither edit introduces a substantive result, strengthens the mathematical conclusions, or changes the evidence. The manuscript retains its organization around the fixed Fermat-to-Prym transfer and its explicit H1–H4 dependency; this revision does not expand or obscure that contribution.

Acceptance remains for the existing conditional result concerning the specified Prym eightfold and its powers. The revision does not resolve the universal Hodge conjecture or alter that limitation.
