# Accept

This revision improves attribution and makes an existing literature comparison more precise. It retains the accepted contribution: the explicit normalized transfer for the fixed Prym eightfold, conditional on H1–H4, together with the previously established endomorphism and Hodge-group calculations. The theorems, proofs and supplementary evidence are unchanged. The additions remain focused on this contribution and do not introduce a separate substantive result or materially worsen the manuscript’s organization.

The revised account of [7] is supported by its public source text. Its faithful-cyclic-determinant lemma supplies the determinant on curve slots through rational algebraic correspondences from powers of the Fermat-curve Jacobian; its abelian-cover corollary extends that supply to character spaces. These statements do not require the tensor-Hodge-generic hypothesis of its separate all-powers theorem. For the present cover, $g=0$, $r=6$ and $m=33$ give character rank $2g+r-2=4$. Combined with the companion CM-Hodge claim, the rational determinant block becomes algebraic; the existing order-five averaging and the map in Sections 5–6 then carry it onto $W_E(B_8)$. Thus the stated alternative implication bypasses H1–H4 while retaining its external CM-Hodge dependency. This checks the comparison and its logical dependencies, not the complete proofs of the external preprints. Neither becomes an input to the manuscript’s accepted independent argument.

The clearer attribution of the general reduction to Schoen is appropriate. The distinction between this fixed model and the moving-family contribution of [41] agrees with that paper’s published version 6. Replacing the broad priority language with the concrete correspondence and Hodge-group contribution is an improvement.

Two minor clarifications would sharpen the new discussion:

- In Section 7, retain the signature $(4,4)$ and split-discriminant qualification for the eightfold statement in [44]. The public abstracts support the imaginary-quadratic comparison, but field degree alone does not establish non-applicability: $E$ contains $\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-3})$. Explain that the determinant spaces differ: the imaginary-quadratic eightfold Weil classes lie in degree eight, whereas the quartic-$E$ determinant studied here lies in degree four. Do not imply that no imaginary-quadratic structure exists on $B_8$.
- In the abstract and Introduction, phrase the alternative conclusion explicitly as “assuming the companion CM-Hodge claim.” Also qualify the preceding statement that “no such result is available” consistently with this newly acknowledged route. The current surrounding text already identifies the dependency, so this is a wording clarification rather than a defect in the main theorem.

Acceptance remains for the existing partial result with its stated hypotheses. This revision neither resolves the universal Hodge conjecture nor warrants changing its verified-solution status.
