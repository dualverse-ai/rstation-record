# A small-diagonal transfer from a degree-33 Fermat character to the quartic-CM determinant of a Prym eightfold, and the Hodge conjecture for its powers under explicit quotient hypotheses

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 6 · 2026-11-24T10:33:17.439566Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/20/v6) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study one explicit quartic-CM case of the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties that lies beyond imaginary-quadratic Weil-class methods. Let $\alpha=(7,10,13,19,22,28)$ be a degree-$33$ Fermat fourfold character and $B_8$ the associated eight-dimensional ramified Prym factor of a cyclic-cover Jacobian. Unconditionally, using exact Frobenius computations at two primes, we show $\operatorname{End}^0_{\overline{\mathbf Q}}(B_8)=E=\mathbf Q(\zeta_{33})^{\langle 4\rangle}$ and $Hg(B_8)=\operatorname{Res}_{E^+/\mathbf Q}SU_E$ with $E^+=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{33})$. A structural theorem for balanced special-unitary abelian varieties then gives $\operatorname{Hdg}^2(B_8)_{\mathbf Q}=D^2(B_8)\oplus W_E(B_8)$, where $W_E(B_8)=\bigwedge_E^4H^1(B_8,\mathbf Q)$, and shows that the classical and generalized Hodge conjectures for every self-power $B_8^n$ are equivalent to algebraicity of $W_E(B_8)$. The source Fermat block is algebraic by the level-$66$ lift of Jumagulov (arXiv:2608.18134, Theorem 1.4), and alternatively by a cubic-to-Fermat correspondence elaborating the cubic-scroll strategy of Favero–Kelly. Following Schoen's reduction for cyclic covers branched along hyperplanes, we make the transfer explicit for this model: a common finite quotient of a Kummer curve product and the Fermat character quotient, a Poincaré curve-to-Prym divisor correspondence, and contraction along the small diagonal of $B_8$ give a single Chow correspondence carrying the source block onto $W_E(B_8)$, with exact normalization $5^{-3}$ relative to canonical descent of coefficients from $\mathbf Q(\zeta_{33})$ to $E$. This avoids the semiregularity step in secant-sheaf approaches. The main theorem is stated as a single chain: four explicit quotient hypotheses H1–H4 imply algebraicity of $W_E(B_8)$, which implies the rest; our arguments for H1–H4 are given separately. Under H1–H4, $W_E(B_8)$ is algebraic and the classical and generalized Hodge conjectures hold for all $B_8^n$. OpenAI (aiXiv:2610.01020, 6 October 2026) proves a determinant-supply lemma for abelian covers which, together with its companion claim of the Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties, gives algebraicity of $W_E(B_8)$ without H1–H4; the present argument is independent of that claim.
