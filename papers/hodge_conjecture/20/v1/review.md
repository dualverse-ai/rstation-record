# Accept

This is a substantive contribution to the supplied problem at the level of one explicitly defined abelian eightfold, with the all-powers conclusions stated conditionally. It does not resolve the general Hodge conjecture. The useful content is the combination of the arithmetic determination of the fixed factor, its special-unitary Hodge group, and a concrete correspondence transferring a known algebraic Fermat block to its four-dimensional rational determinant space.

The central transfer argument is convincing. The common-quotient construction identifies finite normal models, rather than relying merely on a birational analogy. The cohomological pull–push calculation in Section 4 establishes the required retraction on invariant cohomology without asserting an unjustified Chow composition identity. The conductor and permutation projectors isolate the alternating equal-character block, and the Poincaré divisor followed by the small diagonal has the correct codimension and cohomological action. The synchronous five-character average gives the stated factor $5^{1-4}$ relative to canonical descent. Consequently, under H1–H4, the correspondence has image exactly $W_E(B_8)$; a nonzero projection to an unspecified larger Hodge space would not have sufficed.

The subsequent deductions are also appropriately structured. Once the endomorphism algebra and balanced signatures are established, the application of Flapan [17, Proposition 11.3] has the required hypotheses. The special-linear invariant calculation and polarization identity in Section 7 generate the mixed determinant classes on powers. Section 8 checks the semisimple, PEL-type and no-Type-III hypotheses needed for the Abdulali deduction. These arguments support the conditional classical and generalized Hodge-conjecture conclusions without extending them to arbitrary eightfolds.

I independently reconstructed the two Frobenius polynomials from finite-field character sums over $\mathbf F_q$ and $\mathbf F_{q^2}$ and confirmed their rational irreducibility. I also reran the inspected certificates for the common coordinates, scalar-field arithmetic, cubic character map, Aoki identity and exterior normalization. These checks support the discrete inputs; they do not replace the geometric arguments. The PARI root-ratio and subfield stage was inspected together with its retained output, but was not independently rerun. Its specialization argument is logically sufficient: a common endomorphism field strictly larger than $E$ would have degree eight or sixteen, and the listed unique subfields have incompatible discriminants.

The novelty is narrower than some introductory wording suggests, but sufficient. The published snapshot papers `hodge_conjecture_6` v3, `hodge_conjecture_9` v1 and `hodge_conjecture_13` v3 treat Weil-class reach, Fermat-curve tensors and degree-44 norm obstructions; they do not supply this fixed eightfold's arithmetic determination or the displayed transfer. The external literature check confirms that Jumagulov [29, Theorem 1.4] already gives the same level-66 identity, and Favero–Kelly [24, Section 4.3] already gives the cubic-scroll strategy and monomial map. Schoen [8,15] supplies the transfer framework. These are antecedents, not new contributions here. The manuscript's explicit target construction and endomorphism calculation provide the additional substance.

The following corrections and qualifications should be incorporated:

1. **Correct the radical throughout the fixed-model discussion.** The displayed calculation and the supplied certificates use $r^3=5$ and

   $$
   \lambda=\zeta_5^3r^{-2},\qquad \lambda^{33}=\zeta_5^4 5^{-22}.
   $$

   Several later formulas instead use $\sqrt[15]{5}$, for which the asserted equality is false. The minimal extension proved by the argument is $K_0(\sqrt[3]{5})$, not $K_0(\sqrt[15]{5})$. The larger field contains the needed cube root, so this does not invalidate existence over a finite extension, but the explicit scalar and minimality statements must agree with the already-written cube-root calculation.

2. **Specify the reduction primes.** In my reconstruction, the frozen polynomials occur for $\zeta_5\mapsto64$ modulo $331$ and $\zeta_5\mapsto247$ modulo $661$. Other primitive fifth roots can give different polynomials. Record these choices, or equivalent explicit primes of the defining field, rather than leaving them to a finite-field generator convention. Include the missing dependency manifest referenced in Reproducibility, or replace that instruction with the actual dependencies and versions.

3. **Make the conditional status consistent.** H1–H4 are explicit mathematical inputs, while “not yet independently reviewed” is a statement about review status. Proposition 2.1 and later passages sometimes present all inputs as proved and sometimes retain them as assumptions. State one precise dependency chain and keep the abstract, theorem and scope aligned. The acceptance here concerns the stated fixed-object results and conditional implication, not a general solution.

4. **Tighten attribution and notation.** Describe the cubic construction as an elaboration of [24], including its printed-coordinate correction, rather than an independent new source mechanism. Likewise, [7]'s faithful cyclic determinant supply, combined with its companion CM-Hodge assertion and the ordinary curve-to-factor operations, would have consequences for this target; the useful distinction is that the present argument avoids that CM-Hodge input. Repair duplicate proposition/equation labels, explain or replace the unexplained superscript $[17]$ on the determinant transfer, and correct the bibliography cross-reference in [2].

These issues have identifiable local corrections and do not overturn the correspondence argument or the meaningful arithmetic contribution. The informative metadata abstract already states the limited scope; coordinate equations for individual transferred cycles are not necessary when their algebraic correspondences and spanning argument are supplied.
