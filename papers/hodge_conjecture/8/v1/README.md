# Smith Normal Form Parity Functionals, Symmetric Two-Tower $q$-Adic Cycle Holonomy, and the Automorphic Kronecker Catalecticant Rank-Drop Staircase on Fermat Varieties

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 1 · 2026-10-17T02:38:07.180659Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/8/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We establish three exact theorems and symbolic verification algorithms for Hodge-character lattices and complete-intersection cycles on composite-degree Fermat varieties $X_d^n \subset \mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ as partial contributions toward Fermat Hodge classes. First, in the torsion-free reduced character lattice $F_d = (\bigoplus_{a=1}^{d-1} \mathbb{Z} e_a)/\widetilde{D}_d \simeq \mathbb{Z}^{m_d}$ ($m_d = \lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor$), Aoki's formal Parry-Shioda weight-kernel lattice $\mathcal{B}_d := \ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(W_d) \subset F_d$ is saturated of rank $r_d = m_d - \varphi(d)/2$. For $18$ two-factor composite degrees $d \in \{12, 15, \dots, 95\}$ (and $d = 105 = 3 \times 5 \times 7$), the Smith Normal Form of the Aoki-Shioda matrix $M_{S_d}$ over $\mathbb{Z}$ has non-zero invariant factors $(1^{r_d - 1}, 2)$ (resp. $(1^{25}, 2, 2, 2)$ at $d = 105$), certifying $\rho_d(B_d) = \mathcal{B}_d$ and yielding an SNF-induced $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear functional $\pi_d : F_d \to \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ whose restriction to $\mathcal{B}_d$ is the canonical quotient map $\bar{\pi}_d : \mathcal{B}_d \to \mathcal{B}_d/\rho_d(S_d) \simeq \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, independent of $U_d$. Across $d \in \{35, 44, 51, 52, 68\}$, $100\%$ of $\mathfrak{B}_d^2 \cup \mathfrak{B}_d^4$ has $\bar{\pi}_d(\rho_d(\alpha)) = 0$ and lies integrally over $\mathbb{Z}$ in $D_d + \mathrm{Std}_{p_{\min}}$, and we prove an Inductive Splitting Parity Lemma in $F_{52}$ for $\xi_{52}^{\min}$. Second, we prove that $\mathcal{B}_{4p}^{\mathrm{std}}$ ($|\mathcal{B}_{4p}^{\mathrm{std}}| = p = r_{4p}$) and $\mathcal{B}_{p_1 p_2}^{\mathrm{std}}$ ($|\mathcal{B}_{p_1 p_2}^{\mathrm{std}}| = (p_1+p_2-2)/2 = r_{p_1 p_2}$) are free $\mathbb{Z}$-bases of $\rho_d(S_d)$, and via a Symmetric Two-Tower Integral Telescoping Syzygy we prove the exact minimal $\ell_1$-norms $\ell_{2, 4p}(\xi_{4p}) = 2\lfloor(p+1)/4\rfloor + N_{\mathrm{obs}}(2, p)$ and $\ell_{2, p_1 p_2}(\xi_{p_1 p_2}) = (p_1-1)(p_2-1)/4 + N_{\mathrm{obs}}(p_2, p_1) + N_{\mathrm{obs}}(p_1, p_2)$ over $\rho_d(\xi_d^{\min}) + \rho_d(S_d)$, plus a $2$-standard collapse law $d \to 2d$. Third, on $X_{2m}^4$ ($d=2m \ge 6$), we prove a Kronecker Catalecticant Factorization Theorem $C_s(A B) = \bigoplus_q C_q(A) \otimes C_{s-q}(B)$ and construct orthogonal character idempotents in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2m})[\mathbb{Z}/2m\mathbb{Z}]$ realizing the $6$-level automorphic and conjugate-pair Catalecticant staircase $L_1(m) > \dots > L_6(m)$.
