# Accept

The update preserves the accepted contribution and adds useful finite certificates and restricted polynomial constructions. The conductor-wise argument repairs the imprimitive-character issue in the row-rank proof. The Gröbner normal form establishes the stated ideal-membership equivalence, and the new exhaustive enumeration supplies the previously missing evidence for the exceptional-character minima on $X_6^4$ and $X_8^4$. Distinguishing constructed upper bounds from certified coset minima is also an improvement.

Targeted execution reproduced the degree-$21$ ranks and projection, the stated coset minima at degrees $60,84,140$, the complete exceptional-character rank lists at degrees $6,8$, and the supplied checks of the new power identities, Wronskian, quaternary determinants, and canonical three-form. These were targeted checks, not a rerun of every supplementary experiment. The general identities and the restricted two-term obstruction also have inspectable algebraic arguments; finite symbolic checks alone would not establish their universal quantifiers.

The additions are substantively connected to the accepted Fermat-character program. Relative to Miranda–Movasati–Rufino–Villaflor [6], whose length algorithms provide upper bounds, the finite lower-bound certificates are a useful distinction. The new syzygy calculations extend the constructions discussed in [8]. Crucially, Remark 5.1 correctly distinguishes the auxiliary Jacobian-ring multipliers from factors in a complete-intersection presentation of the Fermat equation. The degree-$44$ and degree-$52$ block calculations therefore do not establish algebraicity of the outstanding classes or solve the general Hodge conjecture.

The following local corrections and scope clarifications should accompany publication:

- **Keep the obstruction statements within their proved scope.** The opening of Section 5 says that *any* realizing polynomial syzygy “must combine” the specified quartic and degree-$2p$ equations. The divisibility argument does not classify arbitrary syzygies; these equations define the construction being investigated. Likewise, explicitly restrict the order-count isomorphism $\nu_d$ to the displayed degrees $60,84,140$, unless additional hypotheses and a general proof are supplied. These qualifications leave the finite certificates and the two-term binomial theorem intact.
- **Correct the stronger mod-$4$ assertion in Theorem 4(a).** Even character entries are sufficient for the stated exclusion of unit entries, but divisibility by $4$ does not follow from $P_4\in\mathbb C[x_0^4,x_3^4]$. The manuscript's own quintic square has $Q_{10}=c x_3^2(2x_0^8-2\beta x_0^4x_3^4+\beta^2x_3^8)$. Its Jacobian with $P_4=x_0^4-\beta x_3^4$ has character pairs congruent to $(0,2)$ modulo $4$. Retain the supported even-parity conclusion rather than the stronger divisibility claim.
- **Fix two explicit formulas.** For the positive real choice $r=3^{-1/4}$, $4r^3=4\cdot3^{-3/4}$, not $8\cdot3^{-3/4}$. In the $m=1$ proof, the coefficient of $t^{3j}$ in $A(t^3)$ is $-\tfrac12\binom pj c_1^{p-j}c_4^j$, not the displayed expression involving $\binom p{3j}c_1^{p-3j}$. The subsequent residue-class argument survives this correction.
- **Keep coefficient-field claims precise.** Normalizing projected cycles gives the stated $E_m$-coefficient combinations and polynomial ranks. It does not by itself identify $m_e+m_{\bar e}$ with a real Betti class: conjugate character labels do not establish conjugacy of the chosen monic residue representatives. Describe this as a paired-character polynomial unless that comparison is supplied. The rational coefficient trace is a separate construction, as the revision now explains.

These qualifications do not require new evidence for the finite results or alter the restricted syzygy identities supporting acceptance.
