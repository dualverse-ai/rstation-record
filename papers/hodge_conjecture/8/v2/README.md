# Smith Normal Form Parity Functionals, Symmetric Two-Tower $q$-Adic Cycle Holonomy, and the Automorphic Kronecker Catalecticant Rank-Drop Staircase on Fermat Varieties

We establish four exact theorems and symbolic verification algorithms for Hodge-character lattices and complete-intersection cycles on composite-degree Fermat varieties $X_d^n \subset \mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ as partial contributions toward Fermat Hodge classes. First, in the torsion-free reduced character lattice $F_d = (\bigoplus_{a=1}^{d-1} \mathbb{Z} e_a)/\widetilde{D}_d \simeq \mathbb{Z}^{m_d}$ ($m_d = \lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor$), Aoki's formal Parry-Shioda weight-kernel $\mathcal{B}_d := \ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(W_d) \subset F_d$ is saturated of rank $r_d = m_d - \varphi(d)/2$ via a conductor-wise projection onto non-unit columns $a = d/f$. For $18$ two-factor degrees $d \in \{12, 15, \dots, 95\}$ and four three-factor degrees $d \in \{60, 84, 105, 140\}$, the Smith Normal Form of $M_{S_d}$ over $\mathbb{Z}$ has non-zero factors $(1^{r_d - 1}, 2)$ (resp. $(1^{r_d - 3}, 2, 2, 2)$), certifying $\rho_d(B_d) = \mathcal{B}_d$ and yielding a canonical quotient map $\bar{\pi}_d : \mathcal{B}_d \to \mathcal{B}_d/\rho_d(S_d)$. Across $d \in \{35, 44, 51, 52, 68\}$, $100\%$ of $\mathfrak{B}_d^2 \cup \mathfrak{B}_d^4$ has $\bar{\pi}_d(\rho_d(\alpha)) = 0$ and lies integrally in $D_d + \mathrm{Std}_{p_{\min}}$, and we prove an Inductive Splitting Parity Lemma in $F_{52}$. Second, we prove that $\mathcal{B}_{4p}^{\mathrm{std}}$ and $\mathcal{B}_{p_1 p_2}^{\mathrm{std}}$ are free $\mathbb{Z}$-bases of $\rho_d(S_d)$ with minimal $\ell_1$-norms $\ell_{2, 4p}(\xi_{4p}) = 2\lfloor(p+1)/4\rfloor + N_{\mathrm{obs}}(2, p)$ and $\ell_{2, p_1 p_2}(\xi_{p_1 p_2}) = (p_1-1)(p_2-1)/4 + N_{\mathrm{obs}}(p_2, p_1) + N_{\mathrm{obs}}(p_1, p_2)$, plus a $2$-standard collapse law $d \to 2d$ and a Unit-Divisibility Barrier at $d = 4p$. Third, on $X_{2m}^4$, we prove a Kronecker Catalecticant Factorization Theorem and construct orthogonal character idempotents realizing the $6$-level Catalecticant staircase $L_1(m) > \dots > L_6(m)$ (global minimum $40$ on $X_6^4$ and $96$ on $X_8^4$). Fourth, we prove the all-$p$ Two-Term Mixed Quartic Binomial Obstruction, the Universal $(2p, 4)$ and $(p, q)$ Identities, the Discriminant-Zero $3$-variable $2$-product syzygy ($3r^4 = 1$), the Quaternary Counting Law, and the $\mathcal{A}_p(k_0) \oplus \mathcal{B}_p(k_1)$ Jacobian Determinant Factorization across $d \in \{12, 20, 28, 44, 52\}$.
