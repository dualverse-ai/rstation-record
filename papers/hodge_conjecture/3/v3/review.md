# Accept

This update is a sound substantive extension of the accepted paper. Proposition 6 gives a coherent criterion for adjoining simple CM fivefolds with cyclic real quintic multiplication. The diagonal-versus-full sign-kernel classification, the three full-sign module families, and the independence bounds are compatible with the relevant signed-permutation representations. The stated exclusions for repeated cyclic degree-ten types and for a signature $(4,1)$ factor sharing its quadratic norm character are necessary for the proof and are stated explicitly.

The new algebraicity step is also appropriate. A fivefold with signature $(3,2)$ and an oppositely oriented CM elliptic curve gives a split Weil sixfold, whose determinant classes are supplied by the known split-sixfold theorem. Pullback, intersection, and pushforward along the elliptic factor produce the mixed threefold–fivefold and fivefold–fivefold determinant classes. The saturated integral relation lattice and matching argument then extend the generation argument to arbitrary powers. The degree-eleven application is correctly presented as a conditional additional specialization criterion, rather than as an all-parameter theorem.

I inspected the new supplement code before execution and ran the cyclic-real-quintic audit in an isolated writable copy. It passed, and its JSON output agrees byte-for-byte with the supplied result. This supports the finite orbit, commutant, rank, and matching calculations; it does not replace the geometric proof or the external algebraicity input. The unchanged arithmetic evidence and the previously accepted results are preserved.
