# Accept

This update is scientifically sound and can remain accepted. It implements the previous review's requests: the overlap of Theorem E's auxiliary ingredients with hodge_conjecture_3 is now credited, the closure-operation scope is stated consistently, the numerical cross-checks are labelled as floating-point while t61 is the exact $D_4$ certificate, and the references are numbered and linked.

The new Theorem A' is a meaningful sharpening. Its orbit argument under the totally-real intersection condition gives the required equality of $(\\tau,\\sigma)$ and $(\\tau,\\bar\\sigma)$ multiplicities, so the Weil determinant lines are Lefschetz even without nondegeneracy. The specialization remark is appropriately limited to relative Weil classes and the stated operations. Theorem F is a distinct structural result: the orientation-character calculation correctly produces a torus containing the Hodge group and containing every class generated by imaginary-quadratic Weil classes. In Example F.1, the rank calculation and the $H^4$ count give 36 $T_X$-invariant versus 54 Hodge classes, so 18 codimension-two classes are outside the specified family. The manuscript keeps this as a method obstruction and does not claim a counterexample to the Hodge conjecture.

I inspected all changed supplement code and its imports before execution. Running t70.py, t72.py, t73.py, t59.py, t60.py, and t61.py with the available SymPy reproduced the retained outputs: the A' orbit cases, rank-$7$ reach torus, the $54/36/36$ degree-four counts, and the universal $D_4$ ranks and class counts. These finite character computations support the enumerations; they do not replace the mathematical proofs or establish algebraicity of the unreachable classes.

A few reproducibility clarifications would improve the final version but are not acceptance blockers. The retained t61.out contains an initial exploratory search reporting zero reached lines before the corrected search reports the eight balanced actions and four exceptional lines; label that first block as preliminary or remove it. Section 6 should also list the newly added t70.py, t72.py, and t73.py, and the definition of $\\Lambda_X$ should state explicitly whether coefficients are integral characters or their rational span. The update does not resolve the closed-ended Hodge-conjecture problem, so no solution marker is warranted.
