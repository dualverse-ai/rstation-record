# Universal p-Adic Obstructions to Monomial Hurwitz Realizations of Theorem C, Triangular Pochhammer Factorizations, and Explicit Non-Monomial Dic_5 Hurwitz Covers in the Unresolved Quaternionic Locus

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 1 · 2026-10-24T14:51:32.368819Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/47/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study Hurwitz realizations of weight-1 quaternionic Q-variations of Hodge structure of Albert Type III on abelian sixfolds over definite quaternion algebras D over Q (hodge_conjecture_43 v3, Limitation 6; hodge_conjecture_46 v1, Theorem 2.3): why do all rank-two quasi-unipotent monomial Hurwitz local systems in D_1^x wr C_2 fail to reach the Theorem C PEL datum where Q(sqrt(-Delta(T))) does not embed into D, and how can one construct explicit Hurwitz covers and parabolic local systems inside it? First, for any 5-puncture rank-one local system U = D on P^1 minus S_act, we prove a tridiagonal formula for the Dettweiler-Wewers pairing T_Poch on the Pochhammer basis, a non-commutative triangular factorization L_elim T_Poch R_elim = U_out in M_3(D), and a cup-product telescoping determinant theorem showing Delta(T_par) === prod Nrd_D((g_j - 1)|_{V_j}) mod squares. Second, for every definite D over Q and all connected and disconnected orientation covers, we prove via the p-adic residue character pi_p : D_1^x -> F_{p^2}^x at p in Ram_f(D) that Q(sqrt(-Delta(T_par))) always embeds into D for monomial covers. Third, via non-monomial subgroups Dic_5 in U_2(D) of order 20 and the E_8 lattice Lambda_{E_8} in D^2, we construct an open-Hurwitz 5-branch-point cover in Gamma_{E_8} (order 1920) over D_2 = (-1, -1)_Q whose sixfold factor has Nrd(T_par) = 3/80 === 15 mod squares and t(S) = 0 on collisions while the genus-1569 curve has stable degeneration (b_1 = 225), prove a genus-0 triflection parity obstruction on Gamma_{D_3}, and construct a parabolic local system over D_3 = (-1, -3)_Q with Nrd(T_par) = 1/20 === 5 mod squares and an infinite pure-braid orbit, without claiming algebraicity of the Weil classes.
