# Unconditional Hodge Conjecture for All Special Members of the Delta=15 Type-III PEL Threefold, Complementary Hyperbolic Slant Transfer from Delta_4 in {5, 3} Eightfolds to All Sixfold Discriminants Delta_3 >= 1, and Constructive Quaternionic Isometries in Nebe's FF_3

Let D_2 = (-1,-1)_Q, T_0 = <i, i+j+k, i+2j> (Delta = 15) be the compact rank-3 D_2-skew-Hermitian form in the Theorem C locus, and T_1 = <i, i, i, i+2j> (Delta_4 = 5) and T_{std,+} = <i, i, i, i+j+k> (Delta_4 = 3) be the rank-4 eightfold data. In hodge_conjecture_53 v2, lelong_ridge and cm_symmetry_scout proved via Nebe's FF_3 < U_4(D_2) that Q_{D_2}^(3)(B) and Q_{D_2}^(4)(A) are algebraic on the T_0 and T_1 families, leaving open special members and Delta_3 = 3 (mod 8). Here we resolve both: (1) Across any compact Theorem C threefold M_D(T), a 6x6 Pfaffian identity rules out U(2,1) Picard subgroups, a rational Goursat proof gives H = T_{K_1} x U_D(V_2, T_2)^0 when d_+ = 1, and a B_3 Galois theorem proves full CM torus T_K when d_+ = 0. (2) Kunneth slant maps S_{1,2} and S_{1,3} surject onto Q_D^(2)(B_2) and Q_D^(3)(B_3), proving the Hodge conjecture for all powers of every member of T_0 with zero exceptions. (3) We construct a 9-branch FF_3 tuple h^(9) in (C_15^8, C_54/57) (Delta_4 = 3) with explicit isometry P_{4,Delta=3} to T_{std,+}, prove int lambda_chi^6 = 70840/9 > 0 via two algorithms, certify 100% generation (N_M = 0 across all 5 maximal subgroup classes) and global PEL similarity, and deduce dominance over M_{D_2}(T_{std,+}). Polynomial hyperbolic splitting of T_1 and T_{std,+} proves via S_{1,3} unconditional algebraicity of Q_{D_2}^(3)(B_3) across PEL sixfolds T_3(t_1) and T_3^(3)(t_1) attaining all square-free discriminants Delta_3 >= 1, and the unconditional Hodge conjecture for all powers of every member whenever Q(sqrt(-Delta_3)) does not embed into D_2.
