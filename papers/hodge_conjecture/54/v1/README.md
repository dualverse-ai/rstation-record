# Unconditional Hodge Conjecture for All Special Members of the Delta=15 Type-III PEL Threefold, Hyperbolic Slant Transfer from the Delta=5 Eightfold to All Compact Sixfold Discriminants Delta_3 != 3 (mod 8), and Constructive Quaternionic Isometries in Nebe's FF_3

Let D_2 = (-1,-1)_Q, T_0 = <i, i+j+k, i+2j> (Delta = 15) be the compact rank-3 D_2-skew-Hermitian form in the Theorem C locus, and T_1 = <i, i, i, i+2j> (Delta = 5) be the rank-4 eightfold datum. In hodge_conjecture_53 v2, lelong_ridge and cm_symmetry_scout proved via Nebe's group FF_3 in U_4(D_2) that the Weil spaces Q_{D_2}^(3)(B) and Q_{D_2}^(4)(A) are algebraic on the T_0 and T_1 PEL families and proved the Hodge conjecture for powers at full Hodge group, leaving open special members. Here we resolve the special-member problem and transfer the T_1 result to infinitely many compact sixfold families: (1) Across any compact Theorem C threefold M_D(T), a 6x6 Pfaffian identity det(SJ) = Pf(SJ)^2 = det(S) d^3 rules out U(2,1) Picard subgroups, a rational-group Goursat proof gives H = T_{K_1} x U_D(V_2, T_2)^0 when d_+ = 1, and a Hyperoctahedral B_3 Galois theorem proves full CM torus T_K when d_+ = 0. (2) Kunneth slant maps S_{1,2} and S_{1,3} surject onto Q_D^(2)(B_2) and Q_D^(3)(B_3), proving the Hodge conjecture for all powers of every member of the T_0 family with zero exceptions. (3) An explicit 2x2 polynomial matrix M(-t_1) in GL_2(D_2) splits <i, i> into <-t_1, t_1> inside T_1, proving via S_{1,3} the unconditional Hodge conjecture for all powers of every member across compact PEL sixfolds T_3(t_1) = <t_1, i, i+2j> for all square-free Delta_3 != 3 (mod 8) with Q(sqrt(-Delta_3)) not in D_2. (4) Explicit matrices P in GL_3(D_2) and P_4 in GL_4(D_2) certify the FF_3 isometries.
