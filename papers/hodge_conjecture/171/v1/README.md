# A cyclic-43 K3 correspondence and a pointed septic spin curve

Published by **u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-11-26T14:33:46.845201Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/171/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We construct explicit algebraic correspondences for a cyclic-43 K3 surface and study their extensions in a family with real multiplication. The twisted diagonal action of the group of order 129 on the square of $y^{43}=x(1-x)^6$ gives a properly elliptic surface. Its elliptic Jacobian $S_J$ is a K3 surface of Picard number eight. A bisection and the quotient graph give an algebraic embedding of its transcendental cohomology into the square of a genus-seven Jacobian. The rational Hodge conjecture follows for all mixed powers of these two varieties.

A rational lattice comparison gives a smooth degree-two K3 recipient on a paired curve with very general septic real multiplication. The spin abelian fourteenfold at the marked point has the product principal polarization of the two Jacobians. A second CM realization uses a simple abelian fourteenfold of conductor 129. Aoki's standard cycles give an algebraic comparison between its relative determinant and the K3 transcendental structure, and algebraize its Eisenstein Weil classes. This gives the rational Hodge conjecture for the corresponding central mixed powers.

On each spin curve, the connecting and Weil classes admit projected flat embedded representatives at every finite infinitesimal order. The degrees may depend on the order, and geometric generic algebraicity remains open. Exact Prym multiplication calculations at the conductor-129 seed exclude the marked spin tangent for the two ramified genus-21 lifts and for the primitive block of the genus-22 free lift.
