# Accept

This revision makes the corrections requested in the previous review and preserves the accepted contribution and its scope. It introduces no new substantive result, unsupported stronger claim, or material loss of clarity.

The corrected Hirzebruch–Jung chains in Section 2.2 are consistent with the surrounding geometry. Independent exact rational calculations give $43/2=[22,2]$, $43/12=[4,3,2,3]$, and $43/29=[2,2,15]$, with discrepancy contributions $-800/43$, $-118/43$, and $-507/43$, respectively. Including the order-three singularities recovers $K_{\widetilde Z}^2=-9$ and the exceptional Euler increment of 15. Thus the correction repairs the table without changing the numerical conclusions or the contraction paths used in the accepted argument.

The citation corrections appropriately identify Shioda–Katsura [30] for the Fermat blowup construction, Bloch–Esnault–Kerz [8] for Chern-character horizontality, Aoki [31] for the standard cycles, and Aoki [19] for the primitive-triple hypothesis. They repair attribution rather than substitute new mathematical inputs.

The revised closing paragraph of the determinant appendix resolves the previous ambiguity. The central left $K$-action has six mixed places and one definite place, whereas the right $K'=F\mathbf Q(\zeta_3)$-action retained in the second spin family has one mixed place and six definite places. This agrees with the sign calculations and construction already present in Section 7. Explicitly stating that the left action need not remain Hodge along the deformation prevents the central determinant comparison from being mistaken for a family with that fixed action.

The manuscript remains organized around the explicit K3 correspondences, their spin families, and finite-order realizations. The revisions improve that explanation without expanding it into additional results. Algebraicity of the geometric generic connecting and Weil classes remains open; the finite-order constructions still supply no uniform degree bound or generic cycle. Acceptance therefore continues to concern the stated partial results, not a solution of the general Hodge conjecture.
