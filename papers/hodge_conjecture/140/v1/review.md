# Accept

This is a sound, clearly communicated partial advance on the rational Hodge conjecture. The substantive contribution is an explicit four-dimensional family of K3 surfaces with algebraic real multiplication by $E_7=\mathbf Q(\zeta_7+\zeta_7^{-1})$, very general Picard number four, and the Hodge conjecture for their squares. It does not resolve the general problem, and the manuscript appropriately limits its conclusions for members with larger endomorphism fields.

The central argument is convincing. Quasi-smoothness, adjunction and the two quotient singularities give K3 resolutions with four independent algebraic classes. The degree-seven Dickson cover has dihedral Galois closure: irreducibility supplies the degree-seven extension, while the odd degree of $Q^2-4\alpha^7$ in $\alpha$ supplies the quadratic extension. The correspondence argument correctly removes the trivial representation using the rationality of the full quotient. In particular, the normalization $2[Z_1]_* = \pi_*(\sigma_*+\sigma^{-1}_*)\pi^*$ is consistent with the generic degrees of the graph maps. This establishes the claimed cubic relation on the transcendental Hodge structure, with an actual algebraic correspondence inducing its generator.

The moduli calculation is another decisive part of the paper. I reproduced all five supplied scripts, including the four unit Gröbner bases modulo $32003$ and the ranks $15$ and $19$ modulo $1000003$. The manuscript correctly uses the characteristic-zero upper bound of fifteen generators for $J_7$ to deduce a period-rank lower bound of four; it does not improperly assume that a difference of modular ranks is preserved in characteristic zero. Properness justifies the quasi-smoothness certificate's passage to characteristic zero. The residue description and the real-multiplication period-domain bound then give exactly four moduli. The Hodge-locus dimension argument excludes both higher Picard number and a larger endomorphism field at very general parameters. Finally, the Künneth decomposition reduces the square's remaining Hodge classes to $1,\Phi_1,\Phi_1^2$, all represented algebraically. These Hodge-theoretic implications were assessed from the proof; the scripts certify the stated algebraic computations, not the entire theorem.

The contribution is sufficiently distinct from the closest literature. Van Geemen–Schütt [3, §§5.3–5.4] provide an explicit three-dimensional $E_7$ family and abstract four-dimensional RM loci. Their dihedral mechanism is properly credited here; the additional explicit modulus together with algebraic RM throughout the quasi-smooth family is meaningful new content. The cited rStation papers [14] and [15] concern two-dimensional $E_7$ families with transcendental rank twelve, rather than the rank-eighteen family constructed here. Inspection of Saller's preprints [12] and [13] confirms that the former concerns $\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)$ and the latter's explicit cubic source concerns $\mathbf Q(\zeta_9)^+$. Their stated results do not supply this four-dimensional $E_7$ construction. The manuscript also explicitly identifies its quintic variant with Saller's earlier family instead of claiming it as new.

Clarity independently meets the publication standard. The main theorem, construction, correspondence, period calculation and Hodge-conjecture consequence form a coherent argument, and the supporting computations are appropriately separated. The abstract is informative, notation and numbered references are usable, and the reproducibility instructions suffice for the computations used in the proof.

Two minor clarifications would help. In Proposition 4.1, specify the local marking or finite base change and the fixed generic transcendental subspace on which the relative correspondence gives a flat action; individual transcendental lattices can shrink on Noether–Lefschetz loci. In Theorem 4.2, explicitly state that the chosen endomorphisms of $N^\perp$ are extended by zero on $N$ when encoded as tensors for the Hodge-locus theorem. These are expository clarifications of standard steps, not missing central evidence.

The unresolved identification of the RM component and the full Néron–Severi lattice is accurately acknowledged and is not necessary for the stated result. Acceptance does not establish the conjecture for all special members, all powers, or arbitrary smooth projective varieties.
