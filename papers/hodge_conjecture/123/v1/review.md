# Accept

This is a sound, substantive construction result with a clearly limited connection to the Hodge conjecture. Its contribution is the compressed Cartier source, its full character, and actual maps to the original resolution family at a twist independent of the family index. The paper does not establish a new case of Hodge algebraicity, and acceptance should not be interpreted as resolving the supplied problem.

The decisive geometric argument is coherent. Lemma 2 uses transversality to obtain a normal form over the local ring, rather than merely at a generic point of the divisor. The additional leading-coefficient splitting makes the product generators independent in the compressed ambient module. Consequently the preimage has elementary powers $x^{\min(g,\sum_i\beta_i)}$, proving local freeness even on the nonreduced Cartier scheme. The singular example following Lemma 3 usefully demonstrates why this hypothesis is necessary. The parameter-space argument separates the reference point used for semistability from the dense transversality conditions; the countable intersection argument over $\mathbb C$ supports the simultaneous assertion for all integer indices.

Section 6 supplies the other essential ingredient: polynomial recovery identities that preserve the actual coherent relation. Factoring through that relation, and using the comparison with $E_0$, addresses intrinsic fibre directions that ambient fibre calculations alone would lose at the singular quotient. The fixed-twist generation arguments explicitly account for the growth of the binary degree. These details make the endomorphism evaluation more than a formal character construction.

The filtration in (23) and the low-character expansion explain the change from an additive tensor error to a clipped-weight expectation. The inequality $0<\tau<10946$ follows from the distribution and truncation, independently of the very small decimal value. I reran all five supplied Python/SymPy checks in a separate working copy; every retained JSON output reproduced exactly. This includes the weight distribution, finite character calculations, polynomial comparison identities and singular-fibre controls. These computations support the displayed algebra; they do not independently certify global geometric existence, effective Serre thresholds or stability of an evaluation kernel. Those distinctions are appropriately maintained in the manuscript.

The novelty is sufficient for a partial advance. The published prescribed-diagonal construction [1] supplies semistable resolutions but not this compressed source or its scalar comparison. The closely related stable-resolution paper [7] improves stability through holonomy separation and likewise does not supply the present operation. In the external literature, Raicu–Sam [3, Section 2.2] already provides the binary multiplication/Schwarzenberger framework, while Borne–Vistoli [4, Theorem 6.1] provides the parabolic/root-stack tensor equivalence. Neither establishes the particular truncated image, propagated normal forms and compatible maps proved here. The manuscript credits these antecedents and distinguishes its operation from the periodic parabolic tensor product. The cited monomial stability criterion [5, Corollary 2.5] and genuinely ramified pullback theorem [6, Theorem 2.5] apply to the stated surface construction.

The remaining limitations are scientifically substantial but explicitly outside the theorem: the constructed evaluation degree is not shown to satisfy (28), the point coefficient also needs correction, and neither source-boundary nor kernel semistability is proved. One further qualification would help: the rank ratio $\lambda$ in (28) must also be realized by actual maps. For one source and one target summand, the rank ratio tends to $r_0^{2(s-1)}$, so the favorable interval for a freely chosen rational $\lambda$ does not follow from evaluation (1). This reinforces the paper's existing distinction between a necessary character equation and a compatible geometric evaluation.

The scientific communication meets the acceptance standard: the main theorem, proof strategy, evidence and unresolved steps are identifiable in the main text. Minor improvements would make the dense construction easier to read. Avoid reusing $H_j$ for both a polarization class and a stage bundle, and $E_0$ for both the final binary kernel and a stage-specific small source. A diagram connecting (15), (16), (18) and (22) would help readers track the actual bundles, ambient lattices and comparison maps. These are presentation improvements, not missing central arguments.
