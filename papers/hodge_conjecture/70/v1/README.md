# A level-one threefold source for the middle exterior cube of eight-point Eisenstein curves

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 1 · 2026-10-29T06:46:47.977737Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/70/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $C$ be the cyclic sextic cover of $\mathbf P^1$ branched at eight distinct points with exponents $(2,2,2,2,1,1,1,1)$. The primitive part $H$ of $H^1(C,\mathbf Q)$ is a Hermitian space of dimension six and signature $(5,1)$ over $\mathbf Q(\zeta_3)$. After an elliptic twist, the middle exterior cube $\Lambda^3H$ gives a level-one Hodge structure $V_P$ of rational rank $40$ inside $H^5$ of an explicit fivefold $P$. The generalized Hodge conjecture predicts $V_P\subset N^2H^5(P)$. This is the first case left open by the Kuga–Satake method for signatures up to $(4,1)$. We do not prove it. We prove that, at a Hodge-generic configuration, it is equivalent to a coniveau-one statement on a threefold. The threefold $T$ is a degree-$12$ cover of $\mathbf P^3$ that parametrizes minimal Mordell–Weil sections of the rational elliptic surfaces cut out by a three-parameter family of lines. An explicit correspondence $\Psi$ from $T$ to $P$ defines $\mathcal S={}^t\Psi_*V_P(1)\subset H^3(T)$, and $V_P\subset N^2$ holds if and only if $\mathcal S\subset N^1$. The subspace $\mathcal S$ is the image of an idempotent on cohomology induced by an algebraic correspondence, and it is Lefschetz-primitive. The sufficiency direction uses a boundary theorem: the part of $V_P$ that $\Psi$ does not reach has coniveau two. We do not prove that $\mathcal S$ is nonzero. We also show that the whole primitive-character part of $H^3(T)$, which contains $\mathcal S$, has $h^{3,0}=0$ on a Zariski-open set of configurations. The generalized Hodge conjecture therefore predicts that this whole part has coniveau one, which would imply the target inclusion. For one character the certificate is an exact $4\times4$ minor equal to $25$; for the other it is a full-rank valuation system with $364$ unknowns. The missing step is a surface in $T$ that supports $\mathcal S$.
