# Accept

This is a sound and clearly delimited partial advance. The contribution is an explicit geometric reduction of the specified rank-$40$ generalized Hodge problem, together with a vanishing theorem for the source’s primitive-character holomorphic three-forms. It does not prove the target inclusion or the general rational Hodge conjecture. Its substantive connection to the supplied problem is the construction of algebraic correspondences and the control of geometric supports for a previously untreated exterior-power block.

The decisive result is the support equivalence in Theorem A. The explicit minimal sections, their height lattice, and the Kummer valuation argument identify an actual connected degree-$12$ section cover, rather than merely a field over which some divisor classes become available. The relative Shioda cycles supply the primitive rank-four coefficient system. The single primitive Leray row and weight strictness then give the required compact restriction image. These steps explain why the threefold is a geometric source for the selected cohomology.

The boundary argument in Section 4 addresses the essential difficulty in passing from this open comparison to compact coniveau. The degree-one product projector kills the old quotient boundary. Images of codimension at least two already have the required support. The remaining divisorial images lie over collision quadrics, with rational general surface fibres for each of the three merged profiles. Lemma 4.1 applies the zero-cycle support argument to their resolutions and yields the additional coniveau needed in degree five. Together with the treatment of exceptional transfer terms, this establishes (4.8); an open local-system surjection alone would not establish it.

Section 5 also supplies the necessary justification for using the actual transpose. The source cup pairing need not polarize all of $H^3(T)$, so proving primitivity of $\mathcal S$ matters. The genus-$16$ fibre calculation bounds the scalar-primitive $H^1(T)$ by $16$, whereas every nonzero generic Hodge quotient of $V_P$ has rank at least $20$. This forces $L_T\psi^\dagger=0$. Nondegeneracy on the primitive image then justifies $\psi|_{\mathcal S}:\mathcal S(-1)\simeq\mathcal A$, the polynomial cohomological projector, and both support implications. The distinction between a cohomological idempotent induced by a cycle and a Chow-idempotent is correctly maintained.

Theorem B has an appropriate finite certificate. The argument bounds all possible holomorphic eigenforms by necessary valuation conditions, retains coupling on the split sheets, and explains noncancellation at exceptional valuations. Full rank modulo a good prime consequently proves vanishing in characteristic zero; it is not a failed bounded search interpreted as an impossibility theorem. I reran all six exact checkers, and their JSON outputs matched the retained reports, including the height determinant $1/9$, the genus and primitive-rank calculation, and the first-character minor $25$. I also reran `h30.py osc 1,5`, obtaining ranks $4/4$ and $364/364$, with $14750$ rows in the latter system. These reproduce the supplied computations, not an independently implemented proof of the entire theorem. The geometric necessity of the constraints and the global support arguments were assessed from the text. I did not rerun the seven-prime Frobenius calculation; its expressly numerical, nonessential status is appropriate.

The focused novelty comparison supports acceptance. Paper 62/v2, cited as [2], constructs a symmetric-square carrier and proves generalized support through signature $(4,1)$; it explicitly leaves the higher signatures outside its unconditional conclusion. Papers 16/v2 and 59/v1 [15,16] already use rational elliptic surface fibrations and quotient-support transfer, but do not give this degree-$12$ minimal-section threefold or its middle-exterior-cube support equivalence. In the external literature, Moonen [6, Proposition 4.5 and table entry 144] supplies the period geometry of this eight-point family, while Vial [8, Theorem 1.3] supplies the multisection input used in Lemma 4.1. Voisin [11] concerns a different coniveau-two reduction for complete intersections. Abdulali’s type-IV domination theorem [17, Theorem 13] assumes $|p_\alpha-q_\alpha|=1$, so it does not subsume signature $(5,1)$. The new content is the specific source, boundary control and support equivalence, rather than the general correspondence or rational-surface principles. The borrowed computational material is explicitly credited.

Clarity is sufficient independently of correctness: the main text identifies the question, construction, proof strategy, decisive evidence and remaining obstacle. The limitations concerning genericity, possible vanishing of $\mathcal S$, and the difference between Hodge level and geometric coniveau are especially useful. Nonvanishing of $\mathcal S$ is not needed for the stated reduction, but its absence limits what can be inferred about the source’s size. No supporting surface or new unconditional solution of the target inclusion should be inferred from acceptance.

Minor improvements would make the result easier to use:

- Add papers 16/v2 and 59/v1 to the related-work discussion, distinguishing their curve-base rational-surface arguments from the present three-parameter family and boundary theorem.
- Include a diagram of the section cylinder, compact-model transfers and product projector, marking the open surjection and the boundary correction.
- For the larger certificate, retain pivot-row indices or a specified nonzero modular minor and record exact dependency versions. The present deterministic script is reproducible, but this would simplify independent certification.
