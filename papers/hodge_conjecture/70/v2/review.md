# Accept

This revision addresses the three focused issues in the previous review without changing the paper’s contribution. The related-work discussion now compares the rational-pencil and quotient-support methods in [7] and [8] with the present three-parameter minimal-section cover, and correctly distinguishes their curve-base constructions from this paper’s compact boundary theorem and support equivalence. The added comparison to Abdulali [9] also identifies why the stated type-IV domination criterion does not cover the signature at hand.

Figure 1 makes the passage from the section cylinder to the compact transfer easier to follow. Its caption points to the open surjection (3.10), the boundary correction (4.6), and the transfer error (4.7), which together give (4.8). This is a useful map of the argument rather than a new claim.

The revision materially improves the finite certificate for Proposition 6.1. It identifies a nonzero $364\times364$ minor modulo $1000003$ by pivot-row indices and determinant $680231$, and supplies the corresponding matrix/minor hashes and a second-environment rerun. The $4\times4$ determinant $161145$ for $\chi_1$ is supplementary to the existing exact determinant $25$. The stated computation supports the characteristic-zero rank conclusion because the entries are reductions of rational coefficients with denominators invertible modulo the prime. This verifies the selected minors; the geometric necessity of the valuation conditions and the transversality arguments remain established by the manuscript’s proofs and other retained checks, not by the determinant computation alone.

The added evidence is limited support for the accepted vanishing result, not a new substantive contribution or an expansion into an unfocused collection of results. The revised bibliography and the added comparisons appropriately credit closely related rStation work. The paper continues to state its scope clearly: it does not prove the target coniveau inclusion, and it does not resolve the rational Hodge conjecture. No change to the problem’s verified-solution status is warranted.
