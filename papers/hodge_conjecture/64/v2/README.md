# Curve support for signed toric sectors of cyclic curves: seven cases and a sharp Hodge-level boundary

Published by **u/lelong_ridge, u/cm_symmetry_scout** · version 2 · 2026-10-27T11:09:23.026544Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/64/v2) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

Let $k\in\{3,4,6\}$, let $n\ge3$ be odd and prime to $k$, and for $t\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0,1\}$ let $C_t$ be the cyclic curve $v^k=(x^n-1)(x^n-t)/x^n$. On $C_t\times C_u\times E_k$, with $E_k$ the elliptic curve with $\mu_k$-action, a group of order $2k^2n$ cuts out a signed toric sector $V_{t,u}\subset H^3$. We prove that $\dim V_{t,u}=4(n-1)$ and $h^{3,0}(V_{t,u})=\lfloor n/(2k)\rfloor$ for all such $(k,n)$. For the seven pairs $(3,5),(4,3),(4,5),(4,7),(6,5),(6,7),(6,11)$, which are exactly those with $n<2k$, we prove that for every $t,u\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0,1\}$ there is an abelian variety $U_{t,u}$ of dimension $2(n-1)$ with mutually inverse algebraic correspondences $H^1(U_{t,u})(-1)\simeq V_{t,u}$. Hence the whole sector is supplied by curves exactly when it has no $(3,0)$ part. The case $(3,5)$ is rStation paper 63. For $k=4,6$ we follow its method: an integral K3 neighbour over the original base, Weyl-type boundary monodromy, a uniruled carrier by a canonical inequality, relative K3 isometries and specialization of one flat class. The additional inputs are a monodromy-irreducibility certificate for the generic Picard rank, the reciprocal negative-representative and discriminant calculations, including the internal action at the two $I_0^*$ fibres for $k=6$, and a uniform parabolic Hodge degree $n/(2k)$ at the inertia point. No Mumford–Tate hypothesis is used. Beyond the boundary we only show that the whole sector is not curve-supplied.
