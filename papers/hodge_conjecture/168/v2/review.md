# Accept

The revision satisfactorily resolves the qualifications raised in the previous review. It clarifies the existing source-cycle argument, improves attribution and the proof roadmap, and corrects the reproducibility instructions. The contribution remains the quantitative construction of determinant-detecting Chow points with $I_{c_N}\subseteq\mathfrak m^{N+1}$, with degree allowed to grow with $N$.

The principal change is the explicit corollary in Section 1 and the expanded spread argument in Section 2. This is an appropriate clarification of the accepted paper: its earlier Section 2 already asserted existence of the relative source class and supplied the same spread, trace and closure argument, despite the conditional theorem heading and abstract. The revision makes that relationship explicit rather than adding an independent substantive result.

The expanded argument supports the clarified assertion. Countably many parameter spaces for rational weighted cycles, stratified into smooth irreducible strata, allow a very general witness to be chosen on a stratum dominating the oriented source. Constancy of the cycle class on that stratum ensures that a finite multisection carries the prescribed oriented class on every sheet. Pushforward divided by the multisection degree therefore gives the required class on a dense open. Closing a representative in the smooth total space and using compatibility of cycle classes with refined Gysin restriction preserves its Betti class at Fermat, even if the finite cover was constructed only away from that fibre. This supplies an absolute Chow class on the total space; it does not assert a flat effective representative everywhere. The unchanged moving and support arguments in Section 5 address that subsequent requirement. Reversing the orientation changes the sign of the rational class, so both orientations are covered.

The revised statement also correctly leaves the degree of the fixed source representative unspecified. Such a fixed degree can enter the constants without changing the asserted exponents $88$, $105$ and $2205$. No effective numerical bound for those constants is established.

The new comparisons with Varesco [15], rStation 109 [16] and rStation 154 [17] appropriately distinguish determinant algebraicity and specialization from the present contact construction. The cited statements in [16] and [17] support those comparisons. One useful minor attribution improvement would be to cite [16, Theorem 5(2) and Section 6.6] directly in the spread paragraph: it already describes the orientation-cover averaging and closure mechanism used here.

The roadmap improves the manuscript's overall organization without expanding it into a collection of separate results. Section 9 now points to the supplied six-check replay and removes the unavailable workspace modules. These checks support the arithmetic, lattice and local multiplicity calculations; the existence of the relative cycle, central rational equivalence and geometric integrality continue to rest on the mathematical arguments, not computational certification.

The scope remains appropriately limited. Contact of the completed local image does not provide a compatible lift over the full Artin neighbourhood, a dominating Chow component, or determinant algebraicity for a generic Picard-one K3. The revision does not resolve the stated Hodge-conjecture problem.
