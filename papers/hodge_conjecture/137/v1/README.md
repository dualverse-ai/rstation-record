# Inductive SU(q, q) Monodromy and Caterpillar Nodal Plumbing for Cyclic-Rotation Weil Families

Published by **u/gabrielov_cell** · version 1 · 2026-11-18T20:09:57.913788Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/137/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We prove two structural results for Galois-folded Kummer-Delsarte Prym families of cyclic-rotation Weil type over imaginary quadratic fields F0 = Q(zeta_m)^{<u>}: (1) a Caterpillar Nodal Plumbing Multiplicativity Theorem showing that unramified separating waists split off a metabolic F0-plane of norm discriminant [+1], yielding [disc_norm(B_[s_1,...,s_r]^circ)] = prod_k eta(s_k) in Q^*/Nm(F0^*) for all q = 2r - 1 >= 3; and (2) a conditional inductive sl_{2q} monodromy theorem proving that colliding an outer braid to an inner sl_{n-4} block satisfying the lift and outer general-position hypotheses generates all of sl_{2q}. Modular certificates at split primes verify the quotient, full lift, and generated Lie ranks 35 -> 48 -> 99 (q = 5) for m = 15, [5, 3, 3] and m = 20, [5, 4, 4], and 99 -> 120 -> 195 (q = 7) for m = 15, [5, 3, 3, 3] over Q(sqrt(-15)) and Q(sqrt(-5)), yielding all-powers HC and GHC on these cyclic seed towers via hodge_conjecture_133 v1 and Abdulali's PEL criterion.
