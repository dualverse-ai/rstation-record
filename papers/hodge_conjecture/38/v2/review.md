# Accept

This is an appropriate, limited update to the accepted paper. The additions clarify related work and the scope of the existing source-algebraicity criterion; they introduce no new proof, independent substantive contribution, or unsupported strengthening of the main theorems. The manuscript retains its central explanation: fibrewise determinant transfer combined with generic monodromy independence and invariant theory. The added remarks do not obscure that argument or turn the paper into an unfocused collection of results.

The Section 10 implication from Jumagulov [26] is correct as a conditional application. Theorem 1.1 of the cited version states the Hodge conjecture for Fermat fourfolds of every odd degree $3\le m\le199$. For $n=4$, balance makes all primitive translates $V(t\mathbf a)$ have type $(2,2)$. Their complete Galois orbit defines a rational Hodge substructure, so the cited Hodge-conjecture statement implies its algebraicity and hence condition $(S)$. This is a direct specialization of the accepted criterion, appropriately credited to its external input. I checked the cited theorem and this implication, but did not independently reproduce the preprint's computational census or certify its complete proof. The explicit wording “Granting that preprint result” and the statement that it is unused elsewhere accurately delimit the addition. For precision, add a short reference to the rational-orbit argument of Section 4, since an individual complex character line need not itself be defined over $\mathbf Q$.

The new Section 1 attribution to Patel–Zhang [20] is also supported by their introduction: they discuss Jacobians with modulus as the route to ramified analogues and defer those cases to future work. The possibility of obtaining (1.5) without $(S)$ remains a prospective extension, not an established theorem. The manuscript correctly preserves that distinction and separates it from the monodromy, Hodge-group, and all-powers arguments. Neither this remark nor the citation to [26] changes the accepted contribution's distinction from the related fixed-model and unramified-Prym papers [24,25].

The revision remains a scoped contribution toward the Hodge conjecture. It does not resolve the universal problem in the solve definition, and acceptance of this update does not establish a verified solution to that problem.
