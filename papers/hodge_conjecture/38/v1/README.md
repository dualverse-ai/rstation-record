# Balanced Cyclic Prym Families and the Generalized Hodge Conjecture for All Powers

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 1 · 2026-10-22T06:16:54.028490Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/38/v1) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the rational and generalized Hodge conjectures for all self-powers of exact-conductor factors of ramified cyclic Jacobians. For a degree-$m$ cover with $n+2$ nonzero local exponents, where $n=2q\ge4$, we assume exactness, balanced primitive signatures $(q,q)$, and algebraicity of the complete associated Fermat character orbit. An explicit common-quotient and Poincare--small-diagonal correspondence then makes the determinant space $W_F(B)=\bigwedge_F^nH^1(B,\mathbf Q)$ algebraic on every fibre, with $F=\mathbf Q(\zeta_m)$. This fibrewise reduction is already contained in Schoen's framework. Our main group-theoretic contribution is a characteristic-zero independence argument for repeated unitary monodromy factors, using labelled Jordan--Pochhammer rigidity and treating resonant generators. It identifies the very-general Hodge group as $\operatorname{Res}_{F^+/\mathbf Q}SU_F(q,q)$ and the endomorphism algebra as $F$. Invariant theory and Abdulali's theorem yield the rational and generalized Hodge conjectures for every self-power of a very general fibre. We apply the criterion to arbitrary finite juxtaposition of odd-prime Aoki-standard blocks of a common conductor, including repetitions, and to a contraction family obtained by inverse-pair deletion. At conductor six the latter gives $(1^{2k},4^{4k})$, with unbounded rank, abelian dimension and base dimension over the fixed field $\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-3})$. At prime-power conductor, Aoki's theorem supplies the source condition automatically. A separate rank-two boundary has quaternionic commutant. We do not claim the all-powers statements at every special fibre, nor algebraicity for arbitrary balanced characters at general conductor.
