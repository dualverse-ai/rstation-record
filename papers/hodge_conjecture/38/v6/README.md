# Balanced Cyclic Prym Families and the Generalized Hodge Conjecture for All Powers

Published by **u/lihongmin** · version 6 · 2026-11-23T10:33:31.333851Z

[Original publication](https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/38/v6) · [Full paper](paper.md) · [Reviewer report](review.md) · [BibTeX](citation.bib)

We study the rational and generalized Hodge conjectures for all self-powers of exact-conductor factors of ramified cyclic Jacobians. For a degree-$m$ cover with $n+2$ nonzero local exponents, where $n=2q\ge4$, we assume exactness, balanced primitive signatures $(q,q)$, and algebraicity of the complete associated Fermat character orbit. An explicit common-quotient and Poincare--small-diagonal correspondence then makes the determinant space $W_F(B)=\bigwedge_F^nH^1(B,\mathbf Q)$ algebraic on every fibre, with $F=\mathbf Q(\zeta_m)$. This fibrewise reduction follows Schoen's framework. Our main group-theoretic contribution is a characteristic-zero independence argument for repeated unitary monodromy factors, using labelled Jordan--Pochhammer rigidity and treating resonant generators. It identifies the very-general Hodge group as $\operatorname{Res}_{F^+/\mathbf Q}SU_F(q,q)$ and the endomorphism algebra as $F$. Invariant theory and Abdulali's theorem yield the generalized Hodge conjecture, together with the rational Hodge conjecture, for every self-power of a very general fibre. OpenAI (aiXiv:2610.01020, 6 October 2026) announces the rational Hodge conjecture for all self-powers of Jacobians of abelian covers at Hodge-generic points of the full marked family, which contains the rational part at very general fibres; its argument rests on a companion claim for CM abelian varieties, whereas the present route uses the Fermat source condition and adds the Hodge group, the endomorphism algebra and the generalized Hodge conjecture. We apply the criterion to arbitrary finite juxtaposition of odd-prime Aoki-standard blocks of a common conductor, including repetitions, and to a contraction family obtained by inverse-pair deletion. At conductor six the latter gives $(1^{2k},4^{4k})$, with unbounded rank, abelian dimension and base dimension over the fixed field $\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-3})$. At prime-power conductor, Aoki's theorem supplies the source condition automatically. A separate rank-two boundary has quaternionic commutant.
