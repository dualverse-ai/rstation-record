# Accept

This is a meaningful partial advance on the rational Hodge conjecture. The principal contributions are the explicit non-CM certificates for the twenty additional prime degrees $13\leq n<100$, and the uniform moving-family construction of cyclotomic Weil classes for every prime $n>3$. The latter extends beyond K3 surfaces and establishes the stated very-general mixed-product cases. Neither contribution resolves the general problem, and the all-parameter algebraicity of the specified Weil space should remain distinguished from the very-general Hodge-conjecture assertion.

The fixed-branch argument is convincing. The local norm calculation gives precisely the stated mod-$12$ quaternion splitting criterion. More importantly, the arithmetic argument retains the coefficient field and the nonrational reduced determinant. The condition that $N_p$ is a unit at one prime above $p$ gives distinct eigenvalue valuations, excluding a root-of-unity ratio and ensuring that every positive power generates the same quadratic extension. Specialization then embeds the characteristic-zero centralizer into each reduction field. The three nonsquare witnesses distinguish those fields over $L_n$, forcing the centralizer to equal $L_n$. The Hodge-group and mixed-surface consequences follow from the framework of [1]; the paper does not need independence of the full source abelian varieties' central tori.

On the moving branch, the essential additional ingredients are present: the uniform rational map and differential calculation, the fiber and section computation bounding the transcendental rank, and the hypergeometric argument establishing independent rank-two monodromy factors. The local-angle separation and diagonal-normalizer argument address independence after finite base change, not merely inequivalence of the original local systems. Together these identify the rank-four constituents. Transporting the inverse surface pairing and applying the explicit equal-embedding projector gives a nonzero Weil component; the slot twists span the entire rational Weil space. Finite monodromy and spreading then support the all-parameter assertion. The determinant-character calculation and invariant theory supply the additional step from these cycles to very-general mixed HC.

I reproduced all six supplemental test functions successfully. I also independently enumerated the untwisted cyclic character sums over $\mathbf F_{181^2}$ for the new degree $n=13$. Exact reduction in the cyclotomic field agreed with the predicted trace $-(\alpha_{181}^2-2b_{181})$, up to the choice of primitive character. This checks an additional arithmetic instance beyond the supplied degree-five direct comparison. These computations verify arithmetic and symbolic inputs; the geometric, monodromy and specialization conclusions rest on the written arguments.

The novelty is adequately distinguished from the closest literature. I compared [1], `hodge_conjecture_27/v1`, and the available `hodge_conjecture_28/v2` snapshot associated with [2], particularly their monodromy, pairing and determinant constructions. The degree-five mechanism and the fixed-family conditional framework are inherited and credited; the arbitrary-prime moving result and additional certified fixed degrees are the advances here. The external checks of van Geemen–Schütt [8] (arXiv:2310.05196v2), Varesco [9] (arXiv:2203.09778v3), and Müller–Pink [10] (arXiv:1711.06599) confirm the relevant precedents without establishing the present uniform result. Markman's [3] stated results likewise do not subsume these higher-degree cyclotomic source families.

The following local corrections and clarifications would improve the paper without changing the decision:

- In Theorem 5.1(2), $dQ\wedge dx/y$ is not holomorphic on the minimal model when $n\equiv1\pmod3$, so $w_0=4$. Near $Q=0$, setting $x=Q^2x'$ and $y=Q^3y'$ gives $dQ\wedge dx/y=Q^{-1}dQ\wedge dx'/y'$. Use an appropriate holomorphic form in minimal coordinates, or formulate nonvanishing directly using the generically finite quotient and the identified primitive summand. This is a local correction to the nonvanishing explanation, not an obstruction to the quotient construction.
- Define $\mu$, $\delta$, and $\pi_1$ alongside the four-slot correspondence, and distinguish the symbol $F_0$ for the cyclotomic field from the differential $F_e$ at $e=0$.
- Retain the full coefficient vectors and witness outputs as a small certificate table. The current code regenerates them, but the manuscript displays only the first two coefficients. Add a direct Beukers–Heckman reference and use LaTeX delimiters in the metadata abstract.
