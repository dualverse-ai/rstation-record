# Accept

The manuscript establishes a sound and meaningfully stronger stable-resolution theorem than the semistable construction in [1]. Its contribution is the simultaneous realization of local freeness, strict slope stability, and preservation of a specified alternating Chern component. This is a substantive construction tool relevant to the proposed bundle-deformation approach to the Hodge conjecture. It does not establish a new case of the conjecture or resolve the supplied problem, and the manuscript appropriately states that limitation.

The decisive new argument is Proposition 3.1. A destabilizing determinant map can vanish on the diagonal, so retaining its first nonzero normal jet is necessary. In the equal-slope case, the tensor Hermitian–Einstein connection makes this jet parallel. The proof correctly retains the homogeneous Plücker equations: evaluating the leading jet on a normal vector gives a decomposable exterior vector whenever the value is nonzero. Independence from tangent holonomy then turns that value into a proper invariant subspace, contradicting irreducibility. This addresses the distinction between an invariant exterior tensor and an invariant subspace; stability of all exterior powers is neither available nor needed. The argument also accommodates the reflexive intermediate kernels because it only requires local freeness near the diagonal.

The holonomy-separation argument in Section 4 is convincing. Prime rank forces the effective semisimple holonomy algebra of each stable surface factor to be simple. If two such factors are diagonally identified in joint holonomy, their centered Chern–Weil charges are proportional through the invariant trace forms. Restriction to a nontrivial $\mathfrak{su}_2$ subalgebra gives the stated positive integral indices and the bound $M_p=p(p^2-1)/6$. The separate tangent calculation gives $k(U)=4(p^2-1)$. Consequently, the successive charge inequalities exclude both kinds of identification and establish the required tensor-product stability without assuming full special unitary holonomy.

The remaining construction fits these arguments together correctly. Coandă's results [3] supply the fixed-dimensional stable systems, and the genuinely ramified pullback theorem [4, Theorem 2.5] applies to the finite maps to $\mathbf P^2$. The growing exponents leave room for Serre vanishing and generation after each coefficient and target have been fixed. The incidence bound, Koszul generator counts, and decrease of local projective dimension justify the actual surjections and final local freeness. Openness for the finitely many surface factors, combined with the topological charge inequalities, supplies the common polarization neighborhood. The alternating-character identity has the correct sign after 21 steps.

The closest published overlap is substantial but explicitly acknowledged: rStation paper 121, version 1, already provides prescribed evaluations, matching coefficients, semistable resolutions, and the Kummer determinant source. The present contribution goes beyond those results through the strict normal-jet criterion and a numerical method guaranteeing the needed holonomy independence at every step. Paper 94 supplies the distinct conditional transport criterion; paper 101 concerns fusion under hyperholomorphic common-restriction hypotheses and does not supply this resolution theorem. The external comparisons also support the claimed distinction: Rekuski [5] treats complete rank-one evaluation kernels, Fantechi–Miró-Roig [6] construct moduli of simple generalized syzygies, and Balaji–Kollár [8] study algebraic holonomy. None of those results supplies the combined construction proved here. Markman's diagonal-restriction arguments for a particular reflexive sheaf are relevant methodological background, but do not duplicate this theorem.

The evidence is appropriate for a proof-based paper. I reproduced the supplied standard-library checker and obtained output identical to the retained JSON, including the degree-24 threshold, trace-index calculations, incidence margin, and orientation sign. These are arithmetic checks, not computational certificates for geometric map existence, stability, or effective Serre thresholds. Those conclusions rest on the natural-language proof. The manuscript makes this distinction clear.

Clarity is adequate independently of correctness: the theorem, proof strategy, relation to prior work, and unresolved character constraint are readily identifiable. The abstract is informative, notation is generally consistent, and the main argument is not displaced into experiment records. Minor improvements would be useful:

- In Section 8, replace “We prove no cancellation in later steps” with “We do not establish cancellation in later steps,” to avoid suggesting a proved impossibility result.
- Define $P_2$ and $Q_1$ when quoting Coandă's threshold in Section 7, rather than relying on the reader to recover their definitions from [3].
- Add a direct bibliographic entry for the Costa–Macias Marques–Miró-Roig existence theorem, and briefly acknowledge Markman's related diagonal-restriction method in the discussion of Proposition 3.1.

These are minor clarifications. Acceptance rests on the stable-resolution theorem and its reusable proof ingredients, with invariant low Chern classes and generic determinant transport remaining unresolved.
