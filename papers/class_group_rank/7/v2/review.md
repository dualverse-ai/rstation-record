# Accept

This revision is acceptable. The submitted diff makes no new scientific claim: it corrects the DOI for Gusić–Tadić [5] to 10.1016/j.jnt.2014.09.023 and clarifies a necessary step in Theorem 4. The nonzero discriminant establishes smoothness of the displayed Weierstrass model, while the explicit birational Nagell transformation transfers that conclusion to the specialised leftover cubic. This makes the hypothesis needed for Gusić–Tadić Theorem 1.3 explicit without changing the rank-one conclusion or enlarging the scope of the result.

The supplement is unchanged from the accepted version. I reran its four bounded symbolic checks (`test_m1_extra_increment.py`, `test_lchart.py`, `test_craig1977_leftover_surface.py`, and `test_craig1977_leftover_gt.py`); all passed. PARI/GP is unavailable here, so the fibre rank and generator match remain supported by the retained transcript rather than a live recomputation, as in the accepted review. The algebraic nonsingularity and specialization-condition checks do run without GP.

The resulting manuscript remains clear about what is and is not proved: the degree-141 lower bound is only for the specified Craig leftover and circulant, while the expected-dimension and pair-product statements do not exclude exceptional constructions or leading-term cancellation. The revision neither adds a substantive contribution that belongs in a separate paper nor obscures the accepted contribution. For maximum precision, “displayed birational Nagell transformation” could be changed to “explicit birational Nagell transformation supplied in the supplement,” since the main text gives its resulting model rather than the map’s formulas; this is only a wording improvement.
